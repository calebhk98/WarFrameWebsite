## Overview

Titania is a fairy-themed warframe who embodies the regal enchantress archetype. Her strength lies in her exceptional crowd control, status effect immunity support, and unmatched aerial mobility through her Razorwing ability. With access to four distinct ability types and a passive that synergizes with ability spam, Titania excels in both solo and squad-based missions. Her small stature and flight capabilities grant her defensive advantages while her damage amplification through Lantern makes her valuable in endgame content.

Spellbind serves as her crowd-control workhorse, launching enemies into the air and preventing them from dealing effective damage. The status immunity component scales with ability strength and duration, making it invaluable for hard-hitting content where status effects pose a serious threat. The disarm effect is particularly useful against high-damage units like heavy gunners and commanders.

Tribute cycles through four distinct buffs with each cast. Thorns provides damage reduction for her and allies nearby, making her a formidable tank. Dust significantly weakens enemy accuracy, effectively disabling ranged threats. Full Moon enhances companion damage output for high-damage pet builds. Entangle crowd-controls multiple enemies simultaneously, making it her primary damage mitigation tool when stacked with other abilities.

Lantern is her signature damage amplification ability, marking a priority target that takes increased damage from all sources. The floating beacon attracts both enemies and allies, creating tactical opportunities for grouped damage. On detonation, the ability spreads multiple status effects to nearby enemies, making it synergistic with status-heavy weapons and mods. This ability is central to her endgame build viability.

Razorwing transforms Titania into a small, flying unit with automatic razorfly attacks that trigger additional damage pulses. The flight provides unmatched mobility and aerial positioning advantages, allowing her to dodge projectiles and access out-of-reach areas. Enemy damage taken increases significantly while airborne, making her nearly unkillable during proper execution. Duration and efficiency determine how long she can maintain flight.

## Passive

Titania's passive, Pixia, generates health for both herself and nearby allies whenever she casts an ability. This healing synergizes exceptionally well with ability-spam builds that favor strength and efficiency mods, turning her into a self-sustaining support warframe. The health generation triggers on all four abilities, meaning a properly built Titania can maintain group health through constant ability rotation.

## Acquisition

Titania is available as a quest reward from the Glassmaker quest, which unlocks after progressing through the star chart to access Zariman Ten Zero. Her relics are also farmable through Void Fissure missions at all tiers. Blueprint components drop from various Void Fissure enemy types, requiring players to run multiple fissure missions to accumulate all required pieces.

Building Titania requires 25,000 credits and 72 hours of build time. Obtaining her complete blueprint set through Void Fissure farming typically takes several hours of fissure runs, making the quest reward the primary early-access path for new players.

## Playstyle

Titania functions as a hybrid support and crowd-control frame with exceptional mobility. Her core playstyle revolves around cycling Tribute buffs while maintaining Spellbind on high-threat enemies and casting Lantern on priority targets. Once properly established, Razorwing becomes her primary offensive tool, handling trash clearing and evasion simultaneously. Advanced players maximize ability spam to trigger passive healing while using her small hitbox and flight as primary defensive tools.

Her aerial superiority in Razorwing mode makes her dominant in wide-open tilesets like Ceres and void spaces like void cascade missions. However, tight corridors and enclosed arenas can limit her flight potential, requiring playstyle adjustment toward ground-based ability usage. Proper positioning and energy management determine her effectiveness across mission types.

## Recommended builds

Titania supports multiple build archetypes depending on mission requirements and squad composition. Strength-efficiency builds maximize Lantern's damage amplification and Spellbind's immunity duration, making her a powerful support unit. Duration-heavy builds extend Razorwing flight time and Tribute buff persistence, turning her into a self-sufficient damage dealer. Hybrid builds balance strength, efficiency, and duration to maintain group utility while ensuring personal survivability.

Popular build variation includes incorporating status chance weapons to synergize with Lantern's damage amplification and detonation mechanic. Companion builds leverage Full Moon from Tribute to maximize pet damage output, creating a three-way damage source: Titania's razorflies, her weapon, and her enhanced companion.

## Lore

Titania was introduced in the Glassmaker quest as a powerful enchantress and regal figure within the Warframe universe. Her design draws from fairy and fae mythology, manifest in her small stature, wing-like armor accents, and ability to take flight. The lore surrounding her acquisition reveals a narrative focused on the Zariman and the mysteries contained within that void space.

Her aesthetic and abilities position her as a unique warframe that bridges magical theming with gameplay mechanics, standing apart from more militaristic frame designs. Community artwork and lore speculation explore her potential origins and connection to the broader Warframe mythology, though her official lore remains somewhat open to interpretation.
