## Overview

Gauss Prime is the Orokin-enhanced variant of Gauss, the speed-focused warrior archetype. Released in January 2024, Gauss Prime amplifies the base frame's kinetic mechanics with elevated shield, armor, and polarity flexibility. The frame embodies kinetic energy in concentrated form: "Quicksilver takes form. Gauss Prime brooks no talk of defeat." Prime variants do not fundamentally change the ability kit, but the stat bumps and extra polarity slot allow for more aggressive mod configurations without sacrificing survivability.

Gauss Prime excels in high-mobility missions where continuous movement is rewarded--survival, mobile defense, and fissure runs all leverage the frame's passive shield acceleration and speed bonuses. Unlike other mobility frames that rely on ability casts, Gauss Prime's battery system creates a feedback loop: staying active generates energy for ability use, while ability uptime feeds back into movement and shield strength. This creates a playstyle where standing still is actively discouraged; players who keep moving reap both offensive and defensive benefits.

The frame's signature weapons--Acceltra Prime and Akarius Prime--benefit from bonuses when Gauss is sprinting, reinforcing the "always moving" design philosophy. However, Gauss Prime remains flexible: players can trade some mobility for survivability through proper mod choices, making the frame viable across star chart and steel path content.

## Abilities

Mach Rush is Gauss Prime's primary mobility and offensive tool. At 25 energy per activation, it is extremely cost-efficient, especially when modded for efficiency. The ability deals Impact damage on contact with enemies (knocked down on hit) and generates a shockwave when colliding with environmental obstacles, dealing damage in a small radius. Each cast fills the battery, contributing to the passive's shield-recharge bonuses. The ability synergizes with the passive: as the battery fills, shield recharge becomes faster, allowing Gauss to take brief damage and recover health passively while sprinting between encounters.

In practice, Mach Rush acts as both gap-closer and utility clear. The low energy cost means it can be spammed in long missions without energy management concerns, especially with power efficiency mods. Pairing Mach Rush with Stretch or Augur Reach extends knockback range, making it a reliable crowd-control opener before ability combos.

Kinetic Plating is a toggle ability with zero energy cost--it drains energy only when active over time, consuming roughly 5 energy per second depending on mod choices. This unique energy mechanic makes it distinct from most toggle abilities: it is "free" to activate but has a running cost, rewarding players who activate it strategically rather than leaving it on permanently.

The core value is threefold: (1) damage-to-energy conversion--kinetic damage (Impact, Puncture, Slash, Heat, Cold, Blast) is partially converted back into energy, creating a net-positive energy loop in high-damage environments; (2) stagger immunity--no knockdown or stagger from any kinetic source while plating is active, crucial for maintaining positioning in chaotic fights; (3) scaling resistance--the damage reduction itself (not just conversion) scales with battery level, from roughly 10% at empty to 90% at full battery.

Battery management becomes critical here. A full battery grants near-invulnerability to kinetic damage; an empty battery provides minimal reduction. Pairing Kinetic Plating with quick battery-refill mechanics (Mach Rush spam, Thermal Sunder holds) keeps the defensive benefit maximized. High-armor Gauss Prime can layer plating reduction atop innate armor, achieving substantial effective health in armor-scaling builds.

Thermal Sunder is a dual-mode crowd control and battery utility. The toggle behavior differs from most Warframe abilities: a tap casts Cold burst (fill battery, inflict Cold status), while holding reverses the effect (drain battery, inflict Heat status). This mirrors Gauss Prime's elemental duality and offers tactical flexibility.

Tapping Thermal Sunder fills the battery, feeding both the passive's shield benefits and Kinetic Plating's scaling resistance. The Cold proc chain-slows enemies, creating breathing room in packed encounters. In endurance runs, chain-casting Thermal Sunder taps maintains both battery and crowd control simultaneously.

Holding Thermal Sunder does the inverse: it drains the battery to perform an AoE Heat burst. This sounds counterintuitive but serves a purpose: when the battery is full and abilities like Redline are on cooldown or reserved, dumping battery into Heat status can clear weak adds or break crowd control effects. Some endgame builds intentionally use Heat bursts as a tactical overflow mechanism.

Range mods (Stretch, Augur Reach) extend Thermal Sunder's footprint significantly (base 18m), making it a large-area status applier that synergizes with viral or corrosive priming before heavy-hitter abilities.

Redline is Gauss Prime's ultimate ability and the frame's primary damage amplification tool. At 100 energy, it is the most expensive ability on the kit, but its effects justify the cost. Redline requires the battery to be past the "redline" threshold (typically 50% or higher, scaling with mods like Power Strength) to activate and maintain overdrive.

Once active, Redline grants:
- Fire Rate + 300-500% (varies with mods and battery level)
- Attack Speed + 300-500%
- Reload Speed + 300-500%
- Casting Speed + 300-500%

For weapon-focused Gauss, the fire rate and reload bonuses turn even modest weapons into screen-clearing beams. For ability-focused builds, the casting speed lets ability loops (Mach Rush -> Thermal Sunder -> repeat) fire rapidly, cycling damage and battery effects.

Periodically during overdrive, arcing electricity erupts in a small AoE around Gauss. On deactivation, all accumulated arcs explode simultaneously, dealing electrical damage to nearby enemies. This makes Redline a dynamic ability: maintain it to stack arcs, then deactivate in a packed room for a burst of area control.

The battery requirement and energy cost demand planning. A fully modded Gauss Prime can keep Redline up for 6-8+ seconds per cast, but sustaining overdrive in long missions requires battery management. Some builds rely on Energize arcane or energy orb spawners; others use Redline as a boss-burst tool, reserving it for high-priority targets.

## Passive

**Battery Expansion**: Movement fills Gauss' battery with kinetic energy. Shields recharge up to 120% faster while the recharge delay is up to 80% shorter, scaled to the battery level.

Gauss Prime's passive is the bedrock of the playstyle. Unlike passives that grant flat bonuses, this one scales with activity: the more Gauss moves, the fuller the battery, and the stronger the passive effect. A stationary Gauss has a nearly empty battery and negligible recharge bonuses. A sprinting, wall-sliding, bullet-jumping Gauss has a full battery and near-instant shield recovery.

In practice, this passive makes Gauss Prime exceptionally tanky in mobile missions. During a survival or mobile defense, constant map traversal (even between combat) keeps the battery topped, ensuring shields recharge at superhuman speeds between waves. Combined with Kinetic Plating's scaling resistance and intrinsic 185 armor, Gauss Prime can outlast much tankier frames through sheer shield-uptime optimization.

The passive also rewards aggressive play: staying in combat (where movement is most intense) feeds the battery, which strengthens defensive abilities, which allows closer enemy engagement, which generates more movement--a virtuous cycle. Passive players struggle; active players thrive.

| Armor         | 185   |

Gauss Prime's stat profile favors shields and speed over raw health. At rank 0, the frame is fragile; at rank 30, the 550 shield pool is one of the highest in the game (exceeding tanky frames like Rhino by raw numbers, though Rhino's armor scaling is higher). The 185 armor sits in the mid-range--not exceptional by itself but sufficient to reduce incoming damage by ~30% and pairs well with Kinetic Plating's scaling resistance.

The standout stat is Sprint Speed at 1.5x, the fastest in the game and the enabling factor for Gauss Prime's playstyle. No other frame matches this mobility; combined with bullet-jump and wall-slide mechanics, Gauss Prime can cross entire tilesets in seconds, opening scouting and escape options unavailable to slower frames.

Energy at 175 is above average, supporting ability rotations without excessive mod investment in Primed Flow. The base stat is sufficient for solo play; squad synergies (Trinity energy support, Zenurik focus) mitigate energy scarcity in endurance runs.

## Acquisition

Gauss Prime is obtained through Void Fissure missions via relics. The frame's acquisition is distributed across four relic eras to encourage variety in fissure farming:

- **Blueprint**: Axi B7, Lith N16, Neo M5, Neo T9 (Uncommon rarity)
- **Neuroptics**: Axi P9, Meso H5, Meso T7, Neo E4 (Uncommon rarity)
- **Chassis**: Lith M9, Meso P15, Neo O2, Neo P7, Neo W1 (Uncommon rarity)
- **Systems**: Lith G12, Lith G9, Meso G7, Neo G8 (Rare rarity--bottleneck)

The Systems component is the primary bottleneck, dropping at Rare instead of Uncommon. Efficient farming targets Radiant refinement to maximize Systems drop chance. Alternatively, players can trade platinum on the market for individual components (average 80-120 platinum per component as of May 2026).

Assembly requires 25,000 credits and 5 Orokin Cells, with a 72-hour build time. Orokin Cells drop from Ceres, Saturn, and Deimos missions; players farming relics incidentally obtain cells, minimizing backtracking.

## Playstyle

Gauss Prime rewards aggressive, mobile gameplay. The optimal playstyle is:

1. **Engage with Mach Rush**: Sprint into enemy packs, knocking them down and filling the battery. Repeat as needed, using the mobility to reposition between casts.
2. **Layer Crowd Control**: Use Thermal Sunder taps to Cold-proc nearby enemies, slowing advances and buying time for the primary damage phase.
3. **Activate Kinetic Plating**: Once in the thick of combat, toggle plating to convert incoming damage into energy and unlock near-immunity to stagger--essential for melee weapons or stationary ability setups.
4. **Redline for Burst**: When the battery is full and enemies are dense, activate Redline to boost fire rate and casting speed, then dump weapon or ability damage. Let the arc explosions clear the zone.
5. **Reposition with Dash**: Between waves, use Mach Rush to zip across the map, keeping the battery topped for passive shield boosts when damage resumes.

This playstyle is mission-dependent. In open-world or resource-heavy missions where stopping to farm is required, Gauss Prime's mobility becomes less of an advantage. However, in endless missions (survival, mobile defense, void cascade) where map control and constant engagement matter, Gauss Prime excels. The frame scales well into Steel Path thanks to high shields, armor, and passive healing through shield recharge; Redline's multiplicative damage also scales, making status-heavy weapons viable even against high-armor enemies.

Synergies with weapons:
- **Acceltra Prime / Akarius Prime**: Signature weapons gain reload-speed bonuses while Gauss is sprinting. Stack this bonus with Redline's casting-speed boost for unparalleled fire rate.
- **Glaive Prime / Glaives in general**: The 0% recharge-delay at full battery means instant melee recovery, enabling rapid combo chains.
- **Kuva weapons**: Corrosive or viral kuva weapons primed by Thermal Sunder's status procs then cleared by Redline-boosted fire rate. Synergy-heavy.

Synergies with mods:
- **Power Strength**: Each +10% strength increases Fire Rate and Attack Speed gains during Redline, multiplicative scaling. Aim for 200%+ strength in endgame builds.
- **Duration**: Extends Redline uptime and arc-explosion windows. Balanced builds run 100-150% duration.
- **Range**: Amplifies Thermal Sunder and Redline arc spread, essential for crowd-clearing. Stretch or Augur Reach are near-mandatory.
- **Efficiency**: Reduces Redline energy cost and Kinetic Plating drain. Critical for endurance missions.

## Recommended Builds

Gauss Prime supports multiple build archetypes depending on mission intent:

**Endurance Survivor Build** (Steel Path Survival):
- Focus on efficiency and range to keep Thermal Sunder + Kinetic Plating active indefinitely.
- Mods: Primed Flow, Primed Efficiency, Stretch, Augur Reach, Narrow Minded, Blind Rage, Umbral Intensify, Vitality.
- Arcanes: Energize + Blessing for squad sustain.

**Redline Burst Build** (Boss/Void Fissure):
- Maximize Power Strength to unlock Redline damage. Pair with crit-heavy weapon (Braton Prime, Acceltra Prime).
- Mods: Blind Rage, Umbral Intensify, Umbral Vitality, Primed Flow, Streamline, Narrow Minded, Power Drift, Augment (if applicable).
- Arcanes: Deadhead (on weapon) for crit-chance boost during Redline.

**Kinetic Plating Tank Build**:
- Layer armor mods (Steel Fiber, Iron Skin subsume) with Kinetic Plating for near-invincibility during toggle.
- Mods: Blind Rage (for plating scaling), Umbral Vitality, Steel Fiber, Primed Cont, Primed Flow, Streamline, Transient Fortitude, Narrow Minded.
- Arcanes: Warframe Arcane Guardian for constant +10% armor, stacking during uptime.

Each build hinges on the player's activity level: builds that require constant movement (Endurance) reward mobile play; burst builds (Redline) reward positioning and shot placement; tank builds reward defensive toggling. Gauss Prime's flexibility lies in modular ability customization rather than frame-switching.

## Synergies

Gauss Prime benefits from squad archetypes that enhance movement, energy, or offensive output:

- **Trinity**: Energy Vampire and Well of Life ensure infinite energy for Gauss, allowing unrestricted ability spam. Blessing provides damage reduction scaling, synergizing with Kinetic Plating.
- **Volt**: Speed buff from Volt stacks multiplicatively with Gauss's 1.5x sprint, enabling insane map traversal. Shields Scale further boosts shield recharge.
- **Harrow**: Energy regeneration and crit buffs pair with Redline's damage multipliers. Covenant's damage immunity covers Gauss' relative vulnerability to burst damage.
- **Nekros**: Desecrate ensures resource drops in extended runs; pairs with Gauss's wave-clear to farm efficiently.

Weapons with synergy:
- **Status primaries** (Toxic Lash, Envoy) benefit from Thermal Sunder's Cold priming, enabling viral/corrosive chains before Redline damage.
- **Crit melee** (Nikana Prime, Sydon) benefit from Redline's attack-speed boost and Warframe combo-multiplier preservation (due to instant shield recharge during active combat).

## Lore

Gauss Prime is the Orokin-enhanced form of Gauss, carrying the same kinetic-warrior archetype into Prime rarity. In the Warframe universe, Primes are ancient Orokin designs, predating modern Warframe manufacture by thousands of years. Gauss Prime's introduction in Update 35 suggests the frame was discovered in Orokin vaults, much like other Prime frames, lending credibility to the "golden age" narrative.

The lore codex entry--"Quicksilver takes form. Gauss Prime brooks no talk of defeat"--positions the frame as an unyielding force of motion. Unlike contemplative or controlled frames (Banshee, Octavia), Gauss Prime is raw kinetic energy, neither slowing nor yielding. The "no talk of defeat" line suggests resolve, an unwillingness to stay still long enough for defeat to catch up. This mirrors the mechanical design: staying mobile ensures safety and power; stopping invites danger.

The frame's speed theming aligns with Orokin aesthetics in the lore: the Orokin valued efficiency and perfection. A frame that achieves perfection through pure motion--never stopping, always optimizing movement--resonates with Orokin design philosophy. Gauss Prime embodies this ideal.
