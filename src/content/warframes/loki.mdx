## Overview

Loki is a deception-focused warframe specializing in stealth, crowd control, and tactical repositioning. His toolkit revolves around invisibility, decoys, and strategic disarmament, making him exceptionally potent for spy missions, defense objectives, and high-difficulty sorties where avoiding detection or controlling enemy positioning is essential. The trickster's abilities reward intelligent positioning and timing rather than raw damage output, encouraging players to outsmart enemies rather than outgun them. Loki represents the pinnacle of stealth gameplay in Warframe, combining evasion with crowd control utility that scales effectively into endgame content through proper modding.

With 180 health and 180 shields, Loki prioritizes mobility and evasion over survivability. His armor of 105 is modest, reflecting his design as a hit-avoidance frame rather than a tank. This defensive profile demands smart ability usage--players must rely on Invisibility and Switch Teleport to avoid damage rather than absorb it. The frame's energy pool of 175 and sprint speed multiplier of 1.25x support rapid repositioning and sustained ability spam when modded for efficiency. For players comfortable with tactical thinking and precise ability timing, Loki remains one of the most rewarding frames in the game, delivering exceptional utility across spy runs, rescue missions, and coordinated squad play where crowd control and stealth coordination determine success.

## Abilities

### Decoy

Loki's first ability conjures a holographic copy that instantly draws all nearby enemy attention away from the player. The decoy mimics Loki's appearance and current appearance mods, selling the illusion convincingly to enemies at range. Upon creation, the decoy absorbs a percentage of nearby enemy shields and health, granting Loki temporary overshields. When the decoy is destroyed or times out, it can detonate to stun enemies in a small radius. This ability is invaluable for bypassing security on spy missions, as the decoy will trigger alarms instead of the player, allowing careful disengagement before detonating at range. Modding for range increases the distance at which enemies notice and pursue the decoy, while duration extends the decoy's survival time.

### Invisibility

Loki's second ability envelops himself and nearby teammates in a cloak, rendering everyone invisible to enemy detection. During invisibility, Loki and allies can move freely without triggering alarms or enemy aggression, making this ability critical for spy vault infiltration. Any damage dealt to a squadmate breaks the invisibility effect immediately, creating risk-reward dynamics in coordinated play. Loki himself can remain invisible indefinitely if undamaged, though attacking or casting other abilities ends the cloak. This ability becomes a cornerstone of high-difficulty defense and mobile defense missions where staying undetected throughout prolonged engagements is paramount. Modding for strength increases the radius of ally protection, while duration extends the initial invisibility window before tactical repositioning.

### Switch Teleport

Loki's third ability performs an instantaneous position swap with a targeted enemy, teleporting Loki directly to the enemy's location while the enemy lands at Loki's original position. Both parties experience a brief stun on arrival, during which Switch Teleport can trigger finishers. The tactical value of this ability extends far beyond engagement--it allows Loki to teleport across map geometry, reach isolated objectives, and confuse enemy formations by separating high-priority targets from their squads. Enemies lose their equipped weapons upon switch, forcing them to search for new arms or rely on melee combat. Modding for range increases the maximum distance at which enemies can be targeted, while strength increases the stun duration on swap. Chaining Switch Teleport with careful aim allows traversal of entire mission areas while maintaining crowd control.

### Radial Disarm

Loki's ultimate ability projects a sphere of destructive energy in all directions, stripping all enemies within range of their ranged weapons. Affected enemies revert to melee combat, removing their most dangerous damage output and forcing tactical regrouping. Heavy units like bombards and gunners take significantly longer to locate and re-equip new weapons, creating extended vulnerability windows. This ability is the primary counter to ranged enemy spam in high-level survival and defense missions, instantly defanging entire enemy waves. Modding for range increases the radius of disarm effect, potentially covering entire rooms in extended missions. Modding for strength increases the rearm delay, allowing coordinated squad takedowns of disarmed targets before weapon recovery. In coordinated play, Radial Disarm enables squad-wide damage amplification builds by removing enemy crowd-control weapons and allowing frames like Saryn or Volt to deal uninterrupted damage.

## Passive

Loki's passive ability grants him the capacity to hang from vertical surfaces significantly longer than baseline warframes. This extended hang time facilitates parkour-based gameplay, allowing Loki to perch on walls for positioning, ledge camping in defense missions, or simply finding optimal angles for ability targeting. The passive enables unique movement patterns and sightlines that other frames cannot exploit, rewarding players who master Warframe's movement system with tactical advantages in objective-based missions. Combined with his sprint speed multiplier, Loki's passive encourages an acrobatic, high-mobility playstyle where vertical space control translates to tactical dominance.

## Acquisition

Loki's blueprint and components drop from Sargas Ruk, a Grineer assault commander encountered during assassination missions on Saturn (Tethys node). Like other early warframe bosses, Sargas Ruk requires dedicated farming to obtain all four component blueprints (blueprint, neuroptics, chassis, systems). The drop rates are consistent for early-game acquisition but require multiple runs to guarantee complete component collection. The crafting cost is modest: 15,000 credits plus standard foundry materials including polymer bundles, neural sensors, and plastids. The full frame takes 72 hours to build once all materials are collected. New players typically farm Loki during early star chart progression around Mastery Rank 2-4 on Saturn, making it an accessible acquisition for building a utility-focused frame arsenal early in progression.

## Playstyle

Loki excels in missions requiring stealth, objective protection, or crowd control. In spy vaults, Loki's Invisibility and Decoy allow bypassing entire security systems without triggering alarms, making him the go-to frame for endless spy farming. In defense missions, Loki combines Decoy misdirection with Radial Disarm to prevent enemy access to defenses while isolating high-priority targets for assassination. In survival and mobile defense, the combination of repositioning via Switch Teleport and crowd control via Disarm enables solo endurance farming at high enemy levels. On rescue missions, Loki's stealth and decoy allow infiltrating enemy complexes while drawing fire away from rescued targets.

Optimal Loki builds prioritize ability efficiency (streamline, fleeting expertise) to sustain ability spam across extended missions, paired with duration mods (continuity, augur message) to extend invisibility and disarm effectiveness. Range mods amplify Decoy and Radial Disarm radius, allowing crowd control across entire room scales. For dedicated crowd control, stacking range and strength allows Loki to disarm every enemy in a large mission area with a single cast. For stealth gameplay, balancing duration and efficiency enables extended invisibility windows while maintaining energy reserves for repositioning abilities.

## Lore

Loki was engineered by the Orokin as a master of deception and infiltration, designed to operate covertly behind enemy lines where stealth and psychological tactics determine mission success. His holographic technology and teleportation capabilities represent cutting-edge Orokin engineering focused on evasion rather than confrontation. The Lotus describes Loki as possessing the cunning to turn enemies against themselves--Decoy forces enemies to waste ammunition on illusions, while Disarm strips them of their primary threat. In lore, Loki embodies the Orokin philosophy that the greatest victory is one achieved without firing a shot, where enemies defeat themselves through misdirection and confusion.

Loki Prime, released in Update 13.1, represents an evolutionary refinement with enhanced stat distribution, suggesting the Orokin gradually perfected his frame design through iterations. The prime variant remains incredibly popular in the community due to its superior stats and the visual distinction of its elegant, crown-like helmet. In the modern Warframe timeline, Loki remains central to espionage operations and specialist missions where tactical thinking outweighs raw firepower.
