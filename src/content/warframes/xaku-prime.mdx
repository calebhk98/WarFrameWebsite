## Overview

Xaku Prime is the primed variant of the void-themed warframe, embodying chaos and fractured power through its suite of reality-bending abilities. Introduced in Hotfix 37.0.9 as the newest prime warframe, Xaku Prime represents the pinnacle of experimental frame design, offering substantial improvements over the base Xaku while maintaining its unique playstyle centered on void damage, enemy disarmament, and crowd control through corruption.

With balanced stats across health (269), shields (263), and armor (167), Xaku Prime excels as a versatile damage dealer and crowd controller. The 181 energy capacity supports frequent ability casting, while the sprint speed of 1.07 provides decent mobility for repositioning. Unlike specialized tanks or pure damage dealers, Xaku Prime adapts to diverse mission types through its three-form Lost ability that cycles between crowd control, defensive utility, and offensive burst. The void damage affinity and Sentient resistance stripping make Xaku Prime exceptional for endgame void-heavy content and newer star chart missions featuring Sentient variants.

Xaku Prime fills a unique niche as a frame that gains power through chaos--scattered abilities that seem disjointed at first become a devastating arsenal when players master the cyclic interactions between Xata's Whisper, Grasp of Lohk, and The Lost's three manifestations. For players seeking intellectual engagement and adaptive combat, Xaku Prime rewards positioning and ability sequencing with outstanding damage and control potential.

## Abilities

Xata's Whisper converts all equipped weapon damage to void type for its duration, granting enhanced damage penetration against shielded enemies and synergizing perfectly with void-focused builds. The ability costs 25 energy, making it affordable for frequent recasting and enabling long-duration void damage windows.

The primary value of Xata's Whisper lies in stripping enemy resistances and converting weapon damage type to void, which bypasses many defensive mechanics. In void fissure missions, this conversion is essential--void damage ignores Orokin architecture and certain Sentient defenses. Combined with Grasp of Lohk's stolen weapons, Xata's Whisper transforms captured armaments into hyper-effective tools that retain their base damage while gaining void penetration properties. The ability's low energy cost allows Xaku Prime to maintain permanent uptime on void damage, enabling sustainable long-duration missions.

Grasp of Lohk steals weapons from nearby enemies and suspends them in the air as auto-targeting allies. The stolen weapons' damage scales with enemy level, meaning higher-difficulty content generates more potent armaments. Each stolen weapon adds to The Lost: Deny's void beam multiplier, creating a powerful synergy--more enemies disarmed means more damage for the primary offensive ability.

This ability serves a dual purpose: crowd control through disarmament and damage amplification for The Lost: Deny phase. Grasp of Lohk pairs exceptionally well with high-level enemies and boss encounters where the stolen weapons deal overwhelming damage. The auto-targeting functionality means players can focus on positioning and cycling abilities while floating weapons engage threats. In survival and defense missions, Grasp of Lohk turns enemy weapon pools into a rotating defensive fire network that supplements Xaku Prime's personal DPS.

The Lost cycles through three distinct Warframe abilities, each providing a different tactical function. Accuse summons void fissures that corrupt enemies into allies--this crowd control exceeds most frames' control capacity because corrupted enemies actively fight for Xaku Prime, effectively doubling firepower. Gaze ensnares targets in void tendrils that capture incoming damage, storing it and releasing it when the target is freed--this converts enemy aggression into damage output and provides both crowd control and damage mitigation.

Deny manifests as a devastating void beam that annihilates targets and strips Sentient resistances, making it essential for endgame content. The beam damage scales with weapons stolen by Grasp of Lohk, meaning each stolen weapon multiplies Deny's output. Cycling through all three forms enables adaptive play--Accuse handles crowd control, Gaze provides survivability and damage mitigation, and Deny delivers finishing power. The ability's 75 energy cost is moderate for its versatility.

The Vast Untime unleashes Xaku's outer structure in a destructive blast, leaving behind a skeletal form with increased mobility for the ability's duration. Enemies hit by the shrapnel burst take increased void damage, and critically, all other active ability durations freeze in time--abilities remain active but do not count down, enabling indefinite uptime on buffs like Xata's Whisper or corrupted enemies from Accuse.

This ability fundamentally changes how Xaku Prime sustains in long missions. By casting The Vast Untime before ability durations expire, players can refresh all timers and activate new abilities without losing prior effects. The increased mobility during the skeletal form enables repositioning during dangerous moments, and the void damage boost from shrapnel adds another layer of damage scaling. The 100 energy cost is significant but justified by the ability's defensive and utility value.

## Passive

Xaku Prime's passive grants a chance to dodge incoming weapon damage and provides damage reduction on area-of-effect attacks. Unlike frames that rely on shields or armor for survival, this passive enables raw avoidance--enemies simply miss more often, and area-of-effect damage affects Xaku Prime less severely.

The passive scales with Adaptation-style mods and creates unconventional survivability builds where damage avoidance and mitigation layer multiplicatively. In void fissure missions where crowds overwhelm through volume, the area-of-effect reduction significantly improves durability against exploding barrels, grenades, and area-denial tactics. The passive requires no energy and is always active, making it a reliable foundation for survival.

## Acquisition

Xaku Prime is acquired through void fissure farming. The frame components drop from Void Fissure missions across all relic eras--Lith, Meso, Neo, and Axi--making the farm accessible to all progression levels. As of the latest release in November 2024, Xaku Prime remains unvaulted and regularly farmable. The blueprint drops from Lith X1 and Neo X1 relics at various refinement levels, Systems from Axi A19 and Axi M6, Chassis from Neo G8, and Neuroptics from Lith S16 and Meso E6.

Players can farm relics directly through void fissure missions or purchase components from the player market using platinum, as prime components are tradable. The standard 72-hour build time applies, and the frame requires 5 Orokin Cells plus other standard crafting materials for final assembly.

## Playstyle

Xaku Prime thrives in adaptive, ability-focused gameplay that prioritizes void damage and crowd control over raw statistics. The optimal playstyle cycles abilities in sequence--start with Xata's Whisper to convert weapon damage, use Grasp of Lohk to disarm enemies and amplify The Lost: Deny, then cycle through The Lost forms based on tactical need. Accuse for crowd control, Gaze for defensive mitigation, and Deny for damage output.

The Vast Untime serves as a panic button and sustain tool--cast it before ability durations expire to freeze timers and reactivate buffs, gaining mobility and void damage amplification in the process. This creates a loop where Xaku Prime gradually becomes more powerful as more effects stack, eventually reaching near-permanent uptime on all buffs by chaining Vast Untime casts.

In squad play, Xaku Prime provides void damage boosts, crowd control through corrupted enemies and tendrils, and weapon stripping that disables dangerous enemies. The frame pairs well with complement frames that need DPS time--Trinity for energy, Oberon for healing and status immunity, or Harrow for critical chance stacking.

## Lore

Xaku Prime represents a radical departure from conventional Orokin warframe philosophy--rather than a singular, perfected warrior, Xaku Prime embodies the chaos of multiple broken frames merged into one entity. The prime variant's lore suggests the Orokin discovered fragments of ancient, destroyed frames from the earliest stages of the Void War and attempted to reconstruct them into a unified whole. The result is a frame that cycles between three distinct fighting styles, each originating from different lost warframes.

The Void damage affinity and Sentient resistance stripping indicate Xaku Prime was weaponized specifically to counter Sentient incursions during the final stages of the Old War. The frame's fractured aesthetic--multiple polarities and scattered abilities--hints at a desperate final gambit by Orokin engineers attempting to weaponize the Void's own power against Sentients.

In the modern era, Xaku Prime exists as a enigmatic recovery from the void itself--a blueprint that survived the Old War's conclusion and only now surfaces for the Tenno to reconstruct. Its introduction as the newest prime warframe suggests the Tenno's connection to the void has deepened sufficiently to harness technology even the Orokin struggled to complete.
