## Overview

Jade is a support-focused warframe introduced in Update 36.0 (Jade Shadows) who bridges offense and defense through her Light mechanic. Unlike traditional support frames, Jade combines direct healing, buff cycling, enemy control, and personal survivability into a cohesive kit centered on the Judgment status effect. Her unique double Aura Mod slot setup grants team flexibility unmatched by other frames, making her invaluable for squad composition optimization. Jade excels in both mobile support roles and stationary healing positions, adapting to team needs through her versatile ability kit.

Light's Judgment functions as Jade's primary healing tool and crowd control enabler. The well creates a persistent healing aura that affects all allies within range while simultaneously damaging nearby enemies. The Judgment application is critical--enemies marked by Judgments take increased damage from all sources, amplifying team damage output. This scales with Ability Range (larger well radius, broader effect spread) and Ability Strength (higher healing per tick, increased damage vulnerability percentage). Ability Duration affects how long Judgments persist on enemies, incentivizing duration mods to maximize vulnerability windows. Placed strategically on objectives or defensive positions, the well becomes a nexus for team survival and damage amplification.

Symphony Of Mercy is Jade's primary support ability, providing three distinct buff songs that cycle on demand. Each song grants meaningful scaling to its respective stat--Ability Strength directly multiplies Light's Judgment healing and Ophanim Eyes effectiveness, Weapon Damage turns squad primaries into damage dealers, and Shield Effectiveness transforms shield-stacking allies into unkillable tanks. The song persists until manually cycled or expires after a duration. Critically, duration extends when enemies near Judgments are killed, creating an economic loop where aggressive play rewards longer buff uptime. This incentivizes aggressive support playstyle rather than passive standing aside. Ability Strength increases the buff magnitude, while Ability Duration extends the base duration before requiring recasting.

Ophanim Eyes provides area-wide strip utility and remote ally restoration. The gaze simultaneously slows enemies (reducing their threat) and strips both their defensive layers (Shields and Armor), leaving them vulnerable to follow-up damage. Applied correctly, this ability transitions a chaotic engagement into one where enemies are slow, defenseless, and vulnerable to Judgments from Light's Judgment. The remote revive mechanic is unique in Jade's kit--when the gaze eye passes over downed allies, they are revived instantly, permitting squad recovery without repositioning. Ability Range determines the gaze's size and strip effectiveness coverage, Range and Duration both increase its uptime, and Strength improves the slow magnitude.

Glory On High serves as Jade's ultimate--a personal survival tool that doubles as a squad damage multiplier. Activating the ability launches Jade skyward, granting invulnerability during the ascent and aerial mobility for repositioning. The alternate-fire mechanic is essential: while airborne, pressing alternate-fire detonates all nearby Judgments, creating explosions of Jade Light. Enemies inside Light's Judgment radius empower these explosions, scaling damage based on number of marked enemies. This creates an offensive loop where a well-placed Light's Judgment followed by Glory On High's detonation can clear entire rooms. The ability functions as both an escape tool (invulnerable ascent) and an engagement tool (crowd clear via Judgment detonation). Ability Strength increases detonation damage, Ability Range extends the detonation radius, and Ability Duration extends the invulnerability window.

## Passive

Jade's passive grants two Aura Mod Slots instead of the standard one. This double-aura configuration is unique in Warframe and has profound team-building implications. Most frames choose between damage-supporting auras (Corrosive Projection, Power Donation) or defensive auras (Protection Pledge, Hardened Bonds). Jade can provide both simultaneously, enabling squad configurations impossible on other frames. Common pairs include Corrosive Projection + Power Donation (offense), Corrosive Projection + Protection Pledge (mixed), or Power Donation + Hardened Bonds (support). The passive also applies Judgment markers to enemies hit by certain abilities, synergizing with Light's Judgment and Glory On High's detonation mechanics. No farming or modding change is required--this is a raw quality-of-life upgrade that applies automatically.

## Acquisition

Jade's components drop exclusively from Uranus -- Brutus on Ascension difficulty. Each component (Blueprint, Chassis, Neuroptics, Systems) has a 4.63% drop chance per Ascension completion, requiring an average of 22 runs per component. The mission is a Corpus Void Fissure, meaning players can bring Void Fissure relics to crack open void reactors. Build time is 72 hours (standard for warframes), requiring 25,000 credits and 3 Orokin Cells to construct. No prime variant exists yet; Jade is a relatively recent frame introduced in June 2024.

## Playstyle

Jade's playstyle emphasizes proactive support and team amplification through careful ability sequencing. Begin missions by placing Light's Judgment near common engagement zones (doorways, objective points) to establish a healing anchor and Judgment factory. As combat unfolds, use Symphony Of Mercy to buff whichever stat your squad most needs--Ability Strength for framelock survivability, Weapon Damage for damage dealers, Shield Effectiveness for tank compositions. Cycle the song every 30 seconds or as combat demands. Use Ophanim Eyes to strip dangerous enemy defenses and revive downed allies remotely; the slow enables your squad to reposition safely. Reserve Glory On High for chaotic moments: activate when enemies cluster around Light's Judgment, then detonate accumulated Judgments for area clear. Return to ground, refresh Light's Judgment, and repeat.

In endless missions, adapt to scaling by rotating your aura mods based on squad composition changes and enemy profiles. Early waves prioritize offense (Corrosive Projection + Power Donation), mid-game shifts toward mixed defense (Corrosive Projection + Protection Pledge), and late-game favors defensive stacking (Protection Pledge + Hardened Bonds). Your job is not to carry damage--let your squad handle that--but to enable their damage through Judgment vulnerability application and buff cycling. Prioritize positioning near objectives rather than following gunners; your well-placed Light's Judgment is more valuable than your personal gun.

## Recommended builds

Jade supports three distinct build archetypes depending on your squad's needs. A **Duration/Range build** maximizes Light's Judgment uptime and coverage, using Stretch, Augur Reach, Primed Continuity, and Augur Message for extensive healing wells. A **Strength/Efficiency build** prioritizes Symphony Of Mercy buff magnitude and Ophanim Eyes strip/revive effectiveness, using Umbral Intensify, Augur Message, Streamline, and Fleeting Expertise. A **Balanced support build** spreads investments across Strength, Duration, and Range equally, adding flexible aura slots to respond to squad composition mid-mission. All builds benefit from energy efficiency (Streamline, Fleeting Expertise) to maintain ability spam, and from Duration (Primed Continuity, Natural Talent) to extend buff windows and Light's Judgment persistence.

## Lore

Jade emerged during the Jade Shadows storyline, a period when Zariman-based factions and corrupted void-touched lore elements dominated the narrative. Her themes of judgment, duality between life and death, and support-through-vulnerability reflect Warframe's ongoing exploration of the Void and its philosophical consequences. Her double Aura slots and Judgment mechanic suggest a being of immense internal balance--capable of holding contradictions (healing and harm, support and offense) simultaneously. Unlike Warframes born from traumatic past-selves, Jade appears consciously constructed as a bridge between squad roles, a frame designed for unity rather than individual power fantasy. The Light's Judgment well evokes religious iconography (wells of purification, divine judgment), hinting at Jade's role as arbiter or balance-keeper rather than warrior. No spoiler-heavy lore elements gate her acquisition; she is available from game start.
