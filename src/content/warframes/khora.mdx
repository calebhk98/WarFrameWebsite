## Overview

Khora is a dynamic crowd-control frame that blends melee combat with companion support through her loyal kavat Venari. Her toolkit centers on restraint and punishment, using whips and chains to ensnare enemies while Venari provides both offensive and defensive support based on her active posture. With 365 health and 290 armor, Khora achieves a balanced defensive profile that supports both aggressive melee engagement and tactical positioning. Her 140 energy pool, the highest among basic warframes, fuels her ability spam and sustains both her crowd control and companion management. Khora excels in endless missions, high-level survival content, and Steel Path runs where crowd control and damage mitigation are paramount.

Khora's playstyle revolves around the synergy between her abilities and her companion. Whipclaw serves as her primary damage tool, scaling with mods to deliver consistent area damage. Ensnare immobilizes priority threats, while Strangledome creates a zone of denial that groups and strangles enemies. Venari anchors the entire kit--her three postures (Attack, Protect, Heal) can be cycled to match the tactical situation, from aggressive offensive plays to defensive support when health is low. This fluid adaptability makes Khora one of the most versatile frames in the game, equally viable for solo farming runs or squad support in organized endgame content.

Whipclaw is Khora's primary damage ability and the anchor of her playstyle. It strikes in a forward cone, applying status effects and scaling with both ability strength mods and Khora's equipped melee weapon mods. Unlike many abilities, Whipclaw benefits directly from melee weapon modding, making it one of the few abilities that genuinely incentivizes keeping a strong melee weapon on Khora. The ability consumes no energy, instead relying on a cooldown, allowing Khora to spam it frequently during combat without energy management concerns. When combined with Ensnare, Whipclaw gains additional utility--it can refresh ensnared enemies' status, allowing a single Ensnare cast to trap multiple waves of enemies.

Ensnare is Khora's crowd control anchor, binding a single target in living metal while drawing nearby enemies into the trap. The ensnared enemies take increased damage and remain immobilized, allowing squad members or Whipclaw to safely damage them. The key mechanic here is that Whipclaw's radius refresh allows one Ensnare cast to serve as a semi-permanent trap for groups of enemies--as long as Whipclaw hits the ensnared target, the trap remains active and continues drawing in nearby foes. This synergy creates powerful crowd control windows in packed missions.

Venari is Khora's passive companion and her most unique ability. Unlike other companion-summon abilities, Venari is always active and can be cycled through three distinct postures: Attack (for sustained offensive pressure), Protect (for damage mitigation and shield-sharing), and Heal (for active health recovery). Cycling postures mid-combat allows Khora to adapt to incoming threats--switching to Heal when the squad's health dips, pivoting to Protect when facing heavy burst damage, or maintaining Attack during pushes. If Venari falls, she can be instantly revived with no cooldown, making her effectively immortal in most content. This mechanic transforms Venari from a passive pet into an active tactical tool.

Strangledome is Khora's ultimate ability, weaving a large dome of living chain that traps and strangles all enemies within. Unlike Ensnare, which targets a single enemy, Strangledome creates an area of effect zone that scales with ability range mods. Enemies outside the dome will attempt to destroy it by shooting trapped allies, creating a strategic dynamic where players must protect or eliminate external threats. The real power of Strangledome emerges when combined with Whipclaw--cracking the dome with Whipclaw applies massive damage to all trapped enemies at once, synergizing perfectly with Khora's high-efficiency melee toolkit.

## Passive

Venari functions as Khora's passive, distinguishing her from nearly all other frames. Rather than a minor stat boost, Venari is a full combat-capable companion with three active postures. Her baseline presence grants Khora a movement speed bonus, while her postures scale with mods and Khora's equipment. This makes Venari not just thematic but mechanically central to Khora's effectiveness. The ability to instantly revive Venari means she serves as both a source of damage and a tactical tool for breaking line of sight or drawing enemy fire. Veteran players learn to cycle Venari's postures reactively, using Protect to mitigate burst and Heal to maintain team health during intense engagements.

## Acquisition

Khora's blueprint is obtained from the Zariman Ten Zero void fissure mission, making her an accessible acquisition even for newer players who have unlocked void fissure content. The blueprint and component blueprints are non-trading, requiring each player to farm their own copy. The crafting cost is standard for mid-tier warframes: 25,000 credits plus moderate Void Fissure-era materials. The full frame takes 72 hours to build once all materials are gathered. Since void fissures are infinitely farmable, Khora remains one of the easiest prime-tier equivalents to obtain, making her a popular choice for players entering mid-to-late star chart content or preparing for endless missions.

## Playstyle

Khora thrives in high-enemy-density environments where her crowd control and sustained damage shine. Her playstyle emphasizes cycling between Whipclaw spam, Ensnare refreshes, and Venari posture management to maintain both personal and team survival. In solo play, players will typically maintain Venari in Attack posture for aggressive pushes, switching to Protect when surrounded or Heal when health drops below 50%. The Whipclaw-Ensnare synergy becomes critical in packed rooms--chain Ensnares to multiple targets and layer Whipclaws to stagger entire enemy waves. Strangledome excels against dense, organized enemy formations, particularly in survival missions where resource denial and enemy grouping provide both defensive and economical benefits.

Squad play sees Khora fill a hybrid role. Her crowd control benefits the entire team, her Venari can support with Heal posture, and her consistent damage output ensures she holds her own in DPS rotations. In Steel Path and equivalent-difficulty content, experienced Khora players learn to predict burst damage windows and preemptively swap Venari to Protect, effectively acting as a soft support frame without sacrificing her own damage output. The 140 energy pool supports ability spam, though energy management becomes necessary in extended endless missions without energy economy mods.

Khora's main weakness is her reliance on Venari remaining active--if forced to revive Venari repeatedly, her effectiveness drops significantly. Additionally, her armor, while solid, is not enough to face raw heavy fire without clever positioning and posture cycling. High-level play demands awareness of incoming threat types and the discipline to shift postures reactively rather than maintaining a single configuration.

## Recommended builds

For steel path survival and endless missions, combine Khora with ability strength mods (Umbral Intensify, Augur Intensify) and health/armor mods (Umbral Vitality, Steel Fiber) to maximize Whipclaw damage while sustaining against heavy fire. Pair with Arcane Energize and Arcane Healing to keep the ability spam flowing and health topped off. A detailed guide is available at [Khora Steel Path Build](/builds/khora/steel-path-survival).

For resource farming and Void Fissure speed runs, prioritize ability range (Stretch, Augur Reach) to maximize Strangledome coverage, then layer with range-scaling mods on Ensnare. This build groups enemies into massive clusters for rapid elimination and resource collection. See [Khora Farming Build](/builds/khora/void-fissure-farming) for complete loadout details.

For squad support in organized endgame, build for ability duration and Venari survivability, emphasizing the Heal posture to sustain team health during organized void fissure runs or coordinated Steel Path missions. [Khora Support Build](/builds/khora/support-healer) details this playstyle.

## Synergies

Khora synergizes exceptionally well with high-impact melee weapons like the Galatine Prime or Guandao Prime, since Whipclaw inherits all melee weapon mods. Building a high-crit, high-status melee weapon effectively multiplies Whipclaw's scaling, turning the ability into a true powerhouse. Additionally, Khora pairs strongly with Arcane Energize to fuel her high-energy-cost abilities, and Arcane Healing to sustain both herself and Venari through extended encounters.

With her squad, Khora benefits from Trinity's energy support (allowing unlimited Whipclaw spam) and Protea's damage buffs, which stack with her inherent crowd control to create overwhelming firepower. Conversely, Khora's crowd control and Heal posture support frames like Inaros or Hildryn that rely on crowd control and team sustainability. In void fissure groups, her ability to group and stagger enemies makes her invaluable alongside any DPS-focused frame, effectively acting as both damage dealer and crowd controller simultaneously.

## Lore

Khora emerges from Grineer warrior culture as a frame designed to blend predatory grace with tactical precision. Her aesthetic draws from feline predators and the Grineer's affinity for organic augmentation. Venari, her kavat companion, represents a deeper thematic element--the synergy between frame and beast, suggesting that true strength comes not from individual power but from coordinated partnership. Where most warframes operate as solo operatives, Khora's design philosophy centers on the power of unity and mutual support.

Her combat style reflects this philosophy: she does not simply stun or kill individual enemies. Instead, she restrains them, allows her companion to assault them, and uses environmental hazards (the Strangledome chains themselves) as weapons. This predatory, methodical approach contrasts sharply with other damage-focused frames that rely on overwhelming firepower. Khora, by design, is patient--she controls the battlefield, restricts enemy agency, and then methodically eliminates grouped threats.

Khora Prime, released in Update 31.7, refined her design with improved shields and stat scaling, suggesting the Orokin perfected her original template. The prime's release coincided with the expansion of Zariman content, positioning her as a keystone frame for void-based missions and the challenges of infinite scaling endurance runs.
