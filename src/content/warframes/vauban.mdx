## Overview

Vauban is the model of innovative technology and tactical mastery. Introduced in Update 7.11 (May 2013), he has been reinvented as one of Warframe's premier crowd-control specialists. His arsenal of deployable contraptions and area-denial abilities makes him invaluable for team survival and enemy suppression. Vauban willingly sacrifices safety in pursuit of mayhem, executing combat operations with lethal precision and methodical planning.

Vauban was completely reimagined in Update 26.0: The Old Blood (October 2019), where his arsenal was streamlined and enhanced to keep pace with modern Warframe combat. His signature playstyle centers on establishing technological dominance over the battlefield through clever deployments and devastating orbital support.

## Abilities

### Tesla Nervos

Tesla Nervos deploys mobile electric drones that seek out enemies and deliver continuous electrical shocks. The drones attach themselves to targets and arc electricity to nearby foes, creating cascading electrical chains. Multiple drones can be active simultaneously, allowing players to establish persistent area denial. Power strength increases shock damage output, while power duration affects how long the drones remain active.

### Minelayer

Minelayer is Vauban's versatile crowd-control toolkit, cycling between four distinct mine types. Tether Coil immobilizes clusters of enemies while grouping them together for focused fire. Flechette Orb detonates in a spray of projectiles, dealing significant slash damage in all directions. Vector Pad launches both enemies and allies, useful for repositioning team members or launching foes into environmental hazards. Overdriver temporarily boosts the weapon damage of nearby allies. Each mine type serves a distinct tactical purpose, making Minelayer adaptable to any mission scenario.

### Photon Strike

Photon Strike calls down orbital laser artillery, devastating enemy formations with a single, coordinated strike. Players deploy a targeting beacon that triggers the bombardment moments later, allowing for setup and tactical positioning. Power strength significantly multiplies the damage dealt, scaling Photon Strike from a utility interrupt to a major damage source in endgame content. This ability rewards players who manage cooldown timing effectively.

### Bastille

Bastille establishes an impenetrable containment field that suspends enemies in temporal stasis while stripping armor. All held enemies take increased damage from team members, turning the battlefield into a firing range. Holding the ability button collapses all active Bastilles into a single catastrophic vortex, pulling surviving enemies to the center. This burst-damage mode transitions Vauban from pure support to area nuke, combining crowd control and damage phases seamlessly.

## Passive

Vauban's passive, Reinforced, grants him 25% increased damage against incapacitated enemies. Incapacitation includes crowd-control effects like stun, knockdown, suspension, and hold--any state where the enemy cannot act freely. This passive pairs perfectly with his entire kit, making every ability amplify his damage output. Teammates also benefit from bonus damage against enemies held by Vauban's abilities, encouraging squad coordination.

## Acquisition

Vauban's main blueprint is available directly from the Market for 35,000 credits and 300 platinum. His component blueprints (Neuroptics, Chassis, Systems) are earned through Nightwave offerings for 25 Cred each. Alternatively, players who have completed The Duviri Paradox can farm his blueprints from The Circuit activity. Upon reaching specific tier thresholds on weeks when Vauban is available in The Circuit rotation, players receive his components progressively (Tier 2 for Neuroptics, Tier 5 for Chassis, Tier 8 for Systems, Tier 10 for the main blueprint).

Build time is 72 hours, standard for a warframe. All components require 5 Orokin Cells and various steel resources, typical of mid-tier crafting.

## Playstyle

Vauban excels as a tactical crowd-control anchor for squad missions. Rather than dealing direct damage himself, he transforms the environment to neutralize threats and empower allies. Players should prioritize establishing Bastille fields in high-traffic enemy routes, using the suspension window to let weapons-focused squadmates delete incapacitated targets. Tesla Nervos provides persistent background damage and area denial, allowing Vauban to set automated killzones without constant ability recasting.

Minelayer's versatility demands situational awareness. Vector Pad launches can save falling teammates or accelerate allies toward extraction. Flechette Orb provides emergency area damage when swarms overwhelm defenses. Tether Coil groups scattered enemies for area-of-effect farming and weapon coverage.

Photon Strike serves multiple roles: burst damage against priority targets, emergency room-clear when things spiral out of control, or delayed-damage setup against bosses vulnerable to burst patterns. Holding the camera still while calling strikes, then resuming fire, creates satisfying tactical moments.

Vauban thrives in Corpus Void Fissure missions where enemies cluster predictably, Grineer defensive nodes where choke points amplify his suppression, and endless missions where time-independent crowd control outperforms pure damage. His passive synergizes with allies running melee or status-heavy weapons, further boosting team lethality.

## Builds

Vauban supports multiple build archetypes. Strength-focused builds maximize Photon Strike damage and Bastille armor strip, sacrificing range for raw output. Range-focused builds expand Bastille holding fields across entire rooms, making him effective against open-map missions. Duration builds extend Tesla Nervos uptime and Bastille suspension length, approaching infinite crowd control with proper mod stacking.

Recommended mod priorities are Power Strength (for damage amp to his passive and Photon Strike), Power Duration (for Bastille and Tesla sustain), and Power Range (for Bastille and Minelayer coverage). Efficiency matters less because Vauban's abilities have reasonable energy costs; players rarely run dry outside extended endless content.

Popular augment mods include Repelling Bastille, which pushes enemies outward instead of suspending them, creating crowd-control waves rather than frozen zones. Tesla Bank converts Tesla drones into short-term energy batteries, rewarding players who manage drone positioning.

## Synergies

Vauban pairs excellently with damage-dealing frames that need time to kill suspended enemies. Saryn's spore proliferation ignores crowd control and scales exponentially, making Bastille's incapacitation window perfect for stacking procs. Nezha's Divine Spears stack with Bastille suspension, creating nightmare zones no enemy survives. Frost's Snow Globe anchors to Bastille fields, creating nested defensive layers.

Support frames benefit from Vauban as well. Trinity appreciates crowd control preventing damage spikes; Vauban's incapacitation window gives her time to heal without panic casting. Rhino's Iron Skin refreshes while enemies hang suspended, effectively stacking immortality uptime.

Weapon synergies favor status procs and precision damage. Status rifles and shotguns benefit massively from incapacitated enemies taking increased damage (his passive) and clustering (Minelayer Tether Coil). Crit weapons reward patient positioning, striking suspended enemies who cannot dodge.

Vauban Prime held the highest disposition value when rivens were introduced, suggesting community consensus on his balanced power level. Modern build optimization continues to discover new synergies with post-update frames and meta-shifting balance patches.

## Lore

Vauban is the tactician of the Tenno--a master engineer who weaponizes innovation itself. Unlike frames built for direct combat, Vauban constructs a technological fortress wherever he deploys. His description, "Vauban is the model of innovative technology," references Napoleon's generals and military engineers, hinting at a character obsessed with strategy and fortification.

The Lotus recruits him with deliberate language: "This is Vauban, focused and methodical. Vauban willingly sacrificed his own safety in the name of mayhem. Use him wisely, Tenno." This paradox--methodical yet chaotic--defines his playstyle: calculated deployments orchestrating controlled battlefield pandemonium.

Vauban's update to Update 26.0 reflected developer philosophy about crowd control's role in 2019+ Warframe. Instead of making crowd control obsolete via power creep (as happened to other frames), they modernized Vauban's kit, proving that tactical gameplay remains viable alongside damage-focused alternatives. His reinvention preserved his identity while ensuring endgame relevance.

Vauban Prime and his signature weapons (Fragor Prime, Akstiletto Prime) entered the Prime Vault in March 2018, removing them from relics. They remain inaccessible via normal play unless Vauban Prime returns during Prime Resurgence events. The base Vauban has always remained available, making him one of the few frames never entirely locked behind vault timers.
