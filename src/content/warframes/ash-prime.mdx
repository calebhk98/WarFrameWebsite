## Overview

Ash Prime is the primed variant of the original assassination warframe, boasting significantly improved survivability over the base frame while retaining the core stealth and finisher toolkit. With 35% more shields and 76% more armor than standard Ash, the prime variant remains relevant in Steel Path and endgame void fissures. The enhanced defenses allow more aggressive playstyles, enabling players to tank higher-level enemies while waiting for ability cooldowns or maneuvering between target eliminations.

The prime retains Ash's ninja aesthetic but with an ornate, polished appearance that reflects its Orokin craftsmanship. Ash Prime also has a fractionally faster sprint speed, making it slightly more mobile for repositioning during long missions.

Ash Prime's first ability remains identical to the base frame--spinning projectiles that deal slash damage and impale enemies to walls. The shuriken ricochets between nearby targets, making it excellent for clearing light enemy clusters or softening armored units. At mastery rank 0, shuriken is accessible immediately, making it a reliable tool throughout all star chart content.

The second ability creates invisibility through a smoke cloud. Ash Prime's higher armor pool allows slightly more aggressive invisible plays--rushing through enemies while invisible and triggering slash damage amplification from the passive. The ability functions identically to base Ash, but the improved defenses mean Ash Prime can briefly tank hits immediately after invisibility expires while repositioning.

The ultimate ability summons shadow clones to assault enemy groups. Ash Prime benefits more from high-crit melee weapons due to the improved armor, which scales well with armor-reduction mods like Corrosive Projection. The clones inherit weapon mods and apply slash procs, and the improved defenses allow Ash Prime to stand closer to combat while the clones work.

## Passive

Ash Prime shares the passive with the base frame, amplifying slash Status Effects duration and damage. This passive is fundamental to both Ash variants and benefits from weapons like the Nikana Prime, Redeemer Prime, and other slash-heavy melee weapons. The slash amplification stacks multiplicatively with weapon mods, creating exponential scaling in endgame builds.

## Acquisition

Ash Prime is acquired through void fissure farming. The frame blueprints and components drop from Void Fissure missions in Lith, Meso, Neo, and Axi relic tiers. Unlike the base Ash, the prime components are tradable on the player market, making acquisition flexible--either farm relics directly or purchase components from other players using platinum. Ash Prime is currently unvaulted and regularly farmable during active void fissure rotation windows.

## Recommended builds

Ash Prime's improved defenses enable aggressive crit-based melee builds. Pair with Intensify and Blind Rage for ability strength, then customize for weapon scaling. The [Ash Prime Steel Path Build](/builds/ash-prime/steel-path-crit) details a full steel path-capable loadout.

For players prioritizing survivability, a balanced build with Primed Continuity and Augur Reach stretches Smoke Screen duration while maintaining reasonable ability strength. See [Ash Prime Survival Build](/builds/ash-prime/endurance-farming) for a full guide.

## Synergies

Ash Prime pairs exceptionally well with the same melee weapons as base Ash--Nikana Prime, Redeemer Prime, and Guandao Prime all benefit from his passive slash amplification. The improved armor (185 vs. 105) scales better with armor-stacking mods, making Corrosive Projection and Augur Accord more impactful. 

In endgame, Ash Prime synergizes with Arcane Energize for ability spam sustain and Arcane Blessing for overshield stacking during aggressive plays. The improved shields mean Blessing's overshield caps are higher, further increasing survivability. Zenurik focus amplifies energy regeneration when using operator void mode, allowing near-permanent Blade Storm uptime in missions with extended downtime.

## Lore

Ash Prime represents the Orokin's final refinements to the assassination warframe archetype. The prime variant features upgraded materials and design that suggest the Orokin discovered new alloys and construction techniques after the original Ash's creation. In the lore, Ash Prime would have been deployed for the most critical Orokin targets--high-security facilities and well-protected assets where collateral damage was unacceptable.

The prime variant exists in that liminal space between the Old War and the present day--a blueprint recovered from the void, representing technology the Tenno can now wield. Ash Prime's superior defenses hint at an iterative design philosophy, where the Orokin constantly improved their tools based on field experience.

Ash Prime was eventually vaulted by Digital Extremes as part of the rotating prime vault system, but periodically returns during unvault windows, making it one of the more accessible primes for newer players who join during the right farming season.
