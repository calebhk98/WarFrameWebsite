## Overview

Harrow is a support-focused warframe designed to amplify team utility through damage conversion and critical chance scaling. Unlike dedicated defenders, Harrow builds his arsenal around converting damage into benefits--shields become ammunition for buffs, enemy fire fuels healing waves, and successful shots trigger team-wide effects. His kit rewards precise weapon handling and aggressive positioning, making him equally viable for solo endurance runs and coordinated squad play. With respectable shield capacity (455) and moderate health (270), Harrow balances survivability with the mobility required to stay mobile in higher-level content.

Harrow excels in missions where ally support multiplies effectiveness: void fissure disruptions, kuva survival, arbitrations, and squad-based sorties. His low armor (185) discourages pure tanking, but his passive ability--doubled overshield capacity and mission-start energy--mitigates this through proactive shield generation and early ability spam. Players unfamiliar with ability-centric builds should be aware that Harrow plays fundamentally differently from defensive frames; success depends on ability spam, precise weapon usage, and understanding energy management in long endurance runs rather than trading blows.

## Abilities

Condemn releases a frontal wave of binding energy that immobilizes enemies in place. Each enemy caught by the wave grants Harrow a stacking shield boost, rewarding aggressive ability spam in clustered enemy groups. The ability costs 25 energy and has a brief casting time; it works exceptionally well in corridor-heavy tilesets (Corpus ship sections, Grineer bunkers) where funneling enemies is natural. Ability strength scales both the shield value and the number of enemies it can hold. The mechanic creates interesting tactical moments: pin enemies, grab shields, then use Penance to convert those shields into team fire-rate boosts before the wave wears off.

<AbilityCard
  name="Penance"
  key="2"

  description="Sacrifices shields to dramatically boost reload speed and fire rate for nearby allies. Damage dealt to enemies converts to healing."
/>

Penance sacrifices Harrow's accumulated shields to trigger a team-wide buff granting reload speed and fire rate. This ability uniquely converts outgoing weapon damage into healing waves for Harrow and nearby squadmates, encouraging coordinated shooting against held or slowed targets. The mechanic ties directly to Condemn--immobilize a group, cast Penance, and the resulting fire-rate boost makes the squad devastate trapped enemies while healing. Penance runs on a per-cast basis; there is no duration limit, and the healing persists as long as the fire-rate buff is active. This creates satisfying combat loops in organized squads where one player (or AI companion) triggers weapon hits on Condemned targets while Penance provides the healing.

Thurible is Harrow's channel ability, allowing him to stockpile energy into a holy vessel. Upon release, every enemy killed while the buff is active grants all nearby allies a burst of energy, with headshots generating even larger rewards. This ability encourages both careful weapon discipline (headshot accuracy) and allies understanding the energy reward mechanic. Thurible pairs exceptionally well with high-damage single-target weapons (sniper rifles, beam weapons) where headshots are frequent, and in squads running energy-hungry frames (Mirage, Octavia, Wukong). The ability scales with ability strength (more energy per kill) and ability duration (extended buff window). Importantly, Thurible does not interrupt other actions; Harrow can move, reload, and fire freely while channeling, making it suitable for aggressive playstyles.

Covenant summons a protective aura that grants all nearby allies both a damage-absorbing shield and a critical chance bonus scaling with headshots landed during the buff's duration. This ultimate is team-wide; even players firing from across the map contribute their headshots to the critical bonus pool. Covenant is exceptional in squads running high-crit weapons (Fulmin, Catchmoon, Euphona Prime) because each headshot increases everyone's crit chance, dramatically amplifying total team DPS. The ability scales with ability strength (shield absorption) and duration; modding for both is essential. In solo play, Covenant is still potent (the crit bonus applies to Harrow's own shots), but it truly shines in organized squads where three or four players landing headshots can achieve near-guaranteed critical hits across all weapons.

## Passive

Harrow's passive grants him doubled overshield capacity and starts every mission with full energy. This is one of the most powerful passives in the game for energy-centric frames. The doubled overshield cap (up to 310 at base, scaling with mods like Primed Vigor) provides surprising survivability without investing in health or armor scaling. Mission-start energy is particularly valuable in endless missions where the first 30 seconds determine survival; Harrow can immediately cast his full ability suite, granting shields and buffs before enemies are even dangerous. The passive is entirely passive--it requires no mod investment or active play to benefit from.

Harrow's stats reflect a support-first design. His shield pool is his primary defense, and his armor value is the third-highest among warframes, making him resilient against most mid-tier threats. However, his health is relatively low; armor alone does not make him a tank. The low sprint speed (1.0x multiplier) is unusual but reflects his design intent: ability-reliant frames with large energy pools do not need mobility buffs, and the slower sprint encourages deliberate positioning rather than constant running.

## Acquisition

Harrow is obtained as a blueprint reward from the Chains of Harrow quest, an intermediate-level narrative quest available after completing The War Within. The quest introduces Harrow's lore and provides the main frame blueprint as a completion reward. Unlike assassination-farmable frames, Harrow's blueprint is quest-exclusive and immediately crafted, making acquisition straightforward for players with MR 5+. His component blueprints (Neuroptics, Systems, Chassis) drop from specific higher-level missions:

- Neuroptics from Kuva Fortress survival and spy missions (12% and 16.7% rotations).
- Systems from various Defection missions across Saturn, Neptune, and Phobos (5.45% to 15% depending on rotation).
- Chassis is non-farmable; it is obtained through void fissure corrupted enemy drops or trading with other players.

Each component requires a 72-hour craft time and moderate build materials (Alloy Plate, Plastids, Neural Sensors, rare endo). New players should expect a week or two of farming if pursuing all components independently.

## Playstyle

Harrow's core gameplay loop revolves around rapid ability cycling to maintain both personal survivability and team buffs. The fundamental rotation is: cast Condemn to immobilize a pack of enemies and gain shields, immediately follow with Penance to convert those shields into team-wide reload speed and fire rate while triggering healing from outgoing damage, then weave in Thurible channels during lulls to ensure allies never run dry on energy. This cycle demands active engagement; Harrow is not a frame that casts a buff and steps back. Instead, he must stay close to allies, spam abilities on cooldown, and prioritize positioning where both his damage aura and Penance healing reach the intended targets. Energy management is critical--his 100 base pool is modest, but the doubled overshield passive and mission-start energy (from his passive) provide the runway to establish this loop before energy becomes a constraint. Mods like Primed Flow and Streamline extend the window significantly.

Covenant, Harrow's ultimate, is the linchpin of squad coordination and represents his peak utility. When cast, it grants all nearby allies a damage-absorbing shield and a critical chance bonus that scales with every headshot landed by any team member while the buff is active. This transforms Harrow from a personal buffer into a force-multiplier: if three squadmates are landing headshots on high-crit weapons, the combined bonus can push everyone's crit chance near 100%, turning the entire team into precision damage machines. Positioning is paramount--Harrow must position himself centrally or near the primary damage dealers so the aura radius encompasses the squad. Duration matters as much as strength when modding Covenant; a brief buff window wastes the crit scaling potential, so balancing both stats is essential. In organized squads, calling out Covenant casts and coordinating headshot patterns turns Harrow into a tactical anchor that elevates team DPS exponentially.

Thurible's dual-mode nature--energy restoration on headshots and survival boosts on bodyshots--requires tactical flexibility depending on the mission context. In squad play with energy-hungry frames (Mirage, Octavia, Wukong), prioritize the energy-restore playstyle by committing to headshot discipline and encouraging allies to do the same; this transforms kills into team sustainability. In solo endurance runs or when allies lack energy-hungry builds, Thurible's survival-mode utility (health regeneration and temporary invulnerability effects from certain mods) becomes the focus. The channel itself is non-interrupting, allowing Harrow to move, reload, and fire freely while building the buff--this flexibility encourages aggressive positioning rather than static defense. Learning when to transition between modes (energy-restore for squad synergy, survival for solo push) separates competent Harrow players from exceptional ones.

## Recommended builds

For support-focused gameplay emphasizing Covenant and Penance, invest in ability strength (Intensify, Augur Intensify) and ability duration (Primed Continuity, Augur Reach) to maximize critical bonus uptime and fire-rate buff duration. Pair with energy-sustaining mods (Streamline, Primed Flow) to maintain ability spam. See the [Harrow Support Build](/builds/warframes/harrow/support-squad) for a complete loadout.

For solo endless missions, prioritize ability strength and efficiency (Streamline, Primed Flow) to sustain Thurible channeling and Condemn spam. A brief example: cast Condemn to gather shields, weave in Penance casts between weapon shots to keep healing active, then use Thurible during calm periods. ## Lore

Harrow's origin is intrinsically tied to the quest bearing his name. He is a warrior-priest frame engineered to embody redemption through righteous punishment. The warframe's aesthetic--chains, religious iconography, ritual garb--mirrors the monastic philosophy of his abilities. Lore-wise, Harrow represents a bridge between old Orokin technology and the Tenno's own spiritual evolution; his kit was designed not for conquest, but for communal ascension. His passive ability to start missions with full energy symbolizes readiness and faith; the doubled overshield represents his protector's vow to shield those around him.

The Chains of Harrow quest expands on this theme, exploring the nature of sacrifice, redemption, and the price of power. Harrow Prime, released nearly two years after the original frame, represents an even more refined iteration, suggesting the Orokin had evolved his design significantly before the Tenno's awakening. In-universe, Harrow is not a singular tool but a philosophical stance: weaponized redemption through channeled power and collective strength.
