## Overview

Mag is one of Warframe's original Warframes, available from the start of the game at Mastery Rank 0. She is a master of magnetic force and crowd control, specializing in manipulation of enemy positioning and defense stripping. Mag excels in situations that require careful target management and group control, making her an excellent choice for both new players learning the fundamentals and experienced players seeking refined crowd control mechanics.

The foundation of Mag's kit rests on her magnetic theme: she pulls enemies together, traps them in field effects, and strips their defensive layers while maintaining her own durability through shield management. Her abilities synergize well with each other, creating a playstyle where strategic ability chaining produces powerful results.

Pull is Mag's primary crowd control tool and energy-efficient opener. It knocks down and groups enemies in front of her, making it ideal for setting up combinations with other abilities. The ability's low energy cost makes it spammable during early gameplay when energy efficiency is limited.

Magnetize is Mag's most versatile ability, with two distinct modes of operation. In tap mode, it creates a magnetic field on a target that damages nearby enemies and accumulates Polarize Shards over time. In hold mode, Mag generates a defensive barrier around herself that converts incoming ranged damage into outgoing AOE harm, making her mobile and combat-ready. The duality of this ability allows Mag to adapt to both offensive and defensive situations.

Polarize is Mag's strip and support ability combined into one powerful pulse. It removes enemy armor and shields while bolstering allied shields--a mechanic that makes Mag valuable in group content. The Polarize Shards that remain after the pulse become projectiles that orbit Mag and automatically attack nearby enemies, creating persistent damage output without additional energy investment.

Crush is Mag's ultimate ability, delivering high single-target damage through magnetic force applied directly to an enemy's skeletal structure. It has a moderate range and delivers instant damage, making it effective for eliminating high-threat targets or finishing weakened groups.

## Passive

Mag's passive ability automatically pulls nearby items toward her within an 8-meter radius. This passive applies both to ammo, energy orbs, and rare drops, reducing the need to manually move to pick up resources during combat. The passive is essential to maintaining Mag's ammo and energy economy during extended missions, particularly when other squad members are not nearby.

## Stats

Mag's base statistics reflect a balanced frame suited for both new and veteran players. Her 180 health provides durability comparable to other starter Warframes, while her 455 shield pool offers respectable defense against enemy fire. At 105 armor, Mag is lightly armored and relies on shield replenishment and crowd control rather than heavy plating to survive.

Her energy pool of 140 provides adequate reserves for ability rotation in early game content. The base sprint speed multiplier of 1.0 places her at the standard baseline. At Mastery Rank 0, Mag has no minimum progression gates and can be obtained immediately upon account creation.

## Acquisition

Mag can be acquired through two paths. The most direct method is farming her blueprint and component blueprints from the Sergeant assassination mission on Phobos in the node called Iliad. The Sergeant drops all three components (Neuroptics, Chassis, and Systems) plus the main blueprint, with common drops at 38.72% for Chassis and Neuroptics, and 22.56% for Systems.

Alternatively, players can purchase Mag directly from the in-game Market for 75 platinum, bypassing the farming requirement entirely. The blueprint construction requires 25,000 credits and takes 72 hours to build.

## Playstyle

Mag's playstyle emphasizes tactical ability usage and positioning awareness. Early in a mission, Pull serves as the primary crowd control tool to group and stun enemies, preventing them from spreading out and overwhelming the player. Once enemies are grouped or weakened, Polarize strips their defenses while simultaneously restoring squad shields, making her valuable in cooperative play.

In sustained encounters, Mag cycles between Pull for control and Polarize for defense stripping and shard management. The Polarize Shards that orbit her provide passive damage output against nearby enemies without requiring active casting, allowing the player to focus fire with weapons while the shards handle crowd chip damage.

Magnetize's hold mode shifts her into a defensive stance when necessary, absorbing and reflecting incoming fire--a mechanic that becomes increasingly valuable in high-difficulty content where raw health pools are inadequate. Against single dangerous targets, her fourth ability Crush eliminates threats quickly with minimal setup.

New players should focus on understanding the fundamentals: grouping enemies with Pull, managing shield and armor with Polarize, and using Magnetize to hold priority targets. As experience grows, players discover synergies like creating Shard-filled Magnetize bubbles that explode with high damage, or using Crush in conjunction with other squad members' crowd control.

## Builds

Mag accommodates a wide range of build paths depending on mission objectives and playstyle preference. Early builds prioritize ability efficiency and range to maximize crowd control potential. Mid-game builds often add duration to extend Magnetize's field effects and increase Polarize Shard uptime. Late-game and Steel Path builds emphasize power strength for Crush damage and status chance conversion on Polarize strips.

Recommended build archetypes include **Control Mag** (high range and efficiency for solo crowd control), **Support Mag** (balanced stats with duration for squad shield restoration), and **Damage Mag** (maximum power strength and range for explosive Shard detonation). Each variant leverages different mods from the corpus to achieve distinct tactical advantages.

## Lore

Mag was one of the first Warframes discovered by the Tenno, originating during the Orokin era as a tool for warfare and dominion. Her magnetic control reflects the Orokin's mastery of physics and exotic matter manipulation. In the modern era of Warframe's setting, Mag represents a bridge between raw power and tactical precision--a frame that rewards clever ability chaining and awareness.

The relationship between Mag and magnetism is thematic rather than scientific; her abilities operate on principles beyond conventional physics, allowing her to manipulate not just metal but living bone and energy itself. This thematic flexibility makes her narratively interesting despite her mechanical grounding as a crowd-control utility frame.
