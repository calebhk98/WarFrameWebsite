## Overview

Atlas is a titanic, earth-themed warframe built for melee combat and crowd control through petrification and summoned allies. With the highest armor value in the base-frame roster (475), Atlas is exceptionally durable against physical damage, making him ideal for high-level survival and endurance missions. His abilities center on turning the battlefield itself into a weapon--summoning stone walls, fossilizing enemies, and calling forth earthen allies to fight alongside him.

Unlike ability-spam frames, Atlas is a melee-centric warframe that scales directly with equipped melee weapon damage. His Landslide ability multiplies melee weapon damage and benefits from heavy-hitting weapons like Tribal weapons, stone hammers, and crushing implements. Rumblers provide additional damage output and crowd control, making Atlas excellent for tanking damage while slowly grinding through enemy forces.

## Abilities

Landslide is Atlas's primary damage ability and the anchor of his combat loop. The punch can be chained up to three times, with each successive hit dealing more damage and covering a larger area. Critically, Landslide scales with the equipped melee weapon's damage and mods--a Damage-modded heavy weapon like a hammer or Cortege will hit significantly harder than an unmodded blade. The energy cost decreases with each punch in the combo, rewarding aggressive play. Landslide synergizes beautifully with finisher-enhancing mods and critical-damage setups on heavy melee weapons.

Tectonics summons a rock bulwark that serves both offensive and defensive purposes. The wall blocks line of sight, provides cover, and can be reactivated to crush enemies with the falling rocks. Notably, any damage the bulwark absorbs while standing translates into area-of-effect slash damage when it's destroyed--meaning high-damage bulwarks that absorb heavy fire deliver massive counterattacks. This ability excels in wave-based missions where enemies converge on a single point.

Petrify turns nearby enemies to stone, immobilizing them and multiplying the damage they take from subsequent hits. Petrified enemies can be shattered by any damage source, dropping healing rubble. The ability also heals summoned Rumblers, creating a synergy loop--summon Rumblers, petrify enemies, bash them with Landslide to shatter and convert into heals. In endurance runs, this loop sustains Atlas indefinitely while controlling crowds.

Rumblers summons two stone elementals that independently attack enemies. The summoned brawlers inherit a portion of Atlas's stats and persist until killed or dismissed via recasting. Summoning Rumblers near enemies petrifies them, providing crowd control. When Rumblers expire or are killed, they collapse into healing rubble, further sustaining Atlas. In high-level content, Rumblers provide a damage buffer--enemies attacking the summoned allies instead of Atlas--allowing him to reposition or execute finishing moves.

## Passive

Atlas's passive grants knockdown immunity while standing on solid ground. This is foundational for his playstyle--he cannot be knocked around by crowd control effects, allowing him to stand firm in the center of enemy fire and execute Landslide combos without interruption. The passive is always active and requires no energy, making Atlas one of the most stable frames in the game for rooted positions.

## Acquisition

Atlas is obtained by defeating the Void Cascade boss encounter, which appears in void fissure missions. The blueprint and components drop from the boss, making acquisition tied to void fissure participation. Unlike void fissure relic farming, the Void Cascade blueprint is non-tradable, requiring each player to farm their own copy. The complete build takes 72 hours to craft.

## Recommended builds

For endurance missions, prioritize ability strength (Intensify, Augur Intensify) and ability duration (Primed Continuity) to keep Rumblers alive longer and extend Petrify effect windows. Pair with a high-damage melee weapon modded for crit and slash. The [Atlas Endurance Build](/builds/atlas/infinite-sustain) demonstrates a full loadout capable of solo-farming 60+ minute void fissure runs.

For faster-paced missions, invest in melee weapon efficiency and crit damage instead of duration. Heavy slashing weapons like Guandao Prime multiply with Landslide's damage scaling. See [Atlas Melee Blitz](/builds/atlas/landslide-crit) for optimized weapon synergies and mod configurations.

## Synergies

Atlas's primary synergy is with heavy, high-damage melee weapons. Guandao Prime, Patagium, Gram Prime, and Cortege all synergize exceptionally well because Landslide multiplies the weapon's base damage through its scaling mechanic. Critical-damage mods (Organ Shatter, True Punishment) amplify Landslide's already-high damage caps.

Atlas also synergizes strongly with life-steal mechanics. Overextended lifestrike builds healing with every Landslide hit, while Healing Return heals on all melee weapon attacks. Combined with Rumblers' rubble drops and Petrify's crowd control, Atlas becomes near-impossible to kill in sustained missions.

Arcane Dexterity boosts attack speed on melee weapons, allowing faster Landslide combo chains. Arcane Guardian stacks armor, further increasing Atlas's durability. Magus Elevate (operator arcane) provides a teleport-on-void-mode heal, useful for repositioning during long endurance runs.

## Lore

Atlas embodies the raw, primal power of the earth itself. In Orokin war doctrine, he was designed as an immovable defensive platform--a warframe that could absorb and redirect incoming fire while maintaining offensive pressure through summoned allies. His stone-based abilities suggest Orokin mastery over geomancy and elemental binding, technologies lost after the fall of the Orokin empire.

The lore frames Atlas as a defensive bulwark, a frame deployed to hold territory and weather sustained assaults. Unlike assassins like Ash or agile frames like Volt, Atlas represents a slower, methodical approach to warfare--controlling ground through stone barriers and overwhelming enemies through sheer durability and summoned forces. The Rumblers--stone elementals subservient to Atlas's will--suggest the Orokin could bind elemental spirits to their weapons and frames, a capability now only partially understood by the Tenno.

Atlas Prime does not currently exist in the game, though many players speculate on what an enhanced version might look like. If released, a prime variant would likely feature improved armor and energy pools, further cementing Atlas's role as an unbreakable defensive anchor.
