## Overview

Nezha Prime is the primed variant of Nezha, representing the pinnacle of the Scion of the Burning Wind's power and refinement. Released on October 27th, 2020, this warframe boasts superior armor and sprint speed compared to its standard counterpart, along with enhanced polarities for greater mod flexibility. Nezha Prime was released alongside Zakti Prime and Guandao Prime, forming a cohesive prime weapon set. As of October 5th, 2022, Nezha Prime entered the Prime Vault, making relics containing its components no longer available from standard mission rewards. However, previously obtained void relics can still be cracked, and the Prime Resurgence system may periodically offer Nezha Prime relics through Varzia.

The design philosophy behind Nezha emphasizes mobility and fire-based crowd control. With altered mod polarities and enhanced base stats, Nezha Prime provides superior survivability and flexibility in both defensive and offensive playstyles. The warframe's passive ability grants exceptional mobility bonuses, making it ideal for fast-paced gameplay and hit-and-run tactics. Nezha Prime's aesthetic captures the theme of an ancient prince cloaked in ceremonial finery, with the frame's golden accents and ornamental design reflecting its primed status.

This ability excels in both tactical crowd control and room clearing, particularly when combined with damage amplification effects.

## Passive

Burning Wind grants Nezha Prime exceptional mobility advantages. The passive ability increases slide velocity by 60% and extends slide distance by 35%, far surpassing most other warframes' passive bonuses. This passive synergizes perfectly with fire-based movement abilities, allowing players to execute rapid repositioning strategies. Combined with the warframe's 1.2 sprint speed multiplier, Nezha Prime becomes one of the most mobile frames in the game, enabling hit-and-run tactics against difficult enemies and fast transit across open-world zones.

## Acquisition

Nezha Prime is obtained exclusively through void relic cracking, making it a farming-intensive acquisition. The prime components are distributed across multiple void relic tiers, with relics containing each blueprint component vaulted since October 5th, 2022. Players seeking Nezha Prime must either crack pre-vaulted relics still held in their inventory or await its return through the Prime Resurgence system, which periodically cycles previously vaulted primes through Varzia's offerings in Relays. The base blueprint requires 25,000 credits and three days to build, with each component requiring 12 hours and 15,000 credits to construct separately. All components are built from commonly farmed resources, making construction straightforward once relics are obtained.

## Playstyle

Nezha Prime functions as a hit-and-run mobility specialist with fire-based crowd control capabilities. The warframe excels in missions requiring rapid objective completion and tactical repositioning. Players capitalize on Blazing Chakram's teleportation to dart between cover and priority targets, maintaining distance from heavy threats while applying vulnerability debuffs to dangerous enemies. Fire Walker enables sustained area denial and heat status buildup, softening enemy armor for follow-up damage. Warding Halo provides defensive utility when surrounded, while Divine Spears serves as the primary crowd control tool for neutralizing heavy units or creating windows for advancement.

The playstyle rewards aggressive positioning and ability spam, as proper modding converts Nezha Prime into an energy-regenerating platform. With Arcane Energize and energy-returning mods like Zenurik focus, players maintain near-permanent Blazing Chakram uptime, cycling between offensive and defensive tools as situations demand. In endgame content like Steel Path missions, Nezha Prime shifts toward a more defensive posture, using Warding Halo proactively and positioning carefully to avoid concentrated fire. Against weak enemies, Nezha Prime's mobility and fire damage enable rapid clearing without relying on Warding Halo's defensive properties, instead focusing on pure offense.

## Builds

Nezha Prime supports multiple build archetypes depending on mission type and challenge level. The fire damage build prioritizes elemental mods and damage amplification, leveraging Fire Walker and Blazing Chakram's heat application for crowd damage. The survivability build emphasizes Warding Halo through strength and range mods, creating a pseudo-protective tank capable of withstanding heavy fire. The hybrid build balances offense and defense, optimizing both ability damage and shield strength. Duration mods enhance the effectiveness of both Warding Halo's shield lifespan and Fire Walker's terrain control, while efficiency mods reduce energy drain during prolonged engagements.

For Steel Path survival content, prioritize armor and shield mods alongside Warding Halo augments that reflect damage. For mobile farming missions, reduce ability duration to minimize lock-in animations and maximize repositioning frequency. Against Void Fissure missions, standard damage builds suffice due to low enemy threat levels. Consider arcanes carefully: Arcane Energize provides energy sustain, Arcane Avenger boosts crit chance for weapons, and Arcane Guardian provides shield regeneration during extended combat.

## Synergies

Nezha Prime pairs exceptionally well with frames that benefit from heat damage or energy generation. Trinity as a squadmate provides energy support during ability-spam phases, reducing reliance on Blazing Chakram's orb drops. Volt's speed aura stacks with Nezha Prime's native mobility, enabling absurd transit speeds. Saryn's toxin damage complements Nezha Prime's heat application, creating status effect diversity. For solo play, Zenurik focus school provides passive energy regeneration, enabling continuous Blazing Chakram cycling. Combine Nezha Prime with the Guandao Prime, released alongside the warframe, for thematic weapon pairing and consistent heat element scaling.

Warframe synergies extend to ability combinations. Mesa's Peacemaker triggers on heat-afflicted targets, amplifying damage against enemies ignited by Blazing Chakram. Octavia's beat mapping synchronizes with Blazing Chakram's projectile speed, enabling creative damage timing. Mirage's damage amplification multiplicatively stacks with Warding Halo's offensive reflection scaling, creating burst damage windows. These synergies reward players who understand both Nezha Prime's ability kit and how other frames amplify specific playstyles.

## Lore

In the in-universe lore of Warframe, Nezha represents one of the Tenno's most graceful warriors, drawing thematic inspiration from ancient draconic mythology. The primed variant elevates this concept, presenting a cosmically refined iteration adorned in ceremonial regalia befitting a celestial prince. The description "Scion of the Burning Wind" reflects Nezha's fundamental connection to fire and wind elements, powers granted to the Tenno through their void-touched nature. Nezha Prime's release during Update 29.3 positioned it as a continuation of the Old War's warrior legacy, a frame designed for operators who prioritize speed and firepower over raw durability.

The warframe's design philosophy emphasizes grace and precision. Unlike tank-focused primes, Nezha Prime values positional awareness and ability timing. The golden spears conjured by Divine Spears mirror the aesthetic of Guandao Prime, released simultaneously, suggesting a thematic connection between the weapons and the warrior wielding them. The ceremonial nature of Nezha Prime's appearance contrasts sharply with combat effectiveness, embodying the Tenno principle that power comes in many forms. Players who master Nezha Prime discover a warframe capable of solo clearing the Star Chart while maintaining an aggressive playstyle that rewards mechanical skill and understanding.
