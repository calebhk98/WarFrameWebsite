## Overview

Mesa is the gunslinger of the Warframes, embodying a Western gunfighter aesthetic with her signature dual Regulator pistols. Introduced in Update 15.5 as a bounty from Mutalist Alad V, she has become one of the most iconic frames for players who favor marksmanship and precision firepower. Her design philosophy centers on speed, accuracy, and sustained damage output through her exalted weapons.

Mesa's identity is defined by her relationship with pistols. Her passive ability greatly enhances sidearm performance, and her signature ultimate ability, Peacemaker, transforms her into an unstoppable gunslinger capable of clearing entire rooms with focused, rapid fire. She excels in content that rewards sustained damage and target prioritization, making her a popular choice for both casual and high-level play.

Ballistic Battery allows Mesa to bank the damage her guns deal into a reserve, then release it all as a single amplified shot. The ability encourages aggressive playstyles where Mesa leads enemy fire, converting that incoming threat into offensive potential. This can be particularly effective against tough enemies or bosses where burst damage is valuable. The stored damage scales with the amount of fire Mesa takes, rewarding players who engage directly with their targets.

Shooting Gallery is Mesa's support and disarm ability. She buffs one squad member's damage output while simultaneously crippling enemy firearms, forcing them to reload and reducing their effective threat level. In solo play, Mesa benefits as the primary target when the buff cycles back to her. The ability's crowd control aspect makes it excellent for managing dangerous enemy gunfire, and the damage buff provides consistent team support regardless of the mission type. The jamming effect does not work on all enemy types, particularly those with non-traditional weapon attacks.

Shatter Shield creates a reactive defense that turns enemy firepower into personal benefits. Incoming bullets are reflected back at attackers, while Mesa gains temporary mobility and defensive bonuses for each reflected volley. This ability excels in heavily gunned environments where enemies rely on ballistic weapons. The movement speed boost synergizes well with Mesa's hit-and-run playstyle, allowing her to reposition while maintaining protection. Against non-gun-based enemies (melee attackers, energy projectiles), the shield provides mitigation but not the reflected damage benefit.

Peacemaker is Mesa's ultimate ability and the core of her identity as the gunslinger frame. Upon activation, Mesa draws her Regulator pistols and enters a focused state where she can freely target enemies within a large cone. Each kill narrows the targeting reticle, improving accuracy against remaining threats. The Regulators inherit mods from Mesa's equipped secondary weapon, benefiting from damage, critical chance, status chance, and utility mods. The ability costs 5 energy per shot fired and consumes additional energy per second while held. This ability is instant-cast and allows Mesa to move, though she cannot perform other abilities while it is active. With proper modding, Peacemaker becomes one of the most powerful sustained-damage abilities in the game.

## Passive

Mesa's passive ability enhances her relationship with sidearm weapons while providing a durability boost. She fires dual-wielded pistols 48% faster than other Warframes and reloads single-handed sidearms 24% faster. Additionally, Mesa gains 425 bonus health when not equipping a melee weapon, encouraging pure gunslinger builds where she relies entirely on firearms.

This passive creates synergy with her Peacemaker ultimate, since the Regulators are technically sidearms. The health bonus incentivizes using exclusively primary and secondary weapons, rewarding minimalist loadouts and freeing up mod slots that would otherwise go to melee. The fire rate and reload speed boost apply to all sidearms Mesa uses, making even standard secondary weapons more effective in her hands than in most other Warframes'.

## Acquisition

Mesa is obtained by defeating Mutalist Alad V, a unique boss encounter on the Eris node Boil. The full frame requires collecting all four components (Blueprint, Neuroptics, Chassis, Systems), each dropping from the boss with varying rarity. The Neuroptics and Chassis share a 38.72% drop chance in Rotation C, while the Systems drop at 22.56% rarity in the same rotation. Players must craft each component before assembling the full frame. The blueprint and final assembly require credits and a 72-hour build time.

For players seeking a prime variant, Mesa Prime was available through void relic farming and has since been vaulted as of October 2020, making relics rare or unavailable through normal play.

## Playstyle

Mesa is a precision-focused frame built around sustained gun damage and target prioritization. Her playstyle revolves around entering Peacemaker, locking onto high-priority targets, and systematically eliminating threats while her other abilities manage crowd control and defense.

In team settings, Mesa should open with Shooting Gallery to lock in a damage buff for an ally or herself, then hold Peacemaker to deal damage while Shatter Shield cycles to reflect incoming fire. Ballistic Battery works best as a reactionary tool—storing damage from enemy fire and releasing it as a burst on a priority target.

Solo play emphasizes self-sufficiency. Mesa plays aggressively when Shatter Shield is active, using the reflected damage and movement speed to reposition while enemies reload. She relies on mod setup to maintain energy for sustained Peacemaker use. Efficiency mods become essential for mission length, transforming her from a time-limited explosive gunner into a sustained-fire powerhouse.

Mesa excels in content that rewards target focusing: single-target assassination missions, endless survival where priority kills matter, and missions with heavy gunner enemies (who feed Shatter Shield). She struggles against nullifier units, which suppress her ability to shoot, and against purely melee-focused enemy groups where Shatter Shield reflects no damage.

## Builds

Mesa supports a wide range of build archetypes depending on mission needs and playstyle preference.

**Peacemaker Carry**: Maximize ability strength, efficiency, and duration. This build treats Peacemaker as the primary damage source and sustains it through the mission.

**Tank Build**: Invest in armor, shields, and Vitality. Use Shatter Shield as a permanent protection layer and mod for range to hit more enemies with reflection.

**Balanced Build**: Mix offensive and defensive stats with a focus on ability efficiency to cycle through all four abilities. This approach works well for harder content like Arbitrations.

**Energy Sustain Build**: Stack efficiency and use Zenurik focus to keep Peacemaker active indefinitely in high-level content. Pair with Arcane Energize and Guardian for survivability.

All builds benefit from the passive bonus when dropping melee weapons entirely, freeing up a mod slot over three-mod melee builds.

## Lore

Mesa was introduced to the Origin System under mysterious circumstances. The Lotus presents her as "the vagabond, the outcast," a frame designed for those who walk the lone path. Her gunslinger aesthetic—twin pistols, quick-draw mechanics, and precision focus—suggests a frame engineered for direct confrontation and self-reliance rather than the specialized tactical roles of other Warframes.

Mechanically, her story is told through her abilities. Ballistic Battery implies experience accumulating firepower. Shooting Gallery suggests diplomatic positioning—rotating buffs through squadmates. Shatter Shield reflects a protective philosophy. Peacemaker, her ultimate, embodies her core identity: the fastest draw in the realm, capable of clearing entire battlefields through sheer speed and marksmanship.

Her connection to Mutalist Alad V's laboratory adds narrative texture—she was forged in one of the Corpus' darkest experiments, emerging as a frame dedicated to pure combat efficiency. This origin makes her a hunter-warrior, distinct from the more ethereal or elemental identities of other frames.
