## Overview

Mesa Prime stands as one of Warframe's most iconic damage-dealing frames, embodying the gunslinger archetype with uncompromising firepower and style. She is a lethal enforcer built around secondary weapon mastery and rapid-fire elimination. The Prime variant improves upon the standard Mesa with enhanced survivability, higher health and armor values, and improved mod polarities that enable more flexible loadout customization. Unlike ability-spam frames, Mesa excels when piloted by players who invest in strong secondary weapons and understand how to synergize gun mods with her ability kit.

Mesa Prime's design philosophy emphasizes offensive output over bulk defense. Her kit revolves around weapon amplification, squad support, and her devastating ultimate Peacemaker, which transforms her briefly into an untouchable force of nature. Veterans and newcomers alike find her immensely satisfying to play once they commit to building her around the weapons she was designed to complement. The frame rewards good positioning, smart ability timing, and weapon investment far more than frame mods alone.

Ballistic Battery is Mesa's damage amplification tool. Solo players use it to pre-load damage before engaging priority targets; in group play, teammates can also contribute absorbed damage. The ability duration is short, so timing matters. Battery snapshots remain even if Mesa gets knocked down, making it reliable in chaotic missions.

Shooting Gallery is Mesa's support tool and one of the few frames that meaningfully buffs squadmate weapon damage. It serves double duty: offense for allies, disruption for enemies. High-level builds stack ability duration to maintain long uptime between casts. The enemy jamming effect is a quality-of-life tool that reduces damage intake passively across the mission.

Shatter Shield is deceptively powerful in high-level content. It does not grant invulnerability, but it turns enemy gunfire into their own vulnerability. When combined with Ballistic Battery and smart positioning, Mesa becomes nearly untouchable against ranged enemies. Shatter Shield's reflected damage scales with enemy weapon power, so it excels in late-game Void Fissures and Steel Path missions.

Peacemaker is Mesa's ultimate and the frame's identity. During its duration, Mesa gains near-guaranteed evasion against incoming fire and automatically eliminates targets within range. The ability scales with gun damage mods (Pistol Damage, Hornet Strike, galvanized mods) and ability strength, making it highly scaling-dependent. High-strength builds with good secondary weapons turn Peacemaker into room-clearing capability in mid-game content; in endgame, it becomes a precision elimination tool.

## Passive

Mesa's passive rewards secondary weapon investment with 25% faster reload on single-handed sidearms (like Euphona Prime) and 15% faster fire rate on dual-wielded pistols (like Akjagara Prime). Additionally, when not carrying a melee weapon, Mesa gains 50 bonus health, incentivizing pure gun builds. The passive stacks elegantly with her abilities to create a cohesive secondary-weapon-first playstyle.

## Acquisition

Mesa Prime entered the Prime Vault on October 20, 2020, removing her relics from standard drop tables. However, Digital Extremes periodically brings Prime-vaulted frames back via Prime Resurgence events, during which her blueprints and components drop from designated Void Relics. The most recent resurgence availability was in early 2023.

When available through Relics, Mesa Prime's components drop from Lith, Meso, Neo, and Axi-tier relics, with varying refinement tiers affecting drop chance. Players can also trade for blueprints and components on warframe.market using platinum, though vaulted primes command premium prices.

Once obtained, crafting the full frame requires 25,000 credits, 5 Orokin Cells (from derelict fissures or void cascades), and blueprint components that demand Argon Crystals, Neural Sensors, Nitain Extract, and Morphics—all farmable within the star chart.

## Playstyle

Mesa Prime's playstyle revolves around positioning and weapon investment. Success depends on equipping strong secondary weapons (Tombfinger, Catchmoon, Akjagara Prime, or Akvasto Prime) and modding them for high damage. Ballistic Battery and Shooting Gallery enable burst damage phases; Shatter Shield provides survivability windows; Peacemaker becomes the frame's finishing move or crowd-control tool.

In low-level content, Peacemaker alone clears rooms. In mid-game survival or void fissure runs, players engage Peacemaker selectively on high-priority targets while using gun damage and Ballistic Battery amplification for general gameplay. In Steel Path or Zariman endless missions, disciplined Peacemaker usage combined with gun modding and smart ability rotation becomes essential.

Mesa thrives on farms with stationary objectives (defense, disruption, excavation) where she can position defensively and leverage Peacemaker for waves. She struggles in high-mobility content (spy, rescue) where her abilities are less useful and enemy density is low. Void Fissures and Void Cascade missions are her sweet spot—high-level enemies trigger scaling synergies with Shatter Shield reflection and Peacemaker damage.

## Builds

Mesa Prime builds generally follow one of two patterns: a **Peacemaker-spam build** (high efficiency, high strength, moderate range) or a **hybrid support build** (balanced stats with emphasis on Shooting Gallery uptime). Most players lean toward Peacemaker-spam since the ultimate is her signature ability.

A typical build includes Primed Flow or Primed Continuity for energy or duration, Umbral Intensify for strength, and Hornet Strike plus elemental mods on the modded secondary weapon. The frame's aura slot typically holds Corrosive Projection (steel path) or Rifle Amp (in groups). Exilus slots reward speed mods like Rush or Power Drift.

Players should link Mesa Prime's secondary weapon page for detailed gun-mod synergies, as the frame's damage ceiling is determined more by weapon mods than frame mods. Galvanized mods (post-Sisters of Parvos) scale Peacemaker's automatic targeting into endgame viability.

## Lore

Mesa Prime is the orokinean reflection of the gunslinger frame Mesa, embodying the ancient civilization's advanced manufacturing techniques. The Primes represent humanity's peak technological achievement during the Old War era. Mesa Prime was released alongside Akjagara Prime and Redeemer Prime, three weapons designed to be her perfect complement. Her design echoes the Orokin fascination with elegant, lethal enforcement—a frame built not to protect, but to eliminate with style.

The Prime variants encapsulate Digital Extremes' philosophy of power and rarity. Mesa Prime's vault status and periodic resurgence reinforce her legendary status within the Tenno arsenal. Veterans who farmed her relics treasure her as a symbol of pre-New War mastery, while newer players see her as an aspirational goal—a frame to work toward through the Prime Resurgence rotations.
