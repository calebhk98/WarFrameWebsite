## Overview

Chroma is the dragon-themed warframe, embodying primordial elemental power and the hunter archetype. Introduced in Update 16.0, Chroma stands out as a frame capable of adapting to nearly any combat scenario through elemental versatility. The Lotus herself notes that "when all the land is in ruins, Tenno, only Chroma will remain," reflecting the frame's durability and survivability. Chroma excels at converting incoming damage into offensive capability while supporting squads through elemental buffs, making him equally viable as a solo hunter or a team contributor.

The frame's core identity revolves around mastery of four damage types: Heat, Electricity, Toxin, and Cold. Each element grants different offensive and defensive bonuses, allowing players to tailor their approach to enemy weaknesses, faction resistances, and playstyle preferences. Chroma Prime, released in 2017, improves upon the base frame with higher armor (450 vs. 370) and unlocks the energy stat at 175, making it the superior variant for most applications.

## Abilities

Spectral Scream is the frame's elemental toolkit in one ability. By tapping, players cycle through Heat, Electricity, Toxin, and Cold, each granting different passive bonuses to Chroma and nearby allies. Holding the ability button unleashes a breath attack that applies the chosen element to enemies. The selected element persists and modifies all subsequent abilities, making this a critical ability to use first and whenever tactical needs change. The low energy cost encourages frequent cycling to match enemy compositions.

Elemental Ward's effect depends on the current elemental attunement. Heat grants a damage buff to nearby allies and Chroma; Electricity provides a shield restore and increased movement speed; Toxin applies a damage buff on weapon swap; Cold grants armor and overshield to nearby squad members. This ability reinforces Chroma's role as both a damage dealer and support frame, and its relatively low energy cost makes it spammable for maintaining buffs during extended missions.

Vex Armor is a passive toggle that requires no energy to maintain, making it Chroma's most efficient ability. It rewards aggressive gameplay by converting damage taken and kills into squad-wide buffs. Taking shield damage triggers armor bonuses; losing health triggers weapon damage bonuses. Melee kills boost armor; ranged kills boost damage. This ability transforms Chroma's survivability into offensive power, encouraging players to engage frontally rather than play passively.

Effigy is Chroma's ultimate, summoning a stationary dragon effigy at the cast location. The effigy attacks nearby enemies with the currently selected element, applies crowd control, and grants nearby allies bonuses based on the active element. While channeling as Effigy, Chroma becomes invulnerable but immobile. Effigy serves as both a defensive tool and a way to lock down a position during endless defense missions or difficult encounters. The ability synergizes with Vex Armor, allowing Chroma to summon Effigy while teammates deal with threats.

## Passive

Chroma's passive ability grants extra mobility: wings sprout for an additional jump and bullet jump. This allows Chroma to reach higher areas than most frames and maintain momentum in combat more effectively. The enhanced traversal pairs well with Chroma's aggressive playstyle, enabling quick repositioning to support different squad members or to engage priority targets.

## Acquisition

Chroma's blueprint and components are obtained through Cephalon Simaris in exchange for Simaris Standing. The frame requires completion of progressively harder Star Chart junctions and The New Strange quest line, making Chroma a mid-game acquisition target. Most players unlock Chroma between Mastery Rank 5 and 10, after establishing a foothold in the star chart.

Blueprint acquisition demands completing The New Strange quest, which introduced Chroma to players lore-wise. The three components drop from Neptune, Uranus, and Pluto Junctions respectively, encouraging progression through the outer planets. Total build time is 72 hours, consistent with most warframe blueprints. Construction requires 25,000 credits, which is negligible for players at that progression stage.

Chroma Prime, released in 2017 and currently unvaulted, offers superior stats (particularly +80 armor and unlocked energy). Prime relics drop from void fissure missions in the Void Cascade, making Chroma Prime accessible once players unlock relic opening mechanics.

## Recommended Builds

Chroma's versatility means builds vary widely depending on mission type and playstyle.

**Elemental Tanking Build**: Emphasize Ability Strength and Ability Duration, with Umbral mods to push armor scaling. Pair with Cold Elemental Ward for overshields on allies. Use Vex Armor as the passive damage conversion engine, letting shields and health regenerate between fights while Elemental Ward maintains active bonuses.

**Effigy Turret Build**: Prioritize Ability Range and Duration to keep the effigy effective and long-lasting. Support teammates at a defensive position while the effigy locks down threats. Works exceptionally well in Defense and Mobile Defense missions where a stationary threat is advantageous.

**Aggressive Hunter Build**: Combine high Ability Strength with weapons that trigger Vex Armor bonuses frequently. Use Heat or Electricity for offensive element choices; cycle Spectral Scream to apply weakness damage while Elemental Ward triggers offensive buffs. Pair with heavy-hitting melee or ranged weapons to maximize on-kill bonuses.

## Synergies

Chroma benefits from squadmates who exploit his elemental buffs. Trinity pairs exceptionally well, as she can maintain Chroma's tank role with link damage reduction while Chroma's Elemental Ward supports her back. Harrow synergizes through shield manipulation, triggering Vex Armor armor bonuses on demand. Volt teams benefit from Chroma's speed and damage buffs, allowing aggressive coordinated pushes.

Weapons with low reload times are ideal; Chroma rewards ranged kill frequency through Vex Armor's damage scaling. Melee-focused play activates armor bonuses consistently, making high-base-damage greatswords and polearms excellent complements. Elemental weapons that match Chroma's current element can trigger additional proc chains, though WFCD does not document explicit scaling from element matching.

## Lore

Chroma is an ancient being, older than most Orokin constructs and nearly as old as the Tenno themselves. The warframe embodies dragon mythology within the Warframe universe—a relic of primal power awakened for hunters and soldiers willing to embrace the hunter's path. Chroma's connection to the four classical elements (in this case, damage types) reflects themes seen across human mythology, positioning the frame as timeless and fundamental.

The New Strange quest reveals Chroma as a powerful entity summoned by Cephalon Simaris, tasking the Tenno with retrieving its blueprint from the void itself. This narrative positions Chroma as worthy of careful study and preparation, justifying the multi-stage acquisition process through Star Chart junctions. The warframe's design—a dragon-like silhouette with extended arms and protective plating—visually reinforces its ancient, primal identity.

Within the broader Warframe lore, Chroma represents the Tenno's ability to harness primordial forces and adapt to any threat. The frame's elemental versatility mirrors the adaptability required of any warrior navigating the Origin System's diverse factions and planetary hazards.
