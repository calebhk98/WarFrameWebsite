## Overview

Excalibur Prime is the Founders-exclusive variant of Excalibur, serving as the progenitor of the Excalibur warframe line. Released during the Closed Beta in Update 5.0 (December 2012), Excalibur Prime represents a pinnacle of early Warframe design: a well-rounded offense and mobility-focused frame built for melee combat. With 270 health, 270 shields, 315 armor, and 175 energy, Excalibur Prime trades some durability for pure offensive capability. The frame's primary distinction lies in its innate Swordsmanship passive, which grants increased damage and attack speed when wielding swords--a thematic anchor that influenced all subsequent Excalibur variants.

As a Founder-tier exclusive, Excalibur Prime is no longer obtainable by new players. The Founders Program, which granted exclusive cosmetics and warframes to early supporters, concluded in 2013. Players who did not acquire Excalibur Prime during that window cannot obtain it through normal gameplay; it exists as a historical artifact and status symbol in the community.

Slash Dash is Excalibur Prime's mobility and damage tool rolled into one. It launches the frame forward in a directional slash while spawning a radial specter that deals additional damage. The ability's 25-meter range makes it effective for closing gaps in combat and repositioning. The Slash Status from the specter's strikes can proc on enemies within range, adding utility beyond pure damage. This ability is particularly useful for crowd control and drawing aggro in early-game content.

Radial Blind serves as Excalibur Prime's crowd control and survivability tool. The blinding effect disables enemies in a 20-meter radius for several seconds, opening them to finisher damage and giving the player breathing room to retreat or set up attacks. The 50-energy cost is moderate for the utility provided. Blinded enemies cannot attack, making this ability invaluable for survival in higher-level content where enemies deal significant damage. The finisher window also synergizes with melee-focused builds, allowing coordinated teams to execute high-damage takedowns.

Radial Javelin is Excalibur Prime's primary area-denial ability. It launches javelins in all directions with a 25-meter range, dealing 350 base damage (scaling with ability strength) and ignoring enemy shields entirely. The Slash Status inflicted by the javelins provides additional damage over time. The 75-energy cost is reasonable for the area-coverage and shield-bypass utility. This ability is fundamental to Excalibur Prime's role as an offensive frame; it enables aggressive playstyles and punishes grouped enemies. Radial Javelin remains relevant through mid-level star chart content and even sees use in tactical endgame builds where armor stripping from other frames pairs well with high-damage javelin volleys.

Exalted Blade is Excalibur Prime's signature ability and the frame's defining tool. Activating Exalted Blade equips a summoned sword that scales with the user's melee mods, ability strength, and critical stats, making it potentially one of the most powerful melee weapons in the game. The blade deals Slash damage and can be modded for Crit Damage, Attack Speed, and elemental effects. The 100-energy cost to summon is offset by the weapon's scaling; a fully modded Exalted Blade outperforms nearly all traditional weapons. The ability remains active until deactivation or energy depletion, allowing extended melee combat. This is the cornerstone of Excalibur Prime builds across all difficulty levels, from star chart to Steel Path.

## Passive

Excalibur Prime's passive grants increased damage and attack speed when wielding swords. While the exact percentage values scale with ability strength mods, the passive synergizes directly with Exalted Blade and high-damage sword builds. This passive rewards sword specialization and feeds into the frame's identity as a melee-focused warrior. Players building Excalibur Prime for Exalted Blade often leverage this passive to amplify their already-devastating sword damage and attack speed, creating a feedback loop of offensive power.

## Acquisition

Excalibur Prime is exclusive to the Founders Program, a limited-time initiative (2012-2013) that granted early supporters exclusive cosmetics and warframes. The frame cannot be obtained through normal gameplay, relics, or the market. New players cannot access Excalibur Prime under any circumstance. Existing players who unlocked Excalibur Prime during the Founders era retain permanent access to the frame and its progression; the frame does not expire or require renewal.

The Founders Program concluded to transition Warframe to free-to-play accessibility. While Excalibur Prime's unavailability may frustrate newer players, it reflects the historical nature of the frame and serves as a testament to Warframe's early history. Standard [Excalibur](/warframes/excalibur) remains freely available and shares all the same abilities; Excalibur Prime's distinction is cosmetic and nostalgic rather than functionally superior.

## Playstyle

Excalibur Prime excels as an offensive, mobility-focused melee frame suitable for star-chart missions and intermediate-level endgame content. The primary playstyle revolves around Exalted Blade summoning, using Radial Javelin for area denial, and cycling Radial Blind and Slash Dash for crowd control and repositioning.

In lower-level content (planets, void fissures, void cascade), activate Exalted Blade, advance into enemy clusters, and use Radial Javelin to strip grouped threats. Slash Dash provides gap-closing and mobility when repositioning. Radial Blind serves as an emergency panic button when overwhelmed.

For intermediate Steel Path or void cascade sorties, the same rotation applies but with heightened efficiency. An optimized Excalibur Prime running Exalted Blade with full crit and elemental mods can eliminate level 100+ enemies in seconds. Radial Blind's finisher window becomes critical for survival; blinding dangerous units before they can return fire. Energy management becomes paramount; pair the frame with Zenurik focus or Arcane Energize to sustain high-frequency ability casts.

Excalibur Prime's high armor (315) enables aggressive play; many builds forgo shields entirely and stack health and armor mods. The frame pairs exceptionally well with shield-stripping allies (Ash Prime, Trinity) in squad content, allowing Excalibur Prime to maximize Exalted Blade damage output without defensive overhead.

The frame struggles in endgame endless missions (high-level survival, void cascade infinite) without heavy support or dedicated damage mitigation builds. Boss assassinations favor single-target burst from Exalted Blade rather than area tools. Excalibur Prime is not a tank; survival depends on dealing damage faster than enemies deal it back.

## Builds

Excalibur Prime's primary build archetype is **Exalted Blade Crit-Damage Ramp**, which maximizes the sword's raw killing power. A secondary archetype, **Ability-Strength Crowd Control**, trades damage for defensive utility via Radial Javelin and Radial Blind spam.

**Exalted Blade Ramp Build (Steel Path):**

- Aura: Corrosive Projection (armor strip for allies)
- Exilus: Power Drift (ability strength)
- Mod slots:
  1. Umbral Intensify (ability strength)
  2. Umbral Vitality (health)
  3. Primed Flow (energy pool)
  4. Blind Rage (ability strength, efficiency trade)
  5. Streamline (energy efficiency)
  6. Stretch (Radial Blind and Javelin range)
  7. Piercing Roar (augment for Exalted Blade armor strip)
  8. Adaptation (damage reduction scaling with armor)

This build prioritizes pure Exalted Blade damage and sustainability. Ability strength mods scale both the sword damage and passive damage bonus. Energy efficiency allows rapid ability cycling without constant energy farming. Adaptation pairs with Excalibur Prime's 315 armor to reduce incoming spike damage. Piercing Roar (if available) allows the Exalted Blade itself to strip armor, eliminating the need for dedicated armor-strip allies.

**Radial Javelin Spam (Void Fissures):**

- Replace Blind Rage with Transient Fortitude (strength + duration trade)
- Replace Piercing Roar with Continuity (ability duration for Blind's lingering effect)
- Pair with Zenurik focus for energy regeneration

This archetype emphasizes crowd control through repeated Radial Javelin and Radial Blind casts, trading peak Exalted Blade damage for consistent area denial and survival. The build excels in lower-level fissures where area damage matters more than single-target burst.

## Lore

Excalibur Prime was the first warframe ever deployed--at least, according to some interpretations of Warframe's fragmented lore. The Orokin engineered Excalibur as a warrior prototype during the Old War, a period of galactic conflict between the Orokin empire and an unknown external threat. Excalibur's design philosophy emphasized mobility, melee prowess, and raw offensive power; the frame embodies the Orokin's vision of a perfect warrior-killing-machine.

The Prime variant predates all subsequent Excalibur releases. The standard Excalibur frame, released to new players at account creation, is technically a non-Prime construct--a degraded or civilian copy, depending on one's reading of Warframe lore. Excalibur Umbra, the quest-reward frame, represents a different lineage: a sentient or partially-sentient variant bound to a human Operator.

Excalibur Prime's exclusivity mirrors its lore weight. Only Founders--early adopters who believed in Warframe during its beta period--wield the original warrior. As new players progress and encounter Excalibur Prime-wielding veterans, the frame's cosmetic uniqueness and gameplay equivalence to modern frames create a historical divide. The frame is not stronger; it is older, rarer, and carries the weight of Warframe's early history.
