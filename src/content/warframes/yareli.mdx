## Overview

Yareli is the selfless waverider who emerged from the cold depths as a nymphean sailor. Introduced in Update 30.5 (July 2021), she embodies aquatic grace and fights for the hopeless. Her combat identity revolves around fluid mobility combined with enhanced secondary weapon mastery. The Waverider stands apart from other frames through her signature mount, Merulina—a rideable aquatic companion that transforms how players navigate and engage enemies. Her kit rewards constant movement and aggressive positioning through her passive bonus, making her a mobile, offensively-oriented frame suited to players who favor fast-paced combat and evasive playstyles.

Yareli's design draws thematic inspiration from wave-riding and K-Driving culture within the game. Her elemental focus on water-based attacks creates a cohesive aesthetic across all four abilities. Unlike many frame designs that prioritize raw survivability or damage output, Yareli's strength lies in her exceptional mobility paired with targeted crowd control and scaling damage potential. She fills a unique niche as a frame that makes continuous movement an active combat mechanic rather than a passive bonus, encouraging aggressive, flowing combat rather than defensive camping.

## Abilities

Sea Snares serves as Yareli's crowd control and setup ability. The five globules automatically seek targets within a radius of up to ten meters post-Update 32.2, making the ability effective at softening up enemy groups before committing to more aggressive abilities. The immobilization effect creates opportunities for her team to position for advantage, while the damage vulnerability debuff amplifies subsequent ability chains. Players often use Sea Snares to initiate engagements or to handle scattered enemy formations where precision targeting is difficult.

Merulina is the signature ability that defines Yareli's gameplay loop. While mounted, Yareli gains a dramatic mobility boost and can dash in any direction, allowing both offensive repositioning and evasive maneuvers. The ability fundamentally changes how Yareli controls the battlefield—riding Merulina trades some weapon capability for unparalleled map mobility. Following Update 30.7, Merulina dashing became a core movement tool, enabling vertical traversal and rapid gap closing. For the first four seconds after casting, Merulina gains health instead of losing it, mirroring Iron Skin mechanics and providing temporary invulnerability windows for Yareli. The health pool scales and can be strengthened through mods, making Merulina a defensive tool as well as a mobility option.

Aquablades forms a trio of water-construct melee projectiles that orbit Yareli and automatically attack nearby enemies. The orbiting blades persist and target hostile units independently, making this ability effective as passive area denial while Yareli focuses on positioning or using other abilities. As a Helminth ability, Aquablades can be subsumed into other frames, making it valuable for subsumption strategies. The scalability with ability strength mods allows Aquablades to grow from a utility tool into a legitimate damage source in mid-game content.

Riptide is Yareli's ultimate ability and primary burst damage tool. The initial vortex traps enemies in place, interrupting dangerous attacks and grouping targets. The damage of the outgoing wave scales with the number of enemies caught, rewarding Yareli players for using the ability on grouped foes. Riptide combines crowd control, grouping, and scaling damage into a single high-energy ability, making it her primary tool against dangerous elite enemies or when overwhelming force is needed. The scaling mechanic encourages tactical timing rather than spamming, as holding Riptide until maximum enemies are trapped yields dramatically higher payoffs.

## Passive

Yareli gains increased critical chance on secondary weapons while she has been moving continuously for at least a short duration. This passive directly incentivizes aggressive, mobile playstyles where downtime is minimized. The critical flow bonus stacks on top of secondary weapon mods, allowing dedicated secondary builds to reach exceptional crit rates. Combined with her Merulina dash mechanics, the passive creates a positive feedback loop where players are rewarded for staying in motion and maintaining aggressive positioning. The passive bonus encourages pairing Yareli with high-base-damage secondary weapons that can leverage critical chance scaling.

## Acquisition

Yareli is obtained as a quest reward from "The Waverider" quest, completed by progressing through the Star Chart. Upon quest completion, players receive the Yareli Blueprint. Component blueprints (Neuroptics, Chassis, Systems) come from researching the Ventkids' Bash Lab in a player's Clan Dojo. If the quest blueprint is accidentally sold, a replacement can be purchased from Cephalon Simaris using Standing currency. Blueprint construction requires standard credits and Orokin Cells alongside the individual component blueprints. Total build time is 72 hours, standard for warframes. Alternatively, Yareli can be purchased directly from the Market with Platinum currency, granting immediate access and including a bonus Riven Mod if purchased before completing the quest.

## Playstyle

Yareli excels as a mobile, aggressive skirmisher who dominates through constant motion and area control. Her playstyle centers on mounting Merulina to rush across maps and engage groups before dismounting to use ranged abilities. The synergy between Merulina mobility and her passive critical bonus creates a self-reinforcing loop: riding Merulina maintains critical flow, and dashing during fights keeps the bonus active while Aquablades orbits for passive damage. Sea Snares groups and debuffs enemies, Riptide provides scaling burst damage, and Merulina serves as both escape and offensive repositioning tool. Players leveraging Yareli effectively will keep her moving almost constantly, using Merulina's durability to absorb damage that would punish stationary frames. Her secondary weapon becomes a primary tool rather than a backup option, making critical-chance-scaling pistols or beam weapons excellent choices alongside her ability loadout. Team composition rarely matters for Yareli—she functions independently—but pairing with frames that value debuffs (like damage vulnerability) amplifies the group's output.

## Builds

Yareli's build philosophy centers on balancing Merulina durability with ability efficiency and strength. Ability strength amplifies Riptide scaling and Aquablades damage while boosting Merulina's health pool. Duration mods extend Sea Snares immobilization and provide overall ability sustainability. Range increases Sea Snares seek radius to up to 10 meters (matching post-32.2 design). Efficiency reduces the energy cost of Merulina recasts, critical for sustaining mobility. Tanky builds prioritize health and armor mods on Yareli herself, reducing reliance on Merulina absorption. Damage-focused builds emphasize ability strength and secondary weapon augmentation through Intensify, Power Drift, and Strength-scaling mods. Merulina tank builds layer Primed Vigor, Adaptation, and shield-gating mechanics to maximize the mount's survivability. Aura choices typically include Corrosive Projection for group scaling, Power Donation for team benefit, or Zenurik Affinity for personal energy sustain. Secondary weapons should be selected for critical chance scaling or status status effects that complement Yareli's crowd control focus.

## Synergies

Yareli pairs well with secondary weapons that scale critical chance, such as the Akstiletto Prime, Sonicor, or Despair. Ability strength mods like Umbral Intensify, Power Drift, and Augur Strength maximize Riptide's scaling damage and Aquablades output. Duration mods extend crowd control windows and improve overall sustain. Subsumption of useful abilities into other frames benefits from Aquablades' independence. Companion recommendations favor utility over damage since Yareli's abilities handle offense; Vacuum-aura sentinels or looter companions streamline resource collection. Warframe synergies are minimal—Yareli functions independently—but pairing with Corrosive Projection aura teams or frames that amplify damage create multiplicative advantages. Relic selection and farming strategies can leverage Merulina's map traversal for rapid coverage of open-world terrain and parkour-heavy levels.

## Lore

Yareli emerged from the cold depths as a selfless waverider who fights for the hopeless and strikes fear into oppressors. Her introduction into the Tenno arsenal arrived during Update 30.5 when players completed "The Waverider" quest, a narrative focused on her connection to the Ventkids and K-Driving culture. She embodies the spirit of liberation through motion—riding the waves as both literal metaphor and gameplay mechanic. The Ventkids' fascination with her spawned the Bash Lab research room where her components are crafted, cementing her role within Solaris and the broader Open World ecosystem. Thematically, Yareli represents grace, fluidity, and resistance against stagnation—concepts reflected in her relentless movement focus and aquatic aesthetic. Her story reflects themes of freedom and self-determination, positioning her as an anti-authoritarian warrior who refuses to be held in place.
