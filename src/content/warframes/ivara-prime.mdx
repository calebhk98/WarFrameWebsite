## Overview

Ivara Prime is the primed variant of Ivara, the master hunter and tactical archer of the Warframes. Released on December 12, 2017, Ivara Prime represents the apex of stealth, reconnaissance, and precision weaponry. With her four distinct arrow types, passive enemy-sensing capability, and complete invisibility mechanics, she excels in covert operations, vault runs, and solo farming missions where awareness and precision are paramount.

Ivara Prime is currently vaulted as of November 22, 2023, meaning her relics no longer drop in regular missions. However, she may appear in Prime Resurgence rotations, and her vaulted relics remain tradable between players. Her base stats represent a balanced frame: moderate health and shields, complemented by decent armor and solid energy capacity that supports her ability-heavy playstyle.

## Abilities

Quiver is Ivara Prime's signature ability and one of the most versatile toolkits in Warframe. Each arrow type serves distinct tactical purposes. The Cloak arrow creates a stationary bubble that renders Ivara and allies within range invisible, making it invaluable for bypassing alarms in spy missions or setting up ambushes. Dashwire arrows anchor to surfaces and create temporary ziplines for rapid vertical traversal, enabling mobility and creative platforming solutions. Noise arrows emit a sonic pulse that attracts enemies to a location, effectively crowd-controlling combat zones. Sleep arrows instantly pacify nearby enemies, providing crowd control without alerts. Ability synergy with other frames makes Quiver devastating in squad gameplay.

Navigator transforms Ivara into a precision marksman. This ability takes control of any projectile she fires, whether from Quiver arrows or her equipped weapon, allowing frame-perfect targeting and trajectory adjustments in mid-flight. In high-level content, Navigator enables skill-based damage amplification through careful positioning and timing. Combined with critical-chance mods, Navigator can deliver massive single-target damage while maintaining complete directional control. The ability drains energy per second while active, rewarding efficient target acquisition and quick shots.

Prowl is Ivara Prime's defining stealth tool. Toggling Prowl renders her completely invisible to all enemies and sensors, making her nearly invisible unless she attacks or gets too close. The stolen loot mechanic is exceptional for farming: each enemy killed while Prowl is active drops additional loot, making Ivara Prime the premier choice for resource farming missions. Prowl drains energy per second while active, incentivizing efficient movement and target selection. Her Hunter Instinct passive reveals enemy positions, synergizing perfectly with Prowl for locating high-priority targets without breaking invisibility.

Artemis Bow is Ivara Prime's ultimate ability and damage tool. Upon activation, Ivara summons a spectral bow that scales with her weapon mods and ability strength, firing a massive spread of arrows that automatically target all enemies in range. The ability's damage scales with mods equipped on Ivara's active primary weapon, making mod selection critical. Unlike most ultimates, Artemis Bow can be spammed relatively frequently thanks to its manageable energy cost and her moderate energy reserves, enabling sustained offensive pressure in extended missions.

## Passive

Ivara Prime's passive, Hunter Instinct, automatically reveals all enemies within 50 meters. This persistent information advantage fundamentally shapes her playstyle, allowing proactive threat assessment and tactical positioning. The passive makes her unparalleled for reconnaissance and surveillance missions, and combined with Prowl, it transforms her into an unstoppable solo operator. No other frame matches this level of omniscient awareness at the start of combat.

## Acquisition

Ivara Prime components are obtained exclusively from Void Relics. Her blueprint appears primarily in Axi B4 and a handful of other high-tier relics. Because Ivara Prime is currently vaulted, relics no longer drop in missions. Obtaining her requires either trading for her relics from other players or waiting for Prime Resurgence rotations, which periodically re-introduce vaulted primes into limited-time availability windows.

The full blueprint, chassis, neuroptics, and systems cost 25,000 credits and 175 platinum to craft once all four components are assembled. Construction takes three days of real time. Prime Resurgence events offer relics directly for Void Traces, providing guaranteed access when available.

## Playstyle

Ivara Prime is defined by adaptive, intelligence-gathering playstyle. Her Hunter Instinct passive encourages constant map awareness, scanning for threats before they become urgent. Prowl transforms her into a ghost: once invisibility engages, she becomes untouchable provided she doesn't trigger alarms or proximity detection. This enables aggressive farming strategies in Void Fissure missions, Void Cascades, and high-level content where maintaining invisibility is equivalent to invincibility.

Offensively, Ivara Prime can shift into precision damage mode using Navigator to deliver headshots with guaranteed critical hits, or unleash Artemis Bow for area damage. Defensively, Quiver's Cloak arrows provide squad protection and alarm circumvention. Her flexibility allows solo play in any mission type: spy, survival, defense, capture, or farming runs all suit her toolkit.

The learning curve is moderate. Players must master Prowl energy management, understand when to break invisibility for offense, and memorize spy vault layouts to maximize her potential. Experienced Ivara Prime players treat the frame as a stealth-based glass cannon, investing heavily in ability strength and duration mods while accepting vulnerability if caught.

## Builds

Ivara Prime supports multiple effective build archetypes:

**Invisibility Farmer** focuses on Prowl duration and efficiency, pairing maximum power strength with duration mods to maximize loot theft uptime. Typical mod layout: Intensify, Power Strength, Primed Continuity, Augur Message, Streamline, and Fleeting Expertise.

**Precision Archer** emphasizes weapon synergy with Navigator, using high critical-chance primary weapons and leveraging her damage multipliers. Combinations with Serration, Vital Sense, Primed Critical Damage, and elemental mods maximize arrow damage.

**Balanced Utility** provides a mid-ground approach with moderate investments across ability strength, duration, and range, enabling flexible playstyle adaptation mid-mission.

## Synergies

Ivara Prime pairs exceptionally well with coordinated squad members. Her Cloak arrows enable safe extraction in hostile zones, and her passive information gathering benefits the entire team. Frames that complement her kit include Trinity (energy support for sustained Prowl uptime), Volt (speed boost for improved navigation), and Wisp (mobility and protection).

Her loot-theft passive stacks multiplicatively with other drop-chance boosters: Pilfering Swarm, Despoil, and resource boosters. Farming squads featuring multiple Ivara Primes or hybrid loot-frame compositions exponentially increase farming efficiency.

## Lore

As regal and lethal as a nighttime wood, Ivara Prime represents the apex hunter. Thematically, her prime variant embodies aristocratic refinement applied to perfect predatory instinct. The Orokin, masters of biological and technological perfection, engineered Ivara Prime as the ultimate reconnaissance and infiltration unit. Unlike her standard variant, the prime design emphasizes elegance: her aesthetics reflect a huntress of unparalleled grace and lethality. Lore entries across the Warframe ecosystem identify Ivara Prime as a frame built for precision, patience, and intelligence gathering--traits that marked the most elite Orokin operatives.

Her arrows are not mere projectiles but tactical instruments refined through Orokin craftsmanship. Each arrow type--Cloak, Dashwire, Noise, and Sleep--represents centuries of stealth doctrine perfected. The Artemis Bow, her ultimate, is named after the Greek goddess of the hunt, connecting Ivara Prime to real-world mythology while emphasizing her unmatched predatory capabilities.
