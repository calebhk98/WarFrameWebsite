## Overview

Wisp Prime is the prime variant of Wisp, a support-oriented warframe that excels at providing team buffs and crowd control through her unique Reservoir ability system. Released on July 27, 2023, Wisp Prime features enhanced stats compared to the base Wisp, with increased health, shield, and armor values. Her gilded appearance reflects her status as a prime warframe, featuring the distinctive ethereal aesthetic that characterizes the Wisp design.

The prime variant maintains the same core mechanical identity as the base frame: a utility-focused warframe centered around summoning motes that provide distinct benefits to Wisp and her allies. Her low armor value is offset by her exceptional survivability mechanics through the Void Traverse passive and the flexibility of her kit. Wisp Prime's ability to remain invisible while airborne provides substantial defensive utility, making her difficult to target during active gameplay.

Wisp Prime excels in organized squad play where her support capabilities can be fully leveraged. Her ability to provide consistent buffs through Reservoirs while maintaining offensive pressure through Breach Surge and Sol Gate makes her valuable across various mission types. Unlike some frames that require specific builds to be effective, Wisp Prime's core utilities are available with minimal investment, making her accessible even to players with limited modding resources.

The frame's aesthetic design emphasizes her interdimensional nature through ethereal visuals and the spectral quality of her abilities. Her color schemes typically emphasize bright, ethereal tones that reinforce her mystical character. The prime variant adds gilded accents and more ornate detail work while maintaining the distinctive silhouette that makes Wisp recognizable in combat.

## Abilities

Reservoirs form the foundation of Wisp's support capabilities. The ability functions as a toggle, allowing players to select which type of mote to deploy before casting. Haste Motes provide a 25% movement speed and 25% attack speed bonus to affected allies, making them invaluable for farming and survival missions where rapid progression is desired. Vitality Motes grant a 25% health increase and active regeneration, providing substantial survivability for allies facing heavy damage. Shock Motes emit a stun effect that triggers on nearby enemies, providing crowd control and damage mitigation.

Multiple Reservoirs can exist simultaneously, allowing coordinated players to maintain multiple mote types in active zones. The motes themselves are persistent and travel with affected allies until destroyed or the mission concludes. The ability costs no energy, making Reservoirs a perpetual source of team utility once summoned. Modifying range and efficiency through Wisp's mod setup adjusts the effective radius of mote benefits and cooldown reduction between placements.

Wil-O-Wisp creates a mobile decoy that draws enemy aggression while enabling spatial repositioning for Wisp. The spectral image travels forward at a base speed, moving in the direction indicated during casting. Tapping the ability again teleports Wisp to the image's position, providing both offensive mobility and defensive repositioning. Holding the ability button increases the image's travel speed and queues an automatic teleport upon button release, allowing fluid traversal of large spaces.

This ability serves dual purposes: tactical repositioning during intense combat and map traversal for speedrunning objectives. The zero energy cost makes repeated activation feasible, though cooldown timers between successive uses prevent infinite spam. Modifying strength slightly adjusts the decoy's visual impact and its effectiveness at drawing enemy attention, though the primary value lies in the teleportation mechanic rather than damage mitigation.

Breach Surge deploys an offensive ability that both damages and debuffs nearby enemies. The surge effect causes affected enemies to emit aggressive spark projectiles when taking damage, creating a cascading effect that spreads between enemies in proximity to each other. Targeting a deployed Reservoir with Breach Surge teleports Wisp to that location while doubling the ability's effective range, enabling aggressive play patterns that leverage Wisp's support utility for offensive positioning.

The spark effect generated by the surge creates a significant amount of secondary damage that scales with player damage output and enemy density. In high-level content with numerous enemies, the cascading spark effect can deal substantial total damage across a squad's target area. The combination of offensive damage, defensive spark generation, and teleportation through Reservoirs makes Breach Surge Wisp's most versatile damage ability.

Sol Gate represents Wisp's ultimate damaging tool, channeling a concentrated beam of solar plasma that deals heavy damage in a narrow column. The beam applies progressive vulnerability to affected enemies, increasing their damage taken from subsequent Sol Gate hits. Holding the fire button during channel increases damage output by 100% while consuming additional energy per second, allowing for scaling damage output based on available energy and tactical necessity.

The vulnerability stacking mechanic enables sustained damage strategies where continued focus fire on a single target yields exponentially increasing damage. This is particularly effective against boss-level enemies or priority targets in steel path content. The beam's narrow column makes precise aiming important, rewarding players who invest in positioning and targeting accuracy.

## Passive

Void Traverse grants Wisp invisibility to enemies while airborne. This passive provides exceptional survivability through the simple act of jumping, making Wisp significantly harder to kill during active repositioning. The invisibility breaks upon landing, resetting when Wisp becomes airborne again. Skilled players can leverage this mechanic to maintain near-permanent invisibility by combining deliberate movement patterns with ability usage that keeps Wisp airborne or moving between cover.

The passive synergizes strongly with Wil-O-Wisp teleportation and Breach Surge repositioning, as the spectral movement and dimensional shifting inherently elevate Wisp's physical position during animation sequences. This creates moments of vulnerability reduction during high-risk actions, offsetting some of the survivability burden of Wisp's relatively low armor value.

## Acquisition

Wisp Prime components are obtained through Void Fissure missions by opening void relics that correspond to the desired component:

**Blueprint:** Drops from Axi W3, Meso W3, Meso W4, and Neo W2 void relics in ascending rarity tiers.

**Chassis:** Drops from Lith W3, Lith W4, Meso W5, and Neo W1 void relics in ascending rarity tiers.

**Neuroptics:** Drops from Axi F2, Lith A7, Lith G13, Meso K6, and Neo A13/K6 void relics.

**Systems:** Drops from Axi A17, Axi B7, Lith N15, Lith Q1, Meso G7, Meso T7, and Neo D7 void relics.

Construction requires 25,000 credits and 5 Orokin Cells, with a 72-hour build time. The components drop as non-tradable items; parts can be traded between players before assembly, but the assembled warframe cannot be traded. Obtaining all four components necessitates participating in void fissure missions across multiple relic tiers, with drop chance scaling based on relic refinement level and squad crack success.

## Playstyle

Wisp Prime functions best as a squad support frame prioritizing utility over personal damage output. Optimal gameplay revolves around strategic Reservoir placement in locations where the team will benefit from mote buffs throughout the mission. This requires map awareness and prediction of where squad members will be positioned during key combat phases. Haste Motes become critical during farming missions, where the movement and attack speed bonuses directly reduce mission completion time.

In defensive scenarios, Vitality Motes provide passive healing that reduces reliance on teammate support, enabling squad members to focus on their own roles without anxiety about health management. Shock Motes maintain passive crowd control, preventing dangerous enemies from fully engaging with the squad's position. Layering these benefits through multiple Reservoirs creates scenarios where squad safety improves dramatically despite Wisp's personal damage output remaining modest.

Offensive Wisp gameplay leverages Breach Surge for damage and Wil-O-Wisp for mobility. Breach Surge becomes the frame's highest damage output, particularly when targeting enemies in dense groups or vulnerable positions where spark cascades amplify each other. The vulnerability stacking mechanic enables sustained assault against priority targets. Combining Breach Surge with Wil-O-Wisp teleportation allows aggressive repositioning that takes advantage of the Void Traverse passive's invisibility windows.

Sol Gate functions as an ultimate ability for scenarios demanding maximum damage output. The progressive vulnerability stack mechanic rewards sustained focus fire against single high-priority targets, making it especially useful against boss encounters or steel path elite enemies. The energy cost scaling of the hold mechanic requires energy management, but unlimited energy sources like Arcane Energize enable aggressive sustained channeling.

## Recommended builds

Wisp Prime builds typically fall into two categories: support-focused builds maximizing mote effectiveness and duration, and hybrid damage builds balancing personal offense with squad utility. Support builds emphasize range and duration through Stretch, Augur Reach, and Primed Continuity, enabling mote coverage across entire mission spaces while maintaining their benefits long enough for squad progression. Vitality Mote-focused builds may include Vigor for additional health scaling.

Hybrid damage builds incorporate strength through Umbral Intensify and Blind Rage while maintaining sufficient range for Reservoir utility. These builds sacrifice some survivability for increased ability damage, particularly improving Breach Surge and Sol Gate performance. Exilus slot choices typically include Streamline for efficiency, Power Drift for additional strength, or Cunning Drift for range. Focus builds commonly recommend Zenurik for continuous energy generation, though Madurai provides offensive scaling for damage-focused builds.

Wisp Prime's zero-energy-cost abilities and low aura slot drain make her efficient even on tight mod budgets. Newer players can function effectively with unranked mods, while veterans optimize through perfect rolling and Umbra polarity utilization. The frame's flexibility means multiple viable playstyles can coexist rather than forcing a single "optimal" approach.

## Lore

Wisp Prime represents the gilded ancestor of the modern Wisp, a warframe that drifted through the void between dimensions before the Tenno claimed her for their arsenal. The Orokin sought to create a frame capable of navigating fractured spaces, resulting in a design emphasizing mobility and dimensional awareness. The spectral aesthetic of Wil-O-Wisp and the otherworldly nature of Breach Surge reflect this core design intent.

The lore surrounding Wisp's original purpose centers on the Void Traverse passive: the ability to slip between dimensions grants supernatural survivability that no conventional armor could match. The Reservoirs mechanic, though mechanically simple, echoes Orokin concepts of distributed consciousness and interdimensional coordination. The motes themselves may represent fragments of Wisp's consciousness or fractured aspects of her being, split across dimensions and manifested as distinct beneficial effects.

The prime variant emerges from the Orokin vault tradition, where the finest specimens of their technology await rediscovery. Wisp Prime's enhanced statistics and gilded appearance distinguish her from the base variant while maintaining mechanical identity. Her release timing and void fissure availability situate her within the broader storyline of reactivating Orokin vaults and reintegrating lost technology into modern warframe arsenals.
