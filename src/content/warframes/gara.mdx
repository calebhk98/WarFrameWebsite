## Overview

Gara is a glass-based warframe introduced in Update 22.0 alongside the Plains of Eidolon expansion. She represents a unique defensive and offensive hybrid playstyle centered around crystalline combat abilities. Her arsenal emphasizes crowd control through crystallization, team survivability via glass fortifications, and scalable damage that interacts with enemy health and shield values. Gara's combat philosophy revolves around creating barriers of molten glass that both protect allies and amplify damage against trapped enemies. Her passive ability grants a chance to blind enemies when she casts, adding an extra layer of crowd control to her kit.

Gara's primary identity is the glass warrior archetype. Unlike pure offense or pure defense frames, she excels at maintaining an active battlefield presence where allies gain benefits from her area control. The synergy between her abilities creates a playstyle where positioning and timing are crucial. Her armor value of 160 is respectable for a support-oriented frame, allowing her to survive moderate enemy fire while focusing on ability use rather than weapon damage.

The Plains of Eidolon introduction gave Gara a distinct role in squad compositions. Her ability to create persistent environmental hazards--the expanding glass ring and splinter cloud--meant she could hold territory while teammates focus on eliminating crystallized threats. This made her particularly valuable in long survival missions and endless content where sustained crowd control becomes the primary objective.

Shattered Lash serves as Gara's primary damage ability and pairs directly with Mass Vitrify. The ability has two distinct modes: a quick tap for close-range slashing attacks or a charge for a wider arcing strike with better range. The scaling mechanic means that modding for power strength and ability range dramatically increases Shattered Lash's effectiveness. This ability becomes the key tool for breaking the crystallized ring from Mass Vitrify, sending shards outward that damage nearby enemies.

Splinter Storm transforms Gara into a mobile source of team survivability. The ability creates a persistent cloud of glass shards around Gara that lasts until recast or ability duration expires. Any ally inside the cloud--including Gara herself--receives a damage reduction buff that scales with power strength and duration. This is one of Gara's most valued utilities in squad play; a single cast can enable teammates to tank otherwise lethal damage. The cloud persists while Gara moves, making it mobile crowd control that punishes enemies for approaching too closely.

Spectrorage is Gara's most unique crowd control ability, creating a 360-degree carousel of mirrors around a target location or enemy. Trapped enemies attack the mirror illusions instead of allies, and each mirror destroyed on enemy attacks inflicts damage back to the attacker. Spectrorage scales dramatically with ability range; a larger range creates a wider carousel that affects more enemies. The ability's duration determines how long the effect persists, making duration mods essential for extended control on tough enemies. Spectrorage synergizes well with Splinter Storm, as blinded and clouded enemies are easier to trap.

Mass Vitrify is Gara's signature ultimate ability and the centerpiece of her advanced playstyle. Casting creates an expanding ring of molten glass that grows outward, crystallizing any enemy that touches it. As enemies become crystallized, they contribute their health and shield values to the ring's strength, making the ring progressively more durable as more threats are trapped. The ring also blocks incoming weapons fire, allowing it to serve as a mobile barricade for the squad. Once the expansion is complete, the ring harddens into an impenetrable barrier. Shattered Lash can break segments of the ring, sending razor-sharp shards outward that deal massive damage to nearby enemies.

## Passive

Gara's passive ability grants a chance to create a radial blind lasting 10 seconds whenever she casts any ability. The blind affects all enemies in range and disables their sight, forcing them to attack randomly or stand defensively. This passive stacks with the crowd control provided by Spectrorage and Splinter Storm, creating scenarios where enemy groups become layered in disables. A single ability cast can chain into a radial blind that softens enemy focus while other squad members capitalize on the opening. The passive has no cooldown, meaning consecutive ability casts can apply multiple blinds in rapid succession.

## Acquisition

Gara's blueprint and components drop from two primary sources. Her main blueprint is available directly from Cephalon Simaris inside the Relay after completing the Saya's Vigil quest. This quest is a Plains of Eidolon-specific story mission that introduces the Orokin lore connecting to the region and Gara's origin. For players who prefer to farm her components, both Chassis and Neuroptics blueprints drop from Cetus Bounties on Earth, specifically the Level 5-15 and Level 20-40 bounty tiers. The drop rates are varied across rotations, with Rare drops on the Neuroptics (approximately 8-9% per rotation) and similar rates for the Chassis from multiple bounty difficulty brackets. Players typically run multiple rounds of bounties to accumulate the parts needed for assembly.

Building Gara requires 25,000 credits and takes 72 hours of construction time in the foundry. Like all warframe blueprints, this time can be skipped with platinum for immediate acquisition. The build components--neuroptics, chassis, and systems--must be farmed separately from the Cetus Bounties, making Gara a mid-game acquisition for players who have unlocked the Plains and progressed through Saya's Vigil. Once constructed, Gara appears fully built and rank 0, ready for immediate use and modding.

## Playstyle

Gara functions best as an enabler for squad survival and damage output rather than as a pure damage dealer or tank. Her playstyle revolves around proactive ability casting to establish field control, with Mass Vitrify as the centerpiece engagement tool. A typical engagement begins with casting Mass Vitrify to establish a crystalline barrier, positioning it to intercept advancing enemy forces. As enemies attempt to cross the ring, they become crystallized and contribute their stats to the ring's durability. This converts enemy threat level into team defense, creating a scaling protective layer.

Splinter Storm provides constant passive damage reduction to nearby allies, encouraging teammates to stay grouped for combat efficiency. Casting Splinter Storm between engagements maintains the cloud's coverage, ensuring continuous protection during repositioning. Spectrorage becomes the tool for isolating high-priority threats--heavy gunners, bombards, or unique enemies that cannot be left unchecked. Trapping them in mirrors removes them from the fight temporarily while their attacks damage the mirrors, dealing reciprocal damage.

Shattered Lash acts as both a utility tool and a damage optimizer. Primary uses include destroying Mass Vitrify rings to release shards at optimal moments, hitting enemies through Splinter Storm's cloud, and finishing isolated targets. A charge attack extends Gara's reach and scales significantly with power strength, making it worthwhile to invest in ability damage mods. The passive blind from ability casting pairs naturally with crowd control chains, allowing Gara to layer multiple soft-control effects. Effective Gara players time ability casts to maximize the synergy between blind duration, Spectrorage carousel timing, and Mass Vitrify ring placement. Her playstyle rewards spatial awareness and squad positioning over raw mechanical execution.

Gara excels in defensive and survival-focused mission types. She becomes increasingly valuable in Void Fissure Rescue and Capture missions where protecting evacuation points is critical, Survival endless missions where persistent crowd control and team sustain win the war of attrition, and Defense missions where holding a small area with an expanding ring is ideal. Her team-support focus means she pairs effectively with damage-focused frames that benefit from the breathing room her abilities provide. Trinity synergizes well as a complementary energy/healing support, while Saryn or Mesa can leverage Gara's crowd control to maximize kill efficiency.

## Recommended builds

Gara supports several distinct build archetypes depending on squad composition and mission objective. An ability-duration build prioritizes casting Splinter Storm and Spectrorage repeatedly, using Continuity, Primed Continuity, and Constitution mods to extend crowd control windows. A range-focused build targets Mass Vitrify's expansion size and Spectrorage's mirror carousel radius using Stretch, Augur Reach, and Power Drift, allowing a single cast to control larger areas. Hybrid builds combine moderate range and duration with power strength, using mods like Umbral Intensify or Blind Rage to scale ability damage while maintaining reasonable casting frequency. Most Gara builds include efficiency mods like Primed Flow and Streamline to sustain spam casting in endless missions.

## Lore

Gara is a warframe born from tragedy and sacrifice in the lore of the Plains of Eidolon. According to the quest narrative and codex entries, Gara was once a devoted companion to the Unum--an ancient being of immense power that guards the Plains. When a Sentient creature threatened the Unum directly, Gara made the ultimate sacrifice, engaging the Sentient in desperate combat. Though mortally wounded, she managed to shatter the Sentient completely, fragmenting its enormous body. The shattered remains of that defeated Sentient became the crystalline entities known as Teralysts, which now roam the Plains as both a blessing and a curse.

Gara's warframe design embodies her origin story. Her glass-based abilities reference the shattered Sentient that became her sacrifice's legacy. The theme of crystallization and glass reflects the frozen moment of her last stand, with her abilities metaphorically recreating that encounter through geometric, crystalline aesthetics. Her connection to the Unum and the Plains makes her culturally significant to the Ostron people living in the region. The Ostron revere her as a guardian spirit, a warframe given form through void technology but animated by the spirit of sacrifice.

The Plains of Eidolon introduced players to the broader Warframe mythology beyond the Solar System, revealing the Orokin, the Sentients, and the Void Fissure mechanics. Gara's narrative fits into this larger tapestry, showing how individual sacrifice resonates across time and empowers new guardians--the Tenno--to continue the fight against ancient threats. Her abilities serve as a mechanical metaphor for resilience through crystallization: converting damage and enemy threat into an ever-stronger barrier that protects those who stand with her.
