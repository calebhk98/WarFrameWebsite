## Overview

Citrine is a support-oriented warframe released in Update 32.3 (February 2023) with a focus on crystalline defense and elemental synergy. Her kit centers around creating protective barriers, summoning offensive crystal constructs, and generating resource droplets for her team. She excels in group content where her aura and shell can sustain squadmates through high-damage encounters. Unlike pure damage frames, Citrine trades raw offense for survivability and utility, making her particularly valuable in endurance runs and defensive missions.

The warframe's aesthetic draws inspiration from gemstones and mineral formations, with crystalline armor and a color palette dominated by cool blues and purples. Her design reflects a guardian archetype, reinforced by her lore as the protector of the Zariman colonists Belric and Rania during the Old War.

Preserving Shell is the cornerstone of Citrine's defensive playstyle. The shield strength scales with power strength, and duration scaling extends its active window. Importantly, kills and assists within range fully refresh the shield's strength, making it exceptionally powerful in squad play where teammates contribute to upkeep. The ability excels against burst-damage enemies where raw health pools fail.

The growth hitpoints scale with power strength, and shooting them grants critical multiplier benefits for high-damage weapons, particularly sniper rifles and crit-scaling melees.

## Passive

Citrine's passive, Geoluminesence, emits a continuous health regeneration aura affecting her and allies within 50 meters. The base regeneration stands at 5 health per second, but picking up health orbs increases this value significantly, stacking up to a maximum. This passive is foundational to Citrine's role; the regeneration allows squads to sustain through moderate, continuous damage without relying on dedicated healing frames. The orb-pickup mechanic synergizes elegantly with Fractured Blast and Preserving Shell, creating a loop where offense generates resources that improve defense.

## Acquisition

Citrine blueprints drop from the Tyana Pass Defense mission on Mars, available in Rotation C. All three component blueprints (Neuroptics, Chassis, Systems) share the same mission and rotation, requiring extended Defense runs to farm all parts. The mission is locked behind Heart of Deimos quest completion, which also gates access to Zariman content. Construction requires 72 hours and a market purchase or in-game farming of resources including Orokin Cells.

Because Citrine does not have a Prime variant, the standard version remains the only acquisitionpath. This makes her farmable consistently year-round without vault concerns, though Rotation C spawn rates mean efficient farming benefits from resource boosters and squad coordination.

## Recommended Builds

Citrine's versatility supports multiple build archetypes:

**Support Shield Strength:** Prioritize power strength, duration, and range to maximize Preserving Shell's protective value. A full squad in the shell's radius with continuous refresh becomes nearly unkillable, particularly in endless Defense and Survival missions.

**Ability Duration:** Extended duration on Preserving Shell and Crystallize paralyze time allows for more forgiving ability management, useful in lower-to-mid star chart content where enemy density does not require constant recasts.

**Balanced Efficiency:** Moderate efficiency (around 130-150%) with power strength and range balances Citrine's modest energy pool, allowing consistent four-ability cycling while maintaining reasonable cooldown windows.

## Synergies

Citrine pairs exceptionally well with ability-damage weapons and frames. Her Prismatic Gem provides consistent status priming, which secondary-weapon users or status-focused frames exploit for cascading procs. Frames like Saryn or Mirage benefit from her elemental application on grouped enemies.

Her shield-building nature synergizes with link-based builds and aura mods that convert shields to additional survivability. Squad compositions featuring external damage (crit-focused weapons, raw-damage frames) leverage her crowd control and resource generation without competing for damage scalars.

## Lore

During the Old War, Citrine served as guardian Warframe to Belric and Rania, two residents of the Zariman colony ship. When the Sentients invaded Deimos and the Infestation threatened the moon, the couple activated a biological counter-measure, releasing plague spores across the colonized region. Rather than retreating, Citrine chose sacrifice, transmuting Belric and Rania's bodies into crystalline matrices to preserve them and contain the outbreak.

After Deimos returned to Martian orbit centuries later, void-touched smugglers discovered that separating the crystallized remains would create a Void tunnel linking the two together--a living monument to the couple's bond. From the Void tunnel emerged Citrine's remnants, a new Warframe bearing the crystalline nature of her sacrifice. Her kit mirrors this narrative: her abilities generate fractals and gems from the same crystalline legacy, and her guardian role persists as a protective aura extending that sacrifice to modern squadmates.
