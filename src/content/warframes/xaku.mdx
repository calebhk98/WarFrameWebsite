## Overview

Xaku is a uniquely fragmented warframe composed of broken pieces from multiple lost warframes drifting through the void. Rather than a singular unified design, Xaku embodies the collective power of shattered frames--three distinct essence fragments (representing Vasto's void offense, Boreal's crowd control, and Ivara's strategic prowess) converge into a singular pluriform entity. Introduced in Update 29 (Heart of Deimos) in August 2020, Xaku represents the first truly community-created warframe, with its concept, design, abilities, and aesthetic all shaped by player input during an unprecedented collaborative development process.

With 269 health and 239 shields, Xaku occupies a mid-tier survivability niche, neither fragile nor bulky. The 146 armor provides modest damage mitigation, encouraging tactical positioning and ability-centric gameplay rather than sustained firefights. The passive, Pluriform, grants damage reduction against area-of-effect attacks and a chance to avoid weapon damage entirely--traits reflecting Xaku's fractured nature and distributed essence across multiple void-touched pieces. An energy pool of 160 and sprint speed of 1.02x support active ability casting, though Xaku is not built for sustained sprinting. This statistical profile positions Xaku as a highly technical frame requiring skillful ability management, void damage exploitation, and positioning awareness to reach peak performance in endgame content.

## Abilities

### Xata's Whisper

Xata's Whisper (named after a player-suggested name) imbues Xaku's equipped weapons with void damage and void status effects. When activated, all weapon shots channel void energy, converting their damage type and applying void procs that ignore many enemy resistances and augment ability damage. The ability has no inherent damage stat--its power derives entirely from the weapons Xaku carries and how mods amplify void scaling. This makes Xata's Whisper deeply synergistic with high-crit weapons, multishot setups, and status-focused builds. The ability persists until recast or until The Vast Untime expires (the two interact via unique freeze mechanics).

### Grasp of Lohk

Grasp of Lohk summons void tendrils that steal weapons from enemies in a radius, converting them into autonomous floating armaments that auto-target and attack nearby foes. Stolen weapons deal damage that scales with enemy level, making the ability more potent in high-level missions where enemies are more powerful. Critically, the number of weapons grabbed determines the damage multiplier applied to The Lost: Deny's void beam, creating a complex synergy loop between Xaku's second and third abilities. Each stolen weapon persists until destroyed by enemy fire or until Xaku recasts the ability. Players can strategically position and reposition to grab high-tier enemy weapons (weapons dropped by higher-level units or heavy gunners) for superior firepower.

### The Lost

The Lost is Xaku's signature ability, cycling through three distinct submodes, each drawing from a different broken warframe essence. **Accuse** (the first mode) manifests void fissures that corrupt nearby enemies into temporary allies, similar to Loki's mind-control but with area denial properties. **Gaze** (the second mode) grasps targets in void tendrils that immobilize them; damage dealt to Gaze-affected enemies is captured and re-applied when the target is released, enabling powerful burst combos. **Deny** (the third mode) unleashes a devastating void beam that eradicates all targets in a line and strips Sentient resistances, making it critical for Sentient-heavy missions or Void Fissure content. Cycling between submodes is instant, rewarding players who master ability rotation and read the combat state real-time.

### The Vast Untime

The Vast Untime is Xaku's ultimate, shedding their outer frame in an explosive detonation while assuming a skeletal mobility form with increased sprint speed and reduced collision. Enemies hit by the frame shrapnel take amplified void damage and are weakened to void-type harm. Uniquely, any other active Xaku abilities--Xata's Whisper duration, Grasp's stolen weapons, Gaze's captured damage--pause their timers while The Vast Untime is active and resume when the transformation expires. This time-freeze mechanic creates extraordinary depth: players can set up Xata's Whisper, grab weapons with Grasp, set up Gaze traps, activate The Vast Untime to dodge danger or reposition, and all timers wait patiently for the frame to return to full form.

## Passive

Pluriform grants Xaku two distinct defensive benefits reflective of their shattered, distributed nature. First, Xaku takes reduced damage from area-of-effect attacks (enemy grenades, artillery strikes, ability splashes) compared to the baseline. Second, Xaku has a chance per incoming weapon hit to outright avoid the damage entirely--a mechanic that rewards evasive positioning and ability-based offense over tank-heavy strategies. The passive scales with ability strength mods in some interpretations but is primarily a flat defensive layer. This encourages Xaku players to embrace mobility and ability spam rather than standing still absorbing fire.

## Acquisition

Xaku's acquisition path is split between quest reward and endgame farming. The frame's blueprint is awarded by Cephalon Simaris for 175,000 standing (unlocked after completing the Heart of Deimos quest), or as a direct quest reward during the Heart of Deimos questline. The three component blueprints--Neuroptics, Chassis, and Systems--drop from Cambion Drift bounties on Deimos, with varying drop chances across difficulty tiers (Level 15-25, Level 30-40, and Level 100 bounties). Neuroptics drop from Rotation A of the Level 15-25 bounty; Chassis from Rotation A of the Level 40-60 and Level 100 bounties; Systems from Rotation A and B of the Level 30-40 bounty. Crafting requires 25,000 credits, three Orokin Cells, and standard Deimos-era materials (Pustules, Phasic Cells, etc.), with a 72-hour build time. New players gain access to Xaku's components once they complete the Heart of Deimos quest and unlock Cambion Drift bounties, typically around Mastery Rank 8-10. Unlike most warframe parts, Xaku components are non-tradeable, requiring each player to farm their own copy. The acquisition difficulty is moderate compared to assassination-based frames, but the bounty-gating means players must be comfortable with open-world content and squad-based missions.

## Playstyle

Xaku is a technical, high-ceiling frame requiring real-time ability management and positioning awareness to achieve peak performance. The frame excels in void-focused builds that leverage Xata's Whisper's void-damage buff and Deny's void-beam nuke. Typical playstyle involves opening an engagement by casting Xata's Whisper to empower weapon damage, then repositioning while firing to trigger weapon procs. Once enough enemies cluster, Grasp of Lohk is cast to steal their weapons (or to set up Deny's multiplier bonus). From there, cycling through The Lost's modes responds to the tactical moment: Accuse for crowd control and allies, Gaze for single-target burst, or Deny to delete threat. The Vast Untime acts as both an evasion tool (shedding armor means less collision drag and faster mobility) and a reset button--activating it freezes all other ability timers, allowing Xaku to regain position or energy before resuming the offense.

Advanced players leverage the time-freeze mechanic by stacking Gaze's captured damage before The Vast Untime, then unleashing it after transformation for burst kills. Others exploit Grasp's weapon-count scaling by carefully positioning to grab high-rarity enemy weapons, then amplifying their damage with Deny. In long endless missions, Xaku's void damage and Sentient stripping make them invaluable for Void Fissures or Steel Path content where enemies wear heavy void resistances. The frame demands positioning, ability cycling discipline, and enemy-reading skills but rewards those with extraordinary versatility and damage output.

## Recommended builds

For void-damage amplification and ability spam, equip Xaku with Blind Rage, Augur Intensify, and Umbral Vitality to boost both offense and survivability. Primed Continuity extends The Lost's mode duration and Gaze's damage-capture window. A full void-focus guide is available at [Xaku Void Annihilation](/builds/xaku/void-annihilation).

For a mobility-and-time-freeze build, invest in ability strength (Power Drift, Augur Intensify) and ability efficiency (Streamline, Fleeting Expertise) to spam The Vast Untime for repositioning and resets. The Relentless Lost augment adds active-mode cycling, turning ability rotation into offense. See [Xaku Time Mastery](/builds/xaku/time-freeze) for full mod and playstyle details.

For a weapon-theft strategy, maximize Grasp of Lohk's stolen-weapon potency by balancing range (Stretch, Augur Reach) and ability strength. Pair with a high-damage melee weapon as a failsafe. See [Xaku Weaponsmith](/builds/xaku/weapon-theft) for advanced tactics.

## Synergies

Xaku synergizes most strongly with void-damage scaling weapons, crit-heavy primaries (Kuva Bramma, Zariman Void Cascade), and status-focused melee options (Plague Krasis, Arca Plasmor). The stolen weapons from Grasp of Lohk inherit all mods, meaning a well-built weapon in Xaku's hands amplifies the stolen arsenal exponentially. Arcane Energize sustains ability spam across long missions; Arcane Guardian provides emergency armor scaling when Xaku's passiv e dodge fails. Zenurik focus school boosts energy regeneration during operator void mode, critical for ability-centric builds.

Xaku pairs exceptionally well with squad compositions featuring energy supports (Trinity, Wisp) for endless ability uptime, and with crowd-control frames (Khora, Vauban) for synchronized enemy clustering before Xata's Whisper and Gaze bursts. In Void Fissures, Xaku's Deny is invaluable for stripping Sentient resistances, making the frame essential for coordinated raid-level content.

## Lore

Xaku's origin is entirely unique in Warframe's narrative. Rather than being engineered by the Orokin as a singular cohesive weapon, Xaku was assembled from fragments of broken, abandoned warframes that drifted through the Void--pieces of Ivara's precision, Vasto's void offense, and Boreal's control essences coalesced into one entity. This pluriform nature reflects both literal multiplicity (three frame fragments in one body) and philosophical unity (broken pieces finding strength in union). The Void itself reshaped these shards into a new form, neither wholly one frame nor three, but an entirely novel existence. Xaku's passive damage avoidance and AOE reduction mirror this fractured state--they are too distributed, too broken to be easily harmed in conventional ways.

Lore-wise, Xaku was not discovered by the Tenno but crafted by them, in partnership with the Ostrons and Cephalon Simaris. The quest Heart of Deimos sees the player assisting in the construction and awakening of this new warframe, making Xaku's origin deeply player-centric. Unlike Ash or Mag (engineered by distant Orokin), Xaku is a warframe the Tenno helped birth, giving the frame narrative weight as a symbol of player agency and creative evolution. The frame's abilities--borrowed from lost warframes--suggest a broader lore of warframe experimentation and the Void's power to reshape matter. Xaku's existence implies countless other broken warframe pieces drift in the Void, waiting for a Tenno skilled enough to gather and forge them into new forms.

Post-release lore emphasizes Xaku's role as a Void-touched entity distinct from the Sentients themselves yet capable of leveraging Sentient-stripping void damage. Codex entries hint at Xaku's fractured consciousness--three distinct warframe personalities or essences competing and cooperating within one frame. This creates a lore-gameplay link: The Lost's mode-cycling reflects internal negotiation between three fragment-personalities for control. Xaku is not a single warframe but a democratic collective, each piece contributing power to the whole.
