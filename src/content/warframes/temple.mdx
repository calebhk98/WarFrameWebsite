## Overview

Temple is a rhythm-based warframe that fuses music and elemental damage into a unique, timing-dependent combat framework. Armed with Lizzie, an Infested guitar that serves as both weapon and amplifier, Temple brings heat-focused damage and team support through synchronized ability timing with the Backbeat metronome. With balanced health (405), shield (225), and armor (325) stats, Temple occupies a middle ground between fragility and durability, designed not as a pure tank but as an agile heat-mage who thrives when abilities are synchronized with the pulse of battle itself.

Temple's core mechanic revolves around the Backbeat--a passive rhythm system that rewards players for casting abilities in sync with an internal metronome. Successfully timed casts consume less energy, deal enhanced damage, and unlock secondary effects that chain into team-wide bonuses. This timing-based gameplay differentiates Temple from ability-spam frames, demanding rhythmic, deliberate casting instead of rapid-fire rotations. For players seeking a more interactive, skill-rewarding playstyle, Temple offers engaging musical synchronization mechanics paired with substantial heat-damage output.

The frame's strength lies in ability chaining--Pyrotechnics and Overdrive generate Heat damage in area patterns, while Ripper's Wail provides personal invulnerability and team damage amplification. Exalted Solo, the ultimate, builds on a charge system tied to successful Backbeat hits, then unleashes a high-damage elemental guitar blast with the ability to switch between heat, cold, electric, and toxin flavors mid-aimed attack. Temple's playstyle emphasizes active rhythm management over passive damage--players who master the Backbeat will unlock significant efficiency and damage-per-cast improvements.

Pyrotechnics is Temple's primary single-target heat attack, blasting enemies with pillars of fire that respond to timing. The base cast unleashes a controlled heat-damage pillar at the targeted location. When cast on Backbeat, the ability ignites additional pillars around the impact zone, dramatically increasing coverage. The synced version also extends the duration and heat damage of Ripper's Wail, creating a chain interaction--use Pyrotechnics on-beat to empower the next Ripper's Wail cast. In wave-based missions, chaining timed Pyrotechnics casts builds a rhythm that layers heat effects across the battlefield.

Overdrive is Temple's area-denial heat tool, driving loudspeakers into distortion to create a damaging wave. The base cast knocks down enemies and applies Heat Status, while also temporarily increasing their vulnerability to critical hits. The off-beat version is safe for general damage; however, syncing to Backbeat doubles the Critical Chance vulnerability, turning nearby enemies into squishy targets for follow-up weapon fire. Like Pyrotechnics, successful Overdrive hits on-beat increase Ripper's Wail's damage and duration, incentivizing rhythm-based casting chains across the full ability rotation.

Ripper's Wail is Temple's sustain and team-support ability, fusing personal defense with squad damage amplification. The cast grants Temple brief invulnerability while healing the frame. The true power emerges through team interaction--all allied weapons in Affinity Range gain bonus Heat Damage stacks each time Temple successfully hits enemies with Backbeat-synced abilities. In squad play, a rhythm-aware Temple player becomes an enabler, turning teammates' weapon fire into incendiary volleys. Backbeat-synced Ripper's Wail extends the invulnerability window, providing both survivability and window-extension for executing Exalted Solo casts.

Exalted Solo is Temple's ultimate, summoning Lizzie--the Infested guitar--as an exalted weapon platform. The ability charges based on Backbeat timing (no energy cost when fully charged). Once active, Lizzie unleashes a focused heat-damage beam while Temple holds aim. During aimed fire, players can toggle between heat, cold, electric, and toxin elemental types, allowing dynamic adaptation to enemy armor types. A single Exalted Solo cast can sustained-fire across the entire enemy wave, with higher damage output from successful Backbeat-timed building. Teams should coordinate around Exalted Solo windows to maximize focused-fire damage.

## Passive

Temple's Backbeat is a game-changing passive that transforms ability timing from a quality-of-life tweak into a core mechanical pillar. When Temple casts an ability in sync with the internal Backbeat metronome, ability energy costs decrease significantly (roughly 20-40% depending on mod configuration), and the ability triggers an enhanced version with additional effects. Mastering Backbeat synchronization is essential to Temple's success--off-beat casting works but wastes efficiency, while on-beat casting multiplies damage and duration outputs. The passive runs continuously and requires no energy, making every cast an opportunity for rhythmic optimization.

## Acquisition

Temple is obtained from Hollvania's Solstice Square Defense mission (a recent star chart node). The blueprint and components drop across all rotations, with earlier rotations offering the blueprint and chassis at lower drop chances, while rotation C yields systems at highest chance. Like all warframe components, each piece--blueprint, neuroptics, chassis, and systems--must be farmed individually. The complete build takes 72 hours to craft. Temple is non-tradable, requiring each player to farm the full set.

## Playstyle

Temple's playstyle is rhythmic and timing-sensitive, rewarding players who master the Backbeat and chain abilities synchronously. In survival missions, Temple excels as a mid-tier damage dealer paired with energy efficiency mods. The rotation is straightforward: sync Pyrotechnics -> Overdrive -> Ripper's Wail -> charge and fire Exalted Solo. Each on-beat cast amplifies the next ability's power, building momentum. In squad play, Temple becomes a team amplifier--each synced ability boost to Ripper's Wail translates into weapon-damage-as-heat for the entire squad within Affinity Range.

Temple is not designed for raw ability spam. Instead, players should learn the Backbeat's rhythm, anticipate enemy positions, and time ability casts deliberately. This playstyle suits mission types with breathing room (Survival, Excavation) more than frantic missions like Void Fissure Endless where rhythm breaks. However, once synchronized, Temple's ability-efficiency multipliers offset energy constraints, allowing sustained rotations even on energy-hungry builds.

## Recommended builds

For heat-damage scaling, equip Ability Strength (Intensify, Augur Intensify) and Ability Range (Stretch) mods to maximize Pyrotechnics pillar count and Overdrive coverage. Pair with Primed Flow for energy capacity and Streamline for cooldown reduction between casts.

For squad support, maximize Ability Efficiency (Streamline, Fleeting Expertise) to minimize Backbeat-synced energy costs, allowing rapid ability chaining and more frequent Ripper's Wail casts to keep allies' weapons buffed. Equip Primed Vigor for survivability against early-wave spike damage.

For Exalted Solo focus, gear for high Ability Strength and Duration (Continuity) to sustain the guitar beam and maximize elemental cycling uptime. Use Primary Deadhead or Arcane Energize to restore energy between ultimate casts.

## Lore

Temple emerged from the Void as a frame obsessed with rhythm, sound, and pyrotechnic spectacle. Armed with Lizzie, an Infested guitar that hums with Orokin resonance frequencies, Temple channels destructive sound waves as heat-element attacks. The Backbeat metronome embedded in Temple's consciousness--a relic of Orokin music-magic technology--attunes the frame's abilities to harmonic cycles that amplify power when synchronized.

In Tenno culture, Temple's music-based combat represents a departure from traditional martial approaches. Rather than raw damage scaling or ability spam, Temple demands rhythm, patience, and deliberate timing. The Infested guitar suggests Orokin experimentation with biological-mechanical hybrids, weaponizing living organisms through resonant frequency manipulation. Temple's heat focus and crowd-control through Pyrotechnics/Overdrive hint at ancient sound-based weaponry designed to disorient and incinerate in synchronized waves.

Temple's lore ties to the Solstice event, a thematic celebration of music and performance in the Void. No confirmed Prime variant exists yet, though speculation abounds about whether a "Primed" Lizzie or enhanced Backbeat system might emerge in future updates.
