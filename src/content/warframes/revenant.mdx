## Overview

Revenant is a warframe built from the remnants of an Eidolon, designed to harness Sentient energy as both a defensive shield and an offensive force. With an exceptionally high shield pool of 635 and strong armor of 135, Revenant prioritizes defensive survivability while maintaining high crowd control through his unique thrall system. His toolkit revolves around converting enemies into Enthralled servants, sustaining through Mesmer Skin's damage redirection, and dealing massive area-of-effect damage with Danse Macabre. Released in The Sacrifice update, Revenant represents a fusion of Sentient technology and Warframe design, making him exceptionally potent for higher-level content where both survival and crowd control are paramount.

Unlike frames that rely purely on health or armor, Revenant's defensive strategy centers on shields and crowd control. His moderate health pool of 270 is compensated by his massive shield capacity, which allows for aggressive play styles supported by defensive abilities. The 135 armor and baseline 1.0 sprint speed encourage players to engage enemies directly while relying on ability rotations to sustain shields and control the battlefield. Revenant excels in missions where environmental threats are secondary to enemy pressure, making him ideal for deep void fissures, high-level void cascade bounties, and longer-duration content like survival or defense missions.

## Abilities

### Enthrall

Enthrall targets a single enemy and converts them into a loyal thrall under Revenant's control. Enthralled enemies deal bonus damage to their former allies, effectively turning their firepower against their squad. When thralls die, they explode in a damaging pillar of Eidolon energy, creating a secondary source of crowd control and burst damage. The ability's duration scales with ability duration mods, allowing long-lasting thrall armies to control entire rooms. Pairing Enthrall with high-range builds allows rapid thrall conversion across large enemy groups, while strength-heavy builds make each thrall deal devastating damage to their former allies.

### Mesmer Skin

This ability wraps Revenant in a protective layer of Sentient energy that reflects damage back to attackers and stuns them in the process. The duration scales with ability duration, and each hit absorbed contributes to the stun effect. Critically, any stunned enemies can be Enthralled at zero energy cost, creating a powerful synergy between the second and first abilities. In high-level content, Mesmer Skin provides both offense and defense--it protects Revenant while setting up free Enthrall conversions. Enemies attacking a Mesmer Skin-protected Revenant are automatically stunned, making this ability exceptional for burst-heavy enemy factions like the Corpus or heavy Grineer units.

### Reave

Reave enables Revenant to dash through enemies as a wall of Sentient energy, leeching shields and health from all targets encountered. This ability scales with ability strength and benefits from range mods for larger effective areas. Enhanced versions of Reave are applied to Enthralled targets, allowing greater damage and sustain. The ability serves as both a mobility tool for repositioning and a survivability mechanic through shield and health restoration. In aggressive playstyles, Reave can be cast repeatedly to maintain maximum shields while moving through dense enemy groups, effectively doubling Revenant's already impressive shield pool.

### Danse Macabre

Revenant's ultimate ability erupts with countless Eidolon energy beams that sweep in a devastating circle. These beams intelligently modify their damage type based on enemy defenses encountered, adapting between impact, puncture, and slash to exploit weaknesses. A unique property allows incoming damage to be redirected into the beams, effectively converting enemy fire into additional output. Holding the ability key increases potency--raising status effect chance and damage--but at the cost of higher energy drain per second. In longer engagements, Danse Macabre can be held to maintain sustained area control while thralls finish isolated targets, and Enthralled enemies killed by the ability drop overshield pickups for the squad.

## Passive

Revenant's passive triggers when his shields are fully depleted, releasing a shockwave that knocks down all nearby enemies. This passive transforms a defensive weakness into a tactical advantage--shield break becomes an opportunity to reset positioning or apply crowd control. In high-damage environments where shields are constantly depleted, the passive can activate multiple times per mission, providing consistent crowd control and a few seconds of breathing room. Build strategies that emphasize shield recharge and absorption ensure the passive activates frequently, turning Revenant into a consistent crowd control engine.

## Acquisition

Revenant's blueprint is obtained from Mother's void cascade bounties on the Cambion Drift, the open-world region of Deimos. Unlike traditional assassination boss drops, Revenant requires completion of higher-level bounties that reward the blueprint as the tier-2 or tier-3 completion reward. The bounties take approximately 10-15 minutes per run and scale in difficulty, making Revenant a mid-to-late-game acquisition requiring some star chart progression and basic modded gear. Players must also gather component blueprints from the same bounty source before assembling the full frame. The crafting cost is modest at 25,000 credits, but the overall time investment is higher than early-game frames like Ash or Volt. Once built, Revenant becomes immediately usable for higher-level content, justifying the upfront farming cost.

## Playstyle

Revenant thrives as a frontline controller who uses crowd control to enable both personal and squad survivability. Effective Revenant gameplay revolves around three core rotations: casting Enthrall on dangerous enemies to turn their firepower against their allies, activating Mesmer Skin to trigger stunned enemies and enable free Enthrall casts, and using Reave to maintain shield saturation while repositioning. During higher-level missions, players cycle Mesmer Skin offensively to set up thrall chains, using Reave as both escape and sustain between ability casts. Danse Macabre serves as the ultimate crowd control and damage tool, held as needed to handle dense enemy clusters or defend allies during revive windows.

The passive shield shockwave integrates naturally into Revenant's gameplay loop--rather than avoiding shield depletion, skilled players accept it as a trigger for crowd control, especially when combined with shield restoration from Reave or Arcane Energize. Early-game Revenant builds prioritize ability duration and range to maintain long thrall armies and maximize Mesmer Skin's protective circle. Mid-game builds balance strength for thrall damage with duration for sustained protection. Endgame Revenant builds often invest heavily in strength and duration while accepting lower health through Umbral Vitality, relying on Reave and Mesmer Skin's regeneration to maintain shields above dangerous thresholds.

## Recommended builds

For high-level void fissures and survival missions, stack ability duration mods (Continuity, Primed Continuity, Augur Reach) with Intensify and Augur Intensify to maintain long-duration Mesmer Skin protection while keeping Enthrall thralls alive. Pair with Shield Recharge and Augur Secrets to accelerate shield replenishment between Reave casts. This build transforms Revenant into an unkillable crowd control engine that sustains indefinitely. For endless survival runs, see the [Revenant Void Survival Build](/builds/warframes/revenant/void-survival).

For aggressive bounty farming on the Cambion Drift, prioritize range (Stretch, Augur Reach) and strength (Intensify, Power Strength mods) to maximize Enthrall conversion speed and Danse Macabre damage output. This build sacrifices some duration for faster enemy elimination and reduced farming time. See the [Revenant Bounty Farmer Build](/builds/warframes/revenant/bounty-farm) for full loadout recommendations.

## Synergies

Revenant synergizes exceptionally well with the Arcane Energize setup, which restores energy on nearby allies when shields are hit. This creates infinite ability spam cycles, allowing constant thrall generation and Mesmer Skin refresh. Arcane Guardian provides additional armor scaling to further reduce incoming damage. The Zenurik focus school amplifies energy regeneration during operator void mode, allowing Revenant players to maintain aggressive ability usage even in extended engagements.

Weapon choice has minimal impact on Revenant's effectiveness since his abilities handle offensive output. Melee weapons with status-based procs (like Condition Overload) benefit from the diverse damage types applied by Danse Macabre's adaptive beams. Any high-crit weapon synergizes with Danse Macabre's damage boost while held. Helminth subsumption allows replacing lower-priority abilities--many endgame builds replace Reave with Roar or Empower to boost squad damage output or personal ability strength.

## Lore

Revenant was born from tragedy and technological synthesis. According to the Sacrifice, Revenant's blueprint was discovered as a remnant of an Eidolon, a sentient construct from the void that had been corrupted or destroyed during the Operator's awakening. Rather than discard the technology, Lotus preserved the design pattern within the warframe template, creating a frame that harnesses Sentient energy as both an offensive and defensive tool. This explains Revenant's unique ability to subvert enemy will through Enthrall--the Sentient technology within allows him to impose control similar to how the Sentients themselves propagate through infection and assimilation.

In the lore, Revenant represents the boundary between Orokin engineering and Sentient biology. His Mesmer Skin ability mirrors Sentient damage redirection capabilities, while Danse Macabre echoes the Eidolon's devastating beam attacks. The thrall system suggests Sentient-influenced tech that extends Revenant's consciousness into conquered enemies, making them extensions of his will. This fusion of technologies places Revenant outside the traditional Orokin design philosophy, making him a unique testament to the discovery of hybrid warframe-Sentient designs that could reshape the war against Void entities.
