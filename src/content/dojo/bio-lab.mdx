## Overview

The Bio Lab is the Dojo research room dedicated to biological and Infested-origin technology. It provides blueprints for weapons derived from Infested biomass, Corpus bioengineering, and hybrid biological-mechanical designs. The Bio Lab is one of the five core research labs available in every Dojo and is typically among the first that new clans prioritize constructing due to the weapon families it unlocks.

The lab is thematically consistent with its in-universe framing. The Tenno and their allied researchers study Infested biology not to understand or sympathize with the Infestation as a force, but to extract functional weapon designs from organisms whose natural weapons -- spores, tendons, corrosive secretions -- have been weaponized by the Orokin and continued to evolve during the Infested expansion across the Origin System.

Constructing the Bio Lab requires the same Dojo prerequisite chain as other labs and scales in cost by clan tier.

## Available research

Bio Lab research includes weapon blueprints for several weapon families with Infested or biological design origins. These include weapons featuring biological projectile systems, acid-based damage, and Infested-style status proc emphasis. The research tree is tiered, with initial projects using common materials and later-tier projects requiring rarer materials including Infested-specific components.

Several research projects in the Bio Lab specifically require materials that drop from Infested enemies or from Infested missions, tying the lab's thematic content to the source material for its construction inputs. This is not unique to the Bio Lab -- several Dojo labs have construction requirements tied to the faction their technology originates from.

## Costs

Bio Lab construction costs follow the clan tier multiplier system. Material types required include standard Foundry resources and Infested-specific drops. The lab itself requires a Dojo room slot and the completion of prerequisite rooms in the construction chain.

Research project timers are independent of clan activity -- they run continuously after contribution requirements are met and complete on the stated schedule regardless of how many members are online. Clans who complete contribution requirements quickly experience no additional delay beyond the baseline timer.

## Purpose

The Bio Lab provides weapon access that complements builds focused on Toxin and Corrosive damage, two damage types that are highly effective against Grineer and Corpus enemies respectively. Several Bio Lab weapons have among the highest base Toxin damage outputs in their weapon categories, making them relevant for endgame build optimization.

For Mastery Rank progression, the Bio Lab's weapon catalog provides additional items to rank up, contributing to the total mastery points available from Dojo research weapons.
