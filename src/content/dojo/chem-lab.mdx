## Overview

The Chem Lab is the Dojo research room centered on chemical warfare and Corpus-affiliated weapon technology. It provides blueprints for weapons that emphasize Gas damage, Toxin applications, and chemically exotic delivery systems. Like other Dojo research labs, the Chem Lab must be constructed in the Dojo's room network, costs scale by clan tier, and all research projects within the lab must be completed sequentially through a tiered tree before later blueprints become available.

The thematic framing of the Chem Lab aligns with the Corpus's history of deploying toxic agents and chemical agents as force-multipliers in confined environments such as their gas city installations and research stations. Tenno access to these weapon designs through Dojo research represents the reverse-engineering and adaptation of Corpus chemical weapon technology for Tenno use.

## Available research

Chem Lab research includes weapons with Gas, Toxin, and hybrid chemical damage types as their primary damage profiles. Research projects follow a tiered structure where completion of early projects unlocks later ones. Material requirements scale with project tier -- initial projects use widely available resources, while advanced projects require rarer components.

Some Chem Lab research projects require Corpus-origin materials that drop from Corpus enemies and missions, maintaining a thematic link between the research content and its material source.

## Costs

Chem Lab construction and research costs follow clan tier multipliers. A clan at Ghost tier spends the baseline amount; higher tiers multiply the resource requirements proportionally. The construction timer begins after contribution requirements are met and cannot be expedited through additional contributions.

## Purpose

The Chem Lab expands weapon access for builds that leverage Toxin, Gas, and Corrosive status effects. Gas is a damage type that became more effective against Infested enemies after various status reworks, and weapons with intrinsic Gas or high Toxin base damage are used in builds designed for specific faction matchups.

For players focused on Mastery Rank advancement, the Chem Lab's weapon blueprints provide additional mastery-granting items. Several Chem Lab weapon families have unique combinations of stats that make them competitive choices for specific mission types.

The Chem Lab also contributes to crafting material loops in certain build strategies. Some component blueprints produced through Chem Lab research feed into larger crafting chains for Warframe parts and companion equipment. Completing the full Chem Lab research tree is necessary for clans that want all available weapon blueprints without relying on player-to-player trading.
