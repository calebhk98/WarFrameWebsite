## Overview

Moon Clan is the largest available clan tier in Warframe, with a maximum membership of 1000 players. It represents the apex of the clan size hierarchy and is occupied by the game's largest organized communities, prominent content-creator clans, and long-standing guilds with years of active recruitment behind them.

The 1000-member cap enables a Moon Clan to maintain hundreds of daily active players and function as a semi-public social hub. Many Moon Clans operate with visible public presences -- Discord servers with thousands of members, YouTube or Twitch-affiliated communities, and dedicated event rosters. The logistics of running a Moon Clan resemble those of a community organization rather than a traditional game guild.

Construction and research costs at Moon tier are the highest in the game. The resource multipliers applied to every Dojo room and research project are calibrated to a 1000-member contribution pool, which in practice means that large-scale Moon Clan Dojo buildouts can require hundreds of thousands of each material type across the full construction cycle.

## Member capacity and costs

The 1000-member roster of a Moon Clan is rarely filled with exclusively active players. Active Moon Clans maintain several hundred daily-to-weekly participants, with the remaining slots occupied by seasonal or returning players and community-adjacent members. Slot management -- removing members who have been inactive beyond a clan-defined threshold -- is a continuous background task for Warlords.

Research cost multipliers at Moon tier are applied to all construction. Some advanced research projects, such as those in the Orokin Lab or the final-tier Tenno Lab weapons, require rare components at quantities that necessitate coordinated farming sessions or bulk trading with other players.

## Available rooms and research

Moon Clan Dojos can construct every room type and complete every research project available in the game. The Clan Hall at Moon tier is the largest aesthetic variant, providing the most decoratable space of any tier. This makes Moon Clan Dojos frequent subjects of Dojo decoration showcases in the Warframe community, as the available space permits elaborate thematic builds.

The Dry Dock is available at Moon tier like all others, enabling Railjack construction and the full Railjack mission set. Moon Clan Dojos frequently have multiple Dry Docks to facilitate simultaneous Railjack access for different groups within the clan.

Clan Event scoring at Moon tier places in the top size bracket and competes against other Moon Clans. The potential for sustained high scores in multi-day events is greatest at Moon tier due to the contributor pool size.

## Purpose

Moon Clan exists for large established communities that have grown to require the 1000-member cap for practical reasons -- either because recruitment demand exceeds lower tiers or because the clan's social model depends on a large membership base. The primary tradeoff versus lower tiers is administrative complexity and construction cost, neither of which is prohibitive for an organized community.

For players who join an existing Moon Clan rather than founding one, the experience is principally one of access: a fully built Dojo with all research completed, an active trading and group-finding community, and often a curated decoration showcase that serves as a visual attraction. Founding a Moon Clan and building it from the ground up is among the most resource-intensive long-term projects available in Warframe.
