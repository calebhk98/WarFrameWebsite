## Overview

Shadow Clan is the second tier in the Warframe clan size hierarchy. It supports up to 30 members and occupies the space between the minimal Ghost Clan and the mid-sized Storm Clan. Shadow Clan is the appropriate tier for small-to-medium friend groups or growing casual communities that have outgrown the 10-member Ghost tier cap but do not need or want the organizational overhead of a Storm or higher clan.

Construction and research costs in the Dojo scale upward relative to Ghost Clan, reflecting the larger member base. Shadow Clan is still considered affordable for its size -- a 15 to 30 person group contributing resources regularly can complete a full Shadow Clan Dojo more quickly than a single player completing a Ghost Clan Dojo.

Clan tier is set at the Clan Hall during construction and can be promoted upward by a Warlord with the appropriate standing. Promotion from Ghost to Shadow requires contributing a specific set of materials at the Clan Hall console. Demotion is not available through standard gameplay.

## Member capacity and costs

Shadow Clan's 30-member cap supports a small community with diverse playstyles. The construction cost multiplier relative to Ghost Clan scales the material requirements for each room and each research project proportionally. A research project that requires, for example, 3000 Ferrite in a Ghost Clan requires 9000 Ferrite in a Shadow Clan -- the exact multipliers are documented in the Dojo room construction UI.

The tradeoff is that 30 contributing members can collectively gather the required resources much faster than a 10-person team, making the higher absolute cost less burdensome in practice. Coordinated contribution events, where the clan focuses on a single research project at a time, are common in active Shadow Clans.

## Available rooms and research

Shadow Clan Dojos have access to the full set of Dojo room types. All research labs, the Clan Hall, Barracks, Dueling Room, Breeding Grounds, Obstacle Course, Oracle, and the Dry Dock for Railjack are available at Shadow tier. The Railjack Dry Dock is one of the most strategically important rooms for an active clan, as it enables Railjack construction and serves as the mission launch point for Corpus Proxima and Grineer proxima operations.

Research within each lab follows the same progression as Ghost tier. Earlier research tiers must complete before advanced projects unlock. Shadow Clan research costs are proportional to the tier multiplier but are still accessible for a small active group.

## Purpose

Shadow Clan is the tier most suited to established friend groups and small community clans that have a stable 15 to 30 active members. The 30-member cap is enough for regular Railjack crews, trading groups, and social events without requiring the administrative structure of a larger clan.

From a research access perspective, Shadow Clan provides the same complete Dojo functionality as any tier above it. There are no research projects or rooms exclusive to higher tiers. The difference between tiers above Shadow is purely organizational capacity -- more members, higher costs, different Clan Hall aesthetics, and eligibility for higher-tier Clan Event rewards.
