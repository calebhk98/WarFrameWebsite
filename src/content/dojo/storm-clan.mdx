## Overview

Storm Clan is the third tier in the Warframe clan hierarchy and the first tier that most players consider "medium" scale. It supports up to 100 members. Storm Clan is suitable for active communities that have grown beyond small friend-group size, public clans with moderate recruitment, and clans that participate regularly in Clan Events where higher-tier placement requires a larger active player base.

The Storm tier marks a significant jump in both capacity and organizational requirements. A 100-person clan needs active Warlords and Officers managing recruitment, research coordination, Dojo maintenance, and event participation. Informal management structures that work in Shadow Clan often break down at Storm scale.

Dojo construction and research costs scale proportionally from Shadow. The Storm multiplier makes each room and research project considerably more resource-intensive than lower tiers, but 100 contributors can absorb the cost with a well-organized contribution cycle.

## Member capacity and costs

Storm Clan's 100-member cap is the baseline for competitive Clan Event participation. Many Clan Events have tier brackets that reward clans by size, meaning a Storm Clan competes against other Storm Clans rather than against Moon Clans. This tiered competition structure makes Storm a viable endpoint for clans that want event rewards without scaling to 1000 members.

Construction cost multipliers at Storm tier are calibrated to 100 members contributing at a sustainable pace. In practice, a well-organized Storm Clan can complete major research projects within days of unlocking them through coordinated donation events.

## Available rooms and research

Storm Clan Dojos access the same full room catalog as all other tiers. The Clan Hall at Storm tier unlocks a larger aesthetic variant compared to Shadow, providing more decorating space and a visually distinct central area. All research labs remain available, and the Dry Dock for Railjack construction is present.

Clan Event scores and bonus multipliers in certain events are affected by clan size tier. Storm Clan's 100-member pool provides more potential contributors to timed events, which can affect leaderboard placement in competitive event structures.

## Purpose

Storm Clan is the natural habitat for publicly recruiting mid-size clans, gaming communities with Warframe as a secondary or primary title, and groups that want the social density of a large clan without the extreme administrative overhead of Mountain or Moon tiers. The 100-member cap means the community is large enough for regular group content -- Railjack crews, trading, and endgame farming -- while remaining manageable for a small leadership team.

For clans that aspire to Clan Event competition, Storm is the lowest tier that provides meaningful score potential in multi-day events. Smaller tiers can participate but face a significant scoring disadvantage in events where sustained contribution volume is the primary metric.
