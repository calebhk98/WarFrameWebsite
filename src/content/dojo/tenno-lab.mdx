## Overview

The Tenno Lab is one of the five primary research rooms in a Warframe Dojo. It focuses on technology developed or recovered by the Tenno themselves, including weapon blueprints, Operator-related equipment, and crafting components that do not fit cleanly into the Corpus, Grineer, or Orokin research trees housed in other labs.

Constructing the Tenno Lab requires completing the prerequisite room chain in the Dojo. After construction finishes, clan members can contribute resources to unlock research projects that make blueprints available for purchase with credits from the in-game market equivalent inside the Dojo interface. Once a research project completes in the Tenno Lab, all current and future clan members can purchase the associated blueprint without further research cost.

The Tenno Lab holds several weapon families that are not obtainable through any other method without direct player trading. This makes the lab's completion essential for clans whose members want full weapon access.

## Available research

The Tenno Lab research tree includes weapon blueprints for several Tenno-designed weapon families. Specific research projects correspond to weapon series such as the Strun Wraith, Dera Vandal, and related Tenno-technology variants. Additionally, the Tenno Lab houses research for Sentinel weapons and certain Warframe component blueprints that feed into crafting chains.

Later-tier Tenno Lab research projects require earlier-tier projects to complete first. The full research chain represents a multi-week investment for an active small-to-mid clan, with each project having a completion timer that counts down whether or not clan members are online.

Research material requirements vary by project tier. Early projects use common materials available from Foundry and star chart farming. Later projects require uncommon or rare materials. Coordinating donation contributions from active members is the standard approach in organized clans.

## Costs

Tenno Lab construction costs scale by clan tier. At Ghost Clan, the resource requirement is the baseline amount. At higher tiers, the multiplier applies to all materials. The lab itself requires a room slot in the Dojo, forma to build, credits, and specific material types. The construction timer runs after contribution requirements are met.

## Purpose

The Tenno Lab provides access to a distinct segment of the weapon catalog that is unavailable through Market purchases, mission drops, or Relay vendors. For players who want the broadest possible weapon access for leveling, Mastery Rank advancement, or specific build goals, the Tenno Lab's research is a necessary step.

It is also the lab most associated with Tenno-origin crafting lore -- the idea that Tenno, rather than reverse-engineering Grineer or Corpus technology, produce their own class of weapons through a tradition of craftsmanship and engineering that predates their formal alliance with the Lotus.
