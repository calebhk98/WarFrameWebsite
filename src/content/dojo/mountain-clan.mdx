## Overview

Mountain Clan is the fourth tier in the Warframe clan size hierarchy, supporting up to 300 members. It sits between Storm Clan at 100 members and Moon Clan at 1000 members. Mountain tier is appropriate for large established communities, major guilds from other games that have migrated to Warframe, and organizations with dedicated management teams.

At 300 members, Mountain Clan requires structured leadership. Warlord-level permissions are typically distributed across multiple trusted officers, and recruitment processes become more formalized. Active management of inactive members -- removing long-term inactive players to maintain roster health and free slots -- is a recurring administrative task at this scale.

Dojo construction and research costs at Mountain tier are proportionally larger than Storm. The expectation is that 300 members contribute collectively, which at reasonable activity rates can absorb even large research projects within a few days of coordination.

## Member capacity and costs

The 300-member cap allows Mountain Clan to sustain a large active daily population while maintaining the sub-1000 organizational complexity that Moon Clan introduces. A typical Mountain Clan might have 50 to 100 active daily players, with the remaining roster representing weekly or occasional participants.

Construction cost scaling means that individual rooms and research projects require substantially more materials than at lower tiers. The Mountain multiplier is applied uniformly to all Dojo construction, so even decorative rooms and small utility additions carry the tier's cost overhead.

## Available rooms and research

Mountain Clan Dojos access the same full room catalog as all tiers. The Clan Hall variant at Mountain tier provides additional aesthetic options and a larger decoratable central space. All research labs, the Dry Dock, and specialty rooms including the Obstacle Course and Dueling Arena are available.

Clan Event scoring at Mountain tier competes in a size bracket above Storm but below Moon. The larger contributor pool can sustain higher event scores, and Mountain Clan placement in competitive events is typically strong when the active membership is engaged.

## Purpose

Mountain Clan occupies the space between a community large enough to feel active at any hour and small enough to maintain a coherent clan identity. The 300-member roster provides enough daily traffic for spontaneous group content, trading, and social activity without the relative anonymity of a 1000-member Moon Clan.

Leadership at this scale typically maintains a public-facing Discord or forum, schedules regular clan events, and coordinates Dojo construction through organized contribution periods. The investment required to reach and maintain Mountain tier is significant and is generally only undertaken by groups with long-term Warframe community commitments.
