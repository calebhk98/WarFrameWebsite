## Overview

Ghost Clan is the entry-level clan tier in Warframe, designed for solo players and very small groups. The tier supports a maximum of 10 members, and all Dojo construction costs -- resources, credits, and forma -- are scaled to this population. Ghost Clan is the most accessible path to building a personal or duo Dojo, since the construction costs are a fraction of what larger tiers require for the same rooms.

The Dojo is the clan's private space station, accessible only to clan members. It serves as the central hub for research, social activity, trading, and access to clan-specific mechanics. Ghost Clan members who build and research their Dojo unlock all the same functional content as a Moon Clan Dojo -- the difference is purely in cost scaling and access to the Clan Hall size variants, which affect how the Dojo's layout rooms are arranged.

For a solo player or a small dedicated group, Ghost Clan provides full Dojo functionality with manageable resource investment. Many research blueprints are acquired exclusively through Clan Research, making Dojo construction necessary for specific weapon families and crafting materials.

## Member capacity and costs

Ghost Clan supports a maximum of 10 members. All construction and research costs in the Dojo are scaled per-clan-tier. At Ghost Clan tier, costs are at their lowest multiplier. The resource cost for constructing a standard room, such as the Oracle or Barracks, is lower than the same room in a larger clan tier. The same principle applies to research in the various labs -- fewer materials are required per research project.

Ghost Clan members who are the sole builder of their Dojo are often referred to in the community as solo Dojos. Because all 10 member slots are not needed to access research, a single dedicated player can complete a functional Ghost Clan Dojo at the cost of consistent material collection over multiple sessions.

Promoting a Ghost Clan to Shadow Clan increases the member cap but also increases all future construction and research costs. Clan tier promotion is not reversible through standard means, so players considering solo Dojos should remain at Ghost tier unless they genuinely intend to recruit.

## Available rooms and research

Ghost Clan Dojos can construct every standard Dojo room type, including all research labs (Tenno Lab, Bio Lab, Chem Lab, Energy Lab, Orokin Lab), the Clan Hall, Barracks, Oracle, Obstacle Course, Dueling Room, Breeding Grounds, Dry Dock for Railjack, and all decoration rooms. The Dry Dock room, which enables Railjack construction, is available at all clan tiers including Ghost.

Research projects within the labs follow the same tiered unlock sequence regardless of clan tier -- earlier projects must complete before later ones unlock. Ghost Clan research costs are calibrated to be achievable by a small team contributing regularly without requiring prolonged farming sessions for each project.

## Purpose

Ghost Clan's primary purpose for new or small player groups is providing access to Clan Research blueprints. Numerous weapon families, crafting components, and upgrade materials are locked behind research projects that require a Dojo. Without clan membership and a functional Dojo, these items are inaccessible except through trading with other players.

Secondary purposes include the social functions of the Dojo: a private meeting space, trading post-equivalent rooms for internal clan trades, a dueling arena for practice, and an Obstacle Course for movement training. The Dry Dock in particular serves as the construction and deployment point for a clan's Railjack, which means even Ghost Clan players who want to own and fly a Railjack must build and maintain a Dojo.
