## Overview

Zenurik is the Focus school of storm and intellect. Its symbol is the Star,
representing radiant energy and the power of the mind. Zenurik is the most
widely used Focus school in the game by a significant margin. Its core benefit,
passive energy regeneration through Energizing Dash, effectively eliminates
energy management as a limiting factor for builds that frequently cast abilities.

In a meta where ability uptime often defines a Warframe's contribution to a
squad, Zenurik's energy regeneration is a force multiplier for the entire
loadout. Players who previously relied on [Arcane Energize](/arcanes/arcane-energize)
or energy pads to fuel long ability chains can instead lean on Zenurik and
reallocate those resources elsewhere.

## Way (passive)

Zenurik's Way passive nodes support two different approaches: the Energizing
Dash path (energy regeneration) and the damage augmentation path (Void Stalker
and Contamination Wave). Most players invest in the energy regeneration path
because its returns outweigh damage improvements at every stage of the game.

Zenurik has two Active nodes unlike other schools which typically have one. This
gives Zenurik players more options per Operator session but means Focus investment
must be spread across two costly nodes rather than one.

## Nodes

### Wellspring

Wellspring is Zenurik's first Active node. Activating it places a visible energy
well at the Operator's current position. The well persists for several seconds.
Any Warframe that walks through the well gains a Zenurik pulse that regenerates
energy over time, similar to the effect of Energizing Dash but accessible by
teammates at a fixed location.

In coordinated squads, one player can activate Wellspring at a chokepoint or
objective and multiple teammates can pass through it to simultaneously gain
energy regeneration. This is particularly effective in Defense missions and
Arbitrations where the squad tends to cluster.

Energy cost: 50,000 Focus points.

### Energizing Dash

Energizing Dash is the most important node in the Zenurik tree. After performing
a Void Dash, the Operator immediately triggers a passive energy regeneration
effect on the Warframe and nearby allies. The regeneration pulses every second
for a fixed duration, typically providing 5 energy per second (at base rank).

At maximum Operator rank the regeneration is significantly higher. The exact
values at max rank are not confirmed in WFCD data; community benchmarks report
approximately 5 to 7 energy per second at typical Operator ranks, providing
roughly 50 to 70 energy over the full duration.

Because Void Dashing has a very short cooldown, Energizing Dash can be refreshed
frequently, maintaining near-continuous passive energy regeneration during active
gameplay.

Energy cost: 40,000 Focus points.

### Contamination Wave

Contamination Wave is Zenurik's second Active node, releasing a radial wave from
the Operator that applies a Void damage-over-time effect to enemies within range.
The damage is moderate and scales with Operator Focus rank. Contamination Wave
is used by players who want the Operator to contribute damage while also managing
Energizing Dash for energy sustain, but the ability competes with Wellspring for
energy cost within a single Operator session.

Energy cost: 30,000 Focus points.

### Void Stalker

Void Stalker increases the Operator's critical chance on Void beam attacks. At
base, Operator beams have minimal critical chance. Void Stalker pushes this into
a range where some amp configurations with critical chance bonuses can achieve
meaningful crit rates. The node is secondary to the energy regeneration nodes
for most Zenurik players.

Energy cost: 25,000 Focus points.

## Waybounds

**Inner Might** is the Zenurik Waybound that unlocks a version of energy
regeneration tied to Warframe ability casts rather than Void Dashes. After
casting any Warframe ability, Inner Might triggers a short burst of passive
energy regeneration. This regeneration is weaker than Energizing Dash but
operates passively without requiring Operator transference, making it useful
for players who prefer to stay in Warframe form during combat.

In practice, Inner Might and Energizing Dash stack: a player who Dashes for
Energizing Dash and then casts abilities benefits from both regeneration effects
simultaneously.

**Temporal Drag** causes Void Slings to slow enemies passed through. The slow
is similar to Unairu's Basilisk Gaze but triggered by Sling movement rather than
beam hits. Temporal Drag is primarily useful in missions with dense enemy groups
where repositioning frequently passes through clusters.

Both Waybounds unlock account-wide once purchased.

## Recommended warframes

Zenurik provides value to virtually every Warframe but is most impactful for
frames that spend energy continuously:

- [Saryn](/warframes/saryn) -- Spores, Molt, Miasma, and Toxic Lash all consume
  energy in rapid cycles; Zenurik allows Saryn to cast aggressively without
  managing Energy Orbs between casts.
- [Octavia](/warframes/octavia) -- Mallet, Resonator, Metronome, and Amp all
  together consume significant energy to maintain simultaneously; Zenurik
  keeps all four abilities active.
- [Gauss](/warframes/gauss) -- Redline at 100% Battery drains energy rapidly;
  Zenurik offsets this drain during sustained high-Battery play.
- [Mesa](/warframes/mesa) -- Peacemaker is a channeled ability that drains
  energy per second; Zenurik extends the duration a Mesa player can remain in
  Peacemaker without energy backup.

## Build priorities

1. Unlock Energizing Dash first -- it provides the core energy benefit
   immediately.
2. Unlock Wellspring for squad energy support.
3. Unlock Inner Might Waybound for passive ability-triggered regeneration.
4. Unlock Temporal Drag Waybound for enemy slowing.
5. Unlock Void Stalker and Contamination Wave if Operator damage is a goal.

## Lore

Zenurik is attributed to the Orokin scholar caste -- the researchers and
engineers who studied and harnessed the Void. Its Star symbol represents the
radiating power of knowledge applied at scale. The school's teachings focus
on the idea that the Void is not a weapon but a resource, something to be
channeled and conserved rather than simply unleashed.

Tenno who walk the Zenurik Way are taught that energy management is a form of
discipline, and that the ability to sustain power over time is ultimately more
valuable than any single moment of peak output.
