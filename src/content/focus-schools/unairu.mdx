## Overview

Unairu is the Focus school of stone and endurance. Its symbol is the Eye within
a triangle, representing insight beneath armor. Unairu is the most defensively
oriented school, offering crowd control through petrification, enemy defense
stripping, and buffs that increase the Operator's own survivability. It is the
least common school in competitive end-game contexts like Eidolon hunting, but
it shines in solo Steel Path and missions where enemy crowd control and armor
stripping are more valuable than raw damage.

Unairu's Waybound Stone Skin permanently boosts Operator armor, contributing to
the account-wide toughness of the Operator regardless of which school is active.

## Way (passive)

Unairu's Way passives focus on making the Operator harder to kill and more
disruptive to enemies. Basilisk Gaze applies a slow to enemies hit by Void beam
attacks, making even the act of firing the Operator's beam a debuffing action.
Poise increases the Operator's shield capacity, extending how long it can remain
in combat before needing to retreat into Void Mode.

The school has two Active nodes rather than one, giving Unairu players more
active options per Operator session than other schools.

## Nodes

### Unairu Wisp

The Active node named Unairu Wisp creates a petrifying burst from the Operator's
position. Enemies within the blast radius are first slowed, then frozen in place
for the duration. Petrified enemies cannot attack, move, or dodge, making the
window useful for precision shooting from teammates or for executing vulnerable
priority targets.

Wisp pickups spawn on Operator kills, with a percentage chance per kill. Allied
players who run through the floating Wisp gain a temporary armor and shield
bonus. In organized squads, a dedicated Unairu player can maintain a near-
constant Wisp buff cycle for teammates during dense enemy encounters.

Energy cost: 50,000 Focus points.

### Magnetic Flare

Magnetic Flare is a projectile Active that strips enemy shields and armor within
its blast. The strip percentage is not documented in WFCD data; community testing
reports approximately 50% armor and shield removal per activation at base
Operator rank. The blast radius is moderate, requiring the Operator to position
close to grouped enemies for maximum effect.

Magnetic Flare combines naturally with corrosive or radiation damage Warframes:
Flare reduces the armor/shield layer, then the Warframe ability or weapon cleans
up at increased effective damage.

Energy cost: 40,000 Focus points.

### Poise

Poise is a Passive that increases the Operator's shield pool by a percentage of
its base value. Operator shields regenerate when the Operator enters Void Mode,
so additional maximum shields translate to more hits absorbed before forced
retreat. Poise is most relevant in high-level content where stray enemy fire
can quickly drain Operator shields.

Energy cost: 25,000 Focus points.

### Basilisk Gaze

Basilisk Gaze passively applies a slow to every enemy hit by the Operator's
Void beam. The slow affects both movement speed and attack speed, reducing the
threat level of enemies during engagements. For players who use the Operator
beam as a supplement to Warframe abilities rather than a primary damage source,
Basilisk Gaze converts those supplemental shots into meaningful crowd control
at no additional action cost.

Energy cost: 25,000 Focus points.

## Waybounds

**Unairu Wisp** as a Waybound means any Operator kill has a chance to spawn
a Wisp pickup regardless of which Focus school is active. The buff granted
(temporary armor and shield bonus) is minor but free, adding passive utility
to Operator-heavy playstyles.

**Stone Skin** permanently boosts Operator armor account-wide. Combined with
Magus Husk from Madurai, Stone Skin pushes Operator armor into a range where
even Steel Path enemies cannot one-shot the Operator. The exact armor values
are not confirmed in WFCD data; community benchmarks suggest approximately
200 additional base armor at the Waybound's maximum investment.

Both Waybounds require completing the full Unairu tree and represent a
significant Focus investment.

## Recommended warframes

Unairu's debuffing Magnetic Flare and crowd control from Wisp active
complement frames that deal burst damage to grouped enemies after setup:

- [Nidus](/warframes/nidus) -- Nidus lacks shields and benefits from the
  extra armor from Wisp pickups, while Magnetic Flare softens armored targets
  for Virulence and Larva.
- [Inaros](/warframes/inaros) -- Inaros has no shields but immense health;
  Unairu's Stone Skin Waybound helps the Operator match Inaros's survivability.
- [Nezha](/warframes/nezha) -- Warding Halo provides personal immunity; adding
  Unairu's enemy slow extends the window in which Nezha can freely reposition.
- [Khora](/warframes/khora) -- Strangledome locks down enemies; Unairu's
  Magnetic Flare strips armor from enemies already held by the dome.

## Build priorities

1. Unlock Magnetic Flare to enable active armor and shield stripping.
2. Unlock Stone Skin Waybound for persistent Operator armor.
3. Unlock Unairu Wisp Active for crowd control.
4. Unlock Basilisk Gaze for passive enemy slowing.
5. Unlock Unairu Wisp Waybound for free ally buffs on kills.

## Lore

Unairu draws from the Orokin artisan and builder caste. Its name derives from
the concept of endurance and permanence in Orokin philosophy. The school's
teachings emphasize the Void as a force of stabilization and weight rather than
destruction. The Eye-within-triangle symbol represents the Orokin concept of
seeing through deception to the immutable truth beneath.

Tenno who walk the Unairu Way are taught to resist the Void's corruptive pull
by anchoring their consciousness in physical discipline and armor rather than
raw power.
