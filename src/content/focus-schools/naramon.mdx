## Overview

Naramon is the Focus school of shadow and melee mastery. Its symbol is the
Hand, representing precision and physical combat. Naramon is the school of
choice for melee-centric players because its signature Waybound, Power Spike,
directly addresses the melee combo counter's decay mechanic -- one of the core
limitations of sustained melee damage output.

The combo counter in Warframe decays over time when not actively hitting
enemies. At high combo stacks (12x and above), the damage multiplier is
substantial enough that losing it between engagements is a significant DPS loss.
Power Spike slows this decay, and at the highest stacks effectively freezes it,
allowing players to carry large multipliers from room to room in high-density
missions.

## Way (passive)

Naramon's Way passives support a playstyle that alternates between Operator and
Warframe melee. Surging Dash adds combo hits through Operator movement, meaning
players can increase their combo counter while transitioning between rooms even
without hitting enemies. Disorienting Blow adds light crowd control through
heavy melee attacks.

Void Stalker increases Operator critical chance, making the school marginally
better than others for dedicated Operator-level damage -- though Madurai still
dominates for Operator DPS. Naramon's Operator damage improvements are secondary
to its melee synergies.

## Nodes

### Shadow Step

Shadow Step is Naramon's Active node, placing the Operator into a brief
invisibility state. While invisible, the Operator cannot be targeted by enemies.
The duration is short enough that it functions primarily as an escape or
repositioning tool rather than a sustained stealth option.

Invisibility from Shadow Step does not extend to the Warframe when transference
occurs, so players must plan transitions carefully. Returning to Warframe
immediately after a repositioning Shadow Step can result in the Warframe being
targeted from its last known position.

Energy cost: 50,000 Focus points.

### Surging Dash

Surging Dash is the node that makes Naramon interesting for melee players even
in content with few enemies. Each Void Sling adds a small amount to the melee
combo counter. Combined with Warframe abilities that clear rooms quickly, a
player can Sling through the aftermath of a kill to top off their combo before
engaging the next group.

The combo hits from Surging Dash count as regular combo hits and benefit from
Power Spike's decay reduction at high stacks.

Energy cost: 25,000 Focus points.

### Disorienting Blow

Disorienting Blow gives heavy melee attacks a 50% chance to stagger nearby
enemies. Heavy attacks in the melee rework spend combo counter to deal amplified
damage; Disorienting Blow adds a crowd control effect to this already powerful
attack type. The stagger is brief but long enough to prevent enemies from
counter-attacking during a follow-up combo string.

Energy cost: 25,000 Focus points.

### Void Stalker

Void Stalker passively increases Operator critical chance. The Void beam's base
critical chance is low, so the absolute increase from this node is modest.
However, for players using amps with inherent critical multipliers or Arcane
Acceleration, the critical chance boost can push the Operator's output above
what other schools offer for beam attacks.

Energy cost: 10,000 Focus points.

## Waybounds

**Power Spike** is the reason Naramon is widely leveled regardless of primary
school preference. At high combo stacks (community testing places the threshold
around 12x), the combo counter decays significantly more slowly. At maximum
Focus rank, reports indicate the decay halts entirely above a certain stack
threshold. The exact stack value is not documented in WFCD; field observations
from the community suggest the decay halt occurs around 12x to 15x multiplier.

**Mind Step** increases Operator movement speed after Void Slings, stacking up
to a maximum cap. In fast-paced missions this provides a meaningful mobility
advantage for navigating large tile sets between enemy groups.

Both Waybounds persist across all Focus schools once unlocked, making Naramon
worth leveling even for players who primarily use Zenurik or Madurai.

## Recommended warframes

- [Saryn](/warframes/saryn) -- Saryn's Spores require spreading to maximize;
  high melee combo for Bladestorm/heavy attacks sustains her kill speed.
- [Ash](/warframes/ash) -- Blade Storm and Fatal Teleport both benefit from high
  combo counter; Power Spike keeps Ash's multiplier alive between stealth kills.
- [Excalibur](/warframes/excalibur) -- Exalted Blade generates its own melee
  attacks; Power Spike maintains the combo during heavy-attack spam with
  Exalted Blade builds.
- [Wukong](/warframes/wukong) -- Iron Staff and Twin of the Primus benefit from
  sustained high combo multipliers.

## Build priorities

1. Unlock Power Spike Waybound first -- it provides universal melee value
   immediately.
2. Unlock Surging Dash to build combo between engagements.
3. Unlock Disorienting Blow for heavy attack crowd control.
4. Unlock Mind Step Waybound for mobility.
5. Unlock Shadow Step for emergency repositioning.

## Lore

Naramon is attributed to the Orokin Shadow caste -- the spies, assassins, and
precision specialists who operated in the margins of the Old War. The Hand symbol
represents the capacity for silent and decisive action. Tenno who follow the
Naramon Way are taught to be invisible between strikes and overwhelming when they
commit, reflecting the school's melee and stealth duality.

The school has no elemental affiliation, reflecting the idea that true mastery
lies in the practitioner's intent rather than in the raw force of the Void.
