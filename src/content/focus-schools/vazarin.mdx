## Overview

Vazarin is the Focus school of healing and protection. Its symbol is the Eye,
representing watchfulness over allies. Of the five schools, Vazarin is the most
team-oriented. Its signature Waybound, Protective Dash, is widely considered
the single most powerful Waybound in the game for cooperative play, granting
brief invulnerability to any ally the Operator Void Dashes through.

The school complements support Warframes such as Trinity, Oberon, Wisp, and
Harrow. In organized squad content -- Eidolons, Arbitrations, Steel Path
endurance runs -- a Vazarin Operator acts as the last line of defense,
restoring downed players and granting temporary immunity at critical moments.

## Way (passive)

Vazarin's Way passives layer defensive utility across the entire Operator kit.
Protective Sling applies healing on movement, encouraging constant Void Sling
repositioning as part of play. Mending Unity makes the painful experience of
allies being downed into a recoverable situation rather than a squad wipe
trigger.

The school does not amplify Operator damage in any meaningful way, so players
choosing Vazarin accept reduced personal output in exchange for unmatched
survivability support for their team.

## Nodes

### Void Radiance

Void Radiance is Vazarin's Active node, releasing a blinding pulse from the
Operator's position. Blinded enemies stop attacking and cannot accurately
target players for the duration. The blind radius is roughly the size of a
standard room, making it useful for creating safe windows during revives or
objective captures.

The ability does not deal significant damage. Its value is entirely in the
crowd control window it creates. Players combine it with Protective Dash
moments after: Void Radiance stops enemies, then the Operator dashes through
allied Warframes to grant invulnerability before returning to Warframe form.

Energy cost: 50,000 Focus points.

### Mending Unity

Mending Unity is a Passive node that enhances the standard revive interaction.
When the Operator Void Dashes through a downed ally (one in the bleedout state),
the ally is immediately revived with a percentage of their maximum health. The
percentage is unknown in WFCD data; field observations suggest approximately
25% of maximum health at the base Focus rank.

In practical terms, Mending Unity turns the Operator into a mobile revive tool
that does not require standing still and channeling. This is particularly
valuable in Arbitrations where standard revives require consuming Revive Tokens.

Energy cost: 25,000 Focus points.

### Protective Sling

Protective Sling causes Void Slings through damaged allies to restore health.
The healing scales with Operator rank and the target's maximum health. It is
less impactful than Protective Dash (the Waybound) because it requires the
ally to be below full health rather than preventing damage outright, but it
provides passive healing topping-off between larger Dash interactions.

Energy cost: 25,000 Focus points.

### Void Snare

Void Snare is a second Active node that creates a Void tether anchoring nearby
enemies in place. Rooted enemies cannot move but can still attack, so Void
Snare functions best as a setup tool for allies rather than a full CC solution.
Pairing Void Snare with area-denial abilities from frames like Frost or Vauban
makes the crowd control more complete.

Energy cost: 10,000 Focus points.

## Waybounds

**Mending Soul** grants healing and shield restoration on all Void Dashes through
allies, regardless of which Focus school is active. Once unlocked, it becomes
a permanent passive for the account, benefiting every player's Operator.

**Protective Dash** grants the Operator's Void Dash through a Warframe or allied
player the power to briefly make that ally invulnerable. The invulnerability
window is long enough to absorb a full damage volley from Steel Path enemies.
This Waybound is the reason many players level Vazarin even if they use a
different school as their primary -- the Waybound carries to all schools.

Both Waybounds require completing the full Vazarin node tree and represent a
significant Focus point investment, but the return on Protective Dash in
particular is considered among the highest in the game.

## Recommended warframes

Vazarin's protective toolkit makes the most sense alongside frames whose own
kits support or sustain the team:

- [Trinity](/warframes/trinity) -- Well of Life and Blessing already orient
  Trinity toward keeping allies alive; Vazarin adds Operator-level healing and
  invulnerability windows as a second layer.
- [Oberon](/warframes/oberon) -- Renewal and Hallowed Ground provide passive
  regeneration; Vazarin's active Dash handles spikes of damage that Renewal
  cannot prevent.
- [Wisp](/warframes/wisp) -- Wisp's Reservoirs provide passive buffs; Vazarin
  covers the burst healing that Reservoirs do not offer.
- [Harrow](/warframes/harrow) -- Harrow's Covenant provides invulnerability
  tied to channeling; Vazarin Protective Dash covers teammates during the
  cooldown between Covenants.

## Build priorities

1. Unlock Protective Dash Waybound first -- it provides immediate squad value
   across all content.
2. Unlock Void Radiance for a crowd control tool.
3. Unlock Mending Unity to enable no-channel revives.
4. Unlock Mending Soul Waybound for universal passive healing on Dash.
5. Unlock Protective Sling for continuous top-off healing.

Solo players benefit less from Vazarin and may prefer Zenurik for energy
sustain or Naramon for melee output.

## Lore

Vazarin draws from the Orokin healer caste. Its Eye symbol represents the
constant vigilance required to keep warriors alive in the Old War. The school's
teachings are attributed to Margulis herself, the Orokin founder of the
Tenno program who prioritized the preservation of life even when the Orokin
Council demanded otherwise. Tenno who walk the Vazarin Way carry forward
Margulis's conviction that protection is its own form of power.

The school's association with cold reflects the Orokin medical cryogenics
technology used to preserve injured Warframes during the Old War.
