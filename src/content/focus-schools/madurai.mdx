## Overview

Madurai is the Focus school of fire and aggression. Its symbol is the Phoenix.
Unlocked through the [The War Within](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/The_War_Within)
quest, Madurai centers on maximizing Void damage output and amplifying Warframe
abilities through rapid Operator transference. It is the school of choice for
Eidolon hunters who need to strip Synovia shields quickly and for players who
want to squeeze extra ability damage out of high-strength frames.

The school's signature active ability, Void Strike, is one of the most powerful
single-hit damage multipliers in the entire game. A fully charged Void Strike
can reach 800% bonus Void damage, allowing even a modest amp to one-shot
Teralyst Synovia in organized hunts.

## Way (passive)

The Madurai Way emphasizes offense at every layer of the Operator kit. Passive
nodes reduce transference lag, increase raw beam output, and reward skillful
Void Sling usage with temporary ability strength bonuses. Players who invest in
the full Madurai tree gain an Operator capable of punching far above its stat
floor and a Warframe that benefits from each Operator session.

Phoenix Gaze raises beam damage by 25% and adds a 6% status chance to Void
attacks, opening minor status application that complements elemental amp builds.
Sling Strength gates the strength bonus behind Void Sling usage, incentivizing
active repositioning rather than passive Void Mode camping.

## Nodes

### Void Strike

Void Strike is Madurai's Active node and the core reason players choose this
school for Eidolon hunting. While in Void Mode, the Operator accumulates a
damage multiplier at roughly 100% per second, capping at 800% after eight
seconds. Exiting Void Mode applies the stored multiplier to the next Void beam
attack. Because Eidolon Synovia are immune to most damage types but vulnerable
to Void, a single charged Void Strike can bypass the Synovia's effective HP
with a high-tier amp.

In practice, players alternate between Void Mode charging and beam firing.
Coordinated squads use squad energy or Trinity to maintain Operator energy
between shots. The critical restraint is the eight-second cap: longer camping
does not continue to stack, so timing exits precisely to the cap maximizes
efficiency.

Energy cost: 50,000 Focus points to unlock.

### Power Transfer

Power Transfer is a Passive node that triggers whenever the Operator casts a
Warframe ability immediately after returning from transference. The 50% ability
strength bonus lasts roughly four seconds and does not stack with repeated
casts. It rewards a rhythm of Void Strike, transfer to Warframe, immediately
cast a high-value ability (Volt's Electric Shield, Chroma's Vex Armor, Rhino's
Roar), then transfer back out.

Energy cost: 25,000 Focus points.

### Phoenix Gaze

Phoenix Gaze passively boosts Operator beam damage by 25% and adds a 6% status
chance to Void attacks. The status chance is low but opens the possibility of
applying Void proc effects on grouped enemies. The 25% beam damage increase
applies before crit multipliers, making it a multiplicative gain for amps with
high critical chance.

Energy cost: 25,000 Focus points.

### Sling Strength

Sling Strength grants a temporary ability strength buff to the Warframe after
any Void Sling. The buff is brief, so the intended pattern is to Sling directly
into casting range, cast immediately, and then return to Operator form. It
layers well with Power Transfer when the player chains Sling into transference
into ability cast in rapid sequence.

Energy cost: 10,000 Focus points.

## Waybounds

Waybounds are permanent upgrades that persist across all Focus schools once
unlocked. Madurai contributes two Waybounds to the shared pool.

**Phoenix Talons** adds a percentage of the player's current Focus school rank
as bonus Void damage to both Operator attacks and Warframe abilities with Void
damage. At maximum Focus rank, the bonus is appreciable for both Operator and
frames that deal innate Void damage (Gara Prime, Xaku).

**Magus Husk** grants the Operator a percentage of its base armor, reducing
the otherwise lethal fragility of Operators in high-level content. Magus Husk
stacks with Operator suit armor bonuses from Quills and Vox Solaris rankings.

Both Waybounds require large Focus investments and are gated behind completing
the Madurai node tree before the final Waybound unlock node becomes available.

## Recommended warframes

Madurai's Power Transfer node scales with the abilities of whatever Warframe
the player uses. The strongest pairings are frames whose signature abilities
have high ability strength scaling:

- [Volt](/warframes/volt) -- Electric Shield gains more damage from strength,
  turning it into a reliable Eidolon amp platform.
- [Chroma](/warframes/chroma) -- Vex Armor scales directly with damage taken
  and ability strength, and Power Transfer amplifies Chroma's already large
  buff numbers.
- [Rhino](/warframes/rhino) -- Roar is a multiplicative buff for the whole
  squad; extra strength means more damage for all teammates.
- [Trinity](/warframes/trinity) -- Energy Vampire and Blessing become more
  effective with additional strength, supporting the whole team during long
  Tridolon hunts.

## Build priorities

For Eidolon hunting, the priority order is:

1. Unlock Void Strike (Active node) first -- it is the core damage tool.
2. Unlock Phoenix Gaze for the base beam damage increase.
3. Unlock Power Transfer to gain the ability strength synergy.
4. Invest in Waybound Phoenix Talons for persistent Void damage scaling.
5. Unlock Magus Husk Waybound to improve Operator survivability in Steel Path
   and high-level content.

For general gameplay not focused on Eidolons, Power Transfer and Sling Strength
combine to make Madurai a serviceable offensive school even without charging
Void Strike.

## Lore

Madurai is associated with the Orokin caste of warriors and executioners. Its
symbol, the Phoenix, represents the cycle of destruction and rebirth. The school's
teachings are attributed to the Orokin sage Margulis's counterpart -- the
executioner lineage that created the Warframes not as tools of compassion but as
weapons of war. Tenno who walk the Madurai Way embrace the power of the Void as
an offensive force rather than a meditative one.

The school's connection to heat and fire reflects the Orokin's use of thermal
Void reactions to power their most destructive weapons during the Old War against
the Sentients.
