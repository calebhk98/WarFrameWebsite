## Overview

The Kohm Riven mod applies to the Kohm shotgun, a Grineer-derived rapid-fire weapon that fires an increasing number of pellets per shot as the trigger is held. The Kohm is classified as a shotgun but fires like an automatic weapon, with the pellet count ramping up from a single shot to its maximum as the fire animation spins up. Its status chance per pellet is high, and the increasing pellet count means the effective status chance per second accelerates as the weapon winds up.

The Kohm sits at a 0.85 disposition (three dots out of five), indicating moderate demand. It is used by a dedicated player segment who builds specifically around its ramping mechanic and high slash-damage-per-pellet profile. The mid-range disposition means Riven stats are meaningfully higher than minimum-disposition weapons without being as dominant as high-disposition weapons.

## Disposition

Disposition 0.85 gives the Kohm Riven solid stat values per roll. A multishot roll on a Kohm Riven at this disposition adds a noticeably higher multishot percentage than the same roll would on a 0.5 disposition weapon. Players who enjoy the Kohm's unusual fire mechanic will find that a Riven at this disposition tier rewards investment appropriately.

The 0.85 disposition suggests Digital Extremes views the Kohm as a niche but capable weapon -- popular enough to warrant some disposition reduction from the maximum but not dominant enough to push to minimum.

## Target Stats

Multishot and damage is the core combination because both stats multiply the Kohm's already-high pellet count output. The Kohm fires multiple pellets simultaneously even at base, and multishot adds more. Combining multishot with a flat damage bonus means each pellet hits harder while also hitting more targets.

Status chance and multishot is the status-focused alternative for players building toward rapid slash proc accumulation. The Kohm applies slash procs through its pellets, and more pellets with higher status chance per pellet means more slash procs per second, which accumulate into very high damage-over-time on armored targets.

Critical chance and damage serves a hybrid build that gains some benefit from each damage vector. The Kohm's base crit chance is moderate, so adding crit chance through a Riven can cross useful thresholds when combined with crit mods.

## Roll Strategy

Kohm Riven rerolling at 0.85 disposition provides noticeably better absolute values than minimum-disposition weapons, making each successful roll more impactful. The recommended approach is to target a two-positive roll with one of the priority pairs and a neutral negative, then evaluate whether the third stat (positive or negative) improves or worsens the configuration.

Magazine capacity as a negative is acceptable for the Kohm despite reducing available ammo per reload, because the weapon's playstyle is sustained fire rather than burst -- players typically reload during wind-down transitions rather than mid-engagement. Zoom is universally the ideal negative for any hitscan weapon.
