## Overview

The Atterax Riven applies to the Atterax, a Grineer-derived whip that was once one of the most dominant melee weapons in Warframe before multiple balance adjustments. The Atterax is a slash-damage whip with a long reach and a high critical chance. Players who used the Atterax heavily during its meta period are familiar with its sweeping horizontal attacks that can hit numerous enemies in arc. The weapon maintains a loyal user base because its spin-attack playstyle pairs well with frames like Baruuk and Revenant.

At disposition 1.0 (three dots), the Atterax Riven falls in the middle of the disposition range, providing balanced stat values. The neutral disposition reflects a weapon that is genuinely useful but not among the top meta choices in the current patch.

## Disposition

Disposition 1.0 means the Atterax Riven rolls precisely average values across all stat categories. This is a comfortable tier for Riven investment: stats are meaningfully higher than minimum-disposition Rivens without the extreme premium pricing of dominant weapons. Players who want to build around the Atterax will find the 1.0 disposition a good balance of value and cost.

## Target Stats

Critical chance and critical damage is the highest-ceiling combination because the Atterax has strong base crit stats (25% critical chance, 2x crit multiplier). Pushing both values higher through a Riven creates a weapon that reliably crits on every spin attack while dealing amplified damage per crit proc. Combined with the slash damage type, orange and red crits from a modded Atterax generate significant slash bleed procs on affected enemies.

Damage and critical chance is the safer investment for players who have not fully modded the Atterax for critical hits. Adding flat damage raises the baseline while critical chance improvements pay dividends as other crit mods are added to the build.

Attack speed and critical chance benefits the spin-attack playstyle specifically. Faster attack speed means more whip swings per second, more crit chances per second, and more slash procs per second. For players using Baruuk or Wukong augments that scale with melee speed, the attack speed bonus has compounding value.

## Roll Strategy

Atterax Riven rerolling at 1.0 disposition is straightforward. The values are consistent and each roll produces predictable stat ranges. Players should target critical chance as the anchor stat in any desirable combination. Zoom as a negative on a melee weapon is worth noting: melee weapons do not use zoom at all, so any roll that includes zoom as the negative stat is equivalent to having only positive stats.

On a melee Riven, zoom and recoil negatives are both meaningless, providing effectively a free extra positive stat. This makes Atterax Rivens with zoom or recoil negatives substantially more valuable than the same roll with a damaging negative like negative attack speed or negative critical chance.

## Notable Trade Prices

Atterax Rivens trade at accessible prices around 40 to 100 platinum. Rolls with the zoom or recoil meaningless negatives combined with critical chance and critical damage can trade for 200 to 350 platinum from dedicated Atterax users.
