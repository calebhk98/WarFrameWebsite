## Overview

The Dread Riven applies to the Dread, one of the Stalker's signature weapons and a bow that drops from Shadow Stalker encounter rewards. The Dread is notable for firing semi-circular bladed projectiles that apply slash damage almost exclusively, making it one of the best slash-proc bows in the game. Its critical chance is high (50% base), and it was the standard recommendation for bow builds during several meta periods before other options emerged.

At 1.25 disposition, the Dread Riven offers strong absolute values while remaining affordable on the trade market due to the weapon's moderate current popularity. Players who use the Dread for its heavy slash damage identity will find the Riven meaningfully elevates performance.

## Disposition

Four dots at 1.25 disposition provides generous stat values per roll. The Dread's 50% base critical chance already makes it an excellent candidate for crit-stacking -- adding more through a 1.25 disposition Riven push the weapon well into orange-crit territory on standard builds and into red-crit on optimized configurations. Critical damage at this disposition adds a high multiplier on every crit proc, which interacts powerfully with the slash bleed procs that each crit triggers.

## Target Stats

Critical chance and critical damage is the premier combination. The Dread fires at a moderate pace and relies on each arrow critting for maximum damage. At 50% base crit chance, adding Riven crit chance can approach 80 to 100% crit chance on builds that also include Vital Sense and Point Strike. Critical damage at this tier produces very high red-crit values when the full build is assembled.

Damage and critical chance is the alternative when crit damage is addressed through other mods. Flat damage scaling amplifies both direct hit damage and the slash bleed procs triggered by each arrow.

Multishot and critical chance creates a build that fires two arrows simultaneously while also increasing crit reliability. The Dread's slash proc identity means each extra arrow from multishot is also a potential slash proc source.

## Roll Strategy

Dread Riven rerolling at 1.25 disposition is efficient. Kuva cost per unit of stat gain is reasonable, and the priority combination of critical chance and critical damage appears at a competitive frequency across rolls. Players should target the priority pair and evaluate the negative -- zoom is ideal, recoil is acceptable, puncture is tolerable since the Dread deals primarily slash anyway.

The Dread's naturally strong critical identity means that even a Riven with only one priority stat (critical chance) and an acceptable negative provides genuine performance improvement at moderate Kuva investment.

## Notable Trade Prices

Dread Rivens trade at accessible prices around 30 to 100 platinum. Rolls with critical chance, critical damage, and zoom as the negative are sought by dedicated Dread users and can trade for 200 to 400 platinum. The weapon's storied history in the Warframe meta means there is consistent demand for well-rolled examples even outside of current meta cycles.
