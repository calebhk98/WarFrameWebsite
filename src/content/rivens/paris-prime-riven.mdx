## Overview

The Paris Prime Riven applies to Paris Prime, a Void relic bow that was among the first Prime weapons introduced in Warframe's early history. Paris Prime fires precise single arrows with high critical chance and a charge-to-fire mechanic. It is currently a niche weapon outside the meta, used primarily by players who enjoy the bow playstyle specifically or are building toward specific aesthetics and challenge runs.

Paris Prime carries disposition 1.55, the maximum value in the Riven system. This means every stat rolled on a Paris Prime Riven is the highest absolute value possible for its category across the entire Riven system. A critical chance roll on a 1.55 disposition Riven adds more percentage points than any other weapon's equivalent roll. This makes Paris Prime Rivens fascinating from an optimization standpoint -- they provide enormous stat bonuses that can theoretically push the weapon far beyond what its modest base stats suggest.

## Disposition

The maximum disposition of 1.55 (five dots) exists because Paris Prime is not used at high frequency in the current meta. The generous stat values are Digital Extremes' attempt to incentivize use of underperforming weapons by making their Rivens exceptionally powerful. For players who genuinely enjoy Paris Prime's playstyle, a maximum-disposition Riven represents an opportunity to build a weapon that performs far above expectation for its archetype.

Critical chance rolls at 1.55 disposition can push Paris Prime toward extremely reliable critical hits, and critical damage rolls amplify every crit proc to very high values. Combined with the bow's natural charge mechanic that concentrates damage into a single high-value shot, the theoretical ceiling on a maxed Paris Prime Riven build is genuinely impressive.

## Target Stats

Critical chance and critical damage is the highest-priority combination because Paris Prime is a precision crit-focused weapon. At 1.55 disposition, both values are maximally high, pushing the weapon well past the orange-crit threshold and into reliable red-crit territory with proper mod support. The bow's slow fire rate means every arrow must hit and crit to maintain competitive damage, making both crit vectors essential.

Damage and critical chance suits players who have not fully addressed the crit multiplier through base mods. Flat damage scaling on the Paris Prime's per-arrow damage is valuable at 1.55 disposition because the percentage gains translate to large absolute numbers.

Multishot and critical chance is a niche combination for players using the Paris Prime in builds that fire multiple arrows. At maximum disposition, the multishot values are meaningfully high even for bow archetypes.

## Roll Strategy

Paris Prime Riven rerolling at 1.55 disposition is extremely efficient in terms of Kuva spent per unit of stat gained. Because every roll produces the highest possible values for its stat category, even an average roll with two neutral or positive stats and a manageable negative is a genuinely good outcome.

The recommended approach is to reroll until reaching the priority combination (critical chance + critical damage or critical chance + damage) with zoom or recoil as the negative, then stop. At this disposition, the gap between a good roll and a perfect roll is smaller in relative terms than on lower-disposition weapons.

## Notable Trade Prices

Paris Prime Rivens trade at very accessible prices despite their maximum disposition because demand for the weapon is low. Average prices are around 25 to 60 platinum even for decent rolls. Players who want a maximum-disposition weapon to experiment with can typically acquire a Paris Prime Riven affordably and then invest Kuva in rerolling toward the priority stats.
