## Overview

The Kuva Bramma Riven mod applies to the Kuva Bramma, a Kuva Lich weapon and one of the most powerful area-of-effect bows in Warframe. The Kuva Bramma fires explosive arrows that detonate on impact and scatter cluster bomblets in a wide area-of-effect radius. Its combination of high damage per arrow, massive blast radius, and innate self-damage immunity (on direct hits) makes it the go-to choice for players clearing dense enemy formations in Steel Path and endgame content.

The weapon carries a disposition of 0.5, the lowest tier, reflecting its years of sustained meta dominance. Digital Extremes has kept Kuva Bramma at minimum disposition across multiple balance passes due to its popularity and powerful base profile. Despite this, a good Kuva Bramma Riven provides meaningful improvements because the weapon's base damage is high enough that even modest percentage gains represent large absolute damage increases.

## Disposition

Disposition 0.5 (one dot) means the Kuva Bramma Riven rolls smaller absolute stat values than Rivens on less popular weapons. A multishot roll on a Kuva Bramma Riven will add fewer multishot percentage points than the same roll on a weapon with disposition 1.0 or 1.55. Players deciding whether to invest in this Riven should account for this ceiling when evaluating cost versus benefit.

The Kuva Bramma has remained at minimum disposition for an extended period. Players who already hold a Kuva Bramma Riven should consider it a long-term investment -- the weapon is unlikely to return to higher disposition, but it is also unlikely to drop further.

## Target Stats

Multishot and damage represent the safest investment because both stats amplify every single arrow fired. The Kuva Bramma already deals high damage per projectile; adding both multishot and damage on a single Riven pushes each trigger pull into very high total damage territory. The clustered AoE bomblets also benefit from multishot because more arrows mean more cluster detonations covering the impact zone.

Critical chance and critical damage are viable for players building specifically toward a crit-focused Kuva Bramma configuration. The weapon has moderate base critical stats, so adding crit modifiers through a Riven can tip the build toward reliable orange-crit or red-crit thresholds when combined with standard crit mods.

Damage and punch through is an alternative prioritizing the bow's ability to hit multiple enemies in a line before detonation. Punch through on an explosive projectile means the arrow passes through initial targets and detonates deeper into a formation, placing the AoE center among clustered enemies rather than at the edge.

## Roll Strategy

Kuva Bramma Riven rerolling requires Kuva. The escalating reroll cost means that chasing a specific combination can consume tens of thousands of Kuva before the desired roll appears. The recommended approach is to establish a clear target combination, identify the acceptable negative stats (zoom being ideal), and stop rerolling once a roll meets the minimum threshold rather than chasing the theoretical maximum.

For players on a Kuva budget, purchasing a pre-rolled Kuva Bramma Riven from the trade market with at least one priority positive stat and a neutral negative is a more cost-effective starting point than farming Kuva for extensive rerolls. The delta between a mediocre roll and a perfect roll is smaller at 0.5 disposition than it would be at higher dispositions, so diminishing returns set in early for the Bramma.

## Notable Trade Prices

Kuva Bramma Rivens trade at a premium because the weapon remains popular even after several damage adjustments. Average market price is around 120 platinum for unrolled or casually rolled Rivens. Premium rolls -- specifically multishot, damage, and critical damage with zoom as the negative -- trade significantly higher, commonly 300 to 600 platinum.

Players holding an unwanted Kuva Bramma Riven obtained from Sorties can typically liquidate it for 80 to 150 platinum even without a good roll, making it one of the more reliably valuable unrolled Rivens in the current economy.
