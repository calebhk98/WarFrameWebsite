## Overview

The Lex Riven applies to both the Lex and Lex Prime pistols, with the Prime variant being the obvious platform for Riven investment due to its superior base stats. Lex Prime is a semi-automatic hand cannon that deals high burst damage per shot with a slow fire rate and small magazine. Its identity is precision: each trigger pull is a deliberate, high-value event that rewards accurate aim against elite enemies or bosses.

At 0.85 disposition, the Lex Riven provides good value for players who enjoy the high-caliber pistol playstyle. Lex Prime is used primarily by players who prefer the feel of a hand cannon over the rapid-fire options that dominate the meta, and the 0.85 disposition reflects a weapon that is appreciated but not dominant.

## Disposition

Three dots at 0.85 disposition means the Lex Riven rolls solid stat values. The critical-focused identity of Lex Prime means crit chance and critical damage rolls are particularly impactful here -- the weapon already has good base crit stats (25% crit chance, 2x crit multiplier), so adding more through a Riven pushes toward orange-crit reliability with proper mod support.

## Target Stats

Critical chance and critical damage is the premier combination because Lex Prime is designed around landing high-damage critical hits. Each shot is slow but deliberate, and a shot that crits deals substantially more damage than a non-crit. Adding both crit vectors through a Riven maximizes the damage ceiling per pull.

Multishot and critical chance provides a balance of more shots per trigger pull alongside higher crit probability. Multishot on a semi-automatic pistol is more impactful than on full-auto weapons because each trigger pull that fires two bullets instead of one doubles the value of an accurately placed shot.

Damage and critical damage covers the flat damage scaling combined with a multiplier on crits. This is the correct combination when crit chance is already addressed through base mods and the player wants to maximize the value of each crit that does land.

## Roll Strategy

The Lex Riven is accessible to reroll because the weapon is not meta-dominant and Kuva investment yields good returns at 0.85 disposition. The recommended priority is critical chance combined with either critical damage or multishot, with zoom as the target negative.

Players who obtained a Lex Riven as a reward should consider keeping it for a Lex Prime build rather than selling, as the disposition makes good rolls achievable at moderate Kuva cost compared to minimum-disposition Rivens.

## Notable Trade Prices

Lex Rivens trade in the mid-range of 40 to 120 platinum. Premium rolls with critical chance, critical damage, and a harmless negative reach 200 to 400 platinum for dedicated Lex Prime enthusiasts.
