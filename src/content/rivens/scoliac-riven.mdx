## Overview

The Scoliac Riven applies to the Scoliac, a Grineer-derived whip weapon focused on attack speed and wide-arc clearing. Like the Atterax, the Scoliac uses the whip stance system and benefits from spin-attack mechanics. Its niche is rapid consecutive swings at high attack speed, generating frequent status procs and crit chances across large groups. The Scoliac has a lower base critical chance than the Atterax but compensates with faster natural attack speed.

At 1.25 disposition, the Scoliac Riven offers generous absolute stat values per roll, reflecting the weapon's modest popularity. Players who want a high-attack-speed whip build will find the Scoliac Riven an accessible entry point that delivers good returns on investment.

## Disposition

Four dots at 1.25 indicates the Scoliac is a niche weapon with a dedicated user base. The high disposition translates to large absolute bonus values per roll -- attack speed rolls at 1.25 disposition add substantially more attack speed percentage than the same roll on a 0.5 or 0.85 weapon. For players specifically building around whip spin-attack mechanics, the Scoliac Riven is a cost-effective way to acquire high stat values.

## Target Stats

Attack speed and critical chance is the core pairing for the Scoliac's spin-attack identity. More attack speed means faster spin-attack cycles, enabling more hits per second and more opportunities for each slash proc to accumulate. Critical chance adds value on each of those faster hits. Combined with whip stance mods that amplify spin damage, this combination produces very high sustained damage throughput against grouped enemies.

Critical chance and critical damage is the alternative for players who have already maximized attack speed through other mods. The Scoliac benefits from crit chance scaling similarly to the Atterax, and at 1.25 disposition the crit values per roll are high enough to meaningfully change crit reliability.

Damage and attack speed provides a flat damage multiplier alongside the speed increase. Because spin-attack playstyle hits many enemies simultaneously, flat damage scaling multiplies across all targets per swing.

## Roll Strategy

The zoom and recoil meaningless negative rule applies here as with all melee Rivens. Any Scoliac Riven with zoom or recoil as the negative stat is worth significantly more than one with a damaging negative like negative attack speed. Players rolling or evaluating Scoliac Rivens should prioritize identifying whether the negative is a combat-irrelevant stat.

At 1.25 disposition, even a single priority stat at max values provides a meaningful improvement. Players who find the right combination early in their reroll attempts should consider stopping -- the diminishing returns of chasing perfect values are steeper here because the baseline values are already high.

## Notable Trade Prices

Scoliac Rivens trade at accessible prices around 30 to 80 platinum. Rolls with attack speed, critical chance, and zoom or recoil as the negative can trade for 150 to 250 platinum.
