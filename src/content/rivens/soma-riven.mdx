## Overview

The Soma Riven mod is a randomized enhancement mod exclusive to the Soma and Soma Prime rifles. Soma Prime is one of Warframe's most enduring primary weapons, a high-crit-chance automatic rifle that deals significant slash damage per shot. Its disposition of 0.5 is the minimum value in the Riven system, reflecting the weapon's continued popularity in the meta. Low disposition means each Riven roll grants smaller stat bonuses compared to weapons with higher disposition, capping the ceiling on what a Soma Riven can accomplish.

Despite the low disposition, a well-rolled Soma Riven is worth acquiring because Soma Prime's base stats are excellent. The weapon comes with 30% base critical chance and 3x critical multiplier, which means even modest crit chance additions from a Riven push it into reliable orange-crit territory with proper mod support. The weapon also benefits from its naturally high slash-weighted physical damage -- a positive multishot or damage roll amplifies already-good slash procs.

## Disposition

Soma Riven disposition is 0.5 (one dot out of five), the lowest tier. This means the maximum values for a three-positive Riven on Soma are significantly lower than the same roll on a weapon with disposition 1.55. Players choosing between investing kuva in a Soma Riven versus a different primary Riven should weigh whether the stat gains justify the rerolling cost given the disposition ceiling.

Disposition is adjusted by Digital Extremes approximately every quarter, usually trending downward for popular weapons. Soma Prime has maintained minimum disposition for several update cycles, suggesting it will remain at 0.5 for the foreseeable future.

## Target Stats

The most valuable stat combinations for a Soma Riven are those that amplify the weapon's existing critical hit identity.

Multishot and critical chance together address two of the weapon's highest-scaling multipliers simultaneously. Multishot doubles the effective damage per trigger pull while critical chance increases the proportion of shots that trigger the high crit multiplier. This combination makes every bullet both more likely to land and more likely to crit.

Critical chance and critical damage pair well when the player already has a high multishot value from base mods like Split Chamber. Adding more critical chance past 100% converts shots to red crits (base damage multiplied by the crit multiplier twice), and additional critical damage increases the red-crit value significantly.

Multishot and critical damage is a viable alternative when the build already has high critical chance from other mods. The combination produces more overall damage throughput per shot.

## Roll Strategy

Rerolling a Soma Riven requires Kuva, the currency earned from Kuva Fortress missions, Kuva Flood sorties, and Resource Booster events. Each roll costs Kuva, with the cost escalating per reroll up to a cap.

Because disposition is low, the specific stat values rolled on a Soma Riven will never be as high as on meta weapons with higher disposition. A player with a budget should evaluate whether hunting for a Soma Riven is worth the Kuva investment versus applying the same resources to a weapon with a 1.0 or higher disposition. However, if the player already owns a Soma Riven obtained as a Sortie or Arbitration reward, rerolling toward the priority combos described above is worthwhile because those rolls have genuine impact on the weapon's performance.

Aim for a roll with at least two of the priority positive stats and a neutral-to-negative negative stat. Zoom is the ideal negative because it does not affect damage output or survivability. Recoil is acceptable for experienced players who can compensate. Avoid rolls that include negative multishot, negative critical chance, or negative damage, as these directly undermine the weapon's core identity.

## Notable Trade Prices

Soma Riven pricing varies significantly based on the specific roll. At minimum disposition, the best possible rolls (three positive stats including multishot + critical damage, with a harmless negative) trade for substantially more than average rolls.

Market average sits around 90 platinum, but well-rolled examples with the three-stat priority combination and zoom or recoil as the negative commonly trade for 200 to 400 platinum among players who specifically want to push Soma Prime builds further. Unrolled Soma Rivens trade for 30 to 60 platinum and are a cost-effective entry point for players who want to attempt their own rolls.
