## Overview

The Kuva Nukor Riven applies to the Kuva Nukor, one of the most used secondary weapons in Warframe's endgame. The Kuva Nukor is a microwave pistol that fires a continuous beam capable of chaining between nearby enemies, applying rapid status effects and dealing escalating damage via Condition Overload interactions. Its position as arguably the best secondary weapon in the game for status-intensive builds keeps its Riven disposition pinned at the minimum value of 0.5.

The weapon's value comes from three key properties: the chain-link mechanic that hits multiple enemies simultaneously, an extremely high base status chance per second, and compatibility with the Galvanized Crosshairs and Galvanized Shot mods. A well-rolled Riven amplifies each of these properties, pushing the Kuva Nukor from excellent to exceptional.

## Disposition

Disposition 0.5 is the Kuva Nukor's permanent fixture in the current meta. The weapon is so consistently dominant that it has maintained minimum disposition across balance updates. Players who want to maximize Kuva Nukor performance will still benefit from a Riven because the base stats are high enough that even small percentage multipliers add meaningful damage, but they should not expect the ceiling on gains to match what Rivens do for less popular weapons.

## Target Stats

Multishot and critical chance together address both major damage vectors for the Nukor. Multishot increases the number of beams that hit each chained target, while critical chance pushes the weapon toward more reliable crit procs. The Kuva Nukor has a moderate base critical chance (19%), so adding crit chance brings it closer to the threshold where crit-focused mods like Galvanized Crosshairs become highly efficient.

Critical chance and critical damage is the combination for players who have already addressed multishot through base mods. Critical damage multiplies every crit proc, and the Nukor's fast fire rate means a high crit chance delivers many crit procs per second.

Status chance and multishot benefits players building specifically around the status-chaining identity. More multishot means more procs applied simultaneously, and the Kuva Nukor's status-per-second rate is already exceptional -- adding more makes Condition Overload buff stacks accumulate even faster.

## Roll Strategy

The Kuva Nukor Riven commands a high price both purchased and rolled because the weapon's enduring meta status keeps demand elevated. Rolling this Riven requires significant Kuva investment, and the 0.5 disposition means the statistical improvement from any given roll is moderate in absolute terms.

The key strategy is to establish a minimum acceptable roll: one or two priority positive stats with a neutral negative (zoom is ideal) and sell or hold rather than endlessly rerolling. The marginal improvement from a near-perfect roll versus a merely good roll on a 0.5 disposition weapon is small, and the Kuva cost to chase that marginal improvement is large.

Players starting fresh should consider purchasing a pre-rolled Riven from the trade market with at least multishot and a neutral negative before investing in rerolls. This provides immediate benefit at a known cost.

## Notable Trade Prices

Kuva Nukor Rivens are among the highest-value Riven mods in the game. The average unrolled or casually rolled Riven trades around 200 platinum. Premium rolls with multishot, critical chance or critical damage, and zoom as the negative commonly trade for 500 to 1200 platinum depending on exact values and prevailing market conditions.

Players holding a Kuva Nukor Riven obtained from Sorties or Arbitrations can expect strong sale prices even for mediocre rolls, making it one of the most liquid Riven assets in the economy.
