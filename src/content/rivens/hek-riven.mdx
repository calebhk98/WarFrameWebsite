## Overview

The Hek Riven applies to both the base Hek and the Vaykor Hek, one of the classic burst-damage shotguns in Warframe. The Vaykor Hek is the Steel Meridian syndicate variant with bonus stats, and is the preferred platform for Riven investment because of its superior base damage and the syndicate proc it fires on charged shots. At disposition 1.25, the Hek Riven rolls high absolute stat values -- one of the clearest cases in the Riven system where medium-tier disposition produces substantially better rolls than minimum-tier weapons.

The Hek/Vaykor Hek combination occupies a specific niche: a semi-automatic shotgun for players who want single-target burst rather than rapid-fire spread. The high base damage per pellet and the spread tightness (especially on charged shots) makes it effective against single elite enemies where accuracy compensates for the lower fire rate.

## Disposition

Disposition 1.25 (four dots) indicates the Hek is modestly popular but not dominant in the current meta. The high disposition rewards Riven investment generously -- multishot and damage rolls at 1.25 disposition add significantly more absolute multishot and damage than the same rolls would add on a 0.5 or 0.85 disposition weapon. Players who enjoy the Hek playstyle will find that a Riven meaningfully elevates performance.

The relatively high disposition also means the market price is lower than for meta weapons, because supply from Sortie rewards exceeds the demand from Hek-dedicated players.

## Target Stats

Multishot and damage is the strongest combination for maximizing burst output per shot. The Hek fires multiple pellets per shot that each deal the base damage value, so multishot adds more pellets and damage makes each pellet hit harder. At 1.25 disposition, these values are high enough to noticeably change the weapon's one-shot capability against mid-tier enemies.

Critical chance and critical damage suits the charged-shot playstyle, where the Vaykor Hek's charged fire mode groups pellets tightly and the resulting burst is essentially a single high-damage event. High critical chance and critical damage on a tight pellet spread creates a reliable high-damage burst against single targets.

Damage and fire rate benefits players who prefer the uncharged rapid-fire mode, allowing faster follow-up shots after the initial burst without waiting for the semi-auto cycle.

## Roll Strategy

The Hek Riven at 1.25 disposition rewards aggressive rerolling because the values per roll are high. A single positive stat on a Hek Riven can outperform two positive stats on a minimum-disposition Riven in absolute terms. The recommended strategy is to target the priority combination and stop once reached rather than minimizing negatives unnecessarily.

Magazine capacity is an acceptable negative because the Hek reloads after each shot in some configurations, making the magazine size less critical than on automatic weapons. Zoom is always the safest negative.

## Notable Trade Prices

Hek Rivens trade at accessible prices because demand is moderate. Average market price is around 55 platinum for rolled Rivens with reasonable stats. Perfect rolls with multishot, critical chance, and zoom negative can trade for 150 to 300 platinum among dedicated Hek users.
