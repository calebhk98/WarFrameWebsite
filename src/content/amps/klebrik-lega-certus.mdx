## Overview

The Klebrik-Lega-Certus configuration prioritizes critical damage above all
else. The Certus Brace is the highest critical chance brace available from the
Vox Solaris standing vendor, and at 40% base critical chance this combination
lands critical hits on nearly half of all attacks. The Klebrik Prism fires a
focused seeking beam, while the Lega Scaffold provides a rapid-fire secondary
for sustained critical application.

This combination is favored by players who have invested in Magus Elevate or
other critical-amplifying operator arcanes, and by those who want the highest
consistent damage per trigger pull rather than burst-focused output.

## Components

### Klebrik Prism (Primary fire)

The Klebrik Prism fires a homing beam that latches onto the nearest enemy in
front of the Operator. The beam automatically tracks the target for its
duration, reducing the need for precise aim maintenance. Against a locked
target like a Synovia, the homing behavior ensures near-100% beam contact.

The tradeoff is that Klebrik requires a target to exist within its acquisition
cone. In scenarios where the Operator needs to fire into empty space or at
very small targets, the homing behavior may fail to lock on. Players supplement
Klebrik with the Lega secondary for situations where primary fire is unreliable.

### Lega Scaffold (Secondary fire)

The Lega Scaffold fires a rapid stream of small Void bolts. The high fire rate
means each bolt independently checks the Certus Brace's critical chance,
generating a large number of critical hits per second of sustained secondary
fire. The Lega secondary is energy-intensive, requiring players to manage
Operator energy carefully to avoid depletion mid-fight.

The bolts travel fast and are hitscan, making them easy to aim at stationary
or slow-moving targets. Against the moving Eidolon limbs, the Lega's hitscan
nature is an advantage over projectile scaffolds.

### Certus Brace

The Certus Brace is a Vox Solaris offering, obtained through standing with the
Vox Solaris faction on Fortuna, Orb Vallis. It provides the highest critical
chance bonus of any brace, raising the Amp's critical chance to 40%. At this
level, combined with critical chance buffs from Focus school nodes, the Amp
achieves critical hit rates that rival endgame Warframe weapons.

The Certus Brace represents a late-game unlock, gating high critical output
behind completion of the Vox Solaris reputation grind.

## Damage profile

All damage is Void type. Critical hits deal 2x damage on Void beam attacks
at base critical multiplier. The Certus Brace does not increase the critical
multiplier itself, only the chance. With 40% critical chance, the expected
damage per shot averages 1.4x base damage (0.6 x 1.0 + 0.4 x 2.0 = 1.4).

The practical effect is that the Klebrik-Lega-Certus outputs higher average
damage than configurations with lower critical chance when the higher-damage
shots from critical hits are factored over sustained fire.

## Acquisition

The Klebrik Prism is available from the Quills standing vendor in Cetus.
The Lega Scaffold is available from the Vox Solaris vendor on Fortuna.
The Certus Brace is a Vox Solaris unlock.

This configuration requires progressing both the Quills (Plains of Eidolon)
and Vox Solaris (Orb Vallis) reputations, making it a cross-open-world build.
Players who have not unlocked Fortuna cannot access the Lega Scaffold or
Certus Brace and should use a Quills-only configuration like the 177 Amp.

## Playstyle

The Klebrik-Lega-Certus suits methodical Eidolon hunters who can afford to
take a moment to let the Klebrik beam lock onto a Synovia before firing:

1. Face the target Synovia and activate Klebrik primary; the beam auto-locks.
2. Hold primary fire until energy runs low.
3. Switch to Lega secondary for rapid critical hits while primary energy
   recovers.
4. Return to Void Mode to regenerate Operator energy between cycles.

The auto-lock behavior of Klebrik reduces input demand but makes the player
dependent on the targeting logic. In chaotic multi-target scenarios where
lures and Eidolons are nearby, the beam may lock onto a lure instead of a
Synovia. Players should position carefully to ensure Klebrik targets the
correct object.
