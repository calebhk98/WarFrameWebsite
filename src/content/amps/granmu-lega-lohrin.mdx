## Overview

The Granmu-Lega-Lohrin is known in community shorthand as the Granmu cluster
build. The Granmu Prism fires a cluster of Void grenades that detonate on
impact, dealing area Void damage. Combined with the Lega Scaffold's rapid
burst secondary and the Lohrin Brace's critical bonus, this configuration
excels at dealing area Void damage to grouped enemies and is a strong choice
against the Vomvalysts that accompany Eidolons.

Against Eidolons themselves, the cluster grenades are less precise than beam
or single-projectile prisms but can still strip Synovias if all grenades land
on target. The Granmu is particularly effective when the player needs area
clearing as well as boss damage.

## Components

### Granmu Prism (Primary fire)

The Granmu Prism fires a volley of three Void grenades in a spread pattern.
Each grenade travels along a ballistic arc and detonates on contact with a
surface or enemy, dealing Void damage in a small radius. The three-grenade
spread means the effective area of damage per trigger pull is larger than single
projectile prisms.

The arcing trajectory requires players to aim upward slightly when firing at
enemies at ground level from a distance, similar to how a Warframe grenade
launcher operates. At close range, the grenades travel flat and land near the
target.

### Lega Scaffold (Secondary fire)

The Lega Scaffold fires a rapid stream of Void bolts. For the Granmu build,
the Lega secondary provides precision follow-up damage when individual grenade
placement is needed on specific targets. While Granmu clears groups with its
cluster, Lega handles priority single targets with its high fire rate.

The Lega bolts are hitscan, so switching from Granmu's arcing grenades to the
Lega secondary for a precision shot requires no trajectory adjustment.

### Lohrin Brace

The Lohrin Brace adds 28% critical chance. For the Granmu's cluster, each
grenade independently checks critical chance on its detonation, meaning a
full three-grenade volley has three independent critical rolls. Against a
target where all three grenades land, the expected critical hits per trigger
pull is 0.84 (3 x 0.28).

## Damage profile

All damage is Void type. The Granmu cluster can deal up to three times the
per-grenade damage if all three hit the same target, though the spread pattern
makes this unlikely against small targets. Against large-area targets (Eidolon
bodies, groups of Vomvalysts), the spread increases the chance of hitting
multiple independent entities.

Vomvalysts are Void-aligned enemies that drain Operator energy and protect the
Eidolon. The Granmu's area grenades are effective at clearing multiple
Vomvalysts clustered near the Eidolon during a hunt.

## Acquisition

The Granmu Prism and Lega Scaffold are Vox Solaris offerings from the Fortuna
vendor. The Lohrin Brace is available from the Quills. This is a mixed
Quills/Vox Solaris configuration.

The Vox Solaris components require completing the Vox Solaris introduction
content on Orb Vallis and accumulating Vox Solaris standing. Exact material
requirements are on the Fandom wiki.

## Playstyle

The Granmu-Lega-Lohrin suits an area-denial and crowd-clearing Operator role:

1. Use Granmu primary to scatter Vomvalysts clustered near the Eidolon.
2. Switch to Lega secondary for precision damage on Synovias between grenade
   volleys.
3. Use Granmu at the base of Synovia clusters when the Eidolon's limb is
   extended, aiming at the ground near the Synovia so grenades detonate close.
4. Maintain Madurai Void Strike for the Lega secondary follow-up, where the
   hitscan bolt delivers the strike multiplier reliably.

The Granmu's arc requires practice to land accurately at medium range. New
Amp users may find the Cantic Prism's burst or the Klamora's continuous beam
easier to aim while learning Eidolon positioning.
