## Overview

The Cantic-Shraksun-Lohrin combination is widely known in the community by
its component number shorthand, 177. It is one of the most established meta
Amp configurations for Eidolon hunting due to its balance of consistent beam
damage from the Cantic Prism and strong area burst from the Shraksun Scaffold.
The Lohrin Brace provides a critical chance bonus that improves the Cantic
Prism's sustained beam output.

The 177 Amp trades some of the raw per-shot damage of other configurations for
a more forgiving playstyle. The Cantic Prism's hitscan beam is reliable at all
distances, and the Shraksun Scaffold's secondary fire creates a projectile that
detonates on contact, dealing Void damage in a small radius.

## Components

### Cantic Prism (Primary fire)

The Cantic Prism fires a burst of three Void beams in rapid succession with each
trigger pull. Unlike continuous beam prisms, Cantic fires in discrete bursts,
making it feel closer to a semi-automatic rifle than a sustained laser. Each
burst has a short recovery before the next burst can fire.

The three-beam burst means that on a hit, all three beams can independently
trigger critical hits, applying the Lohrin Brace's crit bonus multiple times per
trigger pull. The burst pattern is tight at medium range and fans slightly at
long range, so players should use it at distances where all three beams
converge on target.

### Shraksun Scaffold (Secondary fire)

The Shraksun Scaffold fires a slow-moving Void explosive projectile. The
projectile travels in a straight line and detonates on contact with a surface
or enemy, dealing Void damage in a radius. The explosion can hit multiple
Eidolon Synovias if they are clustered, making it situationally effective.

The Shraksun Scaffold's projectile is slower than hitscan, requiring lead time
when firing at moving targets. At the distances common in Eidolon encounters,
the projectile travel time is noticeable. Players compensate by firing slightly
ahead of the moving Eidolon's limbs.

### Lohrin Brace

The Lohrin Brace increases both the critical chance and status chance of the
Amp. The critical chance bonus synergizes with the Cantic Prism's burst-fire
pattern, since each individual beam in the burst checks critical chance
independently. A 28% critical chance means on average roughly 0.84 crits per
Cantic burst, with streaks of multiple consecutive bursts all critting in
favorable sequences.

## Damage profile

All Amp damage is Void type. Eidolon Synovias are only vulnerable to Void
damage, making Amp damage type selection straightforward for Eidolon content.
Against regular enemies, Void damage uses a flat scaling rather than the
faction-specific modifiers of elemental damage.

The Cantic Prism's burst output per trigger pull is higher than continuous beam
prisms over the same time window, assuming all three beams hit. The Shraksun
secondary provides a burst supplement when the primary is in recovery.

Critical chance is listed at 0.28 (28%) with the Lohrin Brace. The exact base
Cantic critical chance is not documented in WFCD data; the listed value
reflects community-verified in-game stat screen values.

## Acquisition

All Amp components in this configuration are crafted at the Quills vendor in
Cetus, Plains of Eidolon. The Quills are accessed through the town of Cetus,
requiring the player to have reached Neutral standing with the Quills after
completing the Saya's Vigil quest.

The components require Sentient Cores (from Eidolon lures and Sentient enemies)
and Ostron standing materials. Specific material requirements are not confirmed
in WFCD data and are listed on the Fandom wiki at the acquisition links below.

Advancing Quills standing unlocks higher-tier components. The Cantic Prism
is an Ostron/Quills mid-to-upper-tier component.

## Playstyle

In Eidolon hunts, the 177 Amp workflow is:

1. Build Madurai Void Strike charges in Void Mode.
2. Exit Void Mode and fire the Cantic burst at the target Synovia.
3. Use Shraksun secondary on grouped Synovias or to follow up the primary burst.
4. Return to Void Mode to accumulate the next Void Strike charge.

The Cantic burst is well-suited to targeting the moving Teralyst limbs because
the hitscan beam does not require projectile lead. The Shraksun secondary is
most efficient when the Eidolon raises multiple limbs simultaneously.

Outside of Eidolon hunting, the 177 Amp handles Sentient enemies on the Murex
and in the Heart of Deimos with the same Void damage efficiency, since Sentients
are also vulnerable to Void damage after adaptation.
