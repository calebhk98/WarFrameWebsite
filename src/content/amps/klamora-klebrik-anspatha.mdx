## Overview

The Klamora-Klebrik-Anspatha is an experimental configuration that combines
the Klamora Prism's wide continuous beam with the Klebrik Scaffold's homing
secondary and the Anspatha Brace's energy capacity bonus. This pairing emphasizes
ease of use: both the primary beam's wide hitbox and the secondary's homing
behavior reduce the aim requirements compared to precision configurations.

The Anspatha Brace's energy capacity bonus allows longer sessions with both the
continuous Klamora beam and the energy-hungry Klebrik scaffold before requiring
Void Mode energy recovery. The tradeoff is lower critical chance compared to
Certus or Lohrin configurations.

## Components

### Klamora Prism (Primary fire)

The Klamora Prism fires a wide continuous Void beam. The wide hitbox makes it
the easiest prism to keep on target against moving Eidolon limbs. Players who
struggle with the precision demands of Raplak or Cantic often find Klamora
gives them consistent damage even as they are learning Eidolon encounter
positioning.

Energy drain during continuous fire is the primary limitation. The Anspatha
Brace in this configuration directly addresses this by increasing the maximum
energy pool.

### Klebrik Scaffold (Secondary fire)

The Klebrik Scaffold fires a homing disc that seeks the nearest enemy in the
player's forward cone. Paired with the Klamora, the Klebrik secondary handles
situations where the primary beam cannot be maintained -- for example, when
the Operator must briefly reposition or when an Eidolon limb dips below the
ground briefly. The Klebrik disc finds the target automatically.

The double-homing configuration (wide beam primary, homing secondary) makes
this the most forgiving targeting combination available. Neither attack requires
precise aim if the enemy is within reasonable range.

### Anspatha Brace

The Anspatha Brace increases the Operator's maximum energy, allowing more total
fire time before depletion. For an Amp that has two energy-intensive attack
modes (continuous beam and homing disc), the energy buffer from Anspatha is
more valuable than the critical bonus from Lohrin or Certus would be.

The base critical chance of 0.16 (16%) is the lowest of the popular endgame
configurations, but the extended fire window means more total shots and more
total expected critical hits per transference session despite the lower rate.

## Damage profile

All damage is Void type. The Klamora beam deals continuous Void damage per
tick. The Klebrik secondary detonates on contact for burst Void damage. The
Anspatha Brace does not boost the damage numbers directly, only the capacity
for sustained fire.

The configuration's damage ceiling is lower than Certus-paired alternatives
but its damage floor (the minimum consistent damage an average player achieves)
is higher due to the forgiving targeting on both attack modes.

## Acquisition

The Klamora Prism is a Quills offering. The Klebrik Scaffold is a Vox Solaris
offering. The Anspatha Brace is a Quills offering. This is a mixed
Quills/Vox Solaris configuration.

Both Cetus (Quills) and Fortuna (Vox Solaris) progressions are required.
Exact material costs are on the Fandom wiki.

## Playstyle

The Klamora-Klebrik-Anspatha suits players who prioritize reliability over
peak output:

1. Enter Operator form and hold Klamora primary on the target Synovia.
2. When the primary beam cannot track due to Eidolon movement, switch to
   Klebrik secondary and let the homing disc find the target.
3. Return to primary when the Synovia is re-positioned and the beam can
   reestablish contact.
4. Use the extended Anspatha energy pool to sustain longer sessions before
   returning to Void Mode.

This configuration is recommended for players building their first Vox Solaris
Amp or for players whose playstyle is not suited to burst-timing mechanics.
