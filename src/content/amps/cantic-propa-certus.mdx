## Overview

Cantic-Propa-Certus pairs the Cantic burst prism with the Propa Scaffold's
time-delayed sticky bomb secondary and the Certus Brace's maximum critical
chance bonus. This configuration is one of the strongest for dealing burst
damage to static or slow-moving targets, since the Propa bomb can be placed
precisely on a Synovia and then detonated remotely.

The 40% critical chance from the Certus Brace applies to both the Cantic
burst and the Propa detonation, making this configuration capable of very
high single-interaction damage when Void Strike is combined with a point-blank
Propa explosion.

## Components

### Cantic Prism (Primary fire)

The Cantic Prism's three-beam burst provides sustained damage between Propa
placements. Each of the three beams checks the Certus Brace's 40% critical
chance independently, giving Cantic bursts an average of 1.2 critical hits per
trigger pull (3 x 0.4 = 1.2 expected crits per burst).

In this configuration, the Cantic Prism is the sustained fire tool while the
Propa Scaffold handles burst execution.

### Propa Scaffold (Secondary fire)

The Propa Scaffold fires a sticky bomb that attaches to the first surface or
enemy it contacts. The bomb does not immediately explode. The player detonates
it by firing the secondary again, or it explodes automatically after a timer.

The ability to place a bomb directly on a Synovia before detonating it turns
the Propa into one of the highest burst-damage scaffolds in the game. A
point-blank Propa detonation, enhanced by Madurai Void Strike and the Certus
Brace's critical chance, can deal enormous single-hit Void damage.

The technique requires the Operator to approach close enough to a Synovia to
land the sticky bomb accurately, which demands spatial awareness in the
movement-heavy Eidolon encounter.

### Certus Brace

The Certus Brace from Vox Solaris provides 40% critical chance across all
attacks from the Amp. For the Propa Scaffold, this means the bomb detonation
itself checks critical chance. A critical Propa detonation with Void Strike
active represents the peak single-hit damage achievable from an Operator.

## Damage profile

All damage is Void type. The Propa bomb detonation's damage is not specified
in WFCD data; community reports indicate it deals higher burst damage than
any scaffold secondary in a single activation. Critical hits from the Certus
Brace increase this by 2x, and Madurai Void Strike multiplies the damage
further.

The combined damage ceiling of Cantic-Propa-Certus with a full Void Strike
and critical Propa hit is considered the highest single-hit Operator damage
achievable by a Quills and Vox Solaris-equipped player.

## Acquisition

The Cantic Prism is a Quills offering. The Propa Scaffold and Certus Brace
are Vox Solaris offerings from Fortuna. This is a cross-content configuration
requiring both Plains of Eidolon (Quills) and Orb Vallis (Vox Solaris)
progression.

Players must complete both the Plains of Eidolon introduction quests and the
Fortuna introduction quest (Vox Solaris) to access all three components.

Exact material costs are not in WFCD data and are on the Fandom wiki.

## Playstyle

The Cantic-Propa-Certus workflow in Eidolon hunts prioritizes maximizing the
Propa detonation:

1. Charge Madurai Void Strike in Void Mode.
2. Exit Void Mode and approach a Synovia closely.
3. Fire the Propa secondary to stick the bomb to the Synovia.
4. Detonate by firing the secondary again immediately for point-blank burst.
5. Follow up with Cantic bursts while the Propa charges again.
6. Return to Void Mode to recharge Void Strike.

The approach phase (step 2-3) is the riskiest part. Eidolons deal high damage
to Operators within close range. Operators are fragile, so players must commit
quickly to placing the bomb and immediately back away or re-enter Void Mode
before the next Eidolon attack.
