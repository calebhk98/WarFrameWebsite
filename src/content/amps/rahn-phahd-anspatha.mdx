## Overview

The Rahn-Phahd-Anspatha combines the Rahn Prism's charged burst with the
Phahd Scaffold's seeking secondary and the Anspatha Brace's energy capacity
bonus. This configuration is designed for players who want longer Operator
sessions without returning to Void Mode for energy regeneration. The Anspatha
Brace extends the number of shots the Operator can fire before energy depletion,
increasing the effective damage window per transference.

Rahn fires a powerful charged projectile that deals burst damage on a single
shot, making it a high-risk, high-reward prism for players with accurate aim.
The Phahd Scaffold compensates with its homing secondary for follow-up shots
on moving targets.

## Components

### Rahn Prism (Primary fire)

The Rahn Prism charges a single Void projectile and releases it on trigger
release. The charge time is noticeable, and firing the shot before full charge
delivers a weaker projectile. A fully charged Rahn shot deals more damage per
shot than most other prisms, but the charge time reduces effective fire rate
compared to burst or continuous options.

The Rahn Prism rewards deliberate, patient fire discipline. Players who can
consistently land fully charged shots on Synovias extract more damage per
energy spent than those who rush shots before full charge.

### Phahd Scaffold (Secondary fire)

The Phahd Scaffold's homing disc supplements Rahn's slow fire rate. Between
Rahn charges, players fire Phahd discs at targets. The disc's homing behavior
compensates for the deliberate pace that Rahn demands -- after a charged shot,
firing a Phahd disc as a follow-up does not require precise aim because the
disc self-corrects toward the nearest target.

The combination of Rahn's burst and Phahd's follow-up creates a pattern of
charge-fire-disc-charge-fire-disc that maximizes damage output per unit time
despite Rahn's slow rate of fire.

### Anspatha Brace

The Anspatha Brace increases the Operator's maximum energy capacity. With a
larger energy pool, the Operator can fire more Rahn shots per Operator session
before energy depletion forces a return to Void Mode. For the charge-intensive
Rahn Prism, this directly translates to more charged shots per transference
window.

The Anspatha Brace does not improve critical chance (unlike Lohrin or Certus),
so this configuration accepts lower critical hit rates in exchange for extended
combat endurance per session.

## Damage profile

All damage is Void type. The Rahn Prism's fully charged shot deals high burst
damage. The exact damage values at max charge are not in WFCD data; community
benchmarks compare favorably to the Cantic burst at similar Operator ranks.

Critical chance is 0.16 (16%) with the Anspatha Brace. This is lower than
Lohrin or Certus configurations, reflecting the energy-for-crit tradeoff.
Players who use this build accept fewer critical hits in exchange for more
total shots per session.

## Acquisition

The Rahn Prism is available from the Quills vendor in Cetus. The Anspatha
Brace is also a Quills offering. The Phahd Scaffold is available from the same
vendor at a higher standing tier.

This is a full Quills configuration, not requiring Vox Solaris standing. It
is accessible to players who have progressed the Plains of Eidolon content
without needing to unlock Fortuna.

Exact material requirements are not in WFCD data and are documented on the
Fandom wiki at the links below.

## Playstyle

The Rahn-Phahd-Anspatha suits a deliberate playstyle:

1. Enter Operator form and charge a Rahn shot.
2. Release the fully charged shot at a Synovia.
3. Immediately fire a Phahd disc at the next target while Rahn resets.
4. Continue the charge-fire-disc pattern until energy runs low.
5. Return to Void Mode for energy recovery, using the extended Anspatha
   capacity to time returns less frequently than with smaller energy pools.

Madurai Void Strike pairs well with Rahn: the charged shot delivers the full
multiplier in a single high-damage burst, making the most of the Void Strike
accumulation window.
