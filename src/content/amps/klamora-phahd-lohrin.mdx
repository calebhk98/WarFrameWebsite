## Overview

The Klamora-Phahd-Lohrin combination pairs the Klamora Prism's wide-beam
continuous fire with the Phahd Scaffold's seeking projectile secondary and the
Lohrin Brace's critical bonus. This configuration favors players who prefer
continuous beam output over burst fire, and the Phahd Scaffold's homing
projectile makes it easier to land secondary fire on mobile targets.

The configuration is considered a versatile choice for players who engage in
both Eidolon hunting and general Sentient combat, as the continuous beam is
effective at breaking Synovia shields while the Phahd secondary can chase
fast-moving enemies.

## Components

### Klamora Prism (Primary fire)

The Klamora Prism fires a wide continuous beam of Void energy. The beam has a
larger hitbox than precision prisms, making it easier to stay on target against
moving enemies. However, the beam consumes energy continuously while held,
requiring players to manage Operator energy capacity and regeneration.

The wide beam excels at maintaining sustained contact with large targets like
Eidolon bodies, but can be less efficient than burst-fire prisms against small
Synovias where beam clipping past the target wastes energy.

### Phahd Scaffold (Secondary fire)

The Phahd Scaffold fires a seeking disc of Void energy that homes in on the
nearest enemy within a cone in front of the Operator. The homing behavior makes
it forgiving when targeting fast-moving Eidolon limbs. The disc deals moderate
Void damage and has a small explosion radius on impact.

The seeking mechanic can occasionally target the wrong enemy if multiple
targets are present, so players should ensure the desired target is closest
before firing. The disc travels at a moderate speed and reaches its target
within a second at typical Eidolon engagement ranges.

### Lohrin Brace

The Lohrin Brace adds critical chance and status chance to the Amp. For the
Klamora's continuous beam, the critical chance applies per tick of the beam.
At 24% critical chance, a continuous beam lands a critical hit roughly once
every four ticks, providing meaningful damage spikes without dedicating a
brace slot to a purely crit-focused option.

## Damage profile

All damage is Void type. The Klamora beam deals continuous Void damage and is
effective at stripping Synovia shields on the Teralyst, Gantulyst, and
Hydrolyst. The beam's sustained contact is an advantage when combined with
Madurai's Void Strike, since the charge transfers to the first beam contact
and the continuous damage continues at base rate afterward.

Critical chance of 0.24 (24%) applies per beam tick for the Klamora primary.
The exact base critical chance without the Lohrin Brace is not in WFCD data.

## Acquisition

Components are crafted at the Quills standing vendor in Cetus. The Klamora
Prism is a higher-tier Quills component. The Phahd Scaffold and Lohrin Brace
are available at middle to upper Quills standing tiers.

Materials include Sentient Cores, Intact and Exceptional, as well as Cetus
Wisps and other Plains of Eidolon resources. Exact material lists are on the
Fandom wiki.

Players must complete Saya's Vigil and earn Quills standing to access the
vendor. Standing cap per day limits the grind pace for higher-tier components.

## Playstyle

The Klamora-Phahd-Lohrin approach emphasizes sustained damage over burst:

1. Charge Void Strike in Void Mode.
2. Exit and hold the Klamora primary beam on a Synovia to apply the Void
   Strike multiplier and then sustain damage.
3. Use Phahd secondary fire to chase moving Synovias that the beam cannot
   track as quickly.
4. Return to Void Mode to recharge Operator energy and begin the next
   Void Strike cycle.

The continuous beam playstyle requires more active tracking than burst-fire
prisms. Players who find it easier to maintain beam contact on moving targets
will get more consistent Synovia damage per Operator session than those who
prefer click-timing bursts.
