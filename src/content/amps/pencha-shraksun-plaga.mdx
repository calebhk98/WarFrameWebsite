## Overview

The Pencha-Shraksun-Plaga is an accessible mid-game Amp configuration that
players can build before reaching the highest Quills and Vox Solaris standing
tiers. The Pencha Prism fires sustained Void bolts at a moderate rate, the
Shraksun Scaffold provides explosive burst secondary fire, and the Plaga Brace
offers balanced energy and critical bonuses without the extreme investment of
Certus.

This configuration is recommended for players who have completed Saya's Vigil
and begun Quills reputation but have not yet reached the higher standing tiers
needed for Cantic, Klamora, or Certus components. It provides meaningful
Eidolon hunting performance while serving as a stepping stone toward the
top-tier builds.

## Components

### Pencha Prism (Primary fire)

The Pencha Prism fires a rapid stream of small Void bolts, similar in feel to
an automatic rifle. The fire rate is higher than burst or charged prisms, but
the per-bolt damage is lower. Against Eidolon Synovias, the Pencha requires
more hits to deal equivalent damage but the rapid fire makes it forgiving of
brief aim inconsistencies -- if one bolt misses, the next can land before
the window closes.

The Pencha suits players who have not yet developed the precise aim habits that
Raplak or Rahn demand. Its sustained fire delivers consistent damage as long as
the Operator stays on target.

### Shraksun Scaffold (Secondary fire)

The Shraksun Scaffold fires an explosive projectile that detonates on impact.
For the Pencha build, the Shraksun secondary provides burst damage supplementing
the Pencha's sustained stream. The Shraksun projectile can be used between
Pencha fire to layer damage types and prevent energy over-drain from holding
primary fire continuously.

### Plaga Brace

The Plaga Brace is a middle-tier offering from the Quills, providing a moderate
critical chance increase (to 20%) and a modest energy capacity bonus. It is less
powerful than the Certus or Lohrin Braces at their respective endpoints, but
serves as a bridge brace for players building toward those options.

The Plaga's energy capacity bonus slightly extends how long the Operator can
fire before energy depletion, similar to the Anspatha but at a lower magnitude.

## Damage profile

All damage is Void type. The Pencha's damage per second over sustained fire
is competitive with burst prisms at equivalent Operator ranks, but the burst
ceiling is lower. The Shraksun secondary provides the primary burst moments
that the Pencha cannot deliver on its own.

Critical chance at 0.20 (20%) with the Plaga Brace. This is lower than the
endgame Certus or Lohrin configurations but provides a noticeable improvement
over no brace or a base brace.

## Acquisition

Both the Pencha Prism and Plaga Brace are Quills offerings at mid-tier Quills
standing. The Shraksun Scaffold is also a Quills offering. This is a full
Quills build, requiring no Vox Solaris progression.

Players who have completed Saya's Vigil and accumulated moderate Quills standing
can build this configuration before needing Fortuna access. It is a natural
second-stage Amp after the starter configurations.

Exact material costs are not in WFCD data and are documented on the Fandom wiki.

## Playstyle

The Pencha-Shraksun-Plaga suits a straightforward sustained-fire approach:

1. Charge Madurai Void Strike in Void Mode.
2. Exit and hold Pencha primary on a Synovia to apply the Void Strike multiplier
   and continue dealing sustained damage.
3. Switch to Shraksun secondary for a burst when approaching energy limits or
   when moving to the next Synovia.
4. Return to Void Mode when energy is low to regenerate and rebuild Void Strike.

The Pencha's sustained fire is the most beginner-friendly prism style. New
Operator players who are learning Eidolon positioning find the forgiving fire
rate of the Pencha more comfortable than the timing demands of Raplak or the
arc adjustment of Granmu.
