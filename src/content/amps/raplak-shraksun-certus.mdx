## Overview

The Raplak-Shraksun-Certus combines the Raplak Prism's slow, high-damage
single shot with the Shraksun Scaffold's explosive secondary and the Certus
Brace's 40% critical chance. This configuration demands the highest aiming
precision of the popular configurations but rewards accurate players with
the highest per-shot damage from the primary fire among hitscan prisms.

The Certus Brace pushes the Raplak's single-shot critical chance to 40%,
meaning nearly half of all direct hits land as critical hits. Combined with
Madurai Void Strike, a critical Raplak shot can deal damage rivaling a
Propa detonation at longer range.

## Components

### Raplak Prism (Primary fire)

The Raplak Prism fires a single high-damage Void beam shot on each trigger pull.
Unlike the Cantic's three-beam burst or the Klamora's continuous beam, Raplak
fires one discrete bolt per trigger with a moderate cooldown between shots.

The single-shot nature means that aiming precisely at the target Synovia is
critical. A miss is a complete loss of that shot's energy and damage. Players
who achieve consistent hit rates with Raplak report higher overall DPS than
burst configurations because the Raplak shot's base damage per energy point
is higher.

### Shraksun Scaffold (Secondary fire)

The Shraksun Scaffold fires an explosive projectile that deals Void damage in
a radius. For the Raplak build, the Shraksun secondary covers the gap between
Raplak shots. During the Raplak's recovery window, firing a Shraksun projectile
adds damage without interrupting the next Raplak shot cooldown.

The Shraksun projectile is slower than hitscan and requires lead time at long
range. Players combine Raplak for close-to-medium engagements and Shraksun for
follow-up at the same range.

### Certus Brace

The Certus Brace's 40% critical chance applies to both the Raplak shot and the
Shraksun detonation. For the Raplak's single shot, a 40% chance means the
player crits on 4 out of 10 shots on average, with occasional runs of multiple
consecutive critical hits. During Madurai Void Strike, a critical Raplak shot
multiplies the base Void Strike bonus by the critical multiplier.

## Damage profile

All damage is Void type. Raplak's per-shot damage is not in WFCD data; it is
the highest among standard hitscan prisms based on community benchmarks. The
Shraksun detonation adds area Void damage on secondary fire.

At 40% critical chance and 2x critical multiplier, the average Raplak damage
per shot is 1.4x base (0.6 x 1.0 + 0.4 x 2.0). Over a sustained engagement,
this outperforms lower critical chance configurations assuming equal hit rates.

## Acquisition

The Raplak Prism is a Quills standing offering from Cetus. The Certus Brace
and Shraksun Scaffold are Vox Solaris offerings from Fortuna. This is a
cross-open-world configuration.

Completing both Saya's Vigil (Cetus) and the Fortuna introduction for Vox
Solaris access is required before crafting all components.

Material costs are on the Fandom wiki.

## Playstyle

The Raplak-Shraksun-Certus demands precise aim discipline:

1. Position at medium range where Raplak shots hit reliably.
2. Charge Madurai Void Strike in Void Mode.
3. Exit and fire one Raplak shot at a Synovia.
4. While Raplak recovers, fire a Shraksun secondary at the same target.
5. Fire the next Raplak shot once the cooldown completes.
6. Repeat until Operator energy is low, then return to Void Mode.

Missing Raplak shots is the primary failure mode of this configuration.
Players new to Operator combat should practice on stationary targets before
committing to Raplak in active Eidolon hunts.
