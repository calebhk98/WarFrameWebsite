## Overview

The Lega-Lohrin-Certus is referred to in community discussions as a legacy
configuration because it predates the Vox Solaris expansion that introduced
newer components. It pairs the Lega Prism's rapid-fire bolts with the Lohrin
Scaffold and the Certus Brace. Some older guides and community resources still
reference this combination, and it remains functional for players who prefer
rapid-fire primary output.

The configuration requires cross-progression: the Lega Prism is a Vox Solaris
component, while the Certus Brace is also from Vox Solaris. The Lohrin Scaffold
is a Quills component.

Note: The naming "Lega-Lohrin-Certus" inverts the typical scaffold/brace
shorthand from the components as labeled in the crafting menu. It refers to
Lega as the prism, Lohrin as the scaffold, and Certus as the brace.

## Components

### Lega Prism (Primary fire)

The Lega Prism fires rapid, small Void bolts in a hitscan stream. The fire rate
is the highest of all standard prisms, making it feel like an automatic weapon.
Each bolt independently checks the Certus Brace's 40% critical chance, so a
sustained burst of Lega fire generates many critical hits per second.

The tradeoff is that each individual bolt deals low damage. The Lega's strength
is in volume -- a large number of low-damage crits can exceed the output of
fewer high-damage hits with lower critical rates in sustained fire scenarios.

### Lohrin Scaffold (Secondary fire)

The Lohrin Scaffold has both a critical chance bonus property (on its brace
side) and a scaffold form. As a scaffold, Lohrin fires a sustained Void beam
as its secondary mode, similar to a different prism style. The beam provides
a consistent secondary fire option that contrasts with the Lega's rapid bolts.

Switching from the Lega's bolt storm to the Lohrin beam secondary can maintain
damage output when changing ranges or when the rapid-fire input cadence becomes
fatiguing.

### Certus Brace

The Certus Brace from Vox Solaris provides 40% critical chance. With the Lega
Prism's high fire rate, the number of critical hits per second at 40% critical
chance is higher than any other prism configuration. Over a five-second burst,
a Lega firing at 10 bolts per second generates an expected 20 critical hits
(5 seconds x 10 bolts/second x 0.4 crit rate).

The raw critical hit count per session is the defining characteristic of this
configuration.

## Damage profile

All damage is Void type. The Lega Prism's damage per bolt is not specified in
WFCD data. The configuration's strength is sustained critical output over time
rather than peak single-hit damage.

Critical chance: 0.40 (40%) from the Certus Brace.

## Acquisition

Both the Lega Prism and Certus Brace are Vox Solaris offerings from Fortuna.
The Lohrin Scaffold is a Quills offering from Cetus. This is a mixed
configuration requiring both Quills and Vox Solaris progression.

The legacy designation reflects that newer players with both standing pools
available tend to choose the Cantic-based or Klamora-based configurations
from newer Eidolon hunting guides. This combination persists in older
community resources and remains a valid choice.

Exact material costs are on the Fandom wiki.

## Playstyle

The Lega-Lohrin-Certus suits players who prefer holding fire continuously
over managing burst timing:

1. Charge Madurai Void Strike in Void Mode.
2. Exit Void Mode and hold the Lega primary on the target Synovia.
3. The rapid bolt stream applies Void Strike to the first hit, then continues
   sustained fire at base Void damage rate.
4. Switch to the Lohrin secondary beam when repositioning to maintain
   continuous damage with a different weapon feel.
5. Return to Void Mode when energy is depleted.

The high fire rate of the Lega requires frequent Void Mode returns for energy
recovery unless the player uses the Anspatha Brace (a different configuration).
The Certus Brace here prioritizes critical output over extended fire time.
