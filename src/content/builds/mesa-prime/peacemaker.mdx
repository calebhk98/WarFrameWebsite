## Overview

Mesa Prime is the game's premier auto-targeting damage dealer. Peacemaker activates her Regulators secondary pistols, which lock on to targets automatically within a wide forward cone. This build maximizes Peacemaker's damage output while maintaining the Shatter Shield defensive layer that makes Mesa Prime viable in high-level content.

Arcane Velocity at rank 5 triggers on critical hits to increase Mesa's Regulator fire rate by 60%, stacking with the natural fire rate increase that occurs as Peacemaker remains active. Combined with Madurai focus school's Void Strike charges, Mesa Prime can clear dense Steel Path waves in seconds.

## Loadout

The configuration pairs defensive sustain through Vitality and Redirection with offensive ability support through Intensify and Mesa Waltz. Primed Continuity extends Peacemaker duration per energy unit spent, keeping the channel active longer per cast. Primed Flow provides the energy reserves to open multiple Peacemaker channels across a mission without relying on energy pickups.

Ballistic Battery is Mesa's first ability augment, storing weapon damage dealt over time and releasing it as a burst on the next Peacemaker shot. Charging Ballistic Battery before each Peacemaker activation noticeably increases opening damage against high-health priority targets.

Mesa Waltz, the Peacemaker augment, allows slow movement during the channel. This movement option is essential for survivability in mobile missions like capture or interception, where remaining stationary exposes Mesa to melee enemies or flanking attacks.

## Playstyle

Before entering combat, activate Shatter Shield to reduce incoming projectile damage by 95%. The buff has a long base duration, extended further by Primed Continuity, and requires minimal re-casting during standard missions. Refresh it proactively rather than reactively to avoid brief windows of vulnerability.

Deal weapon damage between Peacemaker channels to fill Ballistic Battery. When entering a new room or facing a boss, release the stored charge into the opening Peacemaker burst. Activate Peacemaker when the enemy group is within the targeting cone and hold the channel until the room is clear. Mesa Waltz allows slow rotation to cover targets outside the initial cone angle.

In defense and interception missions, position near the objective and maintain Peacemaker continuously across waves, refreshing Shatter Shield during the brief inter-wave downtime. Sprint Boost in the aura slot improves repositioning speed between waves and during objective transitions.

## Strengths and weaknesses

Mesa Prime's Shatter Shield is one of the most powerful defensive abilities in the game, reducing projectile damage by up to 95% at maximum power strength. Most Grineer, Corpus, and Sentient units deal almost exclusively projectile or hitscan damage, making Mesa Prime effectively immune to the majority of common damage sources at high ability strength. This defensive layer is what separates Mesa Prime from other damage-dealing frames in Steel Path viability.

Peacemaker's auto-targeting removes the skill ceiling normally associated with high-level gunplay, making Mesa Prime accessible to players of all mechanical skill levels. The targeting algorithm prioritizes the highest-health target in range, which is generally optimal but can occasionally fixate on a heavy unit while ignoring more dangerous units nearby.

The primary vulnerability is melee attacks and damage types that bypass Shatter Shield. Infested charger charges and ancient healers closing distance quickly are the most common threats. In these situations, a crowd-control support frame in the squad (Rhino, Nova, Limbo) significantly extends Mesa's effectiveness.

## Alternatives

Replacing Redirection with Steel Fiber improves armor-scaling survivability in Grineer and Corrupted missions where melee threats are less common. Swapping Sprint Boost for Energy Siphon in the aura slot converts passive energy regeneration into the build's primary fuel, reducing reliance on Arcane Energize procs.

## Notes

Peacemaker's per-shot energy cost increases by 0.5 for each enemy being targeted simultaneously. In rooms with large enemy counts, the channel drains energy rapidly. Activating Peacemaker after enemies have been grouped or partially eliminated by an ally reduces the per-frame energy cost significantly.

Madurai's Void Strike ability charges during operator mode by voiding into enemies; each charged stack adds bonus damage to Mesa's next weapon or Regulator shot. Cycling operator mode between Peacemaker channels and re-entering during charge buildup maximizes total damage per mission.
