## Overview

Volt Prime serves a dual role in this configuration: a personal speed-and-shield utility frame and a squad fire-rate buffer. Speed affects all squadmates in range and scales fire rate by a percentage that increases with power strength. At the strength values provided by Umbral Intensify and Power Drift, Speed provides a meaningful fire rate increase sufficient to noticeably reduce time-to-kill on Eidolon synovias or Steel Path armored enemies when combined with high-fire-rate primary or secondary weapons.

Electric Shield is a deployable barrier that converts all bullets passing through it to Electric damage and adds a flat damage bonus to each shot. When positioned correctly between the player and enemy clusters, every shot the player fires benefits from both the damage conversion and the flat bonus. This converts even modest-damage weapons into viable tools and gives Volt a damage multiplier that functions on a per-shot rather than per-ability basis.

Energy Siphon in the aura slot provides 0.4 energy per second passive regeneration at rank 5, complementing Arcane Energize's probabilistic energy bursts. The combination keeps Speed, Electric Shield, and the Helminth Roar all refreshed without draining the pool.

## Loadout

Umbral Intensify and Umbral Vitality provide the Umbral two-piece set bonus. The additional power strength from Intensify pushes Speed's fire rate component higher, and Umbral Vitality raises Volt Prime's effective health pool. Volt Prime's base health is 300 at max rank, which is low for Steel Path encounters; Umbral Vitality brings this to a survivable level before Arcane Grace contributes passive healing.

Primed Continuity at rank 10 combined with Constitution at rank 10 and Augur Message at rank 5 pushes total duration above 230% of base. At this duration, Speed persists for over 45 seconds per cast, reducing recast frequency to approximately once per minute. Electric Shield similarly extends past 45 seconds, although practical repositioning often requires recasting before expiry.

Fleeting Expertise at rank 4 reduces all ability costs by 40%. Volt's Speed costs 50 energy at base; at full efficiency it costs approximately 16 to 17 energy, allowing multiple casts in rapid succession if a large squad needs Speed refreshed simultaneously. Electric Shield's energy cost is similarly reduced.

Arcane Velocity at rank 5 grants a 60% fire rate increase for 12 seconds on dealing Electric damage. Since Electric Shield converts all shots through it to Electric, every time the player fires through Shield while Velocity is active, the Electric proc chance on enemies maintains Velocity's uptime. The combined Speed fire rate buff plus Velocity fire rate increase produces a very high fire rate cap on weapons that support it.

## Playstyle

Open each encounter by casting Speed, then placing Electric Shield between the primary engagement line and the squad's forward position. Shoot through the Shield to apply Electric Shield's flat damage bonus and maintain Arcane Velocity uptime. Cast Discharge when groups of lighter enemies cluster around the Shield or attempt to flank.

Activate Roar via the Helminth slot immediately after Speed if the squad is about to engage a wave of priority targets. Roar applies multiplicatively to all damage -- including shots through Electric Shield -- so the combined multiplier during the overlap window significantly increases burst output. This window is particularly useful during Sortie assassination phases or Steel Path disruption conduit defense windows.

Recast Electric Shield proactively when repositioning rather than waiting for expiry. A Shield placed at the previous position loses value as the engagement moves; placing it at the new forward position takes priority. Constitution's duration extension means Shield usually survives position changes in linear tile sets without needing early recasting.

## Strengths and weaknesses

The primary strength of this build is the combination of Speed's squad fire rate buff and Electric Shield's per-shot damage conversion. Both buffs apply simultaneously and complement each other -- a faster-firing weapon deals more total damage through Electric Shield because each additional shot triggers the flat bonus again. In Eidolon hunts, this combination has historically made Volt the preferred companion for damage-focused Chroma or Rhino players.

The build's weakness is positioning dependency. Electric Shield must be oriented toward the primary enemy spawn direction to provide its bonus. If enemies spawn from multiple directions simultaneously, the Shield covers only one approach, and shots from other angles receive no bonus. Open-area missions (Orb Vallis bounties, Cambion Drift excavation) are less optimal for this build than corridor-driven tilesets.

## Alternatives

Replacing Augur Message with Narrow-minded increases duration further at the cost of range. This tradeoff suits Eidolon-specific use where squad positioning is fixed and Speed's range needs to cover only a small lakeside area. Reduced range on Discharge is acceptable in Eidolon contexts where clearing small enemy groups is less critical.

Swapping Rolling Guard for Adaptation in the defensive slot is viable if the squad lacks a healer. Adaptation's up-to-90% damage reduction applies faster than Rolling Guard's cooldown in sustained combat, though it requires taking damage to ramp.

## Notes

Volt's Speed buff at this build's configuration provides approximately 25% to 35% fire rate increase to squadmates, depending on the exact power strength calculation with the Umbral set bonus active. The in-game buff HUD shows the exact percentage applied.

Electric Shield placement facing a specific direction means shots must pass through the flat physical shield geometry to receive the bonus. Crouching behind the Shield and firing over it does not trigger the damage conversion; only shots passing through the Shield plane do.
