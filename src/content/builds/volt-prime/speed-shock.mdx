## Overview

Volt Prime's Speed Shock configuration prioritizes dual-utility support that accelerates mission progression while amplifying squad weapon damage output through Electric Shield stacking. The build maximizes power strength to increase shield damage bonus and crowd control effectiveness, while maintaining adequate duration for continuous Speed buff application.

Electric Shield's damage transformation provides scaling benefits independent of weapon elemental mods, enabling electricity-based damage stacking that bypasses typical elemental limitation constraints. This build excels in coordinated team content where movement speed and offensive utility create multiplicative squad advantage.

## Loadout

The configuration balances power strength and duration to maximize both Speed and Electric Shield uptime. Primed Continuity at rank 10 extends Speed duration to approximately 25 seconds, enabling aggressive recast intervals that maintain near-permanent buff coverage. Umbral Intensify and Umbral Vitality enable set bonuses while boosting power strength beyond 175% baseline.

Augur Reach and Stretch together extend Electric Shield range to 40+ meters, ensuring entire squad maintains shield proximity without forced positioning constraints. Streamline manages casting costs to approximately 30 energy per ability, sustainable through Zenurik passive and Arcane Energize.

Power Donation aura grants flat power strength to squad, improving all ability scaling and weapon damage bonus magnitude. Transient Fortitude adds additional power strength while reducing duration, offset by Primed Continuity's overcompensation for Speed uptime.

## Playstyle

Initiate squad advancement by casting Speed to grant all teammates 80% movement acceleration and attack speed bonus. Immediately layer Electric Shield ability to add electricity damage scaling to all outgoing weapon shots, effectively doubling team baseline damage output. Allow shield stacks to persist through combat rotations, building additional stacks as squad engages new enemies.

Maintain Speed recast intervals of approximately 20 seconds to ensure continuous buff availability. Prioritize Speed over Electric Shield refreshing when energy limited, as movement buff provides greater tactical advantage than incremental damage bonuses.

Use Overload ability on high-priority targets or enemy groups that threaten squad safety. Overload's electrical arcs stun nearby enemies, enabling brief crowd control windows for repositioning or emergency revives.

Position centrally near squad to ensure Speed buff range covers all teammates. Maintain proximity to activated shields to benefit from electricity damage scaling on weapons.

## Strengths and weaknesses

Speed's movement acceleration stacks multiplicatively with sprint mods and base movement speed, enabling 100%+ sprint speed increases impossible to achieve through individual frame abilities. Squad-wide attack speed bonus scales all weapon damage types, providing universal damage multiplication independent of elemental affinity.

Electric Shield transforms squad weapon damage into electricity, enabling elemental damage stacking and proc-scaling synergies that benefit status-heavy weapons. Shield charges persist indefinitely until consumed, allowing gradual shield stack buildup throughout extended missions.

The primary weakness is continuous buff maintenance: Speed requires recasting every 20 seconds to prevent buff expiration, and Electric Shield stacks decay if not consistently refreshed. In missions with high enemy spawn density, sustaining both buffs simultaneously demands careful energy management and positioning discipline.

## Alternatives

Replacing Transient Fortitude with Blind Rage increases power strength by 10% while maintaining full duration support. This variant prioritizes maximum shield damage and overload crowd control at the cost of additional efficiency reduction.

Substituting Augur Reach with Constitution provides additional duration extension without range penalties, improving Speed uptime and enabling less frequent recast intervals. This alternative suits content where squad cohesion allows closer positioning.

## Notes

Electric Shield's damage transformation bypasses elemental damage caps, enabling unlimited electricity scaling beyond normal elemental limit constraints. Stack shields actively to maximize damage multiplication.

Overload's electrical arcs trigger on nearby enemies regardless of line of sight, enabling crowd control through obstacles and terrain. Use this ability defensively when squad threatens wipe scenarios.
