## Overview

Loki Prime's Decoy Stealth Aggro build harnesses Invisibility and Decoy for unmatched utility in stealth-dependent missions. This configuration prioritizes ability duration and strength to maintain invisibility uptime while refreshing Decoy cooldowns rapidly enough to support sustained crowd control. The build excels in Spy missions, Rescue operations, and Exterminate sorties where remaining undetected generates higher mission efficiency and faster completion times.

The core strategic approach involves maintaining permanent or near-permanent invisibility while using Decoys as remote aggro pulls for any enemies that might threaten detection. Switch Teleport provides repositioning flexibility without breaking stealth, and Invisibility's energy cost becomes negligible when paired with Streamline and Arcane Energize. This makes Loki Prime one of the most versatile stealth operatives available for solo or squad-based covert objectives.

## Loadout

The mod layout emphasizes duration and efficiency over raw power strength. Primed Continuity at rank 10 extends Invisibility duration to approximately 18 seconds, providing a comfortable window for moving between objectives without constant recasting. Fleeting Expertise at rank 4 reduces Invisibility's energy cost to approximately 40 energy per cast, sustainable indefinitely through passive Energize procs and Naramon energy regeneration.

Umbral Intensify and Umbral Vitality work together to increase Decoy health and shield scaling while providing the Umbral set bonus for better survivability. Stretch and Augur Reach expand Switch Teleport's effective range to allow repositioning up to 40+ meters away, providing significant flexibility for bypassing laser barriers and cameras. Power Drift in the exilus slot maintains additional power strength without reducing efficiency further, a critical balance for energy economy.

## Playstyle

Open each objective room by casting Invisibility and remaining undetected while moving to the hackable terminal or objective location. If enemies occupy the direct approach, preemptively cast Decoy at maximum range to pull aggro, then move around the distracted group while invisible. The Decoy will persist for its full duration, keeping enemy attention divided while the squad hacks or completes objectives.

Once the primary objective completes, either remain invisible and leave the area quietly or cast additional Decoys to cover squad withdrawal if enemies position at exits. Switch Teleport allows repositioning through walls or behind enemies if an alarm triggers unexpectedly, buying time for squad revives or objective completion.

In extended Exterminate missions without strict stealth requirements, use Decoys to group enemies while squad mates apply weapons fire. The combination of Decoy pulls and Invisibility escapes provides safety margins that allow less-geared squad mates to complete content they might otherwise struggle with. Casting Decoy every 8-10 seconds maintains continuous aggro management.

## Strengths and weaknesses

Loki Prime's combination of genuine invisibility and remote aggro control provides unique value no other frame matches. Invisibility cannot be defeated by enemy abilities or corrupted armor, making it the only crowd control that works 100 percent reliably against all enemy types. Decoy aggro pulling is equally consistent, providing safe objective completion in Spy missions even with minimal squad communication.

The primary weakness is Loki's low personal survivability outside invisibility. If Invisibility fails unexpectedly or runs out during repositioning, Loki has limited defensive tools besides Switch Teleport. The build relies on prevention rather than mitigation, making it less forgiving for players who make mistakes during enemy detection phases. Decoy's limited health means even low-level enemies can eliminate it quickly if multiple units focus fire.

## Alternatives

Replacing Fleeting Expertise with Transient Fortitude increases Decoy health substantially for longer survival time in combat, though invisibility casting becomes more expensive. Substituting Arcane Precision for Arcane Grace trades weapon crit bonus for passive healing, improving survivability during unavoidable detection. Dropping Augur Reach for Primed Flow allows Switch Teleport to function on demanding energy budgets while reducing range slightly.

## Notes

Invisibility breaks immediately upon attacking with weapons, so primary damage output comes from squad mates. Decoy aggro does not break invisibility, allowing Loki to remain undetected while pulling enemy attention away. Switch Teleport can be used mid-invisibility to reposition without interrupting the buff, providing unique tactical flexibility for bypassing laser grids and camera sensors.
