## Overview

Nova Prime's Molecular Speed build transforms the frame into a team-wide speedrun accelerant, enabling the fastest possible mission clear times in endless farming rotations. Molecular Prime applies a global speed buff to all allies while simultaneously slowing enemy movement and attack speed, creating a dual-utility effect that compounds team efficiency. This build minimizes mission duration through aggressive range extension and duration maintenance, prioritizing ability uptime over pure damage output.

The build leverages Overextended to push Molecular Prime's range beyond standard tile boundaries, ensuring that speed buff coverage extends to allies regardless of tactical positioning. Blind Rage and Umbral Intensify compensate for range penalties while maintaining sufficient power strength for reliable enemy slowdown application.

## Loadout

The configuration prioritizes ability duration through Primed Continuity at rank 10, extending Molecular Prime uptime to approximately 15 seconds before refresh cycles become necessary. Streamline and Fleeting Expertise manage energy costs, reducing per-cast drain to sustainable levels that align with Zenurik passive recharge and Arcane Energize procs.

Augur Reach stacks with Overextended to achieve 60+ meter Molecular Prime coverage, ensuring squad-wide speed buff application across most tile setups without requiring positional coordination. Null Star benefits from power strength but scales minimally with duration, freeing modslots that could enhance spread radius instead.

Cunning Drift provides consistent power strength while maintaining exilus flexibility compared to Blind Rage alternatives. Power Donation in the aura slot grants flat power strength to the entire squad, synergizing with shared utility frames and optimizing both Molecular Prime effectiveness and weapon damage scaling.

## Playstyle

Initiate tile progression by casting Molecular Prime on clusters of enemies near advancement chokepoints. The ability's speed buff applies instantly to all allies within range, triggering immediate movement acceleration and enemy slowdown. Allow the buff to expire naturally rather than recasting prematurely, as each recast consumes 100+ energy.

Position aggressively near enemy groups to maximize Molecular Prime coverage and ensure slowdown application before squad advancement. Use Worm Hole to bypass environmental obstacles or reposition after casting, enabling shortcuts in stealth-optional or parkour-heavy tiles. Maintain constant movement to minimize exposure to unslowed enemy damage.

Coordinate with squad to avoid overextending past Molecular Prime's range perimeter. Cast from central tile positions rather than squad rear to ensure all allies remain within slowdown application zones. Recast Molecular Prime every 12-15 seconds to maintain continuous buff uptime throughout longer survival rotations.

## Strengths and weaknesses

Molecular Speed provides the fastest mission-completion times available to any single warframe, making this configuration invaluable for infinite-scaling endurance runs where rotation speed determines survival duration. Team-wide speed buffs stack multiplicatively with sprint mods and Bulletjump mechanics, enabling 100%+ sprint speed increases that are impossible to achieve through individual frame abilities.

Enemy slowdown reduces incoming damage before armor considerations, effectively tripling team defense at base Molecular Prime cast rank and duration. The slowdown persists independent of enemy faction, armor type, or mechanical immunity, making it universally applicable across all star chart content.

The primary weakness is Nova Prime's limited defensive mechanics outside Molecular Prime's enemy slowdown. Null Star requires constant attention to maintain full stacks, and the shields it grants scale poorly without dedicated power strength investment. In ultra-high-steel-path tiers, slowdown alone cannot prevent one-shot scenarios, limiting endgame survival ceiling compared to full defensive frames.

## Alternatives

Replacing Power Donation with Corrosive Projection trades squad power strength for enemy armor stripping, improving overall team DPS in content where extended fights justify armor scaling. This variant suits missions with high armor-value enemies like Kuva Liches or Void Cascades rather than infinite endless survival.

Substituting Augur Reach with Transient Fortitude increases power strength by 30% but reduces ability duration significantly, breaking the core Molecular Prime uptime strategy. This alternative is not recommended and should be avoided in favor of maintaining duration above 30 seconds baseline.

## Notes

Molecular Prime's slow effect applies to environmental hazards and projectiles in some tilesets, occasionally blocking squad advancement through areas with active laser grids or spike traps. Coordinate with squad to confirm path clearance before advancing past activated hazard zones.

Worm Hole applies brief invulnerability frames to Nova Prime during teleport, allowing skilled players to dodge unavoidable projectiles by timing repositioning casts between incoming damage waves. This mechanic is not guaranteed on high-latency connections and should not be relied upon as a primary defense strategy.
