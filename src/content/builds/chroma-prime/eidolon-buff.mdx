## Overview

Chroma Prime is the standard buff frame for Eidolon Tridolon hunts. Vex Armor's critical damage component, Fury, grants bonus critical damage multiplier to the entire squad based on the current health damage received. At maximum Vex Armor stacks, this buff reaches up to 275% critical damage at high power strength, which translates directly into the extra damage needed to break Eidolon Synovias in fewer shots per limb.

This specific build targets maximum power strength to push Vex Armor's buff ceiling as high as possible. Blind Rage and Transient Fortitude together exceed 300% power strength when accounting for Power Drift, which requires streamline to offset the efficiency penalty from Blind Rage.

## Loadout

Power strength is the primary optimization target. Blind Rage at rank 10 contributes the highest single-mod power strength in the game at a significant efficiency cost. Transient Fortitude adds a further strength bonus with a duration penalty, which is accepted here because Vex Armor requires re-application every 30+ seconds anyway. Umbral Intensify contributes the Umbral set bonus and fills the remaining strength gap.

Elemental Ward on Electricity element grants bonus fire rate and armor to Chroma and all nearby allies. Bring an [Electricity Chroma](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Chroma) configuration specifically for Electricity element -- changing element requires equipping a specific elemental warframe ability mod or swapping helmets, depending on configuration. The fire rate bonus from Electricity Ward accelerates operator amp and weapon cycling during synovia breaks.

Steel Fiber and Vitality establish the base health and armor pool that Iron Skin scales from. Iron Skin's total absorption capacity is calculated from Chroma's total armor and health values, so higher base values extend Iron Skin's effective duration between reactivations.

## Playstyle

Equip the Electricity element before entering the hunt. Cast Elemental Ward immediately on entering the Plains at night to grant the fire rate and armor buff to all squad members. Cast Vex Armor and allow incoming damage from Eidolon ground strikes or Vomvalysts to stack the buff rapidly. Alternatively, cast Effigy and let the dragon companion absorb damage to generate Vex Armor stacks without requiring Chroma to take direct hits.

Maintain Vex Armor through each Eidolon phase. The buff resets if the ability expires, so re-cast before the duration runs out rather than waiting for the visual stack counter to drop. During Synovia break phases, position to maintain the buff radius over the primary damage dealer in the squad, typically a Volt or Trinity player.

Iron Skin absorbs a fixed amount of damage determined at cast time. Re-cast it between Eidolon roar attacks, which deal high spike damage that can exhaust the shield quickly. Keeping Iron Skin freshly cast at high armor values (300+) provides a comfortable buffer across each hunt phase.

## Strengths and weaknesses

Chroma Prime provides the highest single-frame critical damage multiplier buff available in Warframe, which is why the frame has been the standard Tridolon composition member since Eidolons were introduced. No other warframe provides a comparable team-wide critical damage amplification from a single ability.

The build requires active ability rotation to maintain. Players who do not re-cast Vex Armor proactively or who allow Iron Skin to deplete without re-casting expose the full squad to reduced buff uptime. In a coordinated squad, this reduces total Eidolon kill speed noticeably. Chroma Prime also fills a relatively passive role -- most of the playstyle is casting and repositioning rather than dealing direct damage.

## Alternatives

Running Roar via Helminth subsume over Effigy provides a simpler damage buff at the cost of the Effigy companion's independent damage. Some hunt compositions prefer Roar Chroma for its ease of management. Trinity Prime or Harrow Prime support remains the standard energy-provision pair for Chroma, regardless of Effigy vs. Roar choice.

## Notes

Vex Armor's two components -- Scorn and Fury -- respond differently to incoming damage. Scorn stacks on shield damage, Fury stacks on health damage. To build Fury stacks quickly (the critical damage component), Iron Skin must be depleted so that incoming hits reach health. Allow Iron Skin to run out briefly at the start of each hunt to build Fury stacks, then recast Iron Skin at maximum Fury stack count to lock in the buff.
