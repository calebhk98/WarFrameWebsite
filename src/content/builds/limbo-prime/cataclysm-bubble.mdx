## Overview

Limbo Prime's Cataclysm Bubble Defense build maximizes enemy immobilization and objective protection through overlapping rift manipulations. This configuration prioritizes ability range, duration, and strength to create defensive barriers that isolate threats while keeping the objective secure. The build excels in Defense and Mobile Defense sorties where crowd control and denial generate more team safety than raw damage output.

The core philosophy involves creating a Cataclysm field large enough to encompass the defense objective, then banishing allies or the objective itself into the Rift dimension where no outside enemy can inflict damage. Stasis continues this defensive approach by freezing enemies outside the bubble, effectively creating layered protection through multiple crowd control mechanisms. Duration and range scaling make Limbo's defensive toolkit substantially more effective than other protection-based frames at sustained crowd control.

## Loadout

The mod selection balances range and duration to maximize Cataclysm field coverage duration. Primed Continuity at rank 10 extends Cataclysm's uptime to approximately 16 seconds at max efficiency, providing enough duration for squad revives and emergency responses without constant recasting. Stretch and Augur Reach combine to expand the bubble radius to approximately 30 meters, sufficient to encompass most defense objective rooms and still maintain coverage as enemies advance.

Overextended at rank 5 reduces power strength by 30 percent, an acceptable trade when paired with Umbral Intensify and the Umbral set bonus. The resulting power strength level still provides adequate rift stasis duration for freezing enemies and sufficient Cataclysm damage to eliminate stragglers who remain outside the bubble. Power Drift in the exilus slot adds additional power strength without the efficiency penalty that Blind Rage carries, maintaining better energy economy for sustained casting.

## Playstyle

Begin each Defense round by casting Cataclysm in the center of the objective room, creating a large protective bubble. Position the field to encompass the defense target while leaving space for squad mobility. If the team can fit inside safely, consider banishing the objective directly into the Rift for complete protection, though this approach reduces mobility for squad members supporting it.

Activate Stasis on enemies attempting to advance toward the bubble or approaching from alternate routes. This freeze creates a secondary barrier of immobilized threats that the squad can eliminate methodically. If Cataclysm's duration drops below 5 seconds remaining, preemptively recast to avoid a gap in coverage when enemies rush the objective.

Use Limbo's Rift Surge augment to deal additional damage to rifted enemies while restoring energy on rift kills, improving energy economy beyond what Streamline provides. Banish allies proactively if they take heavy burst damage, buying time for them to heal between rounds without risking the objective.

## Strengths and weaknesses

Limbo's Cataclysm provides unmatched crowd control density for mission types with fixed defensive objectives. No other frame can simultaneously immobilize an entire room of enemies while keeping the objective invulnerable to outside attacks. The layered approach of Cataclysm plus Stasis creates redundancy—if one crowd control source fails, the other maintains protection until energy restores for recasts.

The primary weakness is energy management during extended waves where recasting becomes more frequent than ability cooldowns allow. The high range-to-strength trade also reduces Cataclysm's internal damage per enemy, making the build less efficient at eliminating low-level fodder compared to damage-focused alternatives. Squad coordination becomes mandatory because banished allies lose mobility and external squad support until Limbo casts Rift Surge or enemies die.

## Alternatives

Replacing Overextended with Transient Fortitude shifts the build toward short-burst crowd control in Void Fissures by prioritizing strength over range. This variant uses smaller Cataclysm bubbles but freezes stronger enemies more effectively. Dropping Augur Reach for Cunning Drift allows the exilus slot to provide better mobility while maintaining adequate range for most mission types. Substituting Arcane Tanker with Arcane Aegis provides flat shield regeneration after banishing, improving personal survivability.

## Notes

Cataclysm's internal damage scales with power strength but primarily serves as crowd control rather than elimination. The rift dimension is invulnerable to all outside damage sources, making banished objectives and allies completely protected regardless of enemy firepower. Stasis duration outside the Cataclysm bubble is independent of the bubble's range, allowing Limbo to freeze enemies around corners and hallways beyond the visual field.
