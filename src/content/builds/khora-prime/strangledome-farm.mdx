## Overview

Khora Prime's Strangledome Farm build is the standard configuration for resource-intensive farming missions. Pilfering Strangledome grants a 65% chance per kill inside or near the dome to drop an additional resource instance, effectively tripling total resource yield over a standard run when stacked with a Resource Booster. This build is the go-to frame for farming rare resources such as Orokin Cells, Neural Sensors, and Neurodes from enemies in corresponding missions.

Adaptation at rank 10 provides up to 90% damage reduction against the most recent damage type received, making Khora Prime durable against sustained enemy fire without requiring a dedicated defensive ability rotation. This resilience is critical in endless missions where enemy scaling eventually overcomes static health pools.

## Loadout

Pilfering Strangledome is the central mod of this configuration. At rank 5 it provides the full 65% additional drop chance on kills made while enemies are ensnared in or adjacent to an active Strangledome. The mod occupies slot-8 and has no redundant replacements -- removing it defeats the build's primary purpose.

Primed Continuity and Augur Reach together extend both duration and radius of Strangledome beyond base values. A wider dome captures more enemy spawn patterns, and longer duration reduces the energy cost of dome maintenance across a mission. Stretch adds a further range increase, ensuring the dome's coverage area overlaps with standard corridor and room spawn zones.

Intensify at rank 5 contributes to Whipclaw's damage output, which chains damage to all enemies caught in Strangledome simultaneously. A single Whipclaw cast hits every domed enemy, accelerating kill speed and maximizing the number of Pilfering procs per dome cycle.

## Playstyle

Identify the primary enemy spawn corridor or room in the target mission and cast Strangledome at the center of that zone. Strangledome anchors enemies in place, preventing them from leaving the zone and keeping them inside the pilfering radius. Kill suspended enemies with Whipclaw or with weapons to trigger resource duplication checks on each kill.

Venari defaults to Heal stance, providing continuous health regeneration to Khora Prime based on nearby damage dealt. Keep Venari in Heal stance throughout farming sessions rather than switching to Attack or Guard, as the passive healing from Heal stance scales with enemy density near the kavat.

Zenurik's Energizing Dash replenishes energy during dome re-cast intervals. Between dome placements, dash through enemies with operator mode to activate the energy regeneration channel, then exit operator and re-cast Strangledome before the energy surplus depletes.

## Strengths and weaknesses

Pilfering Strangledome is the most reliable resource-doubling tool in the game for enemy kill-based resources. Unlike Hydroid's Pilfering Swarm, which also duplicates drops, Strangledome keeps enemies immobile inside a defined zone rather than pulling them toward a radial area. This immobilization makes weapons and Whipclaw more efficient, as all targets remain in predictable positions.

The primary limitation is precision of dome placement. If Strangledome is placed too far from enemy spawns, enemies move toward the dome but may die outside the pilfering radius, denying the resource bonus. Familiarize with tile spawn patterns to place domes optimally.

Khora Prime does not strip enemy armor. In Grineer missions with heavy armored units, supplement this build with a Corrosive Projection aura from another squad member, or swap the aura slot to Corrosive Projection and accept the loss of the Resource Drone bonus.

## Alternatives

Replacing Resource Drone with Corrosive Projection makes this build functional in high-level Grineer missions where armor stripping is required for reliable kills. However, Resource Drone contributes an additional 40% resource pickup value that stacks with Pilfering Strangledome and Resource Boosters, so use it whenever the mission faction does not require armor stripping.

## Notes

Pilfering Strangledome's 65% duplication chance applies independently to each enemy kill inside the dome. At high enemy density, the expected additional resource count per dome cycle approaches 65% of total kills, which compounds substantially across extended missions.

Bringing a Nekros with Desecrate as a squad companion does not conflict with Khora's Pilfering Strangledome. Both loot duplication systems check independently per enemy corpse, producing an expected loot multiplier above 2x when both are active simultaneously with a Resource Booster.
