## Overview

Saryn Prime's Spore Spread build is the standard configuration for Corpus and Infested tile farming, focusing on initiating a spore chain that scales Corrosive damage with each successive kill. The build prioritizes ability duration and range to maximize spore uptime and spread radius, while maintaining adequate power strength for consistent Miasma finishers. At rank 5 Arcane Energize, energy economy becomes sustainable through regular enemy kills and energy orb interactions.

Regenerative Molt provides passive healing when Saryn casts Molt within proximity of an active Molt decoy, generating a healing pulse that scales with remaining Molt health. This mod choice improves survivability in Steel Path without requiring a dedicated defensive arcane slot.

## Loadout

The configuration balances duration, range, and strength for reliable spore maintenance. Primed Continuity at rank 10 extends spore tick duration significantly, allowing stacks to build higher before decay. Stretch and Augur Reach together push ability radius well above base, ensuring a single Spores cast covers a standard tile room's enemy population.

Molt Vigor at rank 5 grants a temporary power strength buff after casting Molt, which amplifies the next Miasma or Spores cast. Timing Molt before a Miasma finisher increases the total toxin burst applied to already-infected targets.

Umbral Intensify contributes to the Umbral set bonus alongside Umbral Vitality, providing an additional 25% power strength increase and boosting Saryn's health pool above 800 at max rank. This survivability improvement is significant in Steel Path where enemy scaling pushes damage output into ranges that bypass Molt's health pool.

## Playstyle

Open each encounter by casting Spores on a single enemy and immediately breaking the target with any weapon or ability. The rupture spreads spores to all enemies within the initial target's detection radius. Allow spore stacks to accumulate for two to three ticks before casting Miasma, which doubles Spore damage and strips a percentage of enemy armor simultaneously.

Cast Molt when moving through heavily-defended corridors to leave a decoy that draws enemy fire and triggers Regenerative Molt's healing when the coy takes damage. Re-cast Spores on any fresh enemy groups entering the tile to maintain continuous spread chains.

In survival missions, prioritize maintaining spore spread on life support units and demolishers that arrive on fixed timers. Clear infected waves with Miasma while spores continue ticking on enemies out of direct line of sight.

## Strengths and weaknesses

This configuration clears Corpus and Infested missions more efficiently than most other warframe builds due to Corrosive Spore damage stripping armor and stacking with each kill for exponential scaling. At 20 or more spore stacks on a single target, the per-tick damage scales past most Steel Path enemy health pools within a few seconds. Saryn's Toxic Lash passive augments weapon damage to also apply spore bursts on hits, creating synergy between weapon kills and ability spread.

The primary weakness is the setup time required before Spore stacks peak. In missions with sparse enemies or short encounter windows, Saryn loses efficiency relative to burst-damage frames. The build also struggles against individual high-health targets such as sortie bosses where spore chains cannot establish before the target dies.

## Alternatives

Replacing Regenerative Molt with Transient Fortitude adds a power strength spike that further amplifies Miasma damage, but reduces ability duration by 27.5%. This trade-off suits interception and defense missions with shorter wave windows. Dropping Corrosive Projection for Growing Power in the aura slot provides a status-triggered power strength bonus, useful when weapons apply status reliably.

## Notes

Saryn's Spores interact with Molt by re-infecting the decoy when enemies burst it. This creates an additional spread vector in dense areas. Casting Molt in the center of an infected group and then shooting the decoy spreads spores in a second radial pulse, effectively doubling the spread radius in open rooms.

Miasma's damage doubles for any enemy currently infected with Spores. Always confirm at least one spore stack on priority targets before committing the energy cost of Miasma.
