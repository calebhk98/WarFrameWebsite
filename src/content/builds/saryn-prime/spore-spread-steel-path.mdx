## Overview

This configuration targets Steel Path survival and exterminate missions where enemy level scaling demands both reliable armor stripping and meaningful personal tankiness. Saryn Prime's Spores ability applies stacking Corrosive damage that compounds with each kill, making it naturally suited to dense encounters. The Helminth slot replaces the fourth ability with Roar, converting a limited-use finisher slot into a team-wide and personal damage multiplier that applies before Spore ticks resolve.

Corrosive Projection in the aura slot provides 30% armor reduction per frame carrying it in a squad of four, which compounds with Corrosive Spore stacks to effectively neutralize Grineer Alloy Armor before stacks reach double digits. Running this build solo still benefits from the single aura contribution.

Adaptation is the key defensive choice distinguishing this configuration from standard Spore Spread setups. After taking damage from any source three or more times, Adaptation grants up to 90% resistance to that damage type for 20 seconds. In Steel Path, Grineer Bombards and Corpus Nullifier-backed forces deal sustained damage of a consistent type, enabling Adaptation to reach near its cap quickly and maintain it through continuous incoming fire.

## Loadout

Umbral Intensify and Umbral Vitality together activate the Umbral set bonus, adding a multiplicative 25% bonus on top of each mod's individual contribution. The combined effect raises Saryn Prime's health above 950 at rank 30 and pushes power strength high enough that a single Miasma burst removes a large fraction of Corrupted Bombard health even without full Spore stacks.

Primed Continuity at rank 10 extends Spore tick duration, which directly determines how many kills can accumulate stacks before the Spore buff expires. Longer duration means stacks persist through room-clearing gaps where enemies stop dying momentarily. Augur Message in slot 6 further extends duration while contributing to the Augur set bonus, which partially converts overshield drain into energy.

Streamline and Fleeting Expertise together reduce ability energy cost. Fleeting Expertise at rank 4 provides a 40% efficiency increase with a 20% duration reduction; Primed Continuity at rank 10 more than compensates for that reduction, leaving net duration above base. This is the standard efficiency-duration tradeoff for Saryn builds.

Arcane Energize at rank 5 gives a 60% chance per energy orb pickup to grant 150 additional energy to Saryn and nearby squadmates. In Steel Path survival, enemy density provides a steady stream of energy orbs from kills, which makes this arcane self-sustaining over the course of a rotation.

Arcane Grace at rank 5 provides a 9% health regeneration chance on critical hits, delivering a passive healing layer that synergizes with Saryn Prime's elevated health pool from the Umbral set and Adaptation's damage reduction. The combination of healing and mitigation makes it practical to operate in the open rather than behind cover.

## Playstyle

Begin each encounter by casting Spores on the nearest heavy enemy to ensure the first target survives long enough to spread to multiple others. Any weapon shot or melee hit that breaks the Spore bubble distributes spores outward in a radius governed by ability range. Stretch at rank 5 combined with Cunning Drift pushes total range to 141%, which covers standard Grineer tileset room widths.

After confirming spread, recast Roar before stacking Miasma. Roar applies a multiplicative damage buff to all outgoing damage including Spore ticks, which means every tick on every infected enemy benefits from the multiplier simultaneously. Miasma then doubles current Spore tick damage and strips a percentage of armor from all affected targets. This sequencing -- Spores, spread, Roar, Miasma -- is the standard damage cycle.

In extended survival runs, monitor the Adaptation resistance readout and avoid changing encounter positions once the resistance type has capped. Moving to a tile with exclusively different damage types resets the relevant Adaptation stacks and temporarily removes the defensive layer.

## Strengths and weaknesses

The build handles Infested and Corpus enemies with minimal setup because both factions have low innate armor. Corrosive Spores strip the modest Corpus Robotic armor quickly, and Infested enemies take full Corrosive damage without resistance. Against Grineer, particularly in Steel Path, the combination of Corrosive Projection and Spore stripping reduces the time to full armor removal from many seconds to a few ticks.

The primary weakness is enemy type diversity in some Steel Path nodes. Corrupted Heavy Gunners arrive with Alloy Armor, which resists some damage types, and the armor stripping mechanic is more critical to maintain. If Spores drop from a group mid-rotation, the window before re-spread can expose Saryn to high burst damage before Adaptation re-establishes.

## Alternatives

Replacing Adaptation with Quick Thinking adds an energy-to-health gate that prevents one-shot deaths in exchange for a sudden energy drain. This suits players comfortable with active energy management and Arcane Energize keeping the energy pool topped up. Quick Thinking pairs well with Primed Flow if that mod is available.

Swapping Augur Message for Molt Vigor (when available) introduces a temporary power strength burst triggered by casting Molt, which amplifies the following Miasma or Spores cast. This trades the Augur set bonus duration extension for burst damage windows at the cost of requiring an active Molt cycle.

## Notes

Saryn Prime's base stats give her 300 shield and 500 health at max rank. The Umbral set in this build raises effective health considerably. Spore tick damage at high stacks can exceed 10,000 per tick on a standard Steel Path Grineer Lancer when Roar is active and full strip has occurred; the exact figure depends on enemy level and armor scaling.

When running in a squad, Roar's buff icon is visible to all players. The 40-meter radius ensures all squadmates in the same tile benefit. Coordinate Miasma timing with squadmates who bring their own burst abilities for a combined damage window.
