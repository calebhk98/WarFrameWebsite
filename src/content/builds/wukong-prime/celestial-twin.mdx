## Overview

Wukong Prime's Celestial Twin configuration transforms the frame into a dual-damage platform through clone mirroring, effectively doubling all weapon DPS through independent clone attacks. The build prioritizes power strength and duration to maximize clone uptime and Defy defensive scaling, creating a self-sustaining damage dealer that scales independently from squad support.

Celestial Twin clones benefit from identical weapon loadouts, enabling multiplicative damage scaling that exceeds traditional squad-based DPS multiplication. Combined with Defy's scaling damage reduction, Wukong becomes increasingly tanky throughout extended missions while maintaining offensive capability.

## Loadout

The configuration balances power strength and duration to maximize clone uptime. Primed Continuity at rank 10 extends Celestial Twin duration to approximately 90 seconds, enabling aggressive 40-50 second recast intervals that maintain near-permanent clone availability. Stretch and Augur Reach together extend ability range, though primarily affecting non-essential range mechanics.

Streamline manages Celestial Twin casting costs to sustainable levels, reducing per-cast energy drain to approximately 50 energy. Umbral Intensify and Umbral Vitality enable set bonuses while maintaining health pool above standard frames.

Adaptation provides stacking damage reduction that scales infinitely as enemies attack. Combined with Defy's active damage reduction, Wukong achieves defensive scaling that approaches near-invulnerability in extended content.

## Playstyle

Activate Celestial Twin at mission start to summon permanent clone copy that mirrors all outgoing weapon attacks. Equip identical weapons on Wukong Prime and clone to maximize damage multiplication through paralleled fire patterns. Direct clone targeting through squad positioning and camera angles to focus fire on priority threats.

Maintain constant offensive pressure through clone attacks while positioning defensively. Use Defy ability preemptively before anticipated high-damage scenarios to establish active damage-reduction stacking. Allow Defy stacks to persist through combat rotations, as each enemy attack increases defensive multiplier.

Recast Celestial Twin before duration expires to refresh clone availability, maintaining near-permanent clone presence throughout extended missions. Manage energy economy through Zenurik passive and Arcane Energize interaction.

Use Iron Staff as needed for melee coverage during weapon downtime or for defensive crowd control. Staff attacks benefit from clone mirroring, enabling additional multiplicative damage output.

## Strengths and weaknesses

Celestial Twin enables effective weapon damage doubling through independent clone attacks, creating unique scaling potential that exceeds traditional squad-based damage multiplication. Each weapon equipped on both Wukong and clone mirrors fire patterns independently, resulting in parallelized damage that scales infinitely with weapon investment.

Defy provides scaling damage reduction that approaches near-invulnerability in extended content, as stacks accumulate through engagement and enemy attacks. Combined with Adaptation stacking, Wukong achieves unlimited defensive scaling independent of mod investment.

The primary weakness is clone AI limitation: clones sometimes target non-threats or become stuck on environmental obstacles, reducing overall damage-output efficiency. Additionally, weapon duplication requires massive platinum or crafting investment for prime weapon copies, limiting practical accessibility for newer players.

## Alternatives

Replacing Adaptation with Rolling Guard trades passive damage scaling for guaranteed invulnerability phases. This variant prioritizes predictable defensive windows over infinite scaling, suitable for missions with burst-damage patterns.

Substituting Transient Fortitude with Blind Rage increases power strength while maintaining full duration support. This alternative improves defensive scaling at the cost of additional efficiency reduction.

## Notes

Clone weapon attacks inherit all weapon mod bonuses from equipped loadouts, enabling scaling damage multiplication through traditional weapon modding. Prioritize high-damage-output weapons to maximize clone mirroring effectiveness.

Defy's damage-reduction stacking persists through brief downtime windows, allowing defensive buff maintenance during Celestial Twin recasting sequences. Time recast sequences between anticipated damage bursts to maintain defensive stacking throughout engagements.
