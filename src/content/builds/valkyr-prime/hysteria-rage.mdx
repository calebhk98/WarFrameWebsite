## Overview

Valkyr Prime's Hysteria Invulnerability build creates the most durable melee platform in the warframe roster through invulnerability stacking and Eternal War augment duration extension. This configuration prioritizes power strength and duration to maintain continuous Hysteria uptime while using exalted weapon kills to extend the ability's remaining duration indefinitely. The build excels in Void Fissure missions, Steel Path Survival, and other endgame content where genuine invulnerability provides substantial team advantage.

The core strategic approach involves activating Hysteria at mission start, then relying on Eternal War to refresh the duration with each exalted weapon kill. This transforms Valkyr from a time-limited invulnerable melee fighter into a permanent invulnerable tank capable of engaging any enemy type or density without risk. Energy management becomes the only limitation, solved through Streamline and Arcane Energize on weapon kills.

## Loadout

The mod selection emphasizes power strength and duration for maximum Hysteria effectiveness. Umbral Intensify and Umbral Vitality unlock the Umbral set bonus while scaling exalted weapon damage directly. Blind Rage at rank 8 provides additional 40 percent power strength at the cost of 39 percent efficiency, a favorable trade when paired with Streamline's 30 percent drain reduction and Eternal War's kill-based refresh mechanic.

Eternal War augment at rank 4 extends Hysteria duration by 2 seconds per exalted weapon kill, enabling indefinite uptime when maintaining consistent kill pressure. This augment is essential for sustained invulnerability chains in high-density content where enemies continuously respawn. Continuity combined with Streamline creates a balanced energy economy that allows Warcry and Rend casting between Hysteria activations.

Power Drift in the exilus slot provides additional power strength while maintaining better efficiency than alternative choices. The combination of Intensify, Blind Rage, and Power Drift creates approximately 140 percent power strength scaling, generating exalted weapon damage capable of one-shot eliminating Steel Path enemies.

## Playstyle

Open each encounter by casting Warcry to provide armor buff and movement speed boost, then immediately activate Hysteria. Begin engaging enemies with the exalted weapon while maintaining movement to avoid grouping excessive enemy aggro. Each kill triggers Eternal War to refresh Hysteria's remaining duration by 2 seconds.

Maintain consistent kill pressure throughout the mission to keep Hysteria refreshing faster than it expires. If Hysteria duration approaches zero and no enemies remain in immediate melee range, preemptively recast to avoid temporary vulnerability windows. Rend abilities can be used between Hysteria phases for crowd control, but priority remains on maintaining the invulnerability buff.

In extended survival rotations, recast Warcry every 20-25 seconds to maintain the armor and movement buffs. Hysteria should never fully expire if the build maintains minimum kill requirements of approximately 1-2 enemies per 15 seconds. Squad coordination matters less for Valkyr than other frames because personal invulnerability provides sufficient safety margin for even unoptimized squad compositions.

## Strengths and weaknesses

Valkyr Prime's Hysteria provides genuine invulnerability regardless of enemy firepower, enemy count, or mission difficulty. No damage type, ability, or enemy special mechanic can bypass the absolute invulnerable state while Hysteria remains active. Eternal War transforms this limited-duration buff into an indefinite state when maintained through consistent kills, creating an essentially unkillable warframe platform.

The primary weakness is Hysteria's massive energy cost and energy management complexity during lower-enemy-density phases. If kill pressure drops below sustained levels, Hysteria duration expires faster than Eternal War can refresh, creating brief vulnerability windows. The exalted weapon's melee-only damage type also limits effectiveness against extremely distant or shielded enemies until Valkyr closes range.

## Alternatives

Replacing Eternal War with Hysteric Assault adds crowd control through knockback instead of duration extension, trading invulnerability chains for room control. Substituting Transient Fortitude with Growing Power provides conditional strength bonuses triggered by weapon status procs on exalted weapon attacks. Dropping Blind Rage for Power Drift reduces power strength slightly but allows more flexibility in energy economy and easier mod fitting.

## Notes

Hysteria's invulnerability applies to all damage types including environmental hazards, making Valkyr immune to laser traps and radiation procs while active. Eternal War kills count as exalted weapon kills regardless of overkill damage, so one-shot eliminating groups still only extends Hysteria by 2 seconds. The invulnerability effect ends immediately upon Hysteria deactivation, creating brief vulnerability windows that careful positioning can minimize.
