## Overview

Nyx Prime's Assimilate Tank build exploits the Assimilate augment to convert Absorb from a stationary defensive cooldown into a permanent mobile damage shield. Without Assimilate, Absorb locks Nyx in place; the augment removes this restriction entirely, enabling full weapon use and movement while the nullification field remains active. The result is one of the most damage-immune builds in the game, limited only by energy economy.

Arcane Guardian at rank 5 stacks 900 bonus armor when Nyx takes a hit. Since Assimilate reduces damage taken to near zero before it reaches Nyx's health pool, the armor stacks persist indefinitely without depletion once established. Over the course of a mission, Arcane Guardian can stack its maximum armor bonus, adding a secondary defensive layer for the rare moments Absorb is inactive.

## Loadout

Assimilate at rank 5 is the defining mod of this build. It increases Absorb's energy drain while allowing weapons and movement, a trade-off that is strictly positive in endurance contexts where the mobility gain enables objective completion that would be impossible with the standard stationary Absorb.

Primed Flow at rank 10 provides the energy reserves to sustain Absorb through extended engagements. Streamline at rank 5 reduces ability costs, including Absorb's activation cost and the drain rate per damage absorbed. The combination of Primed Flow and Streamline extends Absorb duration significantly between energy pad uses or Zenurik top-offs.

Adaptation at rank 10 adds a second damage reduction layer that scales up to 90% against the primary damage type received in the last 7 seconds. This stacks with Absorb's inherent negation, creating overlapping protection layers that keep Nyx Prime alive even during the brief intervals when Absorb is being reactivated between releases.

## Playstyle

Activate Absorb at the start of each encounter wave and maintain the ability as a continuous shield while engaging objectives. Use primary and secondary weapons freely during Absorb. When the accumulated damage reservoir reaches a meaningful size (indicated by the energy pulse growing around Nyx), deactivate Absorb briefly to release the stored explosion, then immediately reactivate.

Monitor energy carefully. In high-density enemy rooms, the per-damage drain adds up quickly. If energy drops below 50%, back off from enemy fire briefly, allowing Zenurik's Energizing Dash to refill reserves. Primed Flow provides buffer against unexpected energy drain spikes from large enemy groups.

Cast Mind Control on high-value targets before activating Absorb for sustained fights. A controlled Heavy Gunner or Bombard fires at the squad, providing free Absorb energy accumulation while simultaneously removing a dangerous enemy from offensive rotation.

## Strengths and weaknesses

Assimilate Tank's primary advantage over other survivable frames is universal damage negation. Most tank builds in Warframe use armor scaling, shields, or regeneration mechanics that eventually fail against sufficiently scaled enemy damage. Absorb's negation is absolute -- any damage not bypassing the field is fully absorbed. This universality makes Nyx Prime functional in content types where other tanks struggle, including Archon hunts and Netracell missions with restricted mod selections.

The energy economy is the build's constraint. Against extreme enemy density (50+ enemies firing simultaneously), Absorb drain can exceed Arcane Energize procs and Zenurik regeneration combined. In these situations, carry energy pads as backup. The build is also dependent on the Assimilate augment, which is obtainable only through Steel Meridian standing rank at Bastion or higher.

## Alternatives

Replacing Adaptation with Augur Secrets reduces defensive layering but increases power strength slightly, which raises Absorb's released explosion damage. This is appropriate when Nyx is filling a damage-dealing role alongside tanking. Replacing Corrosive Projection with Energy Siphon in the aura slot improves passive energy regeneration, useful in solo missions without team energy support.

## Notes

Absorb's explosion on deactivation deals damage equal to all accumulated absorbed damage, amplified by power strength. Releasing a large accumulation against a dense group can delete an entire room's worth of enemies. Time releases for maximum density overlap rather than releasing when a single enemy group is at low health.

Mind Controlled allies are ignored by Absorb's explosion radius. Position controlled targets at the edge of expected release zones to preserve them as continued fighters after the explosion clears the surrounding enemies.
