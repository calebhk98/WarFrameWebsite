## Overview

Yareli's Merulina Mobility and Crowd Control build maximizes the unique K-Drive platform for rapid repositioning and area denial through rolling attack crowd control. This configuration prioritizes ability strength and duration to maintain Merulina uptime while using Aquablades for defensive coverage and status application. The build excels in open-world areas, tileset survival missions, and other content where mobility and sustained area crowd control provide significant tactical advantage.

The core strategic philosophy involves summoning Merulina at mission start and remaining mounted throughout encounters for continuous mobility and crowd control application. Yareli's passive bonuses while mounted enhance K-Drive handling and offensive capability, creating a platform where ability execution becomes significantly faster and more effective than baseline. The combination of movement speed and crowd control creates a unique tactical position that emphasizes aggression through constant motion.

## Loadout

The mod selection emphasizes strength and duration for maximum crowd control effectiveness. Umbral Intensify and Umbral Vitality unlock the Umbral set bonus while scaling Aquablades projectile damage and Merulina rolling attack crowd control. Blind Rage at rank 8 provides additional power strength at the cost of efficiency, acceptable when paired with Streamline's drain reduction.

Primed Continuity at rank 10 extends Aquablades duration to maintain defensive barriers throughout encounters. Stretch and Augur Reach expand crowd control radius to ensure rolling attacks hit maximum enemy count. Power Drift in the exilus slot maintains power strength for consistent crowd control application across all mounting phases.

The build requires careful health management to sustain Merulina mounting. Arcane Grace provides passive healing to offset damage taken while mounted, allowing indefinite Merulina uptime in moderate-threat environments. Extended survival missions require squad support for heavy burst damage mitigation.

## Playstyle

Begin encounters by summoning Merulina, then positioning on the K-Drive mount to maximize crowd control range. Execute rolling attacks by accelerating and moving through enemy groups, applying crowd control knockdown to multiple targets with each pass. Use Aquablades to create defensive barriers that intercept incoming projectiles while applying toxin status effects.

Maintain Merulina by managing health through positioning and squad support. Avoid concentrated enemy fire when possible, relying instead on mobility to evade threats while continuing crowd control execution. Rotate between rolling attacks and Aquablades casting based on current tactical situation, prioritizing crowd control when enemy density spikes.

Dismount temporarily if Merulina health drops critically low, allowing the K-Drive to despawn and health to regenerate. Resummon immediately after health recovers to minimum safe levels. This rotation maintains continuous crowd control uptime while preventing unexpected Merulina loss during critical mission phases.

## Strengths and weaknesses

Yareli's Merulina platform provides unique mobility that allows rapid repositioning and crowd control execution simultaneously. The K-Drive rolling attack hits multiple enemies with reliable knockdown, making Yareli effective at area denial in open environments. Aquablades provide defensive coverage that scales with power strength, creating additional squad protection value.

The primary weakness is Merulina's health dependency on squad support and Yareli's limited passive healing. In high-threat environments, Merulina becomes a liability that requires constant babysitting and repositioning to maintain uptime. Tight corridor environments severely limit K-Drive mobility effectiveness, reducing Yareli's tactical advantage in standard tileset encounters.

## Alternatives

Replacing Blind Rage with Power Drift reduces power strength slightly while improving energy economy for sustained ability rotation. Substituting Augur Reach for Primed Flow allows extended mission sessions with larger energy pools for continuous Aquablades spam. Dropping Cunning Drift for Exilus Mastery provides flat duration increase alongside exilus slot flexibility.

## Notes

Merulina health scales with Yareli's shield values, making shield-focused builds more effective at maintaining K-Drive uptime. Rolling attacks from the K-Drive apply crowd control to a radius around the mount, allowing squad mates to capitalize on knocked-down enemies. Aquablades projectiles inherit Yareli's gun mods if used outside the K-Drive, creating hybrid ability-weapon attack patterns.
