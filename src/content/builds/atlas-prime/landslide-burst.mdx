## Overview

Atlas Prime's Landslide One-Shot build maximizes single-target burst damage through power strength scaling and melee weapon synergy. This configuration prioritizes power strength and duration to generate extreme Landslide damage multipliers while using Rumblers augment for crowd control between burst phases. The build excels in Void Fissure missions, assassination targets, and other content where eliminating priority targets quickly provides significant team advantage.

The core strategic approach involves stacking power strength through Umbral mods, Blind Rage, and Transient Fortitude to create Landslide damage scaling that reaches 1000+ damage per hit in late-game content. Each Landslide cast uses the melee weapon equipped during activation, meaning melee mods directly amplify the ability's output. This creates a hybrid ability-and-weapon damage platform where both systems contribute multiplicatively to final burst output.

## Loadout

The mod selection emphasizes power strength above all other attributes. Umbral Intensify and Umbral Vitality provide the Umbral set bonus alongside general power strength scaling. Blind Rage at rank 8 and Transient Fortitude at rank 5 combine to provide approximately 80 percent additional power strength, creating approximately 160 percent total power strength when combined with Intensify and the Umbral set bonus.

Constitution provides duration scaling for longer Rumbler uptime and slightly increased survivability through faster shield regeneration. Streamline ensures energy efficiency remains manageable despite the power strength investment reducing efficiency. Primed Continuity extends Petrify duration for guaranteed Landslide hits on frozen targets.

The build relies on coordinated squad support to provide area crowd control while Atlas focuses on burst damage against priority targets. Rumblers augment creates persistent stone golems that crowd control and tank damage independently, providing area defense while Atlas recharges between Landslide casts.

## Playstyle

Identify priority targets at mission start and position Atlas to approach them safely. Cast Petrify on high-threat enemies to freeze them in place, then advance for Landslide execution. The petrified status guarantees the melee hit lands while preventing target repositioning, allowing maximum damage application without risk of dodge mechanics.

Execute Landslide immediately after Petrify expires or when approaching the priority target. The ability's damage scales with melee weapon mods equipped, so ensure the loadout includes high-damage weapon choices paired with crit-focused builds. Each Landslide cast applies the full melee weapon's damage calculation alongside the ability's base damage, creating explosive burst output.

Between Landslide cooldown periods, cast Rumblers to create persistent crowd control and tank incoming damage. This allows squad mates to focus fire on remaining priority targets while Rumblers handle area threats. Reposition between Landslide casts to maintain melee range access while remaining relatively safe from immediate threat.

## Strengths and weaknesses

Atlas Prime's Landslide provides unmatched single-target burst damage compared to other warframe abilities. The scaling with melee weapon mods creates a unique hybrid system where equipment choices directly impact ability output, allowing optimization through weapon selection. Petrify augment guarantees Landslide hits on frozen targets, making execution simple and reliable even against fast-moving bosses.

The primary weakness is Landslide's melee-range requirement in a game where ranged weapons generally outperform melee alternatives. Extended survival missions with sparse enemy density create awkward pacing where Landslide recharges take longer than enemy spawns, leaving Atlas vulnerable between burst phases. Boss fights against extremely mobile targets like Void Fissure arbiters require careful positioning to maintain range access.

## Alternatives

Replacing Transient Fortitude with Growing Power provides conditional power strength bonuses triggered by weapon status procs, creating synergy with crit-focused melee weapons. Substituting Constitution for Primed Flow allows extended mission sessions with larger energy pools for sustained Landslide casting. Dropping Blind Rage for Power Drift reduces power strength slightly while improving energy economy for less optimization-focused builds.

## Notes

Landslide damage calculation includes the equipped melee weapon's base damage, crit chance, and crit multiplier, making weapon choice essential for optimal burst output. Petrify freezes targets indefinitely until they take damage or the duration expires, providing safe setup windows for uninterrupted Landslide execution. Rumblers persist for their full duration even after Atlas reposition, allowing summon-based crowd control independent of primary ability rotation.
