## Overview

Voruna's Savage Claws build focuses on maximizing wolf summon uptime and pack coordination, transforming the frame into a pack leader that delegates most damage output to four independent wolf instances. The configuration prioritizes ability duration to extend wolf summon lifespan and power strength to amplify each wolf's individual damage, creating multiplicative scaling through pack parallelization.

Adaptation provides passive armor and damage reduction that scales with incoming wolf damage, essentially granting free survivability as wolves tank enemy fire and trigger stacking bonuses. This build excels in endless missions where wolf uptime directly correlates with survival duration and enemy clear speed.

## Loadout

The configuration combines duration and strength to maximize wolf effectiveness. Primed Continuity at rank 10 extends Hunting Pack duration to approximately 50 seconds, allowing aggressive 12-15 second recast intervals while wolves maintain independent threat coverage. Umbral Intensify and Umbral Vitality enable set bonuses while boosting power strength above 175% baseline.

Stretch and Augur Reach together push wolf spawn radius to 70+ meters, enabling deployment across entire tile layouts without repositioning. Streamline manages energy costs, reducing Hunting Pack casting to approximately 50 energy per cast with Zenurik passive regeneration. Transient Fortitude adds additional power strength at the cost of reduced duration, offset by Primed Continuity's dominance.

Adaptation scales infinitely as wolves engage enemies, providing stacking damage reduction that makes Voruna increasingly tanky throughout prolonged survival rotations. Corrosive Projection aura debuffs enemy armor globally, improving all damage sources including wolf attacks.

## Playstyle

Initiate combat by casting Hunting Pack to summon all four wolves near priority target clusters. Monitor wolf positioning and allow them to engage independently while Voruna maintains distance and focuses on threat management. Recast Hunting Pack every 12-15 seconds to refresh wolf summon timers before expiration, ensuring continuous pack presence.

Alternate between melee weapon usage and ability casting based on threat assessment. When wolves establish aggro on enemy groups, switch to melee combat to supplement wolf damage and trigger additional Adaptation stacks. Position aggressively near wolf combat zones to ensure line-of-sight for friendly fire interactions and ability recasts.

Use Voruna's other abilities as needed: Fanged Strike on single high-priority targets, Pilfer to strip armor and increase team damage, and Savage Silence to stun dangerous enemies. Maintain constant awareness of wolf expiration timers and plan recast sequences before packs enter downtime.

## Strengths and weaknesses

Four independent wolf instances create parallelized damage output that scales multiplicatively with pack power strength. Each wolf benefits from Voruna's ability strength independently, transforming a single 175% power strength buff into four separate damage multipliers that compound effective team DPS. Wolves persist even if Voruna enters bleed status, enabling sustained damage through survivability downtime.

Adaptation stacking provides unlimited scaling damage reduction, making Voruna progressively tankier throughout extended missions. Combined with Arcane Grace healing, the frame becomes self-sustaining without requiring external support or defensive modding beyond Vitality.

The primary weakness is wolf AI dependency. Wolves occasionally target low-priority enemies or become stuck on environmental obstacles, reducing overall pack efficiency. Additionally, wolves cannot benefit from companion mods, elemental damage affinity, or weapon-based status effects, limiting team synergy and forcing Voruna to carry independent damage sources.

## Alternatives

Replacing Adaptation with Rolling Guard trades passive damage scaling for brief invulnerability phases, suitable for missions with predictable burst-damage patterns. This variant sacrifices infinite scaling for guaranteed survive windows but reduces endgame-tier effectiveness.

Substituting Transient Fortitude with Blind Rage increases power strength by 10% while maintaining full duration, improving wolf damage at the cost of additional energy efficiency reduction. This alternative suits content where energy economy is less restrictive.

## Notes

Voruna's fourth ability, Savage Silence, grants brief invulnerability frames while executing animation. Use this defensively during high-threat scenarios when wolves are insufficient to handle immediate threats. Timing stuns with enemy burst patterns enables skill-based defensive play.

Wolf pack behavior can be influenced by camera angle and positioning. Point your weapon reticle at specific threats to guide pack aggression toward priority targets rather than relying on wolf AI to identify threats independently.
