## Overview

The Laetum is an Incarnon secondary pistol that fires standard rounds in its base form and transforms into an explosive-round launcher in Incarnon form after the gauge fills. The Incarnon evolutions for the Laetum include an option to add Toxin damage to the Incarnon form, which when combined with Heat from Primed Heated Charge produces a Viral and Heat combined application on each explosive round.

Viral damage reduces enemy maximum health capacity by 325% at six stacks, effectively tripling the damage dealt by all subsequent hits against the affected target. Heat applies a burn damage-over-time that increases damage taken by the burning target by a flat percentage. Together, Viral reduces the effective health threshold while Heat amplifies the damage rate, creating a two-layer debuff that dramatically reduces time-to-kill on any Steel Path enemy type that has health rather than purely shield-based defense.

Galvanized Shot at rank 10 provides a flat bonus per unique status type currently active on the target. With Viral, Heat, and any additional Electric or Cold status procs from the Laetum's base damage profile, Galvanized Shot's bonus can reach its maximum with two to three hits on a target. This makes Galvanized Shot particularly effective with a Viral-Heat combination because both status types contribute independently to the proc count.

Secondary Merciless at rank 5 stacks a damage multiplier on each kill, maintaining the stack for 20 seconds after the triggering kill. In enemy-dense Steel Path encounters, the multiplier stays near maximum throughout a rotation. The Laetum's explosive Incarnon rounds, which hit multiple enemies per shot, generate multiple kill opportunities per trigger pull at high stack counts.

## Loadout

Hornet Strike at rank 10 is the foundational damage multiplier. Barrel Diffusion at rank 5 and Lethal Torrent at rank 5 together add multishot and fire rate, which increases the total damage output in standard form while filling the Incarnon gauge more rapidly. Each additional round from Barrel Diffusion's multishot chance rolls for critical and status procs independently.

Primed Pistol Gambit at rank 10 adds 187% critical chance to the Laetum's base critical chance. The Laetum has a moderate base critical chance; Primed Pistol Gambit combined with Arcane Avenger's 45% on-damage critical chance bonus pushes total critical chance above 150%, producing consistent yellow-to-orange crits. Primed Target Cracker at rank 10 adds 110% critical damage, multiplying the already elevated crit output.

Galvanized Chamber at rank 10 adds stacking multishot on kills. In the Laetum's Incarnon form, each explosive round that generates kills increments the Galvanized Chamber stack, eventually producing two or more projectiles per shot. Additional projectiles each trigger independent explosions, dramatically increasing total area damage output at high stack counts.

Primed Heated Charge at rank 10 is the elemental mod that enables the Viral plus Heat combination when paired with the Toxin Incarnon evolution. The exact interplay between Heat and the Laetum's Incarnon evolution selection depends on the active evolution tree -- ensure Toxin is selected in the Laetum's Incarnon evolution slot before running missions with this build.

## Playstyle

Open each engagement in standard pistol mode, firing at the nearest enemy to build the Incarnon gauge. The Laetum's standard form fires relatively quickly with Lethal Torrent active, filling the gauge in approximately five to seven standard shots against a single target. Activate the Incarnon form via the reload input when the gauge is fully charged.

In Incarnon form, aim at the largest visible enemy cluster and fire. Each explosive round detonates in a radius on impact, applying Viral and Heat to all enemies within the explosion. Switch to personal weapon cover (bullet jump away) if enemies are closer than approximately 6 meters to avoid taking self-damage from the explosion.

After the Incarnon magazine empties, the weapon returns to standard form automatically. The gauge begins refilling immediately from the next standard shots. In dense survival missions, the transition between forms occurs rapidly enough that Incarnon form is available for most wave peaks while standard form handles between-wave maintenance.

## Strengths and weaknesses

Viral plus Heat is the most universally effective damage combination against non-robotic enemies across all factions. Grineer Flesh and Ferrite units take enhanced damage from Viral, Corpus Flesh units are similarly vulnerable, and Infested benefit from the Heat burn proc specifically. This combination avoids the faction-specific optimization trap that plagues elemental builds focused on Corrosive or Magnetic exclusively.

The primary limitation is the explosive self-damage risk in close quarters. The Laetum Incarnon's explosive rounds have a small blast radius that deals self-damage when fired at very close targets. In narrow corridor encounters where enemies are within melee range, the standard form or secondary melee weapon is safer. The explosion radius is smaller than the Kuva Bramma, making the Laetum safer at moderate range but still requiring discipline.

## Alternatives

Replacing Primed Heated Charge with Pathogen Rounds adds a Toxin-only application, which with the Laetum Incarnon's innate elemental (if configured without Toxin) could form an alternative combination. However, Primed Heated Charge provides substantially higher base elemental damage at rank 10 versus standard mod counterparts, making it the preferred choice when the Primed version is available.

Replacing Secondary Merciless with Secondary Deadhead changes the arcane from kill-stacking to headshot-stacking. The Laetum's explosive rounds make headshots inconsistent, so Merciless remains the preferred arcane for this weapon type.

## Notes

The Laetum's Incarnon evolutions are unlocked and configured through the Duviri Circuit. The Toxin evolution must be active in the weapon's evolution tree for the Viral plus Heat combination to apply in Incarnon form. Standard form damage is unaffected by Incarnon evolution selections.

Galvanized Shot's maximum stack in terms of unique status types is effectively six (one per status type: Viral, Heat, Slash, and any additional procs from elemental interactions). The exact damage bonus per stack is shown in the mod description and applies multiplicatively to the weapon's total damage output.
