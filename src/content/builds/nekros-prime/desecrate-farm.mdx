## Overview

Nekros Prime's Desecrate Farming build maximizes resource acquisition through loot multiplication and energy-efficient ability rotation. This configuration prioritizes ability duration and range to keep Desecrate active on maximum enemy corpses per mission, doubling the resource yield compared to standard farming approaches. The build excels in endless mission types like Survival and Defense where corpse accumulation scales continuously throughout the run, generating exponential resource gains.

The core strategy centers on Despoil augment, which converts Soul Punch's health drain into energy regeneration. This transforms Nekros from an energy-starved frame into a self-sustaining farming engine that maintains continuous Desecrate uptime without external energy sources. Paired with Shadows of the Dead for crowd control and additional tanking, Nekros becomes capable of solo farming even high-difficulty nodes with minimal support.

## Loadout

The mod selection balances range, duration, and efficiency for sustainable Desecrate maintenance. Primed Continuity at rank 10 extends Desecrate's duration to approximately 14 seconds, providing enough uptime for multiple enemy waves to die within radius. Stretch and Augur Reach combine to expand Desecrate to a 25+ meter radius, ensuring virtually all corpses within sight line receive desecration without requiring constant repositioning.

Despoil augment at rank 4 converts Soul Punch's health drain cost to energy drain instead, making Nekros energy-positive on casting the ability repeatedly. This synergy with Arcane Grace provides passive healing to sustain the health drain mechanic indefinitely. Power Drift in the exilus slot adds power strength without efficiency penalties, maintaining Despoil's energy generation rate while improving survivability through higher shield values.

Umbral Intensify and Umbral Vitality provide the Umbral set bonus while scaling Desecrate's loot multiplication percentage. Every point of power strength increases the chance of additional loot from corpses, making power strength the highest-priority stat after duration.

## Playstyle

Begin farming runs by casting Shadows of the Dead to populate the zone with minions that provide tanking and crowd control. Allow the squad or shadows to accumulate corpses across the mission area. Once 8-10 enemy bodies litter the ground, cast Desecrate to double the loot from all visible corpses, triggering Despoil to generate energy from the health drain cost.

Position yourself centrally to keep new corpses within Desecrate radius as they spawn. Recast Desecrate every 12-14 seconds as it expires, maintaining continuous loot multiplication throughout the farming session. If health drops below 50 percent, either allow Shadows to draw aggro or cast Soul Punch to trigger both Despoil energy generation and health restoration through Arcane Grace.

Use Nekros's Terrify ability sparingly, only to disable particularly dangerous enemies or refresh Shadow duration when approaching the 60-second expiration. Energy management becomes a non-issue after initial setup, allowing focus to remain on positioning and maintaining corpse accumulation.

## Strengths and weaknesses

Nekros Prime's Desecrate provides unmatched loot multiplication compared to other farming frames. The combination of Desecrate doubling drops and Despoil creating energy generation makes Nekros capable of solo farming unlimited survival waves. Extended farming sessions generate exponentially higher resource yields, with 30-minute survival runs producing twice the loot of equivalent time spent farming alternative nodes.

The primary weakness is lower personal survivability compared to tank-focused frames like Rhino or Hildryn. Desecrate requires enemies to die within range, making Nekros dependent on squad weapon damage or Shadow crowd control. Without adequate Shadow uptime or squad support, dangerous enemies can overwhelm Nekros before corpse accumulation reaches sustainable levels.

## Alternatives

Replacing Despoil with Pilfering Swarm (if bringing Nekros Prime with melee weapon slots) grants additional loot output on melee kills, though this reduces mobility freedom. Substituting Arcane Grace with Arcane Aegis provides flat shield regeneration after casting abilities, improving tankiness in high-difficulty farming. Dropping Augur Reach for Primed Flow allows extended mission sessions without energy concerns while slightly reducing Desecrate range.

## Notes

Despoil's energy generation occurs regardless of whether Desecrate successfully doubles loot, making the augment reliable even in sparse enemy zones. Soul Punch's health drain cost is fixed and unaffected by power strength, ensuring consistent energy regeneration across all power levels. Shadows of the Dead corpses do not receive desecration, but they benefit from periodic health restoration when Nekros casts Soul Punch near them.
