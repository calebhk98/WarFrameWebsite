## Overview

Nezha Prime's Divine Spears build focuses on enemy immobilization through persistent crowd control while maintaining passive invulnerability via Warding Halo stacking. The configuration prioritizes ability duration to extend spear impalement duration and power strength to maximize crowd control effectiveness across entire tile populations.

Warding Halo provides unlimited damage absorption through ability recasting, effectively granting infinite invulnerability in missions where Nezha maintains constant ability energy. Combined with Adaptation stacking, Nezha becomes progressively tankier throughout extended missions while controlling enemy actions simultaneously.

## Loadout

The configuration balances duration, range, and strength to maximize crowd control effectiveness. Primed Continuity at rank 10 extends Divine Spears duration to approximately 20 seconds, enabling aggressive recast intervals that maintain near-permanent enemy lockdown. Stretch and Augur Reach together push Divine Spears range to 60+ meters, covering entire tile rooms with single casts.

Streamline manages energy costs to sustainable levels, reducing casting frequency energy drain to approximately 40 energy per cast with Zenurik passive regeneration. Umbral Intensify and Umbral Vitality enable set bonuses while maintaining health pool above standard frames.

Adaptation provides stacking damage reduction that scales infinitely as enemies attempt attacking Nezha. This effectively creates a defensive loop where enemy attacks directly increase Nezha's tankiness, rewarding high-threat positioning and aggressive crowd control cycling.

## Playstyle

Initiate combat by casting Divine Spears through central enemy clusters to impale all targets in expanding radial pattern. Allow crowd control to persist for full duration, approximately 20 seconds, before enemy impalements naturally expire. Immediately recast Divine Spears before expiration to maintain continuous crowd control, preventing any windows where enemies escape immobilization.

Maintain Warding Halo continuously by recasting every 10-15 seconds to refresh invulnerability stacks. Position aggressively near enemy groups to ensure Nezha absorbs incoming fire and triggers Adaptation stacking. Retreat briefly if halo shields drop below 20% capacity, allowing brief energy recovery before recast.

Coordinate with squad to advance positioning during crowd-control windows. While enemies remain impaled, squad can revive casualties or establish defensive positions without threat pressure. Plan recast sequences to align with squad tactical movements.

## Strengths and weaknesses

Divine Spears immobilizes entire enemy populations simultaneously, preventing all enemy actions including ability casting, projectile fire, and melee attacks. This crowd control effectiveness enables squad-wide protection without requiring dedicated defensive modding beyond Nezha's core abilities.

Warding Halo provides unlimited invulnerability through continuous ability recasting, transforming Nezha into an unstoppable defensive unit in missions where energy management allows consistent casting. Combined with Adaptation stacking, Nezha can tank damage sources that would eliminate other defensive frames instantly.

The primary weakness is crowd control dependency: Divine Spears requires continuous recasting to maintain coverage, and brief downtimes enable enemy actions that threaten squad safety. Additionally, power strength reduction from range-extension mods forces uncomfortable tradeoffs between coverage area and spear impalement damage scaling.

## Alternatives

Replacing Adaptation with Rolling Guard trades passive damage scaling for guaranteed invulnerability phases. This variant prioritizes predictable defensive windows over infinite scaling, suitable for missions with burst-damage patterns.

Substituting Transient Fortitude with Blind Rage increases power strength by 10% while maintaining full duration support. This alternative improves Divine Spear damage output at the cost of additional efficiency reduction.

## Notes

Warding Halo invulnerability frames trigger during ability recasting animation, providing brief defensive windows even if halo shields drop to zero. Use this mechanic defensively during high-burst scenarios by timing recasts between incoming damage waves.

Divine Spears can be recasted to refresh halo stacks and extend spear duration simultaneously. Coordinate dual-cast sequences to maximize resource efficiency while maintaining zero-downtime crowd control.
