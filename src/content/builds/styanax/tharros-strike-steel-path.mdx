## Overview

Styanax's Tharros Strike is an ability that strips a percentage of enemy armor and shields proportional to power strength. At high enough strength, a single Tharros Strike cast removes a meaningful fraction of Grineer Alloy Armor and Corpus Robotic Shield simultaneously, making Styanax an effective setup frame for squads running Viral or Radiation damage combinations against Steel Path enemies above level 100. The Intrepid Stand augment (listed in the mods collection under `intrepid-stand`) extends Tharros Strike's area of effect and adds a knockdown to the proc, preventing counter-attacks in the brief window after strip.

This build prioritizes power strength and duration over maximum range, accepting that Tharros Strike covers a moderate radius centered on the targeted enemy. Styanax's passive provides shield regeneration to himself and nearby allies when he deals damage, making him partially self-sustaining in shield defense even when Tharros Strike is on cooldown.

Arcane Guardian at rank 5 provides 900 bonus armor whenever Styanax is struck, stacking with the build's existing armor value to provide meaningful damage reduction on top of Adaptation. Styanax Prime is not yet available (as of the current build's `updatedAt` date) and this build targets the base Styanax frame, which has lower base armor than prime variants would provide. Arcane Guardian compensates for this.

## Loadout

Umbral Intensify and Umbral Vitality activate the two-piece Umbral set bonus, adding a multiplicative 25% increase to each mod's contribution. Combined with Power Drift, total power strength reaches approximately 185% to 195% of base, pushing Tharros Strike's armor strip percentage above 40% per cast. At two casts in rapid succession, most Grineer heavy units are fully stripped.

Primed Flow at rank 10 provides a large energy pool for Styanax's energy-intensive rotation. Final Stand, Tharros Strike, and Axios Javelin together have significant cumulative energy costs; Primed Flow ensures the pool does not deplete during extended Tharros Strike sequences against armored priority targets.

Intrepid Stand at rank 10 extends Tharros Strike's knockdown radius and slightly increases the strip percentage. The rank 10 value provides the maximum extended radius available from the mod, which covers approximately one full tileset room width at this build's range configuration.

Adaptation at rank 10 provides up to 90% damage reduction against the primary incoming damage type after three or more hits of that type within 20 seconds. In Steel Path, Grineer Bombards and Corpus Bursa units deal sustained Blast or Radiation damage; Adaptation ramps quickly against these consistent damage types and effectively neutralizes the primary threat category within the first rotation of a survival wave.

## Playstyle

Open each Steel Path encounter by casting Tharros Strike on the nearest heavy enemy visible from the engagement position. The knockdown from Intrepid Stand prevents immediate retaliation from the stripped target and nearby enemies in range. Follow with Axios Javelin to deal damage to knocked-down targets, which triggers Styanax's passive shield regeneration pulse for nearby squadmates.

In survival missions, time Tharros Strike casts to coincide with Bombard and Heavy Gunner spawn timers. These enemies possess the highest armor values in the Grineer faction and benefit most from strip. Cast Tharros Strike on the first Bombard visible in each wave, confirm the strip via the enemy armor indicator, then let squadmate weapons or personal weapon DPS finish the target.

Final Stand should be reserved for high-density waves where multiple heavies arrive simultaneously. The ability's cast animation provides a brief invulnerability window that allows Styanax to survive burst damage from multiple sources that would otherwise require dodging. After Final Stand, recast Tharros Strike immediately to strip any surviving heavies before they re-engage.

## Strengths and weaknesses

The primary strength is multi-layer survivability. Adaptation's damage reduction, Arcane Guardian's bonus armor, and Umbral Vitality's elevated health pool together mean Styanax survives sustained Steel Path pressure without requiring active healing abilities. This makes him viable in solo Steel Path survival where healing frames are unavailable.

The primary weakness is that Tharros Strike's strip percentage at this build's strength level (approximately 40%) does not fully strip Grineer Alloy Armor in a single cast against high-level enemies. Full strip requires either two casts or a squad running a second Corrosive Projection aura. The four-slot conclave for auras limits how many Corrosive Projection instances are available in a squad.

## Alternatives

Replacing Adaptation with Quick Thinking provides a different survivability mechanism: converting remaining energy to health to prevent death from a single hit. This suits players comfortable with active energy management and squads where Arcane Energize provides reliable energy. Quick Thinking pairs with Primed Flow's large energy pool.

Replacing Intrepid Stand with Constitution increases Tharros Strike's duration extension without the augment's dependency on mod slot investment. This allows an alternative augment (such as a different Styanax augment if available) without sacrificing the build's duration.

## Notes

Styanax's Axios Javelin, his first ability, restores energy per enemy hit and staggers targets. This passive energy return from Axios Javelin partially offsets the high energy cost of Tharros Strike in combat situations where Javelin can hit multiple enemies simultaneously.

Tharros Strike's armor strip stacks additively with Corrosive Projection's aura contribution. In a squad of four all running Corrosive Projection at max rank, enemy armor is reduced by 120% of base before Tharros Strike applies its percentage-based strip to the remaining armor value. The order of operations matters: aura contributions apply first as a flat reduction, then Tharros Strike strips a percentage of what remains.
