## Overview

Protea Prime fills the squad support role with a toolkit that addresses the three most common resource needs simultaneously: energy, ammunition, and health. Dispensary generates a rotating pickup dispenser that outputs energy orbs, ammo pickups, and health orbs at regular intervals. A single Dispensary placement sustains the energy economy of most ability-intensive frames in the squad without requiring external energy management beyond a single placement per rotation.

Temporal Anchor provides Protea with personal survivability by acting as a rewind mechanism. When activated, Temporal Anchor marks Protea's current position and health state. If Protea is about to be downed, the ability can be triggered to return Protea to the marked state, restoring health and position while dealing burst damage based on all damage taken during the interval. This makes Protea Prime nearly impossible to kill when the ability is used proactively.

## Loadout

Duration and range are the primary optimization targets. Primed Continuity extends Dispensary's active duration, reducing re-cast frequency during missions. Stretch and Augur Reach expand the radius from which squad members can collect Dispensary pickups, covering more of a room from a single placement.

Intensify at rank 5 increases Dispensary's pickup generation rate (which scales with power strength) and boosts Shield Satellite's overshield value. Higher power strength means more frequent energy orb outputs from Dispensary, which is the primary benefit for energy-hungry squad members.

Adaptation at rank 10 protects Protea during engagements. The build does not rely on Iron Skin or other damage negation abilities, so Adaptation's up-to-90% damage reduction against the most-received damage type becomes the primary defensive layer alongside Vitality's health buffer.

Energy Siphon in the aura slot provides passive energy regeneration to all squad members in range, supplementing Dispensary's burst energy pickups with a continuous base regeneration tick.

## Playstyle

Place Dispensary at the mission's focal point -- objective room, defense point, or survival life support zone -- at the start of each round or rotation. The dispenser outputs pickups on a 15-second cycle; at extended duration, a single cast can remain active through multiple enemy waves. Re-cast at the same location as the previous placement expires to maintain continuity.

Cast Shield Satellites to apply overshields to nearby allies. The satellites orbit Protea and can be transferred to squadmates by moving near them. In defense missions, distribute satellites to squadmates covering different angles before each wave.

Temporal Anchor is best activated at the start of high-danger engagements (Archon hunts, sortie-level bosses) as insurance. All damage dealt during the anchor window is converted to a burst damage release on rewind. Against high-health targets, this can release meaningful burst damage while simultaneously negating incoming damage cost.

Grenade Fan provides consistent heat and shrapnel application against enemy groups in a forward cone. Use it during movement to apply status effects and apply heat procs that reduce enemy armor, supplementing the squad's damage output without requiring Protea to stop and engage.

## Strengths and weaknesses

Protea Prime's multi-resource support makes it the most versatile support frame for squad content. Unlike Trinity, which focuses on energy and health, or Harrow, which focuses on energy and reload, Protea addresses energy, ammo, and health simultaneously without requiring ability timing locks or specific buff maintenance. Dispensary functions passively after placement, allowing Protea to remain active in combat while support continues.

Temporal Anchor's rewind mechanic is the most forgiving death-prevention tool in the game, converting damage taken into bonus output rather than simply preventing the damage. For players learning Archon Hunt content or other high-stakes missions, Temporal Anchor provides a safety net that enables progressive learning without full wipe consequences.

The main limitation is Protea's lack of crowd control or armor strip. Dispensary support does not directly contribute to kills, making Protea less valuable in missions where clearing enemies faster is more important than sustaining ally resources.

## Alternatives

Replacing Primed Continuity with Narrow Minded trades range for extreme duration, creating a Dispensary that lasts through entire defense rounds without recasting. This is appropriate for stationary defense and interception missions where Protea holds a fixed position and range is less important than uptime.

## Notes

Dispensary's energy orb generation scales with power strength. At 140% power strength (this build's approximate output), Dispensary generates energy orbs more frequently than at base. For squads containing two or more energy-hungry frames, placing Dispensary centrally and refreshing every 45-60 seconds is sufficient to eliminate energy as a limiting factor.

Shield Satellites grant overshields equal to a percentage of Protea's max shields, which scales with Redirection if equipped. The base build does not include Redirection; squads requiring higher overshield values should swap Adaptation for Redirection in high-risk missions.
