## Overview

Titania Prime's Razorwing Blitz configuration prioritizes sustained aerial combat through extended Razorwing uptime and Dex Pixia damage optimization. The build maximizes power strength to increase Dex Pixia accuracy and Balefire crowd control effectiveness, while maintaining adequate duration for continuous Razorwing sustainability.

Razorwing's aerial mobility provides immunity to ground-based enemy projectiles, effectively tripling squad defensive value by removing one teammate from incoming fire patterns. Combined with Dex Pixia homing projectiles, Titania becomes a self-sustaining aerial DPS platform that scales independently from squad support.

## Loadout

The configuration balances power strength and duration to maximize Razorwing uptime. Primed Continuity at rank 10 extends Razorwing duration to approximately 70 seconds, enabling aggressive 30-40 second recast intervals that maintain near-permanent aerial flight. Augur Reach and Stretch together extend Dex Pixia range to 50+ meters, ensuring projectile tracking across entire tile layouts.

Streamline manages Razorwing casting costs to sustainable levels. Umbral Intensify and Umbral Vitality enable set bonuses while maintaining health pool above standard frames. Power Donation aura grants flat power strength to squad, improving Dex Pixia accuracy and Balefire crowd control effectiveness.

Blind Rage contributes final power strength boost, maximizing Dex Pixia homing accuracy and Balefire armor-strip magnitude. Constitution provides survivability without power scaling, allowing other modslots to enhance ability effectiveness.

## Playstyle

Activate Razorwing at mission start to establish aerial positioning and enable constant Dex Pixia fire without ground-based enemy threat pressure. Maintain continuous hovering while strafing to maximize projectile hit rate on clustered enemies. Use constant vertical movement to dodge incoming air-to-air attacks while keeping focus on priority targets.

Cast Balefire augment on high-threat enemies to trigger armor-stripping pulses and crowd control procs. Coordinate Balefire casting with Dex Pixia fire to maximize combined damage output during aggressive engagement phases.

Manage Razorwing energy consumption by recasting before duration expires, maintaining near-permanent aerial flight throughout extended missions. Land briefly when energy management demands recovery, transitioning to ground-based positioning during brief Razorwing gaps.

Coordinate with squad to maintain target focus while Titania provides aerial DPS support. Avoid positioning directly above revive casualties, as aerial positioning limits accessibility for coordinated revive windows.

## Strengths and weaknesses

Razorwing provides unlimited aerial mobility that grants immunity to 80% of ground-based enemy projectiles, effectively multiplying squad defensive capacity by removing one teammate from incoming fire damage. This defensive benefit enables squad to focus offensive resources rather than allocating positions for defensive coverage.

Dex Pixia homing projectiles track targets independently, enabling passive DPS even while managing active evasion and positioning. Combined with power strength scaling, each projectile deals scaling damage that exceeds most ground-weapon baseline output.

The primary weakness is aerial-positioning limitation: Titania's elevated positioning restricts ground-based squad coordination and makes revive accessibility difficult during critical moments. Additionally, Razorwing requires constant energy management to sustain flight, and brief downtime windows force aerial-to-ground transitions that reduce offensive capability.

## Alternatives

Replacing Blind Rage with Transient Fortitude increases power strength while reducing duration, improving Dex Pixia damage at the cost of more frequent Razorwing recasting. This variant suits missions with discrete enemy spawns rather than infinite-scaling content.

Substituting Augur Reach with Constitution provides additional duration extension without range penalties. This alternative improves Razorwing uptime and reduces recast frequency at the cost of reduced Dex Pixia range.

## Notes

Dex Pixia homing projectiles can trigger on invisible or cloaked enemies, enabling reliable damage even against stealth-based threats. Use this mechanic to address stealth-heavy enemy groups without requiring squad support.

Balefire augment's armor-stripping effect persists beyond crowd control cooldown, enabling sustained armor scaling damage after initial crowd control proc. Coordinate with squad to maximize this mechanic through high-fire-rate weapon usage.
