## Overview

The Felarx is an Incarnon weapon, a special class that begins in a standard firing mode and transforms into a powered Incarnon form when its internal gauge is filled by landing shots on enemies. The Incarnon evolutions for the Felarx can be configured to add Radiation damage to the Incarnon form's shots, which deal direct damage with a high critical chance profile and apply Radiation status that causes affected enemies to lose target priority on the player and begin attacking other enemies.

Radiation damage is specifically effective against Alloy Armor, the damage-resistance type covering Grineer Heavy Gunners, Bailiffs, and Corrupted Heavy Gunners. This damage type advantage in Steel Path scenarios where armored enemies dominate makes Radiation the preferred Incarnon element over alternatives. Radiation status effects on heavy enemies convert them into temporary allies, which both reduces incoming damage and adds a damage source to the encounter.

Blaze at rank 10 adds Heat damage to both fire modes. In base shotgun mode, Heat applies burn status procs that increase all subsequent damage taken by the burning target. In Incarnon mode, Heat combines with the Incarnon's Radiation base to produce a combined elemental damage profile. Vicious Spread at rank 5 adds additional flat damage bonus to the shotgun spread pattern, which benefits both standard and Incarnon modes.

## Loadout

Serration at rank 10 is the foundational damage multiplier for this primary configuration. All shotgun-type primaries benefit from Serration's 165% damage addition, which applies before Galvanized mods and arcane multipliers. Split Chamber at rank 5 adds 90% multishot, nearly doubling pellet count per shot in standard mode.

Point Strike at rank 5 adds 150% critical chance to the weapon's base. Combined with Arcane Avenger's 45% critical chance bonus on taking damage, total critical chance can exceed 200%, placing shots reliably in orange-crit territory. Vital Sense at rank 5 adds 120% critical damage, multiplying the already-elevated crit output.

Galvanized Aptitude at rank 10 provides stacking flat damage per unique status type on the target, rewarding the multi-elemental damage profile from Heat, Radiation, and any Toxin contribution from companion or squad Viral applications. Galvanized Chamber at rank 10 adds stacking multishot on kills, which increases pellet count per shot in standard mode and the number of Incarnon projectiles in the transformed form.

Vicious Spread at rank 5 is a shotgun-specific mod that adds damage at the cost of additional spread. In close-to-medium range engagements, the spread penalty is minimal relative to the damage gain. At long range, the spread creates effective miss potential; this build is intended for mid-range Steel Path tile set encounters rather than open-world long-range targets.

## Playstyle

Fire the Felarx in shotgun mode at enemy groups approaching from standard tile set engagement distances. Each pellet that lands on an enemy charges the Incarnon gauge. In dense groups, a single shot typically fills a substantial portion of the gauge. Activate Incarnon form when the gauge is full by pressing the reload button while the gauge is charged; the weapon transforms and fires the special Incarnon rounds.

During Incarnon form, aim at heavy enemies within the firing cone. Each Incarnon shot applies Radiation status and deals high damage. Maintain Incarnon form until the Incarnon magazine empties, then return to standard mode to refill the gauge. In encounters with continuous enemy waves, the gauge refills within three to five standard shots.

Radiation status confusion causes affected enemies to target nearby enemies rather than the player. When a Heavy Gunner with full Radiation confusion begins firing at allied Lancers, withdraw from direct line of sight and allow the confusion effect to resolve the group naturally. This dramatically reduces incoming damage during dense wave phases.

## Strengths and weaknesses

The Radiation damage advantage against Alloy Armor makes this build specifically effective against mid-to-high Steel Path Grineer content. Versus Corrupted enemies (who also carry Alloy Armor) in Void missions and Orokin Derelict nodes, the same advantage applies. The Radiation status confusion effect adds passive crowd control that supplements Adaptation or other defensive tools.

The primary limitation is the gauge-fill requirement before Incarnon form is accessible. In low-density encounters with few enemies at once, gauge filling takes longer, extending the gap between Incarnon form activations. Building a squad comp with crowd-gathering abilities (Khora's Strangledome, Nyx's Chaos) ensures consistent enemy presence to speed gauge charging.

## Alternatives

Replacing Vicious Spread with Hunter Munitions adds Slash procs on critical hits. Slash proc damage bypasses armor, which provides additional damage against Alloy-armored targets even when Radiation is not the active damage type. This variant suits players who prefer the bleed-stacking interaction for sustained single-target damage.

Replacing Blaze with Hammer Shot adds status chance and critical damage. Hammer Shot's combined bonus increases the chance of Radiation status procs on each Incarnon shot and multiplies crit damage, trading the Heat elemental application for a pure crit-status amplification.

## Notes

Felarx's Incarnon evolutions are selected through the Duviri Circuit or equivalent Incarnon upgrade system. The Radiation evolution selection in the evolution tree must be active for Radiation damage to appear in Incarnon mode; by default, the Incarnon form fires physical damage only. Check the weapon's evolution tree to confirm the Radiation selection is active before running missions with this build.

Galvanized Chamber's multishot stacks reset after 20 seconds without a kill. In missions with unpredictable enemy spawn spacing, maintain at least one kill per 18 seconds to prevent full stack decay during transition phases.
