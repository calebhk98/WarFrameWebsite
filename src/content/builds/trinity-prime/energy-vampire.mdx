## Overview

Trinity Prime's Energy Vampire build transforms the frame into an unlimited energy provider that sustains entire squad ability economies through aggressive target cycling. The configuration prioritizes power strength to maximize energy drain per cast and casting frequency to enable rapid multi-target cycling, ensuring continuous energy availability regardless of squad demand.

Energy Vampire's shield-stripping effect provides direct damage support while Viral status application enables armor-scaling synergy with Corrosive-stacked teammates. Link passive provides permanent squad-wide armor bonuses, increasing team survivability without requiring dedicated defensive abilities.

## Loadout

The configuration balances strength and duration to maximize energy generation while maintaining reasonable casting frequency. Primed Continuity at rank 10 extends Link duration to sustained levels, allowing active armor bonuses to persist throughout extended missions. Augur Reach and Stretch push Energy Vampire range to 50+ meters, enabling targeting of distant high-priority enemies without squad repositioning.

Streamline manages casting costs to approximately 40 energy per cast at rank 5, sustainable through aggressive Zenurik passive cycling and Arcane Energize interaction. Umbral Intensify and Umbral Vitality enable set bonuses while maintaining health pool above standard frames.

Power Donation aura grants flat power strength to squad, improving overall team ability scaling beyond Energy Vampire's immediate benefits. Blind Rage contributes final power strength boost, maximizing energy drain magnitude per cast.

## Playstyle

Initiate squad advancement by identifying high-threat enemies with substantial energy reserves and casting Energy Vampire in rotation. Cast on different targets every 4 seconds to maximize total energy output while distributing damage threat. Prioritize casting on faction leaders or specialist units that command substantial energy pools, maximizing per-cast extraction.

Position aggressively near squad to ensure Energy Vampire splash range covers all teammates, enabling passive Link armor distribution. Maintain constant casting rhythm rather than recasting on single targets, as target switching enables cooldown-based energy refund mechanics.

Coordinate with squad to identify low-energy combat phases and prepare preemptive Energy Vampire rotation sequences. In missions with extended enemy waves, time casting cycles to peak team energy demand moments, ensuring squad never enters energy starvation.

Use remaining ability slots for crowd control or survivability as needed. Blessing provides damage reduction and heal pulses to squad, supplementing energy support with defensive utility during high-damage moments.

## Strengths and weaknesses

Energy Vampire's unlimited energy generation breaks traditional energy economy constraints, allowing teammates to spam high-cost abilities indefinitely without restriction. Each cast drains enemy shields while applying Viral damage, providing dual utility that improves team damage output independent of Energy Vampire's primary support function.

Link's passive armor bonus scales with Trinity's survivability mods, creating multiplicative defensive stacking that becomes increasingly potent as mod investment increases. Combined with Arcane Grace healing, Trinity becomes one of the tankiest support frames available.

The primary weakness is Energy Vampire's inability to function on robot enemies lacking energy reserves. Corpus missions with high robotic unit density force Trinity into pure-support mode without energy generation, reducing overall squad capability. Additionally, shield stripping without armor reduction means endgame enemies scale health significantly faster than Trinity can support.

## Alternatives

Replacing Blind Rage with Transient Fortitude adds power strength while reducing duration, improving per-cast energy drain at the cost of Link uptime. This variant suits shorter-duration missions with discrete enemy spawns rather than infinite scaling content.

Substituting Augur Reach with Constitution provides additional duration extension without range penalties, improving Link persistence and Energy Vampire cooldown management. This alternative prioritizes ability uptime over targeting flexibility.

## Notes

Energy Vampire's shield strip applies before the drain, meaning maximum energy extraction requires fully-charged shields. Prioritize casting on enemies that have recently regenerated shields rather than already-drained targets.

Link's armor scaling enables Trinity to achieve 200+ armor value with modest investment, making the frame viable in Steel Path content despite lack of dedicated defensive abilities. Combine with damage-reduction arcanes for optimal survivability.
