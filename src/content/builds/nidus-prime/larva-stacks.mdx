## Overview

Nidus Prime's Larva Stacks build transforms the frame into a scaling-damage engine that accumulates mutation stacks throughout combat rotations, exponentially increasing all ability power strength as engagements progress. The configuration prioritizes power strength and duration to maximize mutation scaling while maintaining adequate Larva and Virulence uptime.

Mutation stacks provide unlimited damage scaling independent of mod investment, making this build increasingly powerful throughout extended missions. Combined with Larva crowd control and Virulence infection spread, Nidus becomes a self-sufficiently scaling damage dealer that scales faster than enemy health growth in most content.

## Loadout

The configuration balances power strength and duration to maximize mutation scaling while maintaining ability uptime. Primed Continuity at rank 10 extends Larva duration to approximately 18 seconds, enabling aggressive crowd control across entire tile populations. Stretch and Augur Reach together push Larva range to 60+ meters, covering entire rooms with single casts.

Streamline manages energy costs to sustainable levels. Umbral Intensify and Umbral Vitality enable set bonuses while maintaining health pool above standard frames. Power strength scaling stacks multiplicatively with mutation bonuses, creating exponential damage increases beyond typical mod-based scaling.

Transient Fortitude adds additional power strength while reducing duration, offset by Primed Continuity's overcompensation for Larva uptime. Blind Rage contributes final power strength boost, maximizing mutation interaction effectiveness.

## Playstyle

Initiate combat by casting Larva on central enemy clusters to establish crowd control and trigger initial mutation stack accumulation. Cast Virulence immediately to spread infection across grouped enemies while consuming mutation stacks for scaling damage bursts. Allow infection to persist through subsequent Larva casts, enabling continuous stack rebuilding.

Maintain Larva recast intervals of approximately 15 seconds to ensure continuous crowd control coverage. Prioritize Larva casting over Virulence when mutation stacks remain low, as rapid stack accumulation enables exponential scaling benefits. As stacks approach 10+, shift to balanced Larva and Virulence cycling to maintain optimal stack levels.

Coordinate with squad to maximize mutation stack generation by focusing fire on Larva-grouped enemies. Each enemy kill grants mutation stacks, accelerating power strength scaling and enabling rapid progression to late-game damage outputs.

Use Parasitic Link defensively during high-threat scenarios where crowd control alone cannot sustain squad safety. Link provides health drain and brief crowd control, supplementing Larva and Virulence with additional defensive utility.

## Strengths and weaknesses

Mutation stacks scale power strength infinitely, enabling exponential ability damage increases that exceed traditional mod-based scaling. At 20+ stacks, Nidus's ability damage exceeds most other frames' output regardless of mod investment, creating unique endgame scaling potential.

Larva crowd control immobilizes entire enemy populations simultaneously, preventing all enemy actions including ability casting and projectile fire. Combined with mutation scaling, each Larva cast becomes progressively more dangerous to enemies while providing exponential defensive stacking.

The primary weakness is stack-dependency: mutation stacks require consistent enemy kills to sustain scaling, and brief downtime windows cause exponential stack loss. In missions with sparse enemies or long intermissions, Nidus loses scaling advantage and reverts to standard power-strength performance levels.

## Alternatives

Replacing Blind Rage with Transient Fortitude increases power strength while reducing duration further. This variant prioritizes maximum mutation scaling over Larva uptime, suitable for content with high enemy spawn density.

Substituting Transient Fortitude with Constitution provides additional duration extension without power scaling penalties. This alternative improves Larva crowd control persistence at the cost of reduced mutation scaling efficiency.

## Notes

Mutation stack consumption through Virulence enables controlled stack management rather than passive stack decay. Use Virulence casting to maintain optimal stack levels between 5-15 for balanced damage scaling and crowd control coverage.

Parasitic Link's health drain scales with mutation stacks, creating multiplicative defensive benefits as stacks accumulate. Use Link defensively during high-stack scenarios to leverage scaling health sustain.
