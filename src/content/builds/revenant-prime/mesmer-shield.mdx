## Overview

Revenant Prime's Mesmer Shield build exploits the synergy between Mesmer Skin and Enthrall to create a self-sustaining invulnerability loop. Each Mesmer Skin charge absorbs one instance of damage regardless of damage type or magnitude, and killing a thrall replenishes one charge. By maintaining active thralls through Enthrall's chain-spread mechanic, Revenant Prime can sustain near-continuous invulnerability against even the most extreme enemy damage scaling in Steel Path.

The Reave ability provides a secondary defensive mechanism and mobility tool. Dashing through enemies and thralls restores a percentage of max shields and health per target passed through, providing emergency recovery during brief windows when Mesmer charges are depleted.

## Loadout

Power strength and duration are the primary optimization targets. Blind Rage at rank 10 maximizes the initial Mesmer Skin charge count (which scales with power strength) and increases Enthrall's range and effectiveness. Primed Continuity and Fleeting Expertise together produce a net efficiency and duration result that sustains abilities across extended missions.

Enthrall at rank 5 is the augment for the first ability, converting the basic Enthrall cast into a longer-range chain-propagation tool that thrall deaths spread to nearby unenthralled enemies. This chain mechanic is essential for maintaining a constant population of nearby thralls without requiring constant manual Enthrall casts.

Reave at rank 5 provides the defensive recovery component. At high power strength, Reave's shield and health restoration per enemy passed through scales significantly, making it a reliable emergency recovery tool between Mesmer Skin charge regeneration cycles.

## Playstyle

Open each encounter by casting Enthrall on two or three nearby enemies. Allow the thralls to die naturally or kill them with weapons to regenerate Mesmer Skin charges. Dead thralls propagate Enthrall to the next nearest enemy, creating a self-replenishing thrall pool as long as enemy supply continues.

Maintain Mesmer Skin above 3 charges at all times. When charge count drops due to burst enemy focus, cast Reave through the nearest thrall cluster to restore shields and health. The temporary shield and health recovery from Reave buys time for thrall kills to replenish Mesmer charges.

Danse Macabre provides AoE damage for clearing rooms when Enthrall's thrall population is dense. Casting Danse while thralls surround Revenant directs the beam across multiple targets, accelerating their deaths and triggering Enthrall chain-propagation simultaneously.

## Strengths and weaknesses

Mesmer Skin's damage immunity is type-agnostic -- it does not matter whether the incoming damage is physical, elemental, or void-based. Each charge absorbs one hit. This universality makes Revenant Prime one of the most reliable survivability frames against content types that deal unusual damage types, such as Sentient attacks that apply resistance-stacking procs.

The dependency on thrall kills is the build's primary vulnerability. In missions with low enemy density, or in boss encounters where no standard enemies are present to Enthrall, charge regeneration slows dramatically. Carry an energy pad for boss fight phases where Mesmer Skin must be sustained without thrall support.

Revenant Prime's offensive output during Danse Macabre is limited by the ability's activation window and the need to remain stationary. In mobile missions, this limits damage contribution. For players who want active combat, pair with a high-status secondary weapon to maintain kills during Danse downtime.

## Alternatives

Replacing Enthrall with Reave Augment increases dash speed and range, converting Reave into a more aggressive offensive tool. This variant sacrifices the self-sustaining thrall loop for better damage output and mobility, and is appropriate for speedrun configurations rather than endurance builds.

## Notes

Sentient enemies in Eidolon hunts, Veil Proxima Railjack missions, and Murex-related content are immune to Mesmer Skin's charge-absorb mechanic when their resistance adaptation activates. Sentient damage is partially bypassed after adaptation stacks. In Sentient-heavy missions, cycle Reave more frequently and maintain a higher base charge count as buffer.

Enthrall's chain-spread has a maximum range that scales with ability range mods. With Corrosive Projection in the aura slot and Stretch in a mod slot, chain range extends to cover most standard tileset rooms. Wider chains reduce the manual re-cast frequency needed to maintain thrall population density.
