## Overview

Revenant Prime's Mesmer Skin generates a number of charges that each absorb one instance of incoming damage completely, regardless of the damage amount. A single charge absorbs a Bombard rocket, a Nox headshot, or a Corrupted Butcher melee strike identically -- none of them reduce Revenant's health or shields. At high enough duration and charge count, Mesmer Skin effectively makes Revenant Prime unkillable through sustained fire because no individual hit depletes more than one charge.

The Mesmer Shield augment (listed in the mods collection under `mesmer-shield`) adds three additional charges to Mesmer Skin per cast. At this build's power strength values, base Mesmer Skin generates seven charges per cast; Mesmer Shield adds three, bringing the total to ten. With Primed Continuity and Constitution together pushing duration above 200%, those ten charges persist for over 25 seconds per cast, which is sufficient uptime for continuous combat without exhausting charges in standard Steel Path scenarios.

Enthrall converts enemies into thralls that fight for Revenant and cannot be damaged or killed by non-Revenant sources until dismissed. Thralls provide both crowd control and a small energy return on death through Revenant's passive interactions. In extended survival missions, a cluster of Enthralled Heavy Gunners clears room debris and generates passive energy returns as Revenant dismisses them periodically.

## Loadout

Primed Continuity at rank 10 and Constitution at rank 10 together push duration above 200% of base. At this duration, Mesmer Skin lasts approximately 25 to 30 seconds per cast before charges would naturally expire. In practice, charges deplete before the timer runs out in dense Steel Path encounters, so recasting on charge depletion rather than timer expiry is the standard practice.

Umbral Intensify and Umbral Vitality provide the Umbral set two-piece bonus. While Revenant Prime's survivability depends more on Mesmer Skin's charge absorption than raw health, Umbral Vitality raises his effective health pool for the periods between Mesmer Skin casts when no charges are active. Arcane Grace's passive health regeneration on critical hit complements this during weapon-active phases.

Streamline and Fleeting Expertise together reduce ability energy costs significantly. Mesmer Skin's base energy cost is 50; at full efficiency from both mods, this drops to approximately 16 to 17 energy per cast. Enthrall and Danse Macabre have separate higher base costs that are similarly reduced. The combined efficiency allows rapid recasting of Mesmer Skin the moment charges deplete.

Adaptation at rank 10 provides a secondary defensive layer for the brief moments when Mesmer Skin has just been depleted and the next cast is mid-animation. Grineer damage types are consistent enough in a given encounter to ramp Adaptation to high resistance values quickly, covering the Mesmer Skin recast window.

Arcane Energize at rank 5 ensures a 60% chance per energy orb pickup to receive 150 bonus energy. Combined with Fleeting Expertise's cost reduction, this makes Revenant Prime's energy management largely self-sufficient in high-enemy-density Steel Path survival missions.

## Playstyle

Cast Mesmer Skin at the start of each engagement and maintain it continuously. Monitor the charge count displayed on the ability HUD -- when charges reach two or fewer, immediately recast rather than waiting for full depletion. The recast animation for Mesmer Skin is short and can be performed while moving; do not stop to cast.

Enthrall one or two Heavy Gunners or Bombards per wave to establish a defensive perimeter of thralls that draw additional enemy aggression. Thralled heavies fight on Revenant's behalf and cannot be collateral killed by Danse Macabre, which makes them safe to maintain during clearing rotations. Dismiss Thralls only when their health is critically low or when the wave has ended.

Danse Macabre should be used for rapid group clearing when multiple enemies cluster within the limited radius. The channel drains energy per second but provides brief damage immunity during the animation; use it opportunistically when Mesmer Skin charges are full to avoid overlapping defensive layers unnecessarily. Exit Danse Macabre as soon as the immediate threat is resolved to conserve energy for Mesmer Skin recasts.

## Strengths and weaknesses

The ability to absorb any single hit regardless of magnitude is Revenant Prime's defining defensive trait. Unlike health-based tankiness that scales poorly against increasing enemy levels, Mesmer Skin's charge absorption remains equally effective against level 200 Bombards as against level 30 Lancers. This makes Revenant Prime one of the most reliable frames in endurance runs that reach the point where enemy damage would one-shot any fixed-health frame.

The primary weakness is energy dependence. Maintaining Mesmer Skin continuously requires a sustained energy supply. In energy-denial environments (Nullifier fields, Parasitic Eximus auras), Mesmer Skin cannot be recast inside the field. Nullifier bubbles do not remove existing charges, but entering the field prevents recasting until outside, creating a brief window of vulnerability. Avoid pursuing Nullifiers without first clearing their bubble from range.

## Alternatives

Replacing Adaptation with Quick Thinking provides a safety net independent of incoming damage type. Quick Thinking prevents death from any single hit that would deplete health to zero by draining energy proportionally. With Primed Flow's large energy pool (not included in this build but viable as a substitution), Quick Thinking provides multiple lives worth of protection at the cost of lower total efficiency.

Swapping Corrosive Projection for Growing Power in the aura slot suits squads that do not need armor reduction from the aura, typically Corpus-focused missions where Polarize or Shield Disruption already handles shields.

## Notes

Mesmer Skin charges absorb damage from any source including environmental hazards, Eidolon sweeps, and Synapse beam hits. However, they do not prevent status effects from applying. A Toxin proc that bypasses shields applies its damage-over-time even through Mesmer Skin, because the status proc itself is not a single hit but a repeated damage-per-second effect. Adaptation handles Toxin proc residual damage effectively once Revenant Prime has been hit a few times.

Danse Macabre's damage scales with incoming damage while the ability is active, similar to Gara's Splinter Storm. Enemies that hit Revenant during Danse Macabre deal reflected damage in an area. This interaction means high-damage enemies hitting Revenant during Danse Macabre contribute to the ability's output.
