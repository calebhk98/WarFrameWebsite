## Overview

Inaros Prime holds the highest base health pool in the game, reaching 5945 base health at max rank without mods. With Umbral Vitality at rank 10 and the Umbral set bonus, total health exceeds 10,000. This health pool functions as the sole defensive layer -- Inaros has no shields, relying entirely on health, armor, and lifesteal mechanics for sustained survivability.

Negation Swarm, the Scarab Swarm augment, negates all incoming status effects while the swarm is active. This status immunity is the defining advantage over other high-health frames: Radiation, Cold, Toxin, and Magnetic procs that bypass armor or reduce offensive capability cannot affect Inaros while Negation Swarm is maintained. In Steel Path and Arbitrations where status application is near-constant, this immunity converts a persistent vulnerability into a non-issue.

## Loadout

The full Umbral set (Vitality, Fiber, Intensify) delivers the set bonus simultaneously across health, armor, and power strength. Umbral Fiber at rank 10 provides the second highest single-mod armor increase available, and the set bonus adds further armor multipliers that scale Scarab Swarm's armor bonus and Desiccation's damage higher.

Adaptation at rank 10 provides up to 90% damage reduction against the most recently received damage type. Combined with Inaros Prime's high armor from Umbral Fiber and Steel Charge's melee bonus, the effective health pool against common damage types substantially exceeds the nominal 10,000+ health figure.

Scarab Swarm at rank 5 is the base ability -- Negation Swarm is its augment mod equipped in a separate slot. Both must be equipped: Scarab Swarm activates the swarm charge system, and Negation Swarm modifies the ability's behavior to also block status effects. Maintaining Scarab Swarm charge above 50% is sufficient to sustain Negation Swarm's status immunity.

## Playstyle

Build Scarab Swarm charges before entering combat by holding the ability key while targeting enemies at medium range. The charges accumulate based on damage dealt, and the charge bar fills steadily in any encounter with a moderate enemy count. Releasing the ability detonates the scarabs on targets, applying corrosive damage and healing Inaros.

Maintain Scarab charges above the midpoint throughout missions. Status immunity from Negation Swarm is active as long as any charges remain -- falling to zero removes the immunity. Replenish charges reactively by briefly holding Scarab Swarm and targeting nearby enemies.

Desiccation blinds enemies in a cone and restores health on kills from blinded targets. Use it as both an offensive opener when moving into a new room and as emergency health recovery when Inaros's health drops below 50%. Devour pins a single enemy and regenerates health passively; useful for pinning an Eximus unit or heavy to ensure a sustained healing source during extended fights.

Finisher attacks against blinded (Desiccation) enemies deal significantly amplified damage and generate bonus health restoration. When engaging priority targets, open with Desiccation and immediately perform a ground finisher for burst damage and healing simultaneously.

## Strengths and weaknesses

Inaros Prime's health-based defense is inherently simpler to manage than ability-cast defensive builds. There is no shield to recharge, no Iron Skin to re-cast, no charge count to monitor -- the health pool is a passive buffer that only requires active management when it drops below a comfortable threshold. This simplicity makes Inaros Prime accessible and reliable in solo content where there are no squad members to provide support.

The primary limitation is the absence of shields. Magnetic damage directly reduces Inaros Prime's health pool without any shielding intermediary. Against Corpus units with magnetic-oriented weapons and in missions with magnetic hazard zones, Inaros Prime takes more effective damage than shielded tanks. Adaptation builds a DR stack against magnetic quickly, but the initial exposure is a consistent vulnerability.

Low mobility relative to other endgame frames reduces Inaros Prime's effectiveness in objective-based missions requiring fast traversal (capture, spy, rescue). Bullet jumping and parkour remain available, but the frame lacks speed buffs or mobility abilities.

## Alternatives

Adding Roar via Helminth subsume over Devour provides a squad damage multiplier, trading the single-target healing lock of Devour for group offensive support. This is appropriate when Inaros Prime is filling a squad support role in addition to tanking.

## Notes

Inaros Prime's passive ability -- Sands of Inaros -- reduces bleed-out time to zero, allowing Inaros to revive himself by killing enemies while downed. This passive provides a safety net that no other frame in the game offers, making Inaros Prime extremely forgiving for solo endurance attempts.

The self-revive mechanic requires kills during the bleed-out window. Keep a weapon with reliable kill potential equipped (not a slow-firing sniper) to ensure the revive bar fills before the bleed-out timer expires.
