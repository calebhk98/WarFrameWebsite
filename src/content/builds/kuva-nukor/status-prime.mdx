## Overview

The Kuva Nukor is a secondary beam weapon with a unique mechanic: its beam chains between enemies within a radius of the initial target, applying status effects to all chained targets simultaneously. This behavior makes it one of the most effective status application tools in the secondary slot, capable of applying several different status types per second to multiple enemies in a single continuous trigger hold.

Galvanized Shot is the primary damage mod in this configuration. Galvanized Shot provides a flat damage bonus that increases with each unique status type currently active on the target. Since the Kuva Nukor applies Heat, Toxin, Cold, Electric, and Magnetic status types through its base damage distribution and elemental mods, a target hit for two or three seconds typically has four or more active status types, pushing Galvanized Shot's bonus to a high multiplier. This makes Galvanized Shot ideal for the Kuva Nukor because the weapon generates the status procs that Galvanized Shot rewards.

Secondary Merciless at rank 5 adds a stacking damage multiplier that increases by a percentage per kill and maintains stacks over a 20-second window. In enemy-dense Steel Path missions, kills occur frequently enough to keep Merciless stacks at or near maximum throughout a rotation. The stacking bonus compounds with Galvanized Shot, producing peak damage output during sustained engagements.

## Loadout

Hornet Strike at rank 10 provides the largest base damage increase available in the secondary general damage mod category. While Galvanized Shot is the scaling damage source at high status proc counts, Hornet Strike's base multiplier applies to the initial per-shot damage before Galvanized Shot scaling takes effect. This ensures minimum damage is not negligible on the first hit against new targets.

Barrel Diffusion at rank 5 and Lethal Torrent at rank 5 together add multishot and fire rate to the Kuva Nukor. Multishot increases the number of beams fired per trigger pull, which increases the total number of status procs applied per second proportionally. Each additional beam from Barrel Diffusion rolls for a status proc independently, raising effective status chance per second significantly above the base value.

Primed Pistol Gambit at rank 10 and Primed Target Cracker at rank 10 add critical chance and critical damage. The Kuva Nukor has a moderate base critical chance; Primed Pistol Gambit pushes this high enough to trigger orange crits regularly when combined with the beam's fire rate. Primed Target Cracker multiplies the damage of those crits.

Primed Heated Charge at rank 10 adds Heat elemental damage. Heat combines with Toxin from the Kuva Nukor's base Toxin damage component (from the Kuva bonus element; the element varies by Lich progenitor, with Toxin being a common and valuable option) to form Gas, which deals area damage and applies Gas status procs to nearby enemies on contact. Alternatively, Heat alone applies Heat procs that increase damage taken by the target, adding to Galvanized Shot's stack.

Contagion Cloud at rank 5 converts some status proc behavior to increase Toxin proc damage. At rank 5, enemies suffering Toxin status deal increased area Toxin damage on death, which chains between enemies in dense groups.

## Playstyle

Begin each engagement by holding the trigger on the largest visible enemy group to initiate beam chaining. Hold for two to three seconds on the initial target before sweeping to the next cluster; this establishes Galvanized Shot stacks on the first group. Galvanized Shot's bonus persists briefly after the beam moves off target, allowing rapid sweeping between clusters without losing full stack value.

Maintain Secondary Merciless stacks by ensuring kills occur within the 20-second refresh window. In high-density missions, this requires no active management. In lower-density scenarios such as interception or Sortie spy, confirm a kill chain every 15 to 18 seconds to avoid stack decay.

Arcane Velocity at rank 5 activates on Electric damage proc, increasing fire rate by 60% for 12 seconds. The Kuva Nukor applies Electric procs when the beam hits enemies due to its elemental damage interaction. Velocity uptime is therefore tied to beam duration -- continuous beam contact with enemies maintains Velocity indefinitely in dense encounters.

## Strengths and weaknesses

The Kuva Nukor's status-per-second output via beam chaining is among the highest of any secondary weapon. In a squad where the primary and melee weapons provide higher raw damage, the Kuva Nukor serves as a status primer -- its role is to maximize Galvanized Shot stacks and maintain viral proc reduction on enemy health pools for the squad's other weapons. Viral status applied by Kuva Nukor chains reduces effective enemy health by 325% at maximum stacks, more than doubling the damage all other sources deal to the same target.

The primary weakness in solo play is that the weapon's potential is fully realized only against targets that survive the initial beam chain long enough to accumulate six or more Galvanized Shot stacks. Against low-health Steel Path enemies that die in one to two seconds, Galvanized Shot's contribution is limited. The build is most effective against Elite, Heavy, and Boss tier enemies with substantial health pools.

## Alternatives

Replacing Contagion Cloud with Augur Seeker adds a Slash status proc chance, which applies a bleed damage-over-time that ignores armor. Slash procs on armored targets scale better than direct damage since the bleed bypasses the armor damage reduction formula entirely.

Replacing Secondary Merciless with Secondary Deadhead provides a headshot-focused stacking bonus instead of a kill-based one. Deadhead suits precision-aimed secondary use against single large targets; Merciless suits rapid-fire kill-chain scenarios. The Kuva Nukor's beam makes headshots impractical, so Merciless is preferred for this specific weapon.

## Notes

The Kuva Nukor's Kuva element adds a secondary elemental damage type to the weapon's base damage pool. The element type depends on the Kuva Lich progenitor Warframe. Toxin Kuva Nukor (from a Toxin-progenitor Lich) is the most commonly recommended variant because Toxin combines with Heat to form Gas, enabling area-damage procs through Contagion Cloud.
