## Overview

Wisp Prime's Reservoirs ability places buff motes that grant fire rate increases through the Haste Mote and health regeneration through the Vitality Mote. In Eidolon hunts, fire rate directly accelerates Eidolon synovia removal during the night window, and every percentage of fire rate increase translates to proportionally faster limb breaks on Teralyst, Gantulyst, and Hydrolyst targets. The Haste Mote's fire rate bonus applies to all squad members who step through the Reservoir, making Wisp Prime the premier support frame for damage-dealer frames running Rubico Prime or Ferrox.

Duration is the primary stat for this build. High duration means a single Reservoir placement at the Eidolon spawn point covers all three Tridolon phases without recasting, since each phase transition moves the combat to a new lakeside position where the same Reservoirs may still be in range. Primed Continuity, Constitution, and Augur Message together push total duration above 200%, extending individual Reservoir uptime past 40 seconds per placement.

Natural Talent reduces ability animation length, which matters more in Eidolon hunts than in most game modes. Wisp's casting animation for Reservoirs has a notably long windup; Natural Talent cuts this to approximately 60% of its base duration, allowing faster placement during the critical seconds immediately after Eidolon spawn when the squad must reach position before the night window begins draining.

## Loadout

Umbral Intensify at rank 10 provides power strength to the build, but this configuration does not sacrifice duration for maximum strength -- Reservoir buffs scale moderately with strength, and the Haste Mote's fire rate bonus reaches its practical value at approximately 130% to 150% strength. The Umbral set one-piece bonus (from Intensify alone, without Vitality) still provides a meaningful strength increase over standard Intensify without the survivability cost of a pure strength configuration.

Stretch at rank 5 expands the Reservoir placement radius, which determines how far from the mote a player can stand and still receive the buff. A larger radius is directly useful in situations where the Eidolon forces the squad to spread out from the initial placement position. At rank 5, Stretch combined with Power Drift keeps the radius well above base.

Rolling Guard at rank 5 provides an active defensive tool with an 8-second invulnerability window on roll, clearing all status effects. Wisp Prime's passive invisibility breaks when she fires or takes damage, meaning Eidolon sweep damage occasionally hits her when repositioning. Rolling Guard prevents status effects from the sweep's proc from persisting into the next phase.

Arcane Grace at rank 5 adds passive 9% health regeneration on critical hit, complementing Vitality Mote's health regen for Wisp's own sustain. Arcane Energize keeps energy sufficient for continuous Reservoir recasting and occasional Sol Gate use.

## Playstyle

Arrive at the Eidolon lake before nightfall and place both Reservoirs (Haste Mote plus Vitality Mote) at the designated squad camping position. The Shock Mote (third Reservoir type) is optional; this build does not stack all three motes because the energy cost of three simultaneous Reservoir placements exceeds available reserves for continuous maintenance across a Tridolon run.

During Eidolon limb phases, maintain Reservoirs at the current engagement position. If the squad moves for lure charging, place fresh Reservoirs at the new position immediately rather than letting the existing buffs expire. Monitor Reservoir duration timers and recast with a 10-second buffer before expiry to avoid gaps during phase transitions.

Between Eidolon phases, position Wisp at a central point that allows all squadmates to pass through Reservoir radius after the capture animation. Capturing an Eidolon moves the squad rapidly; Wisp should anticipate the destination position and pre-place Reservoirs before the squad arrives.

## Strengths and weaknesses

Haste Mote's fire rate benefit translates directly to kill speed on Eidolon synovias because synovia health pools are finite and the damage window each night limits total shots available. A squad with Wisp's Haste Mote removes synovias noticeably faster than an equivalent squad without it, particularly on Gantulyst's six limb targets and Hydrolyst's twelve.

The weakness is that Wisp's support role produces no direct damage contribution. If the squad's damage dealer is under-geared or disrupted, Wisp cannot compensate by dealing Eidolon damage herself. Sol Gate is available as a backup but its damage does not scale to Eidolon health thresholds at Wisp's strength values in this configuration.

## Alternatives

Replacing Rolling Guard with Primed Flow extends the energy pool for squads that rely on Wisp for continuous Reservoir maintenance across longer Tridolon sessions. This trades active survivability for passive energy reserve, appropriate when a Trinity or Harrow in the squad covers energy needs for the damage dealer separately.

Dropping Augur Message for Augur Reach expands Reservoir radius further, useful in open-field encounters on the Plains of Eidolon where squad separation is wider. This reduces duration slightly but increases the coverage zone per mote.

## Notes

Wisp Prime's passive grants invisibility while airborne. This passive does not protect against Eidolon sweep attacks that target all ground-level players; it only prevents standard enemy targeting during traversal. Staying mobile between Eidolon phase transitions takes advantage of this passive defensively.

Reservoir buffs persist on squad members who move out of the mote's radius after initially receiving the buff. The buff is applied on entry and maintained until the mote expires or the player dies. Lure carriers can move to lure charge positions while retaining the Haste Mote fire rate bonus on their weapons.
