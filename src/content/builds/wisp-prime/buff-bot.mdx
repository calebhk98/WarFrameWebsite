## Overview

Wisp Prime's Buff Bot configuration prioritizes squad utility through overlapping mote deployment, creating a mobile support nexus that coordinates offensive and defensive power across all teammates. The build maximizes mote duration through Primed Continuity and ability strength through Umbral mods, ensuring stable buff application regardless of squad composition or mission tier.

Breach Surge augment converts the fourth ability into offensive crowd control without sacrificing Wisp's primary support function, enabling solo survival when separated from squad or handling high-threat enemies. This build excels in coordinated team content where multiple players benefit from overlapping damage, haste, and healing buffs simultaneously.

## Loadout

The configuration balances power strength and ability duration to maintain all four mote types within range simultaneously. Umbral Intensify and Umbral Vitality enable the Umbral set bonus, granting 25% additional strength while boosting Wisp's health pool above standard frames. Primed Continuity at rank 10 extends mote lifespan to approximately 45 seconds, reducing recast frequency and enabling aggressive positioning.

Augur Reach pushes ability radius to 55+ meters, ensuring that all squad members remain within buff coverage regardless of tile size or tactical spacing. Streamline manages energy costs to approximately 15 energy per mote cast, sustainable through Zenurik passive and Arcane Energize interaction. Transient Fortitude contributes 30% power strength while reducing duration by 27.5%, offset by Primed Continuity's overcompensation.

Breach Surge augment consumes the fourth ability slot, converting Wil-O-Wisp into shield-damage pulses that trigger Breach Surge's signature effect. Constitution provides survivability without power scaling, allowing other modslots to enhance ability effectiveness.

## Playstyle

Initiate squad advancement by deploying all four mote types in high-traffic chokepoints where all teammates maintain proximity. Damage mote grants flat weapon damage bonus, haste mote accelerates attack and movement speed, vitality mote provides passive health regeneration, and unaimed fire mote grants automatic weapon projectile homing to nearby weapons. Allow motes to expire naturally before recasting, typically 35-45 seconds after deployment.

Reposition when advancing through tile changes by collecting existing motes and redeploying in new central locations. Use Wil-O-Wisp with Breach Surge to trigger shield-stripping pulses on high-threat enemies approaching squad positions. Suspend high-priority targets with the fourth ability during mote expiration windows, extending survival duration until mote replacement.

Coordinate with squad to avoid area-of-effect ability spam that destroys motes prematurely. Communicate positioning requirements and encourage teammates to maintain central squad cohesion rather than splitting into isolated flanking positions that leave mote coverage gaps.

## Strengths and weaknesses

Mote buffs provide flat bonuses that scale infinitely with squadmate weapon investment, making this configuration increasingly valuable as team members acquire better weaponry. Damage mote applies bonus damage to all incoming fire regardless of weapon type or elemental affinity, benefiting both primary and secondary weapons simultaneously.

Breach Surge enables offensive utility that allows Wisp Prime to handle solo threats without requiring squad support, distinguishing this build from pure-support variants that force dependency on teammates for offense. The crowd control effect triggers automatically on kill, providing passive threat mitigation.

The primary weakness is mote vulnerability to area-of-effect damage and team ability spam. Mesa's Peacemaker, Saryn's Miasma, and other radial-damage abilities destroy all nearby motes instantly, forcing Wisp to reposition and redeploy continuously in missions with AOE-heavy squad compositions. In survival missions with sparse friendly fire, this limitation manifests as reduced uptime and lower overall buff availability.

## Alternatives

Replacing Transient Fortitude with Blind Rage increases power strength by 40% at the cost of additional efficiency reduction, suitable for missions where energy economy is less critical due to squad-provided energy sources. This variant maximizes mote buff magnitude but increases skill ceiling for energy management.

Substituting Power Donation with Corrosive Projection trades squad power strength for enemy armor stripping, improving overall team damage output in armored-enemy content. This alternative suits Steel Path disruption missions where armor scaling dominates difficulty curves.

## Notes

Motes persist briefly after squad wipe, providing respawn buffs during revive windows. Position final mote deployments before anticipated squad casualties to ensure smooth revive transitions.

Wil-O-Wisp's homing projectiles with Breach Surge trigger area-of-effect pulses, potentially destroying other motes within pulse radius. Delay Breach Surge usage until mote lifespan naturally expires to avoid accidental buff removal.
