## Overview

The Nataruk is a bow-type primary weapon with a precision mechanic: arrows fired at full charge with the reticle at the center position (the moment when arrow oscillation centers precisely) deal a "perfect shot" bonus that substantially multiplies the arrow's base damage. This bonus rewards patient, deliberate aiming and makes the Nataruk one of the highest single-shot damage potential weapons in the primary slot when fired correctly.

The Nataruk was released as part of The New War content update and is obtained through quest completion. It has no Mastery Rank requirement and is accessible to players who have completed the prerequisite quest chain. Despite the bow-type classification, Nataruk arrows pierce through enemies when Punch-through from Primed Shred or similar mods is applied, allowing a single perfect-shot arrow to eliminate multiple enemies in a line.

This configuration focuses on maximizing per-shot damage through critical chance, critical damage, and the Galvanized mods that scale with kills and status procs. Primed Cryo Rounds adds Cold elemental damage, which combines with any Toxin-applying squad companion or secondary to form Viral, reducing enemy health pools and amplifying subsequent arrows. Primary Deadhead at rank 5 provides a stacking damage bonus per headshot kill, rewarding the precision aim that Nataruk's perfect shot mechanic demands.

## Loadout

Serration at rank 10 provides the primary damage multiplier foundation. Split Chamber at rank 5 adds 90% multishot, generating an additional arrow per shot approximately 90% of the time. For bow weapons, multishot adds a second projectile that follows a slightly different trajectory than the primary arrow; both arrows benefit from the perfect-shot bonus if the charge timing was correct.

Point Strike at rank 5 adds 150% critical chance. The Nataruk has a high base critical chance that makes Point Strike's contribution particularly effective -- even a small additional percentage on an already high base pushes the critical chance well above the 100% threshold for guaranteed crits. Vital Sense at rank 5 adds 120% critical damage, multiplying the value of those guaranteed crits substantially.

Galvanized Aptitude at rank 10 provides status-proc-dependent flat damage scaling. Primed Cryo Rounds adds Cold status chance with each hit, and Arcane Avenger procs Electric proc interactions indirectly via squad status applications; the exact stack count varies by encounter and squad composition. Galvanized Aptitude's contribution is most reliable when the player carries a status-primer secondary to guarantee status type coverage.

Galvanized Chamber at rank 10 adds multishot stacks on kills. Each headshot kill from Primary Deadhead also simultaneously triggers a Galvanized Chamber stack increment, creating a double reward for accurate kill shots. At high Galvanized Chamber stacks (6+), a second full arrow accompanies every shot, effectively doubling output during extended kill streaks.

Primed Shred at rank 10 adds fire rate and punch-through. Fire rate reduces charge draw time, which is critical for the Nataruk because the perfect shot window occurs at the precise moment the full-charge animation completes. Lower draw time means less waiting between shots and a faster cadence of perfect opportunities. Punch-through allows arrows to pass through the first enemy hit and continue into subsequent enemies behind, extending the single-arrow impact across multiple targets in linear formation.

Primary Deadhead at rank 5 provides a stacking headshot damage bonus that accumulates per headshot kill and does not decay between kills as long as kills occur within the stack refresh window. For a precision bow that encourages aiming at enemy heads for the critical hit multiplier, Deadhead's stacking synergy is natural. At maximum stacks, the damage bonus from Deadhead is substantial enough to close the gap against high-Steel-Path enemy health thresholds.

## Playstyle

Position at medium range (15 to 25 meters from enemy clusters) to allow full-charge arrow draws without immediate pressure. Track the crosshair oscillation as the arrow charges and release when the reticle is centered. The visual and audio cue for the perfect shot window is distinct -- the crosshair briefly stabilizes and the bow sound changes pitch. Releasing precisely at this moment applies the perfect-shot multiplier.

Aim for enemy heads on each shot to build Primary Deadhead stacks and trigger bonus crit damage from headshot mechanics. In dense groups, prioritize heavy enemy heads to eliminate the highest-threat targets before lighter enemies can be cleared by splash damage from nearby miss shots or companion activity.

Maintain Galvanized Chamber and Primary Deadhead stacks between waves by landing at least one kill per refresh window. In defense missions with fixed wave cadence, time the final kill of each wave to occur within the first five seconds of the subsequent wave beginning, ensuring stack continuity.

## Strengths and weaknesses

The perfect-shot damage multiplier creates a high skill ceiling where accurate players deal dramatically more damage than inaccurate ones. Against single high-health targets like Archons, Eidolon synovias, or Sortie assassination bosses, a sustained sequence of perfect-shot crits can out-damage many auto-fire primaries with higher raw DPS statistics because the per-shot damage scales into values that exceed most weapons' sustained output.

The primary weakness is close-range impracticality. Full charge draw takes time, and at less than 8 meters, enemies close to melee range faster than a charged arrow can be released. Switching to secondary weapon for close encounters and reserving the Nataruk for medium-to-long range is the recommended approach.

## Alternatives

Replacing Galvanized Aptitude with Hunter Munitions adds Slash proc generation on critical hits. At the Nataruk's high critical chance, Hunter Munitions triggers on almost every arrow, stacking Slash bleed procs that bypass Grineer armor. This variant sacrifices the status-scaling damage bonus for guaranteed armor-bypassing bleed, which suits encounters against heavy Grineer armor.

Replacing Primary Deadhead with Primary Merciless provides a kill-stacking bonus rather than a headshot-stacking bonus. Merciless suits encounters with multiple lighter enemies where kill frequency is high but headshot precision is impractical. Deadhead remains preferred for the Nataruk's intended precision-heavy playstyle.

## Notes

Nataruk's perfect-shot window is a brief animation frame that requires practice to time consistently. New players using this weapon should spend several missions in lower-level content focused exclusively on timing the release, as the damage difference between perfect and imperfect shots is large enough to constitute a separate effective weapon tier.

Cold from Primed Cryo Rounds applies Cold status on hit, which can freeze lighter enemies briefly. Against frozen enemies, a perfectly aimed follow-up arrow to the head deals a Frozen status bonus damage multiplier on top of the existing critical and elemental bonuses, further elevating single-target damage output.
