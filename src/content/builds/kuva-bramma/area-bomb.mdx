## Overview

The Kuva Bramma is a bow-type primary weapon that fires explosive arrows, each detonating on impact to deal damage in a radius and release cluster bomblets that scatter and explode in a secondary radius around the point of impact. The combination of the primary explosion and the cluster bomblet field makes each arrow effectively cover an area substantially larger than most other launcher-type weapons at equivalent damage values.

This build prioritizes maximizing per-arrow damage through high base damage mods and multishot, combined with the Galvanized Chamber kill-triggered multishot stacking effect. At high Galvanized Chamber stacks, each arrow releases additional bomblets proportional to the bonus projectile count, multiplying area coverage per shot.

Steel Charge is selected as the aura mod to activate the Vigilante set bonus when combined with Vigilante Armaments. The Vigilante set adds a chance to upgrade a critical hit tier (white to yellow, yellow to orange, orange to red), which applies to the Kuva Bramma's explosion and bomblets. This upgrade chance stacks with Primed Pistol Gambit equivalents in the secondary slot and provides a passive crit tier elevation without requiring additional mod slots dedicated to critical chance.

Galvanized Aptitude provides stacking flat damage bonuses based on status proc count on the target. The Kuva Bramma applies Cold status through Primed Cryo Rounds and the Kuva element's contribution, allowing Galvanized Aptitude's bonus to activate rapidly against any target that survives the initial explosion.

## Loadout

Serration at rank 10 and Heavy Caliber at rank 10 together provide the largest available direct damage multiplication for primary rifles. Heavy Caliber's accuracy reduction is largely irrelevant for a launcher weapon targeting groups rather than individual bodies; aim at floor or wall positions near enemy clusters rather than at specific targets. Serration and Heavy Caliber stack multiplicatively with each other at the damage formula layer before applying to base, maximizing total damage per arrow.

Split Chamber at rank 5 adds 90% multishot, generating an additional arrow per shot approximately 90% of the time. A second arrow doubles both the primary explosion and the associated bomblet field, effectively doubling area coverage. Galvanized Chamber at rank 10 adds further multishot stacks on kills, potentially reaching a second full additional arrow at high stack counts.

Vile Acceleration at rank 5 reduces charge time for the bow draw. The Kuva Bramma requires a full draw for maximum arrow velocity; Vile Acceleration reduces this draw time significantly, enabling faster successive shots. The trade-off is a small critical multiplier reduction that is acceptable given the build's emphasis on raw damage and area coverage.

Vigilante Armaments at rank 5 adds multishot alongside the Vigilante set bonus. Each additional bomblet from multishot rolls independently for the Vigilante critical tier upgrade, creating a probabilistic critical tier elevation across the bomblet field.

Primed Cryo Rounds at rank 10 adds Cold elemental damage. Cold combines with the Kuva element (Toxin for the commonly recommended variant) to form Viral, which increases health damage taken by 325% at maximum stacks. On the first arrow striking a group, Viral status from the Cold plus Toxin combination applies to all enemies in the explosion radius, effectively multiplying the damage of all subsequent arrows and bomblets against the same group.

## Playstyle

Aim arrows at floor, wall, or ceiling positions near the largest enemy cluster visible. Direct hits on enemies are acceptable but not necessary; the explosion radius covers nearby targets even when the arrow strikes the environment. Against standard Steel Path density, a single arrow kills most light and medium enemies within the explosion radius through the combined initial detonation and bomblet spread.

At Galvanized Chamber stacks of 4 or higher, each arrow produces substantially more bomblets than base, covering a much larger floor area. In doorway choke points, a single high-stack arrow can eliminate an entire wave of enemies attempting to push through. Maintain stacks by landing kills before the 20-second decay timer. In high-density missions, this is self-maintaining.

Primary Merciless at rank 5 adds a kill-stacking damage multiplier. The Kuva Bramma's area damage ensures multiple kills per arrow in dense groups, stacking Merciless rapidly. At maximum Merciless stacks, each arrow deals a large multiplier on top of all other damage bonuses, producing kill times on Steel Path heavy enemies of a few seconds.

## Strengths and weaknesses

Area bomb performance peaks in defensive game modes (defense, interception, survival with fixed life support) where enemies funnel from predictable directions and a single well-placed arrow clears the entire approach vector. In disruption missions, Kuva Bramma arrows aimed at conduit approach corridors eliminate demolishers before they reach the conduit.

Self-damage is the primary operational risk. At close range, the explosion can knock the player or apply proc damage to the player's own health pool. Maintain a minimum engagement distance of approximately 8 to 10 meters from the target position. In corridor encounters where this distance is difficult, switch to secondary weapon for close-quarter clearance.

## Alternatives

Replacing Galvanized Aptitude with Hunter Munitions adds Slash status procs to the critical hit chance via the mod's mechanic (Hunter Munitions in the mods collection is available). Slash procs bypass armor, which is useful against Grineer Alloy Armor that resists the Kuva Bramma's base damage types. This variant reduces the status scaling benefit for a guaranteed bleed proc on crits.

Replacing Primary Merciless with Primary Deadhead provides a headshot-focused stacking bonus. Given the Kuva Bramma fires at floor/wall positions rather than enemy heads, Primary Merciless remains preferable for this weapon specifically.

## Notes

The Kuva Bramma's ammo economy is tight. Each arrow consumes one from a magazine of 5 (at base). With Vigilante Supplies (a companion mod that replenishes secondary ammo) active via a companion, primary ammo pickups can be converted. Alternatively, equipping Ammo Drum or Carrier Sentinel with the appropriate precept allows extended sessions without resupply runs.

The Vigilante set bonus from Steel Charge plus Vigilante Armaments provides a 13% chance per projectile to upgrade the critical tier. With the Kuva Bramma generating multiple bomblets per arrow, the cumulative chance that at least one bomblet per arrow upgrades is high, making this passive crit enhancement meaningful.
