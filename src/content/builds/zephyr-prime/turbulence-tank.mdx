## Overview

Zephyr Prime's Turbulence Projectile Tank build maximizes defensive coverage through projectile deflection and crowd control conversion. This configuration prioritizes ability duration and range to maintain continuous Turbulence coverage across entire mission areas while using Funnel Clouds augment to convert deflected shots into area damage. The build excels in Corpus-dominated content, Void Fissures, and other mission types where enemy ranged attacks significantly outnumber melee engagements.

The core strategic philosophy involves activating Turbulence once per encounter and relying on pure projectile deflection to negate all ranged enemy damage output. Funnel Clouds augment enhances this defensive stance by converting deflected rounds into weapon-like damage bursts that eliminate scattered enemies without requiring active ability casting. This creates a passive defensive loop where enemies defeat themselves through poorly-aimed suppressive fire.

## Loadout

The mod selection emphasizes duration and range for maximum Turbulence coverage. Primed Continuity at rank 10 extends Turbulence to approximately 16 seconds per cast, providing comfortable windows between recasts even against high-firepower enemy groups. Stretch and Augur Reach combine to expand Turbulence radius to 25+ meters, covering entire corridor sections and open rooms simultaneously.

Umbral Intensify and Umbral Vitality provide power strength scaling for increased Funnel Clouds effectiveness while unlocking the Umbral set bonus. Blind Rage at rank 8 further increases Funnel Clouds damage without significantly impacting Turbulence's defensive capability. Power Drift in the exilus slot maintains additional power strength while preserving energy efficiency through Streamline's drain reduction.

The build maintains manageable energy costs through Streamline's 30 percent efficiency buff, allowing Turbulence recasting every 14-16 seconds while maintaining sustainable energy regeneration through Arcane Energize on kills.

## Playstyle

Begin encounters by casting Turbulence on yourself or a centrally-positioned location to provide projectile coverage for the entire squad. Allow enemies to fire at Zephyr while Turbulence deflects all shots and Funnel Clouds converts deflected rounds into area damage against surrounding enemies. Reposition occasionally to keep maximum enemy numbers within Turbulence radius.

Use Jet Stream for mobility between objective areas, maintaining Turbulence uptime throughout movement. Against heavy melee-focused enemy groups, use Dive Bomb or Tornado augment to provide offensive capability while Turbulence handles ranged threats. Energy economy becomes simpler than other duration-focused builds because Turbulence does not scale meaningfully with efficiency below 30 percent, making Streamline's buff sufficient for sustained uptime.

Position defensively behind objectives or squad mates, keeping the protective Turbulence field between Zephyr and primary enemy spawn points. Avoid advancing toward melee enemies that cannot be affected by Turbulence deflection mechanics. Instead, allow squad mates to eliminate melee threats while Turbulence and Funnel Clouds handle the primarily ranged encounter.

## Strengths and weaknesses

Zephyr's Turbulence provides unique defensive capability against projectile-heavy enemies by deflecting 100 percent of incoming shots without damage mitigation. No damage type, ability, or enemy special mechanic bypasses Turbulence's deflection, making it the most reliable projectile defense in the warframe roster. Funnel Clouds augment amplifies this defensive advantage by converting deflected fire into offensive output, creating a self-sustaining damage loop.

The primary weakness is Turbulence's inability to affect melee attacks or status effects. Against Grineer Heavy Gunners, Nox units, or other melee-focused threats, Zephyr must rely on squad support or secondary abilities like Dive Bomb. Extended encounters with mixed melee and ranged enemies become challenging because Turbulence handles ranged defense while leaving Zephyr exposed to physical damage sources.

## Alternatives

Replacing Funnel Clouds with Jet Stream augment provides movement speed buff instead of damage conversion, improving repositioning freedom but reducing offensive output. Substituting Augur Reach for Primed Flow allows extended mission sessions with larger Turbulence radius maintained continuously. Dropping Blind Rage for Power Drift reduces power strength slightly while improving energy economy for sustaining longer survival rotations.

## Notes

Turbulence deflects projectiles away from Zephyr and squad mates within radius, providing area protection rather than single-target defense. Funnel Clouds damage scales with power strength but remains independent of Turbulence's core deflection mechanic, allowing Zephyr to maximize both effects simultaneously. Hitscan weapons like Corpus laser guns cannot be deflected because they do not count as projectiles for mechanic purposes.
