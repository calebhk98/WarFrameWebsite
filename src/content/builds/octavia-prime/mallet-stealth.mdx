## Overview

Octavia Prime's Mallet Stealth build is one of the most reliable survival configurations in the game due to its ability to maintain indefinite invisibility through consistent Metronome crouch timing. The build does not depend on enemy kills, ability damage, or external healing sources -- invisibility persists as long as the player maintains the crouch beat interval, making Octavia Prime essentially immune to all direct damage in most encounters.

The Resonator augment allows Mallet to roll and orbit Resonator's movement path, pulling enemies along the path and clustering them around the Mallet. This movement significantly increases Mallet's damage potential in open areas where enemies would not otherwise concentrate near a stationary placement.

## Loadout

Duration is the primary stat for this build. Narrow Minded at rank 10 pushes ability duration to extreme values, extending Mallet's active window and Metronome's beat interval length. The range penalty from Narrow Minded is accepted because the Resonator augment compensates by bringing enemies to the Mallet rather than requiring Mallet to reach enemies at range.

Primed Continuity supplements Narrow Minded's duration contribution and is less essential but provides the secondary percentage bonus needed to hit target duration thresholds. Together with Power Drift in the exilus slot, total duration exceeds 250% of base values.

Umbral Intensify and Umbral Vitality together provide the Umbral set bonus and raise both power strength and health, ensuring Mallet's reflected damage multiplier at rank 5 reaches 2.0x amplification. Higher amplification causes Mallet to return significantly more damage to nearby enemies per shot fired at it.

## Playstyle

Compose a custom Mallet beat pattern in the Mandachord before entering the mission. Simpler patterns with consistent high beats are easier to crouch in time with. After deployment, navigate to the primary enemy spawn point and place Mallet in the path enemies must travel to reach the objective.

Cast Resonator to spawn the rolling companion. Resonator automatically orbits Mallet and drags nearby enemies with it, pulling them into Mallet's aura. Cast Metronome to start the beat. Crouch in rhythm with the bass beat indicated by the visual pulse on screen. Successful crouch timing activates invisibility, which remains active as long as each subsequent crouch lands within the timing window.

Mallet absorbs incoming fire and returns amplified damage to all enemies in range. In rooms with high enemy density, the returned damage alone clears most standard enemies without any weapon interaction. For heavy units and eximus variants, supplement Mallet's passive damage with weapon shots while maintaining invisibility.

## Strengths and weaknesses

Metronome's invisibility is the most reliable stealth mechanic in the game. Unlike other invisibility frames (Loki, Ash) that depend on timed ability casts, Octavia Prime's invisibility renewal depends only on player timing. Invisibility has no hard expiry -- it renews indefinitely through continued beat timing, making Octavia Prime functional for arbitration survival and ESO endurance runs.

The build requires player engagement to maintain. Drifting off the beat rhythm breaks invisibility and exposes Octavia to incoming damage. In this vulnerable window, the survivability of the build drops significantly, particularly against eximus units and heavy gunner types in Steel Path. Players who find rhythm timing difficult should consider the Resonator-only variant that relies on Mallet's damage reflection without invisibility maintenance.

## Alternatives

Dropping Narrow Minded for Overextended trades duration for range, expanding Mallet's damage reflection aura to a much larger radius at the cost of lower power strength. This variant is less efficient for endgame farming but more useful in wide-open tileset rooms where Narrow Minded's range penalty prevents Mallet from covering the full spawn area.

## Notes

Mallet's damage reflection multiplier scales with power strength. At 160% power strength (this build's approximate value) each shot absorbed is returned at 2.0x the original damage value. On Steel Path, where enemy damage output is high, Mallet can kill Grineer Heavy Gunners through reflected damage alone within a few seconds of active fire.

The Resonator augment is an alternative to the base Resonator ability behavior. Equipping Resonator at rank 5 changes the companion to orbit Mallet, creating an automated pulling loop without any additional player interaction after the initial casts.
