## Overview

Octavia Prime's Mallet reflects incoming damage back at enemies at a multiplier that scales with power strength. This build maximizes that reflection multiplier while maintaining enough duration for Mallet to persist through extended survival rotations without constant recasting. The Helminth slot substitutes Roar for a fourth ability, layering a multiplicative damage bonus on top of Mallet's already-amplified reflection. Combined, high-level Steel Path enemies essentially contribute to their own destruction the more they attack Mallet.

Energy Siphon in the aura slot provides passive energy regeneration of 0.4 per second at max rank, which reduces dependence on energy orb pickups during periods when enemies are clustered around Mallet and not moving toward Octavia directly. This passive income keeps Roar refreshed even in slower rotations.

Narrow-minded at rank 10 is an intentional range sacrifice. Octavia's Metronome, Mallet, and Resonator all have their own radii, but Metronome's range only needs to cover Octavia herself for the crouching buff. Mallet's damage reflection radius at reduced range still covers a standard doorway choke point. The power strength gain from removing a range mod and inserting Narrow-minded raises Mallet reflection to a high enough multiplier that enemies with high damage output -- Grineer Bombards, Nox units, Corrupted Heavy Gunners -- die within a few seconds of approaching.

## Loadout

Umbral Intensify at rank 10 with Umbral Vitality at rank 10 grants the two-piece Umbral set bonus -- a 25% multiplicative increase on top of each mod's base contribution. The combined effect raises Octavia Prime's power strength to a level where Mallet's reflection multiplier approaches or exceeds 200% of incoming damage, meaning every hit an enemy lands on Mallet returns twice the damage to the attacker and all nearby enemies.

Primed Continuity at rank 10 and Narrow-minded at rank 10 together push ability duration above 200% of base. This means a single Mallet cast persists for over 60 seconds without requiring a recast. In 20-minute survival runs, the number of Mallet casts needed per rotation drops to two or three, preserving energy for Resonator placement and Roar refreshes.

Streamline at rank 5 and Fleeting Expertise at rank 4 provide 66% total efficiency reduction, which nearly halves the energy cost of all abilities. Octavia's Mallet costs 50 energy at base; with full efficiency this drops close to 17 energy per cast, making the ability essentially free relative to Arcane Energize's energy supply on kills.

Adaptation at rank 10 provides up to 90% damage reduction against damage types that have hit Octavia repeatedly within 20 seconds. In survival missions, Octavia frequently takes incidental damage from off-angle enemies not facing Mallet. Adaptation covers this ambient damage and makes passive Metronome invisibility -- granted by crouching on the musical beat -- less critical as a sole survival mechanism.

## Playstyle

Position Mallet at a doorway or corridor chokepoint where enemies funnel in from a single direction. Cast Resonator immediately after Mallet to create a mobile lure that draws enemies toward Mallet before they can spread out across the room. Activate the Amp buff by crouching on the Metronome beat; the Amp multiplier increases Mallet's damage reflection further when active, and maintaining the buff passively grants invisibility.

Recast Roar every 30 to 40 seconds and immediately refresh Mallet if it has expired. In longer rotations, time Roar to overlap with the period when the largest enemy wave arrives, as the multiplicative bonus applies to every reflection hit occurring while Roar is active. Avoid placing Mallet in open-room centers where enemies can approach from multiple angles simultaneously and stagger their fire -- choke-point placement ensures maximum fire concentration and thus maximum reflection output.

Metronome's visual cue appears as a pulsing ring around Octavia. Crouching when the ring contracts grants the Amp buff; failing the timing causes the buff to drop and invisibility to break. In hectic survival scenarios, maintaining rhythm is easier with a camera angle that keeps the ring visible.

## Strengths and weaknesses

Against Corrupted and Grineer enemies armed with high-damage Bombard rockets and Heavy Gunner fire, Mallet reflection scales dramatically because the damage reflected is a function of what enemies deal, not Octavia's own stats. A Corrupted Bombard firing at Mallet at level 200 in Steel Path kills itself and surrounding enemies faster than most direct-damage abilities can accomplish at the same level.

The primary weakness is melee-dominant factions. Infested Chargers, Ancients, and certain Infested hybrid units deal most of their damage in melee, which does not activate Mallet's reflection unless they directly hit the Mallet object. In these encounters, Mallet reflection provides little value and Octavia relies more on personal weapon damage augmented by Roar.

## Alternatives

Replacing Narrow-minded with Augur Reach expands ability range at the cost of power strength. This allows Mallet to cover larger rooms but reduces the reflection multiplier. This variant suits lower Steel Path tiers (below level 150) where enemies deal less per hit and a larger coverage area matters more than peak reflection.

Growing Power in the aura slot is an alternative to Energy Siphon if the squad can maintain status procs reliably. Growing Power grants a 25% power strength buff on status proc, which stacks additively with Umbral Intensify and Narrow-minded to further raise Mallet's reflection multiplier during burst windows.

## Notes

Octavia Prime's passive reduces Mallet's passive cool-down, which is not directly relevant to this build's rotation but means the interval between Mallet placement and reflection becoming active is shorter. Players who miss the first Metronome beat cycle after placing Mallet can still activate the Amp buff on the second or third pulse without significant downtime.

Roar's duration at this build's configuration lasts approximately 28 to 32 seconds depending on exact stat interaction with Narrow-minded's reduction. Refresh Roar proactively rather than waiting for it to expire to maintain continuous coverage on Mallet's reflection window.
