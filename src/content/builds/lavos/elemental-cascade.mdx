## Overview

Lavos's Elemental Cascade build transforms the frame into a status-priming alchemist that establishes reaction chains across enemy populations. The configuration maximizes Vial Rush range and status application rate, enabling simultaneous Corrosive, Heat, Toxin, and Cold application to entire tile populations before triggering cascading Transmutation Cloud reactions.

Element cycling management enables flexible status configurations: maintaining Corrosive for armor stripping while cycling between Heat for raw damage and Toxin for scaling procs. This build excels in star chart content where reaction chains eliminate enemy groups exponentially.

## Loadout

The configuration balances duration, range, and strength to maintain optimal ability uptime. Primed Continuity at rank 10 extends Transmutation Cloud duration, allowing reaction cascades to persist even after initial Vial Rush priming expires. Stretch and Augur Reach together push Vial Rush range to 60+ meters, covering entire tile rooms with single casts.

Streamline and Fleeting Expertise manage energy costs to sustainable levels. Umbral Intensify and Umbral Vitality enable set bonuses while maintaining health pool above standard frames. Blind Rage contributes final power strength boost without additional drain penalties beyond modslot costs.

Corrosive Projection aura debuffs enemy armor globally, improving all damage types and enabling Corrosive reaction synergy with weapons applying heat or toxin. Cunning Drift provides power strength while maintaining exilus flexibility.

## Playstyle

Initiate combat by cycling element passive to Corrosive, then cast Vial Rush through central enemy clusters to apply poison procs across all targets. Immediately reposition and cast Transmutation Cloud to trigger reaction cascades, consuming status procs while dealing area damage. Allow cloud effects to persist through enemy death, as cascading reactions continue damaging new arrivals.

Cycle element passive between Transmutation Cloud casts to match squad damage types. If teammates apply Heat weapons, maintain active Corrosive cycling to strip armor. If squad focuses on status-heavy melee weapons, shift to Toxin cycling to extend proc duration and enable poison damage scaling.

Maintain Vial Rush on recast intervals of approximately 20 seconds to ensure continuous poison application as initial status procs decay. Use Pilfer to strip armor independently when Corrosive reactions are insufficient, enabling solo threat elimination.

## Strengths and weaknesses

Vial Rush applies all four elemental types simultaneously in single casts, enabling status-heavy weapons and elemental-synergy builds to trigger multiple reactions per hit. Transmutation Cloud's cascading damage scales infinitely as enemy group size increases, making this configuration optimal for endless missions with high spawn rates.

Reaction damage benefits from both ability strength and status-heavy weapons, encouraging team composition diversity and enabling creative synergy opportunities. Lavos's passive element cycling provides strategic flexibility without requiring separate ability casts, freeing modslots for duration or range extension.

The primary weakness is status-dependency: Transmutation Cloud cannot trigger without existing enemy procs, limiting effectiveness against enemies with status immunity or very high armor values. Additionally, reaction damage scales inversely with power strength reductions from range-extension mods, forcing uncomfortable tradeoffs between range and output magnitude.

## Alternatives

Replacing Blind Rage with Transient Fortitude increases power strength while reducing duration significantly. This variant prioritizes immediate reaction damage over sustained ability uptime, suitable for defense missions with wave-based enemy spawns.

Substituting Fleeting Expertise with Constitution provides additional duration without efficiency penalties, improving Transmutation Cloud persistence at the cost of reduced energy management. This alternative suits content with higher energy economy from squad sources.

## Notes

Transmutation Cloud's cascading reactions trigger independently on each status-affected target. Position casts to overlap reaction zones and maximize chain-trigger effects from enemy clustering.

Element passive cycling affects subsequent Vial Rush casts immediately. Plan cycling sequences before casting to ensure matching element selections align with status reaction goals.
