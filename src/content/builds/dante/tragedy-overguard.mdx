## Overview

Dante, introduced in the Whispers in the Walls update, is a damage-dealer and support hybrid built around the Page mechanic. His abilities alternate between Light and Dark verse types, and each alternating cast generates Page stacks. Tragedy consumes all accumulated Pages in a single area blast, dealing damage that scales directly with the stack count. This creates a rhythm-based combat loop where the investment of several ability casts is rewarded with a high-damage finisher.

Overguard, applied via Dante's passive or the Overguard mod, provides damage immunity frames during ability casts. Dante's passive generates Overguard on himself and allies when Pages accumulate, making the entire squad temporarily immune to standard damage during high-stack phases. This defensive contribution makes Dante uniquely valuable in squad content where brief immunity windows align with objective interactions.

## Loadout

Power strength is the primary scaling factor for Tragedy's damage output. Umbral Intensify provides the highest single-mod strength increase with the Umbral set bonus from Umbral Vitality. The combined strength output from this partial Umbral set exceeds 160% base, which multiplies Tragedy's per-Page damage significantly at high stack counts.

Dark Verse at rank 5 is the augment that modifies Dante's second ability, adding additional Page-generation capacity and status application on cast. This accelerates the stack buildup phase and applies crowd-control effects (including slow and status procs) that help the squad deal with enemies during Tragedy's wind-up.

Primed Continuity and Streamline together maintain energy efficiency and ability duration for Noctua and the verse abilities. Noctua at base provides ammo pickups and energy to allies in range; higher duration extends the time window during which allies benefit from its pickups.

## Playstyle

Cycle through Light verse ability (Triumph) and Dark verse ability (Dark Verse) alternately to accumulate Page stacks efficiently. Each alternating cast registers a Page. Cast Noctua between verse cycles to maintain energy for the full sequence. When Page count reaches maximum (determined by power strength and stack cap), release Tragedy for the area finisher.

In single-target encounters against bosses, the Tragedy burst is less effective due to its area nature. Against these targets, Dante contributes through Noctua's squad support and status effects from Dark Verse rather than raw Tragedy damage.

Overguard activates on Page accumulation and provides immunity frames to Dante and nearby allies. Use Tragedy casts during high-density enemy phases, when the initial immunity window covers the most incoming fire. Re-building to maximum Pages after each Tragedy takes approximately 5-6 ability casts, which is achievable within a single enemy wave at standard mission pacing.

## Strengths and weaknesses

Dante's Tragedy Overguard build provides a combination of personal damage output and squad immunity support that few frames can replicate. The Overguard immunity frames are a passive benefit of normal Page accumulation, meaning Dante contributes defensive value to the squad without dedicating any specific mod slot to defensive support. This makes the build efficient in content where both DPS and survivability are required.

The primary limitation is the stack-building phase before Tragedy is usable. Against fast-spawning enemies or in missions with dynamic objective demands, committing 5-6 ability casts to build Pages can leave Dante vulnerable to incoming damage if Overguard was depleted during the previous cycle.

Dante is a relatively recently released frame, so community optimization of loadout variants continues to evolve. The build documented here reflects consensus as of May 2026; check the Overframe database for updated variants as the meta shifts.

## Alternatives

Running a Helminth subsume with Roar over Dante's first ability (Noctua) converts the support pickup generation for a flat damage bonus. This is appropriate for solo content where energy management from Zenurik and Arcane Energize covers the energy need that Noctua fills in squad contexts.

Replacing Umbral Vitality with Overextended trades health for significantly increased ability range, widening Tragedy's blast radius to cover larger rooms. This range trade-off is appropriate in open-tileset missions like Plains or Orb Vallis where tight radius limits Tragedy's effectiveness.

## Notes

Tragedy's damage formula: total damage equals base damage times power strength multiplier, then multiplied by the current Page stack count. Higher stack counts produce exponentially better Tragedy returns per cast cycle. Always spend the minimum casts to reach maximum Pages before triggering Tragedy -- triggering at low stack counts wastes the potential of the highest-damage release.

Dark Verse's status application at rank 5 applies a guaranteed cold proc on direct hit, which slows enemy movement speed. The cold slow synergizes with Tragedy's area cast window: slowed enemies remain in the blast zone for the full damage application rather than moving out of range during the cast animation.
