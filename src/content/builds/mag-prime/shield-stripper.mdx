## Overview

This build transforms Mag Prime into a dedicated armor and shield stripper for Steel Path and endgame content. Polarize at high power strength can remove 100% of enemy armor and shields in a single cast when enemies are sufficiently grouped. The Umbral mod set amplifies both power strength and survivability simultaneously, making Mag Prime durable enough to operate in dense enemy groups where repeated Polarize casts are most efficient.

The Helminth ability Roar replaces Mag's weaker first ability Pull, providing a flat damage multiplier that benefits the entire squad. Coupled with Magnetize's bullet attractor, this loadout enables extraordinarily high single-target damage output against armored targets.

## Loadout

The mod configuration centers on maximizing power strength while maintaining acceptable energy efficiency. Umbral Intensify and Blind Rage together push power strength above 200%, which guarantees a full armor strip on Polarize against Grineer and Corrupted units. Fleeting Expertise at rank 4 offsets most of the efficiency penalty from Blind Rage without sacrificing duration below useful thresholds.

Corrosive Projection in the aura slot reduces enemy armor by 18% before any ability interaction, lowering the effective health pool of armored targets even on frames other than Mag. Stretch at rank 5 extends Polarize and Crush radius to cover larger enemy groups. Augur Message extends ability duration, keeping Magnetize active long enough for squad weapons to deal full amplified damage.

Primed Flow provides the deep energy reserves needed to sustain ability spam across long missions. Arcane Energize at rank 5 triggers on energy pickups, frequently topping off the energy pool mid-mission without requiring Zenurik activation on every wave.

## Playstyle

Open each encounter by casting Polarize on the largest cluster of enemies. At 200%+ power strength the pulse strips all Grineer armor and Corpus shields in one application. Tag a heavy unit or boss with Magnetize immediately after, directing squad fire through the bubble for amplified damage. Cast Roar before high-damage phases to extend its flat damage bonus to the full squad.

Crush serves as a wave-clear finisher when enemies crowd Mag's position. The ability suspends and damages all targets in range, providing a brief opening to reposition or revive allies. In Steel Path survival and defense missions, cycle through Polarize, Magnetize, and Roar on cooldown to maintain consistent armor strip uptime across rotation phases.

Zenurik's Energizing Dash replenishes energy during brief lulls in combat. Use operator mode between waves to trigger the passive regeneration channel and ensure full energy before each new enemy spawn.

## Strengths and weaknesses

Mag Prime's shield-stripper configuration excels in any mission where Grineer or Corrupted armor represents the primary threat. The combination of Umbral mods and Blind Rage drives power strength high enough to guarantee full armor removal, enabling squad weapons to deal unmodified base damage against targets that would otherwise have DR from Iron Skin or Ferrite Armor.

The build's weaknesses center on its energy dependency. Blind Rage's efficiency penalty means each Polarize cast costs significantly more than the base value. Fleeting Expertise compensates partially, but long endurance missions without energy pads may require Zenurik channeling during quieter moments. Nullifiers are a hard counter to Magnetize, dissolving the bubble immediately on contact. Keep operator available for dash-through kills against nullifiers or maintain a weapon capable of bursting through the bubble's health pool quickly.

## Alternatives

Replacing Fleeting Expertise with Primed Continuity and dropping Blind Rage to rank 5 produces a more energy-efficient variant at the cost of reduced power strength (around 170%). This version is better suited for longer solo endurance runs where energy economy matters more than maximum strip reliability.

Narrow Minded can substitute for Augur Message if duration is prioritized over mod synergy, at the cost of reduced Polarize and Crush radius. This trade-off works in corridor-heavy tilesets where enemies naturally cluster.

## Notes

Polarize strips scale with power strength according to the formula: strip percentage = base percentage times power strength multiplier. At 100% base strip and 200% power strength, the effective strip is capped at 100%. The exact base percentage varies by faction: Grineer Ferrite Armor strips at a higher rate than Alloy Armor, so Corrupted Heavy Gunners may need a second cast in long endurance missions where enemy scaling exceeds baseline values.

Mag Prime's Magnetize interacts with projectile weapons by increasing the number of bullet loops. High-fire-rate weapons such as the Soma Prime or Supra Vandal generate exceptional damage inside the bubble. Pair this build with a high-crit rifle for maximum single-target output.
