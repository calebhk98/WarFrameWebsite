## Overview

Mag Prime's Crush ability deals Magnetic damage in a sphere centered on Mag, and with the Fracturing Crush augment (listed in the mods collection under `fracturing-crush`) it additionally strips a percentage of enemy armor from every target hit. This transforms Crush from a damage-dealing finisher into a primary armor-stripping tool in a similar role to Tharros Strike or Pillage. The combination of Pull, which gathers enemies into a cluster, and Crush, which strips all of them simultaneously, provides Mag with one of the few area-of-effect armor strip mechanics available to non-Helminth builds.

Magnetize is Mag's single-target amplifier: it creates a magnetic field around one enemy that causes all projectiles passing through it to orbit and hit repeatedly, effectively multiplying weapon damage against that target by a substantial factor. This build does not maximize Magnetize's radius because single-target projectile cycling is secondary to the pull-and-strip combo as the primary damage loop; Magnetize is reserved for boss targets or Noxes that survive the initial Crush strip.

Polarize strips shields from Corpus enemies and generates energy orbs from each stripped enemy's magnetic fragments. In Corpus missions, Polarize followed by weapon fire deals significantly more damage because shields absorb damage before armor, and removing shields instantly exposes the lower-health robotic or flesh layer. The energy orbs generated by Polarize partially offset the energy cost of the full combo rotation.

## Loadout

Fracturing Crush at rank 5 strips 75% of enemy armor from targets hit by Crush. This is the maximum value at full rank and represents a large fraction of Grineer Alloy Armor that would otherwise require sustained Corrosive procs to reduce. Combining Fracturing Crush's strip with Corrosive Projection's 30% flat reduction means most Steel Path Grineer Heavy Gunners are effectively operating with 25% of their original armor value after the combo.

Umbral Intensify and Umbral Vitality together raise power strength and health through the Umbral two-piece set bonus. Mag Prime's base health of 375 at max rank is low for Steel Path encounters; Umbral Vitality more than doubles this. Combined with Arcane Blessing's stacking maximum health increase (which accumulates per kill over the mission duration), Mag Prime can reach survivable health totals in extended missions.

Primed Continuity at rank 10 extends Magnetize's bubble duration, which determines how long projectiles orbit the affected target and deal damage. Longer Magnetize duration on priority targets means more passes of weapon fire cycling through the bubble, which dramatically increases the total damage a single Magnetize cast generates against Noxes or Elite Lancers.

Adaptation at rank 10 provides the build's primary defensive layer. Since Mag Prime's health is not high enough to simply absorb Steel Path fire without mitigation, Adaptation's up-to-90% damage resistance against the primary incoming damage type prevents deaths during the setup phase before Pull and Crush can clear enemy groups.

Arcane Blessing at rank 5 grants 360 maximum health per kill for 30 seconds, stacking up to 40 times. Over the course of a survival rotation, the accumulated maximum health bonus from Arcane Blessing can reach several thousand additional health points, making Mag Prime substantially more durable as the mission progresses. This passive scaling suits Mag's role as a sustained-presence CC frame.

## Playstyle

Begin each encounter with Pull to gather visible enemies toward Mag's position, which simultaneously applies a knockdown and clusters targets within Crush's radius. Cast Crush immediately after Pull while enemies are still recovering from the knockdown, ensuring the armor strip hits the maximum number of targets. Polarize immediately after Crush generates energy orbs from the stripped enemy fragments while applying shield strip to any Corpus units surviving the combo.

For single high-health targets, cast Magnetize before engaging with primary or secondary weapons. All Slash damage, Viral procs, and weapon crits occurring inside the bubble hit the Magnetized target repeatedly. With Viral damage applied from a status weapon, Magnetize damage scales dramatically against the target's reduced effective health.

## Strengths and weaknesses

The area armor strip from Fracturing Crush is the build's primary differentiator. Most frames require either a Helminth subsume (Pillage, Tharros) or sustained Corrosive proc application to achieve equivalent strip. Mag Prime achieves it natively through her augment, making the slot available for Helminth if desired. In Corpus missions particularly, the combination of Polarize shield strip and Crush armor strip eliminates both defensive layers with two casts.

The primary weakness is range. Crush's base radius is moderate, and Stretch at rank 5 extends it without maximizing it. Enemies that are not within Pull's gathering radius before Crush fires may escape the strip. In tile sets with multiple simultaneous approach vectors, some enemies will be out of position and require individual attention.

## Alternatives

Replacing Fleeting Expertise with Primed Flow provides a larger energy reserve, reducing dependence on Polarize-generated orbs to sustain the rotation. This trades some ability cost efficiency for energy pool safety in missions with lower enemy density.

Swapping Adaptation for Quick Thinking provides a different survivability model more suited to aggressive play. Quick Thinking prevents death from any hit that would reduce health below 2 HP by draining energy instead, functioning as a second health bar. At Primed Flow's energy capacity, Quick Thinking provides multiple lives worth of protection.

## Notes

Mag Prime's passive draws nearby objects toward her when she bullet jumps. This passive interacts with Magnetize by pulling orbs generated from enemy deaths through the Magnetize bubble before they reach Mag, potentially causing orbs to cycle inside the bubble. This is cosmetic and does not affect the orb's pickup properties.

Fracturing Crush's armor strip percentage at max rank is 75%. This figure applies to the enemy's current armor value, not base armor. If Corrosive Projection has already reduced armor by 30%, Fracturing Crush strips 75% of the remaining 70%, leaving the target with approximately 17.5% of their original armor. Two casts of Crush in rapid succession fully strip the target.
