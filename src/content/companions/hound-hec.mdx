## Overview

The Hec Hound is a Sister of Parvos companion variant with an electromagnetic disruption theme. Its Electromagnetic Field ability creates a disruptive zone around the Hound that affects Corpus electronic systems, and its Paralytic Module fires a stun projectile at individual targets. The Hec's combination of area disruption and single-target stun makes it the most crowd-control-focused Hound variant.

The Electromagnetic Field ability is particularly interesting in Corpus missions where robotic enemies have electronic components that can be disrupted. While the exact effect on enemy behavior depends on implementation details that vary across game updates, the general principle of electromagnetic disruption complements anti-Corpus playstyles that emphasize disrupting their technology-dependent faction mechanics.

Like all Hounds, the Hec is obtained through the Sister of Parvos system and cannot be purchased or built directly. Players must engage with Sister cycles until a Sister carrying the Hec Hound appears, then complete the Requiem sequence and convert her to claim the companion.

## Abilities and precepts

**Electromagnetic Field** generates an area of electromagnetic interference centered on the Hound. Corpus robotic enemies within the field experience disruption to their systems, and the field can reduce the effectiveness of Corpus shield-generating technology nearby. The field persists for a duration and has a cooldown between activations.

**Paralytic Module** targets a single enemy with a high-velocity stun projectile. A successfully landed hit applies a temporary paralysis to the target, preventing movement and attacks for the stun duration. The stun provides reliable single-target crowd control that can neutralize a dangerous enemy while the player handles surrounding threats. The module has a cooldown between activations.

## Acquisition

The Hec Hound is obtained by converting a Sister of Parvos who spawns with the Hec Hound as her companion. The Sister conversion process requires: allowing a Sister Candidate to kill the player in a Corpus Railjack mission, tracking and engaging the resulting Sister in Proxima missions, acquiring the correct Requiem mods from Kuva Liches or Slivers, and applying the mods in the correct order at the final confrontation. Selecting Convert at the end transfers the Sister's weapon and Hound to the player.

## Recommended mods

**Survivability:** Standard Medi-Pet Kit, Link Health, and Metal Fiber cover the Hound's survival needs. Hounds cannot self-revive and require Sentinel Revive items for in-mission restoration.

**Crowd control synergy:** The Hec's stun and disruption abilities pair well with player builds that capitalize on stunned or disrupted enemies -- status-based weapons, melee finishers, and ability interactions that have enhanced effects against crowd-controlled targets.

## Playstyle and synergies

The Hec Hound excels in Corpus missions where its electromagnetic theme matches the faction's technology-dependent design. The Paralytic Module's single-target stun provides reliable interruption of dangerous enemies such as Nullifiers, Combas, or Scrambus units that otherwise create problems for ability-reliant builds.

Players who focus on anti-Corpus content -- Spy missions on Corpus tiles, Corpus Disruption nodes, or Corpus Railjack missions -- benefit most from the Hec's thematic and functional fit. The electromagnetic disruption pairs with Magnetic damage builds that target Corpus shield systems.

The stun from Paralytic Module creates opportunities for melee finisher attacks, which deal bonus damage against stunned targets. Melee-focused builds that want companion support for finisher setup find the Hec a complementary choice.
