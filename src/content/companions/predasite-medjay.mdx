## Overview

The Medjay Predasite is a modular Infested companion from Deimos that combines defensive support with passive toxin output. The Predasite class carries two universal passive abilities -- Contagion Cloud and Pack Leader -- and the Medjay head component provides the third ability, Medi-Ray, which periodically heals the player. This healing support makes the Medjay Predasite the most healing-oriented companion option outside of the Kubrow Medi-Pet Kit mod, providing health restoration directly from the companion's kit without mod slot investment.

Predasites visually resemble large quadrupedal Infested predators and were introduced as part of the Heart of Deimos update. Like Vulpaphylas, they use the modular companion system where different component combinations produce different visual and mechanical results. The Medjay head is specifically sought for its healing ability, making it the most popular Predasite head component for support-focused players.

Contagion Cloud's toxin procs occur reactively when the companion takes damage, turning hits against the Predasite into area denial tools that poison nearby enemies. Combined with Medi-Ray's health restoration, the Medjay Predasite fulfills a dual role of offense support (toxin procs) and player defense (health regeneration).

## Abilities and precepts

**Contagion Cloud** triggers when the Predasite receives damage from enemy attacks. A cloud of poisonous gas expands from the companion, applying Toxin status effect procs to all enemies caught within the cloud's radius. Toxin damage bypasses shields and deals damage directly to enemy health, making it effective against Corpus enemies who rely on shield pools. Multiple hits against the Predasite can trigger multiple successive Contagion Clouds, though the ability has an internal cooldown to prevent instant spam.

**Medi-Ray** activates on a periodic timer and restores a portion of the player's health. The heal amount scales with the companion's mod investment in the applicable slot and activates automatically without player action. For frames with low base health or frames that take sustained chip damage, Medi-Ray provides a meaningful baseline recovery that reduces the frequency of health restore item consumption.

**Pack Leader** engages when the Predasite's health reaches zero, converting it into a small larval form rather than permanently destroying it. The larval state regenerates health at an accelerated rate before the companion returns to full size and combat function. The temporary larval form is rarely targeted by enemies due to its small profile, giving the regeneration period a high survival rate.

## Acquisition

Son Tokens from Deimos Conservation activities are exchanged with Son in the Necralisk for Predasite component blueprints. The Medjay head component specifically provides the Medi-Ray ability and must be selected during component assembly to produce a Medjay Predasite. Conservation activities involve locating and tranquilizing specific Deimos wildlife species using Tranq Rifles and species-appropriate lures.

## Recommended mods

**Survival:** Link Health and Medi-Pet Kit provide layered healing support on top of Medi-Ray's companion-based healing. Metal Fiber adds to the base 300 armor.

**Mod efficiency:** Because the Medjay Predasite's three core abilities are built-in rather than slotted precepts, all available mod slots can be devoted to survivability and utility mods. This is a significant advantage over sentinel builds where precept mods consume two to three mod slots.

**Status amplification:** Mods that increase the companion's status proc rate can amplify the frequency and intensity of Contagion Cloud's toxin procs.

## Playstyle and synergies

The Medjay Predasite suits builds where continuous health restoration provides measurable benefit -- particularly for frames that use health tank builds without natural regeneration. Frames like Inaros, who rely entirely on health rather than shields, benefit from Medi-Ray's consistent healing. Nidus with his Parasitic Link mechanic also synergizes with a companion that independently restores health.

The Contagion Cloud toxin proc contribution makes the Medjay valuable in Corpus missions or against Overguarded enemies where shield bypass damage is desirable. Players building toward Gas damage combinations (Toxin combined with Heat) can use the Medjay's toxin procs as a consistent base proc source.

Pack Leader's self-revive mechanic provides mission persistence without requiring Sentinel Revives, making the Medjay Predasite a lower-maintenance companion choice for long missions.
