## Overview

The Adarza Kavat is a combat-support companion whose Cat's Eye ability grants a substantial critical chance buff to all nearby allies. Unlike the Smeeta Kavat, which focuses on resource acquisition, the Adarza directs its utility toward enhancing weapon damage output during combat. For players and squads built around critical hit weapons, the Cat's Eye buff can push already high critical chance values to near-100% or higher, triggering red critical hits consistently.

As a Kavat, the Adarza is incubated from Kavat Genetic Codes carrying Adarza imprints. The subspecies has lower base health than the Smeeta -- 310 versus 390 -- but carries the same base shield and armor values. Survival modding requirements are therefore higher for the Adarza to maintain its presence throughout a mission.

The Reflect ability redirects projectile damage, which provides marginal benefit in most content but can be entertaining against projectile-heavy enemies like Bombards or certain Corpus units.

## Abilities and precepts

**Cat's Eye** activates periodically and grants all allies within a radius of the kavat a significant increase to their critical hit chance. The buff's magnitude and duration scale with the precept's rank. At maximum rank, the boost is substantial enough to push most weapons with moderate base critical chance into consistent critical hit territory. For squads using Sniper Rifles, Bows, or other high-base-crit weapons, the buff frequently enables red crit territory during its duration.

The ability does not affect Operators directly -- as confirmed by a patch in Update 22 that removed the inadvertent Operator buff -- but applies normally to all Warframe weapons held by nearby players.

**Reflect** creates a damage-return effect for a duration, causing projectile attacks that hit the kavat to partially reflect back at the attacker. In content with projectile-heavy enemies, the reflected damage can contribute to kills, though at high content levels reflected damage scales poorly against enemy health pools.

The Adarza has the lowest base health among the two Kavat subspecies but carries the same armor value of 300, which provides reasonable physical damage mitigation.

## Acquisition

Adarza Kavats are incubated using Kavat Genetic Codes with Adarza imprints. Players scan wild Kavats in Orokin Derelict missions using a Codex Scanner or Synthesis Scanner to obtain Kavat Genetic Codes. The imprint type is determined by which Kavat variant was scanned; scanning Adarza Kavats specifically yields codes that produce the Adarza subspecies.

Two codes and two Orokin Cells are required for incubation. The Incubator Upgrade Segment must be installed in the Orbiter first.

## Recommended mods

**Precepts:** Cat's Eye occupies the primary precept slot. Reflect fills the secondary slot.

**Survivability:** Because the Adarza's health pool is the lowest of the Kavat variants, Link Health is especially important to scale its survivability with the player's health mods. Medi-Pet Kit provides regeneration between damage instances.

**Utility:** Primed Animal Instinct adds enemy and loot radar, which improves mission awareness without competing with core function slots.

## Playstyle and synergies

The Adarza is most effective in group content and in missions centered on high critical-chance weapons. Eidolon Hunts with sniper rifle builds benefit significantly: the Cat's Eye proc during a Limb window pushes total critical chance high enough to guarantee red crit hits on each shot, dramatically increasing damage per hit.

For solo missions, the buff still provides its full effect on the player's own weapons. Builds centered around bows, sniper rifles, or Kuva weapons with inherently high critical profiles gain the most value from the Adarza's support.

The Cat's Eye buff does not stack with itself if multiple Adarza Kavats are present in a squad, but the uptime on a single Adarza's buff is sufficient for most engagements.
