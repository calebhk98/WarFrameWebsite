## Overview

The Smeeta Kavat is widely regarded as the most powerful farming companion in Warframe, primarily because of its Charm ability. Charm randomly applies one of several beneficial effects to the player at intervals, and among these effects is a doubled resource pickup that causes every item collected during its duration to yield twice the quantity. For players farming rare resources, Orokin cells, or crafting components, a Charm proc that doubles drops can dramatically reduce the number of missions required to gather sufficient materials.

Kavats are not purchased or built in the traditional sense -- they are incubated from Kavat Genetic Codes obtained by scanning wild Kavats in Orokin Derelict missions. The imprint determines subspecies, so players specifically seeking a Smeeta must acquire Kavat Genetic Codes that carry Smeeta imprints, either by scanning or by trading with other players who have extracted and sold Smeeta imprints through the [warframe.market](https://warframe.market) system.

The Smeeta's lower health pool of 390 means it requires attentive modding to survive in high-level content, but its base armor of 300 -- shared across all Kavat and Kubrow companions -- provides reasonable physical damage mitigation.

## Abilities and precepts

**Charm** is the Smeeta's defining ability and the reason it occupies the companion slot in most resource farming builds. At intervals, Charm applies one of six possible beneficial effects to the player: doubled resource pickups for approximately 2 minutes, doubled credit income, doubled affinity gain, a brief period of invulnerability, a full reload of all weapons, or a restoration of energy to full. The proc selection is random, so players cannot directly control which effect triggers. However, even the less universally useful procs -- like full reload or invulnerability -- provide meaningful benefits during extended missions.

**Mischief** creates a decoy that temporarily redirects enemy attention away from the player. The decoy absorbs aggro during the duration, functioning similarly to Loki's Decoy ability. In solo content, Mischief's misdirection buys time to reposition or heal; in group content its contribution is less noticeable since enemy attention distributes across multiple players.

## Acquisition

Players obtain Smeeta Kavats by incubating Kavat Genetic Codes with the appropriate imprint. Scanning wild Kavats in Orokin Derelict missions yields codes, with each scan providing a Kavat Genetic Code item. At least two codes are required for incubation. Two Orokin Cells are also required as part of the incubation cost.

Alternatively, players can purchase Kavat imprints from other players through the trading system. Imprints encode visual traits and subspecies, so a Smeeta imprint will produce a Smeeta regardless of appearance variation.

## Recommended mods

**Precepts:** Charm occupies the primary precept slot. Mischief fills the second precept slot.

**Survivability:** Medi-Pet Kit provides health regeneration. Link Health scales the kavat's health with the player's own health pool, which can be substantial with warframe health mods. Animal Instinct provides loot and enemy radar.

## Playstyle and synergies

The Smeeta Kavat is the default recommendation for any resource farming mission. The doubled resource Charm proc effectively halves the number of runs required for rare drop farming when it activates, and even during missions where it does not proc, the other effects provide supplemental benefits. Players farming Eidolon Shards, Rare resources on specific planets, or Void Traces find the Smeeta's Charm indispensable.

Pairs well with Nekros's Desecrate or Khora's Pilfering Strangledome, as the Charm double-drop proc stacks with looting abilities, multiplying the effective yield per enemy kill.
