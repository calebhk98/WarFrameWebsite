## Overview

Djinn is a unique sentinel distinguished by two exceptional precepts: Fatal Attraction for enemy grouping and Reawaken for automatic self-revival. The Reawaken precept sets Djinn apart from all other sentinels by allowing it to revive itself after death without player intervention, on a cooldown timer. This self-reviving capability means Djinn sustains its active presence throughout missions even in high-damage environments that eliminate other sentinels permanently.

The Fatal Attraction precept magnetizes enemies and pulls them toward the sentinel, functioning as a companion crowd control tool that synergizes strongly with area-of-effect weapons and abilities. Players who use explosive weapons, area-denial frames, or melee builds benefit from enemies being gathered into a central cluster before they engage.

Djinn's higher build cost of 30,000 credits reflects its more complex mechanical design. The Stinger weapon fires toxin-infused projectiles that complement the companion's crowd-manipulation role.

## Abilities and precepts

**Fatal Attraction** emits a magnetic pulse in a radius around the sentinel, forcing nearby enemies to move toward its position. The pull duration and radius scale with the precept's rank. Because the sentinel moves with the player, Fatal Attraction can position clusters of enemies near the player's weapons or ability areas, maximizing the effectiveness of multi-target attacks. The precept has a cooldown between activations, so the timing of each pulse should align with the player's offensive windows.

**Reawaken** is Djinn's defining precept. When the sentinel is destroyed by enemy damage, Reawaken begins a countdown timer. After the timer expires -- approximately 90 seconds at maximum rank -- Djinn revives itself and resumes normal operation including Fatal Attraction pulses. No player action, revive tokens, or Sentinel Revives are consumed. In very high-level content where sentinels die quickly, Reawaken transforms Djinn into a persistent companion rather than a one-life tool.

The Stinger fires high-velocity toxin-damage projectiles, bypassing shields and dealing damage directly to enemy health. This makes it effective against Corpus enemies whose shield pools would otherwise reduce incoming damage.

## Acquisition

Djinn's blueprint is awarded upon completing The New Strange quest, which also unlocks the Cephalon Simaris system. Players who have already completed the quest can purchase the blueprint from the Market for 75,000 credits. Building requires 30,000 credits and 24 hours of Foundry time, reflecting the higher component complexity compared to other sentinels.

## Recommended mods

**Precepts:** Fatal Attraction occupies one precept slot for enemy grouping. Reawaken fills the second precept slot as the essential survival mechanic.

**Survivability:** Because Reawaken handles sentinel death passively, survivability mod priority is somewhat lower for Djinn compared to other sentinels. Medi-Pet Kit and Link Health still extend the time between deaths, reducing the frequency of Reawaken cooldowns.

**Optional:** Primed Animal Instinct provides enemy radar that helps identify approaching enemies before Fatal Attraction engages, giving the player more warning to prepare area attacks.

## Playstyle and synergies

Djinn's Reawaken precept makes it well-suited to extended missions like Survival, Defense, and Interception where other sentinels die and provide no further benefit. The sentinel's ability to revive itself repeatedly throughout a 60-minute Survival run maintains its utility for the entire duration, while other sentinels become inert after their first death in high-level content.

Fatal Attraction pairs with Warframes that use ground-targeted abilities: Saryn's Spores, Ember's Fire Blast, Khora's Strangledome, and Ash's Bladestorm all benefit from enemies gathered into proximity. The magnetism pull concentrates enemy density in the area of effect, increasing total damage delivered per cast.

The Stinger's toxin output complements builds that require poison procs for Gas or Viral damage combinations.
