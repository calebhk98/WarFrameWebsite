## Overview

The Helminth Charger is an Infested-type companion produced by the Helminth system aboard the Orbiter. Unlike Kavats and Kubrows, it is not incubated from genetic codes but rather grown from a matured Helminth Cyst drained in the Helminth Infirmary. Any Warframe can become infected with the Helminth Cyst; after seven days the cyst fully matures, and draining it via the Infirmary yields Helminth Charger imprints. The companion's biological design reflects its Infested origin -- a large, powerful frame with robust health and a mix of physical and toxin-based offensive abilities.

The Helminth Charger carries one of the higher base health pools among organic companions at 710, paired with 330 base shields and the standard 300 armor that all Kubrow-category companions share. Its offensive kit relies on physical charges that knock down enemies and toxin-based Maggot swarms that deal damage over time. For players who have progressed far enough to unlock the Helminth Segment, the Charger offers an alternative companion style rooted in Infested aesthetics and biology.

The companion's connection to the Helminth system makes it thematically relevant for players building around the Helminth's subsume mechanics, though the Charger itself does not interact mechanically with the subsuming system beyond sharing its source organism.

## Abilities and precepts

**Trample** is a charge attack in which the Helminth Charger accelerates forward and runs through enemies, dealing physical impact damage and applying knockdown to enemies hit during the charge. The ability covers substantial ground quickly, allowing the companion to engage enemies at distance from the player and disrupt enemy formations. The knockdown effect interrupts enemy attack animations, providing incidental crowd control.

**Maggots** releases a swarm of parasitic Helminth Maggots that target the nearest enemies. Each maggot burrows into an enemy and deals continuous toxin damage for a duration before detaching. Multiple maggots can infest the same target, stacking the toxin damage. Toxin damage ignores shields entirely, making the Maggot ability effective against Corpus enemies whose shield pools would otherwise reduce damage from other sources.

## Acquisition

Obtaining a Helminth Charger requires the Helminth Infirmary, accessible on the Orbiter after completing The Sacrifice quest. Any Warframe can be infected with the Helminth Cyst by visiting another player who has an infected frame. The cyst requires 7 days to fully mature, after which the player enters the Helminth Infirmary and drains the cyst. Draining produces Helminth Charger imprints. Two imprints and an Orokin Cell are required to incubate the companion in the standard Incubator Segment.

Helminth Charger imprints can also be traded between players, allowing those who do not own Nidus to potentially obtain imprints from the [warframe.market](https://warframe.market) trading system.

## Recommended mods

**Precepts:** Trample occupies one active precept slot for physical charge attacks. Maggots fills the second precept slot for toxin damage over time.

**Survivability:** Link Health is particularly effective for the Helminth Charger because its high base health responds well to percentage-based links from player health pools. Medi-Pet Kit covers regeneration between engagements. Metal Fiber adds further armor on top of the base 300.

## Playstyle and synergies

The Helminth Charger functions as an aggressive melee companion that charges into enemy groups and applies sustained toxin damage. It pairs naturally with toxin-focused builds and frames that want supplemental poison procs for Gas damage synergies.

For players invested in Nidus-specific content, the Helminth Charger provides thematic consistency and offers a companion whose biology matches the frame's design philosophy. In practical terms, it fills a role similar to other offensive Kubrow-category companions but with a toxin-focused damage profile.

The companion's higher health pool compared to Kavats means it can sustain more hits before going down, making it slightly more durable in mid-level content without extensive survival modding.
