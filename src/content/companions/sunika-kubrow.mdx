## Overview

The Sunika Kubrow is an aggressive combat companion specialized in engaging high-value targets. Its Unleashed ability specifically targets "VIPs and powerful enemies" -- a category that includes mission objective targets, captains, and faction heavy units -- with a brutal takedown that pins the target and deals burst damage. The Savagery ability temporarily enters a frenzy state that amplifies all damage output, making the Sunika capable of meaningful damage contribution during ability windows.

Among the Kubrow subspecies, the Sunika has the second-highest base health at 830, behind only the Sahasa's 940. Combined with the standard 300 armor, the Sunika is a durable companion that can survive prolonged combat engagements without requiring constant healing support. Its shield pool of 350 sits in the mid-range for Kubrows.

Players who prefer an aggressive companion that actively attacks priority targets and contributes to enemy elimination find the Sunika the most combat-capable of the Kubrow variants. The combination of targeted high-damage attacks and a frenzy damage boost gives the Sunika more combat relevance at lower content tiers than utility companions like the Sahasa.

## Abilities and precepts

**Unleashed** identifies high-priority enemy targets -- specifically heavy units, mission objective enemies, and named targets -- and performs a powerful melee takedown. The takedown animation pins the enemy on the ground for several seconds, immobilizing it while the Sunika deals repeated melee hits. Against mission objective targets that must be kept alive (such as Hostage missions), Unleashed avoids lethal outcomes when the target is a captive rather than a kill objective. The ability's targeting priority on heavies makes it most useful in Grineer or Infested missions where durable heavy units appear frequently.

**Savagery** activates on a cooldown timer and boosts the Sunika's damage output by a percentage multiplier for the duration. All subsequent melee strikes during Savagery deal increased damage, compounding the Unleashed ability's burst potential when both activate during the same engagement.

## Acquisition

Sunika Kubrows require a Kubrow Egg from Earth dens, the Incubator Upgrade Segment, and Sunika imprints to guarantee the subspecies. Applying two Sunika imprints during incubation locks the subspecies selection. Imprints can be obtained from other players through the trading system.

## Recommended mods

**Precepts:** Unleashed fills one active precept slot for the high-priority target takedown. Savagery fills the second slot for the frenzy damage boost.

**Survivability:** Link Health scales with the Sunika's already-high base health. Medi-Pet Kit handles regeneration. Metal Fiber provides additional armor stacking.

**Damage:** Players who want to maximize the Sunika's combat contribution can equip Bite and other companion melee-damage mods in the non-precept slots, amplifying both standard strikes and Unleashed's damage output.

## Playstyle and synergies

The Sunika Kubrow provides combat utility in missions that feature distinct high-priority targets. Capture missions, Assassination missions, and any mission type with heavy Grineer spawns give the Unleashed ability regular targets to pin. The takedown mechanic effectively crowd controls the heaviest enemy in the area while dealing damage, reducing the threat level of the most dangerous targets without requiring the player to expend ability energy.

The Sunika pairs well with melee-focused frames where the companion's damage contribution supplements the player's own close-combat output. Frames like Excalibur, Valkyr, or Wukong who engage in melee combat frequently maintain proximity to enemies that the Sunika can also attack, maximizing both Unleashed activation opportunities and Savagery DPS windows.

Against pure damage benchmarks, the Sunika falls behind companion-assisted farming options, but among combat-oriented companions it represents the most functionally complete Kubrow variant.
