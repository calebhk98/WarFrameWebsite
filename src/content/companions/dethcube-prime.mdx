## Overview

Dethcube Prime is the Prime variant of the Dethcube sentinel, designed for players who want their companion to contribute meaningfully to combat rather than serving purely in a utility role. Its defining characteristic is the Energy Generator precept, which spawns energy orbs whenever the sentinel registers a kill. This passive energy production makes Dethcube Prime attractive for ability-reliant frames that struggle to maintain their energy economy without external support.

The sentinel employs the Deth Machine Rifle Prime as its weapon, a beam weapon with high sustained damage potential. The Fired Up precept amplifies this weapon's output with each consecutive hit, creating a ramp-up mechanic that rewards the sentinel's engagement against single durable targets. At lower mastery levels and in content where enemy density is high, the combination of precepts keeps energy reserves healthy throughout extended missions.

Dethcube Prime carries superior base statistics compared to the base Dethcube, with higher armor and an expanded energy pool of 125 that supports more frequent precept activations.

## Abilities and precepts

**Energy Generator** is the sentinel's passive ability and primary draw. Each time Dethcube Prime eliminates an enemy, an energy orb spawns at the kill location. In practice, the kill rate depends on enemy health and the Deth Machine Rifle Prime's damage output, making this more reliable in early to mid-tier content. In high-level Steel Path missions, the sentinel struggles to secure kills independently, reducing the orb generation rate.

**Vaporize** activates the Deth Machine Rifle Prime's beam attack, directing a sustained energy stream at enemies within range. The precept handles targeting automatically, cycling to the nearest enemy. The beam's damage profile benefits from the Fired Up precept when the sentinel maintains continuous fire on a single target long enough to accumulate stacks.

**Fired Up** applies a stacking damage buff to the sentinel's weapon. Each consecutive second of sustained fire on a valid target increases the weapon's damage output. The buff resets when the target dies or the beam breaks, so the precept rewards engagement against tankier enemies over rapid switching between weak targets.

## Acquisition

Dethcube Prime drops from Void relics across Lith, Meso, and Neo tiers. Players should verify current relic assignments through the [Warframe Wiki](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Dethcube_Prime) as relic pools shift with Prime rotations. The blueprint requires 15,000 credits and 24 hours in the Foundry after all components are assembled.

## Recommended mods

**Core survival:** Medi-Pet Kit, Link Health, and Metal Fiber cover the sentinel's durability, allowing it to survive moderate enemy fire long enough to contribute kills and energy orbs.

**Precepts:** Energy Generator occupies the passive precept slot as the primary reason to choose Dethcube Prime. Fired Up amplifies the weapon's kill potential when combined with Vaporize on tankier targets.

**Zenurik alternative:** Players running the Zenurik focus school for energy regeneration may find Dethcube Prime's energy generation redundant; in those setups, Carrier Prime's utility precepts often provide more net value. Dethcube Prime excels with frames that lack innate energy solutions, such as early-game configurations without Arcane Energize.

## Playstyle and synergies

Dethcube Prime serves frames that depend heavily on abilities and lack built-in energy generation. It functions best in mid-difficulty content where the sentinel can consistently achieve kills and trigger Energy Generator. In very low-level content the energy gain is redundant, and in very high-level content the sentinel rarely kills enemies before they are eliminated by player weapons or abilities.

Frames with high-cost abilities that cannot subsume Dispensary or equip Zenurik benefit the most from this sentinel. The pairing with energy-hungry frames like Saryn in mid-tier Survival missions demonstrates the synergy well: Dethcube Prime maintains the energy pool while the frame handles large enemy counts with expensive abilities.

The energy orbs spawn at the enemy's location rather than the player's position, so the benefit decreases in large open maps where kill locations are distant from the player.
