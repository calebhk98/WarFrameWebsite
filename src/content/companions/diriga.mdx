## Overview

Diriga is a combat-focused sentinel with a high base health pool of 700 and a pair of offensive precepts that prioritize electrical damage. It differs from utility-focused sentinels like Carrier or Helios by orienting its entire design toward active enemy engagement. The Electro Pulse precept fires electrical attacks that chain between multiple enemies, while the Verglas weapon fires cold-infused projectiles that complement the status proc theme.

Players who want their companion to function as an active damage dealer rather than a passive support tool find Diriga's kit appealing. The Electric status procs from Electro Pulse chain through groups of enemies, applying the Shocked status that briefly stuns affected targets. Combined with the cold procs from Verglas, Diriga can apply two elemental status effects simultaneously, contributing to status-heavy build strategies.

Diriga's shield pool is lower than most sentinels at 150, making its high health pool and armor the primary survival mechanism. The low shield means status effects that bypass shields affect Diriga more than they affect shield-heavy sentinels.

## Abilities and precepts

**Electro Pulse** releases an electrical discharge that applies the Electric status effect to enemies within the sentinel's operational range. The Electric status chains damage to nearby enemies with a chance proportional to the status proc rate, making it effective in dense enemy clusters where chain reactions compound the damage output. The stunned state from Electric procs briefly interrupts enemy actions, providing incidental crowd control.

**Comet** fires a single high-damage projectile at a targeted enemy, providing burst damage against isolated high-priority targets. The precept supplements Verglas' sustained fire with a higher-damage option for tougher enemies.

The Verglas sentinel weapon fires frozen crystalline projectiles with cold elemental damage. Cold procs apply the Chilled status effect, which reduces enemy movement speed and increases the base chance for other status procs to trigger on affected enemies. This synergizes with Electro Pulse's chaining behavior, as slowed enemies remain in the chain radius longer.

## Acquisition

Diriga can be purchased via the in-game Market for 35,000 credits (blueprint only) or obtained through Nightwave season offerings when available. Building requires 15,000 credits and 24 hours per component. Players who have not progressed far enough in the game to access the Market independently can also check Nightwave Credit offerings during active seasons.

## Recommended mods

**Precepts:** Electro Pulse fills one precept slot for its chaining Electric damage. Comet occupies the second precept slot for burst single-target damage.

**Survivability:** Medi-Pet Kit and Link Health compensate for Diriga's moderate armor and low shield. Unlike shield-heavy sentinels, Diriga relies on its health pool and regeneration to sustain through combat.

**Weapon mods:** The Verglas benefits from cold-enhancing mods and critical chance mods when paired with appropriate sentinel weapon slots.

## Playstyle and synergies

Diriga functions as an active combat companion, contributing status procs and supplemental damage throughout missions. It suits status-heavy player builds where companion procs contribute to the overall proc count required for Condition Overload or similar mod mechanics.

The Electric chaining from Electro Pulse is particularly valuable in Void missions and against Corpus enemies with shield-based defenses, where the Magnetic interaction with Electric can further reduce enemy effectiveness. In Grineer missions, cold procs from Verglas slow armored heavy units, making them easier to manage.

Players who run Viral-based weapons find Diriga's electrical and cold outputs less directly useful, while those who incorporate Magnetic or Radiation builds can make the procs contribute meaningfully.
