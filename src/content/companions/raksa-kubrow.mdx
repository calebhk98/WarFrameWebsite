## Overview

The Raksa Kubrow is a defensive support companion whose Protect ability provides emergency shield restoration when the player's shields are depleted. In shield-dependent builds and early-to-mid content where shields form a meaningful portion of the player's effective health, the Protect ability's one-time reactive refill can prevent health damage from a burst of incoming fire. Combined with the Howl crowd control ability, the Raksa offers a defensive companion option that prioritizes player survival over companion damage output.

Kubrows are incubated from Kubrow Eggs obtained from Kubrow dens in Earth missions. The egg, combined with the appropriate imprint, determines the subspecies during incubation. Raksa imprints produce the Raksa subspecies with its defensive precepts. Players who value the shield recovery mechanic or who play frames with high shield pools find the Raksa a relevant companion choice.

The Raksa's base stats reflect the Kubrow archetype: high health and the standard 300 armor, with a moderately high shield pool of 390. This shield pool is the third highest among the Kubrow subspecies, complementing its defensive orientation.

## Abilities and precepts

**Protect** monitors the player's shield value and activates when shields reach zero. The ability instantly restores the player's shields to their full value and then enters a cooldown period before it can trigger again. The speed of the restoration means it can prevent health damage from attacks that would have hit in the window after shields collapsed. The cooldown after activation limits it to emergency coverage rather than sustained support.

**Howl** causes the Raksa to release a sonic attack that applies the Fear status to nearby enemies, forcing them to run away from the source of the sound for the ability's duration. Panicked enemies cannot attack during the flee animation, providing temporary crowd control. The Howl's range and duration determine how effective it is at clearing immediate threats from the player's vicinity.

The Raksa's three mod slots include a vazarin polarity, which accommodates defensive or support mods at reduced cost.

## Acquisition

Kubrow Eggs drop from Kubrow dens in Earth missions -- nests of grass and leaves that the player interacts with to trigger a Kubrow attack. The eggs are uncommon drops from these dens and may require multiple mission runs to acquire. Once an egg is obtained, the player installs it in the Incubator with a Raksa imprint and an Orokin Cell to begin incubation.

Raksa imprints can be traded between players; purchasing imprints from the trading system provides the subspecies without requiring players to incubate randomly or breed specifically for Raksa traits.

## Recommended mods

**Precepts:** Protect occupies the primary precept slot for its shield restoration function. Howl fills the second slot for crowd control.

**Survivability:** Link Health and Medi-Pet Kit are standard survival mods. The vazarin polarity slot accommodates a companion shield mod at reduced cost.

**Shield builds:** On frames like Hildryn or Mag with large shield pools, the Protect ability restores substantial amounts of shield value per activation, making the Raksa more valuable than on health-tank frames where shields are a minor consideration.

## Playstyle and synergies

The Raksa Kubrow suits defensive playstyles and shield-reliant frames. For players using [Hildryn](/warframes/hildryn) or other frames whose abilities scale with shields, the Protect ability restores a larger effective buffer with each activation. Paired with shield regeneration mods and Arcane Guardian for armor, the Raksa contributes to a layered defensive system.

Howl's Fear effect provides incidental crowd control that can break enemy targeting and allow the player to break line of sight or reposition. In situations where enemy groups are pressing close, the Howl scatter effect gives a few seconds of safe window.

The Raksa is a niche pick compared to farming-oriented companions like the Smeeta Kavat, but for players focused on defensive gameplay it represents a thoughtful companion choice.
