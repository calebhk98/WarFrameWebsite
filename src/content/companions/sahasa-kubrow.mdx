## Overview

The Sahasa Kubrow has the highest base health among all Kubrow subspecies at 940, making it the most durable Kubrow companion in terms of raw hit points. Its Dig precept causes the companion to periodically excavate items during combat, providing a passive supplement to the player's resource income without requiring any player action. The Ferocity precept amplifies the Sahasa's own melee strikes, giving it a modest combat contribution alongside its digging utility.

The Sahasa's health dominance over other Kubrows -- 940 versus the next highest at 830 for the Sunika -- means it survives longer in high-damage environments with equivalent modding. Players who want a Kubrow companion that contributes to loot income while being difficult to kill find the Sahasa a practical choice.

Unlike the Smeeta Kavat's doubled resource Charm proc, which affects all pickups for a duration, the Sahasa's Dig ability generates new items rather than multiplying existing drops. The generated items include a variety of resource types, credits, and occasionally higher-value materials, providing a steady supplemental income stream.

## Abilities and precepts

**Dig** activates during brief pauses in active combat when the Kubrow is not engaged with enemies. The companion lowers its head and paws at the ground, producing an item drop at the dig location after a short animation. The item type is randomly selected from a loot pool that includes common resources, credits, and rare components. The Dig behavior is autonomous -- the player receives the item without moving to the dig site, as the Kubrow brings resources to the player or drops them at a collectable distance.

**Ferocity** temporarily boosts the Sahasa's melee attack power during engagements, increasing the damage of its physical strikes against enemies. The ability activates on a periodic internal cooldown and provides a flat damage multiplier for its duration. At lower enemy levels, the Ferocity-boosted attacks can contribute meaningful chip damage; at very high levels, the scaling falls behind enemy health pools.

## Acquisition

The Sahasa Kubrow requires a Kubrow Egg from Earth Kubrow dens, the Incubator Upgrade Segment, a Sahasa-subspecies imprint, and an Orokin Cell. Random incubation without a Sahasa imprint will produce a random subspecies; two Sahasa imprints must be applied to guarantee the subspecies. Imprints can be purchased from other players through the in-game trading system.

## Recommended mods

**Precepts:** Dig occupies the primary precept slot. Ferocity fills the second slot for the combat damage boost.

**Survivability:** The Sahasa's high health pool makes Link Health extremely effective. Medi-Pet Kit covers health regeneration between damage bursts. Metal Fiber stacks additional armor on the base 300.

**Loot support:** Primed Animal Instinct extends both loot radar and enemy radar, complementing the Sahasa's loot-generation role by helping the player identify additional resource locations on the minimap.

## Playstyle and synergies

The Sahasa works best in missions where combat pauses occur naturally, such as Excavation, Mobile Defense, or any mission with objective wait times. During these pauses, the Dig behavior activates and generates additional items. In continuous combat missions like endless Survival, the Sahasa finds fewer opportunities to Dig, reducing its effective loot contribution.

For credits-focused farming, the Sahasa pairs with Credit Booster purchases or Credit-multiplying frame abilities. The Dig loot pool includes credit drops, and any credit multipliers in effect during the dig apply to credit items uncovered.

Players who prefer the Smeeta Kavat for resource farming will generally maintain that preference, as the Charm double-drop proc provides more concentrated returns. The Sahasa instead provides steady baseline supplemental income across longer missions while offering the highest Kubrow health pool for durability.
