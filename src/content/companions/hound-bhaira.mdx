## Overview

The Bhaira Hound is one of the robotic Corpus companion variants obtained through the Sister of Parvos system, a parallel to the Kuva Lich system that targets Corpus-aligned players. Sisters of Parvos spawn with a Hound companion at their side, and players who successfully convert or vanquish a Sister can claim her Hound as their own companion. The specific Hound type depends on the Sister's spawning conditions and the Corpus weapon she carries.

Hounds are bipedal robotic companions similar in silhouette to Corpus MOAs but with distinct ability sets and a different acquisition path. Unlike MOAs assembled from component parts, Hounds are obtained directly from the Sister system and cannot be configured with different component combinations. Each Hound type -- Bhaira, Dorrclave, Hec, and Sentry -- has a distinct set of built-in abilities that define its combat and utility role.

The Bhaira Hound's abilities focus on self-amplification through Overclock and physical combat through Hound Bite. Base health, shield, armor, and energy values are not present in the WFCD dataset for Hounds; these values are marked unknown per project data policy.

## Abilities and precepts

**Overclock** applies a temporary buff to the Hound's own systems, increasing its movement speed, attack speed, and overall combat contribution. During Overclock, the Hound engages enemies more rapidly and covers distance more quickly, improving its ability to intercept threats. The duration and boost magnitude scale with the ability's rank and any applicable companion mods.

**Hound Bite** executes a lunging physical attack against the nearest enemy. The bite deals heavy physical damage and can interrupt the target's attack animation through a stagger effect. The attack has a moderately short range and requires the Hound to be within melee distance of its target, making it most effective in close-range engagements where enemies press toward the player.

Unlike MOA companions which rely on their head component for ability selection, Hound abilities are fixed by the Hound type and cannot be changed by replacing parts.

## Acquisition

Bhaira Hounds are obtained through the Sister of Parvos system. Players must access the Railjack star chart and engage with Corpus Proxima missions to spawn a Sister Candidate, then allow the Candidate to kill a player and become a Sister. The Sister carries a Hound companion, and the Hound type is determined by the Sister's spawning parameters. After defeating the Sister through her Requiem sequence, the player can choose to convert her (acquiring her weapon and Hound) or vanquish her (acquiring only her weapon).

To obtain a specific Hound type, players must engage with multiple Sister cycles until the desired Hound appears as the Sister's companion.

## Recommended mods

**Survivability:** Medi-Pet Kit, Link Health, and Metal Fiber provide the standard companion survival foundation. Hounds lack the Pack Leader self-revive, so survival modding determines mission persistence.

**Utility:** Primed Animal Instinct provides enemy and loot radar. The Hound's non-configurable ability set means all mod slots are available for survival and utility, similar to the Vulpaphyla's built-in ability advantage.

## Playstyle and synergies

The Bhaira Hound suits players invested in the Sister of Parvos system who have obtained a Sister carrying this specific Hound type. Its Overclock self-buff makes it temporarily more aggressive during combat, and the Hound Bite provides reliable melee pressure on nearby targets.

Hounds in general benefit from builds that keep the companion close to the player, as their melee-oriented abilities require proximity to enemies that are also near the player. Area-denial frames and frames that cluster enemies through crowd control create the density that allows Hound abilities to activate frequently.
