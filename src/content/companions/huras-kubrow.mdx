## Overview

The Huras Kubrow is a stealth-oriented companion whose Stalk ability mimics the Shade sentinel's Ghost precept in function: it applies an invisibility cloak to the player when enemies approach within detection range. This reactive cloaking makes the Huras valuable for stealth-adjacent playstyles, providing an automatic invisibility fallback during missions that benefit from avoiding detection but do not require constant active stealth.

Unlike the Shade Prime sentinel, the Huras also provides active combat utility through its Hunt ability, which pins enemies to the ground with a pounce attack. The combination of passive cloaking and active immobilization gives the Huras a more complete kit than the stealth-only Shade, at the cost of occupying the companion slot with an organic pet rather than a sentinel.

The Huras carries the highest base shield of any Kubrow at 490 and a correspondingly lower health pool of 560, making its survival dependent more on shield mitigation and regeneration than raw health. Players who mod for shield-linked survival find the Huras responds well to Link Shield mods.

## Abilities and precepts

**Stalk** functions as a passive detection-based cloak. When an enemy enters proximity without having already spotted the player, Stalk applies invisibility to both the player and the Kubrow. The cloak remains active for a duration unless broken by the player attacking or casting an ability. Like the Shade sentinel's Ghost precept, Stalk has a cooldown before it can activate again, preventing continuous cloaking. The cloak duration and detection radius scale with the precept's rank.

**Hunt** directs the Huras to leap at and pin an enemy in melee range, holding it immobile on the ground while dealing melee damage. The pinned enemy cannot attack or move during the hold duration, providing effective single-target crowd control. The pin breaks when the hold duration expires or the enemy dies. Hunt prioritizes nearby enemies and activates on its own targeting cycle without player direction.

## Acquisition

Huras Kubrows require a Kubrow Egg from Earth missions' Kubrow dens, the Incubator Upgrade Segment, and Huras imprints. Two Huras imprints applied during incubation guarantee the subspecies. Imprints can be acquired through the player trading system.

## Recommended mods

**Precepts:** Stalk fills the primary precept slot for its passive cloaking function. Hunt fills the second slot for the pounce and pin combat ability.

**Survivability:** Link Shield scales with the player's shield pool and is particularly effective given the Huras's high base shields. Medi-Pet Kit covers health regeneration. Metal Fiber provides armor stacking over the base 300.

**Stealth builds:** Players who combine the Huras with stealth-augmenting mods and frames can achieve high uptime on the Stalk cloak during pauses in combat, allowing mobility through enemy areas with reduced detection.

## Playstyle and synergies

The Huras Kubrow excels in missions that reward stealth or have significant downtime between combat encounters. Spy missions with patrols, Capture missions requiring approach of a target without triggering alarms, and any mission with optional stealth bonus objectives benefit from Stalk's reactive cloaking.

The Huras pairs naturally with frames that have temporary stealth windows, using Stalk as a fallback for the moments between ability activations when the player is briefly visible. Ash and Ivara, whose stealth is ability-gated, find the Huras most complementary, as the Kubrow's Stalk covers gaps in invisibility coverage without consuming ability energy.

For players who want stealth capability without committing to a stealth-focused frame, the Huras provides a companion-slot solution at the cost of other utility options.
