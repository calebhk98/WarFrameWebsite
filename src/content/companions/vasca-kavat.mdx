## Overview

The Vasca Kavat is a vampiric subspecies of Kavat found exclusively in the Plains of Eidolon during nighttime hours. Unlike the Smeeta and Adarza Kavats, which are incubated from codes obtained in the Orokin Derelict, the Vasca is sourced from the Plains of Eidolon open world and carries abilities that reflect its predatory, blood-draining nature. Its Draining Bite ability steals health from enemies and returns a portion to the player, providing a life-steal mechanic that functions as passive healing during combat.

The Vasca shares the same base statistics as the Adarza Kavat -- 310 health, 270 shield, and 300 armor -- making it the lower health-pool Kavat variant. For players who prioritize companion durability, the lower health requires more investment in Link Health mods to achieve survivability equivalent to the Kubrow variants. However, the Vasca's unique life-steal mechanic provides a source of player health restoration not available from the other Kavat options.

Vasca Kavats also have a unique infection mechanic: contact with a Vasca's bite in the Plains of Eidolon can infect an existing Kavat companion with the Vasca mutation, converting it. This infection path allows players who already own a Kavat to convert it without farming codes, though this method requires exposing the companion to Vasca attack.

## Abilities and precepts

**Draining Bite** directs the Vasca to attack a target with a life-draining bite that deals melee damage and siphons a percentage of the damage dealt back as health restoration for the player. The heal percentage scales with the ability's rank and the damage actually dealt. Against tankier enemies with high health pools, Draining Bite's heal amount can be substantial; against low-health enemies, the healing return is minimal. The ability activates on a cooldown cycle independent of the Vasca's standard attack behavior.

**Retaliation** causes the Vasca to direct a portion of incoming damage back at the attacker as reflected damage. The reflected damage scales with the ability's rank. At higher enemy levels, the reflected damage is insignificant compared to enemy health pools, but the ability provides a small passive DPS contribution in lower-to-mid content.

## Acquisition

Vasca Kavat Genetic Codes are obtained by scanning Vasca Kavats in the Plains of Eidolon during nighttime hours. The Plains' day-night cycle is timed, so players must arrive during the night phase to encounter Vasca Kavats in the wild. At least two codes and a Orokin Cell are required for incubation with the Vasca imprint.

The infection route requires taking an existing Kavat into the Plains during a night phase and allowing a wild Vasca to bite the companion. This temporarily infects the Kavat; if the player does not cure the infection, the companion converts to the Vasca subspecies over time.

## Recommended mods

**Precepts:** Draining Bite occupies the primary precept slot for the life-steal mechanic. Retaliation fills the secondary slot.

**Survivability:** Link Health is critical for the Vasca's low health pool. Medi-Pet Kit provides between-engagement regeneration. The Vasca's life-steal does not heal itself, making external healing mods necessary.

**Healing builds:** Frames with moderate health-regen needs benefit from the Draining Bite sustain. Frames that already have extensive health management through other means gain less incremental value from the Vasca's healing.

## Playstyle and synergies

The Vasca Kavat suits health-tank builds that lack built-in healing regeneration and want a companion that provides continuous life-steal income during sustained combat. In long Survival or Defense missions where the player repeatedly takes damage, Draining Bite activations accumulate into meaningful health restoration over the mission duration.

The Vasca's life-steal pairs naturally with health-stacking frames like Inaros or Nidus, where additional health restoration extends the effective health pool further. For shield-reliant frames, the heal returns to health rather than shields, making the Vasca most valuable in builds that maintain shields separately from health management.

Players who use the Adarza Kavat for critical chance or the Smeeta Kavat for farming will find fewer direct overlaps with the Vasca's kit, which occupies a distinct healing support niche.
