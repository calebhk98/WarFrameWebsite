## Overview

Carrier Prime is the Orokin-era Prime variant of the Carrier sentinel, distinguished by its superior base statistics and expanded mod capacity. As one of the most popular sentinels in Warframe, Carrier Prime earns its place in the majority of builds through the Vacuum precept, which automatically pulls all nearby pickups toward the player. This passive collection mechanic eliminates the constant repositioning required in other builds, allowing players to focus entirely on combat and ability management.

The sentinel traces its lineage back to the earliest Orokin automation designs, refined through generations of engineering to produce a companion that prioritizes utility above all else. Its combat precept, Assault Mode, provides supplemental firepower through the Sweeper Prime weapon, which handles crowd clearing at close range while the player engages at distance. The Ammo Case precept converts excess ammo from other weapon slots into the active weapon's ammunition type, a quality-of-life improvement that matters most in long survival or defense missions.

## Abilities and precepts

Carrier Prime equips two active precepts and one passive precept in its default configuration.

**Ammo Case** monitors the player's ammunition reserves and converts surplus ammo from secondary or other weapon pools into ammunition for the currently active weapon. This precept activates automatically when the active weapon's magazine drops below a threshold, sustaining continued fire without requiring the player to hunt for ammo pickups of the correct type. It pairs especially well with weapons that consume large quantities of rounds per second.

**Assault Mode** directs Carrier Prime to engage enemies within proximity using its Sweeper Prime weapon. The precept causes the sentinel to target enemies independently, providing chip damage that contributes to status application and relieves some combat pressure from the player. At higher enemy levels the damage contribution becomes marginal, but the precept's real value lies in its ability to apply status procs that synergize with player weapons.

**Vacuum** is the passive precept that defines Carrier Prime's role in virtually every content type. The precept creates a pull radius of 12 meters around the player, drawing all item pickups -- ammo, energy orbs, health orbs, and resource drops -- toward the player automatically. Before Vacuum existed as a universal precept option, Carrier was the only sentinel that provided this effect, cementing its dominance. Even after its expansion, Carrier Prime remains the most common choice because it combines Vacuum with an active weapon and ammo sustain in a single companion slot.

## Stats

Carrier Prime's base statistics represent a significant improvement over the standard Carrier. The higher armor value of 150 versus 80 substantially increases effective health against physical damage types. The expanded shield pool provides additional buffer against ranged attacks before health erosion begins.

The sentinel occupies six mod slots with the following default polarities: four penjaga, one madurai, and one vazarin. This polarity spread accommodates the precept mods at no additional capacity cost while leaving room for companion health, shield, and armor mods.

## Acquisition

Carrier Prime components drop from Void relics across multiple tiers. The main blueprint drops from Axi and Lith relics, while component blueprints distribute across Lith, Meso, and Neo tiers. Players should consult the current relic drop tables on the [Warframe Wiki](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Carrier_Prime) for the most up-to-date relic assignments, as these shift with Prime rotations.

The complete construction costs 15,000 credits and takes 24 hours of build time in the Foundry. Components must be built individually before the main Carrier Prime can be assembled. If Carrier Prime is in the Prime Vault, its relics will not appear in the current Void fissure reward pool; unvaulting events reintroduce the relics for limited periods.

## Recommended mods

The standard approach to modding Carrier Prime prioritizes survivability mods in the companion slots alongside the two precept mods.

**Survivability:** Medi-Pet Kit provides a regeneration effect, Link Health mirrors a percentage of the player's health onto the sentinel, and Metal Fiber boosts armor. These three mods form the core survival package for all sentinel builds.

**Precepts:** Vacuum (passive pull) and either Assault Mode or Ammo Case occupy two precept slots. Most players run both precept mods but prioritize Vacuum as the essential slot.

**Optional:** Primed Animal Instinct provides loot radar and enemy radar, enhancing map awareness. Verglas and other sentinel weapons can be swapped for the Sweeper Prime if a different damage profile is needed.

## Playstyle and synergies

Carrier Prime fits naturally into almost any playstyle because it requires no adjustment to player behavior. The Vacuum precept handles item collection passively, and Ammo Case reduces the mental load of ammunition management. Players who use high-fire-rate weapons benefit the most from Ammo Case, as these weapons exhaust ammo reserves faster than drop rates replenish them.

In solo play, Carrier Prime is the default recommendation for virtually all mission types. In group play, its benefit diminishes slightly since teammates can apply their own Vacuum effects, but the Ammo Case precept remains uniquely valuable for sustained fire weapons. Carrier Prime pairs well with frames that spawn many enemies in close proximity, such as Khora or Nekros, where the concentration of drops makes Vacuum's collection radius highly productive.

The sentinel's combat contribution should not be the primary consideration when choosing it; its value is almost entirely utilitarian.
