## Overview

Helios Prime is the Prime variant of the Helios sentinel, purpose-built for players who want to complete the Codex without manual scanning effort. Its Investigator precept uses Codex Scanner charges to automatically scan unscanned enemies and environmental objects, filling Codex entries passively as the player progresses through missions. This makes Helios Prime the default choice for players trying to complete the Codex or unlock Synthesis targets for Cephalon Simaris.

Beyond its scanning role, Helios Prime equips the Deconstructor Prime as its weapon, a thrown melee weapon with a unique damage profile. The Detect Vulnerability precept marks a specific weak point on fully scanned enemies, providing a bonus damage opportunity that complements certain weapon builds focused on precision fire.

Helios Prime's elevated health pool of 700 and solid shield of 300 make it one of the more durable scanning-oriented sentinels, though its armor of 100 sits below the Prime average. Players who want both scanning utility and Codex efficiency find Helios Prime indispensable during their first playthrough of the game.

## Abilities and precepts

**Investigator** is Helios Prime's primary function. When the sentinel detects an unscanned entity within its operational range, it expends one Codex Scanner charge to register the scan. The sentinel draws from the player's Codex Scanner inventory, so maintaining a supply of scanner charges is necessary for the precept to function. Synthesis Scanners can substitute if installed via the precept configuration.

**Detect Vulnerability** activates after the sentinel has completed enough scans of a particular enemy type to fill its Codex entry. Once fully scanned, enemies of that type will display a highlighted body part when Helios Prime is equipped, indicating a location where attacks deal amplified damage. The bonus damage percentage scales with the level of the Detect Vulnerability precept mod.

The Deconstructor Prime melee weapon functions differently from other sentinel weapons. Rather than firing projectiles, it throws itself at nearby enemies and returns, dealing melee-type damage on each hit. This allows the weapon to benefit from melee-type mods such as Pressure Point, making it one of the higher-damage sentinel weapons available.

## Acquisition

Helios Prime components drop from Neo and Axi tier Void relics. The main blueprint and component blueprints distribute across multiple specific relics; players should consult the current drop tables on the [Warframe Wiki](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Helios_Prime) for accurate relic names and rarities, as these assignments change with Prime vault rotations. Building the sentinel requires 15,000 credits and 24 hours per component assembly.

## Recommended mods

**Scanning support:** Investigator occupies the primary precept slot. Detect Vulnerability fills the secondary precept slot for the damage bonus on fully scanned targets.

**Weapon mods:** Because Deconstructor Prime uses melee-type damage, mods like Pressure Point, Spoiled Strike, and elemental mods for melee apply directly to it. This contrasts with other sentinel weapons that use ranged-type mod compatibility.

**Survivability:** Medi-Pet Kit, Link Health, and Link Armor bring the sentinel's durability in line with enemy damage at mid and high tiers. These three mods are standard across most sentinel builds.

## Playstyle and synergies

Helios Prime excels during Codex completion and Synthesis target hunting. Players who have not yet finished the Codex should equip Helios Prime for all missions until scanning is complete, as the automation saves significant time compared to manual scanning. Once the Codex is complete, Helios Prime's unique value diminishes unless Detect Vulnerability provides a meaningful damage bonus for the player's current build.

For Cephalon Simaris bounties requiring Synthesis Scanner usage, the Investigator precept substitutes Synthesis Scanners as well, though players should verify the current interaction status on the wiki since precept behavior with Synthesis targets has varied across game updates.

Helios Prime pairs well with precision-focused weapon setups where the Detect Vulnerability weak-point bonus provides a consistent critical hit location. Snipers and semi-auto rifles benefit from a marked target that guides aim toward high-damage zones.
