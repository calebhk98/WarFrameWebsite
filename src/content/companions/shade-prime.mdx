## Overview

Shade Prime is the Prime variant of the Shade sentinel, offering a reactive defensive utility through its Ghost precept. When an enemy approaches within a configurable detection range, Shade Prime cloaks both itself and the player, granting temporary invisibility. This defensive capability makes Shade Prime appealing for stealth-oriented builds and for players who want a last-resort invisibility trigger when they are spotted in a difficult situation.

Unlike Carrier Prime, which offers passive collection utility, Shade Prime demands more attentive gameplay. The Ghost cloak breaks immediately if the player fires a weapon or activates an ability, so the sentinel's value depends on the player's ability to hold their action long enough for threats to pass or for the cloak duration to buy positioning time. Frames with short cooldown invisibility abilities rarely benefit from Shade Prime's Ghost, but those without any innate stealth option gain meaningful protection from it.

The Burst Laser Prime weapon provides reliable ranged damage output, though the Revenge precept that directs it is reactive rather than proactive, activating only after the player takes damage.

## Abilities and precepts

**Ghost** is Shade Prime's signature precept. When an unalerted enemy enters the sentinel's detection radius, Ghost applies an invisibility effect to the player for several seconds. The detection radius and cloak duration are fixed by the precept's rank, not by the player's power strength or duration. Killing the enemy that triggered Ghost while invisible does not break the effect; only attacking with weapons or casting abilities ends it prematurely. The precept has a cooldown after each activation, preventing continuous cloaking.

**Revenge** directs the Burst Laser Prime at any enemy that deals damage to the player. The sentinel will prioritize the attacker over other targets, providing a focused response to the most immediate threat. In practice, Revenge functions as a distraction rather than a damage source against high-level enemies, but the targeting behavior ensures the weapon's fire is directed at meaningful threats rather than random nearby enemies.

The six mod slots include a naramon polarity, which accommodates the Ghost precept mod at reduced capacity cost. The additional slot count compared to many sentinels provides more flexibility for survival and utility mods.

## Acquisition

Shade Prime components drop from Lith and Meso tier Void relics. Current relic assignments and drop rarities appear on the [Warframe Wiki](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Shade_Prime). Building requires 15,000 credits and 24 hours of Foundry time per component.

## Recommended mods

**Precepts:** Ghost at maximum rank extends the detection range and cloak duration significantly. Revenge fills the second precept slot for reactive targeting.

**Survivability:** Medi-Pet Kit and Link Health protect the sentinel from casual enemy fire. Because Shade Prime's benefit depends on it surviving long enough to trigger Ghost repeatedly, maintaining its health pool through mods is essential.

**Optional:** Primed Animal Instinct provides enemy radar to help the player pre-position before Ghost activates, reducing the frequency of surprising close encounters.

## Playstyle and synergies

Shade Prime works best for frames that are already inclined toward stealth or positioning play. Frames like Loki, Ivara, or Ash naturally extend invisibility durations and can use Shade Prime as a gap-filler during the brief windows between ability casts. For frames with no stealth capability, Shade Prime provides occasional protection during repositioning but cannot substitute for dedicated stealth play.

The sentinel is less useful in high-intensity combat where continuous enemy pressure prevents the Ghost cooldown from resetting between activations. In missions with sparse enemy encounters, Ghost triggers less frequently but each trigger provides more meaningful cover. Spy missions and Void Sabotage runs benefit from the cloak in areas with patrolling enemies where maintaining stealth is a priority.

Players who prefer aggressive playstyles relying on constant weapon fire will find Ghost breaks too frequently to provide value; Carrier Prime or Dethcube Prime suits those playstyles better.
