## Overview

Vulklok is a Corpus-aesthetic sentinel that integrates its weapon and sentinel frame into a single combat-focused unit. It is acquired through Cephalon Simaris's Synthesis standing store rather than through relic farming, making it accessible without Prime vault considerations. The sentinel's integrated weapon -- also named Vulklok -- fires high-damage electrical projectiles with a 35% critical chance and 2.5x critical multiplier, one of the stronger base damage profiles among sentinel weapons.

The Vulklok sentinel is notable for its Synthesis-linked acquisition path, connecting it to the Cephalon Simaris gameplay loop rather than Void relic farming. Players who invest in Simaris standing through daily Synthesis targets and scanner usage can unlock Vulklok as a combat companion without engaging with the Prime relic system.

## Abilities and precepts

**Electricity** uses the Vulklok weapon to fire electrical bolts at enemies in range. The weapon's damage profile is purely electricity-based with no physical damage components. Electricity status procs chain to nearby enemies, and the weapon's high critical profile means shots occasionally deal 2.5x or higher damage on critical hits. Against Corpus enemies, pure electricity damage is amplified by their shield vulnerability to the magnetic element that forms when combined with additional procs.

**Shield Charger** monitors the player's shield value and periodically restores shields when they drop below a threshold. This passive support precept supplements defensive shield regeneration, particularly useful in early and mid-game content where incoming damage frequently depletes shields. At very high content tiers where shields are instantly destroyed, Shield Charger's reactive timing may not provide meaningful protection.

The Vulklok weapon's electrical output and critical profile make it one of the higher-performance default sentinel weapons in the game, justifying the Synthesis standing investment for players who prioritize companion combat contribution.

## Acquisition

Vulklok is available from Cephalon Simaris for 50,000 Synthesis standing. Players earn Synthesis standing by completing daily Synthesis target scans using the Synthesis Scanner, with each scan contributing a set amount of standing per scan. The daily standing cap scales with Mastery Rank, so higher-rank players can accumulate the necessary standing faster. Alternatively, the blueprint can be purchased from the Market for 35,000 credits and built in the Foundry for 15,000 credits over 24 hours.

## Recommended mods

**Precepts:** Electricity fills the active precept slot for the sentinel's primary damage contribution. Shield Charger occupies the passive precept slot for its defensive support role.

**Weapon focus:** Because Vulklok's weapon has high critical statistics, critical-amplifying mods for sentinel weapons can increase the weapon's damage output. Players who prioritize sentinel DPS should consider maximizing weapon mods before sentinel body mods.

**Survivability:** Standard Medi-Pet Kit and Link Health mods cover basic survival needs.

## Playstyle and synergies

Vulklok fits naturally into electricity-themed builds and Corpus-focused mission setups where its damage type is most effective. Players who have invested in the Cephalon Simaris system will encounter Vulklok as a natural companion option after unlocking and completing Synthesis tasks.

The Shield Charger precept provides a supplementary defensive layer in builds where shields remain relevant -- lower difficulty content, shield-tank frames like Hildryn, or early-game missions before optimal survival mods are available.
