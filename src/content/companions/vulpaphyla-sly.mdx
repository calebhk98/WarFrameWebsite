## Overview

The Sly Vulpaphyla is an Infested companion native to Deimos, assembled through the modular companion system using Son Tokens earned from Conservation activities. Vulpaphylas are fox-like creatures that carry two distinctive mechanical features common to all Vulpaphyla variants: Viral Quills and Pack Leader. Viral Quills causes the companion to retaliate with viral-damage quills when struck, passively applying Viral status to nearby enemies. Pack Leader allows the Vulpaphyla to survive apparent death by shrinking into a larval form that regenerates before returning to combat.

The Sly variant's head component grants the Desiccate ability, which deals heat damage in a forward cone. Combined with the Viral Quills passive, the Sly Vulpaphyla applies two different elemental status effects -- Viral and Heat -- which together contribute to Gas damage combinations and reduce enemy health through the Viral debuff.

Vulpaphylas are acquired through Deimos Conservation, a stealth-oriented mini-game in which players use appropriate lures and tranq rifles to capture wildlife. Son in the Necralisk accepts captured animals for Son Tokens, which are then exchanged for modular companion component blueprints.

## Abilities and precepts

**Viral Quills** activates passively whenever the Vulpaphyla receives damage. The companion fires a burst of quills outward, hitting nearby enemies and applying the Viral status effect, which reduces the target's maximum health by 50% (stacking up to 325% health reduction at ten stacks). This retaliatory proc occurs on damage receipt rather than on attack, meaning the companion contributes Viral procs continuously throughout combat engagements without player input.

**Desiccate** is the active ability granted by the Sly head component. It releases a cone of desiccating heat that deals damage and applies the Heat status effect to enemies in front of the companion. The Heat status proc causes enemies to heat up over time, dealing damage and increasing all status effects on the target during the heat proc window.

**Pack Leader** engages when the companion's health reaches zero. Rather than dying permanently, the Vulpaphyla shrinks into a small larval form that moves at reduced speed and cannot be targeted. After a regeneration period, the companion revives at partial health and returns to normal function. The larval form is not invulnerable but is rarely targeted by enemies due to its small size.

## Acquisition

Players earn Son Tokens by participating in Conservation activities on Deimos -- capturing Deimos wildlife using appropriate lures and tranquilizer rifles in stealth. Different animal types yield different token quantities. Son in the Necralisk accepts these tokens in exchange for Vulpaphyla head, body, and leg component blueprints. The components are assembled in the Foundry and then combined into the complete companion.

Specific component blueprints determine visual appearance and ability selection; the Sly head provides Desiccate.

## Recommended mods

**Precepts:** Viral Quills and Pack Leader function as built-in abilities rather than mod-slot precepts, freeing the companion's mod capacity for other purposes.

**Survivability:** Medi-Pet Kit, Link Health, and Metal Fiber provide standard survival layers. Pack Leader reduces the urgency of survival modding by providing the self-revive mechanic.

**Status mods:** Companions can equip mods that increase their status proc rate or effect duration, amplifying the Viral Quills application frequency.

## Playstyle and synergies

The Sly Vulpaphyla excels in status-heavy builds where the companion's Viral application supplements the player's own status procs. Viral status reduces enemy health, making subsequent weapon shots deal more total damage against the reduced health pool. Status-focused weapons benefit from the companion independently maintaining Viral stacks on nearby enemies.

The Pack Leader self-revive mechanic reduces the companion management burden in high-level content, as the Vulpaphyla returns to combat after temporary defeat rather than requiring player Revive items. This persistence advantage over sentinels (which die permanently without items) makes Vulpaphylas attractive for players who run long missions.
