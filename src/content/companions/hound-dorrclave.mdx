## Overview

The Dorrclave Hound is a Sister of Parvos companion variant distinguished by its combination of focused target designation and area knockdown capabilities. Unlike Hounds oriented toward self-buffing or healing, the Dorrclave's abilities provide tactical combat support through enemy marking and ground control. The Target Acquired ability gives the player information about the Hound's attack priority, while the Slam Module creates consistent knockdown crowd control in the immediate area.

Hounds are obtained through the Sister of Parvos system and cannot be built or purchased separately. The Dorrclave Hound accompanies specific Sisters depending on spawn conditions, requiring players to engage with multiple Sister cycles to encounter a Sister accompanied by this particular Hound type. Converting the Sister at the end of her Requiem sequence transfers both her weapon and Hound to the player's companion roster.

Like all Hounds, the Dorrclave lacks the Pack Leader self-revive mechanic available to organic Deimos companions. In missions where the Hound takes sustained damage, it requires Sentinel Revive items to restore after death.

## Abilities and precepts

**Target Acquired** marks a specific enemy as the Hound's designated target, directing all attacks toward that unit until it dies. The marking allows the player to observe which enemy the Hound focuses on, enabling tactical coordination. Against durable enemies -- heavies, Eximus units, and named targets -- the sustained focus from Target Acquired causes the Hound to contribute damage consistently to a single priority rather than cycling between targets inefficiently.

**Slam Module** causes the Hound to launch upward and crash down with a ground-slam attack. The point of impact generates a radial shockwave that knocks down all enemies within its area of effect. The knockdown interrupts enemy attacks, applies a brief stagger, and creates a window for the player to attack prone targets or reposition. The ability activates on a cooldown independent of the Hound's standard attack cycle.

## Acquisition

The Dorrclave Hound is claimed by converting a Sister of Parvos whose companion is the Dorrclave variant. Sister conversion requires completing the Sister's Requiem sequence using the correct Parazon Requiem mods on her during Lich hunts or Railjack missions. At the final confrontation, choosing Convert transfers the Sister's weapon and Hound to the player's account.

## Recommended mods

**Survivability:** Medi-Pet Kit, Link Health, and Metal Fiber provide the standard survival foundation. Because Hounds do not self-revive, maintaining high survivability through mods reduces Revive item consumption.

**Utility:** Primed Animal Instinct provides loot and enemy radar. The Slam Module's knockdown radius benefits from companions positioned near the player where enemies cluster.

**Coordination:** The Dorrclave's Target Acquired marking can be read by the player to identify which heavy unit the Hound targets, allowing coordinated weapon fire on the same target to accelerate elimination.

## Playstyle and synergies

The Dorrclave Hound provides tactical value in encounters with mixed enemy groups where priority targeting and knockdown crowd control create advantages. The Slam Module's knockdown is most useful when enemies are pressing toward the player's position, providing a brief safe window for repositioning or healing.

Players who engage in content with durable priority targets -- bosses, Eximus units, and named enemies -- benefit from Target Acquired's focused damage contribution. The combination of marking and slam creates a companion that participates actively in combat encounters rather than passively providing buffs or utility.
