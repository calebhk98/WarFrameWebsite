## Overview

The Chesa Kubrow fills a utility role similar to the Carrier sentinel's Vacuum precept by ensuring item collection at range, but through active retrieval rather than passive pull. Its Retrieve ability causes the companion to run to uncollected item drops and carry them back to the player, covering drops that are out of reach due to terrain, distance, or enemy fire. The Neutralize ability provides crowd control by disarming enemies, stripping their weapons and forcing them into a less dangerous melee-only state.

The Chesa shares its base health value of 940 with the Sahasa Kubrow, giving it the joint-highest health pool among Kubrow subspecies. This durability allows the companion to move across the battlefield toward uncollected drops without being eliminated before completing its retrieval run.

For players who do not use the Carrier Prime sentinel but want some form of item retrieval assistance, the Chesa provides a comparable if different solution. Rather than pulling all items in range continuously like Vacuum, the Chesa actively fetches specific drops that the player would otherwise miss.

## Abilities and precepts

**Retrieve** activates when the companion detects uncollected item drops within its operational range. The Kubrow runs to the drop location, picks up the item, and returns toward the player, at which point the item is added to the player's inventory. The ability covers drops from enemy kills, environmental containers, and mission objective loot. On maps with complex geometry, the Chesa may take indirect paths to reach drops, introducing delay compared to Carrier Prime's instant pull.

**Neutralize** targets an armed enemy and performs a disarm action, ripping the weapon from the enemy's hands. Disarmed enemies either flee or resort to melee attacks, making them significantly less dangerous in ranged combat scenarios. Against Corpus units or Grineer riflemen, removing their weapon neutralizes their primary threat and forces close-range engagement on the player's terms. The ability has a cooldown between activations and prioritizes the nearest armed enemy.

## Acquisition

Chesa Kubrows require a Kubrow Egg from Earth Kubrow dens, the Incubator Upgrade Segment, and Chesa imprints to guarantee the subspecies. Two imprints must be applied during incubation to ensure the Chesa variant. Imprints are available from other players through the trading system.

## Recommended mods

**Precepts:** Retrieve occupies the primary precept slot for its item collection function. Neutralize fills the second slot for the disarm crowd control.

**Survivability:** The Chesa's high health pool means Link Health provides a very large bonus. Medi-Pet Kit sustains health between engagements. Metal Fiber provides additional armor.

**Retrieval efficiency:** The Chesa's effectiveness at item retrieval depends partly on its movement speed across the map. Mods that affect companion movement or agility can slightly improve retrieval timing in spread-out encounters.

## Playstyle and synergies

The Chesa Kubrow works best in missions with large open areas where items scatter broadly after kills, or in missions with terrain that creates pockets where drops land behind obstacles. Excavation missions with multiple dig sites, Defense missions with wide-open arenas, and any mission type where the player remains stationary benefit from the Chesa's active retrieval behavior.

Players who use [Nekros](/warframes/nekros) or [Khora](/warframes/khora) for loot-generation abilities find the Chesa supplements these abilities by fetching drops that spawn at enemy locations distant from the player's position. The Desecrate ability generates drops at enemy corpses across the map; the Chesa retrieves them without requiring the player to traverse the area.

Compared to Carrier Prime's Vacuum, the Chesa provides less reliable item collection per unit time but frees the companion slot for an organic companion with modding advantages. Players who have already invested in a Kubrow companion system and do not want to maintain a separate Carrier Prime relic set can rely on the Chesa for functional item retrieval.
