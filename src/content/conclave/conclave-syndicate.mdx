## Overview

The Conclave Syndicate is the standing-based progression system that governs all Conclave rewards. It is administered by Teshin, the Conclave Master, and operates under the same daily-cap and rank-tier structure as the six standard Tenno-aligned syndicates. Standing is earned exclusively through participation in Conclave PvP matches -- Annihilation, Team Annihilation, Capture the Cephalon, and Lunaro all generate standing.

The syndicate has five ranks, each requiring a standing threshold and a minimum Mastery Rank to advance. Rank advancement unlocks new offerings from Teshin's vendor screen, which is accessed at any Relay from the Conclave room. Unlike combat syndicates, the Conclave Syndicate does not have allied or opposing pairings -- there is no standing penalty from other syndicates for progressing Conclave.

The rewards available through this syndicate are notable to the broader Warframe community because many Conclave-exclusive augment mods apply to PvE gameplay. Players who never intend to continue playing Conclave after obtaining their target augments still invest in the syndicate for those specific mods.

## Rules and mechanics

Standing is earned at the end of each Conclave match. The base amount depends on match duration and mode. A participation bonus is awarded regardless of finish position. A win bonus is added for placing first in free-for-all modes or for being on the winning team in team modes. Kill-count bonuses provide incremental standing for every elimination.

The daily standing cap follows the same formula as other syndicates: the cap scales with Mastery Rank and resets daily. Consistent daily play across multiple days is the expected path to reaching higher ranks. Single-session grinding is capped and cannot be bypassed.

Rank requirements from lowest to highest are Tyro, Recruit, Tempest, Typhoon, and Hurricane. Each rank unlocks a wider selection of offerings and increases the daily standing cap. Rank-up ceremonies require the player to visit Teshin at a Relay in person -- automatic promotions do not occur.

## Augments and rewards

The most sought-after rewards are Conclave augment mods. Unlike other augment categories, Conclave augments apply to both PvP and PvE contexts. Notable examples include augments for Warframe abilities that add bonus effects, altered behavior, or improved efficiency that is not available through any other acquisition method.

Cosmetic rewards include Warframe skins exclusive to the Conclave rank tiers, weapon skins usable on specific weapon families, sigils that display a player's Conclave rank on their Warframe, and animated sigils at the highest rank tiers. These cosmetics are visually distinct from event and Prime Access cosmetics and serve as a visible marker of PvP engagement.

The vendor refreshes offerings when the player advances ranks, adding items that were previously locked. No offerings are time-limited within the Conclave Syndicate itself -- the catalog is persistent, allowing progression at any pace.

## Standing efficiency

The fastest standing generation per unit of time comes from playing the mode in which the player can sustain the highest win rate, multiplied by match frequency. Lunaro matches, being non-lethal, tend to have more predictable outcomes for players who invest in learning the ball mechanics. Annihilation suits players who prefer to control the pace of individual engagements.

Daily cap attainment requires approximately two to five matches depending on match length, win rate, and the mode played. Players who exhaust the daily cap quickly often alternate between Conclave modes to maintain engagement while waiting for the reset.

## Recommended approach to rank

For players pursuing Conclave-exclusive PvE augments, the most direct path is to identify which augment requires which rank, calculate the standing needed from that rank's threshold, and grind the minimum necessary. Tyro-tier augments are available with very little standing investment. Tempest and above require more sustained participation.

For players interested in full Conclave cosmetic completion, Hurricane rank requires the greatest standing investment and is reached only through consistent long-term match participation, as the daily cap makes single-session advancement impossible above Typhoon.

## Lore

Teshin built the Conclave Syndicate around a philosophical argument: that the rewards of combat training should not be abstract. He offers tangible benefits -- augments, cosmetics, standing in the Relay community -- to Tenno who engage honestly with the Conclave regimen. He views the syndicate less as a shop and more as a record of demonstrated commitment. A Hurricane-ranked Tenno has, in Teshin's assessment, spent sufficient time under contested conditions to have internalized the discipline he values.

His relationship with the Conclave rank system evolved after the events of The Second Dream and The War Within. With the Operator revealed and the Tenno's nature understood more fully, Teshin framed Conclave not as preparation for some vague future conflict, but as a practice for the Tenno who had seen what the Void could make of them -- and chose to remain sharp anyway.
