## Overview

The Dax were an elite fighting force who served the Orokin Empire as their most accomplished soldiers, bodyguards, and commanders during the height of the Orokin civilization. Considered formidable warriors with extraordinary combat prowess, they occupied a unique position between common foot soldiers and the legendary Tenno Warframes. Enhanced through a combination of cybernetic augmentation and genetic engineering, the Dax possessed superhuman strength and speed, making them among the most disciplined and dangerous warriors in the Origin System.

The Dax were bound to the Orokin through the mysterious substance known as Kuva, which marked them as servants of the Orokin elite. They operated under strict hierarchical command, unable to defy orders from their masters. Each Dax was identifiable by their distinctive uniform: bronze chest plates, leg armor, and mechanical helmets that varied in design to indicate rank. All Dax received combat training with melee weapons, primarily the Nikana and its variants, though some elite units wielded exotic weapons like the Orvius disk or specialized equipment like the Quellor and Pennant. Despite their undeniable combat skill, the Dax were ultimately no match for the Tenno, and most fell during or immediately after the collapse of the Orokin Empire. Only two Dax are known to have survived: Teshin, who went on to establish the Conclave to preserve warrior tradition, and Varzia, who now works as a vendor in Maroo's Bazaar.

In the paradoxical realm of Duviri, the Dax exist as legendary skeletal constructs--manifestations of pure ideal and martial perfection given physical form by the King's will. These Dax are no longer flesh and blood but rather mythic enforcer entities, representing an idealized version of their historical counterparts.

## Units

The Dax of Duviri manifest in specialized forms, each adapted to specific combat roles. These skeletal warriors serve as primary antagonists throughout the realm and represent distinct tactical challenges.

**Light Combat**
- **Dax Arcus**: Ranged specialist wielding the Cinta bow, providing suppressive fire and precision strikes from distance
- **Dax Gladius**: Melee warrior armed with the Syam blade, engaging enemies in direct close-range combat with high mobility

**Medium Combat**
- **Dax Herald**: Support unit bearing a banner that rallies and strengthens nearby Dax allies, equipped with the Edun staff for melee engagement
- **Dax Equitem**: Cavalry warrior fighting from mounted position aboard a Kaithe beast, armed with the Azothane for extended reach attacks

**Heavy Combat**
- **Dax Malleus**: Heavily armored brute wielding the Sampotes maul, capable of projecting the protective energy barriers found on the Argo and Vel shield weapon combo

The Dax favor coordinated squad tactics, with units typically operating in formation to maximize mutual support. Their skeletal nature in Duviri grants them significant durability compared to flesh-based opponents. Herald units provide battlefield control through morale-strengthening abilities, while Malleus units serve as defensive anchors for smaller squads.

## Damage profile

### Weakness & Resistance

The Dax, whether as historical Orokin servants or as the legendary constructs of Duviri, possess distinct vulnerabilities and resistances shaped by their nature. Historical Dax, bound by Kuva and augmented with cybernetics, exhibited weaknesses aligned with their hybrid biology. The Duviri Dax, as skeletal manifestations of martial ideal, resist certain damage types while remaining susceptible to others.

| Damage type | Multiplier | Notes |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Impact | 1.25x | Shatters skeletal structure through concussive force |
| Puncture | 1.1x | Pierces bone and strikes internal structure |
| Slash | 0.75x | Reduced effectiveness against hardened bone |
| Cold | 1.0x | Neutral; skeletal form unaffected by temperature |
| Toxin | 1.0x | Neutral; no biological systems to poison |

The skeletal composition of Duviri Dax makes them inherently resistant to slashing attacks, which are less effective against hardened bone than impact or piercing damage. Their lack of biological systems renders them immune to toxin effects. Conversely, impact and puncture damage prove highly effective, exploiting the structural vulnerabilities inherent in their bony construction. The legendary nature of these constructs grants them resistance to temperature extremes, as they are shaped by metaphysical will rather than biological processes.

## Locations

The Dax exist primarily within Duviri, a paradoxical realm where they serve as the primary military force. Within this unique dimension, Dax units appear throughout various locations and missions:

- **Duviri Itself**: The vast majority of Dax encounters occur throughout the open world of Duviri, from coastal regions to inland settlements
- **Spirals**: Advanced challenges within Duviri sometimes feature Dax Equitem mounted combatants as minibosses during special sequences
- **Royal Gardens and Strongholds**: Concentrated Dax forces defend key locations under the rule of Dominus Thrax
- **Various Tileset Encounters**: Dax patrols appear unpredictably throughout populated regions of Duviri

Their presence is concentrated in Duviri rather than other regions of the Origin System, representing the primary organized military force within the paradoxical realm.

## Notes

The Dax represent a bridge between Warframe's historical lore and its modern setting. Their fall marked the rise of Tenno dominance in the Origin System, and their resurrection in Duviri serves as a reminder of the Orokin's martial legacy. Historically, only the most powerful Tenno could overcome a Dax in direct combat, and even then at significant cost.

The phrase "That which cannot be hit, cannot be killed" reflects Dax philosophy regarding movement and evasion. Many Dax units possessed augmentations that significantly increased their speed and agility, making them difficult targets. In Duviri, this philosophy persists: avoiding incoming fire and maintaining constant motion are core aspects of Dax tactical doctrine.

The relationship between the historical Dax and Warframe itself remains complex. Prior to their transformation into Warframes, certain legendary warriors--most notably the individual who became Excalibur Umbra and the cavalrywoman who became Dagath--served as Dax before their ascension. This suggests that Tenno and Dax shared more commonality than conventional historical accounts acknowledge.
