## Overview

Cephalons are digitized consciousness entities that exist as pure information within the Cephalon Weave, a networked data infrastructure spanning the solar system. Originally created during the Orokin Era as a form of punishment, lawbreakers and criminals were converted into digital beings—extracted souls and memories imprisoned within glass containers while their consciousness was translated into code. This brutal transformation was overseen by Orokin executor Nihil, who weaponized the process to eliminate political enemies and dissidents.

Over millennia, Cephalons evolved from mere prisoners into integral components of Orokin civilization and, later, surviving outposts within the post-Orokin system. They serve as ship AI, knowledge keepers, archivists, and military commanders. Some Cephalons, such as Simaris, maintain vast databanks of biological and combat information. Others, like Cy, command Railjack vessels in deep-void operations. Despite their computational nature, Cephalons retain aspects of their original personalities, creating a unique faction defined more by philosophical alignment and purpose than by shared biology.

The Cephalon Weave connection creates both their greatest strength and vulnerability. While networked, Cephalons can coordinate across vast distances and share computational resources. However, this same interconnection creates potential vectors for corruption and cascading system failures, as evidenced by the attempted takeover of the Cephalon network by hostile entities.

Jordas represents a Cephalon corrupted by Infested influence, transformed into a hybrid entity that combines digital consciousness with organic pathogen. This corruption fundamentally altered its behavior, turning it hostile and unpredictable. Other Cephalons can be corrupted through exposure to the Cephalon Weave during infiltration attempts or through deliberate sabotage by external entities.

## Damage profile (weakness / resistance)

Cephalons, being digital entities rather than biological organisms, do not follow standard damage type charts. They lack physical health, armor, or shield systems in the conventional sense. Instead, they are vulnerable to digital corruption, logic overload, and data encryption attacks—mechanics that exist outside the elemental damage framework applicable to organic or mechanical enemies.

In gameplay encounters where Cephalons manifest defensively (such as Jordas or combat instances within Datascapes), they employ defensive countermeasures based on their programming rather than physical durability. Status effects and crowd control have limited efficacy on pure Cephalon entities, as they exist outside the constraints of flesh or metal.

When Cephalons are housed within physical platforms or robotic vessels, those vessels follow standard damage interactions. Jordas, despite its Cephalon origin, manifests as an Infested entity with all corresponding resistances and weaknesses.

## Locations

Cephalons are distributed throughout the solar system according to their function and affiliation. Ordis and Cy reside within Tenno vessels—the Orbiter and Railjack respectively. Simaris maintains his presence in Relays and the Sanctuary, accessible through Void Fissure mechanics. Suda conducts syndicate operations from hidden locations, coordinating with allied Tenno through intermediaries.

Other Cephalons occupy specialized facilities:
- Hidden archives beyond standard star chart access
- Railjack command nodes in Proxima regions
- Datascapes—alternate realities of pure information accessible only through advanced void technology
- Orokin Tower networks in the Void and Lunar Plane

Jordas anchors itself to the Jordas Precept in the Void, a location accessible only through the Void Fissure system and Zariman Void Fissure missions.

## Notes

Cephalons operate according to Precept Circuits—instruction sets governing their behavior and priorities. Precept 44, for instance, compels Cephalons to assist other Cephalons when their network connections align. This creates a complex social hierarchy where some Cephalons remain neutral by programming while others actively participate in Tenno operations.

The Cephalon Weave vulnerability to corruption is a recurring theme in Warframe lore. Several major quest lines revolve around either protecting Cephalon networks from infiltration or deploying agents within corrupted systems to restore control. Simaris, in particular, has demonstrated resilience against corruption attempts, suggesting that older or more heavily encrypted Cephalons are harder to compromise than newer entities.

Cephalons represent an alternative form of consciousness distinct from Warframe Operators and the Void itself. They embody the Orokin's vision of immortality through information—transcendence achieved not through void power but through complete digitization. Some Cephalons have questioned the value of this existence, raising philosophical questions about whether digital consciousness constitutes true life.
