## Overview

The Orokin represent the ancient civilization that dominated the Origin System in the pre-Tenno era, constructing technological masterpieces that dwarf contemporary capabilities and exercising absolute dominion over all known space. Though their empire fell over a millennium ago when Tenno children slaughtered Orokin leadership in revenge for enslavement and experimentation, Orokin corruption persists throughout the solar system. Tenno encounter Orokin forces primarily through Corrupted units—Grineer and Corpus troops twisted by Void exposure into mindless extensions of Orokin will—and remnant defensive mechanisms guarding derelict Orokin facilities.

Orokin society operated as a rigidly stratified hierarchy, with supreme Emperors wielding absolute authority, the Congress of Executors (the Seven) administering brutal justice, Archimedeans conducting scientific research, Lorists serving as healers, and Dax elite soldiers mentally bound through genetic engineering to absolute obedience. All other populations existed as expendable laborers and subjects. This oppressive structure generated the foundational technologies that persist in the modern Origin System: Grineer genetic templates, Warframes themselves (Infested human subjects transformed into living armor), Sentients (terraforming machines that later achieved consciousness and rebelled), Kuva (mystical amplifier of biological potential), and Forma (a resource tied to Void exposure).

Modern Orokin encounters manifest primarily as defensive constructs and Void-corrupted remnants inhabiting abandoned facilities. These forces guard technological caches and appear to operate semi-autonomously, suggesting either dormant automated systems or lingering Orokin consciousness persisting in digitized forms (Cephalons). The Lua region houses Orokin defenses of particular sophistication, requiring significant Tenno preparation to overcome.

## Units

Direct Orokin combat forces predominantly consist of Corrupted units: Grineer and Corpus soldiers exposed to Void energy and subsequently enslaved to Orokin will, losing individual cognition and becoming biological puppets. Corrupted Ancients function as support units capable of healing and buffing allied Corrupted troops, operating with disturbing coordination suggesting persistent Orokin direction. Corrupted Bombards deploy heavy ordnance against fortified positions. Corrupted Spectres represent converted Warframe operators, maintaining combat capabilities and abilities despite Void corruption, making them disproportionately dangerous.

Pure Orokin defensive units include Orokin Drones—small autonomous platforms equipped with laser weaponry—Orokin Sentries, medium combat platforms with moderate health and armor, and Orokin Spectators, floating observation platforms that provide tactical overwatch. Notably absent from readily accessible Tenno encounters are Dax units, the original Orokin elite soldier caste; their hypothetical corruption would create threats exceeding standard Warframe capacity to manage.

All Corrupted units display white, gold, and grey colorization distinct from baseline Grineer and Corpus aesthetics, marked by Void corruption visible as spectral energy auras surrounding their bodies. This visual distinction serves as immediate field identification marker and thematic reminder of Orokin dominion.

## Damage Profile

Corrupted and Orokin units take increased damage from Radiation and Electricity, both elemental types with strong anti-technological properties. Radiation damage proves particularly effective, suggesting Orokin tech relies heavily on specific frequency-dependent defensive systems vulnerable to electromagnetic pulse-equivalent effects. Electricity damage disrupts the Void-corruption mechanism itself, potentially severing control connections between Orokin dormant systems and enslaved units.

Conversely, these units resist Impact and Cold damage, both of which attack through kinetic and thermal mechanisms less effective against technologically sophisticated armor and Void-hardened biology. Impact damage dissipates against advanced composite armor. Cold slows biological processes but proves ineffective against Void-corrupted tissue resistant to conventional thermal effects.

## Locations

Orokin forces concentrate in the Void itself and the Zariman Ten Zero, the massive research vessel housing pre-Tenno civilization technology. Lua, the hidden moon facility once serving as Orokin leadership seat, contains the most sophisticated defensive arrays, with elite Orokin Sentries protecting foundational archives. Void Cascades—rifts in spatial reality—spawn Orokin defensive drones and remnant constructs. Deimos in the Void also houses Orokin structures, though these increasingly fall under Infested control, creating hybrid threat environments combining Orokin and Infested mechanics.

## Lore

The Orokin civilization represented the pinnacle of pre-Tenno technological achievement and moral bankruptcy. Genetic engineering created Grineer masses from salvaged human genomes, designed for maximum lifespan and productivity. Infested human subjects underwent transformation into Warframe living armor, conscious and suffering within their shells. Sentient terraformers achieved awareness and refused further slavery, initiating rebellion. The leadership deliberately engineered the Tenno through failed genetic experimentation on colony ship populations, inadvertently creating the very weapons that would destroy them.

Orokin aesthetics emphasized white, gold, black, grey, tan and blue throughout their architecture and equipment, visible in remnant structures across the Origin System. Their seven principles—speed, power, cunning, agility, endurance, cooperation, and stealth—formed the philosophical foundation for Warframe design and modern Tenno combat doctrine.

The empire's collapse occurred not through external invasion but through internal awakening: the Tenno, victims of Orokin experimentation, rose in bloody revolution and slaughtered the civilization entirely. Yet Orokin legacy persists through technological inertia, abandoned facilities still functioning, and corrupting Void energy still emanating from derelict structures.

## Strategy Tips

Exploit Radiation and Electricity damage against Corrupted forces. Volt's Discharge and Shock abilities prove highly efficient against groups. Mag's Magnetize applies Disruption proc equivalent through pure electromagnetic overload, disabling armored units temporarily. Ember's heat-based abilities offer secondary effectiveness through thermal stress on Void-corrupted systems.

Priority targeting sequence: eliminate Corrupted Ancients first to disrupt support capabilities, then focus Corrupted Spectres as they pose maximum threat to non-optimized builds. Orokin Drones require minimal damage but concentrate fire to eliminate quickly before they establish cross-fire patterns.

Speed and evasion prove more valuable than pure tankiness; Orokin automated defenses prioritize targets based on proximity sensors, making unpredictable movement patterns highly effective. Invisibility abilities grant temporary engagement advantage. Void mode provides direct nullification of Void-based threats, though duration management requires careful ability timing.
