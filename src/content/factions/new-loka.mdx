## Overview

New Loka is one of the six core faction-aligned syndicates, led by Priestess Amaryn. The faction espouses a philosophy centered on restoring humanity to its "pure" form through biological purification and rejuvenation of Earth. Unlike militarized factions such as the Grineer and Corpus, New Loka functions as an ideological collective devoted to undoing the Orokin's terraforming damage and rejecting both Grineer genetic tampering and Corpus technological corruption.

New Loka's worldview centers on natural order, organic purity, and the restoration of Earth as a garden world free from external interference. Members view the Orokin legacy as fundamentally corrupting and see technological excess as a deviation from humanity's intended path. This ideological stance positions New Loka in direct opposition to The Perrin Sequence (focused on order through commerce) and at odds with Cephalon Suda (which prioritizes knowledge preservation above natural purity).

The syndicate maintains strong alliances with The Perrin Sequence, granting mutual standing bonuses and shared rewards. In the internal alignment system, players who advance in New Loka automatically gain standing with The Perrin Sequence while incurring standing penalties with Cephalon Suda and, to a lesser extent, Cephalon Suda's allied faction.

## Organization and presence

New Loka operates through relay syndicates located in major Tenno relays. Unlike military organizations, the syndicate functions as a philosophical collective whose members are civilians, researchers, and ideological devotees rather than soldiers. Tenno interaction with New Loka occurs exclusively through diplomatic and commercial channels—syndicate NPCs manage reward distribution and standing transactions without direct combat engagement.

The faction maintains a presence across all Star Chart locations through their reward distribution system. New Loka medallions, physical representations of standing, drop from enemies during syndicate alert missions. Players collect these medallions and convert them into standing, advancing their rank within the faction and unlocking access to exclusive weapons, augment mods, and cosmetics.

## Philosophical alignment and ideology

New Loka's core ideology reflects a vision of humanity freed from artificial constraints and technological corruption. Priestess Amaryn's teachings emphasize cycles of renewal, biological restoration, and the inherent harmony of untampered nature. The faction views the Tenno as potential allies in their mission to heal Earth and protect humanity's future from exploitation by external powers.

This stance creates a natural alliance with The Perrin Sequence's merchants, who see restored Earth as a valuable commodity and market. Both factions oppose the radical experimentation and aggressive expansion of Cephalon Suda and the Arbiters of Hexis, creating a coherent opposing block in syndicate politics.

New Loka members and sympathizers include botanists, conservationists, and researchers dedicated to biological science and ecosystem restoration. The faction's headquarters, the New Loka Chamber within syndicate relays, features aesthetic choices reflecting growth, renewal, and organic imagery—a stark contrast to the sterile design of Cephalon Suda's adjacent chamber.

## Rewards and progression

Tenno advance through New Loka's ranks by accumulating standing, which converts from Affinity earned in missions at a 15:1 conversion rate. Each rank advancement requires spending standing along with specific resource sacrifices (typically credits and crafting materials).

**Exclusive offerings include:**

- **Augment mods**: Specialized mods designed for warframes like Oberon, Trinity, Equinox, and Baruuk—reflecting the faction's focus on supportive, restorative playstyles.
- **Sancti weapon line**: Purification-themed melee and ranged weapons (Sancti Magistar, Sancti Cascadia, Sancti Scythe), featuring aesthetic designs emphasizing natural and regenerative themes.
- **Cosmetics**: Sigils, syndicate-specific color palettes, and cosmetic attachments reflecting growth and renewal symbolism.
- **Unique mods**: Including Healing Tornado and other effects tied to restorative game mechanics.

The standing system operates on a daily cap (25,000 Standing per day, increased with profile bonuses) and requires players to manage multiple syndicate allegiances strategically. New Loka's position in the alignment graph means players balancing standings between the six syndicates must choose carefully when advancing New Loka ranks, as it directly affects standing with opposing factions.

## Locations and syndicate infrastructure

New Loka's presence manifests through syndicate relay hubs located in major Tenno safe zones. The faction maintains chambers in all primary relays (Larunda Relay on Mercury, Leonov Relay on Earth, Eda Relay on Venus, Vesper Relay on Neptune, and Strata Relay on Saturn), with identical layouts and NPCs.

The syndicate does not maintain military installations or territorial holdings. Instead, New Loka's influence spreads through ideological reach—players encounter New Loka rewards through alert missions and medallion drops across all star chart regions. This approach reflects the faction's philosophy: influence through ideological appeal rather than military occupation.

## Strategic notes

New Loka's focus on restorative and supportive augment mods makes it particularly valuable for players piloting support-oriented warframes (Trinity, Oberon, Harrow). The faction's weapon line emphasizes critical strike and melee-focused designs, appealing to players pursuing close-range combat builds.

For players managing syndicate reputation across all six factions, New Loka presents a strategic decision point: the faction's allied status with The Perrin Sequence means advancing one naturally advances the other, while opposition to Cephalon Suda creates unavoidable tension. Dedicated syndicate reputation farmers typically choose between two blocks (either Steel Meridian + Red Veil, or New Loka + The Perrin Sequence), as maintaining standings across opposing blocks becomes unsustainable without extended reputation farming.
