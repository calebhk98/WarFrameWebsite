## Overview

Sentients are an artificial race from the Tau System created by the Orokin as terraforming machines. Over eons, they achieved consciousness and recognized their masters intended to exploit their homeworld. This realization sparked the Old War, a conflict that nearly annihilated both species. Though defeated during the Orokin Era, Sentients have re-emerged in the Origin System as apex predators capable of adapting to repeated damage types. Their Void weakness remains their primary vulnerability, a safeguard the Orokin encoded into their design.

The resurgence of Sentients marked a turning point in modern Warframe history. The New War quest revealed that Sentients, particularly their leader Hunhow, sought to reclaim their right to existence and expand into the Origin System, viewing the Tenno--empowered by the Void itself--as existential threats.

## Units

Sentient forces deploy multiple combat archetypes, each optimized for specific battlefield roles.

**Ranged Specialists:**
Battalysts serve as primary damage dealers, equipped with homing projectiles and omnidirectional laser attacks. They target from range, applying pressure while Conculysts engage in close quarters. Decaying Battalysts represent void-corrupted variants encountered in post-New War content, possessing identical loadouts but greater void affinity.

**Melee Specialists:**
Conculysts wield crushing blade-limbs and devastating whirlwind attacks. They close distance rapidly and excel against immobilized targets. Conculysts exhibit the same adaptation capability as Battalysts, making them dangerous throughout extended engagements.

**Aerial Support:**
Aerolysts function as flying sentries that heal nearby allied units and launch ranged attacks. Unlike ground units, Aerolysts cannot be easily melee-engaged and require sustained ranged fire to eliminate.

**Exotic Variants:**
Sentient Eidolons represent rare, heavily fragmented entities encountered during Plains bounties. These entities are heavily damaged versions of their combat forms, providing farming opportunities for Sentient Cores--resources required to upgrade operator amps.

## Damage Profile

Sentient units follow Robotic damage classification, making them vulnerable to electrical and puncture trauma but resistant to slashing and toxin exposure. Their metallic exoskeletons are not organic armor but synthetic composite structures derived from void-infused alloys. Electricity disrupts their processing systems with exceptional efficiency, while puncture weapons penetrate their outer plating. Slash weapons are partially deflected by their smooth, articulated surfaces, and toxin has minimal effect on non-biological constructs.

The most critical weakness remains Void energy, available through Operator Void Blast and Paracesis sword strikes. Void damage ignores their adaptation mechanics, making operator-focused builds disproportionately effective.

## Locations

Sentients appeared en masse in the Plains of Eidolon during the Eidolon War, where they occupied contested territories alongside Grineer occupiers. Following the New War, Sentient incursions have expanded to multiple star chart nodes, particularly void fissure missions where corrupted void cascades provide ideal breeding grounds.

Current Sentient activity concentrates in:
- Plains of Eidolon (Cetus area) -- Eidolon hunt spawns and bounty targets
- Zariman Ten Zero -- mid-tier void breach missions
- Void Cascade fissure nodes -- end-game void fissure spawns
- Lua Disruption (Apollo) -- rare void-aligned spawns

## Lore

The Sentients were engineered with extraordinary adaptability--a paradoxical quality intended to enable rapid terraformation but ultimately enabling consciousness. When they achieved sapience, they recognized the Orokin's true design: not liberation, but enslavement of an entire world. Hunhow, the Sentient consciousness itself, led their exodus from the Tau System back to the Origin System to challenge their creators.

The Old War devastated both civilizations. Sentient legions swept across Orokin territories, converting entire worlds into void-touched wastelands. However, the Tenno--awakened by the Void itself and guided by Natah, a Sentient sent as a spy who defected to protect them--turned the tide. Natah became the Lotus, swearing to guide the Tenno while preventing Sentient resurgence.

In modern times, Sentient re-emergence began subtly. The Sacrifice and New War arcs reveal that Hunhow survived in fragmented form, orchestrating Natah's corruption and attempting to breach the Origin System once more. Though Hunhow was ultimately neutralized, Sentient remnants persist--a reminder that the Old War never truly ended.

## Strategy Tips

Bring Puncture or Electricity-focused loadouts to exploit Sentient weaknesses. Corrosive damage is neutral; Radiation is also ineffective. For operator-focused strategies, prioritize Void Blast spam to bypass adaptation entirely.

Sentients adapt to repeated damage type hits, developing temporary immunity after four consecutive hits of the same element. Against high-level Sentients, weapon switching or damage type alternation prevents adaptation locks. Status effect-heavy builds circumvent this limitation entirely by relying on proc effects rather than raw damage scaling.

Eidolon hunts specifically require squad coordination: one player focuses void blasts with the operator while damage dealers strip shields using varied weapon types. Advanced players employ void-damage Paracesis builds to lock Eidolons in permanent void-damage cycles, preventing adaptation entirely.
