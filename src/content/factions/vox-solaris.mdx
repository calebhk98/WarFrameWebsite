## Overview

Vox Solaris represents a covert resistance organization and shadow syndicate operating in opposition to Corpus oppression and exploitation. The faction centers on Eudico, a dual-identity figure who leads Solaris United (the public-facing workers' collective) while simultaneously commanding Vox Solaris (the clandestine resistance). This duality reflects the syndicate's strategic necessity—maintaining plausible public identity while conducting resistance operations.

Vox Solaris functions as both a narrative faction within Warframe's storyline and a mechanical syndicate offering unique rewards and progression. The organization maintains connections to The Quills, an enigmatic group with deeper knowledge of the void and Ancient technology. This coalition suggests the existence of far-reaching conspiracies operating beneath the surface of established power structures.

## Units

Vox Solaris operatives consist of trained resistance members drawn primarily from Solaris United's labor workforce. Eudico (Anyo's former supervisor turned revolutionary) serves as the faction's public face and operational commander. The Quills operatives—mysterious void-adepts operating from the Plains of Eidolon—serve as intelligence assets and void-technology specialists integrated into Vox Solaris's operations.

Standard Vox Solaris resistance members appear as combat personnel equipped with modular, improvised weaponry reflecting their working-class origins. These fighters combine industrial tool-weapons (arc welders, plasma cutters) with salvaged Corpus equipment, creating a hybrid aesthetic that emphasizes resourcefulness over military standardization. Higher-tier operatives demonstrate enhanced combat training and access to better-maintained gear.

## Damage profile (weakness / resistance)

Vox Solaris combatants show vulnerability consistent with Corpus-derived equipment and organic resilience. Slash damage penetrates their armor systems effectively, as their gear prioritizes energy resistance over physical durability. Electrical damage remains significant, particularly when disrupting the plasma-based weapons and exoframes many operatives carry.

Impact and cold damage encounter notable resistance, reflecting the Corpus technological heritage in their loadouts. Impact weapons struggle to penetrate layered armor plating designed to withstand concussive force, while cold-element weaponry finds diminished effectiveness against their thermal-management systems. Toxin and viral damage offer moderate effectiveness, targeting biological weakness in their defensive schemes.

Environmental considerations matter when engaging Vox Solaris—their base operations span from Fortuna's heated industrial zones (favoring heat resistance) to the Vallis's frozen expanses (requiring cold adaptation for their personnel).

## Locations

Vox Solaris maintains operational bases across three distinct regions. **Fortuna** serves as their primary hub, with Eudico commanding operations from hidden chambers within the city's industrial infrastructure. **Orb Vallis** hosts secondary operations coordinating with Ventkids and other resistance elements in the frozen wastes. **Plains of Eidolon** represents their connection to The Quills, with joint operations conducted from void-nexus sites and Quill strongholds.

The faction's distributed geography reflects their clandestine nature—no single fortress dominates their presence. Instead, they leverage existing infrastructure (Fortuna's interior, cave networks beneath the Vallis, and void-anomaly zones on the Plains) for operational security. This decentralization makes them resilient to direct military assault while enabling rapid coordination across vast distances.

## Notes

Vox Solaris progression occurs through the mainline Vox Solaris quest, which serves as a comprehensive introduction to Fortuna and the Orb Vallis endgame progression. The quest reveals Eudico's dual role, introduces players to The Quills' void-based technology, and unlocks access to Solaris United standing progression alongside Vox Solaris-exclusive rewards.

The syndicate's rewards emphasize mobility, void-manipulation, and self-sufficiency—reflecting their revolutionary values. Operators gain access to void-strike enhancement techniques, advanced amp technology co-developed with The Quills, and specialized gear for extended Vallis operations.

Lore connections extend across multiple quest lines including "The War Within," "Chains of Harrow," and subsequent content. Vox Solaris exists as part of Warframe's larger narrative exploration of consciousness, liberation, and technological autonomy. Their alliance with The Quills positions them as crucial players in hidden cosmic conflicts operating beyond the Tenno's initial awareness.
