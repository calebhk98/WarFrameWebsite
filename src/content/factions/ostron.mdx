## Overview

The Ostrons represent a mercantile faction inhabiting the settlement of Cetus on Earth's Plains of Eidolon, functioning as traders, bounty brokers, and craftspeople rather than military organization. Described as "a tight knit band of merchants, hucksters and survivors," they have adapted to post-Orokin existence through scavenging, resourcefulness, and commerce. Unlike the militarized Grineer and Corpus, Ostrons prioritize profit and community survival over territorial expansion, making them valuable trading partners for Tenno rather than primary adversaries.

The Ostrons maintain a neutral stance toward all other syndicates, neither aligned with nor opposed to the major powers of the Origin System. This political independence derives from their remote location in Cetus, protected by the Unum—an ancient Orokin Tower repurposed as settlement cornerstone—and their focus on local survival rather than systemic dominance. Their economy runs on Trade goods (rare resources harvested from the Plains), Ostron Plumbat (currency earned through bounties and activities), and crafting services for modular weapons and upgrades.

Tenno encounter Ostrons exclusively through peaceful merchant interactions, with named NPCs such as Konzu offering bounties and Hok providing weapon crafting services. Guard units exist primarily to protect Cetus itself rather than to engage in external conflict, though Tenno activity on the Plains occasionally brings Guard forces into incidental contact with players, particularly during defensive bounty missions.

## Units

Ostron guard forces remain deliberately minimalist compared to militarized factions. Ostron Guards function as basic infantry equipped with conventional projectile weaponry, providing perimeter defense against Eidolon threats and Plains predators. Ostron Rangers deploy at medium range with bows or modified firearms, offering versatility for varied terrain. Ostron Scouts operate reconnaissance roles, identifying threats and coordinating guard movements.

Support units include basic Ospreys—small repair drones maintaining settlement equipment—and defensive turrets placed strategically around Cetus. These forces lack the sophisticated armor, elemental resistance, or biological enhancement of Grineer and Corpus military castes, reflecting their civilian-militia origin. Ostron equipment emphasizes durability and adaptability to Plains conditions rather than pure lethality.

Guard uniforms feature practical desert coloration (tans, browns, warm greys) distinct from Grineer industrial greys or Corpus whites, visually reinforcing their local adaptation and separation from systemic militarization.

## Damage Profile

Ostron units take increased damage from Puncture and Radiation, with Puncture reflecting their reliance on conventional armor (vulnerable to piercing attacks) and Radiation representing electromagnetic interference with their basic mechanical systems. These weaknesses derive from deliberate military simplicity: lacking advanced Void-hardened technology, Ostrons depend entirely on material armor, making concentrated puncture attacks highly efficient.

Conversely, they resist Impact and Toxin damage. Impact resistance reflects reinforced armor construction optimized against blunt trauma. Toxin resistance remains lower than many factions, suggesting limited chemical resilience, yet Ostrons maintain basic defenses reflecting exposure to Plains ecology (various toxic fauna and contaminated water systems).

## Locations

The Ostrons concentrate exclusively in Cetus and surrounding areas of the Plains of Eidolon. Cetus settlement itself sits protected beneath the Unum, an Orokin Tower of considerable height and structural sophistication, providing shelter from Eidolon creatures and environmental hazards. The Plains surrounding Cetus serve as Ostron territory, with various mining operations, fishing spots, and resource extraction sites representing economic activity. Tenno visiting the Plains encounter Ostron merchants in Cetus but guard units only during specific bounty missions or when approaching sensitive resource areas.

## Lore

The Ostrons emerged as survivors in the post-Orokin collapse of human civilization, adapted to life in the Eidolon-plagued Plains through generations of careful adaptation and incremental technological advancement. The discovery of the Unum's protective properties allowed the settlement's establishment and growth, creating a stable base for trade networks and resource extraction. Unlike the systematized expansionism of the Grineer or Corpus, Ostron growth occurred through pragmatic response to immediate challenges: predators, environmental hazards, and resource scarcity.

Ostron culture emphasizes community solidarity ("Kin" status among standing ranks reflects family-like integration), fair trade (refusing to overcharge despite potential Tenno desperation), and respect for the Plains itself. Merchants like Konzu represent the cosmopolitan aspects of Ostron society, while craft NPCs like Hok embody the practical engineering innovation enabling modular weapon development.

The faction gained prominence after the "Awakening" quest revealed Plains of Eidolon content, positioning Ostrons as gateway faction for new players entering endgame content and open-world mechanics. Their continued relevance stems from progressive standing system unlocks and specialized crafting services unavailable elsewhere.

## Strategy Tips

Ostron encounters during Plains bounties require minimal optimization due to low unit threat levels. Standard damage types prove equally effective; no specific elemental loadout excels particularly against Ostron forces. Puncture and Radiation weaponry optimize kill speed through exploiting weaknesses, but conventional builds handle Ostron units trivially.

Combat priority focuses on quick elimination during bounty missions. Ostron Guards present no special tactical challenge compared to equivalent Grineer or Corpus counterparts. Their lower armor and simpler AI patterns make crowd-control abilities (frost, paralyze, stun) highly effective for mission speed-running.

The primary interaction model prioritizes standing gain and mission completion speed rather than combat optimization. Tenno typically engage Ostron guard units incidentally during resource gathering or bounty runs, not as primary challenge encounters. Defensive capabilities prove unnecessary in most Plains activities, allowing pure damage-focused builds.
