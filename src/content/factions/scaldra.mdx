## Overview

Scaldra is a militarized human faction encountered in Warframe: 1999, the alternate-timeline tactical shooter spinoff set in Höllvania during the 1999 pandemic outbreak. They are government soldiers tasked with enforcing martial law and containing a Techrot plague through quarantine, chemical fumigation, and ruthless population control. Unlike the alien factions of the primary timeline, Scaldra represents authoritarian human governance amplified by biosecurity crisis, making them ideologically distinct from Corpus profit-drivers or Grineer genetic despotism.

The faction operates under military hierarchy with officers including Major Neci Rusalka and Lieutenant Viktor Vodyanoi. Their leader enforces strict media blackout, district-by-district fumigation campaigns, and shoot-on-sight protocols against Techrot-infected civilians. Scaldra soldiers believe their brutal methods are necessary to contain an extinction-level threat.

## Units

Scaldra forces employ diverse infantry types and vehicle support, optimized for urban containment and suppression.

**Light Infantry:**
Jaegers serve as baseline rifle soldiers equipped with AX-52s, providing covering fire and patrol capabilities. Flayers are melee specialists wielding chemically-treated sickles, designed to cleave through infected tissue. Barbicans carry heavy shields and grenade arsenals, establishing defensive positions that suppress enemy movements.

**Heavy Units:**
Eradicators deploy area-denial grenades and chemical turrets, controlling territorial zones. Dedicants are heavily armored operatives carrying dual grenade launchers and Overguard, functioning as mobile fortifications. The TI-92 is an armored personnel carrier with turret-mounted suppression systems, providing sustained area damage and vehicle mobility.

**Support:**
Harbingers are floating sentry balloons that provide early warning and automated fire, complicating flanking maneuvers.

## Damage profile (weakness / resistance)

Scaldra armor follows Grineer-derived construction principles--heavy alloy plating resistant to penetrating trauma. Impact and Corrosive damage overcome their defenses; Impact shatters rigid structures while Corrosive eats through composite materials. Gas damage is substantially mitigated by sealed environmental suits and active chemical filtration systems. Infantry carry Efervon canisters on their backs as exploitable weakpoints alongside their heads, allowing tactical targeting to trigger environmental hazards.

## Locations

Scaldra exclusively appear in Warframe: 1999 tactical missions set in Höllvania, a city-state under martial law. They control urban districts, checkpoints, and military installations throughout the campaign. Unlike origin-system factions, Scaldra are confined to the alternate timeline and do not appear in standard Warframe gameplay.

## Lore

Höllvania's government declared martial law following the discovery of Techrot--a fungal pathogen that rapidly metastasizes organic matter into crystalline, hostile constructs. The Techrot spread exponentially, converting infected civilians into chimeric entities (Techrot Beastcaller, Techrot Shrieker, etc.). Overwhelmed public health infrastructure collapsed, forcing military intervention.

Scaldra was activated as the government's containment and extermination force. Major Rusalka implemented totalitarian protocols: complete media censorship, forced evacuations, and the Efervon fumigation program--a bright-green, highly corrosive chemical dispersal system intended to sterilize districts. The chemical is hazardous to organic life but lethal to Techrot entities.

Scaldra soldiers are presented as conscripted humans executing orders they believe protect civilization. They are not evil; they are military personnel in an existential crisis, following command structure without access to information suggesting alternative approaches. The narrative frames them as tragic: neither heroes nor villains, but desperate soldiers in a no-win scenario.

## Notes

Scaldra combat emphasizes squad suppression and vehicle mobility. Their heavy armor and grouped formations reward AoE weapons and crowd-control frames. Impact-focused loadouts (hammers, explosive primaries, Corrosive mods) significantly increase efficiency. Prioritize Eradicators and Dedicants to reduce area denial; then maneuver around Jaegers and Flayers for close kills.

Efervon canisters on infantry backs can be targeted for environmental kills, triggering chemical explosions that damage nearby units. TI-92 vehicles are hard-countered by Void-imbued damage or armor-penetrating primaries. Stealth approaches allow tactical pickup opportunities, reducing engagement difficulty considerably.
