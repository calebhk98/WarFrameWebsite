## Overview

Red Veil is a secretive, radical syndicate composed of operatives willing to employ assassination, terrorism, and psychological warfare to purge the Origin System of corruption. Their motto—"No cost too great, no blood too precious"—encapsulates a philosophy that systemic rot cannot be reformed; it must be cleansed through extreme measures. Rather than presenting themselves as saviors, Red Veil operates as executioners, contacting the Tenno only when their agendas align.

The faction maintains critical alignments within the syndicate hierarchy. Steel Meridian shares their anti-establishment stance and grants a +50% standing bonus to members. Cephalon Suda opposes their methods, and Arbiters of Hexis consider them fundamentally antithetical to order and wisdom.

## Units

Red Veil forces are composed primarily of elite assassins and specialized combatants. When encountered, they deploy Charger Specter units—enhanced clones derived from captured Grineer or Corpus soldiers bonded with Infested technology. These spectral soldiers exhibit heightened mobility and damage output compared to standard units, making them significantly more dangerous than baseline faction troops.

The faction's operative network includes two known senior agents: Cantis and Palladino. Both have coordinated Tenno assistance during crises, though their full organizational structure remains deliberately opaque. Palladino serves as the primary liaison between Red Veil and the Tenno, managing contracts and distributing standing rewards through the relay network. Red Veil deliberately maintains operational security; low-ranked members know only their immediate contacts and mission parameters, with information compartmentalization preventing any single operative from compromising the entire network.

## Damage Profile

Red Veil operatives employ composite armor resistant to slashing trauma but vulnerable to impact shock and cold-induced embrittlement. Impact and Cold damage overcome their defenses, while Puncture and Slash are partially mitigated. Radiation and Corrosive damage remain neutral threats. Stealth-focused loadouts and crowd-control effects prove highly effective against Red Veil assassination teams.

## Locations

Red Veil presence manifests exclusively through relay syndicate missions accessible to aligned Tenno. These operations are not territory-dependent; Red Veil operatives strike from the relays themselves, receiving contracts and distributing standing through anonymous networks.

## Lore

Red Veil emerged from desperation and ideological conviction among those who witnessed the Corpus and Grineer inflict systematic suffering across the Origin System. Unlike more moderate factions that seek balance or profit, Red Veil concluded that negotiation is futile when facing existential rot. Their agents target high-value military and political figures, execute planetary sterilizations, and conduct propaganda campaigns designed to destabilize enemy regimes.

Their recruitment philosophy is ruthless: candidates are assessed not for noble intent but for willingness to sacrifice personal morality for greater outcomes. Palladino, their primary liaison to the Tenno, has stated plainly that she makes no moral judgments—only tactical ones. If the Tenno's objectives align with Red Veil purging targets, both prosper.

## Standing Ranks and Rewards

Red Veil progression spans six positive ranks, beginning at Initiation (0-5,000 standing) and advancing through Respected, Honored, Esteemed, Revered, and Exalted (0-132,000 standing). The nomenclature reflects the faction's reverence for decisive action; members ascend not through time served but through demonstrated commitment to the cause. Affinity earned during combat converts to standing at a 15% rate; three daily Syndicate Alerts supplement standing gain, with Syndicate Medallions (500, 1,000, or 5,000 standing each) found within completed alert missions.

The faction's reward structure emphasizes stealth, assassination capability, and psychological warfare. Early ranks unlock weapon augments designed for burst damage and silent elimination, while advanced tiers grant access to exclusive warframe augments for stealth and mobility frames. Red Veil also provides cosmetic insignia, operative subroutines, and assassination-themed cosmetics. The daily standing cap is shared across all syndicates, requiring players to choose between Red Veil and rival factions.

Red Veil standing relationships demand careful planning. Earning Red Veil reputation grants a +50% bonus to Steel Meridian standing (their ideological ally), but decreases Cephalon Suda standing by 50% (intellectual opposition) and Arbiters of Hexis standing by 100% (absolute incompatibility). This inverse alignment makes balancing multiple factions extremely difficult for Red Veil members.

## Strategy Tips

Tenno earning Red Veil standing should prepare for high-risk, high-reward missions emphasizing assassination and stealth. The faction offers weapon augments for assassination-oriented frames and stealth tools. Bring mobility, crowd control, and burst-damage loadouts to overcome Charger Specter units efficiently. Red Veil standing pairs poorly with Arbiters of Hexis; simultaneous ranking incurs rapid decay penalties that effectively prevent dual progression.

Advanced ranks unlock Warframe augments for stealth and burst-damage capabilities, making Red Veil reputation valuable for operatives specializing in hit-and-run tactics. Players seeking rapid progression should pair Red Veil with Steel Meridian to leverage the +50% standing bonus and streamline daily alert farming.
