## Overview

The Tribunal stands as a relic of the fallen Orokin Empire, representing the highest judicial and executive authority of the most advanced civilization in recorded history. Existing as more of a legendary institution than an active military faction, the Tribunal embodies Orokin power structures, enforcement mechanisms, and the absolute authority that characterized their civilization. Modern Tenno encounter Tribunal influence primarily through void-touched archives, corrupted Orokin constructs, and fragmented lore.

The Tribunal represents Orokin governance at its peak—a body of Executors who made binding judgments on all matters affecting the empire, from scientific research approval to capital punishment. Their authority extended across the entire Origin System, enforced through elite Dax soldiers and advanced technology. Though the Orokin civilization collapsed during the Tenno betrayal, traces of Tribunal power persist as autonomous systems, corrupted constructs, and Void-locked archives.

## Units

The Tribunal's military forces consisted of elite Orokin servants and technological constructs:

**Executors**: Highest-ranking Orokin officials serving the Tribunal. Figures like Executor Ballas, Executor Tuvul, and Executor Karishh wielded immense political and military authority. Executors combined intellectual sophistication with access to bleeding-edge Orokin technology. Most Executors fell during the Tenno uprising, though some escaped into the Void or became immortalized in archives.

**Dax Soldiers**: Elite Orokin military forces serving the Tribunal's enforcement will. The Dax were genetically enhanced warriors equipped with advanced armor and energy weapons, capable of devastating firepower. Dax soldiers appear in modern Warframe primarily as corrupted remnants or void-touched echoes within Zariman Ten Zero and Void Cascade missions.

**Sentient Constructs**: The Tribunal deployed Sentient entities as weapons of last resort during the Old War. These biomechanical intelligences possessed adaptive capabilities enabling resistance to repeated attack patterns. The relationship between the Tribunal and Sentients proved complicated—Sentients were designed tools that eventually developed independent agency and rebelled against control.

## Damage profile

Tribunal forces and their technological remnants reflect Orokin design principles emphasizing versatility and power:

**Weaknesses**: Radiation damage proves particularly effective against Orokin technology, disrupting neural networks and energy matrices. Magnetic damage interferes with Orokin power systems and shielding mechanisms, creating vulnerability windows. Void-based attacks can destabilize anchors binding corrupted Orokin constructs to realspace.

**Resistances**: Void damage becomes difficult to apply against Orokin entities, as the civilization mastered void manipulation technology. Corrosive and slash damage demonstrate reduced efficacy against heavily armored Dax soldiers and energy-shielded constructs. Traditional armor-stripping tactics require sustained application to penetrate Orokin redundancies.

## Locations

Tribunal influence persists in fragmented and void-touched locations:

- **Void Fissures**: Rifts in spacetime containing corrupted Orokin fragments, spawning Corrupted units and reliquaries. These zones represent the Tribunal's persistence beyond death.
- **Zariman Ten Zero**: The research vessel housing Void-touched echoes of Orokin experiments, including dormant Tribunal systems and Dax remnants.
- **Orokin Archives**: Legendary vaults containing knowledge and technology from the Tribunal era, appearing in void-locked reliquaries and hidden void passages.
- **Kuva Fortress**: The fortress originally served Orokin purposes; the Grineer Queens inherited and repurposed Tribunal systems within.

Modern Tribunal presence is archaeological and metaphysical rather than political—remnants of a dead civilization rather than active governing force.

## Lore

The Tribunal emerged as the Orokin Empire's supreme judicial and executive body, governing an interstellar civilization spanning multiple star systems at its height. Composed of the most brilliant minds in human history, Executors wielded absolute authority constrained only by consensus among peers. The Tribunal oversaw all critical decisions: resource allocation, genetic experiments, military strategy, and the creation of new life forms and technologies.

The Tribunal's most consequential decisions involved creating the Warframes. During the Old War, when Sentient invasions threatened Orokin existence, the Tribunal authorized Project Warframe—the infusion of Void-touched human consciousness into biomechanical armor, creating weapons of impossible power. The program succeeded; the Tenno proved instrumental in halting the Sentient onslaught. The Tribunal's triumph proved pyrrhic.

When the Orokin ordered the execution of Margulis, a researcher who had shown compassion to the Tenno, the Tribunal signed its own death warrant. The Tenno turned on their creators in an act of terrible vengeance. Tribunal Executors fell; their forces proved inadequate against combatants powered by Void itself. The civilization collapsed within hours, its infrastructure destroyed, its people slaughtered, its authority ended.

In death, the Tribunal persists as ghost—autonomous systems continuing forgotten protocols, Dax soldiers marching through void-touched archives, and corrupted constructs spawning from Void Fissures. Some lore suggests fragments of Executor consciousness survive in Void-locked data matrices, perpetually executing judgment on infinite void-touched scenarios. The Tribunal exists as both historical reality and cosmic horror, representing the end point of unchecked technological advancement and moral compromise.

## Strategy notes

Modern Tenno rarely encounter the Tribunal directly, instead fighting its remnants: Corrupted units in Void Fissures, Specter constructs echoing Orokin warrior patterns, and automated defenses in Zariman content. Understanding Corrupted unit behavior—their advanced tactics and varied armor types—enables efficient void fissure farming. Bringing frames with strong crowd control and burst damage proves effective against Corrupted forces, which lack the regeneration of Grineer or Corpus equivalents.

Void-themed missions require understanding Void-specific mechanics: Void Cascade hazards deal void damage requiring mitigation through mods, Void Fissure stability affects reward distribution, and Void Shatter interactions with Void-touched enemies demand specialized loadouts. Void reactant collection mechanics reward players for engaging all enemy types within Fissure missions, encouraging balanced damage application.

Players interested in Orokin lore should pursue Zariman Ten Zero content and read collected lore entries scattered across the Origin System. The Tribunal's story intersects with player progression arcs, particularly through the New War quest and post-quest content exploring Void mysteries. Understanding Tribunal context enriches the narrative experience while providing mechanical insight into void-based endgame activities.
