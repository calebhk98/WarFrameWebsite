## Overview

Shadow Stalker is the corrupted, void-infused form of the original Stalker, transformed by Sentient technology into an apex hunting unit. Emerging after the Second Dream quest completion, Shadow Stalker hunts players marked for death across the Origin System. This mercenary exists outside traditional faction boundaries, serving as an independent predator whose singular purpose is retribution and pursuit. Unlike the Stalker's straightforward assassination philosophy, Shadow Stalker exhibits enhanced combat capabilities derived from Sentient adaptation mechanics—a hybrid threat combining mercenary tactics with void-corrupted resilience.

Shadow Stalker emerges with approximately 1.5% base spawn chance plus 0.5% per squad member, making larger teams disproportionately vulnerable to encounters. Once spawned, this entity pursues relentlessly, adapting to player damage output and refusing surrender until defeated or the mission concludes.

## Units

Shadow Stalker operates as a singular entity rather than deploying unit squadrons. However, the boss itself exhibits multiple combat behaviors and weapon systems.

**Primary Arsenal:**
Shadow Stalker wields War, an exalted sword crafted from void-infused Orokin alloy. This melee weapon deals Slash and Electricity damage, striking with devastating force. In addition to War, Shadow Stalker employs:

- **Rhino Charge** — A charging dash attack stunning targets on impact
- **Exalted Blade sword beams** — Ranged directional projectiles covering medium distance
- **Energy grenades** — Lobbed explosives dealing area damage
- **Omnidirectional laser beams** — Void-infused laser patterns striking multiple angles
- **Pull** — A subtle telekinetic grab that repositions targets
- **Dispel** — An anti-buff ability removing player boons temporarily

**Adaptation Mechanic:**
Shadow Stalker exhibits damage adaptation granting temporary 95% resistance to the most-used damage type. This immunity triggers at four health gates (80%, 65%, 45%, 25% remaining health), forcing players to switch damage types or face severe effective-damage reductions for extended periods.

## Damage Profile

Shadow Stalker maintains hybrid armor—a combination of Proto Shield and Alloy Armor derived from Orokin-Sentient fusion technology. This dual-layer system creates complex resistance patterns requiring careful loadout planning.

**Proto Shield (200 HP):**
Vulnerable to Puncture, Heat, and Corrosive. Resistant to Impact and Toxin. Magnetic damage cannot penetrate proto shields.

**Alloy Armor (300 HP):**
Vulnerable to Slash, Electricity, and Magnetic damage. Resistant to Puncture and Cold. Radiation damage cannot penetrate alloy armor.

**Health (750 HP):**
Standard flesh constitution. No exceptional strengths or weaknesses apply to health bars.

Strategically, this means no single damage type covers all three layers optimally. Effective loadouts alternate between puncture-heavy burst phases (stripping shields) and slash-focused sustained damage (shredding armor), with electricity capabilities serving as backup. Radiation builds prove ineffective, while Viral damage offers no advantage.

## Locations

Shadow Stalker manifests in nearly any player-accessible mission after Second Dream completion, excluding:

- Syndicate standing missions (relay-exclusive content)
- Assassination sortie targets (lord-tier bosses)
- Archwing space missions (void-inaccessible regions)
- Open-world landscapes (Cetus, Vallis, Zariman bounties)
- Sorties and Nightmare alerts
- Albrecht's Laboratories (void-sealed dimension)

Probability increases with squad size. Solo players encounter Shadow Stalker approximately 1.5% of the time per mission; full four-player squads experience roughly 3.5% spawn rates.

## Lore

Before void corruption, the Stalker was a lone, bitter warrior—perhaps an Orokin artifact, perhaps a human cultivated by the Sentients as a living weapon. His codex entry is characteristically cryptic: "Some have walked these desolate worlds while you have slept...I am the ghost of retribution." He hunted Tenno autonomously, seeking vengeance for an unspecified massacre during an "empire's ceremonial honor."

After The New War, Hunhow and the resurgent Sentients recognized an opportunity. The Stalker, already immortal and possessed of formidable combat prowess, was remade through void corruption into a hybrid entity embodying both assassin discipline and Sentient adaptation. This transformation elevated the Stalker from a dangerous independent to an apex predator—a tool capable of adapting to player tactics in real time.

Shadow Stalker represents the convergence of two existential threats: the Stalker's deathless persistence and the Sentient capability to evolve faster than prey. Some Tenno theorize Hunhow weaponized the Stalker deliberately, others suggest the transformation was self-imposed by a warrior seeking to transcend limitations. The Stalker's own communication remains sparse and cryptic, leaving his motivations obscured.

## Strategy Tips

Prepare specialized loadouts well before Shadow Stalker spawns. Bring weapons covering both puncture/heat/corrosive (shield-stripping) and slash/electricity/magnetic (armor-penetrating) roles. Alternatively, deploy status-effect builds leveraging Corrosive proc chains to ignore armor scaling entirely.

When Shadow Stalker adapts to a damage type, immediately switch weapons. Continuing puncture damage while Shadow Stalker exhibits puncture immunity wastes shots; leverage the frozen adaptation window to apply different damage types and maintain offense.

Defensive frames (Trinity, Octavia, Wisp) significantly mitigate Shadow Stalker lethality by granting squad-wide damage mitigation or heal procs. Mobility frames (Volt, Zephyr, Loki) excel through evasion and repositioning, reducing Shadow Stalker's accuracy windows.

Experienced players exploit Shadow Stalker's straightforward combat patterns. The boss exhibits predictable ability sequences: Charge attacks, Exalted Blade sweeps, and laser phases occur in rotation. Anticipate these and preposition to maximize distance during laser phases, collapse on Shadow Stalker during cooldowns, and maintain perpendicular approach angles during Charge attacks to evade and counterattack.
