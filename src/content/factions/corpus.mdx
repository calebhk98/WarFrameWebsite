## Overview

The Corpus represent a merchant collective driven by profit, commerce, and individual accumulation of wealth. Founded by Parvos Granum during the final days of the Orokin Era, the faction has evolved into a sprawling commercial and military enterprise spanning multiple planets and space stations. Unlike the Grineer's militaristic hierarchy, the Corpus operates as a competitive collective where individual captains and commanders maintain relative autonomy while adhering to broader profit-driven objectives.

Corpus philosophy prioritizes cold calculation and economic interest above all else. Their internal motto reflects this ethos: "the cold vacuum of space--heartless and bent on taking anything left unprotected." Members are driven by personal gain, and alliances within the faction are transactional rather than loyal. This creates a faction less unified than the Grineer but more adaptable and innovative in their approach to warfare and resource acquisition.

The Corpus employ both human crewmen--cloned soldiers with voice modulators built into their helmets--and increasingly sophisticated robotic platforms. Their technological edge comes from continuous innovation in robotics, shields, and commercial applications of ancient Orokin systems. Joint ventures with Sentient entities, such as the Amalgam program on Jupiter, represent the faction's willingness to pursue profit regardless of the consequences.

## Units

Corpus military strength relies on layered unit types, from basic crew soldiers to specialized mechanical platforms and command-level operatives. The faction's modular approach to unit deployment allows for tactical flexibility across various mission types.

| Unit | Role | Equipment |
|------|------|-----------|
| Corpus Crewman | Basic soldier | Dual pistols; light armor |
| Corpus Ranger | Ranged specialist | Harpak or Karak rifles |
| Corpus Lancer | Medium unit | Burst fire carbine; moderate health |
| Corpus Tech | Support unit | Healing and buff dispensing |
| Corpus MOA | Mobile platform | Heavy ballistic or melee-focused |
| Corpus Osprey | Flying unit | Ranged attacks; tactical support |
| Corpus Nullifier | Special unit | Nullification field; strips abilities |
| Corpus Bombard | Heavy unit | Explosive projectile launcher |
| Alad V | Boss commander | Multiple weapon modes; tactical AI |

MOAs and Ospreys represent the Corpus' emphasis on mechanical units over humanoid soldiers. Nullifiers pose a particular threat to ability-reliant teams due to their nullification fields, which create a bubble that disables player abilities and buffs while the unit remains active. Bombards deliver explosive payloads from range, requiring cover-based positioning to avoid damage.

Alad V appears in both standard missions and specialized assassination contracts. As a command-level operative, he employs multiple weapon systems and tactical repositioning, making him a boss-tier challenge requiring team coordination.

## Damage profile (weakness / resistance)

Standard Corpus units rely heavily on shields and light ballistic armor. Their primary weakness is Magnetic damage, which directly counters shield systems and bypasses armor layers. Puncture damage is also effective, exploiting the puncture weakness inherent to Corpus armor composition.

For health pools, Corpus units employ Ferrite Armor (standard crewmen) or Robotic health (MOAs, Ospreys). Ferrite Armor is weak to Puncture and Cold damage, while Robotic health takes additional damage from Electricity and Puncture.

**Corpus Damage Weaknesses:**
- Shields: Magnetic (primary), Puncture (secondary)
- Ferrite Armor: Puncture, Cold
- Robotic Health: Electricity, Puncture, Radiation

**Corpus Damage Resistances:**
- Shields: Impact resistance (75% of normal damage)
- General: Relatively balanced; no faction-wide strong resistance to any single damage type

Nullifiers present a mechanical threat rather than a damage-type problem. They are best eliminated through positioning and crowd control rather than pure damage output, as their nullification field actively counters ability-based strategies.

## Locations

The Corpus maintain a sprawling presence across the origin system, with major strongholds on Venus, Jupiter, Europa, Neptune, and Pluto. Each region hosts distinct Corpus facility types, from mining operations and energy harvesting stations to research laboratories and military outposts.

**Venus:** Early-game Corpus missions; basic facility infiltrations.

**Jupiter:** Includes the Gas City tileset housing Amalgam hybrids; requires advanced heat management.

**Europa:** Ice planet installations; water-based and cryogenic research facilities.

**Neptune:** Void-proximity research stations; higher-level enemy scaling.

**Pluto:** Extreme-security military installations; advanced robotics and command units.

Corpus presence extends into the Void through Void Fissure relics and specific void-based missions. Certain Railjack nodes feature Corpus capital ships in Pluto Proxima and Earth Proxima regions.

## Notes

The Corpus faction represents a counterbalance to the militaristic Grineer. Where Grineer employ mass production and hierarchical command, Corpus favor specialization and technological innovation. This creates fundamentally different tactical approaches between the two factions.

Corpus robotics, particularly MOAs and Osprey variants, demonstrate increasing sophistication with each major update. The introduction of Amalgam units through forced Sentient cooperation marks a significant evolution in Corpus military capability, though it remains to be seen whether this partnership will endure or eventually fracture due to conflicting interests.

Several Corpus leaders maintain independent agendas despite nominal faction allegiance. Alad V's opportunistic dealings with Sentients, Nef Anyo's aggressive expansion, and various lower-level captains' piracy and mercenary work reflect the faction's true nature: a collection of self-interested actors united only by profit motive.

Corpus divisions include Basic (standard outposts), Juno (space stations), Terra (Orb Vallis, Venus open-world), Vapos (Jupiter Gas City), and multiple Railjack divisions across proximas. Each division maintains distinct unit types and technological focuses tailored to their regional environment.
