## Overview

The Grineer Empire stands as the dominant military power across the Origin System, controlling vast industrial complexes and resource-rich territories through conquest and relentless expansion. Originally engineered as expendable clone laborers by the Orokin Empire, the Grineer underwent militarization during the Old War but were ultimately considered failures in combat effectiveness. However, after the Orokin's collapse, the surviving Grineer regrouped and seized opportunity, adopting the Orokin twins as spiritual queens and building an empire through sheer numerical superiority and brutal tactics.

Grineer society is built upon genetic instability and enforced loyalty. Their clone biology degenerates with each iteration, manifesting genetic defects and shortened lifespans. In compensation, they developed crude but effective cybernetic augmentation, replacing failing biological systems with mechanical substitutes. This combination of biological fragility and technological compensation defines Grineer military doctrine: overwhelming firepower, attrition warfare, and ruthless efficiency. They employ no mercy and recognize no concepts beyond conquest and expansion.

The Twin Queens rule from the Kuva Fortress, their immortal will driving all Grineer operations through an almost religious devotion. Under their absolute authority, the Grineer maintain multiple specialized military corps tailored to environmental conditions, from jungle warriors to space marines to deep-sea laboratory personnel.

## Units

The Grineer field a diverse array of combat units organized by armor classification and operational environment. Their dual-taxonomy system allows efficient deployment across varied mission parameters while maintaining command structure clarity.

**Light Combat**
- **Lancer**: Standard rifle infantry, baseline Grineer soldier with moderate accuracy and mobility
- **Butcher**: Close-range melee specialist wielding chainsaws and crude blade weapons
- **Flameblades**: Incendiary melee units combining fire attacks with close-range combat

**Medium Combat**
- **Scorch**: Flamethrower unit with area denial capabilities
- **Railgun**: Anti-vehicle specialist deployable against heavy Tenno equipment
- **Ballista**: Sniper platforms providing suppression fire from distance

**Heavy Combat**
- **Heavy Gunner**: Armor-plated suppression unit armed with rotary cannons and high ammunition capacity
- **Bombard**: Close-assault tank variant with explosive ordnance dispensers
- **Napalm**: Support unit deploying incendiary area attacks and defensive barriers

**Specialized**
- **Kuva Liches**: Immortal super-soldiers infused with ancient Kuva, each bearing unique elemental abilities derived from captured Warframes
- **Tusk units** (open-world): Plains-adapted variants with longer-range weapons for open combat
- **Drekar units** (deep-sea): Uranus-adapted variants with pressurized armor for aquatic environments

The Grineer favor group tactics over individual skill. Their numerical advantages, superior firepower in concentrated barrages, and relentless pressure tactics exploit weaknesses in isolated defenders. Veterans of Grineer combat report that their effectiveness multiplies with unit density, suggesting command-and-control optimization at squad level.

## Damage profile

### Weakness & Resistance

Grineer combat doctrine emphasizes armor and mobility over biological hardiness. Their clone bodies are fragile and rapidly degenerate, compensated for through heavy cybernetics and protective plating. This creates distinct tactical vulnerabilities and strengths when facing modern Tenno weaponry.

| Damage type | Multiplier | Notes |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Corrosive | 1.75x | Exploits cybernetic augmentation and armor degradation |
| Slash | 1.25x | Cuts through bio-mechanical integration points |
| Impact | 0.85x | Distributed resistance from armor plating |
| Toxin | 0.5x | Genetic resistance to biological attacks |
| Heat | 1.0x | Neutral; armor disperses thermal energy |

**Kuva Grineer** variants show elevated heat resistance due to Kuva infusion, which provides thermal stabilization. Their armor regenerates slowly during combat, making corrosive damage doubly effective as it degradation outpaces biological recovery.

Toxin damage proves least effective due to the Grineer's fundamental genetic engineering. Their clone physiology was optimized to resist Infested technocyte pathogens, and this defensive adaptation translates to general biological poison immunity. Radiation triggers unpredictable mutagenic responses in their defective genetics and is strategically avoided.

## Locations

Grineer territorial control spans multiple planets and orbital installations, representing the Origin System's largest single military polity. Their presence indicates resource extraction, military staging, or active conquest zones:

- **Earth**: Feral forests overtaken by ancient Grineer settlements; Cetus and Iron Wake remain independent
- **Mars**: Desert manufacturing complexes and ground-based launch facilities
- **Ceres**: Industrial asteroid refinery supporting fleet operations
- **Saturn**: Ice-based military installations and supply depots
- **Sedna**: Maximum-security laboratory and weapons development complex
- **Uranus**: Deep-sea research laboratories and pressure-vessel weapons manufacturing
- **Kuva Fortress**: Mobile dreadnought serving as throne of the Twin Queens and highest seat of Grineer authority

## Notes

The Grineer's dominance relies on numerical superiority, industrial capacity, and absolute hierarchical control. Individual Grineer soldiers are competent but unremarkable; their threat emerges from coordinated squad tactics, superior firepower density, and willingness to sustain losses. Their strategy of attrition favors them in prolonged engagements where ammunition and troop replacements flow from infinite industrial complexes.

The Queens' leadership is absolute and unchallenged. Grineer soldiers will fight to death without hesitation, question, or self-preservation instinct. This fanaticism, combined with genetic conditioning, makes negotiation or rebellion virtually impossible.

Kuva Liches represent the apex of Grineer military innovation: immortal soldiers selected from elite troops and elevated through Kuva infusion into being rivaling Warframes in power. Each Lich carries unique elemental affinities and abilities, making them formidable persistent adversaries rather than routine encounters.
