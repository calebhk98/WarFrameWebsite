## Overview

Narmer represents a hybrid empire-religion introduced during "The New War" quest that combines the authoritarian control structures of the Grineer and Corpus with Sentient ideology and Orokin technology. Led by the charismatic but ultimately deceased Ballas and now orchestrated by the mysterious Pazuul, Narmer functions as a brainwashing apparatus designed to impose blind subservience across inhabited regions of the Origin System.

The faction distinguishes itself through biological control mechanisms: golden Narmer Veils that mask the faces of converted victims, producing a red hallucinogenic mist that forces compliance through direct neurological manipulation. Chants of "All as one" reverberate across their controlled territories, marking the loss of individual identity to the collective consciousness. Narmer Deacons enforce compliance and possess the authority to remotely detonate veils on resistors, turning individual units into living weapons. Large circular mirror-like constructs known as All-Seeing Eyes facilitate veil control in populated areas such as Cetus and the Orb Vallis.

Following Ballas's assassination and the subsequent destabilization of the empire, Narmer forces remain active but increasingly fragmented. Unknown leadership continues orchestrating operations in the Plains of Eidolon, the Orb Vallis, and the Zariman, suggesting a surviving hierarchy maintains operational control despite the loss of the faction's ideological figurehead.

## Units

Narmer combat forces consist entirely of brainwashed Grineer and Corpus troops, rendering the faction fundamentally parasitic in military composition. Rather than generate its own soldier caste, Narmer converts existing populations through the Veil mechanism, creating unified but cognitively compromised forces.

Notable Narmer unit types include Narmer Deacons, who function as enforcers and veil operators; Narmer Mercenaries, converted military professionals; Narmer Harrows, ranged specialists displaying organic, flesh-like weapon appendages; and Narmer Flyers, aerial assault units equipped with Sentient-influenced jetpack technology. The most dangerous units are Archons, elite super-soldiers displaying fusion of Sentient biological features and Orokin-derived weaponry, capable of releasing supercharged energy bolts that threaten even seasoned Tenno.

All Narmer units bear distinctive red hologram overlays and golden accents across their armor and equipment, visually emphasizing the Sentient-Orokin fusion that defines their technological identity. This aesthetic extends to their weapons, which frequently display organic, biomechanical characteristics unusual among conventional Origin System militaries.

## Damage Profile

All Narmer units take increased damage from Slash and Toxin damage types, making slashing weapons and poison-based abilities exceptionally effective against converted soldiers. This vulnerability reflects biological compromise: the Veil mechanism interfaces directly with victim neurology, creating exposure to toxin-based manipulation tactics, while slash damage disrupts both the mechanical Veil apparatus and underlying musculature of converted troops.

Conversely, Narmer units resist Magnetic damage, suggesting the Sentient influence confers resistance to electromagnetic interference. Notably, faction-specific damage modifications such as Bane of Grineer do not increase damage output against Narmer variants despite their composition of converted Grineer and Corpus troops. The Veil mechanism appears to overwrite baseline faction identity, rendering such mods ineffective.

## Locations

Narmer operations concentrate in three primary regions: the Plains of Eidolon on Earth, where All-Seeing Eyes maintain veil control over the Ostron settlement of Cetus; the Orb Vallis on Venus, where converted Solaris and Corpus remnants enforce subjugation; and the Zariman Ten Zero, the massive ship formerly housing Orokin research facilities. These territories represent critical control nodes for Narmer expansion, chosen for both pre-existing strategic value and population centers vulnerable to veil conversion.

## Lore

The rise of Narmer occurred during the failed assassination of Ballas in "The New War" quest, when the Sentient commander revealed the true scope of his ambition: not merely conquest of the Origin System but psychological unification of its inhabitants through directed neurological control. The Veil technology represented the culmination of Sentient analysis of both Orokin consciousness-control mechanisms and baseline human neurology, weaponized into a faction-wide control apparatus.

The empire's rapid growth and subsequent instability following Ballas's death suggest either a critical dependence on his leadership or ongoing internal succession struggles. The persistence of Narmer forces despite this leadership vacuum indicates institutional inertia and possible hidden command structures. Community speculation regarding Pazuul's identity and authority levels remain unconfirmed by official sources.

## Strategy Tips

Fight Narmer forces with Slash and Toxin damage. Saryn's Toxic Lash and Miasma combination proves highly effective. Mag's Magnetize ability directly counters veil-based energy mechanics through magnetic overload. Trinity's damage reflection capabilities redirect Narmer firepower against their own units. Speed and target prioritization take precedence: eliminate Deacons first to disrupt veil synchronization, then focus aerial Flyers before they gain positioning advantage.

Defensive builds benefit from toxin resistance mods and armor-based tanking rather than pure shield reliance, as the Veil's neurological component bypasses conventional shield mechanics. Operator void dash remains essential for evading supercharged Archon energy bolts, which track aggressively and deal high damage.

Avoid open terrain where All-Seeing Eyes provide targeting support; utilize cover and vertical movement to break line-of-sight. Narmer forces coordinate more effectively than standard Grineer or Corpus troops due to veil synchronization, making unpredictable movement and flanking tactics significantly more valuable.
