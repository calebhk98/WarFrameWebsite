## Overview

Amalgams are hybrid lifeforms created through the forced integration of Corpus technology with Sentient biology and cybernetics. Born from Alad V's coerced partnership with Sentient entities, these enhanced warriors represent a synthesis of organic and mechanical warfare. Unlike standard Corpus units, Amalgams possess adaptive combat behaviors and regenerative capabilities that make them formidable opponents.

Amalgams were developed as part of a broader initiative to create superior soldiers by blending Corpus engineering with Sentient capabilities. The result is a faction of enemies that exhibits both technological precision and biological unpredictability. They appear exclusively in Jupiter's Gas City tileset, serving as guardians of Corpus installations within that aeronautical environment.

The Amalgams' primary threat stems from their enhanced durability and the tactical variety present within their roster. Units like the Demolyst pose specific challenges with their nullification auras and self-destruct mechanics, while flying variants such as Amalgam Osprey create defensive support structures for their allies. This multi-layered approach to combat demands that players adapt their strategies depending on which Amalgam types spawn in a given mission.

## Units

Amalgam forces consist of both humanoid and mechanical variants, each optimized for specific combat roles. The humanoid units, such as Heqet and Machinist types, wield firearms and deploy tactical abilities. Mechanical units include turrets, drones, and mobile platforms that provide ranged firepower and crowd control.

Demolysts represent a special threat in Disruption-style missions. These units rush directly toward defensive conduits and self-destruct upon reaching their target or when killed. Their nullification auras disable player abilities in a 6.5-meter radius every five seconds, forcing teams to rely on weapon-based damage and mobility rather than ability spam.

## Damage profile (weakness / resistance)

Amalgam units share shield and armor vulnerabilities across all variants, though internal health composition varies. All Amalgams possess shields that respond to standard damage type interactions: Magnetic and Puncture damage are particularly effective against their shielding, while Impact and Cold damage are resisted.

For flesh health, Viral and Toxin damage are highly effective, dealing 75% bonus damage. Slash damage also exceeds baseline effectiveness at 50% bonus. Impact damage is resisted on the flesh layer, reducing its effectiveness by 50%.

The Amalgam faction's defining characteristic is the absence of meaningful armor on most units. Only Alkonost and certain Arca variants carry Ferrite armor, and even then the amounts are modest compared to standard Grineer units. This means armor-stripping mods and Corrosive-damage builds are less critical than they would be against Grineer. Instead, teams should prioritize Viral, Toxin, or Slash damage to minimize time-to-kill on Amalgam threats.

Alkonost units are notably immune to status effects and certain Warframe ability mechanics, requiring pure damage output and crowd control effects that do not trigger status procs (such as knockback or displacement abilities).

## Locations

Amalgams spawn exclusively on the Jupiter Gas City tileset and do not appear in standard star chart missions or other open-world regions. Within the Gas City, up to three distinct Amalgam types can spawn simultaneously, creating diverse tactical scenarios.

The primary locations where Amalgams appear are:
- Jupiter Gas City (all mission types except Themisto)
- Corpus Gas City Spy, Sabotage, Mobile Defense, and Disruption missions
- Void Fissure missions when conducted on Jupiter nodes

Demolysts appear specifically in Disruption-mode missions, where their objective-rushing behavior creates a secondary tactical layer beyond standard enemy engagement.

## Notes

Amalgams drop Hexenon, a rare crafting resource exclusive to Jupiter Gas City, along with Amalgam-specific mods that enhance weapon and Warframe functionality. These drops make Amalgam farming a worthwhile endeavor for players seeking specialized build improvements.

Unlike standard Sentients, Amalgams possess Eximus variants that grant powerful faction buffs to nearby allies, amplifying the threat level when multiple Eximus units cluster together. Community farming strategies often focus on targeting non-Eximus spawns or using tactical abilities to isolate and eliminate buff units before engaging main forces.

The existence of Amalgams reflects the ongoing technological evolution within the Corpus faction and its willingness to compromise its own members' humanity in pursuit of military superiority. They serve as both enemy encounters and a reminder of Alad V's forced cooperation with external entities.
