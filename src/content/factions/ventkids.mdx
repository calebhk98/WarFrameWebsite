## Overview

The Ventkids are a youth syndicate operating from Fortuna's ventilation system. Unlike traditional enemy factions, they represent a community faction aligned with the Tenno. Consisting of children who have escaped Corpus oppression, the Ventkids operate independently within the social hierarchy of Orb Vallis, running K-Drive racing operations and maintaining their culture through competitive trick-riding.

The Ventkids inhabit the ducts and passages above Fortuna's commercial districts, creating a hidden society that thrives on adrenaline and individual skill rather than militaristic structure. Players encounter them as allies and competitors rather than adversaries, making them unique among the game's factions.

## Units

Ventkids primarily operate through two distinct unit types. The standard Ventkid represents the faction's youth members, identifiable by their casual attire and K-Drive boards. K-Drive Riders are Ventkids specialized in aerial combat and trick execution, demonstrating expert vehicle handling during racing events and competitions. Both unit types utilize hover-boards (K-Drives) as their primary mobility system, allowing for rapid movement across the Vallis terrain and through Fortuna's interior passages.

Players rarely engage Ventkids in traditional combat. Instead, interaction involves K-Drive races where Ventkids serve as competitors and quest-givers, or peaceful encounters within Fortuna's headquarters.

## Damage profile (weakness / resistance)

The Ventkids show minor elemental preferences based on their equipment composition. Limited armor plating and reliance on mobility makes them slightly vulnerable to electricity and cold damage, which can disrupt their K-Drives or slow their movement. They demonstrate mild resistance to heat and toxin, suggesting their gear incorporates minimal thermal shielding and organic resilience.

Electrocution presents the most tactical advantage when engaging Ventkids, as electricity can damage their vehicles and stun their movements. Cold weapons offer secondary benefit by reducing their already-limited defensive capabilities. However, most direct combat with Ventkids remains rare, as the syndicate positions itself as neutral allies of the Tenno rather than opponents.

## Locations

The Ventkids maintain presence across two open-world regions. Their primary stronghold lies in **Orb Vallis**, where they conduct K-Drive racing circuits along the frozen plains. Their headquarters exists as an accessible landmark within Fortuna itself, located above the second-floor shops in a graffitied vent section. Secondary operations occur in **Cambion Drift**, where K-Drive races extend across the necralisk's exterior terrain and underground caverns.

Both locations feature race tracks designed for K-Drive competition, with jump platforms, grind rails, and trick zones that showcase the Ventkids' specialized vehicle skills. Players unlock these locations through progression in the Vox Solaris questline.

## Notes

The Ventkids syndicate represents a unique faction type—neither purely enemy nor purely NPC. They function as a competitive community that recognizes Tenno skill and respect. Engagement involves standing progression through K-Drive racing rather than combat missions.

Standing gains with Ventkids occur exclusively through trick execution and race completion. Each trick awards standing points (1 standing per 4 trick points), with daily caps preventing rapid progression. The five Ventkid ranks (Glinty, Whozit, Proper Felon, Primo, and Logical) unlock progressively better K-Drive components and cosmetics rather than weapons or tactical equipment.

Lore connections extend to the Warframe Yareli, whose quest "The Waverider" directly involves Ventkid culture and community values. This connection emphasizes the syndicate's role as a cultural movement rather than a military faction within Warframe's universe.
