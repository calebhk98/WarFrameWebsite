## Overview

The Perrin Sequence is a Corpus merchant faction dedicated to profit through ethical trade rather than conflict. Unlike their parent organization, they pursue prosperity and stability via diplomacy, making them one of the aligned syndicates available to Tenno operatives. Led by Ergo Glast, a former Corpus financier and scientist, the Sequence represents a radical reimagining of Corpus values centered on commerce as cooperation rather than conquest.

The faction maintains several strategic alliances within the syndicate system. New Loka shares their merchant-class interests and grants them a +50% standing bonus, while Arbiters of Hexis and Steel Meridian oppose their ideals, imposing standing penalties when both are ranked simultaneously.

## Units

Perrin Sequence forces employ standard Corpus weaponry and tactics. When encountered on missions, Sequence troops use MOA variants and advanced defense systems typical of Corpus fortifications. The signature "Secura" weapon line carries the faction's branding, featuring modified versions of popular Corpus arms including the Acrid, Grinlok, Spectra, and Lanka. Each carries a hidden "Sequence" effect that scales with faction standing.

The faction does not deploy exclusive combat units; instead, it relies on modified Corpus infrastructure and mercenary forces during territorial disputes. Unlike syndicates that maintain standing armies, Perrin contracts temporary defense systems and MOA units from parent Corpus holdings, deploying them as needed to suppress rival merchant activity or protect trade installations.

## Damage Profile

Perrin Sequence units follow Corpus resistances. Puncture and Cold damage pierce their shields and synthetic armor respectively, while Slash and Electricity are partially negated by their composite plating. Strategic loadouts emphasizing Puncture procs and Cold-based mods significantly improve efficiency against Sequence forces.

## Locations

Perrin Sequence presence is confined to Corpus-controlled territories accessible only to players who have aligned themselves with the faction. Syndicate missions appear on multiple planets via the relay syndicate interface, allowing operatives to earn standing and unlock rank-locked rewards.

## Lore

The Perrin Sequence split from the Corpus Oligarchs centuries ago following ideological irreconciliation. While the Corpus thrived on conflict--weaponizing poverty, disease, and fear to extract profit--the Sequence pursued trade networks that benefited all parties. Ergo Glast formalized the schism, establishing a parallel hierarchy that functions as a balance to Corpus aggression. Their doctrine states they seek "to restore order by bringing prosperity and direction"--a stark contrast to their origins.

Within the syndicate hierarchy, Perrin occupy the merchant-prince archetype: neutral in absolute moral terms, but aligned with Tenno interests insofar as stability enables trade. They neither aid nor oppose the Infested directly; they simply ignore existential threats to profit margins.

## Standing Ranks and Rewards

Perrin Sequence progression unfolds across six positive ranks, each unlocking deeper access to syndicate rewards. Players begin at Initiation (0-5,000 standing) and advance through Associate, Senior Associate, Executive, Senior Executive, and finally Partner (0-132,000 standing), the highest tier. Affinity earned during combat converts to standing at a 15% rate; three daily Syndicate Alerts provide additional standing opportunities via Quittances, which trade for 500, 1,000, or 5,000 standing each.

The faction's reward structure emphasizes economic efficiency and mobility. Senior Executive rank unlocks weapon augment mods tailored to precision and range, while Partner rank grants access to exclusive warframe augments designed for frames that prioritize speed and evasion. The syndicate also offers MOA specters for void relic farming, void relic packs for relic farmers, and cosmetic sigils reflecting faction allegiance. Higher ranks unlock additional decorations and equipment suitable for operatives specializing in quick-hit mission types.

The daily standing cap is shared across all syndicate factions, preventing players from capping multiple syndicates simultaneously. Perrin Sequence standing relationships require strategic consideration: earning Perrin reputation grants a +50% bonus to New Loka standing but decreases Arbiters of Hexis standing by 50% and Steel Meridian standing by 100%, creating mutually exclusive alliance paths.

## Strategy Tips

Operatives earning Perrin standing should expect standard Corpus engagement patterns. Bring Corrosive or Puncture-heavy loadouts to strip armor rapidly. The faction offers significant utility rewards, making Perrin reputation valuable for players focused on efficient resource farming and mission completion.

Tenno aligned with both Perrin and Steel Meridian simultaneously will see standing decay rates doubled; manage rank carefully if pursuing conflicting faction goals. For players seeking balanced progression, pairing Perrin with New Loka maximizes standing efficiency while maintaining access to both factions' offerings.
