## Overview

The Relay Syndicates are not a single faction but a confederation of six independent organizations: Steel Meridian, Cephalon Suda, Arbiters of Hexis, Perrin Sequence, Red Veil, and New Loka. Each operates from player relays across the Origin System, recruiting and equipping Tenno operatives through standing-based reward systems. Collectively, they represent competing ideologies and agendas that stabilize through mutual opposition, ensuring no single faction achieves hegemony.

Players may simultaneously align with multiple syndicates, though their conflicting interests impose standing decay penalties when allegiances become imbalanced. The relay system creates a meta-game of strategic alignment, forcing operatives to choose whose agenda they prioritize.

## Units

The relay syndicates themselves deploy no combat units. They are political and merchant organizations operating from relay stations. However, each member faction—Steel Meridian, Red Veil, Perrin Sequence, and others—maintains independent armed forces deployed during syndicate-controlled missions. These forces vary by faction: Steel Meridian uses Grineer deserter units, Red Veil employs Specter assassins, Perrin Sequence relies on Corpus technology, and others field faction-specific combatants.

Individual syndicate articles document their specific military compositions and doctrine.

## Damage Profile

Relay Syndicates do not have a unified damage profile, as they deploy no direct combat forces. Operatives should refer to individual syndicate entries for faction-specific resistances and weaknesses encountered during standing-aligned missions.

## Locations

All six syndicates maintain permanent presences in player relays across the Origin System. Accessible relay locations include Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, the Void, Lua, and Zariman. Each relay hosts all aligned syndicates' representatives and kiosks, allowing operatives to accept missions, claim rewards, and manage standing simultaneously across multiple factions. Relay syndicate missions bypass traditional star chart limitations, appearing as repeatable contracts available indefinitely to qualified operatives.

## Lore

The Tenno Syndicate system emerged approximately sixty years before the current in-game timeline as a stabilization mechanism following the Forgiven War between Grineer and Corpus. Exhausted by perpetual conflict and recognizing mutual destruction as inevitable, both factions began recruiting Tenno as mercenaries. Ancillary organizations—Steel Meridian, New Loka, Cephalon Suda, Red Veil, and others—recognized the economic opportunity and established relay presences to bid for Tenno loyalty.

The system operates on a delicate balance: any single faction achieving dominance would trigger retaliatory coalitions, which would shatter the relay framework entirely. Thus, the syndicates police each other. Perrin Sequence and New Loka wealthy interests oppose Steel Meridian's military-industrial model. Red Veil's radicalism repels Arbiters of Hexis' intellectual order. Cephalon Suda pursues technology independent of ideology, making her a wildcard that prevents predictable alliance-blocs.

The relays themselves are neutral ground maintained by Cephalon Cy and other relay intelligences, sworn to bar no faction so long as violence within relay boundaries remains forbidden.

## Strategy Tips

Operatives new to the relay system should begin by ranking a single syndicate to understand reward structures and unlock critical items—typically void fissure access, resource blueprints, and early augment mods. Higher ranks unlock warframe and weapon augments tailored to each faction's combat philosophy.

Managing conflicting allegiances requires careful attention. Simultaneous high ranks with opposed syndicates (e.g., Steel Meridian and Perrin Sequence) trigger exponential decay penalties; operatives lose standing in both faster than they gain. Experienced players establish primary and secondary allegiances, allowing strategic rotation between seasons or patches.

Each relay syndicate offers something unique: Steel Meridian provides military augments, Red Veil unlocks assassination tools, Perrin Sequence grants merchant weapons, New Loka offers survival-focused builds, Cephalon Suda branches technology paths, and Arbiters of Hexis deep knowledge. Plan standing allocation around playstyle and long-term goals.
