## Overview

The Syndicate system represents a comprehensive player reputation framework within Warframe, allowing Tenno to align themselves with organized factions pursuing distinct ideologies and agendas across the Origin System. Syndicates are not a single entity but rather a categorical system encompassing multiple independent factions—each offering unique rewards, standing mechanics, and diplomatic consequences. Understanding the syndicate framework is essential for veteran progression, as faction choices directly impact gameplay accessibility and build viability.

Syndicates operate through a standing-based economy where players earn faction reputation through missions, contracts, and resource collection. As players rank up within a syndicate, they unlock increasingly powerful rewards while potentially gaining rivals among opposing factions. The system encourages specialization while penalizing simultaneous pursuit of diametrically opposed ideologies.

## Units

The syndicate system itself does not field units in combat; rather, individual member factions maintain their own military forces. However, the syndicate system spawns consequences for players who achieve negative standing:

**Syndicate Operatives**: Administrative and diplomatic personnel representing each syndicate in relays and throughout the Origin System. These NPCs handle standing transactions, manage reward tiers, and coordinate faction activities.

**Eximus Squads**: Elite enforcement units deployed against players who reach negative standing within a syndicate. At Rank -1, squads consist of 3-5 high-level Eximus units; at Rank -2, full platoons deploy containing 5-8 enhanced enemies with defensive auras and crowd control abilities.

**Medallion Collectors**: Neutral operatives scattered across missions who carry syndicate medallions. These specialized units appear as random mission encounters, carrying faction medallions that players can loot for standing bonuses.

## Damage profile

Eximus squads responding to negative standing reflect their parent faction's damage profiles. Syndicate enforcement doesn't field a unified faction type; instead, each syndicate deploys units matching their own forces. Players encountering syndicate death squads should prepare for mixed damage resistances and varied ability threats.

**Weaknesses**: Radiation damage disrupts Eximus shield generators and neural coordination systems, proving effective across all syndicate-aligned units. Magnetic damage interferes with exoskeletal systems and technological enhancements.

**Resistances**: Cold-based attacks are less effective due to Eximus biological heating and technological regulation. Toxin damage demonstrates reduced impact on heavily augmented Eximus units with advanced immunological systems.

## Locations

Syndicates maintain presence across the entire Origin System through multiple pathways:

- **Relays**: Six major relay stations (Larunda, Orcus, Kuiper, Vesper, Strata, Kronia) serve as neutral ground where all syndicates maintain representatives. Tenno can trade standing and purchase rewards safely within relays.
- **Star Chart Missions**: Syndicate presence extends into all planetary territories, with daily alerts and medallion drops occurring during standard missions.
- **Opponent Territory**: Negative standing results in syndicate death squads spawning during missions within regions controlled by opposing factions.

The syndicate framework is geographically ubiquitous, existing as an overlay across all Tenno-accessible regions rather than claiming territorial dominance.

## Lore

The syndicate system emerged from the post-Orokin power vacuum as various ideological and practical organizations competed to offer Tenno guidance and rewards. Rather than a unified government structure, syndicates represent rival factions united only by mutual acknowledgment of player agency and the market economy of reputation.

Six primary standing syndicates dominate the political landscape: Steel Meridian (revolutionary liberation), Arbiters of Hexis (truth-seeking scholarship), Cephalon Suda (radical research and information), The Perrin Sequence (capitalist trading), Red Veil (anarchist chaos), and New Loka (human supremacy). These ideologies place them in constant tension, with players forced to choose allegiances or suffer standing penalties.

Beyond the six, numerous neutral syndicates operate independently without faction alignment mechanics: Ostron communities (Plains of Eidolon), Solaris United (Venus workers), Entrati enclave (Zariman), and others function as alternative advancement paths. These neutral syndicates allow standing progression without affecting the primary six-faction rivalry system.

The syndicate system fundamentally reflects player agency in a post-post-apocalyptic universe where traditional governments have collapsed and only organized interest groups survive.

## Strategy notes

Tenno pursuing syndicate advancement must first understand the standing acquisition system. Converting mission affinity into standing provides passive progression—approximately 15% of earned affinity converts to standing for any syndicate currently selected. Daily syndicate alerts offer substantial standing bonuses (4,000 baseline, scaling with completed alerts) independent of the 16,000+ point daily cap. Medallions serve as portable standing storage, allowing players to bank rewards during medallion-rich missions and exchange them later.

The standing cap increases by 500 points per Mastery Rank, beginning at 16,000 for MR0 and scaling to 20,000 for MR8+. Players grinding higher ranks must complete multiple daily alerts or run extended missions to maximize standing gains. Medallion farming becomes essential for players seeking rapid rank progression, as the daily cap otherwise limits advancement velocity.

Syndicate alignment carries permanent consequences due to rank penalties. Raising one syndicate's standing inherently reduces standing with rivals by 50% (allied penalties) or 100% (opposed/enemy penalties). Players cannot simultaneously rank up opposed syndicates without extensive grinding, requiring strategic choice about which path to pursue. The system rewards specialization rather than dabbling across all factions.

Death squad mechanics trigger at Rank -1 and escalate at Rank -2, creating pressure against pursuing multiple opposed factions simultaneously. Smart players select complementary syndicates (aligned or neutral) rather than opposing ones, avoiding mandatory combat against faction enforcers during standard missions. The diplomatic consequences are real and create meaningful strategic choices for faction progression.
