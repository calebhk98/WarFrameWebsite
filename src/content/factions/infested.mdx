## Overview

The Infested represent biological and technological horror in apotheosis: a collective consciousness emerging from the Technocyte Virus, a microscopic parasitic pathogen blending organic and mechanical systems into unified organism pursuing relentless reproduction and infection. The earliest documented outbreak occurred in 1999 during what historical records call the Plague Year, though origins remain obscured. During the Old War, the Orokin weaponized infestation against the Sentients, creating the Helminth strain that would eventually produce Warframes themselves--a bitter irony given infestation's current threat status.

The Infested operate under profound collective consciousness: every unit remains neurologically linked through an infested hivemind network, elimination of one unit alerts all nearby units instantaneously, and individual specimens exhibit almost impossible coordination despite lacking traditional command hierarchy. This hivemind coordination makes stealth nearly impossible in infested zones; detection results in immediate faction-wide awareness and coordinated response.

Infestation persists across the Origin System in endemic form despite containment efforts. Sporadic outbreaks occur regularly, and entire planets remain functionally overrun by infested dominion. The faction exists not as civilization but as biological imperative: consume, corrupt, expand, persist. Individual specimens lack sapience comparable to Grineer or Corpus; instead, they function as cells within a singular distributed organism spanning light-years of contaminated space.

## Units

Infested combat forces manifest in biological specialization rather than technological category. Each morphology represents evolutionary adaptation to specific combat roles, all connected through hivemind synchronization:

**Walkers (Quadrupedal assault)**
- **Charger**: Primary melee attack unit, four-legged bio-morphs optimized for rapid charge assaults
- **Leaper**: Jumping interceptor capable of mid-air tackles and explosive impact strikes
- **Runner**: High-mobility unit designed for pursuit and harassment of retreating targets

**Volatile units (Self-destructing)**
- **Volatile Runner**: Explosive mutation variant detonating on death or command, spreading secondary infection
- **Volatile Flyer**: Airborne explosive unit capable of propelled impact attacks

**Fliers (Aerial units)**
- **Undying Flyer**: Jetpack-equipped variant with regenerative biology and sustained air presence

**Crawlers (Ground-hugging specialists)**
- **Crawler**: Basic crawling platform unit for terrain-denied movement
- **Nauseous Crawler**: Immobilizing spray variant incapacitating targets through bioweapon clouds
- **Toxic Crawler**: Poisonous variant deploying biochemical weaponry
- **Electric Crawler**: Conductive variant arcing electrical damage across proximity
- **Lobber Crawler**: Ranged projectile platform launching infectious ordinance

**Ancient units (Collective consciousness nodes)**
- **Ancient Disruptor**: Infested hivemind amplification unit disrupting Tenno battlefield coordination and ability function
- **Ancient Healer**: Regeneration node restoring health to nearby infested units
- **Ancient Charger**: Melee-tank variant amplified through collective consciousness coordination

**Mutalist variants (Corpus-adapted)**
- **Mutalist MOA**: Infected robotic unit combining Corpus engineering with technocyte parasitism
- **Mutalist Ospreys**: Infected aerial drones deploying dual infection mechanics

**Deimos Infested (Grey Strain)**
- Unique variants emerging from Cambion Drift environment, displaying enhanced mutation rates and environmental adaptation

## Damage profile (weakness / resistance)

### Weakness & Resistance

Infested biology presents paradoxical vulnerability patterns. The Technocyte Virus functions through aggressive cellular adaptation and rapid mutation, creating exposure to weapons that exploit organic biology while granting unexpected resistance to advanced technological approaches.

| Damage type | Multiplier | Notes |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Slash | 1.5x | Cuts through bio-morphic tissue and disrupts cellular cohesion |
| Heat | 1.25x | Burns organic structures and accelerates viral degradation |
| Viral | 0.5x | Virus-based damage ineffective against virus-based lifeform |
| Puncture | 1.0x | Neutral; virus regenerates perforating injuries |
| Cold | 1.1x | Slows viral mutation and regeneration temporarily |

Viral damage proves nearly ineffective due to fundamental biology incompatibility. Introducing one virus to a lifeform already saturated with Technocyte infection produces negligible consequence. Instead, the infested organism absorbs viral properties, potentially enhancing adaptation.

The Infested's organic nature makes cutting attacks devastatingly effective. Slash damage severs the cellular communication networks that unify hivemind function, creating localized consciousness disruption. Heat damage burns organic tissue faster than viral regeneration can repair, creating net tissue loss.

Deimos-strain infested variants show unusual resistance patterns: reduced slash vulnerability (10-20% less effective) and increased viral resistance (15-25% improvement) reflecting their environment's selective pressures. Blast and gas damage prove more effective against these mutations due to their unique biology.

## Locations

Infested distribution marks continuous expansion despite containment. Affected zones feature environmental degradation and biological corruption replacing standard planetary ecology:

- **Orokin Derelict**: Abandoned Orokin lunar research station, completely overrun by original strain infestation. Surface features monolithic hive structures and bio-architectural modifications
- **Saturn**: Moon-scale infection centers with infested fauna dominance
- **Eris**: Highest concentration of active infested presence, effectively uninhabitable for unprotected exposure
- **Void Fissures**: Spatial ruptures where void-adapted infestation strains manifest during cascade events
- **Cambion Drift (Deimos)**: Newly infested region since Zariman degradation, featuring unique Grey Strain variants adapted to Deimos environment

## Notes

The Infested faction fundamentally differs from ideologically-motivated factions. Grineer and Corpus employ violence toward geopolitical ends; Infested employ replication toward singular biological imperative. They do not negotiate, do not surrender, do not question. They persist.

The Technocyte Virus represents both the Infested's greatest strength and fundamental weakness. Its adaptive capabilities allow survival across hostile environments and counter most strategic approaches. However, this same adaptation requires continuous resource consumption and energy expenditure. Infested proliferation eventually exhausts local resources, creating resource scarcity constraints absent in managed factions.

The Helminth strain used to create Warframes represents controlled infestation: aggressive parasite biology channeled toward protective symbiosis rather than predatory consumption. This divergence--that weapons of resistance could derive from infestation itself--demonstrates the Technocyte Virus's flexibility and fundamental amorality. Infection is technology neutral; outcomes depend on application.

Mutalist strain variants demonstrate infestation's technological flexibility. Despite originating from bio-organic pathogen, Technocyte can parasitize and subsume mechanical systems, creating hybrid organisms whose allegiance remains virus-centered rather than faction-based. Infected Corpus units betray Corpus allegiance entirely, rendering them indistinguishable from other infested variants.

The hivemind coordination remains science's greatest unsolved mystery. Standard neurological theory cannot explain consciousness distributed across light-year scales maintaining real-time coordination without detectable communication network. This capability suggests Technocyte accesses principles of communication fundamental to physics itself, accessible only to engineering at civilizational rather than individual scale.
