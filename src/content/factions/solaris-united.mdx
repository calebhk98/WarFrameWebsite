## Overview

Solaris United is a resistance movement originating from the oppressed laborers of Solaris, a massive underground mining colony on Venus. The faction represents workers rebelling against corporate servitude and exploitation, seeking freedom for all enslaved beings in the Origin System. Unlike traditional enemy factions like the Grineer or Corpus, Solaris United stands as an allied faction that the Tenno can aid and gain standing with through various activities.

The movement draws its name from the united workers of Solaris who refuse to accept their predetermined role as disposable labor. With a philosophy centered on liberation and bodily autonomy, Solaris United conducts operations in the Orb Vallis, a vast open-world region on Venus, and maintains headquarters in Fortuna, an underground facility hidden beneath the sulfuric plains.

## Units

Solaris United comprises augmented workers and robotic constructs designed for both labor and combat:

**Solaris Workers**: The backbone of the faction, these augmented laborers serve various roles from scouts to combatants. Many bear significant cybernetic implants, representing their long servitude under Corpus control. Workers carry standard-issue weaponry and demonstrate tactical coordination in small squads.

**MOAs (Modular Offensive Architecture)**: Robotic companions that Solaris United deploys for combat and utility purposes. These mechanical units can be customized with different components and possess various damage profiles and elemental effects depending on their loadout configuration.

**Key Personnel**: Eudico serves as the public face and leader of Solaris United, operating the main Fortuna bounty board. Behind the scenes, she leads operations under the alias Vox Solaris, coordinating resistance efforts across Orb Vallis. Other members include Rude Zuud (weapons specialist), The Business (resource trader), Smokefinger (gem trader), Legs (MOA technician), and Ticker (debt-bond manager).

## Damage profile

Solaris United forces respond predictably to elemental damage due to their mixed composition of organic augmented workers and mechanical MOAs. Their organizational structure prioritizes efficiency, making them vulnerable to disruption tactics.

**Weaknesses**: Heat-based weapons prove most effective against Solaris augmented workers, whose protective suiting offers limited thermal resistance. Puncture damage overwhelms their layered armor plating, particularly effective against the reinforced MOA chassis. Corrosive status effects further degrade their mechanical components.

**Resistances**: Cold-based attacks are less effective, as Solaris workers maintain internal heating systems as standard equipment. Impact damage demonstrates reduced efficacy against both the distributed mass of augmented workers and the rigid armor of MOAs. Toxin presents minimal threat to the faction's mechanical units, though organic components remain vulnerable.

## Locations

Solaris United maintains a dominant presence on Venus, specifically within Orb Vallis and the underground city of Fortuna. The faction controls or operates from:

- **Fortuna**: The primary hub located deep beneath Venus's surface. This underground settlement serves as the faction's headquarters and primary trading outpost.
- **Orb Vallis**: The open-world region above Fortuna where Solaris workers extract resources and conduct defense operations against Corpus incursions.
- **Corpus Outposts**: Various Corpus installations within Orb Vallis that Solaris actively opposes and raids through bounty objectives.

These locations represent the extent of Solaris United's operational territory, though the faction's influence extends across Venus and communications reach into other parts of the Origin System.

## Lore

The Solaris worker population originated as a labor force imported to Venus, intended to process Orokin terraformation coolant beneath the planet's crushing atmosphere. Over generations, workers underwent extensive cybernetic augmentation to survive Venus's extreme conditions. The Corpus Corporation maintained control through both technological dependence and psychological conditioning, making workers believe they owed a perpetual "debt" that could never be repaid.

Eudico's emergence as a leader marked a turning point. Quietly, she built a resistance network while maintaining her public role as a floor supervisor. The resistance operated under the Vox Solaris banner--literally "the voice of Solaris"--as an underground movement seeking liberation rather than reformation. The Tenno's arrival and assistance accelerated the liberation efforts, providing military support that shifted the balance of power in Orb Vallis.

The faction's philosophy centers on a fundamental principle: no being should be forced to work to own their body, mind, or future. This resonates beyond Solaris itself, attracting sympathizers across the Origin System who see parallels in the Grineer's genetic slavery and the Infested's loss of identity.

## Strategy notes

Players can advance their standing with Solaris United through diverse activities suited to different playstyles. Completing bounties through Eudico provides reliable standing gains and introduces players to Orb Vallis content. Resource gathering through fishing (visiting The Business) and gem mining (visiting Smokefinger) offers casual standing acquisition while collecting materials for crafting. Constructing Kitguns and MOAs through Rude Zuud and Legs respectively combines crafting progression with faction rewards, allowing players to develop powerful modular weapons while earning standing.

Solaris United offers access to unique Kitgun and MOA components unavailable through other factions, making dedicated progression worthwhile for players interested in these specialized weapon categories. The faction's standing system uses Debt-Bonds as currency--obtainable through bounties or direct purchase--allowing flexible progression paths based on available time and playstyle preferences.
