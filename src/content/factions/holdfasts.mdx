## Overview

The Holdfasts are the four last surviving members of the adult crew aboard the Zariman Ten Zero, a legendary void-touched vessel that vanished during the Orokin Empire's final era. Denied traditional death by the Void's incomprehensible forces, they were resurrected as void manifestations—human consciousness preserved within conceptual embodiment sustained by their crew identities and shared memories. Unlike artificial intelligence or traditional revenants, the Holdfasts retain complete human mindset and perspective, allowing them to serve as diplomatic intermediaries between the Tenno and the Void itself.

Resurrected to purpose, the Holdfasts maintain operations from the Chrysalith, a headquarters established within the Zariman's depths. Their mission centers on stabilizing the Void-touched vessel's degradation while managing the mysterious Void Cascade phenomenon that threatens the Origin System. They operate with profound understanding of Void mechanics accumulated across centuries of posthumous existence, wielding knowledge lost since the Orokin's extinction.

The Holdfasts possess no xenophobic impulse. Their resurrection shattered traditional faction identity; they operate as independent syndicate partners, accepting any aid in their mission to prevent systemic Void collapse. They view the Tenno not as conquerors or saviors, but as necessary collaborators—beings whose unique void attunement makes them essential to Zariman stabilization.

## Units

The Holdfasts operate without conventional military units. Instead, they field four distinct members, each specializing in critical operational domains:

- **Quinn**: Syndicate administrator and bounty manager. Quinn assigns recovery and stabilization contracts to Tenno operatives, matching risk profiles with available personnel. Quinn maintains comprehensive records of all Void Cascade manifestations and crew recovery priorities.

- **Archimedean Yonta**: Primary merchant and resource administrator. Yonta exchanges Voidplume and Zariman materials collected from void expeditions, offering in return Kuva, rare crafting components, orbiter decorations, and exclusive equipment rotated based on seasonal void phenomena. Yonta's inventory reflects deep understanding of rarity and supply chain management.

- **Kira**: Recovery specialist coordinating crew member location and retrieval operations. While data on Kira's specific functions remains limited, crew records suggest involvement in identifying survivors and lost Zariman assets.

- **Cephalon Melica**: Artificial intelligence supporting operations alongside human crew. Melica maintains historical archives and provides technical analysis of Void Cascade data, serving as bridge between crew intuition and objective scientific observation.

- **The Husband**: Senior crew member whose specific role remains classified within Holdfasts operational security protocols. References suggest elder council responsibilities.

- **Gyra**: Support specialist providing logistics and resource management. Gyra coordinates material supply chains and handles procurement of off-world resources needed for Zariman maintenance.

The Holdfasts employ no combat units in traditional sense. Instead, Tenno operatives subsidize their military requirements through bounty-funded expeditions, creating partnership rather than subordination relationship.

## Damage profile

### Weakness & Resistance

The Holdfasts' void-touched nature grants them exotic physical properties defying conventional kinetic modeling. Their bodies exist partially displaced from standard spacetime, rendering traditional impact-based damage less effective while amplifying energy-based harm.

| Damage type | Multiplier | Notes |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Void | 1.5x | Directly disrupts void-manifest biology |
| Slash | 1.25x | Interdimensional cutting pierces manifested form |
| Impact | 0.75x | Distributed across void-displaced volume |
| Corrosive | 1.0x | Neutral; void-touched biology resists corrosion |
| Radiation | 1.1x | Void-touched form sensitive to radiation |

Void damage proves most effective due to fundamental resonance with their altered biology. Energy-based attacks that exploit spacetime manipulation surpass kinetic approaches. Interestingly, corrosive damage—lethal to most biological targets—proves nearly ineffective due to void-touched biological immunity to conventional degradation.

The Holdfasts' resistance to impact damage reflects their semi-corporeal nature. Their consciousness persists partly within void-space itself, allowing distributed absorption of kinetic force across multiple dimensional states. However, concentrated beam weapons prove penetrating.

## Locations

The Holdfasts maintain limited physical presence due to void-touched nature incompatibility with standard orbital environments. Primary operations center on:

- **Zariman Ten Zero**: The derelict void-touched vessel serving as Holdfasts operational hub. Deep interior sections accessible only during void cascade events or through Tenno-facilitated expeditions.

- **Chrysalith**: Designated safe-zone headquarters established within Zariman's depths. Void stabilization maintains this chamber's integrity despite surrounding environmental degradation.

- **Void Cascade zones**: Temporary operational deployments during system-wide void cascade events threatening Origin System stability. Holdfasts emerge to coordinate crisis response and resource recovery operations.

The Holdfasts cannot survive extended exposure to standard spacetime without technological mediation. This limitation restricts their operational footprint to void-attuned environments, making their reliance on Tenno operatives absolute for extrasystemic operations.

## Notes

The Holdfasts represent a unique faction archetype: postmortem consciousness preserved through extraordinary means, allied with Tenno by mutual necessity rather than ideological alignment or military subordination. Their humanity, preserved across centuries of void exposure, grants them moral complexity and individual perspective that distinguishes them from faction-indoctrinated military hierarchies.

The Zariman's degradation presents existential crisis. Should void-cascade destabilization continue unchecked, the vessel's collapse could cascade to Origin System-wide void rupture, threatening all known civilization. The Holdfasts' syndicate model reflects this urgency: they recruit external assistance through resource exchange rather than coercion, acknowledging that survival requires cooperation beyond faction boundaries.

Player progression through Holdfasts standing mirrors traditional syndicate advancement, progressing from "Fallen" through "Angel" ranks, each tier unlocking access to increasingly rare crafting components and void-touched equipment otherwise unavailable. This standing system formalizes the Tenno's role as essential Zariman stabilization asset rather than peripheral ally.

The integration of Cephalon Melica alongside human crew suggests Orokin continuity. As the last known active artificial intelligence descended from Orokin archives, Melica's presence indicates the Holdfasts preserve functional pre-collapse technology—a potential knowledge source for understanding Orokin science and void mechanics.
