## Overview

The Stalker is a renegade assassin and the primary antagonist of early and mid-game Warframe, serving as a personified reminder of the Tenno's origins and the consequences of their choices. Appearing as an invulnerable Warframe wielding powerful weapons, Stalker hunts marked players across the Origin System with a singular purpose: vengeance. Unlike the Grineer, Corpus, or Infested, Stalker represents a singular, sapient threat—a being with motivation, history, and a complicated relationship with the Tenno.

Players receive Death Marks during assassination missions that designate them as targets. Upon receiving the mark, Stalker may randomly appear during subsequent missions across the Origin System, spawning with powerful abilities drawn from multiple Warframes and a deadly arsenal of signature weapons.

## Units

Stalker fights as a lone operator rather than commanding units, making him fundamentally different from faction-based enemies. However, his capabilities and signature weapons define his presence:

**Stalker himself**: A sentient Warframe engineered by the Orokin and driven by personal vendetta. Stalker operates with intelligence and tactical awareness, employing hit-and-run tactics and adaptive combat strategies. He demonstrates mastery of multiple Warframe abilities despite being a single entity.

**Signature Weapons**:

- **Dread**: A powerful bow dealing high damage per shot with excellent crit multiplier. Fires projectiles at moderate speed with significant punch-through capability.
- **Hate**: A scythe weapon with high slash damage and fast attack speed, enabling relentless melee assaults.
- **Despair**: Throwing knives with rapid fire rate and high puncture damage, providing ranged pressure during combat.

## Damage profile

Stalker's vulnerability patterns reflect his nature as an engineered Warframe combined with Orokin enhancements. His armor and shields respond to specific damage types while his combat philosophy adapts to player tactics.

**Weaknesses**: Puncture damage bypasses Stalker's defensive systems, proven effective against both his Warframe plating and Orokin enhancements. Radiation damage disrupts his neural pathways, causing disorientation and reducing combat effectiveness. These two damage types combine to create windows of vulnerability during extended encounters.

**Resistances**: Slash damage demonstrates reduced efficacy, as Stalker's armor distributes impact across a broader surface. Corrosive damage is partially mitigated through Orokin shielding systems. Traditional armor-stripping tactics prove less effective than sustained precision strikes.

## Locations

Stalker operates across the entire Origin System without geographic restriction, appearing during standard missions rather than claiming territory. His spawn locations are unpredictable and mission-dependent:

- **Assassination Missions**: Source of Death Marks that enable Stalker spawning.
- **General Missions**: Stalker may appear in any standard mission type (Exterminate, Defense, Survival, Capture, etc.).
- **Excluded Locations**: Stalker cannot spawn in Archwing missions, free roam (open-world) maps, relays, dojos, or Sorties.

The unpredictability of Stalker's arrivals heightens tension during marked missions, as players must remain vigilant while managing standard mission objectives.

## Lore

Stalker's origin traces to the Old War and Orokin civilization, revealing a tragedy that shaped his vendetta. Originally known as Sorren, he served as a Low Guardian—an elite Orokin soldier enhanced with Orokin technology. During his service, Sorren developed an unauthorized romantic relationship with a woman named Jade, fathering a child together. This violation of Orokin breeding protocols resulted in severe punishment: Sorren was stripped of his humanity and transformed into the first Warframe, his consciousness bound within biomechanical armor as both punishment and recycling of his combat potential.

Forced to watch the Tenno—the very weapons he served alongside—betray the Orokin Empire and slaughter their creators, Sorren's rage crystallized into singular purpose. He became the Stalker, determined to hunt down every Tenno and extract vengeance for the fall of the Orokin and his own transformation. His declaration—"I am your reckoning!"—reflects this burning resentment.

Over time, Stalker's vendetta deepened when he encountered Hunhow, a Sentient entity with its own grievances against the Tenno. Hunhow offered Stalker further enhancement through Sentient technology, elevating him into Shadow Stalker and expanding his capabilities. Recent lore reveals connections between Stalker and his lost past, including unresolved ties to Jade and their offspring, suggesting Stalker's motivations extend beyond simple revenge into protection and restoration.

## Strategy notes

Encountering Stalker requires understanding his combat patterns and exploiting specific tactics. Maintaining distance from his Hate melee attacks is critical, as his scythe possesses devastating reach and attack speed. Long-range weapons like bows, rifles, and launchers allow players to engage from safety while managing his Despair projectiles, which demand evasive movement to avoid consistent chip damage.

Burst-damage weapons prove more effective than sustained fire, capitalizing on brief windows when Stalker emerges from Smoke Screen or Teleport abilities. Squad coordination significantly impacts survival chances—coordinated fire overwhelms his defenses while distributed threat assessment prevents him from focusing on single targets. Defensive abilities like shields, damage mitigation, and healing become essential during prolonged encounters.

Defensive loadouts incorporating armor, shields, and healing mods enable longer survival against Stalker's sustained assault. His Absorb ability requires careful management; attacking while he channels Absorb reflects damage back as Magnetic harm, so ceasing fire and repositioning during this phase prevents self-damage. Understanding his ability rotations enables skilled players to time attacks for maximum impact and minimum risk.
