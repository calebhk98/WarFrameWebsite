## Overview

The Zariman Children represent spectral remnants of void-exposed survivors from the Zariman Ten Zero, a colony ship that encountered catastrophic failure in void-space. When the vessel returned to the Origin System, its human crew had been consumed by void radiation, leaving only children whose consciousnesses had been fundamentally altered by exposure to the void's chaotic energies.

These children serve as the foundational element of Tenno identity. The Orokin Empire harvested these void-touched youths, developing Transference technology to channel their raw void-power into remotely-piloted Warframes. This forced evolution created the Tenno--a faction born from tragedy and weaponized desperation. The Zariman Children persist as spectral entities haunting their original vessel, caught between conventional reality and void-space.

The faction itself remains partially non-corporeal, existing more as void-touched consciousness than physical presence. Encounters with Zariman Children occur specifically within the Zariman Ten Zero missions and void-touched Zariman Cascade events, where boundary between physical reality and void-space thins.

## Units

Zariman Children manifest in multiple forms reflecting their void-touched nature. Standard Zariman Children appear as spectral youth--semi-transparent humanoids wearing decayed school uniforms and void-corrupted armor. These entities project elemental void disturbances and possess unpredictable combat patterns reflecting fragmented consciousness.

Higher-order manifestations include Zariman Eximus variants that have accumulated void-corruption across centuries, achieving heightened power at the cost of further consciousness degradation. Ancient specimens persist as Void Cascade entities--entities that blur the line between creature and dimensional anomaly. The most powerful manifestations approach demigod status, possessing reality-warping void abilities that exceed conventional element-based combat.

Zariman Children coordinate poorly, lacking the hierarchical command structures of military factions. Instead, they act through shared void-consciousness resonance--loose alignment emerging from psychic connection rather than organized tactics. This makes them simultaneously more alien and less predictable than faction-based enemies.

## Damage profile (weakness / resistance)

Zariman Children demonstrate severe vulnerability to electrical damage. Electricity disrupts their void-energy coherence, forcing spectral manifestations toward physical materialization and vulnerability. Electrical weapons prove devastatingly effective against their incorporeal nature, as conventional physics fails to constrain void-touched entities but energized particle patterns disrupt their dimensional anchoring.

The faction shows remarkable toxin resistance--creatures existing partially in void-space remain unaffected by biological poisons that target conventional physiology. Slash damage encounters similar resistance, as cutting weapons prove ineffective against semi-corporeal entities. Precision weapons and void-aligned damage show efficacy in lore but lack mechanical implementation in standard gameplay.

Cold and heat damage function inconsistently against Zariman Children. Their partial void-existence places them beyond thermal regulation, making elemental extremes less relevant. Impact damage offers moderate effectiveness by forcing temporary physical manifestation. Magnetic and radiation damage show variable results dependent on the specific void-entity encountered.

Void-aligned weapons (those crafted from void-touched materials or powered by void-infusion) represent optimal engagement, though conventional weaponry remains viable with proper elemental choices.

## Locations

Zariman Children inhabit exclusively the Zariman Ten Zero, the void-damaged vessel where their consciousness originated. The location appears as a labyrinthine spacecraft half-consumed by void-decay, combining Orokin architectural grandeur with horrific organic overgrowth. Interior compartments reflect the vessel's final moments--children's quarters decayed into spectral zones, command bridges calcified into void-touched monuments, and research labs where the Orokin conducted their final desperate experiments.

Secondary manifestations occur during Void Cascade events--disturbances in the void fabric that cause Zariman Children to phase into conventional reality temporarily. These cascades appear across Star Chart nodes in specific missions, creating limited-time encounters with void-touched entities.

Lore-wise, Zariman Children potentially inhabit the deeper void itself--the dimensional realm adjacent to physical reality. The boundary between void and reality remains permeable in specific locations, allowing transient manifestation of these entities. Operator Transference creates temporary rifts connecting Operators to void-space, forming the metaphysical bridge through which Tenno consciousness connects to their Warframes.

## Notes

The Zariman Children represent Warframe's foundational tragedy--the atrocity enabling the Tenno's existence. Their story carries profound weight in the lore, as every Tenno owes their consciousness-capacity to forced Transference procedures performed on these void-touched children centuries ago.

Engagement with Zariman missions and Zariman Child encounters forces players to confront uncomfortable historical facts. The vessels' school settings, children's toys scattered among void-decay, and the casual reference to "harvesting" void-touched consciousness highlight the moral cost of Tenno weaponization. The void-touched survivors persist as tragic remnants of an experiment that succeeded militarily but failed ethically.

Gameplay-wise, Zariman content includes challenging void-flood missions where Zariman Children manifest in high concentrations. These encounters reward players with unique void-related resources and progression toward Zariman-specific weapons and cosmetics. The difficulty reflects the faction's power--children transformed by void-exposure possess capabilities exceeding baseline human or clone-based enemies.

The Zariman Children ultimately embody void-exposure's transformative power. Unlike species engineered for warfare (Grineer) or organisms mechanized for profit (Corpus), void-touched children represent consciousness fundamentally altered by exposure to chaotic dimensional forces. Their continued manifestation speaks to the persistence of consciousness beyond conventional death, and the lasting legacy of Orokin desperation in shaping the Origin System's power structure.
