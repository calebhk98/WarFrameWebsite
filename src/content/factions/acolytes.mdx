## Overview

Acolytes are a group of six elite enemies who serve the Shadow Stalker. Each Acolyte wears the helmet of the Shadow Stalker while taking on the appearance of a different Warframe, blending the aesthetic of familiar frames with the malevolent purpose of their master. Originally introduced during the limited-time Operation: Shadow Debt event, Acolytes became permanent fixtures of the game with Update 29.5, integrated as special encounters within The Steel Path.

Unlike ordinary enemies, Acolytes are not tied to a specific planet or mission type. They roam The Steel Path's star chart as unpredictable intrusions, spawning in active missions and hunting down players who have dared to push into the game's hardest content layer. Their presence raises the stakes of any Steel Path mission considerably, since they appear without warning and hit with enough force to end unprepared squads quickly.

The six Acolytes -- Angst, Malice, Mania, Misery, Torment, and Violence -- each draw from a distinct Warframe archetype and carry abilities and weapons associated with that archetype. Fighting them requires players to anticipate mechanics they would normally associate with allied Warframes, turned hostile and scaled for high-level play.

## Units

Each Acolyte has a distinct combat style derived from their Warframe inspiration. The table below lists them alongside their base WFCD health and shield values at the default codex rank.

|------|--------------|-------:|-------:|------:|------|
| Angst | Valkyr | 350 | 200 | 0 | Close-range melee attacker |
| Malice | Frost Prime | 350 | 200 | 0 | Ranged damage dealer |
| Mania | Loki | 350 | 200 | 0 | Evasive, teleporting striker |
| Misery | Nekros | 4000 | 200 | 0 | Area denial and support |
| Torment | Mesa | 350 | 200 | 0 | Explosive specialist |
| Violence | Limbo | 350 | 200 | 0 | Melee combatant with phase mechanics |

Misery stands apart from the other five with a base health pool an order of magnitude larger, making it the most durable of the group under standard conditions. All Acolytes begin with no base armor, meaning health-damage types -- particularly Viral -- bypass any armor-reduction step.

The Shadow Stalker, though technically distinct from the Acolytes, acts as their patron and appears in assassination encounters and specific scripted events. The Stalker carries 300 base armor and 750 health, giving it a significantly different damage profile compared to the unarmored Acolytes.

## Damage profile (weakness / resistance)

Acolytes have no base armor, which changes the damage-type calculus compared to armored enemies. Because armor reduction is not a factor, the primary concern is health type and any status-effect vulnerabilities.

Acolyte health is classified as Cloned Flesh in the game's internal type system, which is the same health category used by the Grineer. Cloned Flesh is weak to Slash and Gas damage. Radiation damage also deals increased damage against Acolyte health, making it a reliable damage type when fighting the group.

The shields carried by all Acolytes respond to the standard shield damage type chart: Magnetic damage deals bonus damage against shields, while Impact damage is also effective. Cold damage slows shield regeneration.

Acolytes apply a flat 62.5% damage reduction to all incoming damage at the base level. This reduction means that raw damage numbers on paper do not translate directly to effective damage dealt. Status effects that bypass or circumvent this reduction -- such as Viral stacks reducing maximum health -- become disproportionately valuable. Radiation, which also confuses enemy AI, can cause Acolytes to briefly redirect attention or pause attack patterns.

As of Update 36.0, Acolytes are neutral to all elemental damage types in the sense that no element is outright penalized, but the effective damage floor imposed by the damage reduction makes optimizing for the correct health/shield types more important than it would be against unprotected enemies.

## Locations

Acolytes spawn exclusively within The Steel Path. They do not appear in standard (non-Steel Path) missions, in Archwing missions, in Assassination missions, in Ascension missions, or in Duviri missions. Within those constraints, any active Steel Path mission has a chance to trigger an Acolyte spawn after several minutes of play.

Spawn timing is not fixed. Community observations indicate an average window of three to eight minutes between possible Acolyte appearances, though the exact algorithm is not publicly documented. Players farming Steel Essence -- the currency rewarded by killing Acolytes -- typically run endless mission types such as Survival or Defense to maximize time in mission and thus increase the number of Acolyte encounters per session.

Each kill rewards Steel Essence, which is spent at Teshin's shop in a Relay to purchase exclusive mods, cosmetics, and resources. This economic loop is the primary reason players engage with Acolytes after clearing the initial Steel Path star chart.

## Notes

Acolytes were originally introduced in Operation: Shadow Debt (2016), a limited-time event where they invaded missions across the star chart. At that time, only players with specific alert conditions active would encounter them. Their permanent integration into The Steel Path in Update 29.5 (November 2020) changed their role from event enemy to endgame recurring encounter.

Each Acolyte retains the abilities of the Warframe they impersonate. Mania uses Switch Teleport, which can displace players unprepared for sudden repositioning. Malice uses Magnetize, the Mag ability, which creates a damage bubble. Violence applies Ice Wave and Silence. Players familiar with the ability kits of the source Warframes can anticipate these behaviors and plan accordingly.

Acolytes do not spawn during Sorties, Nightwave missions, or any activity where the Steel Path modifier is not active. They do not appear on Railjack nodes even when those nodes are attempted in Steel Path mode.
