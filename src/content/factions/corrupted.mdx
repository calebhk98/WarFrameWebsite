## Overview

The Corrupted represent a unified enemy faction composed of enslaved soldiers from three normally distinct civilizations—Grineer, Corpus, and Infested—who have been mechanically and psychologically subjugated through neural implants installed by the Orokin. These control devices hijack the consciousness of affected soldiers, permitting the Orokin Neural Sentries to manipulate them as expendable puppets. The Corrupted serve as the primary antagonistic force in the Void and other Orokin-controlled regions, functioning as remnants of ancient automated defense systems still executing their original directives despite the fall of their creators.

The transformation wrought by Orokin neural control is visually dramatic. Corrupted soldiers shimmer with distinctive white and golden coloration, with golden hues pulsating through their corrupted bodies. In Grineer-based variants, crude cybernetics are replaced with elegant golden, symmetrical plates and bionics reflecting Orokin design philosophy. Each enslaved unit gains access to Orokin weapons and defensive technologies far superior to their original faction's equipment, yet they remain bound slaves with no autonomy or independent will. The Corrupted exist in a tragic state between life and machinery, conscious beings trapped in unwilling servitude.

## Units

The Corrupted field combat units derived from all three primary enemy factions, each enhanced with Orokin technology and neural manipulation. Despite their diverse biological origins, all Corrupted units share the golden aesthetic of Orokin control and respond to the Neural Sentries' commands without hesitation.

**Grineer-derived units**
- **Corrupted Lancer**: Standard rifle infantry converted from baseline Grineer soldiers; equipped with Orokin weaponry instead of crude Grineer arms
- **Corrupted Butcher**: Melee specialists originally trained in close-quarters combat; retain aggressive tactics but with Orokin blade technology
- **Corrupted Drahk**: Drahk beasts captured and implanted with neural control; serve as heavy assault units
- **Corrupted Drahk Master**: Commander variants capable of coordinating Corrupted Drahk packs
- **Corrupted Heavy Gunner**: Armor-plated suppression units maintaining their firepower role but dramatically enhanced with Orokin defensive plating
- **Corrupted Bombard**: Close-assault specialists retaining explosive capability but equipped with Orokin ordnance

**Corpus-derived units**
- **Corrupted Crewman**: Standard Corpus infantry converted to Orokin service; equipped with advanced plasma weaponry
- **Corrupted MOA**: Mechanized combat units repurposed from Corpus drone platforms
- **Corrupted Nullifier**: Shielded units originally designed to counter Tenno; their defensive bubbles now serve Orokin defense systems
- **Corrupted Drone**: Smaller automated units providing fire support

**Infested-derived units**
- **Corrupted Ancient**: Heavily mutated Infested specimens implanted with neural control to direct Corrupted operations

**Orokin-native units**
- **Orokin Drone**: Purely mechanical constructs built by the Orokin; appear alongside Corrupted soldiers as extension of Orokin defense networks

**Notable boss variants** periodically encountered in high-level Void missions include Corrupted Vor, a corrupted version of the once-independent bounty hunter, and the Corrupted Jackal, a mechanized boss unit serving Orokin directives in deep Void installations.

## Damage profile

### Weakness and Resistance

Corrupted units, despite their diverse origins, share fundamental Orokin technological characteristics that define their vulnerability. The Orokin neural implants and advanced defensive systems create distinct tactical patterns:

| Damage type | Multiplier | Notes |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Puncture | 1.75x | Pierces Orokin defensive plating and neural control systems |
| Viral | 1.5x | Disrupts biological components underlying Orokin implants |
| Radiation | 0.5x | Orokin shielding actively repels radiation damage |
| Heat | 1.0x | Neutral; Orokin materials are thermally balanced |
| Corrosive | 1.0x | Orokin materials resist acid degradation |

The primary vulnerability lies in puncture-based weaponry, which exploits the architectural weighting of Orokin defensive systems toward kinetic resistance rather than piercing attacks. Viral damage proves second-most effective because it simultaneously damages biological matter and compromises the neural implant's organic interface. Radiation damage, by contrast, is actively neutralized by Orokin energy shields, making it strategically ineffective despite its utility against Grineer. The Corrupted gain innate protection from radiation through their Orokin control systems, a safeguard their original factions lack.

Notably, faction-specific damage modifiers from the Corrupted units' parent factions (Grineer corrosive weakness, Corpus magnetic weakness) do not apply. The Orokin transformation essentially erases faction identity in mechanical terms, replacing natural biology with unified Orokin technology. This homogenization makes Corrupted forces tactically interchangeable regardless of origin, supporting Neural Sentry coordination.

## Locations

Corrupted forces occupy spaces where Orokin authority remains active or was recently reactivated. These are primarily Void-aligned environments and ancient Orokin installations:

- **Void Fissures and Void Cascade**: Tears in reality leading to pocket dimensions where Corrupted defend Orokin artifacts and relics
- **Lua**: The Orokin moon itself, particularly Conjunction Survival missions and other high-level void threats
- **Zariman Ten Zero**: An Orokin passenger vessel trapped in the Void; Corrupted maintain occupation and suppress Void-stranded colonists
- **Undercroft (Duviri Paradox)**: A dimension infused with Orokin aesthetics where Corrupted serve as enforcers
- **Orokin Derelict**: Void-touched orbital ruins where Corrupted protect remaining Orokin technology and research
- **Void Cascade**: Procedurally generated Void incursions spawning Corrupted defenders

## Strategy notes

Corrupted units require precise damage type selection. Puncture-focused builds using rifles, sniper weapons, or heavy piercing attacks are optimal. Viral damage builds, especially those combining slash weapons with toxin mods, provide excellent supplementary pressure. Teams should avoid radiation-focused loadouts as the Orokin neural systems neutralize this damage completely.

Corrupted Nullifiers present the most significant tactical challenge due to their void-field shields that drain ability energy and suppress Warframe powers. Dedicated void-shard weapons or coordinated team focus-fire is necessary to overcome their defenses. Nullifier shields deactivate temporarily after sufficient damage, creating windows for ability use or powered weapon strikes.

The presence of Orokin Drones alongside Corrupted soldiers indicates that automated systems still coordinate with neural-implanted units, suggesting a hybrid command structure between pure machinery and enslaved consciousness. Eliminating drones first can reduce coordination and precision of Corrupted unit tactics.
