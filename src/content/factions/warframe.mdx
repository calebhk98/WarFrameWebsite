## Overview

Warframe represents the player-controlled faction—the Tenno and their biomechanical armor suits. Each player embodies a Tenno, a warrior-consciousness transplanted into a powerful exo-skeleton called a Warframe. The faction encompasses not merely the physical suits, but the consciousness, will, and philosophy of the Tenno operators piloting them.

The Warframe faction emerged from catastrophe. During the Old War, the Orokin Empire faced extinction from Sentient invasion. Desperate, they weaponized survivors of the Zariman Ten Zero—a void-damaged vessel whose child passengers had been transformed by exposure to void-space into beings of inexplicable power. Through Transference technology, these children remotely piloted Warframes, turning the tide of war. That relationship between void-touched operator and biomechanical suit defines the Tenno faction to this day.

## Units

The Warframe faction consists of Tenno operators and their corresponding Warframes. Operators represent the conscious intelligence—void-touched children whose minds navigate the spaces between reality through Transference. Each operator embodies one of five philosophical schools: Madurai (engagement and strength), Vazarin (protection and healing), Naramon (knowledge and cunning), Unairu (endurance and survival), and Zenurik (domination and insight).

Warframes function as biomechanical extensions of operator consciousness. Sixty-plus distinct Warframe designs exist, each offering unique abilities, stats, and playstyles. Frames range from heavily-armored tanks like Rhino to glass-cannon damage specialists like Equinox, from support characters like Trinity to experimental hybrids like Sevagoth. Prime Warframes represent perfected iterations crafted during the Orokin Era, offering enhanced stats and additional polish slots.

Beyond standard Warframes, specialized variants exist: Umbral frames (corrupted originals), Zariman exclusives, and mechanically-unique experimentation platforms like Lavos and Xaku.

## Damage profile (weakness / resistance)

Warframes possess variable defensive profiles dependent on their design and modding. However, baseline Tenno forces encounter weakness against puncture damage—their exoskeletons emphasize energy absorption over physical penetration resistance. Electricity poses secondary vulnerability, particularly to faction-specific electrical modifiers that disrupt Warframe systems.

Conversely, Warframes demonstrate baseline resistance to slash damage, as their armor compositions prioritize cohesion over sectioning. Toxin weapons find diminished effectiveness against the bio-mechanical hybrid nature of Warframe construction. Experienced Tenno substantially modify these profiles through Warframe selection, defensive modding (Steel Fiber, Adaptation, Warding Halo), and ability-based mitigation (Iron Skin, Blessing, Revenant's invulnerability).

The diversity of Warframe defenses makes generalizations imprecise. A modded Grendel absorbs far more toxin than a glass-cannon Wisp, while Octavia's rhythm-based invulnerability phases invalidate traditional damage calculations entirely.

## Locations

Warframes operate across the entire Star Chart—all planets, all mission nodes, and all celestial regions. The faction's presence extends to void-touched spaces including Void Cascades and Void Fissure encounters where Tenno confront Void-contaminated enemies. Player-controlled Warframes also appear in Zariman missions, Void Flood operations, and the ever-expanding endgame content.

Operator-exclusive spaces include the Plains of Eidolon (where operators transference-out to utilize amps), the Cambion Drift, and relay hubs where operators interact socially. The Zariman Ten Zero—the void-damaged vessel where Tenno consciousness originated—serves as both lore epicenter and recurring mission location.

## Notes

The Warframe faction represents player agency within the game world. Every player action—mission completion, enemy defeat, syndicate progression—occurs through Warframe intermediation. This creates a unique narrative position where the player simultaneously experiences the story from inside the faction (as a Tenno pilot) and observes external reactions to the faction's legendary achievements.

Lore-wise, the Tenno possess unparalleled power. Across in-game history, they defeated Sentients, toppled the Orokin Empire, survived systematic extinction attempts, and fundamentally altered the balance of the Origin System. Yet they operate under constraints: memory loss, societal isolation, ongoing conflicts with equally-powered adversaries (Sisters of Parvos, Zariman entities), and the burden of consciousness fragmented across ancient transference technology.

The dual nature of Tenno-Warframe relationship raises philosophical questions central to Warframe's narrative. Do Tenno possess individual consciousness, or do they exist as distributed consciousness across all Warframes simultaneously? The game leaves this intentionally ambiguous, allowing players to construct personal interpretations of their role in the universe.

Five philosophical schools shape Tenno development and combat identity. Schools determine focus school (Madurai, Vazarin, Naramon, Unairu, Zenurik) offering passive benefits and specific void-strike modifiers. This system allows player differentiation beyond equipment choices, creating multiple valid playstyle specializations.
