## Overview

The Murmur is an eldritch faction composed of entities born from raw Void energy, shaped and directed by the being known as the Man in the Wall. Unlike the Grineer or Corpus -- military organizations driven by recognizable goals -- The Murmur represents an entirely alien threat with no conventional motivation. Its units are geometric, stone-like constructs with vaguely humanoid limbs that move with unsettling purpose across their home domain, a region described as "the Great Indifference": an inverted Void environment made up of massive megalithic structures suspended in impossible space.

The faction was unleashed upon Albrecht's Laboratories, also known as the Sanctum Anatomica on Deimos, following the events of The New War and the Whispers in the Walls update. When Ballas attempted to redirect the Void conduit to transport himself and his Narmer followers to the Tau System, the Man in the Wall refocused its attention on the Origin System, sending the Murmur as an advance force. The defense structure known as the Kalymos Sequence was activated to contain the incursion and prevent the Murmur from breaching into the wider Origin System.

What makes The Murmur particularly dangerous is their incomprehensibility. Their units do not follow the damage-type logic of biological or mechanical enemies. Standard anti-armor or anti-shield strategies offer no advantage because their form of existence transcends conventional anatomy. Fighting them requires an understanding of Void-based elemental properties, specifically Electricity and Radiation, which disrupt their energetic coherence.

## Units

The Murmur divides broadly into four tiers of threat: Fragments, Constructs, Rogue Necramechs, and Boss-tier entities.

**Fragments** form the bulk of Murmur combat encounters. These are standard-rank units that appear in large numbers.

| Unit | Role | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hurling Fragment | Ranged attacker | Launches projectiles of condensed Void energy |
| Lumbering Fragment | Melee bruiser | Slow but high health pool |
| Shuffling Fragment | Skirmisher | Fast movement, moderate threat |
| Rupturing Fragment | Area-denial | Detonates on death, dealing area damage |

**Constructs** are hardier, mid-tier enemies with specific behaviors.

| Unit | Role | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gruzzling | Resource thief | Steals Conduit Dockets if not killed quickly |
| The Anatomizer | Elite combatant | Heavy-hitting construct with durable frame |
| The Hollow Vein | Support | Provides buffs to nearby Murmur units |
| The Severed Warden | Elite guardian | Defends key objectives in Sanctum missions |

**Rogue Necramechs** are malfunctioned Entrati defense machines that have been subverted by Murmur influence. They include variants of the Bonewidow, Voidrig, Arcocanid, and Culverin -- originally built to defend Albrecht's Laboratories, now turned against intruders and defenders alike.

**Boss-tier entities** represent the highest threat. Whispers are Void-born doppelgangers that impersonate familiar figures to disorient players. The Fragmented is a multi-phase boss encountered in deep Sanctum expeditions, cycling through four distinct forms: the Suzerain, the Zelator, the Anchorite, and the One.

## Damage profile (weakness / resistance)

The Murmur's Void-energy composition gives them a distinct damage profile. Because they are not biological or mechanical, the usual heuristics break down.

| Damage type | Modifier | Reason |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Electricity | x1.5 | Disrupts energetic coherence |
| Radiation | x1.5 | Destabilizes Void-matter structure |
| Viral | x0.5 | No biological tissue to infect |

Building for Radiation or Electricity output is the highest-priority damage consideration when entering Sanctum Anatomica. Combining them into a Radiation proc (Heat + Cold) or using Electricity-heavy elemental combos will maximize damage per second against all Murmur unit types. Viral, despite being strong against most Grineer and many other factions, is largely wasted here.

Corrosive and Blast damage perform at neutral effectiveness. Slash-based proc strategies (which circumvent armor) offer limited benefit because Fragment-tier units rely on Void-energy durability rather than ferrite or alloy armor types.

## Locations

The Murmur appear almost exclusively in one area of the Origin System: the Sanctum Anatomica on Deimos. This tileset houses Albrecht Entrati's laboratories and serves as the central battleground for the Whispers in the Walls content update. Players access it via the Deimos navigation node after completing the relevant prerequisites.

Within the tileset, The Murmur occupy the deeper, more distorted sections where the Void bleeds into realspace. Surface-level portions of the laboratories may still feature Infestation encounters, but the interior and boss areas transition to Murmur-dominated territory.

Limited Murmur presence is also felt in Duviri and Void-adjacent content, reflecting the Man in the Wall's domain. The Dormizone, a personal player space with Void-themed architecture, occasionally surfaces Murmur-related narrative elements.

## Notes

The Murmur do not respond to standard syndicate-aligned damage mods (Bane-type mods keyed to Grineer, Corpus, or Infested deal their full modifier only to those faction types). When loadout planning for Sanctum Anatomica runs, players should prioritize Electricity or Radiation combination mods rather than faction-specific damage bonuses.

Warframes with strong Electricity-based abilities -- such as Volt -- or those providing Radiation damage through kit synergies (Ember, Chroma with the right elemental configuration) outperform builds tuned for biological factions. Energy management is also important: Murmur encounters in deeper Sanctum expeditions are lengthy, and sustain-focused builds outlast glass-cannon alternatives.

The Gruzzling merits special attention in Conduit Defense-style objectives. Prioritizing it over standard Fragment units prevents resource loss and keeps the mission reward track intact.
