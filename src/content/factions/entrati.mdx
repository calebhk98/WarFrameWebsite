## Overview

The Entrati are an ancient Orokin family who pioneered advanced Void research and technology during the Old War. As descendants of the legendary researcher Albrecht Entrati, they discovered the fundamental principles of Void manipulation and created the Heart--a device essential to Tenno survival. Once dominant Orokin scientists, the family endured the apocalyptic Sentient invasion and subsequent collapse of Orokin civilization by retreating to Deimos, where they adapted to centuries of isolation and infestation. Today, the Entrati serve as allies to the Tenno, sharing ancient knowledge and advanced technology from their hidden Necralisk enclave.

The family operates within an unusual social structure born from their unique biology. Most members have been partially infested by the Technocyte virus, yet they retain their consciousness and intelligence, allowing them to study infestation while resisting complete assimilation. This balance between humanity and biological transformation defines Entrati culture and scientific endeavor.

## Units

The Entrati faction consists primarily of family members and trained specialists, rather than generic soldier units. During Void Fissure incursions and hostile encounters, the following operatives manifest:

- **Entrati Agents**: Combat-capable family members deploying advanced Orokin technology
- **Mother (Euleria/Gomaitru)**: The family matriarch and philosopher-scientist who maintains the Heart and oversees Void operations
- **Father (Vilcor)**: The weapons master who designed Necramech technology and defensive systems
- **Daughter (Kaelli)**: The biologist specializing in Infested behavior and collection management
- **Son (Kermerros)**: The breeding specialist responsible for Infested fauna cultivation

Unlike traditional faction units, Entrati operatives wield sophisticated Orokin-era weaponry and possess regenerative capabilities derived from controlled infestation. They employ tactical retreat and resource denial strategies rather than frontal assaults, preferring to protect their research installations and genetic archives.

## Damage profile

### Weakness & Resistance

The Entrati's infested biology grants them unexpected resilience patterns. Their part-biological, part-technological nature means conventional kinetic damage is absorbed efficiently, while energy-based attacks bypass their hardened carapaces.

| Damage type | Multiplier | Notes |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Viral | 1.5x | Exploits infestation biology |
| Slash | 1.25x | Cuts through bio-synthetic plating |
| Impact | 0.85x | Distributed across reinforced frame |
| Puncture | 1.0x | Neutral effect |

Heat and electricity prove moderately effective, but the Entrati's controlled infestation provides thermal regulation. Corrosive damage is resisted due to biological immunity developed over centuries of exposure. Radiation triggers unpredictable infestation surges and is avoided by engagement protocols.

## Locations

The Entrati maintain primary operations in the Cambion Drift, a region on Deimos recovered after the Zariman incident restored the Heart's function. Secondary presence appears during:

- **Cambion Drift (Deimos)**: The primary staging ground, featuring the Necralisk research enclave and Isolation Vault laboratories where the Entrati conduct Void experiments
- **Isolation Vaults**: Underground chambers containing genetic archives and Void-infused specimens protected by Mother's personal defense networks
- **Void Fissures**: Temporal ruptures where Entrati agents sometimes emerge alongside Void manifestations, especially during void cascade events

The Entrati rarely venture to other planets, preferring to remain in the Void-saturated environment of Deimos where their technology functions optimally and infestation pressure remains controlled.

## Notes

The Entrati represent the closest modern parallel to Orokin civilization, preserving knowledge and technology lost after the Old War's end. Their partial infestation is not a weakness but a strategic adaptation, allowing them to thrive in Deimos's hostile environment while maintaining individual consciousness and family structure.

Players who achieve maximum standing with the Entrati are welcomed as honorary family members and granted the title "Ayatan," signifying acceptance into their collective endeavor. This acceptance reflects the family's pragmatic philosophy: that knowledge and survival supersede biological origin.

The family's use of Necramech technology demonstrates their commitment to both defensive capability and biological study. Unlike the Grineer's crude augmentation, Entrati cybernetics preserve humanity while enhancing capability, reflecting their Orokin heritage of precision engineering.
