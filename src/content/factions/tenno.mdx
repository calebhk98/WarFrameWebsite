## Overview

The Tenno are an enigmatic faction of super-soldiers originating from a catastrophic void-jump accident that fundamentally altered human consciousness and unlocked reality-breaking powers. Distinct from the Corpus, Grineer, Infested, and Orokin, the Tenno represent player-controlled forces operating across the Origin System with near-incomprehensible capabilities derived from exposure to the Void itself.

Individual Tenno lack biological existence in the traditional sense—each player embodies an Operator, a human consciousness exposed to the Void during the *Zariman Ten Zero* catastrophe. Through mysterious technology called Transference, Operators channel their consciousness into biomechanical Warframes, armor-suits of incomparable power. The Tenno philosophy emphasizes adaptation, resilience, and the protection of innocents across the Origin System.

## Units

The Tenno faction comprises exclusively player-controlled entities, making this section describe player-controlled units rather than enemies:

**Operators**: Void-touched humans whose consciousness resides within Orbiters and projects into Warframes. Operators possess innate void-manipulation abilities unlocked through Focus schools, granting capacity for void dashing, transference, and eventually becoming visible avatars in later-game content. Operators represent raw Void power channeled through human willpower and training.

**Warframes**: Biomechanical armor suits housing Operator consciousness, granting superhuman combat capabilities. Each Warframe embodies distinct combat philosophies, from Mag's electromagnetic mastery to Rhino's brute strength. Warframes provide vast ability arsenals, durable health pools, shield systems, and energy reserves for ability casting—rendering individual Tenno capable of soloing content and outmatching organized military forces.

**Orbiters**: Mobile bases providing life support, arsenal management, foundry access, and void jump capability. Each Tenno pilot commands an Orbiter capable of void-jumping to any location in the Origin System, enabling strategic deployment and rapid response.

## Damage profile

As player-controlled entities, Tenno possess no inherent weaknesses or resistances; instead, individual Warframes feature customizable defense profiles through modding. However, gameplay suggests the following:

**Tenno strengths**: Adaptability defines Tenno superiority—players customize weapons, abilities, and mods for any situation, demonstrating unmatched tactical flexibility. Void-based abilities bypass conventional defenses, making Tenno threats even to technologically advanced enemies like Corpus or Sentients.

**Tenno vulnerabilities**: Individual Tenno, despite immense power, remain mortal when unequipped. Operator vulnerability drives strategic decisions; transmission interference, hacking, or Void energy disruption can strand operators from Warframes. Ancient Corrupted units pose unique threats through vacuum effects and Void-based attacks that prove difficult to mitigate.

## Locations

The Tenno faction operates across the entire Origin System and beyond:

- **Origin System Proper**: All planetary territories, from Earth's surface to Sedna's void gates.
- **The Void**: The space between spaces, containing Void Fissure content, the Zariman Ten Zero, and Void-locked artifacts.
- **Relays**: Neutral safe zones where Tenno congregate for trading, diplomacy, and coordination.
- **Orbiters**: Individual mobile bases providing secure forward operating locations.
- **Railjack**: Tenno warships operating in hostile space, extending reach beyond traditional void jump capabilities.

The Tenno claim no single planetary territory; instead, their presence spans all accessible regions simultaneously.

## Lore

Tenno history begins not with awakening but with catastrophe. During the "Void Era," the research vessel *Zariman Ten Zero* conducted an experimental void-jump to test faster-than-light travel. Something went catastrophically wrong. The jump succeeded, but the void's chaotic energies flooded the ship, exposing the crew and child passengers to unfiltered Void exposure. Most died. The survivors—children—underwent inexplicable transformations, developing reality-bending powers that modern science cannot adequately explain.

The children drifted in the void-touched wreckage until the Orokin discovered them. Recognizing their potential, the Orokin created the Transference technology, inventing the process of channeling human consciousness into biomechanical Warframes. The Orokin bred the Tenno into an army of super-soldiers, deploying them against the Sentient invasion during the Old War. The Tenno proved instrumental in halting the Sentient onslaught, earning the Orokin's grudging respect and confidence.

That trust proved fatal. When the Orokin ordered the execution of Margulis, a researcher who had protected Tenno children, the Tenno turned on their creators. The betrayal was total; Tenno slaughtered the Orokin leadership, shattering their civilization. In the chaos, Natah, a Sentient agent, betrayed her own kind to preserve the sleeping Tenno, adopting the alias of the Lotus and guiding their slumber and eventual awakening.

The Tenno reawakened in contemporary times, guided by the Lotus (and later by the mysterious Ordis Cephalon), to find the Origin System fragmented among new powers. The Tenno emerged not as saviors but as agents of justice, operating according to their own philosophical principles while navigating a complex political landscape.

## Strategy notes

Tenno progression emphasizes understanding Warframe abilities, weapon optimization, and modding synergies. Beginners should focus on acquiring starter Warframes (Mag, Loki, Excalibur) and learning core mechanics through Earth and Mars missions. Learning to modulate ability usage alongside weapon damage creates efficient mission completion and resource accumulation.

Mid-game Tenno should pursue Mastery Rank progression by leveling diverse weapons and frames, unlocking access to higher-level content and vendor tiers. Building specialized loadouts for specific mission types—Defense, Survival, Disruption—accelerates progression and enables participation in endgame activities. Understanding elemental damage combinations and status effect interactions with mods separates average players from optimized squad contributors.

Veteran Tenno focus on endgame progression: farming Prime variants, collecting cosmetics, optimizing niche builds, and participating in endgame activities like Steel Path and Arbitrations. Mastery rank 30+ unlocks all weapons and frames while enabling maximum daily standing caps across syndicates. The journey from novice Tenno to veteran spans thousands of hours but offers deep systems for specialization and creativity.
