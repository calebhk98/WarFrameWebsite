## Overview

The Cambion Drift is Warframe's third open-world zone, introduced with Heart of Deimos (Update 29.0) in August 2020. It is set on Deimos, a moon of Mars and the most hostile terrain in the solar system -- an entirely Infested world where the landscape itself is alive and hostile. The Drift sits above the Necralisk, an Orokin research facility now inhabited by the Entrati family, a Void-touched Orokin lineage partially consumed by the Infestation.

The Drift's defining feature is its extremely fast Vome-Fass cycle, completing a full oscillation every approximately 6 minutes and 30 seconds. During Vome the landscape glows blue-white and certain resources and fish are available; during Fass it shifts orange-red with a different set. This rapid cycling creates a resource-gathering rhythm unlike any other open world and demands regular adaptation during bounty runs.

## Hub -- Necralisk

The Necralisk is an Orokin-era biological research facility built into the Infested flesh of Deimos. It serves as the social hub and is populated by the Entrati family -- an ancient Orokin family who have been living in isolation on Deimos for centuries, their minds and bodies partially consumed by the Infestation. Their dysfunction and strained family relationships form the emotional core of the Heart of Deimos narrative.

Mother, the primary vendor and bounty giver, offers the five-tier bounty structure and manages the Token economy. Father, Son, Grandmother, Daughter, and Otak each provide specific services: Father for Necramech and Deimos weapon blueprints, Daughter for fish cutting and standing, Son for conservation, Grandmother for a token-exchange service, and Otak for mining and vault-related items.

The **Entrati** faction governs Necralisk standing and unlocks access to Necramech components, Deimos-specific weapon parts, and Helminth room access. The **Necraloid** faction, a separate group of necromancers within the facility, governs Necramech forma, arcane upgrades, and Necramech-related cosmetics through their own standing track.

## Factions and syndicates

**Deimos Infested** are the primary enemy faction. They differ significantly from standard Infestation -- Deimos enemies include Carnis (predatory packs), Saxum (armored stone-carapace units), Brachiolyst (flying Infested), Boilers, and various mutated types not found elsewhere. Slash, Corrosive, and Heat damage types work well against their Flesh and Ferrite health pools. Certain enemies regenerate health rapidly unless finished quickly.

**Entrati** standing is the Necralisk's main syndicate and is earned through bounties, turning in Mother Tokens, and gifting items to specific family members. Each family member accepts different items (fish parts to Daughter, mining gems to Otak, Infested resources to others) and returns their own token type. Mother converts these into Entrati standing. This multi-step token economy is unique in Warframe and requires tracking multiple conversion chains.

**Necraloid** standing is earned through Orokin Matrices and specific Deimos resources donated at the Necraloid shrine inside the Necralisk. They do not have bounties; their standing is entirely resource-driven.

## Bounties and activities

Mother's bounties cover the standard five-tier structure with Infested-themed objectives: extermination, spy vaults inside biological structures, defend Vitreospina extraction points, and escort missions through hostile Infested terrain. Bounties reward Entrati tokens, Deimos resources, and Necramech components at higher tiers.

**Isolation Vault runs** are a separate bounty type that sends players underground into sealed Orokin vaults beneath the Drift. These vault runs involve navigating a Necramech-required underground section, defeating a Vault guardian, and looting the vault interior. Three nested vault levels exist (one, two, and three vaults chained), each requiring a separate bounty tier. Vault runs are the primary source of rare Entrati mods and certain Deimos weapon blueprints.

**Fishing** in the Drift targets three fish families: Vitreospina, Synathid, and Chondricord. Fish behavior is tied to the Vome-Fass cycle -- each species favors one phase. Spears from Daughter and bait from Son attract specific species. Unlike other open worlds, Drift fish have their own residue mechanic where the correct phase cycle affects where bait functions.

**Mining** on Deimos yields unique gems (Thaumica, Marquise Thyst, Marquise Varra, and the rare Empath minerals) used in Entrati crafting and weapon blueprints. Mining veins are distributed across the surface and in cave systems.

**Conservation** under Son targets Deimos wildlife: Avichaea, Bolarola (variant), Cryptillex, and Predasite. Each species has its own echo-lure and spawn behavior. Perfect captures award Son Tokens and conservation tags.

**Necramech deployment** is the Drift's signature gameplay element. Players can summon a Necramech (Voidrig or Bonewidow) by using the Necramech gear item. Necramechs are essential for Isolation Vaults and the Arcana Vault extension, and they are the only mechanism that can engage certain underground bosses. Necramech parts drop from enemies on the Drift and from vault runs.

## Cycle

The Vome-Fass cycle runs approximately 6 minutes 30 seconds per phase, making it the fastest cycle in the game. Vome is identified by blue luminescence in the terrain and sky; Fass brings orange-red light. The cycle affects:

- Fishing species availability (Vome vs Fass fish)
- Residue deposits on the surface (Vome Residue vs Fass Residue, used in crafting)
- Spawn behavior of certain Infested types
- Vault entry mechanics in specific areas

Warframestat.us provides a live Vome-Fass timer. Players planning extended fishing or mining sessions must account for phase transitions, as the relevant yield windows are short.

## Resources

**Residue types** are central to Deimos crafting. Vome Residue and Fass Residue appear on the landscape during their respective phases and are harvested by approaching glowing pools. Sporulate Sacs, Pustulite, and Empath Residue also appear as collectible deposits. These feed Entrati weapon blueprints and vault-run related crafting.

**Thaumica** is a rare mining drop used in Necramech part recipes. **Orokin Matrices** are obtained from Isolation Vault runs and are Necraloid standing currency.

**Infested Catalyst** is a unique resource that enables Helminth system use -- the Helminth room in the Orbiter requires Infested resources including multiple Catalyst types to unlock abilities for subsuming. The Cambion Drift is the primary source of these items.

## Unique mechanics

**Lure feeding** is a mechanic specific to Deimos wildlife conservation. Son's creatures require feeding with specific Residue types before they respond to echo-lures. Unlike other open worlds where the lure alone works, Deimos adds a preparatory step.

**Helminth subsume system** is unlocked through the Deimos narrative and requires Infested resources that originate on Deimos. While the Helminth room itself is in the player's Orbiter, the Cambion Drift is the primary resource source. Subsuming Warframes to extract their abilities and infusing them onto other frames is a major build-customization option.

**Necramech forma and archon shard** progression for Necramechs is handled via the Necraloid, making the Drift and its underlying economy relevant throughout the endgame for players who want optimized Necramech builds.

**Deimos Arcana bounties** extend the Isolation Vault chain to a fourth level and reward Arcana-exclusive mods. These are obtained from a separate bounty tier in the Necralisk.

## Lore

Deimos is a former Orokin moon that was consumed by the Infestation during the Old War. The Entrati family remained, studying the Infestation's Void-link, and gradually became part of it. Heart of Deimos reveals their fractured family dynamic and the Cambion Drift's role as the site of early Necramech experiments -- the Voidrig and Bonewidow are Entrati designs, built to survive the Infested surface and Void-adjacent threats.

The Void anomaly that periodically appears above Deimos (visible in-orbit as a mission type) is tied to Deimos's unique Void contamination -- the moon phases in and out of a Void state that has been intensifying since the Sentient War.
