## Overview

Zariman Ten Zero is Warframe's fourth open-world zone, introduced with the Angels of the Zariman update (Update 31.5) in May 2022. It is set aboard a massive Orokin colony ship that became lost in the Void and returned decades later with its human passengers -- including the Tenno as children -- fundamentally changed. Unlike the three previous open worlds, the Zariman takes place inside a vast ship interior rather than on a planetary surface.

The zone has no environmental day-night or temperature cycle. Instead, its defining rhythm comes from rotating mission types -- Void Cascade, Void Flood, and Void Armageddon -- each with distinct mechanics that reward different playstyles and loadouts. Enemy levels range from 40 to 60 in standard bounties, placing the Zariman firmly in mid-to-high endgame territory.

## Hub -- Chrysalith

The Chrysalith is the social hub of the Zariman, a repurposed section of the ship stabilized for habitation. The Holdfasts -- a faction of humans who survived the Zariman's Void passage and chose to remain aboard rather than return to the Origin System -- administer the Chrysalith.

Quinn is the primary bounty giver and liaison between the Tenno and the Holdfasts. Yonta handles resource exchange and certain standing transactions. Archimedean Yonta offers lore codex entries and Void-related research. Cavalon the Weave vendor sells Zariman-specific weapons and Voidshell cosmetics. Hombask handles Zariman-specific Warframe customization.

The Holdfasts faction is the primary standing track for the Zariman. Standing is earned through bounties, completing mission types successfully, and donating Voidplume feathers to Quinn. Holdfast standing unlocks Zariman-exclusive weapons (Laetum, Phenmor, Alternox, Hespar, Azothane, Bubonico upgrades), Voidshell skins, and Operator cosmetics.

## Factions and syndicates

**Angel-touched Grineer and Corpus** populate the Zariman. These are standard factions possessed or mutated by Void corruption. They retain their usual damage profiles -- Corrosive against Grineer Alloy Armor, Magnetic and Viral against Corpus -- but certain Void-type enemies require Operator amp damage to fully neutralize.

**The Angels** are the Zariman's most dangerous enemies. Angels are former human Zariman survivors who were completely consumed by Void energy and transformed into towering, powerful entities. They patrol specific sections of the ship and engage automatically if players enter their territory. Angels have extremely high health pools and use Void-energy attacks that require Operator damage (void sinks and operator mode) to efficiently damage their weak points.

**The Holdfasts** are the player-allied faction and the only standing track available on the Zariman. There is no secondary faction with separate rewards. All Zariman standing goes to Holdfasts.

## Bounties and activities

Quinn offers five rotating bounty tiers in the Chrysalith. The Zariman's bounty system differs from other open worlds in that each tier is associated with a specific mission type rather than a generic set of objectives.

**Void Cascade** is the primary horde-defense mission type. Players defend Vitoplast generators from waves of Void-corrupted enemies. Operators must seal Void Fissures by entering and stabilizing them in Void mode. The mission scales in difficulty with each rotation and rewards Pathos Clamps, Voidplumes, and Holdfast standing.

**Void Flood** places players in a time-pressure scenario where they must collect Vitoplast from enemies and deposit it at collection points before the ship's Void containment fails. Enemy density is high and movement efficiency matters significantly. Void Flood rewards differ from Cascade and include unique Zariman mods.

**Void Armageddon** is the endurance variant, pitting squads against sustained waves of enemies at the highest difficulty tier. It rewards the most Voidplumes per run and is the preferred farm for players grinding Holdfasts standing caps.

**Netracell missions** (added post-launch) are stealth-optional exploration challenges inside sealed ship sections. They reward Archon Shard fragments and Tenno-aligned resources unavailable elsewhere. Netracells require a key item and run on a weekly reset cadence.

## Cycle

The Zariman has no environmental cycle analogous to day-night or Vome-Fass. Mission type availability rotates on a timer displayed in the Chrysalith hub. Players choose which mission type to run based on their current farming goal -- Cascade for general use, Armageddon for maximum Voidplume volume, Flood for specific mod drops.

This mission-rotation system makes the Zariman more accessible for solo play than Eidolon hunting or Orb Mother fights, as each activity is completable with a public squad or solo, though squad composition affects efficiency significantly.

## Resources

**Pathos Clamps** are the Zariman's primary crafting currency, used in all Zariman weapon blueprints. They drop from bounty completions and from enemies on the ship. Players farming Zariman weapons typically need large quantities, making Armageddon runs the preferred method.

**Voidplume feathers** (Down, Pinion, Quill, and Crest) are tiered feathers that serve dual purposes: they are standing donations for Holdfasts ranking and are used in some crafting recipes. Down feathers are common; Crest feathers are rare and valued. They drop from enemies during missions and from Angels.

**Thrax Plasm** is a rare Zariman resource that drops from the Thrax Centurion and Thrax Legatus enemy types. It is used in specific blueprints and is a reliable early bottleneck for new Zariman players.

**Aggristone and Nodulite** are Zariman-exclusive resources that feed the secondary Void weapon blueprints.

## Unique mechanics

**Void Sinks** are environmental hazards and interactive objectives throughout the Zariman. In Operator mode, Tenno can enter and stabilize a Void Sink, which is required to progress Cascade missions, neutralize certain enemies, and access hidden areas. This is the primary mechanic that makes Operator progression relevant on the Zariman -- a higher-tier amp accelerates sink-sealing.

**Angels of the Zariman** function as elite patrol enemies similar to Wolf of Saturn Six events. They have extreme health and significant knockback effects, and they drop rare Voidplumes on death. The quest introduces a named Angel (Kira) as the climactic boss of the Angels of the Zariman storyline. Patrol Angels encountered in open missions are unnamed. Engaging them is optional but lucrative for standing.

**Voidshell material system** is exclusively available on the Zariman. Voidshell skins for Warframes have a secondary cosmetic layer that uses Strata Shards purchased from Cavalon. These shards, applied in the Arsenal, change the energy-reactive texture of the Voidshell skin. Different shards produce different material appearances.

**Laetum and Phenmor** are secondary and primary Incarnon weapons respectively, first introduced with the Zariman. They begin as ordinary weapons (both obtained through the Holdfasts syndicate on the Zariman) but can receive Incarnon Genesis upgrades that transform them into high-performance variants. The Zariman is the origin point of the Incarnon weapon system, which later expanded to other weapons via the Circuit game mode.

## Lore

The Zariman Ten Zero was an Orokin colony ship that entered the Void accidentally and returned decades later. Only the children aboard survived -- their minds linked to the Void and transformed into the Tenno. The adult passengers were either killed by the Void or driven mad. The Holdfasts are a surviving faction of individuals who developed Void resistance through exposure rather than the Tenno's transformative immersion.

The New War quest is a prerequisite for accessing the Zariman and concludes the central conflict between the Tenno, the Sentients, and the Lotus's true allegiance. Angels of the Zariman, the follow-up update, brings players back to the ship and deepens the story of those who were left behind during the Tenno's absence.
