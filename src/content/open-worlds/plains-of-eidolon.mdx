## Overview

The Plains of Eidolon is Warframe's first large open-world zone, introduced in Update 22.0 in October 2017. It occupies the surface of Earth, a planet already familiar from the early star chart, and expands it into a sprawling landscape of rolling hills, wetlands, and Ostron ruins. Players enter through the Cetus gate on the day-night boundary of Earth.

The zone's defining characteristic is its 100-minute day-night cycle. During the day the plains are patrolled by Tusk Grineer troops at moderate-to-high enemy levels, and most bounties and resource-gathering activities take place under sunlight. When night falls, three types of Eidolons -- immense spectral Sentient constructs -- rise from the lake and become the primary endgame challenge. Eidolon hunting is one of the most mechanically complex and rewarding activities in the entire game, requiring coordinated squads, specialized amps, and precise timing against a hard nightly window.

## Hub -- Cetus

Cetus is a walled Ostron settlement that serves as the social hub for the plains. The Ostrons are a people who scavenge Orokin towers that periodically emerge from the ocean near their town, trading the salvage for survival. Konzu stands near the gate and offers bounties; other vendors inside the town handle fishing equipment, mining, conservation, and the Quills syndicate entrance.

The Quills are a secretive Ostron sect who serve the crippled Sentient Unum, a consciousness housed inside the great Orokin tower above Cetus. The Quills are the primary source of amp components and Operator cosmetics, and they gate access to Eidolon arcanes through their reputation system.

Players rank up with two syndicates here: the Ostrons (through Konzu's bounties and selling fish and gems), and the Quills (through Eidolon shard donations and capturing Sentient cores). Ostron standing unlocks fishing and mining tools, bait blueprints, and Zaw melee weapon parts. Quills standing unlocks amp components and Operator arcane upgrades.

## Factions and syndicates

**Tusk Grineer** are the dominant enemy faction on the plains. They deploy standard Grineer troop types adapted for open terrain -- Tusk Bolkors (gunship dropships), Tusk Thumpers (ground-pounding armored walkers), and various infantry. Their levels scale from roughly 20 to 40 in open patrol and higher inside bounty targets. Corrosive damage works well against their Alloy Armor, while Slash and Toxin procs bypass their base health effectively.

**The Ostrons** are the civilian faction of Cetus. They do not appear in combat but their standing meter governs access to Cetus commerce. Daily standing caps are tied to Mastery Rank; the community convention is to complete all five bounty tiers per day to maximize gains.

**The Quills** are accessible via the hidden passage inside Cetus. Their standing is earned differently -- by donating Sentient cores and Eidolon shards -- and their offerings are almost entirely gated to Operator-related content: amp prisms, scaffolds, braces, and Arcane upgrades.

## Bounties and activities

Konzu offers five rotating bounty tiers each day-night cycle. Bounties take place inside the plains and consist of sequential objectives: capture targets, defend excavators, eliminate commanders, hack terminals, and more. Bounties reward standing, resources, and -- at higher tiers -- Eidolon-related components, arcane blueprints, and rare mods.

**Fishing** on the plains distinguishes between day and night fish species. Murkrays, Mortus Lungfish, and Mawfish appear during the day; Noctrul and Glappid appear only at night. Fish are traded to Hai-Luk in Cetus for standing and can be filleted for components used in Zaw crafting and some Cetus offerings. Luminous Dye makes fish visible; specific bait attracts specific species.

**Mining** yields Eidolon gems (Sentirum, Nyth, Hesperon, Maprico, Tear Azurite, Crimzian) through the Nosam Cutter or its advanced variant. Gems feed the Ostron standing economy and several Cetus blueprint recipes. Mining veins appear as glowing dots on the scanner display.

**Conservation** (introduced in a later patch via Master Teasonai) lets players tranquilize and capture wildlife -- Condrocs, Mergoos, Kubrodon -- using Echo-Lures and Tranq Rifles. Successful captures grant standing and conservation tags redeemable for cosmetics.

**Eidolon hunting** is the primary endgame activity on the plains and runs only at night. The Teralyst spawns automatically at nightfall, followed by the Gantulyst and Hydrolyst if lure conditions are met. Teams use void-damage amps and specific Warframes to strip Eidolon synovia (weak points), then capture rather than kill to earn arcane rewards and shards. Full tridolon runs (Teralyst, Gantulyst, Hydrolyst all captured in a single night) require highly coordinated teams with maxed amps and strong Warframe arcanes.

## Cycle

The plains cycle runs on a real-time 100-minute loop, divided into approximately 50 minutes of day and 50 minutes of night (the split varies slightly). Warframestat.us provides a live timer. Night is defined by the moment the sun disappears below the horizon near the Cetus gate, and Eidolons become vulnerable at the start of that window. Day fishing species and night fishing species do not overlap; Eidolon hunts are impossible during the day phase.

Players typically use the day phase for bounties, mining, fishing, and open patrol farming. The transition to night is used to set up lure charging routes, and organized hunt squads enter from Cetus immediately at night-start to maximize the available window.

## Resources

The plains produce several resources not found elsewhere. **Cetus Wisps** are the most sought-after, floating over water surfaces at night and required for building Amps. **Eidolon Shards** -- Intact, Exceptional, Flawless, and Brilliant variants -- drop from Eidolon captures and are currency for Quills standing and arcane upgrades. **Sentient Cores** come from Eidolon synovia destruction and smaller Vomvalysts.

Ore types specific to Earth (Iradite, Grokdrul, Murkray parts) feed Cetus blueprint recipes. Rare gems like Sentirum and Nyth come from mining veins and are tradeable with Ostrons for standing.

## Unique mechanics

**Tusk Thumpers** are heavily armored walker drones that patrol the plains and require sustained damage to their exposed legs and knee joints to down. They come in three escalating variants (Thumper, Doma Thumper, Bolkor-class) and carry specific drops including Wisp-adjacent components.

**Ghouls** are Grineer bio-weapons that periodically invade the plains in events and daily rotations, dropping unique mod sets and resources. They appear in specific zones marked on the map and run on independent timers from regular Tusk patrols.

**Void Strike** is a Focus ability (Madurai school) available once the Tenno has completed Saya's Vigil and begun working with the Quills. It charges while the Operator is idle in Void mode and releases as a damage multiplier on the next amp attack -- critical for breaking Eidolon synovia quickly.

## Lore

The plains represent the ruins of Eidolon -- a massive Sentient that fought and fell on Earth during the Old War against the Orokin. The lake at the plains' center contains the remnants of this fallen Sentient. Smaller fragments reconstitute each night as Vomvalysts and the three named Eidolons (Teralyst, Gantulyst, Hydrolyst). The Ostron city of Cetus exists in the shadow of an Orokin tower inhabited by the Unum, a Sentient intelligence that chose coexistence and protects the town.

Saya's Vigil, the quest unlocked by speaking to Saya in Cetus, reveals the backstory of Onkko -- the Quills' leader who vanished into the Unum and became the Quills' guiding voice. The quest is required to unlock Eidolon lens crafting and the Quills faction.
