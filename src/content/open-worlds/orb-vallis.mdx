## Overview

Orb Vallis is Warframe's second open-world zone, released with the Fortuna update (Update 24.0) in November 2018. It occupies the surface of Venus -- an icy, terraformed plateau dominated by the Corpus commercial empire. Unlike the plains' long 100-minute loop, the Vallis runs a much faster 26-minute cycle alternating between Warm and Cold thermia states, which determines the behavior of wildlife and certain mission mechanics.

The zone's primary threat is the Corpus Taxmen, a specialized Corpus deployment enforcing debt collection across Solaris worker communities. Three massive Orb Mothers -- Profit-Taker, Exploiter Orb, and Ropalolyst (accessed separately) -- serve as endgame boss encounters requiring specific preparations. The Profit-Taker fight is unique in Warframe for requiring Archweapons (Archwing weapons used on foot) as the only viable option against its shields, making it a significant gear-check encounter.

## Hub -- Fortuna

Fortuna is an underground Corpus labor city on Venus. Its residents, the Solaris, are Corpus-indentured cyborg workers who took on debt to augment their bodies and now struggle to pay it off. The city is reached through an elevator beneath the Vallis surface.

Eudico, the de facto leader of the Solaris underground, manages the bounty board. Rude Zuud, the weapon vendor, sells Kitgun components and Zaw parts adapted for the Vallis. Little Duck runs the Vox Solaris faction -- an elite cell focused on resisting Corpus control -- from a back room accessible only after reaching maximum Solaris United rank. Biz handles conservation and wildlife tracking.

Players rank with Solaris United through bounties and turning in conservation catches. Vox Solaris standing is earned separately through Toroids and its own missions, and it gates the top-tier Kitgun parts and amp components specific to the Vallis economy.

## Factions and syndicates

**Corpus Taxmen** are the dominant enemy faction. They differ from standard Corpus in having heavier deployment and specialized anti-Tenno forces. Nullifiers, Bosses, Combas, Scrambus, and the large Bursa mech variants all appear. Magnetic and Viral damage are effective against Corpus health pools; Radiation disrupts their shield units.

**Solaris United** is the primary standing faction, representing Fortuna's workers. Ranking up unlocks Kitgun parts, fishing equipment, conservation tools, K-Drive components, and Debt-Bond redemption. Daily standing caps follow the standard MR-based formula.

**Vox Solaris** is the secondary faction, accessible only after maxing Solaris United. Toroid donations from the three Orb Mother sites (Enrichment Labs, Temple of Profit, Spaceport) and Calda, Sola, and Vega Toroids earn their standing. Their rewards include the Operator amp components exclusive to the Vallis and Warframe arcanes from the Profit-Taker rotation.

## Bounties and activities

Eudico offers five rotating bounty tiers with Solaris-flavored objectives: spy vaults, mobile defense nodes, capture, assassination, and exterminate. Each tier has a scaling enemy level and a standing reward plus a bonus objective for additional items. Bounties are the fastest standing-per-hour method for most players before endgame.

**K-Drive racing** is a movement system unique to Orb Vallis (and accessible to a lesser extent in Fortuna itself). K-Drives are hoverboards that players grind, jump, and race with. Roky in Fortuna handles K-Drive mods and races. Completing races earns Ventkids standing, a sub-faction of youth gangs in the Fortuna vents. K-Drives also function as a transit method across the large Vallis terrain.

**Fishing** in the Vallis targets Servofish -- mechanical-organic hybrid creatures that populate the coolant pools. The cold phase affects which Servofish species are active, with some species exclusively appearing in Cold. Servofish are not eaten but dismantled by Rude Zuud for Vallis-specific components. Bait types, fishing spot types (cave-exclusive vs open-water), and phase alignment all affect catch rates.

**Mining** on the Vallis uses the same Nosam Cutter mechanics as the plains but yields different gems: Goblite, Thyst, Rub, Zodian, Amarast, and Venerol. The Vallis has more varied terrain types with cave systems that hold additional mining veins and cave-exclusive fish.

**Conservation** under Biz tracks four animal families: Bolarola, Kubrodon, Pobber, and Sawgaw. Each requires a specific Echo-Lure and tranquilizer approach. Perfect captures earn Ventkids standing and conservation tags for cosmetics and standing.

## Cycle

The Vallis cycle runs 26 minutes total -- approximately 13 minutes warm and 13 minutes cold. The thermal state governs several mechanics. In the Warm phase, Thermia Fractures can be sealed, cave vents are accessible, and certain fish spawn. In the Cold phase, some enemy types have different patrol patterns and cold-phase Servofish dominate. The Exploiter Orb fight uses a Thermia canister mechanic tied to fracture sealing. Warframestat.us shows a live timer for the current phase.

## Resources

**Toroids** are the defining high-value resource of Orb Vallis. They drop exclusively from enemies near the three Orb Mother sites on the map: Calda Toroids near the Enrichment Labs, Sola Toroids near the Temple of Profit, Vega Toroids near the Spaceport, and rarer Crisma and Lazulite Toroids from the Orb Mothers themselves. Toroids are currency for Vox Solaris standing and have no other use.

**Debt-Bonds** are a Corpus-flavored currency representing worker debt owed by Solaris. Multiple tiers exist (Training, Shelter, Medical, Familial, Advances). Lower tiers drop from bounties; higher tiers are only available from the Profit-Taker fight or specific bounty rotations. Debt-Bonds unlock rank progression in Solaris United.

**Thermal Sludge** and other Vallis-exclusive fish components feed Kitgun part recipes. Rare gems (Zodian, Amarast) power high-tier Vallis blueprints.

## Unique mechanics

**Profit-Taker** is a colossal Corpus Orb Mother that requires Archguns to damage its rotating shield types. The encounter requires completing the Vox Solaris quest chain (rank 5 Vox Solaris to unlock the heist missions). Profit-Taker's shield rotates through four damage types simultaneously shown on a HUD indicator, and only Archguns can damage it while shields are active. Warframes with Archgun deployer arcanes or Mech summons have an advantage.

**Exploiter Orb** requires Thermia Fracture sealing to produce Diluted Thermia canisters. Throwing canisters at the Exploiter Orb's heat vents forces the fight into two phases. The encounter is accessible after reaching a certain Vox Solaris standing level and is a notable source of Hildryn's neuroptics and specific arcanes.

**Moa companions** are assembled in Fortuna using components from Solaris vendors, making the Vallis the only place to craft these robotic companions. K-Drive mods are also exclusively obtained here, through races and Ventkids standing.

## Lore

Venus's transformation from a hellish planet into a cold plateau is an Orokin legacy -- the Corpus took over the terraforming infrastructure after the fall of the Orokin empire. Fortuna's workers are bound by debt, a Corpus system designed to never let borrowers escape obligation. The entire Fortuna narrative, from Eudico's veiled resistance to Little Duck's covert operations, is about workers finding dignity and solidarity against corporate exploitation.

The Orb Mothers are repurposed Orokin-era machines that the Corpus partially control and deploy as weapons. Their nature and origin tie into the broader Sentient and Orokin lore but are revealed primarily through mission dialogue rather than a dedicated quest.
