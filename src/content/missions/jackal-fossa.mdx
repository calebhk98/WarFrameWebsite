## Overview

Fossa is an early-game assassination mission on Venus where players encounter the Jackal, a Corpus guardian mech and one of the first major boss encounters in Warframe. The mission takes place within a Corpus outpost on Venus, featuring industrial corridors and mechanical chambers typical of Corpus architecture. For many players, defeating the Jackal marks a significant progression milestone and their first real introduction to assassination-style boss mechanics.

The Jackal is a heavily armored mechanical unit armed with dual laser cannons and area-denial weaponry. Unlike regular enemies, the Jackal exhibits intelligent behavior, repositioning dynamically and using cover. The fight is relatively straightforward compared to later bosses, but it establishes the fundamental assassination mechanic pattern: navigate to the boss, defeat waves of reinforcements, and then focus down the target.

This mission serves both as a gameplay checkpoint and as the primary farming location for Excalibur blueprint drops. Excalibur is widely considered the best early-game frame, making Fossa essential for new player progression. The frame's slashing abilities and high agility make it an excellent gateway warframe for learning core mechanics.

## Mission Type Notes

Assassination missions, even early-game ones, differ fundamentally from standard star chart missions. Instead of eliminating all enemies or holding objectives, players must destroy a specific high-value target. This shift forces different tactical thinking: players can bypass some enemy patrols, focus purely on reaching the boss arena, and concentrate all resources on a single extended fight.

The Jackal fight occurs in a moderately sized arena with partial cover and environmental hazards. The boss telegraphs its major attacks clearly, giving players time to react and learn patterns. This intentional difficulty scaling makes Fossa an excellent teaching mission for newer players learning boss mechanics before facing more demanding encounters.

## Level Range

The Jackal and surrounding Corpus units are level 10-15, placing this mission extremely early in the star chart. Most players encounter Fossa after completing the tutorial and reaching Venus. At these levels, even moderately equipped frames with basic mods can succeed, though health and armor mods are strongly recommended.

The low level range does not diminish the Jackal's value as a learning experience. The mechanical patterns and behavior remain identical at higher difficulties (Void Fissure scaling, Steel Path, etc.), allowing players who return to this mission at higher mastery to face a significantly more dangerous version of the same fight.

## Rewards

The Jackal guarantees the drop of one of three Excalibur blueprint components:
- Excalibur Blueprint (chassis/main blueprint)
- Excalibur Neuroptics (head component)
- Excalibur Systems (core component)

Excalibur is one of the three starter warframes and remains one of the most powerful frames throughout the game when properly modded. Its signature ability Exalted Blade transforms his melee weapon into a pure-energy blade with no durability, making it functionally infinite and scalable to endgame content. This unique mechanic makes Excalibur relevant across every mission type.

## Why Play This Node

New players farm Fossa to acquire Excalibur, establishing a foundation for their progression. Returning players occasionally run Fossa at higher star chart difficulties or in void fissure scaling to challenge themselves with a simplified boss fight at extreme power levels. The mission also teaches assassination fundamentals applicable to every other boss in the game.

The consistent and relatively forgiving nature of the Jackal fight makes Fossa an excellent node for players to test new weapons, frames, or builds. Its accessibility (low level requirement, straightforward mechanics) attracts both grinding players and those seeking a low-stress farming session.

## Strategy Notes

Defeating the Jackal requires attention to a few key tactical points:

**Armor Penetration**: The Jackal is heavily armored. Corrosive damage (toxin and electric) is highly effective, as it corrodes enemy armor and increases damage taken. Early-game players may not have access to corrosive procs but should prioritize armor-stripping frames or high-damage weapons.

**Mobility**: The Jackal's laser cannons have significant range but limited tracking. Constant movement, parkour, and staying at medium range minimizes damage taken. Frames with evasion mechanics or movement augments trivialize the fight entirely.

**Cover Usage**: The arena contains pillars and walls. Using cover to break line-of-sight reduces incoming fire while allowing ranged attacks to continue. Melee-focused frames should use cover to dash-melee in and out quickly.

**Phasing Strategy**: The Jackal has a defensive phase where it deploys additional weapons and becomes more aggressive. Recognizing this transition allows players to adjust tactics, reposition, or use crowd control abilities if available.
