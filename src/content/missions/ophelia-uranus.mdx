## Overview

Ophelia is a mid-to-high level survival mission on Uranus introducing survival mechanics at moderate difficulty. Survival missions differ fundamentally from wave-based content by emphasizing continuous enemy engagement without wave boundaries. Squads protect a life support capsule from enemy destruction while eliminating enemies to extend session duration indefinitely.

Uranus's underwater setting introduces environmental narrative flavor distinct from terrestrial planets. Corpus domination of Uranus presents mid-game players with dedicated Corpus farming opportunities at challenging difficulty levels. Ophelia bridges early mid-game survival experience and higher-level endless survival content, positioning it as essential practice for endgame survival farming.

The mission serves dual purposes: resource farming and affinity grinding. Extended Ophelia runs accumulate void traces and Corpus-specific materials while providing consistent weapon and warframe leveling. The balance between resource rewards and affinity generation makes Ophelia valuable throughout mid-to-endgame progression.

## Mission Type Notes

Survival missions require protecting a life support capsule from enemy destruction across indefinite duration. Unlike defense missions with protected NPCs, survival missions feature a destructible, regenerating life support unit. Enemy waves escalate continuously without discrete boundaries, creating sustained engagement mechanics.

Life support mechanics create timing pressure absent from wave-based missions. Capsule depletion forces extraction or death; extending life support requires eliminating enemies within life support intervals. This creates decision points: players can extract after specific durations or continue with escalating risk for extended resource farming.

Rotation mechanics differ from wave-based missions. Time-based rotations replace wave-based rotations. Five-minute intervals grant rotation A rewards; subsequent five-minute intervals grant rotation B and C. Extended play cycles through rotations repeatedly, enabling extended farming targeting high-value rotation C drops.

## Strategy Notes

Succeeding at Ophelia survival requires managing life support, sustain, and escalation efficiently:

**Life Support Management**: Monitor life support depletion and plan extraction or enemy elimination timing accordingly. Killing enemies replenishes life support; efficient crowd control enables maintaining indefinite duration. Prioritize life support extension through enemy elimination.

**Sustain Optimization**: Extended Ophelia runs require energy and ammunition sustainability. Frames with innate energy regeneration or efficient ability costs sustain indefinitely. Ammunition-efficient weapons or melee options eliminate ammunition concerns.

**Crowd Control Layering**: Introduce multiple crowd control abilities creating overlapping suppression. Stuns prevent enemy positioning; freezes prevent pursuit; disarms reduce incoming fire. Combining crowd control types exceeds pure single-ability approaches.

**Corpus Shield Management**: Sustained shield damage enables efficient enemy suppression. Electricity and heat damage accelerate Corpus unit elimination. Disarm abilities reduce defensive capabilities, permitting aggressive approaches.

**Affinity Rotation Awareness**: Extend runs to capture rotation C rewards after fifteen minutes. The efficiency jump between rotation A, B, and C makes targeting specific rotations valuable. Plan extraction timing around rotation targets.

**Support Integration**: Healing or buffing support extends team viability indefinitely. Trinity's Bless enables aggressive play; Harrow's Penance provides energy; Octavia's Mallet provides damage amplification. Support frames unlock extended run potential.

**Scaling Management**: Endless missions introduce exponential enemy scaling. Understanding squad capacity for sustained crowd control determines practical run duration. Pushing beyond squad capability frequently results in avoidable failures.
