## Overview

The Archon Hunt is a challenging weekly endgame mission that pits players against Archon bosses -- powerful Sentient entities that represent a significant late-game progression checkpoint. Unlike regular assassination missions, Archon Hunts feature rotating boss encounters across different star chart locations each week, requiring players to adapt their loadouts and strategies to the current boss's mechanics. Introduced as part of the New War storyline expansion, Archon Hunts serve as a bridge between standard mission difficulty and Steel Path endgame content.

## Mission details

Archon Hunts rotate on a weekly basis, with three different Archon bosses available across separate star chart nodes. The specific nodes change every Monday, set at levels 45-65. Each location is a standard assassination node on an existing tileset with faction enemies supporting the boss encounter.

Each Archon possesses unique attack patterns, immunities, and vulnerable phases. Archons are Sentient entities, meaning they carry the signature Sentient adaptation mechanic: they gradually build resistance to whatever damage type is used most frequently against them during the fight. This forces players to either switch damage types mid-fight or use builds that spread damage across multiple types to prevent resistance stacking.

Teams typically coordinate damage types to distribute the adaptation burden, or use crowd control frames to prevent the Archon from attacking during damage windows.

## Rewards

Archon Hunts drop Archon Shards, which are slotted directly into warframes to provide permanent stat upgrades -- increased health, shield capacity, ability strength, energy regeneration, or other modifiers depending on the shard's elemental type. Archon Shards are the primary upgrade vector for pushing warframe base stats beyond what mods alone can achieve, and are essential for Steel Path and endgame viability.

Additional drops include rare weapon blueprints and relics with reduced drop rates elsewhere. The weekly format means Archon Hunts provide a consistent but time-gated Archon Shard income that compounds significantly over months of play.

## Recommended loadout

Archon Hunts reward players who prioritize adaptability over single damage-type specialization. Frames like Saryn, Gara, and Khora can apply multiple status types simultaneously to distribute adaptation resistance. High-survivability frames (Rhino, Inaros, Nidus) enable extended engagements where damage is dealt steadily against an adapting boss.

Weapon selection should account for the Sentient adaptation system -- bringing weapons with different elemental configurations or physical damage types allows mid-fight switching as resistances build. A Parazon equipped with Sentient-targeting mods amplifies damage during specific phases of certain Archon encounters. High-status weapons that proc multiple damage types per shot degrade adaptation efficiency naturally.

## Notes

Archon Hunts are weekly recurring content and cannot be completed more than once per week per account for full rewards. Players new to the encounter should prioritize learning the specific rotating boss's mechanics before attempting solo runs. Joining public squads allows newer players to observe enemy patterns while still earning full weekly rewards. Archon Shards accumulated over multiple weeks compound into substantial permanent stat gains -- consistent weekly completion is the primary path to maxed endgame warframe stat bonuses.
