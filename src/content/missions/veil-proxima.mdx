## Overview

Veil Proxima is the outermost and most challenging region of the Empyrean star chart, accessible only via Railjack. It hosts the highest-level Grineer crewships, Grineer fighters, and the Corpus crewship variants that appear in the outer solar region. Enemy levels range from 80 to 100, placing Veil Proxima at the top of the Railjack difficulty spectrum. The region also features the Sentient Anomaly -- a periodic event zone where Sentient forces invade, providing access to the Murex ship and unique rewards unavailable elsewhere in the Empyrean system.

Veil Proxima is entirely distinct from the Zariman Ten Zero or Void Cascade content areas. It is a space combat zone reached through the Railjack Navigation interface, not a Void Fissure or Zariman mission type.

## Mission details

Veil Proxima missions follow standard Empyrean formats: Exterminate (destroy a set number of Grineer crewships and fighters), Skirmish (assault a Grineer base with objectives), and Volatile (destroy a reactor on a crewship). All missions take place in open space around the Veil Proxima region nodes on the outer star chart. The Railjack itself serves as both the primary vehicle and the base of operations throughout each mission.

Grineer crewships in Veil Proxima are heavily armored and require boarding parties to destroy from within (targeting the engine room) or sustained fire from an Apoc-class Railjack weapon. Grineer fighters support crewships in large numbers. The Sentient Anomaly appears as a distinct location node within Veil Proxima; when active, a Murex Sentient warship is present and players can board it to fight Sentient units for exclusive rewards including the Epitaph secondary weapon blueprint.

Squad coordination divides roles between Railjack pilots managing the ship, and strike team members boarding enemy crewships or handling the Sentient Anomaly interior.

## Rewards

Veil Proxima drops high-tier Railjack wreckage (Tier III components and weapons), Avionics, and Void Relics from mission completion rotations. Exterminate and Volatile missions follow A/B/C rotation reward tables. The Sentient Anomaly inside Veil Proxima is the primary source of the Epitaph blueprint and can drop other Sentient-related materials.

Wreckage from Grineer crewship destruction provides raw crafting materials for Railjack repairs and upgrades. Credits earned in Veil Proxima missions are consistent with endgame content rates.

## Recommended loadout

A fully upgraded Railjack with Tier III components (Hull, Shield, Reactor) is required to survive sustained Grineer crewship fire at levels 80-100. Equip particle conversion Shields for Railjack survivability and a strong forward Railjack weapon loadout. For boarding missions, high-damage close-range weapons (shotguns, melee) handle confined crewship corridors efficiently.

Frames with survivability or mobility work well in Veil Proxima boarding scenarios -- Inaros and Rhino absorb the heavy Grineer fire inside crewship interiors while strike teams eliminate reactor cores. During the Sentient Anomaly, bring weapons that deal multiple damage types or change damage types frequently to manage Sentient adaptation. Void damage from operator abilities strips Sentient shields and bypasses adaptation.

## Notes

Veil Proxima is a Railjack Empyrean zone, not a standard star chart mission type. Access requires a functional Railjack built through the Rising Tide questline. The Sentient Anomaly within Veil Proxima activates periodically and is visible on the in-mission node map when present. Sentient units inside the Murex use adaptation mechanics identical to those in Archon Hunts -- rotate damage types to prevent resistance buildup.
