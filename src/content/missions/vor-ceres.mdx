Assassination missions task squads with locating and defeating a single legendary boss enemy. Defeating bosses requires coordinated crowd control, sustained damage application, and tactical positioning.

Vor's assassination pattern emphasizes melee combat and area-denial abilities. The boss employs stunning attacks and position-changing mechanics. ## Strategy Notes

Succeeding at Vor assassination requires understanding boss mechanics and crowd control coordination:

**Boss Ability Recognition**: Study Vor's unique attacks and ability patterns. Understanding ability triggers enables predictive positioning and crowd control timing. Anticipating ability casts prevents avoidable damage.

**Crowd Control Sequencing**: Introduce crowd control abilities creating windows for damage application. Stuns prevent boss escape; freezes lock positioning; disarms reduce offensive capabilities. Staggering crowd control maintains suppression throughout engagement.

**Sustained Damage Focus**: Concentrate squad damage against the single boss target. Split fire reduces efficiency; concentrated fire enables rapid health reduction. Coordinating fire timing maximizes damage throughput.

**Positioning Discipline**: Maintain safe positioning preventing boss melee engagement or ability impacts. Elevated positions provide ranged advantages. Communicating positioning clearly prevents avoidable damage.

**Grineer Weakness Exploitation**: Modding for corrosive and slash damage accelerates boss elimination. Armor stripping via corrosive procs increases damage scaling exponentially. Combined elemental damage optimization reduces engagement duration.

**Energy and Ammunition Sustainability**: Extended engagements demand efficient resource management. Frames with energy regeneration sustain crowd control indefinitely. Ammunition-efficient weapons eliminate ammunition concerns.

**Support Integration**: Healing or buffing support enables aggressive playstyles. Trinity provides healing; Rhino provides defense; Octavia provides damage amplification. Support enables specialized damage optimization.

**Escape Prevention**: Boss escape from engagement areas prolongs missions significantly. Positioning squad members strategically around escape routes prevents unnecessary chase sequences.
