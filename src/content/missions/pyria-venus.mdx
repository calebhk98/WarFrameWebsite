## Overview

Pyria is an early-to-mid game mobile defense mission on Venus introducing mobile defense mechanics at accessible difficulty. Mobile defense missions differ from standard defense by requiring squads to defend an objective that moves across the map. Pyria's moderate difficulty and Corpus opposition make it an excellent introduction to mobile defense while providing solid resource farming.

The mission serves as gateway to mobile defense missions appearing frequently in endgame content. Players mastering Pyria's mechanics gain confidence for higher-level mobile defense missions. The mission's straightforward enemy patterns and moderate scaling enable learning mobile defense fundamentals without extreme challenge.

Pyria also provides introduction to moving-objective tactical requirements. Rather than static defensive positioning, squads must coordinate movement while maintaining defensive capability. This dynamic tactical element appears throughout endgame content, making Pyria essential practice.

## Mission Type Notes

Mobile defense missions task squads with protecting a mobile defensive target while enemies attack. Unlike standard defense with stationary objectives, mobile targets require squads to maintain proximity while moving across map terrain. Squad positioning becomes dynamic; players must balance following the objective with maintaining ranged engagement positions.

Pyria's mobile defense introduces three distinct stages with objective movement sequences. Each stage positions the objective in new locations requiring squad repositioning. Learning objective location transitions and preemptive positioning enables efficient protection.

Movement mechanics create tactical challenges absent from standard defense. Squads cannot establish static strong positions; instead, rotational defensive positioning becomes essential. Squad members must alternate between close-objective positions and ranged defense roles.

## Strategy Notes

Succeeding at Pyria mobile defense requires understanding objective movement and dynamic positioning:

**Objective Following**: Maintain proximity to the mobile objective throughout stages. Squad members should position around the objective rather than behind. Understanding objective destination locations enables preemptive positioning.

**Rotation Management**: Rotate squad members between close-objective protection and ranged support positions. Continuous rotation prevents single squad members from bearing excessive threat. Clear communication regarding position rotation prevents confusion.

**Corpus Mechanics Engagement**: Manage Corpus shield mechanics and ranged suppression while following objectives. Positioning behind cover enables safe ranged engagement while protecting the objective from side approaches.

**Stage Transitions**: Anticipate objective relocation between stages. Positioning near transition paths enables rapid following preventing objective unprotected gaps. Squad members should reposition preemptively before objective movement begins.

**Elemental Advantage Application**: Utilize electricity-based damage for optimal Corpus engagement. Modding for electrical damage and shield-focused abilities accelerates Corpus unit elimination.

**Squad Coordination**: Maintain clear communication regarding positioning and objective location changes. Coordinating rotation timing prevents vulnerable defensive gaps.

**Support Integration**: Healing support enables aggressive positioning. Trinity's healing enables squad members to maintain aggressive pursuit preventing enemy approach.
