## Overview

Apollodorus is an early-game survival mission on Mercury introducing survival mechanics at beginner-friendly difficulty levels. Survival missions differ fundamentally from wave-based content by requiring protection of a life support capsule across indefinite duration while engaging enemies continuously. Apollodorus provides the first survival experience for many players, establishing mechanics applicable throughout endgame progression.

Mercury's early-game positioning makes Apollodorus accessible to new players completing early star chart progression. The mission's forgiving level caps enable learning survival fundamentals without extreme mechanical demand. Players can experiment with sustain strategies and life support management in a failure-tolerant environment.

The mission serves as introduction to time-based rotation mechanics. Unlike wave-based rotations occurring after fixed wave counts, survival missions grant rotation rewards after fixed five-minute intervals. Understanding this distinction establishes patterns applicable to all subsequent survival content.

## Mission Type Notes

Survival missions require protecting a regenerating life support capsule from enemy destruction across indefinite duration. Unlike defense missions with protected NPC objectives, survival capsules feature automatic regeneration when life support depletes. This creates continuous decision-making: squads must eliminate enemies to extend life support or risk capsule destruction.

Apollodorus's survival structure emphasizes life support extension through enemy elimination. Killing enemies replenishes life support, extending mission duration. Squad efficiency directly correlates to sustainable run duration; inefficient damage output restricts viable run length.

Time-based rotations replace wave-based mechanics. Five-minute intervals grant rotation A rewards; subsequent five-minute intervals grant rotation B and C. Understanding rotation timing enables targeting specific rewards through deliberate extraction timing.

## Level Range

Apollodorus's enemies begin at level 6 and scale to level 11, positioning it as one of the earliest survival missions. Players with minimally invested starter gear handle Apollodorus comfortably. The forgiving level scaling enables learning survival mechanics without extreme mechanical demand.

Early Apollodorus runs are intentionally brief to teach life support mechanics. Most player's first Apollodorus run results in early extraction; this is normal. Learning efficient enemy elimination and life support extension comes through repeated runs.

## Strategy Notes

Succeeding at Apollodorus survival requires understanding life support mechanics and sustain fundamentals:

**Life Support Management**: Monitor life support depletion carefully. Killing enemies immediately replenishes life support; allowing life support to fully deplete results in forced extraction or death. Maintain continuous enemy engagement to extend session duration indefinitely.

**Enemy Elimination Efficiency**: Sustained crowd control enables efficient enemy elimination. Introducing basic crowd control abilities significantly extends run duration compared to pure damage approaches. Learning crowd control fundamentals on Apollodorus establishes habits applicable throughout progression.

**Rotation Timing Awareness**: Extend runs past five minutes to capture rotation B rewards. Five-minute sessions grant only rotation A; pushing to ten minutes captures rotation B drops. Understanding this distinction enables deliberate reward selection.

**Damage Sustainability**: Early frames with modest damage output may struggle with sustained enemy elimination. Introducing damage-boosting abilities or modding weapons for elemental damage accelerates enemy elimination without requiring higher-level gear.

**Corpus Mechanics Introduction**: Apollodorus introduces Corpus ranged tactics and shield mechanics. Learning basic Corpus engagement on Apollodorus prepares for higher-level Corpus content. Understanding shield removal and positioning against ranged suppression establishes early tactical foundations.

**Squad Coordination Practice**: Survival missions enable squad coordination development in lower-stress environment. Communicating positioning, ability usage, and target priorities establishes communication patterns applicable throughout team play.

**Affinity Generation**: Extended Apollodorus runs provide reliable weapon and frame affinity. New players can test weapons and frames without mechanical penalty, supporting informed equipment selections.
