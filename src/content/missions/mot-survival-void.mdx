## Overview

Mot is a classic endless survival mission in the Void, an abstract pocket dimension featuring Orokin architecture and reality distortions. Unlike traditional missions with defined objectives and exit points, survival missions task players with remaining alive for consecutive time intervals while holding a position and defending against escalating enemy waves. Mot represents one of the most iconic endgame farming locations in Warframe, beloved by veterans for its challenging enemy density and consistent rewards.

The Void tileset presents a stark aesthetic: crystalline Orokin structures, floating platforms, and energy barriers create a visually distinctive environment. The architecture is both beautiful and treacherous, featuring narrow passages that funnel enemies into killzones and open areas where squad positioning becomes critical. Mot specifically draws from the Void's harder node pools, guaranteeing tougher enemies and more unpredictable spawns compared to easier survival missions.

Survival missions reward persistence and team coordination. The longer players remain alive, the better the rewards become. Mot's position as a high-level survival node means players who excel at extended runs can farm consistently valuable rewards for hours, making it attractive for dedicated farming sessions and endgame currency grinding.

## Mission Type Notes

Survival missions operate under distinct mechanics compared to assassination or excavation missions. Players are placed in a designated area and must survive waves of enemy spawns for designated intervals. Each interval (typically 5 minutes) grants rotation rewards; the reward type (rotation A, B, or C) determines the specific loot. To leave the mission, players must reach an exit portal that appears after each interval, forcing a choice between continuing (with escalating difficulty) or leaving with accumulated rewards.

The Void location, filled with Orokin and Corrupted units, presents a distinct enemy roster compared to Corpus or Grineer tiles. Corrupted enemies are ancient Orokin soldiers and war machines, featuring heavy armor, advanced weaponry, and elemental attacks. They are significantly more dangerous than standard faction units, making Mot a legitimate endgame challenge even for well-geared teams.

Survival difficulty escalates non-linearly. The first 10 minutes are manageable; the second 10 requires active aggression and resource management; the third 10 demands peak performance and squad synchronization. Beyond 30 minutes, only highly optimized squads can maintain efficiency; casual players typically extract by this point.

## Level Range

Mot's enemies are level 40-45 base, placing it at the upper end of the star chart. However, survival mission scaling means that extended runs see enemies level 50, 60, 70, or higher depending on elapsed time. Twenty minutes in, enemy levels reach 80+. Thirty minutes in, they approach 100+. This exponential scaling makes Mot a true endgame test.

Veteran players focus on optimal clear speeds, often completing 30-minute runs in teams of four, extracting with full reward rotations. New players attempting Mot without proper preparation will find themselves overwhelmed after 10-15 minutes.

## Rewards

Survival missions award rotation-based loot. Mot's reward table includes:

**Void Traces**: Essential for void fissure relic farming. Mot consistently provides void traces across all rotations, making it a primary void trace source for players pursuing endless fissure farming.

**Rare Mods**: Drop tables include rare modifications not easily farmed elsewhere. Corrupted mods (Blind Rage, Fleeting Expertise, Narrow Minded, etc.) are exclusive to Void Fissure void missions and Mot, making them highly sought.

**Refined Relics**: Void fissure relics drop with increasing refinement as rotations progress, supporting coordinated relic farming strategies.

**Credits and Affinity**: Survival missions generate substantial credits and warframe/weapon affinity, making them valuable for leveling and funding crafting queues.

## Why Play This Node

Experienced players farm Mot for its combination of challenging gameplay and consistent rewards. Unlike assassination missions that yield specific warframe blueprints, Mot offers broadly valuable materials: void traces, mods, and relics that support nearly all endgame activities.

The mission also serves as a skill validation benchmark. Efficiently executing 20-30 minute Mot runs requires mastery of positioning, crowd control, sustained damage output, and resource management. Many players consider extended Mot survival runs as a measure of warframe-building competency.

Mot also offers social value. Extended survival missions naturally encourage squad communication and team bonding. Many veteran players maintain regular Mot farming squads as a preferred social activity, combining efficient grinding with team interaction.

## Strategy Notes

Successfully extending Mot survival runs requires advanced tactics and team coordination:

**Crowd Control Scaling**: Radial crowd control (Stun, Sleep, Freeze) becomes increasingly valuable as enemies scale. Early runs prioritize damage; extended runs prioritize crowd control to prevent exponential difficulty escalation. A dedicated crowd control frame often outperforms pure damage frames beyond 15 minutes.

**Energy and Resource Management**: Extended runs deplete energy and ammunition reserves. Frames with energy generation abilities (Trinity, Harrow) or weapons with ammo-efficient mods sustain longer. Energy pizzas become valuable consumables for runs exceeding 20 minutes.

**Damage Scaling**: Single-target damage becomes increasingly inefficient as enemies scale. Area-of-effect damage frames (Saryn, Mirage, Equinox) scale better than single-target builds, particularly in crowded spawn rooms.

**Positioning and Movement**: Mot's arena has optimal chokepoints where squads funnel enemies into killzones. Identifying and controlling chokepoints reduces the required defensive effort and enables more aggressive playstyles. Conversely, open arena areas require constant repositioning to avoid being overwhelmed.

**Elemental Damage**: Corrupted units have varied elemental weaknesses. Magnetic damage is moderately effective; heat damage provides scaling benefits. Modding for elemental procs accelerates clear speeds compared to pure physical damage.

**Support Roles**: Extended runs benefit from dedicated support players. Trinity's Energy Vampire sustains the squad's ability usage; Harrow's conditional energy generation provides guaranteed refresh during high-damage windows. Support frames enable damage frames to maintain aggressive playstyles beyond what solo sustainability would allow.

**Squad Composition**: Ideal Mot teams comprise one crowd control specialist, one sustained-damage dealer, one burst-damage dealer, and one support player. This composition handles both early-run efficiency and extended-run survivability. Alternative compositions are viable but require higher individual skill.

**Extraction Timing**: Knowing when to extract is critical. Pushing too long risks a team wipe and total failure; extracting too early leaves potential rewards unclaimed. Experienced teams calculate sustainable extraction points based on squad performance.
