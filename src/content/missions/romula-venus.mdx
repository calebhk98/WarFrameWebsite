## Strategy Notes

Succeeding at Romula defense requires managing Corpus shields and ranged suppression:

**Shield Priority Management**: Designate primary and secondary targets with clear shielding status. Coordinating sustained fire on single targets removes shields before focusing health damage. This sequential engagement prevents scattered ineffective fire against heavily shielded groups.

**Elemental Advantage Application**: Electricity and heat damage accelerate Corpus unit elimination. Electricity procs disable shields temporarily; heat procs create additional damage via burning. Modding for combined electrical-heat or electrical-toxin damage optimizes effectiveness.

**Defensive Cover Positioning**: Corpus ranged suppression demands more defensive cover utilization compared to Grineer content. Establish squad positions behind structural cover with clear sight lines. High-ground positioning provides ranged engagement advantages.

**Crowd Control Layering**: Introduce complementary crowd control abilities creating overlapping suppression. Stuns prevent enemy positioning; freezes prevent pursuit; disarms reduce incoming fire. Combining multiple crowd control types exceeds pure single-ability approaches.

**Energy Weapon Interactions**: Some Corpus units employ energy weapons with extended range. Maintaining awareness of enemy weapon types enables tactical positioning. Electrified enemies require additional shielding or defensive abilities.

**Squad Sustain Focus**: Support frames providing healing, buffing, or crowd control extend team viability. Trinity, Harrow, or Octavia enable aggressive playstyles unsustainable without dedicated support.
