## Overview

Titania is a mid-game assassination mission on Uranus where players confront Tyl Regor, a senior Corpus scientist and engineer responsible for advanced weapons and genetic experiments. The mission takes place within a Corpus asteroid base featuring metallic corridors, pressurized chambers, and underwater sections unique to the Uranus tileset. Tyl Regor represents a significant step up in assassination difficulty compared to early-game bosses, introducing complex mechanics and dangerous attack patterns.

The boss himself is a heavily augmented cyborg wielding dual plasma rifles and commanding a suite of technological advantages including hacking terminals and summoned security units. Unlike the Jackal's relatively simple two-phase fight, Tyl Regor requires sustained focus and adaptive tactics. This mission serves as the primary farming source for Titania, a niche but powerful warframe specializing in crowd control and aerial warfare.

The Uranus tileset itself introduces environmental hazards including electrical floors, toxic gases, and aquatic sections. These environmental factors, combined with the boss's mechanical difficulty, make Titania a checkpoint mission that separates casual players from those ready for late-stage star chart content.

## Mission Type Notes

Assassination missions at the Uranus tier maintain the core mechanic of reaching and defeating a single high-value target, but with significantly increased complexity. Tyl Regor's fight unfolds across multiple phases, each introducing new attack patterns and tactical demands. The arena itself is moderately large, allowing for dynamic repositioning but requiring careful navigation of environmental hazards.

Unlike earlier assassination missions that favor simple stand-and-fight approaches, Titania rewards methodical play. Enemy reinforcements can be overwhelming, forcing players to balance assassination focus with defensive awareness. The multi-phase nature of the fight demands sustained energy and resource management throughout.

## Level Range

Tyl Regor and the surrounding Corpus defenders are level 30-40, placing this mission firmly in the mid-to-late star chart. Players should have mastery rank 8 or higher and access to good mods before attempting Titania. The difficulty scaling, while gentle compared to endgame content, is noticeable: unoptimized frames or low-rank weapons will struggle significantly.

The boss itself becomes dramatically more dangerous in void fissure scaling or Steel Path runs. Its attack speed and damage output increase proportionally, transforming an already challenging fight into an endgame experience.

## Rewards

Defeating Tyl Regor guarantees one of three Titania blueprint components:
- Titania Blueprint (chassis/main blueprint)
- Titania Neuroptics (head component)
- Titania Systems (core component)

Titania is a specialized warframe excelling in crowd control through her Lantern ability, which pacifies enemies in an expanding area, and her Razorwing exalted melee transformation. Though less universally applicable than frames like Mag or Rhino, Titania offers unique mechanical depth and handles nullifier units more effectively than many alternatives. Her crowd control scaling makes her valuable in high-level void fissure content.

## Why Play This Node

Players farm Titania primarily to acquire her warframe blueprints, which are exclusive to this mission. Titania fills a specific role in squad compositions: she excels at neutralizing dangerous ranged enemies and protecting teammates through crowd control. Her relatively high ceiling for optimization makes her attractive to players seeking mechanical complexity and skill expression.

The mission also provides void traces as rotation rewards, supporting void fissure farming. Many players run Titania repeatedly not just for the warframe but to accumulate void traces for relic farming, particularly early in their star chart progression when void fissure access is limited.

## Strategy Notes

Succeeding against Tyl Regor requires attention to several key mechanical points:

**Phase Management**: The fight has distinct phases. Recognizing when Tyl Regor is about to transition (indicated by voice lines and visual cues) allows players to reposition and prepare for new attacks. Each phase introduces different attack angles and positioning demands.

**Energy Starving**: Tyl Regor can hack nearby terminals, draining player energy. Being aware of terminal locations and either destroying them proactively or maintaining enough distance prevents complete energy starvation. Energy frames or Trinity support eliminate this concern.

**Magnetic Vulnerability**: Like all Corpus units, Tyl Regor and his security forces are weak to Magnetic damage (electricity and cold combination). Modding for magnetic procs or using frames with magnetic-aligned abilities dramatically speeds up the kill.

**High-Damage Bursts**: The multi-phase nature of the fight benefits from high-burst-damage weapons or abilities that can execute quick kill phases during punishable animations. Sustained damage frames work but require more mission time.

**Crowd Control Safety**: If energy permits, crowd control abilities protect against incoming fire during extended engagements. Frames like Rhino (Stomp), Nekros (Terrify), or Nova (Null Star) reduce the overall damage taken during the fight.
