## Overview

The Profit-Taker Heist is a specialized cooperative mission on Orb Vallis where players conduct an elaborate assault on the Profit-Taker, a colossal mechanical spider Orb Mother and one of Corpus' most valuable assets. Unlike traditional assassination missions focused solely on defeating a boss, heists introduce multi-stage objectives, environmental mechanics, and resource management. The Profit-Taker represents the culmination of Corpus technology -- a self-repairing mechanical intelligence capable of deploying adaptive shield barriers, commanding defensive turrets, and executing devastating attacks.

This mission requires squad participation and coordination. Players must execute heist stages in sequence, manage shield disruption mechanics, and adapt tactics as the Profit-Taker transitions between defensive phases. The Orb Vallis setting provides environmental hazards and terrain that players can leverage for positioning advantage.

## Mission details

Heist missions are multi-stage cooperative encounters rather than a single combat arena. The Profit-Taker Heist progresses through several distinct phases: players must capture Nullifier Crewships to disable the Profit-Taker's adaptive shields, gather shield disruption components, and deploy them against the boss to open damage windows. This multi-step process requires squad coordination -- failure to execute the disruption sequence forces restarting the phase.

Corpus reinforcements continuously spawn during the heist. Squad roles become meaningful: a designated frame manages the Profit-Taker's aggression while damage dealers focus burst windows during shield-down phases. The Profit-Taker and supporting Corpus units are weak to Magnetic damage (electricity combined with cold), making elemental weapon configuration relevant.

The Corpus and surrounding Corpus forces range from levels 40 to 60. Players without fully invested gear will struggle with the sustained Corpus fire during disruption phases.

## Rewards

Profit-Taker kills drop Garuda component blueprints. Garuda's main blueprint is obtained from the in-game Market; the three components -- Neuroptics, Systems, and Chassis -- are the exclusive drops from the Profit-Taker Heist. Each run drops one of the three component blueprints at random rotation. Farming all three requires multiple runs.

Garuda is an aggressive crowd-control frame with lifesteal mechanics and aerial mobility. Her Seeking Talon ability generates bladed projectiles scaling with ability strength. Her Bloodletting ability provides sustained healing through damage output, enabling playstyles that would otherwise be unsustainable in prolonged combat.

Additional rewards include Hijacked Shield Disruption mods, useful for specific heist mechanics and certain Corpus-focused build configurations.

## Recommended loadout

Archon Shards and fully ranked mods are expected at this content tier. Frames with high survivability or crowd control (Rhino, Trinity, Khora, Nidus) provide sustained durability during multi-phase encounters. Bring weapons modded for Magnetic damage -- combining cold and electric elemental mods -- to efficiently strip Corpus shields from both the boss and reinforcements.

Corpus Tech and Nullifiers among the reinforcement waves require priority targeting to prevent energy disruption and mob accumulation. Carry Ammo Restores or energy-efficient frames to sustain firepower across the full multi-minute encounter. Squad communication regarding shield disruption timing, Nullifier spawns, and phase transitions improves run efficiency.

## Notes

Profit-Taker Heist access requires Old Mate standing rank with Solaris United -- the highest Fortuna syndicate rank, requiring significant Orb Vallis bounty completion. Players who have not reached Old Mate rank cannot start the heist. The Profit-Taker itself is not accessible as a normal assassination target; it spawns only during the heist mission chain. Garuda's main blueprint must be purchased separately from the Market -- only the three component blueprints drop here.
