## Strategy Notes

Succeeding at Amphitrite sabotage requires tactical approach selection and execution excellence:

**Stealth Approach Viability**: Utilize stealth frames for silent objective destruction. Loki's invisibility, Ash's teleportation, or Ivara's stealth enable objective damage avoidance. This approach trades time efficiency for reliability.

**Alarm Management**: Triggering alarms escalates difficulty but remains manageable with crowd control. Designate alarm suppression roles; prepare crowd control before initiating objectives. Disarm-focused abilities reduce incoming fire significantly.

**Objective Location**: Facility layouts vary; common objective spawn points exist in primary chambers and reactor cores. Learning predictable spawn locations accelerates objective discovery. Communication enables rapid relocation to subsequent objectives.

**Direct Engagement Strategy**: Overwhelming force remains viable with specialized squad composition. High-damage frames with crowd control enable suppression of escalated spawning. This approach trades reliability for speed.

**Grineer Weakness Exploitation**: Sustained corrosive and slash damage accelerates enemy elimination. Armor stripping enables exponential scaling. Modding for combined elemental approaches optimizes effectiveness.

**Squad Positioning**: Establish defensive positions around objective locations. Preventing enemy approach during destruction animations enables uninterrupted objective completion. Clear communication regarding positioning prevents chaotic failures.

**Energy and Ammunition**: Sustained crowd control demands efficient resource usage. Frames with energy regeneration sustain crowd control indefinitely. Ammunition-efficient weapons eliminate ammunition constraints.

**Support Integration**: Healing or crowd control support extends squad viability. Trinity enables aggressive positioning; Limbo's stasis creates objective protection; crowd control frames prevent enemy positioning.
