## Overview

Magnacidium is an assassination mission on Deimos where players face Lephantis, a grotesque three-headed infested abomination representing the pinnacle of Infested bioengineering. Unlike mechanized bosses or individual soldiers, Lephantis is a biological horror combining three distinct attack styles and defensive mechanisms. The mission location is the Orokin Derelict, an ancient Orokin vault overrun with Infested corruption, featuring organic tunnels, calcified structures, and bioluminescent infested fauna.

The fight against Lephantis is unique in Warframe: the boss presents three distinct heads that must be managed simultaneously or sequentially depending on player strategy. This multi-target focus, combined with the Infested tileset's environmental hazards and Infested enemy variety, creates a complex assassination experience. Lephantis serves as the exclusive source for Hydroid warframe blueprints, a utility-focused support frame with ocean-themed abilities.

The Deimos location, previously known only as the Orokin Derelict, has become more prominent with Warframe's ecosystem evolution. The infested environmental aesthetic and enemy variety make Magnacidium visually and tactically distinct from Corpus or Grineer assassination nodes.

## Mission Type Notes

Assassination missions against Infested bosses differ significantly from combat against organized factions. Lephantis lacks the tactical sophistication of Corpus commanders or Grineer warlords; instead, it relies on raw biological ferocity, unpredictable attack patterns, and regenerative abilities. The boss's three heads attack independently, creating situations where players must manage multiple threat vectors simultaneously.

The Deimos tileset presents unique environmental factors. Infested blood and organic growths cover surfaces, creating visual complexity that can obscure enemy positioning. Certain Infested units have abilities like poison clouds or acid projectiles, requiring awareness beyond simply defeating the boss. The organic nature of the tileset means cover is less standardized and sometimes ineffective against certain attack angles.

## Level Range

Lephantis and the surrounding Infested hordes are level 35-45, placing this mission in the mid-to-late star chart. The difficulty scaling, while similar to Tyl Regor, feels distinctly different because of the Infested faction's mechanics. Infested enemies regenerate health, apply status effects more readily, and often attack in overwhelming waves. Mastery rank 10 or higher with proper mods is recommended.

In void fissure scaling or Steel Path variations, Lephantis becomes a formidable endgame encounter. Its damage output remains manageable, but the sustained aggression and health regeneration require careful resource management.

## Rewards

Defeating Lephantis guarantees one of three Hydroid blueprint components:
- Hydroid Blueprint (chassis/main blueprint)
- Hydroid Neuroptics (head component)
- Hydroid Systems (core component)

Hydroid is a specialized frame excelling in resource farming through his Pilfering Swarm augment, which increases enemy drops when his Tentacle Swarm ability hits targets. While less suited to direct combat than damage-focused frames, Hydroid's farming utility makes him valuable for dedicated farming squads and players seeking to optimize resource gathering efficiency. His crowd control and sustainability also make him viable for endgame survival missions.

## Why Play This Node

Players run Magnacidium for two primary reasons: acquiring Hydroid blueprints and farming void traces for void fissure participation. Hydroid's farming specialization makes him essential for players focused on material grinding and crafting efficiency. Many veteran players maintain a built Hydroid specifically for resource-focused squad runs.

The unique multi-target nature of the Lephantis fight also attracts players seeking novel boss mechanics. Unlike single-phase bosses, Lephantis rewards multi-target awareness and adaptive tactics. The fight's difficulty, while manageable, provides a meaningful challenge for mid-game players.

## Strategy Notes

Defeating Lephantis requires understanding its three-head mechanic and the Infested faction's characteristics:

**Multi-Target Focus**: Unlike single-boss assassination missions, players must manage three active threat vectors simultaneously. This can mean focusing fire on one head at a time or spreading damage across all three. High-damage frames benefit from focused target elimination; crowd control frames prefer area-of-effect approaches.

**Infested Weakness**: Infested units are vulnerable to Toxin and Fire damage. Corrosive damage is also effective as it bypasses armor-scaling on Infested units. Modding for toxin or fire procs significantly improves clear speed.

**Regeneration Management**: All Infested units regenerate health when out of combat. Prolonged fights where enemies are not actively taking damage allow them to recover. Sustained pressure and crowd control prevent regeneration windows, keeping enemies vulnerable.

**Status Effect Awareness**: Infested attacks apply status effects frequently. Poison clouds cause toxin buildup, slashing attacks apply bleed, and acidic projectiles trigger corrosion. Defensive frames with status immunity or purge abilities mitigate incoming debuffs.

**Tendrils and Tentacles**: Lephantis's tentacle attacks cover significant arena space. Movement and cover usage become critical. Frames with mobility augments or dash mechanics can maintain positioning while avoiding incoming fire.

**Crowd Control Efficiency**: Radial crowd control (Sleep, Stun, Freeze) affects all three heads simultaneously, providing significant damage windows. Frames like Nyx, Titania, or Frost leverage crowd control for faster kill times.
