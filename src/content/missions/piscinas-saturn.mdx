## Mission Type Notes

Sabotage missions differ fundamentally from wave-based content by introducing destructible objectives and stealth mechanics. Rather than holding a position or surviving time, sabotage missions demand locating and destroying specific targets. Successful completion requires eliminating objectives before alarm systems trigger or discovering alternate stealth routes avoiding combat entirely.

Piscinas's sabotage structure emphasizes Corpus facility destruction. Squads must navigate the facility, locate reactor or power core sabotage targets, and initiate destruction sequences. Enemy patrols create dynamic tactical challenges; triggering alarms escalates enemy spawning significantly, demanding either stealth maintenance or overwhelming force.

Stealth approaches enable silent objective destruction with minimal combat engagement. Stealth frames like Loki, Ash, or Ivara excel at sabotage missions by bypassing enemy resistance entirely. Alternatively, aggressive squads can trigger alarms deliberately and overcome escalated enemy spawning through superior firepower. Both approaches remain valid with appropriate team composition.

## Strategy Notes

Succeeding at Piscinas requires selecting tactical approaches aligned with squad composition:

**Stealth Approach Optimization**: Utilize stealth frames for silent objective destruction. Loki's invisibility, Ash's teleportation, or Ivara's stealth arrows enable objective damage avoidance. Mark enemy patrol patterns before committing to objectives.

**Alarm Management**: Triggering alarms escalates difficulty significantly but remains manageable with sufficient crowd control. Designate alarm suppression roles and prepare crowd control abilities before initiating objectives. Disarm-focused abilities prevent enemy fire escalation.

**Objective Location**: Facility maps vary between runs. Learning common objective spawns accelerates location speed. Second objective placement follows predictable patterns in Corpus facilities.

**Escape Route Planning**: Establish predetermined escape routes before triggering alarms. Multiple squad members dying during objective destruction frequently results in mission failure. Coordinate extraction paths enabling rapid regrouping.

**Direct Engagement Viability**: Overwhelming force remains viable with proper squad composition. High-damage frames with crowd control abilities can suppress escalated enemy spawning directly. This approach trades time efficiency for reliability.

**Corpus Shield Management**: Sustained shield damage enables efficient enemy suppression. Combine electrical and heat damage for optimal effectiveness. Disarm abilities reduce defensive capabilities significantly.

**Support Integration**: Healing or buffing support enables aggressive approaches. Trinity's healing, Octavia's tempo, or Limbo's temporal stasis extend squad sustainability.
