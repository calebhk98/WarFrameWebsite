## Overview

Void Cascade missions are event-based void fissure encounters that rotate across the star chart during Void Fissure events. These temporary missions overlay Void Fissure mechanics onto standard mission types, creating hybrid gameplay where players farm prime components while simultaneously engaging in void crack mechanics.

Void Cascades represent the primary endgame farming method for acquiring prime warframe and weapon components, making them economically crucial for players pursuing maxed-out prime builds. The rotating node locations ensure that players must adapt to different tilesets, enemy factions, and mission types during each fissure event.

## Void Fissure Mechanics

Void Cascade missions feature the core void fissure system: void cracks spawn during the mission, and players must collect reactant from defeated enemies to crack open these rifts. Successful reactant collection grants access to void relics--specialized containers holding prime components.

The reactant system creates a secondary objective overlaid onto the primary mission goal. Players must balance their focus between completing the mission and accumulating reactant for crack farming. This dual objective adds complexity and rewards multitasking.

## Mission Type Variety

Unlike static void fissure nodes, Void Cascades rotate through different mission types--Void Cascade Survival, Void Cascade Defense, Void Cascade Disruption--each featuring distinct mechanics and survival strategies. This rotation prevents Void Cascade farming from becoming monotonous while maintaining consistent reward value.

Players must adapt their loadout and strategies based on the current mission type. A survival-focused build differs significantly from a disruption-focused approach, creating skill expression and strategic depth.

## Enemy Composition and Scaling

Void Cascade missions feature Corrupted enemies--heavily armored, shield-heavy units derived from Corpus and Grineer combined with void corruption. These enemies scale across a wide difficulty range, from level 20 for introductory fissures to level 60+ for advanced void cascades.

Corrupted units are mechanically challenging, requiring specific strategies and damage approaches. Their resistance to certain damage types and reliance on shields make status builds and elemental procs particularly effective.

## Rewards and Farming Efficiency

Void Cascade missions are the primary farming route for prime components. The dual reward system--standard mission loot plus void crack relics--creates multiple income streams in a single mission. Players can accumulate relics passively while farming other void cascade missions, then crack them during dedicated void fissure runs.

The rotating node schedule ensures that all void eras remain farmable without requiring players to wait indefinitely for specific prime sets to become available.

## Recommended Loadout and Survival Strategy

Void Cascade success requires frames and weapons optimized for crowd control and sustained damage. High-status weapons synergize well with elemental builds, as corrosive and other procs provide exceptional damage against Corrupted armor. Crowd control frames (Nidus, Saryn, Limbo) excel by managing enemy density.

Survivability is paramount, as extended void cascade runs demand endurance. Defensive frames with built-in healing (Trinity, Wisp, Octavia) scale exceptionally well into higher-level fissures.

Bringing weapons with strong crowd control properties--stuns, knockdowns, or damage procs--significantly improves mission viability.

## Void Fissure Integration and Event Structure

Void Cascades exist within larger Void Fissure events, typically lasting 7-14 days. During these periods, players have windows to farm prime components efficiently. Players pursuing specific prime sets should plan their farming schedule around void fissure event calendars to maximize efficiency.

The event-based structure creates natural reset points where players can reassess their progress and plan future farming strategies.

## Difficulty Progression and Scalability

Void Cascade missions scale smoothly from intermediate to endgame difficulty. New players can attempt lower-level cascades to learn void fissure mechanics, while veterans can push into high-level fissures for optimized farming. The wide difficulty range ensures accessibility across the player base.

Squad coordination dramatically improves survival duration. Teams with distributed roles (tank, healer, damage dealer, crowd control) can sustain extended void cascade runs far longer than solo players.

## Strategic Importance and Economic Value

Void Cascade missions represent the economic backbone of player progression. Prime components acquired from void cascades are traded for platinum, sold to newer players, or kept for personal builds. The void cascade mission type directly translates to character progression and wealth accumulation.

## Notes on Event Timing and Planning

Players should track void fissure event schedules and plan farming sessions around the most valuable void eras for their current needs. Strategic timing ensures players acquire prime components during optimal farming windows rather than waiting for relics to return to the rotation.
