## Overview

E Prime is a foundational exterminate mission on Earth designed for brand-new players fresh from the tutorial. The mission tasks players with eliminating all Grineer forces in a contained facility, introducing the exterminate mission type without overwhelming difficulty. Unlike defense missions requiring objective protection, exterminate missions focus purely on enemy elimination, making them more forgiving for players still developing combat fundamentals.

E Prime serves as the gentle introduction to open-world star chart missions. The extremely low level caps, sparse enemy spawns, and straightforward map design create an ideal learning environment for understanding mission mechanics, movement controls, and basic combat outside scripted tutorial constraints. Players can experiment with weapon handling and ability usage in a failure-forgiving context. Completion grants modest rewards supporting early progression.

The mission's accessibility to MR 0 accounts makes E Prime the first optional mission many players encounter. While starter content like Vor's Prize precedes E Prime, the mission provides the first opportunity for independent farming. This autonomy and sense of achievement reinforce player engagement during critical early-game periods where retention is highest.

## Mission Type Notes

Exterminate missions task squads with eliminating all enemy units within a region. Unlike defense missions with protected objectives, exterminate missions demand only that the squad survive and kill every enemy. Enemy spawns typically occur in waves within predefined zones, and the mission updates when all enemies in the current zone are eliminated.

Exterminate missions feature no rotation rewards. The mission grants a single loot table upon completion, simplified compared to defense or survival missions. This structure makes exterminate missions excellent for straightforward farming or quick syndicate missions. The absence of timing pressure eliminates rotation farming complexity, making exterminate ideal for players learning mission mechanics.

E Prime's exterminate structure permits unhurried clearing. Players can explore the map, locate every enemy, and eliminate them methodically without time-based penalty. This contrasts with survival or disruption missions where pace accelerates or objectives disappear, creating a lower-stress introduction to mission concepts.

## Level Range

E Prime's enemies are level 1-3, the absolute minimum in Warframe's progression. Even unmodded starter gear handles E Prime's threat trivially. This extreme accessibility positions E Prime purely as a pedagogical tool, teaching mission navigation and completion mechanics rather than presenting tactical challenges. Mastery Rank 0 accounts complete E Prime without legitimate threat.

The level range's absolute accessibility removes combat anxiety from new players. Players can focus on understanding interface elements (minimap reading, objective tracking, squad communication) without dividing attention between learning mechanics and avoiding death. This cognitive load reduction supports knowledge retention compared to difficulty-focused missions where learning competes with survival concerns.

## Rewards

E Prime's rewards are modest but essential for early progression:

**Credits**: Exterminate missions grant credits equal to enemy kills and objectives completed. New players fund initial crafting queues with credit farming during early game; E Prime provides accessible credit sources without mechanical demand.

**Common Loot**: Enemy drops include common materials. Players accumulating plastids, ferrite, and copper from E Prime runs support early weapon crafting queues.

**Void Traces**: Exterminate missions grant void traces. Early players building void trace reserves for relic farming can accumulate traces at relaxed pace through E Prime runs.

**Affinity**: Squad members gain modest warframe and weapon experience. New weapons rapidly advance through low levels during E Prime runs, supporting efficient early leveling.

New players farm E Prime early in progression for resource accumulation and frame leveling. The mission serves as the first independent mission introducing squad coordination requirements. Early players learning squad mechanics and communication can practice reviving teammates or coordinating crowd control without mechanical punishment for mistakes.

Veteran players occasionally run E Prime to complete mastery rank challenges for new weapons or frames. The mission's trivial difficulty permits AFK farming or casual conversation while accumulating mastery points toward account-level milestones.

The mission's accessibility makes it ideal for players returning after extended breaks. Running E Prime reacquaints returning players with core mechanics before progressing to challenging content.

## Strategy Notes

Succeeding at E Prime requires minimal tactical complexity:

**Systematic Clearing**: Methodically explore each map zone and eliminate enemies. Using the minimap to track unexplored areas ensures complete enemy elimination without backtracking extensively.

**Ability Experimentation**: E Prime's zero-threat environment is ideal for experimenting with frame abilities. New players should practice ability casting, energy management, and crowd control sequencing without risk of failure.

**Weapon Handling Practice**: New players can develop weapon handling instincts on E Prime's defenseless enemies. Learning weapon swap speeds, reload timings, and damage application prepares for higher-difficulty content.

**Squad Coordination**: Early squads can practice call-outs and revive mechanics on E Prime. Squad leaders can establish communication protocols and positioning conventions applicable to all future missions.

**Efficiency Optimization**: While E Prime never requires efficiency, developing efficient movement and ability usage habits early establishes good practices. Running between zones quickly and using mobility abilities trains movement efficiency applicable to time-pressured missions.
