## Overview

Helene is a mid-game defense mission on Saturn where players protect a Tenno operative while waves of Grineer enemies attack the objective. Defense missions task squads with holding a position and defeating escalating enemy waves for consecutive intervals. Each 5-minute interval completed grants rotation rewards; players may exit after any interval or continue indefinitely for increasing challenge and reward quality.

Helene is one of the most popular leveling nodes in Warframe due to its balanced difficulty, convenient reward structure, and reliable enemy spawns. The Saturn ice planet tileset provides a visually distinctive frozen aesthetic with blue-hued corridors, icy surfaces, and industrial Grineer fortifications. The mission layout is moderately complex with enough cover to enable varied positioning strategies while maintaining sufficient enemy spawn density to keep the squad engaged.

Helene serves dual purposes: new players use it to level frames and weapons to higher ranks, while veteran players run it for consistent affinity generation and void traces. The mission's accessibility (no specific prerequisites beyond reaching Saturn) and low mechanical complexity make it ideal for casual farming sessions. Unlike extended survival runs that demand peak performance, Helene rewards can be accumulated over manageable 10-15 minute sessions, fitting casual play schedules better than marathon farming approaches.

## Mission Type Notes

Defense missions differ from survival missions in several critical ways. Rather than protecting a location, players protect a designated objective (an NPC operative who does not participate in combat). This objective has a health pool and takes damage from enemy attacks. If the objective's health reaches zero, the mission fails immediately regardless of squad status.

The objective's health pool and player positioning requirements create distinct tactical considerations. Early waves are straightforward: eliminate enemies before they overwhelm the objective. Later waves require careful positioning to intercept enemies before they reach the objective, coordinated crowd control to prevent breach attempts, and sustained damage output to prevent enemy accumulation.

The objective's presence enables tactical positioning. High-ground players can defend from elevated positions while the objective remains protected below. Alternatively, dedicated protection players can position directly on the objective while damage dealers operate from range. These positioning strategies create varied playstyles compared to survival missions where protecting yourself is the implicit objective.

## Level Range

Helene's enemies are level 21-26, positioning it as an excellent stepping stone between early-game content and endgame nodes. The difficulty is manageable for players with MR 8 or higher using moderately leveled mods. Unoptimized gear may struggle at higher wave counts, but competent squads can run Helene indefinitely, extracting based on schedule rather than mechanical failure.

Unlike Mot's exponential scaling, Helene's enemy levels increase gradually. A well-equipped squad can run 20+ waves without significant difficulty spikes, making it excellent for extended casual sessions. Pushing to 30-40+ waves introduces tangible difficulty but remains manageable for focused squads.

## Rewards

Helene's defense mission rewards include:

**Plastids**: A common resource used in weapon and gear crafting. Helene provides consistent plastid drops, making it a reliable source for players with empty plastid reserves and active crafting queues.

**Void Traces**: Each defense interval grants void fissure void traces, essential for void fissure relic missions. Helene provides traces at a rate comparable to higher-level survival missions but with less mechanical demand, making it excellent for casual void trace accumulation.

**Orokin Cells**: Rare drops in higher rotations (rotation C and beyond), Orokin Cells are crafting materials for endgame equipment. Helene's accessibility makes it an excellent early Orokin Cell source before players progress to more difficult farming nodes.

**Credits and Affinity**: Defense missions generate solid credits and warframe/weapon affinity, supporting player progression and crafting funding.

## Why Play This Node

New and mid-game players farm Helene primarily for leveling. Rotation A (waves 1-5) grants affinity; rotation B (waves 6-10) grants additional resources. Squads leveling new frames or weapons can complete a 10-wave run in 10-15 minutes, gaining substantial ranks toward level 30.

Veteran players run Helene as a low-stress farming option. Unlike high-level survival missions demanding peak performance, Helene permits casual play and conversation while accumulating valuable resources. Many veterans maintain active Helene squads as a preferred social farming activity, combining efficient grinding with squad interaction.

The mission also serves as practice for new defensive tactics. Players learning crowd control frame mechanics or protective abilities use Helene's moderate difficulty to develop skills before progressing to more demanding missions.

## Strategy Notes

Excelling at Helene defense requires attention to positioning and crowd control optimization:

**Objective Protection**: The objective's placement determines the squad's positioning. Direct protection players position near the objective while ranged players hold elevated or distant positions. Early coordination regarding objective positioning prevents repeated failures from objectives taking unexpected flanking damage.

**Crowd Control Efficiency**: While pure damage capabilities handle early waves efficiently, later waves benefit from dedicated crowd control. A frame providing radial stuns or freezes prevents enemy breaches and reduces incoming damage to the objective significantly. Combining crowd control with damage output creates synergies where the whole exceeds individual parts.

**Ammunition and Energy Sustainability**: Extended runs (15+ waves) deplete energy and ammunition reserves. Weapons with efficient ammo consumption and frames with energy generation abilities sustain longer. Ammunition restores become valuable for squads pushing 20+ waves.

**Grineer Weakness**: Grineer units are weak to Corrosive and Blast damage. Modding weapons for corrosive procs accelerates enemy elimination compared to unmodified weapons. Corrosive procs strip enemy armor, increasing damage taken, creating escalating effectiveness across waves.

**Wave Prediction and Timing**: Each wave introduces new enemy types and positions. Experienced players anticipate spawns and position preemptively. Knowing that wave 6 introduces heavy gunners allows the squad to prepare crowd control and position defensively before that wave begins.

**Positioning Flexibility**: Helene's arena offers multiple elevated positions and sight lines. Squads that leverage architectural features (high ground, cover, chokepoints) can control enemy engagement distances and reduce incoming fire. Conversely, static positioning predictably leads to flanking attempts and objective breaches.

**Support Roles**: Dedicated support players healing the objective, the squad, or buffing allies enable aggressive playstyles for damage dealers. Trinity's Bless ability, Harrow's Penance, and Ember's healing provide sustainability that extends run duration beyond what damage-only squads achieve.
