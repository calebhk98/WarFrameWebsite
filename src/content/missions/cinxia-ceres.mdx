## Overview

Cinxia is a mid-game defense mission on Ceres where players protect a Tenno operative from Grineer assault across escalating waves. Located on the asteroid belt between Mars and Jupiter, Ceres represents a significant difficulty progression from early Earth content while remaining accessible to players with moderately leveled gear. Cinxia introduces enemy variety including elite units and explosive specialists requiring adaptive squad tactics.

The mission serves as a bridge between early linear progression and mid-game farming loops. New players reach Cinxia after completing several Earth and Venus nodes and can farm Cinxia repeatedly as their primary source for alloy plates and plastids. The mission's engagement level makes it ideal for players learning escalation mechanics and squad coordination at moderate difficulty.

Cinxia also serves as preparation for void fissure content. Many players farming Cinxia accumulate void traces toward their first void relic farming attempts. The mission's consistent reward structure enables targeted farming for specific resources, supporting mid-game crafting pipelines.

## Mission Type Notes

Defense missions require protecting an NPC operative across successive waves. Cinxia's waves introduce Grineer elite units including bombards and gunners, escalating tactical complexity compared to early defense missions. Elite units deal substantial damage and require coordinated elimination before positioning around the operative becomes untenable.

Later waves introduce environmental explosives and specialized units creating new tactical challenges. Bomb deployments by Grineer engineers detonate when enemies approach, potentially damaging the operative unexpectedly. Squad awareness of engineer placement and bomb detonations prevents unexpected objective damage.

Rotation mechanics remain consistent. Waves 1-5 grant rotation A; waves 6-10 grant rotation B; waves 11+ cycle through rotations. Understanding rotations enables efficient farming.

## Level Range

Cinxia's enemies progress from level 12 to level 17, significantly more challenging than early nodes but manageable for players with moderately formed loadouts. Modestly invested weapons and defensive frames handle Cinxia's waves with proper tactics. The level scaling permits extended play without sudden difficulty jumps, allowing skill development across multiple runs.

Extended runs beyond ten waves introduce tangible difficulty increases as enemy density and elite unit frequency escalate. Squads managing sustained damage output and efficient crowd control can push Cinxia's scaling considerably.

## Why Play This Node

Mid-game players farm Cinxia primarily for alloy plates and plastids. The mission serves as a primary crafting resource source during progression through mid-game content. Consistent runs accumulate sufficient alloy plates for simultaneous crafting of multiple weapons or frames.

Intermediate players use Cinxia for skill development in escalation tactics. Learning to adapt squad positioning and crowd control as waves escalate directly transfers to endgame endless missions. Cinxia provides a forgiving environment for this skill development compared to high-level nodes punishing mistakes heavily.

Veteran players occasionally run Cinxia for extremely casual farming or mastery rank challenges with new equipment. The mission remains relevant throughout account progression as an accessible farming node requiring no special preparation.

## Strategy Notes

Excelling at Cinxia defense requires understanding escalation, crowd control sequencing, and elite unit tactics:

**Elite Unit Prioritization**: Waves introduce bombards and gunners with substantially higher health and damage output. Squad should prioritize eliminating elite units before addressing regular troops. Ignoring elites allows them to concentrate fire, overwhelming the objective quickly.

**Crowd Control Sequencing**: As waves escalate, efficient crowd control becomes essential. Coordinating crowd control abilities ensures enemies never overwhelm the objective simultaneously. Staggering crowd control abilities maintains continuous enemy suppression across waves.

**Positioning Flexibility**: Cinxia's arena offers multiple defensive positions. Squads should adjust positioning dynamically as enemy spawn patterns shift. High-ground positions provide sight line advantages; ground-level positions enable rapid response to flanking attempts.

**Ammunition Management**: Extended Cinxia runs deplete weapon ammunition. Learning ammunition-efficient weapons and using ammunition restores enables pushing wave counts higher. Melee weapons with innate ammunition remove ammunition concerns.

**Grineer Weakness Exploitation**: Modding weapons for corrosive and slash damage accelerates Grineer elimination as elite units possess heavy armor. Armor stripping via corrosive procs increases damage scaling exponentially.

**Support Sustainability**: Introducing support frames providing healing or energy generation extends squad sustainability significantly. Trinity with Bless, Harrow with Penance, or Octavia with Mallet provide diverse support options enabling aggressive playstyles.
