## Overview

Tiamat is a mid-level salvage mission on Saturn introducing salvage mechanics at moderate difficulty. Salvage missions differ from standard content by requiring protection of excavators against enemy assault. Tiamat represents the first dedicated salvage mission for many players and establishes mechanics applicable throughout endgame progression.

The mission serves as introduction to excavator protection mechanics and resource farming through extraction focus. Players learning salvage fundamentals on Tiamat develop tactics transferable to higher-level salvage missions. The mission's moderate difficulty supports practice without extreme punishment for mistakes.

Tiamat's consistent resource rewards and reliable drops position it as a solid farming node during mid-game progression. The mission's balanced difficulty-to-reward ratio makes Tiamat attractive for players wanting respectable farming without extreme challenge.

## Mission Type Notes

Salvage missions task squads with protecting excavators extracting resources while enemies attack the excavation site. Excavators function similarly to defense objectives but actively generate resources during operation. Squad focus transitions between offense and defense depending on excavator status and enemy threat levels.

Tiamat's salvage structure emphasizes excavator protection while managing enemy waves. Excavators must be defended from enemy contact while digging completes. Squad positioning around excavators prevents enemy approach preventing interruptions.

Progress bars tied to excavator operations create timing pressure distinct from pure time-based missions. Excavators proceed on autonomous timers; protective focus must maintain throughout dig sequences.

## Level Range

Tiamat's enemies range from level 24 to level 28, representing manageable mid-to-high difficulty. Players with moderately invested gear handle Tiamat's waves with proper defensive tactics. The level scaling permits extended play managing multiple excavators in sequence.

Typical Tiamat runs complete within 10-15 minutes depending on excavator count and enemy difficulty. Most successful runs complete two-to-three full excavation cycles.

## Strategy Notes

Succeeding at Tiamat salvage requires understanding excavator protection and sustain mechanics:

**Excavator Positioning**: Establish defensive positions around excavator locations. One squad member should maintain close proximity while others provide ranged support. Preventing enemy contact prevents excavator interruption.

**Wave Management**: Eliminate enemies preventing excavator approach. Focus fire on enemies closest to excavator. Early wave elimination prevents late-wave accumulation overwhelming defense.

**Timing Awareness**: Excavators proceed on autonomous timers. Plan extraction timing after completing target excavation count. Pushing beyond necessary excavators increases risk unnecessarily.

**Energy Sustainability**: Extended Tiamat runs benefit from energy efficiency. Frames with innate regeneration sustain indefinitely. Efficient ability usage prevents energy depletion.

**Crowd Control Efficiency**: Introducing crowd control abilities maintains excavator safety. Stuns prevent enemy positioning; freezes lock placement; disarms reduce fire. Continuous suppression enables uninterrupted digging.

**Resource Accumulation**: Salvage missions generate more resources than standard missions. Multiple excavation cycles accumulate substantial plastid and alloy reserves.

**Support Integration**: Healing or buffing support extends squad viability. Trinity's healing enables aggressive positioning; Octavia's Mallet provides damage amplification.
