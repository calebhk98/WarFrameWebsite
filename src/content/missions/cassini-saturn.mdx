## Overview

Cassini is a mid-game capture mission on Saturn introducing capture mechanics at moderate difficulty. Capture missions differ fundamentally from wave-based content by focusing on a single high-value target requiring capture rather than elimination. Cassini's moderate difficulty makes it an ideal introduction to capture mechanics before advancing to higher-level capture missions.

Saturn's frozen aesthetic and Grineer opposition present familiar faction tactics at moderate challenge levels. Players with moderately invested gear handle Cassini's target capture with proper tactics. The mission introduces capture-specific mechanics enabling smooth progression into endgame capture content.

Cassini also appears frequently in sortie rotations, introducing players to capture mission requirements early. Building competence on Cassini's mechanics prepares for capture requirements in demanding endgame content.

## Mission Type Notes

Capture missions task squads with locating and capturing a single designated target enemy. The target possesses enhanced abilities and substantial health reserves compared to regular enemies. Successfully capturing requires reducing target health to low thresholds before executing capture mechanics.

Cassini's Grineer capture target employs heavy weapons and tactical positioning. Unlike pure combat encounters, capture targets frequently attempt escape requiring squad members to anticipate movements and maintain suppression. Allowing target escape creates extended chase sequences increasing mission duration and difficulty.

Capture missions grant single reward drops upon successful target capture. Unlike rotation-based missions providing multiple reward opportunities, capture missions require successful capture for any reward drops. This creates additional pressure compared to wave-based missions where partial session completion yields rewards.

## Strategy Notes

Succeeding at Cassini capture requires understanding target behavior and efficient capture execution:

**Target Tracking**: Locate the capture target's initial position and anticipate movement patterns. Grineer targets favor defensive high-ground positions. Positioning squad members to intercept anticipated escape routes enables efficient target engagement.

**Sustained Damage Application**: Reduce target health to capture thresholds efficiently. Concentrated fire from multiple squad members accelerates health reduction. Splitting squad fire against individual target reduces damage efficiency significantly.

**Crowd Control Timing**: Introduce crowd control abilities creating windows for damage application. Stuns prevent target evasion; freezes lock target positioning; disarms reduce defensive abilities. Staggering crowd control maintains offensive pressure throughout engagement.

**Capture Mechanics Execution**: Execute capture sequences immediately when health reaches low thresholds. Delaying capture allows target escape requiring extended chase sequences. Coordination among squad members prevents capture timing failures.

**Grineer Weakness Exploitation**: Modding for corrosive and slash damage accelerates target elimination. Armor stripping via corrosive procs creates exponential damage scaling. Combined elemental damage optimization reduces engagement duration.

**Squad Positioning**: Position squad members strategically around target anticipated routes. Establishing perimeter control prevents escape attempts. Communicating positioning clearly enables coordinated response to target movement.

**Energy and Ammunition**: Sustained crowd control and damage output demand efficient resource usage. Frames with energy regeneration sustain crowd control indefinitely. Ammunition-efficient weapons eliminate ammunition concerns.

**Support Integration**: Defensive support enables aggressive target pursuit. Healing frames extend team viability through sustained damage. Buffer frames amplify damage output enabling rapid target elimination.
