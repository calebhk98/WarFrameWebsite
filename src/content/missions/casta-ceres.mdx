## Overview

Casta is a mid-game Exterminate mission on Ceres providing efficient resource farming at moderate difficulty. Exterminate missions differ fundamentally from wave-based content by requiring elimination of all enemies across a tileset without rotation mechanics -- one completion grants one reward. Casta's straightforward objective and Grineer enemy composition at levels 12-17 make it an excellent farming node for Alloy Plates and Plastids during mid-game progression.

The mission serves as a quick farming option when needing these specific resources. Unlike Defense or Survival missions requiring extended time commitment, Casta can be completed within 5-8 minutes, enabling rapid farming cycles with minimal per-run setup.

## Mission details

Casta takes place on the Ceres tileset, a Grineer industrial zone with corridors, catwalks, and machinery rooms. The mission objective is to eliminate all Grineer units in the zone. Enemies range from levels 12 to 17 -- Grineer Lancers, Troopers, and Scorpions form the bulk of the opposition, with the occasional Bombard or Napalm appearing as mid-tier threats. No boss encounter occurs. Mission completion triggers once all enemy units are killed.

Exterminate on Ceres uses the standard Grineer ship tileset. Spawn zones are distributed across the map; checking the minimap for remaining red markers ensures efficient clearing. Squad members can split to cover different sections simultaneously, cutting run times significantly.

## Rewards

Completing Casta rewards one drop from the Ceres Exterminate loot table plus standard mission resources from enemy kills.

- Alloy Plate: Grineer armor material used across dozens of mid-game and late-game blueprints. Ceres Grineer enemies drop Alloy Plate reliably as a common resource.
- Plastids: Nanite-infested tissue material required in most warframe and weapon blueprints. Ceres is one of the primary early Plastids sources alongside Saturn and Uranus.
- Credits: Standard mission credit reward applied on extraction.

Resource drop rates from enemies are unaffected by the Exterminate format -- kills throughout the mission contribute to resource accumulation in addition to the final extraction reward.

## Recommended loadout

Casta at levels 12-17 requires minimal gear investment. Any weapon capable of killing unmodded Grineer within 2-3 shots handles this mission. Warframes with mobility (Volt, Wisp, Gauss) reduce per-run time by moving between enemy clusters faster. Frames with area-of-effect abilities (Ember, Equinox, Saryn) clear groups of Grineer efficiently without individual targeting.

Bring a resource booster if actively farming Alloy Plates or Plastids -- it doubles the resource drops from all enemy kills in the mission. Resource-finding mods (Thief's Wit, Loot Detector) help locate any hidden containers that spawn in the tileset.

## Notes

Casta is not a rotation mission; there are no A/B/C reward cycles. The extraction reward is a single drop. For players needing large quantities of Alloy Plates or Plastids, Casta offers shorter individual runs than Survival missions with comparable per-run yield. Ceres as a planet also unlocks the Cephalon Suda and New Loka syndicate alert mission variants, which can supplement farming sessions.
