## Overview

Arbitrations are post-endgame mission variants that scale to extremely high enemy levels and introduce unique mechanics designed to challenge even fully optimized, veteran players. Every mission type on the star chart has an arbitration variant, from Survival to Defense to Disruption, creating opportunities for diverse playstyle expression within a consistently difficult framework.

Arbitrations are distinguished by the presence of Arbitration drones--flying enemies that periodically spawn and must be destroyed to prevent enemy invulnerability. Additionally, death penalties in Arbitrations are severe: downed players cannot be revived by teammates and instead spawn in the next wave, removing traditional respawn mechanics. This design creates extreme punishment for poor positioning or positioning errors, dramatically raising the skill floor.

## Arbitration Mechanics and Drone System

The defining feature of arbitrations is the arbitration drone mechanic. These invulnerable drones spawn periodically throughout the mission, and while they are present, all enemies gain invulnerability shields. Players must interrupt their primary mission objectives to locate and destroy drones before progressing.

The drone system creates strategic decision points: players must choose between aggressive offensive advancement and defensive drone elimination. Squad coordination becomes essential--some players focus on primary objectives while others manage drone destruction. Failure to coordinate results in energy waste and mission failure.

## Enemy Composition and Extreme Scaling

Arbitration enemies scale from level 60 to level 150 or beyond, depending on mission duration. At these levels, even heavily armored, optimized builds take significant damage. Enemy health and armor scaling are exponential, requiring players to maintain excellent efficiency and avoid wasting ammunition or abilities.

Arbitration presents mixed enemy compositions drawn from all standard factions, ensuring that no single damage type or strategy covers all scenarios. Players must maintain flexibility and adapt mid-mission to changing enemy compositions.

## Rewards and Farming Efficiency

Arbitrations drop void relics, arbitration cosmetics currency, and arcane enhancements. The relics obtained from arbitrations are valuable because they fill gaps in standard farming routes. Requiem and Zariman era relics, in particular, are difficult to farm through alternative routes, making arbitration an important supplementary source.

Arbitration cosmetics currency is used to purchase exclusive cosmetic items and rotating rewards available only through arbitrations. This creates long-term progression incentives beyond simple stat optimization.

## Recommended Loadout and Strategic Approach

Arbitration success demands extreme optimization. Frame selection should prioritize either crowd control (Nidus, Saryn, Limbo) or extreme survivability (Trinity, Wisp, Hydro). Frames with low ability costs or alternative energy generation (Zenurik focus school, Energize arcanes) excel by maintaining uptime under resource constraints.

Weapons should prioritize efficiency and sustainability. High-fire-rate, high-magazine-capacity weapons prevent ammunition starvation. Status-heavy builds leverage elemental procs to scale damage against extremely high-level enemies, while pure-damage builds struggle to keep pace with exponential enemy scaling.

Arcane enhancements become mandatory at arbitration difficulty. Arcane Energize (energy generation) and Arcane Blessing (health restoration) are near-essential for sustained runs.

## Death Mechanics and Punishment

The arbitration respawn system removes traditional downed-player revival mechanics. When a player is downed in arbitration, they remain out of action until the next wave starts, at which point they respawn with reduced resources. This creates severe punishment for positioning errors or resource mismanagement.

Teams must play conservatively, prioritize crowd control, and maintain distance from high-threat enemies. A single squad wipe can end extended arbitration runs, incentivizing careful playstyle and defensive positioning.

## Mission Type Variability

Arbitrations cycle through all standard mission types. Arbitration Survival requires sustained endurance against scaling enemy waves. Arbitration Defense demands crowd control and precise wave management. Arbitration Disruption requires objective completion while managing drones and enemy scaling.

This variability ensures arbitration content remains engaging--players must adapt strategies based on mission type rather than relying on a single optimization.

## Difficulty Tiers and Extraction Strategy

Arbitrations provide extraction points at set intervals (typically 5, 10, 20, 30+ minutes). Early extraction points provide baseline rewards, while extended runs access higher-value reward tables. Players must balance risk and reward--extended runs generate more relics and currency but exponentially increase enemy danger.

Veteran players typically push 20-30 minute arbitrations, extracting before enemy scaling becomes completely unmanageable.

## Squad Composition and Coordination

Arbitrations are optimized for four-player squads with distributed roles. Teams with dedicated tank, healer, crowd control, and damage dealer roles can sustain much longer arbitration runs than suboptimal compositions. Squad coordination becomes as important as individual frame optimization.

Public matchmaking allows newer endgame players to learn from veterans, though pre-arranged squads typically perform significantly better.

## Strategic Importance and Prestige

Arbitrations represent the highest skill-expression endgame activity. Successfully completing arbitrations at extended duration (20+ minutes) signals exceptional player competence. The activity attracts hardcore players seeking maximum challenge.

The arbitration cosmetic reward track creates long-term progression incentives, encouraging repeated runs beyond simple economic value.

## Notes on Extreme Duration Runs

Players attempting arbitrations beyond 30 minutes enter uncharted territory where enemy scaling becomes mathematically untenable for most frames. These ultra-extended runs require perfectly optimized loadouts, near-perfect execution, and squad coordination at the highest level.

Most players establish personal arbitration ceilings (15-20 minutes) and farm within sustainable difficulty ranges.
