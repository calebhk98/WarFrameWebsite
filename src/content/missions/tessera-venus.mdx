## Overview

Tessera is a foundational defense mission on Venus introducing early-game players to Corpus faction mechanics. Corpus forces emphasize ranged attacks and elemental damage compared to Grineer melee-focused tactics. Tessera's level scaling matches early progression, making it accessible to new players while introducing faction-specific tactical considerations that carry throughout mid-game progression.

Venus's Corpus-dominated territories present distinctly different environmental challenges compared to Earth's Grineer strongholds. The corpus tileset features metallic corridors, elevated walkways, and sight lines favoring ranged engagement. Players learning positioning against Corpus ranged units develop spacing and cover techniques applicable to all Corpus missions. Tessera's moderate complexity introduces defensive strategy without overwhelming new players still mastering basic mechanics.

The mission serves dual purposes: early-game resource farming and Corpus faction introduction. Players accumulating circuits for early crafting and learning Corpus behavior patterns on Tessera transfer this knowledge to increasingly difficult Corpus missions. Tessera remains relevant throughout early progression as a reliable farming node for circuits and affinity.

## Mission Type Notes

Defense missions require protecting an NPC operative across successive five-minute waves. Tessera's defense structure matches other defense missions but introduces Corpus-specific enemy behaviors. Corpus units favor elevated positions, suppressive fire, and coordinated ranged attacks compared to Grineer charges and melee dominance. Squad positioning against Corpus requires more defensive cover usage and ranged counterplay.

Corpus shielding mechanics introduce new tactical layers absent from early Grineer missions. Many Corpus units possess energy shields that regenerate if not sustained with continuous fire. Squads must prioritize shield removal before eliminating enemies, changing rotation priorities compared to unshielded Grineer units. Learning shield mechanics on Tessera establishes habits applicable to all endgame Corpus content.

Rotation rewards follow standard defense patterns. Waves 1-5 grant rotation A; waves 6-10 grant rotation B; waves 11-15 grant rotation C. Beyond fifteen waves, rotations cycle repeatedly. Understanding this structure enables efficient farming by extracting after achieving target rotations.

## Strategy Notes

Succeeding at Tessera defense requires understanding Corpus mechanics and elemental advantages:

**Corpus Shielding**: Many Corpus units possess energy shields. Sustained fire removes shields before applying damage to health. Squads should prioritize enemies by shield strength, removing heavy units' shields before dealing with lighter units. Using electricity or heat damage accelerates shield removal.

**Elemental Advantages**: Corpus armor benefits from electricity-aligned damage, but flesh components are weak to electricity and heat. Early players should practice modding weapons for electrical or heat procs to accelerate Corpus elimination.

**Ranged Positioning**: Corpus enemies favor elevated firing positions. Squads should position defensively behind cover, using architectural features to avoid suppressive fire. High-ground positioning for squad members creates reciprocal advantages.

**Objective Protection**: Place one squad member on the objective itself while ranged players establish defensive perimeters. The objective's location dictates positioning; communicating objective location to squad mates prevents flanking.

**Energy Sustainability**: Early frames with modest energy capacity may struggle sustaining crowd control abilities across multiple waves. Learning energy management and efficient ability usage prepares for endgame play.

**Aerial Awareness**: Some Corpus units possess jetpacks enabling elevated positioning. Squads should maintain aerial awareness and counter-position to prevent enemies occupying unchallenged high ground.
