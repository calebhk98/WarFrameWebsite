## Overview

Ambulas Reborn is an assassination mission on Pluto where players face off against Ambulas, a Corpus proxy commander and mechanical lieutenant of the Corpus leadership. The mission takes place within a Corpus Ice Planet facility, featuring industrial architecture and mechanical hazards. This is one of the primary sources for Chroma warframe blueprints, making it a crucial grinding point for players seeking to craft the versatile elemental frame.

The fight against Ambulas is mechanically engaging, requiring players to adapt to its shield mechanics and environmental factors. The boss employs a variety of combat techniques including heavy weapons, deployable sentries, and area-of-effect attacks. Success requires a balanced loadout and understanding of the boss's attack patterns.

## Mission Type Notes

Assassination missions in Warframe are endgame content designed to challenge experienced players with a single, high-priority target rather than waves of regular enemies. In Ambulas Reborn specifically, players must navigate the facility, defeat Corpus defenders and mechanized units, and then confront Ambulas in its arena. The mission allows players to bring up to four squad members, enabling cooperative play strategies.

The Corpus Ice Planet tileset provides environmental variety with frozen corridors, mechanical chambers, and areas populated with defensive turrets. Players familiar with similar tilesets will find the layout moderately complex, with multiple pathways and verticality that can be exploited with parkour.

## Level Range

Ambulas Reborn spawns enemies between level 40 and 50, placing it squarely in the mid-to-late star chart progression. New players can attempt the mission at lower mastery ranks, but success becomes significantly more difficult below mastery rank 10. The scaling nature of Warframe's level system means that properly modded frames and weapons can handle these enemies, though unoptimized gear will struggle.

The boss itself does not scale beyond level 50 base health, but its damage output and tactical behavior create the true challenge. Status effects and crowd control become increasingly valuable here compared to pure damage output.

## Rewards

The primary reward from defeating Ambulas is access to one of three Chroma blueprint drops:
- Chroma Blueprint (chassis/main blueprint)
- Chroma Neuroptics (head component)
- Chroma Systems (core component)

These three blueprints are necessary to fully construct Chroma, a warframe specializing in elemental damage and defensive buffs. The drop chances are distributed across the three parts, meaning players often need to run this mission multiple times to complete the set. Additionally, void fissure void traces are obtained as rotation rewards, supporting endgame relic fissure farming.

## Why Play This Node

Players grind Ambulas Reborn for two primary reasons: acquiring Chroma parts and farming void traces for void fissure missions. Chroma is a powerful warframe for both damage scaling and survivability, particularly in Steel Path and endgame content. Its elemental affinity system makes it valuable for specialized builds targeting specific enemy factions.

Beyond warframe acquisition, this mission serves as a training ground for assassination mechanics. Players learn to manage extended fights against a mobile, intelligent boss rather than relying on crowd control to trivialize encounters. The mechanical complexity develops skills transferable to higher-level endgame content like void fissure auras and Void Cascade.

## Strategy Notes

Effective strategies for Ambulas Reborn include:

**Damage Types**: Corpus units and Ambulas itself are susceptible to Magnetic damage (electricity and cold). Modding weapons with magnetic procs or using frames with magnetic-aligned augments speeds up enemy deletion. Heat damage is also effective on machinery.

**Tankiness**: Frames with high armor or shield gate mechanics (like Hildryn or Harrow) trivialize damage phases. Alternatively, damage frames with proper scaling can burst Ambulas before it deals significant harm.

**Mobility**: The arena is relatively spacious, allowing parkour players to maintain distance and avoid incoming fire. Frames with movement augments or exalted melee weapons can control positioning effectively.

**Energy Management**: Most damage frames require consistent energy to sustain ability usage. Bringing Trinity as a support, using energy-efficient mods, or incorporating energy orbs from ability kills maintains damage output throughout the mission.
