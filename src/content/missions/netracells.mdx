## Overview

Netracells is a Survival-type mission on the moon Deimos within the Zariman tileset, presenting players with an endless combat scenario against waves of Infested enemies. The mission is primarily utilized for farming relics and rare mods, with rotating loot tables that cycle through three distinct reward rotations during extended survival runs.

Unlike traditional star chart survival missions, Netracells features the Infested faction, which brings unique mechanics and enemy types not found on the standard planets. The mission scales well for both intermediate and endgame players, making it suitable for squad play across different skill levels.

## Mission Structure and Rotation System

Netracells operates on a standard Survival mission framework: players are inserted into a tileset and must survive waves of enemy attacks while protecting an objective. The mission has no end point—players can remain indefinitely, though loot rotations incentivize specific extraction times.

The loot rotation system follows the standard Warframe pattern: Rotation A (0–5 minutes), Rotation B (5–10 minutes), and Rotation C (10–20 minutes), after which the cycle repeats. Different rewards are allocated to each rotation, making certain loot grinds more or less efficient depending on how long players intend to stay.

## Enemy Composition and Scaling

The Infested faction dominates this tileset. Enemies include Chargers, Lancers, Parasites, and various specialized Infested units that scale from level 30 to 50. The Infested faction is distinguished by their melee-focused attacks and lack of ranged damage, creating a predictable but relentless offense.

Players should expect constant pressure from melee units and occasional ranged attacks from mutated Corpus-derived Infested. The mission becomes significantly more dangerous as enemy levels climb during extended runs, requiring players to manage ammunition and positioning carefully.

## Rewards and Farming Efficiency

Netracells is a primary farming location for Void and Requiem relics, making it invaluable for players pursuing prime warframe and weapon components. The rotating loot tables ensure that players farming specific relics can optimize their extraction timing.

Additionally, the mission yields void fissure components and Deimos-specific crafting materials used in railjack and void realm progression. Certain mods exclusive to the Infested tileset also drop here with reasonable frequency.

## Recommended Loadout and Survival Strategy

Survival missions demand sustained DPS and excellent survivability. Frames with area-of-effect abilities—such as Saryn, Ember, or Grendel—excel by clearing large enemy groups simultaneously. Crowd control frames like Frost and Limbo prevent enemies from reaching the objective, reducing overall damage taken.

For weapons, high-capacity magazines and fire-rate-oriented builds outperform single-target damage strategies. Bring ammunition-sustaining mods and efficiency builds to extend mission duration without running dry.

Defensive positioning near the objective and managing spawn points dramatically improve success rates. Players should establish killzone patterns and rotate through predictable enemy spawns.

## Void Fissure Integration

Netracells exists as both a regular mission and a Void Fissure node during active fissure events. When active as a fissure mission, players can bring reactant and crack relics simultaneously, creating alternative reward pathways. This dual-mission status makes Netracells one of the most flexible farming nodes for relic-based content.

## Difficulty Progression and Scalability

The mission starts at intermediate difficulty and scales toward endgame challenges as players push extraction times. New players can gain experience with survival mechanics by running short sessions and extracting at the 5-minute rotation A mark. Veteran players can push into 20+ minute sessions for repeated rotation C pulls.

Squad composition significantly impacts sustainability. Teams with dedicated supports, tanks, and damage dealers can extend survival duration far longer than solo players.

## Strategic Importance and Meta Relevance

Netracells represents a core farming route for players pursuing prime equipment progression. The relic drops make this mission economically valuable, as earned relics can be refined, cracked for components, or traded to other players for platinum or prime components.

The mission's existence on an enduring rotation ensures consistent accessibility without requiring players to wait for limited-time events to acquire specific loot.

## Notes on Extended Play

Players attempting to extend Netracells runs beyond 20 minutes should prepare for dramatic enemy scaling. At these prolonged durations, even well-equipped teams face significant threat escalation. Bringing healing items, energy restores, and having clear role assignments improves sustainability in extended runs.
