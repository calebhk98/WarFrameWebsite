## Overview

Xini is a high-level interception mission on Eris featuring Infested opposition at endgame difficulty. Interception missions introduce control-point mechanics fundamentally different from wave-based or survival content. Rather than protecting single objectives or engaging in continuous combat, interception requires controlling multiple points simultaneously while preventing enemy team progress.

Eris's Infested presence introduces unique faction mechanics including melee-focused enemy tactics and biological armor systems. Infested units emphasize close-quarter combat and rapid spawning compared to Corpus or Grineer approaches, creating distinct tactical challenges. Xini represents a significant difficulty spike for players transitioning from earlier faction-dominated content.

The mission's high-level scaling and interception mechanics position Xini as serious endgame farming. Veteran players pursuing optimal farming efficiency frequently incorporate Xini into diverse rotation patterns. The mission's unique mechanic diversity prevents farming fatigue through varied tactical requirements.

## Mission Type Notes

Interception missions task squads with controlling multiple waypoints distributed across maps. Enemy teams attempt capturing these waypoints autonomously. Squads must occupy waypoints to prevent enemy team capture while maintaining presence across the map. Complete enemy team capture of waypoints results in mission failure; maintaining squad control yields mission success.

Xini features four interception points positioned across Eris's terrain. Coordination becomes essential; a single squad member defending a remote point cannot simultaneously support struggling teammates. Squad positioning and rotational defense strategies determine success or failure.

Interception missions introduce progress bars independent of player positioning. Enemies automatically advance progress if left uncontested; squad members must actively occupy points to regain control. Understanding progress mechanics and rotation timing enables efficient point management.

## Strategy Notes

Succeeding at Xini interception requires mastering point control and squad coordination:

**Point Priority Hierarchies**: Establish squad consensus on critical point priority. Defending a single crucial point often succeeds better than spreading thin across multiple points. Coordinate which points are secondary and defensible losses.

**Rotation Timings**: Establish rotation patterns enabling sequential point coverage. Squads should rotate between points systematically rather than remaining stationary. Clear communication prevents simultaneous coverage gaps.

**Infested Weakness Exploitation**: Infested units are weak to heat and toxin damage. Fire-aligned damage accelerates elimination; toxin procs poison enemies creating additional damage. Modding for combined heat-toxin or heat-electricity damage optimizes effectiveness.

**Point Occupation Mechanics**: Squad members must occupy point capture zones actively. Standing near points without occupying capture zones fails to prevent enemy progress. Clear understanding of capture zone boundaries prevents mechanical failures.

**Crowd Control Integration**: Introduce crowd control abilities preventing enemy team occupation. Stuns interrupt team progress; freezes prevent team movement; disarms reduce offensive capabilities. Crowd control enables aggressive point defense.

**Squad Spacing**: Maintain sufficient spacing across points enabling rapid response to escalating threats. A single squad member cannot defend multiple simultaneous point captures. Balance coverage and responsiveness through positional discipline.

**Enemy Positioning Understanding**: Infested spawn patterns follow predictable sequences. Learning spawn locations enables prepositioned crowd control and damage application. Anticipating spawns prevents reactive defensive gameplay.

**Support Viability**: Defensive support enables aggressive point defense. Healing frames extend team sustainability; buff frames enhance damage output; crowd control frames prevent enemy positioning. Support specialization unlocks aggressive point defense strategies.
