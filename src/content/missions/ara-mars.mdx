## Overview

Ara is an accessible defense mission on Mars positioned between early Earth content and higher-level mid-game nodes. The mission introduces Mars's distinctive red rock terrain and provides one of the first dedicated sources for rubedo, a mid-game crafting resource. Grineer forces on Mars follow familiar mechanics but appear in larger concentrations than early nodes, providing appropriate challenge progression for players advancing from early star chart content.

Mars represents a significant milestone in early progression. Players reaching Ara have mastered basic mechanics and graduated from pure learning-focused nodes. Ara rewards lean toward crafting resources rather than pure affinity, encouraging farming-focused gameplay development. The mission's manageable difficulty supports extended farming runs without extreme mechanical demand.

Ara serves as an introduction to deliberate farming node selection. Rather than running all available nodes, players begin identifying specific farming targets. Ara becomes a primary node for players requiring both plastids and rubedo simultaneously, establishing patterns applicable to higher-level farming optimization throughout progression.

## Mission Type Notes

Defense missions require protecting an NPC operative across successive waves. Ara's defense structure matches other defense nodes but emphasizes larger enemy concentrations compared to earlier missions. Players must develop efficiency in crowd management and sustainability as enemy density increases.

Rotation mechanics enable efficient farming. Early waves grant rotation A rewards; mid-waves grant rotation B; later waves grant rotation C. Players can extract after five waves without requiring extended time commitment, or push further for rare rotation C rewards.

The operative's positioning in Ara's arena determines squad positioning. Squads should establish defensive positions with clear sight lines and access to cover. Communication regarding objective location prevents positioning confusion and failed protection attempts.

## Level Range

Ara's enemies range from level 10 to level 12, positioned between early and mid-game content. Players with minimally invested starter gear struggle with consistency, but moderately leveled weapons and defensive frames handle Ara's waves reliably. The level range encourages gear progression while remaining forgiving of mistakes.

Extended Ara runs introduce modest difficulty escalation as enemy density increases. A competent squad managing sustained damage and crowd control can comfortably handle 15-20 waves before difficulty becomes substantially challenging.

## Rewards

Ara's defense rotation rewards support mid-game crafting requirements:

**Plastids**: Universal mid-game alloy resource used across diverse weapon and frame blueprints. Ara's consistent plastid drops position it as a primary plastid source early in mid-game progression.

**Rubedo**: Common metallic resource appearing prominently in mid-game crafting. Many intermediate weapons require rubedo; Ara provides reliable sourcing enabling consistent crafting queue progression.

**Credits**: Modest credit generation per wave supports active crafting queues.

**Affinity**: Standard defense mission affinity generation supports leveling new weapons or frames.

## Why Play This Node

Early-to-mid game players farm Ara primarily for plastids and rubedo. The mission serves as one of the first dedicated sources for rubedo, positioning Ara as an essential farming node during early mid-game progression. Runs accumulate resources enabling simultaneous crafting of multiple weapons or frames.

Intermediate players use Ara as a low-stress farming option when requiring plastids or rubedo. The mission's straightforward mechanics and consistent spawns make Ara extremely efficient for targeted farming compared to higher-level nodes.

New players struggling with early mid-game progression can use Ara as practice for longer defense runs. Learning to manage multiple waves and sustain squad positioning on Ara translates directly to higher-difficulty endgame defense farming.

## Strategy Notes

Succeeding at Ara defense requires managing crowd control and sustain across extended waves:

**Objective Protection Priority**: Establish the objective location and position squad members defensively. One dedicated protection player maintains close objective proximity while damage dealers operate from perimeter positions.

**Wave Flow Management**: Each wave introduces predictable enemy patterns. Anticipating enemy types and positioning preemptively prevents chaotic objective breaches.

**Crowd Control Efficiency**: As waves escalate, efficient crowd control becomes essential. Rotating crowd control abilities prevents simultaneous enemy breaches and maintains defensive control.

**Sustained Damage Output**: Weapons efficient in sustained fire rather than burst damage excel at Mars defense. Assault rifles and beam weapons provide consistent damage across lengthy waves.

**Grineer Weakness Exploitation**: Modding for corrosive damage scales effectiveness dramatically as Grineer possess substantial armor. Armor stripping via corrosive procs exponentially increases damage scaling.

**Ammunition Sustainability**: Extended Ara runs benefit from ammunition efficiency. Bringing ammunition restores or using weapons with small magazines efficiently manages ammunition constraints.

**Squad Composition Flexibility**: Support frames providing healing or buffs enable aggressive playstyles. Rhino with Iron Skin provides personal defense; Mesa with Peacemakers provides ranged damage; Trinity provides healing sustain.
