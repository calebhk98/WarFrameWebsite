## Overview

Ukko is an endgame capture mission in the Void introducing advanced mission mechanics at substantial difficulty. Capture missions task squads with locating and capturing a single target enemy, eliminating objectives fundamentally different from wave-based or survival content. Ukko's location in the Void and Orokin opposition create distinct tactical challenges reflecting late-game progression.

The Void represents a distinctly different setting compared to terrestrial planets. Orokin enemies exhibit unique mechanics absent from standard factions, including energy weapons, teleportation, and sophisticated tactical coordination. Players unfamiliar with Orokin mechanics can expect substantial difficulty increases compared to faction-familiar content.

Ukko serves as introduction to Void-specific content and capture mission mechanics. Players demonstrating competence on Ukko can approach higher-level capture missions and void fissure-related content with confidence. The mission's endgame difficulty supports farming for veteran players with optimized builds while remaining accessible to dedicated intermediate players.

## Mission Type Notes

Capture missions differ fundamentally from wave-based or survival content by introducing a single high-value target requiring capture rather than elimination. The target possesses unique abilities and substantial health reserves, demanding tactical focus and concentrated fire. Successfully capturing the target requires reducing health to low thresholds before executing capture sequences.

Ukko's capture target represents advanced Orokin combat principles. The target employs teleportation, crowd control abilities, and coordinated support patterns distinct from standard enemy encounters. Squad positioning and crowd control become critical; allowing the target to escape creates extended chase sequences extending mission duration.

Capture missions feature no rotation rewards. Mission completion grants single reward drop, simplified compared to wave-based missions. This structure makes capture missions excellent for straightforward farming or quick missions when time-efficiency matters.

## Strategy Notes

Succeeding at Ukko capture requires understanding target abilities and capture mechanics:

**Target Ability Recognition**: Study Orokin target abilities and attack patterns. Understanding teleportation triggers and crowd control sequences enables predictive positioning. Anticipating target movements prevents extended chase sequences.

**Crowd Control Timing**: Introduce crowd control abilities creating windows for damage application. Stuns prevent target escape; freezes prevent pursuit during repositioning; disarms reduce offensive capabilities. Staggering crowd control maintains suppression.

**Capture Execution**: Reduce target health to low thresholds before executing capture sequences. Rapid final-damage coordination enables efficient captures. Delaying final damage risks target escape and extended mission duration.

**Squad Positioning**: Position squad members strategically around target anticipated routes. Establishing perimeter control prevents escape attempts. Communicating positioning clearly enables coordinated response to unexpected movements.

**Energy and Ammunition**: Sustained crowd control and damage output demand efficient resource usage. Frames with energy regeneration sustain crowd control indefinitely. Ammunition-efficient weapons or melee options eliminate ammunition concerns.

**Support Integration**: Defensive support enables aggressive playstyles. Defensive abilities provide damage absorption while damage dealers maintain offensive pressure. Support roles unlock sustained damage output impossible for pure damage compositions.

**Orokin Weakness Exploitation**: Identify Orokin-specific vulnerabilities and exploit them. Energy weapons require different damage strategies compared to armor-focused units. Adapting damage strategies to Orokin mechanics improves efficiency significantly.
