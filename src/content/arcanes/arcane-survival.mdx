## Overview

Arcane Survival extends the bleedout timer when the warframe is downed, giving the player and squadmates more time to perform a revive before mission failure. When the warframe reaches zero health and enters the bleedout state, Arcane Survival checks its trigger chance and on success applies a reduction to the bleedout rate - slowing how quickly the bleedout timer depletes. At rank 5, the 150% bleedout rate reduction effectively freezes or reverses the bleedout timer, providing significantly extended down time before the warframe fully expires.

Bleedout timer management is particularly relevant in solo content where no squadmates are available to revive quickly, and in squad content where the team is engaged and cannot reach the downed player immediately. Arcane Survival's extension provides the additional seconds needed to break off from combat and perform the revive.

## Effect

When the warframe enters the bleedout state, the arcane checks its trigger chance. On success, the bleedout rate reduction applies immediately, extending the remaining time in the downed state. At rank 0, a 10% chance reduces the bleedout rate by 25% - modest extension that adds seconds to the countdown. At rank 5, a 60% trigger chance reduces bleedout rate by 150% - effectively stopping or reversing the timer on proc, dramatically extending survival time. The bonus Arcane Revive at rank 5 provides one additional self-revive per mission.

## Acquisition

Arcane Survival has no listed drop source in the WFCD dataset. Acquisition is most reliable through warframe.market trading, where players list available copies. Legacy event and special content drops may have historically provided the arcane; community resources document historical sources.

## Builds that use it

Arcane Survival suits solo and low-coordination-squad builds where getting revived quickly is not guaranteed. Solo Steel Path players who occasionally make positioning errors benefit from the extended bleedout window that allows them to reorient and survive using solo revive mechanics. New players in difficult content benefit from the additional time while learning to avoid lethal scenarios.

## Notes

The bleedout rate reduction interacts with the base bleedout timer length. A -150% rate reduction at rank 5 means the timer effectively halts on a successful proc. The Arcane Revive at rank 5 provides a self-revive regardless of the bleedout trigger chance - it is always available once per mission. The bleedout trigger is separate from the Arcane Revive; both can contribute to survival in the same downed event.
