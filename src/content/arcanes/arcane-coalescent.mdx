## Overview

Arcane Coalescent extends the duration of active buffs on nearby allies when the warframe achieves kills. Each enemy kill within affinity range triggers an extension to currently active buffs on nearby squad members, accumulating additional time onto whatever ability or arcane buffs are currently running. In missions where buff uptime is critical - support abilities with fixed cast durations, time-limited defensive bonuses - Coalescent allows high-kill-rate frames to effectively extend the team's buff economy through combat output.

The kill-based trigger means the arcane naturally integrates into any combat-focused playstyle. Frames clearing large numbers of enemies rapidly extend buffs across multiple kills per second in dense content, while frames with slower kill rates extend buffs less frequently but still provide support during active engagement phases.

## Effect

Each enemy kill checks for a buff extension event. On success, all active buffs on nearby allies gain additional duration equal to the listed value. At rank 0, kills add 2 seconds per event to active buffs - modest but consistent support during high-kill-rate content. At rank 5, kills add 10 seconds per event - meaningful extensions that can significantly prolong time-limited buffs from support abilities or combat arcanes.

The extension applies for 8 seconds of Coalescent's own trigger window, during which kills generate the duration bonus. Multiple kills during the 8-second window stack their individual duration contributions.

## Acquisition

Arcane Coalescent drops as a rare reward from Zariman Void Cascade endless missions. The rare designation and Void Cascade source make it one of the harder Zariman arcanes to accumulate through regular play. Extended Void Cascade sessions that reach later rotation tiers provide Coalescent in their reward pools. Warframe.market provides an alternative purchase route for players who prefer platinum acquisition over farming extended Zariman missions.

## Builds that use it

Arcane Coalescent suits support-oriented builds on frames that generate high kill counts while simultaneously providing buff abilities to the squad. Trinity builds that cast Blessing (a time-limited damage reduction and health buff) benefit from Coalescent's kill-based extensions keeping Blessing active longer without requiring immediate recasts. Harrow builds that combine Condemn crowd control with high-kill-rate weapon play extend Covenant's invulnerability and critical chance bonuses through Coalescent's accumulation. Wisp builds that drop Mote combinations and then aggressively clear enemies extend Mote duration for squadmates through the kill output.

Frames with AoE clear potential - Saryn, Equinox, Mesa - generate enough kills per second during content to stack meaningful cumulative duration extensions across the squad's active buffs.

## Notes

The buff extension applies to all active buffs simultaneously - ability buffs, arcane buffs, and consumable item buffs each receive the duration addition. Buffs that have already expired are not retroactively extended. The 8-second trigger window means kills must occur within that window to register extensions for that Coalescent proc; the window does not extend with each kill. Allies outside the arcane's range do not receive buff extensions even if they have active buffs. The kill trigger fires on the player's own kills; squadron kills do not count toward Coalescent triggers.
