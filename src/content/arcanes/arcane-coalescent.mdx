## Overview

Arcane Coalescent is a rare buff-extension Warframe arcane that amplifies ally ability duration on enemy kills. This arcane synergizes with support-focused squad builds, enabling extended team utility uptime during high-kill-count scenarios like void cascade farming.

## Effect

When the Warframe kills an enemy, all active buffs on nearby allies have their duration extended. The extension scales from +2 seconds at rank 0 to +10 seconds at rank 5. Multiple kills stack extensions, enabling exponential duration scaling.

## Acquisition

Arcane Coalescent drops as a rare reward from Zariman void cascade endless missions. Extended farming provides reliable acquisition.

## Best on

Arcane Coalescent shines in support-heavy squad builds:

- **Buff-providing Warframes** (Trinity, Harrow, Protea) extend team utility.
- **High-kill-rate void cascades** — frequent kills maximize extension procs.
- **Endless mission support** — extended buffs reduce recasting burden.

## Notes

The arcane requires active kills by the wielding Warframe. Affinity-range kills trigger the effect. Buff stacking mechanics depend on the specific buff type being extended. Particularly valuable in squad-oriented playstyles.
