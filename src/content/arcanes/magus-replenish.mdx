## Overview

Magus Replenish restores operator health as a percentage of maximum health on each Void Sling. The operator's health pool is separate from the warframe's - the operator has its own limited health value that does not benefit from warframe health mods. Sustaining operator health during extended combat phases requires either avoiding hits or finding a healing source. Magus Replenish provides passive self-healing tied directly to the operator's primary movement action, allowing the operator to recover health during repositioning between amp fire windows.

The percentage-based heal scales with the operator's maximum health pool. Operators who invest in Vazarin school passives or Unairu school bonuses that raise operator survivability receive proportionally larger absolute heals from each Sling at higher health totals. At rank 5, each Void Sling heals 30% of operator maximum health - meaning the operator can fully restore from near-death to full health with as few as four Slings, making this arcane a strong self-sustain option for solo operator play.

## Effect

Each Void Sling triggers a heal equal to a percentage of the operator's current maximum health. At rank 0, 5% per Sling provides minor trickle healing during active operator combat. At rank 5, 30% per Sling allows the operator to sustain through significant incoming damage by healing back over a short sequence of Sling movements.

The heal fires immediately on Void Sling activation, before the Sling completes its travel - each Sling provides the heal regardless of landing position or enemy proximity.

## Builds that use it

Magus Replenish suits operators who engage in extended melee-range combat or operate in areas with frequent operator health damage - Zariman void cascade rotations, Eidolon arenas during limb shoot phases, or Void Cascade missions in Zariman where Void Angels and enemy Void entities frequently target the operator. At rank 5, the 30% heal per Sling allows aggressive play patterns where the operator takes hits during amp fire and recovers health through the Sling movement used to reposition.

Unairu school operators who use Void Blast for armor stripping - an ability that requires close range and puts the operator in hazard zones - benefit from Replenish's healing restoring the health spent closing distance to targets.

## Notes

The heal is based on the operator's maximum health, not the warframe's health pool. Warframe health mods have no effect on the heal amount. Operator maximum health can be increased through Vazarin school upgrades and certain operator suits - investing in higher operator maximum health increases the absolute healing per Sling. The heal does not overheal beyond the operator's maximum health. Multiple Slings in rapid succession provide multiple separate heals as each Sling independently triggers the effect.
