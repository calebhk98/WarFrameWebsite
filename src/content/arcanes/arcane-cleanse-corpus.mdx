## Overview

Arcane Cleanse Corpus automatically removes negative status effects when fighting Corpus enemies, providing passive status immunity that scales from partial to complete protection across its rank range. At maximum rank, the arcane grants 100% cleanse chance, effectively making the warframe immune to Corpus-applied status effects without requiring player action. This is particularly impactful in Corpus content because their most common status types - magnetic (which halves shields and disables shield recharge) and cold (which slows movement) - are among the most disruptive effects in the game for certain warframe builds.

Magnetic procs from Corpus weapons are especially problematic for shield-dependent frames. The status drains shields and prevents natural recharge, creating cascading vulnerability. Arcane Cleanse Corpus negates this threat in Corpus-heavy content, allowing shield-gated frames to maintain their defensive mechanic without the proc breaking their survivability loop. Cold procs from Corpus Arctic Eximus enemies are similarly cleansed, keeping mobility intact during encounters that would otherwise slow the warframe significantly.

## Effect

When the warframe receives a negative status effect from a Corpus enemy, the arcane checks its cleanse chance. On success, the status effect is immediately removed. At rank 0, a 20% chance removes one in five Corpus status applications - providing partial relief during high-volume encounters. At rank 5, 100% chance removes every Corpus-applied status effect, functioning as full immunity to all Corpus status procs.

The cleanse triggers after the status is applied rather than preventing application. At lower ranks, some status effects may briefly apply before being cleansed. At rank 5, the cleanse is functionally immediate enough to prevent the status from affecting gameplay.

## Acquisition

Arcane Cleanse Corpus drops as a rare reward from Zariman Void Cascade waves featuring Corpus enemies. Zariman Ten-Zero content is accessible after completing Angels of the Zariman. Corpus-wave Void Cascade runs provide the targeted farming path. Warframe.market provides an alternative acquisition route for players who prefer direct purchase over farming Corpus-specific rotations.

## Builds that use it

Arcane Cleanse Corpus fits any build that regularly engages Corpus enemies in challenging content. Shield-gated builds - frames relying on Adaptation or brief shield-break invulnerability windows for survivability - benefit from the magnetic immunity at higher ranks, preventing the shield drain that breaks the gating mechanic. Warframes with moderate shield pools in Corpus Steel Path or Corpus Railjack content avoid the compounding vulnerability of repeated magnetic procs through Cleanse Corpus's passive removal. Frames running into Corpus Nullifier bubbles and adjacent status threats simultaneously benefit from the cleanse during those high-pressure windows.

## Notes

The cleanse only affects status effects applied by Corpus faction enemies. Status effects from Grineer, Infested, Corrupted, or Sentient enemies are not cleansed by this arcane. The arcane is faction-specific and most valuable in missions with a high proportion of Corpus enemies - it provides no benefit in mixed-faction missions against non-Corpus sources. Stacking multiple copies of Arcane Cleanse Corpus does not increase the cleanse chance beyond the single-arcane maximum at rank 5. The cleanse is a passive reaction and requires no player input to activate.
