## Overview

Arcane Cleanse Corpus is a faction-specific rare arcane providing status immunity against Corpus enemy types. This specialized arcane enables safe farming in Corpus-dominant tilesets and void cascade waves.

## Effect

When fighting Corpus enemies, the Warframe has a 20% chance at rank 0, scaling to 100% at rank 5, to automatically cleanse negative status effects. At maximum rank, Corpus status procs are permanently prevented.

## Acquisition

Arcane Cleanse Corpus drops as a rare reward from Zariman Void Cascade waves fighting Corpus enemies. Corpus-specific void cascade runs provide access.

## Best on

Arcane Cleanse Corpus is ideal for Corpus-focused content:

- **Corpus void cascades** — maintains full immunity to Corpus procs.
- **Venus farming** — counters Corpus magnetic and cold procs.
- **Mixed-faction runs** — Corpus-wave status immunity maintains sustain.

## Notes

Only affects status applied by Corpus units; Grineer and Infested procs are unaffected. Farming requires selective enemy types. Stacks additively with status-resistance mods.
