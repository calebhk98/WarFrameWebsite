## Overview

Magus Accelerant reduces enemy resistance to heat damage whenever the operator uses Void Sling. This debuff lowers the effective armor or innate heat resistance that enemies carry, allowing the operator's amp fire, Warframe heat-damage abilities, and allied weapons dealing heat damage to deal more output against the debuffed target. Because heat damage is a common secondary damage type on many weapon builds -- via fire mods, gas combination, or Ignis-class weapons -- Accelerant extends its utility beyond the operator alone to the broader squad.

Void Sling is the primary operator movement ability, used constantly during repositioning, gap closing, and escape from dangerous situations. Accelerant converts every Sling into a passive debuff application without requiring dedicated targeting or button inputs beyond the standard movement action. The operator's mobility routine doubles as offensive support.

## Effect

Each Void Sling applies a heat resistance reduction to enemies near the landing point. At rank 0, the 12% reduction provides a modest bonus to heat-damage dealers in the squad. At rank 5, a 65% reduction significantly lowers enemy effective heat resistance, amplifying the damage output of all heat-damage sources active in the encounter for the duration of the debuff window.

The debuff targets enemies in the vicinity of the Void Sling destination. Operators who Sling into or through enemy clusters debuff multiple targets simultaneously, enabling heat-damage dealing squadmates to capitalize on the reduced resistance across the group.

## Builds that use it

Magus Accelerant suits operators supporting heat-damage-centric squad compositions. Squads running Saryn -- whose Miasma applies heat and toxin procs -- benefit from the heat resistance reduction during the damage phase. Operators in Zariman content who Sling frequently through enemy clusters apply the debuff repeatedly as part of their normal movement, enabling heat-focused weapon builds among squadmates to see improved performance without dedicated debuff rotations.

Solo operators running heat-damage amps or warframes with heat abilities like Ember also benefit, as each Sling passively softens incoming targets before the Warframe phase ability rotation begins.

## Notes

The heat resistance reduction applies to the damage type specifically -- it does not affect corrosive, toxin, or other adjacent damage types even when combined into gas or viral. The debuff window is tied to the Sling event and applies to enemies within range at the destination; enemies outside the Sling radius do not receive the debuff. Multiple Slings against the same enemy group refresh or stack the debuff depending on game implementation -- frequent Slings sustain the heat vulnerability across extended combat phases.
