## Overview

Magus Lockdown is an Operator arcane that provides crowd control during combat by placing a tethering mine whenever the operator performs a Void Sling. The mine immobilizes enemies in its radius for 4 seconds, preventing them from moving or attacking. This arcane transforms the operator's mobility tool into a tactical crowd-control instrument, especially valuable in high-difficulty missions where enemy positioning determines survival. Unlike warframe-based crowd control abilities, Lockdown operates independently from the warframe's ability system, providing supplementary CC that does not consume the warframe's energy pool.

The arcane was significantly rebalanced in Update 27.3.0 to remove its direct damage output, converting it into a pure control tool. This change reflects its intended role as a support utility rather than a primary damage source. Even with this nerf, Magus Lockdown remains among the most reliable operator-level crowd control options available, especially when paired with void mode arcanes and defensive operator builds. The mine's placement at the operator's location means precise positioning determines how many enemies are tethered in each cast.

The 10-enemy limit per mine means heavily spawned mission types (Survival, Void Fissure) may not immobilize every threat in range. Tethered enemies cannot move but can still fire weapons, making it a positioning tool rather than invulnerability. Multiple mines stacked via quick Void Slings affect separate groups, allowing skilled operators to control larger areas through chaining.

## Acquisition

Magus Lockdown is purchased from Vox Solaris, an operator-focused syndicate faction based in Fortuna on Venus. Reaching Rank 3 (Conductor) in Vox Solaris with Standing allows direct purchase from the vendor. Alternative paths include buying pre-ranked copies from player traders on the market, which often cost 50--120 platinum depending on rank. Initial acquisition requires substantial Plains of Eidolon progression and void-fissure farming to accumulate the 15,000 Standing needed for purchase.

Once obtained, Lockdown ranks quickly compared to other arcanes and requires no special resources beyond standing. The arcane's utility in operator-focused content (Zariman, Void Cascade) makes it valuable for endgame players, ensuring steady demand on trading markets.

## Best on

Operator builds centered on void mode and mobility synergies pair naturally with Magus Lockdown. Operators equipped with Zenurik focus school gains movement speed during Void Mode, allowing rapid repositioning to chain tethering mines across incoming enemy waves. Support-focused operator builds leverage Lockdown's CC while deploying Void Mode healing via Magus Repair or Void Strike damage buffs. Solo endgame players find the arcane invaluable for kiting dangerous enemy types (Nullifier Crewmen, Ancient Healers) away from their warframes.

Conversely, operator builds designed purely for amp damage may deprioritize Lockdown in favor of offensive arcanes like Virtuos Strike or Virtuos Shadow, which directly scale amp output.

## Builds that use it

Zariman void cascade builds almost universally pack Magus Lockdown as a primary crowd control layer, enabling wave clear without relying on warframe abilities. Defection and mobile-mission builds employ Lockdown to create safe corridors for mission objectives. Elite Sanctuary Onslaught builds often include Lockdown alongside Void Mode arcanes (Repair, Replenish) to maintain consistent enemy control while sustaining squad survivability. Farming builds leverage the arcane to trivialize enemy encounters, freeing the warframe to focus on resource gathering rather than combat.

## Notes

Magus Lockdown mines cannot be stacked indefinitely; only one mine persists at a time per operator. The 4-second tether duration allows for smooth rotation if the operator casts another Void Sling before the previous mine expires, creating overlapping control zones. Boss enemies and certain high-tier enemies (Condor Dropships, Eidolons) are immune to the tether effect entirely, limiting the arcane's application in endgame boss encounters. The arcane triggers on any Void Sling cast, regardless of distance traveled, meaning immobile operators can deploy mines repeatedly from one location.
