## Overview

Primary Obstruct rewards precision headshots with stacking armor bonuses, creating a feedback loop where accurate fire builds defensive survivability. The stacking mechanic means that each headshot contributes to increasing total defense, incentivizing continuous aimed fire rather than sporadic headshots. At maximum four stacks, the armor bonus is substantial enough to significantly increase physical damage mitigation, particularly for frames with moderate-to-high base armor values.

The 18-second stack duration is deliberately generous, allowing players to maintain stacks through brief repositioning phases, reloads, or ability casts without losing accumulated defensive value. Builds that perform headshots consistently through sustained aimed fire rarely see their stacks expire during normal combat engagement.

## Effect

Each headshot from the primary weapon applies one armor stack. Up to four stacks accumulate, each providing the per-rank armor bonus for 18 seconds. At rank 0, each stack grants 20% armor (maximum 80% at four stacks); at rank 5, each stack grants 100% armor (maximum 400% at four stacks). For a frame with 300 base armor, four stacks at rank 5 results in an effective 1500 armor total, providing substantial damage reduction that compounds with Steel Fiber and other armor mods.

The 18-second duration per stack allows comfortable uptime maintenance during standard combat; only sustained breaks from headshot activity (extended reloads, ability-only phases) cause stacks to decay.

## Acquisition

Primary Obstruct drops from Zariman Ten-Zero missions and Void Cascade content. The arcane is accessible through standard Zariman progression after completing Angels of the Zariman. Regular Void Cascade runs generate Obstruct through rotation rewards. Precision-primary players often farm Zariman content for Obstruct and complementary headshot arcanes simultaneously. Warframe.market provides direct purchase options for targeted acquisition.

## Builds that use it

Primary Obstruct benefits precision-primary frames with high-armor builds. Sniper rifles and semi-auto primaries that naturally aim for headshots (the optimal shot placement for damage on most enemies) generate stacks without changing playstyle. High-armor frames like Valkyr, Inaros, and Atlas gain the greatest absolute armor value from each stack's percentage bonus. Builds using Covert Lethality in tandem with precision primary fire create dual defensive contributions -- the mod stacks with Obstruct's armor bonus. Steel Path farming where physical damage from Grineer enemies relies heavily on armor mitigation extracts the most defensive value from maximum Obstruct stacks.

## Notes

Headshot detection requires the projectile to hit the designated head hitbox. Explosive weapons and AoE damage do not count as headshots regardless of epicenter location. The four-stack cap prevents indefinite stacking; builds must consistently land headshots but cannot exceed maximum bonus. The armor bonus is a percentage of base armor, not a flat value; frames with 0 or near-0 base armor gain no benefit. Each stack resets its own 18-second timer on new headshot triggers, maintaining maximum stacks through continuous accurate fire.
