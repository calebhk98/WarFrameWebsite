## Overview

Primary Obstruct rewards precision headshots with defensive scaling. On every headshot kill, the arcane grants an armor buff that stacks up to four times. This arcane benefits sniper rifles and precision primary weapons. Early ranks provide 20% armor scaling; maximum rank yields 100% armor per stack for 400% total bonus at maximum stacks.
