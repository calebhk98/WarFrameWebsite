## Overview

Arcane Bullet Jump enhances the warframe's vertical mobility by granting increased jump height after each bullet jump, creating an escalating mobility advantage for players who chain bullet jumps as part of their traversal pattern. Bullet jumping -- the combined crouch-then-jump parkour action -- is the primary fast-traversal movement in Warframe, and it naturally repeats throughout most missions during navigation. Bullet Jump arcane converts this common action into a consistent jump height bonus without requiring altered movement behavior.

The 6-second duration per trigger is long enough to cover most active parkour sequences where a player chains multiple bullet jumps in succession. Repeated bullet jumps within the 6-second window refresh the timer, maintaining the height bonus as long as the player continues active parkour movement. This makes Arcane Bullet Jump effectively passive for players who naturally traverse using bullet jump chains.

## Effect

Each bullet jump triggers the jump height bonus for 6 seconds. At rank 0, +5 meters of additional jump height provides a noticeable vertical boost -- reaching higher platforms or clearing obstacles that would require air rolls or additional jumps without the arcane. At rank 5, +25 meters of additional jump height transforms the warframe's vertical reach substantially, allowing access to elevated positions far above standard jump trajectory ranges and enabling extended air time during aerial combat positioning.

The height bonus applies to the upward velocity component of bullet jumps and subsequent aerial jumps within the buff window.

## Acquisition

Arcane Bullet Jump drops as an uncommon reward from Void Fissure endless mission rotations. Endless Void Fissure missions like Survival, Defense, and Interception provide Bullet Jump as part of their rotation reward pools. The arcane is accessible through standard Void Fissure content at earlier game progression stages without requiring advanced Zariman or Duviri content. Warframe.market provides an alternative purchase route for players who prefer platinum acquisition.

## Builds that use it

Arcane Bullet Jump suits mobility-focused builds and frames that rely on aerial positioning as part of their combat pattern. Zephyr builds that spend extended time in the air benefit from the height bonus to reach elevated positions for Tornado and Dive Bomb setups. Titania in Razorwing mode uses different flight mechanics and benefits less, but standard Titania builds using bullet jumps for evasion gain the height bonus during ground combat transitions. Warframe builds on large tilesets like Orokin Tower, Corpus Gas City, and open plains where vertical traversal significantly affects combat position benefit from the jump height for route optimization.

Builds running Arcane Agility or other speed-based arcanes stack with Bullet Jump's height bonus to create fast, high-mobility traversal patterns that cover ground quickly during navigation phases of longer missions.

## Notes

The jump height bonus applies to vertical velocity and does not affect lateral movement distance during bullet jumps. Archwing jumps and operator bullet jumps use different movement systems and do not interact with this arcane. The buff refreshes on each bullet jump within the duration window -- chain bullet jumps maintain the bonus indefinitely. The 25-meter height increase at rank 5 is substantial enough to reach most in-game ceiling positions and cross gaps that would otherwise require multiple movement inputs. Jump mods in the Warframe mod pool (Power Drift) stack additively with the arcane's height bonus.
