## Overview

Arcane Bullet Jump is a mobility-focused Warframe arcane that grants vertical height bonuses following bullet jumps. This arcane rewards parkour-heavy playstyles by enabling access to elevated positions and extended evasion angles during combat.

## Effect

Each bullet jump grants increased jump height for 6 seconds. The bonus scales from +5 meters at rank 0 to +25 meters at rank 5, enabling Warframes to reach otherwise inaccessible vantage points and execute rapid aerial repositioning.

## Acquisition

Arcane Bullet Jump drops as an uncommon reward from Void Fissure endless missions. Early void fissure progression grants reliable access.

## Best on

Arcane Bullet Jump enhances parkour-focused builds:

- **Mobility Warframes** (Zephyr, Titania, Wukong) maximize vertical traversal.
- **Platforming-heavy tilesets** — enables shortcuts and upper-level access.
- **Defensive positioning** — extra height enables easier evasion angles.

## Notes

The height bonus stacks with existing jump-height mods. Frequent bullet jumping maintains near-permanent uptime. Stacks multiplicatively with movement-speed modifiers for enhanced traversal speed.
