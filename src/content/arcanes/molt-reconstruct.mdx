## Overview

Molt Reconstruct heals the warframe and all allies within Affinity Range proportional to the energy cost of each ability cast. Each energy point spent on the initial cast cost of an ability returns health to the caster and nearby allies -- 1 health per energy at rank 0, 6 health per energy at rank 5. Sustained ability casters on energy-rich builds generate significant cumulative healing across a mission as a byproduct of normal ability use, converting energy expenditure into a passive heal that supports both the player and squadmates.

The Affinity Range -- the standard radius used for affinity sharing and many area-of-effect mechanics in Warframe -- defines the distribution zone. Allies within this radius receive the healing simultaneously with the caster. This makes Molt Reconstruct a passive team support arcane that scales with the caster's ability rotation frequency.

At maximum rank, the arcane also grants one Arcane Revive charge per mission.

## Effect

The heal fires on the initial cast cost of each ability -- not on toggle drain, but on the upfront energy payment when the ability is first activated. An ability costing 75 energy generates 75 health at rank 1, or 450 health at rank 5, distributed to all nearby allies simultaneously. Abilities with high base energy costs provide larger heals per cast.

At rank 5, a warframe casting a 100-energy ability heals 600 health to everyone in range. Frames that cast multiple high-cost abilities per minute can generate several thousand health in healing over a 5-minute mission segment.

## Builds that use it

Molt Reconstruct suits high-energy-expenditure frames that cast abilities frequently and are played in squad content where allies benefit from passive healing. Saryn builds that cast Spore, Molt, Miasma, and Contagion regularly generate healing from each ability cast. Octavia builds casting Mallet, Resonator, and Metronome provide healing pulses to squadmates each time an ability activates. Hildryn, whose abilities convert shields to energy-equivalent costs, may interact with Reconstruct differently depending on implementation.

Support frames like Wisp or Trinity that cast multiple buffing abilities throughout a mission contribute healing as a byproduct of their support rotation -- each buff cast heals nearby allies without the caster dedicating attention to healing.

## Notes

The heal scales with the initial casting cost of the ability -- this is the base energy cost before efficiency mods reduce it. High efficiency builds that reduce ability costs to minimal values also reduce the per-cast healing, as less energy is technically spent. This means Molt Reconstruct performs better on builds with moderate efficiency rather than maximum efficiency reduction. The heal does not apply to the channel drain of toggle abilities -- only the initial activation payment counts. Multiple squadmates each running Molt Reconstruct provide independent healing events per ability cast by each individual, potentially stacking significant total healing in ability-heavy groups.
