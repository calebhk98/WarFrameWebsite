## Overview

Conjunction Voltage provides stacking Reload Speed and Multishot bonuses that accumulate each time the weapon inflicts an Electricity Status effect. Each Electric proc adds one stack lasting 12 seconds, up to a maximum of 40 stacks. With 40 stacks maintained at rank 5, the total bonus reaches +60% Reload Speed and +120% Multishot -- a substantial combined output increase that fundamentally changes the weapon's firing rhythm.

The stacking mechanic rewards sustained Electricity proc generation. Weapons with high status chance and Electric or combined-Electric element (Corrosive, Magnetic, Radiation) apply Electricity procs frequently, building stacks rapidly and maintaining them throughout active combat.

## Effect

Each Electricity Status proc applied by the weapon adds one stack of Conjunction Voltage, refreshing the 12-second stack timer. At rank 0, each stack provides +0.25% Reload Speed and +0.5% Multishot. At rank 5, each stack provides +1.5% Reload Speed and +3% Multishot. At maximum 40 stacks at rank 5, total bonuses reach +60% Reload Speed and +120% Multishot. The 12-second stack duration means each individual stack expires 12 seconds after its last application; sustained Electricity proc application keeps stacks refreshed and at or near the 40-stack cap.

Multishot adds additional projectiles per trigger pull -- each additional projectile fires independently, applying damage and status effects. At +120% Multishot, the weapon fires more than double the base projectile count per shot, multiplying all per-projectile effects including status application probability.

## Acquisition

Conjunction Voltage drops from Zariman Void Fissure rewards at 4% and Void Cascade rotation C at 2%. Access requires completing Angels of the Zariman. Rotation C in Void Cascade requires extended runs to reach; supplementing with Void Fissure sessions targeting Conjunction Voltage drops improves overall acquisition rate. Warframe.market provides direct purchase for players who prefer platinum acquisition.

## Builds that use it

Conjunction Voltage is designed for high-status weapons that apply Electricity procs consistently. Weapons that combine Electricity elements (Corrosive = Electricity + Toxin, Magnetic = Electricity + Cold) apply Electricity procs along with their combined element procs, generating stacks from regular fire. High-status-chance rifles, shotguns, and secondaries with Electric element mods apply many Electricity procs per magazine, building stacks quickly.

The Multishot bonus at 40 stacks provides the greatest practical benefit on weapons where additional projectiles multiply damage output -- shotguns with pellet spread, multi-shot rifles, and bows that benefit from additional arrows. The Reload Speed bonus provides the most value on weapons with slow base reload times, reducing magazine downtime during sustained combat.

## Notes

Stacks expire 12 seconds after the last application, not 12 seconds after the buff was triggered. In active combat with regular Electricity proc application, stacks maintain themselves indefinitely up to the 40-stack cap. Each individual Electricity proc from a single shot that hits multiple pellets counts as separate proc events if the status checks pass independently per pellet. Stacks from Conjunction Voltage are weapon-specific to the weapon that applied the procs; switching weapons does not transfer stacks.
