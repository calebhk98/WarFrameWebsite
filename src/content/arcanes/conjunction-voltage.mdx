## Overview

Conjunction Voltage chains damage through enemies on electrical status procs, making it one of the few weapon arcanes that converts a single-target proc into group damage. Electric status in Warframe already shocks the primary target and one nearby enemy; Conjunction Voltage extends this by chaining the proc to multiple additional targets when the trigger activates. At rank 5, each electric proc reaches up to six additional targets for a 90% damage bonus, enabling electrically-focused secondary builds to clear groups without relying solely on AoE weapon mechanics.

The arcane suits secondaries that apply electric status quickly - through high status chance on electric-damage weapons or through elemental combination builds. Because electric status triggers from any two elemental mods producing Electric damage, Conjunction Voltage works with a range of status-focused modding approaches rather than requiring a dedicated electric weapon.

## Effect

Each electric status proc from the secondary weapon checks the trigger chance. On success, the proc chains to additional targets within range, applying damage and electric stagger to each chained enemy. The damage value is a percentage of the original proc damage, not a flat value, so higher base damage weapons produce stronger chain hits. At maximum rank, six additional targets take 90% of the original electric proc damage. Chain range is fixed and not modified by Power Range or weapon range mods.

The chaining distinguishes Conjunction Voltage from simple damage-boost arcanes: it adds target coverage rather than increasing per-enemy damage. Against single isolated targets the arcane provides no benefit; grouped enemies in void fissure corridors or mobile defense clusters maximize chain value.

## Acquisition

Conjunction Voltage drops from Zariman Void Fissure missions at 4% and Void Cascade rotation C at 2%. Zariman content is the primary farming source; players running daily Void Cascade completions encounter this arcane through rotation C drops. The 4% Void Fissure rate places it alongside other Zariman arcanes of similar tier. Warframe.market listings provide direct acquisition for players prioritizing time efficiency over plat conservation.

## Builds that use it

Secondaries with high electric status application gain the most from Conjunction Voltage. Weapons with electric elemental combinations apply procs quickly enough to trigger Conjunction Voltage consistently in dense enemy groups. Builds pairing the arcane with status-chance mods (Pistol Pestilence, Pathogen Rounds combined with electric) reach reliable proc rates in mid-tier content. The arcane works alongside speed-enhancing arcanes to maintain fire rate between chain procs. Squad play benefits from chain coverage extending reach into areas other players are not targeting.

## Notes

Conjunction Voltage only triggers on electric status procs, not on other elemental procs even if the weapon also deals electric damage. The chain radius is approximate and may not reach enemies behind cover or at significant elevation differences. Chained targets receive the electric stagger effect, helping control enemy movement in open areas. The damage percentage scales from base proc damage before other multipliers apply; critical hits on the initial proc do not affect chain damage directly.
