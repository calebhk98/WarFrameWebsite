## Overview

Fractalized Reset grants a Reload Speed bonus to the equipped weapon on Ability Cast. Every time the warframe activates an ability, the weapon gains accelerated reload speed for 5 seconds. The trigger links ability casting -- a near-universal combat action for ability-focused builds -- to weapon reload efficiency, allowing active ability users to maintain faster weapon cycling throughout combat.

At rank 5, each ability cast triggers +240% Reload Speed for 5 seconds. A weapon with a 3-second base reload reduces to under 1 second during the buff window, enabling rapid re-engagement after magazine depletion for the duration of the bonus.

## Effect

Each ability cast triggers the Fractalized Reset bonus, applying the listed Reload Speed increase to the equipped weapon for 5 seconds. At rank 0, +40% Reload Speed provides a modest acceleration to reload animations. At rank 5, +240% Reload Speed cuts most reload animations to a fraction of their base duration, making weapon reload essentially seamless during the buff window after each ability activation.

Ability-active builds that cast frequently maintain near-constant Reload Speed uptime, since ability casts during the 5-second window trigger fresh Fractalized Reset bonus windows. The bonus applies to any equipped weapon that has a reload mechanic -- rifles, shotguns, pistols in the secondary slot when Fractalized Reset is applied to a secondary weapon arcane slot.

## Acquisition

Fractalized Reset drops from Zariman Void Fissure rewards at 4% and Void Cascade rotation A at 2%. Both sources are available through standard Zariman Ten-Zero content after completing Angels of the Zariman. Void Cascade rotation A appears in the first reward cycle, making Fractalized Reset accessible in shorter Void Cascade sessions. Warframe.market provides direct purchase options for players seeking to bypass farming.

## Builds that use it

Fractalized Reset benefits ability-active builds that regularly cast warframe abilities during weapon combat. Frames that interleave ability casts with weapon fire -- casting a buff or utility ability then switching back to weapon fire, cycling through ability rotations between reload pauses -- naturally trigger Fractalized Reset on each ability activation. Builds on Nidus, Mesa, or Saryn that cast abilities in regular patterns maintain near-constant Reload Speed uptime given their high ability cast frequency.

The arcane is most effective on weapons with relatively slow base reload speeds where the +240% bonus produces the largest absolute reduction in reload time. Sniper Rifles with 3-4 second reloads, pump-action shotguns, or any weapon where reload downtime noticeably interrupts combat cadence benefit most from Fractalized Reset's window.

## Notes

The trigger fires on any ability cast, including ability toggle activations and brief one-shot activations. Channeled ability toggles count at the moment of activation, not continuously during the drain. The 5-second duration is short; builds that cast infrequently may find the bonus expires before the next weapon reload is needed. Builds casting abilities at least every 5 seconds maintain near-continuous Reload Speed coverage. The Reload Speed bonus is additive with reload speed mods from the weapon configuration; both contributions stack to the total reload acceleration.
