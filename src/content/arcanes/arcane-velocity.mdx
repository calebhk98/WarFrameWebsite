## Overview

Arcane Velocity is an uncommon offensive arcane that grants pistol fire rate scaling on critical hits. The 90% trigger chance combined with the 9-second duration creates highly consistent uptime on crit-heavy secondary weapon builds, particularly dual-wield pistols and high-fire-rate sidearms. A max-rank Velocity proc grants +120% fire rate to pistols, more than doubling the weapon's attack speed for 9 seconds. This transforms rapid-fire secondaries from utility tools into endgame-viable damage sources, especially when paired with status-application builds (Tysis, Zakti) where fire-rate scaling increases proc frequency and damage output.

Velocity's primary strength is multiplicative fire-rate stacking with weapon mods and warframe abilities. Frames with inherent fire-rate bonuses (Gyre's Cantata, Wisp's passive projectile speed buff) compound with Velocity to create extreme attack-speed multipliers. The arcane's high trigger rate (90% on crits) and relatively short cooldown (9 seconds vs. 18 seconds for other offensive arcanes) enable near-perpetual buff windows during sustained pistol phases. For weapon-swap builds and secondary-focused playstyles, Velocity rivals Precision as a high-uptime damage multiplier.

The 90% crit-trigger chance is the highest of any arcane in the warframe category, ensuring near-guaranteed uptime on high-crit-chance weapons. Pistols with base crit chance of 20%+ trigger Velocity multiple times per second during sustained fire, creating overlapping buffs that push fire rate to extreme levels. A weapon with 15 base fire rate and +120% Velocity boost reaches 33 rounds per second, effectively tripling damage output during the buff window.

## Acquisition

Arcane Velocity drops from Eidolon Hydrolyst capture at 16.79% chance and extended Hydrolyst fights at 22.23% chance--the highest drop rate for any warframe arcane from Eidolons. The Zariman void skirmish rotation C also drops Velocity at 5.63%, providing an alternate source for players preferring void fissure farming. The Uncommon rarity means leveling costs are minimal, and trading-post prices are among the lowest for any arcane.

Cost to acquire a single max-rank Velocity typically ranges from 5--20 platinum on the trading post, making it one of the most affordable arcanes to obtain, even for new players with limited trading history.

## Best on

Arcane Velocity excels on high-crit-chance pistol-focused builds where secondary weapons are primary or co-primary damage sources. Frames like Mesa (Peacemaker with high crit scaling), Mag (with magnetize secondary scaling), Titania (with rapid-fire projectiles), and gunslinger-oriented warframes leverage Velocity to enable sustained burst damage phases. Dual-wield and rapid-fire secondary builds (Twin Wraiths, Aklex Prime, Zakti Prime with status application) maximize Velocity uptime by landing consistent crits during fire chains. Weapon-swap builds alternate between primaries and secondaries, triggering Velocity during secondary burst phases to maintain near-perpetual attack-speed buffs.

Frames with minimal secondary weapon usage or low-crit-chance sidearms rarely prioritize Velocity, opting instead for more universally applicable arcanes like Energize or Grace.

## Builds that use it

Weapon-swap specialist builds pair Velocity with Precision (damage scaling) or Eruption (crowd control) for multiplicative damage and control across attack phases. Pistol-focused and rapid-fire secondary builds frequently dual-slot Velocity to maximize attack-speed uptime on their primary offense tool. Speedrun builds incorporating secondary weapons use Velocity if the secondary is significant in the kill pattern or dps rotation. Steel Path endgame builds sometimes leverage Velocity as a sustained damage multiplier for avoiding ability-spam playstyles.

## Notes

Velocity's effect applies to all pistol-category weapons, including secondary melee weapons classified as pistols in the game engine. However, the arcane does not affect primary weapons or melee weapons regardless of fire-rate mechanics, limiting its applicability to true secondary-focused builds. Frames that use Mesa's Peacemaker (treated as a primary weapon for arcane purposes in older patches) cannot trigger Velocity from Peacemaker crits, though it will still trigger from other pistol usage. The arcane's restriction to pistols means pure ability-spam or melee-focused builds gain no direct benefit from its installation.

The arcane was rebalanced in Update 24.4.0 to prevent Mesa's Peacemaker from stacking Velocity, ensuring the arcane remained balanced around actual secondary weapon usage rather than exalted weapon interactions.
