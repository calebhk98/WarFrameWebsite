## Overview

Melee Afflictions grants powerful status synergies by applying all damage types on proc. This melee arcane enables status-focused builds to achieve multiplicative status coverage, devastating enemy defenses during void fissure combat.

Simultaneous status applications enable status-duration extensions and combine all elemental effects on single targets, creating devastating crowd control and armor-stripping opportunities.

## Acquisition

Melee Afflictions drops from Zariman Void Fissure (4%) and Void Cascade rotation C (2%). Regular zariman void farming yields this arcane through natural progression.

## Best on

Melee Afflictions excels on high-status melee weapons paired with status-scaling warframes. The comprehensive status coverage enables hybrid damage builds to maximize crowd control and armor stripping during void fissure content.
