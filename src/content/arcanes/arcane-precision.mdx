## Overview

Arcane Precision is a rare offensive arcane that grants secondary weapon damage scaling on headshots. At max rank, landing a headshot grants +300% secondary weapon damage for 18 seconds—a tripling of damage output that transforms secondary weapons from support tools into primary damage sources. The arcane is particularly valuable for weapon-swap builds that alternate between primaries and secondaries, enabling strategic damage phases where headshots trigger Precision buffs that sustain secondary burst damage through subsequent shots.

Precision's design rewards accuracy and positioning: players must land headshots to trigger the buff, making it a skill-expressive arcane that separates optimal play from passive buffing. Unlike Fury (crit-based) or Eruption (automatic on energy pickup), Precision demands player input and positioning discipline. This mechanic makes it invaluable for direct-combat builds where the player is actively aiming weapons, particularly in assassination missions, void fissures, and endgame bounties where headshot opportunities are plentiful.

The 18-second duration allows overlapping Precision procs if the player lands multiple headshots within 18 seconds, creating potential for extended damage windows. Secondary weapons with high rate of fire and precision-friendly scopes (Pox, Twin Vipers Wraith, Zakti Prime) maximize Precision uptime by landing consistent headshots during focused fire phases. Weapon-swap builds that alternate between primaries and secondaries trigger Precision on every primary phase transition, maintaining near-permanent buff status during sustained combat.

## Acquisition

Arcane Precision drops from Eidolon Gantulyst capture at 5.92% chance and extended Gantulyst fights at 7.02% chance, as well as from Zariman void skirmish rotation C at 2.82%. The moderate drop rate places Precision in the accessible tier for Eidolon farming. The Rare rarity means leveling costs and trading-post pricing are moderate compared to legendary arcanes.

Cost to acquire a single max-rank Precision typically ranges from 25--50 platinum on the trading post, making it reasonably affordable for most players with established trading history.

## Best on

Arcane Precision excels on weapon-swap and gunslinger-focused builds where secondary weapon usage is primary or co-primary damage. Frames like Mesa (with Peacemaker scaling), Mag (with magnetize synergy), and frames using Saryn's Venomous Exoskeleton benefit from Precision as a secondary-damage multiplier. Tenno that specialize in accuracy and positioning (speedrunners, challenge-run enthusiasts) leverage Precision to transform secondary weapons into endgame-viable damage sources. Gunslinger builds with dual-wield pistols and frame-specific secondary abilities (Kullervo's melee-secondary hybrid) gain tremendous value from Precision's consistent buff application.

Frames with low secondary weapon usage (pure casters, melee-only builds, ability-spam warframes) rarely prioritize Precision, opting instead for more universally applicable arcanes like Energize or Grace.

## Builds that use it

Weapon-swap specialist builds pair Precision with offensive arcanes like Velocity (fire-rate scaling) or Eruption (crowd control during reload phases) for multiplicative damage and control. Secondary-focused builds (Xorian builds, twin-pistol enthusiasts) frequently dual-slot Precision to maximize secondary burst damage phases. Speedrun builds incorporating secondary weapons use Precision if the secondary is a significant portion of the kill pattern. Crowd-control or pure-ability builds typically omit Precision entirely.

## Notes

Headshots require precise aim and adequate weapon accuracy stats. Weapons with poor accuracy (most shotguns, beam weapons with spread) struggle to land consistent headshots, reducing Precision's practical uptime. Weapons with large reticles, hitscan projectiles, and intrinsic crit scaling (Lato Prime, Telos Akstiletto) maximize Precision synergy by reliably landing headshots. The arcane's requirement for player skill and positioning makes it less universally applicable than passive arcanes like Energize, but its damage output potential in skilled hands rivals Fury for highest damage-scaling arcane.

The headshot mechanic was balanced in Update 27.2.0 to ensure projectile and beam weapons had equitable headshot recognition, preventing weapon-type bias in Precision's utility.
