## Overview

Arcane Protection provides passive resistance to corrosive status effects, reducing the frequency at which corrosive procs successfully apply to the warframe. Corrosive status stacks armor reduction with each application -- up to 26% per stack -- making it one of the most threatening status effects for armor-dependent frames in high-level Grineer and Orokin content. Each corrosive proc that is resisted prevents an armor reduction stack, preserving the warframe's damage mitigation across the encounter.

At rank 5, the 102% resistance chance provides complete immunity to corrosive proc application from external sources. This is particularly impactful for armor-tanking builds -- frames like Valkyr, Inaros, and Rhino rely on their armor values for damage reduction, and corrosive stacks from Grineer enemies can strip this mitigation rapidly in Steel Path content. The bonus Arcane Revive at maximum rank provides an additional emergency self-revive.

## Effect

Arcane Protection is a passive resistance that activates automatically when corrosive status is attempted against the warframe. At rank 0, a 17% resistance chance prevents one in six corrosive procs. At rank 5, 102% resistance provides full immunity to corrosive status from external sources, while the bonus Arcane Revive provides an emergency revive charge.

Corrosive resistance is especially valuable against Grineer Ballista and Bombard units whose weaponry applies corrosive stacks at high frequency.

## Builds that use it

Arcane Protection benefits armor-dependent frames in Grineer content, Orokin Void missions, and Steel Path where corrosive application is frequent and each stack meaningfully reduces damage mitigation. High-armor builds that would otherwise need to manage corrosive strip through other mechanics gain passive protection from the status without dedicating mod slots or ability casts to counteracting the debuff. Solo Steel Path operators facing Grineer units with corrosive weapons particularly benefit from the immunity at rank 5.

## Notes

Corrosive resistance only prevents external enemy sources from applying corrosive status. The player's own weapons and abilities applying corrosive are unaffected. Corrosive immunity does not remove existing corrosive stacks -- only prevents new stacks from applying. Combined with Arcane Guardian or other armor-preservation arcanes, Protection extends the value of armor-based builds in prolonged engagements.
