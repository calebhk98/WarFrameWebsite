## Overview

Arcane Momentum grants a Reload Speed bonus to Sniper Rifles on Critical Hit. When any critical hit lands, there is a 60% chance the arcane activates, providing a substantial reload speed increase for 12 seconds. For Sniper Rifles -- weapons defined by long reload animations that create combat pauses between shots -- reducing reload time directly translates to increased sustained damage output.

At rank 5, a successful trigger provides +150% Reload Speed for 12 seconds. Sniper Rifles with critical hit builds naturally trigger Momentum on high-crit-chance shots, making the bonus available throughout sustained combat as long as critical hits land regularly.

## Effect

Each critical hit with any weapon checks the 60% trigger chance. On success, the equipped Sniper Rifle gains the listed Reload Speed bonus for 12 seconds. The effect applies regardless of which weapon scored the critical hit -- a secondary weapon with a critical hit can activate the bonus that applies to the Sniper Rifle. The 12-second duration is long enough to cover multiple reload cycles on most Sniper Rifles.

At rank 0, +25% Reload Speed shortens reload animations modestly. At rank 5, +150% Reload Speed cuts reload time drastically. For a Sniper Rifle with a 3-second base reload, +150% reduces that to approximately 1.2 seconds, dramatically improving the time between shots in sustained engagements.

## Acquisition

Arcane Momentum drops from Duviri/Endless at Legendary rarity (0.67% Tiers 1 and 3, 4% Tier 9), Skirmish nodes on Pluto, Neptune, and Venus at Uncommon Rotation C (10-10.34%), Eidolon Teralyst (14.95%), and Zariman Ten Zero Void Fissures (9%). The Eidolon Teralyst source provides the highest per-run drop chance and is available nightly during the plains day/night cycle. Zariman Void Fissures offer consistent access for players who prefer non-Eidolon content. Warframe.market provides direct purchase for players who prefer platinum acquisition.

## Builds that use it

Arcane Momentum is built for Sniper Rifle builds that achieve consistent critical hits. Lanka builds on Eidolon hunts combine naturally with Momentum -- Lanka fires in timed shots that charge to high damage with crit multipliers, landing critical hits on every fully charged shot. Vectis Prime with high critical chance mods generates critical hits on most shots, triggering the 60% Momentum proc frequently during sustained fire.

The arcane pairs with Sniper Rifle builds that already use Point Strike, Vital Sense, and high-crit secondaries or melee weapons that generate frequent crits on the side. Frames with innate crit bonus abilities -- Chroma's Vex Armor boosting weapon stats, Mirage's Hall of Mirrors -- extend the critical hit frequency that feeds Momentum procs.

## Notes

The 60% trigger is fixed across all ranks; only the Reload Speed bonus scales with rank. The trigger check applies to critical hits from any weapon in the loadout, not only the Sniper Rifle -- a secondary pistol with high crit chance can serve as a crit generator to keep Momentum active on the equipped Sniper. The bonus is specific to Sniper Rifles; other primary weapon types equipped simultaneously do not receive the reload speed boost. Reloading a Sniper Rifle while the buff is active uses the faster reload time for the buff duration.
