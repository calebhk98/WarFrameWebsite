## Overview

Arcane Momentum specializes in sniper rifle optimization, reducing downtime between precision shots. When landing critical hits with sniper rifles, this arcane has a 60% chance to grant a substantial reload speed bonus lasting 12 seconds. At maximum rank, reload speed increases by 150%, allowing sniper users to fire nearly twice as quickly during the buff window. The 60% trigger rate is exceptionally high compared to other arcanes, ensuring near-permanent uptime when landing consistent critical hits.

Sniper rifles in Warframe occupy a niche role requiring careful aim and positioning. Arcane Momentum removes one of the primary frustrations with the weapon type--lengthy reload cycles--enabling aggressive high-DPS playstyles. The arcane transforms single-shot slowness into sustainable damage output when paired with proper build construction and player skill.

## Acquisition

Arcane Momentum drops from Duviri Zariman endless missions at Tier 1 and Tier 3 as legendary rewards, or from later-game skirmish missions like Pluto and Neptune nodes where it appears as an uncommon drop in Rotation C. The uncommon rarity at skirmish nodes makes Momentum more farmable than other arcanes, with reasonable odds during extended play sessions.

## Best on

Sniper rifles like the Vectis Prime, Lanka, and Kuva Chakkhurr benefit tremendously from Momentum's reload acceleration. Warframes that improve sniper accuracy or critical chance such as Limbo Prime or Ivara Prime synergize well, enabling high crit rates that keep the buff nearly active. Builds pairing Momentum with other precision-based arcanes create all-encompassing headshot specialist configurations.

## Notes

The arcane applies exclusively to sniper rifles and does not affect other primary weapon types. The 60% proc rate is the highest among comparable arcanes, virtually guaranteeing buff uptime for accurate players. The 12-second duration is sufficient for 2--4 reload cycles on most sniper rifles, bridging the gap between critical hits during sustained engagements.

## Sources

Arcane Momentum is accessible through Duviri Zariman endless tiers or Void Cascade skirmish missions at Neptune and Pluto nodes. The uncommon rarity at skirmish nodes makes it one of the more farmable recent-era arcanes, suitable for players focusing on sniper loadouts.
