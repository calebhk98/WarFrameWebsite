## Overview

Exodia Brave is a rare zaw arcane that converts heavy attack kills into energy regeneration, enabling ability-spam playstyles on frames that traditionally rely on external energy support. The stacking mechanic allows up to three simultaneous buffs, creating aggressive energy economy systems where sustained heavy attack chains provide quasi-infinite ability spam windows. This arcane fundamentally enables all-ability builds on energy-hungry frames by shifting the energy-sustain burden from mod loadout to melee execution, freeing mod slots for damage or utility.

Brave's strength emerges in frames with high-damage heavy attacks or ability-scaling heavy attack builds (Xaku, Ash with Shuriken finishers, Mirage). The stackable nature means sustained combat against groups creates permanent uptime; a player landing heavy attack kills every 4 seconds maintains three stacks indefinitely. This passive energy sustain bypasses traditional Primed Flow and Streamline calculations, enabling pure damage builds without energy conservation mods.

## Effect

Exodia Brave activates whenever a zaw's heavy attack kills an enemy. Heavy attacks in Warframe cost energy proportional to combo multiplier; channeled finishers and heavy attack combos both qualify. The triggered buff grants flat energy regeneration per second, not percentage-based recovery. At rank 3, each stack adds 5 energy per second; three active stacks provide 15 energy per second sustained regeneration. On frames with 100-point energy pools, this translates to full recharge every 6--7 seconds without any other energy generation sources.

Stacking works independently: each heavy attack kill refreshes the timer on one stack or adds a new stack if fewer than three are active. Killing three enemies with heavy attacks within the buff window creates a permanent regeneration state that persists until combat ends or no kills occur within a 4-second window.

## Acquisition

Exodia Brave, like Might, is available directly from Hok the zaw vendor in Cetus upon reaching Trusted status with the Ostron faction. This accessibility makes Brave an early-accessible zaw arcane for players breaking into endgame content. No farming or boss fights required; pure standing grind through bounties and missions provides reliable acquisition. The arcane's commonality means trading post prices remain low.

Ranking to tier 3 takes 72 hours of mastering. Brave's early availability and moderate trading value make it one of the most-equipped zaw arcanes across the player base, particularly in energy-hungry ability builds.

## Best on

Exodia Brave excels on frames with high-damage heavy attacks or ability-centric damage dealers that use heavy attacks as tactical tools. Xaku's The Vast Untime heavy attacks scale with Ability Strength, making each kill trigger massive energy stacks. Ash's Shuriken finishers and Excalibur builds using Exalted Blade heavy attacks create sustained energy loops enabling rapid ability casting. Lavos pairs exceptionally with Brave because Transference finishers proc heavy attack kills while enabling operator mobility.

Frames with low innate energy pools (Wisp, Saryn, Octavia) benefit most from Brave's passive regeneration because every 5 energy per second contributes meaningfully to combat sustain. Frames with high energy costs (Mag, Ember) rely on external sustain sources; Brave replaces mod slots, enabling pure damage builds.

## Builds that use it

Heavy attack-focused builds pair Brave with heavy attack damage mods (Pressure Point, Sacrificial Steel, Bloodletting) to maximize kill frequency. Ability-spam builds use Brave to remove energy management constraints, freeing mod slots for Strength, Duration, or Range. Energy-independent builds leverage Brave's regeneration to enable full-DPS loadouts without Primed Flow or Transient Fortitude sacrifices. Support frames use Brave zaws to maintain personal energy independence while prioritizing team utility abilities.

Steel Path endless survival builds often pair Brave with Exodia Might or Contagion to create multi-tool zaws that sustain both health and energy simultaneously. Hybrid offense/defense builds exploit the stacking mechanic to achieve permanent regeneration during any extended fight with killable enemies.

## Notes

Exodia Brave's energy rate is flat regeneration, not percentage-based recovery; a frame with 500 maximum energy gains identical regeneration to one with 100 max energy per stack. Stacks refresh independently; killing one enemy every 4 seconds maintains one permanent stack. The stackable limit of 3 prevents overflow exploitation but means massive overkill during high-kill-rate content. Update 23.0.2 fixed interactions with channeled finishers to ensure Brave activates on all heavy attack kill scenarios. Players should prioritize fast-clearing heavy attack builds to maximize proc frequency.
