## Overview

Eternal Onslaught is an operator arcane that grants Critical Chance when the operator's energy is fully depleted. Running the operator's energy pool to zero triggers the arcane, providing a critical chance bonus for 8 seconds -- converting an energy crisis into a temporary offensive advantage. Operators who intentionally drain their energy through sustained ability use or amp fire trigger the bonus consistently, creating a playstyle that cycles from full energy to depletion for each bonus activation.

At rank 5, Energy Depleted triggers +180% Critical Chance for 8 seconds. On an amp with 15% base critical chance, this bonus adds 180 percentage points to reach 195% critical chance -- exceeding the first tier of orange crits and approaching consistent orange critical hits on amp fire during the window.

## Effect

When the operator's energy pool reaches zero from depletion, Eternal Onslaught activates and applies the listed Critical Chance bonus for 8 seconds. At rank 0, +30% Critical Chance provides a small improvement to amp critical output. At rank 5, +180% Critical Chance dramatically shifts amp critical performance for the 8-second window, turning most amp shots into critical hits.

The energy depletion trigger is specific: energy must be depleted from active use (ability drain, amp fire cost) rather than reaching zero from initial entry into operator mode. Operators who enter with full energy must actively spend it down to zero to trigger Onslaught. After triggering, the energy pool begins natural regeneration; depleting to zero again during the window can reset the buff timer.

## Acquisition

Eternal Onslaught drops from Zariman Void Cascade rewards at 4% and Void Fissure rotation B at 2%. Both sources are part of Zariman Ten-Zero content, accessible after completing Angels of the Zariman. Void Cascade at 4% is the primary farming route -- extended Void Cascade runs naturally accumulate Onslaught copies over multiple sessions. Warframe.market provides an alternative for players who prefer to purchase rather than run multiple Zariman sessions.

## Builds that use it

Eternal Onslaught fits operator builds designed around energy expenditure cycles. Operators who chain Void Slings, activate Focus abilities, and fire amp bursts deplete energy quickly, triggering Onslaught frequently if the cycle reaches zero. The bonus Critical Chance amplifies amp damage during the depletion window -- coupling high-crit-damage amp configurations with Onslaught's crit chance boost produces elevated critical hit damage during the 8-second activation.

Madurai school operators using Void Radiance drain energy quickly through ability activation, pushing toward depletion faster than passive energy use alone. Combining Onslaught with an amp that already has meaningful base critical chance ensures the bonus pushes into red crit territory at rank 5.

## Notes

The trigger fires at the moment energy reaches exactly zero from active depletion -- not when energy is low, but specifically at the depletion event. Operators who stop spending energy just above zero without depleting do not trigger Onslaught. Natural energy regeneration during operator mode restores energy without preventing the trigger; the trigger is tied to the depletion event, not to the energy level sustained afterward. The critical chance bonus applies to all amp fire during the 8-second window. Re-depleting energy within the active window resets the timer, allowing extended windows during sustained combat that repeatedly drains to zero.
