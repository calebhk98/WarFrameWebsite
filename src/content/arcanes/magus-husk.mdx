## Overview

Magus Husk grants the warframe a flat armor bonus each time the operator performs Transference Out - the action of exiting operator mode and returning to the warframe. This bonus activates at the moment of return, buffing the warframe's armor value for the re-entry phase when the player resumes warframe combat. Warframes that rely on armor for damage reduction gain direct defensive benefit from each operator cycling event.

Armor in Warframe reduces physical damage taken by a percentage determined by the armor value and the damage type hitting the frame. Flat armor additions benefit frames with moderate base armor more proportionally than frames with very high or very low base armor - adding 300 armor to a frame with 100 base armor triples the armor value, while the same addition to a frame with 1000 base armor increases it by 30%. Magus Husk scales better on lower-armor frames that lack dedicated armor mods.

## Effect

At rank 0, +50 armor on Transference Out provides a minor defensive bump when returning to the warframe. At rank 5, +300 armor provides a substantial flat increase, meaningfully raising effective health against physical damage types for the return-to-warframe phase. The armor bonus applies immediately on transference out and persists during the warframe phase.

The effect triggers on every Transference Out event - operators who cycle frequently between warframe and operator modes refresh the armor addition regularly, maintaining elevated armor across the mission.

## Builds that use it

Magus Husk suits transference-active warframe builds where the warframe has moderate base armor and benefits from additional flat values - Oberon, Nidus, Nezha - frames that lack dedicated armor mods but spend operator phases actively. Builds that cycle operator mode frequently for other arcane procs or Focus school abilities naturally trigger Husk on each return, accumulating defensive value as a byproduct of normal gameplay.

Frames building toward high-armor combinations via Adaptation and Iron Skin mechanics receive the Husk bonus as an additional layer on top of existing armor investment. The flat value matters most in mid-armor builds where each increment of armor still provides meaningful damage reduction scaling.

## Notes

The armor bonus is a flat addition rather than a percentage increase - it adds directly to the warframe's armor value before the armor-to-damage-reduction conversion applies. This is distinct from percentage armor mods like Steel Fiber, which multiply the base armor value before the flat additions. The bonus does not persist through death - if the warframe is downed and revived, the armor returns to base until the next Transference Out refreshes it. The bonus applies to the warframe, not the operator - operator combat is unaffected by this arcane.
