## Overview

Melee Vortex is a melee weapon arcane that creates crowd-control opportunities through magnetic status effects. When eliminating enemies afflicted with magnetic status, Melee Vortex triggers a 45% chance at max rank to pull all nearby enemies within 18 meters into melee range. The arcane transforms magnetic damage applications into positioning tools, enabling aggressive melee playstyles by automatically clustering enemies. The mechanic rewards status-focused melee builds and synergizes with magnetic damage applications from mods or frames.

The pull range remains fixed at 18 meters across all ranks, with trigger chance scaling from 20% to 45%. The mechanic applies magnetic status requirement independently, requiring either melee weapons with magnetic damage or status spreads from frames. Each pull trigger clusters enemies into melee range, creating opportunities for area-of-effect ability rotations and crowd-focused weapon chains.

## Acquisition

Melee Vortex drops from Zariman void fissure missions as a moderately rare melee arcane. The arcane requires melee weapons with magnetic damage components or magnetic status mods to farm efficiently. Public void fissure groups benefit from coordinating magnetic damage applications to maximize trigger frequency. The arcane's effectiveness scales significantly with weapon attack speed and magnetic damage modifiers applied through mods.

## Best On

Melee Vortex excels on fast-hitting melee weapons with magnetic damage components or those built with magnetic elemental mods like Cold and Electric combinations. Frames with magnetic damage enhancements such as Chroma with vex armor or Saryn with her toxin spreads gain exceptional synergies. Warframes specializing in crowd control, including Limbo with rift mechanics or Nyx with psychic abilities, maximize the arcane's pull frequency. Whip and polearm weapons benefit from the crowd clustering, enabling chain-hit damage multiplication.

Slow-attacking melee weapons without magnetic application underutilize this arcane. Frames focused on other damage types or non-magnetic status spreads gain minimal benefit.
