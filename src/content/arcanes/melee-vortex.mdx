## Overview

Melee Vortex is a melee weapon arcane that combines crowd control with elemental synergy. When a player kills an enemy affected by Magnetic status using a melee weapon, the arcane triggers a random chance to pull nearby enemies into a concentrated area within 18 meters. This mechanic encourages magnetic-focused builds that pair status application with melee weapon selection, creating dynamic crowd management opportunities during combat.

The arcane excels on magnetic-focused builds using weapons like Resonance weapons or hybrid corrosive-magnetic setups that reliably apply the Magnetic status effect. The pull radius of 18 meters covers most standard tileset corridors and rooms, enabling effective crowd consolidation for area-effect ability usage or group damage spike opportunities. Higher ranks increase trigger chance from 20% to 45%, providing more consistent utility during extended encounters.

Melee Vortex drops exclusively from Zariman enemy encounters, with sources including Mocking Whisper, Scathing Whisper, and The Fragmented Suzerain at consistent 6.25% uncommon-rarity drop rates. Multiple Zariman runs efficiently accumulate the arcane toward higher ranks. Squad coordination during Void Fissure farming optimizes resource gathering for relics and void traces simultaneously.

The consistent drop rates across three separate enemy types provide farming flexibility. Players can prioritize preferred mission objectives or tileset types while pursuing Melee Vortex progression.

## Best on

Melee Vortex pairs best with magnetic-focused melee weapons or builds that reliably proc Magnetic status effects. Weapons like Stropha Prime, Plague Kripath Zaw variants, or hybrid corrosive-magnetic polarity setups maximize status application frequency. Frames with magnetic ability synergies, such as Mag or magnetic-enhanced builds, create natural synergy with the arcane's conditional trigger.

The arcane rewards careful status application and kill timing, encouraging players to prioritize Magnetic-afflicted enemies for final kills. Building around consistent magnetic procs transforms Melee Vortex into a reliable crowd control tool that consolidates scattered enemies for area-effect ability spam or coordinated squad focus fire.
