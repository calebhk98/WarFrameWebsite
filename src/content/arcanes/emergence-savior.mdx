## Overview

Emergence Savior provides emergency protection and squad revival at critical moments. This operator arcane enables operators to act as last-resort squad support during dangerous void fissure content.

Emergency overshields and squad revival provide clutch squad recovery mechanisms during dangerous void fissure engagements.

## Acquisition

Emergence Savior is a rare operator arcane dropping from Zariman Void Fissure (3%) and Void Cascade rotation C (1.5%). Regular zariman void farming yields this arcane through progression.

## Best on

Emergence Savior is ideal for operators in squad-support roles. The emergency revival and overshield enable operators to save squad wipes during dangerous content.
