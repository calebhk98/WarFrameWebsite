## Overview

Akimbo Slip Shot grants ammo efficiency to Dual Pistol secondary weapons while the player is sliding or aim gliding. Ammo efficiency reduces the ammunition consumed per shot - at 65% efficiency, shots consume approximately 35% of their normal ammo cost, effectively extending the magazine duration and reducing the frequency of reloads or ammo pickups needed. This bonus is active throughout the sliding and aim glide states, both of which are common in Warframe's fast-movement combat style.

Dual Pistols are paired secondary weapons that fire simultaneously or in alternation from both hands - Akstiletto, Aksomati, Akjagara, Akvasto, and other "Ak-" prefixed weapons, as well as Stubba and similar paired secondaries. The ammo efficiency bonus applies to the combined fire of both pistols, making it effective on high-fire-rate dual pistol configurations that drain ammo rapidly during sustained fire.

## Effect

At rank 0, 15% ammo efficiency while sliding or aim gliding provides a minor extension of effective magazine size during movement. At rank 5, 65% ammo efficiency reduces ammo expenditure to approximately 35% of the base rate - roughly tripling effective magazine longevity during active sliding or aim glide phases.

The bonus is active throughout the sliding and aim gliding states and deactivates when the player transitions to standing, sprinting, or other movement states.

## Builds that use it

Akimbo Slip Shot suits Dual Pistol builds that combine high-fire-rate secondaries with a slide-based combat style. Aksomati Prime builds that sustain continuous fire while sliding through enemy groups benefit from the 65% ammo efficiency extending the effective ammo pool before reload or ammo pickup is needed. Akjagara Prime builds that fire rapidly on approach benefit during the slide-fire sequences that define close-range Dual Pistol gameplay.

Players who bullet-jump and slide continuously through corridors as their primary movement pattern maintain Slip Shot's efficiency bonus throughout large portions of the mission. Slide-attack melee builds that also use their secondary weapon during or between slides find Slip Shot reduces ammo anxiety during the frequent slide-state fire windows.

## Notes

The ammo efficiency specifically requires the player to be in a slide or aim glide state - standing, crouching, or sprinting fire does not benefit. Slide combat is a standard technique in Warframe where players slide through enemies to deal area damage with melee weapons; Slip Shot converts these already-common slides into efficiency windows for the secondary weapon. Aim glide activates during the peak of a bullet jump when aiming - a brief window that combines with Slip Shot's bonus for precision shots during airborne movement. The bonus does not affect ammunition pickup rates or magazine size - it reduces consumption during the active state.
