## Overview

Primary Plated Round provides shield regeneration based on impact procs. On every impact status effect from the primary weapon, the arcane grants a shield buff stacking up to six times. This arcane works well with impact-heavy weapons. Early ranks provide 12% shield scaling; maximum rank yields 72% shield per stack for 432% total bonus at six stacks.
