## Overview

Primary Plated Round grants increased damage per round loaded based on the weapon's maximum magazine size after each Reload. The more rounds a weapon holds at maximum capacity, the higher the damage bonus per loaded round during the buff window. The buff lasts for a set duration after the reload completes, covering the next magazine dump with elevated damage output.

At rank 5, the buff lasts 10 seconds after each reload. Weapons with large magazines that are fired within the 10-second buff window deal increased damage for every round remaining in the chamber, making high-capacity primaries particularly effective with this arcane.

## Effect

On reload completion, Primary Plated Round activates a damage bonus that scales with the weapon's maximum magazine size and persists for the listed duration. The per-round damage increase means weapons with larger magazines receive larger total benefit -- a 60-round magazine weapon receives 60 stacks of the per-round bonus versus a 6-round weapon receiving only 6 stacks. At rank 0, the buff lasts 3 seconds -- sufficient for a few shots. At rank 5, the buff lasts 10 seconds, covering most of the next magazine dump for moderate fire rate weapons.

Weapons with large magazine sizes gain the most damage benefit, while weapons with very small magazines (sniper rifles, low-capacity shotguns) gain less total bonus per trigger.

## Acquisition

Primary Plated Round drops from various Zariman Ten-Zero mission rotations and Void Cascade content at uncommon rarity. The arcane is accessible after completing Angels of the Zariman. Warframe.market provides an alternative for players preferring direct platinum acquisition.

## Builds that use it

Primary Plated Round is most effective on high-magazine-capacity primary weapons. Large-magazine assault rifles and SMG-type weapons with 50-100+ round capacity receive the maximum per-round damage bonus accumulation, and with 10-second buff durations at rank 5, can fire through much of the magazine during the boosted window. Builds that pair Primary Plated Round with Vigilante Supplies (larger effective ammo pool through ammo conversion) or magazine extension mods maximize the magazine capacity that feeds the per-round damage scaling.

Reload speed mods and arcanes pair naturally with Primary Plated Round by reducing the time spent reloading, minimizing the gap between buff windows and allowing faster re-entry into the boosted damage phase after each reload.

## Notes

The buff triggers on reload completion, not when reload begins. Partial reloads that complete early (using emergency reload mechanics or reload cancel) still trigger the buff on completion. The damage bonus scales with the weapon's maximum magazine size, not the current ammo count being loaded -- a weapon that reloads with fewer rounds than maximum still receives the full magazine-size-based bonus. The buff duration is fixed per rank; the arcane's value comes from using the boosted window efficiently by firing during the active duration.
