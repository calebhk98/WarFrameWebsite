## Overview

Exodia Might is a rare zaw arcane that grants aggressive sustain through finisher kills, turning melee executions into health restoration opportunities. The 50% trigger chance ensures reliable lifesteal procs during extended melee combat, providing passive defensive scaling that rewards skilled finisher usage. Unlike flat-value healing arcanes, Might's lifesteal is percentage-based, scaling directly with the player's current health pool and creating stronger mitigation at higher power levels. This makes the arcane particularly valuable for tanks and bruisers that sustain through consistent damage output rather than heavy defensive abilities.

Might's synergy with finisher-boosting mechanics (Covert Lethality, finisher damage mods, Shedu parazon finishers) makes it an enabler for status quo finisher-focused melee builds. The 8-second window allows multiple lifesteal ticks if additional finishers land within the duration, creating overlapping healing windows that can sustain through prolonged encounters. The arcane's modest rarity makes it one of the earliest zaw arcanes available to new endgame players farming Cetus or Plains of Eidolon.

## Effect

Exodia Might triggers whenever the zaw completes a finisher kill on any enemy type. The 50% base chance means one in two finishers will activate lifesteal; in practice, sustained melee builds completing multiple finishers per second can maintain near-continuous healing. The lifesteal percentage applies to all damage dealt by the zaw during the buff window, not just finisher damage. This allows zaws to gain healing from regular melee hits while the buff is active, creating a powerful healing loop in high-kill-rate scenarios.

The healing amount is significant at rank 3: 30% lifesteal applied to a zaw dealing 800 base damage provides 240 health restoration per hit. On fast-attack-speed melee weapons, this translates to hundreds of health per second, effectively converting raw damage output into survivability. The effect scales multiplicatively with Armor Cap mods and Vitality, rewarding high-investment tank builds.

## Acquisition

Exodia Might is one of the most accessible zaw arcanes because Hok (the Cetus zaw vendor) sells it directly for standing. Players reaching Trusted status with Ostron faction can purchase Might without farming additional sources. The guaranteed vendor purchase makes Might the entry-level zaw arcane for players transitioning into endgame melee, often acquired before attempting higher-difficulty content like Tridolon hunts.

Once obtained, Might ranks in 24 hours per rank to tier 3. The arcane's availability and early-game accessibility mean trading post prices remain moderate compared to rare drops from eidolon encounters.

## Best on

Exodia Might performs exceptionally well on finisher-focused builds using high-damage zaws with fast attack speed. Pole Arms and Swords benefit from the lifesteal scaling because their attack speed allows rapid finisher chains. Dagger zaws paired with Covert Lethality enable instant finishers on most enemy types, creating reliable trigger conditions for Might's heal proc. Heavy melee weapons (Hammers, Axes) benefit less because their low attack speed limits finisher frequency.

Warframes with ability-gated finishers (Ash's Teleport Slash, Excalibur's Exalted Blade finishers) or frames that amplify finisher damage (Harrow, Xaku with the Acrithian Scale) maximize Might's value. Tank-focused frames like Rhino, Wukong, and Valkyr create sustainable finisher loops that keep Might's healing active throughout extended fights.

## Builds that use it

Endgame melee builds almost universally pair Might with Exodia Contagion or Exodia Brave to create multi-utility zaw arsenals. Steel Path endless survival builds leverage Might's sustain to allow single-zaw builds without defensive warframe abilities, freeing up mod slots for utility or damage. Support frames use Might zaws to maintain personal durability while dealing area damage to allies. Hybrid DPS-tank builds stack Might with Arcane Guardian or Aegis for layered healing.

## Notes

Exodia Might's lifesteal scales with base damage before any mod multipliers; a zaw with 500 base damage and 5 mods applying +300% damage will heal based on the final 1500 damage dealt. The trigger is classified as a "Finisher Kill" event, meaning it activates only on execution-style kills, not regular melee hits. Builds should pair Might with finisher damage bonuses to increase proc frequency. The 8-second duration window allows overlapping buffs if multiple finishers land within the same timeframe, enabling semi-permanent healing in high-speed melee content.
