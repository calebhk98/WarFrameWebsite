## Overview

Emergence Renewed restores warframe health during transference entry. This operator arcane enables operators to provide passive healing to frames during extended void fissure farming.

| Rank | Trigger Chance | Health Restoration |
|------|----------------|-------------------|
| 0    | 15%            | 30%               |
| 1    | 22%            | 45%               |
| 2    | 30%            | 60%               |
| 3    | 37%            | 75%               |
| 4    | 41%            | 82%               |
| 5    | 45%            | 90%               |

Health restoration enables operators to function as emergency healers during frame sustainability phases.

## Acquisition

Emergence Renewed drops from Zariman Void Fissure (4%) and Void Cascade rotation B (2%). Regular zariman void farming yields this arcane naturally.

## Best on

Emergence Renewed is ideal for operators supporting frames in damage-heavy scenarios. The health restoration enables reduced dependency on external healing.
