## Overview

Magus Drive increases K-Drive board speed for 30 seconds whenever the operator performs Transference In - the action of entering operator mode from warframe mode. This creates an unusual interaction where the operator transition buffs K-Drive performance in the subsequent warframe phase. Players who switch to operator briefly and then return to warframe while mounted on a K-Drive benefit from the buffed top speed during the 30-second window.

K-Drives are hoverboard-type vehicles used in open world zones - the Plains of Eidolon, Orb Vallis, and Cambion Drift. Speed is the primary performance metric for K-Drives in traversal, racing, and trick-scoring contexts. Magus Drive targets this specific vehicle type, making it a niche arcane suited for players who use K-Drives extensively in open world content.

## Effect

Each Transference In activates the K-Drive speed bonus for 30 seconds. At rank 0, a 25% speed increase provides a meaningful pace improvement during open world traversal. At rank 5, a 150% speed increase more than doubles the K-Drive's effective travel speed for the 30-second window - allowing rapid traversal of Plains or Orb Vallis distances that would normally require multiple minutes of riding.

The 30-second duration is generous - it covers substantial open world distances at elevated speeds, and the buff resets each time the player enters operator mode again.

## Builds that use it

Magus Drive suits players who prioritize open world traversal speed and use K-Drives as their primary movement option across Plains of Eidolon and Orb Vallis. Operators running K-Drive racing missions benefit directly - entering operator mode before mounting or during a race to activate the buff, then exiting to receive the speed-boosted ride. Bounty runners who traverse large distances between mission waypoints on K-Drives use Magus Drive to shorten travel times between objectives.

Ventkid reputation farmers who participate in trick scoring while traveling can combine the elevated speed with trick inputs for higher-scoring runs during the buff window.

## Notes

The speed bonus applies specifically to K-Drives - it does not affect Archwing, Velocipod, or standard sprint speeds. The buff activates on Transference In, meaning the player must enter operator mode and then return to warframe while near a K-Drive or already mounted. The 30-second window begins at the Transference In event, not when K-Drive mounting occurs - players who take time to mount after the transition may lose some of the available window. The buff does not apply in standard tile-set missions where K-Drives cannot be deployed.
