## Overview

Exodia Force is a Zaw arcane that extends damage application through status effect triggers. When a melee strike applies any status effect, Exodia Force grants a 50% chance to deal bonus weapon damage to all nearby enemies within the arcane's range. At rank 3, this triggers 200% weapon damage explosions in a 12-meter radius, enabling area-of-effect damage chains from single-target strikes. The arcane synergizes with status-heavy Zaw builds and transforms elemental damage applications into crowd-damage multipliers.

The trigger chance remains consistent at 50% across all ranks, with damage scaling and range increasing linearly. The mechanic applies independently of weapon multishot, allowing fast-hitting status Zaws to generate multiple damage explosions per rotation. Each trigger scales off the equipped Zaw's base damage and modifiers.

## Acquisition

Exodia Force drops from Zariman void fissure missions as a moderately rare Zaw arcane. The arcane requires status-focused Zaw builds to farm efficiently, making elemental damage application essential. Public void fissure groups benefit from coordinating status-spreading strategies and crowd clustering tactics. The arcane's effectiveness scales with attack speed and elemental damage mods applied to Zaw builds.

## Best On

Exodia Force excels on Zaw builds with high status chance and elemental damage components. Frames with status-enhancement abilities such as Saryn or those with elemental damage synergies gain exceptional trigger frequency. Warframes emphasizing crowd control through status applications maximize the arcane's area-damage potential. Fast-striking Zaws with natural elemental damage transform the arcane into consistent crowd-damage output.

Non-status Zaw builds or those focusing on pure physical damage underutilize this arcane. Frames without elemental synergies or status-spreading capabilities gain minimal benefit from the trigger condition.
