## Overview

Arcane Agility grants sprint speed boosts on kills. This warframe arcane rewards aggressive combat playstyles by enabling frames to cover map distance efficiently during void fissure farming runs.

## Effects per rank

| Rank | Proc Chance | Sprint Boost | Duration |
|------|-------------|--------------|----------|
| 0    | 10%         | +20%         | 10s      |
| 1    | 15%         | +30%         | 10s      |
| 2    | 20%         | +40%         | 10s      |
| 3    | 25%         | +50%         | 10s      |
| 4    | 27%         | +55%         | 10s      |
| 5    | 30%         | +60%         | 10s      |

Sprint speed scaling enables rapid map traversal during extended fissure operations while maintaining combat readiness for incoming void manifestations.

## Acquisition

Arcane Agility drops from Zariman Void Fissure (3%) and Void Cascade rotation B (1.5%). Regular zariman void farming yields this arcane naturally.

## Best on

Arcane Agility excels on mobile, kill-focused warframes that benefit from repositioning during void fissure content. The speed boost enables efficient navigation between void manifestation spawns.

## Sources

Zariman void fissure farming provides agility drops. Players regularly participating in void cascade events naturally accumulate this arcane.
