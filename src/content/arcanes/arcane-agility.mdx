## Overview

Arcane Agility grants sprint speed boosts on kills, rewarding aggressive combat playstyles by enabling frames to cover map distance efficiently between enemy engagements. The kill-based trigger creates a momentum mechanic where active combat generates movement speed, allowing frames to quickly reposition between groups, sprint to objectives, or chase down fleeing enemies without relying on ability-based speed buffs.

Sprint speed is one of the most universally useful stats in Warframe across all content types. Faster frames complete objectives more quickly, reach extraction faster in time-limited missions, and reposition between threats more effectively. Arcane Agility provides this benefit as a kill reward, aligning the speed bonus with exactly the situations where mobility matters most -- after successfully eliminating an enemy and needing to move to the next.

## Effect

Each enemy kill checks the Agility trigger chance. On success, sprint speed increases by the rank-dependent percentage for 10 seconds. At rank 0, 20% sprint speed is a noticeable improvement over base movement; at rank 5, 60% sprint speed significantly accelerates frame mobility, exceeding the effect of Rush and Speed Drift mods individually. Multiple triggers within the 10-second window refresh the duration rather than stacking the bonus beyond the rank 5 value.

Sprint speed bonus is multiplicative with base sprint speed, meaning faster frames (Volt, Titania, Nezha) gain larger absolute speed increases from the same percentage bonus.

## Acquisition

Arcane Agility drops from Zariman Void Fissure at 3% and Void Cascade rotation B at 1.5%. The slightly lower drop rates compared to other Zariman arcanes reflect its general-utility nature. Regular Zariman Ten-Zero farming accumulates Agility through sustained runs. Players completing daily Zariman bounties encounter Agility through rotation B drops. Warframe.market provides direct purchase options for players who prefer targeted acquisition.

## Builds that use it

Arcane Agility benefits all frame types but excels on builds where map traversal is a priority. Capture mission builds that maximize mission completion speed benefit from consistent post-kill sprint boosts that maintain momentum between capture targets. Mobile Defense builds that move between terminals frequently find Agility's boost reduces time between objectives substantially. Speed-focused frames that already invest in sprint speed mods (Rush, Speed Drift) compound Agility's bonus on top of existing speed investments for extreme movement values.

## Notes

Sprint speed bonus applies during sprint state; walking speed is unaffected. The bonus does not stack with itself from multiple triggers within the 10-second window; subsequent kills during an active buff only extend the duration. Agility does not affect bullet jump or dodge roll distance, only linear sprint speed. The 30% trigger chance at rank 5 ensures most kills in continuous combat provide the speed bonus, maintaining near-constant sprint enhancement during active engagements. The bonus interacts multiplicatively with Rush and other sprint-speed mods, compounding with existing speed investments.
