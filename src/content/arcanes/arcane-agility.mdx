## Overview

Arcane Agility grants Parkour Velocity on taking damage, providing a reactive mobility boost during dangerous encounters. Parkour Velocity affects bullet jump speed, aim glide velocity, and wall latch movement rather than sprint speed. The damage trigger means the arcane activates precisely when enemies are actively hitting the frame -- rewarding aggressive forward movement by making the frame harder to pin down after sustaining hits.

At rank 5, the arcane provides a 60% chance for +60% Parkour Velocity lasting 18 seconds when damaged. The 18-second duration is generous, sustaining the movement bonus through extended engagements without requiring continuous damage to maintain the effect.

## Effect

Each damage event triggers a 60% chance check. On success, Parkour Velocity increases by the rank-dependent percentage for 18 seconds. Multiple damage events during an active Agility window refresh the duration rather than stacking the velocity beyond the rank value. The trigger fires on any damage source -- enemy projectiles, melee hits, Damage-over-Time ticks, and environmental hazards all qualify.

At rank 0, +10% Parkour Velocity is a minor improvement to movement agility. At rank 5, +60% Parkour Velocity produces noticeably longer bullet jump arcs and faster aim glide traversal. The bonus enhances the frame's ability to evade follow-up attacks, reposition between cover points, and maintain kinetic combat at range from enemies.

## Acquisition

Arcane Agility drops from Zariman Void Fissure rewards at 3% and from Void Cascade rotation B at 1.5%. Both sources are available after completing Angels of the Zariman. Void Cascade rotation B drops are accessible in moderate-length runs. Warframe.market provides direct purchase for players who prefer platinum acquisition.

## Builds that use it

Arcane Agility suits builds that engage enemies at close range and take regular hits during combat. Melee-focused frames that rush into enemy groups, tanking frames that absorb damage to trigger Agility consistently, and aggressive steel path builds that face constant incoming damage all trigger the arcane frequently. The Parkour Velocity bonus complements close-combat frames by making repositioning between enemies faster and bullet jump engagements more dynamic.

Builds that pair Agility with Rush or other Parkour Velocity mods compound the bonus on top of existing mobility investments. Frames running Arcane Agility alongside bullet-jump-damage mods (Motus Impact, Fortitude) gain both the damage bonus on bullet jump and the extended jump range from elevated Parkour Velocity.

## Notes

Parkour Velocity specifically affects bullet jump and aim glide speed, not sprint speed. Players who primarily sprint between engagements rather than using parkour movement gain less value from Agility than players who bullet jump and wall run regularly. The 60% trigger chance at rank 5 is fixed across all ranks -- only the Parkour Velocity value scales with rank. Shield damage that reduces only shields does not count as health damage; the trigger fires on health damage events specifically.
