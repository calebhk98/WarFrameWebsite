## Overview

Residual Boils is a companion arcane from the Residual-Theorem set that spawns volatile hives when a Kitgun kill is secured. On each Kitgun kill, there is a 20% chance that a cluster of volatile hives spawns at the kill location. These hives persist for the rank-dependent duration and explode when an enemy approaches within range, dealing 80 Heat Damage in a 10-meter radius. Additionally, the warframe standing in the Boils area applies Heat Damage type to Theorem Arcanes, synergizing with Theorem arcanes that scale based on nearby elemental damage types.

At rank 3, the volatile hives persist for 12 seconds -- long enough to act as area denial against subsequent waves of enemies approaching through the kill location.

## Effect

On a Kitgun kill, each kill checks the 20% proc chance. On success, volatile hives appear at the kill location and persist for the listed duration. Enemies that approach the hives trigger an explosion dealing 80 Heat Damage in a 10-meter radius from the hive. The explosion is trigger-based -- hives only detonate when an enemy physically approaches, making them persistent area denial that activates when enemies arrive rather than immediately. Standing in the Boils zone applies Heat Damage type to Theorem Arcanes, enabling Theorem arcanes that amplify based on elemental damage type to benefit from the Heat typing.

At rank 0, hives persist 3 seconds. At rank 3, hives persist 12 seconds, providing a longer denial window before expiring.

## Acquisition

Residual Boils drops from various Zariman Ten-Zero missions and Void Cascade content at uncommon rarity. The arcane is accessible through standard Zariman progression after completing Angels of the Zariman. Players building Residual-Theorem arcane sets farm Zariman content for all Residual and Theorem arcanes simultaneously, as the full set synergizes most effectively. Warframe.market provides direct purchase for targeted acquisition.

## Builds that use it

Residual Boils is designed for the Residual-Theorem arcane pairing system. Kitgun-primary builds that kill enemies at high rates generate 20% hive-spawn procs frequently throughout combat, placing volatile hives across the battlefield. Standing in the hive zones activates the Heat Damage type for Theorem Arcanes, enabling any equipped Theorem arcanes to scale with or receive bonuses based on Heat Damage type. The 10-meter explosion radius covers a meaningful area, making hives effective against grouped enemies approaching through choke points.

Builds combining Residual Boils with Theorem Contagion or other Theorem arcanes receive the Heat type activation needed for the Theorem set's elemental interaction mechanics. Kitgun builds with high kill rates maximize the frequency of hive spawns, ensuring the Boils area is consistently maintained near the player's position.

## Notes

The 20% trigger chance is fixed across all ranks; only the hive duration scales with rank. Multiple hive clusters can exist simultaneously from multiple Kitgun kills, creating overlapping explosion zones in high-traffic areas. The explosion from each hive triggers once when an enemy approaches and does not regenerate after detonation. The Heat Damage type applied to Theorem Arcanes by standing in the area is the set mechanic that links the Residual series to the Theorem series -- this interaction only applies while the warframe physically stands within the hive zone.
