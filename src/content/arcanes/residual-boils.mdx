## Overview

Residual Boils spreads toxin status effects from companion attacks to nearby enemies, amplifying the area impact of companion-inflicted toxin procs. When the companion applies a toxin proc to a target, the arcane extends the toxin effect to surrounding enemies within range, creating a contamination zone that damages multiple targets without requiring individual companion attacks on each enemy. This makes toxin-modded companions disproportionately effective at applying damage-over-time across dense enemy groups.

Toxin damage in Warframe bypasses shields and deals damage directly to health, making it effective against Corpus enemies whose shields would otherwise reduce incoming damage. Residual Boils extends this bypass benefit to area coverage, allowing a single companion proc to apply shield-bypassing toxin damage to multiple nearby enemies simultaneously.

## Effect

Each toxin status proc applied by the companion triggers the spread effect. The proc radiates outward from the initially affected enemy, applying toxin damage and status to additional targets within the range. At rank 0, 3 enemies within a smaller radius receive the spread; at rank 5, up to 6 enemies within an expanded radius take the spread toxin proc. The spread proc damage matches the companion weapon's toxin proc damage, not a reduced value.

Companions with dedicated toxin damage builds (Kavats with toxin mods, Sentinels with toxin-modded weapons) generate higher base proc damage, which Residual Boils then spreads at full value to surrounding targets.

## Acquisition

Residual Boils drops from Zariman Ten-Zero missions and Void Cascade rotations. The arcane is available through standard Zariman content after completing Angels of the Zariman. Regular Void Cascade runs encounter Boils as a potential companion arcane reward. Players investing in companion-focused builds often farm Zariman content as it provides multiple companion arcanes in the Residual series simultaneously. Warframe.market provides direct purchase options for targeted acquisition.

## Builds that use it

Residual Boils excels on builds centered on companion toxin damage application. Kavats with Viral modding (Toxin + Cold) generate toxin procs as part of the combined element, allowing Boils to spread toxin independently of the primary element. Sentinels with Verglas (cryo weapon) or toxin-modded sidearms trigger Boils on every successful toxin proc against enemy groups. High-enemy-density content (survival missions, defense waves) provides the most targets for each spread event. Pairing with other Residual arcanes (Residual Viremia, Residual Malodor) creates compound companion proc synergies where each element type triggers its respective spread arcane.

## Notes

The spread requires the companion to land the initial toxin proc; the arcane does not independently generate toxin damage. Companions with slow attack rates benefit less from Boils due to fewer initial procs per minute. Enemies immune to toxin damage (some Infested variants, Alloy Armor heavy units with toxin resistance) resist both the initial proc and the spread. The spread range is fixed and not modified by companion or warframe Range mods. Multiple simultaneous companions in a squad do not stack Boils spread on the same enemy; each companion's spread triggers independently.
