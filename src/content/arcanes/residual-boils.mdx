## Overview

Residual Boils spreads toxin status effects from companion attacks to nearby enemies. This arcane enhances companion toxin procs by creating area contamination zones. Sentinels and Kavats with toxin weapons gain exceptional value. Early ranks affect 3 nearby enemies; maximum rank affects 6 enemies within expanded range.

## Acquisition

Residual Boils drops from various Zariman and Void Cascade missions.

## Best on

This arcane works best on builds that exploit its triggering condition frequently.

## Usage

Residual Boils activates automatically when its trigger condition is met during combat.
