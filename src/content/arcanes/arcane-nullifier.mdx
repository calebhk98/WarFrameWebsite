## Overview

Arcane Nullifier is a Warframe arcane that grants a passive chance to strip enemy shields upon contact with Nullifier Crewman shield domes. The arcane activates when the Tenno physically enters or attacks through a Nullifier bubble, providing a powerful tool against Corpus defensive units. Unlike other shield-stripping mechanics that require ability casts or specific mod combinations, this arcane triggers automatically on engagement, making it ideal for aggressive playstyles. The mechanism provides strong utility in Corpus-heavy missions (Void Fissures, Zariman, Void Cascade) without requiring frame-specific abilities or significant mod slots.

## Effect per rank

Arcane Nullifier builds up a dispel chance from 10% at rank 0 to 60% at rank 5, with the number of shield layers stripped increasing at rank 3 from one to two. The arcane does not have a cooldown between triggers, meaning rapid successive contacts with shield bubbles can stack multiple dispels. At maximum rank, the arcane achieves a 60% chance per contact to strip up to 2 shield layers per trigger event. The mechanic applies to all Nullifier-type enemies across the star chart, including Corpus MOAs, Combas, and elite variants. This arcane scales in value as enemy levels increase, since higher-level Nullifiers spawn with additional shield layers that are more challenging to remove through conventional means.

## Acquisition

Arcane Nullifier drops from Duviri/Endless missions at Legendary rarity (0.67% Tiers 1--3, 4% Tier 9), Zariman Skirmish nodes at uncommon rarity (10% Rotation C), and legacy Eidolon Hydrolyst sources (8%). The most accessible farm involves running Zariman Void Cascade on Rotation C with squad resource boosters, which offer 12.5% drop chance. Duviri Tier 9 provides reliable drops for dedicated solo farmers, though the low drop rate demands significant time investment. For players with strong Eidolon execution, the Hydrolyst remains farmable but is considered obsolete as a primary source given Duviri and Zariman availability. Nullifier arcane acquisition benefits from radioshare squads, as each player's drops are independent.

## Builds that use it

Arcane Nullifier shines on aggressive Corpus farming builds, particularly Void Fissure endless runs where Nullifiers spawn in escalating waves. Volt Prime benefits most, combining this arcane with Overcharge and Shield Polarize for overwhelming shield removal. Mag Prime naturally synergizes through her Magnetize and Polarize abilities, making the arcane a quality-of-life upgrade rather than a core mechanic. Limbo can use Arcane Nullifier to safely farm Void Fissures without risking ability-based shield stripping, relying on passive contact to weaken enemies before Banish. The arcane is less valuable on Grineer-focused missions (void fissures over level 30 tend to be Corpus), making it situational rather than universal. Pairing with weapons that have high attack speed ensures frequent shield bubbles are triggered in rapid succession, maximizing dispel proc chances.

## Notes

Arcane Nullifier only triggers on contact with active Nullifier shield bubbles; it does not provide any benefit against passive Corpus defenses (Comba drones without shields, shielded MOAs). The arcane stacks in effectiveness when multiple squad members equip it, as each player's proc can independently strip additional layers. Unlike Mag's Polarize, Nullifier arcane dispels do not redirect projectiles or grant overshields -- they purely remove shield layers. The arcane's effectiveness scales with enemy level; high-level Nullifiers may spawn with 3+ shield layers, meaning full removal still requires conventional stripping or multiple arcane procs. At endgame levels (170+), this arcane is most effective as a quality-of-life tool rather than a primary damage/defense layer.
