## Overview

Virtuos Null is an operator arcane that enhances amp energy regeneration on successful kills. This arcane provides sustained energy regeneration benefit to the operator, useful for extended void fissure runs and Zariman void cascade missions where maintaining amp uptime is critical.

## Effects per rank

| Rank | Regen | Duration |
|------|-------|----------|
| 0    | +5%   | 4s       |
| 1    | +10%  | 4s       |
| 2    | +15%  | 4s       |
| 3    | +20%  | 4s       |

The arcane grants a percentage-based energy regeneration buff that triggers on operator weapon kills. At maximum rank, the operator receives a +20% amp energy regeneration boost for 4 seconds following each kill, making it especially valuable in high-damage scenarios where kill frequency is consistent.

## Acquisition

Virtuos Null is obtained exclusively from The Quills in Cetus. The arcane is guaranteed as a drop from the Mote rank tier reward pool. Progression through The Quills requires void fissure participation and accumulation of void traces and relics. The Mote rank is the initial entry-level tier, making this arcane one of the earliest operator arcanes available to players.

## Best on

This arcane pairs effectively with operator builds focused on void fissure farming and early-to-mid endgame content. Frames that benefit from rapid operator amp usage, such as those running void cascade farming or Zariman void cascade missions, find value in the sustained energy pool. The arcane shines in squad compositions emphasizing void damage conversion and operator damage output.

## Usage notes

The regeneration buff duration of 4 seconds means kill chain momentum is important for maintaining the effect. In high-density enemy environments, the buff typically remains active throughout extended operator engagement. The percentage-based regeneration means it scales with the amp's maximum energy pool, making it more effective with upgraded amps. The effect triggers on operator weapon kills, not operator ability kills, so weapon-based builds derive maximum benefit.
