## Overview

Melee Influence is a melee weapon arcane that spreads electrical status effects to nearby enemies. When a melee weapon applies electrical status to a target, Melee Influence grants a 20% chance to apply a random elemental status effect to all enemies within the arcane's range for the scaled duration. At rank 5, this extends the range to 20 meters and duration to 18 seconds, creating powerful crowd-control and damage-spreading potential. The mechanic cannot refresh while active, preventing duplicate overlapping debuffs on the same enemy group.

The trigger chance remains constant at 20% across all ranks, with range and duration scaling linearly. The status effect spread mechanic triggers on the initial electrical proc, requiring melee weapons with electrical damage or elemental mods to apply electricity reliably. The arcane's effectiveness scales with weapon attack speed and electrical damage modifiers.

## Acquisition

Melee Influence drops from Zariman void fissure missions as a moderately rare melee arcane. The arcane requires melee weapons with natural electrical damage or status-spreading builds to farm efficiently. Public void fissure groups benefit from coordinating melee weapons capable of consistent electrical application. The arcane's value scales significantly with high-attack-speed melee builds and range-extending mods.

## Best On

Melee Influence excels on fast-hitting melee weapons with electrical damage components like the Destreza Prime or weapons built with electrical mods. Frames with melee synergies such as Excalibur with his exalted blade or Ash with his shadow clone gains exceptional value from the crowd-control spreading. Warframes amplifying melee status application or electrical damage, including Saryn or Chroma, maximize the arcane's proc frequency. Polearms and whips benefit from the range scaling, enabling larger crowd-control zones.

Slow-attacking melee weapons without electrical components underutilize this arcane. Frames focused on ability damage over melee gain minimal benefit.
