## Overview

Arcane Intention boosts accuracy and critical chance during aimed fire. This warframe arcane rewards precision-focused playstyles by providing consistent DPS scaling through dual stat bonuses that compound when paired with crit-optimized weapons. The accuracy bonus reduces weapon spread during sustained fire, which benefits automatic primaries that experience bloom at range. Combined with the critical chance bonus, the arcane enables headshot-focused builds to achieve multiplicative damage benefits during the 10-second buff window.

The trigger condition - on aim - activates reliably in standard combat, since most ranged engagements involve aiming down sights. Weapons with narrow aim-zoom spread gain the most from accuracy scaling, while weapons with tight base spread instead leverage the critical chance component. Maintaining aimed fire throughout the buff window ensures full value extraction from the 10-second duration.

## Effect

Arcane Intention activates on entering aim mode. The accuracy component directly reduces projectile spread, bringing bullets closer to center mass during sustained fire on automatic weapons. The critical chance component adds a flat bonus to the weapon's existing critical chance stat, enabling weapons above 20% base crit to reliably reach orange or red crit tiers when combined with critical mods. At rank 5, plus 60% accuracy and plus 45% crit chance provide sufficient scaling to transform moderate-crit primaries into consistent crit machines during aimed combat.

Both bonuses apply simultaneously from the same trigger, making Intention one of the more efficient arcanes for precision builds that commit to aimed fire rather than hipfire engagement.

## Acquisition

Arcane Intention drops from Zariman Void Fissure (4%) and Void Cascade rotation B (2%). Both sources are accessible through standard Zariman Ten-Zero content, making the arcane attainable without extreme grind. Void Fissure runs provide higher drop rates, so players prioritizing Intention should focus on Void Fissure mission rotations. Trading through warframe.market provides an alternative for players who prefer to convert excess plat rather than farm directly.

## Builds that use it

Arcane Intention fits precision-primary builds centered on aimed engagements. Rifles with crit-focused modding (Point Strike, Vital Sense) reach orange-crit thresholds more consistently with Intention's bonus. Sniper builds benefit from the accuracy component eliminating sway variance at extreme range. Bow builds that aim deliberately between shots extract maximum value from both bonuses during each draw-and-release cycle. Frames that encourage steady aimed fire - Wisp providing speed buffs, Mesa with Ballistic Battery charged - pair well because they reward prolonged aimed engagement windows.

## Notes

Accuracy scaling affects projectile spread but does not change bullet flight path or gravity. Hitscan weapons benefit from tighter grouping; projectile weapons with arc-based flight paths are unaffected by accuracy. The critical chance bonus does not apply to abilities, only to weapon fire. Multiple copies of Intention do not stack; only one arcane of each name is equipped per warframe slot. Re-entering aim mode refreshes the buff duration on each proc.
