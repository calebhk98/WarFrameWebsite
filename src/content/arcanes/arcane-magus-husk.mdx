## Overview

Magus Husk is an Operator arcane that provides team-wide defensive benefits when Void Sling is cast. Unlike solo-focused operator arcanes, Husk encourages group play by granting nearby Warframe allies temporary armor bonuses. This makes it valuable for squad-based void cascade farming and cooperative endgame content.

## Effect

When the operator casts Void Sling, all nearby Warframes gain a flat armor buff that scales from +50 at rank 0 to +250 at rank 5. The effect persists for 12 seconds and can be reapplied by casting Void Sling again during the duration, effectively maintaining permanent uptime in active combat.

## Acquisition

Magus Husk is obtained from Void Cascade missions in Zariman as an uncommon drop reward. The arcane drops from the same pool as other early-tier operator arcanes and becomes more accessible as players gain familiarity with void fissure content.

## Best on

Magus Husk excels in support-focused operator builds:

- **Squad void fissures** — operators providing team buffs enhance overall group survivability.
- **Void cascade multiplayer farming** — consistent reapplication maintains armor buff during extended runs.
- **Eidolon hunt support** — the operator acts as a mobile defensive buff station for the Warframe squad.

## Notes

The armor buff applies only to Warframes within affinity range of the operator. Squad composition and spacing affect its utility. The arcane is particularly valuable in public squads where Warframe defenses may be lower than optimized setups.
