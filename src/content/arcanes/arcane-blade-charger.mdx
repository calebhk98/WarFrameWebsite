## Overview

Arcane Blade Charger is a Warframe arcane that grants bonus melee damage whenever the warframe scores a kill with a primary weapon. The 30% trigger probability means consistent damage increases in any mission featuring kill opportunities. Blade Charger transforms primary weapon engagement into a melee damage amplifier, enabling hybrid playstyles where warframes alternate between ranged and melee combat to maintain stacking buffs. The 12-second duration enables natural reapplication through regular kills, creating near-permanent uptime in missions with dense enemy spawning.

Blade Charger's primary strength lies in its synergy with hybrid weapon loadouts and its independence from ability-based mechanics. Warframes that benefit from both primary and melee damage (Grendel, Wukong, Ash) leverage Charger to supercharge melee output during phases when primary weapons cool down. The arcane was refined in Update 27.2.0 to provide meaningful scaling across all ranks, making even early-rank versions viable for star-chart and syndicate missions.

The 30% proc chance applies per kill, meaning high-fire-rate primary weapons with multiple pellets per second trigger the buff frequently. Damage stacks multiplicatively with existing melee mods and damage buffs, making high-base-damage melee weapons far more efficient than weapon-choice variance. The 12-second duration enables natural buff reapplication through regular primary kills; in survival-type missions with spawning enemies, uptime approaches permanent levels.

## Acquisition

Arcane Blade Charger is obtained through Arbitrations, the high-level endless mission variant available after completing the Star Chart. Players must reach at least rotation B or C (requiring 20+ minutes of mission engagement) to access Charger drops. The 5% drop chance from each rotation tier means dedicated farming requires commitment; averaging 10--20 Arbitration attempts per Charger. Alternatively, players can purchase pre-ranked copies from trading post; prices range from 80--150 platinum depending on rank and current market demand.

## Best on

Hybrid warframes balancing primary and melee damage (Grendel, Wukong Prime, Ash Prime) maximize Charger's trigger frequency. Melee-focused frames (Excalibur, Valkyr, Baruuk) using low-fire-rate primaries (bows, snipers) leverage Charger to convert infrequent primary usage into melee damage spikes. Weapon-swap builds alternating between fast primary clearance and heavy melee finishing moves employ Charger as the cornerstone damage amplifier. Steel Path and endless mission builds pair Charger with survival-oriented frames to maintain consistent damage output throughout extended runs.

Conversely, pure melee or pure ranged builds may deprioritize Charger in favor of defensive or utility arcanes.

## Builds that use it

Steel Path endless survival builds employ Arcane Blade Charger to maintain stacking melee damage throughout extended enemy waves. Weapon-swap farming builds use Charger to convert primary wave-clear into melee finisher damage, enabling efficient enemy elimination cycles. Hybrid damage frames leverage Charger as the primary damage amplifier, stacking triggers with melee ability usage for exponential damage growth. Defense and mobile mission builds pair Charger with warframes that naturally cycle between gun and sword, maintaining uptime through organic playstyle patterns.

## Notes

Arcane Blade Charger's proc rate of 30% applies per kill scored by primary weapons, not per shot. High-fire-rate weapons with multi-pellet shots trigger frequently during dense enemy encounters. The buff applies to all melee damage sources including ability-scaled melee (Whipclaw, Desolate Hands), enabling ability-based melee builds to benefit from Charger amplification. Multiple stacked buffs do not multiply damage; instead, the highest current buff takes precedence. Kill credit mechanics vary by mission type; confirm primary-weapon kills count properly in the intended mission format before committing to builds.
