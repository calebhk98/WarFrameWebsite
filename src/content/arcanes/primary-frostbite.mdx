## Overview

Primary Frostbite scales fire rate based on cold status application. On every cold proc applied by the primary weapon, the arcane grants a fire rate buff that stacks up to five times. This arcane synergizes with cold-element build Soma Prime and Zariman rifles. Early ranks provide 15% scaling per stack; maximum rank yields 75% fire rate per stack for 375% total bonus at maximum stacks.
