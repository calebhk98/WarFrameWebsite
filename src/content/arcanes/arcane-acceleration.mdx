## Overview

Arcane Acceleration is a warframe-exclusive arcane that scales with precision gunplay. On every critical hit with a primary weapon, this arcane has a 30% chance to grant a temporary fire rate buff that lasts 9 seconds. The bonus stacks in power as the arcane ranks up, reaching up to 90% increased fire rate at max rank. Higher ranks also confer bonus revives, making it valuable for solo and group endgame content where sustained firepower matters.

The arcane synergizes exceptionally well with critical-focused primary weapons like sniper rifles, bows, and high-crit rifles. Its trigger condition is frequent enough in typical combat scenarios to feel reliable, though the 30% proc chance means players should not expect constant uptime. The 9-second duration allows for overlapping buffs if critical hits come in quick succession.

## Acquisition

Arcane Acceleration drops from Duviri Zariman endless mission tiers and Void Cascade skirmish missions in the Zariman tileset. The Duviri Endless Tier 1 and Tier 3 nodes offer the best drop rates as legendary rarity rewards, though the odds remain low. Rotation C of Veil and Venus skirmishes offer more consistent opportunities to farm the arcane at rare rarity. Players should prioritize high-tier endless runs or late-rotation skirmish missions for the most efficient farming.

## Best on

Weapons with high critical chance and fire rate benefit most from Acceleration's bonus. Sniper rifles, plasma rifles, and semi-automatic primary weapons see the largest damage-per-second gains. Warframes with innate critical bonuses such as Rhino Prime or Inaros Prime amplify the arcane's potential, turning critical-focused builds into machine-like damage dealers during the buff window.

## Notes

The arcane activates only on primary weapon critical hits, excluding all shotgun variants. The trigger is independent of weapon mods, meaning players benefit from natural weapon criticals and mod-enhanced criticals equally. Stacking multiple Arcane Accelerations across a squad is not possible due to the singleton arcane system, but coordinated critical builds can chain procs in group missions.

## Sources

Arcane Acceleration can be acquired through dedicated Duviri Zariman farming or Void Cascade skirmish farming on later-game nodes. The drop rates vary significantly by mission tier and rotation phase, making long-term farming necessary for most players aiming to fully rank the arcane.
