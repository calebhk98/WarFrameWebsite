## Overview

Residual Malodor is a companion arcane from the Residual-Theorem set that creates a frigid mist zone on Kitgun kills. On each Kitgun kill, there is a 20% chance that a Cold mist appears at the kill location. The mist deals 40 Cold Damage per second to all enemies within the area for the rank-dependent duration. Standing in the mist zone activates Cold Damage type for Theorem Arcanes, synergizing with Theorem arcanes that respond to specific elemental damage types.

At rank 3, the frigid mist persists for 12 seconds, providing sustained Cold Damage area denial and Cold type activation for Theorem arcanes for the full duration.

## Effect

On each Kitgun kill, the arcane checks the 20% trigger chance. On success, a frigid mist zone spawns at the kill location. All enemies in the mist take 40 Cold Damage per second throughout the zone's duration. Cold Damage applies Cold status effect on procs, which reduces enemy movement and attack speed. Standing in the mist activates Cold Damage type for Theorem Arcanes -- this is the set link between Residual Malodor and Theorem arcanes that amplify or trigger based on elemental damage types in the area.

At rank 0, the mist lasts 3 seconds. At rank 3, the mist lasts 12 seconds, covering a longer period of area denial and Theorem type activation per trigger.

## Acquisition

Residual Malodor drops from various Zariman Ten-Zero missions and Void Cascade content at uncommon rarity. The arcane is accessible through standard Zariman progression after completing Angels of the Zariman. Players building Residual-Theorem set combinations farm Zariman content for all Residual and Theorem arcanes simultaneously as the set synergizes most effectively when multiple Residual types are active. Warframe.market provides direct purchase for targeted acquisition.

## Builds that use it

Residual Malodor is part of the Residual-Theorem arcane pairing system. Kitgun-primary builds with high kill rates trigger the 20% mist spawn frequently, placing Cold mist zones throughout active combat areas. Standing in the mist zones activates Cold Damage type for any equipped Theorem Arcanes, enabling the Theorem set mechanics that use elemental damage type as an activation condition.

Builds combining Residual Malodor with Theorem Contagion or other Theorem arcanes use the Cold type activation for the elemental damage type interaction. Cold Damage from the mist also applies Cold status effect procs to enemies standing in the zone, reducing their movement speed and making them easier to target for subsequent Kitgun shots. Defensive mission builds in survival or interception benefit from the area denial that frigid mist provides in chokepoints near defense objectives.

## Notes

The 20% trigger chance is fixed across all ranks; only the mist duration scales with rank. Multiple mist zones from different Kitgun kills can overlap, creating areas of compounding Cold Damage per second. The 40 Cold Damage/s is fixed and does not scale with weapon damage, companion mods, or player stats -- it is a flat area damage value per rank. The Cold type activation for Theorem Arcanes requires the warframe to physically stand within the mist zone; moving outside the zone immediately deactivates the Cold type for Theorem purposes.
