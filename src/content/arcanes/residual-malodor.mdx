## Overview

Residual Malodor creates area denial clouds when companion poison procs trigger. On every poison status effect from companion weapons, a temporary toxic zone forms that damages nearby enemies. Kavats and Sentinels with poison weapons create layered toxin damage zones. Early ranks create small clouds affecting 4 enemies; maximum rank expands range to affect 8 enemies.

## Acquisition

Residual Malodor drops from various Zariman and Void Cascade missions.

## Best on

This arcane works best on builds that exploit its triggering condition frequently.

## Usage

Residual Malodor activates automatically when its trigger condition is met during combat.
