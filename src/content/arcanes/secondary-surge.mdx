## Overview

Secondary Surge provides stacking attack speed bonuses on electric status procs from secondary weapons, creating an escalating fire rate multiplier during electric-focused engagements. The stacking mechanic rewards builds that maintain rapid electric proc application - each proc adds a stack, and maintaining all four stacks simultaneously delivers the maximum fire rate multiplier. Electric secondaries with high status chance apply procs quickly enough to build stacks during the opening shots of an engagement.

Attack speed in Warframe translates directly to fire rate for secondary weapons, meaning all four stacks effectively quadruple the per-stack bonus on top of existing speed mods. For full-auto secondaries, this translates to a substantial increase in rounds downrange per second during the stacked state.

## Effect

Each electric status proc from the secondary weapon applies one attack speed stack. Up to four stacks accumulate independently, each providing the per-rank bonus for a fixed duration. At rank 0, each stack grants 20% attack speed (maximum 80% at four stacks); at rank 5, each stack grants 100% attack speed (maximum 400% at four stacks). Four stacks at rank 5 effectively increases secondary fire rate to five times the base rate combined with existing fire rate mods.

Individual stack durations reset on each new proc trigger, meaning sustained electric proc application maintains maximum four-stack uptime throughout extended engagements.

## Acquisition

Secondary Surge drops from Zariman Ten-Zero missions and Void Cascade rotations. The arcane is accessible through standard Zariman content after completing Angels of the Zariman. Regular Void Cascade runs generate Surge as part of the electric-focused secondary arcane series. Players building electric secondary builds often farm Zariman content as it provides multiple relevant arcanes simultaneously. Warframe.market provides direct purchase options for targeted acquisition.

## Builds that use it

Secondary Surge excels on full-auto electric secondaries with high status chance. The Staticor (electric weapon), Furis with electric modding, and any secondary with 60% or higher status chance apply electric procs rapidly enough to build all four stacks within the first few seconds of combat. Builds combining Lethal Torrent (fire rate mod) with Secondary Surge create extreme fire rate values at four stacks that can briefly saturate enemy groups with projectiles. High-status-chance electric secondaries that combine Surge with other electric proc arcanes (Conjunction Voltage) create layered electric proc synergies.

## Notes

The stacking requires electric status specifically; other elemental procs do not trigger Secondary Surge. Weapons with combined elements (Magnetic = Cold + Electric, Corrosive = Toxin + Electric) still proc electric separately if Electric damage exists in the combination. The four-stack cap prevents unlimited acceleration; builds must balance attack speed with the magazine size and reload speed to maintain effective DPS. Stack timers are individual; a rapid succession of procs builds stacks, but slow proc rates between stacks allow early stacks to expire before new ones accumulate.
