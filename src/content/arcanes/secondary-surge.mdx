## Overview

Secondary Surge increases attack speed when secondary electric procs trigger. On every electric status effect from secondary weapons, the arcane grants attack speed buff stacking up to four times. This arcane synergizes with rapid-fire secondaries. Early ranks grant 20% attack speed; maximum rank grants 100% attack speed per stack.

## Acquisition

Secondary Surge drops from various Zariman and Void Cascade missions.

## Best on

This arcane works best on builds that exploit its triggering condition frequently.

## Usage

Secondary Surge activates automatically when its trigger condition is met during combat.

This arcane provides consistent value scaling throughout extended missions.
