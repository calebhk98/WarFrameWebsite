## Overview

Secondary Surge converts current Energy into a single-shot Damage Multiplier for the secondary weapon on Ability Cast. When an ability is cast, the next secondary weapon shot gains a multiplier that scales with the amount of Energy the warframe has at the time of the cast -- the more energy available when the ability fires, the stronger the next shot. The multiplier scales at 1x per 200 Energy, capped at the rank-dependent maximum.

At rank 5, the multiplier caps at x8 -- a warframe with 1600 or more Energy at cast time charges the next secondary shot to x8 total damage multiplier. The bonus applies to a single shot, then resets until the next ability cast.

## Effect

Each ability cast charges the next secondary weapon shot with a Damage Multiplier based on current Energy / 200 at the time of cast, up to the rank-dependent cap. At rank 0, the multiplier caps at x3 -- 600 Energy at cast yields x3 on the next shot. At rank 5, the multiplier caps at x8 -- 1600 Energy at cast yields x8 on the next shot. The charged shot applies to the next trigger pull on the secondary weapon, then the charge is consumed. If the player does not fire the secondary before the next ability cast, a new charge replaces the old one.

The damage multiplier scales the secondary weapon's full modded output by the multiplier value, making high-base-damage secondaries receive the greatest absolute benefit from a full x8 charge.

## Acquisition

Secondary Surge drops from various Zariman Ten-Zero missions and Void Cascade content at uncommon rarity. The arcane is accessible through standard Zariman progression after completing Angels of the Zariman. Warframe.market provides direct purchase options for targeted acquisition.

## Builds that use it

Secondary Surge is designed for high-energy builds that maintain large energy pools before ability casts. Frames with Primed Flow and high energy capacity (Nidus, Trinity, Wisp with large energy pools) can reach 1600+ Energy with energy management, enabling x8 charges on the next secondary shot consistently. The arcane rewards casting abilities while at high energy -- the trigger is the cast itself, not the energy spent, so expensive-ability builds that cast from full energy pools maximize the Secondary Surge multiplier.

High-damage single-shot secondaries benefit most from the x8 multiplier per shot. Pandero Prime, Pyrana Prime, and Opticor Vandal (if secondary) firing with an x8 charge deal extremely high burst damage on the charged shot. The single-shot charge mechanic makes Secondary Surge a burst tool rather than a sustained damage modifier.

## Notes

The current Energy at the moment of ability cast determines the multiplier -- the energy spent on the cast itself reduces the pool after the multiplier is calculated, not before. High-efficiency builds that spend less energy per cast retain more energy for subsequent charges. The multiplier applies to the next single secondary shot after the cast; the charge is consumed on the first trigger pull regardless of whether the shot hits an enemy. Multiple ability casts before firing the secondary reset the charge -- only the most recent cast's charge is active at any time.
