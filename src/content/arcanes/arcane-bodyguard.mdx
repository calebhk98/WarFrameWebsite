## Overview

Arcane Bodyguard is a warframe arcane designed to sustain companion units during combat. The arcane triggers after accumulating six melee kills within a 30-second window, healing the equipped companion (sentinel, kavat, or kubrow) for a scaling amount. At max rank, this arcane can restore 900 health to companions, providing consistent pet sustainability during extended missions. The arcane rewards aggressive melee playstyles while ensuring pet survivability.

The arcane tracks melee kills within a 30-second window and triggers healing when the threshold is met. The window resets upon trigger, allowing for sustained companion healing across long missions. Frames with high melee attack speed generate triggers more frequently.

## Acquisition

Arcane Bodyguard drops from void fissure missions in the Zariman void tileset. The arcane requires participation in public Void Fissure matches with a melee-focused playstyle to reliably obtain it. Farming groups benefit from coordinating melee loadouts to accelerate kill counts and trigger healing reliably. The arcane's usefulness scales significantly with mission duration and enemy density.

## Best On

Bodyguard excels on frames that rely on companion support during survival or endless missions. Frames like Khora, who synergizes with sentinel abilities, or Chroma with his dragon companion, benefit directly from sustained pet health. Melee-focused frames such as Excalibur, Ash, or Wukong generate kills rapidly enough to maintain consistent healing cycles, making them ideal hosts for this arcane.

Companion-dependent builds gain the most value, particularly in high-level content where pet survivability determines mission success. Non-melee-reliant frames generate kills too slowly to reliably trigger the arcane's effects.
