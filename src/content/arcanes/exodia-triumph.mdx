## Overview

Exodia Triumph is an uncommon Zaw arcane providing self-healing through critical heavy attacks. This arcane transforms Zaw weapons into sustain tools, enabling solo viability and extended missions through consistent health restoration during combat.

## Effect

When a Zaw heavy attack critically hits an enemy, there is a 15% chance at rank 0, scaling to 75% at rank 5, to restore 100-500 health to the wielder. At maximum rank, nearly every critical heavy hit triggers healing.

## Acquisition

Exodia Triumph drops as an uncommon reward from Void Fissure relic cracking at all refinement tiers. Dedicated fissure farming enables reliable acquisition.

## Best on

Exodia Triumph pairs with crit-focused Zaw builds:

- **High-crit Zaws** — sustained heavy-attack chains maintain permanent healing.
- **Solo endgame content** — self-sustain eliminates squad dependency.
- **Long void fissure runs** — healing uptime sustains extended endurance.

## Notes

Healing scales with damage resistance and armor. Tanky Warframes benefit most from the sustain. Heavy attack timing directly controls healing frequency. Stacks with other healing sources.
