## Overview

Exodia Triumph is a Zaw arcane that increases the Additional Combo Count Chance for the equipped Zaw weapon. The Melee Combo Counter in Warframe builds through hits and increments the multiplier used by Blood Rush, Weeping Wounds, and other combo-scaling mods. Additional Combo Count Chance means each hit has an increased probability of counting toward the combo, allowing builds to ramp their combo multiplier faster than the base mechanic alone.

At rank 3, Exodia Triumph grants +50% Additional Combo Count Chance. This allows Zaw builds to build combo to their target multiplier faster, which is especially valuable for builds using Blood Rush -- which scales critical chance with combo -- or Condition Overload -- which scales damage with status proc diversity.

## Effect

Each melee hit checks the Additional Combo Count Chance provided by Exodia Triumph on top of the base combo count probability. At rank 0, +12.5% Additional Combo Count Chance modestly accelerates combo building. At rank 3, +50% Additional Combo Count Chance substantially increases the rate at which the combo counter increments, allowing the multiplier to ramp more aggressively during combat.

The additional chance stacks with base combo mechanics, not multiplicatively. A weapon with a high base hit rate combined with Exodia Triumph's additional combo chance reaches target combo thresholds significantly faster than without the arcane.

## Acquisition

Exodia Triumph drops as an uncommon reward from Void Fissure missions when relics are cracked. The arcane appears in the reward pool across all relic refinement tiers -- Lith through Axi. Players who run Void Fissures regularly for Prime part farming encounter Exodia Triumph as an incidental drop. Multiple copies are needed to rank the arcane to 3. Warframe.market provides an alternative purchase route, with unranked copies trading at modest values as incidental Fissure drops.

## Builds that use it

Exodia Triumph is ideal for Zaw builds centered on combo scaling, particularly Blood Rush builds that depend on combo multiplier reaching high values to achieve significant critical chance bonuses. The faster combo ramp from Exodia Triumph means Blood Rush activates its critical chance bonus earlier in combat, reducing the warm-up period before the build operates at full effectiveness.

Condition Overload builds that use hit frequency to accumulate multiple status types also benefit, as each combo count opportunity that lands contributes to the multiplier while simultaneously applying status effects. Zaws with multiple hits per attack -- Zaw polearms with wide swings, multi-segment attack patterns -- generate more combo count checks per swing, making Exodia Triumph more impactful on multi-hit configurations than single-strike Zaw types.

## Notes

Exodia Triumph is a Zaw-exclusive arcane and can only be equipped in the Exodia slots of player-built Zaw melee weapons. The Additional Combo Count Chance is a separate roll from the base combo count per hit; both checks apply independently on each hit. Building Exodia Triumph stacks with Drifting Contact augment (which increases combo count chance) -- both bonuses accumulate additively. The arcane's maximum rank is 3, not 5, matching other Exodia arcanes which have a reduced rank ceiling compared to standard Warframe arcanes.
