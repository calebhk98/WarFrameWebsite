## Overview

Exodia Triumph is a Zaw arcane that provides self-healing when a Zaw heavy attack scores a critical hit. It is equipped in one of the two Exodia arcane slots available on any Zaw melee weapon and functions as a sustain tool in builds that rely on heavy-attack chains. The arcane is particularly relevant for Zaw builds that already invest in critical chance - Exodia Triumph requires a critical hit to trigger, so it synergizes naturally with high-crit-chance Zaw configurations rather than requiring additional investment to produce procs.

At maximum rank, Exodia Triumph provides a 75% chance to restore 500 health on each critical heavy attack. Against clustered enemies where heavy attack waves hit multiple targets, the healing potential scales with hit count. A wide-sweeping Zaw heavy attack that crits against five enemies at rank 5 may produce multiple healing procs, making Exodia Triumph disproportionately strong against dense enemy formations.

## Effect

When a Zaw weapon performs a heavy attack that critically hits an enemy, there is a percentage chance to restore health to the wielder. At rank 0, the proc chance is 15% and the health restored is 100 per trigger. At rank 5 the proc chance reaches 75% and restores 500 health per trigger. The health restoration is direct and bypasses the damage-gate mechanics that prevent rapid health regeneration from some other sources.

## Acquisition

Exodia Triumph drops as an uncommon reward from Void Fissure missions when relics are cracked. The arcane appears in the reward pool at all relic refinement tiers - Lith through Axi. Players who run Void Fissures regularly for Prime part farming have a chance of obtaining Exodia Triumph as a secondary drop. Multiple copies are needed to rank the arcane to 5, following the same upgrade path as other arcanes through the Mod Station.

The arcane can also be acquired through player trading. Unranked copies trade for modest amounts because fissure farming produces them as incidental drops. Ranked copies trade for higher values depending on the specific rank and market conditions.

## Builds that use it

Exodia Triumph is most effective in Zaw builds that combine high critical chance with heavy attack frequency.

Heavy attack-focused Zaw builds that prioritize critical chance mods - pairing Blood Rush, Organ Shatter, and a crit-boosting riven - generate the crit proc rate needed to trigger Exodia Triumph consistently. Builds with over 60% critical chance on heavy attacks produce enough procs to sustain meaningful healing in extended combat.

Solo endurance builds benefit from Exodia Triumph as a survivability fallback. Warframes that lack built-in healing (Atlas, Limbo, Gauss without augments) can use Exodia Triumph to cover health recovery needs without relying on a dedicated support frame in the squad.

## Notes

Exodia Triumph requires a critical hit to trigger - it does not proc on non-critical heavy attacks. The healing amount is a flat value and does not scale with the weapon's damage output, which means the arcane's relative value is higher on lower-damage Zaw builds than on one-shot configurations where health is rarely at risk. Combining Exodia Triumph with Exodia Brave - which restores energy on heavy attack kills - in both Exodia arcane slots produces a Zaw that self-sustains both health and energy independently.
