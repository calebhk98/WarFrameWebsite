## Overview

Molt Efficiency passively accumulates Ability Duration bonus while the warframe has active shields - the standard energy shield that absorbs incoming damage before it reaches health. Every second with shields active adds duration bonus up to the rank-determined cap: a maximum of 6% at rank 0, scaling to 36% at rank 5. The bonus persists as long as shields remain active, but the accumulation pauses when shields are fully depleted.

This design rewards shield preservation: frames that maintain their shields throughout combat continuously accumulate and sustain the maximum duration bonus. Frames that frequently lose their shields - through combat damage, intentional Shield Gate cycling, or Shield Break builds - see interrupted accumulation and may not reach the maximum cap during depleted phases.

At maximum rank, the arcane also grants one Arcane Revive charge per mission.

## Effect

At rank 0, the arcane generates +1% Ability Duration per second while shields are active, capped at +6% - reached after 6 seconds of uninterrupted shield coverage. At rank 5, +6% per second caps at +36% - reached after 6 seconds of uninterrupted shields. Both ranks reach their cap at 6 seconds of continuous shields, but the rank 5 cap is six times higher in absolute bonus.

The Ability Duration bonus reduces how quickly ability timers count down, extending toggle abilities, buff durations, and temporary effects. At +36%, an ability with a 30-second base duration extends to approximately 41 seconds under the Molt Efficiency bonus.

## Builds that use it

Molt Efficiency suits shield-preserving warframe builds that use toggle or duration-based abilities where extended ability time provides clear value. Harrow builds that maintain shields between Covenant casts sustain the full +36% bonus while their abilities remain active. Protea's kit - which includes shield-generation mechanics - maintains continuous shield coverage that keeps Molt Efficiency's accumulation uninterrupted.

Frames running Shield Gate strategies who deliberately allow shields to strip and regenerate see reduced efficiency from Molt compared to frames that passively sustain shields. Shield-tank builds on Revenant or Hildryn sustain shields continuously, making Molt Efficiency more reliable than on frames where combat routinely depletes shields.

## Notes

The accumulation pauses at zero shields - no bonus is generated while shields are completely depleted. As shields recover from regeneration, accumulation resumes. The +36% at rank 5 is additive with Continuity and Primed Continuity mods, making the total Ability Duration potentially very high on duration-stacking builds. The Arcane Revive at rank 5 functions identically to the consumable item and does not stack with additional Arcane Revive charges beyond total available charges. The bonus does not interact with overshields differently - overshields count as having active shields for accumulation purposes.
