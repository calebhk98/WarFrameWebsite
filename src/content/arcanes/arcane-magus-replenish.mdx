## Overview

Magus Replenish is an Operator arcane that provides energy replenishment to nearby Warframes when Void Sling is cast. This utility-focused arcane enables squad energy sustain, allowing ability-dependent Warframes to maintain offensive or defensive casting without energy starvation.

## Effect

Each Void Sling cast has a chance to restore energy to nearby Warframes. The proc chance scales from 15% at rank 0 to 75% at rank 5, providing reliable energy generation during active combat. When triggered, the arcane restores a small flat amount of energy to affected squad members.

## Acquisition

Magus Replenish drops as an uncommon reward from Void Cascade missions on Zariman. Early progression in void fissure content grants access to this supportive arcane.

## Best on

Magus Replenish is ideal for ability-spam squad compositions:

- **Energy-dependent Warframes** (Mag, Frost, Saryn) benefit from frequent energy replenishment.
- **Endless void cascades** — sustained energy generation supports long farming runs.
- **Squad synergy builds** — the operator becomes an energy support multiplier for the team.

## Notes

The arcane grants energy to nearby Warframes within affinity range but does not directly benefit the operator's own energy pool. Works best with operators who cast Void Sling frequently to maximize proc rate. Stacks with other energy generation sources.
