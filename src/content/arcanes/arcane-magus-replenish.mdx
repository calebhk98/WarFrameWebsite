## Overview

Magus Replenish generates warframe energy restoration events for nearby allies when the operator uses Void Sling. Each Sling checks a trigger chance, and on success, distributes energy to allied warframes within range. This makes the operator an active energy support unit during Void Sling-heavy movement phases, converting repositioning actions into periodic energy pulses for the squad.

Energy sustain is a persistent challenge in endgame content where ability-spam builds drain energy reserves faster than natural regeneration replenishes them. Magus Replenish provides a source that does not require dedicated energy-support warframes in the squad - an operator using Void Sling for standard mobility naturally generates energy restoration events without changing their movement pattern.

## Effect

Each Void Sling triggers a chance check. On success, nearby warframes receive an energy restoration. At rank 0, a 15% trigger chance fires roughly once every seven Void Slings. At rank 5, a 75% trigger chance fires three out of every four Void Slings - near-constant energy distribution during active Void Sling movement. The energy restoration amount is a flat value determined by the arcane's proc, distributed to all warframes within range on each successful trigger.

The restoration applies to warframe energy, not operator energy, supporting ability casting rather than amp fire.

## Acquisition

Magus Replenish drops as an uncommon reward from Void Cascade missions on Zariman Ten-Zero. The arcane is accessible after completing Angels of the Zariman. Regular Void Cascade participation accumulates Replenish copies without requiring targeted farming. Warframe.market provides a direct purchase option for players who prefer platinum acquisition over farming Zariman content.

## Builds that use it

Magus Replenish suits support operator builds designed for energy-dependent squad compositions in Zariman or other endgame content. Void Sling-active operators who move frequently between combat locations trigger Replenish multiple times per minute, distributing energy to warframes that spend their resources on abilities. Squad compositions running high-cost abilities - Nova's Molecular Prime, Wisp's Reservoirs refresh, Frost's multiple Snowglobes - benefit from the supplemental energy that extends ability uptime without requiring each player to invest in energy efficiency mods.

Solo operators in content where ability sustain is critical can use Replenish as a self-support mechanism for their own warframe, with each Void Sling movement restoring energy to the warframe they will return to.

## Notes

The energy restoration applies to warframe energy pools, not operator energy. The restoration amount per proc is a flat value and does not scale with ability strength or duration mods. Allies outside the Replenish range do not receive energy from the proc; the operator must be in range of each squadmate for the energy to distribute. Multiple operators each running Magus Replenish provide independent restoration events per Void Sling, potentially overlapping their energy pulses to allied frames that are within range of multiple operators simultaneously. The trigger chance applies per Void Sling cast regardless of how many enemies or allies are nearby.
