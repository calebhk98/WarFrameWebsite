## Overview

Arcane Victory is a self-healing mechanism triggered through successful precision kills, rewarding aggressive headshot gameplay with sustained survivability. When eliminating enemies with headshots, this arcane has a 75% chance to grant health regeneration lasting 9 seconds. At maximum rank, regeneration reaches 3% per second, restoring significant health totals during the buff window without requiring dedication to specific warframe abilities or mods. The 75% proc rate ensures near-constant uptime for skilled marksmen maintaining kill streaks.

Victory enables aggressive solo playstyles by converting offensive performance directly into defensive value. Dedicated headshot players achieve near-immortality through continuous kill-based healing, eliminating the need for dedicated health-recovery abilities. The arcane transforms skilled weapon usage into comprehensive survivability, rewarding precision with tangible durability gains.

## Acquisition

Arcane Victory drops from Duviri Zariman endless missions at Tier 1 and Tier 3 as legendary rewards, from Void Cascade skirmish missions at Veil nodes in Rotation C as a rare drop, and from Eidolon Teralyst captures. The multiple farming routes provide flexibility, allowing players to pursue Victory through their preferred endgame activity or combine several paths for faster acquisition.

## Best on

Precision-focused warframes like Limbo Prime, Ivara Prime, and Mag Prime synergize exceptionally well with Victory's mechanics. Weapons with high critical multipliers and accuracy like sniper rifles and bows pair perfectly, enabling consistent headshot kills that fuel the regeneration buff. Builds emphasizing both offense and self-sufficiency benefit most from the arcane's lifecycle mechanics.

## Notes

The arcane triggers only on headshot kills, not on regular kills from other damage types. The 75% proc rate is high, virtually guaranteeing buff uptime for players landing consistent headshots. The 9-second duration allows multiple kill streaks to refresh the buff before expiration. Health regeneration applies to the warframe pool, not shields, making it valuable for high-health frames with low shield stats.

## Sources

Arcane Victory is obtainable through Duviri Zariman endless missions, Void Cascade skirmish missions at Veil nodes, or Eidolon Teralyst captures. The variety of farming routes accommodates different playstyles and progression states.
