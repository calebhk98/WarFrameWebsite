## Overview

Arcane Awakening is a mobility-based Warframe arcane that grants ability power bonuses during dash movement. This arcane rewards active movement with ability scaling improvements, enabling extended ability-based damage phases during fast-paced void fissure encounters.

## Effect

When a Warframe dashes, the ability power of all equipped abilities increases by +20% at rank 0, scaling to +100% at rank 5 for 8 seconds. Frequent dashing maintains near-permanent ability buff uptime.

## Acquisition

Arcane Awakening drops as an uncommon reward from Void Fissure endless missions. Early progression through void content grants access to this ability-support arcane.

## Best on

Arcane Awakening is ideal for ability-spam Warframes:

- **Strength-based casters** (Saryn, Mesa, Frost) scale ability damage with power bonus.
- **Fast-dash mobility frames** (Nova, Loki, Zephyr) maintain permanent buff uptime.
- **Endless void fissures** — constant movement sustains ability scaling.

## Notes

Dash frequency directly controls buff uptime. Stationary builds gain minimal benefit. The arcane pairs well with ability-strength mods. Stacks additively with strength modifiers.
