## Overview

Magus Destruct adds secondary explosions to Void Blast hits, converting the melee-range operator attack into an area-of-effect burst that damages nearby enemies. Void Blast -- the close-range void melee strike in operator mode -- hits single targets at its base range, but Destruct's triggered explosions extend the damage zone to a 10-meter radius around each blast hit. This allows operators using Void Blast for armor stripping or crowd control purposes to simultaneously damage clustered enemies within the radius.

Void Blast has use cases beyond the combat tool -- Unairu school operators use it for armor stripping, and its hit generates various arcane trigger checks. Destruct adds damage output to these utility uses, enabling the operator to contribute area damage while performing the Void Blast for other purposes without requiring dedicated area attack inputs.

## Effect

Each Void Blast hit triggers a chance check. On success, a secondary explosion fires centered on the hit point, dealing void damage to all enemies within 10 meters. At rank 0, a 10% chance with 100 damage provides occasional area damage during Void Blast sequences. At rank 5, a 30% chance with 300 damage activates roughly once every three blasts, providing consistent area clearing during sustained Void Blast usage.

The 10-meter radius is fixed and does not scale with ability range mods or Focus school upgrades.

## Acquisition

Magus Destruct drops from Eidolon Hydrolyst capture at 8% and Zariman Void Fissure rewards at 2%. The 8% drop rate from Hydrolyst capture makes Destruct one of the more accessible Eidolon-source operator arcanes. Capturing (not killing) the Hydrolyst at the end of a Tridolon hunt provides the drop. Warframe.market provides an alternative for players who prefer platinum acquisition.

## Builds that use it

Magus Destruct suits operator builds that use Void Blast for utility purposes -- Unairu school armor stripping, Magus-arcane synergy triggers -- and want to extract additional damage value from each blast without changing their combat pattern. Operators fighting grouped Void Manifestations in Zariman content who use Void Blast in melee range can trigger Destruct's explosion to damage surrounding manifestations during the blast. The area damage supplements the operator's amp fire, particularly in content where multiple close-range enemies cluster around a central target.

## Notes

The explosion is void damage type, which benefits from void damage bonuses active on the operator. The explosion is centered on the hit target, not on the operator -- enemies within 10 meters of the hit target receive the damage, not enemies within 10 meters of the operator's position. The Void Blast must successfully connect with a target to trigger Destruct's check; misses do not generate explosion checks. The 10-meter radius provides meaningful coverage in indoor Zariman environments where enemies cluster in corridors and rooms.
