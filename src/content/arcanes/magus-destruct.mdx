## Overview

Magus Destruct enhances Void Blast with a chance to trigger secondary explosions. This operator arcane converts single-target void beam attacks into area-of-effect clearing tools, making it invaluable for operators who need to handle grouped enemies efficiently.

## Effects per rank

| Rank | Trigger Chance | Explosion Damage | Area Radius |
|------|----------------|------------------|------------|
| 0    | 10%            | 100               | 10m        |
| 1    | 15%            | 140               | 10m        |
| 2    | 20%            | 180               | 10m        |
| 3    | 25%            | 220               | 10m        |
| 4    | 27%            | 260               | 10m        |
| 5    | 30%            | 300               | 10m        |

Each void blast has an independent roll for triggering the secondary explosion, allowing skilled void beam operators to generate consistent area damage when hitting enemies in succession.

## Acquisition

Magus Destruct drops from Eidolon Hydrolyst with an 8% chance, making it one of the more frequently available operator arcanes from plains farming. Zariman Void Fissure missions also reward this arcane with lower frequency, providing an alternative path for players who prefer fissure content.

To obtain this arcane, operators need access to Plains of Eidolon and either a squad for eidolon hunting or participation in void fissure excavations. The 8% drop rate makes this one of the more obtainable specialized operator arcanes.

## Best on

Magus Destruct excels on operators using void beam focus builds during fissure missions where crowd control becomes critical. The area damage enables soloists and squads to clear void manifestations efficiently while farming relic drops, making it essential for endgame operator farming strategies.

## Sources

Eidolon captures remain the primary farming route, with Zariman fissures providing supplementary drops. Regular void fissure participation yields this arcane naturally over time, making it a reliable choice for operators transitioning into endgame content.
