## Overview

Secondary Shiver increases the damage that enemies with Cold Status take from any source. Each Cold Status stack on an enemy makes them take additional damage per hit, stacking as Cold Status stacks accumulate. The more Cold Status stacks an enemy has, the more damage they take from all subsequent attacks while those stacks are active. This makes Secondary Shiver a damage amplification arcane rather than a direct damage arcane -- it debuffs the enemy to take more damage from everything, not just from the secondary weapon.

At rank 5, each Cold Status stack on an enemy causes them to take +45% additional damage. With multiple Cold Status stacks active, the damage amplification becomes substantial.

## Effect

Each Cold Status stack applied to an enemy increases the damage that enemy receives by the listed percentage per stack. At rank 0, each Cold Status stack makes the enemy take +7.5% additional damage from all sources. At rank 5, each Cold Status stack makes the enemy take +45% additional damage. An enemy with 4 Cold Status stacks at rank 5 takes 180% additional damage from all attacks -- nearly tripling effective incoming damage before other modifiers.

The bonus damage amplification applies to all damage sources while Cold Status stacks are active -- secondary weapon fire, primary weapon shots, melee attacks, ability damage, and companion damage all deal amplified damage to the Cold-afflicted enemy.

## Acquisition

Secondary Shiver drops from various Zariman Ten-Zero missions and Void Cascade content at uncommon rarity. The arcane is accessible through standard Zariman progression after completing Angels of the Zariman. Warframe.market provides direct purchase options for targeted acquisition.

## Builds that use it

Secondary Shiver is designed for Cold-element secondary weapon builds paired with high-damage primary, melee, or ability follow-up attacks that exploit the damage amplification. The secondary weapon applies Cold Status stacks to the target, and then the player switches to primary weapons, melee, or ability usage to take advantage of the damage bonus. Weapons with high-rate Cold Status application -- secondaries with Cold element modding and high status chance -- build Cold stacks quickly on targets.

The arcane pairs with abilities that apply Cold status (Vauban's Vortex pulling enemies together while Cold stacks accumulate, Frost's Freeze or Snow Globe slowing enemies with Cold procs) to prime enemies before follow-up weapon damage. Builds focused on maximizing Cold Status accumulation through ability and weapon combinations compound Secondary Shiver's per-stack bonus.

## Notes

Secondary Shiver amplifies damage taken by the enemy based on how many Cold Status stacks they carry at the time of the hit. The amplification is not a damage-to-the-player bonus -- it is a vulnerability debuff on the enemy. Multiple Cold Status sources stacking on the same target (from both the secondary weapon and from ability Cold procs) increase the Cold Status stack count and therefore increase the damage vulnerability multiplier. The bonus applies to all damage dealt to the Cold-affected enemy while the status is active; when Cold Status expires, the vulnerability is removed until Cold is reapplied.
