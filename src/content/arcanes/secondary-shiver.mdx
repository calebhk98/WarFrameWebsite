## Overview

Secondary Shiver applies cold-based slowdown when secondary cold procs trigger. On every cold status effect from secondary weapons, nearby enemies experience movement slowing. This arcane enables crowd control through secondary weapons. Early ranks slow enemies by 30%; maximum rank slows by 75%.

## Acquisition

Secondary Shiver drops from various Zariman and Void Cascade missions.

## Best on

This arcane works best on builds that exploit its triggering condition frequently.

## Usage

Secondary Shiver activates automatically when its trigger condition is met during combat.

## Notes

This arcane provides consistent value scaling throughout extended missions.
