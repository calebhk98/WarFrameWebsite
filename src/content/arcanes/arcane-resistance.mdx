## Overview

Arcane Resistance provides passive protection against toxin damage and status effects, a core threat in mid-to-late-game content. The arcane grants a percentage-based chance to completely negate incoming toxin procs, scaling from 17% at rank 0 to 102% at maximum rank. Ranking past 100% allows the arcane to trigger its effect multiple times per application, effectively doubling protection at the highest level. This defensive utility makes Resistance invaluable for players tackling status-heavy enemy compositions without extensive armor stacking.

Toxin is one of the most dangerous status effects in Warframe due to its damage-over-time mechanics and inability to be cleansed passively. Arcane Resistance transforms toxic encounters from dangerous to manageable, allowing warframes with lower base toxin resistance to thrive in Corpus-heavy missions and against infested enemies that frequently apply the status.

## Acquisition

Arcane Resistance drops from Duviri Zariman endless missions at Tier 1 and Tier 3 as legendary rewards, from Void Cascade skirmish missions at Veil nodes in Rotation C as a rare drop, and from Eidolon Teralyst captures. The Eidolon route offers a secondary source for players already farming eidolons, diversifying farming options compared to some newer arcanes.

## Best on

Warframes with lower base toxin resistance or those focusing on pure damage output benefit most from Resistance. Low-armor frames like Nova Prime, Banshee Prime, and Protea Prime gain effective survivalability without sacrificing build slots for defensive mods. The arcane pairs well with status-immunity-focused frames that already manage other damage types, rounding out their defensive profile.

## Notes

At maximum rank, the 102% resistance rate means the arcane triggers twice on average per toxin application, providing near-immunity. The effect is passive and automatic, requiring no input from the player. Status procs from different sources can be resisted independently, allowing one proc to be blocked while another applies. The arcane stacks with frame-specific toxin resistances and mods for layered defense.

## Sources

Arcane Resistance is obtainable through Duviri Zariman endless missions, Void Cascade skirmish missions at Veil nodes, or Eidolon Teralyst captures. The three farming routes provide flexibility, allowing players to pursue the arcane through their preferred endgame activity.
