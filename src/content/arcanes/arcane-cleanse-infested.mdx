## Overview

Arcane Cleanse Infested is a faction-specific rare arcane providing status immunity against Infested enemy types. This specialized arcane is valuable for Deimos farming and Infested void cascade waves.

## Effect

When fighting Infested enemies, the Warframe has a 20% chance at rank 0, scaling to 100% at rank 5, to automatically cleanse negative status effects. At maximum rank, all Infested procs are permanently prevented.

## Acquisition

Arcane Cleanse Infested drops as a rare reward from Zariman Void Cascade waves with Infested enemies. Infested-heavy void cascade farming provides access.

## Best on

Arcane Cleanse Infested is essential for Infested content:

- **Deimos void cascades** — counters Infested toxin and poison.
- **Infested-wave void fissures** — maintains full status immunity.
- **Orokin Void farming** — Infested procs are negated entirely.

## Notes

Only applies to Infested-applied status effects. Grineer and Corpus procs are unaffected. Factional content farming requires specific enemy selection. Stacks with other status-resistance sources.
