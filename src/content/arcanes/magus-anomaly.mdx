## Overview

Magus Anomaly is an Operator arcane that pulls nearby enemies toward the warframe whenever the operator enters via Transference. Enemies within the effect radius are violently drawn toward the warframe's position, consolidating scattered enemy forces into grouped clusters optimal for area-of-effect damage. This arcane transforms operator reentry into an offensive grouping tool, particularly valuable for warframes with strong area damage (Saryn, Equinox, Volt, Loki) that benefit from concentrated enemy positioning.

Anomaly's primary strength lies in its guaranteed trigger on every transference and its ability to consolidate threats automatically without requiring warframe ability usage. Unlike crowd-control arcanes that immobilize enemies, Anomaly focuses them into kill zones, synergizing naturally with explosive and area-damage playstyles. The arcane was adjusted in Update 31.0.2 to prevent pulling certain boss-type enemies, limiting its application in dedicated boss encounters but keeping it relevant for standard star chart and squad-based endgame content.

The pull effect activates whenever the operator leaves Void Mode and returns to the warframe, triggering multiple times throughout extended missions. Enemies pulled toward the warframe stack on top of each other, creating dense clusters where area damage is maximized. Boss-type enemies (Eidolons, Ancient Healers, Nullifier Crewmen) are immune to the pull, preventing cheeses but allowing normal applications in regular combat.

## Acquisition

Magus Anomaly is purchased from Vox Solaris, the Venus-based operator syndicate faction. Reaching Rank 3 (Conductor) with Vox Solaris requires approximately 15,000 Standing, placing Anomaly slightly later in the operator progression curve compared to introductory arcanes. The arcane's availability increases significantly once players reach this standing tier, with regular vendor access thereafter. Pre-ranked copies trade for 50--100 platinum on player markets, depending on current supply and demand from Zariman farming communities.

## Best on

Area-damage warframes (Saryn, Equinox, Volt Prime, Loki Prime) leverage Magus Anomaly's grouping to maximize kill potential with explosive abilities. Weapon-focused builds using heavy AOE primaries (Kuva Ayanga, Torid, Zarr) benefit significantly from consolidated enemy clusters, reducing the spray angle needed for optimal damage. Squad-based endless missions employ Anomaly to organize enemy positions, preventing scattered threats from overrunning teammates. Farming builds pair Anomaly with frames specializing in large-scale clear, turning operator transference cycles into reliable damage multipliers.

Conversely, single-target or defense-oriented frames (Trinity, Mesa, Hildryn) may deprioritize Anomaly in favor of utility arcanes.

## Builds that use it

Zariman void cascade builds stack Magus Anomaly alongside Magus Repair to combine grouping and survivability. Wave-clear farming builds use Anomaly as the cornerstone grouping mechanic, enabling efficient AOE clear across enemy spawn waves. Elite Sanctuary Onslaught builds employ Anomaly to consolidate spawning enemies, preventing scattered threats from splitting squad attention. Survival endless builds leverage the arcane to keep enemy forces bunched around the survival pod, enabling predictable defense positioning and concentrated CC deployment.

## Notes

Magus Anomaly activates whenever the operator leaves any state and re-enters the warframe, including exiting Void Mode and ability-casting transference toggles. The pull effect is instantaneous and does not interrupt enemy attacks; enemies pulled toward the warframe maintain their attack patterns and may still damage the warframe. Boss-type enemies, Condor Dropships, Eidolons, and other immune enemies are unaffected by the pull entirely, requiring alternative crowd control for encounters featuring these threats. The arcane's trigger rate makes it reliable for farming content but less impactful in missions requiring sustained high-enemy-difficulty engagement.
