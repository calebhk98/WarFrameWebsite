## Overview

Arcane Bellicose boosts critical chance on successful headshots. This warframe arcane rewards precision marksmanship with sustained damage scaling during void fissure content.

| Rank | Headshot Chance | Crit Bonus | Duration |
|------|-----------------|-----------|----------|
| 0    | 15%             | +25%      | 10s      |
| 1    | 22%             | +35%      | 10s      |
| 2    | 30%             | +45%      | 10s      |
| 3    | 37%             | +55%      | 10s      |
| 4    | 41%             | +65%      | 10s      |
| 5    | 45%             | +75%      | 10s      |

Critical chance scaling stacks multiplicatively with existing crit mods and crit-scaling abilities, enabling explosive damage output during extended void fissure waves.

## Acquisition

Arcane Bellicose drops from Zariman Void Fissure (4%) and Void Cascade rotation B (2%). Regular zariman void farming yields this arcane naturally.

## Best on

Arcane Bellicose excels on precision-focused frames with high-crit primary weapons. The bonus damage enables headshot-farming builds to achieve sustained DPS scaling.
