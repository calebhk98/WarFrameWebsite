## Overview

Arcane Aerial is a Warframe arcane that grants a temporary accuracy and critical chance bonus each time the Warframe performs a bullet jump. It is installed in one of the Warframe's arcane slots and rewards playstyles that integrate bullet jumping into combat movement rather than using parkour only for traversal. The arcane is notable for providing a +100% critical chance bonus at rank 5, which is among the larger flat critical chance additions available in the arcane system and significantly affects weapons that are close to or above the 100% crit threshold.

The proc condition -- a bullet jump -- is freely available with no cooldown and costs no energy, making Arcane Aerial one of the easiest arcanes to maintain active uptime on. Players who naturally bullet jump between combat positions maintain the buff almost continuously, as the 5-second duration covers the time between typical jump intervals in active combat.

## Effect

Performing a bullet jump triggers the arcane and applies a simultaneous accuracy buff and critical chance buff. The accuracy buff starts at +30% at rank 0 and scales to +150% at rank 5. The critical chance buff starts at +20% at rank 0 and scales to +100% at rank 5. Both buffs share the same 5-second duration at all ranks. Performing another bullet jump before the timer expires refreshes both buffs.

The accuracy bonus affects the weapon's projectile spread and hipfire accuracy, benefiting weapons that degrade in accuracy during sustained fire. The critical chance bonus stacks additively with weapon mods and other critical chance bonuses.

## Acquisition

Arcane Aerial drops as a rare reward from Void Fissure endless missions at rotation C. Void Interception, Void Survival, and Void Defense missions that run to rotation C (30 minutes in Survival, round 4 and every fourth round thereafter in Interception) have a chance to produce this arcane on top of the standard relic reward. Because rare rotation-C drops have lower occurrence rates than common or uncommon rewards, consistent acquisition requires multiple extended fissure runs. The arcane can be traded between players.

## Builds that use it

Arcane Aerial benefits weapon builds that rely on critical hits and that operate in playstyles where bullet jumping is part of the combat rhythm.

Crit-focused primary builds on frames like Rhino, Nezha, or Nova -- where movement is natural but not particularly fast -- gain the crit bonus by bullet jumping at the start of each engagement and periodically refreshing it during repositioning. Weapons with a base critical chance of 20-30% that are already running Point Strike see meaningful pushes toward orange-crit thresholds when Arcane Aerial's +100% is active alongside standard crit mods.

Parkour-intensive frames like Zephyr or Loki maintain near-permanent buff uptime because their natural movement cadence includes frequent aerial maneuvers that trigger the proc between every other target engagement.

## Notes

The 5-second duration means that stationary combat -- standing in place firing at a fixed target -- will not maintain the buff after the initial trigger expires. The arcane works best for players who move between targets rather than holding a static firing position. The accuracy bonus has minimal practical impact on hitscan weapons with already-high base accuracy, but for projectile weapons with wide fire cones, the accuracy improvement is genuinely noticeable for hipfire engagements.
