## Overview

Arcane Aerial is a rare movement-based Warframe arcane granting accuracy and crit bonuses whenever the Warframe performs a bullet jump. This arcane rewards mobility-focused playstyles, enabling aggressive weapon accuracy and crit scaling during fast-paced combat in void fissures and endgame content.

## Effect

Each bullet jump grants the Warframe a temporary accuracy and critical chance buff lasting 5 seconds. Accuracy increases from +30% at rank 0 to +150% at rank 5, while critical chance gains +20% to +100%. Frequent bullet jumping maintains nearly permanent uptime.

## Acquisition

Arcane Aerial drops as a rare reward from Void Fissure endless missions at rotation C. Dedicated void fissure farming enables reliable acquisition.

## Best on

Arcane Aerial synergizes with mobility and weapon-focused builds:

- **Parkour-heavy Warframes** (Zephyr, Loki, Nova) maintain permanent buff uptime.
- **Crit-based weapons** — the crit bonus amplifies damage scaling exponentially.
- **Endless void fissures** — constant mobility maintains accuracy and crit.

## Notes

Bullet jump frequency directly controls buff uptime. Slow-moving builds gain minimal benefit. The arcane pairs exceptionally well with crit-based primary weapons. Stacks multiplicatively with weapon crit mods.
