## Overview

Longbow Sharpshot is a legendary bow-specific arcane that grants massive critical chance and critical multiplier bonuses on charged shots. The dual bonus transforms moderate-crit bows into extreme damage dealers during the 10-second buff window, with both crit chance and multiplier scaling simultaneously. At rank 5, the +150% crit chance and x3.0 multiplier bonus enable even bows with 25% base crit to reach near-certain red crit territory during the window.

The charged shot trigger is inherent to bow mechanics -- all bows require holding fire to reach maximum power -- making Sharpshot compatible with any bow without requiring playstyle adjustments. Players who already aim for precise charged shots naturally activate Sharpshot's trigger on every well-timed release.

## Effect

Each charged shot from a bow weapon checks the Sharpshot trigger chance. On success, both critical chance and critical multiplier bonuses activate simultaneously for 10 seconds. At rank 0, +50% crit chance and x1.5 multiplier are meaningful improvements; at rank 5, +150% crit chance and x3.0 multiplier create extreme critical hit values. The multiplier bonus applies on top of existing Vital Sense and other critical multiplier mods, enabling bow weapons to reach crit multiplier values of 5x-8x during active windows.

The 10-second buff duration covers multiple charged shots at standard charge speeds, maintaining the bonus across sequential targets in a combat sequence.

## Acquisition

Longbow Sharpshot is a legendary bow arcane dropping from Zariman Void Fissure at 5% and Void Cascade rotation B at 2%. The 5% drop rate from Void Fissure is among the higher rates for legendary arcanes, making Sharpshot more accessible than many comparable tier arcanes. Warframe.market lists Sharpshot at elevated plat values reflecting its power level and bow-specific niche.

## Builds that use it

Longbow Sharpshot is essential for crit-focused bow builds. The Dread, Paris Prime, and Lenz with crit-focused modding reach guaranteed orange and red crits during Sharpshot windows, enabling single-shot eliminations on heavily armored enemies. Builds using Amalgam Serration (primary damage mod) alongside Sharpshot's crit bonus push bow damage to extreme single-hit values during the buff. Warframe abilities that immobilize enemies (Rhino Stomp, Vauban Bastille) create opportunity windows for multiple charged Sharpshot-buffed headshots during the stasis period.

## Notes

The trigger fires on completing a charged shot, not on initiating the charge. Holding without releasing does not trigger Sharpshot; completing the charged release does. The 30% trigger chance at rank 5 means approximately one in three charged shots activates the buff. During a 10-second buff window, subsequent charged shots that would trigger Sharpshot simply refresh the duration rather than stacking additional crit chance. Crit chance above 100% produces orange crits (2x multiplier); above 200% produces red crits (3x multiplier) before Sharpshot's bonus is applied.
