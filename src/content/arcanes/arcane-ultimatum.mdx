## Overview

Arcane Ultimatum rewards melee finisher executions with significant armor scaling, transforming tactical finishing moves into defensive powerups. When executing a finisher kill with melee weapons, the arcane grants a substantial armor bonus lasting 45 seconds. At maximum rank, finisher kills grant 1200 armor, stacking multiplicatively with existing armor values to create dramatically increased damage reduction. The long 45-second duration allows players to maintain near-permanent buff uptime during normal combat pacing where finishers occur regularly against weaker enemies and wounded high-priority targets.

Ultimatum converts skill-based finishing gameplay into comprehensive defensive scaling, enabling aggressive playstyles where players deliberately trigger finishers as part of their combat rotation. Finishers in Warframe are inherently risky mechanics that leave players vulnerable, making the defensive reward particularly valuable for managing survivability during execution windows.

## Acquisition

Arcane Ultimatum drops from Duviri Zariman endless missions at Tier 1 and Tier 3 as legendary rewards, or from Void Cascade skirmish missions at Veil and Neptune nodes in Rotation C as rare drops. The Duviri route offers higher drop rates for legendary rarity, while skirmish missions provide more frequent access through earlier rotation phases.

## Best on

Warframes specializing in finisher-based gameplay like Ash Prime, Garuda Prime, and Nidus Prime benefit tremendously from Ultimatum's mechanics. Melee builds incorporating finisher-acceleration mods or abilities unlock the arcane's potential, transforming offense into defense. High-armor frames like Rhino Prime gain multiplicative benefits, with Ultimatum bonuses amplifying existing armor values for exponential damage reduction.

## Notes

The arcane triggers only on finisher executions, not regular melee kills. Finishers occur when enemies are sufficiently wounded and stand near the player, requiring deliberate positioning and tactical awareness. The 45-second duration is long enough to maintain buff uptime during extended combat sequences. Armor bonuses stack multiplicatively with base armor, meaning high-armor frames gain significantly larger effective health increases than low-armor frames receiving identical numeric bonuses.

## Sources

Arcane Ultimatum is obtainable through Duviri Zariman endless missions or Void Cascade skirmish missions at Veil and Neptune nodes. The Duviri route offers the best farming rates for players seeking consistent legendary drops.
