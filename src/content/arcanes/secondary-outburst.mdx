## Overview

Secondary Outburst converts the melee Combo Multiplier into Critical Chance and Critical Damage bonuses on the secondary weapon when the player switches from melee to secondary. On weapon swap, all accumulated Combo Multipliers are consumed and each consumed Combo unit provides the listed bonus to both Critical Chance and Critical Damage for 30 seconds. This creates a powerful melee-to-secondary handoff: build high melee combo, then swap to the secondary weapon to deliver amplified critical hits with the accumulated combo investment.

At rank 5, each consumed Combo Multiplier provides +20% Critical Chance and +20% Critical Damage to the secondary weapon for 30 seconds. With a high melee combo of 12x (12 combo units consumed), the secondary gains +240% Critical Chance and +240% Critical Damage -- a massive boost that dramatically shifts critical hit performance for the 30-second window.

## Effect

When the player swaps to the secondary weapon from melee (or from any weapon with an active melee combo), the current Combo Multiplier count is consumed entirely. Each consumed combo unit adds the listed bonus to both Critical Chance and Critical Damage for 30 seconds. At rank 0, each combo unit adds +5% to both stats. At rank 5, each combo unit adds +20% to both stats. The 30-second window is long enough to use the secondary weapon extensively before the buff expires, and the melee combo must be rebuilt before the next swap can apply a fresh boost.

Higher melee combo counts at the time of swap yield higher total bonuses. Builds that drive melee combo high (Blood Rush, Drifting Contact, spin attack combos) before swapping maximize the Secondary Outburst conversion.

## Acquisition

Secondary Outburst drops from various Zariman Ten-Zero missions and Void Cascade content at uncommon rarity. The arcane is accessible through standard Zariman progression after completing Angels of the Zariman. Warframe.market provides direct purchase options for targeted acquisition.

## Builds that use it

Secondary Outburst is purpose-built for melee-to-secondary hybrid builds. Melee builds using Drifting Contact and Blood Rush build high combo quickly during initial melee engagement, then swap to the secondary weapon at peak combo to apply the converted Critical Chance and Damage bonus. A combo of 10x at rank 5 provides +200% Critical Chance and +200% Critical Damage on the secondary -- enough to guarantee red crits on most secondary weapons with moderate base critical chance.

The 30-second window allows extensive secondary weapon use after the swap. Precision secondaries (Pyrana Prime, Pandero, Knell) that deal high damage per shot benefit most from the amplified critical multiplier, delivering concentrated burst damage during the converted-combo window.

## Notes

The conversion consumes all Combo Multiplier at the moment of weapon swap -- the melee combo counter resets to zero. The player must rebuild melee combo through melee combat before another swap can apply a fresh Secondary Outburst bonus. The buff applies to the secondary weapon's critical stats for the full 30-second duration regardless of whether the player switches back to melee during that window. Each swap event checks the current combo count at that moment; swapping at low combo yields low bonus, so timing the swap to peak combo maximizes the Secondary Outburst benefit.
