## Overview

Secondary Outburst creates explosions on secondary weapon weak point hits. On every weak point critical hit with a secondary weapon, an area explosion triggers dealing weapon damage. This arcane rewards precision secondary play. Early ranks deal 50% weapon damage in 6m radius; maximum rank deals 250% damage in 12m radius.

## Acquisition

Secondary Outburst drops from various Zariman and Void Cascade missions.

## Best on

This arcane works best on builds that exploit its triggering condition frequently.

## Usage

Secondary Outburst activates automatically when its trigger condition is met during combat.

This arcane provides consistent value scaling throughout extended missions.
