## Overview

Theorem Demulcent is a warframe arcane that synergizes with Residual Arcanes, a unique mechanic introduced in the Zariman update that creates persistent damage zones. Standing in these zones activates a stacking weapon damage buff that persists even after leaving the zone, creating powerful offensive momentum during extended combat. The arcane's design encourages mobility around zone positioning while maintaining offensive damage scaling.

The damage stacking mechanic allows players to build up to 15 stacks at 2-12% damage per second (depending on rank), reaching up to 180% weapon damage multiplier before modifiers. At higher ranks, additional arcane revives enhance survivability, making Theorem Demulcent a flexible option for survivability-focused builds that still amplify damage output significantly. The 20-second persistence outside zones ensures the buff remains active during repositioning phases.

The stacking mechanism resets when leaving a Residual Arcane zone, but the current stacks persist for 20 seconds before decaying. This design rewards active zone usage during combat while allowing temporary repositioning without losing accumulated buffs entirely. The arcane revive bonus at higher ranks provides valuable insurance during high-difficulty content.

## Acquisition

Theorem Demulcent drops exclusively from Deimos Isolation Vault encounters in Cambion Drift, with rotation C providing the drop table. The arcane appears across all difficulty levels (30-40, 40-50, 50-60) with consistent 25% uncommon-rarity drop rates and 50% common-rarity rates. Farming multiple vault runs efficiently accumulates the arcane towards higher ranks.

Players seeking rapid progression should prioritize high-level vaults (50-60) for faster farm cycles, while newer players can access lower-difficulty versions. Coordinating with squad mates to optimize void fissure farming also yields void traces for relic upgrades during the same missions.

## Best on

Theorem Demulcent pairs exceptionally well with warframes capable of creating or synergizing with Residual Arcane zones. Frames like Gauss (through speed/momentum gameplay), Wisp (through teleport pads), or dedicated zone-control abilities maximize zone duration and stack generation. Weapons with high attack speed or fire rate benefit most from the damage scaling, as more weapon actions occur during peak stacking phases.

Builds emphasizing duration and range of ability zones amplify Theorem Demulcent's effectiveness. The arcane rewards aggressive playstyles that maintain proximity to zone centers while dealing damage, creating dynamic moment-to-moment decisions about positioning versus damage scaling.
