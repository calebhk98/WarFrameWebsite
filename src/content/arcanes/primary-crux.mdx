## Overview

Primary Crux is a weapon-exclusive arcane that rewards precise accuracy and weak point targeting. On every successful weak point hit with a primary weapon, the arcane grants a temporary buff window that increases both status chance and ammunition efficiency. This arcane synergizes exceptionally well with precision-focused weapon builds and frames that amplify status effect application, such as Saryn and Mag.

The dual benefits of status scaling and ammo conservation make Primary Crux valuable for both sustained DPS builds and status-overflow strategies. Early ranks provide modest scaling; maximum rank yields 30% status chance plus 6% ammo efficiency per stack, enabling players to achieve 300% status with ten stacks while consuming ammunition far more conservatively. The 10-second duration and stackable mechanic reward continued precision shooting.

## Acquisition

Primary Crux drops from Zariman tileset mission caches and elite vault completions at rare rarity. The H-09 Apex and H-09 Efervon Tank nodes offer the best acquisition rates among standard Zariman missions. Hoellvania bounty caches in void-touched regions provide secondary drop sources at uncommon rarity. Players should prioritize high-difficulty Zariman endless runs or vault-opening skirmishes for efficient farming of max-rank copies.

## Best on

Precision-focused primary weapons with high critical chance and magazine capacity synergize best with Crux. Soma Prime, Zariman-era rifles, and sniper weapons with weak point multipliers benefit from the stacked status scaling. Frames like Lavos and Nekros that amplify status application turn this arcane into a exponential damage modifier.

## Usage

Primary Crux activates automatically whenever a weak point critical hit lands on an enemy. The buff stacks independently up to 10 times, with each stack refreshing the 10-second timer. Continuous precision shots against weak points maintain buff uptime, allowing disciplined players to sustain near-maximum stacking throughout extended engagements. Missed shots do not penalize the player; buff stacks persist for the full duration.

## Notes

Weak point hits include headshots and critical hits against weak spots such as Corpus drone cores and Grineer Heavy Gunner chest plates. The arcane does not activate on body shots or environmental damage. Ammo efficiency stacking is particularly effective with weapons that have large magazine pools, enabling mission completion without ammunition pickups.

## Sources

Primary Crux is restricted to Zariman-themed endgame content. Players seeking to fully rank multiple copies for different weapons should engage with extended Zariman void fissure sessions and vault-heavy bounty chains.
