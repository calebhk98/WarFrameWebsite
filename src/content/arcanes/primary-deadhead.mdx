## Overview

Primary Deadhead is a weapon arcane for primary rifles that rewards precision headshot elimination. Each headshot kill grants a stacking damage bonus lasting 24 seconds, with a maximum of 3 stacks. At rank 5, this yields 360% total damage bonus (120% per stack), making precision play exponentially rewarding. The arcane additionally provides 30% headshot multiplier bonus and 50% weapon recoil reduction at max rank, dramatically enhancing accuracy-focused builds and enabling sustained precision gameplay.

The 24-second buff window far exceeds most weapon arcanes, allowing stacks to accumulate passively across extended engagements. The recoil reduction dramatically improves sustained accuracy with rapid-fire primary weapons, creating a synergistic bonus loop. The headshot multiplier bonus compounds with weapon mods and frame abilities.

## Acquisition

Primary Deadhead drops from Zariman void fissure missions as a rare reward for precision-focused players. The arcane requires consistent headshot kills to farm efficiently, demanding proficiency with primary weapons. Public void fissure groups benefit from having stable aiming conditions and low-latency connections to maximize headshot detection. Sniper rifles and precise assault rifles accelerate farming progression.

## Best On

Primary Deadhead excels with precision rifles such as the Latron Prime, Rubico Prime, or Zarr. Frames that enhance weapon accuracy like Harrow with his steady aim bonus or Chroma with vex armor scaling gain exceptional synergies. Warframes with headshot-multiplying mods or abilities transform this arcane into exponential damage scaling. Slow-firing but high-damage primaries benefit maximally from the extended buff duration and recoil reduction.

Fast-firing weapons with high spread benefit moderately due to reduced headshot consistency. Weapons without natural accuracy curves struggle to maintain triple-stack uptime.
