## Overview

Arcane Ice provides passive resistance to heat status effects, reducing the frequency at which fire procs successfully apply to the warframe. Heat status causes a panic reaction that forces the affected target to stop and pat out flames, briefly interrupting all actions including movement, attacking, and ability casting. In high-difficulty content where heat procs from Scorches, fire Eximus enemies, or Corpus electrical-heat units occur frequently, heat panic interrupts can compromise defensive ability uptime and offensive momentum.

At rank 5, the 102% resistance chance provides complete immunity to heat status from external sources, eliminating the panic interruption from enemy attacks. Frames that rely on maintaining ability casts without interruption -- channeled abilities, sustained ability sequences -- particularly benefit from heat immunity in content where enemies apply heat status at high rates. The bonus Arcane Revive at maximum rank provides an emergency self-revive charge alongside the heat protection.

## Effect

Arcane Ice passively resists heat status application each time a heat proc attempt occurs against the warframe. At rank 0, a 17% resistance chance prevents roughly one in six heat procs. At rank 5, 102% resistance provides complete immunity to heat status from external sources, plus one Arcane Revive charge for self-revival during missions.

The heat panic prevention is particularly valuable for melee-range combat where Grineer Scorches and fire Eximus units apply heat procs on contact.

## Builds that use it

Arcane Ice suits builds in content featuring high-frequency heat proc sources -- Grineer Scorches in Kuva Fortress missions, fire Eximus enemies in endgame survival, Corpus Hyena Pack enemies that apply heat. Frames casting channeled abilities during melee-range engagement benefit from heat panic immunity, allowing uninterrupted ability usage during close-range combat.

## Notes

Heat resistance prevents the panic interrupt and heat damage-over-time from status application. It does not prevent damage from heat damage type weapons -- only the status proc component is resisted. The arcane provides no protection against blast, radiation, or other combined elemental statuses that involve heat as a component. Heat procs from the warframe's own weapons or abilities are unaffected by the resistance.
