## Overview

Virtuos Tempo is an operator arcane that grants fire rate bonuses on operator weapon kills. With a 60% proc chance, this arcane provides burst damage scaling during extended void fissure and Zariman void cascade farming, enabling operators to maintain kill momentum with accelerated amp fire rate. The effect scales from +15% fire rate at rank 0 to +60% at maximum rank, with an 8-second duration allowing for chained kill procs.

Virtuos Tempo grants a stacking fire rate buff on operator weapon kills, with a consistent 60% trigger chance across all ranks. At maximum rank, successful kills grant a +60% fire rate increase for 8 seconds, dramatically accelerating amp attack speed. The flat 60% proc chance means reliable uptime on sustained kill chains during high-density farming runs.

## Acquisition

Virtuos Tempo is obtained exclusively from The Quills in Cetus. The arcane is guaranteed as a rank reward from the Observer tier progression. The Observer rank is the second tier in The Quills standing system, requiring advancement past Mote rank through void fissure participation and void trace accumulation. Reaching Observer status provides access to mid-tier void fissure reward pools.

## Best on

This arcane pairs effectively with rapid-fire amp configurations used in void fissure farming and Zariman void cascade missions. Operators focused on sustained, high-frequency weapon attacks derive maximum value from the fire rate scaling. The 8-second duration allows continuous reactivation during enemy-dense sections, making it valuable in endless void fissure runs. Frames with void keystones or abilities that increase void damage complement the sustained offensive output provided by Virtuos Tempo.

## Usage notes

The 60% proc chance means the arcane typically triggers on roughly two out of every three operator kills, providing reliable fire rate uptime during efficient farming. The 8-second duration allows for stacking buff windows in high-kill-rate scenarios, maintaining bonus fire rate throughout extended engagement periods. Rapid-fire amps and multi-pellet amps benefit most from the accelerated attack speed, making amp selection important for optimizing this arcane's effectiveness.
