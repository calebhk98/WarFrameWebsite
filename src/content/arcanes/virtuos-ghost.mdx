## Overview

Virtuos Ghost is a rare Operator arcane providing evasion through chance-based invisibility procs when Void Damage hits. This defensive utility arcane enables operators to survive high-pressure endgame encounters by triggering stealth windows during sustained Amp DPS phases.

## Effect

Each successful Void Damage hit from the operator's Amp has a 15% chance at rank 0, scaling to 75% at rank 5, to grant the operator 4-5 seconds of invisibility. At maximum rank, nearly every other shot triggers stealth, enabling near-permanent evasion during active combat.

## Acquisition

Virtuos Ghost drops as a rare reward from Eidolon Gantulyst captures during void fissure hunts. The increased rarity reflects the arcane's strong defensive utility.

## Best on

Virtuos Ghost excels in evasion-focused operator builds:

- **Solo Eidolon hunts** — invisibility enables solo trinity-style survivability.
- **High-level void cascades** — frequent stealth windows prevent squad wipes.
- **Operator-centric builds** — maximizes personal survival without Warframe support.

## Notes

Invisibility breaks on Amp attack or ability cast. The proc chance ties to hit frequency, making high-fire-rate amps more reliable for stealth uptime. Does not grant invulnerability during invisibility break window.
