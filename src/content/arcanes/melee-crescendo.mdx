## Overview

Melee Crescendo is a melee weapon arcane that accelerates combo counter progression through finisher mechanics. Each finisher kill grants a permanent initial combo counter boost lasting for the entire mission duration. At rank 5, this arcane provides 6 free combo stacks on every mission start, eliminating the ramp-up period for melee combo-based damage scaling. The arcane transforms finisher playstyles into sustained-damage multipliers and synergizes with combo-dependent frames and weapons.

Each finisher kill permanently increases the baseline combo counter for the current mission. Melee weapons with high-hit-count combos benefit exponentially from the stacking boost. The mechanic applies independently of other combo modifiers, allowing for multiplicative combo scaling when paired with frame abilities and mods.

## Acquisition

Melee Crescendo drops from Zariman void fissure missions as a moderately rare melee arcane. The arcane requires finisher-focused playstyles to farm efficiently, making eximus and heavy unit encounters valuable farming targets. Public void fissure groups benefit from coordinating finisher mechanics and crowd-control setups to maximize trigger frequency. The arcane's effectiveness scales with finisher pickup frequency and enemy density in missions.

## Best On

Melee Crescendo excels on fast-hitting melee weapons with high combo counters per strike such as the Pangolin Sword or Guandao. Frames with finisher bonuses like Ash with smoke bomb execution or Chroma with vex armor gain exceptional synergies. Warframes emphasizing melee combo scaling through abilities, including Khora or Wukong, maximize the arcane's permanent stacking potential. Polearms and fast weapons benefit most from the consistent combo preservation across the mission.

Slow-attacking melee weapons without high hit counts underutilize this arcane. Frames focused on ability rotation rather than melee combat gain minimal benefit from combo stacking.
