## Overview

Magus Vigor is a team-support Operator arcane that grants health bonuses to nearby Warframes when Void Sling is cast. This arcane shifts the operator into a sustain role, providing emergency healing and extended bulk to squad members during intense void cascade encounters.

## Effect

Upon each Void Sling cast, nearby Warframes gain a temporary health percentage bonus ranging from +50% at rank 0 to +250% at rank 5. The effect lasts 12 seconds and can be refreshed by recasting Void Sling, enabling sustained health augmentation throughout combat.

## Acquisition

Magus Vigor drops as an uncommon reward from Void Cascade missions on Zariman. The arcane becomes available early in void fissure progression and pairs well with other support-focused operator builds.

## Best on

Magus Vigor is essential for defensive squad builds:

- **Squishy Warframe support** — operators enhance low-armor frames like Frost or Mag.
- **Extended void cascades** — sustained health buffs keep squads alive through long runs.
- **Eidolon hunts** — provides emergency survivability when Warframe shields fail.

## Notes

The health bonus applies as a temporary buffer that stacks multiplicatively with existing Warframe health. It does not heal—only increases maximum effective pool. The operator's Warframe is included in the area effect, allowing self-buff.
