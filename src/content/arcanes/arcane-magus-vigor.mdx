## Overview

Magus Vigor grants a flat maximum health bonus to the warframe each time the operator exits operator mode via Transference Out. Each return from operator mode applies the rank-dependent health bonus, expanding the warframe's maximum health pool for the duration of the buff. At rank 5, each Transference Out event adds +600 flat maximum health.

The flat health bonus persists for a fixed duration after the Transference Out event. Operators who cycle frequently between operator mode and warframe mode refresh the bonus at each Transference Out, maintaining elevated maximum health throughout combat. Unlike healing arcanes that restore existing health, Vigor increases the maximum health ceiling, providing room to absorb additional damage before reaching critical thresholds.

## Effect

Transference Out -- pressing the transference key to return from operator mode to warframe mode -- triggers the Magus Vigor bonus. Each activation adds flat maximum health to the warframe. At rank 0, +100 health provides a minor expansion to the health pool. At rank 5, +600 health is a significant addition for most frames: a frame with 300 base health nearly doubles its effective maximum during active operator cycling.

The maximum health bonus applies immediately at the Transference Out moment. When the buff expires, maximum health returns to the base value; any current health above the new maximum is capped accordingly. Frames that engage in frequent operator cycling -- entering operator mode for ability use, amp attacks, or Focus school activations, then returning to warframe -- maintain the bonus more consistently than frames that spend extended time in warframe mode without transference cycling.

## Acquisition

Magus Vigor drops as an uncommon reward from Void Cascade missions on Zariman Ten-Zero at 2.5%. The arcane becomes accessible after completing Angels of the Zariman. Regular Void Cascade participation accumulates Vigor copies over multiple sessions without requiring specific rotation targeting. Warframe.market provides direct purchase for players who prefer platinum acquisition.

## Builds that use it

Magus Vigor fits operator-active builds that cycle through transference regularly. Builds that enter operator mode for Void Strike charging (Madurai), amp combat against Eidolons or Orphix, or Unairu passive armor strip all generate multiple Transference Out events per mission, refreshing the health bonus at each return to warframe mode. Frames that pair Vigor with Magus Elevate or Magus Nourish use the Transference Out cycle to simultaneously restore health and expand the health ceiling.

High-base-health frames benefit most in absolute terms: a frame with 1000 maximum health gains 60% additional capacity from +600 Vigor at rank 5, while a frame with 200 base health gains 300% additional capacity from the same bonus. Frames with no natural health recovery paired with Vigor gain a larger effective health pool to sustain incoming damage between healing events.

## Notes

Transference Out specifically means returning from operator mode to the warframe by pressing the transference key. Entering operator mode does not trigger the bonus. The health bonus is applied additively alongside Vitality and other maximum health sources. The bonus duration is fixed; extended warframe combat phases without transference cycling allow the buff to expire. Frames that briefly enter operator mode then immediately return trigger the bonus with minimal time in operator mode. Magus Vigor stacks with Arcane Intention if both arcanes are equipped, with Intention scaling by channeled ability count and Vigor scaling by Transference Out frequency.
