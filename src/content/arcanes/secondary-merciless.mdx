## Overview

Secondary Merciless is a weapon arcane for secondary weapons that scales damage with consecutive kills, mirroring the Primary Merciless effect. Each enemy elimination within the 4-second window grants a stacking damage bonus, up to 12 stacks maximum. At rank 5, this yields 360% cumulative damage (30% per stack), transforming Secondary Merciless into a high-performance damage multiplier for gun-focused combat. The arcane additionally grants 30% reload speed at max rank, accelerating magazine exchanges during kill streaks.

Each kill refreshes the 4-second timer, allowing stacks to persist through continuous engagement. The reload speed bonus synergizes perfectly with high-fire-rate secondary weapons, enabling faster magazine cycles during momentum phases. Magazine capacities amplify the arcane's sustained stacking potential during crowd-heavy encounters.

## Acquisition

Secondary Merciless drops from Zariman void fissure missions as a rare weapon arcane reward. The arcane requires sustained kill streaks to maximize farming efficiency, making high-damage secondary builds essential. Public void fissure groups benefit from having ammo-abundant secondaries and tight enemy clustering to maintain kill momentum. The arcane's effectiveness scales directly with weapon base damage and attack speed.

## Best On

Secondary Merciless synergizes exceptionally with high-fire-rate secondaries like the Akbolto Prime, Tombfinger (kitgun), or Zakti. Weapons with large magazine capacities amplify the arcane's sustained stacking potential during extended engagements. Chaining kills becomes exponentially rewarding, making crowd-control frames like Nyx or Limbo exceptional farming partners. The reload speed bonus particularly benefits weapons with slow reload times when paired with ammo conversion setups.

Slow-firing secondaries struggle to maintain stack uptime during combat. Weapons without natural crowd-control benefits underutilize this arcane's potential.
