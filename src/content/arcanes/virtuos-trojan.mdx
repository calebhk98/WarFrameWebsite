## Overview

Virtuos Trojan is an operator arcane that converts void damage to viral damage on operator weapon hits. This elemental conversion arcane enables operators to exploit health-pool vulnerabilities in biological enemies and leverage viral damage procs for sustained damage output in Zariman void cascade and void fissure missions. The conversion reaches 98% at maximum rank, making operators devastatingly effective against flesh-heavy enemy compositions.

## Effects per rank

| Rank | Void to Viral Conversion |
|------|------------------------|
| 0 | 24% |
| 1 | 48% |
| 2 | 72% |
| 3 | 98% |

Virtuos Trojan converts a percentage of incoming void damage directly to viral damage on each hit. At rank 3, the conversion reaches 98%, transforming void-focused operator builds into effective viral damage dealers. Viral damage excels against Infested units and heavily-fleshes enemies, making this arcane particularly valuable in content where biological enemies predominate. The conversion happens per-hit, allowing rapid-fire amps to deal substantial viral damage across target groups.

## Acquisition

Virtuos Trojan is obtained from Vox Solaris, the syndicate faction tied to Fortuna open-world progression. The arcane is guaranteed as a rank reward from the Hand tier within the Vox Solaris standing system. Reaching Hand rank requires advancing through earlier Vox Solaris tiers via Fortuna bounties, defeating the Profit-Taker Eidolon, and accumulating standing through commodity trading and faction reputation activities.

## Best on

This arcane excels in operator builds designed for Infested-heavy encounters and missions where biological enemies comprise the majority of opposition. Void damage amps paired with Virtuos Trojan effectively become viral-focused weapons, allowing operators to trigger viral procs and leverage health-multiplication mechanics. Endgame Zariman void cascade runs and void fissure disruption missions with Infested spawns benefit substantially from the damage conversion and proc potential.

## Usage notes

The damage conversion applies per-hit, making rapid-fire amp configurations significantly more effective at distributing viral damage across enemy groups. Unlike damage-addition arcanes, conversion arcanes redirect existing damage output rather than increasing total output, making them tactical choices for specific enemy compositions. At 98% conversion rank, operators employing void-damage amps become primarily viral damage dealers, requiring careful build planning to exploit viral damage scaling and proc mechanics.
