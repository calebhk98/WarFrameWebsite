## Overview

Magus Vigor restores flat health to the warframe each time the operator performs Transference Out -- returning from operator mode to warframe mode. This converts the Transference Out action into a healing event, providing a health injection at the moment the player resumes warframe play. The bonus applies to the warframe's health pool and scales from 100 health at rank 0 to 600 health at rank 5.

Health restoration on Transference Out is valuable in combat scenarios where the warframe takes damage during engagement phases. When the player enters operator mode to use their amp, Focus school ability, or Void Dash, they frequently leave the warframe briefly exposed or simply pause the warframe's combat contribution. Returning to the warframe with Magus Vigor restores health that may have been lost during the operator phase or before it.

## Effect

At rank 0, +100 health on Transference Out provides a meaningful flat heal for frames with lower health pools or frames running Arcane Energize where operator cycling is frequent. At rank 5, +600 health provides substantial restoration -- a significant fraction of most warframes' maximum health -- making each transference cycle a healing event that sustains the frame across extended engagements.

The heal fires at the instant of Transference Out and cannot exceed the warframe's maximum health. If the warframe is already at maximum health, the heal has no effect. Repeated operator cycling refreshes the heal on each return.

## Builds that use it

Magus Vigor suits warframes that take significant damage during combat phases and use Transference cycling as part of their rotation -- Saryn builds that cycle into operator for Spore spread and return to cast, Octavia builds that reposition Mallet via Void Dash, or Nova builds using operator mode for Focus school utility. The heal-on-return pattern makes each cycle productive for health maintenance without requiring dedicated healing arcanes or abilities.

Frames with moderate health pools -- Oberon, Ember, Nidus in early stacks -- benefit proportionally more from the flat 600 health than high-health frames like Inaros where 600 health represents a smaller percentage of total health. Builds that cycle operator mode multiple times per minute accumulate substantial healing across a mission from the repeated Transference Out procs.

## Notes

The health restoration applies to the warframe, not the operator -- operator health is a separate pool unaffected by this arcane. The heal occurs at Transference Out, not at Transference In, meaning it applies when the player returns to the warframe rather than when they leave. If the warframe is killed while the player is in operator mode, the Transference Out event occurs to a dead warframe and the heal does not apply. The flat value is not affected by ability strength mods or warframe health mods -- 600 health is always 600 health regardless of Vitality investment.
