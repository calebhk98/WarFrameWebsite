## Overview

Arcane Fury is a rare offensive arcane that grants multiplicative melee damage scaling on critical hits. The 60% trigger chance on crits combined with the 18-second duration ensures that melee-heavy warframes maintain near-permanent damage buffs during sustained combat. A max-rank Fury proc grants +180% melee damage, tripling the wielded weapon's base output for the buff duration. This transforms melee-centric builds from mid-tier damage sources into endgame-viable powerhouses, particularly when paired with critical-chance scaling mods and warframe abilities that enhance melee output.

Fury's primary strength is multiplicative damage stacking: a melee weapon with high base crit chance and high damage benefits exponentially more from Fury than low-crit weapons. Hybrid frames that divide attention between abilities and melee (Garuda, Wukong, Baruuk) leverage Fury to maintain viable melee damage output during extended missions. The arcane's consistent uptime in crit-focused builds makes it competitive with pure ability-damage arcanes on damage-per-second metrics.

The 60% crit-trigger chance and 18-second duration create significant uptime in melee-focused playstyles, particularly with high-crit-chance weapons and warframe abilities that amplify crits (Saryn's Venomous Exoskeleton, Ash's Shuriken, Rhino's Roar). A melee weapon with 20%+ crit chance procs Fury roughly once every 1--2 seconds during sustained attack chains, creating overlapping damage buffs that compound into near-permanent 180% damage multiplication.

## Acquisition

Arcane Fury drops primarily from Eidolon Hydrolyst capture missions at 11.24% chance and extended Hydrolyst fights at 13.76% chance, making it one of the higher-rate arcane drops from Eidolons. The Zariman void skirmish rotation C also drops Fury at 2.82% chance, providing an alternate source for players avoiding Eidolon hunts. The Rare rarity means leveling costs and trading-post pricing are reasonable, placing Fury in the mid-tier of acquisition difficulty.

Cost to acquire a single max-rank Fury typically ranges from 25--60 platinum on the trading post, depending on supply and demand cycles.

## Best on

Arcane Fury excels on melee-focused warframes where sustained critical hits are virtually guaranteed: Garuda (ranged and melee hybrid), Wukong (melee with exalted staff), Baruuk (melee-centric), Ash (crit-scaling shurikens), and hybrid damage frames. Weapon-swap builds that cycle between melee and ranged weapons use Fury as a melee-phase multiplier, triggering the effect during melee chains and maintaining it for minimal downtime. Pairing Fury with crit-scaling weapons (Orthos Prime, Nikana Prime, Guandao Prime) and warframes with built-in crit multipliers (Garuda's Bloodletting, Ash's Shuriken) creates synergistic damage scaling.

Pure ability-spam frames (Mag, Saryn, Mesa with Peacemaker) rarely prioritize Fury because they spend minimal time in melee. Frames that use melee as a supplementary defense tool (Wisp, Protea) may skip Fury in favor of pure defense or ability-damage arcanes.

## Builds that use it

Steel Path melee builds and hybrid melee-ability builds frequently pair Fury with other offensive arcanes (Eruption, Velocity) for multiplicative damage scaling across different attack types. Exalted weapon builds (Wukong, Baruuk, Kullervo) often dual-slot Fury for consistent damage uptime on their scaled weapons. Speedrun builds incorporating melee may use Fury if the melee phase is significant enough to justify the arcane slot. Crowd-control or survival-first builds typically omit Fury in favor of defensive arcanes.

## Notes

Fury's trigger chance applies to any melee critical hit, whether from normal attacks, ability-triggered hits, or exalted weapon crits. This makes the arcane invaluable for exalted weapon frames where the weapon's high scaling and guaranteed crit stats create frequent trigger opportunities. The damage bonus is multiplicative with other modifiers, meaning a Fury proc on a weapon with 200% base damage and 200% from mods results in a multiplicative output (base * (1 + mods) * (1 + Fury)) rather than additive.

The arcane was balanced in Update 27.2.0 to ensure melee-centric playstyles remained viable in endgame content, preventing pure-ability or pure-gun dominance.
