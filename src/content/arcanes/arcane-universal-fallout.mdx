## Overview

Arcane Universal Fallout spreads status effects to nearby enemies. This warframe arcane enables status-heavy builds to convert single-target procs into area crowd control.

## Effects per rank

| Rank | Trigger Chance | AOE Radius |
|------|----------------|-----------|
| 0    | 10%            | 15m       |
| 1    | 15%            | 15m       |
| 2    | 20%            | 15m       |
| 3    | 25%            | 15m       |
| 4    | 27%            | 15m       |
| 5    | 30%            | 15m       |

Area status spreading enables rapid crowd control during groups of void manifestations.

## Acquisition

Arcane Universal Fallout drops from Zariman Void Fissure (4%) and Void Cascade rotation A (2%). Regular zariman void farming yields this arcane naturally.

## Best on

Arcane Universal Fallout excels on status-focused frames handling multiple enemies. The area effect enhances crowd control during void fissure waves.
