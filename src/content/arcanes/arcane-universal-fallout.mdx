## Overview

Arcane Universal Fallout generates Universal Orbs when enemies die after being afflicted with Radiation Status by a warframe ability. Each Radiation status applied by an ability gives that enemy a per-rank chance to drop a Universal Orb on death. Universal Orbs restore both Health and Energy, making them broadly useful regardless of the player's current resource state.

Abilities that inflict Radiation status -- whether through direct elemental damage or proc mechanics -- feed into the Fallout orb generation. Frames with Radiation-applying abilities benefit most, as each Radiation proc on each enemy becomes an independent orb-drop chance when that enemy dies.

## Effect

When an ability inflicts Radiation Status on an enemy, that enemy gains a chance to drop a Universal Orb upon death. At rank 0, each Radiation proc gives a 1% orb drop chance per affected enemy. At rank 5, each proc gives a 6% chance. Enemies with multiple Radiation stacks from repeated ability hits accumulate more chances, since each separate proc application adds an independent 6% chance per stack on death. Frames that apply Radiation rapidly across many enemies generate a steady stream of Universal Orbs from killed enemies throughout combat.

Universal Orbs restore both Health and Energy on pickup, making each orb doubly valuable compared to single-type orb drops. The orbs appear at the enemy's death location and are picked up by proximity.

## Acquisition

Arcane Universal Fallout drops from Zariman Void Fissure rewards at 4% and Void Cascade rotation A at 2%. Access requires completing Angels of the Zariman. Void Cascade rotation A is reached frequently in standard runs, making the arcane accessible through regular Zariman content participation. Warframe.market provides an alternative acquisition path for players preferring to purchase rather than farm.

## Builds that use it

Arcane Universal Fallout benefits Radiation-ability builds where multiple enemies receive Radiation procs from abilities before dying. Saryn using Spores with added Radiation element, Nidus applying Radiation through Virulence, and frames using Helminth-subsumed abilities that deal Radiation damage apply Radiation procs at high frequency during combat, setting up multiple enemies with orb-drop potential simultaneously. Ember's World on Fire with Radiation element additions and Equinox builds with Radiation damage injection provide sustained Radiation proc fields that prime large enemy groups.

At 6% per proc, the arcane provides consistent but not dominant orb generation -- it supplements rather than replaces active energy orb recovery from Trinity, Zenurik Focus, and Energy Siphon. The value compounds in situations with dense enemy populations where many Radiation-proced enemies die in rapid succession.

## Notes

The Radiation Status must be applied specifically by abilities, not by weapons. Weapon Radiation procs do not trigger the Fallout orb-drop chance. Each individual Radiation proc application on an enemy is an independent chance -- if an ability applies 3 Radiation procs to one enemy, that enemy has three separate 6% checks on death at rank 5 (roughly 17% cumulative chance for at least one orb). The orb-drop chance checks occur at the moment of enemy death, not at proc application. Enemies that die without the Radiation proc reaching its full duration still trigger the orb check if the proc was active at death.
