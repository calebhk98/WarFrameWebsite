## Overview

Secondary Kinship grants Critical Chance to the secondary weapon based on the number of ally warframes the player is actively buffing. While ability buffs from the player's warframe are active on allied warframes, the secondary weapon's Critical Chance increases by the listed amount per ally buff maintained. Frames that cast persistent team buffs -- Wisp's Reservoirs, Oberon's Hallowed Ground, Rhino's Roar across squadmates -- benefit from the Critical Chance bonus on their secondary weapon simultaneously.

At rank 5, each actively buffed ally adds +20% Critical Chance to the secondary weapon. In a squad of 4 players, buffing all 3 allies adds +60% Critical Chance, significantly boosting the secondary weapon's crit performance during the buff window.

## Effect

While the player's warframe is actively buffing one or more allied warframes with ability-based buffs, the secondary weapon gains the listed Critical Chance bonus per buffed ally. At rank 0, each buffed ally adds +3% Critical Chance. At rank 5, each buffed ally adds +20% Critical Chance. The bonus is active only while buffs are maintained -- if ability buffs expire or allies leave range, the Critical Chance bonus decreases correspondingly.

The bonus is concurrent with the buff -- both the team buff and the secondary weapon bonus are active at the same time, rewarding the player for being in a buff-active support role.

## Acquisition

Secondary Kinship drops from various Zariman Ten-Zero missions and Void Cascade content at uncommon rarity. The arcane is accessible through standard Zariman progression after completing Angels of the Zariman. Warframe.market provides direct purchase for targeted acquisition.

## Builds that use it

Secondary Kinship is designed for support-role warframes that maintain persistent team buffs. Wisp running Reservoirs of Haste, Breach, and Mend simultaneously buffs nearby allies with multiple buff types, potentially counting as multiple active buffs per ally for Critical Chance accumulation. Rhino's Roar buff applied to squadmates maintains the bonus for Roar's full duration. Harrow's Covenant, Oberon's Renewal, and Hildryn's Haven all apply persistent buffs to allies that maintain the Secondary Kinship bonus while active.

The arcane is most effective in full 4-player squads where 3 allies can all receive buffs, maximizing the Critical Chance bonus. Solo play provides no benefit since no ally warframes are present to buff.

## Notes

The definition of "buffing ally warframes" follows the game's ability buff detection -- abilities that apply a tracked status buff to ally warframes count. Temporary buffs that expire quickly may not maintain consistent Critical Chance bonus unless recast. The bonus is per buffed ally, not per buff type -- if one ally receives multiple buffs from the same ability, it may still count as one ally for the per-buff calculation, depending on implementation. Secondary Kinship has no benefit in solo play where no ally warframes are available to be buffed.
