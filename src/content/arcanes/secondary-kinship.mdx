## Overview

Secondary Kinship provides defensive buffing when secondary weapons reload. On every reload action, the arcane grants all resistances to the player and nearby allies. This arcane works well on tactical secondaries. Early ranks grant 15% resistances; maximum rank grants 75% all resistances.

## Acquisition

Secondary Kinship drops from various Zariman and Void Cascade missions.

## Best on

This arcane works best on builds that exploit its triggering condition frequently.

## Usage

Secondary Kinship activates automatically when its trigger condition is met during combat.

## Notes

This arcane provides consistent value scaling throughout extended missions.
