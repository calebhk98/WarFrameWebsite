## Overview

Arcane Impetus is a warframe arcane that amplifies ability effectiveness through status effect triggers. When abilities inflict unique status effects on enemies, the arcane grants bonus Ability Strength and Ability Efficiency for 20 seconds at all ranks. At max rank, the arcane provides 6% Ability Strength and 3% Ability Efficiency per trigger, scaling with ability usage patterns. The arcane incentivizes status-focused ability rotations and synergizes with frames specializing in elemental or crowd-control abilities.

## Effect

When abilities inflict unique status effects on enemies, the Warframe gains a stacking Ability Strength and Ability Efficiency bonus for 20 seconds. Each unique status type applied refreshes the buff window. At rank 5, each trigger grants 6% Ability Strength and 3% Ability Efficiency.

Each trigger from a unique status effect refreshes the buff window to 20 seconds. Frames with abilities that apply multiple status types per cast can chain triggers consistently, stacking benefits across long engagements. The efficiency bonus reduces the energy cost of subsequent ability uses.

## Acquisition

Arcane Impetus drops from Zariman void fissure missions as a rare reward. The arcane requires farming void fissures with status-focused ability builds to maximize drop efficiency. Public fissure groups benefit from having diverse frames equipped to increase overall enemy kill diversity and trigger chances. The arcane's value increases with ability cooldown reduction mods that enable faster trigger cycles.

## Best On

This arcane thrives on frames with status-applying abilities such as Saryn with her poison-focused kit, Mirage using illusions and explosions, or Ember deploying heat-based crowd control. Nidus, accumulating multiple mutation stacks through ability use, gains exceptional value from the efficiency scaling. Frames relying on high ability costs or frequent ability rotations maximize the efficiency bonus's impact.

Frames without status-applying mechanics or those emphasizing weapon damage over abilities gain minimal benefit from this arcane's effects.

## Builds that use it

Status-focused warframe builds running Saryn (Spores apply multiple status types per cast), Mirage with elemental explosions, or Ember with heat-based crowd control gain full value from Impetus. Nidus builds focusing on Mutation stack accumulation through ability cycling benefit from the efficiency reduction lowering energy costs per Virulence cast. Nezha builds applying Heat and Puncture through Blazing Chakram and Fire Walker contribute multiple unique statuses per rotation.

Frames that spread or proc many status types per ability cast are preferred; frames relying on a single non-elemental ability type gain little.

## Notes

The arcane tracks unique status effect types, not total proc count. Applying the same status type repeatedly does not stack additional triggers. Frames with abilities that apply two or more distinct elemental damage types in a single cast maximize trigger frequency. The 20-second buff window is generous and typically covers multiple ability rotations at standard cast speeds.
