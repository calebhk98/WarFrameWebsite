## Overview

Arcane Warmth provides passive protection against cold damage and status effects, essential for Void Cascade endgame missions where corpus and infested enemies apply cold procs liberally. The arcane grants a percentage-based chance to negate incoming cold status applications, scaling from 17% at rank 0 to 102% at maximum rank. At the highest rank, the 102% chance allows double-negation of cold procs, essentially providing immunity to the status. This defensive utility removes a major survivability obstacle in specific mission tilesets and enemy compositions.

Cold status effects in Warframe slow movement speed and attack speed, creating dangerous situations where players become vulnerable to incoming damage. Arcane Warmth eliminates this vulnerability pattern, allowing aggressive playstyles without fear of being slowed during critical moments. The arcane is particularly valuable in Void Cascade and corpus-heavy sorties.

## Acquisition

Arcane Warmth drops from Duviri Zariman endless missions at Tier 1 and Tier 3 as legendary rewards, or from Void Cascade skirmish missions at Neptune and Pluto nodes where it appears as an uncommon drop in Rotation C. The uncommon rarity at skirmish nodes makes Warmth more accessible than some competing arcanes, with reasonable drop odds during extended farming sessions.

## Best on

Speed-focused frames like Nova Prime, Titania Prime, and Wukong Prime benefit most from Warmth, as cold procs are particularly harmful to their playstyles. Frames optimized for high mobility and reaction time gain significant utility from eliminating the speed penalty cold applies. The arcane pairs well with parkour-heavy builds that depend on movement speed for survivability and damage output.

## Notes

At maximum rank, the 102% resistance chance provides near-immunity to cold status effects through double-triggering. The effect is passive and automatic, requiring no player interaction. Status procs from different enemies can be resisted independently, allowing layered protection in high-threat environments. The arcane stacks with frame-specific cold resistances for additional defense.

## Sources

Arcane Warmth is obtainable through Duviri Zariman endless missions or Void Cascade skirmish missions at Neptune and Pluto nodes. The uncommon rarity at skirmish locations makes it one of the more farmable recent-era arcanes, accessible to players regularly engaging with Void Cascade content.
