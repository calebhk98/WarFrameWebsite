## Overview

Arcane Strike is a melee-focused arcane that bolsters attack speed during active combat. Every hit with a melee weapon has a 15% chance to trigger a temporary attack speed bonus lasting 18 seconds. At maximum rank, this bonus reaches 60%, transforming slow heavy weapons into rapid strike platforms. The arcane rewards aggressive melee playstyles and scales with attack frequency, making it potent on fast-attacking weapons and weapon combinations that generate numerous hits per second.

Melee combat in Warframe is foundational to endgame survival, and Arcane Strike bridges gaps in naturally slow-attacking weapons. Slower weapons like greatswords and heavy polearms benefit most from the speed augmentation, while already-fast weapons like claws and whips gain noticeable additional scaling. The 18-second duration is long enough to sustain through most room clears when maintained through consistent hitting.

## Acquisition

Arcane Strike drops from Duviri Zariman endless mission Tier 1 and Tier 3 nodes as a legendary reward, or from Void Cascade skirmish missions at Veil and Venus nodes in Rotation C as a rare drop. Like other recent-era arcanes, Duviri offers higher rarity drops but lower accessibility for newer players. Skirmish missions provide more frequent opportunities at later-game nodes.

## Best on

Heavy melee weapons with long swing times benefit most from Arcane Strike. Greatswords, polearms, and heavy blades see dramatic improvements in DPS when the arcane buff is active. Warframes specializing in melee damage like Valkyr Prime, Excalibur Umbra, or Ash Prime synergize well with the arcane's bonus. Builds emphasizing status effect procs through melee hits also scale well, as the attack speed increase adds to total hit count.

## Notes

The arcane triggers on all melee hits including status procs and swings against destructible objects. The 15% proc rate means consistent uptime during continuous combat scenarios. Attack speed gains are additive with other buffs, allowing them to stack with frames like Wisp or mods like Fury for multiplicative damage increases during buff windows.

## Sources

Arcane Strike can be obtained through dedicated Duviri Zariman endless runs or Void Cascade skirmish farming at later-game nodes. The farming requirement is moderate compared to other arcanes, making it accessible to players engaging with mid-to-late-game content regularly.
