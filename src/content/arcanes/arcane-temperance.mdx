## Overview

Arcane Temperance reduces the damage the warframe takes while being revived by a squadmate. Revive animations leave the warframe completely vulnerable to enemy fire -- the frame is immobile and cannot use abilities during the revive channel. Temperance directly mitigates incoming damage during this window, increasing the odds the revive completes before the frame is downed again.

At rank 5, Arcane Temperance provides -90% Damage Taken During Revive, reducing almost all incoming damage to negligible levels for the duration of the revive animation. This makes successful revives far more likely in high-damage environments such as Steel Path or endurance missions.

## Effect

While a squadmate is actively reviving the warframe, all incoming damage is reduced by the listed percentage. At rank 0, -15% damage reduction during revive provides a modest defensive improvement. At rank 5, -90% damage reduction means an enemy attack that would deal 1000 damage instead deals only 100 during the revive window. This allows revives to complete successfully in combat situations where the unprotected frame would normally be killed again before the revive finishes.

The damage reduction applies only during the revive animation itself -- from when a squadmate begins the revive channel until it completes or is interrupted. Normal combat outside revive windows is unaffected.

## Acquisition

Arcane Temperance drops from Zariman Void Fissure rewards at 4% and Void Cascade rotation C at 2%. Access requires completing Angels of the Zariman. Rotation C drops require sustained Void Cascade runs to reach; pairing Temperance farming with Void Fissure sessions provides more frequent drop opportunities. Warframe.market provides a direct purchase route for players preferring platinum-based acquisition.

## Builds that use it

Arcane Temperance benefits any squad-based build where getting revived is a realistic occurrence. Frames that engage in high-risk combat -- aggressive melee frames, low-health caster frames in Steel Path -- may be downed during sustained content. With Temperance equipped, squadmates can safely complete revive channels even while enemies continue to attack the downed frame. This is especially relevant in endless content where losing a revive attempt wastes time and can cascade into a failed run.

The arcane has no value in solo play where revives are self-revives, or for frames with invulnerability-on-down mechanics. In coordinated squads running endurance Steel Path, Temperance extends each player's effective survival by making revives reliable rather than risky.

## Notes

The damage reduction applies during the squadmate's revive channel, not during the brief invulnerability window after standing up. If the revive is interrupted (squadmate moves away, enemy interrupt), the damage reduction window ends. Self-revives via tokens do not count as the revive-from-squadmate trigger. Arcane Temperance is a support-oriented defensive arcane with very narrow activation conditions -- it provides no benefit outside of the specific revive window. The -90% at rank 5 does not fully negate all damage; high-damage enemy attacks can still deal residual damage during revive. Frames with high base armor and high max health survive revive windows better than glass-cannon frames even with Temperance, since the reduction is proportional.
