## Overview

Arcane Temperance reduces status duration to minimize crowd control vulnerability. This warframe arcane enables frames with low status resistance to mitigate enemy status effects during void fissure content.

## Effects per rank

| Rank | Trigger Chance | Status Reduction |
|------|----------------|-----------------|
| 0    | 15%            | 25%             |
| 1    | 22%            | 35%             |
| 2    | 30%            | 45%             |
| 3    | 37%            | 55%             |
| 4    | 41%            | 65%             |
| 5    | 45%            | 75%             |

Status reduction enables fragile frames to remain functional during heavy status application scenarios.

## Acquisition

Arcane Temperance drops from Zariman Void Fissure (4%) and Void Cascade rotation C (2%). Regular zariman void farming yields this arcane naturally.

## Best on

Arcane Temperance excels on frames vulnerable to status procs. The duration reduction enables survival of heavy crowd control chains.
