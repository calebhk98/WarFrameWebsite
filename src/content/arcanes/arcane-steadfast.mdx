## Overview

Arcane Steadfast is a rare Warframe arcane providing defensive scaling tied to knockdown mechanics. This arcane transforms knockdown vulnerability into survivability by granting armor scaling when the Warframe is knocked down, rewarding aggressive positioning in high-damage endgame content.

## Effect

When a Warframe is knocked down, Arcane Steadfast grants a stacking armor buff lasting 6 seconds. The buff scales from +50 flat armor at rank 0 to +250 at rank 5. Multiple knockdowns can stack the buffs, enabling exponential armor scaling during chaotic late-rotation void fissures.

## Acquisition

Arcane Steadfast drops as a rare reward from Void Fissure endless missions, specifically from rotation C (at 20+ minutes). Void Fissure farming is the primary acquisition method.

## Best on

Arcane Steadfast is ideal for disruptive, crowd-control-heavy Warframes:

- **Heavy crowd-control frames** (Grendel, Atlas, Frost) that expect knockdowns.
- **Tank-focused builds** that leverage knockdown frames for armor stacking.
- **Endless void fissures** — stacking knockdowns maintain high armor throughout runs.

## Notes

The arcane incentivizes taking knockdowns as a survivability trade-off. Knockdown-immunity builds nullify the benefit. Works well with status-immune builds that allow safe knockdown exposure. Stacks additively with armor mods.
