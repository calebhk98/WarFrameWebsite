## Overview

Arcane Steadfast grants a chance on each ability cast for the next three abilities to cost no Energy. When the trigger fires, the next three ability activations -- whether casting, toggling on, or activating any ability -- consume zero energy, providing three free casts regardless of ability cost. This can cover high-cost abilities like Absorb, Exalted Blade activations, or expensive ultimates without drawing on the energy pool.

At rank 5, a 20% trigger chance means one in five ability casts activates the free-ability window. In a high-ability-cast mission, this can provide dozens of free energy saves throughout the run, reducing energy drain substantially for active ability builds.

## Effect

Each ability cast checks the Steadfast trigger chance. On success, the following three ability activations cost zero energy. The three free casts can be any combination of abilities; the warframe does not need to cast specific abilities to consume the free charges. At rank 0, 5% trigger chance means the window activates rarely -- roughly one in twenty casts. At rank 5, 20% trigger chance provides a free-ability window on one in five casts, noticeably reducing total energy expenditure across sustained combat.

The free-ability charges carry over between casts; if two free casts remain when the player repositions or pauses, those charges remain available until used. Casting a channeled ability that activates on a free charge still consumes energy for its ongoing drain; only the initial activation cost is waived.

## Acquisition

Arcane Steadfast drops as a rare reward from Void Fissure endless rotation C. Reaching rotation C requires extended time in endless Void Fissure missions -- Survival or Defense missions that cycle through A, B, and C rotations over multiple reward intervals. The rare designation and rotation depth make Steadfast one of the more time-intensive Zariman arcanes to farm through normal play. Warframe.market provides a purchase option for players who prefer platinum acquisition over committing to extended endless Fissure sessions.

## Builds that use it

Arcane Steadfast benefits high-ability-cast builds that frequently activate abilities and encounter energy limitations. Frames with expensive ultimates -- Mirage's Total Eclipse (requires sustained casts), Octavia's Mallet and Resonator combo, Nova's Molecular Prime chain casting -- benefit from free-cast windows covering their costliest activations. Energy Siphon and energy restore pads cover sustained energy recovery; Steadfast provides burst free casts that offset the biggest single-cast expenses.

Builds that rely on rapid ability cycling rather than channeled ability maintenance gain the most from Steadfast. Each cast in a rotation is a Steadfast trigger check, and with 20% trigger chance at rank 5, a four-ability rotation cycling every few seconds accumulates free windows that noticeably offset total energy consumption over a mission.

## Notes

The free-cast window applies to the next three casts after the trigger, regardless of ability type. Channeled abilities receive a free activation cost on the initial toggle but continue to drain energy for their sustained component. The trigger is per ability cast, not per ability type -- the same ability cast repeatedly counts as new trigger checks each time. At rank 5, 20% trigger chance is relatively low compared to other arcane trigger rates; builds relying on Steadfast for significant energy management should supplement with traditional energy economy options (Fleeting Expertise, Primed Flow, energy restore pads). The arcane is a supplemental energy tool, not a replacement for energy management mods.
