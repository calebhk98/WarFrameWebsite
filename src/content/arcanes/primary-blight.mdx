## Overview

Primary Blight builds Critical Damage and Multishot bonuses each time the equipped primary weapon applies a Toxin status effect to an enemy. Each toxin proc adds one stack of the combined bonus, with each stack lasting 12 seconds independently. Up to 40 stacks can accumulate simultaneously - at rank 5, 40 stacks provide +144% Critical Damage and +72% Multishot, a substantial compound bonus that grows as more toxin procs occur during sustained combat.

Toxin status is applied by weapons with toxin damage, the Viral combination element, and Gas. Primary weapons built around toxin proc application - Ignis Wraith with toxin mods, corrosive-hybrid primaries, or Gas-element primaries - can stack Blight rapidly during heavy-fire combat phases, compounding critical output and multishot chance simultaneously.

## Effect

Each toxin proc on an enemy adds one stack of +3.6% Critical Damage and +1.8% Multishot at rank 5, lasting 12 seconds from when that specific proc occurred. At 40 stacks, the total bonus reaches +144% Critical Damage and +72% Multishot. Stacks decay independently, so maintaining 40 stacks requires roughly 3-4 toxin procs per 12-second window to replace expiring stacks.

At rank 0, 40 stacks provide +24% Critical Damage and +12% Multishot - meaningful but significantly lower than the rank 5 ceiling.

## Builds that use it

Primary Blight suits high-fire-rate or multi-projectile primaries that apply toxin status frequently - Ignis Wraith with toxin or gas mods, Kuva Bramma with toxin-element builds, or Amprex builds applying toxin via elemental combination. Weapons that deal consistent status procs per shot maintain stack count more reliably than weapons with low status chance, where toxin procs occur infrequently.

Critical primary weapons running Blood Rush, Argon Scope, and Primary Blight simultaneously can reach extremely high effective critical damage during sustained combat phases where all three arcanes and mods are active at maximum stacks. Saryn builds where the player fires a primary weapon during Spore spread phases also apply toxin procs from Spore damage that may trigger Blight - though Saryn's own toxin comes from abilities rather than the weapon directly.

## Notes

The Critical Damage bonus multiplies critical hit values - not critical hit chance. This means Blight is most effective on high-critical-chance builds where crits occur frequently enough to benefit from the multiplied damage ceiling. Multishot adds additional projectiles per shot, each dealing independent damage - at +72% Multishot from 40 stacks, a single-projectile weapon fires effectively 1.72 shots per trigger pull on average. Both bonuses scale independently and are additive with mod-based Critical Damage and Multishot values. Stack count is per-arcane, not per-enemy - any toxin proc regardless of which enemy was affected adds to the arcane's stack pool.
