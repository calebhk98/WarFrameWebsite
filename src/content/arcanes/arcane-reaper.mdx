## Overview

Arcane Reaper resets ability cooldowns and restores energy on kills, enabling ability-spam builds to maintain continuous rotation chains during high-kill-rate content. The dual benefit - cooldown refresh plus energy restoration - addresses both the time and resource constraints that limit ability usage frequency. Frames that exhaust their energy rapidly and depend on cooldown completion find Reaper particularly impactful because it resolves both bottlenecks simultaneously in a single trigger.

At rank 5, the 30% trigger chance means approximately one in three kills activates the effect, which in high-density content translates to multiple activations per second during sustained combat. The energy restore of 225 per trigger covers most single-cast high-cost abilities outright, potentially chaining back-to-back ability casts without requiring passive energy regeneration.

## Effect

Each enemy kill checks the Reaper trigger chance. On success, all ability cooldowns reset to immediately usable and 225 energy restores to the frame's energy pool at rank 5. At rank 0, 75 energy restores per trigger - sufficient for lower-cost abilities. The cooldown reset allows abilities with lengthy recovery periods (Exalted weapons, prolonged stasis effects) to re-activate sooner than their standard duration allows, enabling sustained combat phases with powerful ability combinations.

Energy restoration does not exceed the frame's maximum energy cap. Kills from companion attacks or squadmate weapons do not trigger Reaper.

## Acquisition

Arcane Reaper is a legendary arcane dropping from Zariman Void Fissure at 5% and Void Cascade rotation B at 2%. The 5% Void Fissure drop rate makes it moderately accessible for consistent Zariman farming. Legendary status means Reaper provides an Arcane Revive at rank 5 as an additional benefit. Warframe.market lists Reaper at elevated plat values reflecting its legendary tier and universal ability synergy.

## Builds that use it

Arcane Reaper is essential for ability-spam frames where cooldown resets enable continuous rotation chaining during extended missions. Octavia's synced ability rotations benefit from cooldown resets enabling faster tempo maintenance. Volt's Speed and Static Wall chain-casting during time-critical missions (Mobile Defense, Interception) benefits from instant cooldown recovery. Mesa's Peacemaker, which has no traditional cooldown but benefits from energy restoration for other abilities during active Peacemaker phase, pairs Reaper's energy gain productively with secondary ability casts. Frames running high-cost ability loadouts (Rhino, Frost, Gara) that deplete energy quickly benefit from Reaper's dual energy plus cooldown contribution on consistent kills.

## Notes

The cooldown reset applies to all warframe abilities simultaneously, not only the ability most recently cast. If no abilities are on cooldown at the moment of trigger, only the energy restoration applies. The 30% trigger chance at rank 5 is per kill, meaning rapid consecutive kills in dense groups provide multiple activation opportunities each second. Energy restoration respects the warframe's current energy cap and does not carry over excess. The legendary bonus (Arcane Revive) provides one additional self-revive per mission when carried - this functions independently of the main Reaper effect.
