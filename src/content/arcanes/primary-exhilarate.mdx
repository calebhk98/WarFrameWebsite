## Overview

Primary Exhilarate is a weapon arcane that rewards tactical reloading. On every reload action, the arcane grants a temporary damage buff that stacks up to three times. This synergizes well with magazine-based primary weapons that benefit from aggressive reload patterns. Early ranks provide moderate scaling; maximum rank yields 60% damage per stack, enabling 180% total damage multiplier at maximum stacks.

The arcane works best with weapons featuring rapid reload animations and frequent reload triggers during combat rotations. Soma Prime, Zariman-era rifles, and auto rifles gain significant value from sustained stacking during extended firefights.

## Acquisition

Primary Exhilarate drops from Void Cascade skirmish missions in the Zariman tileset at uncommon rarity. Players should prioritize extended endless runs to accumulate multiple copies for ranking.

## Best on

High-capacity magazine weapons benefit most from this arcane, particularly rifles with fast reload animations.

## Usage

The arcane activates automatically on every reload, stacking up to three times with each new buff refreshing the 15-second timer.

## Notes

Ammunition efficiency and reload speed mods extend the buff uptime through faster reload cycles. The arcane provides consistent damage scaling throughout extended missions.

## Sources

Primary Exhilarate is restricted to Zariman void cascade content for acquisition.
