## Overview

Secondary Deadhead is a weapon arcane for secondary weapons that rewards precision headshot elimination. Each headshot kill grants a stacking damage bonus lasting 24 seconds, with a maximum of 3 stacks. At rank 5, this yields 360% total damage bonus (120% per stack), making precision play exponentially rewarding. The arcane additionally provides 30% headshot multiplier bonus and 50% weapon recoil reduction at max rank, dramatically enhancing accuracy-focused secondary builds.

The 24-second buff window far exceeds most weapon arcanes, allowing stacks to accumulate passively across extended engagements. The recoil reduction dramatically improves sustained accuracy with rapid-fire secondaries, creating a synergistic bonus loop. The headshot multiplier bonus compounds with weapon mods and frame abilities enhancing critical strikes.

## Acquisition

Secondary Deadhead drops from Zariman void fissure missions as a rare reward for precision-focused players. The arcane requires consistent headshot kills to farm efficiently, demanding proficiency with secondary weapons. Public void fissure groups benefit from having stable aiming conditions and low-latency connections to maximize headshot detection. Accurate secondary weapons like the Sonicor Prime accelerate farming progression. The extended 24-second buff window enables stacking multiple bonuses before expiration through deliberate precision play.

## Best On

Secondary Deadhead excels with precision secondaries such as the Aksomati Prime, Dual Toxocyst, or Dex Furis. Frames that enhance weapon accuracy like Harrow or Chroma gain exceptional synergies. Warframes with headshot-multiplying mods transform this arcane into exponential damage scaling. Slow-firing but high-damage secondaries benefit maximally from the extended buff duration and recoil reduction, enabling sustained precision gameplay.

Fast-firing weapons with high spread struggle to maintain headshot consistency. Weapons without natural accuracy benefit moderately from the recoil reduction.
