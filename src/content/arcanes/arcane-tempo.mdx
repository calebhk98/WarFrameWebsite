## Overview

Arcane Tempo grants a Fire Rate bonus to Shotguns on Critical Hit. When any critical hit lands, there is a 15% chance the arcane activates, providing a significant fire rate increase to the equipped Shotgun for 12 seconds. Shotguns benefit substantially from fire rate bonuses -- faster cycling between shots and faster follow-up pellet spreads increase sustained damage output and status application frequency.

At rank 5, a successful trigger provides +90% Fire Rate to Shotguns for 12 seconds. Shotgun builds that achieve consistent critical hits -- through high critical chance mods, Critical Delay, or crit-boosting arcanes on the shotgun -- activate Tempo regularly during sustained combat.

## Effect

Each critical hit from any weapon checks the 15% trigger chance. On success, the equipped Shotgun gains the listed Fire Rate bonus for 12 seconds. The trigger can fire from critical hits on any weapon, not only the Shotgun -- a secondary with high critical chance can serve as a crit generator to activate Tempo on the equipped Shotgun. At rank 0, +15% Fire Rate is a modest improvement to shotgun cycling speed. At rank 5, +90% Fire Rate nearly doubles the number of shots per second, dramatically increasing pellets delivered per second and status proc frequency from multi-pellet spread.

The 12-second duration covers multiple firing cycles for most shotguns. Sustained combat with a critical hit source maintains regular Tempo trigger opportunities throughout the engagement.

## Acquisition

Arcane Tempo drops from Duviri/Endless at Legendary rarity (0.67% Tiers 1 and 3, 4% Tier 9), Skirmish nodes on Pluto, Neptune, and Venus at Uncommon Rotation C (10-10.34%), Void Cascade (8.5%), and Zariman Ten Zero Void Fissures (9%). Skirmish missions and Zariman Void Fissures provide the most accessible sources at comparable drop rates. Duviri Tier 9 provides higher single-run odds but demands more time per run. Warframe.market provides direct purchase for players who prefer platinum acquisition.

## Builds that use it

Arcane Tempo is purpose-built for Shotgun builds that achieve critical hits. Tigris Prime with Primed Point Blank and critical hit mods fires critical hits regularly, triggering Tempo's fire rate bonus to increase the number of shots landing per magazine. Arca Plasmor builds with high crit chance mods combine critical hits and the fire rate bonus to push damage per second to peak levels. Kohm builds that naturally crit with high pellet counts generate many crit checks per shot, raising Tempo trigger frequency above what the 15% base chance suggests.

The arcane pairs with frames that enhance weapon critical chance: Chroma with Vex Armor boosting critical stats, Mirage with Hall of Mirrors (each copy can independently crit and trigger Tempo checks). Secondary weapons with high crit chance can generate off-weapon crit events that keep Tempo active on the primary Shotgun while using a different weapon type.

## Notes

The 15% trigger chance is low compared to many other arcane triggers. Shotgun builds relying on Tempo should include natural crit generation from the shotgun itself or a companion weapon to maximize trigger frequency. The fire rate bonus is Shotgun-specific; other weapon types equipped simultaneously do not receive the bonus. Triggering a second critical hit while the buff is active refreshes the 12-second timer rather than stacking fire rate. Shotguns with innately high fire rate benefit proportionally more from the percentage-based bonus in raw shots-per-second terms.
