## Overview

Arcane Tempo is a Warframe arcane that converts energy expenditure into attack speed bonuses. The arcane triggers whenever the Warframe's energy pool is drained by any source (ability casts, channeled abilities, Helminth subsumption drain, environmental energy damage) and provides scaling attack speed boosts. At maximum rank, the arcane grants 65% attack speed for 10 seconds per energy drain event. This mechanic creates powerful synergies with high-energy-cost ability builds and channel-oriented playstyles, transforming defensive energy management into offensive damage scaling. The arcane is particularly valuable on frames that maintain sustained channeled abilities or cast high-cost ultimates repeatedly, effectively rewarding aggressive ability usage with melee and weapon DPS scaling.

## Effect per rank

Arcane Tempo's attack speed bonus scales from 15% at rank 0 to 65% at rank 5, with a consistent 10-second duration across all ranks. Multiple energy drain events do not stack the buff; instead, each new drain refreshes the duration from 10 seconds. This incentivizes building high-cost abilities that drain large energy pools rapidly, as more drain events per minute increase effective uptime. At maximum rank with consistent energy spending (every 5--8 seconds), players can maintain near-constant 65% attack speed bonuses. The 10-second window is sufficient to cash in the bonus before the next ability cast, creating a tight feedback loop for active playstyles. Energy drain from mods like Fleeting Expertise, energy-hungry abilities (Exalted Blade, World on Fire) and Helminth subsumes maximize proc frequency.

## Acquisition

Arcane Tempo drops from Duviri/Endless (0.67% Legendary Tiers 1--3, 4% Rare Tier 9), Skirmish nodes (10% Uncommon Rotation C), Void Cascade (8.5% Uncommon), and Zariman Void Fissures (9% Uncommon). The most accessible farm involves Skirmish missions on Pluto, Neptune, and Venus, offering consistent uncommon (10%) drop rates over 10--15 minute runs. Void Cascade and Zariman Void Fissures provide similar drop chances with variable mission times. Duviri Tier 9 farming is viable for solo players but requires significant time commitment. Radioshare coordination in any of these sources dramatically improves farming efficiency, as drop rates apply independently to each squad member. The arcane's multiple farmable sources make it more accessible than Arcane Blessing.

## Best on

Arcane Tempo excels on high-energy-cost ability builds that naturally trigger frequent drain events. Excalibur Umbra with Exalted Blade channeling creates continuous drain events, maintaining near-permanent 65% attack speed uptime during melee engagements. Lavos leverages his ability-forward playstyle and natural energy drain to keep the buff active indefinitely. Octavia builds using high-cost songs (Amp, Resonator, Metronome) generate multiple drain events per ability rotation, effectively quadrupling attack speed scaling during active play. Hybrid weapon + ability frames (Saryn with weapon builds, Volt with melee) gain exceptional value from the passive DPS multiplier while maintaining their ability rotation. The arcane is less effective on low-energy-cost builds, frames with energy restoration mechanics (Trinity, Harrow), or pure AoE builds that do not use melee weapons frequently.

## Notes

Arcane Tempo triggers on all sources of energy drain, including channeled abilities, augment drain effects, and Zariman environmental energy siphons. The arcane does not distinguish between drain types; continuous drain from a single channeled ability counts as sustained triggering, refreshing the duration without stacking. Attack speed bonuses apply to melee weapons, weapon-based abilities (Exalted Blade, Landslide), and frame channeled attacks. At very high attack speed values (200%+), animation lock from fast attacks can exceed energy regeneration rates, requiring careful build balance. The arcane pairs exceptionally well with Breach Surge augment and Zariman void corruption effects that grant energy drain mitigation, enabling aggressive playstyles without risk of energy starvation. Combining with Helminth subsumption drains (Ensnare, Cascade, etc.) creates additional drain triggers independent of frame abilities, maximizing uptime potential.
