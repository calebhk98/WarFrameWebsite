## Overview

Melee Exposure strips enemy armor on slash status procs. This melee arcane synergizes with slash-focused builds by converting proc mechanics into armor vulnerability stacking.

| Rank | Proc Chance | Armor Reduction | Duration |
|------|-------------|-----------------|----------|
| 0    | 15%         | -25%            | 10s      |
| 1    | 22%         | -35%            | 10s      |
| 2    | 30%         | -45%            | 10s      |
| 3    | 37%         | -55%            | 10s      |
| 4    | 41%         | -65%            | 10s      |
| 5    | 45%         | -75%            | 10s      |

Armor stripping stacks independently, enabling rapid enemy vulnerability scaling during sustained melee engagements with high-slash-chance weapons.

## Acquisition

Melee Exposure drops from Zariman Void Fissure (4%) and Void Cascade rotation A (2%). Regular zariman void farming yields this arcane through natural progression.

## Best on

Melee Exposure is ideal for slash-focused melee weapons like scythes paired with armor-scaling warframes. The armor reduction enables hybrid damage builds to maximize enemy vulnerability.
