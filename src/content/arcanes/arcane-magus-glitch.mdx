## Overview

Magus Glitch provides team invisibility through temporary Void Cloak buffs when Void Sling is cast. This arcane transforms the operator into a support role by granting nearby Warframes brief stealth windows, enabling aggressive repositioning or emergency evasion during dangerous encounters.

## Effect

Each Void Sling cast grants all nearby Warframes a 4-second Void Cloak effect at rank 0, scaling to 8 seconds at maximum rank. The invisibility ends when the Warframe attacks or takes damage. Multiple operators can stack Glitch procs for extended stealth coverage.

## Acquisition

Magus Glitch is an uncommon drop from void cascade missions on Zariman. Early progression in void fissure content makes this arcane accessible to newer operators.

## Best on

Magus Glitch shines in stealth-focused squad compositions:

- **Invisibility-dependent Warframes** (Ash, Ivara, Octavia) benefit from repeated cloak refreshes.
- **High-risk void cascades** — the emergency stealth enables wipe recovery.
- **Spy mission void fissures** — stealth uptime is critical for silent progression.

## Notes

Void Cloak breaks on weapon fire or ability cast, limiting its utility for aggressive playstyles. The operator's own Warframe remains vulnerable when casting Sling. Best utilized in coordinated squads.
