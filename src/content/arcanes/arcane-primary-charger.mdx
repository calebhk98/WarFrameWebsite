## Overview

Arcane Primary Charger boosts primary weapon fire rate on reload. This weapon arcane rewards aggressive reload discipline and maintains offensive momentum during prolonged void fissure void engagements.

## Effects per rank

| Rank | Trigger Chance | Fire Rate Boost | Duration |
|------|----------------|-----------------|----------|
| 0    | 15%            | +20%            | 8s       |
| 1    | 22%            | +30%            | 8s       |
| 2    | 30%            | +40%            | 8s       |
| 3    | 37%            | +50%            | 8s       |
| 4    | 41%            | +55%            | 8s       |
| 5    | 45%            | +60%            | 8s       |

Fire rate scaling stacks multiplicatively with existing fire rate modifications, enabling primary weapons to achieve significant DPS increases during extended void fissure farming sessions.

## Acquisition

Arcane Primary Charger drops from Zariman Void Fissure rewards (3%) and Void Cascade rotation A (1.5%). Regular zariman void content participation yields this arcane naturally.

## Best on

Arcane Primary Charger is ideal for sustained-fire primary weapons like rifles and shotguns. The fire rate buff enables high-ammo-capacity primaries to leverage increased magazine throughput during void fissure content.

## Sources

Zariman void fissure farming provides the most accessible source. Players regularly completing void fissures naturally accumulate primary charger copies.
