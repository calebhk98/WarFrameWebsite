## Overview

Arcane Primary Charger grants a Primary Weapon Damage bonus when a melee kill is secured. Each melee kill checks a 30% trigger chance, and on success, all primary weapon damage increases by the listed percentage for 12 seconds. The arcane creates a melee-to-primary handoff rhythm: engage weak clusters of enemies with melee to secure kills and trigger the bonus, then switch to primary weapons to deliver the boosted output against more durable targets.

At rank 5, a successful melee kill provides +300% Primary Weapon Damage for 12 seconds. A primary weapon that deals 1000 damage per shot would deal 4000 damage per shot under this bonus -- a fourfold increase that dramatically accelerates clearing heavy enemies or bosses after the buff activates.

## Effect

Each melee kill checks the 30% trigger chance. On success, primary weapon damage increases by the listed percentage for 12 seconds. The bonus applies to all damage from primary weapons during the window -- base damage, elemental damage, and status-based damage all scale with the multiplicative increase. At rank 0, +50% Primary Weapon Damage provides a meaningful boost to follow-up primary shots. At rank 5, +300% primary damage on secondary kill triggers is a substantial multiplier that scales effectively with already-modded primary weapon builds.

The 12-second window allows sustained primary weapon fire or multiple charged shots to benefit from the bonus. Builds that mix melee into their rotation to cull weaker enemies then pivot to primary weapons for priority targets are the intended gameplay pattern.

## Acquisition

Arcane Primary Charger drops from Zariman Void Fissure rewards at 3% and Void Cascade rotation A at 1.5%. Access requires completing Angels of the Zariman. Rotation A in Void Cascade is the most frequently reached rotation in standard runs, making the arcane available in most extended Void Cascade sessions without needing to farm deep rotations. Warframe.market provides an alternative for players preferring to purchase rather than farm through Zariman content.

## Builds that use it

Arcane Primary Charger suits hybrid melee-primary builds that combine both weapon types in regular rotation. Frames with strong melee abilities that cull small enemies -- Excalibur using Exalted Blade on crowds, Saryn with melee augment interaction -- generate frequent melee kills that feed primary weapon buff windows. The arcane is particularly effective on primary weapons with high base damage or charge mechanics: Lanka, Rubico Prime, and Cedo deal concentrated high-damage shots that amplify strongly with a +300% bonus.

Heavy Attack melee builds that perform single-kill heavy attacks against isolated enemies and then immediately switch to primary weapons for the boss or heavy enemy behind them benefit from the consistent 30% trigger on each kill. Builds on frames that can reset melee combo quickly (Berserker Fury, Blood Rush stacks via exonus arcane sets) can cycle rapidly between melee kills and primary bonuses.

## Notes

The 30% trigger chance is fixed at all ranks; only the damage bonus scales. The bonus applies to primary weapons in general, meaning any primary equipped benefits regardless of weapon category -- rifles, shotguns, and bows all receive the bonus. The buff does not stack from multiple melee kills within the duration; a second trigger while the buff is active refreshes the 12-second timer. Melee kills from exalted weapons (Excalibur's Exalted Blade, Valkyr's Talons) count as melee kills for trigger purposes.
