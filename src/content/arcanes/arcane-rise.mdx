## Overview

Arcane Rise provides damage scaling while airborne. This warframe arcane rewards mobile, parkour-focused playstyles by incentivizing jump attack usage during void fissure content.

Consistent airborne damage enables momentum-based gameplay where jump attacks generate continuous damage bonuses.

## Acquisition

Arcane Rise drops from Zariman Void Fissure (4%) and Void Cascade rotation A (2%). Regular zariman void farming yields this arcane naturally.

## Best on

Arcane Rise excels on parkour-focused warframes with high jump attack rate. The aerial damage boost complements slide attack and bullet jump builds.
