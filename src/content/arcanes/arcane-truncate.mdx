## Overview

Arcane Truncate is a rare energy-management Warframe arcane that prevents energy loss through proc-based restoration. This arcane enables infinite-uptime ability builds by creating energy-positive feedback loops during sustained ability casting.

## Effect

Whenever the Warframe loses energy (from casting abilities, drain, or enemy effects), there is a 15% chance at rank 0, scaling to 75% at rank 5, that the lost energy is immediately restored. At maximum rank, nearly every energy expenditure is negated.

## Acquisition

Arcane Truncate drops as a rare reward from Void Cascade rotation C missions (20+ minutes). Extended void cascade farming provides consistent drops.

## Best on

Arcane Truncate is essential for ability-spam builds:

- **High-drain ability frames** (Mesa, Equinox, Saryn) maintain permanent uptime.
- **Endless void cascades** — ability casting sustains energy indefinitely.
- **Energy-negative builds** — restores balance in drain-heavy mod setups.

## Notes

The proc chance applies per energy expenditure; frequent ability casting maximizes restoration rate. At rank 5 with high cast frequency, energy becomes nearly a non-resource. Stacks with energy-restoration mods for exponential scaling.
