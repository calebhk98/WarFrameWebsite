## Overview

Arcane Truncate is a Warframe arcane that gives a percentage chance to restore energy whenever the Warframe loses energy. It is installed in one of the Warframe's arcane slots and functions as a passive energy management tool for builds that spend energy rapidly through ability casting or continuous drain abilities. At rank 5, the 75% restoration chance means that, on average, three out of every four energy expenditures are immediately refunded, dramatically reducing net energy consumption over the course of a mission.

The arcane is most relevant in endgame content where energy density from pickups alone is insufficient to sustain high-cast-frequency builds without external energy sources like Zenurik Focus or Arcane Energize. Arcane Truncate occupies a different niche from Arcane Energize: while Energize provides burst energy restoration on health orb pickup, Truncate provides incremental restoration tied directly to casting frequency, making it more consistent for builds that cast continuously rather than in bursts.

## Effect

Each time the Warframe spends energy - whether from casting an ability, using a channeled drain, or losing energy to an enemy ability like Energy Leech - Arcane Truncate has a chance to restore the amount of energy that was just spent. At rank 0 the restoration chance is 15%. At rank 5 the chance reaches 75%. The restoration is instant and occurs at the moment of energy expenditure, before any energy floor checks.

The arcane does not activate on energy gained from pickups, Zenurik flows, or other external sources. It only responds to energy loss events.

## Acquisition

Arcane Truncate drops as a rare reward from Void Cascade missions at rotation C, which corresponds to approximately 20 minutes of continuous mission time. The Zariman Void Cascade mission type is available after completing the Angels of the Zariman quest. Rotation C rewards appear at wave 6 and every third wave thereafter in Zariman cascades. Players targeting this arcane should plan for extended runs that reach the rotation C threshold. The arcane can be traded between players through standard trade channels.

## Builds that use it

Arcane Truncate is most effective for Warframes that cast abilities at high frequency over sustained engagements.

Ability-spam builds on frames like Mesa, Equinox, Garuda, and Octavia rely on consistent energy supply to maintain their offensive or support loops. At rank 5, these builds recover energy on three out of four casts on average, which compounds with natural energy regeneration to create near self-sufficient energy loops in the absence of energy orbs or external support.

Energy-drain builds - frames running Fleeting Expertise and Streamline to reduce costs while maintaining a continuous toggle ability - benefit from Truncate because each energy tick from the drain ability has a 75% chance to return the energy spent, effectively reducing the net drain rate substantially.

Steel Path endurance runs where energy density from fallen enemies is lower than standard content also favor Arcane Truncate as a background sustain tool alongside a primary energy source like Zenurik.

## Notes

At rank 5, the expected value per energy expenditure is 0.75 energy returned for every 1 energy spent, giving an effective 75% discount on all ability costs when averaged over many casts. In practice, variance means some casts cost full energy and others are free, but the law of large numbers smooths this over a full mission. The arcane does not interfere with ability cost-reduction mods - Fleeting Expertise and Streamline apply first, and Truncate then operates on the reduced cost, slightly lowering the absolute energy that might be returned per cast compared to running it without cost reduction.
