## Overview

Theorem Contagion spreads toxin contagion from operator void abilities. On every void blast ability cast, toxin spreads to nearby enemies dealing void damage. This arcane synergizes with Zariman void-focused builds. Early ranks infect 4 enemies at 50% void damage; maximum rank infects 8 enemies at 250% void damage.

Theorem Contagion activates automatically when its trigger condition is met during combat.
