## Overview

Arcane Eruption is an uncommon utility arcane that triggers knockdown effects whenever the warframe or squad picks up an energy orb. At max rank, the arcane guarantees a knockdown with every energy pickup, providing consistent crowd control in endgame missions where energy pickups are plentiful. The knockdown effect stuns nearby enemies, interrupting attacks and creating breathing room for aggressive ability rotations or team repositioning. Eruption's primary strength lies in its passive crowd-control scaling: the more energy-hungry the squad composition, the more frequently Eruption procs, multiplying the arcane's utility.

Unlike Energize and Grace which provide personal defense or sustain, Eruption is a utility and crowd-control tool that enhances team survivability through active enemy suppression. Squad-wide energy sustain builds that stack multiple Energizes create secondary knockdown chains through overlapping Eruption procs, creating emergent crowd-control effects that can lock down high-threat targets. The arcane is particularly valuable in Void Fissure endless missions where squad energy management and crowd control are equally critical.

| 4 | 85% | Near-guaranteed knockdown on most energy pickups. |
| 5 | 100% | Guaranteed knockdown on every energy pickup. |

The knockdown effect applies to all nearby enemies within a radius proportional to the energy-pickup radius (approximately 15--20 meters). Multiple squad members with Eruption can proc independently, creating chained knockdowns if they pick up orbs in sequence or simultaneously. At max rank, a 4-player squad with energy sustain can maintain near-perpetual enemy crowd control, trivializing offense for many endgame encounters.

## Acquisition

Arcane Eruption drops from Eidolon Gantulyst capture at 9.25% chance, from Zariman void skirmish rotation C at 5.63% chance, and from Duviri Void Cascade Tier 9 at 4.0% chance. The multiple drop sources make Eruption one of the more accessible arcanes to farm, with a combined 18--20% drop rate across Eidolon and Zariman content. The Uncommon rarity means leveling costs are minimal, and trading-post prices remain modest even for max-rank copies.

Cost to acquire a single max-rank Eruption is typically 5--20 platinum on the trading post, making it one of the cheapest arcanes to obtain, even for players with limited trading history.

## Best on

Arcane Eruption synergizes best with energy-spam warframes that naturally generate energy pickups through ability casting and loot collection: Mag, Saryn, Mirage, Mesa, Ember, and Fire-and-forget casters. Frames that use Energize in tandem with high-drain ability rotations create emergent Eruption synergy where knockdowns from Eruption combined with Energize sustain keep squads alive and enemies suppressed. Support frames like Trinity benefit enormously from Eruption because every Energize pickup from squad members triggers an independent crowd-control proc, multiplying the team's collective knockdown rate.

Frames with low energy drain and ability cast frequency (Protea, Wisp with proper efficiency builds) may not trigger Eruption frequently enough to justify its slot, opting instead for offensive or survival-focused arcanes.

## Builds that use it

Ability-spam damage builds pair Eruption with Energize and Grace to create self-sustaining crowd-control + damage loops. Support builds using Trinity often dual-slot Eruption to multiply knockdown frequency across the squad, enhancing team survivability beyond pure healing. Void Fissure and Void Cascade endless runs frequently incorporate Eruption for its passive crowd-control contribution at zero active cost. Speedrun builds may omit Eruption if crowd control is unnecessary, relying instead on pure damage output and enemy elimination speed.

## Notes

Knockdown effects are interrupted by many enemy abilities and mechanics. Shield-carrying enemies and nullifier bubbles block knockdowns, meaning Eruption's utility is not universally reliable across all endgame missions. However, in open-world content, Void Fissures, and standard endless mission types, Eruption's crowd control is consistently valuable. The arcane's effectiveness scales with squad energy sustain: a squad without Energize generates far fewer energy pickups, making Eruption much less potent.

The knockdown mechanic was balanced in Update 27.2.0 to ensure consistent utility across arcane types, preventing single-arcane dominance of crowd control.
