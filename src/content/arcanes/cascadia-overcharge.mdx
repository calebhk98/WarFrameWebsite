## Overview

Cascadia Overcharge is a secondary weapon arcane that provides massive critical chance bonuses while the player maintains active overshields. Introduced alongside Angels of the Zariman, this arcane incentivizes defensive playstyles that maintain overshield buffers while still dealing consistent damage. The synergy with overshield-generating abilities and arcanes creates interesting build opportunities around survivability and offense.

The arcane's conditional nature rewards careful shield management and synergizes well with abilities like Hildryn's Aegis Storm or arcanes like Arcane Blessing that provide consistent overshield refreshes. During extended engagements where overshields remain active, the critical chance bonus can exceed base weapon critical stats by several hundred percent, making it exceptionally powerful on critical-oriented secondary weapons.

| 4    | +250%                | Active overshields |
| 5    | +300%                | Active overshields |

Each rank doubles the critical chance multiplier, with maximum rank providing a +300% bonus that stacks additively with base weapon critical values. The effect activates only while the player maintains any amount of active overshield, dropping instantly when shields deplete.

## Acquisition

Cascadia Overcharge drops from Zariman Void Angels encounters, with the highest rates coming from Void Angel enemies at 1.67% rarity. The guaranteed common-rarity drop from the Holdfasts (Cavalero Angel) mission provides reliable access for players building towards the arcane. Multiple runs provide consistent progression towards higher ranks.

Players farming for this arcane benefit from tackling higher-level Void Angel encounters, as the drop chance scales with enemy difficulty. Paired farming with Cascadia Flare and Cascadia Empowered during Zariman runs maximizes resource efficiency.

## Best on

Cascadia Overcharge pairs exceptionally well with high-critical-chance secondary weapons like the Epitaph, Acceltra, or Akmagnus. Weapons with base critical chances above 20% leverage the bonus most effectively, turning modest crits into consistent forced procs. The arcane encourages pairing overshield-generation builds with crit-focused loadouts for synergistic gameplay.

Warframes capable of maintaining overshields--such as Frost through shield mechanics, Hildryn through Aegis Storm, or frames using Arcane Blessing--maximize uptime on the bonus. Speed-focused gameplay combined with reactive overshield management keeps the bonus active during most encounters.
