## Overview

Molt Vigor grants a temporary Ability Strength boost to the next warframe ability cast following any Operator Ability use. An Operator Ability includes Focus school active abilities -- Void Dash, Void Blast, Unairu's Void Shadow, Madurai's Phoenix Talons, Zenurik's Wellspring, and other school-specific active skills. Using any of these in operator mode primes the warframe's next ability cast with a strength bonus, then consumes that primer on the first ability activation after returning to warframe mode.

The mechanic creates a synergy loop between the operator's Focus school combat and the warframe's ability output: the operator ability applies the primer, the player returns to warframe mode, and the first ability cast benefits from the Ability Strength bonus. Players who regularly cycle between operator Focus abilities and warframe abilities can sustain this primer on every other warframe cast, effectively maintaining the bonus during active transference cycling.

At maximum rank, the arcane also provides one Arcane Revive charge per mission.

## Effect

At rank 0, +15% Ability Strength on the next warframe ability applies to the first cast after an operator ability. At rank 5, +45% Ability Strength boosts the next warframe cast significantly -- for abilities with linear damage or heal scaling, this represents a 45% output increase on that cast.

The bonus is consumed by the first warframe ability cast after the operator ability trigger. Subsequent casts in the same warframe phase do not benefit unless the player returns to operator mode, uses another operator ability, and then casts again.

## Builds that use it

Molt Vigor suits ability-damage builds on frames that regularly use both operator Focus abilities and high-impact warframe abilities in the same combat rotation. Nova builds that cycle into operator for Void Dash repositioning and then return to cast Molecular Prime benefit from the +45% Strength bonus applying to Prime's damage and slow -- increasing the Molecular Prime output on the first post-return cast. Limbo builds using operator mode and then casting Banish or Cataclysm gain the strength bonus on the ability that defines the Limbo zone's utility.

Frames paired with Zenurik school that cast Wellspring in operator mode then return to warframe to begin ability casting receive the Molt Vigor bonus on their first cast while also benefiting from Wellspring's energy regeneration -- combining operator utility with warframe output efficiently.

## Notes

The bonus is consumed on the first warframe ability cast after the operator ability event. Using multiple operator abilities before returning to warframe does not stack multiple Molt Vigor primes -- only one primer is active at a time. The Arcane Revive at rank 5 provides a mission-safe insurance charge. The strength bonus is additive with other Ability Strength sources -- Intensify, Umbral Intensify, Power Drift, and the Molt Vigor bonus all add together before the total multiplier applies to ability output.
