## Overview

Secondary Encumber is a weapon arcane for secondary weapons that amplifies status effect application. When a status effect proc triggers on an enemy, Secondary Encumber grants a 24% chance at max rank to immediately apply a second random status effect. This arcane dramatically increases elemental effect diversity and status buildup rates, enabling rapid enemy ailment stacking. The mechanic particularly benefits status-focused secondary weapons and status-spreading frames.

## Effects per Rank

| Rank | Extra Proc Chance |
|------|---:|
| 0 | 4% |
| 1 | 8% |
| 2 | 12% |
| 3 | 16% |
| 4 | 20% |
| 5 | 24% |

Each status application independently rolls the trigger chance, allowing multiple bonus procs from rapid-hitting weapons. The random status effect is selected from the available damage types in the current mission environment, creating unpredictable but powerful status combinations. The mechanic stacks with frame abilities that enhance status application rates.

## Acquisition

Secondary Encumber drops from Zariman void fissure missions as a moderately rare weapon arcane. The arcane requires status-focused secondary builds to farm efficiently, making high-status-chance weapons and corrosive/viral builds essential. Public void fissure groups with diverse status-spreading frames accelerate farming progression. The arcane's value scales directly with weapon status chance.

## Best On

Secondary Encumber excels with high-status-chance secondary weapons like the Euphona Prime, Tysis, or Akbolto Prime. Frames that amplify status application such as Saryn with her toxic spreads or Harrow with status rate bonuses gain exceptional synergies. Status-focused warframes like Mirage with her illusion spreads or Nidus building mutation stacks benefit substantially. Weapons with natural elemental damage components maximize the arcane's proc frequency.

Weapons without status effects or accuracy penalties gain minimal benefit. Precision-focused secondaries lacking status components underutilize this arcane.
