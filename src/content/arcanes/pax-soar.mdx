## Overview

Pax Soar improves three interconnected aerial combat stats simultaneously: accuracy while airborne, recoil reduction while airborne, and aim glide duration. Together these three bonuses support a mobile, airborne combat style where the player uses aim glide windows for precision shooting with the Kitgun. The extended aim glide duration provides more time to take accurate shots from an elevated or airborne position, while the accuracy and recoil bonuses ensure the shots placed during that window land precisely.

Kitguns are modular secondary weapons built through the Ventkid market on Orb Vallis. Pax arcanes are exclusive to the Kitgun slot and are installed directly on the completed weapon rather than in the typical secondary arcane slot. Pax Soar is one of four Pax arcane options and targets aerial engagement rather than kill-triggered procs or battery performance.

## Effect

At rank 0: +12.5% accuracy while airborne, -12.5% weapon recoil while airborne, and +1.3 seconds aim glide/wall latch duration. At rank 3: +50% accuracy while airborne, -50% weapon recoil while airborne, and +5 seconds aim glide/wall latch duration.

The +5-second aim glide extension at rank 3 is particularly impactful - base Warframe aim glide duration is approximately 3 seconds, so Pax Soar nearly triples the available aim glide window, allowing extended mid-air targeting sequences before landing. Wall latch duration also extends by the same amount, enabling longer wall-mounted shooting positions.

## Acquisition

Pax Soar is obtained through Ventkid-related content on the Orb Vallis, Venus, through standing rewards, blueprint crafting, or trading. The WFCD dataset does not document specific drop tables. Community resources and the in-game Ventkid market offer current sourcing information. Warframe.market provides direct platinum purchase from other players.

## Builds that use it

Pax Soar suits Kitgun builds focused on aerial gunplay - bullet-jumping into position, activating aim glide, and firing precisely during the extended float window. Frames with strong aerial ability synergies - Zephyr's Tailwind that extends airborne time, Titania's Razorwing for permanent flight, or Garuda's Seeking Talons for gap closing - benefit from Pax Soar's extended aim glide as part of their movement pattern.

Parkour-heavy playstyles that use vertical movement and wall-running through mission environments benefit from both the extended wall latch duration (allowing longer wall-mounted shooting positions) and the improved airborne accuracy during movement sequences. High-accuracy Kitgun chambers like Rattleguts or Catchmoon aim builds see the 50% accuracy bonus reduce projectile spread further, tightening effective range during airborne fire.

## Notes

The accuracy and recoil bonuses apply specifically while airborne - including both the natural fall phase after a jump and the aim glide phase. Ground-based fire does not benefit from these modifiers. The aim glide extension applies to all weapons while the Kitgun with Pax Soar is equipped in the secondary slot, not just to the Kitgun itself - this means primary weapon users who switch to aim glide also benefit from the extended window. Wall latch duration similarly extends regardless of which weapon the player is aiming during the wall mount.
