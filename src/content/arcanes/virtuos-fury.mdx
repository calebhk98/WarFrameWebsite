## Overview

Virtuos Fury is an Operator arcane that amplifies the damage output of the Operator's Amp weapon when the Amp successfully deals Void damage. It is installed in the Virtuos arcane slot and is primarily valued for Eidolon hunt content, where the Operator's Amp is the primary damage source against Eidolon synapses and Eidolon shields. At maximum rank, Virtuos Fury adds +150% damage to all Amp hits for 8 seconds following any successful Void damage contact, effectively tripling Amp output from a single hit onward.

The arcane's proc condition - a successful Void damage hit - is trivially met in any content where the Operator shoots the Amp at enemies. Once the initial hit lands and the buff activates, the 8-second window refreshes with each subsequent hit, creating near-permanent uptime during sustained Amp fire. This makes Virtuos Fury one of the highest-value offensive Operator arcanes available.

## Effect

When the Operator's Amp deals Void damage to an enemy, Virtuos Fury activates a damage-amplifying buff on all subsequent Amp attacks. At rank 0 the buff provides +30% Amp damage. At maximum rank 5 the buff reaches +150% Amp damage. The buff lasts 8 seconds at all ranks. Each successful Void damage hit refreshes the 8-second window, so continuous Amp fire on enemies maintains the buff without interruption.

The damage bonus is additive with other Amp damage modifiers from Focus Schools and Amp arcane slots, contributing a large flat percentage bonus to the total damage formula.

## Acquisition

Virtuos Fury drops from Eidolon Teralyst captures during Void Fissure hunts on the Plains of Eidolon. Players who run Eidolon hunts while a Void Fissure is active on the Plains receive arcane drops from the Eidolon capture at the end of each Teralyst kill. The approximate drop chance is 3% per Teralyst capture in this context. The arcane can also be traded with other players through standard trade channels.

Consistent Eidolon farming over multiple night cycles is the fastest path to acquiring Virtuos Fury at rank 0. Ranking the arcane to 5 requires multiple copies. Each copy of Virtuos Fury increases the rank when combined at the Mod Station, following the same arcane upgrade process as all other Warframe arcanes.

## Builds that use it

Virtuos Fury is a core arcane for any Operator build focused on Eidolon takedowns or Amp-heavy damage output.

Eidolon hunt builds treat Virtuos Fury as near-mandatory. The Eidolon's synapses require sustained Amp damage to break, and the +150% bonus at rank 5 directly halves the time needed to destroy each synapse compared to a build without the arcane. Hunt squad coordination revolves around managing the Eidolon shield cycle, and faster synapse breaks reduce the number of shield cycles required.

Void Cascade mission builds benefit from Virtuos Fury when the Operator is tasked with eliminating Void Angel targets, which require Amp damage. The damage bonus reduces the time spent in Operator form per Angel kill, improving overall mission pace.

## Notes

The 8-second buff timer means a brief pause in firing will not drop the buff immediately. Operators who need to dodge or reposition between shots maintain the Virtuos Fury bonus as long as the gap between shots stays under 8 seconds. At rank 5, the damage bonus is large enough that the Amp's effective damage output is substantially different before and after the first proc, so initiating engagement with the Amp as early as possible establishes the buff before the high-priority phase of the fight begins.
