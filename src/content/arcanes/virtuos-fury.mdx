## Overview

Virtuos Fury is an Operator arcane that amplifies Void Damage output from the operator's Amp when damaging enemies. This offensive-focused arcane is essential for Eidolon hunts and other endgame content where Void Damage burst DPS determines success. At maximum rank, Virtuos Fury provides a 150% damage multiplier to all Amp attacks for 8 seconds.

## Effect

When the operator's Amp successfully hits an enemy with Void Damage, a stacking buff is applied that increases all subsequent Amp damage by +30% at rank 0, scaling to +150% at rank 5. The buff lasts 8 seconds and refreshes with each successful hit, enabling near-permanent uptime during active combat phases.

## Acquisition

Virtuos Fury drops from Eidolon Teralyst captures during void fissure hunts. Players farming Eidolons for relics and other rewards frequently obtain this arcane. The drop rate is approximately 3% per capture.

## Best on

Virtuos Fury is mandatory for endgame operator builds:

- **Eidolon hunts** — the damage buff is critical for efficient Eidolon takedown.
- **High-tier Void Fissure missions** — Amp DPS directly scales reward farming efficiency.
- **Void Cascade solo speedruns** — damage buff accelerates objective completion.

## Notes

The buff refreshes on each hit, making the damage bonus nearly permanent during sustained Void Damage output. Pairs exceptionally well with high-Fire-Rate amps and Void Blast upgrades. Stacks additively with other damage mods.
