## Overview

Arcane Affinity is a rare experience-focused Warframe arcane that amplifies affinity (experience) gains when farming. This arcane accelerates Warframe and weapon leveling, significantly reducing time-to-mastery during endgame progression farming.

## Effect

When the Warframe gains affinity, all subsequent affinity gains receive a multiplicative bonus scaling from +30% at rank 0 to +150% at rank 5 for 12 seconds. Continuous farming maintains near-permanent buff uptime, exponentially accelerating leveling.

## Acquisition

Arcane Affinity drops as a rare reward from Void Cascade rotation C missions (at 20+ minutes). Extended void cascade farming provides consistent drops.

## Best on

Arcane Affinity is essential for efficiency-focused progression:

- **Mastery Rank farming** — accelerates weapon/frame leveling expeditiously.
- **Endless void cascades** — sustained farming maintains permanent buff.
- **Squad farming groups** — shared experience gains trigger frequent procs.

## Notes

The buff applies to all sources of affinity (weapon kills, ability kills, squad affinity). Multiplicative scaling makes the arcane exponentially effective in high-kill-rate scenarios. Pairs well with affinity-sharing squad builds.
