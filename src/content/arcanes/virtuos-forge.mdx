## Overview

Virtuos Forge grants fire rate bonuses after void dashing in operator mode, creating a synergy between operator mobility and warframe weapon performance. Void Dash is the operator's primary traversal ability, triggered by dashing in midair to propel the operator at high speed. By converting each void dash into a fire rate buff upon returning to the warframe, Forge rewards players who actively use operator mode for repositioning rather than remaining exclusively in their warframe.

The arcane suits operator-active playstyles that cycle between warframe and operator mode frequently. Builds using Madurai school for Void Sling damage or Zenurik for energy regen already perform regular void dashes; Virtuos Forge adds weapon performance value to those same traversal actions.

## Effect

Performing a void dash in operator mode triggers the fire rate buff, which activates when returning to the warframe. At rank 0, 20% fire rate for 10 seconds is sufficient to noticeably accelerate weapon cycling; at rank 5, 100% fire rate for 15 seconds effectively doubles firing speed for the entire duration. The buff applies to the active weapon at the time of re-entry into the warframe and refreshes the duration on each subsequent void dash return.

The fire rate bonus stacks additively with Speed Trigger, Lethal Torrent, and other fire rate mods installed on the weapon, compounding with existing fire rate investments.

## Acquisition

Virtuos Forge drops from Zariman Ten-Zero missions and Void Cascade content. The arcane is accessible through standard Zariman progression after completing Angels of the Zariman. Regular Void Cascade runs accumulate Forge copies through rotation rewards. Warframe.market provides direct purchase options for players seeking targeted acquisition without extended farming.

## Builds that use it

Virtuos Forge benefits builds where operator void dashing is a regular part of the combat cycle. Madurai school builds that use Void Sling for damage proc void dashes frequently, activating Forge consistently. Full-auto primary weapons with high fire rate caps benefit most from doubling their effective firing speed during the buff window. Builds that use transference for energy management (Zenurik Void Sling for energy restore) already perform dashes often, converting those dashes into fire rate bonuses without additional effort. The arcane pairs well with secondary fire rate arcanes to create compound fire rate stacking during active buff windows.

## Notes

Void Dash must be performed in operator mode to trigger the buff; warframe rolls, slides, or sprint-boosts do not activate Forge. The buff activates on returning to the warframe, not on performing the void dash in operator mode. Operators who use void sling for combat without returning to the warframe do not benefit from the fire rate bonus during that combat phase. The 15-second duration at rank 5 covers multiple weapon reload cycles, making Forge particularly efficient for weapons with standard 2-3 second reload speeds.
