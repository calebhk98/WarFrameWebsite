## Overview

Virtuos Forge grants fire rate bonuses on void dash repositioning. On every void dash usage, the arcane grants temporary fire rate scaling. This arcane rewards aggressive operator positioning. Early ranks grant 20% fire rate for 10s; maximum rank grants 100% fire rate for 15s.

Virtuos Forge drops from various Zariman and Void Cascade missions.

This arcane works best on builds that exploit its triggering condition frequently.

## Usage

Virtuos Forge activates automatically when its trigger condition is met during combat.
