## Overview

Exodia Hunt is a Zaw arcane that enhances ground slam crowd control. When a player performs a ground slam melee attack, Exodia Hunt grants a 50% chance to pull nearby enemies into melee range. At max rank, the pull effect reaches 12 meters, enabling aggressive repositioning and crowd clustering from mid-range encounters. The arcane transforms ground slams into reliable crowd-control tools and synergizes with heavy-attack-focused Zaw builds.

The trigger chance remains consistent at 50% across all ranks, with pull range scaling linearly. The mechanic applies on ground slam activation, allowing rapid trigger cycles with frequent jump-slam rotations. Multiple ground slams in succession can stack pull effects, creating exponential crowd clustering.

## Acquisition

Exodia Hunt drops from Zariman void fissure missions as a moderately rare Zaw arcane. The arcane requires Zaw melee builds with frequent ground slam capabilities to farm efficiently. Public void fissure groups benefit from coordinating heavy-attack rotations and crowd clustering tactics. The arcane's effectiveness scales with attack speed mods and movement-based ability frequency. Ground slam frequency bonuses from mods significantly accelerate farming progression for this arcane.

## Best On

Exodia Hunt excels on Zaw builds with high attack speed or those emphasizing ground slam mechanics. Frames with movement enhancements or ground-slam-boosting abilities such as Excalibur with his exalted blade gain exceptional synergies. Warframes with jump-frequency bonuses or mobility focus maximize the arcane's trigger rate. Heavy-damage Zaw constructs benefit from the crowd clustering enabling chain-hit damage multiplication.

Light, fast-striking Zaw builds without ground slam emphasis underutilize this arcane. Frames lacking mobility or jump mechanics gain minimal benefit from the trigger condition.
