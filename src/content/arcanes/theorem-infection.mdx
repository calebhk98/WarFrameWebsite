## Overview

Theorem Infection is a warframe arcane that amplifies damage output of companions and summoned allies positioned within Residual Arcane zones. This arcane functions identically to Theorem Contagion in mechanical function, providing stacking damage multipliers to all nearby summoned units and pets. The distinction allows players to collect both Theorem support arcanes for squad composition flexibility or dedicated companion-focused builds.

The arcane specializes in creating powerful synergies with pet-reliant warframes, enabling companions to function as primary damage dealers rather than supplementary utility. With stacking up to 15x and persistence for 20 seconds outside zones, coordinated pet management combined with Residual Arcane zone usage creates exponential damage scaling throughout mission duration. The 90-meter aura covers most standard tileset areas, making zone positioning the primary tactical consideration.

## Acquisition

Theorem Infection drops exclusively from Deimos Isolation Vault Rotation C encounters at 15% uncommon-rarity rates. Unlike Theorem Demulcent, no guaranteed drops exist outside vault farming. Multiple run cycles across difficulty tiers (30-40, 40-50, 50-60) provide consistent progression toward higher ranks. Squad coordination during void fissure farming optimizes resource gathering across all Cambion Drift materials simultaneously.

The consistent 15% drop rate means dedicated farmers should expect 6-7 vault runs per arcane rank on average. Combining vault runs with void fissure participation maximizes void traces for relic refinement during the same playtime investment.

## Best on

Theorem Infection excels on any warframe with significant companion or summoned unit reliance. Prime candidates include Khora Prime (Venari scaling), Atlas Prime (rumblers), Wisp (motes), Nekros Prime (shadows), and Volt Prime (electric shield scaling). Syndicates offering companion enhancement mods unlock additional scaling multipliers when combined with Theorem Infection buffs.

The arcane encourages aggressive pet deployment within Residual Arcane zones, creating dynamic positioning gameplay around zone management. Frames with teleportation abilities can burst-stack pets into zones for explosive damage windows, while mobile frames can maintain zone proximity to keep buffs active throughout engagements.
