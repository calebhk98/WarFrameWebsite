## Overview

Eternal Eradicate is an operator arcane that boosts Amp Damage when the operator uses an Operator Ability. Activating any Focus school ability -- Madurai's Void Radiance, Unairu's Void Shadow, Zenurik's Energizing Dash, Naramon's Void Snare, or Vazarin's Guardian Shell -- triggers the damage bonus to the operator's Amp for 8 seconds. This creates a natural synergy between ability usage and amp combat: activating a Focus ability at the start of an operator engagement sets up an elevated damage window for the amp shots that follow.

At rank 5, using an Operator Ability grants +60% Amp Damage for 8 seconds. Operators who activate their Focus ability at the beginning of each operator engagement maintain consistent high-damage amp windows throughout missions involving frequent transference cycling.

## Effect

Each Operator Ability activation triggers the Eternal Eradicate bonus, applying the listed damage increase to all Amp shots for 8 seconds. At rank 0, +10% Amp Damage is a modest bonus. At rank 5, +60% Amp Damage significantly increases amp output during the window -- an amp that normally deals 1000 damage per shot deals 1600 during the Eradicate buff. The 8-second window covers multiple amp fire sequences before expiring, making it worth activating a Focus ability as the first action upon entering operator mode.

If the same operator ability has a cooldown longer than 8 seconds, there will be gaps between Eradicate windows. Abilities with shorter cooldowns or multiple charges maintain higher uptime.

## Acquisition

Eternal Eradicate drops from Zariman Void Cascade rewards at 4% and Void Fissure rotation C at 2%. Rotation C in Void Fissure requires extended runs to reach, making Eradicate one of the rarer amp arcanes in the Zariman pool. Void Cascade at 4% is the more accessible primary farming path. Warframe.market provides a direct purchase alternative for players who prefer platinum acquisition.

## Builds that use it

Eternal Eradicate benefits operator builds that use Focus abilities actively during combat. Madurai school operators who activate Void Radiance for the initial damage burst before sustained amp fire naturally trigger Eradicate's damage window. Unairu operators who activate abilities for armor stripping then follow up with amp fire against stripped enemies benefit from both effects simultaneously -- the armor strip increases effective amp damage further while Eradicate adds a flat damage boost on top.

Eidolon hunt operators who follow a strict ability-then-amp rotation for Teralyst, Gantulyst, and Hydrolyst limb breaks gain the most concentrated benefit: activating the Focus ability activates Eradicate, then all limb-break amp shots land during the damage window.

## Notes

The trigger requires an active Operator Ability input -- passive Focus school bonuses that are always active do not trigger Eradicate. The 8-second buff begins at ability activation, not at the end of the ability's effect duration. For abilities with instantaneous effects (Void Radiance burst), the amp damage window begins immediately. The damage bonus applies to all amp fire during the 8-second window, not just the next shot. Activating a second Focus ability while the buff is active refreshes the 8-second timer rather than stacking additional damage bonuses.
