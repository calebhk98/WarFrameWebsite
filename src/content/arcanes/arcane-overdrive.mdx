## Overview

Arcane Overdrive is an uncommon attack-speed arcane that grants weapon speed bonuses whenever a Warframe casts an ability. This arcane synergizes with ability-centric builds, enabling weapon-ability hybrid playstyles where frequent casting triggers sustained attack speed scaling.

## Effect

Each ability cast grants a temporary attack speed bonus to the Warframe's equipped weapons, lasting 12 seconds. The bonus scales from +25% at rank 0 to +125% at rank 5. Frequent ability casting maintains near-permanent uptime.

## Acquisition

Arcane Overdrive drops as an uncommon reward from Zariman void cascade endless missions. Early progression through extended cascades grants access.

## Best on

Arcane Overdrive enhances ability-spam hybrid builds:

- **Ability-centric Warframes** (Frost, Mag, Nova) with quick recycle times.
- **Attack-speed-scaling weapons** — the bonus amplifies weapon DPS exponentially.
- **Endless cascades** — sustained ability casting maintains permanent buff.

## Notes

Ability-casting frequency directly controls buff uptime. Stationary ability casters gain maximum benefit. Stacks multiplicatively with attack-speed mods on weapons. Synergizes well with rapid-cast-time abilities.
