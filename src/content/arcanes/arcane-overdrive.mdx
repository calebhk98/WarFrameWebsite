## Overview

Arcane Overdrive grants weapon attack speed bonuses triggered by ability casts, linking warframe ability usage to melee and weapon speed increases. Each ability cast triggers the attack speed buff for 12 seconds, encouraging builds that integrate ability use and weapon combat rather than purely one or the other. Frames that cast abilities as part of their combat rotation - applying buffs, debuffs, or area effects - receive the attack speed bonus for the weapon combat that follows each cast.

The 12-second duration is long enough to cover substantial weapon combat following an ability cast, particularly for builds with moderate ability cast rates rather than rapid spam. Single-ability casters who use one powerful ability and then switch to weapon combat benefit from the extended window that allows weapon play to continue under the arcane's buff before the next cast is needed.

## Effect

Each warframe ability cast triggers the attack speed bonus for 12 seconds. At rank 0, +25% attack speed provides a noticeable improvement in melee and ranged weapon speed. At rank 5, +125% attack speed nearly doubles weapon animation speed, substantially increasing damage-per-second for weapons that scale well with attack rate. The 12-second window allows multiple ability-weapon cycles before the buff expires.

Attack speed at 125% affects melee animation speed, fire rate for some melee-related mechanics, and the speed of attack wind-up animations. The practical effect is faster hit registration and higher raw damage output per unit of time.

## Acquisition

Arcane Overdrive drops as an uncommon reward from Zariman Void Cascade endless missions. Extended Void Cascade runs that continue through multiple rotation cycles provide Overdrive in their reward pools. The arcane is accessible after completing Angels of the Zariman. Warframe.market provides an alternative for players who prefer direct platinum purchase over farming Void Cascade endless runs.

## Builds that use it

Arcane Overdrive fits hybrid melee-ability builds where the player regularly casts abilities as part of their combat rotation before continuing weapon attacks. Atlas builds using Landslide as both damage and trigger, then following with punch-through melee attacks, benefit from the attack speed bonus during the physical attack phases. Excalibur builds using Exalted Blade while casting Slash Dash to extend combos gain weapon attack speed from each ability use, accelerating exalted blade hit rates. Baruuk's Desert Wind exalted melee is triggered by ability usage and benefits from subsequent attack speed during exalted weapon engagement.

Warframes with fast-casting low-cost abilities maintain near-constant Overdrive uptime by casting abilities between attacks, keeping the 12-second window continuously refreshed.

## Notes

The attack speed bonus applies to equipped primary, secondary, and melee weapons. Some weapon types with hard-capped animation times may not fully utilize the attack speed bonus at extreme values. Ability casts from any source - including augment triggers, exalted weapon activations - count toward the Overdrive trigger. The bonus does not stack beyond a single application - casting while Overdrive is active refreshes the timer rather than increasing attack speed further. Builds that rely on channeled abilities rather than cast-and-release abilities generate fewer discrete cast events and may maintain lower average Overdrive uptime.
