## Overview

Arcane Truculence is a warframe arcane designed for overguard-dependent frames. Whenever a warframe gains 3,000 or more overguard in a single application, the arcane releases a radial viral attack that automatically applies maximum viral status stacks to all enemies within the expanding area. At rank 5, this attack reaches 30 meters, enabling crowd-wide status application. The arcane synergizes with overguard-granting frames and transforms defensive mechanics into offensive crowd-control tools.

The overguard threshold remains fixed at 3,000 across all ranks, with radial attack range scaling linearly. The radial viral application triggers independently of weapon mods or frame abilities, applying maximum status stacks automatically. Multiple triggers in rapid succession stack the status duration on affected enemies.

## Acquisition

Arcane Truculence drops from Zariman void fissure missions as a rare reward. The arcane requires overguard-focused frame builds to farm efficiently, making Zariman fissure missions with overguard-granting squad composition ideal. Public void fissure groups benefit from coordinating frames capable of frequent 3,000+ overguard applications. The arcane's effectiveness scales with overguard-generation frequency and enemy density.

## Best On

Arcane Truculence excels on frames with inherent overguard generation such as Caliban with his shield restoration or Grendel with his tank mechanics. Frames equipped with overguard-granting mods or those receiving overguard support from other frames maximize the arcane's trigger frequency. Nekros with his companion-support synergies and frames designed around defensive abilities transform the arcane into a crowd-control multiplier. Any frame stacking defensive bonuses through mods gains exponential value.

Frames without overguard sources underutilize this arcane. Non-defensive frames lacking overguard-generation paths gain minimal benefit from the trigger condition.
