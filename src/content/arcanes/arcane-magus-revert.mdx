## Overview

Magus Revert is a rare Operator arcane that enables energy efficiency gains by refunding Void Sling's energy cost on successful proc. At maximum rank, the arcane achieves a 75% chance per cast to completely refund the ability, allowing operators to spam Void Sling repeatedly during void cascade missions and endgame void fissure activities.

## Effect

Each cast of Void Sling has an independent chance to refund the full energy cost of the ability. Starting at 15% at rank 0, the proc chance scales to a maximum of 75% at rank 5. This creates a snowball effect where lucky streaks allow nearly free mobility and damage output, while unlucky stretches require normal energy management.

## Acquisition

Magus Revert drops as a rare reward from Void Cascade missions on Zariman. Progression through void fissure content unlocks access to this arcane, with drop rates improving in higher-difficulty cascades. Zariman void relic fissures provide the most reliable farming method.

## Best on

Magus Revert benefits energy-intensive operator builds:

- **Void Sling spam builds** — operators who rely on repeated repositioning maximize refund uptime.
- **Zariman endless void cascades** — the energy refund sustains high-frequency ability casting.
- **Eidolon hunts** — operators can maintain offensive positioning longer with less energy management stress.

## Notes

The 75% chance at max rank approaches "almost always succeeds," making the arcane pseudo-infinite in practice during active combat. Energy management becomes secondary to ability rotation optimization. The arcane pairs well with Void Sling augments that enhance damage or crowd control.
