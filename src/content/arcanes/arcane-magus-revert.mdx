## Overview

Magus Revert (Zariman variant) provides a chance to refund the energy cost of each Void Sling cast. Unlike the positional-return mechanic of the non-Zariman Magus Revert, this variant focuses on energy sustainability -- each Void Sling checks the proc chance, and on success, returns the energy spent on the cast back to the operator's energy pool. At rank 5, a 75% chance provides near-constant energy refunds during active Sling sequences, effectively eliminating most of the energy cost of high-frequency Void Sling usage.

Void Sling is the operator's primary movement ability but also functions as an offensive tool in some builds, dealing void damage at the destination and interacting with various Magus and Virtuos arcanes that trigger on Sling events. Energy management in operator mode limits how frequently the operator can Sling, fire the amp, and use Focus school abilities. Magus Revert reduces this constraint by returning energy on most casts, extending operator uptime without requiring energy regeneration sources.

## Effect

Each Void Sling triggers a chance check. On success, the full energy cost of that Sling is returned to the operator's pool immediately. At rank 0, a 15% refund chance fires roughly once every seven Slings -- providing occasional free casts during sustained movement. At rank 5, a 75% chance fires on approximately three out of every four Slings, making each Sling sequence dramatically less costly in energy terms.

At 75% refund probability, the expected energy cost per four Slings is approximately one Sling worth of energy (the three refunded casts and one paid cast average to 25% of the raw energy cost across the sequence).

## Acquisition

Magus Revert (Zariman) drops as a rare reward from Void Cascade missions on Zariman Ten-Zero at approximately 2.5% chance. The rare classification means lower drop frequency within the Zariman reward pool compared to common and uncommon arcanes. Regular Void Cascade farming accumulates copies over time. Warframe.market provides a direct platinum purchase route, which may be more efficient than farming for this rare arcane specifically.

## Builds that use it

Magus Revert suits operators who use Void Sling as both a primary movement tool and a combat ability, and who run energy-intensive loadouts where other operator abilities also compete for the energy pool. Operators running Void Sling-triggered arcanes on both slots -- Magus Melt, Magus Cadence, Magus Accelerant -- benefit from the refund reducing the compound energy drain of frequent Sling-triggered procs across multiple arcanes.

Zenurik school operators who generate energy regeneration pools via Wellspring supplement Revert's energy returns, maintaining operator energy across extended combat phases. Void Cascade mission operators who Sling extensively between Void Angel positions and overflow points benefit most from the sustained energy sustain Revert provides during the mission's high-mobility phases.

## Notes

The refund triggers at the moment of the Sling cast check, before the Sling completes its travel. Failed proc rolls still expend the full energy cost as normal. The 75% rate at rank 5 is not a guarantee -- individual sessions may see streaks of consecutive refunds or consecutive paid casts based on RNG. In extended play sessions, the average approaches the stated probability. Multiple Void Sling refund sources in a squad do not share procs -- each operator's Magus Revert functions independently for that operator's own Slings.
