## Overview

Residual Viremia creates viral infection auras when companion viral procs trigger. On every viral status effect from companion weapons, a temporary aura zone forms that spreads viral damage to nearby enemies. Kavats with viral weapons gain stacking infection zones. Early ranks create small auras; maximum rank expands to larger areas affecting many enemies simultaneously.

## Acquisition

Residual Viremia drops from various Zariman and Void Cascade missions.

## Best on

This arcane works best on builds that exploit its triggering condition frequently.

## Usage

Residual Viremia activates automatically when its trigger condition is met during combat.

## Notes

This arcane provides consistent value scaling throughout extended missions.
