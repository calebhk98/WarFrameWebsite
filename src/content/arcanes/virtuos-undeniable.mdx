## Overview

Virtuos Undeniable is a rare Operator arcane that rewards critical hits with escalating Void Damage bonuses. This arcane incentivizes high-crit-chance amp builds, enabling exponential damage scaling during sustained fire phases in Eidolon hunts and endgame void fissure content.

## Effect

Each critical hit from the operator's Amp grants a stacking Void Damage buff increasing all subsequent attacks by +40% at rank 0, scaling to +200% at rank 5. The buff lasts 8 seconds per stack and refreshes on subsequent crits, enabling powerful burst phases.

## Acquisition

Virtuos Undeniable drops as a rare reward from Eidolon Teralyst captures. The arcane favors high-critical-chance amp configurations and rewards precise aiming.

## Best on

Virtuos Undeniable synergizes with crit-focused amp builds:

- **High-crit amps** — builds with 50%+ crit chance maintain near-permanent buff uptime.
- **Eidolon burst phases** — crit chains trigger exponential damage scaling.
- **Efficient tridolon farming** — enables sub-50-minute runs with proper optimization.

## Notes

Buff stacking encourages rapid-fire, high-accuracy gameplay. Consecutive crits refresh the buff window rather than stacking linearly. Pairs exceptionally well with Virtuos Fury for combined damage multipliers.
