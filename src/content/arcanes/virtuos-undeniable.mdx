## Overview

Virtuos Undeniable amplifies void damage output by granting stacking damage bonuses when the operator's amp scores critical hits. Each critical hit from the amp checks and triggers a void damage buff that compounds with subsequent critical hits, creating escalating burst windows during sustained amp fire phases. This makes the arcane particularly valuable for high-crit-chance amp configurations that maintain consistent critical hit rates, as each crit both deals amplified damage and reinforces the next attack's damage bonus.

Critical hits from amps are influenced by the amp's base critical chance and the operator's Focus school bonuses. Building for high crit chance - through amp part selection and Focus upgrades - maximizes the frequency at which Undeniable triggers, creating sustained high-damage windows rather than sporadic bursts.

## Effect

Each critical hit from the operator's amp triggers a void damage buff for 8 seconds. At rank 0, +40% void damage provides a significant damage increase per trigger. At rank 5, +200% void damage triples the base void damage output during the active buff window. The 8-second duration persists through continued amp fire, and critical hits during the buff window refresh the timer rather than stacking additional multipliers beyond the listed value.

The buff interacts with the amp's base void damage, multiplying the output of each subsequent hit during the active window.

## Acquisition

Virtuos Undeniable drops as a rare reward from Eidolon Teralyst captures during Void Fissure Eidolon hunt sessions on Plains of Eidolon. Capturing - not killing - the Teralyst provides the drop; completing the chain through Gantulyst and Hydrolyst is not required for this specific arcane. The rare designation reflects its competitive power level for amp-focused operators. Warframe.market provides a direct purchase alternative.

## Builds that use it

Virtuos Undeniable performs best on amp builds with critical chance at 50% or higher, where critical hits occur frequently enough to maintain near-constant buff uptime. The Raplak prism with high critical multiplier configurations, combined with Virtuos Undeniable, creates high burst damage windows against Eidolon synovias. Madurai school operators that use Void Sling to close distance before opening with sustained amp fire benefit from Undeniable's escalating damage during the sustained fire phase.

Tridolon hunt optimizations that focus on speed runs use Undeniable to maximize damage during each Eidolon shield break phase, reducing the time window needed to down each synovia and accelerating overall hunt completion.

## Notes

The void damage buff applies only to void-type damage from the amp, not to operator ability damage or companion attacks. The buff does not transfer to the warframe when returning via transference - it only applies during operator mode amp fire. Critical hits that trigger Undeniable during an active buff window reset the 8-second timer without adding additional damage percentage beyond rank maximum. High-fire-rate amps that score multiple critical hits per second maintain the buff window more consistently than single-shot amps with longer intervals between crits.
