## Overview

Magus Firewall generates Void Particles while the operator holds Void Mode (the operator's invisible, energy-draining stealth state). Each particle lasts for a duration that scales with rank -- from 10 seconds at rank 0 to 60 seconds at rank 5 -- and grants 12.5% damage reduction while active. Up to 6 particles can accumulate simultaneously, providing a maximum of 75% damage reduction when all are active. Each hit that strikes the operator consumes one particle, reducing the stack and the associated damage reduction.

This creates an absorptive defense layer: the operator soaks hits with particle charges rather than taking raw damage. The longer particle duration at higher ranks allows the operator to hold accumulated charges across longer engagements without the stack decaying from time alone -- only incoming damage depletes the charges.

## Effect

While Void Mode is active, the operator generates one Void Particle per second until reaching the 6-particle cap. Each particle independently provides 12.5% damage reduction, so the full 6-stack grants 75% damage reduction to operator health. When the operator takes a hit, one particle is consumed, dropping the stack by one charge and the reduction by 12.5%.

At rank 0, particles last 10 seconds -- limiting how long the operator can maintain charges outside of active Void Mode generation. At rank 5, the 60-second duration means accumulated charges persist long after exiting Void Mode, allowing the operator to build up a full 6-particle stack in Void Mode and then fight at full charge for up to a minute before the charges expire from duration rather than hits.

## Builds that use it

Magus Firewall suits operators who spend regular time in Void Mode as part of their combat rotation -- Unairu school operators, operators managing Eidolon limb phases where incoming fire is frequent, or operators in Zariman Void Cascade who enter Void Mode between amp fire windows. The particle generation during Void Mode makes the invisibility phase productive for defense buildup rather than just evasion.

Operators in high-incoming-damage content like Eidolon arenas -- where errant Teralyst beams or Vomvalyst swarms strike the operator -- benefit from the 75% reduction at full stack, allowing the operator to sustain more hits before being forced to retreat. Building to full 6 particles before a high-damage engagement phase provides a buffer that reduces the operator's need to manage health through other arcanes.

## Notes

Particles are consumed one per hit rather than per damage packet -- a single attack consumes one particle regardless of how much damage it would deal. This means many fast low-damage hits deplete the stack quickly, while slow hard hits are more efficient to absorb per-charge. Void Mode costs operator energy to sustain -- the charge buildup phase requires ongoing energy investment. The particle count and duration are separate mechanics: duration tracks how long each individual particle lives, while count tracks how many are currently active. An operator can have particles at varying remaining durations simultaneously.
