## Overview

Secondary Enervate reduces enemy offensive effectiveness when secondary weapon status procs trigger, providing a debuff mechanic that extends beyond raw damage into team utility. Enemies affected by the power reduction deal less damage and apply weaker effects, benefiting all squadmates in range of the debuffed target. Status-heavy secondary builds that apply procs at high frequency maintain near-constant debuff uptime on priority targets, particularly bosses and elite enemies with powerful attacks. The arcane converts any status type on the secondary - corrosive, viral, heat, electric, slash - into an opportunity for the power reduction debuff.

This utility-focused arcane contrasts with damage-boosting secondaries by reducing incoming threat rather than increasing outgoing damage. Players building for squad support or survivability-focused content find Secondary Enervate complementary to healing and mitigation tools already in their toolkit.

## Effect

Each status proc applied by the secondary weapon triggers the power reduction check. On success, the affected enemy has its effective output reduced for the debuff duration. At rank 0, the 20% reduction noticeably lessens damage spikes from elite enemies; at rank 5, 100% reduction effectively neutralizes enemy offensive power for the buff duration. The debuff does not stack from multiple procs of the same type but refreshes duration on new successful triggers. Multiple different status types applied simultaneously each check the trigger independently, increasing overall debuff uptime.

## Acquisition

Secondary Enervate drops from Zariman Ten-Zero missions and Void Cascade content. The arcane appears in rotation rewards across Void Cascade A through C, making it broadly available through standard Zariman farming. Players consistently completing daily Zariman bounties encounter Secondary Enervate through natural rotation accumulation. Warframe.market provides a secondary acquisition route for players prioritizing speed over farming.

## Builds that use it

High-status secondary builds pairing Secondary Enervate with multi-element modding generate frequent procs to maintain debuff uptime. Status-focused pistols with 80% or higher status chance after modding apply procs quickly enough to sustain the power reduction throughout extended encounters. Support-oriented frames (Trinity, Wisp, Harrow) that already contribute team utility through abilities pair well because Secondary Enervate extends defensive contribution into secondary weapon usage. Steel Path content where elite enemies deal significantly amplified damage benefits most from the power reduction mechanic.

## Notes

The power reduction applies to the specific enemy hit by the status proc, not to all enemies in an area. Multi-targeting secondaries that apply status to several enemies simultaneously trigger the debuff on each target independently. The debuff does not affect enemy armor or resistances, only their effective offensive output. Combined with armor-stripping mods (Corrosive stacking) the arcane provides both offensive and defensive contributions from a single secondary weapon slot.
