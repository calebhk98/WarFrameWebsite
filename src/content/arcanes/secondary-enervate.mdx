## Overview

Secondary Enervate weakens enemies when secondary weapon status procs trigger. On every status effect applied by secondary weapons, the arcane reduces enemy power effectiveness. This arcane enables debuff-focused secondary builds. Early ranks reduce power by 15%; maximum rank reduces by 75%.

| Rank | Effect |

| 0    | Base effect |
| 5    | Maximum effect |

## Acquisition

Secondary Enervate drops from various Zariman and Void Cascade missions.

## Best on

This arcane works best on builds that exploit its triggering condition frequently.

## Usage

Secondary Enervate activates automatically when its trigger condition is met during combat.

## Notes

This arcane provides consistent value scaling throughout extended missions.
