## Overview

Melee Fortification grants a flat armor bonus for 10 seconds each time the equipped melee weapon scores a kill. The bonus activates on each kill and refreshes on subsequent kills within the window, allowing players who maintain consistent melee kill pace to sustain the armor buff throughout combat. Melee weapons with high attack speed or crowd-clearing attacks provide kill opportunities frequently enough to maintain near-permanent uptime in dense enemy encounters.

Armor reduces physical damage taken by converting it according to the armor-to-damage-reduction formula. Flat armor additions are additive with base armor and are then multiplied by percentage armor mods. Fortification's flat bonus integrates into this calculation and benefits from armor multiplier mods on the frame, making the arcane more effective on builds that already invest in armor.

## Effect

At rank 0, +35 armor on melee kill provides a minor defensive boost during active melee combat. At rank 5, +210 armor on melee kill provides a substantial flat increase -- for a frame with 300 base armor, this represents a 70% armor increase during active melee kills, meaningfully raising effective health against physical damage. The 10-second window requires at least one kill per 10 seconds to maintain the buff at full value.

The buff refreshes its 10-second timer on each new kill, meaning a frame that kills repeatedly never sees the bonus expire during active combat.

## Builds that use it

Melee Fortification suits melee-focused warframes who engage in sustained close-range combat where kill frequency is high -- Excalibur builds with Exalted Blade, Ash builds using Blade Storm for rapid kills, or Baruuk builds in Desert Wind phases. Frames running Steel Path content where enemies have elevated health need consistent kill pace to maintain the buff, making the arcane more valuable on builds with strong melee scaling rather than frames that use melee as a secondary damage source.

The arcane pairs well with armor-stacking builds: frames running Adaptation, Steel Fiber, and Fortification combine the flat bonus with percentage multipliers, and each melee kill refreshes all three layers simultaneously during active combat.

## Notes

The armor bonus is flat and additive with base armor before percentage armor multipliers apply. The buff applies to the warframe's armor value, reducing physical damage across all damage types that interact with armor. The 10-second timer means frames that momentarily stop killing -- during reload, ability casts, or enemy-sparse transitions -- may lose the buff before the next kill opportunity. In high-density content, this is rarely an issue; in low-density or spread-out encounters, sustaining the buff requires deliberate melee kill pacing. The arcane activates only on kills by the equipped melee weapon, not on kills by abilities, primary, or secondary weapons.
