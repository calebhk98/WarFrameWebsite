## Overview

Melee Duplicate triggers bonus heavy attacks on successful heavy strike activation. This melee arcane enables heavy-attack-focused builds to achieve multiplicative damage output through automatic attack chains during void fissure combat.

## Effects per rank

| Rank | Trigger Chance | Duplicate Effectiveness |
|------|----------------|------------------------|
| 0    | 10%            | 50%                    |
| 1    | 15%            | 70%                    |
| 2    | 20%            | 90%                    |
| 3    | 25%            | 110%                   |
| 4    | 27%            | 130%                   |
| 5    | 30%            | 150%                   |

Bonus heavy attacks consume stamina independently, enabling chain attacks during extended void fissure void waves with proper stamina management.

## Acquisition

Melee Duplicate drops from Zariman Void Fissure (3%) and Void Cascade rotation C (1.5%). Regular zariman void farming yields this rare arcane through progression.

## Best on

Melee Duplicate excels on heavy-attack-focused melee weapons like polearms paired with heavy-attack-scaling warframes. The bonus attacks enable explosive damage scaling during void fissure content.
