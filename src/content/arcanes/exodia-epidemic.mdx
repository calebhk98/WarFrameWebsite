## Overview

Exodia Epidemic is a Zaw arcane that transforms mobility mechanics into crowd-control tools. After performing a bullet jump or double jump, landing a ground slam automatically emits a forward-facing shockwave that suspends all affected enemies in the air. At rank 3, the shockwave reaches 30 meters and suspends enemies for 3 seconds, enabling aggressive repositioning and crowd vulnerability windows. The arcane rewards active jumping mechanics and synergizes with mobility-focused warframes and Zaw builds.

The shockwave triggers automatically on ground slam following any bullet jump or double jump, creating opportunities for rapid crowd-control cycles. The suspension duration prevents enemy retaliation and enables coordinated team follow-up damage. Multiple shockwaves refresh suspension stacks on affected enemy groups.

## Acquisition

Exodia Epidemic drops from Zariman void fissure missions as a moderately rare Zaw arcane. The arcane requires mobility-focused builds with frequent jump-slam rotations to farm efficiently. Public void fissure groups benefit from coordinating jump patterns and ground slam timing to maximize shockwave frequency. The arcane's effectiveness scales with movement-speed bonuses and air-mobility enhancements. Mobility-stacking warframes that chain jumps frequently generate consistent shockwave triggers throughout engagements.

## Best On

Exodia Epidemic excels on Zaw builds paired with high-mobility warframes such as Nova with her wormhole mobility or Nezha with his firewalker movement. Frames with jump-enhancement abilities or those emphasizing parkour mechanics maximize the arcane's trigger rate. Excalibur Umbra with his exalted blade and mobility synergies gains exceptional value. Any frame equipped with mobility or momentum-based gameplay transforms the arcane into sustained crowd control.

Heavy, grounded builds without jump emphasis underutilize this arcane. Frames lacking mobility or air-combat frequency gain minimal trigger value.
