## Overview

Arcane Battery increases maximum energy capacity on energy orb pickups. This warframe arcane enables energy-hungry frames to achieve sustainable ability spam during void fissure content.

Energy pool scaling stacks with existing mods and enables high-cost ability frameworks to achieve reliable power spam during extended void fissure engagements.

## Acquisition

Arcane Battery drops from Zariman Void Fissure (4%) and Void Cascade rotation A (2%). Regular zariman void farming yields this arcane through natural progression.

## Best on

Arcane Battery excels on ability-spam-focused frames that consume high energy budgets. The energy pool boost enables efficient ability-rotation management during prolonged void fissure waves.
