## Overview

Arcane Battery converts the warframe's armor value into additional maximum energy, linking defensive and energy stats into a single scaling relationship. Each armor point contributes a fractional maximum energy bonus, up to a hard cap of 1000 bonus energy. Frames with high armor values benefit most from this arcane, as they gain the largest energy pool expansion from their existing armor investment.

At rank 5, the conversion rate is 0.3 energy per armor point. A frame with 500 armor gains 150 bonus maximum energy; a frame with 1000 armor gains 300 bonus energy; a frame with 3333 or more armor reaches the 1000 energy cap. The bonus energy is permanent for as long as the arcane is equipped -- there is no trigger condition or duration.

## Effect

The energy bonus from Arcane Battery applies passively based on the warframe's current armor stat. As armor increases through Adaptation stacks, arcane buffs, or other temporary armor sources, the energy bonus increases proportionally up to the 1000 cap. If armor decreases from corrosive status strips, the energy bonus decreases accordingly.

At rank 0, 0.05 energy per armor point yields 5 bonus energy on 100 armor -- minimal benefit at low armor values. At rank 5, 0.3 energy per armor point yields 30 energy on 100 armor, 150 energy on 500 armor, and caps at 1000 energy bonus on 3333+ armor. The cap prevents the arcane from becoming disproportionate on extreme tank builds, but most high-armor frames will see 200-600 energy expansion from their natural armor investment.

## Acquisition

Arcane Battery drops from Zariman Void Fissure rewards at 4% and from Void Cascade rotation A at 2%. Both sources are available after completing Angels of the Zariman. Void Cascade rotation A occurs at the first rotation interval. Warframe.market provides direct purchase for players who prefer platinum acquisition.

## Builds that use it

Arcane Battery fits armor-tank builds that want to expand their energy pools without sacrificing mod slots to Primed Flow or Fleeting Expertise. Valkyr at high armor values with Warcry active can reach 3000+ armor, pushing Battery toward its 1000 energy cap. Inaros builds running Adaptation stack high armor during combat and benefit from the passive energy expansion as armor grows. Rhino Iron Skin builds that invest in Ironclad Charge augment to accumulate iron skin armor see the energy bonus scale with their iron skin armor stacks up to the cap.

Arcane Battery complements Arcane Energize effectively -- Battery expands how much energy can be stored, while Energize restores energy frequently. Together they enable sustained high-cost ability usage on tank frames that naturally accumulate armor.

## Notes

The energy bonus from Arcane Battery is not free energy -- it increases the maximum capacity, not the current energy value. Frames must still refill the expanded pool through energy orbs, Zenurik focus, or ability-based energy recovery. The 1000 bonus energy cap is separate from the warframe's base maximum energy; total maximum energy can exceed 1000. The bonus is dynamic and responds to armor changes in real time. Corrosive status procs that reduce armor temporarily decrease the energy cap, which may cause current energy to exceed the new cap -- any excess energy above the new cap is not lost but cannot be regenerated past the current cap until armor recovers.
