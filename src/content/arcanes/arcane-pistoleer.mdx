## Overview

Arcane Pistoleer is a warframe arcane that rewards precise secondary weapon use. When eliminating enemies via pistol headshots, the arcane grants a 60% chance to activate ammo efficiency bonuses lasting 12 seconds. At max rank, the efficiency boost reaches 102%, effectively doubling ammo magazine efficiency. The arcane encourages precise aiming with secondary weapons and provides sustained resource efficiency during longer combat encounters.

The 60% trigger chance remains consistent across all ranks, with the ammo efficiency scaling linearly. The 12-second buff window allows for overlapping efficiency bonuses if headshot kills occur frequently. Magazine capacity increases proportionally, reducing reload frequency during extended engagements.

## Acquisition

Arcane Pistoleer drops from Zariman void fissure missions as a moderately rare reward. The arcane requires developing accuracy with secondary weapons to farm efficiently. Frames with headshot-boosting abilities or warframe buffs that enhance secondary weapon effectiveness accelerate farming progression. Public void fissure groups with accuracy-focused players complete farms more rapidly. The arcane pairs exceptionally well with secondary-weapon-focused warframes and precision-damage mods that multiply headshot effectiveness.

## Best On

Pistoleer excels on frames that specialize in secondary weapons or have synergies with precision-based gameplay. Harrow with his headshot-bonus abilities and Chroma with his vex armor scaling benefits significantly from this arcane. Hildryn utilizing secondary weapons as primary damage sources and Octavia with her synergistic buffs gain substantial value from the ammo efficiency scaling.

Frames without secondary weapon emphasis or those prioritizing melee or ability damage gain minimal benefit from this arcane.
