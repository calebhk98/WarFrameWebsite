## Overview

Exodia Contagion is a legendary zaw arcane that transforms melee combat into area-of-effect devastation through aerial mobility. By launching explosive projectiles during aim gliding, Contagion rewards aggressive positioning and movement-oriented playstyles that emphasize bullet jumping and parkour. Unlike traditional melee arcanes that scale with static stat bonuses, Contagion creates independent projectiles that inherit the zaw's critical chance, critical damage, and status chance, making it synergistic with crit-focused zaw builds. The arcane fundamentally changes how players approach engagement ranges, enabling mid-range area damage from a purely melee weapon.

Contagion's primary strength lies in its mobility integration: every bullet jump or double jump followed by aim gliding becomes an offensive opportunity. In open-world farming and corridor-based mission types, the constant loop of jump-glide-fire-land creates continuous damage output that exceeds traditional melee damage-per-second calculations. The projectile nature allows Contagion zaws to trigger on-hit effects that normally require melee contact, such as Galvanized Mods and lifesteal procs, effectively expanding the melee mod ecosystem into mid-range territory.

The arcane does carry restrictions: it activates only once per airtime; the player must touch the ground to reset the trigger. This cooldown prevents infinite chaining in zero-gravity or Limbo Rift scenarios and encourages active ground engagement between aerial strikes. For stationary fights in narrow corridors, Contagion's value diminishes compared to pure damage arcanes.

## Effect

The projectile launches from the zaw and explodes on impact with enemies or terrain. The explosion applies the zaw's full damage tuple (physical splits, elemental composition, critical multiplier, and status chance) to all targets within the blast radius. This means a radiation-damage zaw with 25% status and 2.5x critical multiplier creates explosions that proc Radiation status on 25% of enemies and land critical hits according to the zaw's stats. Patch Update 29.5 cemented this interaction, transforming Contagion into a builds-enabling arcane rather than a novelty mechanic.

At maximum rank, the projectile damage scales with the zaw's melee combo multiplier, allowing combo builds to dramatically increase per-strike output. Enemies within 30 meters of the impact point take full damage; falloff begins beyond that range. The effect triggers every bullet jump or double jump if the player initiates aim gliding afterward; no cooldown applies between jumps on the same airtime, but touching the ground resets the counter.

## Stats per rank

Exodia Contagion does not have traditional per-rank stat scaling; instead, ranks unlock visual effects and patch-log refinements. Rank 0 provides the base mechanic; Rank 3 unlocks the +1 Arcane Revive bonus shared across higher-tier arcanes. The projectile damage itself is not a ranked statistic but depends entirely on the host zaw's configuration and the player's combo meter.

## Acquisition

Exodia Contagion drops exclusively from Zariman Void Skirmish missions as a guaranteed vendor purchase from the Operational Supply Kin faction. Unlike eidolon-sourced arcanes that require difficult boss captures, Contagion can be purchased for standing once the player reaches Allied status with Operational Supply Kin. Skirmish farming remains the only repeatable source for multiple copies. A single run takes 8--12 minutes depending on squad efficiency, making Contagion farmable for most endgame players within 2--4 hours of focused effort.

Once obtained, Contagion ranks like all zaw arcanes and requires 24 hours per rank to master. The arcane's trading value depends on zaw-building demand within the community; it remains moderately priced on the trading post compared to warframe arcanes.

## Best on

Exodia Contagion excels on mobility-focused zaw builds prioritizing rapid area clearing and parkour-integrated combat. Viral/Gas build Zaws leverage the projectile inheritance to trigger status procs at range; Radiation and Corrosive zaws enable utility applications like armor strip through Contagion procs. Melee weapons with high base damage and attack speed (Polearms, Swords) maximize the frequency of explosive triggers. Exodia Contagion performs poorly on heavy, slow melee weapons like Hammers where airtime becomes a disadvantage rather than an asset.

Frames that enable extended airtime or reduce the cost of bullet jumping (Nova, Wukong, Lavos with Transference perks) synergize exceptionally well with Contagion, creating combat loops where the arcane becomes the primary damage dealer. Conversely, frames with limited mobility tools or abilities that restrict movement (Limbo, Frost) reduce Contagion's uptime and should lean on alternative arcanes.

## Builds that use it

Most zaw arcane builds pair Contagion with an offensive secondary like Exodia Might or Exodia Brave to create hybrid damage and utility builds. Steel Path endless survival builds use Contagion zaws to maintain add-clear pressure while melee frames handle priority targets with heavy attacks. Support frames equip Contagion zaws as mobility-scaled damage dealers that free up warframe ability slots for utility rather than DPS. Hybrid utility builds exploit Contagion projectiles triggering on-hit mod effects, enabling Galvanized combo scaling on a pure projectile source.

## Notes

Contagion projectiles ignore line-of-sight requirements for explosive area damage, allowing attacks through Limbo's Rift or around cover. The interaction with Volt's electric shield was fixed in Update 27.4 to prevent perpetual bounces. Players equipping Contagion should prioritize zaw builds with high status chance and critical hit scaling to maximize the utility of inherited projectile stats. The arcane's restriction to one proc per airtime encourages varied engagement patterns rather than stale mechanical abuse.
