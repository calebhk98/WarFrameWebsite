## Overview

Arcane Crepuscular boosts Ability Strength and Final Critical Multiplier while the warframe is invisible. Both bonuses are passive and persist for the entire duration of the invisibility state, making this arcane well-suited to stealth frames that maintain invisibility for extended combat windows. Invisible frames already gain the benefit of enemies not targeting them; Crepuscular converts that tactical advantage into direct damage amplification as well.

At rank 5, the arcane provides +30% Ability Strength and +3x Final Critical Multiplier while invisible. Ability Strength scales damage-dealing abilities, healing abilities, and buff magnitudes simultaneously. Final Critical Multiplier adds directly to the critical hit damage multiplier after other critical modifiers are applied, meaning each critical hit deals substantially more damage during the invisible window.

## Effect

Arcane Crepuscular activates automatically whenever the warframe enters an invisible state from any source -- Ash's Smokescreen, Ivara's Cloak Arrow, Loki's Invisibility, Octavia's Resonator silence zone, or operator Void Mode return invisibility. The bonuses apply for the full duration of the invisible state and drop immediately when invisibility ends. There is no trigger chance and no cooldown; the arcane is purely state-conditional.

At rank 0, +5% Ability Strength and +1x Final Critical Multiplier apply during invisibility. At rank 5, these grow to +30% Ability Strength and +3x Final Critical Multiplier. Frames that maintain long invisibility durations through Duration mods or augments sustain the bonuses reliably throughout missions. The Critical Multiplier bonus is particularly impactful for melee-focused builds that use invisible frames to set up stealth multiplier attacks.

## Acquisition

Arcane Crepuscular drops from Zariman Void Fissure rewards at a 4% chance and from Void Cascade rotation A at 2%. Both sources are accessible after completing Angels of the Zariman. Void Cascade rotation A occurs at the first rotation interval, making early drops obtainable without committing to extended runs. Warframe.market provides a direct purchase option for players preferring platinum acquisition.

## Builds that use it

Arcane Crepuscular is a natural fit for invisibility-centric warframes. Ivara builds that maintain permanent invisibility through Prowl use the +30% Ability Strength on abilities cast during Prowl and gain enhanced critical multiplier on the Artemis Bow alt-fire or melee attacks. Ash Bladestorm builds benefit from the Ability Strength bonus amplifying Bladestorm damage while invisible during smoke. Loki builds using Hushed Invisibility melee approach receive both the critical multiplier for heavy attacks and the Ability Strength for Radial Disarm's crowd control radius.

Frames using Arcane Trickery -- which grants invisibility on finisher attacks -- can trigger Crepuscular through stealth finisher chains, applying the bonus between attacks. Any frame with Arcane Trickery, Crepuscular, and a melee weapon builds a short-window strength-and-crit cycle around the finisher mechanic.

## Notes

The arcane checks the invisible state continuously; equipping it on a frame that never uses invisibility provides no benefit. Invisibility from Warframe abilities, Operator Void Mode transitioning back, and certain augment effects all count. The Ability Strength bonus does not apply to passive abilities or abilities that do not scale with Power Strength. Final Critical Multiplier is a separate multiplier applied after the base critical damage calculation, making it multiplicatively stronger than comparable base crit multiplier bonuses.
