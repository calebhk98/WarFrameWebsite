## Overview

Arcane Crepuscular grants emergency damage reduction when health falls below 50%, providing clutch survival windows during dangerous encounters where health management becomes critical. The threshold-based trigger ensures the arcane activates precisely when defensive support is most needed -- not at full health during safe moments, but specifically when the frame is under enough pressure to reach the halfway health point. This design makes Crepuscular complementary to active defensive play rather than passive survivability stacking.

The 8-second duration provides meaningful recovery time, allowing players to pop health restores, trigger healing abilities, or retreat to safety after the mitigation activates. At rank 5, 90% damage reduction is substantial enough to withstand most incoming attacks while recovering.

## Effect

When health drops below 50% of maximum health, Arcane Crepuscular checks the trigger chance. On success, damage reduction activates for 8 seconds. At rank 0, 30% damage reduction provides minor mitigation against moderate burst damage; at rank 5, 90% damage reduction essentially halves all incoming damage by multiplicative stacking, enabling sustained survival against attacks that would otherwise be lethal within a few hits. The reduction applies to all damage types including elemental and physical.

The trigger fires on crossing the 50% threshold from above, not repeatedly while already below 50%. Players at 30% health do not continuously trigger Crepuscular; they must recover above 50% and drop back below again for a new trigger opportunity.

## Acquisition

Arcane Crepuscular drops from Zariman Void Fissure at 4% and Void Cascade rotation A at 2%. Both sources are accessible through standard Zariman Ten-Zero content after completing Angels of the Zariman. Rotation A drops occur consistently in Void Cascade runs. Warframe.market offers direct purchase for players who prefer platinum-based acquisition.

## Builds that use it

Arcane Crepuscular excels on balanced tank builds that sustain damage but have healing tools to recover above the 50% threshold between trigger events. Frames with moderate health pools that recover health through abilities (Inaros Devour, Nidus Mutation, Trinity Well of Life healing self) cycle through the 50% threshold regularly, triggering Crepuscular consistently. Solo Steel Path survival builds where burst damage spikes from elite enemies are dangerous benefit from the emergency mitigation during spike events. Pairing with Life Strike melee (health on heavy attack) provides the healing needed to reset the trigger condition efficiently.

## Notes

The threshold trigger fires once on crossing below 50% health; remaining below 50% does not continuously activate the arcane. Recovering above 50% health resets the trigger condition, allowing subsequent drops below 50% to trigger Crepuscular again. The 8-second mitigation window starts immediately on trigger and does not extend beyond 8 seconds regardless of continued damage. The proc chance at rank 5 is 30%; the remaining 70% of threshold crossings do not trigger mitigation. Shield depletion does not count toward the 50% health threshold; only actual health reduction triggers the check.
