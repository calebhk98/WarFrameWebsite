## Overview

Shotgun Vendetta boosts reload speed on shotgun kills. On every shotgun kill, the arcane grants a reload speed buff stacking up to three times. This arcane rewards consecutive shotgun kills. Early ranks grant 25% reload speed; maximum rank grants 125% reload speed per stack for rapid consecutive kills.

## Acquisition

Shotgun Vendetta drops from various Zariman and Void Cascade missions.

## Best on

This arcane works best on builds that exploit its triggering condition frequently.

## Usage

Shotgun Vendetta activates automatically when its trigger condition is met during combat.

This arcane provides consistent value scaling throughout extended missions.
