## Overview

Pax Bolt grants simultaneous Ability Efficiency and Ability Strength bonuses on the next warframe ability cast within 4 seconds of a headshot kill with the equipped Kitgun. Landing a headshot kill primes both modifiers, and the first ability cast within the 4-second window benefits from both the reduced cost and the increased output simultaneously. This makes Pax Bolt a burst-damage amplifier for ability casters who can aim precisely with their Kitgun.

Kitguns are modular secondary weapons crafted through the Ventkid market on the Orb Vallis, combined from chambers, loaders, and grips chosen by the player. Each Kitgun configuration can equip Pax arcanes -- four arcanes exclusive to this weapon type. Pax Bolt is one of four Pax options, combining a dual-modifier buff on headshot kill with the 4-second activation window.

## Effect

At rank 0, +7.5% Ability Efficiency and +7.5% Ability Strength on the next ability cast within 4 seconds provides a modest bonus. At rank 3, +30% Ability Efficiency and +30% Ability Strength on the next cast within 4 seconds provides a significant dual buff -- the same ability both costs less energy and deals more damage or provides stronger utility for that single cast.

The buff is consumed by the first ability cast after the headshot kill, within the 4-second window. If no ability is cast within 4 seconds, the buff expires unused.

## Acquisition

Pax Bolt is crafted or obtained through Ventkid-related content on the Orb Vallis, Venus, where Kitgun components and Pax arcanes are available through standing rewards and crafting blueprints. The WFCD dataset does not document specific drop tables for this arcane. Community drop tables and the Ventkid market offer current sourcing information. Warframe.market provides direct platinum purchase from other players.

## Builds that use it

Pax Bolt suits Kitgun-carrying warframes that blend precision gunplay with high-impact ability casting. A frame that can reliably land headshot kills with their Kitgun -- Ash with Bladestorm enabling repositioning to headshot angles, Mesa whose Peacemaker eliminates enemy crowds leaving specific targets for headshots, or Gauss whose speed enables headshot positioning -- benefits from chaining headshot kills into boosted ability casts.

The dual Efficiency and Strength bonus makes the next ability simultaneously cheaper and more effective, which benefits high-cost high-damage abilities: Nova's Molecular Prime (100 energy, damage scaling), Saryn's Miasma (100 energy, scaling damage), or Wisp's Sol Gate (50 energy per second). Landing a headshot kill then immediately casting one of these abilities costs less energy and delivers more output than the base cast.

## Notes

The buff window is 4 seconds -- tight enough to require deliberate ability casting rather than opportunistic casting. Players who headshot-kill an enemy and then fight through a crowded engagement may find the window expires before they have an opportunity to cast. In practice, the arcane rewards players who identify a headshot kill opportunity, consciously activate the buff, and immediately cast their highest-impact ability. Multiple headshot kills within the 4-second window do not stack multiple primes -- only one primer is active at a time, and the most recent kill's trigger is the active buff.
