## Overview

Arcane Cleanse Grineer is a Warframe arcane that provides faction-specific status cleansing against Grineer enemies. It is installed in one of the Warframe's arcane slots and activates automatically when the Warframe receives a negative status effect from a Grineer-origin source. At maximum rank, the arcane guarantees that every Grineer-applied status effect is immediately cleansed, effectively granting full status immunity against that faction.

The Cleanse arcane family includes parallel versions for each of the four main factions -- Grineer, Corpus, Infested, and Corrupted -- and they all function on the same mechanic. Arcane Cleanse Grineer is the most commonly sought version because Grineer enemies apply some of the most disruptive status effects in the game, including Corrosive armor-stripping procs, Slash bleeds, and Radiation confusion. On Steel Path content where Grineer enemies hit harder and apply statuses more frequently, the cleansing protection becomes a tangible survival improvement.

## Effect

When the Warframe receives a negative status effect from a Grineer enemy, Arcane Cleanse Grineer triggers with a percentage chance to immediately remove the effect. At rank 0 the trigger chance is 20%. At rank 5 the chance reaches 100%, guaranteeing removal of any Grineer-applied status on the first tick. The cleanse applies to any Grineer-origin status regardless of damage type, including Corrosive, Slash, Heat, Radiation, Toxin, and Impact statuses applied by Grineer weapons.

The arcane does not apply to status effects from Corpus, Infested, or Corrupted sources. Content involving mixed enemy factions may expose the Warframe to uncleansed status effects from the non-Grineer faction present.

## Acquisition

Arcane Cleanse Grineer drops as a rare reward from Void Cascade missions on the Zariman tileset when the Grineer faction is the enemy type present during the cascade event. Zariman Void Cascade missions rotate between factions; runs with Grineer enemies present provide access to Grineer-variant Cleanse drops. Players targeting this specific arcane should start Void Cascade missions and confirm the faction before committing to a long run. The arcane can also be traded between players.

## Builds that use it

Arcane Cleanse Grineer benefits any Warframe that operates in Grineer-heavy content and lacks built-in status immunity.

Steel Path Grineer missions on nodes like Sedna, Kuva Fortress, and the Grineer-aligned Zariman waves apply Corrosive procs that strip armor over time and Slash procs that bypass Warframe health gating. At rank 5 with guaranteed cleansing, the Warframe remains immune to armor strip and bleed from Grineer sources, which extends effective health and ability performance throughout long endurance sessions.

Warframes without innate status immunity -- most frames outside those with specific ability-based immunities like Harrow, Rhino, and Revenant -- gain the most from Arcane Cleanse Grineer. Frames that would otherwise be vulnerable to Corrosive armor degradation retain their full armor value through cleanse uptime.

## Notes

At rank 5 with 100% trigger chance, the arcane removes every Grineer status immediately as it is applied. The cleanse is reactive rather than preventive -- the status registers briefly before being removed. This means instant-damage-on-apply statuses like Toxin may still deal their initial burst damage before the cleanse resolves. Combining this arcane with Arcane Aegis or a health-regeneration source covers the brief window between status application and removal in very high-damage scenarios.
