## Overview

Arcane Camisado restores health on finisher attacks. This warframe arcane enables melee-focused frames to maintain health sustain through execution mechanics during void fissure combat.

| 1    | 22%            | 75             |
| 2    | 30%            | 100            |
| 3    | 37%            | 125            |

Health restoration triggers consistently during high-finisher-rate combat, enabling aggressive melee playstyles to sustain through void fissure encounters.

## Acquisition

Arcane Camisado drops from Zariman Void Fissure (4%) and Void Cascade rotation C (2%). Regular zariman void farming yields this arcane naturally.

## Best on

Arcane Camisado excels on melee-focused frames with high-execution-rate weapons. The health sustain enables solo void fissure runs without external healing support.
