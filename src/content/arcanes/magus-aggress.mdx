## Overview

Magus Aggress is an Operator Arcane that provides temporary Power Strength bonuses upon Transference entry. The arcane is particularly valued by Operators who rely on ability augmentations and strategic stat boosts during squad support phases.

## Effects per rank

| Rank | Proc Chance | Power Strength | Duration |
|------|-------------|----------------|----------|
| 0    | 10%         | +20%           | 10s      |
| 1    | 15%         | +30%           | 10s      |
| 2    | 20%         | +40%           | 10s      |
| 3    | 25%         | +50%           | 10s      |
| 4    | 27%         | +55%           | 10s      |
| 5    | 30%         | +60%           | 10s      |

The buff applies when transitioning from Warframe to Operator mode via Transference. This works well in squad scenarios where operators frequently switch between void damage and support roles during fissure missions.

## Acquisition

Magus Aggress is obtained from Eidolon Hydrolyst captures in the Plains of Eidolon and from Void Fissure mission rewards in Zariman. To consistently farm this arcane, players should have a competent amp (minimum 2-1-2 configuration) and reliable squad composition for Eidolon hunts.

The drop chance from Hydrolyst is approximately 6.0%, making this one of the moderately rare operator arcanes. Zariman rewards provide an alternative farming method for players who prefer void fissure content over plains hunting.

## Best on

Magus Aggress is most effective on Operators equipped with high-damage void beam configurations, where the bonus power strength amplifies both damage output and ability proc rates. It complements operator builds focused on void blast and void spikes synergy during extended eidolon sequences or void fissure excavations.

## Sources

Eidolon captures provide the primary source, rewarding players who master void-based combat mechanics. The consistent stat bonus makes this arcane a staple choice for endgame Operator optimization during tenno progression through Plains content.
