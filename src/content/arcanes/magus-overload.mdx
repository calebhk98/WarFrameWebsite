## Overview

Magus Overload provides energy sustain for squads during void fissure content. Operators equipping this arcane become mobile energy batteries, distributing void-powered restoration to teammates engaged in prolonged fissure excavations and wave-based missions.

The energy restoration applies to all nearby allies, synergizing with operator squad roles that focus on defensive support and sustain during prolonged void fissure operations.

## Acquisition

Magus Overload is primarily obtained from Zariman Void Fissure mission rewards and Void Cascade bonus rotations. This arcane requires regular participation in zariman void content, making it most accessible to players with moderate void ranking progression.

## Best on

Magus Overload is essential for Operators in support-focused squad compositions. Void-focused operators can maintain group energy sustainability while contributing offensive void damage, creating a hybrid damage-support playstyle for endgame fissure farming.

## Sources

Zariman Void Fissure content provides the most reliable farming source. Players grinding void relic refinement naturally accumulate this arcane, making it ideal for operators transitioning into dedicated void support roles.
