## Overview

Residual Shock chains electric damage between enemies via companion electric procs. On every electric status application, the arcane jumps electricity to nearby targets, creating multiplicative damage chains. Sentinels with electric weapons excel with this arcane. Early ranks chain to 2 enemies at 25% weapon damage; maximum rank chains to 5 enemies at 125% weapon damage.

Residual Shock drops from various Zariman and Void Cascade missions.

Residual Shock activates automatically when its trigger condition is met during combat.
