## Overview

Arcane Tanker is a Warframe arcane that grants substantial armor bonuses whenever the warframe equips an Archgun, persisting for 60 seconds after equipment. The flat armor addition (200--1200 depending on rank) transforms fragile damage-focused frames into mobile tanks during Archgun phase, enabling fearless positioning during high-mobility missions (Railjack, Archwing, open-world bosses). Unlike armor-scaling abilities that require ability-casting, Tanker provides automatic defense simply through equipment selection, making it universally applicable across all warframe types.

Tanker's primary strength lies in its simplicity and guarantee: equipping an Archgun automatically triggers the buff without conditional triggers or probability mechanics. Archgun-heavy mission types (Empyrean, Eidolon Archgun phases) leverage Tanker to enable fragile frame choices during dangerous mobility phases. The 60-second duration provides persistent armor even after unequipping the Archgun, allowing bonus carry-through into subsequent phases or mission transitions.

Armor scales damage reduction multiplicatively based on armor-to-incoming-damage ratios. A warframe with 100 base armor receiving 1200 bonus reaches 1300 total armor, providing approximately 87% damage reduction against physical damage. High-armor frames (Valkyr, Rhino) stacking Tanker reach near-immunity levels to most incoming projectile damage. The 60-second duration extends beyond typical Archgun deployment cycles, creating passive armor buff carry-through into post-Archgun combat phases.

## Acquisition

Arcane Tanker is obtained through Arbitrations, the high-level endless mission variant available after star-chart completion. Players must reach rotation A or C (requiring 10+ minutes of mission engagement minimum) to access Tanker drops. The 5% drop chance from each qualifying rotation tier means dedicated farming requires significant time investment; average 10--15 Arbitration runs per Tanker drop. Alternatively, pre-ranked Tanker copies trade for 80--140 platinum on player markets, reflecting moderate demand from Archgun-focused builds.

## Best on

Fragile damage frames (Wisp, Volt, Saryn) pair Tanker with Archgun phases to gain survivability during high-mobility encounters. Archgun-heavy mission types (Railjack Empyrean, Archon Hunt phases) employ Tanker to reduce squishy-frame reliance, enabling broader warframe choice. Open-world boss encounters (Eidolons, Archon bosses) leverage Tanker to enable unarmored frames to survive Archgun-centric combat phases. Mobility-focused builds (Nova, Loki) use Tanker as the primary survivability tool during Archgun deployment, converting mobility into survivability through passive armor.

Tank-focused frames (Rhino, Hildryn, Grendel) may deprioritize Tanker, preferring to use arcane slots for offense or utility given their already-high base armor pools.

## Builds that use it

Railjack Empyrean builds employ Arcane Tanker to enable fragile DPS frames during Archgun waves, expanding squad composition flexibility. Archon Hunt runs use Tanker to provide mid-phase armor boosts during Archgun-required encounters. Open-world farming builds leverage Tanker to enable low-armor farming frames to survive boss encounter Archgun phases without switching to dedicated tank frames. Eidolon-hunting builds pair Tanker with team role diversification, allowing pure-damage operators to gain survivability during Archgun phases without dedicating a squad slot to tanking.

## Notes

Arcane Tanker's trigger is automatic on Archgun equipment; no proc chance or condition applies. The 60-second buff persists independently of Archgun active usage, enabling passive armor carry-through into subsequent phases or ability-casting cycles. Multiple Tanker stacks do not occur; only one active buff per warframe at a time. Armor calculation uses the existing armor formula: damage reduction equals armor divided by (armor + 300), creating exponential returns on high-armor values. High-armor frames (Valkyr with 600 base armor) stacking Tanker reach values where most physical damage barely scratches the warframe.
