## Overview

Primary Dexterity is a weapon-exclusive arcane that grants passive damage scaling based on melee kill counts. When equipped on a primary weapon, consecutive melee kills with any melee weapon in the loadout trigger cumulative damage bonuses that apply to the primary. The arcane rewards hybrid play styles where operators seamlessly alternate between ranged and melee damage phases, creating exponential scaling windows.

This arcane excels in endless void fissure missions and Zariman skirmish runs where kill counts accumulate rapidly. The 20-second duration and stackable buff model enable disciplined players to maintain near-maximum scaling throughout extended engagements. Maximum rank yields 60% damage per stack, reaching 360% multiplicative bonus at six stacks. The buff applies to all primary weapon damage types simultaneously.

## Acquisition

Primary Dexterity drops from Void Cascade enemies in Mania, Malice, and Angst void-touched zones at rare rarity. These enemies spawn during escalating Zariman tier missions and Void Fissure rotations. Players should prioritize extended Void Cascade skirmish runs where named enemy spawns are guaranteed, allowing for consistent drop opportunities. Higher-tier endless missions offer improved drop rates compared to finite mission types.

## Best on

Hybrid melee-primary builds gain the most value from Dexterity. Warframes with enhanced melee speed such as Wukong Prime or Excalibur Umbra that switch to primary weapons for elemental damage or status application benefit enormously from this arcane. Melee-focused frames pairing a high-damage primary as a swap weapon for elite enemies see exponential scaling throughout missions.

## Usage

Primary Dexterity activates automatically when any melee weapon achieves a kill. The buff applies to the equipped primary weapon for 20 seconds, stacking up to six times independently. Continuous melee kills maintain high stacking; gaps in melee activity allow stacks to expire. Players should plan loadouts around rapid melee kill patterns followed by primary weapon bursts against tougher enemies during the buff window.

## Notes

Melee kills from finisher animations, environmental traps, or allied damage do not trigger this arcane; only direct melee weapon hits count. The arcane rewards playstyle synergy between melee and primary weapons, making it particularly valuable for mission tiers where enemy density supports sustained melee engagement.

## Sources

Primary Dexterity is restricted to Void Cascade void-touched Zariman content. Players seeking multiple max-rank copies for different loadout configurations should commit to extended void fissure mission chains targeting the Mania, Malice, and Angst spawning zones.
