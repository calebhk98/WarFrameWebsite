## Overview

Arcane Phantasm grants a movement Speed bonus when the warframe successfully blocks an attack. Blocking with a melee weapon active triggers a 45% chance check, and on success, the warframe gains elevated movement speed for 18 seconds. The block trigger is consistent for melee builds that hold block during combat, allowing active blocking to serve both its defensive function and an arcane trigger role simultaneously.

At rank 5, a successful block grants +60% Speed for 18 seconds. The 18-second duration is long enough that a single trigger covers an extended combat engagement before the bonus needs refreshing. Melee builds that block frequently in close-range combat maintain reliable speed uptime through repeated block events.

## Effect

Each successful block checks the 45% trigger chance. On success, the warframe gains the listed Speed bonus for 18 seconds. Speed in Warframe affects movement velocity directly -- sprint speed, slide speed, and general locomotion all benefit from the percentage increase. At rank 0, +10% Speed is a modest movement improvement. At rank 5, +60% Speed substantially increases how quickly the warframe can reposition, close on enemies, or retreat during the buff window.

The 45% trigger chance is fixed across all ranks; only the Speed bonus and duration of the effect change. Blocking frequently during melee combat ensures the trigger has regular opportunities to fire. Block-heavy melee styles -- holding block while approaching enemies, blocking during heavy attack wind-ups -- generate multiple trigger checks per encounter.

## Acquisition

Arcane Phantasm drops from Duviri/Endless at Legendary rarity (0.67% Tiers 1 and 3, 4% Tier 9), Skirmish nodes on Pluto, Neptune, and Venus at Uncommon Rotation C (10-10.34%), Zariman Ten Zero Void Fissures (9%), and Eidolon Hydrolyst (8%). Zariman Void Fissures and Skirmish nodes offer the most accessible farming routes at comparable drop percentages. Eidolon Hydrolyst is available nightly and provides 8% per run for squads with established Eidolon capture efficiency. Warframe.market provides direct purchase for players who prefer platinum acquisition.

## Builds that use it

Arcane Phantasm suits melee-focused builds that hold block as part of their combat routine. Melee builds using block-combo weapons -- Gunblades that fire on heavy attack/block presses, polearm builds that approach enemies with block held -- naturally accumulate block events and trigger Phantasm frequently. Frames with melee-supportive passives or abilities (Excalibur's Exalted Blade, Valkyr's Hysteria, Atlas with Landslide) spend extended time in melee range where blocking is a natural defensive input.

The +60% Speed bonus at rank 5 also enables hit-and-run melee tactics: close, deal damage, block an attack to trigger Phantasm, then use the speed bonus to reposition before the next engagement sequence. Frames without innate mobility -- slower frames like Inaros or Rhino in default configuration -- benefit more from the speed bonus in absolute terms compared to already-fast frames.

## Notes

The block trigger requires active melee blocking, meaning the melee weapon must be drawn and the block input held when an attack lands. Passive blocking through Guardian mods or automatic block animations may not trigger the arcane depending on how the game classifies the block event. The Speed bonus is additive with other movement speed bonuses from mods such as Rush and from warframe abilities like Volt's Speed. The 45% trigger chance means that on average, slightly fewer than half of all blocks activate the bonus, so melee builds with very infrequent blocking may not maintain consistent uptime.
