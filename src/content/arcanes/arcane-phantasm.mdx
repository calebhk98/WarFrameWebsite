## Overview

Arcane Phantasm is a Warframe arcane that rewards stealth gameplay by summoning temporary spectral copies of the Tenno that aid in combat. The arcane triggers on stealth kills (eliminating enemies without breaking invisibility), creating a phantom duplicate that mirrors the Tenno's equipped weapon and attacks for a scaling duration. The phantom deals percentage-based damage from the Tenno's weapon, scaling from 25% at rank 0 to 75% at rank 5. This mechanic transforms stealth-focused builds into multiplied damage engines, particularly effective on frames like Ivara, Ash, and Loki that can maintain invisibility for extended periods or reset it frequently. The arcane is niche but exceptionally powerful when combined with stealth augments and invisibility extensions.

## Effect per rank

Arcane Phantasm summons a phantom clone that lasts between 8 and 18 seconds depending on rank, with damage inheritance scaling from 25% to 75%. The phantom inherits all equipped weapon mods, meaning modded weapon damage scales fully into the phantom's attacks. Multiple phantoms can stack from consecutive stealth kills, creating a swarm effect that dramatically increases burst damage. The phantom targets enemies independently and can be directed indirectly by repositioning the Tenno. Duration scaling is linear, and the damage inheritance is calculated from total modified weapon damage, including critical multipliers and status effects applied by the Tenno's mods. At rank 5, a fully modded riven weapon can create phantoms dealing 75% of the weapon's buffed damage, effectively tripling total output during the phantasm window.

## Acquisition

Arcane Phantasm drops from Duviri/Endless at Legendary rarity (0.67% Tiers 1--3, 4% Tier 9), Skirmish nodes on Pluto/Neptune/Venus at uncommon (10%) Rotation C, Zariman Void Fissures at uncommon (9%), and legacy Eidolon Hydrolyst (8%). The most farmable source involves Zariman Void Fissure missions, which offer consistent uncommon drops without the long grind of Duviri Tier 9. Skirmish missions provide similar drop rates with faster mission times, making squad farming viable. Duviri Tier 9 scaling difficulty makes it less accessible for casual farmers but provides the best single-run odds. Radioshare coordinated farms in Zariman are optimal for players with limited time, as squad resources and boosters can be pooled for efficiency.

## Builds that use it

Arcane Phantasm creates powerful synergies with stealth-oriented builds, particularly Ivara Prime with her Dashwire augment and Navigator for indefinite invisible aiming. Ash with Smoke Shadow augment and invisibility extensions can spam stealth kills while phantoms provide additional damage output. Loki with Hushed Invisibility (augment) and high critical chance daggers scales the arcane's damage inheritance to overwhelming levels. The arcane is less valuable on non-stealth frames or on frames that cannot consistently apply stealth kills (Frost, Limbo without setup). Combining Arcane Phantasm with Finisher mods (Shred, Rupture, Executioner) ensures stealth kill triggers even on high-level enemies. Pairing with a crit-modded weapon guarantees the phantasm inherits massive critical damage multipliers, making even modest base weapons capable of sustained high output.

## Notes

Phantoms inherit the Tenno's current weapon only; switching weapons after a phantom spawns does not change the phantom's armament. The arcane's effectiveness is limited by invisibility duration and cooldown availability; frames without reliable stealth resets struggle to maintain phantom uptime. Phantoms do not inherit companion mods or effects, only main weapon mods. The arcane was balanced in a 2025 patch to reduce phantom AI aggression at stealth breakpoints, preventing accidental invisibility breaks through phantom enemy engagement. Steel Path enemies at very high levels (170+) can kill phantoms, reducing their usefulness at endgame despite high damage inheritance; the arcane shines most at medium difficulty content (Void Fissure 40--80 range).
