## Overview

Cascadia Empowered is a secondary weapon arcane that provides bonus damage matching the damage type of status effects applied to enemies. Unlike its sister arcanes in the Cascadia family, this arcane rewards diverse elemental playstyles by dynamically matching damage type. Any status effect proc—heat, cold, toxin, electric, or physical—triggers flat bonus damage of the same type, enabling flexible build construction around multi-element weapons.

This arcane excels on status-heavy secondary weapons or hybrid crit-status builds where consistent procs are achievable. The scaling bonus damage grows significantly at higher ranks, with maximum rank providing 750 flat bonus damage per proc. In extended encounters against tanky enemies, maintaining status application keeps the bonus relevant throughout mission duration.

The damage bonus activates instantly when a status effect applies to an enemy, regardless of the status type. Multiple simultaneous status effects (e.g., heat and toxin on the same enemy) trigger separate bonus damage instances, rewarding multi-element loadouts. The bonus is flat and therefore scales well against both light and heavily armored targets.

## Acquisition

Cascadia Empowered drops from the same Zariman Void Angels encounter pool as other Cascadia arcanes, with Void Angel enemies providing the highest drop rate at 1.67% rarity. The guaranteed drop from the Holdfasts (Cavalero Guardian) mission offers deterministic progression towards higher ranks. Farming runs in Zariman efficiently gather all three Cascadia arcanes simultaneously.

The common-rarity drop path through Holdfasts provides a reliable baseline, while higher-difficulty Void Angel encounters supply rarer copies at accelerated rates. Players can prioritize grinding specific Zariman missions based on squad composition and survival preferences.

## Best on

Cascadia Empowered synergizes best with status-focused secondary weapons like the Zakti Prime, Pristine Condition Cycron, or hybrid crit-status Catharsis builds. Weapons with multiple elemental damage types unlock additional bonus damage triggers per target. Corrosive and Viral combinations prove especially effective, creating frequent procs that sustain the bonus damage output.

Pairing with frames that amplify status application—such as Grendel's projectile inflation or Mirage's beam specialization—maximizes proc frequency. Speed-running and crowd-clearing playstyles benefit most, as the constant proc stream keeps bonus damage consistently active across waves of enemies.
