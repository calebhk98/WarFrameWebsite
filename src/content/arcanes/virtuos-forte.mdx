## Overview

Virtuos Forte is a rare utility arcane that grants attack speed bonuses when the operator's Amp reloads. This cycling-focused arcane rewards players who manage reload timing effectively, enabling burst-phase DPS amplification during Eidolon hunts and other endgame void fissure content.

## Effect

When the operator reloads their Amp, they gain a temporary attack speed buff lasting 6 seconds. The buff scales from +20% at rank 0 to +100% at rank 5, directly doubling the Amp's fire rate at maximum rank. Strategic reload management enables near-permanent uptime.

## Acquisition

Virtuos Forte drops as a rare reward from Eidolon Hydrolyst captures during void fissure hunts. The arcane requires progression through all three Eidolons (Teralyst, Gantulyst, Hydrolyst) to farm reliably.

## Best on

Virtuos Forte pairs with sustained-damage amp builds:

- **Tridolon hunts** — reload timing optimizes burst phase damage windows.
- **Zariman void cascades** — constant reload cycles maintain attack speed buff.
- **Endless void fissures** — cycling uptime extends survival through attrition fights.

## Notes

The reload must complete successfully to trigger the buff—interrupted reloads do not grant the bonus. High-reload-speed amps benefit most from this arcane's mechanics. Stacks multiplicatively with other fire-rate modifiers.
