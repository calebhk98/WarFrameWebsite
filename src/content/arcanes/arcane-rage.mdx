## Overview

Arcane Rage rewards precision marksmanship with substantial damage bonuses. When a player lands a headshot with a primary weapon, the arcane has a 15% chance to grant a powerful damage buff lasting 24 seconds. At maximum rank, the damage bonus reaches 180%, transforming weapon performance for the entire duration. The longer duration compared to other precision-based arcanes allows players to chain headshots into a prolonged damage state, making it ideal for headshot-focused playstyles.

The arcane particularly rewards disciplined aim and deliberate target priority. Because headshots are situational and require active player engagement, Arcane Rage fits players who enjoy skill-based combat over passive bonuses. The 24-second window is long enough to sustain through most encounter phases, encouraging aggressive play against priority targets.

## Acquisition

Arcane Rage can be farmed from Duviri Endless Tier 1 and Tier 3 nodes where it drops as a legendary reward, or from Void Cascade skirmish missions at Veil and Neptune nodes in Rotation C as a rare drop. The Duviri route offers higher drop rates but depends on reaching the intended tier and surviving long enough. Skirmish missions at later-game nodes provide more consistent access for players with sufficient gear.

## Best on

Headshot-optimized weapons such as sniper rifles, burst rifles, and accurate semi-automatic primaries maximize Arcane Rage's potential. Warframes with innate accuracy bonuses or built-in headshot scaling like Ivara Prime or Wisp Prime create powerful synergies. The arcane pairs well with crits as both reward weapon performance and precision, enabling builds that capitalize on landing every shot.

## Notes

The arcane triggers on primary weapon headshots only. It does not activate on secondary headshots or melee finishers. The 15% trigger rate means experienced headshot players will proc the buff roughly once every 6--7 headshots on average, providing consistent uptime during combat. The long 24-second duration allows planning around when the buff expires and orchestrating follow-up engagements.

## Sources

Arcane Rage is obtainable through Duviri Zariman endless missions or Void Cascade skirmish mission farming. Dedicated farming runs targeting Tier 1 or Tier 3 Duviri endless and later-node skirmish rotations yield reliable access to the arcane at various rarity tiers.
