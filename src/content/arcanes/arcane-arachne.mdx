## Overview

Arcane Arachne is a rare warframe arcane that rewards positional mastery by granting substantial damage bonuses whenever the player wall-latches during combat. The 30-second buff window and stackable nature mean wall-based mobility becomes a primary offensive tool rather than a navigation aid. Unlike pure damage arcanes that scale with stat tables, Arachne incentivizes active parkour integration into combat loops, turning environmental interaction into mechanical advantage.

Arachne's primary strength lies in reward density: a single wall-latch provides +100% damage (at rank 5) for 30 seconds of sustained combat. In open-world missions with vertical architecture (Grineer Galleons, Corpus hangars), coordinating wall-latches with ability rotations creates synergistic damage windows. The bonus applies globally to all damage sources, enabling frames with diverse damage types (ability spam, weapon combos, summons) to scale identically from wall-mechanic triggers.

## Effect

Arcane Arachne activates whenever the warframe initiates a wall-latch (pressing the melee key or jump while sliding into a vertical surface). The 30-second buff grants flat damage multiplier to all outgoing damage: ability damage, weapon damage, companion damage, and environmental hazards all benefit. Multiple wall-latches within the buff window refresh the duration but do not stack the bonus; only one instance of Arachne's damage buff can be active at a time.

The damage multiplier scales multiplicatively with mods, meaning +100% Arachne bonus applied to a warframe with 300% ability strength results in 6x total damage output (1.0 base + 3.0 mods + 1.0 Arachne = 5.0 multiplier). This makes Arachne particularly valuable on ability-damage-focused builds where scaling multipliers compound dramatically.

## Acquisition

Arcane Arachne drops primarily from Eidolon Hydrolyst encounters with roughly 12--14% drop chance per capture, making it moderately common among eidolon farm rewards. Tridolon hunters farming consistently obtain Arachne once every 6--8 farm sessions. Zariman Void Skirmish rotation C offers a 2.8% alternate source at significantly lower difficulty but longer mission duration. Duviri Endless provides rare legendary drops (0.67%) for extreme-difficulty farming routes.

Ranking to tier 5 takes 120 hours of mastering. Arachne's moderate drop rate and utility across diverse build types keep trading post prices accessible for most endgame players.

## Best on

Arcane Arachne excels on ability-damage-focused warframes with high strength builds: Saryn, Equinox, Mirage, and Protea benefit from the multiplicative scaling applied to ability output. Frames with vertical-environment affinities (Titania, Wukong, Lavos) naturally integrate wall-latching into combat, triggering Arachne buffs as part of core playstyles. Weapon-hybrid builds leverage Arachne's global damage scaling to boost both ability and gun-damage simultaneously.

Frames with limited defensive tools benefit from Arachne's engagement reward system: taking time for wall-latches creates offensive downtime that converts into damage windows, encouraging active positioning rather than static gameplay. Conversely, stationary frames or ability-restricted builds (Limbo's Rift, Frost's Bubble) gain minimal value because wall-engagement becomes a disadvantage.

## Builds that use it

Ability-spam builds universally equip Arachne to convert wall-mechanic navigation into damage scaling. Steel Path endless survival builds use Arachne to spike damage output periodically during wave transitions, enabling kill streaks that reset threat. Hybrid offensive-mobility builds leverage Arachne's engagement reward to encourage active parkour combat loops. Speed-running builds utilize Arachne during boss fights or high-value targets, timing wall-latches to maximize damage windows.

Mobile frames pair Arachne with Streamline and Primed Flow to maintain ability spam during constant wall-engage cycles. Stationary ability builds (Saryn with Miasma) use Arachne to enable periodic offensive resets between ability casts.

## Notes

Arcane Arachne triggers only on initial wall-latch activation, not on prolonged latch duration; holding a wall does not refresh the buff. Wall-latches in indoor corridors and vertical environments trigger more frequently than outdoor areas. The damage bonus applies multiplicatively to existing mods and ability strength, creating exponential scaling in builds with high strength investment. Parkour-centric warframes naturally maintain near-permanent Arachne uptime through regular wall engagement during mission progression. The 30-second duration window ensures buffer overlap during continuous wall-based combat.
