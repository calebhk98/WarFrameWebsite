## Overview

Arcane Fury is a warframe arcane that rewards melee-focused playstyles with stacking damage bonuses on kill. Each melee kill grants a percentage damage buff for a scaled duration, stacking up to 5 times. This arcane encourages aggressive close-quarters combat and rewards momentum-driven gameplay where players chain kills together for exponential damage scaling. The duration scaling per rank ensures kills within rapid succession maintain maximum stacks throughout extended encounters.

The arcane synergizes exceptionally well with melee-focused warframes like Wukong, Valkyr, Excalibur, and Galatine Prime builds. The scaling mechanism transforms melee gameplay into a rhythm-based damage engine where maintaining kill chains directly translates to sustained damage multipliers. With proper positioning and weapon selection, players can maintain near-permanent maximum stacks during standard mission content.
