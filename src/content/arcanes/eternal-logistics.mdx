## Overview

Eternal Logistics boosts Amp Ammo Efficiency after each Void Sling. Every time the operator performs a Void Sling, amp fire gains the listed Ammo Efficiency bonus for 8 seconds. Higher Ammo Efficiency means each amp shot consumes less ammo -- at sufficient efficiency values, the amp can sustain longer fire sequences before depleting its ammo pool and entering the recharge cycle.

At rank 5, Void Sling activates +72% Amp Ammo Efficiency for 8 seconds. Operators who Void Sling frequently -- using it for both movement and damage positioning -- activate Eternal Logistics as a natural consequence of their movement pattern, maintaining elevated ammo efficiency throughout active operator engagements.

## Effect

Each Void Sling triggers the Eternal Logistics bonus, applying the listed Ammo Efficiency increase to the amp for 8 seconds. At rank 0, +12% Ammo Efficiency modestly extends the amp's firing duration before ammo depletion. At rank 5, +72% Ammo Efficiency substantially reduces ammo cost per shot, allowing sustained amp fire for significantly longer before the recharge cycle begins.

Ammo Efficiency interacts with amp firing mechanics by reducing the ammo cost of each shot proportionally. An amp with 100 ammo capacity at +72% efficiency effectively sustains the firepower of a much larger ammo pool. Void Slings performed during combat keep the 8-second window refreshed, maintaining the efficiency bonus throughout operator combat phases.

## Acquisition

Eternal Logistics drops from Zariman Void Cascade rewards at 4% and Void Fissure rotation A at 2%. Void Fissure rotation A is the most frequently reached rotation in Void Fissure runs, making Logistics accessible in short to medium-length Zariman content sessions. Both sources are accessible after completing Angels of the Zariman. Warframe.market offers an alternative purchase route for players who prefer direct acquisition.

## Builds that use it

Eternal Logistics benefits operator builds that use Void Sling actively for both movement and combat positioning. Operators who Void Sling to reach elevated positions, dodge enemy projectiles, or close distance on targets generate Logistics triggers as a byproduct of active movement, maintaining ammo efficiency without dedicated effort. Continuous-beam amp configurations that deplete ammo quickly benefit most from the efficiency bonus, extending the sustainable fire window before the recharge state begins.

Eidolon hunt operators benefit from Logistics during extended limb-break sequences where sustained amp fire is required across multiple energy shard phases. Void Slings between Eidolon positions keep the efficiency bonus active, allowing more continuous amp fire during the damage windows between Eidolon movements.

## Notes

The ammo efficiency bonus applies from the moment of Void Sling activation and persists for 8 seconds. Multiple Void Slings within the 8-second window refresh the timer rather than stacking additional efficiency. The bonus applies to the primary amp fire mode; alternate fire mechanics may have independent ammo pools that are affected separately depending on the amp configuration. Void Sling distance and direction do not affect the trigger -- any Void Sling input activates Logistics regardless of trajectory. The efficiency bonus does not affect amp recharge rate; only the ammo consumption rate during active fire is reduced.
