Eternal Logistics provides energy sustain through amp attacks. This arcane enables operators to maintain sustained void beam fire during extended zariman void fissure farming without external energy support from squad members.

Eternal Logistics drops from Zariman Void Cascade rewards (4%) and void fissure rotation A completions (2%). Regular zariman void farming yields this arcane through natural progression.
