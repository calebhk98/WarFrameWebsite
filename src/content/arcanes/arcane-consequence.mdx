## Overview

Arcane Consequence is a Warframe arcane that rewards precision headshot gameplay with enhanced mobility and the ability to revive after death. The arcane synergizes with long-range weapons and frames that emphasize accuracy over area damage. Its headshot mechanic rewards skilled players with both immediate mobility bonuses and a safety net for risky engagements.

## Effect per rank

The arcane's primary effect increases Parkour Velocity following successful headshots, with the maximum-rank unlock granting charges for automatic revives. The duration of the parkour boost remains constant at 18 seconds across all ranks, but the velocity multiplier scales from 10% at rank 0 to 60% at rank 5. Starting at rank 3, the arcane also accumulates up to 1 Arcane Revive charge (displayed in the HUD), allowing the Tenno to automatically revive at their location without relying on teammates. Each revive resets all status effects and restores partial health.

## Acquisition

Arcane Consequence drops primarily from Duviri/Endless missions and Zariman Skirmish nodes, with legacy drops from Eidolon Teralyst captures. The arcane appears as a legendary drop in lower-tier Duviri runs (approximately 0.67% chance per tier), increasing to 4% in Tier 9 normal runs. For more consistent farming, Skirmish missions on Pluto, Neptune, and Venus offer uncommon (10%) drop rates on Rotation C. Eidolon Teralyst remains available but has been superseded by Duviri as the primary source since the introduction of that star chart expansion.

## Best on

Arcane Consequence excels on frames built for precision roles that already invest in headshot gameplay. Trinity Prime with a high-accuracy rifle can leverage both the mobility bonus and the revive charges for safer solo survivability. Nova Prime, when configured for control rather than kill speed, benefits from the repositioning mobility to set detonation points and escape threats. Titania benefits particularly well when flying at range with her weapon, as Parkour Velocity applies while airborne, effectively increasing her max flight speed. The revive mechanic provides meaningful defense for high-risk solo runs or steel path missions where a single mistake would normally end the attempt.

## Usage in Warframes

Arcane Consequence is niche by design, best suited to players who already practice headshot accuracy and want a reward mechanic beyond raw damage numbers. The parkour boost is most noticeable in tilesets with heavy verticality (Void Fissures, Void Cascade) where rapid repositioning prevents damage. The revive mechanic is valuable for endurance runs past two hours where even defensive frames may face unavoidable chip damage. Pairing this with weapons that have innate high critical chance (Rubico Prime, Velocitus, Gorgon Prime) ensures the headshot condition triggers regularly. The arcane is not recommended for AoE-focused builds or endurance runs below 90 minutes, as the conditional trigger and single revive charge offer diminishing value compared to flat defensive arcanes like Arcane Guardian.

## Notes

The parkour velocity bonus does not stack with Volt's Speed ability; they operate on separate mechanics and the higher value takes precedence. The revive mechanism bypasses team revival state, meaning a player with a charged Arcane Consequence will revive even if the squad has no revives remaining. However, the revived Tenno will have reduced shields/health and must reach cover quickly or a second death will result in true mission failure. This arcane was buffed in a 2025 update to include the revive component, making it significantly more valuable than earlier iterations that provided only mobility.
