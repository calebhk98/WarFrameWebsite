## Overview

Magus Melt is a rare Operator arcane that grants offensive capability by applying heat status to nearby enemies when Void Sling is cast. This arcane shifts the operator from pure support into hybrid damage-support, enabling proc-based crowd control and passive damage amplification.

## Effect

Each Void Sling cast triggers a heat proc on nearby enemies with a scaling probability. At rank 0, the chance is 20%, increasing linearly to a guaranteed 100% proc at rank 5. Heat status causes burning damage-over-time and can spread to nearby enemies, enabling exponential area denial.

## Acquisition

Magus Melt drops as a rare reward from Void Cascade missions on Zariman. The increased rarity reflects the arcane's offensive output compared to support-focused peers.

## Best on

Magus Melt pairs well with offense-focused operator builds:

- **Damage-boosting operator augments** — synergizes with offensive Void Sling builds.
- **Zariman defense/survival** — heat procs freeze enemies and amplify squad damage.
- **Eidolon hunts** — provides supplementary damage and status application to eidolons.

## Notes

Heat procs trigger weapon damage amplification (enemies with status are weaker), benefiting the entire squad's damage output. The operator's Warframe attacks also benefit from enemy status vulnerability. Stacks with elemental mods on equipped weapons.
