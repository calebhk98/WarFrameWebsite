## Overview

Magus Revert enables the operator to instantly return to their pre-Sling position by activating Void Sling a second time within a 3-second window after the initial Sling. This reverse-dash mechanic allows the operator to Sling into range for an amp shot or ability application and then immediately pull back to their starting position without spending energy on the return movement. The return also restores a flat amount of operator health - scaling from 10 at rank 0 to 60 at rank 5 - adding a minor healing component to each successful revert.

The strategic value lies in positioning: the operator can advance into hazard positions, fire the amp, and retract in under 3 seconds without investing additional Sling charges or energy. This is particularly useful in Eidolon hunt scenarios where the operator needs to close range on Eidolon limbs briefly and then retreat from the proximity danger zone before taking damage.

## Effect

The first Void Sling establishes a revert point. If the operator casts Void Sling again within 3 seconds, instead of sling-traveling forward, the operator returns to the original position at no energy cost and heals the listed health amount. A 3-second cooldown follows each successful revert before the mechanic is available again.

At rank 0, 10 health restored on revert provides minor sustain during the advance-and-retreat pattern. At rank 5, 60 health restored provides meaningful recovery per cycle, helping the operator maintain health across multiple advance-fire-revert rotations.

## Builds that use it

Magus Revert suits operators who use Void Sling for precise engagement positioning rather than sustained repositioning. Eidolon hunters who need to close range on limb targets briefly - landing within amp effective range, firing, and retreating before the Eidolon recovers - benefit from the revert allowing a single-movement round trip rather than two separate Slings. The no-energy-cost return conserves operator energy for additional advance cycles during the same Eidolon limb phase.

Operators in Zariman Void Cascade who enter high-density enemy zones for Void Blast applications then need to exit quickly benefit from Revert as an immediate retreat mechanism. The health restoration on each revert contributes to sustain during these aggressive close-range patterns.

## Notes

The revert window is 3 seconds from the initial Sling - the second Sling within this window triggers the return; a second Sling after the window functions as a normal Sling forward. The 3-second cooldown between reverts limits the advance-fire-retreat pattern to one revert per 3 seconds, requiring the operator to wait before the mechanic refreshes for the next approach. The return travel is instantaneous - the operator appears at the origin position rather than sliding back along the Sling path. If the operator's original position is no longer accessible (geometry changed, occupied by an enemy), the return behavior may be affected.
