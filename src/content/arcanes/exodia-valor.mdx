## Overview

Exodia Valor is a Zaw arcane that grants Additional Combo Count Chance specifically when hitting Lifted enemies. The Lifted status effect suspends enemies in the air, and while they are in this state, each hit with the Zaw has an additional chance to count toward the melee combo multiplier beyond the standard base chance. This conditional combo acceleration rewards builds that intentionally lift enemies as part of their combat pattern.

At rank 3, Exodia Valor provides +200% Additional Combo Count Chance on Lifted enemies, dramatically accelerating combo building against airborne targets. Combined with the base combo count probability, hitting Lifted enemies builds the combo counter far faster than ground-based combat alone.

## Effect

While an enemy is in the Lifted state, each Zaw hit checks the Additional Combo Count Chance from Exodia Valor in addition to the base combo count roll. At rank 0, +50% additional chance on Lifted targets provides a meaningful uplift to combo building speed against Lifted enemies. At rank 3, +200% Additional Combo Count Chance creates an extremely high probability of combo count increments on each hit against Lifted enemies, pushing the combo multiplier to high values rapidly during Lift windows.

Enemies remain Lifted for a limited duration based on the source of the Lift status. Maximizing Zaw hit rate against Lifted enemies during the suspension window extracts the most combo count value from Exodia Valor.

## Acquisition

Exodia Valor drops as an uncommon reward from Void Fissure relic cracking across all refinement tiers. Unlike Zariman-specific arcanes, Valor is available through standard Void Fissure content, accessible earlier in game progression. Players cracking relics during Fissure missions have a consistent chance to encounter Valor drops. Warframe.market provides an alternative purchase route for players preferring direct acquisition.

## Builds that use it

Exodia Valor is purpose-built for Zaw builds that include a source of Lift status. Air attacks naturally apply Lift to enemies they hit; Jump attacks, certain warframe abilities (Banshee's Sonic Boom, Rhino's Stomp), and Zaw attacks with innate Lift procs create the Lifted state that Exodia Valor bonus applies to. Builds that pair Exodia Valor with air attack-heavy combat patterns lift enemies and then immediately follow with ground combo attacks to exploit the elevated combo count chance during the Lift window.

Blood Rush builds that rely on high combo multiplier for critical chance scaling benefit from the rapid combo ramp that Exodia Valor provides during Lift windows, reaching the critical chance multiplier thresholds faster per engagement.

## Notes

Exodia Valor is Zaw-exclusive and can only be equipped in the Exodia arcane slots of player-built Zaw melee weapons. The maximum rank is 3, matching other Exodia arcanes with a reduced rank ceiling versus standard arcanes. The Additional Combo Count Chance only applies while the target is in the Lifted state -- hits on non-Lifted enemies benefit only from the base combo count probability without the Exodia Valor bonus. Combining Exodia Valor with Exodia Triumph in the two Exodia slots is not possible since only one of each arcane type can be equipped per slot.
