## Overview

Exodia Valor is an uncommon Zaw arcane providing movement speed bonuses following heavy attacks. This arcane incentivizes aggressive heavy-attack playstyles while improving overall battlefield mobility and chase capability during void fissure encounters.

## Effect

Each heavy attack grants a temporary movement speed buff lasting 6 seconds. The bonus scales from +20% at rank 0 to +100% at rank 5. Frequent heavy attacks maintain near-permanent mobility buff uptime.

## Acquisition

Exodia Valor drops as an uncommon reward from Void Fissure relic cracking. All refinement tiers provide drops.

## Best on

Exodia Valor enhances mobility-heavy Zaw builds:

- **Fast-attack Zaws** — constant movement bonus from rapid heavy-attack chains.
- **Chase-heavy builds** — enables pursuing mobile enemies effectively.
- **Roaming void fissures** — sustained mobility accelerates objective completion.

## Notes

The movement bonus applies to sprint and parkour speed equally. Stacks multiplicatively with other movement-speed sources. Frequent heavy-attack timing maintains buff uptime. Synergizes well with attack-speed mods.
