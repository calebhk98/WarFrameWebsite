## Overview

Exodia Valor is a Zaw arcane that provides movement speed bonuses following heavy attacks, coupling offensive melee actions with mobility improvements. As a Zaw-exclusive arcane, it only equips on player-built melee weapons crafted through Ostron Zaw assembly rather than standard market weapons. Each heavy attack triggers the movement speed buff, making Valor attractive for aggressive melee builds that frequently use heavy attacks as primary damage events and also need to reposition rapidly between combat engagements.

The 6-second movement speed window covers enough time to pursue enemies or reposition to a defensive location following a heavy attack swing. Valor is particularly useful in open-world content -- Cetus plains, Cambion Drift -- where distances between enemies are large and mobility between engagements significantly affects combat efficiency. Heavy attacks on open-world fauna, enemy units, or objective-adjacent threats all trigger the buff, allowing constant repositioning acceleration during open-world mission pacing.

## Effect

Each heavy attack with the Zaw grants a movement speed buff lasting 6 seconds. At rank 0, +20% movement speed provides a modest acceleration for the buff duration -- noticeable but not transformative. At rank 5, +100% movement speed doubles the warframe's sprint and traversal speed, dramatically accelerating repositioning after a heavy attack swing. The doubled movement speed at max rank enables rapid pursuit of retreating enemies or quick escape from dangerous zones after committing to a heavy attack animation.

The buff applies to all movement including sprinting, sliding, and bullet jumps, making it a comprehensive mobility enhancement rather than only a sprint speed increase.

## Acquisition

Exodia Valor drops as an uncommon reward from Void Fissure relic cracking across all refinement tiers. Unlike Zariman-specific arcanes, Valor is available through standard Void Fissure content, which is accessible at earlier game progression stages. Players cracking relics during Fissure missions have a consistent chance to encounter Valor drops across all relic tiers. Warframe.market provides an alternative purchase route, and the arcane trades at moderate values given its Zaw-specific restriction.

## Builds that use it

Exodia Valor suits heavy-attack-focused Zaw builds where the player regularly uses heavy attacks as the primary damage event. Spin-attack or single-heavy-swing builds on Zaw polearms or heavy blades generate heavy attack triggers frequently enough to maintain near-permanent Valor uptime during active combat. Frames that play mobility-heavy -- Ash with Teleport for rapid repositioning, Zephyr with Turbulence for aerial mobility -- benefit from the stack amplification of Valor's movement bonus on their already-mobile combat pattern.

Cetus Zaws used in Plains of Eidolon bounty content benefit from Valor's traversal acceleration during open-world movement between objectives and enemy groups. The additional speed also assists in reaching Eidolon spawn points before the hunt window expires.

## Notes

Exodia Valor is Zaw-exclusive and cannot be equipped on non-Zaw melee weapons. The arcane does not require a successful hit to trigger -- the heavy attack action itself, regardless of whether it connects, generates the movement buff. The buff applies from the moment the heavy attack completes its animation and persists for the full 6-second duration regardless of subsequent actions. Repeated heavy attacks refresh the timer rather than adding additional movement speed beyond the listed value. The movement bonus interacts with sprint speed mods in an additive fashion, with both bonuses contributing to overall movement capability.
