## Overview

Arcane Shield provides passive resistance to impact status effects, reducing how often impact procs successfully apply to the warframe. Impact status staggers the affected target, briefly interrupting movement and animation - in high-difficulty content, repeated impact stagger interrupts ability casting, weapon firing, and movement in ways that can be lethal. The passive resistance prevents stagger application without requiring the player to run Handspring or similar anti-knockback mods.

At rank 5, the 102% resistance chance provides complete immunity to impact status application from external enemy sources. Impact procs are common from Grineer heavy weapons, Corpus projectile units, and Sentient attacks, making impact immunity broadly relevant across factions in Steel Path and Arbitration content. The bonus Arcane Revive at maximum rank provides an emergency self-revive charge in addition to the impact protection.

## Effect

Arcane Shield passively resists impact status application each time an impact proc attempt occurs. At rank 0, a 17% resistance chance prevents roughly one in six impact procs. At rank 5, 102% resistance provides complete immunity to impact status from external sources, while the Arcane Revive provides a self-revive charge.

The stagger prevention is particularly valuable during ability cast windows where interruption from impact stagger would otherwise cancel or delay ability usage.

## Builds that use it

Arcane Shield suits caster frames that rely on uninterrupted ability cast sequences during combat. Frames with channeled abilities - Volt's Discharge, Nidus's Virulence, Saryn's Miasma - that can be interrupted by impact stagger maintain ability uptime more consistently with impact immunity. In Steel Path content where Grineer heavy unit volleys apply frequent impact procs, impact immunity prevents the stagger cycle that breaks offensive momentum.

## Notes

Impact resistance prevents the stagger or knockdown component of the impact status, not the physical damage from impact-damage weapons. Weapons dealing impact damage still deal their full physical damage; only the status proc application is resisted. The Arcane Revive at rank 5 is consumed on use and does not regenerate within a mission.
