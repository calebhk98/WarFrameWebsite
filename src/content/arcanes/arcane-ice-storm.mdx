## Overview

Arcane Ice Storm triggers area freeze effects during cold status application. This warframe arcane synergizes with cold-focused builds by providing crowd control and area damage.

|------|----------------|-------------|

| 1    | 15%            | 140         |
| 2    | 20%            | 180         |
| 3    | 25%            | 220         |
| 4    | 27%            | 260         |
| 5    | 30%            | 300         |

Ice wave triggering enables area denial during cold-status-heavy combat while providing continuous CC opportunities.

## Acquisition

Arcane Ice Storm drops from Zariman Void Fissure (4%) and Void Cascade rotation A (2%). Regular zariman void farming yields this arcane naturally.

## Best on

Arcane Ice Storm excels on cold-heavy warframes and freeze-focused builds. The consistent crowd control complements ice-based damage scaling.
