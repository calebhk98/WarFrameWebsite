## Overview

Magus Cloud grants brief invisibility windows during void blasting sequences. This operator arcane provides defensive utility by creating evasion opportunities, allowing operators to reposition safely during dangerous void fissure engagements or eidolon hunts.

| Rank | Trigger Chance | Invisibility Duration |

| 1    | 15%            | 4.4s                  |
| 2    | 20%            | 4.8s                  |
| 3    | 25%            | 5.2s                  |
| 4    | 27%            | 5.6s                  |
| 5    | 30%            | 6s                    |

Invisibility breakage rules follow standard operator stealth mechanics. Attacking or being hit ends the effect, encouraging tactical void blast timing during prolonged void fissure sequences.

## Acquisition

Magus Cloud drops from Eidolon Hydrolyst captures with a 4% chance and from Zariman Void Fissure missions with 2% frequency. The dual farming sources make this arcane accessible to both plains hunters and fissure farmers.

## Best on

Magus Cloud excels on defensive-playstyle operators who prioritize evasion and repositioning. Combined with void dodge and transference dash, this arcane enables operators to kite dangerous enemies during extended void fissure content while maintaining offensive void output.

## Sources

Eidolon plains hunting remains the primary source, though zariman void fissure participation provides supplementary drops. Operators practicing void beam mechanics naturally accumulate this arcane over time.
