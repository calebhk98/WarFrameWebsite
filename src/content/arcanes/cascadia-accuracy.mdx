## Overview

Cascadia Accuracy boosts accuracy and fire rate on critical hits from secondary weapons. The dual buff rewards crit-optimized secondary builds by converting every critical proc into a short but meaningful DPS window. Accuracy improvements reduce projectile spread during sustained full-auto fire, tightening shot grouping on automatic pistols and machine pistols that experience bloom after the first few rounds. Fire rate scaling compounds this by increasing rounds landing in the tighter spread window per second, making subsequent shots during the buff more reliable and more frequent.

The critical-hit trigger means builds that already invest heavily in crit chance maintain near-constant uptime on the buff. A secondary with 50% or higher critical chance effectively maintains the accuracy and fire rate bonuses through most of a firefight, reducing dependency on the trigger chance at rank 5.

## Effect

Cascadia Accuracy activates on any critical hit from the secondary weapon. Both accuracy and fire rate bonuses activate simultaneously for 10 seconds. The accuracy improvement applies to projectile spread during continuous fire, not to weapon sway or hipfire penalty. The fire rate bonus is multiplicative with fire rate mods, so weapons already running Gunslinger or Lethal Torrent benefit from strong compounding. At rank 5, plus 60% accuracy and plus 45% fire rate create a substantial output window that heavy-hitting automatic secondaries exploit during dense enemy encounters. The 10-second window refreshes on each new critical hit, maintaining perpetual uptime when crit chance is sufficiently high.

## Acquisition

Cascadia Accuracy drops from Zariman Void Fissure at 4% and Void Cascade rotation B at 2%. Standard Zariman Ten-Zero content drops this arcane naturally through regular mission completion. Players farming Zariman Void Fissure as their primary source collect Cascadia Accuracy at a steady pace alongside other Zariman arcanes. The arcane trades at moderate plat values on warframe.market for players preferring direct purchase over extended farming sessions.

## Builds that use it

Crit-stacked secondaries with automatic fire patterns extract the most value from Cascadia Accuracy. Full-auto or burst-fire secondaries with high crit stats benefit from both accuracy tightening and fire rate increase during the buff window. Builds combining Point Strike, Primed Pistol Gambit, and Hollow Point with Cascadia Accuracy push effective DPS well beyond what individual crit mods provide alone. Mesa's Peacemaker paired with crit-scaled pistols benefits particularly well because the rapid automatic fire generates frequent crit procs to keep the buff window refreshed throughout combat.

## Notes

Fire rate scaling applies to weapon fire rate only, not reload speed or holster speed. Accuracy improvement reduces spread during sustained fire on weapons with bloom mechanics; hitscan weapons benefit most from this component. Projectile weapons with fixed trajectories receive less value from the accuracy bonus. The 10-second buff duration resets on each new critical hit, so consistent crits maintain near-perpetual uptime during active engagements. Building toward 100% crit chance effectively removes dependency on the trigger chance and ensures the buff never lapses mid-fight.
