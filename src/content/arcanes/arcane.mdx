## Overview

Arcane enhancements are a category of equippable upgrades in Warframe that provide passive or triggered bonuses to warframes, operators, weapons, Zaws, Kitguns, and amps. Each arcane has a specific type that determines which equipment slot it occupies: warframe arcanes slot into the arcane panel of the Arsenal, operator arcanes apply in the Focus/Operator loadout, and weapon arcanes (primary, secondary, melee, amp) attach to their respective weapon type.

Arcanes are leveled from rank 0 to their maximum rank by combining duplicate copies of the same arcane at increasing quantities. Each rank increases the potency of the effect -- either the trigger chance, the stat value, or the duration of the bonus. Most arcanes in Warframe have a maximum rank of 5, requiring multiple copies to level fully. Kitgun Pax arcanes max at rank 3 and require fewer copies.

Arcanes do not occupy a mod slot and do not count toward the mod capacity. They are an additional layer of customization beyond the standard modding system. Two arcane slots exist on warframes, one arcane slot on amps, and one on most weapons.

## Arcane Categories

Warframe groups arcanes into several major categories:

- **Warframe arcanes** -- equip to the warframe directly, triggering on combat events such as kills, headshots, ability casts, or incoming damage.
- **Operator arcanes** (Magus, Virtuos, Eternal) -- equip to the operator loadout, triggering on Void Sling, Void Blast, Void Mode, or Transference events.
- **Primary arcanes** -- equip to primary weapons, triggering on reload, status procs, headshots, or kills.
- **Secondary arcanes** -- equip to secondary weapons, triggering on similar weapon-specific events.
- **Melee arcanes** -- equip to melee weapons, triggering on kills, heavy attacks, or status effects.
- **Kitgun arcanes** (Pax series) -- exclusive to Kitgun secondary weapons, providing passive or triggered bonuses specific to the Kitgun type.
- **Amp arcanes** -- equip to amps, providing bonuses to void damage output or operator survivability during amp usage.
- **Zaw arcanes** (Exodia series) -- exclusive to Zaw melee weapons, providing unique triggered effects.

## Acquisition

Arcanes are obtained from several major sources. Eidolon hunts in the Plains of Eidolon drop operator arcanes as mission rewards when capturing Eidolons. The Scarlet Spear, Plague Star, and similar limited events provide specific arcanes in their reward pools. The Zariman Ten-Zero open world area provides Zariman-exclusive arcanes from void cascade and void flood missions. Syndicates and the Holdfasts standing vendor on Zariman sell arcanes directly for standing. Trading between players and Warframe.market provide additional acquisition routes for all arcanes.

## Ranking Arcanes

Upgrading an arcane requires duplicate copies of the same arcane placed in the upgrade interface. The number of copies required increases at each rank. The formula is cumulative: reaching rank 5 from rank 0 requires the base copy plus additional duplicates totaling the full upgrade cost. Arcane Enhancement items can be used in some systems to provide experience toward ranking without requiring duplicates.

## Notes

Arcane effects stack on the same slot if two different arcanes are equipped -- warframes have two slots and both arcanes function simultaneously. Running two copies of the same arcane in both slots is allowed and the effects stack, providing doubled trigger chances or doubled bonuses depending on implementation. Some arcanes have effects that explicitly do not stack or cap at fixed values regardless of how many copies are active. The WFCD warframe-items dataset documents rank-by-rank stat values for all arcanes, providing a canonical reference for arcane scaling data.
