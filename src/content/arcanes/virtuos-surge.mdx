## Overview

Virtuos Surge is an utility-focused Operator arcane that grants energy restoration bonuses when picking up energy drops. This support-oriented arcane enables sustained Amp uptime during extended endgame encounters by synergizing with energy restoration mechanics in squad play.

## Effect

When the operator picks up an energy drop, the Amp gains a temporary energy drain reduction buff lasting 10 seconds. The buff provides +50% energy efficiency at rank 0, scaling to +250% at rank 5, effectively extending Amp firing duration per energy reserve.

## Acquisition

Virtuos Surge drops as an uncommon reward from void cascade missions on Zariman. Early progression in void fissure content grants access to this support arcane.

## Best on

Virtuos Surge is ideal for energy-management-heavy builds:

- **Long void cascade runs** — energy efficiency extends farming endurance.
- **Squad support operators** — sustains Amp uptime while focusing on team utility.
- **Low-drain amp builds** — amplifies already-efficient fire patterns.

## Notes

The buff applies specifically to Amp energy consumption, not Warframe energy. Multiple energy pickups do not stack the buff—each pickup resets the duration. Pairs well with squad energy-generation arcanes on other operators.
