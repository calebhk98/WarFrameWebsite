## Overview

Virtuos Surge generates amp energy bonuses when the operator or nearby warframe picks up an energy orb, converting the common energy pickup resource into a burst of operator amp capacity. Energy orbs drop regularly during combat from killed enemies and certain environmental sources, making the trigger frequent in missions with active enemy engagement. Operators who coordinate orb pickups with active amp combat maintain elevated energy reserves without relying solely on natural regeneration or Focus school energy abilities.

Amp energy determines how long the operator can fire their amp before requiring energy recovery. The additional energy from Surge extends the operational window before the amp runs dry, which is particularly relevant during extended Void Manifestation fights in Zariman content or during Eidolon synovia-break phases where continuous amp fire is critical to time-sensitive damage windows.

## Effect

Each energy orb pickup triggers an amp energy restoration bonus for 10 seconds. At rank 0, +50% energy to the amp provides a meaningful extension of amp operational time per pickup. At rank 5, +250% energy is a substantial injection that, combined with the base energy restoration from the orb, can fully replenish the amp's energy pool and then some, providing extended fire windows after each pickup.

The 10-second duration means the energy bonus extends for a window beyond the immediate pickup, allowing the operator to exploit orb pickups as pre-engagement preparation before entering sustained amp fire phases.

## Acquisition

Virtuos Surge drops as an uncommon reward from Zariman Void Cascade missions. The arcane is accessible after completing Angels of the Zariman. Regular Void Cascade participation provides Surge drops naturally over time. Warframe.market provides a purchase option for players who prefer platinum acquisition over farming.

## Builds that use it

Virtuos Surge suits operator builds in missions with regular energy orb availability -- survival missions, mobile defense, and active combat scenarios where enemies drop orbs consistently. Operators in squad play benefit when Harrow or Zenurik-school warframes generate energy orbs through their abilities, providing additional amp energy from the orbs that squadmates produce. Zariman Void Cascade operators who frequently enter operator mode to interact with Void Manifestations use orb pickups between encounters to maintain amp readiness.

Amp builds with large energy pools benefit proportionally more from the 250% bonus at rank 5, as the percentage translates to a larger absolute energy restoration value on high-capacity configurations.

## Notes

The energy bonus applies to the amp energy pool, not the warframe energy pool. Orbs picked up by the warframe during operator mode are credited to the operator for this arcane's trigger. The 10-second duration starts at the moment of pickup. Multiple orb pickups within the 10-second window do not stack energy beyond the listed percentage; each pickup resets the timer. Missions with sparse energy orb drops -- such as some Railjack or Deimos environments -- provide fewer trigger opportunities per minute, reducing average uptime compared to high-orb-density missions.
