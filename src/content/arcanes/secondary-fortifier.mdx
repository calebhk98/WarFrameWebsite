## Overview

Secondary Fortifier grants two simultaneous benefits against Overguard enemies: it deals multiplied Extra Damage to Overguard and converts that damage into Overguard for the player. Every 100 damage dealt to an enemy's Overguard layer generates 1 Overguard for the player, and all Overguard damage from the secondary weapon is multiplied by the rank-dependent factor. The result is a self-sustaining system where attacking Overguard enemies builds the player's own Overguard while also stripping enemy Overguard faster through the damage multiplier.

At rank 5, the secondary weapon deals 8x Extra Damage to enemy Overguard, and every 100 of that damage generates 1 Overguard for the player. Attacking a heavily Overguarded enemy quickly generates substantial player Overguard as a defensive resource.

## Effect

When the secondary weapon damages an enemy's Overguard layer, two effects trigger simultaneously: the damage dealt is multiplied by the rank-dependent factor (at rank 5, x8 Extra Damage), and 1 Overguard is added to the player's pool for every 100 damage dealt to the enemy's Overguard. At rank 0, the secondary deals x3 Extra Damage to enemy Overguard, generating 1 player Overguard per 100 Overguard damage. At rank 5, x8 damage multiplied means more total Overguard damaged per shot, which generates more player Overguard per attack.

Player Overguard functions as an additional buffer beyond shields and health, providing an extra layer that must be depleted before other defensive layers are attacked.

## Acquisition

Secondary Fortifier drops from various Zariman Ten-Zero missions and Void Cascade content at uncommon rarity. The arcane is accessible through standard Zariman progression after completing Angels of the Zariman. Warframe.market provides direct purchase options for targeted acquisition.

## Builds that use it

Secondary Fortifier is specialized for content where Overguard enemies appear in significant numbers. Eximus units in Steel Path, high-level Arbitrations, and late-game Void Cascade waves all feature Overguarded Eximus units. Secondary weapons with high damage output against Overguard -- Overguard is not reduced by armor, making it simpler to calculate damage -- strip Eximus Overguard quickly while simultaneously building the player's own Overguard pool.

High-fire-rate secondary weapons that deal consistent damage against Overguard generate player Overguard quickly through the 1-per-100-damage conversion. A secondary dealing 1000 damage per shot to Overguard generates 10 player Overguard per shot, which can accumulate into a meaningful defensive buffer against subsequent enemy attacks.

## Notes

The Extra Damage multiplier applies specifically to damage dealt against an enemy's Overguard layer -- once Overguard is depleted and the weapon hits shields or health directly, the multiplier no longer applies. The player Overguard generation rate is tied to the raw damage dealt to Overguard (after the multiplier) -- the multiplier also increases the rate of player Overguard generation. Player Overguard generated by Secondary Fortifier does not scale with frame stats or mods; it is a flat 1-per-100-Overguard-damage conversion. The secondary weapon must physically damage the Overguard layer; area-of-effect shots that partially damage Overguard still count for generation calculations.
