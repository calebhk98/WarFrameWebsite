## Overview

Secondary Fortifier boosts allied armor when secondary weapon kills occur. On every secondary weapon kill, nearby teammates receive a temporary armor buff. This arcane enables support-focused secondary builds. Early ranks grant 20% armor; maximum rank grants 100% armor to allies.

## Effects per rank

| Rank | Effect |
|------|--------|
| 0    | Base effect |
| 5    | Maximum effect |

## Acquisition

Secondary Fortifier drops from various Zariman and Void Cascade missions.

## Best on

This arcane works best on builds that exploit its triggering condition frequently.

## Usage

Secondary Fortifier activates automatically when its trigger condition is met during combat.

## Notes

This arcane provides consistent value scaling throughout extended missions.
