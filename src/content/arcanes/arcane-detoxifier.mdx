## Overview

Arcane Detoxifier is an operator-exclusive arcane that grants passive resistance to Toxin status effects. As the arcane ranks up from 0 to 5, the percentage chance to resist incoming Toxin procs scales linearly from 17% to 100%, providing deterministic immunity at maximum rank. This arcane is essential for operators facing Infested units and toxic Corpus proxies that rely on poison-based attacks to disable targets.

The status effect immunity stacks with focus school defenses such as Naramon's evasion and Zenurik's regeneration. Operators deploying Detoxifier experience significant survivability gains during extended void fissure encounters and Zariman skirmish runs where toxin damage dominates enemy arsenals. Early ranks provide moderate protection; max rank eliminates toxin buildup entirely, enabling aggressive void dash repositioning without fear.

## Acquisition

Arcane Detoxifier drops from Zariman Void Cascade skirmish missions at uncommon rarity, with rare-tier drops available in higher-difficulty Void Fissure instances. Players should prioritize late-rotation skirmish completions on the Zariman tileset, particularly those in Void Cascade and void-touched expansion zones. Early rotation completions offer modest drop rates; advancing to higher rotations significantly improves acquisition odds.

## Best on

Operators conducting extended Zariman endless runs or solo void fissure mission chains benefit most from Detoxifier's passive toxin immunity. Defensive focus school builds pairing Detoxifier with Zenurik regeneration create nearly unkillable operators, particularly when combined with void dash invulnerability windows.

## Usage

Arcane Detoxifier automatically triggers whenever the operator would receive a Toxin status effect from enemy attacks. No active ability or timing is required; the arcane functions passively during all combat phases. The operator can sustain Infested-dense missions or Corpus toxin-heavy nodes without relying on curative items or temporary invulnerability.

## Notes

Arcane Detoxifier resists the status effect only; raw toxin damage still applies. The arcane excels against Infested eximus units and Corpus Drones that layer toxin procs repeatedly. Combining this arcane with Detoxification auras or focus passives provides layered immunity against poison mechanics.

## Sources

Arcane Detoxifier availability is restricted to Zariman-related endgame content. Players seeking to fully rank this arcane should commit to extended Void Cascade skirmish farming or void fissure relic opening sessions.
