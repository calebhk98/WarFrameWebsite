## Overview

Magus Cadence is an Operator arcane that enhances mobility by granting sprint speed boosts when using Void Sling, the operator's primary movement and evasion ability. This arcane serves operators focused on fast repositioning and hit-and-run playstyles, particularly in endgame content where rapid mobility can mean the difference between survival and elimination.

## Effect

Upon casting Void Sling, the operator gains a temporary sprint speed multiplier that stacks with other movement mods and abilities. The effect grants +15% sprint speed at rank 0, scaling to +75% at maximum rank 5, with the buff lasting 12 seconds regardless of rank. The cooldown between procs is minimal, allowing frequent reapplication during extended void cascade missions.

## Acquisition

Magus Cadence drops as an uncommon reward from Void Cascade missions on Zariman. It is tied to void fissure mechanics and progression through the Zariman tileset. Players completing higher-difficulty cascades have improved odds of obtaining this arcane.

## Best on

Magus Cadence pairs well with operators that prioritize evasion and battlefield control:

- **Void Sling-heavy builds** — operators who cast Void Sling repeatedly benefit most from the consistent speed buff window.
- **Zariman farming** — the arcane is especially useful while farming the same tileset for relics and other rewards.
- **Hit-and-run eidolon hunts** — operators who reposition frequently during tridolon runs gain significant quality-of-life improvements.

## Notes

The sprint speed bonus applies only to the operator, not the Warframe. Void Sling reactivates the buff timer, potentially keeping uptime close to permanent during active combat. The arcane's modest power level makes it a solid early-game operator enhancement rather than endgame-mandatory.
