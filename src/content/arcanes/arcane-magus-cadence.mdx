## Overview

Magus Cadence is an Operator arcane that enhances mobility by granting sprint speed boosts when using Void Sling, the Operator's primary traversal ability. It is installed on the Operator's Magus arcane slot and benefits any playstyle that uses Void Sling regularly for repositioning rather than purely for combat output. The arcane is designed around the Zariman tileset introduced in the Angels of the Zariman update, where the Void Cascade mission type rewards Magus-type arcanes and encourages active Operator play between objectives.

Operators who use Void Sling frequently during Zariman missions naturally maintain the sprint speed bonus without dedicating attention to proc management. The 12-second duration is long relative to the time between typical Void Sling casts, so uptime is effectively continuous for active Operators. The maximum +75% sprint speed is a meaningful mobility improvement that compounds with Warframe sprint-speed bonuses and Focus School passive upgrades, resulting in noticeably faster traversal across the expansive Zariman interior.

## Effect

Upon casting Void Sling, the Operator gains a temporary sprint speed multiplier that stacks with other movement modifiers. The buff grants +15% sprint speed at rank 0 and scales to +75% at maximum rank 5. Duration is 12 seconds at all ranks. The sprint speed bonus applies to the Operator form only and does not carry over to the piloted Warframe.

Because Void Sling has no internal cooldown, repeated casts refresh the timer continuously. Operators engaged in extended Void Cascade missions who sling between objectives can maintain the sprint bonus without interruption for entire mission runs.

## Acquisition

Magus Cadence drops as an uncommon reward from Void Cascade missions on the Zariman tileset. The Void Cascade mission type is available after completing the Angels of the Zariman quest and reaching the Zariman Ten Zero relay. Completion rewards cycle through rotation tables; Magus Cadence appears in the general uncommon pool across all Zariman Void Cascade rotations.

Players targeting Magus Cadence specifically should run Void Cascade in a full squad to reach higher cascade level thresholds, which correlates with improved reward-table rolls. The arcane can also be traded with other players through standard Warframe trade mechanics.

## Builds that use it

Magus Cadence fits into any Operator build that invests in traversal speed and frequent Void Sling use.

Zariman farming builds benefit most directly because the bonus is active throughout Cascade missions in the same tileset where the arcane drops. Operators who chain Sling casts to reach activation points quickly shorten run times and improve hourly reward yields.

Eidolon hunt builds use Magus Cadence as a traversal improvement between shards. Moving from one Eidolon limb synapse to another quickly matters when the squad is racing the night timer, and the sprint bonus closes ground faster between engagement phases.

Solo endurance builds also benefit when the Operator needs to cover large areas between objectives without a squad providing energy or synergy support.

## Notes

The sprint speed bonus applies only to the Operator, not to the Warframe body left behind during Operator mode. Transferring back to the Warframe does not carry the bonus. The arcane's effectiveness is highest for Operators who spend significant portions of a mission in Operator form rather than using it only for limited-duration ability triggers. At rank 3 and above the bonus is strong enough to justify the arcane slot on any mobility-focused Zariman run.
