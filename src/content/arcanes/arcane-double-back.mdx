## Overview

Arcane Double Back is a warframe arcane that grants damage resistance on mobility actions. The arcane stacks resistance bonuses whenever the player performs dodge rolls, double jumps, or bullet jumps, providing up to 25% damage reduction at max rank while remaining active. The mechanic rewards aggressive movement and encourages staying mobile during combat encounters. Each trigger from a mobility action refreshes the 4-second buff window, allowing stacks to persist indefinitely through continuous evasion plays.

Each stack lasts for 4 seconds and refreshes with each trigger. The arcane incentivizes continuous movement, making it synergistic with warframes that benefit from mobility or have frequent jump mechanics.

## Acquisition

Arcane Double Back drops from void fissure missions in the Zariman tileset. The arcane is obtainable through public matchmaking in Void Fissure nodes, making it relatively accessible compared to reward-gated alternatives. Farming efficiency improves with a team that prioritizes defense or survival objectives to maximize reward extraction chance.

## Best On

This arcane shines on warframes with frequent evasion mechanics or enhanced mobility. Frames like Loki with his invisibility-dodge playstyle, Limbo leveraging rift jumps, or aggressive momentum-based frames like Titania benefit from the consistent stacking potential. The arcane also pairs well with defensive abilities that trigger on jumps, creating synergy with warframes featuring movement-locked mechanics.

Warframes with high shield or health pools gain less proportional value, while those relying on evasion through mobility gain maximum benefit. Trinity and Nova, which reposition constantly, make excellent candidates for equipping this arcane.
