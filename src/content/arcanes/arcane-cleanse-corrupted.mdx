## Overview

Arcane Cleanse Corrupted is a faction-specific rare arcane providing status immunity against Corrupted enemy types. This specialized arcane is valuable for Void Fissure content and Corrupted-enemy-heavy missions.

## Effect

When fighting Corrupted enemies, the Warframe has a 20% chance at rank 0, scaling to 100% at rank 5, to automatically cleanse negative status effects. At maximum rank, all Corrupted procs are permanently prevented.

## Acquisition

Arcane Cleanse Corrupted drops as a rare reward from Zariman Void Cascade waves featuring Corrupted enemies. Void cascade runs with Corrupted spawns provide access.

## Best on

Arcane Cleanse Corrupted is ideal for Void-heavy content:

- **Void Fissure missions** — Corrupted procs are nullified throughout.
- **Zariman Corrupted waves** — maintains full status immunity.
- **Orokin Void Survival** — negates Corrupted status application.

## Notes

Only affects status applied by Corrupted units. Grineer, Corpus, and Infested procs are not affected. Void-specific farming requires Corrupted enemy selection. Stacks with other status-resistance mechanics.
