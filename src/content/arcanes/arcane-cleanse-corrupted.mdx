## Overview

Arcane Cleanse Corrupted automatically removes negative status effects applied by Corrupted enemies, scaling to complete immunity at rank 5. Corrupted units -- Orokin-controlled enemies found in Void Fissure missions, Orokin Vaults, and Zariman content -- apply a range of status effects including radiation, cold, and heat. Radiation proc is particularly disruptive as it causes the warframe to attack allied players and companions, converting a defensive situation into a threat to the team. Cleanse Corrupted eliminates this faction-specific status hazard passively during Corrupted content.

Radiation is uniquely dangerous in squad play because the affected player deals damage to teammates during the proc duration. In high-difficulty Corrupted content like Steel Path Void Survival or Arbitrations with Corrupted enemy rotations, radiation procs from multiple enemies can break squad coordination. Arcane Cleanse Corrupted at rank 5 provides complete immunity to this effect, allowing the player to operate without risk of accidentally harming squadmates during Corrupted-heavy encounters.

## Effect

When the warframe receives a negative status effect from a Corrupted enemy, Cleanse Corrupted checks its cleanse chance and removes the status on success. At rank 0, a 20% chance provides partial relief from Corrupted status application. At rank 5, 100% chance makes the warframe fully immune to all Corrupted-applied status effects, including radiation, cold, heat, and any other proc Corrupted units apply.

The cleanse is passive and activates without player input. At maximum rank, it functions as a permanent status immunity field specifically against Corrupted faction sources.

## Acquisition

Arcane Cleanse Corrupted drops as a rare reward from Zariman Void Cascade waves featuring Corrupted enemies. Zariman Ten-Zero content is accessible after completing Angels of the Zariman. Corrupted-wave Void Cascade runs provide the primary farming path. Warframe.market provides a purchase option for players who prefer direct platinum acquisition over farming specific Corrupted-wave rotations.

## Builds that use it

Arcane Cleanse Corrupted is most valuable for players who regularly farm Void Fissure missions and Orokin Vault content where Corrupted enemies are the primary faction. Squad-play builds benefit from radiation immunity at rank 5, as the cleanse prevents friendly-fire incidents during Corrupted content that would otherwise require the player to actively manage radiation proc duration. Frames running solo Void Survival or Void Defense where Corrupted spawns are continuous benefit from sustained status immunity throughout the entire mission.

## Notes

The cleanse only affects status effects from Corrupted faction enemies. Grineer, Corpus, and Infested procs are not cleansed by this arcane. Corrupted enemies in Zariman Void Cascade and standard Orokin Void missions both count as Corrupted for the arcane's faction check. The arcane provides no benefit in missions where Corrupted enemies do not appear. At rank 5, the complete cleanse eliminates the brief proc window that would apply at lower ranks, providing effectively immediate status removal for Corrupted-source procs.
