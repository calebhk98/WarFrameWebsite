## Overview

Secondary Dexterity rewards headshots with damage scaling on secondary weapons. On every headshot with pistols or secondary weapons, the arcane grants a damage buff stacking up to five times. This arcane synergizes with accurate secondary weapons and critical-focused builds. Early ranks provide 15% damage; maximum rank yields 75% per stack for 375% total bonus.

Secondary Dexterity activates automatically when its trigger condition is met during combat.
