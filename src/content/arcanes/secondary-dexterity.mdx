## Overview

Secondary Dexterity rewards headshots with stacking damage bonuses on secondary weapons, creating a precision-momentum system where each accurate headshot builds toward a higher damage multiplier. The stacking mechanic incentivizes consistent headshot placement over random body shot fire, rewarding player skill with progressively higher damage output. At maximum five stacks, Secondary Dexterity provides one of the highest single-arcane damage bonuses available for secondaries.

The 16-second stack duration is generous compared to many combat arcanes, allowing players to maintain stacks through brief non-combat intervals such as reloading, objective completion, or brief repositioning phases without losing accumulated bonuses.

## Effect

Each headshot from the secondary weapon applies one damage stack. Up to five stacks accumulate with 16-second independent timers. At rank 0, each stack provides 15% damage (maximum 75% at five stacks); at rank 5, each stack provides 75% damage (maximum 375% at five stacks). Five stacks at rank 5 more than quadruple the weapon's base damage output, compounding with existing damage mods to enable extremely high per-shot values.

The 16-second timer per stack means five-stack uptime is achievable and maintainable during sustained headshot-focused combat without requiring every single shot to be a headshot.

## Acquisition

Secondary Dexterity drops from Zariman Ten-Zero missions and Void Cascade content. The arcane is accessible through standard Zariman progression after completing Angels of the Zariman. Regular Void Cascade rotation rewards include Dexterity as part of the precision-secondary arcane pool. Players running precision secondary builds farm Zariman content for Dexterity and complementary headshot arcanes. Warframe.market provides direct purchase options for targeted acquisition.

## Builds that use it

Secondary Dexterity excels on accurate semi-auto or burst-fire secondaries that naturally target head hitboxes. Sniper-secondary crossover builds (Knell Prime, Pyrana Prime) with high crit chance and accuracy maintain five-stack headshot chains during focused combat phases. Builds combining Dexterity with Arcane Bellicose (crit on headshot) create dual headshot reward systems where each accurate shot both builds the damage stack and refreshes the crit buff. Mesa's Shatter Shield ability combined with secondary DPS builds benefits from full five-stack Dexterity during the defensive ability's active window. High-accuracy automatic pistols that fire controlled bursts at head level generate stacks quickly without sacrificing full-auto DPS.

## Notes

Headshot detection requires projectile contact with the designated head hitbox. Explosive weapons and AoE do not count as headshots regardless of origin. The five-stack cap prevents infinite accumulation; after five stacks, headshots only refresh individual stack timers rather than adding new stacks. Each stack's 16-second timer counts independently, so the first stack acquired may expire while the fifth stack is still accumulating. Maintaining headshot-focused fire prevents this by refreshing all stacks before they individually expire. The damage bonus type is the same as the weapon's modded damage type, subject to normal enemy mitigation.
