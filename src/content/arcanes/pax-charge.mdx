## Overview

Pax Charge reduces the Battery Recharge Delay on Kitguns that use a battery-type magazine system. Battery Kitguns - those built with battery-type loaders - draw from a regenerating battery rather than a conventional ammo pool. After the battery is depleted or the player stops firing, a recharge delay period begins before regeneration starts. Pax Charge shortens this delay by a percentage, reducing downtime between firing windows.

Kitguns are modular secondary weapons built through the Ventkid market on Orb Vallis. The loader component determines the magazine type - battery loaders create the regenerating battery mechanic that Pax Charge targets. Kitgun configurations using conventional magazine loaders do not have a Battery Recharge Delay and therefore do not benefit from this arcane.

## Effect

At rank 0, the 12.5% reduction provides a minor decrease in recharge delay - a Kitgun with a 2-second base recharge delay would see approximately 0.25 seconds cut from the delay start. At rank 3, the 50% reduction halves the recharge delay - a 2-second base delay becomes approximately 1 second, significantly reducing the downtime period before battery regeneration begins.

Shorter recharge delay means the battery starts filling sooner after fire ceases or battery depletes, improving sustained damage output on battery Kitguns during extended engagements.

## Acquisition

Pax Charge is obtained through Ventkid-related content on the Orb Vallis, Venus. The Ventkid syndicate provides access to Kitgun components and Pax arcanes through standing rewards and blueprints. The WFCD dataset does not document specific drop tables for this arcane. Community drop tables and the in-game Ventkid market offer current sourcing information. Warframe.market provides direct platinum purchase for players who prefer trading.

## Builds that use it

Pax Charge suits battery Kitgun builds where the Battery Recharge Delay is a meaningful bottleneck in sustained fire performance. Catchmoon and Tombfinger Kitgun configurations built with battery loaders that create natural firing gaps benefit from Pax Charge's reduced delay between bursts. Players who use battery Kitguns as their primary damage source in extended combat scenarios - Disruption runs, Survival, or Steel Path farming - see the most benefit from eliminating delay-based downtime.

The arcane is particularly relevant on battery Kitguns with short battery capacity and high fire rate, where the battery depletes quickly and the recharge delay period represents a significant fraction of the overall fire cycle. A 50% delay reduction at rank 3 meaningfully increases the effective fire availability across a sustained engagement.

## Notes

Pax Charge specifically targets the Battery Recharge Delay - the pause before regeneration begins - not the regeneration rate itself. Faster recharge rate would fill the battery faster once regeneration starts, while Pax Charge makes regeneration start sooner. The combined effect of starting sooner and filling at the standard rate reduces total downtime effectively but does not accelerate the fill speed. Battery Kitgun builds that pair Pax Charge with Primed Fast Hands (affecting the conventional reload analog on applicable configurations) can further minimize downtime. The arcane has no effect on Kitguns built with conventional magazine loaders that use standard ammo pickups.
