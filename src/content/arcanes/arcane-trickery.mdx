## Overview

Arcane Trickery is a legendary warframe arcane that grants conditional invisibility on finisher kills, enabling aggressive melee playstyles to include escape and repositioning tools. The 15% trigger chance ensures reliable proc frequency during sustained melee combat; most finisher-focused builds maintain near-permanent invisibility through overlapping buffs. Unlike ability-gated invisibility (Ash, Ivara, Loki), Trickery scales with melee execution skill rather than energy management, rewarding finisher-spam playstyles with automatic defensive resets.

Trickery's primary advantage lies in decoupling invisibility from ability slots: frames without native stealth abilities gain defensive utility while preserving all four ability slots for damage or utility. The invisibility grants full re-positioning windows during extended encounters, allowing players to reset aggro on dangerous priority targets or retreat from unfavorable positioning. Combined with Covert Lethality and finisher damage modifiers, Trickery creates pseudo-infinite stealth loops in high-difficulty content.

## Effect

Arcane Trickery activates whenever the warframe or its weapons complete a finisher kill on any enemy type. Finishers triggered by Exalted Blade (Excalibur), Ash's Teleport Slash, melee ground finishers on downed enemies, and Covert Lethality executions all trigger Trickery. The 15% base trigger chance means finishers activate invisibility one in seven times on average; sustained finisher chains create overlapping invisible windows that persist indefinitely during active kills.

The invisibility effect prevents enemy targeting and projectile homing, effectively removing the warframe from the threat pool. Invisible frames can move freely without triggering alarms or pulling new enemies. Crouching during invisibility enables stealth damage multipliers from finisher mods and surprise melee initiation. The visibility window persists until the buff expires or the player attacks; attacking while invisible consumes the buff and reveals the frame.

## Acquisition

Arcane Trickery drops primarily from Eidolon Hydrolyst encounters with a 20% drop chance per capture, making it farmable through organized tridolon hunt groups. A single Hydrolyst capture takes 8--15 minutes; farming dedicated hunters can obtain one Trickery every 40--75 minutes. Alternatively, Zariman Void Skirmish rotation C offers a 5.6% drop chance at significantly lower difficulty but longer mission duration. Duviri Endless provides rare legendary drops (0.67%) for extreme-difficulty farm routes.

Once obtained, Trickery ranks in 24 hours per tier to maximum. The arcane's competitive farm sources and moderate drop rates keep trading post prices moderate, reflecting its powerful utility in endgame builds.

## Best on

Arcane Trickery performs exceptionally well on melee-focused warframes with built-in finisher bonuses: Ash's teleport finishers, Excalibur's Exalted Blade finishers, and Xaku with blade-oriented builds all maximize proc frequency. Frames with no native invisibility (Mag, Rhino, Nidus) gain significant defensive value by equipping Trickery, turning melee engagement into stealth-gated gameplay. Tank-focused frames pair Trickery with heavy armor builds to create hybrid durability through both damage absorption and evasion.

Frames with limited defensive abilities (Wisp, Frost without Bubble) benefit most from conditional invisibility as a pseudo-life-save mechanic. Conversely, frames with ability-locked invisibility (Ash, Loki, Ivara) benefit less because ability slots already provide superior control.

## Builds that use it

Finisher-focused builds universally pair Trickery with Covert Lethality and heavy attack damage mods to create instant-kill invisibility loops. Steel Path endless survival builds use Trickery to reset threat periodically during extended waves, preventing overwhelming enemy density. Support frames use Trickery as a repositioning tool to escape dangerous situations while maintaining team utility. Hybrid melee-ability builds leverage Trickery's decoupling from ability slots to enable full ability loadouts without sacrificing defensive tools.

Melee-spam endless mission builds use Trickery to enable perpetual stealth phases, dramatically reducing damage taken during extended content. Raid-focused teams pair Trickery frames with crowd control to ensure maximum uptime on threat resets.

## Notes

Arcane Trickery's 15% trigger chance applies per finisher kill event, not per second; sustained finisher chains create overlapping invisible windows. Attacking while invisible ends the buff prematurely but does not prevent melee damage from applying during the final hit. The invisibility is classified as a status effect and can be dispelled by enemy abilities that strip buffs. Finishers on already-invisible enemies (via Ash's invisibility ability) still trigger Trickery independently. Update 27.3.0 clarified the "On Finisher Kill" terminology to resolve ambiguity around channeled finishers.
