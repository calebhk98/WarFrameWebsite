## Overview

Arcane Healing provides a passive chance to resist Radiation status effects applied to the warframe by enemy attacks or environmental sources. Radiation status in Warframe causes the affected target to be confused -- warframes affected by Radiation will briefly attack allies, which in practical terms means the player's frame will not respond normally to inputs during the confusion window. Resisting Radiation prevents this disruption entirely for the resisted proc.

At rank 5, the 102% resistance chance exceeds the nominal cap, functioning as complete immunity to Radiation status application from external sources. Enemy Radiation procs from Corrupted enemies, Sentients, and certain Orokin content are negated. The arcane provides no healing -- despite its name, Arcane Healing's effect is Radiation resistance per WFCD data.

## Effect per rank

Each incoming Radiation status proc checks against the resistance chance. On success, the proc is negated and does not apply to the warframe. At rank 0, 17% resistance negates roughly one in six Radiation procs -- a modest reduction in how often Radiation disrupts play. At rank 5, 102% resistance is effectively 100% immunity; Radiation procs from enemy sources cannot apply to the warframe.

The resistance functions identically to other status resistance arcanes: it is a passive chance applied per incoming proc, not a stack or buff with a duration.

## Acquisition

Arcane Healing drops from Duviri/Endless missions and Zariman Skirmish rotations, with older sources from Eidolon Hydrolyst captures. In Duviri, the arcane appears as a legendary drop with approximately 0.67% chance in Tiers 1 and 3, and 4% in Tier 9 normal difficulty. Skirmish missions on Neptune and Venus offer more farmable uncommon drops (10.34%) on Rotation C. The Hydrolyst remains a viable but outdated source for players with existing Eidolon farming routines. For consistent acquisition, Duviri Tier 9 offers the best combination of drop rate and availability, though Tier 1 farming with radshare squads can yield results more quickly through repeated runs.

## Builds that use it

Arcane Healing works best on weapon builds that guarantee slash procs through heavy slash weighting or mods like Bloodhound Rounds and Shred. Condition Overload Mirage Prime benefits significantly, triggering healing every time her clones proc slash and scaling the effect with elemental damage. Nidus leverages the passive healing to complement his existing regeneration mechanics, effectively doubling health gain during infection healing windows. Hybrid weapon builds on frames like Excalibur Umbra or Ash Prime gain value from converting their slash weapon investment into survivability without dropping damage mods. The arcane is less valuable on pure ability-focused frames like Saryn that do not directly apply slashes, though her Venom Dose augment does cause slash effects on all enemies.

## Notes

Arcane Healing applies only to slash status procs; impact and puncture status effects do not trigger it. The healing persists through invulnerability periods (Warding Halo, Prism, Null Star) but is not applied while invulnerable. Multiple procs can overlap, with the game engine tracking all active healing instances and summing them each tick. Heavy slash builds with 80%+ slash damage can ensure the arcane activates on almost every kill, though the modest healing values (60 per second maximum) cap the utility at high-level content. The arcane was rebalanced in a 2025 patch to be more consistent; earlier versions sometimes failed to trigger on rapid consecutive procs. Pairing with Rupture mod boosts slash chance further, creating synergies that make this arcane shine on dedicated melee builds.
