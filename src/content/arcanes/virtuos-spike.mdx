## Overview

Virtuos Spike is an operator arcane that converts void damage to puncture damage on operator weapon hits. This damage conversion arcane allows operators to adapt their amp loadout to enemy armor profiles and resistance patterns, providing tactical flexibility in void fissure missions and Zariman void cascade runs. The conversion scales progressively with rank, reaching near-total conversion at maximum rank.

## Effects per rank

| Rank | Void to Puncture Conversion |
|------|---------------------------|
| 0 | 24% |
| 1 | 48% |
| 2 | 72% |
| 3 | 98% |

Virtuos Spike converts incoming void damage directly to puncture damage on hit. At rank 3, the conversion reaches 98%, making this arcane particularly effective against heavily-armored opponents where puncture damage excels. The conversion happens per-hit, allowing operators to exploit enemy armor weaknesses and bypass damage resistance modifiers tied to void damage immunity.

## Acquisition

Virtuos Spike is obtained from Vox Solaris, the primary faction for Fortuna open-world progression. The arcane is guaranteed as a rank reward from the Agent tier within the Vox Solaris standing progression. Advancement through Vox Solaris requires completing Fortuna bounties, defeating Profit-Taker Eidolon, and accumulating standing through commodity trading and syndicate reputation activities.

## Best on

This arcane excels in operator builds designed for armor-heavy enemy encounters, particularly in void fissure disruption and Zariman void cascade farms where Grineer and Corpus units with heavy armor are prevalent. Void damage amps paired with this arcane effectively become puncture-focused weapons, allowing operators to leverage puncture damage multipliers and armor-stripping mechanics. High-damage amp configurations benefit most from the conversion effect.

## Usage notes

The damage conversion occurs on each individual hit, making rapid-fire amps more effective at distributing converted damage across targets. The 98% conversion at maximum rank means the operator's void damage output effectively becomes puncture-focused, requiring consideration of enemy armor types and weaknesses. Unlike damage addition effects, conversion arcanes do not increase overall damage output but redirect it to match enemy vulnerability patterns, making them tactical choices for specific mission types.
