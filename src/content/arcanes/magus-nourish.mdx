## Overview

Magus Nourish empowers Operators with healing capabilities through Transference entry. This arcane transforms operators into emergency squad healers, providing progressive health restoration during dangerous void fissure content and extended survival missions.

## Effects per rank

| Rank | Trigger Chance | Healing Potential |
|------|----------------|-------------------|
| 0    | 10%            | 25% HP            |
| 1    | 15%            | 35% HP            |
| 2    | 20%            | 45% HP            |
| 3    | 25%            | 55% HP            |
| 4    | 27%            | 65% HP            |
| 5    | 30%            | 75% HP            |

Health restoration is percentage-based, scaling with the maximum health of affected allies. This makes the arcane equally effective on heavily armored frames and agile squishier builds during squad engagements.

## Acquisition

Magus Nourish drops primarily from Zariman Void Fissure content and Archon Hunt void-tier mission completions. The lower drop rate from Archon hunts makes Zariman fissure farming the primary source for this operator arcane.

## Best on

Magus Nourish is ideal for Operators dedicated to squad support roles, particularly in endgame void fissure content where squad health management becomes critical. Pairing with void sustain warframes maximizes operator utility during extended zariman farming sessions.

## Sources

Regular Zariman void fissure participation yields this arcane consistently. Players progressing through archon hunts on void tiers naturally accumulate magus nourish copies, making it accessible to endgame operators seeking healing-focused playstyles.
