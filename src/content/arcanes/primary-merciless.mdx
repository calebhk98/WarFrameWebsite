## Overview

Primary Merciless is a weapon arcane for primary guns that scales damage multiplicatively with consecutive kills. Each enemy elimination within the 4-second window grants a stacking damage bonus, up to 12 stacks maximum. At rank 5, this results in 360% cumulative damage (30% per stack), transforming Primary Merciless into a high-performance damage multiplier for sustained combat. The arcane additionally grants 30% reload speed at max rank, accelerating magazine exchanges during kill streaks.

Each kill refreshes the 4-second timer, allowing stacks to persist through continuous engagement. The reload speed bonus at rank 5 synergizes perfectly with high-fire-rate primary weapons, enabling faster magazine cycles during momentum phases. At maximum stacks, total damage reaches 360% bonus (not multiplicative).

## Acquisition

Primary Merciless drops from Zariman void fissure missions as a rare weapon arcane reward. The arcane requires sustained kill streaks to maximize farming efficiency, making high-damage primary builds essential. Public void fissure groups benefit from having ammo-abundant weapons and tight enemy clustering to maintain kill momentum. The arcane's effectiveness scales directly with weapon base damage and attack speed. Players should prioritize weapons with large magazine capacities to extend kill chains without reloading, maintaining momentum across extended engagements.

## Best On

Primary Merciless synergizes exceptionally with high-fire-rate weapons like the Soma Prime, Braton Prime, or Phantasma. Weapons with large magazine capacities amplify the arcane's sustained stacking potential during extended engagements. Chaining kills becomes exponentially rewarding, making crowd-control frames like Saryn or Limbo exceptional farming partners. The reload speed bonus particularly benefits weapons with slow reload times like sniper rifles equipped with ammo conversion setups.
