## Overview

Bonewidow is the Infested-influenced melee Necramech, designed for aggressive frontline engagements and health-sustaining close-quarters combat. Where Voidrig holds ground behind a wall of shields and ranged fire, Bonewidow charges into enemy formations and sustains itself through lifesteal and an active defense shield. Its health pool of 1880 is the highest of any Necramech, and its armor of 480 provides substantial physical damage reduction that amplifies that effective health significantly.

Introduced alongside Voidrig with the Heart of Deimos update in August 2020, Bonewidow was designed to give players a mech that felt aggressive rather than defensive. The combination of Meathook for lifesteal, Shield Maiden for active blocking, and Exalted Ironbride for high burst melee damage creates a combat loop that rewards pilots who engage enemies directly rather than maintaining distance. Veterans of Warframe's melee-focused Warframes (Excalibur, Wukong, Atlas) tend to gravitate toward Bonewidow because its rhythm mirrors that of an exalted weapon build.

Bonewidow is the correct choice for Isolation Vault content when the pilot wants to melee through vault encounters quickly. Its Meathook ability sustains health mid-combat, reducing the downtime spent waiting for shields to recharge that Voidrig pilots experience. For open-world content where enemies spread across large areas, Voidrig's ranged focus becomes more valuable, but inside the close confines of underground Deimos vaults, Bonewidow excels.

## Abilities

**Meathook (1)** is Bonewidow's sustain engine. On first activation, Bonewidow drives a hook into a nearby enemy and holds them suspended while siphoning their health. The stolen health replenishes Bonewidow's own health pool, providing a meaningful mid-fight recovery tool. On second activation, the hooked enemy is hurled like a projectile, dealing impact damage to both the victim and any enemies they collide with during flight. Against clustered enemy groups the collision damage adds up quickly, and the hurl can knock down multiple units simultaneously. Meathook's lifesteal amount scales with Necramech Intensify, making ability strength mods directly useful for survivability.

**Shield Maiden (2)** activates a large forward-facing physical shield that intercepts all incoming projectile and melee damage from the blocked arc. While the shield is raised, reflected damage returns to attackers at a percentage of absorbed values, punishing enemies that focus fire on Bonewidow's front. The secondary mechanic is significant: melee attacks performed while Shield Maiden is active receive a damage bonus, rewarding pilots who maintain the shield through engagements rather than dropping it to swing freely. The playstyle is similar to Warframe's Rhino Iron Skin crossed with Nidus's melee aggression -- raise the shield, move into position, deliver empowered Ironbride hits, and sustain with Meathook when health erodes.

**Firing Line (3)** is the crowd-control ability in Bonewidow's kit. The mech sweeps a force beam across the surrounding area, dragging nearby enemies toward a central point and suspending them in place for a brief window. The mechanical purpose is setup: gathering a scattered enemy group into a tight cluster where Exalted Ironbride swings hit multiple targets simultaneously, or where a single Gravemines cast from a nearby Voidrig in co-op play destroys the entire cluster. Firing Line's gathering radius is wide enough that it catches enemies from most angles, making it useful even against fast-moving Deimos enemies.

**Exalted Ironbride (4)** is the signature ability and the reason Bonewidow is classified as a melee mech. Activating Exalted Ironbride summons an enormous exalted greatsword that replaces Bonewidow's arch-gun weapon slot for the duration. The Ironbride deals very high base melee damage and has cleave range that hits multiple enemies per swing. The damage type is primarily slash, which shreds health on enemies with no armor or resistances. Exalted Ironbride scales with both melee mods applied to the ability's own mod loadout and Necramech ability strength -- making it one of the game's clearest examples of stacking two scaling systems on a single damage source. Pilots who build for Ironbride specifically will socket Necramech Intensify and then run a separate mod configuration on the Ironbride itself.

Bonewidow's health and armor combination provides excellent effective health against physical damage types. The relatively low shield pool of 430 means Bonewidow does not benefit as much from shield-focused mods, and pilots should instead prioritize direct health mods (Necramech Vitality) and the passive recovery from Meathook. In practice, Bonewidow's survival is active -- pilots who use Meathook regularly to steal health maintain their health pool far better than pilots who ignore it and rely on passive regeneration.

## Acquisition

Bonewidow shares the same acquisition pathway as Voidrig: completing Heart of Deimos unlocks Father at the Necralisk, who sells blueprint pieces and accepts Otak Tokens. The Neuroptics, Chassis, and Engine blueprints each drop from Isolation Vault bounties at different vault tiers.

Players new to Necramech farming should complete Tier 1 Isolation Vault runs first, as the introductory tier drops the components for both mechs and can be completed solo before tackling the more demanding Tier 2 and Tier 3 vaults. Each vault tier requires activating a specific number of Necraloid caches before the final boss encounter. Bringing a Warframe with strong crowd control (Limbo stasis, Nova slow, Frost snow globe) makes vault clearing significantly faster for newer players.

The complete Bonewidow requires 35,000 credits and 72 hours of build time per component. Rushing individual components costs platinum. The main blueprint purchase from Father requires sufficient Entrati standing, which accumulates through Deimos bounties and vault completions.

## Intrinsics Progression

Necramech Intrinsics are earned by piloting any Necramech. The five trees (Gunnery, Piloting, Engineering, Tactical, Command) each cap at rank 10 and apply universally to both Voidrig and Bonewidow.

**Gunnery** improves arch-gun performance and unlocks aim-glide targeting at higher ranks. For Bonewidow, Gunnery is a lower priority than for Voidrig because the primary damage source is Ironbride rather than the arch-gun, but higher Gunnery still improves backup weapons between Ironbride casts.

**Piloting** increases movement speed and dodge distance. Piloting is more valuable for Bonewidow than for Voidrig because Bonewidow's melee combat requires constant repositioning. Higher Piloting ranks let the mech close gaps quickly and withdraw to safety after Meathook casts.

**Engineering** reduces ability energy costs across all abilities. Given that Bonewidow's entire offensive output revolves around activating and maintaining Exalted Ironbride, Engineering is extremely valuable -- it lets pilots sustain Ironbride longer before needing to deactivate and recover energy.

**Tactical** increases ability damage and radius. For Bonewidow, Tactical rank directly multiplies Ironbride's damage output and increases Firing Line's gather radius, making Tactical one of the highest-priority trees for an Ironbride-focused build.

**Command** governs autonomous behavior when the mech is not piloted. Low Command means the Necramech will stand idle after the Tenno dismounts; higher Command enables autonomous attack and patrol behaviors.

## Recommended Mods

Bonewidow uses the same Necramech mod pool as Voidrig. The key difference in build philosophy is that Bonewidow heavily favors Necramech Intensify and direct health mods over shield mods.

**Offensive:** Necramech Intensify (+Ability Strength) scales both Ironbride damage and Meathook lifesteal. This is the highest-priority mod for a dedicated Ironbride build.

**Survivability:** Necramech Vitality (+Health) boosts the already-large health pool. Necramech Deflection has lower priority here than on Voidrig because Bonewidow's shield pool is modest. Instead, run Necramech Steel (if available) for direct armor improvement.

**Duration:** Necramech Continuity (+Ability Duration) extends Shield Maiden's active window and Exalted Ironbride's summon duration, directly increasing sustained DPS output.

**Range:** Necramech Stretch (+Ability Range) increases Firing Line's gather radius and Meathook's targeting range, improving setup reliability before Ironbride swings.

Exalted Ironbride has its own separate mod slots that accept standard melee mods. Filling Ironbride with high-damage melee mods (Pressure Point, Primed Fever Strike, Corrupt Charge) provides a second layer of damage scaling on top of Necramech Intensify.

## Lore

Bonewidow's design draws on Infested biological engineering crossed with Orokin precision manufacturing. The name references the Deimos Infested ecosystem, where the Cambion Drift is populated by creatures with exposed skeletal structures and bio-mechanical growths -- the Bonewidow's visual design echoes those organic shapes in a weaponized context.

The Entrati built the Necramechs as autonomous war machines that could also be piloted by Operators during combat emergencies. Bonewidow represents the aggressive doctrine: a weapon of mass melee destruction designed to neutralize Sentient units that had adapted to ranged fire by simply closing distance and overpowering them physically. The Ironbride's exalted weapon mechanic references the Orokin's tendency to design weapons that augment biological capability far beyond natural limits.

The Bonewidow Necramechs encountered as enemies during Isolation Vault bounties are legacy units running without a pilot, their control systems corrupted by centuries of Infested exposure on Deimos. Acquiring and piloting a personal Bonewidow unit is the Tenno's way of reclaiming that legacy for the Heart of Deimos' reconstruction effort.
