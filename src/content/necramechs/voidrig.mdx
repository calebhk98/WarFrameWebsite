## Overview

Voidrig is the Grineer-derived heavy Necramech, a towering war machine originally engineered by the Entrati during the Old War to combat Sentient forces. Unlike Bonewidow's melee focus, Voidrig is built around sustained ranged firepower and defensive shielding. Its high shield pool of 850 makes it the premier choice for sustained gunfighting inside Isolation Vaults and Deimos open-world content. When pilots need to hold a position under concentrated fire while pouring rounds into armored targets, Voidrig is the correct tool.

The Necramech system was introduced with the Heart of Deimos update in August 2020, adding an entirely new pilotable mech type to Warframe. Voidrig and its companion mech Bonewidow represent the two distinct playstyle philosophies of that system -- one built for heavy weapons and defensive endurance, the other for aggressive melee and mobility. Voidrig's signature weapon, the Arquebex, fires high-velocity void projectiles in Guard Mode, making it one of the highest-damage stationary platforms in the game.

Pilots summon the mech using a Necramech Summon gear item, available after completing Heart of Deimos. Once summoned, the Tenno pilots the Voidrig directly, with their Warframe abilities replaced by the mech's four-ability kit until they dismount. The mech levels independently and has its own mod slots distinct from the pilot's Warframe.

## Abilities

**Necraweb (1)** is a ranged crowd control ability that hurls a canister of graviton fluid. On impact it spreads a wide mire over the target area, dragging down enemy movement speed significantly. Enemies caught in the field are slowed enough that even fast Grineer units become easy targets for the mech's weapons. The slow duration is long enough that a single cast clears a lane for several seconds of free shooting. It is most useful in open-ground fights where multiple enemies are approaching from a single vector.

**Storm Shroud (2)** wraps the Voidrig in a crackling electrical field that provides two simultaneous benefits: it reduces incoming damage to the mech and discharges electrical damage onto enemies that close to melee range. The damage reduction is meaningful in high-level Isolation Vault content where enemy attacks can erode even the large Voidrig health pool quickly. The electric discharge punishes melee-range enemies, which is especially useful inside the cramped vault corridors where Necramechs are most commonly deployed. Duration mods applied through Necramech Continuity extend the window significantly.

**Gravemines (3)** scatters a radial pattern of proximity mines around the mech. Each mine detonates individually when an enemy triggers it or after a timer expires. The combined damage from a full Gravemines cast is substantial, and the wide placement arc means incoming enemies from multiple angles will trigger detonations simultaneously. The ability pairs naturally with Necraweb: slow the enemy advance first, then lay mines into the path while enemies are still in the slow field. The mines linger long enough that they remain dangerous after Necraweb expires.

**Guard Mode (4)** is Voidrig's defining ability and the reason it dominates heavy combat scenarios. The mech locks into a stationary stance and equips the Arquebex, a dedicated Guard Mode weapon. During Guard Mode, Voidrig's structural integrity is substantially increased, and the Arquebex fires void projectiles that deal very high damage per shot. The trade-off is complete loss of mobility: the mech cannot move or dodge during Guard Mode. Skilled pilots activate Guard Mode behind cover, use the Arquebex to delete priority targets, then deactivate and reposition. The Arquebex's damage scales with Necramech Intensify, making ability strength mods a direct multiplier on Guard Mode output.

## Acquisition

Voidrig becomes accessible after completing the Heart of Deimos quest, which unlocks the Necralisk settlement on Deimos and introduces the Entrati syndicate. The main blueprint and three component blueprints (Neuroptics, Chassis, Engine) are obtained from Father, the Entrati vendor who runs the Isolation Vault bounty system.

Component blueprints drop from Isolation Vault bounties at different tiers. The main blueprint is purchased from Father's shop after reaching a certain Entrati standing rank. Once all four blueprints are assembled, Voidrig requires 35,000 credits and 72 hours of build time per component before the final assembly can begin in the Foundry.

Isolation Vault runs are the most efficient way to farm the component blueprints. Each vault tier (Loid Tier 1, Tier 2, Tier 3) drops different components, and squad coordination significantly speeds up vault clearing. Players who complete the Isolation Vault bonus objective also receive Otak Tokens, which contribute toward Entrati standing.

## Intrinsics Progression

Necramech Intrinsics are a separate rank system that govrades pilot proficiency inside all Necramechs (both Voidrig and Bonewidow). There are five Intrinsic trees, each capping at rank 10. Intrinsics are earned by piloting a Necramech and scoring kills or completing objectives while mounted.

**Gunnery** improves weapons fire-rate, reload speed, and at higher ranks unlocks aim-glide auto-targeting. For Voidrig, Gunnery directly boosts the effectiveness of all arch-guns and the Arquebex during Guard Mode.

**Piloting** increases movement speed, sprint speed, and adds dodge distance. The mobility gains are modest on Voidrig compared to Bonewidow, but higher Piloting still helps with repositioning between Guard Mode activations.

**Engineering** reduces ability energy costs and improves ability regeneration. Since all four Voidrig abilities consume energy, Engineering sustains longer ability uptime in extended Isolation Vault fights.

**Tactical** adds passive benefits including increased ability damage multipliers and expanded ability range. Tactical synergizes with Guard Mode -- high Tactical Intrinsics increase Arquebex damage further.

**Command** governs the Necramech's AI behavior when not piloted by the Tenno, including autonomous targeting and patrol behavior. Command is the least combat-critical tree for active pilots but affects idle behavior between summons.

## Recommended Mods

Necramech mods are a distinct category from Warframe or Companion mods and drop exclusively from Necramech enemies in Isolation Vaults or from Loid in the Necraloid syndicate shop.

**Survivability:** Necramech Vitality (+Health), Necramech Deflection (+Shield Capacity), and Necramech Redirection (+Shield Recharge Rate) form the defensive core. Voidrig's shield-dependent sustain makes Deflection especially valuable.

**Offense:** Necramech Intensify (+Ability Strength) scales Guard Mode Arquebex damage directly. Necramech Stretch (+Ability Range) increases Necraweb slow radius and Storm Shroud discharge radius.

**Duration:** Necramech Continuity extends Storm Shroud uptime significantly, keeping the damage reduction and electric discharge active for longer windows.

**Efficiency:** Necramech Efficiency reduces ability energy costs, enabling more frequent Necraweb and Storm Shroud activations without depleting the energy pool.

The ideal configuration prioritizes Intensify and Vitality in the eight standard slots, with Continuity and Efficiency filling the remaining space depending on content difficulty.

## Lore

Voidrig's design reflects Entrati engineering philosophy during the Old War: overwhelming force delivered through layered defenses. The Entrati were a Martian noble family who served the Orokin as researchers and operators of the Void junction. When the Sentient invasion reached Deimos, their Necramechs became the last line of ground-based defense for the underground Cambion Drift.

The Voidrig's name references the Void itself -- the extradimensional energy source that powers Tenno abilities and that the Sentients feared and could not easily counter. Guard Mode's Arquebex fires concentrated Void energy, paying tribute to that design lineage. The graviton canisters in Necraweb draw on Entrati understanding of mass-manipulation fields, technology they also applied to their architectural work beneath Deimos.

The Necramechs were eventually buried along with the rest of the Entrati's Deimos complex when the Infested took over the moon. Their reactivation by the Tenno during Heart of Deimos represents a recovery of Old War technology, overseen by what remains of the Entrati family.
