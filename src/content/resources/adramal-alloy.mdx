## Overview

Adramal Alloy is a crafted metal compound produced from Adramalium ore mined in the Cambion Drift on Deimos. The refining process purges Infested spores and contaminants from the raw ore, yielding a highly versatile alloy used in Necramech components, K-Drive parts, and kitgun crafting. Otak, the resource trader in the Necralisk, sells the reusable blueprint for 1,000 Entrati standing (available at Rank 0 - Neutral, requiring no syndicate progression beyond initial contact).

Adramal Alloy is a Deimos-exclusive material. It has no connection to Zariman or Void content. Players progressing through the Heart of Deimos update encounter it as a foundational crafting ingredient for Necramech construction.

## Where it drops

Adramal Alloy is not found as a drop. It is a Foundry-crafted resource. Each batch of 20 requires 1,000 Credits, 20 Adramalium, 20 Travoride, 600 Plastids, and 15 Lucent Teroglombes, with a 1-minute build time.

Adramalium -- the primary input -- is obtained by mining yellow mineral veins (yellow mining lesions) on the Cambion Drift on Deimos. Adramalium also drops from Rolizor and Spitia Infested Cysts found across the Cambion Drift. Travoride is similarly mined from Cambion Drift deposits. Lucent Teroglombes drop from Cambion Drift wildlife and resource nodes.

## Used for

Adramal Alloy appears in 12 different blueprints, with total requirements across all blueprints reaching 605 units. The largest consumers are Necramech components: the Bonewidow Engine requires 120 units and the Voidrig Casing requires 60 units. K-Drive parts and kitgun components require moderate quantities. Players constructing a full Necramech loadout should farm enough Adramalium for several hundred Adramal Alloy batches before initiating the build queue.

## Tips

Use the Sunpoint Plasma Drill or an upgraded mining cutter for higher yield per yellow vein. Activate the Cambion Drift mining overlays on the minimap by equipping the Thief's Wit or Loot Detector mod to spot vein clusters efficiently. Focus mining runs on the rocky cave systems in the eastern Cambion Drift for yellow vein density. Plastids (needed for each batch) drop from Deimos missions directly, reducing the need to farm them elsewhere.

## Notes

Adramal Alloy cannot be traded between players. WFCD confirms `tradable: false`. The Adramalium ore component is also non-tradeable (`tradable: false`). Both materials are exclusive to Deimos. Adramal Alloy has no relationship to Zariman void zones -- it is a purely Deimos-based crafting resource.
