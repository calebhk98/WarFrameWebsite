## Overview

Curse Of Hearing is a mystical void-touched resource representing residual sensory corruption inflicted by Zariman's void exposure. This resource enables operator cosmetic customization with thematic void-corruption aesthetics. The item embodies Warframe's lore where the Void grants and curses simultaneously, creating duality in power acquisition that defines operator progression through Zariman challenges.

## Where it drops

Curse Of Hearing drops exclusively from Void Cascade missions in Zariman, appearing in enemy loot tables and mission completion rewards. Void-touched enemies carry higher concentrations of curse-type resources; targeting priority enemy types accelerates collection. Mission difficulty scaling affects drop rates; harder cascades produce proportionally greater rewards.

## Used for

Players utilize Curse Of Hearing to obtain operator cosmetic customizations reflecting void-touched aesthetics. These cosmetics provide visual distinction while conferring no mechanical advantages. Trading this resource with syndicate vendors unlocks specialized operator appearance modifications unique to Zariman progression paths.

## Tips

Farm Curse Of Hearing during void fissure events when Zariman becomes rotation-featured mission selection. Coordinate group farming to divide void-touched enemy attention and maximize efficiency. Accumulate multiple curse variants simultaneously; balanced collection across all curse types enables flexible cosmetic builds.
