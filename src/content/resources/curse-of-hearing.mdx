## Overview

Curse Of Hearing is the auditory-perception component of the three-curse series from the Albrecht's Laboratories content on Deimos, working alongside Curse Of Seeing and Curse Of Knowing. WFCD describes it as containing "knowledge that will help Fibonacci prevent enemies from breaking free of The Sanctum," with instruction to "Feed curses to the Jahu Gargoyle in your Clan Dojo to re-calibrate its defensive capabilities." The "Hearing" aspect represents the acoustic surveillance layer of Fibonacci's monitoring system -- the ability to detect enemies through sound rather than sight or intellectual pattern recognition.

The three curses together (Seeing, Knowing, Hearing) constitute a complete multi-sensory awareness framework for the Sanctum's containment systems, reflecting the Entrati Lab's narrative theme of paranoid vigilance against void-entity escape.

## Where it drops

Curse Of Hearing drops from Albrecht's Laboratories bounties on Deimos, alongside the other two curse types. The three curses share the same bounty rotation pools, so a single farming session produces a mixed assortment of all three. Enemy loot within the Laboratories tileset during active mission play also contributes hearing curses to the farming pool. Completing the full bounty chain at each difficulty tier maximizes access to all rotation slots where curses appear.

## Used for

Curse Of Hearing serves two documented purposes. First, it contributes to Fibonacci's auditory surveillance of the Sanctum within the Entrati Lab narrative, enhancing the containment system's ability to detect escaping entities through sound. Second, and more tangibly, curses are fed to the Jahu Gargoyle in the Clan Dojo as offering items that maintain its defensive calibration. All three curse types must be regularly supplied to the Gargoyle for it to remain fully calibrated. Beyond the Gargoyle mechanic, curses also contribute to Albrecht's Laboratories standing progression.

## Tips

- All three curses drop from the same Albrecht's Laboratories bounties; there is no need to farm one curse type separately from the others, as they accumulate together naturally.
- Run the full bounty chain at the highest tier your loadout can handle efficiently; higher-tier bounties offer better curse yields across all three types per Rotation C slot.
- Clan members can pool curses collectively to keep the Jahu Gargoyle maintained; coordinate through the Clan chat to determine which curse type is currently needed most.
- The Entrati Lab's highest-tier bounties (Level 115-120) provide the best curse accumulation rate per time invested.

## Notes

Curse Of Hearing is not tradeable and must be personally earned through Deimos activity. The auditory metaphor in the curse name connects to the Entrati Lab's broader surveillance narrative -- Fibonacci is "listening" for the sounds of containment breaches within the Sanctum. Players participating in the Albrecht's Laboratories content accumulate all three curse types as a natural byproduct of bounty farming, with the Jahu Gargoyle providing a meaningful destination for the accumulated inventory.
