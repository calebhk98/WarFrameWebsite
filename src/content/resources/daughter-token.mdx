## Overview

Daughter Token is the personal currency of Daughter, the naturalist member of the Entrati family in the Necralisk on Deimos. WFCD describes it as "A token of Daughter's gratitude," earned through the Entrati family token exchange system. Daughter's specialization in the natural ecosystem of the Cambion Drift -- particularly fishing and Conservation animal interaction -- shapes her vendor inventory, which includes fishing lures for Deimos's Servofish, Infested bird Conservation tools, and Deimos-exclusive blueprint components. Players interested in Deimos fishing or Conservation must accumulate Daughter Tokens to purchase the necessary equipment from her.

Like other Entrati family members, Daughter issues her own token type. The family cross-trading system allows players to convert surplus tokens from other vendors into Daughter's currency during direct visits to her in the Necralisk.

## Where it drops

Daughter Tokens are obtained by visiting Daughter in the Necralisk and trading excess Entrati tokens (from Father, Mother, Son, Grandmother, or others) for her own. The conversion happens through a direct menu interaction. Additionally, Cambion Drift bounties reward tokens from all Entrati family members across their rotation pools -- bounty completion produces a mix of token types that players can then redistribute to the appropriate vendor. Higher-tier bounties yield more tokens per completion across all family types.

## Used for

Daughter Tokens advance standing with the Entrati family through Daughter's specific contribution track. Her vendor specialization means tokens spent with her unlock Deimos fishing lures (required for catching Servofish), Conservation tools for Deimos's Infested avian species, and blueprints for fish-part-derived crafting materials. Players who want the full range of Deimos activities -- particularly fishing for Servofish oils needed in Deimos equipment recipes -- must prioritize Daughter Token accumulation to purchase the appropriate lures.

## Tips

- Run Cambion Drift bounties across all difficulty tiers to generate a broad mix of Entrati tokens, then visit each family member to convert excess tokens into their specific currency.
- Daughter's fishing lures are essential for Deimos Servofish -- acquire them early if you plan to pursue Deimos fishing as part of your progression strategy.
- Conservation tools from Daughter enable Deimos bird Conservation, which awards Entrati standing through a different pathway from bounties.
- Balance token distribution across all five Entrati family members (Father, Mother, Daughter, Son, Grandmother) to avoid maxing out one standing track while neglecting others.

## Notes

Daughter Tokens are not tradeable between players and must be earned through Deimos participation or Entrati token exchange. The five Entrati family members each issue distinct tokens; spending the wrong token type with the wrong vendor wastes potential standing. Confirming Daughter's current vendor inventory before farming her tokens ensures the farming effort aligns with specific gear needs.
