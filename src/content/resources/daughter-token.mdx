## Overview

Daughter Token represents specialist currency issued by Daughter through the Entrati syndicate on Cambion Drift. These tokens facilitate access to specialized Necralisk modifications, weapon blueprints, and exclusive equipment available solely through Daughter's vendor offerings. Token acquisition through bounty completion establishes standard farming methodology for players pursuing comprehensive Necralisk arsenal development.

## Where it drops

Cambion Drift bounties at all difficulty tiers yield Daughter Tokens upon completion. Higher-tier bounties produce proportionally greater token quantities; committed farmers prioritize elite-difficulty bounty sequences. Void fissure missions throughout Cambion Drift similarly yield tokens through completion rewards and enemy loot tables, creating flexible farming options.

## Used for

Daughter's syndicate vendor requires substantial token expenditures for accessing restricted equipment blueprints and specialized modifications. Necralisk component acquisition depends on consistent token supply for sustainable equipment progression. Certain warframe augment mods and specialist weapons locked behind Daughter's offerings drive continuous token farming demand.

## Tips

Rotate between Cambion Drift bounty tiers to maintain psychological engagement during extended farming sessions. Pair bounty farming with void fissure relic progression for dual-objective advancement. Accumulate tokens during favorable event periods featuring bounty reward multipliers or enhanced syndicate standing.

## Notes

Entrati syndicate family hierarchy places Daughter as specialist supplier distinguishing her from Mother and Father offerings. Token economics remain stable due to consistent late-game demand for Necralisk-exclusive equipment. Players committed to complete Necralisk arsenal development establish long-term token farming routines supporting sustained equipment acquisition.
