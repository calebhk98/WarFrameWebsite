Cortichrome is a crafting resource introduced with the Jade Shadows update, tied to combat with the Juggernaut Behemoth on Deimos. WFCD identifies its uniqueName as `/Lotus/Types/Gameplay/JadeShadows/Resources/JuggernautBehemothItem`, directly connecting it to the Juggernaut Behemoth enemy type. The Juggernaut Behemoth is the elite variant of the standard Juggernaut Infested enemy found on Deimos. It represents a significant combat challenge compared to regular Deimos enemies, making Cortichrome a reward for sustained engagement with high-level Cambion Drift content.

## Where it drops

Cortichrome drops from the Juggernaut Behemoth at a 100% chance per kill, according to WFCD drop data. The Juggernaut Behemoth spawns in the Cambion Drift during specific mission conditions or as a rare open-world encounter. Unlike resources with distributed enemy drops, Cortichrome is exclusively tied to this single enemy type, making Juggernaut Behemoth hunting the only reliable farming route. The guaranteed drop rate (100%) means every successful Behemoth kill produces Cortichrome, but encounter frequency depends on spawn conditions within the Cambion Drift.
