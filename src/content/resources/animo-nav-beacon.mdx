## Overview

Animo Nav Beacon is a specialized component resource obtained exclusively from Ambulas assassination missions on Jupiter. The beacon's function is tied directly to the Corpus faction's robotic navigation systems, particularly the Ambulas production facility. This resource is essential for players seeking to craft Nidus warframe and related equipment blueprints, making it a key farming target during early-to-mid progression.

## Where it drops

The sole reliable source of Animo Nav Beacon is the Ambulas assassination mission on Jupiter. Players must defeat the Ambulas boss in order to receive the beacon as part of mission rewards. The drop is guaranteed when Ambulas is defeated with squad completion bonuses applied. Solo players will receive a smaller drop than full four-player squads, making coordinated farming more time-efficient.

## Best locations

Jupiter Ambulas missions are the only confirmed source of Animo Nav Beacon, so all farming must be directed to this specific boss encounter. Optimal efficiency comes from running the mission repeatedly in succession to minimize load times between attempts. Farming with three squadmates ensures maximum reward drops per run. The mission can be completed in approximately 3-5 minutes per attempt on normal difficulty settings.

## Used for

Animo Nav Beacon is a required component in Nidus warframe's blueprint, with typical quantities ranging from 5 to 15 units per craft depending on the specific component being built. The beacon also appears in some post-Nidus blueprints, making it valuable to stock beyond single-craft requirements. Mid-game players typically need multiple runs to accumulate sufficient quantities.

## Tips

Farm Animo Nav Beacon in public squads to benefit from completed mission bonuses and increased drop rates. Bring a damage frame to complete Ambulas encounters quickly and efficiently. Banking extra beacons allows for rapid re-crafting if equipment is damaged or lost in combat. The mission's difficulty scales reasonably, so even moderate-level players can participate with difficulty adaptations.
