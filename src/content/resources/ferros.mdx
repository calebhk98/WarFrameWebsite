## Overview

Ferros represents processed iron alloy components harvested from Grineer military infrastructure and combat units. These metallic structures form foundational components within Grineer weapon construction and heavy armor modification systems. Ferros acquisition through Grineer engagement supplies essential materials for players pursuing Grineer-based equipment arsenal development.

## Where it drops

Grineer faction enemies throughout all star chart regions reliably drop Ferros during combat engagement. Heavily-armored Grineer units including Bombards and Heavy Gunners prioritize Ferros drops. Grineer fortress-themed missions yield concentrated quantities compared to standard planetary missions.

## Used for

Grineer weapon blueprint construction critically depends on Ferros availability as primary metallic component. Advanced heavy armor modification systems require sustained Ferros supply. Certain warframe augments featuring armor enhancement mechanics similarly depend on Ferros availability.

## Tips

Run Grineer fortress-themed missions for concentrated Ferros acquisition. Bring armor-penetration loadouts maximizing heavily-armored Grineer enemy elimination efficiency. Accumulate reserves during favorable farming periods supporting future equipment construction.

Metallic processing reflects Grineer industrial engineering philosophy emphasizing durability and raw manufacturing capability. Ferros stability ensures indefinite storage viability. Players pursuing comprehensive Grineer equipment arsenal establish consistent Ferros farming traditions.
