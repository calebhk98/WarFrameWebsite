## Overview

Ferros is a rare mining resource from the Plains of Eidolon on Earth. WFCD describes it as "a resilient metal that is hardened by impurities within," establishing it as an Eidolon-region ore whose unusual mineral composition -- with impurities strengthening rather than weakening the metal -- gives it distinctive physical properties useful in crafting. The Red Mining Veins on the Plains of Eidolon are the primary source, and Tusk Thumper enemies also drop Ferros as rare loot when destroyed.

Ferros belongs to the Plains of Eidolon mining resource family alongside Coprun, Alapon, and Sentirum. The mining resource system requires a Nosam Cutter or Sunpoint Plasma Drill to extract ore from mining nodes on the plains.

## Where it drops

Ferros comes from two sources on the Plains of Eidolon: Red Mining Veins and Tusk Thumper-class enemies. Drop rates from WFCD:

| Source | Drop Chance |
|---|---|
| Tusk Thumper | 16.68% |
| Tusk Thumper Bull | 25.02% |
| Tusk Thumper Doma | 33.36% |
| Narmer Thumper | 16.68% |
| Narmer Thumper Bull | 25.02% |
| Narmer Thumper Doma (highest) | 33.36% |

Red Mining Veins on the Plains of Eidolon are the primary source for consistent Ferros farming. Scan Mining Veins with the Codex Scanner to identify Red Vein deposits (as opposed to Blue Veins, which yield gem-type resources). The Sunpoint Plasma Drill gives better ore yields than the Nosam Cutter due to improved precision hitting.

Tusk Thumpers are large Grineer walker machines that patrol the Plains of Eidolon; Doma-tier Thumpers offer the highest Ferros drop rate at 33.36% per kill.

## Used for

Ferros is processed into refined components through blueprints sold by Old Man Suumbaat at the Cetus market. The refined form is used in weapon blueprints and equipment requiring the resilient metal's hardened properties. Players pursuing full Plains of Eidolon crafting content -- including Zaw melee weapons and Amp components from the Ostron -- will encounter Ferros as a recurring material.

## Tips

- Equip the Sunpoint Plasma Drill for Plains of Eidolon mining; its improved precision circle hitting gives better ore yield from Red Mining Veins compared to the Nosam Cutter.
- Hunt Tusk Thumper Doma-tier walkers on the Plains during daytime; they offer 33.36% Ferros chance and also drop Coprun, Iradite, and other Plains resources.
- Use the Terrain Visibility overlay in the Plains to locate Red Mining Veins more easily; veins appear as glowing ore deposits on rock faces.
- Mining and Thumper-hunting can be combined in the same Plains session for efficient Ferros accumulation alongside other Eidolon resources.

## Notes

Ferros is specific to Plains of Eidolon and cannot be mined on Orb Vallis (which has its own ore set) or Cambion Drift (which has mining lesions). Players who have not accessed Cetus and the Plains of Eidolon cannot farm Ferros. The "impurities" that harden Ferros in the lore reflect the unique mineral environment of the Plains of Eidolon, shaped by centuries of Sentient and Eidolon activity near the lake.
