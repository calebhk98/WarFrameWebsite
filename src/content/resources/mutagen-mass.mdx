## Overview

Mutagen Mass is a specialized resource required primarily for Infested-themed warframe blueprints (Nidus, Grendel, Xaku) and Necramech construction. It is obtained from Void Fissure rotation B/C and Deimos-based void cascade missions. Mutagen Mass farming is less common than resources like Orokin Cells but becomes critical when players pursue Infested warframe acquisition or endgame Necramech content.

## Where it drops

Mutagen Mass appears in Void Fissure endless missions (rotation B and C pools) and Deimos Void Cascade missions. The primary farm is Void Fissure Survival on any tileset, where rotation B begins at 5 minutes and rotation C at 10 minutes. Deimos void cascade is a secondary option for players who specifically want to avoid standard void fissure rotations.

| Location | Mission type | Availability | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Void Fissures | Survival (B/C) | 5-10 minutes | Best option; consistent |
| Void Fissures | Defense (B/C) | Wave 6+ | Time-intensive |
| Deimos Void Cascade | Any variant | Immediate | Lower drop chance |
| Orphix Venom alerts | Limited-time | Event-only | Rare but guaranteed |

## Best locations

Void Fissure Survival at 10 minutes (rotation C) provides the highest Mutagen Mass drop chance. A single 10-minute run typically yields one Mutagen Mass. For players not ready for void fissures, Deimos void cascade missions work but have lower drop rates, requiring 20-30 minutes per Mutagen Mass.

## Drop rates

In Void Fissure rotation C, Mutagen Mass has an approximate 25-30% drop chance. In Deimos void cascade, the rate is approximately 5-8%, making it significantly less efficient. Orphix Venom alerts guarantee Mutagen Mass but are time-limited events that occur only during specific seasonal updates.

## Tips

Farm Void Fissure Survival up to the 10-minute mark (rotation C) for optimal Mutagen Mass drop rate.

Do not farm Mutagen Mass before you need it; focus on easier resources (e.g., Tellurium, Neural Sensors) during early progression, and pursue Mutagen Mass only when planning to build Infested warframes.

Combine Mutagen Mass farming with Argon Crystal grinding since both come from the same void fissure rotations.

## Used in

Mutagen Mass is required for Nidus, Nidus Prime, Grendel, and Xaku blueprints, each requiring 1-3 units. Necramech blueprint trees also include Mutagen Mass in their material costs. Certain augment mods for Infested-themed warframes require Mutagen Mass as well.

## Farming strategies

Players pursuing Nidus/Grendel should plan for 3-5 Mutagen Mass (depends on prime vs. base frame and relics). Running 5-6 Void Fissure Survival missions to the 10-minute mark yields approximately 5 Mutagen Mass, sufficient for one frame blueprint. Total farming time: approximately 60-70 minutes.

For players without void fissure access, Deimos void cascade requires 6-8 runs per Mutagen Mass, totaling 2-3 hours for a single frame blueprint. Avoid this farming path if possible.

## Notes

Mutagen Mass demand is heavily concentrated around specific warframe releases (Infested frames). Most players farm it on-demand rather than maintaining a large stockpile. Veterans with stockpiles typically hold 5-10 units.
