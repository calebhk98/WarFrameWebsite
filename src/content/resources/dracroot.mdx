## Overview

Dracroot is a plant resource native to Duviri, the spiral realm of Dominus Thrax. WFCD describes it as "a staple of the Wyrmling's diet," establishing it as the primary food source for Duviri's Wyrm creatures -- the dragon-like entities that inhabit the spiral realm. The Wyrm and Wyrmling imagery in Duviri connects to the dragon motifs woven throughout Thrax's emotional landscape. Dracroot as a Wyrmling food source positions it as part of the ecosystem sustaining Duviri's fauna, suggesting the plant's chemical composition or energy content is particularly suited to the metabolic needs of Void-touched creatures like the Wyrmlings.

Dracroot is one of three primary Duviri plant resources alongside Eevani and Connla Sprout, each with distinct lore connections to Duviri's inhabitants and environment.

## Where it drops

Dracroot appears in Duviri Endless mode drop tables and the Duviri Circuit. Drop rates sourced from WFCD:

| Source | Drop Chance |
|---|---|
| Duviri/Endless: Tier 1 (Normal) | 0.67% |
| Duviri/Endless: Tier 3 (Normal) | 0.67% |
| Duviri/Endless: Tier 1 (Hard) | 2.58% |
| Duviri/Endless: Tier 3 (Hard) | 2.58% |
| Duviri/Endless: Tier 4 (Hard) | 2.58% |
| Duviri Circuit | 0.25% |

The Normal mode drop rate (0.67%) is very low -- fewer than 1 in 100 reward opportunities -- making Hard mode Duviri Endless (2.58%) significantly more efficient for farming Dracroot. The Duviri Circuit rate (0.25%) is the rarest source. Players farming Dracroot should prioritize Hard mode Duviri Endless Tier 1, 3, or 4 for the best drop rates.

## Used for

Dracroot is used in Duviri crafting recipes and can be traded with Acrithis in Duviri for Duviri-exclusive items. Players pursuing Incarnon weapon genesis adapters and other Duviri-exclusive gear from Acrithis's vendor will encounter Dracroot as one of the plant resources Acrithis accepts or requires. Duviri crafting blueprints that incorporate plant materials from the spiral realm use Dracroot alongside Eevani and Connla Sprout.

## Tips

- Run Hard mode Duviri Endless specifically when farming Dracroot; the 2.58% rate in Hard mode is nearly four times higher than Normal mode's 0.67%, making the difficulty investment worthwhile.
- Hard mode Duviri Endless Tier 1 provides the same 2.58% rate as Tier 3 and Tier 4 -- earlier tiers are faster to reach, so farming Tier 1 Hard mode is efficient for pure Dracroot farming.
- Combine Dracroot farming with Eevani and Connla Sprout farming in the same Duviri Endless sessions, since all three Duviri plants share similar drop tables in the same tiers.
- Build a loadout effective in Hard mode Duviri; Steel Path access and high-power Warframe and weapon configurations are recommended for Hard mode Duviri survival.

## Notes

Dracroot's very low Normal mode drop rate (0.67%) means players should not expect consistent Dracroot accumulation from casual Duviri sessions at Normal difficulty. The resource is realistically accessible only through dedicated Hard mode farming. Because Duviri Endless Tier rewards are structured in cycles, reaching Tier 1, 3, or 4 requires completing earlier tiers first -- plan sessions to reach the targeted tier consistently rather than exiting early.
