## Overview

Atmo Systems is a modular component resource primarily obtained from Venus mission rewards and specialized excavation activities. This system modulates environmental pressure compensation for advanced equipment, particularly Archwing and Railjack subsystems. The resource is moderately rare and requires dedicated farming to accumulate sufficient quantities for complex blueprint requirements.

## Where it drops

Venus excavation missions serve as the primary farming location for Atmo Systems, with significant drops from enemy loot tables and container rewards. The resource occasionally appears in Void Cascade event rewards when Venus-based events are active. Standard mobile defense and survival missions on Venus offer secondary sources but at reduced rates compared to excavation-focused nodes.

## Best locations

Venus excavation missions provide optimal Atmo Systems yields per time investment, especially when running longer mission sessions with higher container spawn density. Pairing excavation runs with public squad sessions increases drop rates through mission completion bonuses. Venus Void Cascade events temporarily boost Atmo Systems availability and should be prioritized when they occur.

## Used for

Atmo Systems appears in Archwing and Railjack component blueprints with quantities typically ranging from 8 to 20 units per item. Players pursuing space combat content or Railjack progression will encounter Atmo Systems as a critical bottleneck resource. Banking extra systems allows flexibility in crafting timelines and build experimentation.
