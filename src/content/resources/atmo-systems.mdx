## Overview

Atmo Systems is a component resource obtained from Profit-Taker Orb Heist missions on Venus. According to WFCD, the description reads: "A modular component that compensates for the multitude of atmospheric conditions encountered in the Origin System. Obtained by completing Heists on the Orb Vallis (Venus)." The resource drops from completing all four phases of the Profit-Taker Orb Heist on the Orb Vallis and is also available as a direct reward from the Vox Solaris syndicate agent.

Atmo Systems are required primarily for Hildryn component blueprints and for Railjack-related crafting. Hildryn is the Warframe that powers abilities with Shields rather than Energy, making her a distinctive defensive frame unlocked through Orb Vallis content. Since Atmo Systems come from the same content area where Hildryn components drop, farming both together is efficient.

The Profit-Taker Orb is one of the two large Orb bosses on Venus, alongside the Exploiter Orb. Both require completing Fortuna and Vox Solaris reputation prerequisites before the Heist sequence unlocks.

## Where it drops

Atmo Systems drop from Profit-Taker Orb Heist missions at Rotation C. WFCD records four drop sources: Phases 1, 2, 3 (Level 40-60) each have a 10% Uncommon drop of 5X Atmo Systems at Rotation C, and Phase 4 (Level 50-60) has an 11.43% Uncommon drop at Rotation C. The Vox Solaris Agent also awards Atmo Systems as a standing trade reward.

Rotation C in Heist missions requires completing the full phase bonus objectives. Completing the Heist across all four phases in sequence maximizes Atmo Systems acquisition per session, as each phase contributes independently to Rotation C reward pools.

## Used for

- Hildryn chassis, neuroptics, and systems blueprints
- Railjack house components and avionics systems
- Corpus-themed atmospheric equipment in Archwing crafting trees
- Progressing Vox Solaris crafting gates requiring Orb Vallis materials

## Tips

Run all four Profit-Taker Heist phases in order; each phase awards its own Rotation C reward independently. A full squad of four players allows faster phase completion and scales boss health proportionally but does not reduce per-player rewards. A resource booster doubles Atmo Systems from all phase rewards.

Vox Solaris standing is required to unlock the Heist sequence. Complete Fortuna bounties and sell fish to Little Duck to build standing. The Profit-Taker Heist unlocks after reaching Vox Solaris rank "Instrument." Plan Heist farming after unlocking all four phases, as phase unlocking requires sequential completion.

## Notes

- Atmo Systems are non-tradeable between players.
- All four Heist phases contribute independently; running only Phase 4 is not optimal for Atmo Systems farming.
- The Vox Solaris Agent in Fortuna sells Atmo Systems directly for standing, which supplements heist drops during early farming.
- WFCD lists this drop as "5X Atmo Systems" per Rotation C reward, meaning each reward grants five units.
- Atmo Systems do not drop from standard Venus star chart missions; only Profit-Taker Heist and Vox Solaris standing are confirmed sources.
