## Overview

Damaged Necramech Pod is a structural containment component for Necramech construction, described by WFCD as "A modular component for a Necramech. It's damaged and in need of repair," sourced from "Enemy Necramechs found in Isolation Vault Bounties on the Cambion Drift (Deimos), and purchased from Loid in the Necralisk after completing Isolation Vaults." The pod serves as a modular housing unit within the Necramech chassis -- a structural shell that contains and protects internal Necramech systems. Its modular nature reflects the Orokin design philosophy of building large mechanical constructs from replaceable sub-components.

Father in the Necralisk sells Damaged Necramech Pods at neutral standing, making this one of the more accessible Necramech components through direct purchase.

## Where it drops

Damaged Necramech Pods drop from enemy Necramechs encountered in Isolation Vault Bounties on the Cambion Drift. WFCD's drop table shows Father's vendor at the Entrati neutral standing tier as a 100% availability source -- meaning Father reliably stocks pods for direct purchase using Entrati tokens. Loid in the Necralisk also sells Damaged Necramech Pods after players unlock his shop through Isolation Vault completion. The combination of Father's vendor and Loid's shop provides two reliable non-RNG acquisition routes alongside the Isolation Vault enemy drops.

## Used for

Damaged Necramech Pods are required for both the Bonewidow and Voidrig Necramech blueprints. The pod is a structural housing component, meaning every Necramech build needs it regardless of which type is being constructed. Multiple pods may be required depending on blueprint specifications. Father's guaranteed stock makes pod acquisition more straightforward than other Necramech components that depend entirely on Isolation Vault drop chance.

## Tips

- Check Father's vendor first before committing to Isolation Vault farming for pods; if he stocks them at neutral standing (which WFCD drop data indicates he does at 100% availability), purchasing directly with Entrati tokens may be the simplest approach.
- Once Loid's shop is unlocked, buying pods from Loid provides a standing-cost alternative to Father's tokens, giving two vendor options for pod acquisition.
- If running Isolation Vaults for other Necramech components anyway, enemy Necramech drops will also yield pods as a natural byproduct, eliminating the need to farm pods separately.
- Bring a full squad to Isolation Vaults for faster objective completion; the vault requires multiple simultaneous interactions that benefit from coordinated team play.

## Notes

The Damaged Necramech Pod is distinct from the Damaged Necramech Weapon Pod (used for Necramech weapon crafting). The frame pod belongs to the chassis construction, while the weapon pod is part of the weapon system. Confirm which pod variant a specific blueprint requires before purchasing or farming to avoid accumulating the wrong component type.
