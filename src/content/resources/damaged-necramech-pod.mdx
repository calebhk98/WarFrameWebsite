## Overview

Damaged Necramech Pod components represent operator containment and control systems extracted from void-corrupted Orokin mechanized suits. These specialized pods enable operator-to-Necramech transference, forming essential functional bridges between player consciousness and mechanized vessel. Pod acquisition represents mandatory requirement for any player pursuing viable Necramech operation, making consistent farming essential for endgame progression.

## Where it drops

Necramech pods drop exclusively from Necramech-type enemy units encountered throughout Deimos and void fissure missions. Environmental destruction of mechanized containment structures yields additional pod fragments; thorough exploration identifies hidden pod caches within void-corrupted chamber networks. Difficulty scaling amplifies drop rates; elite mission difficulties produce proportionally enhanced pod quantities.

## Used for

Necramech frame construction depends critically on pod availability; each frame requires functional pod for operator transference mechanics. Specialized operator modification systems incorporate pods for void-corruption resistance upgrades. Secondary applications span experimental warframe augments and advanced void-focused equipment requiring specialized containment systems.

## Tips

Farm Deimos void fissure missions exclusively for maximum efficiency; triple-objective farming combining pod collection with relic progression and void creature elimination optimizes time utilization. Bring full-squad coordination to maintain enemy spawn rates across extended farming sessions. Accumulate pod reserves during favorable rotation periods.

## Notes

Pod functionality transcends cosmetic condition; advanced fabrication processes restore full operational status regardless of damage extent. Orokin design ensures pods maintain consistent value across extended timeframes; long-term resource hoarding remains strategically sound. Players completing Necramech collections signal extraordinary endgame achievement within broader player communities.
