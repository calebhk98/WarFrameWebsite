## Overview

Void-Rig Capsules are sealed compartments containing highly volatile void-energy compounds. These advanced components derive from the corrupted void machinery encountered in the Zariman Ten-Zero and serve as foundation materials for Necramech void augmentation and specialized Void-Rig equipment. The capsules remain stable only through containment fields that dissipate upon harvesting. These materials represent a significant component of the Zariman crafting ecosystem. Understanding their properties and acquisition methods is essential for successful progression through advanced void content. The materials maintain their void-energy saturation indefinitely when stored properly.

## Where it drops

Void-Rig Capsules drop exclusively from disruption mission completions and elite void enemies within the Zariman. These specialized components appear less frequently than common voidplume materials, requiring targeted farming of higher-difficulty Void Cascade variants. Special containers in restricted Zariman areas yield capsules reliably. Dedicated farming routes through void rifts optimize crest collection efficiency. Enemy escalation levels directly correlate with improved drop chances. Container-specific farming provides reliable secondary income streams.
