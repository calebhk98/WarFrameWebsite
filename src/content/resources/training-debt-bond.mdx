## Overview

Training Debt-Bond is a currency issued by the Corpus Taxmen against Solaris workers to cover the cost of vocational and specialist training. These bonds accrue substantial interest and represent the debt burden carried by members of Solaris United, the labor syndicate in Fortuna on Venus. The bonds serve a practical function for players: converting them to Solaris United standing through Ticker, or using them to rank up within the syndicate and gild companions.

Training Debt-Bond is the lowest tier of Debt-Bond available in the Fortuna economy. Ticker, a resident of Fortuna, buys and sells Debt-Bonds in exchange for credits and common resources, providing an alternative to bounty farming. Players who complete Orb Vallis bounties at the lowest tier (level 5-15) earn Training Debt-Bonds as rotation rewards alongside other Fortuna currency.

## Where it drops

Training Debt-Bonds drop from the Level 5-15 Orb Vallis Bounty offered by Eudico in Fortuna on Venus. Rotation A and B rewards award 2x Training Debt-Bond at approximately 19-20% chance. Rotation C awards a single bond at approximately 19-46% chance depending on bounty stage. Ticker in Fortuna also sells Training Debt-Bonds directly in exchange for credits and common star chart resources such as Morphics and Plastids, providing a reliable alternative when bounty rewards are unfavorable.

Training Debt-Bonds do not drop from enemy kills, containers, or standard mission completion outside of bounty rotation rewards. The only acquisition paths are Orb Vallis bounties and direct purchase from Ticker.

## Used for

Training Debt-Bonds convert to 100 Solaris United standing each when turned in to Ticker. Early Solaris United ranks require 2 bonds per rank for the first three ranks, making a small reserve sufficient for initial syndicate progression. Beyond syndicate ranking, 10 Training Debt-Bonds are required to gild a MOA or Hound companion, unlocking the companion's stat bonuses and allowing further customization. Ticker's shop occasionally lists other reward exchanges during special events such as Star Days.

## Tips

Run the lowest-tier Orb Vallis bounty (Level 5-15) repeatedly for efficient Training Debt-Bond accumulation. Each completion cycles through three rotation stages, each potentially rewarding bonds. Ticker's offerings reset daily; check Ticker's shop each login as direct purchases can supplement bounty gaps without additional farming time. For gilding companions, stockpile 10 bonds before initiating the gilding process in the Foundry.

## Notes

Training Debt-Bond cannot be traded between players through the in-game trading post. WFCD confirms `tradable: false`. Ticker's credit-and-resource exchange system is the primary alternative to bounty farming; it is not player-to-player trading. Higher-tier Debt-Bonds (Medical, Shelter, Familial, Advances) require higher-level Orb Vallis bounties and are used for higher Solaris United ranks.
