## Overview

Calda Toroid is a rare component resource found in the Orb Vallis on Venus. Toroids in Warframe are energy-storing devices, and the Calda Toroid specifically draws its name from the Calda Thermal Lure system used by Corpus forces in the Orb Vallis. It is one of four toroid variants (alongside Crisma, Sola, and Vega) tied to specific enemy groups and locations on Orb Vallis.

Calda Toroids are required for constructing Hildryn Warframe components and for other Venus-related crafting recipes. The rarity of toroids reflects their role as progression gates for content associated with Orb Vallis and its factions.

## Where it drops

Calda Toroids drop from Coildrive and Coolant Raknoid enemies near the Temple of Profit area of Orb Vallis. The Exploiter Orb boss encounter also rewards toroids upon completion. Unlike common resources, toroids do not drop in standard mission rotations; they require killing specific enemy types in dedicated Orb Vallis patrol zones.

Spawnrates for Coildrives and Coolant Raknoids near Temple of Profit are higher when the Corpus alert level in the area is elevated. Maintaining alert status by engaging enemies without completing the bounty extends enemy spawns.

## Used for

- Garuda Warframe component blueprints (chassis, neuroptics, systems)
- Hildryn Warframe component blueprints
- Baruuk Warframe component blueprints
- Additional Corpus-themed weapon and gear blueprints requiring Orb Vallis materials

## Tips

Raise the alert level near Temple of Profit by engaging Corpus patrols to spawn Coildrives and Coolant Raknoids continuously. Nekros with Desecrate doubles effective drop rates per enemy killed. Bring a Warframe with strong crowd clearing capability for sustained farming. A resource booster doubles yields from all enemy drops for the duration.

## Notes

- Calda Toroids are non-tradeable between players.
- The Temple of Profit area is accessible without completing Vox Solaris standing prerequisites.
- All four Orb Vallis toroid types (Calda, Crisma, Sola, Vega) are required across different crafting recipes; plan farming routes to collect all four during extended Orb Vallis sessions.
- Coolant Raknoids also drop near the Enrichment Labs area as a secondary farming location.
