## Overview

Carbides are resilient reinforcement materials used to strengthen the plated armor sheets fitted to Grineer Ships, as described by WFCD. This industrial application reflects the Grineer's engineering doctrine of layering heavy metallic armor over their vessels and installations. Carbide composites are known for their extreme hardness and heat resistance in real-world materials science -- properties that translate into Warframe's lore as armor reinforcement for the massive warships fielded by the Grineer faction.

Carbides belong to the Railjack material tier, meaning they drop from Eximus-tier enemies in the Grineer Shipyard tileset and from Railjack mission loot across the Earth, Saturn, and Veil Proxima Railjack nodes.

## Where it drops

Carbides drop from Eximus units within the Grineer Shipyard tileset during standard ground missions. Eximus enemies are powered-up variants with an aura effect and an extra health threshold; they appear throughout the Shipyard but are not guaranteed spawns in every mission. The Railjack node network across Earth Proxima, Saturn Proxima, and Veil Proxima also yields Carbides from enemy fighters, crewships, and mission cargo. Running extended Grineer Shipyard survival or defense missions increases Eximus spawn frequency as mission time increases, improving Carbide yield from ground sources.

## Used for

Carbides are Railjack crafting materials used in subsystem and component blueprints unlocked through the Railjack's Avionics and intrinsic progression systems. The armor-reinforcement context of Carbides makes them particularly relevant to defensive Railjack component blueprints (shield and armor systems). Players building toward optimal Railjack configuration will encounter Carbides alongside Pustrels, Cubic Diodes, and Fresnels as the Railjack material tier.

## Tips

- Grineer Shipyard Survival missions are the best ground source for Carbides through Eximus spawns; Eximus enemy density scales with mission duration, so longer runs yield more Carbides per session.
- Railjack missions in Earth Proxima are accessible earlier than Saturn or Veil Proxima and provide Carbides from the outset of Railjack play.
- Assign crew members with high Engineering stats to maximize loot collection from destroyed Railjack enemy fighters and crewships during space combat.
- Run Railjack Earth Proxima nodes when other Proxima resources are not specifically needed; Earth Proxima missions are shorter and allow faster cycling for Carbide accumulation.

## Notes

Carbides are a Railjack material and do not appear in open-world resource pools or standard planetary drop tables outside the Grineer Shipyard tileset. Players who have not progressed through Rising Tide and built a Railjack cannot access Railjack-sourced Carbides. The Grineer Shipyard Eximus route provides access for players in the early-to-mid Railjack progression stage when the highest-tier Proxima nodes are not yet available.
