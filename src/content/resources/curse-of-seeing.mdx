## Overview

Curse Of Seeing is the visual-perception component of the three-part curse series from the Albrecht's Laboratories content on Deimos, alongside Curse Of Knowing and Curse Of Hearing. WFCD describes it as containing "knowledge that will help Fibonacci prevent enemies from breaking free of The Sanctum," with the instruction to "Feed curses to the Jahu Gargoyle in your Clan Dojo to re-calibrate its defensive capabilities." The "Seeing" aspect represents Fibonacci's need for enhanced visual surveillance within the Sanctum -- a containment system within Albrecht's Laboratories designed to prevent dangerous entities from escaping.

The curse series reflects the Entrati Lab's narrative theme of surveillance, awareness, and the paranoid vigilance required to maintain the Sanctum's integrity against void-touched threats.

## Where it drops

Curse Of Seeing drops from Albrecht's Laboratories bounty missions on Deimos. It appears in the rotation reward pools of the various Laboratories bounty tiers, from the lower-difficulty entry bounties to the high-end Level 115-120 chain. Enemy loot within the Laboratories tileset may also yield curses during active mission play. The three curse types share the same drop sources, meaning a single farming session produces a mix of all three curse variants rather than exclusively one type.

## Used for

Curse Of Seeing has two primary applications. Within the Entrati Lab storyline, it enhances Fibonacci's ability to visually monitor the Sanctum, contributing to the ongoing containment narrative. Mechanically, curses are fed to the Jahu Gargoyle in the Clan Dojo -- a decorative structure with interactive mechanics that accepts curses as offerings to maintain its defensive functions. Players in Clans who want a fully calibrated Jahu Gargoyle need all three curse types. For individual players, the curses also serve as Albrecht's Laboratories standing currency.

## Tips

- All three curses (Seeing, Knowing, Hearing) drop from the same Albrecht's Laboratories bounties; farm them simultaneously rather than targeting one type specifically.
- Completing the full bounty chain each session maximizes rotation reward access, where curses primarily appear.
- Coordinate with Clanmates to pool curses for the Jahu Gargoyle offering, as the Gargoyle benefits from collective contributions rather than individual hoarding.
- Higher-tier Laboratories bounties at Level 95-100 and 115-120 offer improved curse drop rates across all three types compared to entry-level bounties.

## Notes

The Jahu Gargoyle mechanic in the Clan Dojo uses curses as a shared resource. Players in solo play or without Clan access still receive curses as Albrecht's Laboratories progression items but cannot contribute to the Gargoyle mechanism. All three curse types are non-tradeable and must be earned personally through Deimos participation. The Seeing, Knowing, and Hearing curses collectively represent a full spectrum of awareness required for Sanctum security within the Entrati Lab lore.
