## Overview

Void Gel Orbs are rare crystalline structures formed by the interaction of Void energy with biological and mechanical matter. Primarily discovered in the Zariman Ten-Zero, these orbs serve as essential materials for Void Cascade operations and void-related crafting recipes. The glossy, gelatinous exterior masks a complex internal structure that responds strongly to void disturbances, making them invaluable for advanced void technology. Each Void Gel Orb contains concentrated void-energy essence that has been naturally occurring within the Zariman's corrupted zones.

## Where it drops

Void Gel Orbs drop primarily from special containers and enemies encountered during Void Cascade fissure missions on the Zariman Ten-Zero. The chance increases during higher-tier fissure runs, particularly in rotation B and C of endless mission types. Players can identify void-touched containers by their distinctive ethereal glow. Special elite enemies dropped within void rifts have higher chances of yielding orbs. Mission difficulty directly correlates with drop frequency and quantity, making harder void cascades preferable for dedicated farmers.

## Best locations

Zariman Void Cascade missions, especially Void Cascade Cascadia and harder void fissure activities, offer the highest density of Void Gel Orbs per mission. Running these missions with a resource-gathering warframe such as Hydroid or Nekros significantly improves farming efficiency. Team-based runs generate additional bonuses through completion multipliers. Endless void missions provide continuous spawn mechanics that guarantee consistent orb generation as escalation increases.

## Used for

Void Gel Orbs feed into Zariman progression systems and relics designed specifically for Void Cascade content. They also contribute to void-energy-based crafting blueprints in the void arsenal. Several void-attuned weapons and equipment pieces require Void Gel Orbs during their construction phase. Advanced void-aligned warframe augments also demand orb supplies for specialized crafting recipes.

## Tips

Farm Void Cascades during void fissure events for double resource boosters and increased enemy spawns. Bring a team with strong crowd-control abilities to farm efficiently, as void-touched enemies can become overwhelming at higher difficulties. Using a farming-optimized loadout speeds up clear times, directly improving orb yields per hour. Void-energy-resistant warframes handle prolonged void exposure better during extended farming sessions.

## Notes

Void Gel Orbs are relatively recent additions to the game, and their drop rates scale well with mission difficulty. They are not tradeable on warframe.market, making farming the primary acquisition method. Most endgame players accumulate significant orb supplies while pursuing other void-cascade objectives.
