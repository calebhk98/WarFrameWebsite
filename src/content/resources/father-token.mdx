## Overview

Father Token is the syndicate currency issued by Father, the eldest son of the Entrati family in the Necralisk on Deimos. WFCD describes it as "a symbol of Father's trust and confidence," positioning it as a personal authentication token representing Father's endorsement of the player's work within the Entrati family. Father is the Necralisk vendor specializing in Necramech-related equipment -- including Bonewidow and Voidrig component blueprints, the Cortege Necramech weapon, and the Morgha Necramech weapon. His token is the currency required to purchase his most valuable offerings.

Father Tokens are part of the Entrati family token economy on Deimos. The five Entrati family members each issue their own token: Grandmother, Mother, Father, Daughter, and Son. Players accumulate these tokens by converting resources and other tokens between family members, then spending them on desired blueprints or converting them for standing.

## Where it drops

Father Tokens are obtained directly from Father in the Necralisk. WFCD confirms they are "obtained from Father in the Necralisk on Deimos." Father does not drop tokens from enemy kills; instead, players bring him specific resources and other Entrati tokens in exchange for Father Tokens. Father's exchange table includes materials from Cambion Drift mining, Necramech combat, and cross-token trades from other Entrati family members.

The token exchange system means Father Tokens require farming the input materials Father requests -- typically Cambion Drift gems (Embolos, Thyst, Purified Oolite), Infested resources, and Mutagen Samples -- rather than farming a specific mission node directly.

## Used for

Father Tokens are spent with Father to purchase Necramech-related blueprints and components. His inventory includes:

- Bonewidow Necramech component blueprints (Capsule, Casing, Engine, Weapon Pod)
- Voidrig Necramech component blueprints (Capsule, Casing, Engine, Weapon Pod)
- Cortege Necramech weapon blueprint and components
- Morgha Necramech weapon blueprint and components

Players building either Necramech type or their associated Necramech weapons require Father Tokens as the primary purchasing currency. Father Tokens are also convertible to Entrati family standing through Grandmother's token-exchange service in the Necralisk.

## Tips

- Check Father's daily exchange table in the Necralisk to see which resources he currently accepts for token conversions; his offerings rotate.
- Bring Cambion Drift mining resources (especially gems from Blue Mining Lesions like Embolos and Thyst) as these frequently appear in Father's exchange list.
- Convert excess tokens from other Entrati family members (Daughter, Son) to Father Tokens through Grandmother's conversion system when those tokens are not immediately useful.
- Plan Necramech component purchases before committing tokens; each Necramech type requires four components and all must be purchased before assembly can begin.

## Notes

Father's role as the Necramech specialist within the Entrati family token economy makes his token a priority purchase target for players pursuing either Necramech. Unlike most resource farming where enemies drop materials, Father Tokens are entirely vendor-gated -- the farming activity is accumulating the input materials Father accepts, not farming missions for the tokens directly. New Deimos players should identify Father's current exchange materials and prioritize collecting them on their first Cambion Drift farming sessions.
