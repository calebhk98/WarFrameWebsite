## Overview

Father Token represents specialized currency issued by Father through the Entrati syndicate on Cambion Drift. These tokens facilitate access to Father's exclusive equipment offerings and family-patriarchal focused customizations. Token acquisition through bounty completion establishes standard farming methodology for players pursuing Father-specific Necralisk equipment development.

## Where it drops

Cambion Drift bounties at all difficulty tiers yield Father Tokens upon completion. Higher-tier bounties produce proportionally greater token quantities. Void fissure missions throughout Cambion Drift similarly yield tokens through completion rewards.

## Used for

Father's syndicate vendor requires substantial token expenditures for accessing specialized Necralisk equipment blueprints. Certain father-specific warframe augments require sustained token supply. Advanced family-patriarch equipment construction depends on token availability.

## Tips

Balance token farming across Entrati family vendors (Mother, Daughter, Father) ensuring comprehensive access. Combine bounty farming with void fissure relic progression for dual-objective advancement. Accumulate tokens during favorable event periods.

## Notes

Entrati family patriarch heritage positions Father as provider of mature technology and wisdom-based equipment. Token economics remain stable supporting indefinite farming viability. Players pursuing comprehensive Entrati family equipment collections establish multi-vendor farming traditions.
