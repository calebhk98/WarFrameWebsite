These potent cores contain sophisticated adaptive-learning algorithms and void-response subroutines. Exceptional-grade cores represent optimal harvest results from sentient elimination and enable construction of specialized sentient-slayer equipment. These materials represent a significant component of the Zariman crafting ecosystem.
