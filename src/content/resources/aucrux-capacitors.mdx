## Overview

Aucrux Capacitors are delicate power systems that fuse permanently with a Corpus Obelisk's engines upon installation, as described by WFCD. The Corpus Obelisks are the massive automated weapon platforms deployed by the Corpus in their Proxima regions for Railjack defense. The capacitors regulate and supply power to these enormous gun platforms, and once installed, the fusing process makes them non-recoverable -- which is why "unused capacitors are therefore, highly prized." Players recover unused capacitors from destroyed Corpus systems before installation completes, making each Aucrux Capacitor a salvaged component from the Corpus Proxima war economy.

Aucrux Capacitors belong exclusively to the Railjack material tier, obtainable only through Railjack missions in the Corpus Proxima regions.

## Where it drops

Aucrux Capacitors drop from Railjack missions across Venus Proxima, Neptune Proxima, and Pluto Proxima. Enemy fighters, crewships, and mission cargo within Corpus Proxima nodes yield Aucrux Capacitors as Railjack loot. Extended Railjack missions that clear more enemy formations per run produce better capacitor yields. Corpus Obelisk destruction during Railjack missions is specifically thematic for this material; destroying Obelisk platforms may yield capacitors as part of the mission salvage.

## Used for

Aucrux Capacitors are Railjack crafting materials for Corpus Proxima-tier component blueprints. The power-regulation function described by WFCD makes them relevant to Railjack avionics dealing with energy management, shield systems, and power distribution. Players building toward advanced Railjack configurations for Corpus Proxima missions will encounter Aucrux Capacitors in the same tier as Cubic Diodes and Fresnels as core Corpus Proxima materials.

## Tips

- Venus Proxima Railjack missions are the most accessible source; begin farming Aucrux Capacitors from the earliest available Corpus Proxima nodes.
- Neptune Proxima missions have higher enemy levels and denser formations; more enemies per mission means more capacitor drops per run compared to Venus Proxima's early-tier missions.
- Assign crew members with the highest Combat intrinsics to maximize loot from destroyed enemies and crewships; crew accuracy in combat influences how many enemy ships are destroyed and thus how many drops are generated.
- Aucrux Capacitors do not drop from ground missions; Railjack access through Rising Tide is a hard prerequisite.

## Notes

Aucrux Capacitors are Railjack-exclusive materials unavailable through any ground mission, open world, or standard planetary node. The Corpus Obelisk narrative context (capacitors fused permanently on installation) reflects the Corpus engineering economy of purpose-built, non-recoverable components. Players who have not completed Rising Tide and assembled a Railjack cannot access any Proxima node and therefore have no path to Aucrux Capacitors.
