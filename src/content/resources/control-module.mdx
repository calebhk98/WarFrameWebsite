## Overview

Control Module is an uncommon resource representing recovered Corpus neural interface technology. Unlike truly common resources, Control Modules drop less frequently and are found primarily on Europa, Neptune, and in Void missions. They are required for building several warframes and companions, often creating a mid-game bottleneck for players who have not yet unlocked the outer planets.

Players frequently cite Control Module alongside Neural Sensors and Neurodes as one of the "annoying three" -- resources that interrupt blueprint construction because they do not drop as abundantly as the truly common materials.

## Acquisition

Europa is the earliest planet where Control Modules drop at a meaningful rate. The ice tileset on Europa features Corpus enemies that drop Control Modules alongside Rubedo and other Europa resources. Neptune is the other primary source.

The Void tileset also provides Control Modules from containers and rare enemy drops, making them a passive acquisition for players running void fissures frequently.

| Planet | Mission | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Europa | Any survival or defense | Primary early farm |
| Neptune | Any mission | Higher-level alternative |
| Void | Fissure missions | Passive acquisition |

## Used In

Control Modules are required for several warframe component blueprints across multiple generations. Companion and sentinel blueprints often require 1 Control Module. Clan Dojo Energy Lab research for power-related technologies uses Control Modules as a key ingredient. Necramech Voidrig and Bonewidow component blueprints also consume Control Modules.

## Strategy

Farm Control Modules on Europa during normal star chart progression. They accumulate gradually without dedicated farming for most players. If you need a stockpile urgently, run Void fissures on Neptune or Europa-tier Void nodes and collect Control Modules from containers. Nekros desecrate helps increase drop rates per enemy.
