## Overview

Mytocardia Spore is a fungal resource obtained in the Orb Vallis open world on Venus. WFCD describes it as "a fungal body prized for its varied uses" with its primary location as Orb Vallis on Venus. It drops from Orb Vallis Bounty rotation rewards at multiple difficulty tiers. The resource is used in Fortuna crafting and Solaris United standing exchanges and is a standard Orb Vallis material accumulated during bounty runs on Venus.

## Where it drops

Mytocardia Spore drops in Rotation A of multiple Orb Vallis Bounty tiers per WFCD data: Level 5-15 and Level 10-30 Orb Vallis Bounties both confirm drops in Rotation A. The Rotation A slot is the most frequently rewarded position in a bounty sequence, making Mytocardia Spore one of the more accessible Orb Vallis resources. Running consecutive Orb Vallis Bounties of either the lower or mid-tier difficulty provides consistent yields. Resource Boosters increase the quantity received per rotation reward.

## Used for

Mytocardia Spore is used in Fortuna crafting blueprints and Solaris United standing exchange trades on Venus. It is a component in Moa companion crafting, K-Drive parts, and other Fortuna-exclusive equipment accessible through the Legs, Roky, and Boon vendors. Players working toward Orb Vallis endgame content should accumulate Mytocardia Spores alongside Thermal Sludge and Spinal Core Sections during regular bounty farming sessions.

## Tips

Run Level 5-15 or Level 10-30 Orb Vallis Bounties for Rotation A rewards that include Mytocardia Spore. Lower-tier bounties are faster to complete and still award the spore in Rotation A, making them efficient for early stockpiling. Staying in the Orb Vallis across multiple bounty cycles without extracting reduces loading time per run. Resource Boosters apply to all bounty reward quantities.

## Notes

Mytocardia Spore is not tradeable through Warframe.market. Access to the Orb Vallis requires completing the Vox Solaris introductory quest on Venus. The resource is designated as a plant type in WFCD taxonomy despite the fungal description, which may reflect a broad classification convention in the game's resource system.
