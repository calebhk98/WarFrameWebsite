## Overview

Neurodes are one of the most critically important rare resources in Warframe. Nearly every warframe component blueprint requires at least one Neurode, making them a persistent demand across all levels of play from new players building their first warframe to veterans constructing the newest Prime releases. Neurodes drop at a low rate from Infested enemies on Eris and the Orokin Derelict, and from containers on Lua.

Unlike most common resources that accumulate passively, Neurodes require deliberate farming to maintain an adequate stockpile. Running out of Neurodes blocks warframe construction and forces a farming detour. Many veterans maintain a buffer stock of 20-50 Neurodes to avoid construction interruptions.

## Acquisition

The most efficient Neurode farm for mid-to-late game players is Orokin Derelict Survival. The Infested enemies on the Derelict tileset drop Neurodes at a higher rate than Earth, and the mission is accessible once players have the Derelict key (previously required a key; now the Derelict is accessed via the Cambion Drift or Orokin tileset). Nekros desecrate dramatically improves Neurode drop rates in Derelict Survival.

For earlier-game players, Earth is the starting point. Bounties on the Plains of Eidolon (Cetus) reward Neurodes in Tier 1 and Tier 2 rotation pools. The Lephantis boss on the Orokin Derelict is guaranteed to drop a Neurode.

## Used In

Neurodes are required in quantities of 1-2 for virtually every warframe component blueprint. Weapon blueprints for mid-to-high tier weapons also require Neurodes. Companion blueprints (Kubrow and Kavat components) use Neurodes. Clan Dojo Energy Lab and Bio Lab research nodes require Neurodes for biological and neural interface research projects.

## Strategy

The best Neurode farming strategy for most players is Orokin Derelict Survival with a Nekros Prime using the Despoil augment mod (which causes Nekros to spend health instead of energy for Desecrate, enabling constant activation). A 20-minute run with Nekros and a speed/damage frame for efficient clearing typically yields 6-12 Neurodes -- enough to cover 3-6 warframe component builds.

Run Plains of Eidolon bounties when doing open-world content to passively accumulate Neurodes without dedicated farming. Tier 2 and 3 bounties have the best Neurode rates in their rotation pools.
