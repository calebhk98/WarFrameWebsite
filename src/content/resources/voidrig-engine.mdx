## Overview

Void-Rig Engines are advanced propulsion modules featuring void-powered turbine systems capable of accelerating Void-Rig Necramechs to extraordinary velocities. Harvested from the void-corrupted mechanical units in Zariman, these engines retain active void-energy circulation even after extraction. The hum of a functional void engine indicates proper void-energy flow. These materials represent a significant component of the Zariman crafting ecosystem. Understanding their properties and acquisition methods is essential for successful progression through advanced void content. The materials maintain their void-energy saturation indefinitely when stored properly.

## Where it drops

Void-Rig Engines drop exclusively from boss-tier encounters and elite Necramech units in the highest-difficulty Void Cascade missions. Zariman disruption boss rewards guarantee engine drops, and special locked containers in void-rifts yield engines upon completion of puzzle sequences. Dedicated farming routes through void rifts optimize crest collection efficiency. Enemy escalation levels directly correlate with improved drop chances. Container-specific farming provides reliable secondary income streams.

## Best locations

Pursue Zariman disruption missions at maximum difficulty to encounter Necramech bosses consistently. Extended void rift expeditions at high escalation levels spawn engine-dropping elite enemies regularly. Team-based fissure runs with high-difficulty settings accelerate engine acquisition. Experienced void farmers recommend focusing on high-difficulty variants for maximum yield per mission. Endurance farming strategies prove superior to rapid-clear approaches for these resources. Team coordination dramatically improves efficiency metrics.

## Used for

Void-Rig Engines feed into Necramech propulsion augments, Void-Rig mobility recipes, and advanced warframe void-power enhancement blueprints. These engines represent the apex of Zariman crafting materials, gating the most powerful void-enhancement options. These crafting materials feed directly into progression gating systems. Maintaining adequate supplies prevents progression bottlenecks. Future content updates may require increased quantities.
