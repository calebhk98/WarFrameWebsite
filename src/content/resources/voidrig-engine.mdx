## Overview

Voidrig Engine is a component resource used to craft the Voidrig Necramech, the offensive Necramech variant introduced with the Heart of Deimos update. According to WFCD, the description reads: "Engine component for the Voidrig Necramech." The Voidrig is a combat-focused Necramech with high damage output and heavy weaponry, distinct from the Bonewidow melee-oriented Necramech.

Necramechs are large mechanical units that Tenno can pilot after completing the Heart of Deimos quest. The Voidrig Necramech requires four components assembled separately before combining in the Foundry: Capsule, Casing, Engine, and Weapon Pod.

The Cambion Drift on Deimos is the primary location for all Voidrig component farming. Isolation Vault bounties and the open Cambion Drift provide the main drop routes, with Necramech enemies spawning as part of the Deimos ecosystem.

## Where it drops

Voidrig Engine drops from Necramech enemies and Isolation Vault bounty rewards on Deimos. The Cambion Drift open world spawns hostile Necramechs, including Voidrig variants, as part of the Infested ecosystem controlled by the Entrati family. Defeating these hostile Necramechs awards component drops including the Engine.

Isolation Vault bounties, accessible from Mother in the Necralisk hub, send squads into underground vault networks for multi-stage objectives. These bounties feature dedicated Necramech combat encounters with guaranteed component reward opportunities. Higher-tier Isolation Vault bounties have better component drop rates and award multiple components per run.

## Used for

- Voidrig Necramech blueprint (requires Capsule, Casing, Engine, Weapon Pod)
- Advancing Entrati standing and Deimos progression gates
- Piloting the Voidrig in missions after construction, including Railjack Murex and Orphix objectives

## Tips

Run Tier 3 Isolation Vaults for the best chance at Engine drops alongside other Voidrig components. A full squad allows simultaneous rotation through vault objectives and increases total enemy spawns. Cambion Drift open-world Necramech encounters serve as a secondary Engine source between vault runs.

The Entrati syndicate standing requirement for Isolation Vault access is moderate; players who complete Heart of Deimos and spend time in the Cambion Drift for initial resource gathering will naturally unlock vault bounties. Bringing a tank-capable Warframe is advisable for vault interiors where Necramechs are confined and deal high damage.

## Notes

- Voidrig Engine is non-tradeable between players.
- The correct WFCD item name is "Voidrig Engine" (one word, Voidrig), not "Void-Rig Engine."
- All four Voidrig components (Capsule, Casing, Engine, Weapon Pod) share similar drop locations; run Isolation Vaults for all four simultaneously.
- The Voidrig Necramech is distinct from the Bonewidow; both require separate components and offer different combat roles.
- Heart of Deimos completion is required before Deimos Necramech farming is accessible.
