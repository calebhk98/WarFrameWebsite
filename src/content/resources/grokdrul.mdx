## Overview

Grokdrul is a Plains of Eidolon resource tied to Cetus and the Ostron faction on Earth. It is used in Plains-exclusive crafting recipes and Ostron standing exchanges. WFCD drop data confirms it appears as a Cetus Bounty reward on Earth and as a drop from Narmer Thumper enemies on the Plains. As a Cetus-specific resource, Grokdrul accumulation is a natural byproduct of farming Plains bounties during early progression.

## Where it drops

The primary confirmed drop sources for Grokdrul per WFCD are Cetus Bounty rewards on Earth, specifically from Level 5-15 Cetus Bounty runs, Rotation B. Narmer Thumper enemies on the Plains of Eidolon also drop Grokdrul as a kill reward. A secondary source appears in Saturn Conclave (Team Annihilation) rotation rewards, though this is an unusual acquisition path that most players would not pursue. Running Cetus Bounties is the standard farming method.

## Used for

Grokdrul is used in Plains of Eidolon crafting recipes associated with the Ostron faction and Cetus. It is a component in Eidolon-hunting preparation items and Operator amp crafting materials available through the Quills syndicate and Cetus vendor. Players working through Eidolon Hunt preparation should stockpile Grokdrul alongside Iradite and Fish Parts to avoid supply gaps.

## Tips

Run Level 5-15 Cetus Bounties on Earth to collect Grokdrul efficiently alongside other Plains resources like Iradite and Fish Parts. Resource Boosters increase the yield per rotation reward. Completing the five stages of a Cetus Bounty provides multiple reward opportunities per run. Narmer Thumpers appear during Narmer alerts on the Plains of Eidolon and provide Grokdrul as drops; engage them when they appear to supplement bounty yields.

## Notes

Grokdrul is not tradeable through Warframe.market. Access requires completing the initial Cetus introduction and reaching the Plains of Eidolon on Earth. The Conclave drop source is functionally inaccessible to most players, as Conclave PvP participation is uncommon; Cetus Bounties remain the practical farming route.
