## Overview

The Exilus Adapter is a specialized crafting component that unlocks the Exilus mod slot on warframes, primary weapons, secondary weapons, and melee weapons. The Exilus slot accepts a restricted set of utility-focused mods (called Exilus mods) without consuming capacity from the main mod slots, effectively adding one bonus mod to the build. For warframes, Exilus mods include mobility options like Handspring, Fortitude, Power Donation, and Cunning Drift. For weapons, Exilus mods include Vigilante Supplies, Terminal Velocity, and Shred.

Exilus Adapters are moderately scarce and players must decide which items benefit most from the additional slot before spending them. Endgame players typically prioritize Exilus Adapters for their highest-use warframes and meta weapons.

## Acquisition

Exilus Adapters are not craftable by recipe in the traditional sense -- they are obtained from Nightwave season tier rewards (1 Adapter per applicable tier), Sortie mission rotation pools (approximately 3% chance), and Syndicate offerings at Rank 4 for several syndicates. Direct platinum purchase from the market (20 platinum each) is the guaranteed acquisition method.

The Exilus Adapter Blueprint can be acquired from Nightwave and is craftable in the Foundry using Orokin Reactor-equivalent ingredients, but the recipe requires an Orokin Catalyst Blueprint and additional components, making it resource-intensive compared to direct acquisition.

## Used In

Each Exilus Adapter is consumed when applied to a warframe or weapon to unlock its Exilus slot permanently on that item. Unlike Forma, unlocking the Exilus slot does not reset the item to rank 0. Once unlocked, the Exilus slot remains available for any future mod swaps. Each unique warframe and weapon requires its own separate Exilus Adapter, making it expensive to equip across a large arsenal.

## Strategy

Prioritize Exilus Adapter deployment on your most-used warframes and meta weapons. For warframes, the Exilus slot provides excellent utility via mods like Power Donation (team energy regeneration), Cunning Drift (aim glide and Parkour velocity), or Handspring (knockdown recovery). For primary weapons, Vigilante Supplies (chance to upgrade ammo to Sniper rounds) and Terminal Velocity (projectile speed) are popular choices for Exilus weapon slots.

Accumulate Exilus Adapters from Nightwave each season (typically 1-3 per season) and save them for your highest-priority builds. Do not spend platinum on Exilus Adapters unless you urgently need one for an active build.
