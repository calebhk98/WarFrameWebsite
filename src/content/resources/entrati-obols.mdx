## Overview

Entrati Obols are Orokin-era coins from the Entrati family's scientific establishment. WFCD describes them as items where "during the Orokin era, these obols were a commonly recognized mark of the Entrati family," and notes that "some Archimedeans even used them as good luck talismans." The Entrati family were prominent Archimedeans (the Orokin scholarly class) and their obols served as both currency and status symbols within Orokin academic circles. The obols now drop in Albrecht's Laboratories on Deimos, where Albrecht Entrati conducted his Void research -- their presence in the labs reflects the Entrati family's deep historical connection to this research site.

Entrati Obols are a broader currency resource within the Albrecht's Laboratories content ecosystem on Deimos, distinct from the Voca-type standing tokens.

## Where it drops

Entrati Obols drop from multiple Albrecht's Laboratories mission types on Deimos. Drop rates sourced from WFCD:

| Source | Drop Chance |
|---|---|
| Level 65-70 Albrecht's Laboratories Bounty, Rotation C | 6.78% |
| Deimos/Effervo (Assassination) | 9.09% |
| Deimos/Nex (Exterminate) | 9.09% |
| Deimos/Persto (Survival), Rotation A | 9.68% |
| Deimos/Cambire (Alchemy), Rotation A | 14.29% |
| Deimos/Munio (Defense), Rotation A | 14.29% |

Alchemy and Defense missions on Deimos offer the highest single-source drop rates at 14.29%, making Cambire and Munio the most efficient dedicated farming nodes. Effervo (Assassination) and Nex (Exterminate) at 9.09% each are also viable and tend to be faster missions, trading drop rate for shorter run time.

## Used for

Entrati Obols are used in Albrecht's Laboratories content for Necraloid syndicate progression and crafting Albrecht Entrati's research vessels. The research vessel construction system introduced in Whispers in the Walls requires Obols alongside Voca resources (Shrill, Bellow, Echo) and other Deimos materials. Players pursuing the Necraloid syndicate's highest offerings -- including Operator Amp components, Helminth Segment, and Tenebrous Ephemera -- need Entrati Obols as part of the standing and crafting economy.

## Tips

- Run Deimos/Cambire (Alchemy) or Deimos/Munio (Defense) for the highest Entrati Obol drop rates at 14.29% per Rotation A; these are significantly better than the bounty Rotation C rate.
- Alchemy missions on Deimos require transmuting elemental vials in the correct sequence; bring a Warframe with area-of-effect abilities to manage enemy pressure during alchemical transmutation events.
- Combine Entrati Obol farming with Voca resource collection (Shrill, Bellow, Echo Voca) in the same Albrecht's Laboratories sessions to accumulate all Deimos endgame materials efficiently.
- Defense missions on Deimos (Munio) allow players to stay for extended rotations; multiple Rotation A cycles in a single Munio session produce consistent Obol accumulation.

## Notes

Entrati Obols are limited to Albrecht's Laboratories content on Deimos and cannot be obtained from Cambion Drift bounties, Isolation Vaults, or any mission outside the Albrecht's Laboratories node system. The 14.29% drop rate at Cambire and Munio appears at Rotation A -- not the later rotations -- meaning shorter mission runs that exit at Rotation A are fully valid for Obol farming and often more time-efficient than extended runs targeting later rotations.
