## Overview

Damaged Necramech Engine is the propulsion core component required for crafting both Necramech types (Bonewidow and Voidrig) in Warframe. WFCD describes it as "An engine designed for use in a Necramech. Unfortunately, it's damaged," sourced from "Enemy Necramechs found in Isolation Vault Bounties on the Cambion Drift (Deimos), and purchased from Loid in the Necralisk." The "damaged" qualifier reflects the scavenged nature of the component -- players strip it from defeated enemy Necramechs, and the Necralisk's fabrication capabilities restore it to working order during the crafting process.

Necramech construction represents one of Warframe's most resource-intensive crafting projects, requiring all five Damaged Necramech parts (Casing, Engine, Pod, Weapon Barrel, Weapon Receiver, Weapon Pod) in quantity.

## Where it drops

Damaged Necramech Engines drop from enemy Necramechs encountered in Isolation Vault Bounties on the Cambion Drift. These bounties spawn Necramech-type enemies that drop components upon defeat. Loid in the Necralisk sells Damaged Necramech components directly, including Engines, once players have completed enough Isolation Vaults to unlock his inventory. The WFCD drop table also lists Father's vendor at the Entrati (Father) neutral standing tier as a source, with 100% availability -- meaning Father reliably stocks these components for direct purchase using Entrati standing.

## Used for

Damaged Necramech Engines are required for both the Bonewidow and Voidrig Necramech blueprints. Each Necramech blueprint requires multiple Damaged Necramech parts; check the Necralisk's Foundry blueprint requirements for exact quantities. The Engine is one of the harder components to accumulate in quantity due to the effort of Isolation Vault farming, making Loid and Father's vendor particularly valuable as supplementary purchase sources.

## Tips

- Run Arcana Isolation Vault Bounties on the Cambion Drift for the best chance of enemy Necramech encounters; the Arcana tier features more Necramech spawns than the standard Isolation Vault.
- Check Father's vendor immediately after reaching neutral Entrati standing -- if he sells Damaged Necramech Engines directly, purchasing them with Entrati tokens may be faster than RNG drops.
- Loid's shop in the Necralisk unlocks Necramech parts after completing a sufficient number of Isolation Vaults; progress toward this unlock early if you plan to build a Necramech.
- Bring Corrosive or Radiation damage profiles effective against the armored Necramech enemy type to reduce time-per-kill in Isolation Vault runs.

## Notes

Necramech construction requires all five component types. Farming them in parallel (running Isolation Vaults for all components simultaneously) is more efficient than farming each component sequentially. The Damaged Necramech Engine specifically is needed for both Necramech types, so farming it benefits any Necramech crafting goal.
