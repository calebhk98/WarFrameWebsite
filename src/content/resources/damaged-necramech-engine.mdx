## Overview

Damaged Necramech Engine components represent core propulsion systems recovered from defunct mechanized units. These energized power cores enable functional movement and ability execution in Necramech frames while serving specialized applications in railjack engineering. Engine acquisition represents critical bottleneck in Necramech construction, demanding consistent farming commitment from players pursuing complete mech arsenal development.

## Where it drops

Deimos void fissure missions consistently yield Necramech engines through enemy drops and environmental cache rewards. Void creature encounters and Necramech-type enemy units prioritize engine drops; targeting these enemies optimizes farming efficiency. Mission difficulty directly amplifies engine drop rates, encouraging maximum-difficulty mission selection for serious farmers.

## Used for

Necramech frame blueprints mandate substantial engine quantities as primary power system components. Railjack propulsion system upgrades incorporate engines for enhanced speed and tactical maneuverability. Certain warframe subsume abilities and void-focused modifications require engine materials, expanding applications beyond Necramech-exclusive purposes.

## Tips

Prioritize high-difficulty Deimos void fissure missions to maximize engine yield per mission completion. Combine engine farming with void relic farming to achieve dual-objective progression. Maintain steady farming cadence during event periods featuring favorable rotations; accumulate reserves supporting future Necramech construction timelines.

## Notes

Engine quality assessment focuses on functionality rather than cosmetic condition; crafting processes restore operational status regardless of damage extent. Orokin engineering principles ensure engine components retain value indefinitely; long-term stockpiling strategies remain viable. Players completing full Necramech suites demonstrate extraordinary endgame dedication and farming commitment.
