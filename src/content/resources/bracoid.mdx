## Overview

Bracoid is a rare biological material described by WFCD as "A rare bacterial entity that increases the spread of Infestation found on Infested Ships." This biological function -- facilitating Infested spread -- positions Bracoid as an active agent within the Infested ecosystem aboard captured or infested vessels. The Bracoid organism propagates Infested biological matter across ship surfaces, making infested vessels more dangerous while producing the unique biological substrate that Bracoid represents when harvested.

As a Railjack-tier material, Bracoid belongs to the Proxima-exclusive resource pool, making it inaccessible through standard star chart missions.

## Where it drops

Bracoid drops from Railjack missions, specifically from enemy units associated with the Infested Ship tileset found in the Veil Proxima region. Enemy fighters, crewships, and ground enemies within Infested-class Railjack nodes yield Bracoid as mission loot. Extended Veil Proxima Railjack runs that clear more enemy units per mission produce better Bracoid yields. The Infested Ship tileset in Railjack features unique biological enemy types whose loot tables include Bracoid.

## Used for

Bracoid feeds into Railjack component blueprints that require biological or Infested-origin materials. Its rare classification means it appears as a lower-volume ingredient in advanced Railjack crafting, often used for components dealing with Infested-themed Railjack subsystems or as a general rare Railjack material in high-tier component recipes. Players advancing deep into Railjack progression will encounter Bracoid requirements in the most demanding component blueprints.

## Tips

- Focus Railjack farming in Veil Proxima Infested mission nodes; the Infested ship encounter types in Veil are the primary Bracoid source within the Railjack node network.
- Assign crew members with high Combat intrinsics to maximize loot acquisition from destroyed enemy fighters and crewships during space combat.
- Extended Railjack survival or defense variants in Veil Proxima increase time-on-mission and total enemy count per run, improving Bracoid accumulation per hour.
- Bracoid is not available from ground missions; Railjack access through the Rising Tide quest is a prerequisite for any Bracoid farming.

## Notes

Bracoid is a Railjack-exclusive material that does not appear in the standard planetary drop tables or any open-world resource pool. It is specifically associated with the biological warfare dimension of Infested Ship encounters in the Railjack content. Players who have not completed Rising Tide and built a Railjack have no access to Bracoid regardless of progression level in other content areas.
