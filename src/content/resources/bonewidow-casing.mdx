## Overview

Bonewidow Casing is a structural plating component critical to assembling the Bonewidow Necramech. The casing provides the skeletal framework and protective shell essential to necramech mobility and durability. Farming casings requires engagement with Bonewidow encounters on Deimos, positioning casing acquisition as a progression milestone for players pursuing advanced necramech builds. The material composition reflects Infested biomechanical engineering, combining organic bone structures with technological hardening processes that provide exceptional protection.

## Where it drops

Bonewidow Casings drop from Bonewidow boss encounters during necramech combat on Deimos. The casing drop is guaranteed upon boss defeat with full squad participation, making coordinated farming highly efficient and rewarding. Void fissure events on Deimos occasionally yield casings as bonus event rewards during Deimos-focused content periods, temporarily boosting acquisition rates. Solo players receive smaller drops than full four-player squads, incentivizing team farming sessions for optimal resource accumulation.
