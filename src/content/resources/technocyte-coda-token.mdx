## Overview

Technocyte Coda Token is the Infested equivalent of the Kuva Lich Token, generated when a player converts a Technocyte Coda -- a powerful Infested adversary that functions as the Infested faction's nemesis system counterpart to the Kuva Lich. Coda are created when an Infested enemy survives a lethal encounter and mutates into a persistent personal adversary. Converting a Coda via the Requiem mod sequence produces a token that can transfer control of that Coda to another player.

The Technocyte Coda system mirrors the Kuva Lich system. Players hunt down a Coda across Infested-controlled missions, apply the correct Requiem sequence with a parazon, then choose to either vanquish the Coda (destroying it permanently) or convert it (creating the token and gaining it as a spectre ally). The Coda carries a specific Coda weapon variant as its associated reward.

## Where it drops

Technocyte Coda Tokens are not found in standard drop tables. A token is created only upon successfully converting a Technocyte Coda using the correct Requiem mod combination and selecting convert at the final confrontation on Deimos. Coda adversaries are tied to the individual player who triggered their creation; they cannot be inherited from another player's session. Missions on Deimos (Cambion Drift) provide the typical encounter ground for tracking Coda adversaries throughout the hunt.

## Used for

A Technocyte Coda Token transfers the specific converted Coda -- including its carried Coda weapon and bonus percentage -- to another player via the Lich trade interface. This allows players who have converted Codas carrying desirable weapons (such as the Coda Hema or Coda Sporothrix) to trade control to buyers without those buyers needing to hunt and convert their own Coda. The receiving player gains the Coda as an ally spectre.

WFCD lists `tradable: false` at the item level. The transfer occurs through the dedicated nemesis trade UI, not through standard trading post item slots. Eleanor in Fortuna sells Coda weapons for the unique currency dropped by vanquishing Codas, providing an alternative acquisition path.

## Tips

Check the Coda's carried weapon and its bonus elemental percentage before converting. A Coda Hema or Sporothrix with 50%+ bonus damage is significantly more trade-valuable than one with 25%. Research current demand on warframe.market before hunting to target worthwhile Coda weapon types. Converting rather than vanquishing is only advantageous if the token trade value exceeds the direct Eleanor currency value of vanquishing.

## Notes

Technocyte Coda Token cannot be placed in standard trading item slots. The nemesis trade UI must be used to transfer the token. WFCD confirms `tradable: false` for this item. The Lich Token functions identically for Kuva Liches (Grineer adversaries), while this token is exclusive to Technocyte Codas (Infested adversaries).
