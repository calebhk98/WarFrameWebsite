## Overview

Ferrite is an early-game common resource found on Earth, Mercury, Neptune, and in the Void. It represents raw iron-based ore mined or recovered from Grineer mining operations on Earth and Mercury. Ferrite is one of the first resources new players encounter and is used in many early blueprint components for starter warframes and weapons. Most players accumulate enough Ferrite naturally without dedicated farming during normal star chart progression through Earth and Mercury.

## Acquisition

Ferrite drops primarily from Grineer enemies on Earth and Mercury and from Grineer/Orokin enemies in the Void. Earth has several high-efficiency Ferrite farming nodes: the dark-sector defense on Everest (Earth) provides a resource drop rate bonus and is a reliable source for bulk Ferrite during early game.

| Planet | Mission | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Earth | Everest Dark Sector Defense | Early access, drop rate boost |
| Mercury | Any survival/defense | Low-level, high drop density |
| Neptune | Survival/Defense | Mid-level with Ferrite drops |
| Void | Survival | Drops alongside void traces |

## Used In

Ferrite appears in blueprints for several starter warframes (Excalibur, Volt, Frost components) and beginner weapons. Clan Dojo research also uses Ferrite for entry-level research nodes. Because Ferrite is an early-game resource, high-level players rarely need to farm it deliberately.

## Strategy

New players should not worry about Ferrite farming -- standard Earth and Mercury missions produce sufficient quantities. If running low during a building session, a 15-minute dark-sector defense run on Everest (Earth) produces thousands of Ferrite units quickly. Experienced players with a Nekros and resource booster can clear Ferrite shortfalls in a single session.
