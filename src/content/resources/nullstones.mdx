## Overview

Nullstones is a gem-type resource described by WFCD as not conforming to several laws of physics, making it prized for optimizing sensitive equipment. It drops in high-level Earth Skirmish missions and from Apex Membroid enemies at Level 76-100 difficulty. Rare and Reinforced Orokin Storage Containers at the same difficulty level also yield Nullstones. The resource is associated with Railjack content and Sigma series component crafting, placing it in the advanced progression tier.

## Where it drops

Per WFCD data, Nullstones drops from the following sources: Apex Membroid enemies at Level 76-100, Rare Orokin Storage Containers at Level 76-100, Reinforced Orokin Storage Containers at Level 76-100, and Earth Skirmish missions at Bendar Cluster and Iota Temple nodes. The Level 76-100 enemy requirement means this resource is farmable only by players with well-developed loadouts capable of handling Steel Path-equivalent difficulty in Railjack-linked missions. Skirmish missions in the Earth Proxima area of the Railjack system are the primary farming route.

## Used for

Nullstones is used in Railjack Sigma series component crafting and related endgame blueprints. It is a component in the construction of Railjack ship parts available through the Dry Dock. Players developing their Railjack for advanced Void Storm and Corpus/Grineer Proxima missions should accumulate Nullstones alongside Cubic Diodes and Carbides to complete the Sigma component sets.

## Tips

Run Earth Proxima Skirmish missions in the Railjack to collect Nullstones from both Apex Membroid kills and Storage Containers. Bring a loadout optimized for quickly destroying enemy fighters and boarding crew, which increases Membroid encounter rate. Focus on clearing Containers found in Derelict and Orokin tile variants within the Skirmish mission to maximize drop opportunities. Resource Boosters apply to Container and enemy drop quantities.

## Notes

Nullstones is not tradeable through Warframe.market. Access requires a functional Railjack with at least basic gear capable of surviving Earth Proxima Skirmish missions. The gem classification reflects its unusual physical properties rather than a standard mineral category. Players without Railjack access cannot farm Nullstones through alternative means.
