## Overview

Enigma Gyrum is the completion token awarded for solving Enigmas in Duviri. WFCD describes it as "a token celebrating the successful solution of a Duviri Enigma," with the source listed as "earned by completing Enigmas in The Duviri Experience." Enigmas are puzzle-style challenges scattered throughout the Duviri spiral realm -- areas of platforming, logic puzzles, and environmental navigation separate from the main combat loops. Solving an Enigma awards a Gyrum token, distinguishing it from the Duviri combat resources (Dracroot, Eevani, Connla Sprout) which come from enemy kills and loot table drops.

The word "Gyrum" relates to circular or spiral motion, consistent with the cyclical, repeating nature of Duviri's reality where time and space are caught in a eternal return pattern linked to Dominus Thrax's emotional state.

## Where it drops

Enigma Gyrum is awarded exclusively from completing Enigmas in The Duviri Experience game mode. Enigmas are specific puzzle encounters found in Duviri that must be actively sought out rather than obtained through normal combat. Each Enigma completion rewards a Gyrum token. Enigma locations in Duviri are distributed across the five Courts of Duviri -- the different emotional zones (Anger, Envy, Sorrow, Fear, Joy) -- and appear in different positions each spiral cycle.

WFCD lists the source as earned specifically from Enigma completion within Duviri; the Gyrum does not appear in standard enemy loot tables, bounty rotations, or Circuit mode rewards.

## Used for

Enigma Gyrum tokens are traded with Acrithis in Duviri for Duviri-exclusive items including Incarnon weapon adapters, Duviri cosmetics, and access to specific Duviri crafting blueprints. Acrithis is the Duviri merchant who specializes in puzzle-related items, and her inventory refreshes across spiral cycles. Players who want Duviri-exclusive Incarnon genesis adapters -- which transform older weapons into Incarnon variants -- will need to accumulate Enigma Gyrum through consistent puzzle completion in Duviri sessions.

## Tips

- Complete all Enigmas found in a single Duviri spiral cycle before exiting; Enigma positions reset each cycle, but completing multiple Enigmas per session maximizes Gyrum accumulation per time invested.
- Use the Duviri map overlay to track visited Court locations and identify remaining Enigma puzzle sites within each Duviri session.
- Enigmas require puzzle-solving rather than combat; bring a Warframe with good mobility for platforming-type Enigmas and plan puzzle-solving time as a separate activity from combat resource farming.
- Combine Enigma completion with Teshin's Cave interactions and Court boss kills in the same Duviri spiral for comprehensive progression per session.

## Notes

Enigma Gyrum is unique among Duviri resources in that it comes purely from puzzle completion rather than combat or loot table drops. This distinguishes it from Dracroot, Eevani, and Connla Sprout (which appear in Endless tier drop tables) and from Duviri Circuit rewards. Players who engage primarily with Duviri's combat systems without actively seeking Enigma puzzles will not naturally accumulate Gyrum. Dedicated puzzle-solving sessions are required for players whose primary goal is Gyrum-gated Acrithis items.
