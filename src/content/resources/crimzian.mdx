## Overview

Crimzian is a blood-red gem mined from Blue Mining Veins across the Plains of Eidolon. Its deep crimson coloring is distinctive among the Plains gem types, which also include Azurite, Devar, Phasmin, and others. The gem occupies the rare-to-uncommon tier within the Blue Vein gem table, making it less common than Azurite but more accessible than the truly rare gems like Nyth. Crimzian feeds into the Ostron crafting economy at Cetus, where Old Man Suumbaat offers blueprints to cut raw gems into higher-tier forms.

The gem's name combines "crimson" (its color) with a gemstone suffix, following the naming convention applied to other Eidolon mineral resources.

## Where it drops

Crimzian is extracted from Blue Mining Veins found throughout the Plains of Eidolon. Blue Veins appear as glowing blue nodes in rock faces and outcrops across the plains landscape. Extracting them requires a mining tool -- the Nosam Cutter (or upgraded versions) -- and performing the extraction minigame accurately for maximum yield. Tusk Thumpers and Narmer Thumpers also carry Crimzian at 16.68% drop chance per kill, providing a combat-based supplement to mining. Thumpers spawn when players run for extended distances in the plains.

## Used for

Crimzian serves as a raw gem for Cetus crafting and standing advancement. Old Man Suumbaat in Cetus sells blueprints to process Crimzian into higher-tier cut gems, following the same alloy conversion pattern used by other Plains gem resources. Specific weapon components and upgrade materials within the Ostron economy list Crimzian as a required ingredient. The Marquise Crimzian is the cut version of the gem, used in the most demanding Cetus crafting recipes.

## Tips

- Blue Mining Veins on the Plains of Eidolon are denser in certain geological zones; community mining maps mark the reliable Blue Vein clusters, particularly near cave entrances and cliff faces.
- Upgrade to an Advanced Nosam Cutter or Sunpoint Plasma Drill for increased yield per vein extraction -- low-tier cutters produce fewer gems per node.
- Thumper hunting is an efficient way to accumulate Crimzian alongside Ostron standing from the combat encounter itself; check the Thumper's drop table for which gems it carries.
- Combine mining runs with fishing (Gara Toht Lake at night) and Cetus Wisp collection for a comprehensive Plains resource loop per session.

## Notes

Crimzian is distinct from the cut Marquise Crimzian, which is the processed form requiring a blueprint from Suumbaat. Most Cetus equipment recipes specify which tier of gem is required; confirm the recipe before farming to avoid collecting raw Crimzian when the cut form is needed. Neither version is tradeable between players; both must be personally obtained through mining or Thumper farming.
