## Overview

Kuaka Spinal Claw is a wildlife-derived component resource obtained by hunting small Kuaka creatures on the Plains of Eidolon on Earth. WFCD data confirms it drops from Ashen Kuaka, Coastal Mergoo, and Ghost Kuaka enemy variants, which are passive wildlife types on the Plains tileset. The resource description states that "the sinew from the Kuaka's spinal claws can be tanned to make resilient and flexible leather," indicating it is a processed wildlife material used in Ostron crafting on Cetus. It is associated with Cetus-based crafting and Ostron standing exchanges.

## Where it drops

Kuaka Spinal Claw drops from killing Kuaka wildlife on the Plains of Eidolon. WFCD confirms three source types: Ashen Kuaka, Coastal Mergoo, and Ghost Kuaka. Kuaka are small, passive creatures that appear throughout the Plains tileset in groups. Coastal Mergoo are bird-like wildlife found near the coastal waterways. Ghost Kuaka appear in specific terrain types on the Plains. Using weapons with area-of-effect damage to kill nearby Kuaka groups quickly is an efficient farming approach. Wildlife drop rates are affected by enemy lure mechanics and proximity to their spawn areas.

## Used for

Kuaka Spinal Claw is used in Plains of Eidolon crafting recipes associated with the Ostron faction on Cetus and in Quill syndicate item exchanges. It is a component in the crafting of Eidolon-related items and Operator amp materials. Players pursuing Eidolon Hunt builds should stockpile Kuaka Spinal Claw alongside other Plains wildlife materials to avoid gaps during amp component crafting.

## Tips

Hunt Kuaka during the Eidolon Plains' daytime phase when visibility is high and spawn locations are predictable. Kuaka appear in small groups and are passive, making them easy targets for quick kills before they scatter. Bring a secondary weapon with splash damage to hit multiple Kuaka in a single shot for efficiency. Resource Boosters increase the quantity received per kill. Running the Plains exit-enter loop refreshes wildlife spawns for sustained farming sessions.

## Notes

Kuaka Spinal Claw is not tradeable through Warframe.market. Access requires unlocking the Plains of Eidolon on Earth by completing the Saya's Vigil quest. The resource appears both in the item drop list and the Saturn Conclave Team Annihilation alternate source listed in WFCD data, though the Conclave source is considered a side channel; Plains wildlife farming is the standard approach.
