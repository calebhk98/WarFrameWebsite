## Overview

Gyromag Systems is a Solaris United resource obtained on Venus through the Profit-Taker Orb fight in the Orb Vallis. It is one of three Corpus Raknoid-derived resources (alongside Repeller Systems and Atmo Systems) used in Fortuna crafting and Solaris United standing purchases. The resource is locked behind completion of the Profit-Taker Heist missions, which require reaching Solaris United's top standing rank before the fight is accessible.

## Where it drops

Gyromag Systems drops in Rotation C reward slots from all four phases of the Profit-Taker Orb fight on Venus. WFCD data confirms drops from Profit-Taker Phase 1 (Level 40-60), Phase 2 (Level 40-60), Phase 3 (Level 40-60), and Phase 4 (Level 50-60) in Orb Vallis. An additional source is the Vox Solaris standing vendor, where Gyromag Systems can be purchased with Solaris United standing points. Running consecutive Profit-Taker runs is the most efficient acquisition method given the Rotation C placement.

## Used for

Gyromag Systems is used in Fortuna crafting recipes for Orb Vallis-exclusive equipment and as a Solaris United standing exchange commodity. It is a required ingredient for several Moa companion and K-Drive components accessible through Fortuna vendors. Players working toward Orb Vallis endgame content should farm Gyromag Systems alongside Repeller Systems and Atmo Systems to unlock the full crafting catalog.

## Tips

Completing all four phases of the Profit-Taker Heist in a single session maximizes Gyromag Systems yield per time investment. Bring a squad to reduce fight duration, as the Profit-Taker is a Warframe and Operator DPS check that rewards coordinated play. Resource Boosters do not affect the quantity received from rotation rewards, but do increase any incidental drops from enemies killed during the fight. Standing-purchased Gyromag Systems from Vox Solaris is an alternative when farming time is limited.

## Notes

Gyromag Systems requires completing the Profit-Taker quest chain, which is itself locked behind reaching the Solaris United's maximum rank before Heist missions unlock. Players must work through the Fortuna standing system progressively before accessing this resource. The resource is not tradeable on Warframe.market.
