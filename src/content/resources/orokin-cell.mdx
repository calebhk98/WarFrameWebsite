## Overview

The Orokin Cell is one of the most universally required resources in Warframe. Players need it to build nearly every warframe blueprint and many weapon blueprints, making it essential for progression and star chart advancement. Orokin Cells drop exclusively in Void Fissure missions, which are accessed through void-tiered relics.

## Where it drops

Orokin Cells are obtained primarily through Void Fissure missions (the endless void-based endless missions) and Void Sabotage. Void Fissure missions include Defense, Survival, Excavation, and Interception variants. The most efficient Void Fissure mission for consistent cell farming depends on your playstyle and available warframes.

| Mission type | Location | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Void Fissure Defense | Void | 5-minute waves; rotation A cells |
| Void Fissure Survival | Void | 5-minute intervals; rotation A cells |
| Void Fissure Excavation | Void | Per-extraction rotation; reliable rewards |
| Void Sabotage | Void | Single-run; guaranteed cell + void traces |

## Best locations

Void Sabotage offers a guaranteed Orokin Cell upon completion, making it the most efficient single-run option for farming. No endless mission participation is required, and the sabotage mission is quick (3-5 minutes). However, if you prefer endless missions, Void Fissure Survival and Defense both offer rotation A cells at consistent intervals.

For newer players without optimized farming loadouts, Void Sabotage is strongly recommended because the cell is guaranteed and does not depend on loot tables. A single Void Sabotage run guarantees one cell.

## Drop rates and rotations

In Void Fissure endless missions, Orokin Cells appear in the rotation A reward pool (first five minutes of Survival or Defense, first three extractions in Excavation). The drop chance in rotation A is approximately 25%, making endless fissures less efficient than sabotage for pure cell farming. Void Sabotage guarantees one cell per run, making it the optimal farm.

## Tips

Bring void-tiered relics to access Void Fissure missions. If you do not have void relics, farm lower void-tier missions or buy them via void traces from the Void Fissure vendor. Void Traces are earned passively in Fissure missions and can be traded for additional relics.

Void Sabotage is the fastest farm because it guarantees one cell and requires no endless grind. Run Void Sabotage repeatedly for the most efficient cell acquisition rate.

## Used in

Orokin Cells are mandatory for building most warframe blueprints (e.g., Mag, Grineer, Void Fissure rewards). They are also required for many weapon blueprints, companion blueprints, and other high-tier crafting recipes. A single warframe blueprint often requires 1 Orokin Cell, meaning players must farm many cells throughout the game.

## Notes

Orokin Cells are a bottleneck resource for newer players due to the need to farm Void Fissures. However, once you have a reliable void relic supply and a capable warframe, cell farming becomes routine. Many veteran players maintain a stockpile of 20+ cells for future blueprint needs.

## Sources

The primary source for Orokin Cells is Void Fissure missions, which are documented in the Void Fissure page of the official wiki. Void Sabotage is the secondary guaranteed source. Both are listed in the WFCD `warframe-items` JSON under resource drop tables.
