## Overview

Detonite Injector is a ClanTech component described by WFCD as "the basis for explosive and incendiary weapons." It is one of three core ClanTech materials (alongside Fieldron and Mutagen Mass) produced through Clan Dojo research and required for a broad range of Grineer-faction weapon blueprints. The Injector packages volatile explosive compounds into a pressurized delivery system, enabling the construction of incendiary and fragmentation-based weapons that define the Grineer military arsenal.

Like the other ClanTech trio members, Detonite Injectors are not found as raw drops from enemies. They are crafted in the Clan Dojo Chem Lab from Detonite Ampules or obtained directly through Grineer Invasion mission rewards.

## Where it drops

Detonite Injectors are produced in the Clan Dojo Chem Lab from raw Detonite Ampules, which drop from Grineer enemies across the mid-to-late star chart. Once the Chem Lab has been researched and unlocked, players can craft Injectors in the Foundry. The direct alternative is Grineer Invasion missions: when an active Invasion lists Detonite Injector as a reward, completing three Invasion missions for the Grineer side yields a Detonite Injector without requiring Dojo access. Monitor the star chart for Invasion windows, as they represent the fastest single-session acquisition route for solo players without Clan membership.

## Used for

Detonite Injectors are required for a wide array of Grineer-faction weapon blueprints unlocked through Dojo research, including the Hind (burst rifle), Karak (automatic rifle), Sobek (shotgun), Strun (shotgun), Grakata (automatic rifle), and Latron (semi-auto rifle). Quantities per craft range from 1 to 5 depending on weapon tier. Maintaining a stock of 10 to 20 Injectors prevents delays when new research becomes available or when multiple weapons are queued for crafting simultaneously.

## Tips

- Check the star chart daily for Grineer Invasions listing Detonite Injector rewards; three successful Invasion missions yield direct Injectors without requiring Dojo contribution.
- Farm Detonite Ampules from Grineer enemies on Mars, Phobos, Sedna, or any high-density Grineer planet and combine them into Injectors through the Foundry.
- Build reserves of all three ClanTech materials (Fieldron, Mutagen Mass, Detonite Injector) in parallel to avoid sequential research bottlenecks.
- Joining a Clan with an established Chem Lab removes the sample-farming step, since the Lab produces Injectors continuously once research is complete.

## Notes

Detonite Injector is the Grineer-faction equivalent of Fieldron (Corpus) and Mutagen Mass (Infested). Each of the three ClanTech materials corresponds to a specific faction's weapon research in the Dojo. Grineer Invasion rewards are the most reliable non-Dojo source; plan crafting schedules around active Invasion windows to avoid extended delays between weapon builds.
