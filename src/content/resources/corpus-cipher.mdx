## Overview

Corpus Cipher is a legacy component resource in Warframe. According to WFCD, the description reads: "A cipher used to decode Corpus intel. Crafting component required to create a CORPUS VOID KEY." The Corpus Cipher is a holdover from an earlier game system when Void Keys were craftable items needed to access the Void tileset for Relic-equivalent missions.

With the transition of the Relic and Prime Vault systems in modern Warframe, the Corpus Cipher role has diminished significantly. The Void Key system it supported has been largely replaced by the Void Relic system. However, Corpus Cipher remains in the game item database via WFCD and can still be encountered as a drop or legacy crafting ingredient.

Corpus Ciphers drop from Corpus faction enemies encountered across multiple planets including Venus, Jupiter, Europa, Neptune, and Pluto. Any Corpus-controlled node features enemies that can drop the Cipher. WFCD does not list specific percentage drop rates for Corpus Cipher in its current dataset, reflecting its legacy status.

## Where it drops

Corpus Cipher drops from Corpus faction enemy kills across Corpus-controlled star chart nodes. The Cipher is associated with the Corpus Void Key blueprint, which required it before the Void Relic system superseded Void Key crafting. Farming for this item specifically is rarely needed in current gameplay; players typically accumulate Corpus Ciphers passively while farming other resources on Corpus planets.

Neptune and Jupiter are the most enemy-dense Corpus planets, offering the highest volume of Corpus enemy encounters per mission. Survival and Defense mission types on these planets sustain indefinite enemy spawns, which maximizes the passive accumulation of Corpus drops including Ciphers.

## Tips

Do not farm Corpus Ciphers specifically unless a legacy blueprint requires them. Players pursuing modern Warframe content -- Prime Warframes, new weapons, Railjack -- will not need significant quantities. If a blueprint calls for Corpus Ciphers, run any Corpus survival or defense mission and collect ciphers as passive drops from enemy kills.

Neptune Outer Terminus (Mobile Defense) and Jupiter Ganymede (Disruption) are good general-purpose Corpus farming nodes that yield Corpus Ciphers alongside more commonly needed resources like Plastids and Oxium. Equip a Warframe with Desecrate (Nekros) or Pilfering Swarm (Hydroid) to increase overall drop rates during the session.
