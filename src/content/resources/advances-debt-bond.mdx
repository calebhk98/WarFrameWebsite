## Overview

Advances Debt-Bonds are a specialized currency earned through Zariman Ten Zero activities. As a virtual form of currency specific to the Void-touched zones of Zariman, they serve as a means to reward players for completing challenging missions in the corrupted void environment. These bonds represent payment obligations tracked through the void itself and are used to fund various upgrades and equipment from the Zariman Syndicate vendors.

## Where it drops

Advances Debt-Bonds are obtained exclusively through Zariman Ten Zero bounty activities. Players earn them upon completion of bounties at any tier, with higher-tier bounties providing greater rewards. Additionally, successful completion of Void Cascade missions yields substantial quantities of this bond type, making farming efficiency dependent on choosing appropriate difficulty levels matched to team capability.

## Used for

This currency serves as the primary trading medium for the Zariman Syndicate, specifically for purchasing advancement-related equipment and upgrade blueprints. Components like amplifier parts, enhanced mods, and access to restricted Zariman areas require spending accumulated Advances Debt-Bonds. Strategic budgeting of these bonds is necessary for players pursuing multiple Zariman progression paths simultaneously.

## Tips

Farm Advances Debt-Bonds during high-tier bounties when your team is sufficiently geared to complete them efficiently. The void-touched nature of Zariman means standard debuffs apply; bringing appropriate frames like Void Fissure-resistant warframes accelerates mission completion. Pairing bounty farming with daily standing caps ensures optimal resource accumulation without wasteful overflow.

## Notes

Advances Debt-Bonds cannot be traded between players and are account-bound upon acquisition. Players cannot exceed typical syndicate standing caps even when farming bond variants, so prioritize spending between farming sessions. The bond system replaced older Zariman reward structures in favor of void-themed variations, creating a cohesive thematic economy within the Zariman tileset.
