## Overview

Advances Debt-Bond represents the mid-tier debt currency of the Solaris United syndicate economy in Fortuna on Venus. WFCD describes it as representing situations where "a rig jockey needs a helping hand, so they get an advance on the next pay," with "massive interest rates almost guarantee they'll be paying that off forever." This characterization captures the predatory lending practices of the Corpus financial system on Venus, where workers take advances on future wages under impossible repayment conditions -- a central element of the economic oppression driving the Solaris United resistance narrative.

Advances Debt-Bonds occupy the fourth tier in the five-tier debt bond series: Training, Shelter, Medical, Advances, and Familial (highest). They are required for Solaris United rank advancement and vendor access at the mid-to-high progression range.

## Where it drops

Advances Debt-Bonds drop from Orb Vallis bounties through Eudico in Fortuna across multiple tiers. The Level 30-50 and Level 50-70 bounties each award 2 bonds at 16.67% chance per Rotation A and B. The bonds appear across all active bounty tiers in the Orb Vallis; higher tiers maintain similar drop rates. WFCD confirms they are "obtained from Bounties in the Orb Vallis" through the standard rotation system, making any active Fortuna bounty run a viable acquisition route.

## Used for

Advances Debt-Bonds are the fourth-tier currency in the Solaris United standing economy. Players approaching Solaris United rank advancement beyond the mid-tiers require Advances Debt-Bonds alongside lower tiers (Training, Shelter, Medical) for rank-up costs. The bonds also serve as trading currency with Little Duck and other Fortuna vendors for specific Kitgun parts, Hound component blueprints, and Moa components. Farming Advances Debt-Bonds provides a critical bottleneck material for players committed to the full Fortuna progression track.

## Tips

- Run the highest Orb Vallis bounty tier that can be completed efficiently; all tiers include Advances Debt-Bonds in their rotation rewards, but faster mission completion yields more bonds per hour.
- Advances Debt-Bonds are tradeable between players on warframe.market; purchasing them from other players is an option if Fortuna farming time is limited.
- Complete the full bounty chain each session to maximize rotation access -- bonds appear in both Rotation A and B, meaning full completion hits two reward slots.
- Combine bounty farming with Orb Vallis Conservation and fishing to accumulate Solaris United standing from multiple sources per Vallis session.

## Notes

The five debt bond tiers form a progression series: Training Debt-Bonds are the most common and Familial Debt-Bonds are the rarest. Advances Debt-Bonds sit at the fourth tier, making them more scarce than the lower three but more available than Familial bonds. Players should farm all bond types simultaneously during Orb Vallis bounty runs rather than targeting one tier at a time, since the same bounty rewards provide a natural spread across all tiers over multiple sessions.
