## Overview

Gallium is an uncommon resource representing a silvery liquid metal found in Grineer mining and military installations on Mars and Uranus. It is a step above common resources in scarcity and is required for several warframe and weapon blueprints encountered in the mid-game. Players often encounter Gallium as a mild bottleneck when building warframes available in the Mars-to-Saturn range.

Unlike most resources, Gallium has a notable guaranteed drop from the boss encounter with Captain Vor and Lieutenant Kril on Exta (Ceres) and Lieutenant Lech Kril on War (Mars), providing new players with a reliable source from their first boss encounters.

## Acquisition

For dedicated Gallium farming, Mars Survival or Uranus Survival missions provide the best rates via sustained enemy drops. Boss missions on Mars (War, defeating Lech Kril) provide guaranteed Gallium drops as a reliable early source for new players who need small quantities quickly.

| Source | Rate | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Mars Survival | Moderate | Good for larger quantities |
| War, Mars (Lech Kril) | Guaranteed drop | Small quantity, fast mission |
| Uranus missions | Moderate | Higher level alternative |

## Used In

Gallium appears in the crafting requirements for mid-tier warframe components including Ember, Frost, and several other classic warframes. Various weapon blueprints incorporating liquid metal components also require Gallium. Clan Dojo chemical research nodes use Gallium for metallurgical processes.

## Strategy

Farm Gallium through Mars Survival missions with a Nekros for loot enhancement. For small quantity needs (1-5 Gallium), run the Lech Kril boss on War (Mars) for a guaranteed drop per run -- the boss mission takes 5-7 minutes and is reliable for topping up your supply. For larger stockpiles, a 20-minute Mars Survival run yields 5-15 Gallium on average.
