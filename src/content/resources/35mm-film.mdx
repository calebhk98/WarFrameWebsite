## Overview

35mm Film is a component resource that serves niche crafting purposes primarily tied to Zariman void fissure content. This nostalgic-themed material reflects the aesthetic of the Zariman tileset and appears occasionally in mission rewards. The resource is relatively uncommon and typically only required for specialized equipment blueprints. The cinematographic reference embedded in its name suggests connection to early digital recording technology, reflecting the Zariman's retro-futuristic atmosphere within the void itself.

## Where it drops

The primary source of 35mm Film is void fissure missions on the Zariman Ten Zero. Players engaging in excavation and survival missions with void fissures active can expect drops from enemies and container loot. The drop rate is modest and typically requires several runs to accumulate meaningful quantities. Void fissure modifications and public squad bonuses do not increase this resource's availability, making dedicated farming sessions important for consistent acquisition. The resource appears sporadically across all Zariman node types but concentrates in longer-duration mission types.

## Best locations

Zariman Ten Zero excavation missions offer the highest concentration of 35mm Film per mission run, though the resource remains uncommon compared to standard drops on other planets. Running multiple void fissure missions in succession is more efficient than attempting single runs, allowing players to build momentum and optimize container spawning. Extended survival sessions on Zariman generate more container spawns overall, improving per-hour farming efficiency substantially.

## Used for

35mm Film is consumed primarily in the Stropha blueprint and other Zariman-associated equipment blueprints. Early-game crafters are unlikely to need this resource until attempting endgame or Zariman-exclusive builds. The resource also appears in cosmetic and thematic equipment blueprints connected to Zariman cultural artifacts, making it relevant for players pursuing complete aesthetic builds themed around void content.
