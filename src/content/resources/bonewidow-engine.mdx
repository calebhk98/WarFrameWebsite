Bonewidow Engine is the power and propulsion core of the Bonewidow Necramech, essential for mobility and operational functionality. This high-tech component governs the necramech's movement systems and tactical responsiveness. Farming engines requires defeating Bonewidow on Deimos, positioning engine acquisition as a critical progression milestone for necramech builders.

Bonewidow Engines drop exclusively from Bonewidow boss encounters during Deimos necramech combat. The engine drop is guaranteed upon defeating Bonewidow with full squad participation. Void fissure events on Deimos occasionally yield engines as bonus event rewards during focused event periods.
