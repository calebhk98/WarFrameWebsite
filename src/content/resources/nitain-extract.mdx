## Overview

Nitain Extract is a bottleneck resource for mid-game progression because it is required for several popular warframe blueprints (Ash, Equinox, Ivara, and more) but has very limited farming methods. Unlike most resources, Nitain Extract cannot be reliably "farmed" in traditional missions; instead, it is awarded through Alerts, Nightwave challenges, and occasional Tactical Alerts. This scarcity is intentional, designed to encourage players to engage with the alert system regularly.

## Where it drops

Nitain Extract availability is determined by real-time world events (Alerts) and seasonal Nightwave challenges. There is no dedicated mission or planet where you can run endless Nitain Extract farms. Your only reliable options are:

1. **Nightwave weekly challenges** -- Each week, Nightwave offers one or two Nitain Extract rewards as part of the seasonal weekly challenge pool.
2. **Alerts** -- Real-time alerts pop up every few hours and occasionally offer Nitain Extract as a reward. These are time-limited (24 hours) and cannot be replayed.
3. **Void Cascade (Void Fissure variant)** -- Void Cascade missions have an approximate 2-3% Nitain Extract drop chance, making this method extremely inefficient (not recommended).

## Best locations

The only practical source is Nightwave. Check the current Nightwave season for available Nitain Extract weekly challenges and complete them to earn Nitain on a predictable schedule. Alerts are unreliable; you must log in during the 24-hour window and complete the alert mission, which requires active daily engagement.

| Source | Availability | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Nightwave weekly challenges | Current season | 1-2 per week |
| Alerts | Real-time | Irregular; roughly 1 per day |
| Void Cascade | Void | Drop chance &lt;3%; not viable |

## Alert system

When a Nitain Extract alert appears, you have 24 hours to complete the associated mission. Alerts are live real-time events; there is no archive or replay system. You must log in, locate the alert on your star chart, and complete it within the time window. Alerts are often challenging (high level) to ensure players actively participate rather than passively collect rewards.

## Nightwave strategy

Nightwave is the most reliable source of Nitain Extract. Each season lasts approximately 12 weeks, and each week typically offers 1-2 Nitain Extract rewards in the weeklies section. A single season can yield 12-24 Nitain Extract if you complete all available weeklies. Prioritize Nitain-rewarding weeklies over other cosmetic rewards.

## Tips

Check the Alerts tab in-game regularly. Enable notifications if your platform supports them, so you do not miss Nitain Extract alerts.

Do not farm Void Cascade for Nitain Extract; the drop chance is abysmal. Use Nightwave and Alerts exclusively.

Plan your Nitain Extract usage ahead of time. If you need 6 Nitain Extract for upcoming blueprints, target Nightwave seasons that offer at least that many.

## Used in

Nitain Extract is required for warframe blueprints including Ash, Ash Prime, Equinox, Equinox Prime, Ivara, Saryn (alternative builds), and several cosmetic items and augment mods. A single warframe blueprint requires 2-3 Nitain Extract, so farming for multiple frames is time-consuming.

## Farming challenges

Nitain Extract farming is deliberately limited to encourage regular login and engagement with game systems (Alerts, Nightwave). Unlike traditional resources that can be farmed at any time, Nitain Extract availability is gated by real-time events. This means newer players may experience bottlenecks if they need frames requiring Nitain but do not have enough available from alerts and Nightwave.

## Notes

Many veteran players maintain a Nitain Extract stockpile (20+) accumulated over many seasons. New players should prioritize completing Nightwave weeklies during their first few seasons to build a personal reserve for unexpected blueprint needs.
