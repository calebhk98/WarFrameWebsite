## Overview

Neural Sensors are mid-game resources required for several warframe blueprints and certain weapon blueprints. They are obtained primarily from Jupiter missions and, to a lesser extent, from Railjack content on Europa. Neural Sensors are less scarce than some late-game resources but still time-consuming to farm, particularly for newer players early in progression.

## Where it drops

Neural Sensors appear on Jupiter as enemy drops from Corpus faction, making any Jupiter mission viable for farming. The highest concentration drops from Jupiter Void Fissure missions, where enemies spawn in greater density. Europa Railjack missions also offer Neural Sensors through loot tables, but this requires Railjack access and is more time-intensive.

| Location | Mission type | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Jupiter | Any Corpus mission | Low drop chance; multi-resource farm |
| Jupiter Void Fissures | Defense / Survival / Disruption | Higher density; best option |
| Jupiter Sabotage | Single-run | Fast; guaranteed drops |
| Europa Railjack | Crewship defeats | Time-intensive; secondary source |

## Best locations

Jupiter Void Fissure Survival is the best dedicated Neural Sensors farm. Running a 10-15 minute Void Fissure Survival match typically yields 1-2 Neural Sensors. Alternatively, Jupiter Sabotage offers a quicker single-run farm (3-5 minutes) if you prioritize speed over quantity.

For newer players without void relic access, running any Jupiter mission (e.g., Void Cascade or standard Jupiter Survival) works but is slower due to lower enemy density and drop chances.

## Drop rates

In Jupiter Void Fissure missions, Neural Sensors have an approximate 8-12% drop chance per enemy killed, with higher rates in endless missions due to scaling enemy levels. In standard Jupiter missions, the rate drops to approximately 3-5%, requiring longer runs to accumulate Neural Sensors.

## Tips

Farm Jupiter Void Fissures instead of standard Jupiter missions for significantly faster drop rates.

Bring resource-boosting warframes (Pilfering Swarm Hydroid, Despoil Nekros) or use a resource booster to double Neural Sensors drops.

Combine Neural Sensors farming with void trace grinding by running void fissures; you build void traces passively while farming sensors.

## Used in

Neural Sensors are required for Nova Prime blueprint (neural sensors x5), Equinox Prime blueprint, and several other warframe and weapon blueprints. Each blueprint typically requires 1-5 Neural Sensors, depending on the blueprint rarity.

## Farming strategies

A standard farm is 2-3 runs of Jupiter Void Fissure Survival (10-15 minutes each = 30-45 minutes total) to net 2-3 Neural Sensors, sufficient for a single warframe blueprint. Players needing multiple frames should expect 2-3 hours of farming for a full stockpile (10-15 units).

For players early in star chart progression, standard Jupiter missions (Void Cascade, regular Survival) work but are roughly twice as time-intensive compared to void fissure variants.

## Notes

Neural Sensors are abundant enough that farmers rarely stockpile more than 10-20 units. They are a "as-needed" resource for most players rather than a severe bottleneck like Nitain Extract or Argon Crystals.
