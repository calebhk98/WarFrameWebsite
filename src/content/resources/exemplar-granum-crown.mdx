## Overview

Exemplar Granum Crown is the highest tier of the three Granum Crown types used on Corpus Ship missions. WFCD describes it as "an ancient token of high esteem from the Corpus Founder himself," positioning it as the most prestigious of the Corpus Founder Parvos Granum's authority tokens. The three Granum Crown tiers -- standard Granum Crown, Riven Granum Crown, and Exemplar Granum Crown -- are used to interact with Granum Specters on Corpus Ship missions and to enter the corresponding tiers of Granum Void challenges. Exemplar is the hardest to obtain and accesses the highest-tier Granum Void encounter.

The Granum Void is a pocket dimension associated with Parvos Granum's imprisonment, and the Crown system reflects the hierarchical power structure of the Corpus Founder's legacy within Corpus society.

## Where it drops

Exemplar Granum Crowns drop from the highest-level Corpus Treasurer enemies on Corpus Ship missions and from the Extended Granum Void rotation reward. Drop rates from WFCD:

| Source | Drop Chance |
|---|---|
| Extended Granum Void, Rotation A | 16.67% |
| Exemplar Granum Crown Cache | 100% |

Corpus Treasurer enemies are rare spawns that appear during Corpus Ship missions. They appear carrying a strongbox and flee from players; killing them before they escape rewards Granum Crowns at varying tiers based on the Treasurer's level and mission tier. The Exemplar tier Treasurer appears in the highest-level Corpus Ship missions (generally Pluto and Corpus Ship Arbitrations).

## Used for

Exemplar Granum Crowns have two primary uses: entering the Extended Granum Void and crafting the Protea Warframe. Throwing an Exemplar Crown at the Granum Specter hologram in Corpus Ship missions activates the Extended Granum Void challenge, where players must eliminate a quota of Granum Specters within the time limit for rotation rewards. One Protea component blueprint requires an Exemplar Granum Crown directly as a crafting ingredient, making it mandatory for players who want to build Protea from scratch.

The Extended Granum Void's Rotation A reward is itself an Exemplar Granum Crown at 16.67%, creating a self-sustaining loop for players farming both Protea components and Extended Void rewards simultaneously.

## Tips

- Run Corpus Ship missions on Pluto nodes (like Hydra) for the highest probability of encountering Exemplar-tier Treasurer enemies.
- Use a fast Warframe (Volt, Gauss, Wisp) or the Parkour Sprint mechanics to chase down Treasurer enemies before they escape the mission area.
- Equip a Looter Kavat or Pilfering Swarm Hydroid to maximize Treasurer enemy drop chances on kill.
- The Extended Granum Void Rotation A rewards an Exemplar Crown at 16.67%, making the Void itself a viable source for additional Crowns once you have enough to enter.

## Notes

Exemplar Granum Crowns are mission-specific to Corpus Ship tileset missions. They cannot be obtained from any ground mission outside Corpus Ships or from Railjack, open worlds, or standard planetary nodes. Players who want to farm Protea should plan dedicated Corpus Ship farming sessions on Pluto specifically for Treasurer encounters, as Treasurer spawns are not guaranteed in every mission run.
