## Overview

Dagonic is a rare gem from the Cambion Drift on Deimos, described by WFCD as "Rivulets of Exocrine fluid, hardened over passing centuries." The Exocrine fluid referenced is the biological secretion of the Infestation organism that covers Deimos, meaning Dagonic is literally the solidified waste product of the Infested biological mass, crystallized through extreme age and geological pressure. This origin reflects the Cambion Drift's unique ecosystem where biological and mineral processes have merged over millennia of Infested occupation.

The WFCD description specifies multiple drop sources: Blue Mining Lesions, Sagiant Entrati Caskets, and Bonus Vault rewards from Isolation Vault Bounties -- giving Dagonic more acquisition pathways than most Cambion Drift gems.

## Where it drops

Dagonic drops from three distinct sources on Deimos. Blue Mining Lesions on the Cambion Drift are the primary mining source, yielding Dagonic from the rare tier within the Blue Lesion loot table. Sagiant Entrati Caskets -- special environmental containers found within the Cambion Drift -- also drop Dagonic when opened. Bonus Vault rewards during Isolation Vault Bounties provide a mission-reward source that does not require mining; completing the bonus objectives within a vault run rewards Dagonic alongside other rare components.

## Used for

Dagonic serves as a rare gem component in Necralisk crafting recipes for weapons and equipment available through Deimos progression. As a gem derived from the Infested biological process, it appears in recipes for Infested-aligned or Deimos-themed items available through the Necralisk vendors. Several Deimos weapon blueprints and component upgrades list Dagonic alongside other Cambion Drift materials in their crafting requirements.

## Tips

- Blue Mining Lesions on Deimos are the most consistently accessible source; equip a Sunpoint Plasma Drill or Advanced Nosam Cutter for optimal extraction yield from rare-tier gem nodes.
- Integrate Sagiant Entrati Casket hunting into Cambion Drift traversal -- these environmental containers are not always obvious, so scan the environment actively while running bounty objectives.
- Running Isolation Vault Bounties for Necramech components simultaneously awards Dagonic from the Bonus Vault reward pool, creating efficient overlap between Necramech farming and gem accumulation.
- Mining runs in the Cambion Drift night cycle may offer different Lesion spawn patterns; experiment with both day and night timing for the best mining route.

## Notes

Dagonic is classified as a gem type (Blue Lesion output) rather than an ore, which means Red Mining Lesions on Deimos do not yield it. Blue Lesions are the correct mining target. The three drop sources (Blue Lesions, Sagiant Caskets, Isolation Vault Bonus Vaults) provide good acquisition flexibility, making Dagonic less reliant on pure RNG mining than other Cambion Drift rare gems.
