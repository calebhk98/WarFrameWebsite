## Overview

Dagonic is a rare void-touched material harvested from Zariman Ten Zero's most dangerous regions. This crystalline substance forms through void corruption of mundane matter, possessing unique properties that enable advanced crafting applications. High rarity classification reflects both scarcity and demanding acquisition requirements, making Dagonic a premium resource for players pursuing cutting-edge equipment upgrades.

## Where it drops

Dagonic appears exclusively in high-difficulty Void Cascade missions within Zariman, particularly from elite void-touched enemy units and environmental caches found in void-corrupted chambers. Lower mission difficulties produce negligible quantities; dedicated farmers prioritize maximum-difficulty cascades for reliable acquisition. Void fissure event rotations featuring Zariman amplify drop rates through void-enhanced loot mechanics.

## Used for

Advanced operator equipment blueprints incorporate Dagonic as primary material components. These recipes require substantial quantities, incentivizing dedicated farming campaigns during favorable farming periods. Certain warframe augment mods and void-enhanced equipment modifications depend on Dagonic availability, making sustained supply chains valuable for endgame progression.
