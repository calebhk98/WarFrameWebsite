## Overview

Mood Crystal is a gem-type resource from the Warframe 1999 content update, tied to the Hollvania open world and the Hex syndicate. WFCD describes it as "sleek, stylish, and soothing," identifying it as a crafted gem material used in WF1999 content. It drops from high-tier Hollvania WF1999 Bounty rewards at the Level 85-90 tier, making it an endgame resource requiring substantial mission difficulty to acquire. Mood Crystal is used in Hex syndicate crafting and WF1999 equipment manufacturing.

## Where it drops

Mood Crystal drops in Rotation C of the Hollvania WF1999 Bounty at Level 85-90 difficulty per WFCD data. This is a high-level bounty tier requiring well-modded loadouts capable of handling level 85-90 enemies. Running the full 5-stage bounty for Rotation C rewards is the primary acquisition method. The high difficulty tier means this resource is inaccessible to early-game players and is intended as a reward for experienced players engaging with WF1999 endgame content.

## Used for

Mood Crystal is used in Hex syndicate crafting recipes and WF1999 equipment blueprints associated with the Hollvania content update. It is a component in crafting WF1999-exclusive weapons and Warframe augment systems accessible through the Hex faction vendor. Players progressing through WF1999 content should accumulate Mood Crystals during high-tier bounty runs to avoid crafting bottlenecks.

## Tips

Run the Level 85-90 Hollvania WF1999 Bounty with a squad to improve completion speed and survivability at the high enemy level. Aim for full 5-stage completions to access Rotation C rewards reliably. Prioritize frames and loadouts tuned for Steel Path equivalent difficulty, as enemies at this tier have heavy armor and high health pools. Resource Boosters apply to bounty drop rewards, increasing yield when active.

## Notes

Mood Crystal is not tradeable through Warframe.market. Access requires completing the WF1999 quest chain to unlock the Hollvania open world and reaching sufficient standing with the Hex syndicate to access high-tier bounties. The Level 85-90 bounty tier is among the most demanding open-world content in the game at the time of this writing.
