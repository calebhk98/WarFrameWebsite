## Overview

Pyrol is a tarnished, brittle mineral ore found exclusively on the Plains of Eidolon on Earth. It forms in red mineral deposits scattered across the open world and also drops from Tusk Thumpers, the large Grineer mechanized walkers that patrol the plains. Pyrol is a primary ingredient in Pyrotic Alloy and Tempered Bapholite, two refined materials used in Cetus-based crafting. Acquiring Ostron Visitor rank with the Cetus faction requires 40 Pyrol as part of the rank-up offering.

Pyrol is specific to the Plains of Eidolon and is not found in other open-world zones such as Orb Vallis, Cambion Drift, or Zariman. New players encounter it during early Cetus missions when gathering resources for syndicate ranking or alloy crafting.

## Where it drops

Mine Pyrol from red mineral veins visible across the Plains of Eidolon using any mining drill obtained from Ostron merchants in Cetus. Red veins appear as reddish crystal outcroppings on the ground and rock faces throughout the plains. The Nosam Cutter or any upgraded mining tool improves yield efficiency. Mining sessions during daytime hours provide better visibility for locating red vein deposits.

Tusk Thumpers also drop Pyrol on defeat. The standard Tusk Thumper drops at approximately 16.7% per kill; Tusk Thumper Bulls drop at 25.0%; Tusk Thumper Domas (the largest variant) drop at 33.4%. Thumpers spawn at fixed locations throughout the plains and can be summoned by running over the surface for extended periods.

## Used for

Pyrol is a prerequisite material for two refined alloys. Pyrotic Alloy, crafted in the Foundry, requires 20 Pyrol per batch of 20. Tempered Bapholite similarly requires 20 Pyrol per batch alongside Bapholite ore. Both refined materials are used in Cetus-tier weapon and component blueprints. Achieving Ostron Visitor rank (the first syndicate rank with the Cetus syndicate) requires gifting 40 Pyrol among other resources to Konzu.

## Tips

Equip a Thief's Wit or Animal Instinct mod to help locate mining veins more easily on the plains minimap. Focus mining circuits on rock faces near the rocky outcrops south of the Gara Toht Lake for high red vein density. Bringing a resource booster doubles ore yield from each mining attempt. If Thumper combat is preferred, bring a weapon with Radiation damage to strip Thumper armor efficiently, then collect Pyrol from the wreckage.

## Notes

Pyrol cannot be traded between players. WFCD confirms `tradable: false`. Pyrol is not obtainable from standard star chart missions, Void Fissures, or other open world zones. It is exclusively a Plains of Eidolon resource.
