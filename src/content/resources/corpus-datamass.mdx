## Overview

Corpus Datamass is an encrypted Corpus intelligence container used as a crafting component for the Corpus Void Key. WFCD describes it as "A Corpus datamass containing encrypted intel. Crafting component required to create a CORPUS VOID KEY." The datamass physically represents a Corpus data storage unit -- a compact information device containing mission-critical encrypted intelligence. In the early and mid-game context of Warframe's mission structure, Void Keys were the original access system for Void missions before the Riven and Relic systems replaced them.

The Corpus Datamass is now primarily a legacy crafting resource, relevant to players completing old Void Key blueprints or accumulating materials for collections. Its presence in the loot table of Corpus missions is a remnant of Warframe's pre-Relic reward structure.

## Where it drops

Corpus Datamass drops from Corpus enemies across multiple planets in the Corpus-controlled star chart. Enemy kills and container loot during Corpus missions yield datamasses as secondary drops. Extended Corpus missions on planets like Neptune, Europa, and Venus provide the highest enemy density per hour, maximizing datamass accumulation through volume of enemies killed. Corpus Spy missions, where datamass-style objectives are naturally thematic, can also yield them from mission rewards and environmental containers.

## Used for

The primary historical use of Corpus Datamass is in crafting Corpus Void Keys -- the access items for the original Void mission structure. With the modern Relic and Void Fissure system replacing Void Keys, the datamass has limited contemporary relevance outside of niche crafting or collection purposes. Players who obtain old Void Key blueprints through legacy content can still craft them using Corpus Datamasses. The item may also serve minor objectives in mission-specific scenarios involving Corpus intelligence data.

## Tips

- Corpus Datamasses accumulate naturally through normal Corpus mission play; dedicated farming is rarely necessary unless pursuing a specific Void Key blueprint.
- For legacy Void Key purposes, run extended Corpus survival or defense missions on Neptune or Europa where enemy density is highest and container spawns are frequent.
- Corpus Spy missions on Lua or other high-tier nodes yield datamasses alongside intelligence-objective mission rewards.
- If a Void Key blueprint is the goal, check whether the required datamass quantity is small enough to accumulate from a single extended session rather than across multiple farming trips.

## Notes

The Corpus Datamass predates the current Relic and Void Fissure system by several years and is primarily encountered as a legacy drop. Most contemporary Warframe progression does not require Corpus Datamasses, making them secondary farming targets during normal Corpus mission play. Players who accumulate excess datamasses during natural Corpus progression can safely store them without concern for overflow, as they have no stacklimit issue and take up minimal inventory space.
