## Overview

Corpus Datamass is a container of encrypted Corpus intelligence data obtained from Corpus faction enemies on Neptune. This encrypted data serves as essential material for intelligence processing and data-based equipment blueprints. The datamass represents the convergence of Corpus technological sophistication and information control systems.

## Where it drops

Corpus Datamass drops from Corpus faction enemies across Neptune and Corpus-controlled tilesets with consistent frequency. The resource appears in enemy drops and specialized data node containers. Extended mission durations increase enemy spawns and container creation, improving datamass yields per hour.

## Best locations

Neptune excavation and survival missions provide optimal Corpus Datamass yields compared to other Neptune mission types. Extended sessions maximize enemy encounters and container spawning. Public squad farming benefits from completion multipliers and faster mission completion. Prioritizing longer missions increases per-hour efficiency.

## Used for

Corpus Datamass is consumed in intelligence processing and data equipment blueprints with typical quantities ranging from 15 to 40 units per craft. Players pursuing data-focused builds encounter datamass as required components. Banking reserves enables rapid equipment crafting.
