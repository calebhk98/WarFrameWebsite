## Overview

ISOs are crystalline currency units generated during void-energy cascades throughout the Zariman Ten-Zero. These ethereal tokens represent concentrated void-manipulated value and serve as the primary trading currency within void-aligned systems. ISOs are distinct from standard credits and represent specialized economic transactions unique to void commerce. These materials represent a significant component of the Zariman crafting ecosystem. Understanding their properties and acquisition methods is essential for successful progression through advanced void content. The materials maintain their void-energy saturation indefinitely when stored properly.

## Where it drops

Void Cascade missions reward ISOs upon completion, with higher difficulty tiers yielding additional quantities. Void rift exploration generates ISOs from container harvesting and completion bonuses. Extended fissure runs generate continuous ISO supplies as mission-reward scaling progresses. Dedicated farming routes through void rifts optimize crest collection efficiency. Enemy escalation levels directly correlate with improved drop chances. Container-specific farming provides reliable secondary income streams.

## Best locations

Run higher-difficulty Void Cascade missions on the Zariman for maximum ISO rewards per mission runtime. Endless survival rotations generate continuous ISO multipliers based on escalation level. Team-based void fissures generate bonus ISOs through squad completion bonuses. Experienced void farmers recommend focusing on high-difficulty variants for maximum yield per mission. Endurance farming strategies prove superior to rapid-clear approaches for these resources. Team coordination dramatically improves efficiency metrics.

## Used for

ISOs purchase void-enhancement gear, Zariman cosmetics, and specialized equipment from Zariman void vendors. These currency units enable players to customize Void-Rig functionality and acquire exclusive void-aligned cosmetic options unavailable through standard credit transactions. These crafting materials feed directly into progression gating systems. Maintaining adequate supplies prevents progression bottlenecks. Future content updates may require increased quantities.

## Tips

Farm ISOs during bonus ISO event periods when double or triple rewards are active, maximizing currency generation per hour. Run endless void fissure missions to reach high escalation levels triggering ISO multiplier mechanics. Team play generates additional bonus ISO payouts. Resource boosters during event periods multiply yields exponentially. Optimized warframe loadouts reduce mission times significantly. Squad coordination yields superior results to solo farming attempts.

## Notes

ISOs cannot be converted to standard credits but serve as the exclusive currency for void-specific purchasing. Unlike tradeable resources, ISO supplies accumulate exclusively through active farming, not through market transactions. Supply accumulation should match consumption rates for sustainable crafting. Storage management becomes important at advanced progression levels.
