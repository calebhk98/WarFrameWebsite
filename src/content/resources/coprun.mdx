## Overview

Coprun is a soft, malleable metal mined from Red Mining Veins across the Plains of Eidolon. WFCD confirms its source as "Plains of Eidolon (Earth) from Red Mining Veins," establishing it as a baseline metallic ore within the Ostron crafting economy. Its softness and malleability make it suitable for forming the structural foundations of Cetus-crafted equipment rather than the harder alloy components needed for weapon edges or precision components. Coprun is the most common metallic resource from the Plains, functioning as a volume ingredient alongside Ferros in many recipes.

As a Red Vein product, Coprun is distinct from the gem-tier resources (Azurite, Crimzian, Devar) that come from Blue Veins. Red Veins consistently yield ore-type outputs (Coprun, Ferros) while Blue Veins produce gems.

## Where it drops

Coprun is extracted from Red Mining Veins found throughout the Plains of Eidolon. These appear as reddish-orange glowing nodes in rock faces and hillside outcrops. Any tier of Nosam Cutter can extract Coprun, though upgraded cutters produce more ore per successful extraction. The Plains Red Veins respawn when the instance reloads, so systematic mining routes through known Red Vein clusters produce consistent yields per Cetus visit. Enemy drops do not yield Coprun; mining is the exclusive acquisition method.

## Used for

Coprun feeds into Ostron equipment recipes accessible through the Cetus vendors. It is a common volume ingredient -- recipes use it in moderate-to-large quantities as a base metal rather than as a specialty component. Several early-to-mid Cetus weapon parts list Coprun alongside Ferros and gem materials. The Axidrol Alloy blueprint sold by Smokefinger on Fortuna converts Plains ores into alloy form, which may include Coprun-derived components in the conversion chain.

## Tips

- Red Mining Veins are concentrated in the hillside and cliff regions of the Plains of Eidolon; check community-sourced mining maps to plan efficient routes through high-density Red Vein areas.
- Coprun mines quickly with any cutter tier, so even a basic Nosam Cutter produces acceptable yields; save the Sunpoint Plasma Drill upgrade for gem farming where extraction precision matters more.
- Combine mining runs with Ferros collection from the same Red Veins -- both Coprun and Ferros appear in Red Vein loot, allowing both to accumulate in the same session.
- At night, mine alongside Cetus Wisp collection near water sources to make full use of the Plains session before returning to Cetus.

## Notes

Coprun is the most accessible of the Plains metallic resources, making it rarely a bottleneck in Ostron crafting. Players new to the Plains should prioritize acquiring the Nosam Cutter from Old Man Suumbaat before significant Coprun farming, as freehand extraction (without a cutter) is not possible. The resource cannot be traded and must be mined personally.
