## Overview

Cetus Wisps are ethereal, luminescent resources unique to the Plains of Eidolon open world on Earth. They appear as floating glowing orbs hovering just above the surface of water bodies scattered across the Plains. Cetus Wisps are a dedicated Eidolon-content resource required for crafting Focus Greater Lenses, Operator Amp components, and other Eidolon-hunting gear, making them essential for players progressing into the Operator focus system.

Unlike standard mission resources, Cetus Wisps are collected by physically approaching them in the open world -- they emit a distinctive glow and float in place until collected. They do not drop from enemies and cannot be found in containers.

## Acquisition

Cetus Wisps spawn near all water surfaces on the Plains of Eidolon: the lake near the Cetus gate, rivers flowing across the Plains, and small ponds scattered throughout the tileset. They appear during both day and night cycles on the Plains, though some players report slightly higher spawn density during the night cycle (Eidolon active period). Each Plains visit spawns a fixed number of Wisps across the map, and they respawn each time you re-enter the Plains.

The most efficient Wisp farm involves using a mobility frame (Titania, Zephyr, or Volt with speed mods) to cover the Plains rapidly and collect all visible Wisps before extracting and re-entering to reset spawns. Archwing (if unlocked) enables very fast travel across the large Plains tileset.

| Method | Wisps per run | Time per run |
|---|---|---|
| Ground run (mobility frame) | 4-8 | 5-10 minutes |
| Archwing sweep | 6-12 | 3-7 minutes |
| Bounty rotation B/C | 1-3 | 10-15 minutes |

## Used In

Cetus Wisps are required for Greater Focus Lens blueprints (used to upgrade focus schools with the Operator system). Operator Amp components from the Hok's Anvil vendor in Cetus require Cetus Wisps. Various Ostron crafting items traded at Cetus also use Cetus Wisps as ingredients. Eidolon Lures used in Teralyst hunts also require Cetus Wisps to construct.

## Strategy

The fastest method is to use Titania in her Razorwing form (she shrinks to fairy size and flies freely) to sweep water bodies across the Plains. Titania's flight speed combined with her small hitbox allows precise navigation near water surfaces for Wisp collection. Aim for a complete Plains sweep in under 5 minutes, extract, then re-enter for another run.

If you only need a small quantity (3-5 Wisps), a single Plains visit during a standard bounty run generates enough from incidental water body traversal.
