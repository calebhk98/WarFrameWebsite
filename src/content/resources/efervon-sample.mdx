## Overview

Efervon Sample represents chemical compound specimens extracted from Corpus laboratory facilities on Venus. These volatile chemical samples retain reactive properties enabling specialized crafting applications restricted to advanced Corpus technology assembly. Efervon acquisition requires accessing well-defended Corpus installation zones, establishing sampling as endgame-oriented farming activity.

## Where it drops

Venus Corpus missions featuring laboratory facility themes yield Efervon Samples through environment loot and enemy drops. Laboratory-specialized Corpus units including Scientists and Tech personnel prioritize sample drops; targeted elimination maximizes acquisition efficiency. Excavation-themed Venus missions provide concentrated sampling opportunities compared to standard mission types.

## Used for

Advanced Corpus technology blueprints incorporate Efervon as primary chemical component. Volatile compound weapons and specialized chemical-based systems depend on sustained Efervon supply. Experimental equipment modifications featuring chemical enhancement mechanics similarly leverage Efervon's reactive properties.

## Tips

Prioritize Venus laboratory-themed missions for concentrated sample acquisition. Run squad farming maintaining consistent enemy spawn rates throughout extended campaigns. Accumulate reserves during favorable farming periods supporting future technology construction objectives.
