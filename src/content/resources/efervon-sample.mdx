## Overview

Efervon Sample is a corrosive material from the WF1999 game mode in Höllvania. WFCD describes it as "a sample of the corrosive substance that Scaldra use to purge Techrot," with a warning to "handle with care." The Scaldra are the military faction occupying Höllvania in 1999, and Efervon is the chemical agent they deploy to combat the Techrot infestation spreading through the city. The material is corrosive -- hazardous to handle -- but its anti-Techrot properties make it a valuable research resource. Players recover Efervon Samples from the battlefield after Scaldra operations, acquiring the material from the wreckage of combat between Scaldra forces and Techrot entities.

The dual nature of Efervon -- a Scaldra military weapon repurposed as a research material by The Hex -- reflects the morally complex relationships in the WF1999 narrative where former enemies' tools are recycled by the resistance.

## Where it drops

Efervon Samples drop from Höllvania bounties in the WF1999 game mode. Drop rates sourced from WFCD:

| Source | Drop Chance |
|---|---|
| Level 55-60 WF1999 Bounty, Rotation C | 14.63% |
| Level 105-110 WF1999 Bounty, Rotation C | 14.00% |

Both available bounty tiers offer very similar drop rates (14.00-14.63%), making the easier Level 55-60 bounty nearly as efficient per drop as the hardest available tier. Players with lower-power loadouts can farm Efervon Samples from the lower-tier bounty without significant efficiency loss compared to the Level 105-110 tier.

## Used for

Efervon Samples are resources within The Hex syndicate economy in Höllvania. They feed into WF1999 crafting recipes and The Hex standing progression. The anti-Techrot chemistry of Efervon makes it relevant to WF1999 equipment that deals with Techrot containment, research into the Techrot organism's biological vulnerabilities, or weaponization of the Scaldra's own counter-infestation methods. Players pursuing the full range of WF1999 blueprints will encounter Efervon Samples as a crafting ingredient.

## Tips

- Run Level 55-60 WF1999 Bounties for Efervon Samples; the 14.63% Rotation C drop rate is nearly identical to the highest-tier bounty's 14.00%, making the lower tier more time-efficient due to faster enemy levels.
- Combine Efervon Sample farming with Experimental Arc-Relay and other Höllvania Rotation C targets in the same bounty sessions.
- Efervon Samples appear at Rotation C -- complete the full bounty chain to reach this rotation rather than extracting early.
- Higher-difficulty WF1999 content features Scaldra and Techrot in higher concentrations; area-of-effect Warframes improve kill efficiency in denser enemy encounters.

## Notes

Efervon Samples are exclusive to Höllvania WF1999 content and cannot be obtained from standard planetary nodes, Railjack, or open worlds outside Höllvania. The material's hazardous handling note (WFCD: "handle with care") is a narrative touch reflecting the corrosive nature of the Scaldra's chemical weapons. Players who have not unlocked WF1999 content through its quest entry point do not have access to Höllvania and therefore cannot farm Efervon Samples.
