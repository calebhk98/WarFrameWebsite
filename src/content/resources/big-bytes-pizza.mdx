## Overview

Big Bytes Pizza is a miscellaneous resource found within the Zariman Ten Zero tileset. Thematically connected to the Zariman's pre-void-corruption civilian infrastructure, this item represents decadent food supplies preserved in the corrupted void. It serves both as a collectible resource and a trading commodity for various Zariman transactions, adding depth to the syndicate economy.

## Where it drops

Big Bytes Pizza is recovered from void fissure cache drops and treasure containers scattered throughout Zariman missions. Fortune favors thorough exploration; players who systematically check suspicious wall areas and void rifts discover these items more reliably. The drop rate scales with mission difficulty, encouraging higher-tier objective attempts for increased rewards.

## Used for

This resource is primarily utilized for cosmetic trading and exotic equipment acquisition within the Zariman economy. Players barter Big Bytes Pizza to specialized vendors for unique cosmetics reflecting the civilization aspects of Zariman lore. The item also serves as currency for trading with other players seeking specific cosmetic configurations.
