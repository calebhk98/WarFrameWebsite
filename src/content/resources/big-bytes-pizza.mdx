## Overview

Big Bytes Pizza is a rare Hex Treasure from the WF1999 game mode set in 1999 Höllvania. WFCD describes it as "Who doesn't like pizza? Trade this Hex Treasure with Lettie to earn standing with The Hex," and classifies it internally as a rare HexDogTag item tied to Lettie's vendor. The pizza box represents the consumer food culture of the late 1990s embedded in Höllvania's retro-futuristic setting, where fast food chains like "Big Bytes" (a pun combining digital "bytes" with pizza bites) are part of the civilian infrastructure left behind in the Void-frozen 1999 timeline.

Big Bytes Pizza is the rare-tier Hex Treasure in Lettie's trade pool, making it the most sought-after item for Lettie's standing but the least commonly found.

## Where it drops

Big Bytes Pizza appears in the Rotation C reward pool of the Level 105-110 Höllvania bounty at 4% chance per WFCD data. This is a low drop rate at the highest bounty tier, making Big Bytes Pizza the most difficult Hex Treasure to farm consistently. Environmental caches and exploration containers within Höllvania missions provide a supplementary find chance during standard bounty runs, though the primary targeted farming route is the Level 105-110 Rotation C slot.

## Used for

Big Bytes Pizza is traded with Lettie in The Hex syndicate hub to earn standing. As the rare-tier Hex Treasure for Lettie, it likely awards significantly more standing per trade than common Hex Treasures (such as Cheddar Crowns Cereal). Players who want to maximize Lettie's standing efficiency will prioritize Big Bytes Pizza over the more common Hex Treasures, despite its lower drop rate. The standing economy with The Hex syndicate gates access to Höllvania's most desirable equipment blueprints.

## Tips

- Run the Level 105-110 Höllvania bounty when your build can handle the enemy level efficiently; the full chain completion is necessary to access Rotation C where the pizza appears.
- The 4% drop rate means multiple runs before a single pizza drop should be expected; plan several Level 105-110 bounty sessions rather than a single session.
- Combine Big Bytes Pizza hunting with Experimental Arc-Relay farming at the same bounty tier to maximize the value of each high-difficulty run.
- Explore thoroughly during high-tier bounty runs; environmental caches may contain Big Bytes Pizza as a bonus find outside the rotation reward tables.

## Notes

Big Bytes Pizza is not a crafting component and cannot be consumed -- it functions purely as a Lettie standing currency. Its rarity within the Level 105-110 Rotation C pool reflects its value within The Hex's social economy. The Hex syndicate access requires completing the prerequisite WF1999 quest chain, and the Level 105-110 bounty requires advanced combat capability. New Höllvania players should focus on lower-tier bounties for initial Hex standing before targeting the pizza's high-tier source.
