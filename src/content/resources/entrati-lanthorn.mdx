## Overview

Entrati Lanthorn is a ceremonial resource from the Entrati family's ancient Orokin-era practices. WFCD describes it as "a ceremonial beacon used to map Void locations on to real space" -- a navigational instrument that projects Void coordinates onto physical space, allowing travelers to chart pathways through the Void. The Entrati family, who maintained a research outpost on Deimos before the Infestation, used these lanthorns as part of their work studying the Void and the Tenno. The item drops from both Zariman Ten Zero and Albrecht's Laboratories (Deimos), connecting it to both the Zariman ship's Void exposure and the Entrati family's Deimos research.

Entrati Lanthorn is a rare Rotation C or B reward with sub-6.67% drop rates, making it one of the more time-intensive materials to accumulate in quantity.

## Where it drops

Entrati Lanthorns drop from Zariman Ten Zero bounties and specific Albrecht's Laboratories (Deimos) missions. Drop rates sourced from WFCD:

| Source | Rotation | Drop Chance |
|---|---|---|
| Zariman Ten Zero Level 50-55 Bounty | C | 4.35% |
| Zariman Ten Zero Level 60-65 Bounty | C | 3.39% |
| Zariman Everview Arc Void Flood | B | 6.67% |
| Deimos/Cambire Alchemy | B | 5.00% |
| Deimos/Munio Defense | B | 5.00% |
| Deimos/Persto Survival | B | 5.00% |

Zariman Void Flood missions offer the best single-source drop rate at 6.67% from Rotation B. The Deimos Albrecht's Laboratories missions provide 5.00% each across three different mission types, making them a viable alternative for players already farming Deimos content.

## Used for

Entrati Lanthorns are crafting components in recipes tied to the Necraloid syndicate (Cephalon Entrati's research faction) and Albrecht's Laboratories content. Players pursuing the Necraloid's highest-tier offerings for Necramech parts and the Helminth Segment require Entrati Lanthorns. The Void-navigation function of the lanthorn makes it relevant to recipes for equipment interacting with Void energy -- consistent with the Entrati family's research focus on Void phenomena and Tenno operations.

## Tips

- Zariman Void Flood missions offer the highest single-source drop rate (6.67%) and are fast enough to run repeatedly; Rotation B rewards are reached after the second conduit capture.
- Combine Entrati Lanthorn farming with Zariman standing accumulation from Void Flood missions to maximize efficiency per session.
- Running Deimos Albrecht's Laboratories bounties when Lanthorns are needed alongside other Deimos materials (Echo Voca, Bellow Voca, Entrati Obols) reduces farming fragmentation across game zones.
- Entrati Lanthorns are tradeable on warframe.market if accumulated stock is insufficient and dedicated farming time is limited.

## Notes

Entrati Lanthorn rewards appear in Rotation B of Zariman Void Flood and Rotation C of Zariman bounties -- not Rotation A. Running missions only to Rotation A will miss the primary drop windows. Full mission completion to the designated rotation is required to access the drop table. The 3.39-6.67% drop range means multiple runs per item are statistically expected; plan for 15-30 runs to accumulate 5-10 Lanthorns depending on luck.
