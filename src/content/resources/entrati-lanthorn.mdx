## Overview

Entrati Lanthorn represents specialized bioluminescent technology crafted by Entrati family artisans. These void-illumination devices serve functional and aesthetic purposes throughout Cambion Drift operations. Lanthorn acquisition through syndicate participation enables access to specialized Entrati equipment and cosmetic customizations reflecting family craftsmanship traditions.

## Where it drops

Cambion Drift bounties at all difficulty tiers yield Entrati Lanthorns upon completion. Higher-tier bounties produce proportionally greater Lanthorn quantities. Entrati syndicate quest completion similarly rewards substantial Lanthorn quantities, creating flexible acquisition pathways.

## Used for

Entrati syndicate vendors exchange Lanthorns for specialized equipment blueprints and cosmetic customizations. Void-light technology systems incorporate Lanthorns for illumination and void-attuned functionality. Certain warframe modifications feature Lanthorn-dependent enhancement mechanics.

## Tips

Balance Lanthorn farming across Entrati family vendors (Mother, Daughter, Father) ensuring comprehensive equipment access. Combine bounty farming with void fissure relic progression for dual-objective efficiency. Accumulate Lanthorns during favorable event periods.

## Notes

Bioluminescent craftsmanship reflects Entrati family heritage emphasizing void-light manipulation and biological-technical fusion.
