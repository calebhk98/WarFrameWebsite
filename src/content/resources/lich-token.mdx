## Overview

Lich Token is a special currency item generated when a player converts a Kuva Lich -- a powerful Grineer adversary created through the Kuva Lich system. When a player chooses to convert a Kuva Lich rather than vanquish it, the Lich becomes an ally, and a Lich Token representing that Lich is placed in the player's inventory. This token encodes the specific Kuva Lich's weapon, ephemera, and attributes.

The Kuva Lich system involves tracking down and eliminating a personal nemesis across Grineer-controlled planets. Players must identify the correct sequence of Requiem mods to dispatch the Lich through repeated parazon attempts. A converted Lich becomes a spectre ally, but its token can be transferred to another player who wants that specific Lich's weapon or ephemera without hunting it from scratch.

## Where it drops

Lich Tokens are not found in mission drop tables and do not drop from enemies or containers. A Lich Token is created only by successfully converting a Kuva Lich using the correct Requiem mod sequence and choosing the "convert" option at the final confrontation. The Lich must have been created by that player -- Liches are personal adversaries tied to specific accounts and star chart progression. The confrontation takes place in a dedicated mission node on the Kuva Fortress or any Grineer planet where the Lich has claimed territory.

## Used for

A Lich Token is used exclusively to trade the associated converted Kuva Lich to another player through the in-game Trading Post. The receiving player takes over the Lich as an ally spectre. This system allows players to acquire specific Kuva weapons or ephemeras carried by that Lich without personally hunting it. Once the token is traded, the original player loses the Lich and its associated weapon, while the recipient gains control.

WFCD lists `tradable: false` at the item level, reflecting that the token itself cannot be sold in the standard trade UI; the transfer is facilitated through a dedicated Lich trade interface rather than standard item trading.

## Tips

Convert a Kuva Lich carrying a desired weapon -- for example, a Kuva Nukor with high bonus damage -- then trade the token to a player willing to pay in platinum. Research which Kuva weapons are currently in demand before initiating a Lich hunt to maximize trade value. The Kuva Lich's weapon bonus percentage and ephemera status both affect trade desirability.

## Notes

Lich Tokens cannot be placed in standard trading; the dedicated Kuva Lich trade window must be used. Each Kuva Lich carries one of many possible Kuva weapon variants with a bonus damage percentage ranging from 25% to 60%. The Technocyte Coda Token functions identically but represents a converted Technocyte Coda (Infested adversary) rather than a Kuva Lich.
