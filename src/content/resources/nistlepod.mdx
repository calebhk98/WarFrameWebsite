## Overview

Nistlepod is an Orokin-engineered plant resource described by WFCD as used by "the Nistlebrush to trap and kill prey," found on the "Plains of Eidolon on Earth" and within Lua and Orokin Derelict architecture. The Nistlebrush's carnivorous behavior -- using its pods to trap prey -- reflects the Orokin's biological engineering philosophy of creating organisms with functional, often predatory roles. The Nistlepod itself is the individual seed or growth pod structure that the Nistlebrush deploys in hunting, giving it a specialized biological structure that makes it valuable for Apothic crafting.

Nistlepod does not drop from enemies. It grows as a harvestable plant node in specific tileset areas that match the Orokin biome aesthetic where the Nistlebrush naturally occurs.

## Where it drops

Nistlepod plants grow in the Orokin-architecture areas of Lua and the Orokin Derelict tileset. On Lua, they appear in outdoor areas and corridors where visible plant growth is present within Orokin structures. Lua Mobile Defense and Exterminate missions allow thorough tileset exploration, making them efficient for Nistlepod collection. The Orokin Derelict tileset's organic-Orokin hybrid corridors also contain Nistlepod plants in areas where the Infestation has partially consumed the Orokin architecture. A Codex Scanner or Synthesis Scanner highlights plant nodes within detection range.

| Location | Mission types |
|---|---|
| Lua | Mobile Defense, Exterminate, Spy |
| Orokin Derelict | Survival, Defense, Exterminate |

A Conclave Variant Team Annihilation mission also offers 10x Nistlepods at 3.16% per Rotation A/B as an alternative source for competitive mode players.

## Used for

Nistlepod is a required ingredient for crafting Apothics -- consumable items used to trigger Infested Catalyst encounters in Hive Sabotage missions. The Infested Catalyst blueprint from Hive Sabotage is needed for constructing the Helminth Infested room in the Clan Dojo, which is a prerequisite for the Warframe Subsuming system through the Helminth. Players who want the Helminth system must complete Hive Sabotage at least once, making Nistlepod a mandatory farming target. Clan Dojo Bio Lab research projects that require organic plant components also consume Nistlepod.

## Tips

- Equip a Codex Scanner or Synthesis Scanner when entering Lua or Orokin Derelict missions; the scanner minimap overlay shows plant nodes within its detection radius.
- Lua Exterminate missions are fast and allow broad exploration of the tileset for plant harvesting while simultaneously completing the mission objective.
- Bring a fast-movement frame (Volt, Gauss, Titania) to efficiently cover the Lua or Derelict tileset between plant node locations without losing time to slow traversal.
- Confirm the full Apothic recipe before farming -- there are three Apothic types and each requires different plant combinations; Nistlepod appears in at least one but not all.

## Notes

Nistlepod does not respawn within the same mission instance. Exiting and re-entering the mission resets plant node positions. Because Apothic crafting requires multiple plant types across different tilesets (Lua, Orokin Derelict, and Plains of Eidolon), plan harvesting trips to cover all required plants across different missions rather than farming each type separately.
