## Overview

Nistlepod is a plant-type resource found growing in the Orokin architecture of Lua and the Orokin Derelict. It appears as a distinctive plant cluster that can be harvested by approaching and interacting with it. Nistlepod is a specialized resource consumed primarily by Apothic crafting (used to access Infested Catalyst missions for the Hive Sabotage quest line) and by certain weapon blueprints.

As a plant resource, Nistlepod does not drop from enemies but is found as a harvestable node in specific tileset areas. Players unfamiliar with plant locations often overlook Nistlepods during normal mission runs.

## Acquisition

Nistlepod plants grow in the Orokin-architecture areas of Lua -- specifically in the outdoor areas and corridors with visible plant growth. Lua Mobile Defense and Exterminate missions are good for Nistlepod collection because they allow thorough tileset exploration. The Orokin Derelict tileset also contains Nistlepod plants in its organic-Orokin hybrid corridors.

| Location | Notes |
|---|---|
| Lua missions | Outdoor and corridor areas |
| Orokin Derelict | Organic corridor sections |

## Used In

Nistlepod is required for crafting Apothics, which are key items used in the Hive Sabotage mission variant for accessing Infested Catalyst blueprint rewards. Certain weapon blueprints from the Corpus line and Clan Dojo Bio Lab research projects also require Nistlepod as an organic component.

## Strategy

Collect Nistlepod opportunistically during Lua missions and Orokin Derelict runs. Because it is a harvestable plant rather than a standard drop, dedicated farming requires methodically searching Orokin-architecture rooms on each mission visit. Bring a frame with high sprint speed (Volt, Gauss) to efficiently cover the tileset.
