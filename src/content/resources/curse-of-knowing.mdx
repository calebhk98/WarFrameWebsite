## Overview

Curse Of Knowing is one of three curse-type resources tied to the Entrati Lab storyline on Deimos, alongside Curse Of Seeing and Curse Of Hearing. WFCD describes it as containing "knowledge that will help Fibonacci prevent enemies from breaking free of The Sanctum," and instructs players to "Feed curses to the Jahu Gargoyle in your Clan Dojo to re-calibrate its defensive capabilities." This dual function -- storyline currency and Dojo structure feed -- is unique among resource types and ties directly into the Entrati Lab's narrative about Fibonacci, the enigmatic research intelligence managing containment within Albrecht's Laboratories.

The three curse types (Knowing, Seeing, Hearing) represent different aspects of awareness and perception, thematically linked to Fibonacci's need for enhanced sensory and intellectual surveillance within the Sanctum.

## Where it drops

Curse Of Knowing drops from the Albrecht's Laboratories bounty missions on Deimos. Like other Entrati Lab currencies, curses appear in the rotation reward pools across the bounty tiers available within Albrecht's Laboratories. The specific drop rates vary by bounty tier and rotation slot; higher-tier bounties offer curses at better rates or in larger quantities. Enemy drops within the Laboratories tileset may also yield curses as secondary loot during mission play.

## Used for

Curse Of Knowing has two documented uses according to WFCD. First, it helps Fibonacci keep enemies from breaking free of The Sanctum within the Entrati Lab narrative context, functioning as a progression item tied to the Albrecht's Laboratories story content. Second, and more mechanically relevant, curses are fed to the Jahu Gargoyle within the Clan Dojo to re-calibrate its defensive capabilities. The Jahu Gargoyle is a Dojo decoration with interactive mechanics that responds to curse offerings. Players in active Clans will find all three curse types needed to keep the Gargoyle properly calibrated.

## Tips

- Collect all three curse types (Knowing, Seeing, Hearing) simultaneously during Albrecht's Laboratories farming sessions, since they drop from the same bounty pool and serve related purposes.
- Prioritize completing the full Albrecht's Laboratories bounty chain to access all rotation reward slots where curses appear.
- Coordinate with Clan members to ensure curses are shared and submitted to the Jahu Gargoyle collectively rather than hoarding them individually.
- Higher-tier Entrati Lab bounties at Level 95-100 and 115-120 offer improved curse drop rates per rotation compared to lower tiers.

## Notes

The three curses represent distinct aspects of the Fibonacci surveillance system: Knowing deals with intellectual knowledge and recognition, Seeing with visual surveillance, and Hearing with auditory detection. Together they form a complete awareness system for Sanctum security as described in the Entrati Lab lore. Curses are not tradeable between players and must be personally earned through Deimos participation. Non-Clan players cannot contribute to the Jahu Gargoyle mechanic but still receive curses as part of normal Entrati Lab progression.
