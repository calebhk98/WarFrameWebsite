## Overview

Damaged Necramech Weapon Pod is a modular weapon system component for Necramech construction. WFCD describes it as "A broken weapon system intended for a Necramech," sourced from "Enemy Necramechs found in Isolation Vault Bounties on the Cambion Drift (Deimos), and purchased from Loid in the Necralisk after completing Isolation Vaults." The weapon pod housing contains the mounting and targeting infrastructure for the Necramech's autonomous combat weapon systems, distinct from the frame pod (which contains the Operator interface) and the barrel/receiver components (which form the weapon itself).

Father in the Necralisk stocks Damaged Necramech Weapon Pods at neutral standing with 100% availability, providing a reliable vendor route alongside the Isolation Vault combat source.

## Where it drops

Damaged Necramech Weapon Pods drop from enemy Necramechs encountered in Isolation Vault Bounties on Deimos. Father's vendor in the Necralisk offers them at neutral standing (100% availability per WFCD), making direct purchase with Entrati tokens a reliable alternative to RNG drops. Loid in the Necralisk also sells Weapon Pods after sufficient Isolation Vault completion unlocks his inventory. The combination of two vendor sources and direct enemy drops gives Weapon Pods more acquisition flexibility than some other Necramech components.

## Used for

Damaged Necramech Weapon Pods are required for Necramech weapon blueprints including the Cortege (heavy cannon weapon) and Morgha. Both of Warframe's Necramech types (Bonewidow and Voidrig) have weapon systems that require the Weapon Pod component. Multiple pods per weapon blueprint are common, so maintaining a stockpile reduces crafting delays when pursuing the full Necramech weapon arsenal.

## Tips

- Father's vendor at neutral Entrati standing provides Weapon Pods at 100% availability; check his inventory during Necralisk visits and purchase pods with Entrati tokens to supplement RNG farming.
- Running Isolation Vaults for Necramech frame components (Casing, Engine, Pod) simultaneously generates Weapon Pod drops from enemy Necramech kills, enabling parallel accumulation.
- Unlock Loid's Necralisk shop through Isolation Vault completion; having both Father's and Loid's vendor routes available maximizes purchase options per Necralisk visit.
- The Weapon Pod is distinct from the frame Pod used in Necramech chassis construction; confirm the correct component type is in stock before purchasing.

## Notes

The Damaged Necramech Weapon Pod and the standard Damaged Necramech Pod are separate items serving different blueprint requirements. The Weapon Pod is part of the weapon system; the frame Pod is part of the chassis. Players building both a Necramech frame and its weapons need both pod types in their farming plan. Father's vendor at neutral standing is the most accessible early-game acquisition route while Isolation Vault RNG accumulates.
