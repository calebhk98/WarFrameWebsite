## Overview

Morphics are an uncommon resource representing polymorphic organic-metallic compounds found in Grineer and Corpus installations. They drop on Mercury, Mars, Europa, and Pluto and are required for warframe neuroptics blueprints and the Orokin Reactor blueprint. Like Neurodes and Gallium, Morphics are in the category of "uncommon resources that become a mild bottleneck" for active builders.

New players encounter Morphics early via Captain Vor boss drops on Mercury, providing a guaranteed small supply. The Jackal boss on Venus is another reliable early source. For larger quantities, Mars missions provide a moderate drop rate.

## Acquisition

Morphics are obtained from boss kills and standard enemy drops on their applicable planets. Running Capture missions on Mars is an efficient method because of short mission time and guaranteed completion rewards that sometimes include Morphics. The Jackal boss on Venus has a small guaranteed Morphics drop per kill.

| Source | Rate | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Mars any mission | Low-moderate | Consistent passive drops |
| Jackal boss (Venus) | Guaranteed small drop | Reliable for 1-2 per run |
| Mercury missions | Low | Early game access |

## Used In

Morphics are required for many warframe neuroptics blueprints and the Orokin Reactor crafting blueprint. Several weapon blueprints also require 1 Morphics as an ingredient. Because Orokin Reactors themselves require Morphics, players who craft Reactor blueprints should maintain a Morphics buffer.

## Strategy

Accumulate Morphics passively through Mars and Mercury progression. If you run low, a few quick Capture missions on Mars provide Morphics alongside other Mars resources. The Jackal boss is a reliable source for small top-up quantities when you only need 2-3 Morphics quickly.
