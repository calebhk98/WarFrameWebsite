## Overview

Damaged Necramech Casing represents armor plating recovered from deactivated or defeated Necramech units throughout Deimos and void-touched regions. These structural components form the foundational building blocks for constructing functional Necramech frames and serve secondary applications in advanced railjack modifications. Recovery requires defeating or dismantling void-corrupted mechanized units, positioning casing acquisition as dangerous endgame endeavor reserved for well-equipped Tenno.

## Where it drops

Necramech units scattered across Deimos and void fissure missions drop casing upon defeat or environmental destruction. Zariman void creatures and corrupted mechanical entities yield additional casing quantities; environmental hazard navigation rewards thorough explorers with caches hidden throughout void-corrupted chambers. Drop frequency scales with enemy rarity; elite Necramech variants prioritize casing drops over standard loot.

## Used for

Necramech frame construction requires substantial casing quantities as primary structural component. Railjack command module upgrades incorporate casing for enhanced hull integrity and protective systems. Additional applications span operator equipment modifications and advanced void-themed upgrades unavailable through conventional crafting methods.

## Tips

Run Deimos void fissure missions to combine casing farming with relic progression objectives. Bring Necramech-effective loadouts optimized for rapid unit elimination. Coordinate farming with squad members to maintain consistent enemy spawn rates and casing drop frequency throughout extended farming sessions.

## Notes

"Damaged" classification reflects authentic scavenged condition; crafting processes restore structural integrity through advanced fabrication techniques. The Orokin origin of Necramech technology ensures materials remain extraordinarily valuable for players pursuing complete Necramech arsenal development. Resource stockpiling during favorable farming periods supports long-term Necramech construction ambitions.
