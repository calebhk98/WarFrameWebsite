## Overview

Damaged Necramech Casing is the exterior armor shell component required for Necramech frame construction. WFCD describes it as "The damaged remains of a Necramech supporting frame," sourced from "Enemy Necramechs found in Isolation Vault Bounties on the Cambion Drift, and purchased from Loid in the Necralisk after completing Isolation Vaults." The supporting frame designation indicates this is the external structural layer of the Necramech -- the visible plating that houses and protects all internal components. Recovering it from a defeated enemy Necramech means peeling apart the outer shell of a destroyed unit for salvage.

Like other Damaged Necramech components, the casing is available through Father's neutral-standing vendor in the Necralisk in addition to direct enemy drops.

## Where it drops

Damaged Necramech Casings drop from enemy Necramechs in Isolation Vault Bounties on the Cambion Drift. Father's vendor in the Necralisk stocks casings at neutral standing with 100% availability per WFCD drop data, providing a direct purchase route. Loid's Necralisk shop also carries casings once unlocked. The availability through two Necralisk vendors makes casings easier to supplement than components that rely purely on low-percentage bounty rotation drops.

## Used for

Damaged Necramech Casings are required for both the Bonewidow and Voidrig Necramech blueprints. Each Necramech frame needs a casing alongside the Engine, Pod, and weapon system components. The casing quantity required per blueprint depends on the specific blueprint's crafting cost. Players building their first Necramech should plan casing acquisition as part of the overall Deimos farming strategy alongside the other four Damaged Necramech frame components.

## Tips

- Father's vendor at neutral Entrati standing stocks casings at 100% -- check his inventory on every Necralisk visit and use excess Entrati tokens to purchase casings without relying solely on Isolation Vault RNG.
- Run Isolation Vaults to simultaneously gather all five Necramech frame components; Necramech enemy kills in the vault produce all component types including casings as random drops.
- Use the Arcana Isolation Vault variant for higher Necramech encounter frequency and thus more component drops per vault run.
- Radiation and Corrosive damage profiles are effective against Necramech armor in Isolation Vault encounters; matching the damage type to the enemy reduces time-per-kill and increases runs per hour.

## Notes

The casing is the first Necramech frame component that most players encounter in Father's vendor inventory. Purchasing casings early with Entrati tokens is an efficient use of standing during the initial Deimos progression phase. Necramech construction requires all five frame components (Casing, Engine, Pod, and Weapon components); a shortage of any single component blocks the final crafting step regardless of the others.
