## Overview

Voidplume Crest is a rare component resource harvested from Void Angels on the Zariman Ten-Zero. According to WFCD, its description reads: "The fine plumes from a Void Angel's crown." Void Angels are large hostile entities encountered during Zariman missions, and the Crest specifically drops from the crown region of these creatures after defeating them in combat.

The Voidplume series includes five variants: Down, Pinion, Quill, Vane, and Crest. Each drops from different Zariman Bounty tiers or specific Void Angel interactions. Crest is the highest-tier Voidplume variant and is required for the most demanding Zariman crafting recipes, including high-rank Warframe components for frames introduced with the Zariman content update such as Gyre and Styanax.

Zariman Ten-Zero is a ghost ship trapped in the Void since the Zariman incident, reachable via the Relay after completing The New War quest. Missions on Zariman operate through the Chrysalith social hub, with bounties dispatching squads into the shipboard tileset for timed objectives.

## Where it drops

Voidplume Crest drops from Zariman Ten-Zero Bounties at the highest difficulty tier: Level 70-75, Rotation C. WFCD records an 11.9% drop chance at that rotation. Rotation C rewards require completing the bounty final stage or achieving the full bonus objectives; partial completions yield Rotation A or B rewards, which drop lower-tier Voidplume variants instead.

Void Angels also drop Voidplume materials during open-world-style Zariman mission encounters when engaged directly. Combat-focused mission types on Zariman spawn Void Angels as escalating threats; defeating them awards Voidplume items outside of the bounty system.

## Used for

- Gyre Warframe chassis, neuroptics, and systems blueprints
- Styanax Warframe component blueprints
- High-tier Zariman equipment requiring Void Angel-derived materials
- Other Zariman-locked blueprints requiring Voidplume Crest specifically

## Tips

Run Level 70-75 Zariman Bounties and complete all objectives to reach Rotation C rewards. Lower-tier bounties yield Down, Pinion, Quill, or Vane instead of Crest. A resource booster doubles all material drops from Zariman bounty completions and Void Angel kills. Nekros with Desecrate does not apply to bounty reward drops, but it does apply to Void Angel enemy drops during open combat segments.

Check bounty objectives before entering -- some Zariman bounty types have bonus conditions that are required to reach Rotation C. Completing bonus conditions in every stage ensures maximum reward tier access.

## Notes

- Voidplume Crest is non-tradeable between players.
- The Zariman Ten-Zero is accessible only after completing The New War quest.
- WFCD records only one confirmed drop location: Zariman Ten-Zero Level 70-75 Bounty, Rotation C at 11.9% chance.
- Voidplume Crest should not be confused with Voidplume Down, Pinion, Quill, or Vane -- each is a separate item used in different recipes.
- Stock up during the first time through Gyre or Styanax crafting; subsequent Zariman frames requiring Crest share the same farming route.
