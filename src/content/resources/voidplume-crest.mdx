## Overview

Voidplume Crests are delicate feather-like structures composed of solidified void energy, commonly found embedded within corrupted void fauna of the Zariman Ten-Zero. These distinctive crests shimmer with ethereal patterns and are prized for their void-conductive properties. The intricate patterns within each crest reflect the chaotic nature of void disturbances. When harvested properly, the crests maintain their ethereal glow, making them visually distinctive among void-cascade materials. The crest's crystalline structure enables perfect transmission of void-aligned energy signatures.

## Where it drops

Voidplume Crests drop exclusively from void-touched enemies and environmental containers within Zariman void rifts. Running void rift missions yields steady supplies, with higher rarity rotations guaranteeing drops. Void Cascade difficulty tiers correlate directly with crest yield rates. Enemy corpses in void-corrupted zones reliably yield crests upon defeat. Special void containers hidden throughout rift areas yield bonus crest drops when discovered and opened.

## Best locations

Focus on Zariman void rift missions, particularly those ending in rotation C, as these offer the highest crest density per mission runtime. Void Cascade activities also reward crests generously, especially during team-based fissure operations. Extended void survival runs provide continuous crest generation as enemy escalation increases. Endurance-focused void fissure teams report exceptional crest yields per gaming hour invested.

## Used for

Voidplume Crests combine in void-relic blueprints and void-enhancement recipes. Several void-aligned warframe augments and weapon blueprints demand Voidplume Crests as core materials. Crafting void-specific cosmetics and gear also relies on steady supplies of these components. Void-focused warframe builds frequently consume crests for equipment optimization and augmentation.

## Tips

Group farming with void fissure squads accelerates crest collection by enabling faster mission completions. The feather-type resources tend to cluster near void-corrupted spawn points, making area-denial abilities valuable during farming runs. Vacuum-type warframe mods improve crest collection efficiency dramatically. Rotation-focused farming strategies maximize yields per session.

## Notes

Voidplume Crests cannot be purchased on the market, making farming the only viable acquisition path. Their drop rate is generous enough that most players accumulate surplus supplies organically while progressing through Zariman content. Bulk storage recommendations suggest maintaining 500+ crest reserves for sustained void-equipment crafting.
