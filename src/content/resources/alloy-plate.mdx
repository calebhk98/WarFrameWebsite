## Overview

Alloy Plate is a ubiquitous crafting resource found across many planets in Warframe. It represents reinforced metal plating recovered from Grineer and Corpus facilities. As one of the most broadly used common resources, Alloy Plate appears in blueprints for a large number of warframes, weapons, and Dojo research projects. Players accumulate it passively throughout normal star chart progression.

## Acquisition

Alloy Plate drops from almost all enemy types on its applicable planets. The most efficient dedicated farm is the dark-sector survival on Venus (Romula), which offers a 25% resource drop rate boost and is accessible relatively early in the game. The high enemy density in Survival missions means Alloy Plate accumulates rapidly over a 10-20 minute run.

| Planet | Recommended Mission | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Venus | Romula Dark Sector Survival | 25% drop rate boost |
| Ceres | Seimeni Dark Sector Defense | Defense for organized squads |
| Sedna | Any Survival or Defense | Higher level with good density |

## Used In

Alloy Plate is required for dozens of warframe blueprint components, making it one of the resources players consume most rapidly. Weapons with mechanical components and Dojo research trees for advanced labs also require Alloy Plate in the hundreds or thousands. Necramech components introduced in the Heart of Deimos update also consume Alloy Plate.

## Strategy

Farm Alloy Plate on Venus (Romula) Dark Sector Survival with a Nekros or Hydroid for enhanced resource pickup rates. A 20-minute run typically yields 3,000-5,000 Alloy Plate units, enough to cover several concurrent blueprint projects. Resource boosters double this yield, making active farming sessions highly productive during resource booster windows.
