## Overview

Cortege Receiver is a component resource used to craft the Cortege, a Necramech-class weapon designed for use while piloting a Necramech on Deimos. According to WFCD, the description reads: "A Necramech weapon-crafting component." The Cortege is a heavy Necramech weapon that fires explosive projectiles, and the Receiver is one of three components needed to build it alongside the Barrel and Stock.

The Cortege is the signature weapon of Necramech combat builds on Deimos, dealing high area-of-effect damage with Infested-tier explosive projectiles. Crafting the Cortege requires completing the Heart of Deimos quest and accessing Isolation Vault bounties from the Necralisk hub. Players who invest in Necramech gameplay on Deimos find the Cortege central to many combat encounters in that content area.

## Where it drops

Cortege Receiver drops from Necramech enemy encounters and Isolation Vault bounty rewards on Deimos. WFCD does not list specific weighted drop percentages for Cortege components, consistent with other Deimos Necramech weapon parts. The Cambion Drift open world hosts hostile Necramechs -- including Voidrig and Bonewidow variants -- that drop Cortege components and other Necramech materials upon defeat.

Isolation Vaults are the dedicated farming route: three-tier underground bounties that spawn Necramech enemies as part of the objective progression. Tier 3 Isolation Vaults provide the most efficient drop environment due to higher enemy density and better reward rotations compared to lower tiers.

## Used for

- Cortege Necramech weapon (requires Barrel, Receiver, and Stock assembled in the Foundry)
- Progressing Entrati syndicate standing for Heart of Deimos content completion
- Equipping the Cortege on Voidrig or Bonewidow Necramechs for Cambion Drift combat and Railjack Orphix missions

## Tips

Run Isolation Vaults sequentially -- all three tiers in a single session -- to maximize Cortege Receiver drops per farming run. Enemy spawn counts in Tier 3 vaults are higher and more consistently feature Necramech units compared to lower tiers. Bring a crowd-control Warframe to manage Infested swarms while focusing on Necramech kills for component drops.

The Cortege Barrel and Stock drop from the same locations as the Receiver. Track all three components together during farming to avoid redundant runs. A squad that covers vault areas thoroughly, including any hidden Necramech spawn areas at the back of vault chambers, increases total component yield per run.

## Notes

- Cortege Receiver is non-tradeable between players.
- WFCD does not list specific drop percentage rates; acquisition is confirmed through Isolation Vault and Cambion Drift Necramech encounter sources per the Fandom wiki.
- All three Cortege components (Barrel, Receiver, Stock) drop from the same locations and can be farmed simultaneously.

- The Cortege weapon is used in Necramech mode only and cannot be equipped on standard Warframe loadouts.
