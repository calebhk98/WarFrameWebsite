## Overview

Esher Devar is the refined, cut form of Devar ore from the Plains of Eidolon on Earth. WFCD describes it as "polished and cut to perfection," positioning it as a precision-cut gemstone produced from the raw Devar ore found in Blue Mining Veins on the Plains. The raw form, Devar (described as "a cloudy crystal"), is harvested from Blue Mining Veins and Tusk Thumper enemies, then brought to Old Man Suumbaat in Cetus for processing into the cut Esher Devar form. The cut version is required for crafting blueprints that specify the refined material.

Esher Devar belongs to the Plains of Eidolon Blue Mining Vein gem family, which also includes Crimzian, Devar, and Sentirum. The processing step (raw ore to cut gem) is unique to Cetus gems and is handled through Suumbaat's vendor in the Cetus market.

## Where it drops

Esher Devar is obtained by processing raw Devar ore through Old Man Suumbaat's blueprint in Cetus. The blueprint for Esher Devar is "sold by Old Man Suumbaat in Cetus on Earth" (WFCD). Devar ore itself comes from:

- Blue Mining Veins on the Plains of Eidolon (mined with Nosam Cutter or Sunpoint Plasma Drill)
- Tusk Thumper drops (16.68% for Tusk Thumper, scaling up to 33.36% for Tusk Thumper Doma)
- Conclave Variant Team Annihilation at 3.16% per Rotation A and B for 10x Esher Devar

Mining Blue Veins on the Plains is the most direct and consistently scalable route. The Conclave source provides Esher Devar directly (already cut) but requires PvP engagement.

## Used for

Esher Devar is used in Cetus crafting blueprints for Zaw melee weapon parts, Amp prism and scaffold components from the Quills syndicate, and other Plains of Eidolon crafting requiring cut gem materials. The Quills syndicate's Amp component blueprints use Plains gems including Esher Devar as components, making them relevant to Operator Amp progression.

## Tips

- Mine Blue Veins on the Plains specifically -- they yield gem-type resources (Devar, Crimzian) while Red Veins yield metal-type resources (Ferros, Coprun, Pyrol).
- Use the Sunpoint Plasma Drill over the Nosam Cutter; its improved precision reduces ore waste from imprecise hits on the mining mini-game.
- Purchase the Esher Devar blueprint from Suumbaat and queue cuts in batches; each processing run converts raw Devar into Esher Devar, so accumulate raw ore before visiting Cetus.
- Hunt Tusk Thumper Doma-tier walkers on the Plains to supplement mined Devar with enemy drops (33.36% at the Doma tier).

## Notes

Esher Devar is the cut/refined version of Devar, not a standalone drop -- players must both mine and process to acquire it. Raw Devar and Esher Devar are different items in the crafting system; some blueprints specifically require the cut Esher Devar form while others accept raw ore. Check blueprint ingredient lists carefully to determine whether to bring raw ore or purchase the cut-gem blueprint from Suumbaat.
