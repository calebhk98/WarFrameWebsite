## Overview

Iradite is an open-world resource exclusive to the Plains of Eidolon on Earth. It appears as distinctive orange-red mineral clusters attached to cliff faces and rocky outcroppings scattered across the Plains. Iradite is required for crafting Operator Amps at Hok's Anvil in Cetus and for building Zaw melee weapons, making it an essential open-world resource for players progressing into Operator content.

Unlike randomly dropped resources, Iradite is gathered by approaching its cluster nodes and interacting with them (or using the mining laser tool for certain nodes). The nodes are fixed-location respawn points that reset each time you enter the Plains.

## Acquisition

Iradite clusters appear most commonly near the rocky terrain and cliff faces of the Plains, particularly in the central and western portions of the map. Players new to the Plains can follow the cliff edges to find clusters efficiently. A single Plains visit with a mobility frame sweeping cliff routes typically yields 15-30 Iradite per visit depending on map coverage.

Cetus Tier 1 and Tier 2 bounties reward Iradite in their rotation pools as a secondary resource reward, making bounties a reliable source of Iradite alongside other Plains resources.

| Method | Rate | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Plains sweep (cliff edges) | 15-30 per visit | Reliable, fast with mobility |
| Bounty rotation rewards | 3-5 per bounty | Passive alongside gameplay |

## Used In

Iradite is consumed by Operator Amp blueprints from Hok's Anvil in Cetus, which are the primary tools for Eidolon Teralyst shield-stripping during Eidolon hunts. Zaw melee weapon blueprints also require Iradite as a Plains-specific material component. Various Ostron crafting recipes and decorations use Iradite.

## Strategy

Farm Iradite during normal Plains bounty sessions by sweeping cliff-face areas between bounty objectives. A dedicated Iradite route along the western cliff walls of the Plains yields large quantities per visit without significant additional time investment. Use a mobility warframe (Volt, Gauss) to move quickly between cluster locations.
