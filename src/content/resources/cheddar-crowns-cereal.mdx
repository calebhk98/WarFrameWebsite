## Overview

Cheddar Crowns Cereal is a Hex Treasure item from the WF1999 game mode, set in the retro-futuristic Höllvania of 1999. WFCD describes it as "a nostalgic treat for the ultimate '90s kid! Remember those commercials with the little king in the crown?" and specifies that it is traded with Lettie to earn standing with The Hex syndicate. The item is one of several branded consumer goods from the 1999 setting that function as collectible Hex Treasures, reflecting the pop-culture references embedded in the WF1999 narrative. Its appearance as a common cereal box in the Warframe universe plays on late-20th-century breakfast food nostalgia.

In game terms, Cheddar Crowns Cereal is a standing currency for the Lettie vendor within The Hex, not a standard crafting material.

## Where it drops

Cheddar Crowns Cereal is classified internally as a Hex Dog Tag item tied to Lettie (Quincy). It drops from exploration and cache rewards within the WF1999 Höllvania content, including bounty reward pools and environmental containers found during missions. As with other Hex Treasures, it appears in mission loot across the Höllvania tileset. Bounty completion rewards and hidden treasure caches scattered through Höllvania buildings yield cereal as part of the collectible treasure series unique to this game mode.

## Used for

Cheddar Crowns Cereal is traded with Lettie in The Hex syndicate hub within Höllvania to earn standing. Lettie's specific Hex Treasure items (including Cheddar Crowns Cereal) grant standing toward The Hex faction, enabling players to rank up and access Lettie's vendor inventory. This standing economy is separate from the broader Höllvania bounty standing earned through mission completion. Collecting Hex Treasures and trading them with the appropriate Hex member (Lettie, Arthur, etc.) is the primary standing mechanic for The Hex syndicate.

## Tips

- Explore Höllvania mission areas thoroughly; Hex Treasures including cereal appear in buildable containers, on shelves, and in environmental caches within buildings.
- Run Höllvania bounties and check for secondary cache objectives that reward Hex Treasures rather than combat-focused resources.
- Each Hex Treasure corresponds to a specific Hex member's standing; trade Cheddar Crowns Cereal specifically to Lettie rather than other members for maximum efficiency.
- Combine Hex Treasure hunting with standard bounty completion and Efervon Sample farming in the same Höllvania sessions to make each run multi-purpose.

## Notes

Cheddar Crowns Cereal does not function as a crafting component -- it is purely a standing currency for Lettie's Hex Treasure exchange. The 1999 branding reflects the WF1999 update's broader aesthetic of placing recognizable consumer products into a Warframe-adjacent retro setting. Collecting the full range of Hex Treasures across all Hex members unlocks the maximum standing potential with The Hex syndicate.
