## Overview

The Orokin Reactor (informally called a "Golden Potato" by the Warframe community, for its golden-yellow icon) is the warframe equivalent of the Orokin Catalyst. Applying an Orokin Reactor to a warframe, companion, or Archwing permanently doubles its maximum mod capacity from 30 to 60 points. Without a Reactor, a rank-30 warframe cannot equip a full optimal build of high-drain mods -- many builds require 58-60 total mod capacity, making the Reactor non-negotiable for endgame performance.

Like the Orokin Catalyst, the Reactor is precious and scarce. Veterans treat Reactors as major resource investments and plan carefully which warframes to reactor first. New players who receive their first Reactor should spend it on their primary warframe to maximize build quality from the start.

## Acquisition

Orokin Reactor Blueprints are craftable (24-hour build) using materials including Orokin Cell, Morphics, and Alloy Plate, but the pre-built Reactor is the more valuable form. Alert missions reward pre-built Reactors at a similar frequency to Catalysts (1-3 times per week historically). Nightwave season reward tracks award 1-2 Reactors per season at milestone tiers.

Veterans advise checking alert missions daily and completing every Reactor-rewarding alert mission. The Warframe companion app provides alert notifications so players can respond quickly before an alert expires.

## Used In

Each Orokin Reactor is permanently consumed when applied to a warframe, companion, or Archwing. Players typically apply Reactors to warframes they have committed to as permanent roster members, especially those with expensive mod builds (Saryn Prime, Mesa Prime, Wisp Prime, Nidus Prime all benefit enormously from the extra capacity). Archwings used in Railjack and open-world content also benefit from Reactor application if those modes are actively played.

## Strategy

Apply your first Orokin Reactor to your primary workhorse warframe -- the one you use for most missions. For most players, this is a strong damage frame like Saryn Prime or a versatile utility frame. Hold subsequent Reactors until you commit to additional permanent roster warframes.

Never sell a warframe with an Orokin Reactor installed unless you are certain you will never use that warframe again. The Reactor is consumed and cannot be removed once applied. Plan your warframe choices thoughtfully before spending Reactors.
