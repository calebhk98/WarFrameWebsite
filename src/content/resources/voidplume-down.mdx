## Overview

Voidplume Down refers to the soft, downy material that accumulates within void-corrupted areas of the Zariman. This fine substance accumulates in pockets of void energy and can be harvested from defeated void entities and environmental sources. The material is extremely light yet durable, making it ideal for void-aligned equipment padding and insulation in void-reactive gear. These materials represent a significant component of the Zariman crafting ecosystem. Understanding their properties and acquisition methods is essential for successful progression through advanced void content. The materials maintain their void-energy saturation indefinitely when stored properly.

## Where it drops

Void Cascade missions yield Voidplume Down consistently, particularly from standard void enemies rather than rare elite variants. Container harvesting in void rifts provides reliable yields, and the material drops frequently enough that steady farming generates abundance quickly.
