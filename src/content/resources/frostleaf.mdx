## Overview

Frostleaf is a cold-environment plant resource in Warframe, classified as a Plant type. It grows in frigid biomes on Venus, Neptune, and Pluto. Collecting Frostleaf requires using a Codex Scanner or Synthesis Scanner on the plant nodes found in those planets' outdoor tilesets. The plant is listed as one of the ingredients for the Sunrise Apothic, an item used in the Silver Grove quest to unlock the Titania Warframe.

## Where it drops

Frostleaf grows as a scannable plant node on Venus, Neptune, and Pluto. Unlike dropped resources, it must be found in the environment and scanned with a Codex Scanner or Synthesis Scanner -- shooting or melee attacking the plant does not yield the resource. Each node yields up to 8 items with a Resource Booster active. Venus has accessible Frostleaf in outdoor tileset areas; Neptune and Pluto provide additional farming opportunities at higher node levels. The plant appears as a pale blue-green frond in rocky, frost-covered terrain.

## Used for

Frostleaf is a required ingredient for the Sunrise Apothic blueprint. The Sunrise Apothic is used during the Silver Grove quest to interact with the Silver Grove shrine and progress the quest. Outside of the Silver Grove questline, Frostleaf has no current additional uses in crafting blueprints tracked by WFCD data.

## Tips

Use a Synthesis Scanner with Crosshairs upgrade to identify plant locations at range while moving through the tileset. Resource Boosters double the yield from each scan. Focus on Venus tileset nodes if farming at lower mastery ranks, as Neptune and Pluto require completing additional star chart junctions first. The plant does not spawn in indoor Grineer or Corpus tilesets; look specifically for the outdoor snow and frost terrain variants.

## Notes

Frostleaf is not tradeable through the in-game market. Players who have completed the Silver Grove quest no longer need to maintain a stock unless planning to craft additional Apothics for other players. The WFCD description confirms it grows on Venus, Neptune, and Pluto and requires Codex Scanner collection rather than standard kill-drop mechanics.
