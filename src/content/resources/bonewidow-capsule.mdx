## Overview

Bonewidow Capsule is a specialized construction component exclusive to the Bonewidow Necramech blueprint. The Bonewidow is Warframe's melee-focused Necramech, distinguished from the Voidrig by its emphasis on close-range combat using the Ironbride heavy sword and the Meathook and Shield Maiden abilities. The capsule forms part of the internal systems housing within the Bonewidow frame, providing structural containment for the mech's core mechanical systems.

Bonewidow construction requires four blueprint-specific components: Capsule, Casing, Engine, and Weapon Pod. All four must be assembled from Deimos farming sources before the Bonewidow can be built. The capsule is typically the component that new Necramech builders encounter earliest in the Isolation Vault farming process.

## Where it drops

Bonewidow Capsules drop from Bonewidow-type enemy Necramechs during Isolation Vault Bounties on the Cambion Drift. The component is specific to the Bonewidow enemy variant and does not drop from Voidrig-type Necramechs. Deimos void fissure missions that feature Bonewidow Necramech encounters also contribute to capsule accumulation. Because Isolation Vault enemy Necramech type distribution is not fully controlled by the player, building a stockpile of capsules requires multiple vault runs to encounter sufficient Bonewidow enemies.

## Used for

Bonewidow Capsule is required for the Bonewidow Necramech blueprint. Players who want to craft the Bonewidow specifically -- rather than the Voidrig -- need this capsule as a mandatory component. The Bonewidow's combat specialization (melee) makes it the preferred Necramech for players who want to engage endgame Deimos content up close. Accumulating enough capsules alongside the other three Bonewidow-specific components (Casing, Engine, Weapon Pod) completes the crafting prerequisite.

## Tips

- Run Isolation Vault Bounties and prioritize combat with any Bonewidow-type enemy Necramechs encountered; they are the sole source of Bonewidow-specific capsules.
- Multiple Isolation Vault runs are needed to generate enough Bonewidow encounters for capsule accumulation; plan for 10-20 vault runs depending on enemy type distribution.
- Check Father's vendor in the Necralisk for Bonewidow Capsules -- some Necramech components are available through Entrati standing purchases, which can supplement RNG drops.
- Bring Radiation damage loadouts against Necramech armor for efficient kills; the Necramech enemy type's Alloy Armor layer takes bonus damage from Radiation.

## Notes

Bonewidow Capsule is distinct from the generic Damaged Necramech components used in both Necramech types. It only contributes to the Bonewidow blueprint -- accumulating extra capsules beyond the blueprint requirement does not accelerate other Necramech construction goals. Players building both Bonewidow and Voidrig must farm Bonewidow-specific components (Capsule, Casing, Engine, Weapon Pod) and Voidrig-specific components separately.
