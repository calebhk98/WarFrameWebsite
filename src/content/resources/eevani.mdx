## Overview

Eevani is a plant-type resource from the Duviri open world, described by WFCD as having leaves that "Duviri citizens have been known to mash into a paste that alleviates the discomfort caused by fresh wounds." This medicinal use ties Eevani into the everyday life of Duviri's citizens -- the fictional people living within Dominus Thrax's cyclical emotional realm. The plant's healing properties suggest a role in the natural medicine of Duviri's world, predating any technological crafting application.

Despite being classified as a fish type in WFCD's item database (a quirk of data organization), Eevani behaves as a plant-type resource gathered through Duviri's resource acquisition systems rather than through fishing mechanics.

## Where it drops

Eevani appears in Duviri Endless mode at Tier 1 and Tier 3 completion rewards, offering 30 Eevani at 0.67% chance per tier. In the Duviri Circuit, it drops at 1% per run in the rare category. Plant nodes across the Duviri open world also yield Eevani through the standard harvest interaction used for other Duviri botanical resources (Dracroot, Connla Sprout, Kovnik). Systematic exploration of Duviri's landscape while running Endless loops provides both passive plant harvesting and the chance at batch drops from rotation rewards.

## Used for

Eevani contributes to the Duviri resource economy accessed through Teshin's Cave and the Duviri syndicate vendors. It appears as a crafting component or standing currency for Duviri-exclusive items, including weapons from the Duviri weapon set and Warframe components tied to the Duviri story progression. Players pursuing the complete Duviri catalog will encounter Eevani as a required ingredient across multiple recipes.

## Tips

- Duviri Endless Tier 1 and Tier 3 drops give 30 Eevani at once when they land; plan rotation completion to hit both tiers efficiently within a single session.
- While traversing Duviri between objectives, use a Synthesis Scanner to highlight plant nodes; Eevani plants appear as harvestable nodes in the open landscape.
- Combine Eevani farming with Dracroot, Connla Sprout, and other Duviri plant harvesting since all grow in the same world and can be gathered on the same routes.
- The Duviri Circuit offers a 1% drop rate -- better than the 0.25% legendary tier reserved for the rarest Duviri resources -- making it a reasonable secondary source if Endless runs become repetitive.

## Notes

Eevani cannot be traded between players and must be personally gathered within Duviri. The prerequisite for Duviri access is the completion of The Duviri Paradox quest. Players who have not unlocked Duviri will find no access to Eevani from any other game mode. The wound-healing lore description suggests Eevani's practical importance in Duviri's world independent of its use in Tenno crafting.
