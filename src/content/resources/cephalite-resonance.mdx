## Overview

Cephalite Resonance is a rare event resource introduced during Nightwave Series 3: The Glassmaker. It is a byproduct of glass-based Cephalon Simaris research related to the Glassmaker serial killer investigation. The resonance is used primarily to craft cosmetics and event-exclusive rewards tied to the Glassmaker Nightwave arc.

Unlike persistent resources that remain farmable indefinitely, Cephalite Resonance was tied to the Glassmaker event period. Players who participated during the event may retain stockpiles; newly joined players may find it unavailable until Digital Extremes reruns the Glassmaker or offers its associated crafting recipes through other means.

## Where it drops

Cephalite Resonance was obtainable during the Nightwave: Glassmaker season through completing Glassmaker investigations -- special crime scene puzzles accessible via Nightwave bounty missions. Each completed investigation awarded a set amount of Cephalite Resonance. The resource did not drop from standard enemy kills; it was purely a Nightwave reward.

During the event, players completed a series of increasingly difficult Glassmaker cases, each rewarding Cephalite Resonance and other Nightwave-exclusive currency.

## Used for

- Glassmaker-themed Warframe cosmetics and armor sets
- Cephalon Simaris research cosmetic blueprints available during the Nightwave season
- Decorations and sigils tied to the Glassmaker storyline

## Tips

If the Glassmaker Nightwave season is not currently active, Cephalite Resonance cannot be farmed. Check the Nightwave offerings screen for whether the season is running. Players who saved resonance from prior seasons should preserve it for any new craftable recipes released with future Glassmaker continuations.

## Notes

- Cephalite Resonance is non-tradeable between players.
- The resource availability depends entirely on Digital Extremes running or re-running the Glassmaker Nightwave series.
- WFCD lists this resource in its database but its drop sources reflect event-specific acquisition that does not align with standard planetary drop tables.
- Check the Warframe website or in-game Nightwave screen for current season status.
