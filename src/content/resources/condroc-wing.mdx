## Overview

Condroc Wing is a component resource harvested from Condroc creatures on the Plains of Eidolon. According to WFCD, the description reads: "The talon on a condroc's wing is sharp enough to tear through bone." Condrocs are large bird-like creatures that roam the Plains in several variants -- Common, Emperor, and Rogue -- and all three drop wings when killed.

Condroc Wings are one of several Plains of Eidolon creature resources used in Cetus crafting. Alongside fish parts, Eidolon Gems, and other Plains materials, Condroc Wings appear in Ostron crafting recipes available from Hok's Anvil and Suumbaat's shop in Cetus. Players who pursue Zaw construction or Plains-specific equipment will encounter Condroc Wings as a required component.

The Plains of Eidolon is the open-world area outside Cetus on Earth, accessible after completing the Saya's Vigil quest. The plains operate on a day/night cycle; Condrocs are visible during daytime and less active at night.

## Where it drops

Condroc Wings drop from all three Condroc creature variants on the Plains of Eidolon: Common Condroc, Emperor Condroc, and Rogue Condroc. WFCD records all three as Common rarity drops with a 100% drop chance, meaning every Condroc killed drops at least one wing. The Emperor Condroc is larger and more aggressive than the standard variant, while Rogue Condrocs appear as smaller patrol groups near cliff edges and elevated terrain.

A secondary source is the Saturn Conclave Variant Team Annihilation mission, which awards 10X Condroc Wing at Rotations A and B at 3.16% chance each -- a PvP-mode source that most players will not pursue for farming.

## Tips

Condrocs spawn in open areas of the Plains during daytime. Run across the plains on foot or use the Archwing Launcher to quickly cover distance between Condroc clusters. Nekros with Desecrate increases drop yields from each Condroc kill. A resource booster doubles all wing drops per kill.

Target Emperor Condrocs when visible, as they have the same drop rate as Common Condrocs but take more effort to locate; Common Condrocs are more numerous and spawn in larger flocks near Cetus Gate and the central grassland areas. A single daytime Plains run with Nekros typically yields 30-50 Condroc Wings before extraction.

## Notes

- Condroc Wings are non-tradeable between players.
- All three Condroc variants (Common, Emperor, Rogue) drop wings at the same rate per WFCD.
- The Saturn Conclave source awards 10X at once but requires PvP gameplay; ignore this route for standard farming.
- Condroc Wings should not be confused with other creature parts from different Plains animals.
- The Plains of Eidolon requires Saya's Vigil quest completion and Cetus access through Earth Junction.
