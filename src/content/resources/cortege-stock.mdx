## Overview

Cortege Stock is a crafted Necramech weapon component used exclusively to assemble the Cortege, a Necramech arch-gun weapon. The stock is not a drop from enemies; it is built in the Foundry using a blueprint purchased from Loid at the Clearance Modus standing tier in the Necralisk on Deimos. Assembling the stock requires a Damaged Necramech Weapon Stock as one of its primary ingredients alongside Deimos-specific gems and resources.

The Cortege itself is a Necramech arch-gun, meaning it is equipped in the Necramech weapon slot and used only while piloting a Necramech unit. Players building out their Necramech loadout will need to complete the Cortege Stock as part of the broader Cortege weapon assembly chain: the weapon requires a separately crafted Barrel, Receiver, and Stock before the final weapon can be constructed.

## Where it drops

Cortege Stock does not drop directly from enemies. It is crafted using a blueprint purchased from Loid in the Necralisk on Deimos at the Clearance Modus Entrati syndicate standing tier. The blueprint costs Necraloid standing to unlock.

The primary ingredient requiring active farming is the Damaged Necramech Weapon Stock, which drops from Isolation Vault and Arcana Isolation Vault bounties on the Cambion Drift at Rotation C. The remaining crafting ingredients -- Cabochon Embolos, Tempered Bapholite, and Hexenon -- are Deimos-specific resources and gems obtained from mining and enemy drops on the Cambion Drift.

## Used for

Cortege Stock is consumed entirely in the construction of the Cortege Necramech weapon. It is a one-time consumable component; each Cortege assembled requires one Cortege Stock. Players who want multiple Corteges or who scrap and recraft must farm all component blueprints and materials again from scratch.

The Cortege is the only weapon that uses Cortege Stock. There are no alternate recipes or consumable uses for the stock outside of Cortege assembly.

## Tips

Farm Damaged Necramech Weapon Stock from Isolation Vault Rotation C bounties on the Cambion Drift as the primary bottleneck. The Isolation Vault bounty drops Damaged Necramech Weapon Stock at 3% chance per Rotation C completion across multiple vault tier levels. Running the highest available vault tier (Level 50-60 Arcana) yields the same drop chance but provides other high-value drops simultaneously.

Cabochon Embolos and Tempered Bapholite are obtained from gem mining nodes on the Cambion Drift using a mining tool. The Dueling Fangs mining cutter or Focused Nosam Cutter improves yield from gem deposits. Hexenon drops from enemies on Jupiter; stock up during general farming sessions when visiting that planet.

## Notes

The Cortege Stock blueprint appears in Loid's inventory after reaching the Clearance Modus standing tier with the Necraloid syndicate. The Necraloid syndicate is distinct from the Entrati family syndicate and has its own standing accumulation through Isolation Vault bounties and trading Residue materials.
