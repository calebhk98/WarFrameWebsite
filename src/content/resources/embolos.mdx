## Overview

Embolos is a rare gem formed within the Infestation of the Cambion Drift on Deimos. WFCD describes it as a "rare gem" that "forms in a clotted mass within the Infestation," emphasizing that its origin is biological rather than purely geological -- the Infestation process on Deimos concentrates minerals into unusual crystal formations as the Infested tissue metabolizes the environment. The gem's name relates to the medical term "embolus" (a blockage or clot in a vessel), consistent with WFCD's "clotted mass" description suggesting the gem forms at points where the Infestation's growth has blocked and concentrated mineral deposits.

WFCD notes it can only be extracted with the Advanced Nosam Cutter or Sunpoint Plasma Drill, making it a higher-tier mining resource requiring upgraded equipment compared to common Cambion Drift mining materials.

## Where it drops

Embolos drops exclusively from Blue Mining Lesions on the Cambion Drift. WFCD specifies: "This Rare Gem can only be mined by the Advanced Nosam Cutter or Sunpoint Plasma Drill." Standard mining tools cannot extract Embolos; players must upgrade their mining equipment before Embolos becomes accessible. Blue Mining Lesions are the gem-type mining nodes on the Cambion Drift (as distinct from Red Lesions, which yield metal-type resources). Lesion locations vary across Cambion Drift terrain, with the highest concentrations typically found in the elevated cave and overhang areas of the Drift.

The requirement for advanced mining tools means new players accessing Deimos for the first time cannot immediately farm Embolos -- they must first craft or acquire the Advanced Nosam Cutter or Sunpoint Plasma Drill from the Ostron in Cetus or the Solaris in Fortuna.

## Used for

Embolos is used in Cambion Drift crafting blueprints requiring rare gems, and features in Father's daily exchange table as an input material for Father Tokens. Players building Necramech components through Father's vendor in the Necralisk will encounter Embolos as one of the gem materials Father accepts in his rotating trade list. Cambion Drift gem-based crafting blueprints for Entrati family equipment similarly require Embolos alongside other Blue Lesion gems like Thyst and Purified Oolite.

## Tips

- Use the Sunpoint Plasma Drill over the Advanced Nosam Cutter when farming Embolos; the Drill's precision mini-game circle is more forgiving and produces better gem yields per mining attempt.
- Enable the Synthesis Scanner before entering the Cambion Drift; the Loot Detect upgrade highlights mining lesion locations on the minimap within detection radius.
- Blue Lesions and Red Lesions are visually distinct on the Cambion Drift -- Blue Lesions appear as blue-tinged Infested growths versus the reddish ore veins of Red Lesions.
- Check Father's daily exchange table in the Necralisk before farming Embolos; confirm he currently accepts Embolos for Father Tokens to avoid farming materials not currently in his rotation.

## Notes

Embolos is rare even among Cambion Drift Blue Lesion gems; individual mining attempts on Blue Lesions can yield Embolos, Thyst, Purified Oolite, or Veridos, with Embolos being the rarest tier. The need for advanced mining tools and the rare classification mean accumulating Embolos for recipes requires dedicated mining sessions rather than incidental farming. The Infestation-origin lore makes Embolos narratively unique among gem resources -- most gems form through geological processes, but Embolos is a biological crystallization product of the Deimos Infestation.
