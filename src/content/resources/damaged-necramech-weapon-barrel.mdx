## Overview

Damaged Necramech Weapon Barrel is the projectile delivery component for Necramech-mounted weapons. WFCD describes it as "The damaged barrel of a heavy-duty Necramech weapon," sourced from "Isolation Vault and Arcana Isolation Vault Bounties on the Cambion Drift (Deimos), and purchased from Loid in the Necralisk after completing Isolation Vaults." The barrel channels the high-energy projectiles generated by the Necramech's power systems, and its heavy-duty designation reflects the extreme power output of Necramech-scale weaponry compared to standard Warframe weapons.

Like all Damaged Necramech components, the barrel is scavenged from enemy Necramechs and requires the Necralisk's fabrication capabilities to restore during crafting.

## Where it drops

Damaged Necramech Weapon Barrels appear in Rotation A of both the standard Isolation Vault Bounty and the harder Arcana Isolation Vault Bounty at 3% chance per roll. Rotation A is the first reward slot in the bounty chain, appearing after the first stage completion. This means barrels can drop earlier in the bounty chain than Weapon Receivers (which appear in Rotation B), potentially allowing shorter farming sessions when barrel count is the limiting factor. Loid in the Necralisk sells them directly once his shop is unlocked through Isolation Vault completion.

## Used for

Damaged Necramech Weapon Barrels are essential for all Necramech weapon blueprints. The Cortege (Voidrig's primary weapon) and Morgha (Bonewidow's primary weapon) both require a barrel alongside the Receiver and Stock/Pod components. Players aiming to arm their Necramech with fully functional primary and secondary weapons must farm multiple barrel units. The quantity needed depends on how many Necramech weapons are in the build plan.

## Tips

- Rotation A appears after Stage 1 of the Isolation Vault; even partial bounty completion (stopping after Stage 1) still accesses the barrel's drop slot, which can be efficient when only barrels are needed.
- Running full bounty chains covers both Rotation A (barrel) and Rotation B (receiver) in the same session, allowing parallel farming of both weapon components.
- Unlock Loid's Necralisk shop through Isolation Vault completion as early as possible; his guaranteed stock removes RNG from barrel acquisition.
- Bring damage types effective against Necramech armor (Radiation, Corrosive) to maximize Necramech enemy kill speed in Isolation Vault runs.

## Notes

Necramech weapon components share the same 3% drop rate across Rotation A (barrel) and Rotation B (receiver) in both standard and Arcana Isolation Vaults. The key difference is which rotation slot each occupies. Coordinating farming to complete full chains consistently hits both rotation slots, making each vault run doubly efficient for weapon component accumulation. Loid's shop represents the most reliable non-RNG acquisition route once unlocked.
