## Overview

Void Traces are a farming-focused currency obtained passively during Void Fissure missions. They serve as the gateway to void fissure content: you spend Void Traces at a vendor (Loid at the Void Fissure hub) to purchase or refinement additional void relics. Unlike resources that require dedicated farming, Void Traces accumulate naturally while you run void fissure missions for other resources (Orokin Cells, Argon Crystals, etc.). This design ensures players maintain a sustainable void relic supply without requiring separate farming sessions.

## Where it drops

Void Traces drop exclusively in Void Fissure missions as mission rewards, not from enemy kills. Every void fissure mission (Defense, Survival, Excavation, Disruption, Mobile Defense, etc.) grants Void Traces upon completion or upon mission stage completion. The amount scales with mission difficulty and player contribution level.

| Mission type | Traces per run | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Void Fissure Defense (5 min) | 10-20 | Minimal timer; early-game friendly |
| Void Fissure Survival (5 min) | 10-20 | Same rate; passive grind |
| Void Fissure Excavation | 8-15 per extraction | Cumulative; longer missions better |
| Void Fissure Disruption | 15-30 | Higher engagement; better return |

## Best locations

All void fissure missions grant similar Void Traces per unit time, averaging 2-4 traces per minute of playtime. The "best" location is whichever fissure mission aligns with your current farming goal. If you are farming Orokin Cells (rotation A), you benefit from running longer missions (10-15 minutes) to maximize relic usage and passive Void Trace income.

## Farming strategy

Void Traces are not farmed directly; instead, they accumulate as a side benefit of farming other void resources. A standard workflow:

1. Identify a void resource you need (e.g., Argon Crystals, Orokin Cells).
2. Run void fissure missions targeting that resource.
3. Void Traces accumulate passively during these runs.
4. When Void Traces accumulate to 100+, visit Loid and purchase additional relics.

This ensures sustainable void relic supply without dedicated trace farming.

## Void relic purchasing

Loid (the Void Fissure vendor at the void hub) offers two services:

1. **Relic refinement** -- Spend Void Traces to increase the rarity tier of a relic you already own (Intact -> Exceptional -> Flawless -> Radiant). Radiant relics have 50% higher drop rates for rare items.
2. **Relic purchasing** -- Buy additional relics outright with Void Traces (100-200 traces per relic, depending on rarity and your standing).

## Tips

Do not dedicate specific time to farming Void Traces. Run void fissure missions for other resources and let Void Traces accumulate passively.

Refine relics to Radiant tier when possible. A Radiant relic has a 50% higher drop chance for rare items, significantly improving Void Fissure reward quality.

Budget traces conservatively. A 10-run void fissure campaign (10 Survival missions at 15 minutes each = 150 minutes) generates approximately 300-400 Void Traces, enough to refinement 3-4 relics or purchase 1-2 new ones.

## Used in

Void Traces are exclusively used for purchasing and refinement void relics at the Loid vendor. They are the direct gateway to void fissure content participation; without Void Traces, you cannot participate in fissures beyond a few runs with pre-farmed relics.

## Void relic economy

The void relic system creates a self-sustaining economy: you run void fissures to farm resources, earn Void Traces passively, and use traces to sustain your relic supply. Players who run consistent void fissure content never run out of Void Traces and always have relics available. New players should farm relics aggressively early (running void cascade once or twice per session) until they establish a reserve of 200+ traces.

## Notes

Void Traces are abundant for active players. The passive grind ensures that players engaged in void fissure content naturally accumulate enough traces to sustain relic purchasing indefinitely. Inactive players may find their trace supply depleted if they do not run fissures regularly.
