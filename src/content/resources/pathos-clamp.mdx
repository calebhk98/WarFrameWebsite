## Overview

Pathos Clamp is a Duviri-exclusive resource tied to the Orowyrm boss fight in the Duviri Paradox. WFCD describes it as "the manifestation of Thrax's malevolence that shapes the Orowyrms' outbursts," indicating it is the solidified aggression of the Duviri's primary antagonist. It drops upon defeating the Orowyrm at the conclusion of a Duviri Circuit or Duviri open world run. Pathos Clamp is listed as a parent item for both Kullervo and the Rauta weapon, confirming its role as the primary Duviri crafting material for these items.

## Where it drops

Pathos Clamp is rewarded upon defeating the Orowyrm in Duviri per the WFCD description. The Orowyrm appears at the conclusion of a Duviri Paradox run, both in the open-world exploration mode and the Circuit game mode. Each Orowyrm defeat in either mode yields Pathos Clamp. The resource drops on completion of the kill, not as a mission rotation reward, meaning every Orowyrm kill guarantees at least one Pathos Clamp drop. The Duviri Circuit mode provides additional runs per session due to its repeatable structure.

## Used for

Pathos Clamp is required for crafting Kullervo (the Duviri Warframe) and the Rauta (a Duviri-exclusive shotgun) per WFCD parent data. These are the primary endgame rewards from the Duviri Paradox content and represent the main use case for accumulating Pathos Clamps. Players farming Kullervo or Rauta should run the Duviri Circuit repeatedly, as each Circuit completion ends with an Orowyrm fight and guaranteed Pathos Clamp drop.

## Tips

The Duviri Circuit is more efficient than the open-world Duviri run for Pathos Clamp farming because the Circuit's paced structure allows multiple completions per play session. Complete all five Circuit stages without extracting early to reach the Orowyrm at the end. Resource Boosters increase the quantity of Pathos Clamps received per kill. Bringing a high-damage loadout optimized for the Circuit's random Warframe selection helps sustain efficient runs.

## Notes

Pathos Clamp is not tradeable through Warframe.market. Access requires completing the Duviri Paradox quest, which becomes available after finishing The Duviri Paradox main story quest on the Navigation screen. The resource name and description link it thematically to Dominus Thrax, the primary antagonist of Duviri, and the Orowyrms he commands in the game's lore.
