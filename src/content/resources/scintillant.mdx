## Overview

Scintillant is a rare open-world resource exclusive to the Cambion Drift on Deimos. It is one of the most notoriously difficult resources to farm in Warframe -- not because it requires advanced gameplay, but because its spawn rate on the Cambion Drift is low and its floating pickup mechanic can be easy to miss while exploring the Infested landscape. Scintillant is required for Helminth Segment blueprints, making it a key bottleneck for players unlocking the Helminth system.

Scintillant appears as small, glowing, floating orbs similar to Cetus Wisps but within the biopunk aesthetic of the Cambion Drift. They hover near Infested growth nodes and Orokin ruins scattered across the map.

## Acquisition

Scintillant spawns appear at fixed locations on the Cambion Drift map near Infested growth nodes and the outer boundaries of the Cambion Drift tileset. Isolation Vault bounties reward Scintillant in their B and C rotation pools, making bounties the most reliable active farming method. Conservation hunts with Son (the animal handler at the Necralisk) occasionally reward Scintillant as bonus capture rewards.

| Method | Rate | Notes |

| Isolation Vault bounty B/C | 1-2 | Most reliable source |

## Used In

Scintillant is most importantly required for unlocking the Helminth Subsume system: the Helminth Segment blueprint from Son requires multiple Scintillant. Necramech component blueprints (Voidrig and Bonewidow parts) also require Scintillant. Various Entrati crafting offerings and decorations use Scintillant as well.

## Strategy

Focus on Isolation Vault bounties for reliable Scintillant acquisition. Each Tier 3 Isolation Vault run has a rotation C reward that includes Scintillant, and completing the full Vault chain (three tiers) often yields 2-4 Scintillant per run. Supplement by sweeping the Cambion Drift between bounty stages for additional ambient Scintillant pickups.

Run with a mobility frame (Volt, Gauss, or Titania) to cover the Cambion Drift sweep quickly between bounty stages. Use your minimap to spot Scintillant nodes -- they appear as glowing dots when in proximity.
