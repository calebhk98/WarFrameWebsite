## Overview

Dusklight Sarracenia is a carnivorous plant adapted to the polluted industrial environment of Grineer Shipyards. WFCD describes it as "a robust, carnivorous plant" that "has adapted to conditions in polluted waters," with the source being "Grineer Shipyards found commonly on Ceres." The Sarracenia genus in real botany refers to pitcher plants -- carnivorous plants that trap prey in fluid-filled tubes -- and the Dusklight variant has adapted this predatory mechanism to the toxic, chemically contaminated water environments of Grineer industrial facilities. Its survival in such conditions gives its biological chemistry unusual properties useful in Apothic crafting.

The plant grows in the Grineer Shipyard tileset, which appears on Ceres as well as in other Grineer industrial missions, but Ceres provides the most consistent access to this tileset for farming purposes.

## Where it drops

Dusklight Sarracenia grows as harvestable plant nodes within the Grineer Shipyard tileset. WFCD confirms it is "obtained by using a Codex Scanner on Dusklight Sarracenia plants growing in Grineer Shipyards found commonly on Ceres." As with all plant resources, the Dusklight Sarracenia does not appear in enemy loot tables -- it must be physically located and scanned to harvest. Plant nodes appear in specific static locations within the Shipyard tileset rather than as random spawns.

The Codex Scanner or Synthesis Scanner must be equipped and aimed at the plant node to harvest it. Equipping the Synthesis Scanner with the Loot Detect upgrade highlights plant nodes on the minimap within its detection radius, making searches more efficient. Ceres missions with the Grineer Shipyard tileset include Exterminate and Mobile Defense node types.

## Used for

Dusklight Sarracenia is required for Apothic crafting. The three Apothic types -- Twilight, Sunlight, and Shadow -- each use different plant combinations. Dusklight Sarracenia appears in one of these Apothic recipes. Apothics are consumable items used to trigger Infested Catalyst encounters in Hive Sabotage missions. The Infested Catalyst from Hive Sabotage is required to construct the Helminth Infested Room in the Clan Dojo, making Apothic crafting (and Dusklight Sarracenia farming) mandatory for players pursuing the Helminth Subsuming system. Clan Dojo Bio Lab research requiring plant-derived biological compounds also uses Dusklight Sarracenia.

## Tips

- Run Ceres Exterminate missions for Grineer Shipyard tileset access; Exterminate allows free exploration of the mission area to locate all plant nodes before extracting.
- Equip a Synthesis Scanner with the Loot Detect upgrade to highlight Dusklight Sarracenia nodes on the minimap within detection range.
- Bring a fast-movement Warframe (Volt, Gauss, or Titania in Razorwing form) to efficiently traverse the Shipyard tileset between plant node locations.
- Confirm the correct Apothic recipe before farming; there are three Apothic types and not all require Dusklight Sarracenia -- farming the wrong plant wastes time.

## Notes

Dusklight Sarracenia is specific to the Grineer Shipyard tileset on Ceres and does not appear in the Cambion Drift, Orb Vallis, Plains of Eidolon, or any non-Grineer-industrial tileset. Players farming all plants for Apothic crafting must plan separate farming sessions across multiple tilesets: Ceres (Dusklight Sarracenia), Orb Vallis (Frostcap), and Lua or Orokin Derelict (Nistlepod). Plant nodes do not respawn within a single mission session; re-entering the mission resets node positions.
