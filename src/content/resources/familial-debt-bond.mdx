## Overview

Familial Debt-Bond is the fifth and rarest tier in the Solaris United debt bond series on Venus. WFCD describes it as representing debts "unpaid by one generation are passed to the next," with "working to pay them off is seen as something to be proud of" -- capturing the generational debt slavery the Corpus impose on the Solaris workers of Fortuna. Familial bonds represent debts so large and compounding they span entire family lines, passed from parent to child under the Corpus credit system. This lore framing makes Familial Debt-Bonds the most narratively significant of the five bond tiers and the rarest to acquire.

The five bond tiers in order from most common to rarest: Training, Shelter, Medical, Advances, and Familial. Familial is required for the highest Solaris United rank advancement milestones.

## Where it drops

Familial Debt-Bonds drop from Orb Vallis bounties at 16.67% per Rotation A, B, and C across all active bounty tiers from Level 40-60 through Level 100. WFCD confirms they are "obtained from Bounties in the Orb Vallis (Venus), or purchased from Ticker in Fortuna (Venus)." Ticker is a Corpus defector in Fortuna who sells debt bonds directly using Credits or Platinum, providing an alternative acquisition route for players who have accumulated standing to trade with her.

Despite having the same 16.67% drop chance per rotation as lower-tier bonds, Familial bonds are rarest because players require far more of them for high-tier rank advancement while accumulating fewer naturally.

## Used for

Familial Debt-Bonds are required for Solaris United rank advancement at the highest tiers, specifically for reaching Instrument and Old Mate rank within the syndicate. These top ranks unlock the most powerful Kitgun and Moa parts, the Hound component blueprints, and exclusive cosmetics from Little Duck. Players who complete the full Fortuna progression track -- including farming Profit-Taker Orb and its related Vox Solaris content -- will require a significant stock of Familial bonds.

## Tips

- Run the highest Orb Vallis bounty tier completable within your loadout's power level; all tiers share the same 16.67% per rotation drop rate, so faster completion beats harder bounties for bonds per hour.
- Complete full bounty chains each session to hit Rotations A, B, and C in sequence, generating three bond-drop opportunities per run.
- Check Ticker in Fortuna each day; she sells specific bond types using Credits, which can supplement RNG farming gaps when a specific bond tier is in high demand.
- Familial Debt-Bonds are tradeable on warframe.market; purchasing them from other players is viable when farming time is scarce.
- Combine bounty sessions with Conservation and fishing activities in Orb Vallis to accumulate Solaris United standing from multiple sources simultaneously.

## Notes

Familial Debt-Bonds are rarer in practice than the 16.67% drop rate suggests because the total volume of bond rewards per session is low. Players farming lower-tier Solaris United ranks will naturally acquire some Familial bonds as byproduct drops before needing them. Stockpiling them throughout Fortuna engagement rather than farming them on demand is more efficient for endgame rank progression.
