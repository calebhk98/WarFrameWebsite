## Overview

Cubic Diodes are electronic components harvested from Corpus units and equipment throughout Venus and Corpus tileset missions. These precision components function as critical building blocks for advanced Corpus technology and serve essential roles in constructing high-end weapon blueprints. The angular cubic form enables efficient data transmission within Corpus automated systems, making them indispensable for engineers seeking reliable electronic assembly materials.

## Where it drops

Cubic Diodes drop reliably from Corpus enemies across all mission types on Venus and within Corpus ship tilesets. Corpus Techs, Prosecutors, and Shield Lancer units carry these components; targeting these elite units increases acquisition speed. Defense missions on Venus provide consistent drops throughout all waves, while Void Fissure missions amplify drop rates through void-enhanced loot tables.

## Used for

Multiple Corpus weapon blueprints require Cubic Diodes as primary electronic components. Advanced frames and equipment upgrades incorporate these diodes for extended functionality. The component appears in numerous crafting recipes; early-game players accumulate them naturally through progression while endgame players farm intentionally for specific weapon construction.

## Tips

Run Venus Defense missions for steady Cubic Diode income combined with other material drops. Bring loot frames like Hydroid or Khora to increase drop frequency per enemy defeated. Pair Venus farming with void fissure missions for enhanced rewards and void relic progression simultaneously.

## Notes

Cubic Diodes cannot be obtained from Grineer or Infested factions; Corpus exclusive sourcing means mission selection matters significantly. The cubic molecular structure referenced in the name reflects Corpus engineering precision philosophy. Players stockpiling Cubic Diodes for future weapon releases optimize long-term crafting efficiency.
