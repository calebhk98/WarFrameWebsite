## Overview

Cubic Diodes are vital components used in the creation of Corpus photon-networks, according to WFCD. These electronic components -- named for their cubic molecular geometry enabling efficient light-wave transmission -- serve as the fundamental building blocks of Corpus optical and photonic communication systems. The Corpus faction's advanced photon technology requires stable, precise electronic components like diodes to manage directional energy transfer within their weapons and communication arrays, making Cubic Diodes a foundational material in the Corpus engineering ecosystem.

Cubic Diodes belong to the Railjack material tier, obtainable from Eximus units in the Corpus Ice Planet tileset and from Railjack missions across multiple Venus-related Proxima regions.

## Where it drops

Cubic Diodes drop from Eximus-tier enemies within the Corpus Ice Planet tileset on Venus -- the same tileset found in missions like Corpus Defense and Survival on colder Venus nodes. Extended ground missions in the Corpus Ice Planet tileset increase Eximus spawn frequency as mission timer increases, improving Cubic Diode yield per session. Railjack missions in Venus Proxima, Neptune Proxima, and Pluto Proxima also yield Cubic Diodes from enemy fighters, crewships, and mission cargo, providing a space-combat source alongside the ground tileset option.

## Used for

Cubic Diodes are Railjack crafting materials required for Corpus-associated Railjack component blueprints. The photon-network application described by WFCD aligns with Railjack systems dealing with targeting, energy management, or communication subsystems within the Corpus-tier Railjack crafting chain. Players building toward optimal Railjack configuration in the Corpus Proxima regions will encounter Cubic Diodes alongside Carbides, Fresnels, and Bracoids as the Railjack material set.

## Tips

- Run Corpus Ice Planet tileset Survival missions on Venus for consistent ground-source Cubic Diodes; Eximus units spawn more frequently as survival timer increases, so longer runs are more efficient per hour than short missions.
- Railjack Venus Proxima missions are the most accessible Railjack source; begin farming Cubic Diodes from Venus Proxima as early Railjack play generates them naturally.
- Assign crew members with high Combat intrinsics to maximize loot pickup from Railjack enemy kills during space combat phases.
- Combine Cubic Diode farming with Corpus-related Warframe resource farming (Nano Spores, Salvage) during Corpus Ice Planet ground phases to maximize per-session resource variety.

## Notes

Cubic Diodes do not appear in open-world resource pools or drop from non-Eximus ground enemies on standard Corpus planets. The Eximus restriction on the ground source means farming efficiency depends on staying in missions long enough for enhanced enemy tier spawns. Players without Railjack access can still farm Cubic Diodes from the Corpus Ice Planet tileset ground missions.
