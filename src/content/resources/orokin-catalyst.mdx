## Overview

The Orokin Catalyst (informally called a "Blue Potato" by the Warframe community, due to its blue icon resembling a potato shape) is one of the most valuable resources in the game. Applying an Orokin Catalyst to a weapon permanently doubles its maximum mod capacity from 30 to 60 points. This doubling is mandatory for building high-performance endgame weapon configurations -- without it, a rank-30 weapon cannot equip a full suite of high-drain mods.

Orokin Catalysts are scarce and precious. Players receive them primarily from Nightwave season rewards, periodic alert missions, and events. Because every weapon in a player's arsenal eventually needs a Catalyst for endgame viability, veteran players accumulate and spend Catalysts constantly over hundreds of hours of play.

## Acquisition

Orokin Catalyst Blueprints are craftable in the Foundry using Argon Crystal, Neural Sensors, and other components (24-hour build time). The pre-built Catalyst is the rarer reward form, available from alerts, Nightwave, and events without the Foundry wait.

Community veterans strongly advise new players to avoid spending platinum on Orokin Catalysts except in emergencies -- they are available for free through Nightwave (which awards 1-3 per season) and periodic alert missions (which appear 1-3 times per week historically).

| Source | Frequency | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Nightwave tier reward | 1-2 per season | Most reliable free source |
| Alert mission | ~1-3 per week | Watch the alerts tab |
| Event missions | Varies | Major events reward Catalysts |
| Market purchase | 20 platinum | Last resort |

## Used In

Each Orokin Catalyst is consumed when applied to a weapon. Unlike leveling or Forma, the Catalyst permanently doubles the weapon's mod capacity (even after Forma resets). New players should apply Catalysts only to weapons they plan to keep and invest in long-term. Meta primary weapons (Kuva Bramma, Torid, Incarnon weapons) and popular secondary weapons (Laetum, Tenet Cycron) are the highest-priority Catalyst targets for endgame players.

## Strategy

Hold Orokin Catalysts until you have identified your long-term weapon choices. Do not apply a Catalyst to a weapon you are "testing" -- wait until you know the weapon is part of your permanent arsenal. Participate in every event that rewards Catalysts as guaranteed drops. Watch the Alerts tab and the Warframe companion app alert notifications for Orokin Catalyst alert missions.

Prioritize: primary weapons that carry most content (rifles, bows, shotguns for Steel Path) before applying to secondary or melee unless those are your primary combat tools.
