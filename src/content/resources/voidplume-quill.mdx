## Overview

Voidplume Quills are slender, pointed formations of void-crystallized matter commonly harvested from void-corrupted fauna in the Zariman Ten-Zero. Each quill retains intrinsic void-reactive properties that make it invaluable for armor-plating and protective equipment crafting. The sharp geometric edges make quills particularly suited for defensive equipment construction. These materials represent a significant component of the Zariman crafting ecosystem. Understanding their properties and acquisition methods is essential for successful progression through advanced void content. The materials maintain their void-energy saturation indefinitely when stored properly.
