## Overview

Bonewidow Weapon Pod is a specialized construction component exclusive to the Bonewidow Necramech blueprint. Unlike generic Damaged Necramech components used by both Necramech types, the Bonewidow Weapon Pod is specific to the Bonewidow frame and houses its unique melee-focused weapon mounting system. The Bonewidow Necramech specializes in close-range combat and melee weapons, so its weapon pod reflects this design philosophy with integrated mounting points for heavy melee armaments.

Bonewidow's construction requires four distinct blueprint components: Capsule, Casing, Engine, and Weapon Pod. All four must be acquired before the Bonewidow can be assembled in the Foundry. The Weapon Pod is typically the component that creates the most farming difficulty due to its Necramech-type-specific drop source.

## Where it drops

Bonewidow Weapon Pods drop from Bonewidow-type enemy Necramechs encountered during Isolation Vault Bounties on the Cambion Drift. The drop is specifically tied to the Bonewidow variant of the enemy Necramech rather than any generic Necramech type. If the Isolation Vault encounters primarily spawn Voidrig-type enemy Necramechs, Bonewidow Weapon Pod accumulation slows. Deimos void fissure missions that feature Necramech combat also provide a secondary source when Bonewidow variants spawn.

## Used for

Bonewidow Weapon Pod is a required component for the Bonewidow Necramech blueprint. Without sufficient Weapon Pod count, the Bonewidow cannot be constructed regardless of other component availability. The Bonewidow's weapon system -- the Ironbride heavy sword and shield setup -- connects directly to the Weapon Pod as its physical mounting point within the mech frame.

## Tips

- Run Isolation Vault Bounties on Deimos and focus combat on any Bonewidow-type enemy Necramechs that appear; their kills are the primary pod source.
- If Voidrig enemy Necramechs are spawning instead of Bonewidow types, continue running vaults rather than abandoning -- enemy type distribution is somewhat random and Bonewidow variants will appear across multiple runs.
- Check Father's vendor in the Necralisk for Bonewidow-specific components; some Necramech parts are available through standing purchase rather than pure RNG drops.
- Bring Radiation damage (effective against Alloy Armor) and Viral or Corrosive for the Necramech's health pool to optimize kill speed against enemy Necramechs.

## Notes

Bonewidow Weapon Pod is separate from the generic Damaged Necramech Weapon Pod used in the Cortege and Morgha weapon blueprints. The Bonewidow-specific component only contributes to the Bonewidow frame blueprint; accumulating generic Weapon Pods alongside it addresses the weapon crafting requirements without fulfilling the frame construction need. Plan component farming with both the frame and weapon requirements in mind.
