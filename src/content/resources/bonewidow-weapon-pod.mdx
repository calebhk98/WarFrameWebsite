## Overview

Bonewidow Weapon Pod is the integrated weaponry mounting system for the Bonewidow Necramech. This critical component houses the necramech's tactical weapon systems and hardpoints. Farming pods requires defeating Bonewidow in necramech encounters on Deimos, positioning weapon pod acquisition as an essential component of the complete necramech assembly progression.

## Where it drops

Bonewidow Weapon Pods drop exclusively from Bonewidow boss encounters during Deimos-based necramech combat. The pod drop is guaranteed upon defeating Bonewidow with appropriate squad configuration. Void fissure events on Deimos occasionally yield weapon pods as bonus event rewards during Deimos-focused event periods.
