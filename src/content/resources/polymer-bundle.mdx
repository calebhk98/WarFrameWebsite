## Overview

Polymer Bundle is a common Warframe resource representing recovered synthetic polymer materials from Corpus and Grineer manufacturing. It drops primarily on Mercury, Venus, and Uranus and is used in a significant number of mid-tier warframe and weapon blueprints. Many players find themselves running low on Polymer Bundle when building multiple warframes simultaneously, making it one of the more commonly farmed common resources despite its widespread availability.

## Acquisition

The best dedicated farm for Polymer Bundle is the Assur Dark Sector Survival on Uranus. Assur has a 25% resource drop rate boost, high enemy density typical of Uranus missions, and the Grineer enemies on the tileset drop Polymer Bundle reliably. A 20-minute Assur survival run with a Nekros or Hydroid yields 3,000-6,000 Polymer Bundle units.

For newer players without access to Uranus, Venus and Mercury provide Polymer Bundle from enemy drops and containers at a lower but still adequate rate.

| Planet | Mission | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Uranus | Assur Dark Sector Survival | Best rate with boost |
| Venus | Any mission | Early access, moderate rate |
| Mercury | Any mission | Low-level, lower density |

## Used In

Polymer Bundle is required for mid-tier warframe components including Nyx and Zephyr, as well as a variety of secondary weapon blueprints. Clan Dojo research also consumes Polymer Bundle for intermediate technology nodes.

## Strategy

Farm Assur Dark Sector Survival on Uranus with a resource-enhancing frame (Nekros, Hydroid) for efficient bulk Polymer Bundle collection. A 20-minute run typically covers several warframe builds worth of requirements. If you have a resource booster active, prioritize Polymer Bundle farming during that window to maximize your stockpile.
