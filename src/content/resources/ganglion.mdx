## Overview

Ganglion is a biological resource found exclusively on Deimos, the Cambion Drift open world. The WFCD description identifies it as "a fallen clump of entangled nerve fibers, redundant organs, and thin sensory membranes," consistent with the Infested organic material theme of the Cambion Drift environment. It is obtained primarily through Cambion Drift Bounty rewards at level 5-15 tier. Ganglion is used in Entrati-related crafting and resource exchanges on Deimos.

## Where it drops

Ganglion drops as a bounty reward from Cambion Drift bounties on Deimos. The Level 5-15 Cambion Drift Bounty, Rotation A is the confirmed drop source per WFCD data. Running multiple bounty cycles per session is the most reliable method to accumulate Ganglion quickly. Resource Boosters increase the quantity received per drop. Some enemy types native to Cambion Drift may also drop Ganglion as a secondary reward, though bounties provide the most consistent source.

## Used for

Ganglion is used in Cambion Drift crafting recipes and Entrati syndicate resource exchanges. It is an Infested biological component used in the crafting of Cambion Drift-exclusive equipment and Helminth system resources. Players engaging with Deimos content should accumulate Ganglion alongside other Cambion Drift resources to avoid bottlenecks when crafting Necramech components or Helminth food items.

## Tips

Run the Level 5-15 Cambion Drift Bounty on Deimos repeatedly for the most consistent Ganglion yield. A Resource Booster doubles the quantity received per rotation reward. Joining a squad running bounties in parallel reduces per-player time investment by sharing completion credit. Deimos-specific resource farming is most efficient during Cambion Drift active periods when enemy spawn density increases.

## Notes

Ganglion is not tradeable through Warframe.market. Its exclusive source on Deimos means players must progress through the Heart of Deimos quest to unlock the Cambion Drift before accessing it. The resource name and description reference the Infested neural tissue found throughout the Cambion Drift environment.
