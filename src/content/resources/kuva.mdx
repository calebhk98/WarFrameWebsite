## Overview

Kuva is a mid-to-late-game currency earned primarily through Kuva Lich and Sister of Parvos encounters. It serves as the resource for rerolling riven mods, building kuva weapons, and crafting endgame augment mods. Unlike static resources, Kuva farming is intertwined with the nemesis system (Kuva Liches and Sisters), making it a more engaging and variable farm than traditional mission-based grinding.

## Where it drops

Kuva is obtained through several methods: (1) Kuva Fortress void cascade missions, where it drops from enemies and sarcophagi. (2) Kuva Lich captures, where each defeated Lich yields 600 Kuva as a baseline reward (modified by difficulty and circumstances). (3) Sister of Parvos encounters, offering similar Kuva rewards to Lich captures. (4) void fissure void cascade on Kuva Fortress, which has low drop rates but is available for passive grinding.

## Best locations

Kuva Lich farming is the primary method. Engage with Liches by running nodes they influence, then challenge and defeat them. Each Lich capture yields 600-3500 Kuva depending on your Sister/Lich level and special conditions. For continuous Kuva generation, establish multiple Liches in sequence and defeat them repeatedly.

Alternatively, farming Kuva Fortress void cascade missions (rotation A/B) provides passive Kuva gains while grinding other resources, though the rate is significantly lower than Lich farming.

## Kuva lich system

Kuva Liches are custom-generated nemesis enemies created when you damage an Acolyte in mission (Kuva weapons with high affinity trigger Lich creation). Each Lich requires you to discover their Requiem sequence (three mods that bypass their damage immunity in reverse order) to defeat them. Defeating a Lich yields Kuva and a Kuva weapon with random elements. The Lich system encourages repeated engagement rather than one-time farming, providing steady Kuva income.

## Farming strategies

For dedicated Kuva farming: Create multiple Liches and defeat them sequentially. A well-optimized Lich hunt (learning Requiem mods, defeating the Lich) takes 30-60 minutes per Lich and yields 600-3500 Kuva depending on the Lich level. Experienced players farm 5-10 Liches per week for steady Kuva income.

For passive Kuva grinding: Run void cascade missions on Kuva Fortress while farming other void fissure resources. You will accumulate Kuva slowly but without additional time investment.

## Tips

Do not farm Kuva until you need it. Riven rerolls are expensive (400-1600 Kuva per reroll depending on riven rank), and Kuva demand spikes when players attempt to craft perfect rivens.

Complete Void Fissure void cascade missions on Kuva Fortress during your relic-grinding sessions; passive Kuva accumulates without dedicated farming time.

## Used in

Kuva is used exclusively for rerolling riven mods (primary sink; 400-1600 per reroll), crafting kuva weapons, and building kuva-themed augment mods. A single perfect riven reroll campaign can consume 10000+ Kuva, making Kuva a "never enough" resource for endgame modders.

## Lich system engagement

The Kuva Lich system is designed to provide narrative progression alongside resource farming. Rather than mindless mission grinding, you hunt a custom-generated enemy with unique personality. Defeating Liches yields Kuva and a personal Kuva weapon. The system encourages repeated engagement and offers social trading (buying/selling Liches to other players, though this requires coordination outside the game).

## Notes

Many endgame players maintain a steady Lich rotation to ensure consistent Kuva income. New players should engage with the Lich system early to understand the Requiem farming loop, which is a core endgame mechanic.
