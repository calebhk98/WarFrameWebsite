## Overview

Plastids are a mid-game common resource representing synthetic polymer materials extracted from Grineer and Infested biomechanical organisms. Found on Saturn, Uranus, Eris, and Pluto, Plastids are required for many mid-to-late tier warframe components and energy weapon blueprints. Unlike early-game resources like Ferrite and Salvage, Plastids are encountered later in progression and can become a bottleneck for players rapidly building mid-tier warframes.

## Acquisition

Elara Dark Sector Survival on Saturn is the standard Plastids farm. This node has a 25% drop rate bonus and Saturn's Grineer enemies drop Plastids at a solid rate. A 20-minute Elara run typically produces 1,500-3,000 Plastids units depending on squad composition and resource-enhancing frames used.

Eris is an alternative Plastids source with high-level Infested enemies that drop Plastids alongside other Infested-specific resources. Eris missions are harder but appropriate for more experienced players looking to farm Plastids alongside mutagen samples.

| Planet | Mission | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Saturn | Elara Dark Sector Survival | 25% boost, primary farm |
| Eris | Any Survival or Defense | Higher level, dual-purpose farm |
| Uranus | Any mission | Mid-level alternative |

## Used In

Plastids are required for Saryn Prime, Mesa Prime, and many other popular warframe components. Energy weapon blueprints (beam weapons, plasma launchers) often require Plastids for their synthetic material requirements. Clan Dojo Chem Lab research also consumes large quantities of Plastids for bio-engineering research nodes.

## Strategy

Farm Elara Dark Sector Survival on Saturn once you have access to Saturn. Bring Nekros or Hydroid with a resource-boosting ability. A 20-minute run with Nekros using Despoil (health cost for constant desecrate) generates significantly more Plastids per enemy kill than without. If you have a resource booster, an Elara session yields double the normal output, covering many blueprint projects at once.
