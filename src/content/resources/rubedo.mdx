## Overview

Rubedo is a distinctive crimson-red common resource in Warframe, representing a crystalline mineral found near Orokin structures and in the outer solar system. Its distinctive red color makes it one of the most visually recognizable resources in the game. Rubedo appears in crafting recipes for warframes and weapons across many content generations and is encountered early in progression on Phobos, with continued relevance through endgame Void missions.

## Acquisition

Rubedo drops from enemies on Phobos, Earth, Sedna, and Europa, as well as from resource containers in all applicable tilesets. The Void tileset is also a source of Rubedo, making it a useful secondary resource for players farming void fissures. Phobos has the earliest access to Rubedo in the game and is suitable for new players who need to farm it.

| Planet | Mission | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Phobos | Any mission | Early access to Rubedo |
| Sedna | Survival/Defense | High-level, good density |
| Void | Any fissure | Passive Rubedo alongside relics |

## Used In

Rubedo is required for a wide array of warframe blueprints spanning from early-generation frames through more recent releases. Weapon blueprints incorporating Orokin crystalline materials also require Rubedo. Clan Dojo research nodes in the Energy Lab and Chem Lab consume Rubedo for various research projects.

## Strategy

Rubedo is easy to accumulate passively on Phobos during normal star chart progression. If you need large quantities, run survival missions on Sedna or the Void while fissure farming to accumulate Rubedo as a secondary benefit. There is rarely a need for dedicated Rubedo farming unless undertaking extensive Dojo research.
