## Overview

Oxium is a unique uncommon resource in Warframe with a critical gameplay distinction: it drops exclusively from Oxium Osprey enemies (Corpus drones) and is completely lost if the Osprey self-destructs upon reaching a player or objective. Players must kill Oxium Ospreys before they detonate to collect the Oxium they carry.

This mechanic makes Oxium farming distinctly different from all other resources -- it rewards awareness and rapid target prioritization rather than simply staying in mission longer. Oxium is required for Archwing content, Zephyr warframe construction, and Corpus-technology weapon blueprints.

## Acquisition

Jupiter Corpus missions are the primary Oxium source. Io Defense on Jupiter spawns regular Oxium Ospreys during waves, and players with area-of-effect damage abilities can eliminate Ospreys before they self-destruct. Exterminate missions on Jupiter are also popular because the linear mission structure and high enemy count maximize Osprey encounter frequency.

The key rule: prioritize Oxium Ospreys immediately upon spotting them. They begin moving toward players or objectives and detonate if they reach their target. Use fast-targeting AOE abilities (Ember Fireball, Mesa Peacemaker, Volt's Electric Shield through other attacks) to eliminate them before they reach detonation range.

| Planet | Mission | Tip |

| Jupiter | Exterminate | High Osprey count |

## Used In

Oxium is primarily consumed by Archwing component blueprints (Itzal, Amesha, Odonata, and Elytron wings and body parts). Zephyr warframe blueprints require Oxium for its chassis and systems. Corpus-tech weapons with anti-gravity components also require Oxium. Clan Dojo Tenno Lab research for Archwing-related technology uses Oxium in significant quantities.

## Strategy

Run Io Defense on Jupiter with a damage-focused warframe that has reliable single-target takedown capability. Prioritize every Oxium Osprey immediately -- they appear as large spherical Corpus drones and begin moving immediately upon spawn. Use area-of-effect abilities during wave clear that also catch Ospreys in their range. Nekros desecrate does not help with Oxium collection since the Osprey must be killed before detonation.

A focused 30-minute Io session typically yields 100-300 Oxium depending on wave count and Osprey spawn rates.
