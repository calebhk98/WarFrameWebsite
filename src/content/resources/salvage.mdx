## Overview

Salvage is one of the most common crafting resources in Warframe, found in abundance on Mars, Jupiter, and Sedna. It represents recovered scrap metal and mechanical components from Grineer and Corpus structures. New players encounter Salvage almost immediately upon reaching Mars and will accumulate thousands of units naturally through star chart progression without dedicated farming.

## Acquisition

Salvage drops from most enemy types on Mars, Jupiter, and Sedna as a random loot pickup. Resource containers scattered throughout mission tilesets are especially reliable for Salvage drops. Because of its commonality, players typically do not need to farm Salvage deliberately unless they are undertaking large-scale clan research or building multiple warframes simultaneously.

The fastest dedicated farms for Salvage are the dark-sector missions on Mars, where the resource drop rate is boosted by 25% or more depending on the node. Dark Sector Survival on Mars (Wahiba) is a popular choice because it combines solid Salvage income with decent experience.

| Planet | Mission | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Mars | Dark Sector Survival | Boosted drop rate |
| Jupiter | Any mission | Corpus tileset drops |
| Sedna | Defense / Survival | High-level with resource drops |

## Used In

Salvage is consumed by many warframe chassis blueprints and various weapon components. It is also a common ingredient in Clan Dojo research projects, which may require tens of thousands of units for advanced technologies. Players building their Dojo should maintain a healthy Salvage stockpile.

## Strategy

Do not dedicate sessions to Salvage farming unless you are specifically running out during a construction queue. Standard star chart progression on Mars and Jupiter generates enough Salvage to build multiple warframes without interruption. Use a resource booster (obtained from login rewards or the in-game shop) to double Salvage drops during active farming sessions if needed.
