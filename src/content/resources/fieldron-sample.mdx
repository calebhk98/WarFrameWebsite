## Overview

Fieldron Sample represents laboratory-extracted shield compound aliquots derived from Corpus research initiatives. These controlled specimen portions maintain shield-generation properties enabling advanced crafting applications. Sample acquisition through Venus missions supplies specialized materials for players pursuing experimental shield technology development.

## Where it drops

Corpus scientist units throughout Venus and laboratory facility zones drop Fieldron Samples during combat engagement. Scientist-specialized Corpus personnel prioritize sample drops; targeted elimination optimizes acquisition. Laboratory-themed missions yield concentrated quantities compared to standard Venus missions.

## Used for

Advanced Corpus weapon blueprint construction incorporating experimental features mandates Fieldron Sample availability. Experimental shield-enhancement systems similarly require sustained sample supply. Certain warframe augments featuring experimental shield mechanics depend on sample availability.

## Tips

Prioritize Venus laboratory-themed missions for concentrated sample acquisition. Bring anti-Corpus loadouts optimizing scientist unit elimination efficiency. Accumulate reserves supporting future experimental equipment development.

## Notes

Laboratory specimen designation reflects Corpus research methodology emphasizing controlled compound isolation. Sample stability supports indefinite storage viability. Players pursuing experimental Corpus equipment development establish consistent sample farming traditions.
