## Overview

Fieldron Sample is a Corpus research material recovered from destroyed Corpus equipment. WFCD describes it as a "destroyed Fieldron" that "could further research into super-heated containment fields," establishing it as salvaged field-containment technology from Corpus weapons research. The sample form is the raw precursor material that Clan Dojo Energy Labs refine into full Fieldron units through the ClanTech research process. It drops across all major Corpus-controlled planets -- Venus, Mars, Jupiter, Europa, Neptune, and Pluto -- making it one of the most broadly available ClanTech precursor materials.

Fieldron Samples belong to the ClanTech material trio alongside Mutagen Samples and Detonite Ampules. Each of the three feeds a different Dojo lab: Fieldron Samples feed the Energy Lab, Mutagen Samples feed the Bio Lab, and Detonite Ampules feed the Chem Lab. The trio collectively unlocks the full range of Dojo research weapons.

## Where it drops

Fieldron Samples drop from general enemy kills on all Corpus-controlled planets: Venus, Mars, Jupiter, Europa, Neptune, and Pluto. Unlike many resources tied to specific mission types, Fieldron Samples appear in the standard Corpus loot table and accumulate through normal mission play. Resource containers on Corpus nodes are an additional reliable source. Corpus Invasion missions also award Fieldron Samples as mission-completion rewards, making invasions a time-efficient acquisition route when they appear in the Invasion feed.

## Used for

Fieldron Samples are the input material for crafting Fieldron in the Clan Dojo Energy Lab. Each Fieldron requires 10 Fieldron Samples and a 12-hour build time in the Energy Lab. Fieldron is in turn required for numerous weapon blueprints -- especially ClanTech weapons researched through the Energy Lab such as the Dera, Spectra, and Glaxion. Players unlocking Dojo weapon research will find Fieldron (and therefore Fieldron Samples) a recurring bottleneck material.

## Tips

- Run any standard Corpus mission on Jupiter or Neptune to accumulate Fieldron Samples alongside other useful drops like Neural Sensors and Orokin Cells.
- Monitor the Invasion alert feed; Corpus Invasions that offer Fieldron Samples as rewards provide a concentrated burst of samples per invasion-completion credit.
- Bring a Resource Booster and a Smeeta Kavat with Charm for double-drop chances on farming runs.
- Fieldron Samples stack indefinitely in inventory; banking a surplus early prevents bottlenecks when Dojo research queues open up.

## Notes

Fieldron Samples are one of the oldest crafting materials in Warframe and have remained a staple since the ClanTech Dojo system launched. Because they drop broadly across six planets, players who engage with Corpus content naturally accumulate them without dedicated farming. The 10:1 Sample-to-Fieldron conversion ratio means maintaining a large Fieldron stock requires sustained sample farming, especially for Clans actively unlocking multiple Energy Lab weapon blueprints simultaneously.
