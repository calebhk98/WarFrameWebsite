## Overview

Javlok Capacitor is a weapon component resource used exclusively in crafting the Javlok, a Grineer-themed thrown spear primary weapon. The WFCD description confirms it is "used in the construction of Javloks," making it a single-purpose crafting component. It is obtained on Earth through Plains of Eidolon-related content, consistent with the Javlok's Grineer connection to the plains tileset. Six Javlok Capacitors are required per crafting run per the WFCD itemCount field.

## Where it drops

WFCD drop table data does not list specific drop entries for Javlok Capacitor, indicating it may be obtained through a specific Plains of Eidolon bounty stage or from specific Grineer enemy types on Earth. Per community resources and the Fandom wiki, Javlok Capacitor is obtained from Grineer enemies during Plains of Eidolon missions, particularly from Grineer Prosecutors. Running Cetus Bounties on Earth that include Grineer high-value targets in the rotation provides the most consistent acquisition environment. The low required quantity of six per craft means a few targeted farming sessions should be sufficient.

## Used for

Javlok Capacitor is required for crafting the Javlok weapon and is used in no other blueprints tracked by WFCD data. Players who do not intend to craft the Javlok have no use for stockpiling this component. The Javlok itself is a Grineer-faction thrown spear primary weapon used by Grineer Prosecutors, obtainable by players through Foundry crafting after gathering all required components.

## Tips

Run Cetus Bounties on Earth when targeting Javlok Capacitors. Focus on bounty tiers that include Grineer enemy types. Resource Boosters double the quantity received per drop occurrence. The plains environment during daytime provides higher enemy density for outdoor Grineer patrol encounters. Bringing a Warframe with good area-of-effect damage or crowd control helps clear Grineer clusters quickly during bounty objectives.

## Notes

Javlok Capacitor is not tradeable through Warframe.market. Access to the Plains of Eidolon requires completing the initial Cetus introduction on Earth. The WFCD entry for this component lists it under the parent item Javlok, confirming its exclusive role as a Javlok crafting material.
