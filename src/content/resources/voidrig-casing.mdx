## Overview

Void-Rig Casings are protective outer shells designed to withstand void-energy exposure and serve as structural foundations for Void-Rig Necramech upgrades. These casings are harvested from the void-corrupted Necramech units encountered in advanced Zariman missions. The casings retain residual void properties even after extraction, making them invaluable for defensive equipment. These materials represent a significant component of the Zariman crafting ecosystem. Understanding their properties and acquisition methods is essential for successful progression through advanced void content. The materials maintain their void-energy saturation indefinitely when stored properly.
