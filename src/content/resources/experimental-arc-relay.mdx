## Overview

Experimental Arc-Relay is a Höllvanian engineering component from the WF1999 game mode set in 1999 Höllvania. WFCD describes it as proof that "Höllvanian ingenuity knows no bounds," calling it "a cutting-edge device brimming with untamed innovation." Arc-relays in Höllvania are experimental electrical communication and power-routing devices developed by the city's engineers independent of Corpus or Grineer oversight, reflecting the scrappy underground ingenuity of Höllvania's residents in the face of the Techrot infestation and Scaldra military occupation.

The "unregulated" nature emphasized in the description distinguishes these devices from Corpus photon-network Cubic Diodes or standard electrical components -- they are improvised, uncertified, and potentially unstable, making them valuable precisely because they bypass regulated supply chains.

## Where it drops

Experimental Arc-Relays drop from Höllvania bounties and specific mission types in the WF1999 game mode. Drop rates sourced from WFCD:

| Source | Drop Chance |
|---|---|
| Level 55-60 WF1999 Bounty, Rotation C | 4.88% |
| Level 85-90 WF1999 Bounty, Rotation C | 12.96% |
| Level 105-110 WF1999 Bounty, Rotation C | 12.00% |
| Antivirus Bounty (Exterminate) | 14.29% |
| Exterminate: Scaldra | 14.29% |
| Exterminate: Techrot | 14.29% |

The Level 85-90 bounty and the dedicated Exterminate missions offer the best individual drop rates. Exterminate mission types (Antivirus Bounty, Scaldra Exterminate, Techrot Exterminate) all share the 14.29% rate and tend to be faster to complete than full bounty chains, making them efficient targets for Arc-Relay farming.

## Used for

Experimental Arc-Relays are crafting materials within the Hex syndicate economy in Höllvania. They feed into WF1999 equipment blueprints and standing accumulation with The Hex. The experimental electrical components of the arc-relay make it relevant to crafting items dealing with power routing, signal transmission, or Techrot-countermeasure technology in the WF1999 content ecosystem.

## Tips

- Run Höllvania Exterminate mission types (Antivirus Bounty, Scaldra, Techrot) when Arc-Relays are the primary target; the 14.29% rate and fast mission completion make these more efficient per minute than full bounty chains.
- The Level 85-90 WF1999 Bounty at 12.96% Rotation C is the best bounty-chain source; access requires completing lower-tier Höllvania bounties to unlock higher difficulty tiers.
- Combine Arc-Relay farming with other Höllvania resource goals (Efervon Samples, Hex Treasures) to reduce total farming sessions needed.
- Higher-difficulty Höllvania missions produce denser enemy formations; area-denial or crowd-control Warframes improve kill efficiency in Exterminate modes.

## Notes

Experimental Arc-Relays are exclusive to WF1999 content and cannot be obtained from standard planetary nodes, Railjack, or open-world missions outside Höllvania. Players who have not progressed into the WF1999 quest content do not have access to Höllvania and therefore cannot farm this resource. Arc-Relay drops appear at Rotation C of most bounty tiers, meaning partial bounty completion misses the primary drop window.
