## Overview

Experimental Arc Relay represents cutting-edge electrical component technology developed within Corpus research laboratories. These prototype arc-generation systems enable advanced weapon construction and specialized electrical enhancement mechanics. Arc relay acquisition through Corpus facility missions supplies essential components for players pursuing experimental electrical-based equipment development.

## Where it drops

Corpus elite units throughout Corpus Ships and laboratory facilities drop Arc Relays during combat encounters. Scientist-specialized Corpus personnel prioritize relay drops; targeted elimination optimizes acquisition. Laboratory-themed missions yield concentrated quantities compared to standard facility engagement.

## Used for

Experimental weapon blueprint construction incorporates Arc Relays as primary electrical component. Advanced electrical enhancement systems similarly depend on Arc Relay availability. Certain warframe augments featuring electrical mechanics require sustained Arc Relay supply.

## Tips

Prioritize laboratory-themed Corpus missions for concentrated Arc Relay acquisition. Bring electrical-damage-focused loadouts amplifying Corpus enemy elimination efficiency. Accumulate reserves during favorable farming periods supporting future equipment construction.

## Notes

Prototype designation reflects cutting-edge Corpus electrical engineering research and experimental methodology. Relay durability ensures indefinite storage viability supporting long-term accumulation. Players pursuing experimental electrical equipment arsenal establish consistent Arc Relay farming traditions.
