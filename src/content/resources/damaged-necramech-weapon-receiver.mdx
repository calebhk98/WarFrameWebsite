## Overview

Damaged Necramech Weapon Receiver is a component for Necramech weapon construction, described by WFCD as "A broken frame that can be used in the construction of a Necramech weapon," located in "Isolation Vault and Arcana Isolation Vault Bounties on the Cambion Drift (Deimos), and purchased from Loid in the Necralisk." The receiver is the ammunition feeding and firing control mechanism of a Necramech weapon -- the component that links the barrel to the trigger system and manages shot cycling. All Necramech-mounted weapons require a Damaged Receiver as part of their blueprint.

The "damaged" designation reflects the scavenged origin of these components, stripped from defeated enemy Necramechs during Isolation Vault engagements.

## Where it drops

Damaged Necramech Weapon Receivers drop from Isolation Vault Bounty Rotation B at 3% chance and from the harder Arcana Isolation Vault Bounty Rotation B at the same 3% rate. Rotation B appears after the second stage of the bounty chain. The 3% rate is low, making Loid's Necralisk shop a meaningful supplement -- once unlocked, Loid sells Weapon Receivers directly for Entrati standing, bypassing RNG entirely. Running multiple full bounty chains per session (always completing through Rotation B at minimum) maximizes per-session yield from drops.

## Used for

Damaged Necramech Weapon Receivers are required for all Necramech weapon blueprints, including the Cortege heavy weapon and Morgha, both of which are the primary armaments for Bonewidow and Voidrig respectively. Each weapon blueprint requires multiple Receivers alongside the corresponding Barrel and Stock/Pod components. Players building a complete Necramech weapon arsenal will accumulate a significant Receiver farming investment.

## Tips

- Run the Arcana Isolation Vault Bounty at the highest difficulty you can sustain; the Arcana tier features more enemy Necramech spawns and the same Rotation B drop rate as the standard vault.
- Always complete through at least Rotation B of the bounty chain (stage 2) to access the Weapon Receiver drop slot; exiting after Rotation A misses the primary source.
- Unlock Loid's shop by completing enough Isolation Vaults; once available, buying Receivers with Entrati standing is a reliable supplement to RNG drops.
- Bring a squad for Isolation Vault runs; the vault requires interacting with multiple objectives simultaneously, and a full squad completes these faster, increasing runs per hour.

## Notes

Necramech weapon components (Receiver, Barrel, Stock/Pod) are distinct from the Necramech frame components (Casing, Engine, Pod). Both sets are required for a complete Necramech loadout. The Weapon Receiver has the same 3% drop rate across both Isolation Vault tiers, making consistent bounty completion more important than vault selection for this specific component.
