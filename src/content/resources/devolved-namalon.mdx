## Overview

Devolved Namalon is a processed form of Namalon, described by WFCD as having been "retrieved from its Infested state, restored to worth and utility." Namalon is an Infested mineral deposit on Deimos that has been overtaken by the biological corruption spreading across the Cambion Drift. Devolved Namalon represents a de-Infested, purified version of this ore, restored to a usable state for crafting purposes. The term "devolved" in this context means the Infested corruption has been reversed or stripped away, returning the mineral to its base form.

This processing model -- where raw Deimos materials must be refined or purified before use -- is characteristic of the Necralisk's approach to the Cambion Drift's corrupted ecosystem. Otak, the Necralisk's enthusiastic researcher, sells the blueprint for Devolved Namalon, enabling players to process their raw Namalon into the refined form.

## Where it drops

Devolved Namalon is not a direct drop from enemies or mining nodes. The blueprint for crafting Devolved Namalon is sold by Otak in the Necralisk on Deimos. Players must first acquire raw Namalon from the Cambion Drift (collected from Infested mineral deposits during mining runs), then use the Otak blueprint to process it in the Foundry into Devolved Namalon. This two-step acquisition mirrors other Deimos alloy resources like Adramal Alloy and Axidrol Alloy.

## Used for

Devolved Namalon feeds into Deimos equipment blueprints that require processed or refined plant-mineral hybrid materials. Its use in crafting recipes reflects the Necralisk's engineering focus on extracting usable components from Deimos's corrupted landscape. Several Entrati weapon blueprints and component upgrades available from Necralisk vendors list Devolved Namalon as a required ingredient alongside other Deimos-specific resources.

## Tips

- Purchase the Devolved Namalon blueprint from Otak in the Necralisk early in Deimos progression; having the blueprint ready prevents delays when raw Namalon begins accumulating from mining runs.
- Farm raw Namalon from Infested mineral deposits in the Cambion Drift during standard Deimos exploration and combine it into Devolved Namalon batches in the Foundry between missions.
- Check blueprint craft costs from Otak to understand the raw Namalon-to-Devolved Namalon conversion ratio and pre-farm accordingly.
- Mining runs that target Namalon deposits will also encounter other Deimos ores (Adramalium, Hespazym) simultaneously, making broad mining routes more efficient than focusing on a single ore type.

## Notes

Devolved Namalon is a Foundry-crafted material rather than a directly farmed drop. Players who have not purchased Otak's blueprint cannot produce Devolved Namalon regardless of how much raw Namalon they have accumulated. Visiting Otak and acquiring the full catalog of Necralisk blueprints early is recommended to avoid processing bottlenecks later in Deimos progression.
