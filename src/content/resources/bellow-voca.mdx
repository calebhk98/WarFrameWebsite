## Overview

Bellow Voca is the deepest expression of Voidtongue, etched into stone -- described by WFCD as "an unlikely asset in the construction of Albrecht's vessels." This characterization reveals two things: Bellow Voca is a manifestation of Voidtongue (the language of the Void itself, which Albrecht Entrati spent his career studying), and it contributes materially to the construction of vessels within Albrecht's Laboratories. The "bellow" quality suggests the lowest frequency range of Voidtongue utterances, contrasting with the Shrill Voca (highest) and Echo Voca (quietest). Albrecht's long search for these Voidtongue expressions forms the narrative backdrop for the Entrati Lab content.

Bellow Voca is the mid-tier of the three Voca types within Albrecht's Laboratories standing economy.

## Where it drops

Bellow Voca appears in Rotation C of two Albrecht's Laboratories bounty tiers. The Level 75-80 bounty drops Bellow Voca at 12.4% chance, while the Level 95-100 bounty offers it at 13.16%. Both represent Rotation C -- the final reward tier requiring full bounty chain completion. The relatively consistent drop rate across both tiers (around 12-13%) means the Level 75-80 tier is farmable earlier in Deimos progression while the Level 95-100 tier offers no significant rate improvement, making tier choice primarily a matter of mission difficulty tolerance.

## Used for

Bellow Voca is a currency and crafting material within the Albrecht's Laboratories syndicate. It contributes to standing accumulation with the Entrati Lab faction and feeds into blueprints for Entrati Lab-exclusive equipment. Specifically, WFCD notes its role in "the construction of Albrecht's vessels" -- research constructs within the Laboratories that require Voca materials. Players pursuing the full Entrati Lab catalog encounter Bellow Voca in multiple recipe requirements alongside Echo Voca and Shrill Voca.

## Tips

- The Level 75-80 bounty offers Bellow Voca at 12.4% -- effectively the same rate as the Level 95-100 bounty's 13.16%; choose the difficulty tier based on farming speed rather than rate optimization.
- Always complete the full bounty chain; Rotation C only triggers at full completion, and Bellow Voca does not appear in Rotation A or B.
- Combine Bellow Voca farming with Shrill Voca and Echo Voca collection since all Voca types drop from the same Albrecht's Laboratories bounty pools.
- Higher-tier bounties (Level 115-120) provide access to Echo Voca alongside Bellow Voca; if your loadout can handle it, running the highest tier covers multiple Voca types simultaneously.

## Notes

Bellow Voca is the entry point into the Voca progression within Albrecht's Laboratories, available from Level 75-80 bounties. Echo Voca (the rarest) requires Level 115-120 bounties. Shrill Voca occupies an intermediate position. Farming all three Voca types in parallel is more efficient than sequential progression through each tier. All Voca items are non-tradeable and must be earned through personal Deimos participation.
