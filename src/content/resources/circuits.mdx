## Overview

Circuits are an early-to-mid game common resource representing electronic circuit components recovered from Corpus manufacturing facilities and Grineer machinery. They drop on Venus and Ceres and are consumed by a very large number of warframe component blueprints, making them one of the most frequently depleted common resources for active builders. Players building several warframes in rapid succession often find Circuits to be a bottleneck alongside Polymer Bundle and Plastids.

## Acquisition

The most efficient Circuits farm is Seimeni Dark Sector Defense on Ceres. This node has a 25% resource drop rate bonus and is an accessible mid-game node. Defense missions are efficient because enemy density is high and there is no need to travel through the tileset -- enemies come to the objective. A squad of four players can complete 20-wave Seimeni rotations in 10-12 minutes, generating thousands of Circuits per session.

| Planet | Mission | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Ceres | Seimeni Dark Sector Defense | 25% boost, best rate |
| Venus | Any survival or defense | Early game access |

## Used In

Circuits appear in dozens of warframe chassis, neuroptics, and systems blueprints across multiple warframe generations. They are also used in companion equipment blueprints and Clan Dojo energy lab research. Kubrow gene masking and imprinting procedures also consume Circuits.

## Strategy

New players should farm Seimeni Ceres Defense once they have access to Ceres. A 20-wave session (roughly 12 minutes) generates enough Circuits for 3-5 standard warframe components. Bring Nekros for desecrate loot enhancement to maximize per-enemy drops. Do not neglect Circuits -- running out during a building rush forces a farming detour that interrupts progress momentum.
