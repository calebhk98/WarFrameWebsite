## Overview

Argon Crystals are unique void-related resources with a critical mechanic: they decay after 24 hours of leaving the Void, becoming unusable. Players must plan carefully when farming these crystals because hoarding large quantities is impossible. Argon Crystals appear in high-level Void Fissure missions and are used for crafting Orokin catalysts and late-game blueprints.

## Where it drops

Argon Crystals drop exclusively from Void Fissure endless missions, appearing in the rotation B and C reward pools. This means they are not available until 5-10 minutes into a Survival mission, 3+ extractions into Excavation, or equivalent progression in other endless Fissure types. The longer you stay in the mission, the higher the probability of receiving Argon Crystals.

| Mission type | Availability | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Void Fissure Survival (B/C) | 5+ minutes | Best option; consistent rate |
| Void Fissure Defense (B/C) | Wave 6+ (30+ minutes) | Time-intensive for a single crystal |
| Void Fissure Excavation (B/C) | 4+ extractions | Second-best option |
| Void Sabotage | Never | Rotation A only; no B/C access |

## Best locations

Void Fissure Survival is the most efficient farm for Argon Crystals because rotation B arrives at 5 minutes and rotation C at 10 minutes, allowing players to grab crystals and exit quickly. In a 10-minute Survival run, the crystal drop chance in rotation C is approximately 25-30%, but you can chain multiple runs to reach a target stockpile before decay.

## Decay mechanic

Argon Crystals decay after 24 hours outside of the Void. Once they decay, they become "Void Tears" in your inventory and are unusable for any blueprint. Plan your farming sessions to occur shortly before using the crystals, or farm on-demand rather than stockpiling.

## Tips

Farm Argon Crystals close in time to when you need them. A 10-minute Void Fissure Survival run nets one crystal; doing two such runs gives you 2 crystals good for 24 hours.

Bring strong damage or resource-boosting abilities (e.g., Pilfering Swarm on Hydroid, Despoil on Nekros) to maximize loot drops and complete rotations faster.

Use a void relic with high void trace cost so you can farm void traces passively while crystal farming; this sustains your void relic supply.

## Used in

Argon Crystals are required for Orokin Catalyst blueprints (mandatory for Orokin Catalysts; without them, you cannot max-rank equipment slots). Several endgame warframe blueprints also require 1 Argon Crystal. High-tier weapon blueprints from Void Fissure rewards may consume Argon Crystals in their build recipes.

## Farming strategies

For experienced players, a single 10-minute Void Fissure Survival run often yields one Argon Crystal. The optimal strategy is to run Survival until rotation C (10 minutes), then extract immediately. If you need multiple crystals, plan for multiple runs within a 24-hour window.

## Notes

The 24-hour decay is a deliberate game mechanic designed to prevent indefinite stockpiling and to encourage regular void fissure engagement. Many players farm Argon Crystals only when immediately needed to avoid waste due to decay.
