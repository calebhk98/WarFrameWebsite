## Overview

Bioplasma is an exotic biological-technological hybrid resource obtained from Corpus vessel interiors and research facilities. This rare material combines organic and synthetic properties, serving as a cornerstone component for bio-technological equipment. The resource's hybrid nature reflects Corpus advancement in bioengineering and mechanical augmentation across their fleet operations.

## Where it drops

Bioplasma drops from Corpus ship tilesets and facility-based missions with modest frequency. The resource appears in container loot and enemy drops during combat encounters on Corpus-controlled planets and vessels. Extended mission durations increase container spawning and provide more opportunities for bioplasma acquisition.

## Best locations

Corpus ship excavation and survival missions provide optimal Bioplasma yields compared to shorter mission types. Running extended sessions on Void Fissure Corpus nodes occasionally yields bonus bioplasma drops. Neptune and Europa-based Corpus missions serve as reliable farming locations with consistent drop patterns.

## Used for

Bioplasma is consumed in bio-technology blueprints and biological augmentation equipment with typical costs ranging from 15 to 45 units per craft. Players pursuing biological-focused builds encounter bioplasma as a frequent bottleneck resource. Accumulating reserves enables rapid equipment iteration and build testing.

## Tips

Focus farming on extended Corpus ship missions to maximize container spawns. Farm in public squads for mission speed bonuses and increased rewards. Equip loot radius mods to capture bioplasma drops from greater distances. Bank extra resources to enable rapid equipment crafting without bottleneck delays.
