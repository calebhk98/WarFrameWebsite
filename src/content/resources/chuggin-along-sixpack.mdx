## Overview

Chuggin' Along Sixpack is a Hex Treasure collectible from the WF1999 game mode, set in the retro-futuristic Höllvania of 1999. WFCD describes it as "A set of liquid comfort perfect for anyone who deserves a moment to unwind. Ideal for someone carrying the weight of the world on their shoulders," and specifies it is traded with Lettie (identified internally as the Arthur-associated Hex Dog Tag) to earn standing with The Hex syndicate. The beverage packaging reflects the 1999 setting's pop-culture consumer goods aesthetic, where branded products from the era appear as collectible in-world items.

The sixpack joins other Hex Treasures (Big Bytes Pizza, Cheddar Crowns Cereal, On-Lyne Cd, 35mm Film) as part of The Hex's social economy, where finding and trading these nostalgic items with specific syndicate members builds relationship standing.

## Where it drops

Chuggin' Along Sixpack drops from the Höllvania Level 75-80 bounty at a 15.56% Rotation C rate per WFCD drop data. This is one of the higher Hex Treasure drop rates among Rotation C items, making the Level 75-80 bounty tier a reliable farming target for this specific item. Exploration caches and environmental containers within Höllvania missions also yield Hex Treasures; thorough map exploration during bounty runs can produce additional sixpacks as secondary finds.

## Used for

Chuggin' Along Sixpack is traded with Lettie (or the specific Hex member associated with this Hex Treasure type) to earn standing with The Hex. The Hex syndicate economy on Höllvania uses Hex Treasures as social currency -- finding and sharing items that resonate with specific Hex members builds trust and standing with the faction. Standing accumulation through Hex Treasure trading is separate from the bounty-completion standing economy, providing an exploration-based supplement to bounty farming.

## Tips

- The Level 75-80 Höllvania bounty provides a 15.56% Rotation C drop for Chuggin' Along Sixpack; run the full bounty chain rather than exiting early to access Rotation C.
- Explore all side rooms and environmental areas in Höllvania missions; Hex Treasures appear as interactive objects in civilian and storage areas within the building-heavy tileset.
- Trade accumulated Hex Treasures with Lettie during Höllvania visits between bounty runs to maintain standing progress alongside the main farming activity.
- Combine Sixpack collection with other Höllvania Hex Treasure farming (Cheddar Crowns Cereal for Lettie, Big Bytes Pizza for the rare category) in the same sessions to maximize standing across multiple Hex members.

## Notes

Chuggin' Along Sixpack is not a standard crafting resource -- it functions purely as a social standing currency within The Hex. Its cultural appeal within the Höllvania setting (as a comforting beverage for "someone carrying the weight of the world") connects to The Hex members' personal narratives and the working-class atmosphere of the 1999 Höllvania setting. The item cannot be traded between players in the standard trade system.
