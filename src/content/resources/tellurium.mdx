## Overview

Tellurium is a mid-to-late-game resource primarily used for Archwing and Railjack crafting. Unlike some rarer resources that have limited farming locations, Tellurium drops from Sedna's Grineer enemies and is available through Archwing missions on Uranus. Players pursuing endgame Railjack content or Archwing equipment will need to farm Tellurium regularly.

## Where it drops

Tellurium is found on Sedna and Uranus. On Sedna, it drops from any Grineer enemy at a low drop chance (approximately 2-3%), making it a "byproduct" farm rather than a dedicated grind. On Uranus, Archwing missions offer higher drop rates, especially during Sharkwing (underwater) archwing combat sections.

| Location | Mission type | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Sedna | Any Grineer mission | Low drop chance; multi-resource farm |
| Uranus | Archwing Defense / Mobile Defense | Higher drop chance; pure archwing |
| Uranus | Sharkwing missions | Underwater archwing; best drop rate |
| Alert missions | Archwing variants | Occasional alerts; guaranteed drops |

## Best locations

Uranus Archwing missions, particularly Sharkwing (underwater) variants, offer the best Tellurium drop rates. A single Sharkwing Excavation or Defense run on Uranus typically yields 1 Tellurium. Sedna works for players not ready for Uranus level enemies, but the drop chance is significantly lower, requiring 15-20 minute Survival runs to obtain a single Tellurium on average.

## Drop rates

In Uranus Archwing missions, Tellurium has an approximate 8-12% drop chance per enemy killed. In Sharkwing sections, the rate increases to approximately 12-15% due to the denser enemy spawns. Sedna's drop rate is approximately 2-3%, making it inefficient for dedicated farming once Uranus is accessible.

## Tips

Do not farm Tellurium on Sedna unless you are pre-Uranus. The drop chance is too low relative to mission completion time.

Farm Uranus Archwing missions with a full squad for faster spawns and more loot tables. Bring a resource-booster if available; it doubles Tellurium drops.

Combine Tellurium farming with Void Fissure void-trace grinding by running void fissures on Uranus, if available (events occasionally enable this).

## Used in

Tellurium is mandatory for Archwing blueprint trees, including Archwing weapons and components. It is also required for several Railjack component blueprints, such as Reactor blueprints and weapon systems. Advanced weapon blueprints that require Railjack materials often include Tellurium in their cost.

## Farming strategies

For players pursuing Railjack content, a standard farm is 3-4 runs of Uranus Archwing Defense (roughly 15-20 minutes total) to net 3-4 Tellurium. This is sufficient for most Railjack progression milestones.

For early-mid game players still locked out of Uranus, Sedna is acceptable but time-consuming. Expect to spend 30-45 minutes per Tellurium at Sedna drop rates.

## Notes

Tellurium demand spikes significantly during Railjack content updates. Many players farm stockpiles (10-20 units) in preparation for new Railjack components.
