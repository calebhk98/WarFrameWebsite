## Overview

Devar is a rare cloudy crystal from the Blue Mining Veins of the Plains of Eidolon. WFCD describes it simply as "A cloudy crystal," located on the "Plains of Eidolon (Earth) from Blue Mining Veins and Thumpers." Its cloudiness distinguishes it visually from the clearer or more vivid gems found in the same Blue Veins (Azurite, Crimzian), reflecting the distinct mineral composition of each gem type in the Plains ecosystem. Devar functions as the raw ore form that can be processed into Esher Devar -- a polished and cut version sold through Old Man Suumbaat's blueprint service in Cetus.

The Devar/Esher Devar pair follows the same raw-ore-to-cut-gem processing chain seen with other Plains gem resources, where the raw material is mined and then refined into a higher-tier crafting component.

## Where it drops

Devar drops from Blue Mining Veins scattered across the Plains of Eidolon. Mining requires a Nosam Cutter or higher-tier mining laser, with better tools yielding more gems per extraction. Blue Veins appear as blue-glowing nodes in rock formations across the plains terrain. Tusk Thumper variants also carry Devar in their rare drop table; killing Thumpers while traversing the plains provides a combat supplement to the primary mining source. The Conclave Variant Team Annihilation Rotation A/B drops the cut Esher Devar at 3.16% -- but this applies to the processed form, not raw Devar.

## Used for

Devar's primary use is as input material for the Esher Devar blueprint sold by Old Man Suumbaat in Cetus. Processing raw Devar through Suumbaat's blueprint produces Esher Devar, which is the cut gem form required by higher-tier Cetus equipment recipes. Some basic Ostron crafting recipes may also call for raw Devar directly before the processing step. Players who want Esher Devar for advanced gear must first accumulate raw Devar and then process it via the Foundry.

## Tips

- Blue Veins on the Plains of Eidolon cluster in rocky hillside and cliff areas; use community mining maps to identify high-density Blue Vein zones for efficient routes.
- Upgrade to an Advanced Nosam Cutter or Sunpoint Plasma Drill for better gem extraction yields -- the mining minigame rewards precision, and better tools extend the acceptable accuracy window.
- Thumper hunting during Plains traversal adds Devar to the loot pool from combat; Thumpers appear naturally when running long distances across the plains and can be engaged with any loadout.
- Purchase the Esher Devar blueprint from Suumbaat on your first Cetus visit to ensure you can convert raw Devar immediately when needed.

## Notes

Devar and Esher Devar are separate items. Recipes list one or the other specifically; confirm which is needed before mining. The cloudy appearance of raw Devar contrasts with the polished transparency of Esher Devar, reflecting the visual difference between unprocessed and cut gemstones. Neither form is tradeable between players and must be personally acquired.
