## Overview

Fieldron is a refined ClanTech material produced in the Clan Dojo's Energy Lab. WFCD describes it as "ClanTech weaponized containment field to contain superheated substances," establishing its function as an engineered field-containment unit used as a component in energy-based and heat-based weapons. It is crafted from Fieldron Samples -- salvaged fragments of destroyed Fieldron devices -- through the Energy Lab's blueprint. Fieldron is one of three core ClanTech materials alongside Mutagen Mass (from the Bio Lab) and Detonite Injector (from the Chem Lab), and all three are required for different sets of researched weapons.

Corpus Invasion missions occasionally award Fieldron directly as a completion reward, providing an alternative to Dojo crafting for players with access to a Clan.

## Where it drops

Fieldron is primarily crafted, not dropped. The Energy Lab blueprint (standard in all Clan Dojos with an Energy Lab built) consumes 10 Fieldron Samples and a 12-hour build timer to produce one Fieldron. Players without Clan access can occasionally acquire Fieldron from Corpus Invasion rewards when invasions offer it as a mission-completion bonus. WFCD confirms the sources as "Clan Research (Energy Lab in the Dojo) and Corpus Invasion Rewards."

The Energy Lab itself requires Clan Research to unlock and build, and Fieldron production is gated behind a functioning Energy Lab and available Fieldron Samples. The 12-hour build time means sustained Fieldron production requires planning ahead of weapon crafting sessions.

## Used for

Fieldron is required for ClanTech weapon blueprints researched through the Energy Lab. These include energy-based weapons like the Dera assault rifle (fires heat rounds), the Spectra sidearm, and the Glaxion rifle. Several Primed and Prisma variants of ClanTech weapons also require Fieldron as a core component. Players pursuing the full ClanTech weapon roster will farm Fieldron repeatedly as a mandatory crafting material.

The broader ClanTech ecosystem connects Fieldron to energy-weapon research, while Mutagen Mass covers biological weapon research and Detonite Injector covers explosive weapon research -- together, the three materials define the Dojo's three weapon research tracks.

## Tips

- Queue Fieldron production in the Energy Lab proactively; the 12-hour build timer means starting production before you need the output prevents bottlenecks.
- Monitor Corpus Invasion missions in the Alerts feed for Fieldron rewards; these offer Fieldron without the 12-hour wait and supplement Dojo crafting.
- Keep a surplus of Fieldron Samples (from Venus, Mars, Jupiter, Europa, Neptune, or Pluto general farming) to maintain a production queue in the Energy Lab.
- Players in a Clan with multiple active members benefit from the increased Fieldron production capacity of higher-tier Dojo modules.

## Notes

Fieldron is the Energy Lab analog to Detonite Injector (Chem Lab) and Mutagen Mass (Bio Lab). Players who have not joined a Clan and built an Energy Lab cannot produce Fieldron and are limited to Corpus Invasion supply. For Clan-building players, Fieldron production rate is one consideration when planning Clan Research progression -- Energy Lab research must complete before weapon blueprints using Fieldron become available.
