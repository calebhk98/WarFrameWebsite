## Overview

Forma is one of the most universally important and consumed resources in all of Warframe. It is used to add polarity to a mod slot on a warframe, weapon, companion, or Archwing, enabling players to equip more mods with a lower total mod capacity cost. Because optimal builds typically require 3-6 Forma applications per item (each application resets the item to rank 0 and requires re-leveling to rank 30), Forma is a constant demand resource throughout every stage of the game.

Forma Blueprints are the crafted version requiring 24-hour build time in the Foundry. Pre-built Forma can be obtained from void relic rewards, Nightwave, sorties, and direct platinum purchase. The blueprint drops from many different void relics as the 2X Forma Blueprint variant (yielding 2 Forma for one blueprint build).

## Acquisition

Void Fissure missions are the primary source of free Forma. Nearly every void relic in every tier (Lith through Axi) has a Forma Blueprint as a common or uncommon reward. Running large numbers of fissures generates a steady supply of Forma Blueprints that compound over time. Players who run 10-20 void fissures per day naturally accumulate significant Forma Blueprint stacks.

Nightwave season reward tiers include Forma Bundles (3 Forma each) as direct rewards at specific mastery tiers, providing guaranteed Forma to players who engage with the Nightwave challenge system weekly. Sorties reward a Forma Bundle (3 Forma) approximately 7% of the time as a rotation pool item.

| Source | Forma per acquisition | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Void Fissure relic reward | 1 (blueprint) or 2 (2x blueprint) | Common from fissures |
| Nightwave tier reward | 3 (bundle) | Seasonal milestones |
| Sortie reward | 3 (bundle) | ~7% drop rate |
| Market purchase | 1 per 35 platinum | Always available |

## Used In

Forma is consumed every time a player applies it to an item. Standard builds require 3-6 Forma per warframe or weapon to fully optimize polarities. High-efficiency endgame builds may use up to 8 Forma on a single item. Popular, must-have warframes like Mesa Prime, Saryn Prime, and Wisp Prime each require 4-6 Forma for their optimal builds, and any player building a diverse arsenal will use hundreds of Forma over their playtime.

## Strategy

Never purchase Forma Blueprints; they are extremely common from void fissure relic rewards. Build all Forma Blueprint stacks consistently -- queue them in the Foundry (24-hour build time each) and maintain a Foundry queue of 5-10 at all times. This passive Forma production runs in the background while you play other content.

Prioritize Forma for the warframes and weapons you use most frequently before spreading to secondary items. A focused Forma investment on your main 3-5 weapons and 2-3 warframes produces the most meaningful build improvement per Forma spent.
