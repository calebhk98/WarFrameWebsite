## Overview

Vitus Essence is the special currency of the Arbitration endgame mode in Warframe. It drops from Arbitration Shield Drones -- unique shielded drone enemies that appear in Arbitration missions and protect nearby enemies from all damage while alive. Killing an Arbitration Shield Drone has a chance to drop a Vitus Essence pickup.

Vitus Essence is spent at the Arbiters of Hexis store in any relay to purchase Arbitration-exclusive mods, including some of the most powerful endgame mods in the game. Adaptation (damage resistance stacking), Marked for Death (damage multiplication through status effects), and other unique mods are gated behind Vitus Essence purchases, making Arbitrations a required endgame activity for players seeking optimal builds.

## Acquisition

Vitus Essence drops from Arbitration Shield Drones that are killed during Arbitration missions. Each killed drone has a moderate chance (approximately 10-25%) to drop 1 Vitus Essence. More drones killed per mission means more Essence per run. Endurance Arbitration runs (30+ minutes) generate more Essence than short rotations.

Arbitrations are high-stakes missions: players have one life (no revives), enemies are level 60+ and scaled, and the rewards are only available to players who have completed all star chart nodes. This gate ensures Arbitrations are accessible only to experienced players.

| Session length | Approximate Vitus Essence |
|---|---|
| 10 minutes (rotation A) | 1-3 |
| 20 minutes (rotation B) | 3-6 |
| 40 minutes (rotation C+) | 8-15 |

## Used In

Vitus Essence is spent in the Arbiters of Hexis Arbitration store at any relay. Key purchases include: Adaptation mod (highest priority), Marked for Death, Cascade, Rolling Guard, Thermal Sunder, and other rotation-specific mods that are unavailable from other sources. The store also sells Forma Bundles and Ayatan Sculptures for Essence at favorable rates.

## Strategy

Run Arbitration Survival with a durable frame (Inaros, Nidus, Revenant) and prioritize killing every Arbitration Shield Drone immediately. These drones are visually distinct (tethered shields around enemies) and must be targeted first. A damage frame with strong area clear (Saryn, Octavia) efficiently destroys large numbers of shielded enemies per rotation.

Plan your Vitus Essence purchases: buy Adaptation first as it is the highest-impact mod from the store and has no other acquisition method. Save remaining Essence for other exclusive mods based on your current build priorities.
