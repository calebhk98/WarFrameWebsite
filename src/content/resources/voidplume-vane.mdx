## Overview

Voidplume Vanes are broad, wing-like formations of void-energy crystallization harvested from void entities in the Zariman Ten-Zero. These lightweight structures possess natural aerodynamic properties and void-reactive resonance, making them critical for warframe wing augments and air-manipulation equipment. The vanes shimmer with characteristic void luminescence. These materials represent a significant component of the Zariman crafting ecosystem. Understanding their properties and acquisition methods is essential for successful progression through advanced void content. The materials maintain their void-energy saturation indefinitely when stored properly.
