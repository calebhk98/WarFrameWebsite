## Overview

Fresnels is a rare Orokin material used in Railjack construction for creating tears in space that allow safe Void passage. WFCD describes it as "a rare Orokin material used to create a tear in space and allow safe passage through the Void," establishing it as a specialized Void-engineering substance that the Orokin developed for interstellar and void-transiting vessels. The name references the Fresnel lens -- a type of optical device that focuses light across a flat plane -- which is consistent with a material that focuses or opens Void access points rather than raw physical tearing.

Fresnels belong to the Corpus Proxima Railjack material tier alongside Aucrux Capacitors and Cubic Diodes, forming the core material set for Corpus Proxima Railjack construction and upgrades.

## Where it drops

Fresnels drop from Railjack missions across Venus Proxima, Neptune Proxima, and Pluto Proxima. Enemy fighters, Corpus crewships, mission cargo, and environmental loot containers within Corpus Proxima space nodes yield Fresnels as Railjack loot. The rarity designation means they drop less frequently than common Railjack materials, requiring extended Proxima farming to accumulate sufficient quantities for blueprint construction.

| Proxima | Difficulty | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Venus Proxima | Lowest | Earliest access; lower enemy density |
| Neptune Proxima | Mid | More enemy formations per mission than Venus |
| Pluto Proxima | Highest | Most enemies per run; highest Fresnel yield per mission |

Neptune and Pluto Proxima missions produce better Fresnel yields per run due to higher enemy density resulting in more drop opportunities compared to Venus Proxima's lower-tier missions.

## Used for

Fresnels are crafting materials for Corpus Proxima-tier Railjack component blueprints. Their Void-passage function (creating tears in space) makes them relevant to Railjack avionics and components dealing with Void navigation, engine efficiency, and jump systems. Players building toward advanced Railjack configurations for Corpus Proxima missions encounter Fresnels alongside Aucrux Capacitors and Cubic Diodes as the core Corpus Proxima material trio.

## Tips

- Neptune Proxima offers better enemy density than Venus Proxima; the increased fighter and crewship count per mission generates more Fresnel drop opportunities per run.
- Assign Railjack crew members with high Combat intrinsics to maximize enemy kills per mission; more destroyed ships means more loot drops including Fresnels.
- Run Railjack Exterminate or Defense node types on Corpus Proxima nodes for high enemy concentration missions that maximize Fresnel accumulation.
- Fresnels are Railjack-exclusive; completing Rising Tide and assembling a Railjack is a hard prerequisite before any Proxima farming is possible.

## Notes

Fresnels cannot be obtained from any ground mission, open-world zone, or standard planetary node. The Corpus Proxima tier is only accessible to players who have both assembled a Railjack (Rising Tide quest) and progressed into the Corpus Proxima node system. The Void-tearing function described by WFCD connects Fresnels narratively to the Orokin's mastery of Void manipulation, the same technology underlying the Tenno's Transference and the Orbiter's Void jump capability.
