## Overview

Cerulean Fungus Extract is an organic material extracted from Cerulean Fungus colonies found on Earth. WFCD describes it simply as "Extracted from a Cerulean Fungus colony," reflecting the direct biological harvesting method required to obtain it. The cerulean (blue-green) coloring of the fungus species distinguishes it from other Earth flora, suggesting it thrives in specific shaded or moist terrain conditions within the Earth tileset.

Like other plant and fungus resources in Warframe, Cerulean Fungus Extract does not drop from enemies. It must be personally harvested by locating fungus colonies during Earth missions and interacting with them. This makes it a resource that rewards map exploration and environmental awareness rather than combat efficiency.

## Where it drops

Cerulean Fungus Extract is obtained by scanning or harvesting Cerulean Fungus colonies within the Earth tileset. A Codex Scanner or Synthesis Scanner can locate fungus clusters and extract the material upon interaction. The fungus grows in specific environmental zones within the procedurally generated Earth missions -- typically in darker, shaded areas of the forest tileset. Systematic exploration of Earth missions with an active scanner highlights fungus locations on the minimap, allowing efficient collection during otherwise combat-focused runs.

## Used for

Cerulean Fungus Extract is one of the organic ingredients for Apothics -- the consumable items needed to trigger Infested Catalyst encounters in Hive Sabotage missions. Hive Sabotage is a mission variant that awards the Infested Catalyst blueprint, required for constructing the Helminth Infested room in the Clan Dojo (necessary for the Warframe Subsuming system). Players pursuing the Helminth system must complete Hive Sabotage, making Cerulean Fungus Extract a mandatory farming target at some point in the progression chain. The extract also appears in Bio Lab research recipes within the Clan Dojo for players developing the organic research track.

## Tips

- Equip a Codex Scanner before entering Earth missions; the scanner overlay highlights harvestable fungus colonies on the minimap within detection range.
- Earth Spy missions allow thorough exploration of the tileset because the primary objective (hack terminals) encourages visiting multiple rooms, naturally exposing more fungus colonies per run.
- Combine Cerulean Fungus Extract farming with other Earth resource collection (Morphics, Kubrow eggs, Neurodes) during the same mission runs to maximize productivity.
- Identify which specific Apothic you need and confirm its full ingredient list before farming; different Apothic types require different plant combinations alongside the fungus extract.

## Notes

Cerulean Fungus Extract cannot be traded between players and must be personally harvested. Because Apothic crafting is a prerequisite for Hive Sabotage, players aiming for the Helminth system should gather all required Apothic ingredients in a single coordinated farming session across Earth and Orokin Derelict missions rather than piecemeal across many sessions.
