## Overview

Detonite Ampule is an uncommon resource representing recovered Grineer explosive chemical capsules. It drops on Ceres and Jupiter and is primarily consumed by Clan Dojo Chem Lab research through the Detonite Injector crafting process. Players building out their Clan Dojo research tree in the Chemistry Lab encounter Detonite Ampules as a gating resource.

Unlike general warframe or weapon construction resources, Detonite Ampules are used almost exclusively for Dojo research, making them a specialized resource that only becomes relevant when pursuing Clan technology.

## Acquisition

Detonite Ampules drop from Grineer enemies on Ceres as an uncommon loot drop and from resource containers. For dedicated farming, Ceres Dark Sector Survival (Seimeni) with its resource drop rate boost is the best source, particularly because it also generates Circuits and Alloy Plate simultaneously -- a multi-resource efficiency win.

| Planet | Mission | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Ceres | Seimeni Dark Sector | 25% boost, combined farm |
| Jupiter | Any mission | Alternative source |

## Used In

Detonite Ampules are the primary ingredient for Detonite Injectors, which are required for many Chem Lab research projects in the Clan Dojo. If your clan is progressing through Chem Lab research (Torid, Penta, Angstrum, and other explosive-type weapon blueprints), you will need to farm Detonite Ampules specifically for the Injector production runs.

## Strategy

Farm Detonite Ampules during combined Seimeni sessions when also farming Circuits. The dark sector boost applies to all resources simultaneously. Coordinate with your clan to farm Injector components together rather than having each player solo-farm the required quantities, reducing total farming time significantly.
