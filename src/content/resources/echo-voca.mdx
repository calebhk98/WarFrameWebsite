## Overview

Echo Voca is the rare-tier standing token of the Albrecht's Laboratories syndicate on Deimos. WFCD describes it as "the quietest and most elusive of Voidtongue utterances. Sought after but never found by Albrecht." Voidtongue is the language of the Void as understood by Albrecht Entrati -- the patterns of Void energy that carry meaning for those sensitive to the Void's nature. The Echo Voca represents the faintest and most difficult-to-perceive Voidtongue utterance, one that Albrecht himself spent his research career pursuing without success. That it now appears as a physical resource in his laboratories carries a melancholy irony.

Echo Voca is the highest tier of the three Albrecht's Laboratories Voca tokens: Shrill Voca (common), Bellow Voca (uncommon), and Echo Voca (rare). It is the rarest and most valuable of the three for standing and crafting purposes.

## Where it drops

Echo Voca drops exclusively from the highest-tier Albrecht's Laboratories bounty. WFCD confirms the only source is the "Deimos/Albrecht's Laboratories Level 115-120 Entrati Lab Bounty, Rotation C" at 14.71%.

| Source | Drop Chance |
|---|---|
| Level 115-120 Albrecht's Laboratories Bounty, Rotation C | 14.71% |

The Level 115-120 bounty is the hardest available Albrecht's Laboratories content. Players who have not reached endgame power levels (high-ranked Arcanes, maxed Warframe builds) will struggle to complete this bounty efficiently. The 14.71% drop rate at Rotation C means the Echo Voca does not drop from earlier bounty tiers -- only the highest available tier provides access.

## Used for

Echo Voca is used for Necraloid syndicate standing at the rare tier and as a crafting component in Albrecht Entrati's research vessel blueprints. The vessel construction system introduced in Whispers in the Walls requires all three Voca types (Shrill, Bellow, Echo) in varying quantities. Players pursuing the Necraloid syndicate's highest rank and the associated rewards -- including Tenebrous Ephemera, rare Operator Amp parts, and Helminth Segment access -- need Echo Voca as the primary bottleneck material for high-tier standing and vessel crafting.

## Tips

- Build an endgame-ready loadout before farming Level 115-120 Albrecht's Laboratories content; the enemy power level at this tier requires steel-path caliber builds to farm efficiently.
- Run the Level 115-120 bounty to Rotation C and exit; the Echo Voca only drops at Rotation C, and extending beyond Rotation C in the same run provides lower-priority rewards.
- Combine Echo Voca farming with Stela and Grotesque Splinter collection from the Level 115-120 bounty to accumulate all high-tier Albrecht's Laboratories materials per session.
- Echo Voca is tradeable on warframe.market; purchasing from other players is viable for players who have not yet reached endgame power levels.

## Notes

Echo Voca's single source -- the Level 115-120 bounty at Rotation C -- makes it the most access-gated resource in the Albrecht's Laboratories system. The lore of the Echo Voca being what Albrecht sought but never found connects to the thematic arc of Whispers in the Walls, where Albrecht's unfinished research into the Murmur's Voidtongue drives the questline. Players who complete Whispers in the Walls and engage with Albrecht's Laboratories content will find Echo Voca the primary pacing material for Necraloid progression.
