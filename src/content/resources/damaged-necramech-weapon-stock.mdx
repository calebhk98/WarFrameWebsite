## Overview

Damaged Necramech Weapon Stock is a crafting component recovered from Isolation Vaults on the Cambion Drift on Deimos. It is described in WFCD as the fractured back portion of a large Necramech weapon. The "Damaged" designation reflects the salvaged state of the component; it retains full crafting utility despite its worn appearance. Once acquired, Damaged Necramech Weapon Stock is used as a primary ingredient in assembling higher-quality crafted Necramech weapon components such as Cortege Stock, Damaged Necramech Weapon Barrel, and related parts.

The component is rare, appearing only in Rotation C of Isolation Vault and Arcana Isolation Vault bounties, and can also be purchased directly from Loid in the Necralisk after completing The War Within quest. Players building a full Necramech weapon loadout will encounter Damaged Necramech Weapon Stock as one of the main farming targets in Deimos bounty progression.

## Where it drops

Damaged Necramech Weapon Stock drops at 3% chance per Rotation C completion from the following Cambion Drift bounty missions:

- Isolation Vault bounty at Level 30-40 (Rotation C)
- Isolation Vault bounty at Level 40-50 (Rotation C)
- Isolation Vault bounty at Level 50-60 (Rotation C)
- Arcana Isolation Vault bounty at Level 30-40 (Rotation C)
- Arcana Isolation Vault bounty at Level 40-50 (Rotation C)
- Arcana Isolation Vault bounty at Level 50-60 (Rotation C)

All vault tiers share the same 3% Rotation C drop chance. Loid in the Necralisk also sells Damaged Necramech Weapon Stock directly for Necraloid standing after the player completes The War Within quest, providing an alternative to bounty farming.

## Used for

Damaged Necramech Weapon Stock is consumed as an ingredient in assembling the following crafted Necramech weapon components:

- Cortege Stock -- requires 1 Damaged Necramech Weapon Stock per assembly
- Damaged Necramech Weapon Barrel
- Damaged Necramech Weapon Pod
- Damaged Necramech Weapon Receiver

Each of these crafted components is a prerequisite for assembling specific Necramech arch-gun weapons. Players planning to build multiple Necramech weapons should accumulate Damaged Necramech Weapon Stock across multiple Isolation Vault farming sessions.

## Tips

Rotation C requires completing three full bounty stages before the reward is issued. Prioritize efficient squad compositions to clear Isolation Vault stages quickly and reach Rotation C in fewer attempts. Public squads generally speed up vault completion.

Loid's standing shop is a reliable fallback for players who have accumulated enough Necraloid standing but have poor luck with Rotation C drops. The War Within quest is a prerequisite for accessing Loid's full inventory; completing it early in Deimos progression unlocks this option.

Stock up on multiple Damaged Necramech Weapon Stocks in a single farming session before transitioning to other crafting phases. Each major Necramech weapon requires multiple crafted components, each of which may consume one or more stocks.
