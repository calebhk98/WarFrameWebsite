## Overview

Frostcap is a plant resource engineered by the Orokin for environmental adaptation. WFCD describes it as having been "engineered by the Orokin to convert airborne chemicals into breathable air," establishing it as a biological air-purification organism developed during the Orokin era. The Orb Vallis on Venus, with its own atmospheric chemistry altered by Corpus terraforming and Orokin-era environmental modification, hosts Frostcap plant nodes as a remnant of this Orokin bio-engineering program. The plant's air-conversion function made it practically useful for colonization efforts -- and continues to make its biological chemistry valuable for Apothic crafting.

The uniqueName `/Lotus/Types/Gameplay/Venus/Resources/VenusMushroomItem` identifies Frostcap as a mushroom-type plant resource native to Venus's Orb Vallis environment.

## Where it drops

Frostcap grows as harvestable plant nodes in the Orb Vallis on Venus. As with other plant resources, it does not drop from enemies -- it must be physically located and harvested by approaching the plant node with a Codex Scanner or Synthesis Scanner equipped. Frostcap nodes appear in sheltered areas of the Orb Vallis terrain, particularly in the colder elevated regions where Venus's atmospheric chemistry is most consistent with the Orokin-era environmental conditions the plant was engineered for.

Scanning with the Synthesis Scanner activates the Codex minimap overlay that highlights nearby resource nodes within detection radius, making active scanning essential for efficient Frostcap farming. The Vasca Kavat companion's Hunter Recovery trait reduces the time needed between harvesting and locating new nodes.

## Used for

Frostcap is required for Apothic crafting. Apothics are consumable items used to trigger Infested Catalyst encounters in Hive Sabotage missions. There are three Apothic types: the Twilight Apothic, the Sunlight Apothic, and the Shadow Apothic. Each requires different plant combinations. Frostcap appears as an ingredient in at least one Apothic type, making it a mandatory farming target for players pursuing the Helminth Infested room in the Clan Dojo (which requires completing Hive Sabotage at least once with an Apothic). Clan Dojo Bio Lab research projects that require Orb Vallis plant extracts also consume Frostcap.

## Tips

- Equip a Synthesis Scanner with the Loot Detect upgrade before entering Orb Vallis; the Loot Detect upgrade extends the minimap highlight range for resource nodes significantly.
- Use a fast Warframe (Volt, Gauss, Titania in Razorwing) to cover Orb Vallis terrain quickly between Frostcap nodes; the Vallis is large and nodes can be spread across elevation changes.
- Frostcap nodes do not respawn within the same mission session; exit and re-enter Orb Vallis to reset node positions between farming runs.
- Confirm the Apothic recipe before farming -- verify which of the three Apothics requires Frostcap to avoid farming the wrong plant combination.

## Notes

Frostcap is one of several Orokin-engineered plants in Warframe that persist as harvestable resources in areas associated with Orokin colonization (the Orb Vallis, Lua, Orokin Derelict). The biological air-purification function described by WFCD is consistent with the Orokin practice of creating purpose-built organisms for environmental engineering. Players farming all plants for Apothic crafting should plan multi-location sessions: Frostcap requires the Orb Vallis while other Apothic plants (Nistlepod, Dusklight Sarracenia) require different tilesets.
