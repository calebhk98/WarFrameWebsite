Entrati Lab Dog Tag represents identification credentials carried by Entrati family laboratory personnel and security systems. These authentication tokens enable access to restricted Entrati research facilities and specialized experimental equipment. Dog tag acquisition through Cambion Drift encounters supplies essential access credentials for players pursuing advanced Entrati laboratory research participation.

Cambion Drift void creatures prioritize Dog Tag drops upon defeat. Laboratory-specialized environment zones feature concentrated dog tag caches. Mission completion similarly yields tags through advanced-tier rewards.
