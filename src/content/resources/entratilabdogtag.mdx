## Overview

Entratilabdogtag (internal designation `/Lotus/Types/Gameplay/EntratiLab/Resources/EntratiLabDogTag`) is the base-tier resource within Albrecht's Laboratories' syndicate token system on Deimos. The Albrecht's Laboratories content introduced in the Whispers in the Walls update organizes its standing currency into tiered Voca resources -- Shrill Voca (common), Bellow Voca (uncommon), and Echo Voca (rare) -- which represent Voidtongue utterances preserved in stone from Albrecht Entrati's research. The `EntratiLabDogTag` base item is the root template of this tier system, used internally by the game engine to organize the dog-tag-style standing currency structure shared across Warframe's syndicate content.

WFCD lists this item without a public-facing description, indicating it functions as a system-level resource rather than a player-visible item with lore text. Players interacting with Albrecht's Laboratories will encounter the named Voca variants (Shrill, Bellow, Echo) as the actual collectable and tradeable currencies rather than this base item directly.

## Where it drops

Albrecht's Laboratories bounties on Deimos are the source of all Entrati Lab syndicate resources. The specific named Voca items drop from different bounty tiers:

| Item | Bounty Tier | Drop Chance |
|---|---|---|
| Shrill Voca (Common) | Level 55-60 and 65-70, Rotation C | 13.04-13.56% |
| Bellow Voca (Uncommon) | Level 75-80 and 95-100, Rotation C | 12.40-13.16% |
| Echo Voca (Rare) | Level 115-120, Rotation C | 14.71% |

The base Entratilabdogtag token may appear as part of this progression system and feeds into the broader Albrecht's Laboratories standing mechanics.

## Used for

Resources within the Albrecht's Laboratories system are used for Necraloid syndicate standing, crafting Albrecht Entrati's research vessels, and purchasing items tied to the Whispers in the Walls content. The Necraloid syndicate on Deimos manages access to Operator Amp components, Cephalon research items, and the Helminth Segment blueprint -- making the Albrecht's Laboratories resource system relevant to players pursuing both Operator progression and Helminth customization.

## Tips

- Focus on named Voca items (Shrill, Bellow, Echo Voca) when farming Albrecht's Laboratories standing; these are the player-visible currencies used in Necraloid trades.
- Albrecht's Laboratories bounties scale in level from 55-60 to 115-120; the highest-tier bounties unlock Echo Voca (the rarest Voca) at 14.71%.
- Bring a resource-booster loadout to Albrecht's Laboratories bounties to maximize standing resource acquisition per run.
- Combine bounty runs with farming Entrati Obols, Necracoil, and Vessel Capillaries from the same Albrecht's Laboratories missions to accumulate all Deimos endgame materials simultaneously.

## Notes

The `EntratiLabDogTag` designation follows the same naming convention as other Warframe syndicate token systems -- Zariman uses `ZarimanDogTag` variants (Voidplume Down, Vane, Quill, Crest), while Höllvania uses `HexDogTag` variants (Cheddar Crowns Cereal, Big Bytes Pizza, etc.). The Albrecht's Laboratories system maps this convention to Voidtongue utterances preserved in stone, consistent with the lore of Albrecht Entrati's research into the Void and the Murmur entity.
