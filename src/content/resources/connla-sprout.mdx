## Overview

Connla Sprout is a plant resource from Duviri, described by WFCD as having water that "courses through Connla Sprouts" that "tastes sweet and refreshing." This characterization positions the Connla plant as one of the more benign and pleasant organisms within Duviri's often oppressive emotional landscape, providing a refreshing contrast to the melancholy and isolation that define the Duviri setting. Duviri citizens presumably use Connla Sprouts as a food or medicinal resource, consistent with the broader theme of Duviri as a self-contained civilization struggling to survive within Dominus Thrax's loop.

Connla Sprout is one of several Duviri plant resources (alongside Dracroot, Eevani, Kovnik, and others) that contribute to the Duviri crafting economy managed through Teshin's Cave.

## Where it drops

Connla Sprouts appear in Duviri Endless mode at Tier 1 and Tier 3 completion rewards, offering 30 sprouts at 0.67% chance per tier. Plant nodes distributed across the Duviri open world also yield Connla Sprouts through the standard plant harvest interaction -- approaching and interacting with plant clusters while a Synthesis Scanner highlights nearby nodes. The Duviri Circuit is a secondary source but at lower or unconfirmed rates compared to the Endless drop table.

## Used for

Connla Sprouts contribute to the Duviri resource economy accessed through Teshin's Cave. They serve as crafting ingredients for Duviri-exclusive weapons and components tied to the Duviri story track. The Duviri weapon set (introduced with The Duviri Paradox update) requires various combinations of Duviri plant and mineral resources, including Connla Sprouts, for their blueprints. Players pursuing the complete Duviri catalog will encounter Connla Sprouts across multiple crafting chains.

## Tips

- Duviri Endless Tier 1 and Tier 3 awards large batches (30x) when the 0.67% drop triggers; plan rotations to hit both tiers per Endless session.
- Use a Synthesis Scanner while traversing the Duviri world between combat objectives; plant nodes highlight on the minimap, allowing passive Connla Sprout collection during objective travel.
- Combine all Duviri plant farming (Connla Sprout, Dracroot, Eevani, Kovnik) in a single systematic harvesting route through the open world, as all plants grow in the same environment.
- The Duviri open world features procedurally placed plant clusters that vary in position between sessions; explore broadly rather than relying on fixed coordinates.

## Notes

Connla Sprout is not tradeable between players. Duviri access requires completing The Duviri Paradox quest, and Connla Sprouts only appear within Duviri content. The large batch size of Endless drops (30x per successful roll) means accumulation is efficient when the drops occur, but the 0.67% rate introduces run-to-run variance. The pleasant taste described in the WFCD description is a lore detail connecting the plant to Duviri's civilian culture rather than any gameplay mechanic.
