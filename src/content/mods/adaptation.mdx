## Overview

Adaptation is a rare warframe mod that grants escalating resistance to damage types the player receives. When equipped, each time a warframe takes damage of a specific type, that warframe gains a stacking resistance buff against that damage type for a duration. The resistance caps at 90 percent, making it one of the most powerful defensive tools available for scaling into endgame content.

## Acquisition

Adaptation drops exclusively from Arbitrations, the endgame endless mission variant. It appears as a rare reward in both Rotation A and Rotation B of Arbitration fissures, with slightly higher drop chance in later rotations. Because it is tradable, players can also purchase it from other players through the warframe.market or in-game trading channels.

## Builds that use it

Adaptation is considered a staple defensive mod for any warframe attempting Steel Path, Arbitrations, or endless void fissure runs. Frames with low armor benefit especially, as the mod's damage-type-specific scaling bypasses armor calculations. However, even high-armor frames gain significant survivability by stacking Adaptation with other defensive mods. Solo players and speedrunners particularly value its low drain cost and ability to scale indefinitely with sustained damage.

## Notes

Adaptation's resistance stacking creates a dynamic defense that grows stronger the longer a warframe remains in combat. The mod does not grant invulnerability windows like Rolling Guard; instead, it gradually reduces incoming damage as combat continues. With maxed rank and high fusion, a warframe can achieve near-immunity to specific damage types while taking sustained hits. The buff can be reset by avoiding damage for its duration, making positioning and cover important. The mod was historically controversial due to overpowered synergies with other defensive tools, prompting several balance updates to prevent near-total invincibility in certain configurations.
