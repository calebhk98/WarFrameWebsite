## Overview

Ravage is a critical-damage melee mod providing up to 60% critical-damage scaling at rank 5. The mod is fundamental for any crit-focused melee build and works synergistically with critical-chance mods to enable exponential damage scaling. Ravage is particularly valuable on weapons with high natural critical multipliers where additional critical-damage scaling stacks multiplicatively.

## Stats per rank

Ravage scales linearly from 10% critical damage at rank 0 to 60% at rank 5, providing consistent critical-damage enhancement for crit-focused builds.

## Acquisition

Ravage is a rare mod obtained through mid-game star chart progression and enemy drops. Players typically encounter it on Mars, Jupiter, and later planets during mid-game farming. The mod is also available through relic farming and marketplace trading.

## Usage

Ravage is essential on crit-focused melee builds where critical hits deal significant damage. Pair it with critical-chance mods like Blood Rush or Primed Pistol Gambit to create crit builds that scale multiplicatively. On weapons with high natural crit multipliers like Nikana Prime or Orthos Prime, Ravage enables additional damage scaling that compounds with other multipliers.

In squad play, Ravage combined with high crit-chance values ensures consistent critical hits that trigger additional effects or crowd control from crit-focused mods. The mod scales poorly on weapons with low or zero critical multipliers, making it less valuable for pure-impact weapons. On Condition Overload builds, Ravage provides additional damage to critical hits that proc status effects.

## Common builds

Ravage appears on all crit-focused melee builds. Nikana Prime, Orthos Prime, and Dragon Nikana builds commonly use Ravage as a primary crit-damage mod. The mod is less common on status-focused builds that prioritize status effect damage over critical-damage scaling.
