## Overview

Anemic Agility is a pistol trade-off mod providing fire-rate scaling at the cost of damage reduction, enabling rapid-fire secondary builds that emphasize bullet volume over individual-shot power. At rank 5, the 60% fire-rate boost combined with 30% damage penalty creates net-positive DPS on high-ROF secondaries, particularly those with large magazine capacity. The 4-point drain makes Anemic Agility space-efficient.

Anemic Agility excels on pistols with moderate damage and high fire rates, where the damage penalty is offset by bullet volume. Weapons like the Akstiletto Prime or dual-wield pistols transform into suppressive secondaries capable of sustained fire without reload interruptions. Status-focused builds leverage Anemic Agility for rapid proc stacking, enabling status overflow through sheer application speed.

Anemic Agility occupies a secondary slot competing with pure damage, crit scaling, and utility mods. For rapid-fire builds, Anemic Agility enables viable secondary strategies; for slow, hard-hitting secondaries, damage scaling outperforms fire-rate. The trade-off is contextual, requiring careful consideration of weapon type and build goals.

## Acquisition

Anemic Agility drops rarely from mid-level Corpus units, particularly in level 20-35 star chart missions. Natural progression yields the mod passively; deliberate farming of specific Corpus nodes accelerates acquisition.

## Builds that use it

Anemic Agility dominates rapid-fire secondary builds, particularly on weapons with high magazine capacity. Slow, hard-hitting secondaries skip Anemic Agility entirely, focusing on damage scaling. Status-overflow builds leverage the fire-rate bonus for rapid proc stacking.

## Notes

The 60% fire-rate bonus more than compensates for the 30% damage penalty on high-ROF weapons through exponential bullet volume increases. The trade-off heavily favors fire-rate-focused builds, enabling competitive secondary-weapon setups that rival primary weapons in sustained output. Status-focused builds particularly benefit from the fire-rate scaling, as rapid applications multiply status proc frequency dramatically. Pairing Anemic Agility with dual-wield pistols and status-chance mods enables rapid crowd control through status overflow, particularly effective against armor-heavy enemies susceptible to status-based crowd control mechanics.
