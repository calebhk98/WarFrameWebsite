## Overview

Critical Delay is a primary rifle mod that provides +200% critical chance and -20% fire rate (x2 for bows). With a low 4-point drain, making it inexpensive to slot, this mod fits efficiently into primary rifle builds. The Naramon (-) polarity aligns with standard primary rifle builds, minimizing the need for Forma investment to slot it without capacity penalty. At maximum rank 5, the mod delivers its full effect, making rank-up investment worthwhile whenever mod capacity allows.

The mod occupies a competitive slot in the primary rifle mod configuration. Players must evaluate it against alternatives that address damage, survivability, or utility depending on mission type and personal playstyle preference. For players who rely heavily on the effect this mod provides, it becomes a staple inclusion across most loadouts, while players optimizing for raw damage output may substitute it situationally.

Critical Delay can be obtained through various gameplay activities. Players who need the mod quickly can often find it through trading with other players via the in-game trade channel or warframe.market. Farming directly from drop sources provides a more sustainable long-term supply if the player plans to run multiple copies or level several through Fusion. New players should prioritize this mod whenever it appears in their collection, as even low-ranked copies provide immediate value. Veteran players typically maintain a fully-ranked copy and may invest in a second copy if running multiple loadouts that benefit from the effect simultaneously.

## Notes

Critical Delay has remained a relevant mod choice throughout Warframe's history, maintaining its utility despite power creep and balance patches.
