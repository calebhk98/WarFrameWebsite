## Overview

Eternal War is an augment mod for Valkyr's Warcry ability, converting Warcry from a time-limited buff into a renewable resource based on melee kills. Each melee kill extends Warcry's duration by up to 2 seconds at rank 3, enabling indefinite uptime during sustained combat. The augment transforms Valkyr from a stat-boosting frame into a melee-centric powerhouse whose aggressive playstyle naturally sustains her buffs.

Eternal War synergizes perfectly with Valkyr's melee-focused identity and her Talons exalted weapon, creating a positive feedback loop: more melee kills extend Warcry, which buffs melee stats, enabling more kills and further extensions. In endless missions and Steel Path content, Eternal War enables effectively permanent Warcry uptime, turning Valkyr into an unkillable whirlwind of damage.

The augment requires Valkyr ownership and Warcry leveling, limiting accessibility. However, for Valkyr mains, Eternal War is nearly mandatory for aggressive playstyles. The 6-point drain is acceptable for augments providing such powerful gameplay changes. Combining Eternal War with Berserker Fury or Bloodletting creates absurdly high attack-speed scaling.

## Acquisition

Eternal War is available from syndicate vendors at Exalted Rank standing, requiring significant reputation farming with a single syndicate (Steel Meridian, Red Veil, New Loka, or Perrin Sequence). Alternatively, trading with other players or relying on lucky void fissure drops provides shortcuts.

## Builds that use it

Eternal War dominates aggressive Valkyr builds, particularly melee-focused Steel Path builds. Frames without heavy melee emphasis don't use Eternal War. Valkyr's endgame builds universally include Eternal War for indefinite buff uptime, making it essential rather than optional for endgame Valkyr play.

## Notes

Eternal War requires sustained melee kills to maintain uptime. In missions with long downtime between enemies (some void cascades), Warcry may expire if kills stop occurring. However, in typical endless mission pacing, Eternal War sustains itself naturally, creating powerful gameplay loops that reward aggressive play. The augment's synergy with melee-focused ability casting and weapon selection creates a build identity centered entirely around maintaining offensive momentum through continuous combat engagement. Valkyr players often pair Eternal War with duration-scaling mods to maximize the extended buff window, enabling sustained aggression without ability recasting.
