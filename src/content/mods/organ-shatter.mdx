## Overview

Organ Shatter is the melee-equivalent of Point Strike plus Vital Sense, providing both critical chance and critical damage multiplier scaling. This dual-scaling mod transforms melee weapons from steady damage dealers into burst-spike engines where every critical hit deals exponential damage. For critical-focused melee weapons, Organ Shatter is often the difference between competitive and dominant damage.

The mod's effectiveness scales dramatically with weapon base critical chance and critical multiplier. Weapons designed around critical hits (daggers, swords with high crit multiplier) unlock Organ Shatter's full potential. However, even lower-crit-multiplier weapons benefit from the critical damage bonus when crit chance is high.

## Acquisition

Organ Shatter is an uncommon drop from all enemy units. New players acquire their first copy after a few missions, and multiple ranks farm trivially through combat. The mod is available from the Market for a reasonable credit cost.

## Builds that use it

Organ Shatter appears in virtually every critical-focused melee build. High-crit-multiplier weapons (Nikana Prime, Ceramic Dagger, Pathocyst) rely on Organ Shatter for damage scaling. Hybrid critical-status builds also include Organ Shatter to leverage both damage paths.

## Notes

Organ Shatter's critical damage bonus is multiplicative with weapon base critical multiplier. A weapon with 2.0x base critical multiplier gains +5.5x additional damage at rank 10, resulting in a 7.5x final critical multiplier. This swing is why critical-focused melee weapons scale so dramatically with Organ Shatter.

Weapons with low base critical multiplier (1.5x or lower) see diminishing returns from Organ Shatter's critical damage bonus. Conversely, weapons designed around critical hits (2.5x+) transform into one-hit-kill engines with Organ Shatter, Weeping Wounds, and high critical chance mods.
