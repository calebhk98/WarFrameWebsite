## Overview

Freeze Force is a Warframe augment mod for Frost's Freeze ability. When held rather than tapped, Freeze coats all allies within 15 meters in a Cold damage bonus lasting up to 40 seconds at max rank. At rank 3, the buff grants 100% additional Cold damage to all allied weapons, applying to every hit and compounding with elemental combination mods. The range is fixed at 15m regardless of Power Range modding.

The Cold damage bonus is particularly useful when combined with other elements to form combined damage types. Cold plus Toxin creates Viral damage, which is one of the strongest damage-type combinations against Grineer and Corpus. Cold plus Electricity creates Magnetic, effective against Corpus shields. Freeze Force turns Frost into a support frame capable of directly buffing squad damage output, not merely protecting with Snow Globe.

## Acquisition

Freeze Force is available through the Steel Meridian syndicate offering. Players with sufficient Steel Meridian standing can purchase it directly. The mod is tradeable via in-game trade and warframe.market.

## Builds that use it

Freeze Force suits Frost builds designed for squad support in Steel Path survival, defense, or interception missions. Pair it with Duration mods to extend the buff window past the base 40 seconds, reducing recast frequency in long missions. Power Efficiency mods help sustain Freeze casts, which spend energy each use.

Squad compositions benefit most when multiple members run weapons with already-embedded Electricity or Toxin mods, enabling them to achieve combined damage type procs from the Cold buff. Trinity or Harrow can maintain energy for Frost while Freeze Force provides the elemental uplift.

## Notes

- The buff applies to allied Warframes, Companions, and Operator weapons within range.
- The 15m radius is not affected by Power Range mods.
- Holding Freeze is required to trigger the ally buff; tapping Freeze still freezes a single target normally.
- Duration mods scale the 40-second base proportionally.
