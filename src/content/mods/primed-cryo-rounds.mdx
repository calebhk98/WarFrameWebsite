## Overview

Primed Cryo Rounds is the endgame cold damage mod for primary weapons, providing up to 165% cold damage scaling at rank 10. The primed version of Cryo Rounds with extended ranks enables high-level elemental builds on rifles, shotguns, and other primary weapons. Combined with other elemental mods and status-effect amplification, Primed Cryo Rounds enables freeze-proc builds that incapacitate enemies mid-combat or synergize with damage amplification from freeze procs on vulnerable targets.

## Acquisition

Primed Cryo Rounds is a legendary drop available only through relic farming, void fissures, or marketplace trading. The mod is vaulted periodically, making it increasingly valuable during vault periods. Players obtain this mod by running fissures with full squads farming specific relic drops, or through direct marketplace trades with other players.

## Usage

Primed Cryo Rounds is essential for cold-damage-focused primary builds in endgame content. Equip it alongside other elemental mods to create combined damage types like Corrosive (cold + toxin) or Viral (cold + toxin) that bypass armor or reduce enemy damage output. The 165% cold scaling at rank 10 enables one-shot or two-shot clear times against high-level enemies when paired with critical-chance and damage-multiplication mods.

The mod is particularly effective on weapons with high base damage, high fire rates, or high status chances that naturally proc cold effects. In squad play, coordinate freeze procs with teammates to enable amplified damage on frozen targets. The freeze status effect from Primed Cryo Rounds enables crowd control that keeps threatening enemies immobilized during extended survival missions.

## Common builds

Primed Cryo Rounds appears on all high-end elemental primary weapon builds. Corrosive builds on Kuva Zarr, Prisma Gorgon, and Soma Prime commonly use Primed Cryo Rounds for additional elemental scaling. The mod is particularly valuable on weapons with guaranteed freeze mechanics or weapons that naturally benefit from high status chance scaling.
