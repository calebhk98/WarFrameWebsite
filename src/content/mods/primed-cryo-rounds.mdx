## Overview

Primed Cryo Rounds is the endgame cold damage mod for primary weapons, providing up to 165% cold damage scaling at rank 10. It is the primed version of Cryo Rounds, extending from 3 ranks to 10 ranks and tripling the maximum cold damage bonus. Combined with other elemental mods and status-effect amplification, Primed Cryo Rounds enables freeze-proc builds that incapacitate enemies mid-combat or synergize with Viral and Corrosive combined elements on high-status primary weapons.

## Acquisition

Primed Cryo Rounds is sold exclusively by Baro Ki'Teer, the Void Trader, when he visits a relay. Baro appears roughly every two weeks and rotates a different selection of primed mods and exclusive items each visit. The cost is paid in Ducats (obtained by trading unneeded prime parts at Ducat Kiosks in relays) and credits. Players who miss a Baro visit must wait for the next rotation, which may not include this mod.

Primed Cryo Rounds also appears on warframe.market for platinum trades with other players, providing a reliable alternative when Baro's current stock does not include it.

## Builds that use it

Primed Cryo Rounds is essential for cold-damage-focused primary builds in endgame content. Equip it alongside other elemental mods to create combined damage types such as Viral (cold + toxin) that reduces enemy health effective hit points, or Corrosive (electric + toxin) for armor strip, reserving the cold slot for additional status procs. The 165% cold scaling at rank 10 significantly boosts elemental damage output when paired with critical-chance and damage-multiplication mods.

The mod is particularly effective on weapons with high base damage, high fire rates, or high status chances that naturally proc cold effects. In squad play, cold procs from Primed Cryo Rounds enable crowd control that keeps threatening enemies slowed during extended survival missions.

## Notes

Primed Cryo Rounds is a Baro Ki'Teer exclusive and is not obtainable from relics, bounties, or standard drop tables. WFCD confirms `drops: []`. The mod requires a fully Orokin Catalyzed primary weapon and careful mod slot management to justify the drain at lower build budgets. Players on a tighter mod budget can use the standard Cryo Rounds (maxRank: 3, +55% cold) until Baro stocks the primed version.
