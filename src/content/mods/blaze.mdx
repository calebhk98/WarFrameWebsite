## Overview

Blaze is a fundamental shotgun elemental mod combining flat damage scaling with heat damage, enabling fire-damage-focused builds. The dual functionality provides both general damage increases and elemental-type specialization, making Blaze valuable on shotgun builds prioritizing heat procs or fire-damage synergies. At rank 3, Blaze provides 60% damage and 60% heat scaling, creating substantial elemental damage scaling before additional status mods.

## Stats per rank

Blaze scales both damage and heat from 15% each at rank 0 to 60% each at rank 3, providing dual scaling benefits that synergize with heat-focused gameplay.

## Acquisition

Blaze is a rare mod obtained through mid-game star chart progression and enemy drops. Players encounter it on Venus, Jupiter, and later planets during mid-game farming. The mod is also available through relic farming and marketplace trading.

## Usage

Blaze is a core elemental mod for heat-focused shotgun builds, enabling fire-damage procs and status-effect synergies. Pair Blaze with other elemental mods to create combined damage types like Lava (heat + electricity) or Radiation (heat + electric). The dual damage and heat scaling enables both offensive and status-effect benefits from a single mod.

On shotguns with high status chance like Sobek or Drakgoon, Blaze enables rapid heat-proc spreading that applies burn effects across multiple enemies. In squad play, heat procs provide crowd control through dot effects while Blaze's damage scaling maintains personal damage output. The mod is less valuable on weapons already optimized for other damage types like cold or toxin.

## Common builds

Blaze appears on heat-focused and general elemental shotgun builds. Sobek builds often use Blaze as the primary heat-scaling mod, combining with other elemental mods to create Lava or Radiation builds. The mod is common on status-focused shotguns that benefit from rapid heat-proc spreading.
