## Overview

Seeker is a pistol utility mod providing pure accuracy scaling, enabling precision playstyles on secondary weapons. At rank 3, the 30% accuracy bonus tightens weapon spread, enabling consistent headshots and reducing ammo waste on inaccurate pistols. The 4-point drain makes Seeker space-efficient, fitting comfortably alongside damage scaling mods in secondary builds.

Seeker excels on pistols with low base accuracy, particularly burst-fire secondaries or spread weapons. The accuracy bonus transforms unreliable secondaries into viable precision tools capable of consistent headshot chains. Paired with crit scaling mods, Seeker enables secondary builds that compete with primary-weapon damage through better shot placement.

Seeker occupies a secondary slot competing with damage mods, fire-rate mods, and status scaling. For accuracy-dependent playstyles, Seeker is nearly mandatory; for spray-and-pray builds, pure damage outperforms accuracy. The contextual value makes Seeker optional rather than mandatory, unlike primary-slot utility mods.

## Acquisition

Seeker drops rarely from high-level Corpus units, particularly in end-game star chart missions. Dedicated farming of level 40+ Corpus nodes yields Seeker within a few hours. Trading or void fissure luck provide faster acquisition.

## Builds that use it

Seeker appears in precision secondary builds, particularly on burst-fire pistols or accurate single-shot weapons. Spray-and-pray builds skip Seeker entirely, focusing on pure damage. Melee-hybrid builds leverage Seeker's accuracy for ranged setup opportunities.

## Notes

Accuracy affects weapon spread and projectile convergence. Higher accuracy tightens spread, enabling more pellets to hit targets at distance. Combining Seeker with naturally accurate pistols creates pinpoint secondaries capable of precision play, enabling skillful gameplay rewarding careful aiming. Weapons like the Akjagara Prime or Akvasto Prime transform from spread weapons into precision tools through accuracy stacking, enabling headshot chains and high-consistency gameplay. The accuracy bonus becomes increasingly valuable on weapons with poor base accuracy, as marginal improvements on high-accuracy weapons yield diminishing returns while terrible-accuracy weapons gain substantial consistency benefits. Pairing Seeker with crit mods enables secondary weapons to compete with primary damage outputs through improved precision playstyles.
