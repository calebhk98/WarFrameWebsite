## Overview

Redirection is the foundational shield-capacity mod in Warframe, providing a multiplicative boost to warframe shields at a low drain cost and high rank ceiling. At max rank, Redirection doubles shield capacity, making it an essential include in most defensive builds, particularly on shield-based frames (Volt, Nova, Mag). The 2-point drain cost makes Redirection one of the most efficient mods in the game, freeing capacity for offense, utility, or additional defensive layers.

Redirection pairs synergistically with shield-recharge systems (Void Shield, Equilibrium, shield-restore augments) and shield-gating mechanics (Grit, Fortitude) to create comprehensive defense. Frames without native shield scaling still benefit significantly from Redirection's multiplicative boost, as the doubling effect at rank 10 applies equally regardless of base shield value. For endurance content, Redirection is nearly non-negotiable on shield-focused frames, often appearing in every build slot.

The mod's universality makes it a farming priority for new players, and its common rarity ensures accessibility within the first few missions. Veteran players keep multiple copies at different ranks for different build capacities, showcasing the mod's perennial relevance across the entire progression curve.

## Acquisition

Redirection is ubiquitous across Warframe's loot tables, dropping from nearly every enemy faction on every mission type. Within the first 10 missions, most players have multiple copies; farming is unnecessary. High-rank copies may require a bit more grinding, but the common rarity ensures steady acquisition through natural play.

## Builds that use it

Redirection appears in nearly every shield-focused build: Volt, Nova, Protea, and Mag builds all rely on Redirection as a cornerstone. Endurance survival builds stack Redirection with shield-restore abilities to maintain shield gates. It also appears in mobility builds where frames trade heavy armor for shield-based tanking, relying on repositioning and shield upkeep for survival.

## Notes

Redirection is multiplicative: it scales the warframe's base shield stat. A frame with 225 base shield gains 225 additional shield per 100% capacity increase. The mod is particularly powerful on shield-heavy frames but remains valuable even on low-shield frames, since the percentage boost is fixed. Combining Redirection with other shield mods (Vitality for health, Armor for mitigation) creates layered defense that synergizes better than any single defensive mod alone.
