## Overview

Fury is a melee attack-speed mod providing baseline speed scaling for weapons, enabling rapid-hit playstyles that emphasize attack frequency over individual-hit power. At rank 3, the 30% attack-speed boost transforms weapon speed significantly, enabling combo chains and status stacking through rapid applications. The uncommon rarity and 4-point drain make Fury accessible and space-efficient.

Fury excels on weapons with moderate attack speed that benefit from increased frequency, particularly on status-focused builds where rapid application multiplies effect potential. Dual-wield weapons and fast-hitting weapon types pair particularly well with Fury, enabling overwhelming attack-chain tempo. Combined with melee range mods, Fury enables aggressive repositioning playstyles.

Fury occupies a melee slot competing with crit mods, damage mods, and status mods. For status-focused builds, Fury is nearly mandatory; for crit builds, crit scaling often outperforms speed. The contextual value makes Fury essential for status overflow strategies but optional for pure-crit or pure-damage builds.

## Acquisition

Fury drops as an uncommon drop from Grineer units, particularly in early-to-mid star chart missions. Natural progression yields multiple copies passively; deliberate farming of Grineer-heavy nodes accelerates acquisition.

## Builds that use it

Fury dominates status-focused melee builds, appearing in nearly every status-overflow strategy. Pure-crit melee builds may skip Fury in favor of crit scaling. Hybrid builds balancing status and crit often include Fury for rapid status application.

## Notes

Attack speed scales multiplicatively with other speed sources, enabling additive stacking. Combining Fury with Berserker Fury (on crit) or Bloodletting (on health drain) creates absurdly fast attack chains capable of stunning or killing enemies through speed alone. The 30% boost at rank 3 is accessible early; Primed variants provide marginal improvements for late-game optimization. Status-focused melee builds particularly leverage Fury for rapid application stacking, turning low-status-chance weapons into viable status platforms through sheer application speed. Weapons with inherent attack-speed penalties or slow base speeds benefit most from Fury, gaining the most relative improvement through speed scaling.
