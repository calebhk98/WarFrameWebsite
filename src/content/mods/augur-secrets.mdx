## Overview

Augur Secrets provides ability strength scaling at a low drain cost, making it one of the most efficient strength mods available before accessing Transient Fortitude or Intensify. The mod is particularly valuable for builds where ability damage or effectiveness is critical but drain is constrained. Augur Secrets enables early-game strength builds and bridges the gap between starter mods and endgame damage scaling options.

## Acquisition

Augur Secrets is a rare mod obtained through star chart progression and enemy drops. Players typically encounter it in mid-game content on Venus, Jupiter, and beyond. The mod is searchable through relays and available on the trading market, making it accessible once players reach mastery rank thresholds for trading.

## Usage

Augur Secrets excels on strength-dependent warframes like Nova, Banshee, or any frame with scaling ability damage. Combined with Intensify or Transient Fortitude, Augur Secrets enables high-strength builds without excessive drain. The mod is particularly useful on ability-spam builds where every percentage of damage scaling improves mission efficiency.

In group play, damage-focused builds using Augur Secrets benefit from the additional ability output, enabling faster enemy clear times. The mod pairs well with ability-efficiency mods, creating balanced builds that excel in extended missions. Augur Secrets is often used as a bridge mod during progression when players have not yet accessed higher-tier alternatives.

## Common builds

Augur Secrets appears on damage-focused ability builds where strength is prioritized but drain is limited. Saryn, Ember, and Mirage builds commonly use Augur Secrets for additional damage scaling. Support frames occasionally use it to increase buff potency, though duration-focused builds may prioritize other mods.
