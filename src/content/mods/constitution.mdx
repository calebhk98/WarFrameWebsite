## Overview

Constitution is a hybrid warframe mod combining knockdown recovery speed with ability duration, providing both defensive and offensive utility. The dual benefit creates minimal opportunity cost: frames get faster knockdown recovery, reducing vulnerability windows, while simultaneously extending ability timers for sustained damage or crowd control. At rank 3, the 40% recovery speed combined with 28% duration bonus makes Constitution a solid all-rounder for frames that benefit from both properties.

The mod particularly excels on ability-heavy frames where extended duration improves uptime, but frames are also frequently knocked down (melee-focused frames, aggressive positioning builds). Melee weapons and frames that rush into crowds benefit from the recovery speed, while the duration bonus stacks multiplicatively with other duration sources. Constitution fills a defensive slot that might otherwise go to Vitality or Quick Thinking, trading raw tank stats for active mitigation and ability scaling.

With 10-point drain, Constitution occupies a significant portion of a build's capacity, making the trade-off more critical than lower-drain defensive mods. However, frames with ability slots to spare and frames suffering knockdowns find Constitution more valuable per-point than pure defensive scaling, especially in endurance content where knockdown frequency becomes problematic.

## Acquisition

Constitution drops rarely from high-level Grineer enemy units, particularly in end-game star chart missions (Neptune, Void, Pluto). Dedicated farming of level 40-50+ Grineer nodes yields Constitution within a few hours of grinding. Trading with other players or running void fissures provides alternatives.

## Builds that use it

Constitution appears in duration-scaling builds for frames with ability uptime concerns (Frost, Oberon, Saryn, Limbo). Melee-focused frames and aggressive positioning builds value the knockdown recovery bonus. Frames lacking defensive mod slots often skip Constitution in favor of pure damage or pure tank scaling, creating a decision point for hybrid builds.

## Notes

Knockdown recovery speed reduces the duration of the knockdown animation by the listed percentage. Even at 40% recovery, knockdowns remain interruptions - the bonus merely shortens the window. Combining Constitution with Sure Footed creates redundancy, as both address knockdown problems; most builds choose one or the other, not both.
