## Overview

Primed Pistol Gambit is the endgame critical chance mod for secondary weapons, providing up to 187% critical chance scaling at rank 10. The primed variant enables crit-focused secondary builds that dramatically increase damage output through critical multipliers and allows weapons to achieve absurdly high critical chance percentages when combined with other crit-chance mods. At rank 10, Primed Pistol Gambit is an essential component of critical-damage secondary weapon builds in the highest-difficulty content.

## Acquisition

Primed Pistol Gambit is a legendary drop available through void fissure relics and marketplace trading. The mod comes from dedicated void relic farming on fissure nodes or through trading with other players who have completed relic farm runs. The mod's popularity and moderate drain cost make it a high-demand marketplace item.

## Usage

Primed Pistol Gambit is mandatory for crit-focused secondary weapon builds, particularly on weapons with high base critical multipliers like Epitaph or Despair Prime. Equip it alongside Primed Target Cracker and other crit-boosting mods to achieve critical chance well above 100%, guaranteeing critical hits on every shot. The low drain cost of only 2 capacity enables space for additional damage mods like Hornet Strike or elemental scaling mods.

In squad play, crit-focused secondary builds enable rapid enemy elimination and complement melee-oriented warframe builds. The high critical chance scaling enables weapons to deal critical damage consistently, triggering status effects and crowd control at amplified damage values. Primed Pistol Gambit is particularly effective on weapons with guaranteed freeze, poison, or slash procs that activate on critical hits.

## Common builds

Primed Pistol Gambit appears on all high-end crit-focused secondary weapon builds. Weapons like Epitaph, Kuva Nukor, and Catchmoon Kitgun commonly use Primed Pistol Gambit as the primary crit-chance mod. The mod is essential for one-shot or two-shot secondary builds that enable rapid enemy clear in endgame missions.
