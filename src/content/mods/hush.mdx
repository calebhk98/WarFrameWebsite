## Overview

Hush reduces enemy awareness of gunfire, enabling stealth-focused gameplay on primary weapons. The mod is valuable for players prioritizing stealth missions or avoiding enemy aggression in early-game content. As a common, low-drain mod, Hush is accessible early in progression and scales up to 62% detection reduction at rank 3, making it effective for maintaining stealth runs alongside silent melee weapons and abilities.

## Stats per rank

Hush scales from 25% detection reduction at rank 0 to 62% at maximum rank, providing substantial stealth utility without requiring high drain investment.

## Acquisition

Hush is a common mod obtained through early star chart progression and general enemy drops. Players typically acquire it within the first few hours of gameplay on Earth. The mod is frequently available as loot from containers and enemy units across all planets, making it easy to rank up.

## Usage

Hush enables stealth weapon builds on primary rifles and shotguns, allowing players to maintain undetected status during stealth challenges. Pair Hush with silent melee weapons like daggers or silent warframe abilities like Ivara or Loki abilities to maintain full invisibility. The mod becomes less relevant in combat-focused builds where stealth is not a priority, but remains valuable in spy missions and assassination contracts requiring undetected approaches.

At rank 3, Hush provides over 60% detection reduction, making gunfire nearly imperceptible to enemies. When combined with invisibility abilities or other stealth mechanics, Hush enables near-perfect stealth runs where even detected enemies hesitate to engage. The low drain cost makes Hush an excellent primary weapon mod choice for new players experimenting with stealth playstyles.

## Common builds

Hush appears on stealth-focused primary weapon builds, particularly on bows and rifles used in spy missions or assassination scenarios. Warframes with invisibility abilities often equip Hush on their primary to maintain stealth while handling ranged threats. The mod is less common in combat-focused endgame builds where damage output takes priority over detection avoidance.
