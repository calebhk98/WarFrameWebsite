## Overview

Gourmand is a Warframe augment mod for Grendel's Feast ability. With the mod equipped, casting Feast costs Health instead of Energy, dropping from 300 Health at rank 0 to 200 Health at rank 3. In exchange, each enemy swallowed by Glutton grants additional Armor -- 150 armor per enemy at rank 3 -- substantially increasing Grendel's survivability in high-density fights.

The health cost replaces the energy cost entirely, making Grendel energy-independent for his swallow ability. This frees up mod slots normally reserved for efficiency, and it enables Feast casting on frames with very little energy pool. Combined with high-armor modding through Umbral Vitality and Steel Fiber, Grendel can reach substantial armor totals when keeping enemies swallowed during extended endurance runs.

## Acquisition

Gourmand is available through the New Loka syndicate offering. Players with sufficient New Loka standing can purchase it directly. The mod is tradeable through in-game trade and warframe.market.

## Builds that use it

Gourmand suits tankline Grendel builds in Steel Path endurance missions where consistent enemy swallowing is feasible. Maximum Health mods such as Umbral Vitality absorb the per-cast health drain comfortably and pair with Armor stacking to make the lost health trivial on a high-effective-health Grendel.

Energy builds that skip Gourmand prioritize Streamline and Fleeting Expertise for cost reduction instead. Gourmand is a direct tradeoff between energy management and health investment, making it better in situations where enemies are plentiful enough to keep Glutton full and the armor bonus active continuously.

## Notes

- The health cost is fixed at the per-rank value and is not affected by Power Efficiency mods.
- Armor gained from swallowed enemies resets when Regurgitate or Pulverize empties Glutton.
- Grendel's base armor combined with the per-enemy bonus scales well with Adaptation for significant damage reduction.
- New Loka and Steel Meridian are allied syndicates; either can provide access to the offering.
