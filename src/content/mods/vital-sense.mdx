## Overview

Vital Sense increases the damage multiplier applied when landing critical hits, amplifying the reward for precise aim and critical-focused weapons. While Point Strike scales critical *chance*, Vital Sense scales critical *damage*, making the two complementary. A weapon with Vital Sense at rank 10 can achieve 2.0x to 4.0x+ damage per critical hit depending on base multiplier, enabling burst-damage playstyles and one-shot builds.

Vital Sense is the damage multiplier counterpart to Point Strike. Together, they form the foundation of critical-focused primaries. Solo, Vital Sense amplifies crit-heavy weapons but requires adequate critical chance to be worthwhile. In combination with Point Strike, Vital Sense creates exponential scaling where each critical hit becomes a massive damage spike.

## Acquisition

Vital Sense is a common drop from enemy units. New players acquire their first copy early in progression, and additional ranks are straightforward to farm. It is also available from the Market for 50,000 credits.

## Builds that use it

Vital Sense appears alongside Point Strike in virtually every critical-focused rifle build. High-crit-multiplier weapons (Soma Prime, Fulmin, Lanka) and sniper rifles (Vectis Prime) rely on Vital Sense to scale critical hits into one-shot territory. Headshot-focused playstyles leverage Vital Sense as a core scaling mod.

## Notes

Vital Sense's effectiveness scales with critical chance and base critical multiplier. A weapon with low critical multiplier (1.5x) sees marginal gains; a weapon with 3.0x or higher multiplier transforms into a critical-scaling engine. A weapon with low critical chance also sees reduced benefit, as the high damage multiplier is wasted if crits land infrequently.

At rank 10, combined with a weapon's base 2.0x critical multiplier, Vital Sense enables a final 5.3x damage multiplier per critical hit. With Point Strike, this creates the potential for one-shot kills on most star chart enemies and consistent heavy burst damage on armor-scaled endgame content.
