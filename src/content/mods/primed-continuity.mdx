## Overview

Primed Continuity is a legendary-tier warframe mod that boosts ability duration, making it one of the most universally useful mods in the game. Unlike its unprimed counterpart, Continuity, Primed Continuity reaches 55% ability duration at maximum rank, providing a 5% larger bonus per rank. This mod is essential for almost every warframe build, as duration affects the effectiveness of crowd control, support, and damage-over-time abilities.

Primed mods are more powerful versions of standard mods, requiring additional catalytic converters to level but offering superior stat progression. Primed Continuity's low energy drain of 4 at base capacity makes it an economical choice for even energy-efficient builds, while its madurai polarity matches most aura mods and slots.

## Acquisition

Primed Continuity is a relic drop, obtainable exclusively from void fissure missions by opening void relics. As a legendary rarity mod, it appears in select relic drop tables at low rates. Farming for Primed Continuity requires identifying relics that contain it in their reward pools and running fissure missions with those relics refined to higher refinement levels (Flawless or Radiant) to increase drop odds. Trading with other players remains the most efficient path for newer players, as the relics containing this mod are limited in availability and the drop rate is minimal.

## Builds that use it

Primed Continuity is included in almost every warframe build. Crowd control frames like Limbo, Saryn, and Titania benefit from extended crowd control duration. Support frames like Khora and Protea gain extended buff windows. Defensive frames like Inaros and Grendel maintain health regeneration and protective effects longer. No matter the role, any warframe relying on ability effects benefits from Primed Continuity's duration bonus.

Extended duration amplifies the utility of many synergy mods. Pairing it with Primed Flow creates a highly survivable frame with strong ability uptime. Combining it with Blind Rage or Transient Fortitude allows balancing strength penalties with increased ability window time. For endless missions and high-level content, Primed Continuity often becomes mandatory, as even short-duration effects need to persist through extended enemy rotations.

## Notes

Ability duration scales subtractive effects as well: mods like Transient Fortitude apply their duration penalty multiplicatively, so Primed Continuity cannot fully negate the downsides of corrupted mods. However, it still allows fine-tuning of ability uptime without sacrificing raw stats on many builds.

The mod was released as a primed variant relatively early in the game's lifecycle, making it available in trading markets more readily than some other primed mods. Its consistent utility across all warframe archetypes ensures sustained demand from both new and experienced players.
