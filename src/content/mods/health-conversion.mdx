## Overview

Health Conversion transforms warframes into armor-scaling tanks by stacking armor bonuses whenever a status effect is applied to the player. This mod rewards tanking hits in enemy fire and enables builds that embrace incoming status procs as power sources. Builds using Health Conversion can achieve extreme durability by intentionally triggering status effects and converting them into armor layers.

The mod pairs exceptionally well with status-heavy weapons and frames that ignore status effects (like Lavos or Sevagoth). The armor stacking creates exponential durability scaling that can turn fragile frames into unkillable tanks. Health Conversion transforms survivability from a passive tank stat into an active, dynamic resource that rewards aggressive combat positioning.

## Acquisition

Health Conversion drops from Void Relic fissures and void mission rewards. It is a rarer mod requiring dedicated void fissure farming or Zariman void node runs to acquire.

## Builds that use it

Health Conversion is core to tanking builds on frames like Rhino (Iron Skin scaling), Nidus (Mutations), and Lavos (immune to status effects, enabling build freedom). It also appears in condition overload melee builds where status effect application is constant. The mod transforms survival into an active scaling system, rewarding builds that deliberately trigger status effects.

## Notes

Health Conversion stacks only on status effects applied to the warframe itself, not on enemies. This requires either intentional status-taking or builds that apply status effects to themselves (like Lavos or Nidus with specific setups). Each new status effect application grants a new stack, so high-status-chance weapons trigger stacks frequently.

At rank 3, 50% armor per stack with 10 maximum stacks means a 175-armor warframe can achieve 1050 total armor through status stacking alone, reducing all damage taken by roughly 83%. Combined with other armor mods, Health Conversion enables extreme durability breakpoints that allow even squishy frames to tank endgame content.
