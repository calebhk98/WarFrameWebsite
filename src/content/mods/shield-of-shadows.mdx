## Overview

Shield of Shadows is a Nekros augment mod that enhances his Shadows of the Dead ability. The mod transforms Nekros's shadows from pure offense into protective meat shields that absorb incoming damage and gain shields on hit. This augment significantly improves Nekros's survivability by distributing incoming damage among multiple targets and allowing shadows to build up shield layers during combat.

The augment transforms Shadows from glass cannons into durable defenders. In high-level content where Nekros is vulnerable to direct fire, Shield of Shadows enables aggressive positioning and extended mission durations. The shield scaling on hit creates a scaling defense that grows stronger as enemies attack the shadows, rewarding aggressive combat.

## Acquisition

Shield of Shadows drops from Void Relic fissures and is also available from Void Cascade or Zariman void mission rewards. Like most augment mods, it is a rarer drop requiring Void Relic farming or dedicated mission running on void nodes.

## Builds that use it

Shield of Shadows is the linchpin of defensive Nekros builds. Builds focused on Desecration farming (resource economy) incorporate Shield of Shadows to enable safe positioning near high-density enemy clusters. It also pairs well with Range and Strength mods to maximize both damage absorption and shield generation.

## Synergies and build considerations

Shield of Shadows pairs excellently with Ability Duration mods to extend shadow lifetimes, ensuring shadows remain active throughout combat encounters. Frames with shadow-spawning mechanics or minion-based abilities benefit from Shield of Shadows as a survivability layer. The augment is particularly effective in Void Fissure: Survival missions where sustained farming at high enemy density requires both damage output and survivability.

## Notes

Shield of Shadows requires active shadows to be effective; without enemies to attack the shadows, no shields build up. Builds must balance Ability Strength (for shield scaling) and Ability Range (for shadow spreading) to maximize the augment's benefit. In missions with low enemy density, the augment's impact diminishes significantly. However, in high-density missions like Void Cascade or Void Fissure: Survival, the augment provides exponential value as shadows absorb hits constantly.

At rank 3, 100% damage absorption means Nekros's shadows take all incoming damage that would otherwise hit Nekros directly. Combined with shadow durability and proper positioning, this creates a full damage redirection system that allows Nekros to farm endlessly without personal risk. Nekros becomes almost invulnerable when shield stacking reaches maximum, transforming survival from a concern into an afterthought in most mission types.
