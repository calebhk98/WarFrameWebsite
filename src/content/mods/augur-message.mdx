## Overview

Augur Message is a utility mod providing ability duration scaling, particularly useful for warframes whose survivability or damage output depends on maintaining active effects. As a relatively low-drain common mod, Augur Message is accessible to players early in progression and provides consistent value across multiple playstyles. The mod pairs well with any ability-focused build and enables extensions of crowd control, protective abilities, and damage-over-time effects.

## Stats per rank

Augur Message provides linear duration scaling from 4% at rank 0 to 24% at maximum rank.

## Acquisition

Augur Message is a common mod obtained through early-game star chart progression. Players receive it as drops from standard enemy encounters or from loot caches. The mod's low drain cost and common rarity make it easily accessible for new players seeking their first ability-scaling options.

## Usage

Augur Message becomes valuable on warframes where duration directly improves mission completion rates. Crowd-control frames like Inaros or Grendel benefit from extended stun durations, while damage-over-time frames like Saryn or Mirage benefit from extended damage windows. The mod is often paired with Primed Continuity or Umbral Continuity for mid-to-high-level content where ability stacking and duration extension are critical.

In support roles, duration scaling directly impacts how long protective or buff abilities last, improving squad sustainability. The relatively low cost of Augur Message compared to primed alternatives makes it an excellent early-to-mid-game choice before players access higher-tier mods.

## Common builds

Augur Message appears on most ability-focused builds where duration is a secondary stat. It complements Streamline or Fleeting Expertise on caster frames, ensuring abilities remain active long enough to reach recast timing. Support frames commonly use Augur Message for extended protection bubbles or buff durations.
