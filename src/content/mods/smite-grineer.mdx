## Overview

Smite Grineer is a faction-specific damage mod providing conditional damage scaling against Grineer units, enabling specialized builds for Grineer-heavy content. At rank 3, the 40% damage bonus creates significant offensive improvement in Grineer-dominated missions. The uncommon rarity and 4-point drain make Smite Grineer accessible and space-efficient.

Smite Grineer excels in fixed-mission contexts where Grineer are guaranteed enemies, particularly in star chart missions and void cascades. The mod's condition-based nature makes it less valuable in mixed-faction missions, creating decision points about build flexibility. Combined with other faction-specific mods (Smite Corpus, Smite Infested), Smite Grineer enables specialized setups for each enemy type.

Smite Grineer occupies a melee slot competing with pure damage, crit mods, and elemental mods. For Grineer-focused missions, Smite Grineer provides meaningful damage; for balanced-faction missions, pure damage outperforms conditional scaling. The trade-off is contextual, requiring careful consideration of mission composition.

## Acquisition

Smite Grineer drops as an uncommon drop from all Grineer units, making it one of the easier faction-specific mods to acquire. Natural progression through Grineer-heavy missions yields multiple copies passively within the first 20 missions.

## Builds that use it

Smite Grineer appears in specialized Grineer-farm builds, particularly for void cascades and star chart grinding. Mixed-faction missions often skip Smite Grineer in favor of pure damage, since the conditional bonus only applies to Grineer. Speed-runners maintain multiple mod setups, swapping faction-specific mods per mission type.

## Notes

Faction-specific damage mods stack multiplicatively with other damage sources, creating exponential scaling against specific enemy types. Combining Smite Grineer with elemental damage and status scaling creates overwhelming damage against Grineer, enabling one-shot or near-one-shot playstyles. The 40% boost at rank 3 is substantial, enabling viable oneshot builds on moderate-damage weapons through conditional stacking. Smite Grineer enables specialized builds that completely optimize against Grineer encounters while potentially underperforming in mixed-faction missions. Speed-runners maintain multiple mod loadouts, swapping faction-specific mods before entering faction-guaranteed missions for maximum efficiency.
