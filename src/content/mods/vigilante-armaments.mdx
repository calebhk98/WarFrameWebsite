## Overview

Vigilante Armaments is a warframe mod that grants a flat multishot bonus to all equipped weapons, stacking with weapon-specific multishot mods for multiplicative damage scaling. Unlike weapon mods that are slot-specific, Vigilante Armaments applies universally to primaries, secondaries, and melee, making it exceptionally efficient for frames that swap between weapon types mid-mission. The 4-point drain and common rarity make it accessible early, rewarding new players with meaningful damage output.

Multishot mechanics work by multiplying bullet count per shot, stacking multiplicatively with other sources. A weapon with 1.5x base multishot combined with Vigilante Armaments at rank 5 (1.6x) produces 2.4x pellets per shot, drastically increasing damage output and status-proc application. This mod is particularly powerful on spread weapons (shotguns, beam rifles) and status-focused builds that rely on hitting multiple enemies per shot.

Vigilante Armaments differs from other warframe mods in that it provides a universal damage multiplier that scales across all damage types. Frames lacking weapon-slot specialization benefit most, as the 60% multishot bonus applies equally to their full arsenal regardless of mod redundancy concerns. For glass-cannon or elemental-overflow builds, Vigilante Armaments is nearly mandatory.

## Acquisition

Vigilante Armaments drops from void fissure missions as a relic reward, accessible once players unlock void fissure content (post-Second Dream). Running public void fissure squads on any difficulty tier guarantees multiple copies within a farming session. The common rarity ensures availability without excessive grinding.

## Builds that use it

Vigilante Armaments appears in nearly every high-damage warframe build, particularly damage-focused frames like Rhino (Roar), Nekros (Desecrate), and Saryn (Miasma). Frames focused on weapon damage rather than ability damage prioritize Vigilante Armaments alongside weapon-specific multishot mods. Elemental status builds leverage the multishot for rapid proc application, making Vigilante Armaments essential for status-overflow strategies.

## Notes

Multishot is additive with other multishot sources: two mods granting +30% each stack as additive bonuses (1.3x * 1.3x = 1.69x final), not multiplicative. Vigilante Armaments stacks additively with weapon mods like Split Chamber or Scattered Justice, meaning diminishing returns appear at higher multishot values. However, the universal application across all weapon slots makes it more valuable than slot-specific alternatives on generalist frames.
