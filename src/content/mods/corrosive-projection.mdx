## Overview

Corrosive Projection is one of the most impactful aura mods in Warframe, providing party-wide armor reduction without consuming warframe mod capacity. At rank 5, this aura strips 18 percent armor from all enemies within affinity range, making it nearly mandatory equipment for endgame team builds. The negative drain means it actually grants additional capacity, creating a net positive modding benefit while providing massive team utility.

The armor reduction is flat percentage debuff stacking multiplicatively with team applications, enabling four-player squads to trivialize even Steel Path enemy defenses. Corrosive Projection fundamentally changes engagement dynamics against armor-heavy factions like Grineer, reducing the need for dedicated armor-stripping abilities and enabling pure damage-focused frame compositions.

## Acquisition

Corrosive Projection drops as an uncommon aura mod from universal enemy drops and void relic reward pools. The high drop frequency makes obtaining ranked copies trivial compared to rare mods. Players naturally accumulate Corrosive Projection copies through normal mission farming, eliminating the need for dedicated farming sessions.

Alternative acquisition paths include void relic farming, syndicate vendor purchases, and Maroo's Bazaar trading with other players. The uncommon rarity and high drop frequency make Corrosive Projection one of the most accessible endgame aura mods. New players can reliably equip this mod within their first few missions against any faction.

## Usage

Corrosive Projection is essential equipment for endgame team missions, particularly Steel Path content and endless mission scaling. Equipping this aura enables team compositions that prioritize damage output over defense, as armor reduction directly translates to survivability improvements through faster enemy elimination.

Squad synergy with Corrosive Projection is multiplicative when multiple players equip the aura simultaneously. Four-player teams with rank-5 Corrosive Projection create cumulative armor reduction stacking, reducing heavily-armored enemies to minimal threats. This multiplicative stacking is the primary reason teams reliably clear Steel Path mission content.

Frames specializing in armor strip like Ash, Frost, and Hydroid benefit especially from Corrosive Projection because squad armor reduction stacks with their own active strips, creating nearly total armor negation. However, even pure damage frames gain enormous value from this aura due to the passive squad benefit without modding investment.

## Notes

Corrosive Projection is one of the most universally recommended mods in Warframe. Nearly every endgame player and squad equips this aura, making it effectively mandatory for team-oriented content. The negative drain requirement combined with universal armor reduction makes this aura an absolute no-question choice across all warframe builds.

The naramon polarity is uncommon but manageable through polarity investments. The -1 drain allows additional capacity for damage or utility mods, making Corrosive Projection a net positive modding choice that simultaneously provides team utility. Frames maximizing capacity economy benefit most from equipping this aura, turning mob squads into pure damage machines.
