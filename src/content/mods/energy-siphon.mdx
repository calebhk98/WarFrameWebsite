## Overview

Energy Siphon is the primary aura for sustaining energy generation without relying on external sources like Energy Siphon specters or operators. It provides consistent passive energy regeneration to the entire squad, making it invaluable for warframes with high energy consumption abilities. As an aura that adds capacity instead of consuming it, Energy Siphon is often paired with efficiency and strength builds to maximize ability uptime.

## Stats per rank

Energy Siphon provides continuous energy regeneration per second, scaling from 0.1 per second at rank 0 to 0.26 per second at maximum rank.

## Acquisition

Energy Siphon is a common drop from enemy forces across all factions. Players typically obtain it early in progression through standard star chart missions on Earth. Due to its universal utility, Energy Siphon is one of the first aura mods new players should prioritize ranking up alongside armor-building mods and survivability enhancements.

## Usage

Energy Siphon becomes mandatory for sustained ability spam builds, particularly on energy-hungry warframes like Nova, Ember, and Saryn. Combined with high efficiency mods and Zenurik's Energizing Dash, Energy Siphon enables virtually unlimited ability uptime. The aura is especially critical in endless missions where external energy sources are unavailable or unreliable.

In squad play, designate one player as the primary Energy Siphon provider; having multiple instances does not stack multiplicatively. Pair Energy Siphon with efficiency mods like Streamline and Fleeting Expertise for maximum benefit. On frames with built-in energy restoration, such as Caliban or Gyre, Energy Siphon serves as a failsafe against energy starvation during high-intensity encounters.

## Common builds

Energy Siphon is standard on caster frames in endless survival and defense missions. High-efficiency Ember, Saryn, Nova, and Wisp builds all rely on Energy Siphon as their primary aura to sustain constant ability usage. The mod works synergistically with energy economy mods in the exilus slot and with operators using Zenurik focus schools.
