## Overview

Guided Effigy is a Warframe augment mod for Chroma's Effigy ability. While Effigy is active and positioned, holding the ability key directs the Effigy to travel toward Chroma's aim point, dealing damage to every enemy it passes through. At rank 3, the Effigy deals 4,000 damage per second during transit and restores 5 Energy for each enemy it passes through. On arrival at the destination, the Effigy roars, stunning nearby enemies.

Without this mod, Effigy is a stationary turret that Chroma must reposition manually by casting the ability again. Guided Effigy transforms it into a mobile offensive tool, enabling Chroma to redirect his Effigy mid-mission without the full cost of deactivating and recasting. The energy return per enemy passed scales the ability's sustainability in dense missions.

## Acquisition

Guided Effigy is available through the Cephalon Suda syndicate offering. Players with sufficient standing can purchase it. The mod is tradeable through in-game trade and warframe.market.

## Builds that use it

Guided Effigy suits Chroma builds in open-world areas and Defense missions where enemy positions shift throughout the encounter. Standard Chroma builds that invest heavily in Duration to sustain Vex Armor benefit from Guided Effigy's ability to reposition Effigy without breaking the sustained buff cycle.

Energy economy builds pair Guided Effigy with Arcane Energize or Trinity support to offset Effigy's ongoing energy drain, while using the energy return per enemy to partially sustain the repositioning cost. Power Strength investment raises the transit damage, which becomes meaningful against armored Sortie-level enemies during transit.

## Notes

- The Effigy's arrival roar stun radius is fixed and not affected by Power Range mods.
- Transit damage type is not documented in WFCD levelStats; refer to the Fandom wiki for elemental type details.
- The Effigy resumes its stationary turret behavior after arriving at the aim point.
- Effigy must already be deployed; Guided Effigy does not function on initial cast.
