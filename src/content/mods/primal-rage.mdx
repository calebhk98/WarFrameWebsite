## Overview

Primal Rage is a rare warframe augment mod for Wukong's Primal Fury ability. While Primal Fury is active, each enemy kill grants a stacking critical chance bonus that starts at 8% per kill at rank 0 and increases to 15% per kill at rank 3. The bonus decays at 1% per second, meaning sustained kill speed is necessary to maintain high critical chance stacks. This mechanic rewards aggressive, uninterrupted combat inside Primal Fury, making the mod particularly effective in dense enemy scenarios such as Survival, Defense, and Exterminate missions.

Wukong's Primal Fury staff is a melee weapon with inherently high base damage, making the compounding critical chance from Primal Rage translate directly into more frequent high-damage critical hits. Pairing Primal Rage with critical damage mods such as Blood Rush or Organ Shatter amplifies the return per stack significantly. The 1%/s decay rate means that in sparse missions, the stack will drain before the next kill opportunity; the mod is most effective when the player stays in melee range of clustered enemies. A Wukong build centered on Primal Fury as the primary damage source benefits most from this augment, while builds relying on abilities that pause melee combat see less benefit.

## Acquisition

Primal Rage is offered by two opposing syndicates. Arbiters of Hexis members at the Maxim rank and New Loka members at the Flawless rank can purchase the mod for syndicate standing. Because these syndicates are opposed, a player must choose one alliance; purchasing from the enemy syndicate requires trading with allied players. Player trading through the in-game system or warframe.market is also a viable route.

## Builds that use it

Primal Rage belongs in Primal Fury melee builds for Wukong that prioritize sustained close-range combat. Pair it with Gladiator Might and Organ Shatter to fully capitalize on the critical chance stacks. Arcane Fury synergizes well, triggering on critical hits generated by the stacked crit chance. Narrow Minded reduces range on Wukong but does not impact Primal Fury directly, so duration and efficiency mods can be included without hurting the augment. Iron Staff (Wukong passive) handles the weapon slot, so mod capacity focuses entirely on warframe slots. Avoid builds that cycle frequently out of Primal Fury mode, as deactivating the ability likely resets the stacked bonus.

## Notes

The critical chance bonus stacks additively with the base critical chance of the Primal Fury staff. The decay rate of 1%/s is continuous and does not pause between kills, so brief gaps between enemies will still cause stack loss. The maximum theoretical stack is bounded only by kill rate and mission density. On Steel Path missions with heavy enemy density, Primal Rage can maintain high stacks consistently, making it stronger in that context than in standard star chart missions.
