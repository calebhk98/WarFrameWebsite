## Overview

Intensify is a rare warframe mod that increases ability strength by up to 55% at maximum rank without any drawback. Unlike corrupted mods such as Blind Rage (which reduce efficiency) or Transient Fortitude (which reduce duration), Intensify provides pure strength scaling. This makes Intensify the most economical strength mod for balanced builds that cannot afford the trade-offs of corrupted alternatives.

Intensify is a staple mod for frames that benefit from ability strength without specialized scaling. Frames like Ember, Thermal Sunder, and Chroma use Intensify to maximize damage without sacrificing survivability or energy economy. Support frames like Trinity and Oberon layer it into builds to enhance link damage and healing effectiveness. Intensify's 6-point drain and madurai polarity make it a natural fit for most offensive warframe builds.

## Acquisition

Intensify drops from void fissure missions and various enemy sources across most tilesets. As a rare-tier mod, it appears in common loot pools and relic drop tables at moderate frequencies. New players obtain Intensify early through natural progression and void fissure runs. Farming for Intensify is straightforward, and trading for it is economically viable even early in a player's career due to its widespread availability.

## Builds that use it

Intensify is included in most strength-focused builds. Chroma stacks Intensify with other strength mods to achieve absurd elemental damage output. Ember uses it to maximize fire damage without accepting efficiency penalties. Grendel layers it into builds to boost ability scaling for endgame content. Even support frames like Protea and Trinity benefit from the raw strength bonus, enhancing link damage and ability scaling across their ability suites.

The mod synergizes well with other strength-scaling mods: stacking Intensify with Power Drift, Blind Rage, or umbral mods creates exponential damage scaling. For frames without access to corrupted strength mods (newer players or frames where Blind Rage's efficiency penalty is untenable), Intensify provides the highest non-corrupted strength scaling available, making it a versatile default choice.

## Notes

Intensify's lack of drawback makes it a safe choice for almost any build, though frames with low base strength values or weak scaling see minimal benefit. Frames like Limbo or Grendel prioritize range or other stats, making Intensify a lower-priority mod on those builds. Understanding whether a frame's abilities scale well with strength is critical to effective mod allocation.

The mod's 10-rank structure requires significant fusion investment, though its rare rarity makes catalysts affordable. The madurai polarity is common for strength mods, though dedicated strength builds often include multiple madurai-polarity mods that require forma to accommodate. Strategic build design balances the capacity costs of Intensify against the survivability and utility benefits of defensive or utility mods.
