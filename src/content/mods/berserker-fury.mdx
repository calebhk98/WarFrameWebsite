## Overview

Berserker Fury increases melee attack speed and stacks faster with consecutive hits, creating exponential damage scaling as combat continues. This mod transforms melee DPS from a static value into a snowballing system where longer fights yield higher attack speeds. For melee-focused builds, Berserker Fury is often the difference between acceptable and dominant damage scaling, especially in endgame content where sustained melee combat is the primary playstyle.

The mod rewards aggressive, sustained melee combat. Frames with melee channeling abilities (Atlas's Landslide, Excalibur's Exalted Blade) and melee-focused playstyles unlock Berserker Fury's full potential. The stacking system encourages uninterrupted attack chains, making the mod a natural fit for aggressive melee weapons and frames.

## Acquisition

Berserker Fury is a common drop from enemy units, particularly melee-oriented enemies and general loot tables. New players acquire their first copy early in progression. Multiple ranks farm trivially through repeated combat, and the mod is available from the Market for 50,000 credits.

## Builds that use it

Berserker Fury appears in virtually every melee weapon build. Melee-focused frames (Valkyr, Sevagoth, Xorang) make Berserker Fury mandatory for weapon scaling. Channeling builds (Landslide Mag, Exalted Blade Excalibur) leverage Berserker Fury for exponential damage growth as abilities sustain attack stacks.

## Notes

Berserker Fury stacks decay on a timer without continuous hits. Breaking attack chains resets stacks, so the mod rewards sustained, uninterrupted melee combat. Builds that rely on ability cycling or weapon-switching suffer from stack decay, while builds that sustain melee loops maximize Berserker Fury's benefit.

At rank 10 with 11 maximum stacks of 55% attack speed each, Berserker Fury enables a 6.05x attack speed multiplier at full stacks. Combined with Primed Fury and weapon base attack speed, this creates melee weapons that attack several times per second, enabling true melee-focused dominance in the endgame.
