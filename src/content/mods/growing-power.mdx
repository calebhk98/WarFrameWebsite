## Overview

Growing Power is an aura mod that grants Ability Strength stacks to nearby allies whenever a status effect-affected enemy is killed. This mod transforms the early mission phase into a power-ramping engine, where successful status effect application and management unlock exponential damage growth. Squads with status-effect weapons or frames gain massive bonuses, making Growing Power one of the strongest squad auras for coordinated teams.

The mod rewards strategic play: players who build for status effects unlock team-wide damage boosts that compound over the mission. A solo player with status procs benefits less, but in full squads running coordinated status builds, Growing Power enables damage scaling rivaling dedicated buff frames.

## Acquisition

Growing Power is a rare Void Relic reward from void fissure missions. Farming void relics and running fissures is the primary path to acquisition. It is also available as a potential drop from higher-level void missions and Zariman node completions.

## Builds that use it

Growing Power is a staple aura in coordinated squad runs and status-effect-focused builds. Status builds on weapons like Cedo (Corrosive), Ignis Wraith (Heat/Corrosive), and frames with status-spreading abilities (Ember, Ash, Octavia) pair naturally with Growing Power to create exponential damage scaling. Solo Nekros Desecrate builds benefit less, but squad-wide coordinated farming gains massive efficiency.

## Notes

Growing Power's stacks decay slowly (typically 10 seconds without a status kill refresh). This requires sustained status effect application throughout the mission. Builds without status effects or in missions with very low enemy density (defense waves, excavation) struggle to maintain stacks. However, in endless missions like Void Cascade with high enemy density and coordinated squads, the aura is transformative.

The negative drain (-4) means Growing Power actually increases total mod capacity (aura slots drain negatively for beneficial auras), making it a free addition to any squad composition. At rank 5, the 90% total Ability Strength bonus rivals dedicated buff frames like Wisp or Rhino, making it one of the most impactful squad auras available.
