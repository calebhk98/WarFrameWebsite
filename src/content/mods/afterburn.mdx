## Overview

Afterburn is a Warframe augment mod for Chroma's first ability, Spectral Scream. When Spectral Scream is deactivated, Chroma launches an elemental projectile that deals damage equal to 25 damage per second the ability was active at rank 0, scaling to 100 damage per second at rank 3. The total damage is capped -- the cap increases per rank, reaching a maximum of 200 damage at rank 0 up to 500 at rank 3 per WFCD level data. The projectile inherits the elemental type of Chroma's active elemental alignment (set by Chroma's passive or energy color).

This augment incentivizes holding Spectral Scream active for longer before deactivating, converting the animation time into burst damage output on release. The projectile's element matches whatever elemental aura Chroma has equipped, enabling the mod to align with a broader Chroma build focused on a specific elemental damage type.

## Acquisition

Afterburn is acquired from the four Arbiters of Hexis, New Loka, Cephalon Suda, or Steel Meridian syndicates as an augment offering. Syndicate augment mods are purchased using syndicate standing earned through daily syndicate missions and medallion collection. Players must reach sufficient standing rank with the syndicate offering Afterburn before it becomes available in the syndicate store.

Afterburn also appears on warframe.market as a player trade item, providing a platinum-based alternative for players who have not reached the required syndicate standing tier.

## Builds that use it

Afterburn suits Chroma builds oriented around burst elemental damage rather than pure Vex Armor buffing. Pair with high ability-strength mods (Umbral Intensify, Transient Fortitude) to increase the base damage of Spectral Scream's elemental aura, which also feeds into the Afterburn projectile's damage calculation. Builds using Chroma's heat or electric elemental alignment gain the most benefit from the projectile against enemy types with matching elemental weaknesses.

This augment is most useful in content where Spectral Scream would be run for extended durations before deactivation, such as mission scenarios where sustained elemental hazard is valuable. It is less common in pure Vex Armor buffing builds where Spectral Scream is rarely activated at all.

## Notes

Afterburn replaces the standard passive deactivation behavior of Spectral Scream with the elemental projectile launch.
