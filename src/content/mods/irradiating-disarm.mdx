## Overview

Irradiating Disarm is an augment mod for Loki's Radial Disarm ability. Radial Disarm strips all enemies in range of their ranged weapons, forcing them to engage in melee. Irradiating Disarm adds a Radiation status effect to every enemy hit by Radial Disarm, lasting 4 seconds at rank 0 and scaling to 9 seconds at rank 3. Radiation status causes affected enemies to attack their allies, turning disarmed enemies into friendly fire hazards within their own faction.

The combination of disarming enemies and inflicting Radiation confusion creates powerful crowd control that neutralizes both ranged and melee threats simultaneously. Disarmed enemies armed with knives and batons are substantially less dangerous than ones carrying Grineer rifles; adding Radiation confusion means even those melee-equipped enemies actively harm each other.

## Acquisition

Irradiating Disarm is available from Cephalon Simaris at the Sanctuary in any relay. Players purchase it for Simaris standing earned through Synthesis scanning of target creatures. It also appears in syndicate stores of New Loka or Steel Meridian depending on current offerings. Player trading through warframe.market provides a platinum alternative.

## Builds that use it

Irradiating Disarm is most effective on Loki builds emphasizing crowd control and team support in crowded enemy environments. Pair with high ability-range mods (Stretch, Augur Reach) to maximize the radius of Radial Disarm, applying both disarm and Radiation confusion to as many enemies as possible. Narrow Minded degrades ability range and reduces Irradiating Disarm's effectiveness.

In Grineer and Corpus missions, Radial Disarm with Irradiating Disarm eliminates the primary threat from Bombards, Heavy Gunners, and Corpus Tech by disarming their launchers and rockets while causing them to fire on each other. This makes Loki with Irradiating Disarm a strong support pick for Steel Path defense and endless missions where enemy density is high.

The Radiation duration of 9 seconds at rank 3 gives squadmates ample time to eliminate confused enemies or to reposit before Radial Disarm's cooldown expires.

## Notes

Irradiating Disarm requires Loki or Loki Prime to equip -- it is ability-specific to Radial Disarm. WFCD confirms `drops: []`; the mod does not appear in standard mission drop tables. The Radiation proc applied by this mod stacks with elemental Radiation damage from weapons, enabling additional confusion uptime in sustained encounters. Loki's invisibility through Switch Teleport or Decoy allows safe repositioning between Radial Disarm casts.
