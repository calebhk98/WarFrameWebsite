## Overview

Enemy Sense is a warframe utility mod providing a constant minimap awareness aura that reveals enemy positions within an expanding radius. At rank 5, the 30-meter radar range covers most tileset rooms, enabling tactical awareness without ability casts or visual line-of-sight. The 2-point drain makes Enemy Sense highly efficient for quality-of-life, fitting comfortably into most builds alongside offensive or defensive mods.

The mod excels in higher-difficulty content where enemies spawn from unexpected angles, enabling preemptive positioning and ability usage. Frames with crowd-control abilities (Limbo, Equinox, Nyx) maximize Enemy Sense by pre-casting crowd control in anticipation of incoming threats. Solo players and defensive frames benefit most, as the radar advantage translates directly into survivability through preparation.

Unlike active-ability information gathering, Enemy Sense is passive and cost-free, providing constant vigilance. This makes it ideal for casual players and frames with limited energy pools. In organized squads, the information advantage is reduced since communication often replaces radar reliance, but for public missions and solo play, Enemy Sense significantly improves tactical effectiveness.

## Acquisition

Enemy Sense drops rarely from mid-level Grineer and Corpus units. Farming specifically for Enemy Sense requires running level 20-35 missions with patient grinding. Most players acquire Enemy Sense passively through leveling, making deliberate farming inefficient compared to farming other mods.

## Builds that use it

Enemy Sense appears in quality-of-life and defensive builds, particularly on frames lacking map-wide information gathering (Nyx, Limbo, Oberon). Damage-focused builds often skip it in favor of offensive mods, but endurance and solo-play builds value the positioning advantage. Frames with slow-casting crowd control appreciate Enemy Sense for pre-positioning before threats arrive.

## Notes

The radar reveals enemy position but not their level, faction, or distance—only a directional indicator on the minimap. At 30 meters maximum, larger tilesets may have blind spots, requiring careful attention to the radar's coverage limits. Combining Enemy Sense with minimap-aware gameplay dramatically improves tactical decision-making, particularly in endless missions where enemy spawns become predictable.
