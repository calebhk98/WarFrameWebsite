## Overview

Transient Fortitude is a corrupted warframe mod that increases ability strength at the cost of ability duration. As a corrupted mod, it presents a trade-off: gaining up to 55% ability strength while losing up to 27.5% ability duration. This polarization forces build designers to choose between raw damage output or extended crowd control and buff windows, making it useful for frames where damage scaling is prioritized over ability persistence.

The mod is particularly effective on frames with instant-effect abilities (damage bursts, one-time applications) or abilities where duration is not the primary benefit. Frames like Saryn, Volt, and Ash gain significant advantage from Transient Fortitude's high strength scaling at higher ranks. Its madurai polarity places it among ability-focused mods, integrating seamlessly into offensive builds.

## Acquisition

Transient Fortitude drops exclusively from void fissure missions on the Orokin Derelict tileset. Players must locate void fissures in the Derelict region and complete missions with void relics equipped to access the reward screen. Its 4.17% drop rate from the Derelict Vault makes it a moderately difficult mod to obtain, requiring repeated Derelict void fissure runs or trading with other players. As a corrupted mod, it becomes automatically max-ranked upon acquisition, eliminating the leveling step required for standard mods.

## Builds that use it

Transient Fortitude pairs well with frames that dump damage in short ability windows. Volt's Overload benefits from the raw strength scaling without relying on persistent duration. Saryn Prime uses it to boost spore damage while accepting shorter poison duration windows. Ash and Revenant both leverage increased ability strength for higher damage output on their signature moves.

The mod is sometimes swapped in and out of builds based on mission type. Endless survival and defense missions often drop Transient Fortitude in favor of duration-scaling mods like Primed Continuity, while short-burst damage missions (void cascade, single-wave defenses) prioritize the strength bonus. This flexibility makes Transient Fortitude a valuable tool for adaptable build design.

## Notes

Corrupted mods were introduced as challenge drops from specific tileset fissures, representing illegal or unstable frame modifications. Transient Fortitude's strength-duration trade-off cannot be fully negated by stacking duration mods: the penalty applies multiplicatively to duration values, so even maximum-duration builds still lose 27.5% of their final duration stat.

Some warframes have abilities where reduced duration is actually beneficial: frames with cooldown-dependent or proc-stacking mechanics sometimes prefer Transient Fortitude. However, such niche applications are rare, and most meta builds avoid the duration penalty entirely in favor of pure strength mods like Blind Rage or umbral mods.
