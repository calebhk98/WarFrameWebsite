## Overview

Hornet Strike is an uncommon pistol mod that increases damage output for secondary weapons. Functioning as the pistol equivalent to Serration for rifles, Hornet Strike provides identical damage scaling and remains fundamental to secondary weapon builds. Since secondary weapons often fill tactical roles such as status application or burst damage, Hornet Strike enables pistols to scale alongside heavy primary weapons in endgame content.

## Acquisition

Hornet Strike drops from universal enemy loot pools, making it farmable through general combat against any faction. It also appears as a guaranteed Rotation B reward in Defense missions, allowing efficient targeted farming. Like Serration, new players acquire Hornet Strike naturally through early progression while building secondary weapons.

## Builds that use it

Hornet Strike appears in virtually all secondary weapon builds. Pistols like the Sonicor, Twin Vipers, Euphona Prime, and Aklex Prime all utilize it as a foundational damage multiplier. Status-focused secondaries pair it with elemental and physical damage mods to trigger status effects on priority targets. Single-target burst secondaries stack it with critical mods for high damage per shot. The low drain cost allows pairing with utility mods and status applicators.

## Notes

Hornet Strike provides the same +165 percent maximum damage bonus as Serration, making secondary weapons viable primary damage sources when properly modded. The Madurai polarity aligns with offensive mods, though formas enable polarity adjustment. The mod scales identically to Serration despite secondary weapons having lower base damage, creating an imbalance where modded pistols often outdamage rifles on a per-shot basis due to critical or status scaling differences. Primed Pistol Gambit and other modified variants exist for players seeking higher damage caps, though Hornet Strike remains the universally accessible baseline for all secondary builds.
