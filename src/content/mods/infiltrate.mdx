## Overview

Infiltrate is a Warframe augment mod for Ivara's Prowl ability. With the mod equipped, Ivara can move through laser barriers without triggering them while in Prowl. Additionally, she gains a flat movement speed bonus of up to 25% at rank 3. Together these effects transform Ivara into an unrestricted infiltration frame capable of completing Spy missions without disabling a single alarm, regardless of vault layout.

The ability to pass through laser barriers is categorically unique. No other frame or ability in the game can traverse active lasers without triggering vault alarms. Infiltrate makes Ivara the primary recommendation for solo flawless Spy vault completions, including Lua Spy vaults with complex laser grids that other stealth options cannot bypass.

## Acquisition

Infiltrate is available through the Perrin Sequence syndicate offering. Players with sufficient Perrin Sequence standing can purchase it directly. The mod is tradeable through in-game trade and warframe.market.

## Builds that use it

Infiltrate is nearly universal on Ivara Spy builds. Pair it with high Duration mods (Primed Continuity, Constitution) to sustain Prowl for full vault completion without recasting. Power Efficiency mods reduce the per-second energy drain of Prowl, making sustained infiltration more manageable through long vaults.

Sprint Speed mods and Arcane Agility combine with Infiltrate's movement speed bonus to make Ivara's Prowl movement noticeably faster, reducing mission completion time. Some builds also slot Primed Sure Footed to prevent knockdowns that would break Prowl during combat transitions.

## Notes

- Infiltrate does not make Ivara immune to other detection systems such as cameras or sensor bars; only laser barriers are bypassed.
- The movement speed bonus applies only while Prowl is active; it does not carry over when Prowl is toggled off.
- Laser bypass works in all Spy vault types, including the laser grids in Lua Spy vaults.
- Perrin Sequence and New Loka are opposing syndicates; players cannot earn standing with both simultaneously.
