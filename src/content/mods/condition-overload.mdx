## Overview

Condition Overload is one of the most powerful melee mods available, providing scaling damage bonuses for each unique status effect active on enemies. With six distinct status types in Warframe (heat, cold, electric, toxin, magnetic, and corrosive), a fully-procced enemy can take 480 percent additional damage at maximum rank. This mod transforms status-focused melee builds from support utility into raw damage powerhouses.

The mod rewards careful status effect application and elemental weapon design. Melee weapons with high base status chance or weapons paired with status-spreader abilities can rapidly stack multiple procs, exponentially increasing damage output against heavily-armored enemies. Condition Overload has defined melee meta strategies for over a decade.

## Acquisition

Condition Overload drops from Void Fissure void cascade reward pools. The mod is guaranteed as a rare drop in both Lith and higher-tier void cascade pools with drop rates between 3-5 percent. Extensive farming of Zariman or void fissure relics may be required to obtain a ranked copy.

Players farming Condition Overload should target Neo and Axi void cascade rewards for higher drop rates. Running Void Fissure missions with squad members also increases the likelihood of obtaining this rare drop, as more reward tables are rolled per mission. Patience and sustained void fissure farming remain the primary path to acquisition.

## Usage

Condition Overload is essential equipment for status-focused melee builds. Pair this mod with high-status-chance weapons like the Nikana Prime, Reaper Prime, or Atterax. Combine with elemental damage mods (Molten Impact, Shocking Touch, Freezing Slash, Toxic Lash) to build status effect stacks quickly.

The mod synergizes excellently with melee frames that apply their own status effects, particularly those with armor-stripping or crowd control abilities. Saryn, Gara, and Khora pair exceptionally well with Condition Overload builds, as their abilities rapidly apply multiple status types to entire enemy groups.

Against high-armor enemies in Steel Path survival, status-spreader melee builds with Condition Overload achieve damage values rivaling dedicated damage-buff frames. The key is rapidly applying all six status types simultaneously, creating multiplicative damage scaling that trivializes even level-150 enemies.

## Notes

Condition Overload remains meta in 2026 due to its unparalleled scaling potential. Status spread is one of the few reliable ways for melee weapons to achieve indefinite scaling in endless missions. Skilled players coordinate with teammates to apply different status types, maximizing the damage multiplier pool.

The drain cost is significant at 7, requiring either Orokin Catalyst optimization or careful mod selection. However, the damage ceiling is so high that even modest efficiency sacrifices are worthwhile. Status-focused melee remains one of the highest-damage playstyles in endgame Warframe.
