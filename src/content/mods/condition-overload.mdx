## Overview

Condition Overload is one of the most powerful melee mods available, providing scaling damage bonuses for each unique status effect active on enemies. Warframe has numerous damage status types -- heat, cold, electric, toxin, blast, gas, magnetic, radiation, viral, corrosive, and physical types (slash, puncture, impact) among others. Stacking multiple distinct status types on a target at maximum rank can yield up to 80% additional melee damage per unique proc type active simultaneously. This mod transforms status-focused melee builds from support utility into raw damage powerhouses.

The mod rewards careful status effect application and elemental weapon design. Melee weapons with high base status chance or weapons paired with status-spreader abilities can rapidly stack multiple procs, exponentially increasing damage output against heavily-armored enemies.

## Acquisition

Condition Overload drops from Void Fissure void cascade reward pools. The mod appears as a rare drop in both Lith and higher-tier void cascade pools with drop rates between 3-5%. Extensive farming of Zariman or void fissure relics may be required to obtain a ranked copy.

Players farming Condition Overload should target Neo and Axi void cascade rewards for higher drop rates. Running Void Fissure missions with squad members increases the number of reward tables rolled per mission. Patience and sustained void fissure farming remain the primary path to acquisition.

## Builds that use it

Condition Overload is essential equipment for status-focused melee builds. Pair this mod with high-status-chance weapons like the Nikana Prime, Reaper Prime, or Atterax. Combine with elemental damage mods (Molten Impact, Shocking Touch, Freezing Slash, Toxic Lash) to build status effect stacks quickly.

The mod synergizes with melee frames that apply their own status effects, particularly those with armor-stripping or crowd control abilities. Saryn, Gara, and Khora pair well with Condition Overload builds, as their abilities rapidly apply multiple status types to entire enemy groups.

Against high-armor enemies in Steel Path survival, status-spreader melee builds with Condition Overload achieve damage values rivaling dedicated damage-buff frames. The key is rapidly applying as many distinct status types as possible simultaneously, creating multiplicative damage scaling effective against level-150 enemies.

## Notes

Condition Overload remains a top-tier melee mod due to its unparalleled scaling potential against enemies affected by multiple status procs. Status spread is one of the few reliable ways for melee weapons to achieve indefinite scaling in endless missions. The drain cost of 10 requires either an Orokin Catalyst or careful mod configuration to fit alongside other essential mods. Status-focused melee remains one of the highest-damage playstyles in endgame Warframe.
