## Overview

Barrel Diffusion is the pistol-equivalent of Serration, providing direct damage scaling for secondary weapons. This mod is foundational to pistol builds, enabling scaling from early game through endgame. A ranked Barrel Diffusion transforms secondary weapons from supplementary options into viable primary damage sources, especially for pistol-focused warframes like Mesa or Equinox.

The mod's pure damage scaling is straightforward but effective. Unlike primary weapons where mods like Heavy Caliber or multishot create playstyle variation, Barrel Diffusion is a universal power tool for any pistol build. Paired with other secondary mods, Barrel Diffusion enables pistols to compete with primaries in sustained damage.

## Acquisition

Barrel Diffusion is a common drop from all enemy units. New players acquire their first copy within the first few hours. Additional ranks farm trivially, and the mod is available from the Market for 50,000 credits.

## Builds that use it

Barrel Diffusion appears in virtually every secondary weapon build. Pistol-focused frames (Mesa, Equinox) rely on Barrel Diffusion as a core component of their damage equation. Hybrid primary-secondary builds also include Barrel Diffusion to enable secondary weapons as backup or switching damage sources.

## Notes

Barrel Diffusion's drain is lower than Serration (5 vs. 4 for rifles), reflecting the lower power level of secondary weapons compared to primaries. However, the scaling per rank is identical, making Barrel Diffusion equally cost-effective for its slot within secondary weapons.

At rank 10, 165% damage scales secondary weapons into viable endgame tools. Combined with multishot and crit mods, pistols can become primary-competitive damage sources, especially on builds that leverage weapon-switching synergies (Ash's Seeking Shuriken augment, Harrow's Warding Thurible, Saryn's Miasma).
