## Overview

Enemy Radar is an aura mod that reveals enemy positions on the minimap within a large radius, providing critical situational awareness to the entire squad. This utility aura provides squad-wide tactical information, enabling coordinated positioning and preventing ambushes. While it offers no direct combat bonuses, the information advantage translates directly into coordinated squad play, reduced surprise damage from flanking enemies, and more efficient map clearing in void fissures and endless missions where enemy density is high. The mod is one of the most underrated auras in the game for non-endgame players.

The mod is universally useful across all playstyles and squad compositions. Solo players gain safety through better navigation and avoided surprise attacks, while squads leverage the shared radar coverage to coordinate positioning and ability usage. Enemy Radar is often paired with other utility auras like Corrosive Projection or Growing Power, occupying the team's dedicated aura slot for information advantage over raw damage scaling. In squad-based farming runs, the reduced confusion and faster clear times often outweigh damage-focused aura choices, especially when squad coordination is the bottleneck.

## Acquisition

Enemy Radar is a common drop from all enemy types across the star chart. New players acquire their first copy within the first hour of gameplay, and multiple ranks are trivial to farm from any mission. It is also available from the in-game Market for a modest credit cost, making it one of the earliest utility auras available.

## Builds that use it

Enemy Radar is most impactful in void fissure runs, endless missions, and squad-based content where situational awareness drives tactical advantage. Scout-style builds and solo farming often include Enemy Radar for safety during solo runs. In competitive squad play, Enemy Radar competes with offense-oriented auras like Corrosive Projection, making squad composition decisions important. Many experienced squads allocate one slot to Enemy Radar while others run damage auras, creating a balanced information and damage output split.

## Notes

Enemy Radar reveals enemy positions as dots on the minimap but does not provide enemy health, threat level, damage type, or status information. This means it shows locations but not tactical context. In high-level play, squads sometimes forego Enemy Radar in favor of damage-focused auras like Corrosive Projection, but for learning players, solo runs, and casual group play, the safety benefit is significant. The negative drain (-2) actually adds mod capacity, making Enemy Radar a free addition to any squad composition from a slot perspective.

At rank 5, the 100-meter radius covers most mission tiles, enabling full-map awareness for many mission types including Void Cascade and most star chart levels. Combined with vacuum mods and squad coordination, Enemy Radar unlocks efficient farming strategies that depend on knowing enemy positions and avoiding wasted travel time between objectives.
