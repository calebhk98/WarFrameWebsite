## Overview

True Steel is the foundational critical-chance mod for melee weapons, providing a pure critical-chance boost that enables crit-scaling builds. At rank 3, the 30% critical chance boost transforms weapons with moderate crit multipliers into viable critical platforms capable of chaining critical hits. The common rarity and 4-point drain make True Steel accessible early, becoming a staple in virtually every melee build.

True Steel excels on weapons with decent crit multipliers, stacking synergistically with other crit sources (Blood Rush, Critical Damage mods). Combined with status scaling (Condition Overload, Bloodletting), True Steel enables hybrid builds balancing crit and status. For pure crit builds, True Steel is nearly non-negotiable, appearing in almost every melee setup.

The mod's universality makes it a farming priority for new players, though the common rarity ensures passive acquisition through normal gameplay. Veteran players maintain multiple copies at different ranks for different weapon tiers, showcasing True Steel's perennial relevance across all melee builds.

## Acquisition

True Steel drops from all enemy factions universally, making it one of the easiest mods to acquire passively. Running any mission yields multiple copies within minutes. High-rank copies require minimal grinding.

## Builds that use it

True Steel appears in virtually every crit-focused melee build, dominating hybrid and pure-crit strategies. Non-crit melee builds skip True Steel entirely, focusing on status or pure damage instead. Endgame melee builds universally include True Steel, making it essential for competitive melee play.

## Notes

Critical chance scales multiplicatively with critical damage, creating exponential damage growth. Combining True Steel with Blood Rush and other crit mods enables absurdly high critical frequency, enabling one-hit kills on light targets. The 30% boost at rank 3 is sufficient for viability; rank 10 variants or primed alternatives provide marginal improvements. Weapons with naturally high crit multipliers benefit most from True Steel, as the percentage boost scales off base multiplier values. Weapons with poor base crit chance see less benefit from True Steel alone, requiring additional crit-stacking mods like Blood Rush or Pressure Point for viable crit builds.
