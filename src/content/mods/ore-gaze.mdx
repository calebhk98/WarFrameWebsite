## Overview

Ore Gaze is a rare warframe augment mod that enhances Aten's Petrify ability. When equipped, Petrify automatically scans any petrified enemy into the Codex, removing the need for a separate scanner. More importantly, killed petrified enemies gain a percentage chance to drop additional loot on top of their standard drop table. This makes Ore Gaze highly relevant for resource farming, since Aten's Petrify can affect multiple enemies simultaneously, multiplying the number of targets eligible for bonus drops. The mod fills the augment slot on Aten builds oriented around material gathering rather than combat damage. Its Zenurik polarity aligns naturally with many ability-strength configurations, reducing drain impact on typical Aten loadouts.

The loot chance scales from 10% at rank 0 to 25% at rank 3. Because the bonus triggers per enemy killed while petrified, dense petrify coverage yields the largest return. Players who pair Ore Gaze with Aten on high-density Infested or Grineer nodes can expect a measurable increase in rare resource yield per mission compared to runs without the mod. The Codex scanning benefit has secondary value for players completing bestiary entries without purchasing scanner charges, though this alone would not justify the augment slot cost.

## Acquisition

Ore Gaze is offered as a syndicate reward by two opposing syndicates. Red Veil members at the Exalted rank and Steel Meridian members at the General rank can both purchase the mod for standing. Because these two syndicates are allied, a player allied with either one can access both offerings without rank penalties. Trading with other players is also viable; the mod is uncommon on warframe.market and typically trades for moderate platinum.

## Builds that use it

Ore Gaze belongs in Aten farming builds targeting resource-dense missions. Pair it with range mods such as Stretch and Augur Reach to maximize the area of effect of Petrify, ensuring more enemies are petrified simultaneously. Ability-strength mods are not required for the loot chance component since that scales with mod rank, not power strength. A typical loadout pairs Ore Gaze with Despoil (Nekros augment synergy is irrelevant here -- Aten is not Nekros), so focus on range and efficiency to keep Petrify available on cooldown. Khora and Hydroid offer competing loot-chance augments on different frames; Ore Gaze is the Aten-specific equivalent for players committed to that frame.

## Notes

Ore Gaze's loot chance applies to each enemy individually and is independent of other loot-chance effects, meaning it stacks additively with resource boosters (which double drop quantity, not chance) and multiplicatively in effect with loadouts that extend Petrify duration. The Codex scan is automatic upon killing a petrified enemy regardless of whether a scanner is equipped. The mod does not affect the drop tables of Petrified enemies -- it only adds an additional chance roll on top of the standard table.
