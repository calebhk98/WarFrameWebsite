## Overview

Thermal Sunder is an augment mod for Xaku's Thermal Sunder ability, converting single-target elemental stripping into area-of-effect status spreading. At rank 3, the 15-meter radius enables cascade effects where status procs on one target spread to nearby enemies, creating exponential status buildup across enemy groups. The augment transforms Thermal Sunder from utility into primary crowd-control, enabling status-overflow strategies.

Thermal Sunder Augment excels in status-focused builds where elemental scaling defines the strategy. Spreading heat, cold, electric, and toxin procs across groups enables rapid status accumulation, enabling stuns, freezes, blinds, and damage amplification cascading across entire rooms. The augment is particularly powerful in void cascade missions where large enemy clusters enable maximum spread efficiency.

The augment requires Xaku ownership and Thermal Sunder leveling, limiting accessibility to Xaku mains. However, for status-focused players, Thermal Sunder Augment enables powerful crowd-control playstyles. The 6-point drain is acceptable for augments, and the area-of-effect utility makes it nearly mandatory for crowd-control builds.

## Acquisition

Thermal Sunder Augment is available from syndicate vendors at Exalted Rank standing, requiring significant reputation farming with a single syndicate. Trading or void fissure drops provide faster acquisition paths without grinding reputation.

## Builds that use it

Thermal Sunder Augment dominates status-focused Xaku builds, particularly in high-enemy-density missions. Non-status Xaku builds skip the augment entirely, focusing on direct damage or crowd control instead. Status-overflow squads appreciate Xaku's cascade effects, making the augment valuable for group crowd-control strategies.

## Notes

Status spreading stacks additively with other AoE crowd-control sources, enabling powerful control chains. Combining Thermal Sunder Augment with weapon-based elemental attacks creates exponential status buildup, turning Xaku into a status-focused powerhouse. The 15-meter radius at max rank covers most tileset rooms, enabling room-wide control through strategic ability usage. Thermal Sunder Augment enables crowd-control overflow strategies where cascading status effects create rapid room-wide stuns or freezes. The spreading mechanic allows single target ability applications to affect entire rooms through careful positioning, turning Thermal Sunder into an effective area-denial tool despite being nominally a single-target ability.
