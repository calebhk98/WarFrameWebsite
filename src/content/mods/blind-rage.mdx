## Overview

Blind Rage is a corrupted warframe mod that maximizes ability strength at the cost of ability efficiency. At rank 10, it grants 99% ability strength while reducing efficiency by 55%, making it one of the most powerful strength-scaling mods available. Blind Rage is the signature corrupted mod for strength-based builds, enabling oneshot damage levels or extreme crowd control scaling that would be impossible with standard mods alone.

Blind Rage is almost mandatory in high-level content builds. Frames like Equinox, Mirage, and Valkyr use it to reach damage caps for endgame content. The efficiency penalty is offset by energy management techniques: using Streamline, Primed Flow, or external energy restoration systems (Arcane Energize, energy restoration warframes) mitigates the energy cost inflation. This interplay between Blind Rage and energy sustainability creates complex but rewarding build decisions.

## Acquisition

Blind Rage drops exclusively from void fissure missions on the Orokin Derelict. Like other corrupted mods, it requires completing Derelict void fissure missions (with void relics) to access the reward pool. Its 4.17% drop chance from the Derelict Vault makes farming moderately time-intensive. Blind Rage auto-ranks to maximum upon acquisition, skipping the fusion leveling phase other mods require. Most experienced players obtain it through trading rather than farming the derelict repeatedly.

## Builds that use it

Blind Rage is the core mod for nearly every damage-focused warframe build. Saryn uses it to maximize spore scaling and achieve endgame-viable damage per second. Equinox stacks it with strength boosters to deal absurd night-form damage. Valkyr and Ash use Blind Rage to enable reliable oneshot abilities against high-level enemies. Even support frames like Trinity layer it into their builds to maximize link damage when energy restoration is the primary role.

The mod synergizes with strength-scaling warframe passives and helminth abilities. Frames like Gauss or Xaku gain multiplicative benefits when strength stacking is the intended playstyle. Combining Blind Rage with other strength mods (Intensify, Power Drift, Umbral Intensify) creates exponential damage scaling that defines endgame viability.

## Notes

The efficiency penalty makes Blind Rage incompatible with many frames that lack external energy restoration or that rely on rapid ability spam. Frames like Equinox's day form or Nova need Streamline, Primed Continuity, or external energy to function with Blind Rage equipped. This forcing function ensures that Blind Rage builds require intentional synergy design rather than simple stat stacking.

Ability efficiency calculations are multiplicative: a -55% efficiency modifier stacks with other efficiency changes. For example, an ability with 100% efficiency and Blind Rage equipped becomes 45% efficiency (100% - 55% = 45%), doubling the energy cost. Pairing with Streamline (which adds 30% efficiency) brings effective efficiency to 75%, reducing the energy cost multiplier to 1.33x instead of 2.22x.
