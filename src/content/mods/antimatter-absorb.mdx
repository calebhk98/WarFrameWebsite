## Overview

Antimatter Absorb is a Warframe augment mod for Nova's Antimatter Drop ability. With the mod equipped, the Antimatter Drop particle absorbs projectiles fired at it from within a radius that grows from 2m at rank 0 to 5m at rank 3. Each absorbed projectile increases the power of the particle's final explosion. In missions with dense projectile-heavy enemies such as Grineer Bombards or Corpus crewmen, the particle accumulates significant absorbed damage before detonation.

The mod converts Nova from a straightforward damage dealer into a reactive offensive frame. Players guide the particle through enemy fire, using the absorbed damage to amplify the explosion well beyond what the base ability deals. In Sortie and Steel Path missions where enemy damage output is high, Antimatter Absorb allows Nova to use that same enemy firepower against them.

## Acquisition

Antimatter Absorb is available through the Cephalon Suda syndicate offering. Players with sufficient Cephalon Suda standing can purchase it. The mod is tradeable through in-game trade and warframe.market.

## Builds that use it

Antimatter Absorb suits Nova builds paired with Wormhole or slow-speed Molecular Prime timing where the player can guide the particle through sustained enemy fire. Power Strength and Range builds amplify the base explosion while Antimatter Absorb multiplies the total with absorbed projectile damage.

Squad play using a Trinity for energy restoration enables continuous Antimatter Drop spam, making it practical to fire one particle per engagement and absorb as many projectiles as possible before detonation. The mod is less effective in Infested missions where most enemies attack in melee, producing few absorbable projectiles.

## Notes

- Absorbed damage is additive on top of the particle's base explosion; the exact absorption formula is documented on the Fandom wiki.
- The absorption radius is centered on the particle itself as it travels, not on Nova.
- Guiding the particle requires manual aim; the particle follows the direction Nova moves her reticle after casting.
- Cephalon Suda and Arbiters of Hexis are allied syndicates; either provides access to the offering.
