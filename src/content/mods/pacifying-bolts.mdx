## Overview

Pacifying Bolts is an augment mod for Nyx's Psychic Bolts ability, converting single-target crowd control into squad-wide defensive support. Each bolt now applies a damage reduction buff that stacks across multiple bolts, enabling Nyx to transform from a crowd-control frame into a support powerhouse. At rank 3, the 25% damage reduction per bolt creates significant durability improvements for squads, particularly in organized play.

Pacifying Bolts enables cooperative Nyx gameplay, where crowd control serves a dual purpose: disabling threats while simultaneously buffing allies. The augment is particularly powerful in squads relying on sustained positioning, as the buffs enable aggressive play without proportional durability loss. Nyx becomes a supportive control frame rather than a selfish crowd-control utility.

The augment requires Nyx ownership and Psychic Bolts leveling, limiting accessibility to Nyx mains. However, for squad-oriented players, Pacifying Bolts transforms Nyx into a valuable team member. The 6-point drain is acceptable for augments, and the support utility makes it nearly mandatory for group missions.

## Acquisition

Pacifying Bolts is available from syndicate vendors at Exalted Rank standing or above, requiring significant reputation farming with a single syndicate. Trading with other players or lucky void fissure drops provide shortcuts to acquiring the mod without grinding.

## Builds that use it

Pacifying Bolts dominates support-oriented Nyx builds, particularly in organized squads with high communication. Solo Nyx builds may skip Pacifying Bolts in favor of offensive augments. Squads running Nyx appreciate the group damage reduction, making Pacifying Bolts valuable in endgame squad content.

## Notes

Damage reduction stacks additively across multiple bolts, capping at some value (typically around 75-90% total). Multiple applications from high ability strength Nyx builds can approach diminishing returns. Regardless, the augment's support utility transforms Nyx's role, enabling team playstyles previously impossible with selfish crowd control. Pacifying Bolts enables squad compositions that would otherwise struggle with survivability, turning Nyx into a force multiplier for risky play styles. Squad coordination around Nyx's support enables aggressive offense that would normally result in wipes, creating synergistic gameplay where crowd control and damage reduction balance risk and reward perfectly.
