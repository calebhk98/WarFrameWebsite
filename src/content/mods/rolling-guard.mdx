## Overview

Rolling Guard is a rare warframe mod that grants temporary invulnerability whenever the player performs a dodge roll. Upon activation, the warframe becomes invulnerable for a brief duration and automatically removes all active status effects. With a cooldown timer between uses, Rolling Guard provides both reactive defense and crowd-control immunity in high-level missions.

## Acquisition

Rolling Guard drops from universal enemy loot tables across all factions, with slightly higher rates from Infested units. It is tradable and can be purchased from other players. Farming involves running missions against various enemy types or relying on marketplace purchases for a faster acquisition.

## Builds that use it

Rolling Guard suits mobile, dodge-focused warframes like Loki, Ivara, Gauss, and Sevagoth. It synergizes well with parkour-heavy playstyles where the player remains mobile and performs frequent dodge rolls. Frames with quick-dash mechanics benefit from the reliable invulnerability window and automatic status-effect cleanse. The mod shines in mission types with heavy crowd-control spam such as Void Cascade and high-level Corpus Void Fissure missions.

## Notes

Rolling Guard's effectiveness depends on player skill and playstyle. The mod only triggers on successful dodge rolls, requiring active input and timing to maximize its defensive value. The status-effect removal component makes it especially valuable against status-heavy enemies like Bombards, Ancient Disruptors, and Toxic Ancients. At maximum rank, the seven-second cooldown allows for nearly permanent uptime in combat when the player dodges frequently. The invulnerability window provides i-frames that prevent damage during panic moments, offering both offensive and defensive value by allowing repositioning or ability casting without interruption.
