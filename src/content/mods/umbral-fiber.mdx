## Overview

Umbral Fiber is a legendary warframe mod that increases armor and grants Tau Resistance. Introduced alongside Umbral Vitality and Umbral Intensify in Update 23.0, this non-tradable, non-transmutable mod forms part of the powerful Umbra mod set. The armor scaling makes it invaluable for frames with high base armor seeking additional durability in high-level content.

## Acquisition

Umbral Fiber is exclusively obtained as a quest reward by completing The Sacrifice. Like Umbral Vitality, it is non-tradable and non-transmutable, meaning it cannot be farmed through relics or obtained from other players. Players receive one copy upon quest completion.

## Builds that use it

Umbral Fiber sees heavy use in defensive and tank-focused builds. Armor-stacking frames like Rhino, Nova, Vauban, and Saryn utilize it effectively. When paired with the other Umbra mods, the significant set bonus transforms Umbral Fiber into one of the most efficient defensive options in the game. High-armor frames benefit disproportionately from its percentage scaling, sometimes doubling or tripling effective durability in challenging missions.

## Notes

The Umbra set bonus mechanic makes equipping two or more mods from the set highly worthwhile. With Umbral Fiber, Umbral Vitality, and Umbral Intensify together, each mod receives an 80 percent boost, effectively multiplying their contributions. Armor reduction mechanics (Corrosive Projection, enemy Corrosive effects) reduce this mod's effectiveness proportionally. The Tau Resistance component complements the primary armor scaling, providing utility against Sentient faction enemies and environmental hazards in The New War and Zariman missions.
