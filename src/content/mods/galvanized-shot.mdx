## Overview

Galvanized Shot is a rare primary weapon mod from the Zariman tileset void cascade reward pools. It provides a stacking damage and multishot bonus that scales with eliminations, making it an essential choice for high-level primary weapon builds. The mod rewards active engagement and sustained firepower by granting bonus benefits with each defeated enemy.

The combination of damage and multishot makes this mod particularly efficient for scaling weapon performance in endgame content. Unlike base damage mods that provide fixed increases, Galvanized Shot's stacking mechanic allows primary weapons to scale dynamically throughout a mission as kills accumulate.

## Acquisition

Galvanized Shot drops exclusively from Void Fissure void cascade rewards in the Zariman tileset. The mod appears in both Lith and Meso void cascade pools with drop rates between 4-5 percent. Players must participate in Void Fissure missions using void relics to access this reward.

To farm Galvanized Shot efficiently, run Zariman void fissure missions repeatedly. The drop rate is low enough that extensive farming may be required, but the multishot and damage combination makes it worthwhile for primary weapon enthusiasts building endgame loadouts.

## Usage

Galvanized Shot fits into critical slots in primary weapon builds targeting high-damage output. The stacking mechanic rewards weapons with large magazine sizes and rapid fire rates, allowing players to quickly accumulate kills and reach maximum stacks.

Common primary weapons that synergize well with Galvanized Shot include the Braton Prime, Soma Prime, and Phantasma. Pair it with other damage-scaling mods like Serration or Hornet Strike variants for multiplicative scaling. The multishot bonus stacks additively with other multishot sources, making it stack efficiently in most loadouts.

The stacking mechanic begins immediately on mission start, with each eliminated enemy incrementing the bonus. Stacks persist until the player goes down or the mission ends, rewarding consistent play and effective target prioritization.

## Notes

Galvanized Shot is one of the rarer primary weapon mods and competes with other high-tier damage options like Serration or Galvanized Chamber. The stacking mechanic makes it stronger in longer missions where sustained engagement accumulates significant bonuses.

The mod requires a vazarin polarity slot, which is less common on primary weapons than madurai but still compatible with most rifle and gun frames. Consider using Orokin Catalyst potatoes and matching polarities when building around this mod.
