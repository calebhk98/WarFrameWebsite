## Overview

Blood Rush is an uncommon melee mod providing stacking critical chance bonuses that scale with combo multiplier. At maximum rank with full combo, this mod grants 40 percent additional critical chance, enabling sustained builds to achieve extreme crit rates on high-base-crit weapons. The combo scaling mechanic makes aggressive melee combat directly reward continuous engagement through exponential crit scaling.

This mod is foundational to critical-focused melee builds, particularly those pairing with heavy crit multiplier sources. The synergy between Blood Rush and critical multiplier mods creates multiplicative damage scaling that makes crit builds one of the highest-damage melee archetypes. Weapons with natural crit chance combine with Blood Rush to achieve crit rates exceeding 100 percent at full combo.

## Acquisition

Blood Rush drops as an uncommon mod from melee-based enemy units across all factions. Killing Heavy Gunners, Bombards, Noxious Corpus, or other melee-equipped enemies yields reliable drops. Extended farming runs against melee-heavy enemy types quickly accumulate ranked copies without requiring relic farming.

The uncommon rarity makes Blood Rush more accessible than rare drop mods, enabling newer players to build crit-focused melee weapons earlier in their progression. Void Relic rewards and vendor sources also provide additional acquisition paths, though enemy farming remains the fastest source for obtaining multiple ranked copies.

## Usage

Blood Rush is essential for crit-focused melee builds, particularly those pairing with high-crit-multiplier weapons like the Nikana Prime or Fragor Prime. Combine with Organ Shatter or other critical multiplier mods to achieve multiplicative damage scaling. The combo scaling means sustained combat becomes exponentially more powerful as combo stacks accumulate.

Weapons with high base critical chance benefit most from Blood Rush, as the mod amplifies existing crit advantages into overwhelming damage multipliers. Condition Overload can be paired alongside Blood Rush for hybrid builds that leverage both status and critical scaling simultaneously. The interaction between these mods creates the most powerful melee builds available.

Against Steel Path enemies and endless mission scaling, crit-focused melee weapons with Blood Rush achieve damage values rivaling dedicated damage-buff frames. The continuous engagement required for combo maintenance creates intuitive gameplay where player skill directly translates to damage output through combo chain maintenance.

## Notes

Blood Rush has been central to melee meta design since its introduction, remaining viable even across multiple power creep cycles. The combo-scaling design creates satisfying gameplay feedback where sustained combat directly produces visible damage improvements. This mechanic endures as one of the most elegant scaling systems in Warframe.

The madurai polarity matches most melee weapons, simplifying build construction. The drain cost is reasonable at 6, leaving mod capacity for Condition Overload, critical multiplier mods, and elemental effects. Efficient builds accommodate Blood Rush alongside multiple damage-scaling sources for exponential late-game scaling.
