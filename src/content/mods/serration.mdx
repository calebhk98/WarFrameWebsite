## Overview

Serration is an uncommon rifle mod that increases damage output for primary weapons in the rifle category. As one of the most iconic and foundational mods in Warframe, Serration has appeared in the game since launch and remains a staple in virtually every rifle build across all mission types and difficulty levels. Its straightforward damage multiplier and low drain cost make it accessible and essential for new players.

## Acquisition

Serration drops from enemy loot tables across all factions and planets, making it farmable through general combat. It also appears as a guaranteed Rotation B reward in Defense missions throughout the star chart, allowing targeted farming. Because it is one of the earliest available power mods, new players acquire Serration naturally during progression while leveling frames and weapons.

## Builds that use it

Every rifle build in the game incorporates Serration. Rifles like the Braton, Paris Prime, Tiberon Prime, and Opticor rely on Serration as a foundational damage multiplier. The mod combines with other offensive mods like Split Chamber (multishot), Heavy Caliber (damage at accuracy cost), and elemental damage mods to create scaling damage output. Even at lower mastery ranks, Serration remains one of the highest-impact mods available.

## Notes

Serration's simple design belies its fundamental importance to rifle scaling. The +165 percent bonus at maximum rank effectively doubles rifle damage, providing the second-largest damage multiplier available (after weapon-specific prime mods). Its universality and low drain cost mean it fits comfortably into almost any rifle build. The Madurai polarity aligns well with other offensive mods, though players can use an Orokin Forma to adjust polarities if needed. Variants like Primed Serration and Spectral Serration offer improved scaling for players seeking higher damage caps.
