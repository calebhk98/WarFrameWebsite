## Overview

Accelerated Blast is a dual-purpose shotgun mod providing both fire-rate and puncture damage scaling. The combination of attack-speed and impact-type damage makes it valuable for shotgun builds prioritizing rapid fire and armor-penetration mechanics. Accelerated Blast is particularly effective on semi-auto or pump-action shotguns where fire-rate scaling has multiplicative impact on overall damage output.

## Stats per rank

Accelerated Blast scales both fire rate and puncture damage from 15% at rank 0 to 60% at rank 3, providing dual scaling benefits that synergize well with rapid-fire shotgun mechanics.

## Acquisition

Accelerated Blast is a rare mod obtained through mid-game star chart progression and enemy drops. Players encounter it on Mars, Jupiter, and later planets. The mod is also available through relic farming and marketplace trading once players reach sufficient mastery rank.

## Usage

Accelerated Blast shines on rapid-fire shotguns where fire-rate scaling improves sustained damage output. The dual fire-rate and puncture scaling enables builds that prioritize armor-penetration and sustained DPS over burst damage. Equip it on shotguns like Sobek or Drakgoon where rapid fire and puncture damage synergize with ability effects or elemental scaling.

The mod becomes less valuable on slow, heavy-hitting shotguns like Strun Wraith where single-shot damage matters more than fire rate. In squad play, Accelerated Blast enables rapid-fire shotgun builds that provide crowd control through status effects and armor piercing through puncture damage stacking.

## Common builds

Accelerated Blast appears on rapid-fire shotgun builds that prioritize fire-rate scaling. Sobek builds often use Accelerated Blast for additional fire-rate and puncture scaling. The mod is less common on heavy shotguns that benefit more from damage mods like Point Blank or elemental scaling.
