## Overview

Antitoxin is a defensive warframe aura mod that provides direct toxin damage resistance, stacking linearly with other resistance sources. While toxin damage itself is relatively uncommon compared to heat or corrosive procs, certain enemy factions (Infested, some Corpus units) favor toxic attacks. This mod shines in missions where Infested or specialized Corpus enemies dominate, converting incoming toxin damage into survivable hits. At rank 5, the 45% reduction significantly mitigates toxin-focused enemy squads, particularly when combined with other status resistance tools.

Antitoxin occupies a defensive slot that could alternatively house utility or strength mods, making the decision contextual. For general star-chart progression, Antitoxin is overkill on most nodes. However, for high-level void fissures, derelict missions, or void cascade runs against Infested swarms, the flat resistance provides meaningful damage reduction. The mod scales better when paired with frames that have innate toxin resistance (Inaros, Nidus) or frames capable of rapid healing (Trinity, Oberon).

The 4 drain cost and uncommon rarity make Antitoxin accessible early in progression, serving as a stepping stone toward understanding status resistance mechanics. Many veteran players skip Antitoxin in favor of all-purpose defensive mods like Redirection or Vitality, but its niche role in Infested-heavy content justifies its inclusion in specialized builds.

## Acquisition

Antitoxin drops frequently from enemy units, particularly Infested and Corpus factions, making it one of the easiest mods to acquire passively. Running any Infested-heavy mission (Eris, Orokin Derelict) guarantees Antitoxin drops within minutes. The uncommon rarity ensures it appears in nearly every mission's loot table involving those factions, making deliberate farming unnecessary.

## Builds that use it

Antitoxin appears most commonly in dedicated Infested-node farming builds (Derelict Survival, Abyss runs) where status resistance becomes a quality-of-life concern rather than a necessity. Frames running Equilibrium or heal-based ability subsumed abilities benefit more from toxin resistance, as the survivability allows sustained aggression without needing defensive recasts. Inaros, Nidus, and toxin-resistant frames appreciate the additional layer of protection.

## Notes

Toxin resistance reduces incoming toxin damage by the listed percentage, not status proc chance. Toxin procs (poison effect) remain unaffected; status-resistance mods like Grineer Resistance or Status Resistance are required to avoid toxic buildup. For endurance missions where toxin-resistant enemies spawn frequently, Antitoxin becomes more valuable than flat health/shield mods, creating a trade-off based on mission design and enemy composition.
