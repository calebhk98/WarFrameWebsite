## Overview

Hammer Shot is a rare dual-stat rifle mod that simultaneously increases critical damage multiplier and status chance. At rank 3, it provides +60% critical damage and +80% status chance, making it one of the more cost-efficient two-stat mods available for the rifle slot. The combination suits rifles that have a moderate critical chance but benefit from amplified critical hits alongside a higher rate of status procs for elemental builds.

Rifles with a high base critical chance, such as the Lanka or Rubico Prime, gain larger absolute critical damage output from the +60% multiplier because critical hits occur frequently enough for the bonus to apply often. At the same time, the +80% status chance at rank 3 means a weapon with a 10% base status chance reaches 18% after the additive bonus, which is meaningful for builds targeting elemental proc stacking. Hammer Shot competes directly with Vital Sense for the pure critical damage slot and with High Voltage or Thermite Rounds for the status-element slot, so it is best viewed as a hybrid choice when both stats contribute to the build rather than when one dominates.

## Acquisition

Hammer Shot drops from high-level Grineer units. The mod is a rare drop, appearing more frequently in missions with dense Grineer spawns such as Sedna Survival and Kuva Fortress nodes. Farming a few hours of Grineer-heavy Survival missions typically yields multiple copies. Trading with other players through warframe.market is a faster route if the drop rate proves inconsistent.

## Builds that use it

Hammer Shot suits rifles in Slash-proc or viral-slash builds where both status chance and critical damage contribute to the damage formula. On a rifle with 25% critical chance and 15% status chance, Hammer Shot brings the status chance to 27% while adding substantial critical damage, enabling a viable middle path between pure crit and pure status builds. Pair it with Serration for base damage, Split Chamber for multishot, and an elemental mod such as Infected Clip to combine status procs with the increased crit multiplier. Builds relying entirely on critical chance scaling, such as those running Point Strike alongside Vital Sense and Argon Scope, may prefer Vital Sense in this slot for the higher pure critical damage value. The Vazarin polarity on Hammer Shot does not match common rifle configurations, so a polarity swap via a Forma may be necessary to avoid drain penalties.

## Notes

The critical damage bonus from Hammer Shot is additive with other critical damage mods in the same slot category. The status chance bonus is also additive. Neither bonus scales with ability strength or external modifiers; both are fixed percentages applied to the weapon's base stat. The mod has no faction-specific restrictions and applies to all rifle-type weapons that accept rifle mods.
