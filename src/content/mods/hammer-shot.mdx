## Overview

Hammer Shot is a utility rifle mod providing accuracy and knockdown-chance scaling, enabling crowd-control through projectile impact. At rank 3, the 45% knockdown chance creates frequent crowd control effects, interrupting enemy attacks and enabling repositioning windows. The accuracy bonus improves headshot consistency, enabling precision playstyles on accurate rifles.

Hammer Shot excels on weapons with moderate accuracy that benefit from tightened spread, particularly beam rifles and burst-fire weapons. The knockdown scaling transforms accurate weapons into crowd-control tools, enabling defensive playstyles where interruption prevents incoming damage. Rifles like the Grineer Somachord or Lanka benefit significantly from both accuracy and knockdown components.

Hammer Shot occupies a utility slot competing with damage scaling, fire-rate scaling, and crit mods. For crowd-control builds, Hammer Shot provides both offensive utility and defensive safety; for pure damage builds, damage scaling outperforms utility. The 6-point drain is substantial for utility, making accuracy+knockdown trade-offs contextual.

## Acquisition

Hammer Shot drops rarely from high-level Grineer units, particularly in end-game star chart missions (Void, Sedna, etc.). Dedicated farming yields the mod within a few hours. Trading or void fissure luck provide faster acquisition without grinding.

## Builds that use it

Hammer Shot appears in crowd-control rifle builds, particularly on weapons with low base accuracy (shotguns) or on utility-focused frames. Pure-damage builds skip Hammer Shot entirely, focusing on crit or fire-rate scaling. Melee-hybrid builds leverage Hammer Shot's accuracy for ranged setup opportunities.

## Notes

Knockdown chance is independent of critical hits or headshots, applying universally to all shots. Combined with other crowd-control sources (Pacifying Bolts, thermal sunder), Hammer Shot enables status-overflow crowd control. The accuracy bonus improves consistency, enabling easier headshots on fast-moving targets. Weapons with high accuracy inherit better precision from even marginal accuracy bonuses, making Hammer Shot particularly effective on already-accurate rifles. The knockdown effect scales with enemy weight; heavier units resist knockdown more effectively, requiring higher chance thresholds or additional crowd-control stacking to maintain crowd control against armor-heavy enemies.
