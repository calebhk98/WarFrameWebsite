## Overview

Equilibrium converts dropped health and energy orbs into their counterparts, creating a self-sustaining economy for warframes that struggle with energy or health management. When picking up an energy orb, the mod converts a percentage to health; when picking up a health orb, it converts a percentage to energy. This bidirectional conversion is invaluable for frames that burn energy quickly (like Limbo or Octavia) or for sustaining through long endless missions where both resources are precious. The mod essentially enables infinite missions by converting excess of one resource into deficit of another.

The mod's utility scales dramatically with ability strength, energy drain patterns, and orb spawning mechanics. Frames with high-cost abilities like Limbo's Stasis or Octavia's Resonator benefit enormously, as the increased orb conversion synergizes perfectly with resource consumption patterns. Equilibrium transforms energy sustain from a careful management problem into a largely automatic process, allowing frames to focus on ability usage rather than resource micromanagement. Paired with Desecrate on Nekros or Pilfering Swarm on Khora, Equilibrium becomes the foundation of resource farming and endurance mission viability.

## Acquisition

Equilibrium drops as a rare reward from Void Fissure Exterminate missions opened with Void Relics. Tenno must farm void relics and crack them in fissures to obtain the mod. Void Relic fissures are accessible from approximately mid-star chart onward, making Equilibrium a mid-to-late-game acquisition that dramatically improves mission viability once obtained.

## Builds that use it

Equilibrium appears in almost every long-endurance build for energy-hungry frames. Limbo, Octavia, and Harrow commonly use Equilibrium as a lynchpin of their sustain strategy, often pairing it with Quick Thinking for maximum safety. Energy-hungry builds on any frame benefit immensely, particularly in endless missions like Void Fissure: Survival and Void Cascade where orb generation is high and energy scarcity is the primary limiting factor for extended runs. Farming builds on Nekros with Desecrate also use Equilibrium to sustain energy for continuous ability casting.

## Notes

Equilibrium requires a working orb generation system to be effective. Frames or playstyles that do not spawn many health or energy orbs through vacuum or natural enemies gain less benefit. Conversely, frames with orb-spawning abilities (Nekros's Desecrate, Khora's pilfering mods, or companions with Synth Deconstruction) unlock Equilibrium's full potential by creating consistent orb streams throughout the mission. In void fissures with high enemy density, orb spawning is nearly constant, maximizing Equilibrium's effectiveness.

At rank 5, 100% conversion means every energy orb picked up becomes a health orb equivalent and vice versa, enabling perfect energy management for builds that generate enough orbs through combat or farming. This makes Equilibrium the primary sustain tool for frames designed around ability spam in endless content, transforming what would be a resource-management nightmare into an effortless process.
