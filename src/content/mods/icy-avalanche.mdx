## Overview

Icy Avalanche is a Warframe augment mod for Frost's Avalanche ability. When Avalanche is cast, all allied Warframes within Affinity Range receive an Overguard coating that scales with the number of enemies hit. At rank 3, each enemy struck by Avalanche grants 60 Overguard to nearby allies, plus an additional 20% of Frost's total Armor as further Overguard per enemy. The result scales directly with both enemy density and Frost's armor investment.

Overguard acts as an additional layer of protection that absorbs damage before health and shields, and it cannot be bypassed by status effects that normally ignore shields. In densely packed missions with high Frost armor builds, Icy Avalanche can apply several thousand Overguard points to the entire squad, providing meaningful survivability uplift across Steel Path content.

## Acquisition

Icy Avalanche is available through the Arbiters of Hexis syndicate offering. Players with sufficient Arbiters of Hexis standing can purchase it directly. The mod is tradeable through in-game trade and warframe.market.

## Builds that use it

Icy Avalanche is a strong choice for Frost in squad defense or survival missions where consistent Avalanche casting is part of the rotation. Pair with maximum Steel Fiber and Umbral Fiber to increase Frost's base armor, which directly raises the Overguard value granted per enemy. High Power Range ensures Avalanche catches as many enemies as possible, maximizing the total Overguard applied per cast.

The mod rewards proactive Avalanche timing when enemy groups converge, granting peak Overguard just before waves reach defensive objectives or squad members. It is most effective in organized squads where teammates stay within Affinity Range to benefit from each cast.

## Notes

- Overguard granted by Icy Avalanche does not scale with Power Strength; only Frost's armor total and enemy count matter.
- Affinity Range is typically the squad's shared affinity radius; Warframes outside this range do not receive the Overguard.
- Overguard decays between casts; Avalanche must be recast when the previous Overguard expires.
- Arbiters of Hexis and Cephalon Suda are allied syndicates; either provides access to the offering.
