## Overview

Fracturing Crush is a Warframe augment mod for Mag's Crush ability. When equipped, Crush gains a 50% increase to casting speed, and surviving enemies have their armor permanently reduced by up to 75% at max rank while being immobilized for up to 7 seconds. The armor strip is a flat percentage reduction applied directly, making it highly effective against heavily armored Grineer and Corrupted units regardless of their base armor value.

The mod is particularly valuable on Mag in Steel Path content, where armor scaling makes standard damage builds ineffective. Pairing Crush's armor strip with a Corrosive or Viral damage primary amplifies the value considerably. The immobilization effect also provides crowd control, keeping stripped enemies in place for follow-up attacks.

## Stats per rank

Fracturing Crush scales from rank 0 to rank 3. The casting speed bonus is fixed at 50% across all ranks. Armor strip and immobilization duration increase with each rank.

## Acquisition

Fracturing Crush is available through the Steel Meridian syndicate offering. It can also be traded between players via the in-game trade chat or warframe.market. The mod requires reaching a sufficient standing tier with Steel Meridian to purchase it directly.

## Builds that use it

Fracturing Crush appears in armor-strip-focused Mag builds for Steel Path survival and defense missions. It is most commonly slotted alongside Power Strength mods such as Umbral Intensify and Blind Rage to maximize range for Crush. Some builds replace heavy range investment with Fracturing Crush's immobilization to compensate for shorter reach.

Status-based squad builds bring Fracturing Crush so that Crush's armor strip enables teammates with slash or toxin procs to deal full damage without needing Corrosive themselves. The fixed 50% casting speed bonus makes Crush safe to use even in tight corridors.

## Notes

- The armor reduction stacks with Corrosive status and Corrosive Projection aura but does not exceed 100% total reduction.
- Immobilization prevents enemies from moving but does not stop them from attacking or using ranged abilities.
- The casting speed bonus applies to the full Crush animation, not just the initial windup.
- Steel Meridian alignment is required to purchase; New Loka and Arbiters of Hexis are opposing syndicates.
