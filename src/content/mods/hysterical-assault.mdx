## Overview

Hysterical Assault is a Warframe augment mod for Valkyr's Hysteria ability. While Hysteria is active, aiming and then attacking causes Valkyr to lunge across the battlefield toward a targeted enemy, covering up to 50 meters at max rank. The lunge replaces Valkyr's normal melee attack input, giving her a mobile gap-closer that functions throughout the Hysteria duration.

Without this mod, Hysteria locks Valkyr into close-range melee combat. Hysterical Assault gives her the ability to chase down fleeing enemies, cross open terrain without exiting the invulnerability window, and reposition instantly. This makes Valkyr effective in open tileset missions such as Orb Vallis and Plains of Eidolon where enemies spread across large distances.

## Acquisition

Hysterical Assault is available through the Steel Meridian syndicate offering. Players with sufficient Steel Meridian standing can purchase it directly. The mod is also tradeable via in-game trade and warframe.market.

## Builds that use it

Hysterical Assault suits Valkyr builds designed for open-world missions or endless modes where enemy density is spread out. Standard Hysteria builds focus on melee damage through Berserker Fury and condition overload stacks; Hysterical Assault adds mobility without changing the damage formula.

Builds that run Primed Continuity to extend Hysteria duration benefit most from Hysterical Assault since the lunge cost in energy remains constant while the active window grows. Pair with Power Strength to maximize Hysteria's base damage output for each lunge strike.

## Notes

- The lunge consumes no additional energy beyond Hysteria's standard drain.
- Enemy targeting for the lunge follows the aim reticle; precision targeting is easier with aim glide active.
- The lunge does not extend beyond the stated range; enemies beyond 50m at max rank cannot be targeted.
- Hysterical Assault does not function outside of Hysteria.
