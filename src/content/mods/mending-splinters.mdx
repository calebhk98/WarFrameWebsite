## Overview

Mending Splinters is a rare warframe augment mod for Gara's Splinter Storm ability. Without the augment, Splinter Storm coats Gara and nearby allies in a glass shell that absorbs and reflects damage. With Mending Splinters equipped, each additional target covered by Splinter Storm provides a health regeneration bonus to all affected targets. At rank 0, each covered target grants 10 health per second; at rank 3, this increases to 15 health per second per covered target.

This turns Gara into a support frame capable of providing both damage mitigation and active healing simultaneously. In a four-player squad, if Splinter Storm is spread to all three allies, the total regeneration for each covered player reaches 60 health per second at rank 3, which is substantial against the damage sources found in mid-tier content. The augment is particularly effective in Defense and Survival missions where Gara can maintain Splinter Storm coverage across an entire stationary squad. Mending Splinters does not replace dedicated healing from frames like Trinity or Wisp but provides meaningful passive recovery that reduces the dependency on energy orbs and life-steal mechanics.

## Acquisition

Mending Splinters is offered by two syndicates. Arbiters of Hexis members at the Maxim rank and New Loka members at the Flawless rank can both purchase the mod for standing. These syndicates are opposed, so a player aligned with one can trade with players of the other to access the mod without changing allegiance. Player-to-player trading through the in-game system or warframe.market is the primary route for those not aligned with either syndicate.

## Builds that use it

Mending Splinters fits into Gara support builds in squad content. Range is the most important stat to maximize -- Stretch, Augur Reach, and Overextended increase the radius of Splinter Storm's spread, making it easier to keep multiple allies covered. Ability strength affects the glass shell's damage absorption but does not scale Mending Splinters' health regeneration value, which scales only with mod rank. Duration mods such as Primed Continuity extend the window in which Splinter Storm is active without requiring recast, reducing ability energy pressure. Arcane Energize or Zenurik focus school supports energy sustain, allowing Gara to maintain Splinter Storm coverage consistently. Avoid duration reductions from Overextended by compensating with Constitution or Primed Continuity.

## Notes

The health regeneration from Mending Splinters applies to each covered target independently. Spreading Splinter Storm to three allies generates three separate heal ticks per second for everyone in range. The regeneration rate does not scale with ability strength, only with mod rank. At rank 3, the 15 HP/s per covered target is a fixed value regardless of strength modifiers. Gara herself counts as a covered target, so the total regeneration pool includes her own coverage contribution.
