## Overview

Power Drift is an exilus mod that balances ability strength scaling with crowd-control resistance, fitting the exilus mod slot without competing with traditional drain-consuming mods. The dual functionality makes Power Drift valuable on almost any warframe build, providing both offensive and defensive benefits in a single mod slot. At rank 5, Power Drift provides 15% ability strength and 30% knockdown resistance, making it one of the most efficient utility strength options available.

## Stats per rank

Power Drift scales from minimal 2.5% strength and 5% knockdown resistance at rank 0 to 15% strength and 30% knockdown resistance at maximum rank.

## Acquisition

Power Drift is a rare mod obtained through mid-game star chart progression and enemy drops. Players typically encounter it on Jupiter, Saturn, and later planets. The mod is also available through trading once players reach sufficient mastery rank thresholds.

## Usage

Power Drift is one of the few exilus mods worth ranking to rank 5, providing meaningful strength scaling and crowd-control mitigation. Equip it on any warframe where additional ability strength helps mission efficiency without needing to sacrifice other essential mods. The knockdown resistance secondary effect provides utility that becomes increasingly valuable in high-level missions where enemy knockdown effects accumulate rapidly.

Power Drift works exceptionally well on frames that already use exilus slots for utility effects, creating builds that gain both offensive and defensive improvements. The mod scales poorly compared to Intensify alone, but the exilus slot limitation makes Power Drift an excellent slot-filler that provides consistent value across all playstyles.

## Common builds

Power Drift is a staple exilus choice on most endgame warframe builds. Damage-focused caster frames use Power Drift for additional ability scaling, while tankier frames use it for both strength and crowd-control mitigation. The mod appears on builds ranging from solo-oriented to squad-support configurations, providing consistent utility across different mission types.
