## Overview

Umbral Vitality is a legendary warframe mod that increases health and grants Tau Resistance, part of the Umbra mod set introduced in Update 23.0 with The Sacrifice. This non-tradable, non-transmutable mod synergizes with other Umbra mods to provide set bonuses, making it essential for endgame warframe builds that require durability.

## Acquisition

Umbral Vitality is obtained exclusively as a quest reward upon completing The Sacrifice. Because it is non-tradable and non-transmutable, it cannot be acquired through relics, void fissures, or player trading. All players receive exactly one copy after finishing the quest.

## Builds that use it

Umbral Vitality appears in almost every defensive warframe build at endgame. Tanky frames like Rhino, Nezha, Inaros, and Wukong frequently pair it with Umbral Fiber and Umbral Intensify to maximize the Umbra set bonus. Steel Path survival and endless mission builds especially benefit from the raw health multiplier to offset scaling enemy damage.

## Notes

Umbral mods form a set with Umbral Fiber and Umbral Intensify. When two mods from the set are equipped, the equipped mods each gain a 30 percent power multiplier. With all three equipped, the bonus increases to 80 percent per mod, making the set one of the strongest mod combinations available. The Tau Resistance component helps against Sentient enemies and certain environmental hazards, providing utility beyond raw health scaling.
