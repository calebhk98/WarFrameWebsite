## Overview

Primed Flow is a legendary warframe mod that increases maximum energy capacity by up to 185% at rank 10, making it the most efficient energy-scaling mod in the game. Energy is the resource required to cast most warframe abilities, so Primed Flow extends the number of consecutive ability casts before energy restoration becomes necessary. For endless missions and endgame content, maximizing energy capacity ensures sustained offensive or defensive capability.

Primed Flow's 4-point drain is minimal for its benefit, and its naramon polarity complements many energy-efficient mods and exilus slots. Unlike its unprimed counterpart (Flow, which caps at 150%), Primed Flow's 185% cap provides an additional 35% bonus, making it the preferred version for virtually all builds. This mod is particularly critical on warframes with high-cost abilities (Mag, Saryn, Inaros) or on support frames that cast frequently (Protea, Khora, Trinity).

## Acquisition

Primed Flow is a relic drop exclusively from void fissure missions opened with void relics. As a legendary rarity mod, it appears in a limited selection of relic drop tables. Farming Primed Flow requires identifying which relics contain it and refining them to Flawless or Radiant quality to improve drop chances. The relic availability is limited, making farming for this mod exceptionally time-intensive. Most players obtain Primed Flow through trading with other players who have farmed the relics, often at premium prices due to its universal utility and rarity.

## Builds that use it

Primed Flow is a staple in virtually every warframe build that relies on ability casting. Energy-hungry frames like Mag Prime, Saryn Prime, and Octavia necessitate Primed Flow for consistent gameplay. Support frames like Trinity and Oberon use it to maintain squad buffs without downtime. Even frames with modest ability costs benefit from Primed Flow's energy pool to extend survivability during long runs.

The mod synergizes with energy regeneration systems: Arcane Energize triggers more frequently with larger energy pools, allowing quick recovery after high-cost ability sequences. Helminth abilities and subsumed power dynamics also scale well with higher energy reserves, giving Primed Flow strategic value beyond pure stat maximization.

## Notes

Energy restoration mechanics interact differently with energy capacity. Gaining percentage-based energy refunds (from abilities or arcanes) scales with current maximum energy, so Primed Flow indirectly boosts the value of energy-restoration effects. Certain warframes like Harrow or Mesa benefit exponentially from the larger pool when their overcharge mechanics are active.

Primed Flow's naramon polarity is valuable for builds that fill exilus or activity-specific polarity requirements. Its low drain leaves ample room for other critical mods, allowing builders to achieve both energy sustainability and other tactical objectives without excessive capacity trade-offs.
