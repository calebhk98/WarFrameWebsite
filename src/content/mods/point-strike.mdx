## Overview

Point Strike is a universal rifle critical chance mod that dramatically increases the likelihood of landing critical hits. This mod is foundational to crit-based damage builds, where each critical hit applies a multiplier effect (determined by the weapon's critical multiplier). For weapons with high base critical chance or critical multiplier, Point Strike unlocks exponential damage scaling.

The mod's effectiveness scales directly with weapon critical multiplier. A weapon with 1.5x critical multiplier sees marginal gains; a weapon with 3.0x critical multiplier transforms into a critical-stacking engine. Combined with Vital Sense and other crit mods, Point Strike enables burst-damage and headshot-focused playstyles.

## Acquisition

Point Strike is a common drop from enemy units across all factions. New players acquire their first copy within the first few missions, and additional ranks are simple to farm by repeating any mid-level mission. It is also purchasable from the Market for 50,000 credits.

## Builds that use it

Point Strike appears in virtually every critical-focused rifle build. High-crit-multiplier weapons like Soma Prime, Tiberon Prime, and Fulmin benefit immensely. Sniper-focused builds (Vectis Prime headshots, Lanka burst) rely on Point Strike as a core component of their damage equation. Hybrid critical-status builds also include Point Strike to leverage both scaling paths.

## Notes

Point Strike is strictly multiplicative with base critical chance. A weapon with 16% base crit chance gains 16 + (16 * 1.65) = 42.4% crit chance at rank 10. This compounding effect is why crit-focused weapons scale so dramatically with Point Strike.

Weapons with very low base critical chance (under 10%) see diminishing returns from Point Strike, as the final crit chance may cap below practical thresholds. Conversely, weapons designed around critical hits (like the Lanka or Dread) transform into viable endgame primaries with Point Strike and Vital Sense.
