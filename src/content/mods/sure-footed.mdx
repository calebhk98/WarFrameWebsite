## Overview

Sure Footed is a warframe utility mod providing a probabilistic knockdown resistance that helps frames maintain control during chaotic combat encounters. At rank 3, the 60% knockdown resistance significantly reduces interruptions to mobility and ability casting, allowing frames to reposition quickly without being thrown by explosive attacks. The 6-point drain is substantial for a utility mod, but the reliability provided justifies the investment in high-damage, fast-paced content.

Knockdown mechanics interrupt ability casts, deplete momentum-based mobility, and force repositioning, all of which hurt damage output and survivability. Sure Footed mitigates this friction, enabling uninterrupted ability rotations and maintaining aggressive positioning against knockdown-heavy enemies (Grineer with explosives, Sentients). Frames without native knockdown immunity rely on Sure Footed to maintain offensive tempo.

Sure Footed trades ability-scaling mods for utility, creating a choice point for builds. Frames with limited ability slots or frames relying heavily on defensive abilities prioritize Sure Footed, while glass-cannon builds skip it in favor of offensive scaling. High-level endurance content weighs the scale toward Sure Footed, as knockdowns become increasingly disruptive.

## Acquisition

Sure Footed drops rarely from high-level Grineer enemy units, particularly in end-game star chart missions (Neptune, Pluto, Void). Dedicated farming of these nodes yields Sure Footed within a few hours. Public void fissure squads on higher tiers provide an alternative grinding path.

## Builds that use it

Sure Footed appears primarily in endurance and defensive builds, particularly on frames with slow casting or mobility-based survival (Mesa, Rhino, Mag). Frames with innate knockdown immunity or rapid repositioning abilities (Wukong, Nova) often skip Sure Footed. Melee-focused frames benefit significantly, as knockdowns interrupt combos and weapon-swap timing.

## Notes

Sure Footed is probabilistic, meaning a 60% chance to resist still allows knockdowns to occur. In extended endurance missions, expect knockdowns despite the mod - it's mitigation, not immunity. Frames with hard crowd control (Limbo's freeze, Nyx's mind control) pair well with Sure Footed, as control abilities prevent enemy attacks entirely, reducing knockdown encounters.
