## Overview

Steel Charge is one of the oldest and most widely used aura mods in Warframe, providing a squad-wide boost to melee damage. As an aura with negative drain (adds 4 capacity when equipped), it effectively enables stronger builds while supporting melee-focused gameplay. The mod is essential for melee weapons and melee-oriented warframes, and its universal squad scaling makes it valuable in any mission type.

## Stats per rank

The mod scales linearly from rank 0 to rank 5, with each rank adding 10 percentage points of melee damage bonus to the entire squad.

| Rank | Drain | Melee damage bonus |
|------|-------|--------------------|
| 0    | -4    | +10%               |
| 1    | -4    | +20%               |
| 2    | -4    | +30%               |
| 3    | -4    | +40%               |
| 4    | -4    | +50%               |
| 5    | -4    | +60%               |

## Acquisition

Steel Charge drops as a common aura mod from enemy forces across all factions. It can be acquired early in the game through general enemy combat on Earth and Mars. The mod is also available through syndicate vendors and trading on the market. Due to its fundamental importance, many players equip Steel Charge on their first melee weapon once they acquire it.

## Usage

Steel Charge benefits any squad running melee-focused builds. The +60% melee damage bonus at rank 5 makes the difference between outdated and current-end-game melee performance. Players prioritize this mod for high-level missions, steel path endgame content, and dedicated melee loadouts. The aura is particularly effective with weapons that have high base damage, high critical multipliers, or high status chances where additional damage scaling stacks multiplicatively.

Combine Steel Charge with melee damage mods like Primed Pressure Point, Blood Rush, and Condition Overload for exponential scaling. In squad play, coordinate with teammates who bring complementary auras—Energy Siphon provides energy sustain while Steel Charge provides damage, ensuring both offense and sustainability.

## Common builds

Steel Charge is the standard aura choice for most melee builds. Typical setups pair it with power strength and efficiency mods for ability-focused frames, or pure damage and critical-chance scaling on pure-damage frames like Reaper Prime or Vastilok builds.
