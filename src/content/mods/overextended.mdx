## Overview

Overextended is a corrupted warframe mod that maximizes ability range at the cost of ability strength. At rank 5, it grants 90% ability range while reducing strength by 60%, making it the highest range-scaling mod in the game. Overextended defines range-focused builds, enabling zone-control and area-of-effect abilities to cover massive areas while accepting reduced individual ability damage.

Overextended is essential for frames built around crowd control and area denial. Limbo uses Overextended to banish entire rooms. Loki extends decoy range for team support. Hydroid and Khora benefit from extended loot radius on their looting abilities. Grendel achieves massive swallow radius for energy economy. The trade-off between range and strength is acceptable on these frames because their role relies on coverage over damage per hit.

## Acquisition

Overextended drops exclusively from void fissure missions on the Orokin Derelict, matching the acquisition path of other corrupted mods. Its 4.17% drop rate from the Derelict Vault makes farming moderately difficult. Like all corrupted mods, Overextended auto-ranks to maximum upon acquisition. Most players obtain it through trading rather than repeatedly farming the derelict void fissures.

## Builds that use it

Overextended dominates range-focused builds. Limbo stacks it with Stretch and augur mods to banish entire tilesets. Loki uses it to deploy decoys far from allies for area denial. Titania extends her flight range and hover zones. Grendel gains massive swallow range to consume enemies from unexpected distances. Garuda benefits from extended bleed aura range.

The mod synergizes poorly with strength-scaling damage frames: using Overextended on Equinox or Saryn sacrifices their damage scaling for marginal range benefits that don't enhance their roleplay. However, frames with proc-based or crowd-control-based abilities leverage the range bonus to affect more enemies per cast, making the strength penalty acceptable or even beneficial.

## Notes

Overextended is notable for having only 5 ranks instead of the typical 10, limiting its fusion requirement. This makes it slightly more accessible than 10-rank corrupted mods, though still challenging to farm. The vazarin polarity matches many defensive and support mods, fitting naturally into crowd-control and utility builds.

Range scaling interacts differently across ability types. Banish has a fixed radius that doesn't scale (making Overextended's benefit less impactful), while Loki's Decoy deploy distance scales directly with range mods. Understanding which abilities benefit from range is critical to effective Overextended builds, as equipping it on range-irrelevant abilities wastes capacity for minimal benefit.
