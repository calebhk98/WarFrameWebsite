## Overview

Hall Of Malevolence is a Warframe augment mod for Mirage's Hall of Mirrors ability. Each enemy kill increases the damage dealt by Mirage's doppelganger copies by a fixed percentage per rank, with the bonus accumulating throughout the mission without a cap. At max rank, every kill grants 5% additional doppelganger damage, making the mod exponentially more effective as a mission progresses and enemy count climbs.

The mod transforms Hall of Mirrors from a static damage multiplier into a snowballing power source. In long survival or defense missions, Mirage with Hall Of Malevolence can reach doppelganger damage percentages several hundred percent above base, compounding with her native ability strength. This synergy makes Mirage one of the few frames whose damage output naturally increases the longer a mission lasts.

The bonus resets when Hall of Mirrors is deactivated or expires.

## Acquisition

Hall Of Malevolence is available through the Red Veil syndicate offering. Players aligned with Red Veil can purchase it once the required standing tier is reached. It is also tradeable between players through in-game trade chat or warframe.market.

## Builds that use it

Hall Of Malevolence is a staple in Mirage endurance builds for Steel Path survival and defense. Typical builds pair it with maximum Power Strength to increase the base doppelganger damage multiplier, with Hall Of Malevolence compounding on top. Duration mods such as Primed Continuity keep the ability active for full mission segments.

Pair Mirage with Inaros, Trinity, or another energy sustain frame in squad play to maintain Hall of Mirrors continuously. The mod is less useful in short exterminate missions where kill count does not have time to build; reserve it for endless mission formats.

## Notes

- Doppelganger damage bonus stacks additively with base damage multipliers from ability strength mods.
- The stack resets if Hall of Mirrors drops, so energy management is critical to maintain momentum.
- Red Veil and New Loka are allied syndicates; either can provide access to the offering at high standing.
- The mod does not affect Mirage's personal damage, only the doppelganger copies.
