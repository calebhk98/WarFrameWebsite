## Overview

Lethal Torrent is the multishot-and-fire-rate augment for secondary weapons, combining two of the most valuable damage scaling mechanics into a single mod. This mod transforms pistol DPS from modest to competitive, enabling secondary weapons to function as primary damage sources in coordinated builds. The dual scaling (fire rate and multishot) multiplicatively increases damage output, making Lethal Torrent one of the most impactful secondary mods.

The mod's value scales with pistol base fire rate and magazine capacity. Weapons already designed for high fire rate (Plinx, Gaze, Twin Vipers) unlock Lethal Torrent's full potential. However, even low-fire-rate secondaries (Dex Furis, Single-target pistols) benefit from the multishot scaling, making Lethal Torrent universally applicable.

## Acquisition

Lethal Torrent is an uncommon drop from all enemy units. New players acquire their first copy within the first few missions, and multiple ranks are straightforward to farm. It is available from the Market for a modest credit cost.

## Builds that use it

Lethal Torrent appears in virtually every secondary weapon build, especially on high-fire-rate pistols. Pistol-focused frames (Mesa) make Lethal Torrent mandatory for weapon scaling. Hybrid builds that leverage secondary weapons as switching damage sources also include Lethal Torrent to maximize DPS during secondary rotations.

## Notes

Lethal Torrent's dual scaling (fire rate + multishot) creates multiplicative damage growth. A 100% fire rate increase combined with 100% multishot is not additive (200%); it is multiplicative (2.0x fire rate * 2.0x shots per magazine = 4.0x total projectiles). This exponential scaling is why Lethal Torrent is so impactful.

At rank 5, 120% fire rate and 120% multishot create a 2.2x fire rate multiplier and 2.2x projectile count per magazine. On a pistol with 600 RPM and 40-round magazine capacity, this transforms the effective DPS from 400 damage/sec to over 1900 damage/sec (ignoring damage-per-shot). This swing is why Lethal Torrent is mandatory for all secondary-focused builds.
