## Overview

Split Chamber is the endgame multishot mod for primary rifles, providing up to 150% multishot scaling at rank 10. The extended multishot enables weapons to fire multiple projectiles per shot, multiplicatively increasing damage output and status-proc spread. Split Chamber is considered nearly mandatory on all rifle builds, particularly on weapons with high base damage where additional pellets scale multiplicatively.

## Stats per rank

Split Chamber scales from 15% multishot at rank 0 to 150% at maximum rank 10, enabling weapons to fire up to 2.5x their normal projectile count per shot.

## Acquisition

Split Chamber is a rare drop from star chart missions, particularly in high-level content on Saturn, Uranus, Neptune, and Pluto. The mod is also available through relics and marketplace trading. Players typically obtain Split Chamber through endgame relics or by farming late-star-chart missions.

## Usage

Split Chamber is near-mandatory on all endgame rifle builds, enabling weapons to maximize damage output through multishot scaling. The 150% multishot at rank 10 causes single-shot weapons to fire 2-3 projectiles per trigger, multiplicatively increasing total damage. Combined with critical chance mods and elemental scaling, Split Chamber enables high-tier primary weapon builds that match ability-focused damage output.

On status-focused rifles like Kuva Zarr or Prisma Gorgon, Split Chamber enables rapid status-effect spreading that procs multiple conditions per shot. In survival or defense missions, Split Chamber combined with high-fire-rate weapons enables crowd control through status effects while maintaining high personal damage. The high drain cost is justified by the multiplicative damage increase it provides.

## Common builds

Split Chamber appears on all endgame rifle builds. Weapons like Telos Akimbo, Kuva Zarr, Prisma Gorgon, and Soma Prime commonly use Split Chamber as the primary multishot mod. The mod is essential for weapons designed to spread status effects across multiple enemies simultaneously.
