## Overview

Tribute/Power Donation is an augment mod for Titania's Tribute ability, transforming self-focused damage buffs into squad-wide energy support. At rank 3, Titania grants nearby allies 30% energy restoration per use, enabling energy-hungry team members to maintain ability spam. The augment converts Titania from a solo-scaling frame into a support caster, shifting her role dramatically toward group gameplay.

Tribute Power Donation enables cooperative Titania gameplay where energy support becomes the primary contribution. In squads with high-drain abilities (Saryn's Miasma, Mag's Crush spam), Titania's energy donation dramatically extends mission viability. The augment is particularly powerful in organized squads where build coordination leverages energy support most effectively.

The augment requires Titania ownership and Tribute leveling, limiting accessibility to Titania mains. However, for squad-oriented players, Tribute Power Donation transforms Titania into a valuable support frame. The 6-point drain is acceptable for augments, and the energy support utility makes it nearly mandatory for group missions with energy-dependent frames.

## Acquisition

Tribute Power Donation is available from syndicate vendors at Exalted Rank standing, requiring significant reputation grinding with a single syndicate. Trading with squad members or relying on chance void fissure drops provides faster acquisition paths.

## Builds that use it

Tribute Power Donation dominates support-oriented Titania builds, particularly in organized squads. Solo Titania builds skip Power Donation entirely, focusing on personal damage instead. Squads running energy-dependent frames appreciate Titania's support, making the augment valuable in endgame group content.

## Notes

Energy restoration affects all allies within a radius of Titania, creating squad-wide benefits. The energy granted is a percentage of each ally's max energy, scaling based on ability strength modifiers. Combining Titania's energy donation with other energy support sources (Trinity, Harrow) creates energy-abundant squad dynamics, enabling indefinite ability uptime across the entire team. Tribute Power Donation transforms Titania from a self-scaling offensive frame into an essential squad support member, particularly valuable in organized teams with energy-hungry ability users. The augment enables aggressive strategies that would normally deplete team energy pools, turning Titania into a resource enabler that multiplicatively improves squad damage output through sustained ability casting.
