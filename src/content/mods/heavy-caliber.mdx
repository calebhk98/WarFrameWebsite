## Overview

Heavy Caliber trades accuracy for massive raw damage, increasing rifle damage at the cost of weapon accuracy. The accuracy penalty spreads shot grouping, making it a tradeoff: spray weapons and large-magazine rifles absorb the accuracy loss through volume of fire, while precise weapons (snipers, semi-auto rifles) suffer greatly. Heavy Caliber is universally useful on close-range spray weapons like Ignis and area-denial weapons like Amprex.

The mod embodies a playstyle choice: close-range spray, area damage, or high fire rate builds can absorb accuracy loss easily, while precision and single-target weapons cannot. Understanding weapon engagement ranges and spray patterns is crucial to leveraging Heavy Caliber effectively.

## Acquisition

Heavy Caliber is a common drop from all enemy types. New players acquire their first copy early, and multiple ranks farm easily through repeated missions. It is also available from the Market for 50,000 credits.

## Builds that use it

Heavy Caliber is core to spray-weapon builds (Ignis Wraith, Amprex, Gaze) and area-denial primaries (Cedo, Quanta). Close-range primaries like the Kuva Chakkhurr benefit from the damage with minimal accuracy loss. However, precision weapons (Vectis Prime snipers, Lanka) intentionally skip Heavy Caliber because accuracy is load-bearing for their playstyle.

## Notes

The accuracy penalty's severity depends on weapon base accuracy and engagement range. Hitscan weapons at close range suffer minimal accuracy penalty because shots naturally spread over distance. Projectile weapons or very long-range builds are heavily penalized. Always test Heavy Caliber's impact on your intended engagement range before committing a mod slot.

At rank 10, 165% damage is massive, often the difference between one-shot and two-shot kills on high-armor enemies. The -165% accuracy penalty at long range creates a massive spread, but for close-range spray and area weapons, this is a worthwhile tradeoff for the damage scaling.
