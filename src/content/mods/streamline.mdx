## Overview

Streamline is a rare warframe mod that increases ability efficiency by up to 30% at maximum rank, reducing energy costs proportionally. Ability efficiency affects how much energy each ability consumes: higher efficiency means lower per-cast costs, allowing more consecutive casts before requiring energy restoration. Streamline is essential for high-cast-rate builds and for offsetting efficiency penalties from corrupted mods like Blind Rage.

Streamline is considered a near-mandatory mod for most builds due to energy economy's critical importance in endgame gameplay. Frames with high-cost abilities (Equinox, Volt, Inaros) require Streamline to maintain sustainable ability spam. Even frames with low base costs benefit from Streamline's efficiency boost, extending their ability window before needing energy restoration. Its minimal 4-point drain and naramon polarity make Streamline an economical choice for almost any build.

## Acquisition

Streamline is a common relic drop available from void fissure missions. As a rare-tier mod, it appears in most relic drop tables at reasonable frequencies. New players obtain Streamline relatively early through natural progression and void fissure farming. Its widespread availability makes Streamline accessible without excessive farming, and trading for it is economically viable even early in a player's progression.

## Builds that use it

Streamline is included in virtually every build that values energy sustainability. Equinox uses Streamline to manage high ability costs during night form offensive spam. Volt stacks it with other efficiency mods to maintain rapid overcharge cycles. Trinity and Oberon use Streamline to sustain squad support abilities without draining energy pools. Even frames with modest ability costs benefit from the efficiency gains, as reduced costs accumulate across entire missions.

The mod synergizes exceptionally with Blind Rage, offsetting the majority of its efficiency penalty. A Blind Rage build with Streamline reduces the net efficiency penalty from -55% to -25%, making high-strength builds sustainable. Frames that pair Blind Rage and Streamline achieve both extreme ability strength and acceptable energy economics, defining the meta for damage-focused endgame builds.

## Notes

Ability efficiency calculations are multiplicative: a +30% efficiency boost multiplies ability costs by 0.7x instead of subtracting a flat value. This means efficiency gains are more valuable on high-cost abilities and less impactful on low-cost abilities. For example, reducing a 100-energy ability to 70 energy is more valuable than reducing a 10-energy ability to 7 energy, since the savings enable more high-cost casts.

Streamline's 5-rank structure requires less fusion investment than 10-rank mods, making it budget-friendly for new modders. The naramon polarity matches many utility and efficiency-focused mods, fitting naturally into sustainability-focused builds without requiring forma investment.
