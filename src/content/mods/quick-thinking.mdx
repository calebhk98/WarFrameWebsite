## Overview

Quick Thinking is a survival mod that redirects health damage into energy drain, extending the window before instant death by converting incoming burst damage into a resource that can be restored through Equilibrium, Zenurik, or ability casting. When all energy is depleted, Quick Thinking grants a brief period of protection that prevents the next lethal blow, allowing time to recover, reposition, or take a revive. This mod is particularly valuable for energy-rich builds and frames that stack energy regeneration, as it transforms energy pools into supplementary health insurance.

The mod is controversial in high-level play: while it saves careless players from inevitable squad-wipes, relying on Quick Thinking often indicates poor resource management and positioning awareness. However, for sustained long endurance runs where burst damage is inevitable despite careful play, Quick Thinking is a critical safety valve. Energy-rich builds using Primed Flow combined with Equilibrium or Zenurik unlock Quick Thinking's full potential by ensuring the energy pool is large enough to absorb spike damage while maintaining mobility and ability spam capability.

## Acquisition

Quick Thinking drops from Void Relic fissures and is one of the rarer mods that requires dedicated void fissure farming or void mission running to acquire reliably. The mod's rarity reflects its power level and impact on endurance mission viability, making it a highly sought after addition to any deep-endurance build.

## Builds that use it

Quick Thinking appears most commonly in long-endurance builds (hour+ Void Fissure: Survival, Void Cascade) where survivability and energy management are balanced concerns. Frames with high energy pools (Primed Flow, Zenurik focus, energy-generating abilities) integrate Quick Thinking seamlessly into their setup. Limbo, Octavia, and Trinity builds often use Quick Thinking as a failsafe layer that bridges the gap between ideal resource management and the reality of unpredictable enemy damage spikes.

## Notes

Quick Thinking requires available energy to redirect damage. If a warframe has no energy pool, the mod is completely ineffective, making the protection timer the only benefit. Builds that generate energy through Equilibrium, Zenurik energy conversion, Harrow's Thurible, or ability-based regeneration unlock this mod's potential fully. Conversely, builds with low total energy pools see minimal benefit from damage redirection because the energy pool depletes too quickly.

At rank 5, 100% damage redirect means every health hit is converted entirely to energy drain. A burst of 500 damage that would kill you outright instead costs 500 energy and triggers the protection timer if energy depletes immediately. This swing is often the difference between mission success and failure in solo endurance runs, turning what should be a death into a brief vulnerability window requiring enemy crowd control or repositioning.
