## Overview

Despoil is an augment mod for Nekros's Desecrate ability, converting the energy cost into a health cost, enabling indefinite corpse desecration without energy management. At rank 3, only 10 health per corpse is required, making Despoil extraordinarily efficient for farming. Combined with Equilibrium, the health drain is trivial—each desecrated corpse yields more drops that replenish health through Equilibrium, creating a sustainable loop for endless farming.

Despoil fundamentally changes Nekros's playstyle from energy-constrained to health-constrained, which is a significant advantage. Energy regen sources (Zenurik, Harrow, Primed Flow) become irrelevant for Desecrate spam, freeing those mod slots for offensive or utility mods. In endless farming missions (Void Cascade, Abyss), Despoil enables continuous looting without ability disruption, increasing total drops per mission.

The augment requires Nekros ownership and Desecrate leveling, limiting accessibility to Nekros mains. However, for Nekros players, Despoil is nearly mandatory in farming builds, turning Nekros into a resource-gathering juggernaut. The 6-point drain is acceptable for augments, and the health cost's insignificance makes Despoil one of the most efficient augments in the game.

## Acquisition

Despoil drops from void fissure rewards in Steel Path void cascades, accessible after completing the main star chart and unlocking Steel Path endgame content. Running Steel Path void cascade squads at any difficulty tier yields Despoil within a few farming sessions.

## Builds that use it

Despoil is exclusively used on Nekros farming builds, where it enables continuous loot generation without energy constraints. Infinite-duration Desecrate builds stack Despoil with Equilibrium, Pilfering Swarm, and duration scaling for resource farming mastery. Non-farming Nekros builds often skip Despoil in favor of offensive augments.

## Notes

Despoil's health cost is trivial when paired with Equilibrium, creating a net-positive loop where desecration generates more resources than the health cost. This synergy makes Despoil one of the most powerful farming augments available. Frames without Equilibrium find Despoil less attractive, as the health cost becomes a real resource sink.
