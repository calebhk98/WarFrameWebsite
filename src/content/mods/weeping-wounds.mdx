## Overview

Weeping Wounds is an uncommon melee mod providing stacking status chance bonuses that scale with combo multiplier. At maximum rank with full combo, this mod can provide over 56 percent additional status chance, transforming low-status weapons into reliable proc engines. The stacking mechanic rewards sustained melee engagement, making combat flow and momentum direct mechanical advantages in damage output.

The combo scaling design encourages aggressive, continuous combat rather than hit-and-run tactics. Weeping Wounds transforms the melee combo counter from a minor damage boost into a critical component of status-focused build architecture. High-combo builds become exponentially more effective throughout fights as the modifier amplifies.

## Acquisition

Weeping Wounds is uncommon and drops from melee-based enemies across all factions and planets. Killing Heavy units, Broadsword-wielding enemies, or other melee-focused faction units yields reliable drops. Extended melee-focused farming sessions reliably accumulate multiple copies for ranking up.

Alternatively, players can purchase Weeping Wounds from Void Relic rewards or Zariman void cascade pools, though the uncommon rarity makes farming through combat faster. Syndicate rewards and other vendor sources also occasionally offer this mod, providing alternative acquisition routes for players avoiding combat farming.

## Usage

Weeping Wounds is core equipment for status-focused melee weapons lacking innate status chance. Pair this mod with elemental damage mods (Molten Impact, Shocking Touch, Freezing Slash) to rapidly apply status procs. The combo scaling means sustained engagements reward aggressive offense with exponentially increasing status application rates.

Weapons like the Destreza Prime or Lesion, which lack high base status chance, become incredibly effective status applicators when equipped with Weeping Wounds. Combine with Condition Overload for multiplicative damage scaling, transforming these weapons into endgame capable melee choices even in Steel Path content.

The mod's main weakness is its scaling requirement, making it perform poorly in burst-damage playstyles or against isolated high-level enemies. Weapons designed for sustained combat against groups benefit most from Weeping Wounds, as maintaining combo becomes second nature during typical mission profiles.

## Notes

Weeping Wounds has been a staple melee mod since its introduction, remaining relevant across all Warframe updates. The combo-scaling design creates intuitive gameplay feedback where sustained combat directly translates to increased damage and status application. This design remains elegant even as power creep affects the broader melee meta.

The madurai polarity is standard on melee weapons, simplifying build construction. The drain cost is manageable at 7, leaving room for Condition Overload and other critical melee mods. Efficient modding can fit both Weeping Wounds and Condition Overload on most weapons, creating the standard status melee meta.
