## Overview

Vigilante Fervor is a hybrid rifle mod providing both fire-rate and critical-chance scaling, enabling balanced damage builds that don't sacrifice speed for precision. At rank 5, the 30% fire-rate and 30% crit bonus create synergistic damage scaling that multiplies damage output through both bullet volume and individual-shot damage. The uncommon rarity and 4-point drain make Vigilante Fervor accessible and space-efficient.

Vigilante Fervor excels on weapons with moderate crit chance that benefit from both fire-rate and crit scaling. Burst-fire rifles with decent crit multipliers transform into critical-focused weapons capable of chaining headshots. Balanced weapons without specialized crit or fire-rate focus benefit most from dual-scaling, enabling viable all-rounder setups without build specialization.

Vigilante Fervor occupies a primary slot competing with pure-crit mods, pure-fire-rate mods, and damage mods. The balanced approach makes Vigilante Fervor ideal for new players or casual builds lacking build optimization. Endgame builds often specialize toward crit or fire-rate exclusively, but Vigilante Fervor enables viable hybrid strategies.

## Acquisition

Vigilante Fervor drops from void fissure missions as a relic reward, accessible once players unlock void fissure content. Running void fissure squads at any difficulty tier yields Vigilante Fervor within one farming session. The uncommon rarity ensures frequent drops.

## Builds that use it

Vigilante Fervor dominates balanced rifle builds, particularly for new players or casual content. Endgame builds often skip it in favor of specialized crit or fire-rate mods. Hybrid builds that don't commit to pure strategies benefit from Vigilante Fervor's balanced scaling.

## Notes

The dual-scaling nature makes Vigilante Fervor particularly powerful on weapons already possessing decent crit multipliers or fire rates. Weapons with naturally high crit chance scale crit bonuses better; high-ROF weapons scale fire-rate bonuses more. Regardless, the balanced approach enables viable gameplay across a wider weapon roster. Vigilante Fervor enables beginner builds that don't require deep optimization knowledge to be viable, creating accessible paths to endgame weapon scaling. The mod's universal applicability across weapon types makes it valuable for players rotating between weapon loadouts, enabling consistent DPS without constant reoptimization.
