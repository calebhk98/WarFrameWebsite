## Overview

Pilfering Strangledome is an augment mod for Khora's Strangledome ability, granting enemies killed inside the dome a chance to drop additional resources. At rank 3, the 35% chance to generate bonus drops makes Khora an exceptionally powerful farming frame. Combined with Pilfering Swarm and Despoil Nekros, Khora becomes a farming juggernaut, enabling resource-rich missions that yield double or triple normal loot.

Pilfering Strangledome transforms Khora from a damage/utility frame into a farming powerhouse, creating incentive to position Strangledome over high-value target groups. The augment is particularly powerful in endless missions where continuous kills trigger the chance repeatedly, generating exponentially more resources. Paired with Equilibrium, Khora sustains indefinite Strangledome uptime through life-drain mechanics.

The augment requires Khora ownership and Strangledome leveling, limiting accessibility to Khora mains. However, for Khora players, Pilfering Strangledome transforms farming efficiency dramatically. The 6-point drain is acceptable for augments, and the resource-generation potential makes it nearly mandatory for farming builds.

## Acquisition

Pilfering Strangledome drops from void fissure missions as a relic reward, accessible once players unlock void fissure content. Running void fissure squads at any difficulty tier yields Pilfering Strangledome within a few farming sessions. The rare rarity makes it moderately common in void fissure drops.

## Builds that use it

Pilfering Strangledome dominates Khora farming builds, where it multiplies resource generation rates. Non-farming Khora builds may skip Pilfering Strangledome in favor of offensive augments, but endgame farming builds universally include it. Khora's role as a farming frame is defined by Pilfering Strangledome's presence.

## Notes

The drop chance applies independently to each enemy killed in the dome, making high-kill-count missions exceptionally rewarding. In Void Cascade or Abyss missions with heavy enemy density, Pilfering Strangledome generates absurd quantities of resources. The augment scales multiplicatively with other drop-boost sources (Khora's passive, Equilibrium), creating exponential farming efficiency. Khora's passive grant increases drop chance further, turning Pilfering Strangledome into an essential component of her farming identity. The combination of Pilfering Strangledome and Pilfering Swarm creates one of the most efficient farming combinations available, enabling resource accumulation rates that dominate endgame farming strategies.
