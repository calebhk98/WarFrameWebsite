## Overview

Galvanized Aptitude is a rare primary weapon mod providing stacking damage bonuses that scale with the number of unique status types affecting enemies. The mod offers a base status chance increase alongside an on-kill damage bonus that grows with each distinct status type already applied to the target. At maximum rank, the mod grants +80% status chance and up to +40% direct damage per status type on kill, stacking up to two times. This dual effect rewards aggressive status application strategies where multiple status types are spread before engaging a target.

The damage scaling mechanic makes Galvanized Aptitude particularly valuable for status-focused builds where spreading elemental effects becomes increasingly effective as the mission progresses. Spreading multiple status types to a target before the killing blow maximizes the on-kill damage bonus, rewarding coordinated elemental modding and deliberate status setup.

## Acquisition

Galvanized Aptitude drops exclusively from Void Fissure void cascade rewards in the Zariman tileset. The mod appears in both Lith and Meso void cascade pools with drop rates around 4-5 percent. Players must run Void Fissure missions with void relics to access this reward.

Farming Galvanized Aptitude requires participation in Zariman void fissure relics repeatedly. The low drop rate necessitates patience, but the status scaling and damage combination makes it valuable for status spreader builds targeting endgame content.

## Builds that use it

Galvanized Aptitude excels in builds prioritizing status effect application. Pair this mod with elemental damage mods like Hellfire or Chilling Reload to spread status procs effectively. Weapons with naturally high fire rates benefit most, as rapid fire enables faster status accumulation. Shotguns and assault rifles pair well with Galvanized Aptitude.

The stacking mechanic means direct damage per status type stacks twice with on-kill procs, creating significant bonus damage when maintaining status coverage across an enemy group. Players eliminating enemies maintain high stacks, enabling sustained pressure in higher-level missions.

## Notes

Galvanized Aptitude's on-kill damage bonus stacks up to 2x, making it most effective in high-density missions where kills happen rapidly and the bonus has time to refresh. The vazarin polarity requirement is less common on primaries than madurai but remains compatible with most rifle builds. The low base drain of 2 makes it easy to slot without forma.
