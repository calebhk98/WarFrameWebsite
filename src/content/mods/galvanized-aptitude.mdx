## Overview

Galvanized Aptitude is a rare primary weapon mod offering stacking bonuses to accuracy and status chance, scaling with eliminations. This mod is tailored for status-focused builds where spreading elemental effects becomes increasingly effective as the mission progresses. The accuracy bonus helps maintain precision while the status scaling rewards players for maintaining offensive momentum.

The dual bonuses make this mod particularly valuable for weapons with innate accuracy problems or status-heavy builds. The stacking mechanic encourages extended engagements where maintaining high hit rates and status application accumulates significant bonuses throughout the mission.

## Acquisition

Galvanized Aptitude drops exclusively from Void Fissure void cascade rewards in the Zariman tileset. The mod appears in both Lith and Meso void cascade pools with drop rates around 4-5 percent. Players must run Void Fissure missions with void relics to access this reward.

Farming Galvanized Aptitude requires participation in Zariman void fissure relics repeatedly. The low drop rate necessitates patience, but the status scaling and accuracy combination makes it valuable for status spreader builds targeting endgame content.

## Usage

Galvanized Aptitude excels in builds prioritizing status effect application. Pair this mod with elemental damage mods like Hellfire or Chilling Reload to spread status procs effectively. The accuracy bonus ensures projectiles land consistently, making the status chance bonus more reliable.

Weapons with naturally high fire rates benefit most from this mod, as rapid fire enables faster status accumulation. Shotguns and assault rifles pair well with Galvanized Aptitude. Combine with mods like Infected Clip or High Voltage to maximize status proc frequency as stacks increase.

The stacking mechanic means status application becomes exponentially more effective as the mission progresses. Players eliminating enemies maintain high stacks, enabling rapid status spreading that trivializes armor strips and crowd control needs in higher-level missions.

## Notes

Galvanized Aptitude fills a unique niche for status-primary builds. The combination of accuracy and status chance is relatively rare on mods, making this especially valuable for weapons struggling with either stat baseline.

The vazarin polarity requirement is less common on primaries than madurai but remains compatible with most rifle and gun warframes. Consider matching polarities and using catalyst potatoes to maximize mod efficiency when building status-heavy primary loadouts.
