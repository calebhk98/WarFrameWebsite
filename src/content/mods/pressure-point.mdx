## Overview

Pressure Point is a common melee mod that increases damage output for melee weapons. As the melee equivalent to Serration and Hornet Strike, Pressure Point enables all swords, polearms, axes, scythes, and other hand weapons to scale into endgame content. Despite being classified as common rarity, it ranks among the most essential mods for melee builds and appears in virtually every melee loadout.

## Acquisition

Pressure Point drops from universal enemy loot pools during combat against any faction. It appears frequently in early-game drops, making it readily available for new players. The mod can also be found in void fissure missions and syndicate void cascade activities. Because it is common rarity and drops frequently, players acquire multiple copies naturally during weapon leveling.

## Builds that use it

Every melee build incorporates Pressure Point as a foundational damage multiplier. Weapons like the Nikana Prime, Gram Prime, Plague Kripath, and Zariman Glaive all rely on it. Melee builds combine Pressure Point with attack speed mods such as Fury or Primed Fury, critical mods like Blood Rush and Weeping Wounds, and elemental damage mods to achieve exponential scaling. The higher drain cost compared to Serration and Hornet Strike reflects melee weapons' tendency to fill more mod slots with utility mods.

## Notes

Pressure Point provides identical damage scaling to Serration and Hornet Strike despite higher drain cost. This reflects melee weapons' higher base attack speeds and the prevalence of melee-specific mods like combo multiplier and status mods that require additional slots. Primed Pressure Point offers improved scaling for endgame builds, though the standard version remains viable through all content when paired with Bloodhound mods and critical chance scaling. The Madurai polarity aligns with offensive and critical mods. Melee weapons benefit disproportionately from Pressure Point's damage multiplication because combo multipliers stack multiplicatively with the base damage bonus, creating exponential damage growth during sustained combat.
