## Overview

Vile Acceleration is a rifle damage-scaling mod providing 60% fire-rate boost at max rank, enabling rapid-fire builds that emphasize bullet-count over individual pellet damage. At rank 5, weapons transform from measured pacing to overwhelming suppressive fire, multiplying damage output through sheer volume. Vile Acceleration pairs synergistically with multishot sources (Vigilante Armaments, Split Chamber), creating exponential bullet counts.

Vile Acceleration excels on weapons with high magazine capacity and manageable recoil, enabling sustained fire through entire magazines without reload delays. Weapons like the Soma Prime, Glaxion, or Ignis benefit tremendously, as rapid fire converts their inherent strengths (mag capacity, beam coverage) into overwhelming DPS. Combined with status-focused builds, Vile Acceleration enables rapid status proc stacking, leveraging status chance into fast-kill potential.

Vile Acceleration occupies a primary-weapon slot competing with crit scaling, damage scaling, and elemental scaling. For rapid-fire weapons, the fire-rate boost is nearly mandatory; for slow, hard-hitting weapons, crit scaling outperforms fire-rate. The 4-point drain is acceptable for damage mods, fitting comfortably alongside other scaling sources.

## Acquisition

Vile Acceleration drops rarely from high-level Corpus units, particularly in end-game star chart missions. Dedicated farming of level 40-50+ Corpus nodes yields the mod within a few hours. Trading with players or void fissure luck provide faster acquisition.

## Builds that use it

Vile Acceleration dominates rapid-fire rifle builds, appearing in nearly every beam-rifle and high-ROF primary build. Slow, hard-hitting weapons skip Vile Acceleration in favor of crit mods. Status-overflow builds leverage Vile Acceleration for rapid proc stacking, enabling fast crowd control through burst status applications.

## Notes

Fire-rate scaling multiplies actual DPS beyond its percentage boost, as more shots per second enable faster magazine depletion and higher overall damage phases. Combining Vile Acceleration with magazine-capacity mods creates suppressive weapon archetypes capable of solo-carrying endurance missions through sheer bullet output. The mod's effectiveness scales with weapon ammo pools; weapons with large magazines transform into unstoppable bullet hoses, while weapons with small magazines benefit less from pure fire-rate without capacity expansion. Pairing Vile Acceleration with ammo-efficiency builds enables infinite magazine runs without reload interruptions, creating sustained damage phases impossible without the fire-rate scaling.
