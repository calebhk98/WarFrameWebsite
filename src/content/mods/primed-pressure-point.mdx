## Overview

Primed Pressure Point is the quintessential endgame melee damage mod, providing up to 165% melee damage scaling at rank 10. It is universally considered the most essential melee mod for all weapon types and builds, forming the foundation of every high-end melee loadout. The primed variant extends beyond the standard Pressure Point's rank 5 cap, enabling exponential damage scaling when combined with Blood Rush, Condition Overload, and other multiplicative damage mods.

## Stats per rank

Primed Pressure Point scales linearly from 15% melee damage at rank 0 to 165% at rank 10, providing the highest baseline melee damage multiplier available in a single mod slot.

## Acquisition

Primed Pressure Point is a legendary mod available through void relic farming and marketplace trading. The mod comes from dedicated void fissure runs farming specific relics or through direct trading. Its universal necessity makes it one of the highest-priority farming targets for players entering endgame content.

## Usage

Primed Pressure Point is mandatory on all endgame melee builds without exception. Equip it as the first melee mod in every build, before elemental scaling, before status mods, and before utility additions. The 165% damage scaling at rank 10 combines multiplicatively with Blood Rush (which scales crit damage), Condition Overload (which scales on status procs), and weapon-specific bonuses to create exponential damage output.

On high-crit weapons, Primed Pressure Point plus Blood Rush creates near-unlimited damage scaling. On status-focused weapons, Primed Pressure Point plus Condition Overload enables one-shot clear against steel path enemies. The mod is so fundamental that its presence or absence determines whether a melee build functions for endgame content. In squad play, Primed Pressure Point enables rapid enemy elimination that keeps pace with ability-focused warframe clear speeds.

## Common builds

Primed Pressure Point appears on 100% of endgame melee builds. All weapon types—swords, polearms, claws, whips, and glaives—prioritize Primed Pressure Point as their first and most critical mod slot. The mod is irreplaceable and has no viable alternatives at endgame difficulty levels.
