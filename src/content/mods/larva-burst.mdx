## Overview

Larva Burst is a Warframe augment mod for Nidus's Larva ability. By default, Larva pulls enemies into a central point and holds them there. With Larva Burst equipped, recasting Larva while it is active detonates the ball, dealing Toxin damage that scales with how many enemies were captured. At rank 3, the base explosion deals 600 Toxin damage with a stacking multiplier per enemy caught, within an 8-meter radius.

The stacking damage mechanic rewards holding enemies in the Larva ball for as long as possible before detonating. Large groups yield dramatically more damage than solitary targets. Toxin damage bypasses Corpus shields entirely, making Larva Burst particularly effective against shield-heavy Corpus enemies at any content level.

## Acquisition

Larva Burst is available through the Cephalon Suda syndicate offering. Players with sufficient Cephalon Suda standing can purchase it directly. The mod is tradeable through in-game trade and warframe.market.

## Builds that use it

Larva Burst works best in high-density missions such as Infested survival or Defense where Larva can consistently capture five or more enemies before detonation. Nidus builds pairing Larva Burst with high Range mods (Overextended, Stretch) maximize the initial capture radius, filling the Larva ball faster and increasing the per-detonation damage ceiling.

Ability Strength mods do not scale the burst damage directly; the base value and stacking formula are fixed. However, high-mutation Nidus builds that focus on sustained mutation stacks pair naturally with Larva Burst since both abilities encourage staying in combat and accumulating enemies continuously.

## Notes

- The detonation consumes the Larva; enemies are released immediately after the explosion.
- Toxin damage from the burst applies status procs, which can trigger poison stacks on surviving enemies.
- The explosion radius is affected by Power Range mods at rank 3, reaching up to 8m at 100% range.
- Cephalon Suda and Arbiters of Hexis are allied syndicates; either provides access to the offering.
