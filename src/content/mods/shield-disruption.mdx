## Overview

Shield Disruption provides passive crowd control against shielded enemies, particularly useful against Corpus-heavy missions and high-level grineer units with shield generators. The aura temporarily disables enemy shields on hit, allowing the squad to overwhelm hardened targets more efficiently. As a mod that does not consume aura capacity, Shield Disruption pairs well with any damage-focused loadout, especially in endurance runs against high-tier shield-dependent enemies.

Shield Disruption starts at a 25% trigger chance and scales to 66% at maximum rank, guaranteeing near-constant shield disruption against consistent fire.

## Acquisition

Shield Disruption drops as an uncommon aura mod from enemy forces. Players obtain it through star chart missions, particularly in Corpus-dominated planets like Venus and Jupiter. The mod becomes increasingly relevant in sortie missions and steel path content where shielded enemies require dedicated counter-strategies.

## Usage

Shield Disruption excels in anti-Corpus missions and against high-level shield-dependent enemies. Pair it with high-fire-rate weapons or rapid-hitting abilities to reliably trigger the disruption effect. The aura becomes essential in team compositions where consistent crowd control and shield control are needed. When combined with Corrosive Projection or Void Damage, Shield Disruption ensures enemies cannot recover shields mid-combat.

In endless missions, Shield Disruption prevents shield regeneration stalls where enemies would otherwise need secondary focus-fire to break through. The aura works particularly well with teammates using status-heavy weapons or abilities that can trigger the disruption proc reliably.

## Common builds

Shield Disruption is commonly equipped on tanky warframes running support or crowd-control roles. Frames like Rhino, Inaros, and Grendel often slot Shield Disruption to provide utility while maintaining high survivability. The mod complements status-effect builds that rely on consistent weapon hits to proc additional effects.
