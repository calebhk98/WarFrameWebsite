## Overview

Brief Respite converts energy expenditure into shield generation, creating a survivability-through-efficiency playstyle that rewards ability spam. The aura scales only when overshields are inactive, making it ideal for warframes without shield-gating mechanics or those building for pure tank strategies. Brief Respite is particularly valuable on shields-focused frames like Protea or Sevagoth, where shield gating is redundant and energy-to-shield conversion maximizes defensive output.

## Stats per rank

Brief Respite scales from 25% conversion at rank 0 to 50% at maximum rank, doubling defensive output compared to starting ranks.

## Acquisition

Brief Respite is an uncommon aura drop from general enemy combat. Players obtain it through standard star chart progression on Earth and later planets. The mod becomes increasingly valuable once players access frames with high ability-spam rotation patterns and need additional survivability without relying on armor or health scaling.

## Usage

Brief Respite shines on frames that constantly cast abilities, converting their energy expenditure into active defense. Pairing it with high-efficiency builds and energy regen mods creates a self-sustaining survival loop where shield generation keeps pace with damage intake. On frames like Protea, Sevagoth, or Caliban, Brief Respite enables aggressive, ability-forward playstyles without sacrificing survivability.

The aura works synergistically with shield-restoration mods like Adaptation and Kinetic Shear. In squad play, teammates with high ability uptime benefit most; passive-ability frames derive minimal value. Brief Respite becomes mandatory in some builds that intentionally avoid overshield mechanics to maximize the conversion effect.

## Common builds

Brief Respite is essential on pure-shield warframes and high-ability-spam builds. Protea, Sevagoth, and Caliban builds commonly use Brief Respite as the primary defensive aura. The mod is also useful on energy-efficient support frames like Trinity Prime or Hildryn, where shield conversion complements healing and support utilities.
