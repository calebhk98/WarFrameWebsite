## Overview

Primed Reach is the endgame melee range mod, providing up to 3 meters of additional range at rank 10. The extended reach from Primed Reach fundamentally changes melee weapon gameplay, enabling polearms and glaives to clear entire rooms without repositioning. Combined with area-of-effect abilities and weapons, Primed Reach creates infinite-range tactical advantages that define efficient melee playstyles in endgame content.

## Stats per rank

## Acquisition

Primed Reach is a legendary mod available through void relic farming and marketplace trading. The mod is highly sought after for melee-focused builds, making it a frequent target of farming campaigns and trading activity. Players obtain Primed Reach through dedicated relic farming or by trading with other players.

## Usage

Primed Reach is essential for polearm and glaive builds, transforming short-range melee weapons into long-range engagement platforms. Equip it on any melee weapon where range extends tactical options, particularly on area-of-effect or auto-spreading weapons like Zariman Glaive Prime or Stahlta. At rank 10, +3 meters of additional range enables engaging enemies across entire rooms without repositioning.

Combined with AoE abilities like Glaive throw or Tenet Agendus slam attacks, Primed Reach enables crowd control that keeps the squad safe at distance. On pure-damage weapons like polearms with high base damage, Primed Reach enables efficient clear speeds that match or exceed ability-based farming. In squad play, extended melee range enables supporting teammates while maintaining personal safety.

## Common builds

Primed Reach appears on all polearm, glaive, and extended-reach melee builds. Weapons like Zariman Glaive Prime, Plague Kripath polearms, and Hate Scythe commonly use Primed Reach as the primary range mod. The mod is particularly valuable on weapons with area-of-effect mechanics that benefit from extended range scaling.
