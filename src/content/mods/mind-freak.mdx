## Overview

Mind Freak is a rare warframe augment mod for Nyx's Mind Control ability. Mind Control normally takes over a single enemy and directs it to attack its former allies. With Mind Freak equipped, the controlled enemy's outgoing damage is massively multiplied, and the target also gains bonuses to movement speed and attack speed. At rank 3, the controlled enemy deals +1000% of its base damage and gains +25% movement and attack speed. The damage multiplier applies to the enemy's own attacks, effectively turning the target into a high-output turret fighting for the player's team.

The damage bonus scales only with mod rank, not with Nyx's ability strength. This makes Mind Freak viable even on low-strength Nyx builds, as the bonus is additive to the base value defined by the mod itself. The +25% movement and attack speed bonus is fixed regardless of mod rank and provides the controlled enemy with more frequent attack opportunities. On high-value targets such as Bombards, Napalms, or Nullifier Crewmen, the combination of extreme damage output and faster attacks makes Mind Control with Mind Freak a significant source of burst damage in mid-to-late content.

## Acquisition

Mind Freak is offered by two syndicates. Arbiters of Hexis members at the Maxim rank and New Loka members at the Flawless rank both offer the mod for standing. These syndicates are opposed, so players aligned with one can obtain the mod from the other only through player trading. Warframe.market and in-game trading are reliable alternative acquisition routes.

## Builds that use it

Mind Freak is paired with Nyx builds that use Mind Control as a primary damage dealer rather than purely as crowd control. Ability duration mods such as Primed Continuity and Constitution extend the control window, keeping the buffed enemy active longer per cast. Range mods are not critical for Mind Control itself, which targets a single enemy regardless of range. Ability strength does not affect the damage bonus from Mind Freak but does scale Absorb and Psychic Bolts, so mid-strength builds are common. Pairing Mind Freak with Assimilate (Nyx's Absorb augment) provides survivability while the controlled enemy deals damage.

## Notes

The damage bonus from Mind Freak is applied to the controlled enemy's own weapon and ability damage output. It does not affect the damage output of the Nyx player. Selecting high-damage enemy types such as Bombards or Sortie-tier units maximizes the return on the 1000% multiplier, as the base damage scales with enemy level. Against Corpus factions where high-damage units are less common, the same mechanic provides less burst value. The +25% speed boost is a fixed mod effect and is not influenced by ability strength.
