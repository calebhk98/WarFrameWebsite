## Overview

Stretch is an uncommon warframe mod that increases ability range by up to 82.5% at maximum rank. Unlike corrupted mods, Stretch provides range scaling without any drawback, making it the most economical range mod for most builds. Its low rarity, minimal energy drain of 4, and naramon polarity make Stretch accessible even to new players while remaining useful in endgame builds.

Stretch is considered a core mod for any frame that benefits from area-of-effect ability coverage. It pairs naturally with other range mods to achieve massive AoE overlap. Frames like Limbo, Hydroid, Khora, and Grendel prioritize Stretch as a mandatory slot because range directly improves their primary gameplay mechanics. Even frames where range is secondary (like Equinox or Saryn) often include Stretch for crowd control positioning.

## Acquisition

Stretch is available from standard mod sources: enemy drops, void relics, and mission rewards across various tilesets. As an uncommon rarity mod, it appears in most enemy loot pools and relic drop tables. New players obtain Stretch naturally through early-game progression, making it one of the first range mods available. Farming for Stretch is minimal because it drops frequently from common enemy types and void fissure missions.

## Builds that use it

Stretch is a staple in virtually every range-dependent warframe build. Limbo stacks Stretch with Overextended and Augur Reach to achieve room-wide banish coverage. Loki uses it to place decoys further away from active areas. Mirage extends her hall-of-mirrors projectile spread distance. Titania increases flight radius and hover zone coverage. Even non-range frames like Mag benefit from Stretch when used with crowd-control focused builds.

The mod synergizes exceptionally well with other range mods: stacking Stretch with Overextended, Augur Reach, or Power Strength mods creates overlapping bonuses that define meta range builds. Teams with multiple range-focused frames (Limbo, Grendel, Hydroid) multiply coverage by coordinating ability placement, making Stretch investment worthwhile for group content.

## Notes

Stretch's 10-rank structure requires significant fusion investment, though its uncommon rarity makes catalysts relatively affordable. The naramon polarity is common enough to fit most builds without polarization, though dedicated range builds often forma to accommodate all range mods in favorable polarities.

Range scaling is ability-specific: some abilities don't scale with range (Banish has fixed radius), while others scale directly (Discharge, Grendel's Swallow). Understanding which frame abilities benefit from range is critical to Stretch effectiveness, as equipping it on range-agnostic abilities wastes capacity that could improve other stats.
