## Overview

Galvanized Chamber is a rare primary weapon mod providing pure stacking damage bonuses that scale with eliminations. This mod represents a straightforward damage scaling solution, rewarding continuous engagement and kill momentum. The high drain cost is offset by the raw damage scaling potential, making it essential for endgame primary weapon builds targeting maximum sustained output.

The damage stacking mechanic ensures that weapons become progressively more effective throughout missions. Unlike fixed damage mods, Galvanized Chamber rewards player skill and engagement, making it particularly valuable for content requiring sustained firepower against high-level enemies.

## Acquisition

Galvanized Chamber drops exclusively from Void Fissure void cascade reward pools in the Zariman tileset. The mod appears in both Lith and Meso void cascade rotation pools with a drop rate between 4-5 percent. Players must participate in Void Fissure missions using void relics to access these rewards.

Farming Galvanized Chamber requires repeated Zariman void fissure runs. The low drop rate necessitates extended farming sessions, but the high damage scaling makes it worth the investment for primary weapon enthusiasts pushing into Steel Path and endless mission content.

## Usage

Galvanized Chamber serves as a cornerstone damage mod for primary weapon endgame builds. The stacking mechanic transforms weapons into increasingly powerful tools as eliminations accumulate. Combine this mod with Serration or other base damage sources for multiplicative scaling that reaches extreme damage values in longer missions.

Weapons with rapid fire rates benefit most from Galvanized Chamber, as they accumulate stacks faster and maintain higher average damage throughout engagements. Soma Prime, Braton Prime, and Phantasma pair exceptionally well with this mod, reaching weapon-balancing damage levels in high-level content.

The madurai polarity is standard on most primary weapons, simplifying build construction. Stack with critical chance and critical multiplier mods to maximize the damage stacking synergy, turning even modest base damage weapons into tier-one endgame primary choices.

## Notes

Galvanized Chamber is arguably the most versatile primary weapon damage mod, fitting into nearly every viable primary weapon build. The pure damage focus and reasonable polarity requirement make it more universally applicable than status-focused alternatives.

The stacking mechanic resets on death or mission completion, encouraging careful play and target prioritization. In Steel Path survival missions, skilled players can maintain high stacks indefinitely, achieving weapon damage levels rivaling dedicated damage frames with similar modding investments.
