## Overview

Narrow Minded is a power-modulation mod providing a trade-off between ability duration and ability range, making it essential for frames whose abilities benefit from extended duration over area coverage. At rank 10, the 99% duration boost extends ability timers to durations that enable indefinite uptime with proper energy management, while the 66% range penalty confines effects to immediate surroundings. This mod is cornerstone for duration-scaling frames like Frost, Nyx, Limbo, and Saryn, enabling playstyles centered on sustained ability maintenance.

The mod's power lies in enabling builds where ability uptime becomes nearly permanent, transforming defensive or crowd-control abilities into passive buffs. Frost can maintain armor buffs indefinitely; Nyx can keep targets mind-controlled for extreme durations; Limbo can extend Cataclysm coverage through cast chains. The range penalty is irrelevant for abilities that target a small area (single-target controls) or that cover full tilesets already (Limbo, Mirage).

Narrow Minded creates a distinct playstyle fork: range-focused builds use Power Range and stretch, while duration builds use Narrow Minded and intensify ability scaling. For frames where both range and duration matter equally, the trade-off becomes difficult to justify, but for single-ability-spam builds, Narrow Minded is nearly non-negotiable.

## Acquisition

Narrow Minded drops as a relic reward from void fissure missions, particularly at higher difficulties (Lith/Meso for beginners, Neo/Axi for veterans). Running void fissure squads on Axi difficulty yields multiple copies within a farming session. The rare rarity makes it less common than common mods, but void fissures reward it frequently enough for dedicated farming.

## Builds that use it

Narrow Minded dominates duration-scaling builds for Frost (globe duration), Nyx (mind control), Limbo (cataclysm/stasis), Saryn (spore duration), and Oberon (renewal). Any frame whose primary damage or survival comes from sustained ability uptime benefits from Narrow Minded. Conversely, range-focused frames (Loki, Equinox, Mesa) often avoid Narrow Minded entirely.

## Notes

The range penalty is multiplicative with other range reductions, stacking poorly with frames that already suffer range penalties (some augments). However, for frames like Limbo where range is less critical, the penalty is negligible. Combining Narrow Minded with natural duration scaling (Augur Message, Constitution) creates extended uptime builds that enable afk playstyles on tankier frames.
