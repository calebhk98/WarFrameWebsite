## Overview

Point Blank is a fundamental shotgun damage mod providing up to 90% damage scaling at rank 5. The mod is the primary damage booster for shotgun-focused builds and is nearly universal on endgame shotgun weapons. Point Blank enables mid-tier shotgun performance and serves as the foundation before specialized scaling mods like Primed Cryo Rounds or elemental variants.

## Stats per rank

Point Blank scales linearly from 15% damage at rank 0 to 90% at rank 5, providing consistent damage enhancement across shotgun archetypes.

## Acquisition

Point Blank is an uncommon mod obtained through early star chart progression and enemy drops on Earth and Mars. Players typically acquire Point Blank within the first few hours of progression and rank it early as a core shotgun damage component.

## Usage

Point Blank is essential on all shotgun builds, providing the primary damage scaling before applying elemental or specialty mods. Equip it as the first shotgun mod on any build, then add elemental scaling, status mods, or critical-chance enhancements. At rank 5, the 90% damage bonus enables shotguns to reach mid-tier viability in early star chart content.

In squad play, Point Blank combined with additional damage mods enables shotgun damage output that keeps pace with ability-focused clear speeds on low-to-mid-tier missions. On high-crit shotguns like Sobek or Drakgoon, Point Blank provides the baseline damage before critical-chance scaling takes effect.

## Common builds

Point Blank appears on all shotgun builds as a foundational damage mod. Early-game builds pair Point Blank with elemental scaling and basic critical-chance mods. Endgame shotgun builds often replace Point Blank with Primed alternatives or use Point Blank as a secondary damage mod to maximize total scaling.
