## Overview

Primed Heated Charge is a secondary pistol mod that provides +165% heat. With a moderate 6-point drain, typical for its tier, this mod fits efficiently into secondary pistol builds. The Naramon (-) polarity aligns with standard secondary pistol builds, minimizing the need for Forma investment to slot it without capacity penalty. At maximum rank 10, the mod delivers its full effect, making rank-up investment worthwhile whenever mod capacity allows.

The mod occupies a competitive slot in the secondary pistol mod configuration. Players must evaluate it against alternatives that address damage, survivability, or utility depending on mission type and personal playstyle preference. For players who rely heavily on the effect this mod provides, it becomes a staple inclusion across most loadouts, while players optimizing for raw damage output may substitute it situationally.
