## Overview

Preparation increases the starting energy available to a warframe at mission start. This early energy boost enables aggressive ability use from the opening moments of a mission, bypassing the initial ramp-up time that energy management typically requires. For missions where early burst damage or crowd control is crucial, Preparation provides a massive advantage.

The mod is particularly valuable in high-level content where early energy availability determines survival. A Mag with Preparation and Magnetize can lock down targets immediately; a Limbo can establish Stasis coverage before threats materialize. Preparation transforms the mission's opening seconds from a vulnerable scramble into a controlled initiative grab.

## Acquisition

Preparation is an uncommon drop from universal enemy loot. New players can expect to acquire their first copy after a few hours of gameplay. Additional ranks drop naturally through combat and are straightforward to farm by repeating mid-level missions.

## Builds that use it

Preparation is essential for burst-oriented builds and frames with energy-intensive opening abilities. Builds on Ash (Bladestorm spam), Mirage (damage scaling), and Limbo (map-wide Stasis control) often include Preparation. It also pairs well with Fleeting Expertise and Streamline for aggressive ability cycling that demands immediate energy availability.

## Synergies with other mods

Preparation works best alongside other efficiency mods like Streamline and Fleeting Expertise. The starting energy boost allows the first critical seconds of a mission to feature full-strength ability spam before settling into sustained ability cycling that depends on external energy generation. For frames like Revenant or Gyre with energy-hungry builds, Preparation provides the critical buffer needed to establish initial crowd control and damage before energy regeneration kicks in.

## Notes

Preparation only affects mission start energy. Energy regeneration, Recovery mods, and Equilibrium conversions all apply after mission start, so Preparation's benefit is front-loaded. In missions where survival depends on the first few seconds (Steel Path Void Fissure, level 100+ Endless), the mod's utility is pronounced. The early energy advantage often determines whether aggressive ability spam is possible from mission opening.

The 150% starting energy at rank 3 means a 175-energy warframe begins a mission with 262 energy instead of 87, a net gain of 175 energy that is immediately available for casting. This swing is often the difference between a controlled start and a panicked scramble for survival. For endurance-focused players, Preparation is one of the earliest mods that unlocks aggressive opening strategies.
