## Overview

Buzz Kill is a slash-damage-specific melee mod providing pure slash scaling, enabling slash-focused builds that emphasize physical damage type. At rank 5, the 90% slash boost significantly amplifies slash-heavy weapons, particularly whips and slashing swords. The mod pairs synergistically with Condition Overload, stacking slash procs with physical damage boost for exponential scaling.

Buzz Kill excels on weapons with high slash damage or slash-proc focus, transforming them into bleed-stacking machines capable of rapid enemy deletion. Weapons like the Nikana Prime or Gram Prime benefit tremendously from slash scaling, as their inherent slash frequency multiplies the mod's impact. Combined with status scaling, Buzz Kill enables overwhelming bleed cascades.

Buzz Kill occupies a melee slot competing with other damage types, crit mods, and status mods. For slash-focused builds, Buzz Kill is nearly mandatory; for balanced-damage weapons, elemental damage often outperforms slash. The 6-point drain is acceptable for damage mods, fitting comfortably alongside other scaling sources.

## Acquisition

Buzz Kill drops rarely from high-level Infested units, particularly in Infested-heavy missions (Eris, Orokin Derelict). Dedicated farming of high-level Infested nodes yields the mod within a few hours. Trading or void fissure luck provide faster acquisition.

## Builds that use it

Buzz Kill dominates slash-focused melee builds, appearing in bleed-overflow strategies. Balanced-damage weapons skip Buzz Kill in favor of elemental or balanced scaling. Hybrid builds may include both Buzz Kill and elemental damage for comprehensive scaling.

## Notes

Slash damage scales multiplicatively with status chance, creating exponential bleed potential. Combining Buzz Kill with Condition Overload and status-proccing weapons enables overwhelming scaling, particularly against armor-heavy enemies susceptible to slash procs. The 90% boost at rank 5 is substantial, enabling viable slash builds on light-damage weapons through scaling alone. Weapons with high natural slash damage inherit exponential benefit from Buzz Kill, turning them into overwhelming bleed machines. Slash procs scale with enemy health, enabling long-duration kills on heavily-armored targets through passive bleed damage that ignores damage mitigation mechanics.
