## Overview

Primed Fury is the endgame attack speed mod for melee weapons, providing up to 55% attack speed scaling at rank 10. The extended attack speed enables rapid hit-stacking with multiplicative damage mods like Blood Rush and Condition Overload, creating exponential damage scaling that enables one-shot or near-instant enemy elimination. Primed Fury is particularly valuable on fast weapons where additional attack speed stacks scale damage multiplicatively rather than linearly.

## Stats per rank

Primed Fury scales from 5% attack speed at rank 0 to 55% at maximum rank 10, providing substantial speed enhancement that dramatically increases damage output through hit-stacking mechanics.

## Acquisition

Primed Fury is a legendary mod available through void relic farming and marketplace trading. The mod's popularity for fast-weapon builds makes it a high-demand commodity. Players obtain Primed Fury through dedicated relic farming campaigns or through direct trading with other players at market rates.

## Usage

Primed Fury is essential on fast-weapon melee builds, particularly on dual-wield weapons, claws, and daggers that naturally have high attack speeds. The 55% speed bonus at rank 10 enables hit-stacking with Blood Rush to create crit-damage scaling that grows exponentially with each successive hit. On Condition Overload builds, Primed Fury enables rapid status-proc stacking that reaches 10-stack buff multiplicatively.

Combined with Bloodlust or equipped on warframes with attack-speed bonuses like Wukong or Valkyr, Primed Fury enables near-infinite attack speed that creates damage output exceeding ability-based farming. In squad play, rapid hit-stacking enables crowd control through status effects while maintaining high personal damage output. The mod is less essential on slow weapons like hammers or greatswords, where attack speed scaling provides less multiplicative benefit.

## Common builds

Primed Fury appears on all fast-melee builds, particularly dual-wield sword builds, claw builds, and dagger builds. Weapons like Dual Kama Prime, Twin Vipers Wraith, and Hirudo commonly use Primed Fury as the primary attack-speed enhancement. The mod is invaluable on Condition Overload builds that rely on rapid status-stacking for exponential damage scaling.
