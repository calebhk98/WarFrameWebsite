## Overview

Void Relics are the primary mechanism for acquiring Prime Warframe components, Prime weapon parts, and Forma Blueprints. Each Relic contains six possible rewards distributed across rarity tiers: three common slots, two uncommon slots, and one rare slot. Opening a Relic in a Void Fissure mission cracks it and presents the player with one reward, plus the option to select a squadmate's reward instead. Understanding refinement, Reactant collection, and reward selection mechanics is essential for efficient Prime farming.

Void Fissures are specific mission nodes that appear on the star chart and rotate approximately every hour. Each Fissure mission corresponds to a Relic era: Lith, Meso, Neo, or Axi. Only Relics of the matching era can be cracked in that Fissure. A Lith Fissure mission accepts only Lith Relics; an Axi Fissure accepts only Axi Relics.

Within a Fissure mission, Corrupted enemies spawn alongside normal enemies. Killing Corrupted enemies yields Reactant, a temporary pickup that must be collected 10 times to charge the Relic. Failing to collect 10 Reactant before mission end means the Relic is not cracked and no reward is presented. The Relic is not consumed on failure.

Squads of four provide the most reward options at the end screen. With four players each cracking a (possibly different) Relic, all four rewards appear simultaneously and any player can select any of the four. This sharing mechanic is the core of coordinated Prime farming: running four Radiant Relics of the same type in a squad of four gives each player four chances at the rare drop per mission.

## Prerequisites

- At least one Void Relic obtained (drops from missions, Syndicates, Nightwave, and Void Fissure rewards)
- Void Traces for refinement (farmed from Void Fissure missions automatically when Reactant is collected)
- Access to at least one Fissure mission matching the era of the Relic to be cracked
- A functional warframe and weapon loadout capable of surviving the Fissure mission's enemy level

## Steps

Identify the target Prime part and locate the Relic that contains it. Use the in-game Relic segment in the Star Chart UI or the WARFRAME Wiki's [Void Relic drop tables](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Void_Relic) to find which specific Relic contains the target component. Note whether the target part occupies the common, uncommon, or rare slot of that Relic -- this determines how much refinement is worthwhile.

Refine the Relic before entering the Fissure mission. Open the Relic in the Relic Refinement screen (accessible from the menu or from the Fissure mission load screen). Refinement tiers are Intact, Exceptional, Flawless, and Radiant. Each tier costs a set number of Void Traces and increases the probability of the rare slot item dropping while decreasing the probability of common slot items.

| Refinement tier | Void Trace cost | Rare drop chance | Common drop chance |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Intact | 0 | 2% | 76% (two slots) |
| Exceptional | 25 | 4% | 70% (two slots) |
| Flawless | 50 | 6% | 64% (two slots) |
| Radiant | 100 | 10% | 50% (two slots) |

Radiant refinement is worth the Trace cost when targeting rare parts. For common parts, Intact refinement is sufficient and preserves Void Traces for future Radiant refinements.

Select a matching Void Fissure mission from the Alerts section of the Star Chart. Choose a mission type that suits the warframe build -- Survival Fissures are reliable for players who want long sessions; Capture Fissures complete in under two minutes and are efficient for fast cracking of large Relic inventories.

During the mission, kill Corrupted enemies and collect Reactant. Reactant glows a distinctive gold and emits a pickup sound distinct from normal resources. Collect 10 Reactant before mission end. In endless missions (Survival, Defense), Reactant collection carries over between rotations; collect 10 to crack the Relic at the first rotation, then optionally crack additional Relics in subsequent rotations.

At the end-of-mission reward screen, evaluate all four displayed rewards (your cracked Relic reward plus squadmates' rewards). Select the highest-priority item. Priority order: rare target part > uncommon target part > platinum-valuable parts for trade > common target parts > Forma Blueprint > lowest-value common parts.

## Void Traces farming

Void Traces are earned passively during every Void Fissure mission where 10 Reactant is collected. Each completed Fissure mission awards between 6 and 30 Void Traces. The amount is partially random but increases with Relic refinement tier and mission completion bonuses.

Void Traces are capped at 1,200 per player. Spending Traces through refinement before reaching the cap is necessary to avoid losing earned Traces. Players farming for a specific rare part should refine Relics to Radiant as soon as sufficient Traces accumulate.

Running Fissure missions with non-priority Relics at Intact refinement is an efficient Void Trace banking strategy. Each completed mission provides Traces that can fund future Radiant refinements of higher-priority Relics.

## Reward sharing mechanics

When four players each crack a Relic in the same mission, all four rewards appear simultaneously on the reward screen. Any player can select any of the four rewards, including a squadmate's. This is the critical mechanic for efficient targeted farming.

Coordinate with squadmates using Relic-share squads. For maximum efficiency, all four players should run Radiant Relics containing the same target part. This gives each player four rolls at 10% rare drop chance per mission rather than one roll, effectively compressing farm time by a factor of four for rare drops.

Public Fissure lobbies provide random Relic sharing but still offer three additional reward options per mission. Even without coordination, public Fissure missions improve rare drop access compared to solo play.

## Era selection guide

Lith Relics are the most abundant and cheapest to acquire. They drop from low-level missions throughout the star chart. Lith Relics contain older Prime parts and Forma Blueprints at common slots. Use Lith Fissures (frequently appearing on Earth, Mars, and low-level nodes) for Lith cracking.

Meso Relics drop from mid-star-chart missions. Saturn, Jupiter, and Phobos missions commonly reward Meso Relics. Meso Fissures appear regularly and support fast cycling due to available mission types including Capture.

Neo Relics contain recent Prime parts and are the primary source of currently unvaulted Prime components. Neo Fissures appear on higher-level nodes and are commonly run as Survival or Interception missions. Neo Relics trade at higher platinum values due to the recency of their contents.

Axi Relics contain the rarest Prime components and the highest-tier parts. Axi Fissures appear on Void nodes and on high-level late-game planets. Void Survival on the Void planet is the most efficient Axi Relic cracking method, providing fast Reactant collection and sustained rotation rewards.

## Common mistakes

Running Radiant refinement on Relics that contain the target part in a common slot is a Void Trace waste. Radiant refinement improves the rare slot probability specifically; common slot items become less likely. If the target part is a common drop, Intact refinement preserves Traces while still granting access to the part.

Entering Fissure missions without verifying the Relic era match results in being unable to crack the Relic. The mission will not allow cracking a Lith Relic in an Axi Fissure. Double-check era before entering.

Skipping Reactant collection because the mission objective is complete fails the Relic crack. In Exterminate Fissures, players sometimes kill the required enemies and head to extraction before collecting 10 Reactant. Wait until Reactant count reaches 10 before extracting.

Ignoring squadmate rewards when a better option is visible is a frequent error. The reward screen displays all four rewards simultaneously. Selecting "your" reward by habit when a squadmate has the specific rare part you need discards a free acquisition.
