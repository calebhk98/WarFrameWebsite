## Overview

The New War is one of Warframe's longest and most narratively significant quests, serving as the culmination of the core Tenno storyline established since The Second Dream. The quest requires a broad selection of prerequisite quests to be completed and imposes specific warframe and loadout requirements during its missions that cannot be pre-emptively circumvented. Preparation is essential: players who enter underprepared may find sections where their standard build cannot function as expected.

This guide covers the prerequisites, preparation steps, and common pitfalls for entering The New War, without spoiling the quest's narrative content beyond what is mentioned in Digital Extremes official pre-quest communications.

## Prerequisites

The codex quest journal lists all prerequisites for The New War. Core requirements include The Second Dream, The War Within, Natah (unlocked after completing Sedna junction), The Sacrifice, Chains of Harrow, and Heart of Deimos. Completing Saya's Vigil and Mask of the Revenant is recommended but not strictly required. Check the codex journal on the in-game quest screen to verify all items are marked as completed before installing.

Mastery Rank is not a hard gate, but Mastery Rank 5 ensures access to all intermediate gear needed for the operator combat and warframe switching sequences. Players below MR 5 may find some equipment options restricted.

## Steps

Complete any outstanding prerequisites. The most commonly missed prerequisite is Heart of Deimos, which is not part of the traditional quest chain from Second Dream but is required due to its lore connections to The New War's Infested story elements.

Build and level a warframe roster with at least three frames covering different roles. The New War forces use of specific frames at scripted points; having those frames built and leveled (Rank 30) ensures the scripted segments play at maximum effectiveness. Details on which frames are required are in the full quest narrative; have at least Excalibur, Rhino, and one additional support frame ready without seeking spoilers in advance.

Upgrade the operator amp to at least a tier-2 configuration (obtainable from Onkko or Quills standing). Operator combat sections in The New War require dealing damage via Void Blast and Void Sling -- a Mote Amp functions but is slower. A tier-2 Shwaak or Raplak configuration provides significantly better performance.

Stock gear wheel consumables before starting. Energy Restore pads (medium or large) and Specters provide insurance during difficult mission segments. Synthesis Specter (from Cephalon Simaris) provides an emergency fallback; bring one for the most challenging fight sequences.

Read the quest checklist one final time and install The New War from the quest segment terminal in the orbiter. The quest is long and session-based -- it is not necessary to complete in one sitting, but choose a time with at least 2-3 hours available for the most engrossing portions.

## Tips

The New War includes cinematic sequences that cannot be skipped on the first playthrough. Plan accordingly for viewing time. Cutscenes are replayable after completion via the codex cinematic library.

Some New War missions use borrowed loadouts or temporary warframes with pre-set builds. Familiarize with those warframes' basic ability layouts beforehand to avoid entering the scripted sequences unfamiliar with the kit.

After completing The New War, unlock and prioritize: Archon Hunt access (weekly high-value endgame), Kahl's Garrison weekly missions (Grendel parts, Styanax parts, and circuit resources), and the Narmer bounty system on Plains and Orb Vallis. The New War opens significantly more endgame content than any other single quest.

Operator amp progression is also accelerated by The New War's completion. The quest opens new Quills and operator content that scales focus point rewards significantly compared to pre-New War Eidolon farming alone.

## Gear recommendations by category

Warframe: having three distinct warframes at Rank 30 provides coverage for the scripted frame-switching sequences without spoiling which frames are required. Rhino Prime, Excalibur, and a support frame such as Trinity or Wisp cover tank, melee, and support roles respectively. These three frame archetypes appear across the most common scripted combat encounters in the quest.

Operator amp: the 2-1-2 configuration (Shwaak prism, Pencha scaffold, Lohrin brace) is the standard beginner Tridolon and quest-combat amp. Obtain this from Onkko at Quills rank Architect or above. If Quills standing is insufficient, any tier-2 or higher amp from the Vox Solaris operator equipment vendor provides a substantial upgrade over the Mote Amp.

Weapons: carry a high-damage primary for fighting Sentient enemies (which adapt to damage types mid-combat, requiring weapon switching or multi-element builds) and a melee weapon for scripted Umbra sequences. Sentients adapt to damage types dealt to them, developing resistance after a set number of hits with the same element. Switching damage types every few seconds maintains optimal damage throughout encounters.

## Common mistakes

Installing The New War before completing Heart of Deimos blocks progression at a specific narrative checkpoint requiring Deimos-related systems. The installation screen warns about prerequisites but does not enforce all of them at install time.

Using ability-spam builds that depend on high energy economy may underperform in mission segments that lock out specific abilities or modify energy capacity. The New War's scripted combat sequences occasionally restrict ability use as narrative devices.

Not upgrading the operator amp before starting leaves critical operator combat sections underpowered. Even a basic tier-2 amp upgrade dramatically improves operator combat effectiveness in the quest's scripted encounters compared to the default Mote Amp.

## Prerequisite quest summary

The following quests are confirmed prerequisites for The New War. Completing each in order is the path of least friction:

The Second Dream: introduces operator and Void mechanics. Unlocks operator mode for the player.

The War Within: expands operator power. Unlocks kuva mechanic, Kuva Fortress, and the Transference ability. Required before The New War.

Natah: introduces the Sentient faction's relationship with the Tenno. Available after completing Uranus Junction. Must be completed before the quest chain leading to The New War.

The Sacrifice: introduces Excalibur Umbra and expands the Sentient lore. Unlocks Excalibur Umbra as a permanently available warframe, no further crafting required.

Chains of Harrow: introduces Harrow and the Rell story. Required as a lore prerequisite for The New War's narrative arc.

Heart of Deimos: introduces the Deimos open world and Entrati faction. Required because of Deimos' lore connections to events in The New War.

Saya's Vigil and Mask of the Revenant are recommended but not strictly listed as mandatory in all sources. Completing them prevents any edge-case gating.

## Post-quest content unlocks

Completing The New War opens a wave of new content that constitutes the bulk of current endgame progression:

Archon Hunts (weekly): three-mission chains yielding Archon Shards for permanent warframe stat bonuses. The most sustained endgame grind system.

Kahl's Garrison (weekly): Kahl-175 Grineer missions with distinct mechanics and rewards. Provides Grendel Prime parts, Styanax parts, and Incarnon seeds for the Duviri Circuit.

Narmer Bounties: modified versions of Cetus and Orb Vallis bounties with additional challenges and unique Narmer rewards including Archon Shards.

Veilbreaker story quests: introduction to Kahl's Garrison and expanding lore after The New War's main narrative conclusion.

Players who complete The New War should also revisit the Plains of Eidolon and Orb Vallis bounty tables, as Narmer versions of existing bounties are added to the rotation with distinct reward tables that include materials otherwise unavailable through standard bounty play.
