## Overview

Steel Path is a difficulty modifier that applies +100 levels to all enemies throughout the star chart, effectively creating a parallel endgame version of every mission node. Standard star chart enemies that cap at level 15-30 become level 115-130 in Steel Path, requiring substantially different warframe builds and mod configurations. Steel Path is not a separate game mode -- it is the same missions with increased enemy scaling, status immunity on Eximus units (via Overguard), and dramatically higher damage output from enemies.

Completing Steel Path serves three goals: unlocking the first-completion Vitus Essence bonus per node, farming Steel Essence from Acolyte mini-boss kills in endless missions, and progressing through the Steel Path Honors daily system for escalating rewards.

## Prerequisites

Before attempting Steel Path, verify that the warframe and weapon build can survive 30 seconds in a level 130 mission without dying. Common benchmark tests include running a standard defense mission on Steel Path against the first five waves on any node and evaluating whether the loadout sustains itself without consumables.

Frames with ability-based damage immunity (Revenant Prime, Rhino Prime, Nyx Prime) or very high health pools (Inaros Prime) are the most reliable for initial Steel Path exposure. Frames that rely on shields exclusively lose most of their effective health pool against level 130+ enemies without supplemental Shield Gating mechanics.

Weapons with high critical chance and critical multiplier deal reliable damage at level 130, whereas status-dependent weapons require careful elemental pairing against specific factions. Against Steel Path Grineer, Corrosive damage before Puncture and Slash is necessary; against Corpus, Toxin bypass of shields before Viral amplification is standard.

## Steps

Enable Steel Path by selecting the Steel Path toggle on the navigation screen. The star chart shifts to show the Steel Path overlay with all locked nodes in red. Start with exterminate and capture missions on low-level planets (Earth, Mars) to acclimate to the increased enemy damage before attempting higher-difficulty mission types.

Prioritize completing nodes across all planets before committing to an endless farming session. Each unique Steel Path node completion grants 100 Vitus Essence (not Steel Essence). Vitus Essence is the Arbitration currency exchangeable for specific mods; collecting all node bonuses funds several Adaptation or Rolling Guard purchases that substantially ease further Steel Path farming.

After all nodes are cleared, begin Steel Path endless mission farming for Steel Essence. Choose survival or disruption missions with high enemy density. Acolyte mini-bosses spawn every 5 minutes in endless missions and drop 2 Steel Essence on kill. At 20 minutes per session, expect 8 Steel Essence per run.

Exchange Steel Essence at Teshin for Arcane Adapters first -- this is the most impactful use of early Steel Essence income. Each Arcane Adapter allows a melee weapon to equip one arcane slot, adding significant sustained damage or survivability bonuses to melee-focused builds.

Complete the daily Steel Path Honors task (one Steel Path mission per day) for the rotating daily reward, which includes bonus Steel Essence, forma, or exclusive cosmetics depending on the current rotation.

## Strengths and weaknesses of Steel Path

Steel Path provides scaling rewards that normalize over time into a sustainable endgame economy. Steel Essence, Vitus Essence, and the improved drop tables on Steel Path endless missions provide better resource-per-hour than equivalent standard missions for players with capable builds.

The difficulty scaling removes nearly all of the standard game's safety margin. Warframe builds that relied on excessive damage output to eliminate enemies before they could attack -- viable in standard star chart missions -- fail on Steel Path because enemies survive long enough to deal significant damage. Survivability-first build philosophy is the correct approach for initial Steel Path entry.

## Tips

Overguard on Eximus units (introduced as a mechanic in a 2022 update) makes Eximus enemies immune to all crowd control abilities until their Overguard layer is depleted. Attacks from weapons and area abilities that deal direct damage remove Overguard normally; CC abilities like sleep, stasis, and freeze do not function until Overguard is gone. Build for damage output against Eximus or bring abilities that inherently deal Overguard-stripping damage.

Steel Path enemies deal 130% additional damage to Operators in Void Tenno form. Avoid extended operator combat unless using a high-tier amp and Void Sling mobility to stay out of extended enemy fire.

## Common mistakes

Attempting to use crowd-control-focused frames (Limbo, Trinity without adaptation) without accounting for Eximus Overguard stalling their CC toolkit leads to unexpectedly high incoming damage. Address Eximus units with direct damage before relying on CC.
