## Overview

The Eidolons are ancient Sentient colossi that emerge from Cetus Lake on the Plains of Eidolon at night, retreating at dawn. Three Eidolon variants exist in ascending difficulty: Teralyst, Gantulyst, and Hydrolyst. Completing the Tridolon (all three in sequence during a single night cycle) is the primary source of Arcane Enhancement drops and Eidolon Shard materials. Higher-tier Eidolons yield rarer Arcanes and Brilliant or Radiant Eidolon Shards, used for Focus School progression and lens crafting.

The core mechanic of Eidolon hunting is Synovia destruction. Each Eidolon has multiple Synovia weak points (glowing nodes on limbs) that must be destroyed using operator Void damage through an amp. Standard weapon fire deals no damage to Synovias. After all Synovias are destroyed, the Eidolon becomes momentarily vulnerable to weapon fire for a final kill or capture phase.

## Prerequisites

The Mote Amp (default starter amp) is insufficient for efficient Tridolon hunting. Craft or purchase a higher-tier amp from Onkko (the Quills vendor in Cetus). Common early upgrade choices include the 2-1-2 Shwaak amp configuration, which balances burst damage and energy efficiency. Community resources at [warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Amp](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Amp) list all available components and their stats.

Madurai focus school is recommended for Void Strike charges, which accumulate during operator void mode and release as bonus Void damage on the next operator amp shot. High Void Strike stacks can one-shot Synovias on lower-tier Eidolons and significantly accelerate Synovia destruction on the Hydrolyst.

## Steps

Enter the Plains via the Cetus gate after a night cycle begins. Wait for the night transition to start (Konzu in Cetus announces night cycle transitions). Eidolons spawn automatically at night; the Teralyst's location is marked on the minimap as a large enemy indicator.

Capture Eidolon Lures by approaching small Vomvalyst creatures near the water and using operator Void Blast to absorb them into nearby floating Lure objects. Two lures are required for Teralyst capture; three lures are required for Gantulyst and Hydrolyst capture. Charge each lure by keeping it near three Vomvalyst kills after it is captured.

Bring the charged lures to the Eidolon. Keep lures within 50 meters of the Eidolon throughout the fight to maintain tether range. Begin Synovia destruction: switch to operator mode, activate Void Strike charges from Madurai, and fire Void Blast directly at the glowing Synovia weak points. Each Synovia takes 3-10 amp shots depending on amp tier.

After all Synovias are destroyed, the Eidolon knees and enters a vulnerability phase. During this window, deal weapon damage to its main body. For Tridolon runs, capture rather than kill the Teralyst: the capture requires all lures to be charged and positioned correctly. Capture yields more Eidolon Shards and Arcanes per Eidolon than killing.

Capturing the Teralyst spawns a Gantulyst automatically nearby. Begin the lure and Synovia sequence on the Gantulyst immediately. Gantulyst has more Synovias and more health than Teralyst. Capturing Gantulyst spawns the Hydrolyst, the final and most difficult Eidolon in the chain.

## Tips

Chroma Prime provides the highest Vex Armor critical damage buff of any support frame. In a standard Tridolon composition, Chroma (or Chroma Prime) fills the buff role, increasing squad critical damage multiplier by up to 275% at high power strength. Trinity Prime provides energy for the Chroma and damage-dealing squad members. Volt Prime provides a shield that amplifies projectile damage, particularly beneficial for sniper-rifle Synovia shooting methods used by some players.

Brilliant Eidolon Shards (from Gantulyst and Hydrolyst captures) are used to craft Eidolon Lenses, which have the highest Focus conversion rate (2.25%). Each Tridolon run provides enough shards to craft lenses progressively while running Eidolons for Arcane accumulation.

Arcane Energize (rank 5) drops from Hydrolyst captures at approximately 8% chance per shard reward roll. This is the most valuable single Arcane for general game use. Expect 25-50 Hydrolyst captures to obtain a full set of 21 Arcane Energize needed for rank 5.

## Common mistakes

Using unranked or low-ranked operator amp components significantly reduces per-shot Void damage. Amp parts must be crafted at the max available rank and have their intrinsic affinity maxed through operator use to perform at their listed damage values.

Losing lures during the fight (by allowing them to take too much damage from Eidolon AoE) prevents capture on the vulnerability phase. Keep lures behind the squad relative to the Eidolon's attack directions, and avoid positioning lures in Eidolon stomp zones.
