## Overview

The Eidolons are ancient Sentient colossi that emerge from Cetus Lake on the Plains of Eidolon at night, retreating at dawn. Three Eidolon variants exist in ascending difficulty: Teralyst, Gantulyst, and Hydrolyst. Completing the Tridolon (all three in sequence during a single night cycle) is the primary source of Arcane Enhancement drops and Eidolon Shard materials. Higher-tier Eidolons yield rarer Arcanes and Brilliant or Radiant Eidolon Shards, used for Focus School progression and lens crafting.

The core mechanic of Eidolon hunting is Synovia destruction. Each Eidolon has multiple Synovia weak points (glowing nodes on limbs) that must be destroyed using operator Void damage through an amp. Standard weapon fire deals no damage to Synovias. After all Synovias are destroyed, the Eidolon becomes momentarily vulnerable to weapon fire for a final kill or capture phase.

## Prerequisites

The Mote Amp (default starter amp) is insufficient for efficient Tridolon hunting. Craft or purchase a higher-tier amp from Onkko (the Quills vendor in Cetus). Common early upgrade choices include the 2-1-2 Shwaak amp configuration, which balances burst damage and energy efficiency. Community resources at [warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Amp](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Amp) list all available components and their stats.

Madurai focus school is recommended for Void Strike charges, which accumulate during operator void mode and release as bonus Void damage on the next operator amp shot. High Void Strike stacks can significantly accelerate Synovia destruction, especially on the Hydrolyst.

## Steps

Enter the Plains via the Cetus gate after a night cycle begins. Wait for the night transition to start (Konzu in Cetus announces night cycle transitions). Eidolons spawn automatically at night; the Teralyst's location is marked on the minimap as a large enemy indicator.

Locate Eidolon Lures floating near bodies of water on the Plains. Arm each lure using operator Void Blast. Two lures are required for Teralyst capture; three lures are required for Gantulyst and Hydrolyst capture. Charge each lure by keeping it near Vomvalysts and killing the Vomvalysts close to the lure. Each lure requires three Vomvalyst kills to become fully charged.

Bring the charged lures to the Eidolon. Keep lures within range of the Eidolon throughout the fight to maintain tether capability. Begin Synovia destruction: switch to operator mode, activate Void Strike charges from Madurai, and fire Void Blast directly at the glowing Synovia weak points. Each Synovia requires multiple amp shots depending on amp tier.

After all Synovias are destroyed, the Eidolon kneels and enters a vulnerability phase. During this window, deal weapon damage to its main body. For Tridolon runs, capture rather than kill the Teralyst: capture requires all lures to be charged and positioned correctly. Capture yields more Eidolon Shards and Arcanes per Eidolon than killing.

Capturing the Teralyst spawns a Gantulyst automatically nearby. Begin the lure and Synovia sequence on the Gantulyst immediately. Gantulyst has more Synovias and more health than Teralyst. Capturing Gantulyst spawns the Hydrolyst, the final and most difficult Eidolon in the chain.

## Tips

Chroma Prime provides the Vex Armor critical damage buff in a standard Tridolon composition. Vex Armor scales with incoming damage, building up a multiplicative critical damage bonus that benefits the entire squad. Trinity Prime provides energy for the Chroma and damage-dealing squad members. Volt Prime provides a shield that amplifies projectile damage, particularly beneficial for sniper-rifle Synovia shooting methods used by some players.

Brilliant Eidolon Shards drop from Gantulyst and Hydrolyst captures and are used to craft Eidolon Lenses, which have the highest Focus conversion rate (2.25%). Regular Eidolon Shards come from Teralyst captures. Each Tridolon run provides enough shards to craft lenses progressively while running Eidolons for Arcane accumulation.

Arcane Energize (rank 5) is one of the most valuable arcanes for general gameplay. It drops from Hydrolyst captures. Expect multiple Hydrolyst captures to obtain a full set needed for rank 5. The number required is 21 copies of Arcane Energize to reach rank 5 (combining 1 + 2 + 4 + 6 + 8 copies across ranks 0-5).

## Vomvalyst management during lure charging

Vomvalysts are the small Sentient-aligned creatures that wander the Plains at night. They exist in two states: a physical form and a spectral form after death. When killed in their physical form, Vomvalysts convert to spectral form rather than dying fully. Spectral Vomvalysts drift toward Cetus Lake unless captured by an armed lure. When a spectral Vomvalyst reaches an armed lure, it charges the lure by one charge count. Each lure requires three spectral Vomvalysts to become fully charged.

Killing Vomvalysts near charged lures wastes the charge progress -- killing a Vomvalyst converts it to spectral form, which then charges the nearest lure. If a lure is already full, the spectral Vomvalyst despawns without effect. Coordinate lure charging by killing Vomvalysts in sequence near partially-charged lures rather than killing all visible Vomvalysts at once.

## Common mistakes

Using unranked or low-ranked operator amp components significantly reduces per-shot Void damage. Amp parts must be crafted and have their intrinsic affinity maxed through operator use to perform at their full damage values.

Losing lures during the fight (by allowing them to take too much damage from Eidolon AoE) prevents capture on the vulnerability phase. Keep lures behind the squad relative to the Eidolon's attack directions, and avoid positioning lures in Eidolon stomp zones.

Attempting the Tridolon chain without pre-charging lures for Gantulyst and Hydrolyst wastes time in the night cycle. After capturing the Teralyst, immediately begin arming and charging three lures for the next Eidolon before the Gantulyst arrives at its spawn point.

## Amp configurations for Eidolon hunting

Amp parts are acquired from the Quills (Plains of Eidolon) and Vox Solaris (Orb Vallis) syndicates. Each amp has three components: a prism (main fire), a scaffold (secondary fire), and a brace (passive stats). Parts are numbered 1-7 in the order they unlock as syndicate standing increases.

Common Tridolon amp configurations referenced in the community use three-digit shorthand (e.g., 2-1-2) corresponding to prism-scaffold-brace. The Shwaak prism (part 2) fires a wide-spread charged blast useful for hitting multiple Synovias simultaneously. The Pencha scaffold (part 1) provides a reliable secondary fire. The Shraksun scaffold (part 6, from Vox Solaris) provides burst damage for high-end runs. For beginners, any amp beyond the Mote Amp is a substantial improvement; reaching the 2-1-2 configuration provides a viable Tridolon hunting setup for most squads.

## Reward table overview

Teralyst capture provides: Eidolon Shard (small), Radiant Eidolon Shard (small), Brilliant Eidolon Shard (uncommon), and Arcane drops from the common pool. Teralyst killing provides fewer shards than capture.

Gantulyst capture provides: Brilliant Eidolon Shard (guaranteed), additional Arcane drops from the uncommon pool. The Brilliant Shards from Gantulyst are required for Eidolon Lens crafting.

Hydrolyst capture provides: Radiant Eidolon Shard (guaranteed), a larger Arcane drop pool including the highest-tier Arcanes (Arcane Energize, Arcane Blessing). Hydrolyst is the primary reason for completing the full Tridolon chain.

Arcanes from higher-tier Eidolons appear in the Eidolon Arcane trade market. Players who run Tridolons regularly and obtain duplicate Arcanes can sell them via warframe.market to players without Eidolon access.

Night cycle timing on the Plains of Eidolon follows a real-time schedule. Each day-night cycle on the Plains lasts approximately 100 real-time minutes total, with roughly 50 minutes of night. Check the in-game navigation screen's Plains of Eidolon panel to see the time remaining until the next night cycle begins. Eidolons can only be engaged during the night phase; failing to complete a Tridolon before dawn ends the hunt and forces the team to return to Cetus and wait for the next night cycle.
