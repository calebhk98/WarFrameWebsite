## Overview

The Circuit is a roguelite progression mode within Duviri that assigns players random warframes and weapons from their personal collection (or starter sets if unowned) and tasks them with completing a series of progressively harder missions. Players accumulate Decrees -- passive ability and stat buffs selected between missions -- that stack over the course of a run. The Circuit provides unique rewards including Warframe blueprints, Incarnon Genesis adapters, Riven Mods, and the Duviri-exclusive Prex cards.

The Circuit exists in two variants: Normal Circuit uses standard starmap-level enemies, and Steel Path Circuit requires Steel Path access and applies Steel Path scaling throughout. Steel Path Circuit rewards are more valuable, particularly for Incarnon Genesis drops.

## Prerequisites

Duviri access requires completing The Duviri Paradox quest, which introduces the Duviri realm and its gameplay systems. The quest is available from the star chart after completing earlier story quests. First-time Duviri players unfamiliar with Decree mechanics should complete a few Duviri Open World sessions before attempting the Circuit to become comfortable with movement, Kaithe combat, and Decree selection strategies.

## Steps

Access The Circuit from the Duviri main menu (after loading into Duviri via the star chart) or directly from the Duviri node on the star chart. Select Normal or Steel Path Circuit at the difficulty screen.

Before the first mission, the game displays the current Circuit reward options -- three or four possible items for the run's final reward slot. Select the target item from this menu. Note that the reward pool rotates on a weekly basis, and certain high-value items (Incarnon Genesis adapters for specific weapons) only appear during specific weeks.

Each Circuit stage presents three Decree choices. Decrees provide passive bonuses that persist through all subsequent stages. Common Decrees include: bonus ability strength, bonus melee critical chance, health restoration on ability use, and enemy slow on kill. Select Decrees that complement the assigned warframe's kit whenever possible. When using an unfamiliar assigned frame, prefer defensive Decrees (health restoration, damage reduction) over offensive specialization Decrees.

Complete seven stages -- each stage is a short objective mission lasting 3-7 minutes. Stage mission types include Exterminate, Defense, Capture, and Survival. On completion of stage 7, claim the Circuit reward from the reward screen.

## Tips

Incarnon Genesis adapters are among the most valuable Circuit rewards. These adapters install on specific base weapons (Braton, Lato, Skana, and others) and add an Incarnon Evolution path that transforms them into powerful endgame variants. The weekly rotation determines which Incarnon adapters are available, so plan farming weeks around the specific adapter needed for a target weapon.

Decree selection strategy varies by run. In early stages (1-3), prioritize defensive Decrees to build survivability for later stages where enemy level scaling is higher. In mid and late stages (4-7), pivot to offensive Decrees that accelerate kill speed once the run's difficulty is established.

The assigned warframes in Normal Circuit are drawn from the player's owned collection. Owning a broader selection of warframes increases the odds of being assigned a frame with a familiar kit. In Steel Path Circuit, assigned frames can include any frame in the current season's pool regardless of ownership, which may assign frames the player has not used before.

Incarnon weapons obtained from Circuit rewards become significantly more powerful after completing their Incarnon Evolution unlocks, which involve in-mission challenges. After receiving an Incarnon Genesis adapter, check the Evolution requirements and complete them progressively across normal gameplay rather than grinding them in Circuit specifically.

Riven Mods offered as Circuit rewards are worth selecting even when the weapon type is not immediately desired. Rivens for high-disposition weapons retain trading value on warframe.market and can be sold for platinum to fund other purchases.

## Duviri Paradox quest and Circuit access

The Duviri Paradox quest is an unconventional entry point -- it can be played before completing prior main quests, making it accessible to newer players earlier than most endgame content. The Circuit, however, becomes relevant primarily at mid-to-endgame because its highest-value rewards (Incarnon Genesis adapters, high-tier Riven Mods) benefit players who already have foundational weapons and frames.

New players entering Duviri for the first time should complete the open world Duviri experience (outside the Circuit) to learn Kaithe movement, Dax combat mechanics, and the Duviri Decree system before attempting Circuit runs. The open world sessions also provide Decrees in a lower-stakes environment ideal for learning Decree categories and synergies.

## Common mistakes

Ignoring Decree synergies and selecting randomly results in inconsistent power scaling. Decrees that amplify ability damage have limited value on frames assigned without strong ability kits (Inaros, Grendel). Identify the assigned frame's primary power source (melee, abilities, or weapons) and align Decree choices accordingly.

Leaving the Circuit between stages (not abandoning, but returning to Duviri navigation) does not save progress -- Circuit progress is saved only on completing each stage fully. Ensure a full run can be completed before starting.

Selecting a Circuit reward item without checking its weekly availability wastes a run on items obtainable through other means. Cross-reference the weekly reward rotation against current inventory needs before committing to a run.

## Decree categories and synergy building

Decrees are divided into categories that affect different aspects of gameplay. Understanding the major categories allows faster assessment of each Decree choice:

Ability Decrees: increase ability strength, range, duration, or add secondary effects to specific ability types (heat abilities, cold abilities, or type-agnostic buffs). Valuable on frames with strong ability kits (Octavia, Saryn, Mag).

Weapon Decrees: increase critical chance, critical multiplier, damage, fire rate, or status chance for specific weapon categories (melee, primary, secondary). Valuable on weapon-focused builds assigned frames with minimal ability kits.

Movement and utility Decrees: increase sprint speed, jump height, provide damage reduction on dodge, or add health regeneration triggers. These provide survivability without combat power, appropriate when assigned frames lack strong offensive ability or weapon synergies.

When Decrees appear that overlap (e.g., two consecutive melee critical chance offers), selecting both stacks them additively, creating substantial buffs for melee combat by late stages.

## Weekly reward rotation planning

The Circuit's reward pool rotates weekly. Checking the current week's offering before starting a run ensures effort is directed toward obtainable targets. The Digital Extremes website and warframe.fandom.com/wiki/The_Circuit maintain current rotation information.

Incarnon Genesis adapters are the highest-value weekly Circuit reward for most players. Each adapter is associated with a specific base weapon, and the weapon list rotates so that each weapon appears in the Circuit periodically. Players who need adapters for specific weapons track the rotation and farm those weeks specifically.

Warframe blueprint components available through Circuit allow players who missed market or event availability to obtain those frames. These are a secondary target compared to Incarnon adapters for most endgame players but represent essential acquisition routes for newer players.

Circuit runs are repeatable. There is no weekly cap on the number of runs allowed. Each completed run awards the selected reward from the offered pool. Players who want multiple copies of the same weekly item (multiple Incarnon Genesis adapters for the same weapon, to apply to duplicates or trade) can run the Circuit multiple times in the same week.
