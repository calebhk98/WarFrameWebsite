## Overview

The Circuit is a roguelite progression mode within Duviri that assigns players random warframes and weapons from their personal collection (or starter sets if unowned) and tasks them with completing a series of progressively harder missions. Players accumulate Decrees -- passive ability and stat buffs selected between missions -- that stack over the course of a run. The Circuit provides unique rewards including Warframe blueprints, Incarnon Genesis adapters, Riven Mods, and the Duviri-exclusive Prex cards.

The Circuit exists in two variants: Normal Circuit uses standard starmap-level enemies, and Steel Path Circuit requires Steel Path access and applies Steel Path scaling throughout. Steel Path Circuit rewards are more valuable, particularly for Incarnon Genesis drops.

## Prerequisites

Duviri access requires completing The Duviri Paradox quest, which introduces the Duviri realm and its gameplay systems. The quest is available from the star chart after a minimum Mastery Rank is reached. First-time Duviri players unfamiliar with Decree mechanics should complete a few Duviri Open World sessions before attempting the Circuit to become comfortable with movement, Kaithe combat, and Decree selection strategies.

## Steps

Access The Circuit from the Duviri main menu (after loading into Duviri via the star chart) or directly from the Duviri node on the star chart. Select Normal or Steel Path Circuit at the difficulty screen.

Before the first mission, the game displays the current Circuit reward options -- three or four possible items for the run's final reward slot. Select the target item from this menu. Note that the reward pool rotates on a weekly basis, and certain high-value items (Incarnon Genesis adapters for specific weapons) only appear during specific weeks.

Each Circuit stage presents three Decree choices. Decrees provide passive bonuses that persist through all subsequent stages. Common Decrees include: bonus ability strength, bonus melee critical chance, health restoration on ability use, and enemy slow on kill. Select Decrees that complement the assigned warframe's kit whenever possible. When using an unfamiliar assigned frame, prefer defensive Decrees (health restoration, damage reduction) over offensive specialization Decrees.

Complete seven stages -- each stage is a short objective mission lasting 3-7 minutes. Stage mission types include Exterminate, Defense, Capture, and Survival. On completion of stage 7, claim the Circuit reward from the reward screen.

## Tips

Incarnon Genesis adapters are among the most valuable Circuit rewards. These adapters install on specific base weapons (Braton, Lato, Skana, etc.) and add an Incarnon Evolution path that transforms them into powerful endgame variants. The weekly rotation determines which Incarnon adapters are available, so plan farming weeks around the specific adapter needed for a target weapon.

Decree selection strategy varies by run. In early stages (1-3), prioritize defensive Decrees to build survivability for later stages where enemy level scaling is higher. In mid and late stages (4-7), pivot to offensive Decrees that accelerate kill speed once the run's difficulty is established.

The assigned warframes in Circuit are drawn from your owned collection in Normal mode. Owning a broader selection of warframes increases the odds of being assigned a frame you know well. In Steel Path Circuit, assigned frames can include any frame in the current season's pool regardless of ownership, which may assign frames you have not used before.

Completing a full Circuit run in under 50 minutes qualifies for a bonus reward in some seasonal variants. Check the Circuit interface for active bonus conditions before each run.

## Common mistakes

Ignoring Decree synergies and selecting randomly results in inconsistent power scaling. Decrees that amplify ability damage have limited value on frames assigned without strong ability kits (Inaros, Grendel). Identify the assigned frame's primary power source (melee, abilities, or weapons) and align Decree choices accordingly.

Leaving the Circuit between stages (not abandoning, but returning to Duviri navigation) does not save progress -- Circuit progress is saved only on completing each stage fully. Ensure a full run can be completed before starting.
