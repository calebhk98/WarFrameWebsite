## Overview

The Helminth is a biological entity integrated into every Orbiter. It is unlocked through The Sacrifice quest and, once a Helminth Segment is installed, can subsume Warframe abilities. Subsuming a Warframe absorbs one of its abilities into the Helminth's ability pool. That ability can then be infused onto any other Warframe, replacing one of the target Warframe's four base abilities.

The Helminth system transforms Warframe building from a fixed four-ability kit into a partially modular system. Subsuming universally-applicable abilities onto Warframes that lack those specific buffs substantially improves their performance in endgame content. The system also enables niche synergies: infusing a speed buff onto a slow frame, a damage amplifier onto a frame with high built-in survivability, or a utility ability onto a frame that already handles damage through its own kit.

Below is a ranked reference of the 25 highest-priority subsume abilities, organized by impact tier. Rankings reflect community consensus as documented by the Warframe community (r/Warframe meta discussions and Overframe.gg build analytics) combined with in-game mechanics analysis. This list reflects the game state as of the listed `updatedAt` date; ability strength values referenced come from the WFCD `warframe-items` dataset.

## Prerequisites

- The Sacrifice quest completed
- Helminth Segment crafted and installed (the segment blueprint is available from Nightwave Nora Cred shop for 100 Cred)
- Sufficient Helminth resources for each subsume
- Rank 30 duplicate of the warframe to be subsumed

## Steps

Review the priority list below and identify which subsume abilities address gaps in the current build collection. Cross-reference with current Warframe builds to determine where each ability would be most impactful.

Acquire a duplicate of the highest-priority warframe on the list. Run the boss assassination or relic farm for the warframe's components. Build in the Foundry and level the duplicate to rank 30 on Hydron Defense or Adaro Stealth runs.

Feed the duplicate into the Helminth at the Segment console. Subsume takes 23 hours and costs a combination of Helminth resources. The cost varies per warframe but typically includes Bile, Lumacons, Pheromones, Pustulite, and Synthula in moderate quantities. Farm Helminth resources from Infested missions across the Origin System.

Select the newly subsumed ability in the Helminth Segment menu, then navigate to the target Warframe and choose which of its four abilities to replace. The infused ability replaces the selected ability slot permanently (until the infusion is removed, which also costs Helminth resources).

Test the infused configuration in the Simulacrum against Steel Path-level enemies before deploying in missions.

## Tier 1 explanations (ranks 1-5)

**Roar (Rhino):** Roar is the single most impactful subsume in the game for offensive builds. It amplifies all damage from all sources by a percentage dictated by Ability Strength. At base 250% Strength, Roar provides approximately 50% bonus to all warframe damage output and ability damage. This transfers directly onto every frame's damage abilities without interaction restrictions. Rhino is farmable from the Jackal boss on Venus; building a duplicate is straightforward.

**Dispensary (Protea):** Energy management is the limiting factor for many Warframe builds. Dispensary generates three rotating pickups: energy, ammo, and health restores. With sufficient Ability Duration, a single Dispensary cast maintains a constant energy stream. This subsume effectively removes energy as a constraint from any frame it is infused onto. Protea requires purchasing or completing her associated quest.

**Pillage (Hildryn):** Armor stripping is a mandatory function in high-level Grineer content where armor multipliers reduce incoming damage to near-zero. Pillage strips armor and shields in a radius simultaneously. A fully-charged Pillage with 100% Ability Strength strips 50% armor per cast; two casts reach full strip. Hildryn requires Mastery Rank 15 and Archwing segments; plan acquisition accordingly.

**Eclipse (Mirage):** Eclipse's damage boost in bright tile sets (permanent in outdoor maps and well-lit indoor maps) exceeds Roar's bonus multiplicatively in specific configurations. Eclipse stacks with Roar, making a Mirage subsumed onto a Rhino-adjacent frame potentially the highest damage ceiling in the game for a single ability slot. Mirage is farmable from the hidden Orokin lab tileset missions.

**Gloom (Sevagoth):** Gloom's slow aura and lifesteal combination provides one of the most powerful defensive toolsets in the game. Slowing nearby enemies to 15% movement and attack speed effectively neutralizes the threat of fast Grineer or Infested units. The simultaneous lifesteal provides self-sustain proportional to damage dealt. Sevagoth is obtained from Void Storm missions in the Railjack system.

## Resource management for Helminth

Helminth resources accumulate over time through feeding the Helminth segment with equipment and resources. Each resource type is produced by feeding specific item categories:

- Bile: feed Exilus Adapters or specific enemies
- Lumacons: feed Mods
- Pheromones: feed Relics
- Pustulite: feed Arcanes
- Synthula: feed Helminth-exclusive plant items (gathered on Orb Vallis or Deimos)

Feeding the Helminth is free and occurs independently of subsuming. Regular Helminth feeding prevents resource shortages during subsume queues. Feed excess Mods and Relics between subsumes to maintain healthy Lumacons and Pheromone reserves.

## Tips

Prioritize Roar first among all subsumes. Roar's universal damage amplification benefits literally every offensive Warframe build and is available from one of the most accessible bosses in the game (Jackal on Venus). No other single subsume provides comparable cross-build value.

Infuse subsume abilities on slot 1 by convention when replacing a non-critical ability. This keeps the active abilities on keys 2, 3, and 4 in their original positions and reduces muscle-memory errors when switching between Warframes.

Test every infused ability in the Simulacrum at full Ability Strength to confirm it functions as expected on the target Warframe. Some abilities scale differently depending on the host Warframe's base stats or aura configuration.

## Common mistakes

Subsuming the only copy of a rare warframe is irreversible. The subsume destroys the warframe. Always maintain one copy in the Arsenal before subsuming the second copy. For Primed warframes, subsume a non-Prime duplicate when available, preserving the Prime variant.

Replacing a Warframe's kit-defining ability with a subsume ability destroys the frame's synergy. For example, replacing Saryn's Spores with Roar eliminates the frame's core damage vector; the Roar bonus on a Saryn with no damage ability is strictly worse than keeping Spores and removing a less essential ability.

Ignoring Helminth resource capacity causes subsume queues to fail mid-series when resources run out. Monitor the Helminth resource interface regularly and feed between subsuming sessions to prevent shortfalls.
