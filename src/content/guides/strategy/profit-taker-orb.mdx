## Overview

The Profit-Taker Orb is a giant Corpus spider boss on the Orb Vallis (Fortuna) that requires reaching the maximum Solaris United standing rank (Old Mate) before Phase 4 can be attempted. The fight revolves around matching the Profit-Taker's rotating elemental and physical shield type with the appropriate damage type to remove the shield, then using an Archgun (ground-deployed Arch-weapon via Gravimag) to deal direct health damage to the unshielded Profit-Taker.

The Profit-Taker is one of the primary sources of Toroids (high-value rare resources) and is the primary source for Profit-Taker specific Arcanes. It also drops Ventkids standing and Solaris United standing materials.

## Prerequisites

Fortuna's questline begins with Vox Solaris, which introduces Eudico and the Solaris Underground. Advancing through Solaris United standing requires completing bounties and delivering Toroid and resource materials to Eudico. Old Mate standing requires maximum Fortuna reputation tier, which takes several weeks of daily bounty play.

Gravimag installation on an Archgun is a separate requirement from owning the Archgun. The Gravimag blueprint is available from the Clan Dojo's Tenno Lab and requires resources to craft, or it can be purchased directly. Install the Gravimag via the Arsenal's Archgun customization menu -- only Gravimag-equipped Archguns can be deployed in non-Archwing ground missions.

## Steps

Load into Fortuna and accept the Profit-Taker Orb Phase 4 bounty from Eudico. Travel to the Orb Vallis via the transit to Fortuna, then navigate to the Enrichment Labs area where the Profit-Taker spawns.

The Profit-Taker opens with an initial phase requiring the squad to deploy Archguns directly against its body. Deal as much Archgun damage as possible during this initial window before shields activate.

When the Profit-Taker activates its shields, observe the shield type displayed on the HUD indicator or on the Profit-Taker's glowing energy pattern. The shield cycles through a range of damage types including both elemental and physical types (Impact, Heat, Cold, Electric, Toxin, Magnetic, Radiation, and others). Switch weapons or apply the matching damage type to destroy the active shield. Carrying weapons with multiple damage type options or using multiple weapon setups ensures coverage for the full shield rotation.

After the shield breaks, call the Archgun via the Gear Wheel (not the Archwing activation -- Gravimag deployment via gear slot). Maintain sustained fire against the Profit-Taker's body until ammo depletes or the shield reactivates. Archgun Ammo Restore gear items refill deployed Archgun ammo and are essential for long fights or less-experienced teams.

Destroy legs throughout the fight. All legs of the Profit-Taker can be damaged regardless of shield status; destroying all legs simultaneously causes a prone phase where the Profit-Taker is held down and takes bonus damage from all sources.

## Tips

Rhino Prime (with Roar active for squad damage boost) and Chroma Prime (with Vex Armor for critical damage amplification) are the standard damage support frames for Profit-Taker. Volt Prime's electric shield amplifies projectile damage through the shield, benefiting Archgun shots fired through it.

The Corvas Archgun (beam-type Arch-gun) provides consistent sustained damage against the Profit-Taker's body. The Velocitus (charged shot sniper Arch-gun) provides burst damage but requires careful timing between charge shots.

Bring Archgun Ammo Restores (craftable from the gear menu or purchasable from the market) in gear slots. A standard fight may deplete one full Archgun ammo reserve; planning for two deploys is safer for less-experienced squads.

Preparing a secondary weapon that can apply multiple elemental damage types quickly is important for the shield cycling phase. A status-focused Kuva or Tenet weapon with a Corrosive and Heat build covers several shield types simultaneously when both the elemental combination and its component elements activate independently.

## Phase 1-3 overview

Phase 1 (Eavesdrop): introduces the Profit-Taker encounter narrative. The mission involves hacking a Corpus data terminal while under attack by Corpus forces. No Profit-Taker combat occurs; this phase functions as a narrative introduction.

Phase 2 (Heist): players retrieve a Corpus cryopod while being pursued by progressively escalating Corpus forces. The Profit-Taker appears briefly but cannot be attacked. This phase familiarizes players with the Orb Vallis environment and Corpus enemy density at Fortuna-tier levels.

Phase 3 (Steal): players must disable Profit-Taker shield pylons placed around the Orb Vallis surface. Disabling pylons draws Profit-Taker aggression, and the encounter introduces the shield element cycling mechanic in a controlled lower-stakes format before the full Phase 4 fight.

Completing Phases 1-3 in separate sessions (each is its own bounty instance) is normal. The phases unlock permanently upon completion; they do not need to be repeated unless running them for alternative rewards (standing, Toroids).

## Common mistakes

Attempting Phase 4 before completing phases 1-3 in order is not possible -- each phase must be completed in the previous session before the next unlocks. If Phase 4 is unavailable, check that all earlier phases were completed.

Applying the wrong element to the shield deals zero damage. The shield type cycling includes physical damage types as well as elemental types -- prepare coverage for all types, not only the six basic elements.

## Profit-Taker rewards and farming motivation

The Profit-Taker is the most efficient source for Toroids -- rare resource items used for advancing Solaris United standing at high ranks and for several crafting recipes. Three Toroid types drop from Profit-Taker: Calda Toroid, Sola Toroid, and Vega Toroid. Each Toroid type is associated with a specific location on the Orb Vallis and also drops from Profit-Taker itself. Running Phase 4 is the most reliable per-run Toroid acquisition method compared to grinding enemy encounters at specific map locations.

Profit-Taker also drops Profit-Taker Arcanes, a unique subset of arcanes exclusive to this encounter. These include arcanes that benefit players in specific Fortuna-tier content. The Arcane drop pool contains both common utility arcanes and rarer farming-oriented ones; tracking the current drop rates on warframe.fandom.com guides which Arcanes are realistic targets per run count.

## Solaris United standing progression

Old Mate rank requires reaching the maximum Solaris United standing tier. The standing cap per day scales with Mastery Rank: higher MR players can earn more standing per day. Standing is gained through bounty completion (Eudico in Fortuna), Toroid donations (2000 standing per Toroid turned in), and Solaris collectibles found in Orb Vallis.

Toroids donated for standing are consumed and not returned. Plan the Toroid allocation between standing advancement and crafting recipe requirements before donating large quantities. At Old Mate rank, Toroid donation remains available but provides diminished returns relative to the standing cap.

## Phase 4 run optimization

Speed-running Profit-Taker Phase 4 is an established farming meta. Players who optimize their squad composition (Rhino Roar, Volt shield, two damage dealers) and loadout (Corvas Archgun, multi-element status weapons) can complete Phase 4 in under three minutes. The limiting factors are shield cycling speed (dependent on how quickly the damage type switch is executed) and Archgun damage per phase.

For players beginning Profit-Taker farming, planned three-to-five minute runs are more realistic. Focus on shield type recognition and Archgun ammo management first; optimize speed after the mechanics are comfortable.

Profit-Taker runs can be done solo with appropriate builds. A solo Profit-Taker setup requires a frame that can self-sustain against the Profit-Taker's damage output, a versatile multi-element weapon setup for all shield types, and an Archgun capable of dealing meaningful health damage. Solo runs are slower than optimized squads but provide full reward distribution to the solo player.
