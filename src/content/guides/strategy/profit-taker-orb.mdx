## Overview

The Profit-Taker Orb is a giant Corpus spider boss on the Orb Vallis (Fortuna) that requires reaching the maximum Solaris United standing rank (Old Mate) before Phase 4 can be attempted. The fight revolves around matching the Profit-Taker's rotating elemental shield with the appropriate damage type to remove the shield, then using an Archgun (ground-deployed Arch-weapon via Gravimag) to deal direct health damage to the unshielded Profit-Taker.

The Profit-Taker is one of the primary sources of Toroids (high-value rare resources) and is the only guaranteed source for Profit-Taker specific arcanes. It also drops Ventkids standing and Solaris United standing materials.

## Prerequisites

Fortuna's questline begins with Vox Solaris, which introduces Eudico and the Solaris Underground. Advancing through Solaris United standing requires completing bounties and delivering Toroid and resource materials to Eudico. Old Mate standing requires maximum Fortuna reputation tier, which takes several weeks of daily bounty play.

Gravimag installation on an Archgun is a separate requirement from owning the Archgun. The Gravimag blueprint is available from the Clan Dojo's Tenno Lab and requires a Gravimag blueprint, Platinum, or weekly Nightwave cred to obtain. Install the Gravimag via the Arsenal's Archgun customization menu -- only Gravimag-equipped Archguns can be deployed in non-Archwing ground missions.

## Steps

Load into Fortuna and accept the Profit-Taker Orb Phase 4 bounty from Eudico. Travel to the Orb Vallis via the transit to Fortuna, then navigate to the Enrichment Labs area at the top of Orb Vallis where the Profit-Taker spawns.

The Profit-Taker opens with an Archgun phase requiring the squad to deploy Archguns directly against its body before shields activate. Deal as much Archgun damage as possible during this initial window.

When the Profit-Taker activates its shields, observe the shield element displayed on the HUD indicator or on the Profit-Taker's glowing energy pattern. Switch weapons or apply the matching damage type. The shield cycles through all six elemental types over the course of the fight, with each cycle requiring a different matching element. Having a Corrosive-plus-Fire status application weapon covers many of the cycling shield types, but manual ammo type switching (or multiple weapon setups) may be needed for complete coverage.

After the shield breaks, call the Archgun via the Gear Wheel (not the Archwing activation -- Gravimag deployment via gear slot). Maintain sustained fire against the Profit-Taker's body until ammo depletes or the shield reactivates. Archgun Ammo Restore gear items refill deployed Archgun ammo and are essential for long fights or less-experienced teams.

Destroy legs to trigger a stagger phase. All eight legs of the Profit-Taker can be damaged regardless of shield status; destroying all eight simultaneously causes a prone phase where the Profit-Taker is held down and takes bonus damage from all sources.

## Tips

Rhino Prime (with Roar active for squad damage boost) and Chroma Prime (with Vex Armor for critical damage amplification) are the standard damage support frames for Profit-Taker. Volt Prime's electric shield amplifies projectile damage through the shield, benefiting Archgun shots fired through it.

The Corvas Archgun (beam-type Arch-gun) provides consistent sustained damage against the Profit-Taker's body. The Velocitus (charged shot sniper Arch-gun) provides burst damage but requires careful timing between charge shots. At Gravimag rank 40+, both weapons can clear a Profit-Taker health phase in roughly equivalent time.

Bring Archgun Ammo Restores (craftable from the gear menu or purchasable from the market) in gear slots. A standard fight depletes one full Archgun ammo reserve; experienced squads with optimized builds may complete the fight on a single Archgun deploy, but planning for two deploys is safer.

## Common mistakes

Attempting Phase 4 before completing phases 1-3 in order is not possible -- each phase must be completed in the previous session before the next unlocks. If Phase 4 is unavailable, check that all earlier phases were completed.

Applying the wrong element to the shield deals zero damage. The shield type cycling is fixed per rotation but occurs multiple times per fight. Pay attention to the shield indicator and switch damage sources quickly when the shield type changes.
