## Overview

Nightwave is a seasonal reward system in Warframe, hosted by the mysterious pirate radio broadcaster Nora Night. Each Nightwave Series runs for approximately 10 weeks and features a set of Daily, Weekly, and Elite Weekly challenge acts. Completing acts earns Nightwave Standing, which levels up the player's Nightwave rank. Each rank rewards Nora Cred, a currency spent at the Nightwave store for Potatoes (Orokin Reactors and Catalysts), Aura Mods, Helminth Segment blueprint, and cosmetics.

Reaching rank 30 (the base maximum) and then continuing to earn Standing enters the player into Prestige ranks. Each Prestige rank also rewards Nora Cred, extending the currency income beyond the base cap. Maximizing Nightwave completion -- reaching high Prestige ranks -- requires consistent daily and weekly act completion throughout the full Series duration.

Nightwave is one of the most reliable sources of Orokin Reactors and Catalysts in the game. A single Orokin Reactor or Catalyst costs 20 Nora Cred; a Potato from the Market costs 20 platinum. Efficient Nightwave completion replaces a significant platinum expense with game-time investment.

## Prerequisites

- Access to the Nightwave terminal on the Orbiter (available immediately from the beginning of the game)
- Sufficient progression to complete the current Series' acts (some acts require specific content access)
- Knowledge of which acts are currently available in the current Series

## Steps

Establish a daily Nightwave habit. Open the Nightwave terminal at login and review the active acts. Daily acts are the most time-sensitive: they reset every 24 hours and cannot be retroactively claimed. Each Daily act grants 1,000 Standing and typically requires 5 to 15 minutes to complete.

Complete all Daily acts first before addressing Weeklies. Daily acts often align with normal mission play: Capture missions, Ayatan Sculpture collection, Synthesis targets, and resource gathering. Running these acts during normal farming sessions makes them nearly free in terms of extra time investment.

Assess the weekly act list at the start of each Monday reset. Weekly acts refresh every seven days. Prioritize completing Elite Weekly acts (7,000 Standing each) before standard Weeklies (4,500 Standing each). The elite acts typically require longer missions -- Sanctuary Onslaught, Index runs, Steel Path missions -- that benefit from deliberate session scheduling rather than casual play.

Plan the weekly session schedule. Completing all Elite Weeklies and standard Weeklies requires approximately two to four hours of dedicated play per week, depending on act requirements. Spreading this across three sessions per week (roughly one hour each) prevents act fatigue and ensures consistent completion.

Track progress toward rank 30 using the Nightwave terminal's progress bar. Each rank requires approximately 10,000 Standing. At rank 30, the system enters Prestige mode and each subsequent Prestige rank also rewards Nora Cred. Continue completing acts in Prestige mode to maximize Cred income.

## Nora Cred spending priority

| Item | Cost (Nora Cred) | Priority |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Orokin Reactor / Catalyst | 20 each | Highest -- essential for every build |
| Aura Mod (Corrosive Projection, etc.) | 20 each | High -- expensive to obtain otherwise |
| Ayatan Anasa Sculpture | 15 | Medium -- free Endo via Maroo's Bazaar trade |
| Forma Bundle | 40 | Medium -- useful but craftable through normal play |
| Helminth Segment Blueprint | 100 | One-time high priority for new players |
| Nitain Extract | 15 | Low -- also drops from Ghoul Purge and alerts |
| Kuva | 20 per 2,000 Kuva | Low -- better sources exist |

Purchase Orokin Reactors and Catalysts before any other item if a Potato supply deficit exists. New players who lack Reactored and Catalyzed warframes and weapons lose significant build capacity from underpowered equipment. Two Potatoes (Reactor + Catalyst) for a newly built frame and weapon pair cost 40 Cred total -- achievable in the first few weeks of a Series for consistent players.

After Potatoes, purchase Aura Mods that are missing from the collection. Corrosive Projection (reduces enemy armor for the squad), Energy Siphon (passive energy regen), and Steel Charge (melee capacity bonus) are the most universally valuable. Each Aura Mod purchased from Nightwave saves approximately 20 to 60 platinum that would otherwise be spent on trade chat.

## Standing income reference

| Act type | Standing granted | Reset period |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Daily (standard) | 1,000 | 24 hours |
| Weekly (standard) | 4,500 | 7 days |
| Elite Weekly | 7,000 | 7 days |

A full week of maximum completion (3 Dailies per day over 7 days + 3 Weeklies + 2 Elite Weeklies) yields approximately 21,000 + 13,500 + 14,000 = 48,500 Standing per week. Over 10 weeks, this totals approximately 485,000 Standing. At 10,000 per rank, this equates to roughly 48 ranks -- rank 30 plus 18 Prestige ranks -- assuming all acts are completed every week.

Missing one week's worth of acts reduces total Standing by approximately 48,500, which corresponds to roughly four to five Prestige ranks and four to five Nora Cred.

## Act types and preparation

Some Nightwave acts require specific preparation:

**Sanctuary Onslaught (Complete 4 Stages):** Requires a high-damage warframe with good range and AoE. Saryn Prime, Mesa Prime, and Gara Prime are reliable choices. The act completes after stage 4 (wave 8); extract at 20 minutes for the minimum required completion.

**Rescue Mission (Complete without Hostage taking damage):** Requires careful route planning or a long-duration Warframe that can clear enemies before the hostage encounters them. Use Octavia's Mallet ahead of the rescue path to pre-clear enemies.

**Ayatan Sculpture Hunt (Find an Ayatan Sculpture):** Visit Maroo's Bazaar for the weekly guaranteed Ayatan Sculpture mission. The mission takes under 3 minutes and completes this act instantly.

**Synthesis (Synthesize 3 Targets):** Requires Synthesis Scanners from Cephalon Simaris. Purchase scanners from Simaris before attempting; confirm which targets are active in the Synthesis terminal.

**Steel Path (Complete 3 Steel Path missions):** Requires a full endgame build. Steel Path missions count toward this act regardless of mission type; choose fast mission types (Capture, Exterminate) to complete quickly.

## Prestige tracking and Series end

Nightwave Series end dates are announced on the official Warframe forums and the in-game news feed. Series typically end with one to two weeks of advance notice. Ensure all available acts are completed before the end date, as uncompleted acts and uncollected Standing cannot be claimed retroactively after the Series closes.

After a Series ends, Nora Cred expires. Spend all Cred before the Series end date. Priority purchases if Cred is about to expire: Orokin Reactors and Catalysts first, then Aura Mods, then Ayatan Sculptures for Endo.

Intermission Nightwave periods between Series offer a reduced act set with slower Standing accumulation. Completing Intermission acts maintains Nora Cred income during the gap and prevents a standing drought before the next Series launches.

## Tips for stuck acts

Some act requirements are ambiguous. Common confusion points:

- "Complete 3 missions without dying" counts as restarting the mission at the loadout screen. Operator revives do not break the run; dying and being respawned by a squadmate does not break the run. Only the [FAILED] screen at mission failure breaks it.
- "Kill X enemies with a specific element or weapon type" counts kills in any mission type. Run a high-density mission like Survival or Defense to hit kill counts faster.
- "Complete 5 missions on Lua" includes all mission types; Lua is a specific star chart tile set. Run Capture missions on Lua for the fastest completions.

## Common mistakes

Spending Nora Cred on Nitain Extract as a first purchase is inefficient. Nitain Extract also drops from Ghoul Purge bounties and specific alerts; it is not as scarce as Potatoes and Aura Mods, which have fewer reliable acquisition routes outside Nightwave.

Completing all Weekly acts on Monday then ignoring Nightwave for the rest of the week misses all Daily acts for the remaining six days (6,000 Standing lost per day, 36,000 over the remaining week). Weeklies and Dailies must both be consistently tracked.

Not tracking the Series end date leads to rushing incomplete act trees in the final week, which may require more time than is available if the player was unaware of the deadline.

Skipping acts labeled as difficult or time-consuming ignores their Standing value. A 4,500-Standing Weekly takes 30 minutes of targeted play; skipping it forfeits a third of a rank. Evaluate time cost versus Standing value before skipping.
