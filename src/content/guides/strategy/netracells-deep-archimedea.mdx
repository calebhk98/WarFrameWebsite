## Overview

Netracells and Deep Archimedea are two endgame weekly mission types introduced in the Whispers in the Walls and The New War updates respectively. Netracells are research-focused missions within Albrecht's Laboratories tile sets that require hunting and destroying targets hidden in Orokin glass cells. Deep Archimedea applies rotating mod and gameplay restrictions to three consecutive missions, demanding build adaptability from players who rely on a narrow set of optimized loadouts.

Netracells provide standing, exclusive mods (Grimoire arcanes), and Orokin research-related rewards. Deep Archimedea provides Archon Shard Abyss rewards and Incarnon Genesis adapters for participating fully each week.

## Prerequisites

Whispers in the Walls is the quest that introduces Dante, the Grimoire, and the Necraloid faction alongside the Netracell mission type. The quest walkthrough introduces the cell-destruction mechanic and the Albrecht's Laboratories tileset. Completing the questline before attempting weekly Netracells is required for access.

Deep Archimedea requires The New War completion as a separate gating condition. The two systems are independent but share the weekly reset timing, making them natural pairs for a weekly endgame checklist.

## Steps

Navigate to Albrecht's Laboratories via the star chart and select any available Netracell mission. Inside the mission, enemies are standard enemy types for the tileset. The Netracell target is a hidden cell within the environment -- use the mission's objective markers to locate the cell's general area, then scan the room for the glowing Orokin cell object.

Destroying the Netracell completes the primary mission objective. Cells have a health pool and require sustained damage to break. Area-of-effect weapons break cells faster than single-target hitscan weapons. Run five Netracell missions per week to earn the full weekly key bonus (additional rotation C reward).

For Deep Archimedea, review the three weekly restrictions before finalizing loadout. Restrictions commonly include: no specific arcanes, no specific mod sets (Umbral mods, Augur mods), no companion abilities, or no specific warframe ability types. Build a fallback loadout for each restriction category: an energy-management build that does not rely on commonly-restricted arcanes, a survivability build that does not use Umbral mods, etc.

Complete all three Deep Archimedea missions in sequence. The three missions each have their own restriction on top of the shared weekly restrictions. Mission 1 introduces the restrictions at minimal enemy level; missions 2 and 3 escalate enemy level and restriction severity. Plan the most restriction-resilient build for mission 3.

## Tips

Dante's Noctua provides an energy-generation alternative in Deep Archimedea weeks where energy restoration arcanes are restricted. Protea Prime's Dispensary is a second option for energy provision without arcane dependency.

Adapting to Deep Archimedea restrictions is the primary skill floor for this content. Players who maintain two or three viable warframe builds across different stat profiles (strength-focused, tank, support) adapt to most restriction combinations without requiring new frame investment.

Netracell targets sometimes spawn in locked rooms with puzzle-activated doors. Inspect all side rooms and scan consoles to unlock restricted access areas before concluding the cell is absent from obvious locations.

Completing Netracell missions quickly (5-8 minutes each) makes the five-weekly-run requirement manageable within a short play session. Bring frames that clear rooms quickly to locate the cell and extract efficiently rather than spending excess time on enemy encounters.

Deep Archimedea rewards are tied to completing all three missions in a single weekly attempt. Partial completion (1-2 missions) yields reduced rewards. Plan the full three-mission set before starting to ensure a complete run is achievable in one session.

## Whispers in the Walls content integration

The Whispers in the Walls update introduced Dante, a warframe with abilities tied to the Albrecht's Laboratories setting. Dante's Tragedy ability synergizes with the Overguard mechanic, making him a strong frame for Netracell content where Eximus units are common. His Noctua primary weapon provides on-hit energy generation independent of arcanes, making him naturally compatible with Deep Archimedea restrictions that limit energy sources.

The Grimoire, introduced in the same update, is a secondary weapon with unique damage properties useful in Albrecht tileset missions. Grimoire projectiles interact with specific environmental objects in the Albrecht's Laboratories in ways that accelerate mission completion in some configurations.

Orokin Research standing obtained through Netracell rewards contributes to Necraloid syndicate standing (introduced in Deimos content). Necraloid provides Helminth subsumable abilities via its own standing path, making Netracell farming doubly valuable for players who want to expand their Helminth library.

## Common mistakes

Bringing a single specialized build to Deep Archimedea that relies entirely on one mod or arcane that the weekly restriction removes. The restriction removal is absolute -- the restricted item is unequippable for the session, not just reduced in effect. Check all three mission restrictions before squad formation.

Completing only 1-4 Netracells per week and missing the fifth bonus reward. Each run takes 5-8 minutes; the fifth run's bonus reward is proportionally valuable given the marginal time investment.

Starting Deep Archimedea with mission 3's build configuration for all three missions without accounting for restriction escalation. Mission 1 allows testing the restriction-adjusted loadout at lower stakes before enemy scaling increases in missions 2 and 3.

## Netracell mission structure

Albrecht's Laboratories missions occur in the Orokin-themed tileset introduced with Whispers in the Walls. The tileset features research labs, glass corridors, and Void-infused environmental hazards. Netracell objectives consist of locating a glowing Orokin glass cell structure within the mission's exploration area and destroying it.

Cells are not marked directly on the map at the start of the mission; the objective marker leads to the general area, requiring manual search in adjacent rooms. Cells always appear in enclosed rooms or at the end of hallways. Bringing area-scan abilities (Ivara's Prowl combined with loot radar, or Limbo's Banish revealing loot through walls) speeds up cell location.

Once located, the cell must be destroyed. Cells absorb damage from all sources; high-rate-of-fire weapons or AoE abilities deplete the health pool faster than single-shot weapons. After destruction, the mission completes and rewards are distributed. Netracell missions do not require fighting to the extraction point -- the cell destruction ends the mission immediately.

## Deep Archimedea restriction categories

Restrictions in Deep Archimedea fall into predictable categories that repeat across rotation weeks. Preparing counter-builds for each category eliminates preparation failures:

Arcane restrictions: specific arcanes are disabled. Common restricted arcanes include Arcane Energize and Arcane Avenger. Counter-build: an energy-sustain frame (Energizing Dash from Zenurik, Protea Dispensary, or Helminth Pillage) that does not depend on Arcane Energize.

Mod set restrictions: specific mod sets are disabled (Umbral mods, Augur mods, Acolyte mods). Counter-build: a survivability configuration that uses standard Vitality + Adaptation without Umbral or Augur sets.

Companion restrictions: companions or companion abilities are disabled. Bring a companion-independent build that does not rely on sentinel buffs or Kavat buffs.

Warframe restriction: specific warframes may be banned entirely for that week's Deep Archimedea. Rotate to a secondary frame for the restricted week.

Building a two-build rotation for Deep Archimedea covers most restriction combinations: a standard steel path build with full optimization, and a restriction-tolerant build that strips out mod sets and arcanes in favor of standalone mods. The restriction-tolerant build performs below full optimization but functions across nearly all weekly restriction types.
