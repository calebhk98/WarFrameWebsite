## Overview

Archon Hunts are weekly three-mission challenges that culminate in a boss fight against one of three Archon variants: Boreal (cold-element, Corpus faction missions), Nira (toxin-element, Infested faction missions), and Amar (heat-element, Grineer faction missions). Each Archon drops an Archon Shard on defeat -- Amber Shards from Amar, Crimson Shards from Nira, and Azure Shards from Boreal. These shards are installed in warframe slots to provide permanent stat bonuses that scale with the warframe's base stats.

Archon Hunts refresh on a weekly reset and are the primary means of acquiring Archon Shards for endgame min-maxing. Since warframes have up to five shard slots and shards can be upgraded using multiple shards of the same type, long-term Archon Hunt participation is the expected path to fully shard-optimized frames.

## Prerequisites

The New War quest is a mandatory gate for Archon Hunt access. The quest introduces the Archon characters as antagonists and establishes the weekly system. Completing The New War also unlocks Kahl's Garrison (weekly missions for additional build resources) and expands the available weekly endgame content significantly.

Archon Hunt missions have no stated Mastery Rank minimum, but enemy levels reach 100-120, making standard starmap builds ineffective. Survivability-first builds are recommended -- Archon boss mechanics deal burst damage that can eliminate underbuilt warframes in two or three hits.

## Steps

Navigate to the Archon Hunt via the relay terminal or directly from the navigation screen's special missions category. The three missions must be completed in order: two preparatory missions (exterminate-type and a disruption or defense variant) followed by the Archon boss fight.

During the Archon boss fight, Archon Crystals spawn around the arena periodically. These crystals sustain the Archon's health regeneration if left active. Identify crystal locations on the minimap and destroy them immediately on spawn before they restore significant boss health. Crystals are vulnerable to all damage types.

Each Archon has a specific elemental weakness. Amar (the heat Archon associated with fire) takes bonus damage from cold weapons; Nira (the toxin Archon) takes bonus damage from heat; Boreal (the cold Archon) also takes bonus damage from heat. Check the weekly Archon's element before loadout selection and prioritize weapons with the matching damage type.

After defeating the Archon, extract and receive the Archon Shard reward. Navigate to the Arsenal in a relay and access the Archon Shard interface to install shards into warframe slots. Each slot accepts one shard and provides a permanent bonus to the installed warframe based on shard type and color.

## Tips

Tauforged Archon Shards are a rare enhanced variant of standard shards that provide 50% higher bonuses. They are obtained randomly instead of standard shards during Archon Hunt completions at a lower probability than standard shards. High-priority slots (power strength bonuses for critical ability frames) are where Tauforged Shards provide maximum return.

Arcane Adapter installation on melee weapons adds a second source of stat bonuses alongside Archon Shards, creating compounding stat gains for melee-heavy builds. Plan shard installation around weapon usage to ensure shard bonuses complement the primary damage source.

Archon Hunt disruption missions require completing conduit sequences without allowing more than one conduit failure. Bring a frame with reliable enemy disruption or movement speed to reach conduits quickly. Volt Prime (for speed buffs) or Nova Prime (for slowing demolisher units) are common picks for disruption preparatory missions.

Amber Shards from Amar grant bonuses related to ability strength and weapon critical stats. Crimson Shards from Nira offer health, armor, and status duration bonuses. Azure Shards from Boreal provide energy-related bonuses. Plan shard configurations around each frame's core stat requirements before collecting shards to avoid mismatched installations.

## Archon Shard slot unlocking

Warframes begin with some Archon Shard slots locked. Slots unlock progressively as the frame reaches higher rank (rank 30 unlocks all standard slots). To equip Archon Shards, the frame must be at rank 30 and the Archon Shard interface accessed via the Arsenal in a relay or the orbiter. The interface shows all shard slot positions, currently-installed shards, and bonus options for the selected shard type.

Standard shards provide bonuses worth planning around: Azure Shards commonly add 50 energy max or 25% energy efficiency, meaningful on frames that run energy-heavy builds without Zenurik. Amber Shards adding 10% ability strength per shard across multiple slots provide cumulative power strength bonus equivalent to equipping an extra Intensify at reduced mod capacity cost.

## Common mistakes

Placing Archon Shards on frames or in slots that do not align with actual gameplay needs wastes a weekly investment. Plan shard configuration in advance, identifying which stat the frame needs most at the ability or base level. Power strength shards on ability-heavy frames and armor shards on health-scaling tanks are generally the highest-priority slots.

Not verifying which Archon is active before entering results in mismatched elemental loadout. Each Archon in the weekly rotation has a different weak element; check the rotation before configuring weapons.

Archon Hunt completions do not provide additional rewards beyond the shard for replaying the same weekly rotation. Each weekly Archon Hunt completion grants one shard. Players who want to accumulate shards faster must wait for the weekly reset. Trading for duplicate shard types from other players is not possible -- Archon Shards are account-bound and cannot be transferred.

Kahl's Garrison missions, unlocked after completing The New War and the Veilbreaker questline, provide an adjacent weekly endgame activity. Kahl missions are played as the Grineer soldier Kahl-175 with a separate ability set. Completing Kahl missions earns Garrison stock items used to purchase additional Archon Shard duplicates, warframe component blueprints (Styanax, Grendel), and Incarnon seeds for the Duviri Circuit. Combining Archon Hunts and Kahl missions in the same weekly session covers the majority of the structured weekly endgame checklist.

## Archon Shard installation strategy

Each warframe has five Archon Shard slots that accept one shard per slot. Shards provide different bonus types based on color (Amber from Amar, Crimson from Nira, Azure from Boreal). Within each color, the player selects which specific bonus the shard provides from a preset list for that shard type.

Amber Shard bonus examples: bonus ability strength, critical chance when above 50% health. Crimson Shard bonus examples: bonus health, armor, status duration. Azure Shard bonus examples: bonus energy max, shields, energy regeneration rate.

Plan shard installation around the frame's intended role before spending shards. A Saryn Prime optimized for Spore spread benefits most from ability strength and range bonuses from Amber and specific Azure shards. An Inaros Prime tank benefits from Crimson armor and health bonuses. Shards can be removed and transferred to other frames, but doing so costs the shard -- the removed shard is lost rather than returned. Purchase another shard from Archon Hunt completion to replace it.

## Tauforged Shard mechanics

Tauforged Shards provide 50% larger bonuses than standard shards. The probability of receiving a Tauforged Shard instead of a standard shard on any given completion is not guaranteed; it is a subset of the normal completion reward pool. Players doing long-term Archon Hunt participation progressively accumulate Tauforged Shards for high-priority slots.

Priority order for Tauforged Shard installation: ability strength shards on frames that scale heavily with power strength (Saryn, Nidus, Mirage); armor shards on tank builds (Inaros, Rhino) where the armor bonus scales with base armor values; energy shards on frames that spend the most energy per cast (Trinity, Octavia).

Standard shards are used to fill slots while waiting for the desired Tauforged variant. A fully standard-shard-filled frame is still a meaningful progression step; Tauforged shards in the same slots are an incremental upgrade rather than a categorical difference in most cases.
