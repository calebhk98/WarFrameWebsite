## Overview

Archon Hunts are weekly three-mission challenges that culminate in a boss fight against one of three Archon variants: Boreal (cold-element, Corpus faction missions), Nira (toxin-element, Infested faction missions), and Amar (heat-element, Grineer faction missions). Each Archon drops an Archon Shard on defeat -- Amber Shards from Amar, Crimson Shards from Nira, and Azure Shards from Boreal. These shards are installed in warframe slots to provide permanent stat bonuses that scale with the warframe's base stats.

Archon Hunts refresh on a weekly reset and are the primary means of acquiring Archon Shards for endgame min-maxing. Since warframes have up to five shard slots and shards can be upgraded using multiple shards of the same type, long-term Archon Hunt participation is the expected path to fully shard-optimized frames.

## Prerequisites

The New War quest is a mandatory gate for Archon Hunt access. The quest introduces the Archon characters as antagonists and establishes the weekly system. Completing The New War also unlocks Kahl's Garrison (weekly missions for additional build resources) and expands the available weekly endgame content significantly.

Archon Hunt missions have no stated Mastery Rank minimum, but enemy levels reach 100-120, making standard starmap builds ineffective. Survivability-first builds are recommended -- Archon boss mechanics deal burst damage that can eliminate underbuilt warframes in two or three hits.

## Steps

Navigate to the Archon Hunt via the relay terminal or directly from the navigation screen's special missions category. The three missions must be completed in order: two preparatory missions (exterminate-type and a disruption or defense variant) followed by the Archon boss fight.

During the Archon boss fight, Archon Crystals spawn around the arena periodically. These crystals sustain the Archon's health regeneration if left active. Identify crystal locations on the minimap and destroy them immediately on spawn before they restore significant boss health. Crystals are vulnerable to all damage types.

Each Archon has a specific elemental weakness. Amar (heat/fire Archon) takes bonus damage from cold weapons; Nira (toxin Archon) takes bonus damage from heat; Boreal (cold Archon) takes bonus damage from heat as well. Check the weekly Archon's element before loadout selection and prioritize weapons with the matching damage type.

After defeating the Archon, extract and receive the Archon Shard reward. Navigate to the Arsenal in a relay and access the Archon Shard interface to install shards into warframe slots. Each slot accepts one shard and provides a permanent bonus to the installed warframe based on shard type and color.

## Tips

Tauforged Archon Shards are a rare enhanced variant of standard shards that provide 50% higher bonuses. Tauforged Shards are obtained randomly instead of standard shards during Archon Hunt completions. The probability of a Tauforged Shard is approximately 20% per completion. High-priority slots (power strength bonuses for critical ability frames) are where Tauforged Shards provide maximum return.

Arcane Adapter installation on melee weapons (via Steel Essence exchange at Teshin) adds a second source of stat bonuses alongside Archon Shards, creating compounding stat gains for melee-heavy builds. Plan shard installation around weapon usage to ensure shard bonuses complement the primary damage source.

Archon Hunt disruption missions require completing conduit sequences without allowing more than one conduit failure. Bring a frame with reliable enemy disruption or movement speed to reach conduits quickly. Volt Prime (for speed buffs) or Nova Prime (for slowing demolisher units) are common picks for disruption preparatory missions.

## Common mistakes

Placing Archon Shards on frames or in slots that do not align with actual gameplay needs wastes a weekly investment. Plan shard configuration in advance, identifying which stat the frame needs most at the ability or base level. Power strength shards on ability-heavy frames and armor shards on health-scaling tanks are generally the highest-priority slots.

Not verifying which Archon is active before entering results in mismatched elemental loadout. Each Archon in the weekly rotation has a different weak element; check the rotation before configuring weapons.
