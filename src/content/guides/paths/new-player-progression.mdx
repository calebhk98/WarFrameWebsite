## Overview

Warframe's progression is driven by Mastery Rank, which increases as the player levels warframes, weapons, and companions to rank 30. Mastery Rank gates access to specific weapons, features, and game modes. The path from new player to endgame involves a series of unlocks: quests that introduce new mechanics, Junction completions that open new planets, and Mastery Rank thresholds that expand available content.

Unlike many games, Warframe's power comes primarily from the Mod system rather than raw item level. A maxed Serration mod on a rank 0 weapon provides more damage than a rank 30 weapon with no mods. Understanding the Mod system early and investing in ranking up key mods (Serration, Vitality, Redirection, Streamline, Continuity) provides the foundation for all future progression.

The structure above follows the natural unlock order of Warframe's star chart. Individual rates of progression vary significantly based on playtime; a player who runs 2 hours per day will typically reach Steel Path in 3 to 4 weeks, while a casual player (30 minutes per day) may take 3 to 4 months to reach the same milestone.

## Prerequisites

- A Warframe account (free-to-play; no purchase required)
- The game client downloaded and installed
- The Awakening tutorial completed

## Steps

Follow the milestone order above sequentially. Each milestone builds on the previous, and skipping ahead is rarely possible due to Junction and quest requirements that enforce the intended unlock sequence.

The most common new player mistake is rushing through content without leveling equipment fully. Every warframe, weapon, and companion leveled to rank 30 grants Mastery XP. Selling unneeded equipment before rank 30 permanently forfeits that Mastery XP.

Use the in-game Codex to track quest unlock requirements. The Codex (accessible from the navigation menu) lists every quest, its prerequisites, and whether it is currently available. Working through the main quest chain in order (Vor's Prize, the Second Dream, The War Within, Chains of Harrow, Sacrifice, New War) reveals the core narrative while naturally unlocking key game systems.

## Junction order guide

Junctions are special nodes on the star chart that bridge planets. Each Junction has a checklist of requirements:

| Junction | From | Key requirement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Venus Junction | Mercury | Complete 3 Mercury missions |
| Mars Junction | Venus | Kill 150 enemies with Heat damage |
| Phobos Junction | Mars | Complete the Archwing quest |
| Ceres Junction | Mars | Kill 100 enemies with Slash damage |
| Jupiter Junction | Ceres | Complete a Spy mission |
| Europa Junction | Jupiter | Defeat the Raptors boss |
| Saturn Junction | Europa | Kill 500 enemies with Cold damage |
| Uranus Junction | Saturn | Defeat the Hyena Pack boss |
| Neptune Junction | Uranus | Kill 150 enemies with Toxin damage |
| Pluto Junction | Neptune | Complete 3 Void missions |
| Sedna Junction | Pluto | Defeat the Ambulas boss |
| Void Junction | Sedna | Complete Natah quest |
| Kuva Fortress Junction | Void | Complete The War Within quest |

Completing all Junctions on a planet unlocks every node on that planet and progresses the story quests that require specific planet access.

## Mastery Rank gate reference

Key Mastery Rank thresholds and what they unlock:

| MR | Unlocks |
| ---: | --- |
| 2 | Player-to-player trading enabled |
| 5 | Syndicates available; The War Within minimum requirement |
| 8 | Most mid-game weapons; full Syndicate standing access |
| 10 | Riven Mod equipping; many high-tier weapons |
| 12 | Advanced secondary weapons; some Prime variants |
| 16 | High-tier primary weapons |
| 20 | Top-tier weapons; most build-critical items unlocked |
| 30 | Maximum Mastery Rank (as of update cycle) |

Reaching MR8 provides access to the primary trade economy and Syndicate standing system. MR10 enables Riven Mods. MR16 and above unlock the best weapons but are not required for any specific content type.

## Recommended mods for new players

Prioritize acquiring these mods before worrying about Primed or Corrupted variants:

For Warframes:
- Vitality (health; drops universally from enemies)
- Redirection (shield; drops universally)
- Streamline (ability efficiency; drops from Grineer Ballista)
- Continuity (ability duration; drops from Grineer Warden)
- Intensify (ability strength; drops from Infested Runners)

For primary weapons:
- Serration (damage; very common drop or purchasable for Credits)
- Point Strike (crit chance; drops from Corpus Crewman)
- Vital Sense (crit damage; drops from Corpus Sniper Crewman)
- Infected Clip (Toxin damage; drops from Infested Swarm Moa)

These mods are not Primed and do not require rare drops; they are available from normal mission play and establish a functional build foundation before Primed and Corrupted mods become available at later Mastery Ranks.

## Tips

Warframe's global chat is active on every login. The "Trade" channel in the chat sidebar allows player-to-player trading. At MR2+, use the trade channel to acquire key mods that have not yet dropped naturally. Common mods traded at low prices include Streamline, Continuity, and Flow.

Daily login rewards provide a rotating selection of resources, mods, and occasionally Orokin Reactors. Logging in every day accumulates these bonuses even without extended play sessions. The login reward system also provides a Milestone reward at specific day thresholds (7, 30, 50, 100 days, etc.).

Foundry build times run in real time, including offline. Queue multiple builds before logging out for the day; the builds complete while the player is away and are ready for collection on the next login.

The WARFRAME Wiki at [warframe.fandom.com](https://warframe.fandom.com) is comprehensive and regularly updated. Any question about drop locations, quest requirements, or mechanics is answered there with citations to patch notes and verified data.

## Common mistakes

Selling or dismantling equipment before rank 30 forfeits the Mastery XP from that item permanently. Always rank items to 30 before disposal; leveling an item to rank 30 in the Simulacrum or during a mission takes roughly 30 to 60 minutes for most weapons.

Purchasing warframes or weapons from the Market for Credits without checking whether they are farmable through boss assassination or crafting wastes Credits that could go toward essential mods. Most warframes are farmable; only Prime variants require platinum or relic farming.

Ignoring the Mod system and relying on raw item ranks alone provides insufficient power for mid-game missions. A rank 0 Warframe with maxed mods outperforms a rank 30 Warframe with empty mod slots. Invest Credits in Mod Fusion early.

Attempting to rush to the endgame by leveling only the minimum equipment to pass Mastery tests leaves large gaps in Mastery XP that eventually require backtracking. Level everything methodically from the start.

## Orokin Reactors and Catalysts

Orokin Reactors (gold Potatoes) double the mod capacity of a Warframe. Orokin Catalysts (blue Potatoes) double the mod capacity of a weapon. Without a Reactor or Catalyst, a Warframe has a mod capacity of 30 at rank 30, which is insufficient to equip a full set of high-drain mods. With an Orokin Reactor installed, the same frame has a capacity of 60, enabling a complete endgame build.

Orokin Reactors and Catalysts are scarce for new players. Primary sources include:

- Nightwave Nora Cred shop (20 Cred per Reactor or Catalyst) -- the most reliable source
- Daily login milestone rewards at specific consecutive login day thresholds
- Alert missions (rare, but check the in-game Alerts section daily)
- Invasions that offer Potatoes as faction bribes for completing a mission chain
- The WARFRAME Market (purchase with platinum from other players)

Install Orokin Reactors and Catalysts on primary build weapons and the main active Warframe first. Do not install them on equipment that will be sold after ranking to 30, as the Potato is consumed and cannot be recovered on sale. Plan Potato usage carefully; they are limited and each installation is permanent on that equipment.

## Managing Credits and the economy

Credits are the primary in-game currency earned from missions. They fund mod fusion (upgrading mods to higher ranks), Foundry builds, Market purchases, and syndicate standing offerings. New players frequently run out of Credits during the mod fusion phase because Primed mods at high ranks cost tens of thousands of Credits per rank-up.

The fastest early-game Credit farm is the Dark Sector missions on Ceres (Seimeni) and Eris (Hieracon), which have a Credit income bonus from their Dark Sector status. Running five waves of Seimeni Defense yields more Credits than a standard mission of equivalent length.

Later in progression, the Index on Neptune becomes the primary Credit source. The Index is a competitive mission where players earn Credits by banking points against a financial limit. Medium-risk Index runs return 200,000+ Credits per completion. Index access requires a functional high-damage build since enemies scale to match the risk tier.

## Social systems and clan joining

Warframe has a Clan system that provides access to a Dojo, which hosts trading rooms, research labs, and crafting stations for Dojo-exclusive items. Joining an existing clan is the fastest way to access Dojo research without building a Dojo from scratch.

Clan trade rooms enable in-game trading without using the Trade Chat. Items traded through a clan Dojo do not require meeting in a relay. Joining any active clan, even a solo or ghost clan, provides access to Dojo facilities.

Research labs in the Dojo allow clan members to unlock research blueprints for weapons and gear not available elsewhere. Clan research requires resources donated by members; newer players contribute what they can while benefiting from research unlocked by established members.
