## Overview

Railjack Intrinsics are the progression system governing Railjack crew competency. Five Intrinsic categories exist: Piloting, Gunnery, Engineering, Tactical, and Command. Each category has 10 ranks. Intrinsic ranks unlock passive improvements and active abilities for the player's role within the Railjack. Higher Intrinsic ranks are required to fully operate an endgame Railjack configuration.

Intrinsic Points are earned from Affinity generated during Railjack missions. All kills -- by the player, by NPC crew, by the Railjack itself -- contribute to the Affinity pool that converts to Intrinsic Points at mission end. Intrinsic Points are then spent in the Intrinsics menu to purchase individual ranks.

The fastest Intrinsic farming location in the game is Gian Point in the Veil Proxima. Veil Proxima is the highest tier of Railjack proxima and has the densest fighter and crewship spawn rates. Completing Gian Point missions in 5 to 10 minutes with a capable crew yields significantly more Intrinsic Points per hour than lower proxima tiers.

Command Intrinsics are the highest-priority category for most players because they unlock NPC crew members who can pilot, gun, and repair the Railjack autonomously. A fully crewed Railjack with Command Intrinsics at rank 9 or 10 can function in Veil Proxima with minimal manual intervention, freeing the player to focus on boarding phases and objective completion.

## Prerequisites

- Rising Tide quest completed (provides the Railjack dry dock and enables Railjack construction)
- A built Railjack (requires farming component blueprints or purchasing from the Market)
- Proxima access corresponding to the target farming node: Veil Proxima requires completing the Neptune Proxima chain first
- For Gian Point specifically: completion of the Earth, Saturn, and Neptune Proxima mission chains

## Steps

Build and configure the Railjack before beginning Intrinsic farming. An underpowered Railjack takes excessive damage in Veil Proxima and extends mission time significantly. At minimum, equip Mk III components (Shield, Engine, Reactor, Plating) before running Veil Proxima nodes. Railjack components are crafted from parts dropped in Proxima missions or purchased from the Market.

Unlock the Veil Proxima by completing the mission chain: Earth Proxima (Venus, Earth) to Saturn Proxima (Pluto, Saturn) to Neptune Proxima to Veil Proxima. Each proxima tier has an associated mission chain that must be completed before higher-tier missions are available.

Navigate to Gian Point in Veil Proxima. This node has the highest fighter and crewship spawn rate in the game. Destroy all fighters and crewships as quickly as possible. Each kill contributes Affinity to the Intrinsic pool. Completing the mission objective (varies by node type) and extracting ends the mission and converts accumulated Affinity to Intrinsic Points.

Spend Intrinsic Points in the Intrinsics menu after each mission. The recommended spending priority (see the table below) is Command first, then Tactical, then the remaining categories based on preferred playstyle.

Run repeat Gian Point missions until Command Intrinsics reach rank 9 (NPC crew slots unlock progressively at ranks 1, 3, 5, 7, and 9 of Command). Once the third crew member slot is unlocked at Command rank 9, configure NPC crew to fill Gunner, Engineer, and Pilot roles to maximize Railjack autonomy.

## Intrinsic category priority guide

| Category | Key ranks | Primary benefit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Command | 1, 3, 5, 7, 9 | Unlock crew member slots; crew farms Intrinsics passively |
| Tactical | 5 | Tactical Blink for fast-travel from Railjack to boarding target |
| Gunnery | 5, 9 | Improved turret efficiency; Void Hole ability at rank 9 |
| Engineering | 4, 7 | Battle Forge at rank 4 (emergency resources); max Forge efficiency |
| Piloting | 5 | Boost duration and afterburner efficiency |

Command ranks 1 through 9 represent the single most impactful Intrinsic investment in the tree. At Command rank 9, the third crew slot enables a fully autonomous Railjack crew (pilot, gunner, engineer) while the player boards enemy crewships or completes ground objectives. This configuration is the foundation of efficient Veil Proxima farming.

## NPC crew configuration

NPC crew members are hired from the Ticker in Fortuna or from the Relay market. Each crew member has randomized stats in three skill categories: Piloting, Gunnery, Engineering, Combat, and Endurance. Hire crew members with high scores in the role they will occupy on the Railjack.

Configure crew assignments in the Railjack configuration menu:

- Slot 1 (Pilot): Assign a crew member with high Piloting score. The pilot keeps the Railjack moving and away from hazards during boarding phases.
- Slot 2 (Gunner): Assign a crew member with high Gunnery score. The gunner targets fighters and crewships automatically.
- Slot 3 (Engineer): Assign a crew member with high Engineering score. The engineer repairs hull breaches and deposits resources into the Forge automatically.

NPC crew members with high Combat and Endurance scores also perform better during boarding defense when the player is away from the Railjack on a ground objective.

## Proxima tier comparison

| Proxima tier | Fighter level | Intrinsics per 10 min (estimate) | Notes |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Earth Proxima | 1-30 | 300-500 | Starting zone; low yield |
| Saturn Proxima | 20-40 | 500-800 | Mid-tier; viable for early Intrinsics |
| Neptune Proxima | 30-50 | 800-1,200 | Good yield; required gateway to Veil |
| Veil Proxima | 40-60 | 1,500-2,500+ | Highest yield; Gian Point optimal |

Veil Proxima yields two to five times the Intrinsic Points per hour compared to Neptune Proxima due to fighter density and crewship spawn frequency. Transitioning to Veil Proxima as soon as the Railjack meets the minimum survivability threshold is the single largest efficiency gain available in Intrinsic leveling.

## Tips

Bring a resilient warframe to Gian Point. During boarding phases, enemies on Veil Proxima crewships are level 40-60. Inaros Prime, Rhino Prime, or Octavia Prime provide survivability for board-and-destroy phases without requiring active energy management.

The Railjack's Dome Charge ability (available from specific Railjack battle avionics) damages crewships from the Railjack itself. Configuring Dome Charges reduces the number of boarding events needed per mission, accelerating mission completion.

Run Gian Point in a squad of four with all four players owning capable Railjacks. Squadron Railjack missions allow two Railjacks; more players means faster enemy clearing and shorter mission times. A coordinated squad can complete Gian Point in under 5 minutes, maximizing Intrinsic Points per hour.

Railjack missions also yield Bile, Carbides, Fresnels, and other crafting resources needed for avionics and Railjack upgrades. Running Intrinsic missions simultaneously collects materials needed for Railjack improvements, making Intrinsic farming double as equipment upgrade grinding.

## Common mistakes

Running Earth or Saturn Proxima for Intrinsics beyond the tutorial phase is significantly less efficient than Veil Proxima. The Intrinsic per hour rate difference is large enough to justify building toward Veil access before settling into a farming routine.

Ignoring Command Intrinsics in favor of maxing other categories first delays access to NPC crew, which is the largest quality-of-life upgrade in Railjack. Without crew, the player must manually manage piloting, gunnery, engineering, and boarding simultaneously, which is both inefficient and stressful.

Neglecting to upgrade the Railjack's components before running Veil Proxima results in constant hull breach events and excessive repair time, reducing mission efficiency. Invest in Mk III components before transitioning to Veil.

Skipping the mission objective to focus only on fighter kills is not always faster. Completing the mission objective triggers the extraction timer and converts all accumulated Affinity to Intrinsic Points. Staying in mission indefinitely without extracting eventually reaches diminishing returns as fighter density decreases.
