## Overview

Focus Points are the Operator progression currency unlocked after completing The Second Dream quest. They fund Focus School ability nodes -- passive and active Operator powers that augment both the Operator and the bonded warframe. Five Focus schools exist: Zenurik, Naramon, Vazarin, Unairu, and Madurai. Each has a distinct playstyle benefit; most players develop one primary school to completion before investing in others.

The daily Focus cap is 250,000 points. Once the cap is reached, no additional Focus can be earned until the next daily server reset. Efficient Focus farming aims to hit the cap in as short a session as possible.

Focus is earned through the Convergence system: Convergence Orbs spawn periodically during missions and, when collected, multiply Focus gain by 8x for 45 seconds. Outside of Convergence windows, Focus gain from enemy kills is negligible. The practical Focus farming strategy therefore revolves entirely around collecting Convergence Orbs and maximizing kills within each 45-second window.

Eidolon hunts on the Plains of Eidolon provide the highest Focus yield per session for players with the correct amp and warframe setup. Madurai's Void Strike ability converts accumulated Operator kills into massive Focus bursts, enabling single-hunt sessions that approach or reach the daily 250,000 cap.

## Prerequisites

- The Second Dream quest completed (required to unlock Operators and Focus Schools)
- A built Amp (higher tier Amps yield more damage against Eidolon synovias, critical for Eidolon Focus farming)
- Access to Cetus (for Eidolon Focus farming) or Lua / Zariman (for alternative routes)
- Focus Lens equipped on warframe and weapons (required to convert Affinity into Focus)
- At least one Greater Focus Lens for the primary school (obtained from Eidolon hunts or Baro Ki'Teer)

For Eidolon Focus farming specifically:
- Saya's Vigil quest completed (provides access to the Plains of Eidolon night cycle)
- An Eidolon-viable warframe (Volt for shields, Trinity for energy/healing, Harrow for critical damage)
- Void Strike unlocked in Madurai school (for the burst Focus method)

## Steps

Install a Focus Lens on the warframe and on at least one weapon being used during the farming session. Without a lens, Affinity from kills does not convert to Focus. Greater Lenses provide double the conversion rate of standard lenses; prioritize equipping Greater Lenses on the slots that accumulate the most Affinity (primary weapon for kill-heavy missions, warframe for ability-kill missions).

Choose the appropriate Focus farming method based on current progression:

**Convergence Orb method (all progression stages):** Run any mission type and collect Convergence Orbs. Maximize kills within the 8x multiplier window after each orb pickup. High-density survival or defense missions spawn more enemies per unit time, increasing Focus generated per Convergence window. Collect every Convergence Orb seen; each orb lasts 45 seconds, which is the only window where Focus gain is meaningful.

**Madurai Void Strike method (Eidolon-capable players):** Before entering the Plains at nightfall, open the Operator menu and activate Void Strike. Void Strike does not activate immediately; it stores Operator kills as exponential multiplier stacks while you remain in Operator mode. Spend 45 minutes before the Eidolon hunt killing enemies in Operator mode across any mission to accumulate maximum Void Strike charges. Then enter the Plains at night and use the stored charges against Eidolon synovias. The resulting Focus burst from each synovia break can reach tens of thousands per strike.

**Lua Disruption method (post-star-chart completion):** Run Apollo on Lua in Disruption mode. Thrax Centurion enemies spawn during later conduit rounds and yield high Focus on kill. This method does not require Eidolon access and is sustainable solo with a high-damage warframe.

**Zariman Garv mission method (Whispers in the Walls access required):** Complete the Garv standing mission on Zariman Ten Zero. Void Angel-tier enemies on Zariman grant large amounts of Affinity and Focus. Completing the Garv objective while maintaining kills across the map maximizes Focus per session.

Track the daily Focus cap. Once 250,000 is earned across all schools, no further Focus can be generated until reset. Plan sessions to hit the cap cleanly each day rather than running long sessions that yield diminishing returns.

## Focus school priority guide

| School | Primary benefit | Recommended for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Zenurik | Energizing Dash restores energy over time to nearby warframes | Almost all warframe archetypes; energy-hungry builds |
| Naramon | Shadow Step melee combo duration never decays; Power Spike extends combo | Melee builds; Condition Overload setups |
| Vazarin | Protective Dash instantly heals warframes; Void Immunity prevents damage | Support players; solo survivability |
| Unairu | Void Shadow creates invulnerability; Caustic Strike strips armor in Operator mode | Endgame armor stripping against Grineer |
| Madurai | Void Strike stores Operator kills for damage burst; highest raw Operator damage | Eidolon hunters; raw damage amplification |

Zenurik is the default recommendation for players leveling their first school. Energizing Dash removes the need for energy restores in most content and enables builds that would otherwise run out of energy. After completing Zenurik, Naramon is the second priority for melee-focused players.

## Tips

Use the maximum-tier Amp available for Eidolon Focus farming. Higher-tier Amps break synovias faster, enabling more breaks per night cycle. A slow Amp extends per-hunt time, reducing the number of Eidolons killed per night and therefore the Focus earned per session.

During Convergence windows, activate any AoE ability that kills multiple enemies simultaneously. Saryn's Spores, Volt's Discharge, and Equinox's Maim scale Focus per kill by the 8x multiplier, meaning wide AoE kills during the window multiply Focus far more than single-target kills.

Do not switch between Focus schools mid-session. Focus earned from Affinity goes to the currently active school. Switching schools wastes session focus on a school that may already be capped, or underfills the desired school.

Naramon's Power Spike passive is worth unlocking early even when Naramon is a secondary school. The melee combo duration extension it provides benefits all melee builds regardless of the active school.

## Common mistakes

Equipping standard Focus Lenses instead of Greater Lenses significantly reduces Focus income for no in-game cost reason. Greater Lenses are available from Eidolon hunts and should replace standard lenses as soon as one is obtained. The conversion rate difference is 2x, which halves the time to reach the daily cap.

Running Focus farming sessions without collecting Convergence Orbs is near-zero yield. The 8x multiplier from Convergence is the entire basis of meaningful Focus gain. Players who ignore Convergence Orbs during missions earn trivial Focus per hour.

Leveling nodes outside the active primary school before the primary school is completed delays access to high-value passives. Each school's most impactful abilities require investing through a node chain; spreading investment thin delays access to the best nodes in all schools.

Attempting Eidolon Focus farming without an adequate Amp leads to incomplete hunts that grant no completion bonuses. Eidolon completion bonuses are a significant portion of the Focus earned from each hunt. Ensure the Amp can reliably break synovias before committing to Eidolon Focus sessions.
