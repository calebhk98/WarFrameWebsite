## Overview

Kuva is the principal endgame currency in Warframe, required for Riven Mod rerolling, Kuva Weapon creation through Lich systems, and various high-tier crafting recipes. Kuva is obtained from three primary sources: Kuva Siphon and Flood missions (500-700 kuva per completion), Kuva Fortress Survival (continuous kuva drops from enemies and rotation rewards), and the Kuva Guardian boss on the Kuva Fortress.

This guide covers the most efficient methods for accumulating kuva, explains the Siphon destruction mechanic, and details how kuva interacts with the Kuva Lich and Requiem systems.

## Prerequisites

- Completed The War Within quest (unlocks Kuva mechanic and Kuva Fortress access)
- At least Mastery Rank 5 for Kuva Fortress node access
- Parazon with Requiem mod slots available (for Lich vanquishing)

## Steps

For raw kuva acquisition, run Kuva Fortress Survival at Taveuni. This mission awards kuva passively from enemy drops (affected by Resource Booster) and provides rotation rewards at the five-minute intervals. Each rotation contains a chance for kuva bundles. Run in groups of four with enemy density-boosting frames to maximize kill count and kuva drop frequency.

For Kuva Siphons, navigate to any star chart node currently hosting a Siphon mission (marked with a Kuva icon in navigation). The Siphon hovers above the mission area and extends cloud braids that drain kuva from the environment. Destroy all eight braids before the Siphon completes its sequence to receive the 500 kuva reward. Each braid is destroyed by shooting or performing a bullet jump through it -- physical contact from bullet jump is faster than weapon fire.

The Kuva Flood is a higher-difficulty variant of the Siphon with enemies scaling approximately 25 levels above standard Siphon difficulty. Flood rewards 700 kuva per successful completion. The braid destruction mechanic is identical to standard Siphons.

For Requiem Relic farming (required to obtain Requiem mods needed for Lich killing), run Kuva Siphon and Flood missions with a Kuva Lich active. The Lich's influence causes a Kuva Larvling to spawn during certain missions; mercy killing the Larvling claims it as a Lich and starts the Requiem sequence. Requiem Relics also drop from Siphon and Flood mission rewards.

## Tips

Operator mode during Siphon missions enables fast braid destruction through Void Sling. Each Void Sling pass through a braid cluster can destroy one or more braids simultaneously. A practiced operator can complete all eight braid destructions rapidly using sequential routing through the cloud clusters.

Requiem mod order matters for Lich killing. The correct sequence of three Requiem mods must be equipped on the Parazon in the exact order the Lich's hidden sequence requires. The order is determined by stabbing the Lich repeatedly and observing which slots validate (marked with a checkmark) across multiple encounters. Record the confirmed positions between missions to narrow the sequence efficiently.

Each Parazon Requiem mod has a charge count. Each Lich confrontation, whether successful or unsuccessful, consumes one charge from each mod in the equipped configuration. Stock multiple copies of each Requiem mod to avoid running out of charges mid-sequence. Additional Requiem Relics from Siphon and Flood missions provide new mod copies to replenish depleted ones.

The Kuva Lich system generates a Kuva Weapon with a random element and bonus percentage when the Lich spawns. Players farming specific weapons should check if the weapon variant has the desired element before committing to the full Requiem sequence -- vanquishing a Lich with an undesired weapon is a significant investment without payoff.

Khora Prime with Pilfering Strangledome and Nekros with Despoil (which converts Health Orbs to energy for Desecrate uptime) are complementary farming frames for Kuva Fortress Survival. The combination maximizes resource drops per enemy killed, which includes Kuva bundles from the rotation loot table.

## Lich conversion and trading

After completing the Requiem sequence, the player chooses to vanquish or convert the Kuva Lich. Vanquishing yields the Lich's Kuva Weapon. Converting turns the Lich into a Specter ally deployable in missions. Converted Liches can be traded to other players; the receiving player gets the converted Lich (with its weapon and element) and can then choose to vanquish it for the weapon.

Lich trading requires both players to visit the Crimson Branch in a Clan Dojo. The selling player must have the Lich converted (not vanquished). The receiving player must not currently have an active Lich. Kuva Weapon-specific Lich trades are common on warframe.market when players want specific element combinations or high-percentage weapon bonuses without farming the full Requiem sequence themselves.

Trading a Lich with a 60% elemental bonus on a desirable Kuva Weapon can fetch significant platinum on the secondary market. Check warframe.market listings for the specific weapon type and element before deciding whether to vanquish or convert for trade.

## Common mistakes

Failing to destroy all eight braids before the Siphon escapes is the most common error. The Siphon progresses through its sequence on a timer; prioritize braid destruction over killing enemies. Enemies respawn continuously; the Siphon does not.

Equipping Requiem mods in the wrong order or using incorrect mods fails the mercy kill. If the Parazon sequence is wrong, Lich health regenerates from near-death and the Lich retreats. Track validated positions carefully and retry with corrected positions on the next Lich encounter.

Using a Resource Booster to improve Siphon yield does not work -- Resource Boosters only affect enemy drops and mission cache loot, not Siphon or Flood kuva rewards. Save Resource Boosters for Survival missions where enemy kuva drops scale with the booster.

## Kuva sources compared

Kuva Siphon: 500 kuva per completion, available as a rotating mission on random star chart nodes. Accessible from mid-game after completing The War Within. Approximately 5-8 minutes per completion including travel and the mission itself. Reliable for daily kuva income alongside other farming.

Kuva Flood: 700 kuva per completion, same braid destruction mechanic as Siphon but with significantly higher enemy levels. Requires a capable endgame build. Provides 40% more kuva than a standard Siphon at the cost of increased challenge. Recommended over Siphons for players with Steel Path-capable builds.

Kuva Fortress Survival (Taveuni): Provides kuva from enemy drops throughout the mission and from rotation rewards. Resource Boosters double the enemy drop kuva. The mission can be run indefinitely, making it the highest-yield source for players who can dedicate 30+ minutes to a single session. Best used when a large kuva investment is needed for a Riven reroll binge.

Kuva Guardian boss: Available on the Kuva Fortress and provides kuva on defeat. Lower yield per time investment than Survival but accessible as a daily bonus and does not require braid destruction mechanics.

## Requiem mod and Lich system integration

Kuva Liches are generated from Kuva Larvlings that spawn during missions on standard star chart planets when a player has Mastery Rank 5 or higher. The Lich selection determines the Kuva Weapon type and element. The weapon's elemental bonus percentage is displayed when the Larvling spawns -- players can inspect the Larvling before performing a mercy kill and choose not to claim it if the weapon or element is undesirable.

Requiem Relics are obtained as rewards from Kuva Siphon and Flood missions. Opening Requiem Relics yields Requiem mods (Lohk, Jahu, Ris, Fass, Vome, Netra, Khra, Xata) needed to build the Parazon sequence. Each Requiem mod must be used once per Lich kill attempt and degrades in charge count with each unsuccessful use. Running Siphons concurrently with Lich hunting maintains Requiem mod supply.
