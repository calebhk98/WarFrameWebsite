## Overview

Kuva is the principal endgame currency in Warframe, required for Riven Mod rerolling, Kuva Weapon creation through Lich systems, and various high-tier crafting recipes. Kuva is obtained from three primary sources: Kuva Siphon and Flood missions (500-700 kuva per completion), Kuva Fortress Survival (continuous kuva drops from enemies and rotation rewards), and the Kuva Guardian boss on the Kuva Fortress.

This guide covers the most efficient methods for accumulating kuva, explains the Siphon destruction mechanic, and details how kuva interacts with the Kuva Lich and Requiem systems.

## Prerequisites

- Completed The War Within quest (unlocks Kuva mechanic and Kuva Fortress access)
- At least Mastery Rank 5 for Kuva Fortress node access
- Parazon with Requiem mod slots available (for Lich vanquishing)

## Steps

For raw kuva acquisition, run Kuva Fortress Survival at Taveuni. This mission awards kuva passively from enemy drops (affected by Resource Booster) and provides rotation rewards at the 5-minute intervals. Each rotation contains a chance for kuva bundles, with the C rotation offering the highest kuva quantities. Run in groups of four with enemy density-boosting frames to maximize kill count and kuva drop frequency.

For Kuva Siphons, navigate to any star chart node currently hosting a Siphon mission (marked with a Kuva icon in navigation). The Siphon hovers above the mission area and extends cloud braids that drain kuva from the environment. Destroy all 8 braids before the Siphon completes its sequence to receive the 500 kuva reward. Each braid is destroyed by shooting or performing a bullet jump through it -- physical contact from bullet jump is faster than weapon fire.

The Kuva Flood is a higher-difficulty variant of the Siphon with enemies scaling 25 levels above standard Siphon difficulty. Flood rewards 700 kuva per successful completion. The braid destruction mechanic is identical to standard Siphons.

For Requiem Relic farming (required to obtain Requiem mods needed for Lich killing), run Kuva Siphon and Flood missions with a Kuva Lich active. The Lich's influence causes a Kuva Larvling to spawn during the mission; mercy killing the Larvling claims it as a Lich and starts the Requiem sequence. Alternatively, Requiem Relics drop from Siphon mission rewards.

## Tips

Operator mode during Siphon missions enables fast braid destruction through void dash. Each void dash through a braid cluster can destroy two or three braids simultaneously. A practiced operator can complete all 8 braid destructions in under 20 seconds using sequential dash routing.

Requiem mod order matters for Lich killing. The correct sequence of three Requiem mods must be equipped on the Parazon in the exact order the Lich's hidden sequence requires. The order is determined by stabbing the Lich repeatedly and observing which slots validate (marked with a checkmark) across multiple encounters. Record the confirmed positions between missions to narrow the sequence efficiently.

The Kuva Lich system generates a Kuva Weapon with a random element and bonus percentage ranging from 25% to 60% when the Lich spawns. Players farming specific weapons should check if the weapon variant has the desired element before committing to the full Requiem sequence -- vanquishing a Lich with an undesired weapon is a significant investment without payoff.

## Common mistakes

Failing to destroy all 8 braids before the Siphon escapes is the most common error. The Siphon progresses through its sequence on a timer; prioritize braid destruction over killing enemies. Enemies respawn continuously; the Siphon does not.

Equipping Requiem mods in the wrong order or using incorrect mods fails the mercy kill. If the Parazon sequence is wrong, Lich health regenerates from near-death and the Lich retreats. Track validated positions carefully and retry with corrected positions on the next Lich encounter.
