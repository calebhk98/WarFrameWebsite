## Overview

Void Traces are the refinement currency for Void Relics. They are collected passively during Void Fissure missions by killing Corrupted enemies -- enemies that have been transformed by Void energy and spawned alongside standard mission enemies when a Fissure is active. Refining a relic with Void Traces improves the probability distribution of its drop table, most importantly increasing the rare (gold) slot probability from 2% to as high as 10% at Radiant tier.

Understanding trace economics -- how quickly traces accumulate, what the cap is, and when to spend versus hold -- is essential for players farming specific prime components efficiently.

## Prerequisites

- Access to at least one active Void Fissure mission (available in the navigation screen)
- One or more relics of the appropriate era to run during Fissure missions

## Steps

Run any Void Fissure mission to collect Void Traces passively. Corrupted enemies appear throughout the mission alongside standard enemies when the fissure is active. Each corrupted kill has a chance to drop 1-3 traces. Traces are distributed equally to all squad members within pickup radius -- there is no competition for drops.

Standard Fissure Capture missions (Hepit for Lith, Ukko for Neo) provide approximately 20-40 traces per 90-second run. This is the highest traces-per-minute rate available for most eras. Survivals provide more total traces per session but require longer commitment per rotation.

Once sufficient traces are collected, refine relics before the next Fissure run. Open the Star Chart navigation, select a Fissure mission, choose the relic to run, and select refinement tier. Radiant refinement costs 100 traces and changes the rare slot probability from 2% to 10%. This represents the single most impactful action for targeted prime part farming.

Plan trace expenditure around session goals. Refining four relics to Radiant for a squad run costs 400 traces (4 x 100). Collecting 400 traces at Hepit takes approximately 10-12 runs (roughly 20 minutes). Budget trace generation against planned refinement consumption for sessions of 1-2 hours to ensure traces never sit idle at the cap.

## Tips

The 1200 trace cap is reached quickly during active play. Players who run Fissures without spending traces hit the cap within 1-2 hours of continuous play. Check the trace count before each session and spend on queued relics to maintain room for incoming drops.

Radiant refinement is not always optimal. For farming common and uncommon drops (Forma Blueprints, common prime parts), Intact relics are sufficient because the probability of obtaining any non-rare result is already high. Radiant refinement provides the greatest value improvement on rare slots specifically. Reserve traces for relics where the target item occupies the rare (gold) position.

When running relics in a squad of four where all players bring the same Radiant relic, the effective rare selection probability from the shared pool is substantially higher than individual Radiant probability. In a four-player Radiant squad, at least one rare slot is displayed for selection more than 30% of the time, and any player can select any other player's relic reward.

Void Traces do not expire and have no season-based reset. Traces carried across vault cycles retain full value. However, refined relics from previous vault cycles may no longer contain desirable parts -- refine relics session-by-session rather than stockpiling.

## Common mistakes

Running relics without collecting enough reactant before mission end invalidates the relic run. Each Fissure mission requires collecting 10 reactant items dropped by Corrupted enemies before the objective completes to unlock relic rewards at mission end. In Capture missions, this means collecting 10 reactant before capturing the target. Kill Corrupted enemies proactively as they spawn rather than rushing directly to the capture target.

Spending all traces on sub-optimal relics (refining Intact relics that contain only common drops) wastes traces that could fund rare-slot Radiant upgrades. Always check the relic's drop table before refinement.
