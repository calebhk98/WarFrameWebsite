## Overview

Void Traces are the refinement currency for Void Relics. They are collected passively during Void Fissure missions by killing Corrupted enemies -- enemies that have been transformed by Void energy and spawned alongside standard mission enemies when a Fissure is active. Refining a relic with Void Traces improves the probability distribution of its drop table, most importantly increasing the rare (gold) slot probability from 2% to as high as 10% at Radiant tier.

Understanding trace economics -- how quickly traces accumulate, what the cap is, and when to spend versus hold -- is essential for players farming specific prime components efficiently.

## Prerequisites

- Access to at least one active Void Fissure mission (available in the navigation screen)
- One or more relics of the appropriate era to run during Fissure missions

## Steps

Run any Void Fissure mission to collect Void Traces passively. Corrupted enemies appear throughout the mission alongside standard enemies when the fissure is active. Each corrupted kill has a chance to drop 1-3 traces. Traces are distributed equally to all squad members within pickup radius -- there is no competition for drops.

Standard Fissure Capture missions (Hepit for Lith, Ukko for Neo) provide approximately 20-40 traces per 90-second run. This is the highest traces-per-minute rate available for most eras. Survivals provide more total traces per session but require longer commitment per rotation.

Once sufficient traces are collected, refine relics before the next Fissure run. Open the Star Chart navigation, select a Fissure mission, choose the relic to run, and select the refinement tier. The refinement tiers and their trace costs are: Exceptional (15 traces), Flawless (25 traces from Exceptional, 40 total from Intact), and Radiant (100 traces from Flawless, 140 total from Intact). Radiant refinement changes the rare slot probability from 2% to 10%, representing the single most impactful action for targeted prime part farming.

Plan trace expenditure around session goals. Refining four relics to Radiant for a squad run costs 400 traces (4 x 100). Collecting 400 traces at Hepit takes approximately 10-12 runs (roughly 20 minutes). Budget trace generation against planned refinement consumption for sessions of 1-2 hours to ensure traces never sit idle at the cap.

## Tips

The 1200 trace cap is reached quickly during active play. Players who run Fissures without spending traces hit the cap within 1-2 hours of continuous play. Check the trace count before each session and spend on queued relics to maintain room for incoming drops.

Radiant refinement is not always optimal. For farming common and uncommon drops (Forma Blueprints, common prime parts), Intact relics are sufficient because the probability of obtaining any non-rare result is already high. Radiant refinement provides the greatest value improvement on rare slots specifically. Reserve traces for relics where the target item occupies the rare (gold) position.

When running relics in a squad of four where all players bring the same Radiant relic, the effective rare selection probability from the shared pool is substantially higher than individual Radiant probability. In a four-player Radiant squad, at least one rare slot is displayed for selection more than 30% of the time, and any player can select any other player's relic reward.

Void Traces do not expire and have no season-based reset. Traces carried across vault cycles retain full value. However, refined relics from previous vault cycles may no longer contain desirable parts -- refine relics session-by-session rather than stockpiling. An Exceptional relic containing a vaulted part may still be useful as a squad filler, but Radiant refinement on it wastes 100 traces that could go toward active-vault content.

Exceptional refinement (15 traces) is an underused middle option. When the target part occupies an uncommon slot rather than the rare slot, Exceptional increases uncommon slot probability meaningfully without the high trace cost of Radiant. Save Radiant for rare-slot targets only.

Void Fissure mission tracker: the navigation screen's Void Fissure panel shows all currently active Fissure missions by era (Lith, Meso, Neo, Axi) and mission type. Fissure missions rotate every hour, so the available Capture Fissures change across the day. Checking the Fissure panel before planning a farming session ensures the most efficient mission type is available for the intended relic era. During times when no Capture Fissure is active for the needed era, Defense or Interception Fissures serve as replacements.

## Common mistakes

Running relics without collecting enough reactant before mission end invalidates the relic run. Each Fissure mission requires collecting 10 reactant items dropped by Corrupted enemies before the objective completes to unlock relic rewards at mission end. In Capture missions, this means collecting 10 reactant before capturing the target. Kill Corrupted enemies proactively as they spawn rather than rushing directly to the capture target.

Spending all traces on sub-optimal relics (refining Intact relics that contain only common drops) wastes traces that could fund rare-slot Radiant upgrades. Always check the relic's drop table before refinement.

Misreading the refinement costs is a common error. The per-tier cost from each preceding tier is: 15 to reach Exceptional, 25 to reach Flawless, 100 to reach Radiant. Calculating total traces needed from Intact (40 for Flawless, 140 for Radiant) helps plan farming sessions before they begin.

## Refinement probabilities explained

Each relic contains six possible rewards arranged in three rarity tiers. Common (bronze) slots: three items at equal probability. Uncommon (silver) slots: two items at equal probability. Rare (gold) slot: one item.

At Intact (no refinement):
- Each common slot item: approximately 25.33% each
- Each uncommon slot item: approximately 11% each
- Rare slot item: approximately 2% total

At Exceptional (15 traces):
- Common slot items: reduced slightly in favor of uncommon and rare
- Rare slot item: approximately 4%

At Flawless (25 additional traces, 40 total from Intact):
- Rare slot item: approximately 6%

At Radiant (100 additional traces, 140 total from Intact):
- Rare slot item: 10%

The exact probabilities shift based on relic type. Check the in-game relic preview screen which shows each reward and its rarity color before committing to refinement. The displayed rarity colors (bronze, silver, gold) directly correspond to common, uncommon, and rare probability tiers.

## Squad synergy with Radiant relics

When four players in a Fissure squad each open a relic, the reward selection screen shows all four players' rewards simultaneously. Each player can pick any of the rewards shown -- including rewards from other players' relics. This system means that running the same relic in a coordinated squad multiplies effective access to each relic's rare slot.

If all four squad members run the same Radiant relic, the rare item from each relic is equally likely to appear in the shared selection pool. Running mixed relics (different names, different eras) in a squad still shares rewards, but reduces the probability that the specific rare desired by a player appears in the selection pool. Coordinating relics with squad members before a crack session maximizes rare item access per session.

When farming for a specific rare item without a coordinated squad, running Radiant relics in a public squad still benefits from any shared selection even if other players' relics are Intact. Even one Radiant relic in the squad improves the chance of the desired rare appearing compared to all players running Intact.
