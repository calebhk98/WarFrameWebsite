## Overview

Prime Warframes and Prime weapons are enhanced versions of base items, featuring improved base stats and an additional Orokin Reactor or Catalyst pre-installed equivalent (the gold Orokin Reactor look). Primes require farming their component parts from Void Relics opened during Void Fissure missions. A complete Prime Warframe requires at least three component parts (Neuroptics, Chassis, Systems) plus a Main Blueprint, each housed in a specific Relic.

Prime farming has two distinct phases: first, identifying which Relics contain the needed parts; second, efficiently acquiring those Relics and cracking them for the target drops. These phases require different activities: Relic acquisition comes from regular star chart missions, while Relic cracking requires Void Fissure missions.

The secondary market on warframe.market provides an alternative acquisition route: players can trade Prime parts with other players for platinum. Understanding part values enables rational decisions about whether to farm parts directly or purchase them while selling excess drops.

Baro Ki'Teer, the Void Trader, visits relays bi-weekly and occasionally offers Prime parts for Ducats and Credits. During Prime Resurgence events, Baro's inventory expands to include vaulted Prime parts that are not otherwise available from the current Relic pool. Monitoring Baro's schedule is essential for tracking vaulted Prime acquisition windows.

## Prerequisites

- Access to Void Fissure missions (available early in the star chart)
- At least one Void Relic in inventory
- Mastery Rank sufficient to equip the target Prime item (Primes typically require higher MR than base variants)
- For advanced farming: Radiant Relic coordination requires Void Traces sufficient to refine Relics to Radiant tier
- Ducats for Baro Ki'Teer purchases (earned by trading Prime parts at Baro's kiosk)

## Steps

Research the target Prime item's component requirements using the in-game Codex or the WARFRAME Wiki. Each Prime item requires between two and five components depending on type. Identify every Relic that contains each component and note the slot (common, uncommon, rare) for each.

Acquire the required Relics through regular mission play. Relics drop from most mission types as rotation rewards. The era of Relic (Lith, Meso, Neo, Axi) determines which Fissure mission type is required to crack it. Recently released Prime items appear in Neo and Axi Relics; older items in Lith and Meso.

Refine the Relics targeting rare components to Radiant before cracking. Radiant refinement increases the rare slot probability from 2% to 10%, a fivefold improvement. Spend Void Traces on Relics containing rare target components; leave common-component Relics at Intact.

Form or join a Void Fissure squad with four players all bringing the same Radiant Relic. The reward-sharing mechanic at mission end displays all four cracked Relic rewards simultaneously, giving each player four chances at the target drop per mission.

At the end-of-mission reward screen, select the target component. If no player cracked the target part, select the highest-trade-value alternative to sell or convert to Ducats.

Repeat until all components are collected. Build the Prime item in the Foundry using the standard build time (72 hours for Warframes, 12 hours for weapons in most cases).

## Relic era guide

Different Prime items drop from different Relic eras. The era generally correlates with the age and tier of the Prime item, though Digital Extremes periodically rotate which Relics are "In Rotation" (currently available from mission drops).

| Era | Mission sources | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lith | Earth, Mars, Mercury, Venus missions | Most abundant; common older Prime parts |
| Meso | Jupiter, Saturn, Phobos missions | Moderate abundance; mid-tier parts |
| Neo | Sedna, Neptune, Pluto missions | Recent Prime parts; Survival and Defense reliable |
| Axi | Void missions, high-level Endless | Rarest; contains current and endgame Primes |

## Vault status and unvaulting

Prime items cycle in and out of the "Prime Vault." Vaulted Primes have all their associated Relics removed from the in-game drop pool, making them unobtainable from missions. Vaulted Primes can only be acquired through:

- Trading with other players on warframe.market or in-game trade chat
- Baro Ki'Teer Prime Resurgence events (periodic large unvaulting events)
- Dedicated Prime Unvaulting events where 2-3 Primes temporarily return to the drop pool

Check the WARFRAME Wiki's vault tracker to determine whether a target Prime is currently vaulted before beginning a farming campaign. Farming for a vaulted Prime through Relics is impossible; purchasing from players is the only option until an unvaulting event.

## Ducat economy and trade value optimization

Baro Ki'Teer accepts Ducats (earned by trading Prime parts at Baro's kiosk) and Credits for his inventory. Understanding Ducat values prevents accidentally selling rare or high-value parts:

| Part rarity | Ducat value |
| --- | ---: |
| Common Prime parts | 25 Ducats |
| Uncommon Prime parts | 50 Ducats |
| Rare Prime parts | 100 Ducats |

Never sell rare Prime parts for Ducats if those parts have meaningful platinum trade value. A rare Prime part worth 40-200 platinum on warframe.market provides far more purchasing power than 100 Ducats. Only sell rare parts for Ducats when they have low trade value or when Ducats are urgently needed for a specific Baro item.

Common parts like Forma Blueprints (from common Relic slots) are ideal Ducat sources, as they have low or no platinum trade value and are plentiful.

## Warframe.market integration

warframe.market is a player-to-player trading platform for Prime parts and other tradeable items. Checking part prices before farming sessions enables rational decisions:

- Parts priced below 10 platinum are usually faster to farm directly.
- Parts priced 50 to 200 platinum may be worth purchasing if the farming time cost is high.
- Parts priced above 200 platinum are prime trade targets: farm excess copies and sell them to fund other purchases.

The site's price history shows whether a part's value is stable, rising (new Prime recently released), or falling (part becoming farmable again from unvaulting). This informs whether to buy now or wait for better prices.

## Baro Ki'Teer Prime Resurgence

Prime Resurgence events allow players to earn Aya (a special currency) from missions, then spend Aya at Varzia (located at Maroo's Bazaar on Mars) for specific Relics containing vaulted Prime parts. Aya drops from Void Fissure missions during the event period.

This is the only non-player-trade method to obtain vaulted Primes outside of a standard unvaulting event. Track Prime Resurgence schedules on the official Warframe forums or warframe.fandom.com to plan Aya farming sessions during the event window.

## Common mistakes

Farming for vaulted Prime parts through normal Relic acquisition is impossible; players frequently run missions searching for Relics that have been removed from the drop pool. Always verify vault status before starting a farming campaign.

Running Intact Relics for rare Prime parts when Void Traces are available is inefficient. The 2% intact rare drop rate versus 10% Radiant rate means five times as many missions are required on average. Invest Traces in refinement before cracking.

Selling rare Prime parts for Ducats without checking their platinum value is a common early-game mistake. Parts worth 100 platinum on the market should be traded to players, not converted to 100 Ducats (which purchase much less from Baro than 100 platinum buys on the market).

Cracking Relics solo instead of in a squad wastes the reward-sharing opportunity. A solo run presents one reward option; a four-player squad presents four. Always use the squad matchmaking for Fissure missions.
