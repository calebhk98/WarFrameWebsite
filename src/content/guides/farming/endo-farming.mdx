## Overview

Endo is the universal upgrade currency used to rank up mods. Every mod in Warframe requires Endo to advance beyond rank 0; maxing a Primed mod to rank 10 requires 40,000 Endo. The aggregate Endo demand across a full build -- multiple Primed mods at maximum rank -- typically exceeds 200,000 Endo for a single warframe. Efficient Endo farming is therefore one of the most important mid-to-late game activities.

Endo drops from enemies, breakable containers, Ayatan Sculptures traded at Maroo's Bazaar, and mission reward rotations. The most efficient Endo per hour comes from Arbitrations, where Vitus Essence can be exchanged for large Endo blocks. For players without Arbitration access, Seimeni Dark Sector Defense on Ceres provides a reliable mid-game alternative.

Ayatan Sculptures deserve special attention. These collectible items are found in missions and awarded by Nightwave. Each sculpture filled with Ayatan Stars yields significantly more Endo when traded at Maroo's Bazaar than when sold empty. Filling sculptures before trading them is always the correct choice.

## Prerequisites

For Arbitration farming (endgame route):
- All standard star chart nodes completed (required to unlock Arbitrations)
- Endgame-capable warframe and weapon builds for level 60-80 enemies
- Access to a relay to reach the Arbitrations kiosk

For Seimeni Dark Sector farming (mid-game route):
- Mars Junction completed to access Ceres
- Mastery Rank sufficient to equip current weapons
- Survivability-focused warframe build

For Helene Saturn farming (early-game route):
- Saturn junction completed
- Any functional frame and weapon loadout

## Steps

Determine which Endo farming route is appropriate for current progression. Players who have not completed the full star chart should use Seimeni or Helene. Players who have completed every node should prioritize Arbitrations for maximum Endo per hour.

**Arbitration route:** Navigate to the Arbitration kiosk in any relay (marked on the relay map). Check the current mission type and tile set. Arbitrations rotate every hour. Survival and Defense Arbitrations are the most common, and both provide Vitus Essence at defined intervals. In Survival Arbitrations, Vitus Essence drops from enemies killed. In Defense Arbitrations, Vitus Essence drops from enemies on specific wave intervals.

Collect Vitus Essence during the Arbitration mission and exchange it at the Arbitration kiosk for Endo. The exchange rate is 6 Vitus Essence for 3,500 Endo. Players completing 30-minute Arbitration Survival missions typically accumulate 12 to 20 Vitus Essence per run, yielding 7,000 to 11,666 Endo per run via exchange.

**Seimeni route:** Navigate to Seimeni on Ceres. Select the Dark Sector Defense mission. Run 5-wave cycles, extracting at the rotation A reward or continuing to rotation B for better mod drop chances. Endo drops from both enemies and rotation rewards. A Nekros with Despoil augment consistently increases Endo yield by reapplying Desecrate on corpses.

**Ayatan route:** Check all missions run during normal gameplay for Ayatan Sculptures. Maroo's Bazaar on Mars hosts a weekly mission that guarantees one Ayatan Sculpture. Trade all filled sculptures at Maroo's Bazaar; selling to Maroo yields 150% of what selling from inventory provides. See the table below for Endo values.

## Ayatan Sculpture Endo values

Ayatan Sculptures must be filled with Ayatan Stars before trading for maximum value. Amber Stars (small) and Cyan Stars (large) fill sculpture slots. Stars are found in missions and containers throughout the star chart. The trade value scales with the number and type of stars inserted.

| Sculpture | Stars needed | Endo value (filled) |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Ayatan Amber Star | -- | 150 (sell from inventory) |
| Ayatan Cyan Star | -- | 450 (sell from inventory) |
| Ayatan Anasa | 6 Amber, 1 Cyan | 6,500 |
| Ayatan Ayr | 4 Amber | 1,500 |
| Ayatan Valana | 4 Amber, 1 Cyan | 3,200 |

Always insert stars before trading. An empty Anasa sculpture trades for roughly 2,000 Endo; a filled Anasa trades for 6,500.

## Tips

Bring Nekros with the Despoil augment to any Endo farming mission. Desecrate rerolls each corpse for a second loot chance. In Defense and Survival missions where enemies die in large groups, Nekros consistently increases total Endo yield by 20 to 40% over runs without Desecrate.

Resource boosters do not affect Endo pickup quantity. However, Endo found in containers does double with a resource booster active. Resource boosters improve Endo income from breakable containers on the tile set but not from direct enemy kills or mission rewards.

Daily Sorties reward one of several possible items, including an Endo cache of 4,000 Endo. When short on Endo, always complete the daily Sortie and select the Endo reward option over cosmetic options. Over 365 days of consistent Sortie completion, this alone provides 1.46 million Endo.

Nightwave challenges frequently reward Ayatan Sculptures and Ayatan Stars. Complete as many Nightwave acts as possible each week, as the cumulative Sculpture income from Nightwave is substantial over a full Series.

When selling excess mods, selling duplicate maxed mods that are not needed for any build converts wasted mod storage directly to Endo at a high rate. A rank 10 Primed mod sold provides more Endo than most single farming sessions.

## Common mistakes

Selling Ayatan Sculptures without filling them with stars is the most common Endo efficiency error. An empty sculpture sells for a fraction of its filled value. Always spend time filling sculptures with stars before any trade.

Farming Endo on Helene (Saturn) beyond early-game progression is inefficient. Helene provides adequate early-game Endo but scales poorly compared to Seimeni (which has a Dark Sector bonus) or Arbitrations.

Ranking up rarely-used or duplicate mods before ranking up build-critical Primed mods is a priority error. Endo invested in a rarely-used mod is locked into that mod; Endo is not refundable. Rank up mods that appear in active builds first.

Ignoring the weekly Maroo's Bazaar Sculpture mission costs 6,500 or more Endo per week from forgone Ayatan Anasa trades. The mission takes under 3 minutes and is one of the most time-efficient passive Endo sources in the game.

Attempting Arbitrations without a complete endgame build results in mission failure. Arbitration enemies scale to level 60-80 immediately and have specific mechanics (one active Arbiter drone grants targeted-enemy immunity). Read the Arbitration ruleset before the first attempt to avoid wasted mission time.

## Mod selling for Endo

Every mod in the game can be sold for Endo at the rate listed in the mod's detail panel. Selling mods provides Endo directly from the inventory without requiring a mission. This method is most effective for clearing duplicate maxed mods or low-value mods that are no longer needed.

Primed mods at max rank provide the highest Endo per sell of any single item in the game. A rank 10 Primed mod is worth significantly more Endo than a rank 10 standard mod. Players who have accumulated multiple maxed copies of the same Primed mod can sell duplicates for a large Endo windfall. However, this is a one-time action that cannot be reversed; only sell duplicates, not the last copy of a needed mod.

Common mods below rank 5 provide minimal Endo per sell. The best use of these mods is to dissolve the lowest-drain common mods (rank 0-2) to free inventory slots rather than to generate meaningful Endo.

Corrupted mods acquired from Orokin Vault runs can be sold for moderate Endo if duplicates accumulate. Corrupted mods do not sell for as much as Primed mods but provide more than standard rare mods. Identify which Corrupted mods are used in active builds and sell all additional copies.

The Mod dissolve mechanic (selecting mods in the Upgrade screen and choosing Dissolve) converts mods to Endo at the same rate as selling. Use dissolve to convert mod stacks that have accumulated beyond one or two usable copies. Running Arbitrations or Seimeni passively accumulates common mod drops; dissolving these at the end of each session provides supplemental Endo income from drops that would otherwise only grant Credits.

## Endo from daily login rewards

Daily login rewards include rotating Endo caches at specific login milestone days. The amount increases with consecutive login days. While not a primary farming method, logging in every day accumulates passive Endo over weeks and months. Players who maintain a 300-day login streak receive Endo rewards substantially larger than early milestone rewards.

The Endo from login rewards compounds meaningfully when combined with Sortie Endo rewards (4,000 per Sortie completion when selected) and Nightwave Ayatan Sculpture rewards. Together, these passive daily sources can contribute 10,000 to 20,000 Endo per week with minimal dedicated farming time, covering the Endo cost of one or two Primed mod rank-ups.
