## Overview

Prime parts are components of Prime warframes and weapons, obtained by opening Void Relics in Void Fissure missions. Each relic contains six possible rewards at varying rarity tiers: three common slots (bronze), two uncommon slots (silver), and one rare slot (gold). Radiant refinement dramatically improves the rare slot probability, making relic refinement the most impactful single action for targeted prime part acquisition.

This guide covers the complete farming loop: which missions produce which relic eras efficiently, how to identify the relics needed for specific prime components, the refinement system, and squad coordination strategies for maximizing rare drops per hour.

## Prerequisites

- Completed Vor's Prize to unlock the star chart
- At least one active Void Fissure mission available (check the navigation screen)
- Sufficient void traces for intended refinement tier (15 for Exceptional, 25 for Flawless, 100 for Radiant)

## Steps

Identify the target prime component from the relic database at [warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Void_Relic](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Void_Relic) or via the in-game codex. Each prime part specifies which relic era (Lith, Meso, Neo, Axi) it belongs to. Cross-reference with the active drop table to confirm which specific relic contains the desired part.

Farm relics of the appropriate era from the recommended missions listed in this guide. Void Capture missions (Hepit for Lith, Ukko for Neo) complete in under 90 seconds and provide one relic per completion, making them the highest relics-per-hour sources for their respective eras. Endless missions like Disruption at Apollo on Lua award relics every 2-3 rotations but allow multiple rewards per session.

Refine target relics using void traces collected during Fissure missions. The four refinement tiers are Intact (base), Exceptional (15 traces), Flawless (25 traces), and Radiant (100 traces). Radiant refinement raises the rare slot probability from 2% to 10%, a 5x improvement. For commonly-needed rare parts, Radiant refinement is the standard before any crack run.

Form a squad of four and have all players equip copies of the same relic (ideally all Radiant). During the Fissure mission, collect 10 reactant by killing corrupted enemies before the mission objective completes. At mission end, each player selects from a pool that combines all four players' relic drops -- effective rare drop access is all four players' gold slots simultaneously. With four Radiant relics, the combined rare slot probability per session is approximately 34% for any individual rare.

## Tips

Void traces accumulate passively during any Fissure mission. Each corrupted kill in a Fissure has a chance to drop traces, and traces are shared across the squad pickup radius. The trace count per run varies but averages 20-40 per Capture mission. Spending traces on lower-priority relics can become bottlenecked in high-volume farming sessions; prioritize refining only the specific relics needed for desired rare parts.

Rotation scheduling matters for endless missions. Disruption at Apollo (Lua) awards Neo relics on rotation A and Axi relics on rotations B and C. Running four conduits per round (rotation C reward) optimizes Axi relic acquisition per mission. Abandoning at rotation B (two conduits) trades relic quantity for time efficiency.

Prime vaulting cycles affect demand. Parts from recently-unvaulted primes spike in value on warframe.market shortly after vault release and drop gradually over the following weeks. Tracking vault cycles allows prioritization of high-value targets before market price normalization.

Relic refinement tiers and their precise costs: Exceptional upgrades from Intact for 15 traces, Flawless upgrades from Exceptional for 25 traces (or 40 traces total from Intact), and Radiant upgrades from Flawless for 100 traces (or 140 traces total from Intact). Refining directly from Intact to Exceptional requires only 15 traces if Flawless and Radiant tiers are not needed for the target part.

## Relic acquisition in specific contexts

New players completing the early star chart will encounter Void Fissures before they have many relics. Completing any Fissure mission still grants the Void Traces even without a relic equipped; bringing a relic is not mandatory to participate in the Fissure activity, but is required to receive the relic reward at mission end.

Syndicates (Steel Meridian, Perrin Sequence, Red Veil, Arbiters of Hexis, Cephalon Suda, New Loka) sell relics at their respective standing tiers. Buying relics from syndicate shops is an efficient use of standing for players who need specific era relics without farming the standard relic-source missions.

Prime Access and Unvaulting events periodically add previously-vaulted prime parts back to the relic drop pool. Monitoring the active relic drop table (updated at each Prime rotation) ensures effort is focused on relics that currently contain the desired parts. The in-game codex, warframe.fandom.com, and community tools like tenno.zone maintain current drop table information.

## Common mistakes

Using Intact relics for rare parts is the most common error. Intact rare probability is 2%, making expected rare drops per 50 runs only 1. Refinement to at least Exceptional (3.33%) or Radiant (10%) is essential for any targeted farming.

Selecting a squad relic reward that is not the target part misses the squad-sharing benefit. When all four players run the same relic, any player can select any relic reward shown from any player's pool. Coordinate squad intent before selection to avoid selecting a common part when the rare was available.

Skipping reactant collection in Fissure missions invalidates the run. Collect all 10 reactant from corrupted enemies before the mission objective completes. In Capture missions, kill corrupted enemies on the way to the target rather than rushing directly to the capture point.

## Relic era sources by mission type

Lith relics (Tier 1) are the easiest to farm. Hepit in the Void is a Capture mission that completes in under 90 seconds and drops one Lith relic per run. Aten (Void Capture) is a secondary option with slightly longer run times. Void Defense missions on Belenus also drop Lith relics on rotation A.

Meso relics (Tier 2) drop from Io on Jupiter (Defense), Void Capture missions on Belenus, and from Neo survival missions that also appear in the Void rotation. Players with Jupiter access can farm Io Defense for consistent Meso drops every 10 waves.

Neo relics (Tier 3) are efficiently farmed at Ukko in the Void (Capture). Berehynia on Sedna (Interception) drops Neo relics and is popular for its short interception rounds. Hydron on Sedna also drops Neo relics as a secondary source while serving as a leveling location.

Axi relics (Tier 4) require longer missions. Apollo on Lua (Disruption) is the primary recommendation, giving Axi relics on rotations B and C. Hieracon on Pluto (Excavation) is reliable for Axi accumulation per hour with consistent four-excavator rotations.

## Void Fissure mission types compared

Capture Fissures: fastest completion, one relic per run, 20-40 traces per run. Best for relic-per-hour volume when the priority is accumulating a specific relic quickly.

Survival Fissures: multiple rotations possible per mission entry, 5-minute rotation intervals. Better for long sessions where leaving the mission between runs is inefficient. Drop one relic per rotation with higher Endo or secondary rewards mixed in.

Disruption Fissures: conduit-based, reward on rotation schedule per conduit completed. More complex mechanics but allows efficient squad play. Apollo on Lua alternates between Neo and Axi relics across rotations, making it the best source for both tier 3 and tier 4 relics in a single mission type.

Defense Fissures: wave-based with rewards at 5-wave intervals. Slower per relic than Capture but provides extended play sessions without needing to restart the mission. Io on Jupiter is the standard Meso Defense farm.
