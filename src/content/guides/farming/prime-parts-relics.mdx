## Overview

Prime parts are components of Prime warframes and weapons, obtained by opening Void Relics in Void Fissure missions. Each relic contains six possible rewards at varying rarity tiers: three common slots (bronze), two uncommon slots (silver), and one rare slot (gold). Radiant refinement dramatically improves the rare slot probability, making relic refinement the most impactful single action for targeted prime part acquisition.

This guide covers the complete farming loop: which missions produce which relic eras efficiently, how to identify the relics needed for specific prime components, the refinement system, and squad coordination strategies for maximizing rare drops per hour.

## Prerequisites

- Completed Vor's Prize to unlock the star chart
- At least one active Void Fissure mission available (check the navigation screen)
- Sufficient void traces for intended refinement tier (25 for Flawless, 100 for Radiant)

## Steps

Identify the target prime component from the relic database at [warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Void_Relic](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Void_Relic) or via the in-game codex. Each prime part specifies which relic era (Lith, Meso, Neo, Axi) it belongs to. Cross-reference with the active drop table to confirm which specific relic contains the desired part.

Farm relics of the appropriate era from the recommended missions listed in this guide. Void Capture missions (Hepit for Lith, Ukko for Neo) complete in under 90 seconds and provide one relic per completion, making them the highest relics-per-hour sources for their respective eras. Endless missions like Disruption at Apollo on Lua award relics every 2-3 rotations but allow multiple rewards per session.

Refine target relics to Radiant using void traces collected during Fissure missions. Radiant refinement raises the rare slot probability from 2% to 10%, a 5x improvement. For commonly-needed rare parts (Odonata Prime Wings, Tigris Prime Receiver), Radiant refinement is the standard before any crack run.

Form a squad of four and have all players equip copies of the same relic (ideally all Radiant). During the Fissure mission, collect 10 reactant by killing corrupted enemies before the mission objective completes. At mission end, each player selects from a pool that combines all four players' relic drops -- effective rare drop access is all four players' gold slots simultaneously. With four Radiant relics, the combined rare slot probability per session is approximately 34% for any individual rare.

## Tips

Void traces accumulate passively during any Fissure mission. Each corrupted kill in a Fissure has a chance to drop traces, and traces are shared across the squad pickup radius. The trace count per run varies but averages 20-40 per Capture mission. Spending traces on lower-priority relics (bringing Intact to Radiant costs 100 traces per relic) can become bottlenecked in high-volume farming sessions; prioritize refining only the specific relics needed for desired rare parts.

Rotation scheduling matters for endless missions. Disruption at Apollo (Lua) awards Neo relics on rotation A and Axi relics on rotations B and C. Running 4 conduits per round (rotation C reward) optimizes Axi relic acquisition per mission. Abandoning at rotation B (2 conduits) trades relic quantity for time.

Prime vaulting cycles affect demand. Parts from recently-unvaulted primes spike in value on warframe.market shortly after vault release and drop gradually over the following weeks. Tracking vault cycles allows prioritization of high-value targets before market price normalization.

## Common mistakes

Using Intact relics for rare parts is the most common error. Intact rare probability is 2%, making expected rare drops per 50 runs only 1. Refinement to at least Flawless (3.33%) or Radiant (10%) is essential for any targeted farming.

Selecting a squad relic reward that is not the target part misses the squad-sharing benefit. When all four players run the same relic, any player can select any relic reward shown from any player's pool. Coordinate squad intent before selection to avoid selecting a common part when the rare was available.
