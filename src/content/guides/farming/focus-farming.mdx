## Overview

Focus is the operator progression currency that powers the five Focus schools: Madurai, Naramon, Unairu, Vazarin, and Zenurik. Each school provides unique passive and active abilities for the operator and warframe. Focus is earned by converting affinity gained during missions, using Focus Lenses equipped on warframes and weapons. The conversion rate depends on lens tier: standard lenses convert 1.25% of affinity, Greater Lenses convert 1.75%, and Eidolon Lenses convert 2.25%.

This guide covers the three most efficient methods for focus farming, explains the daily cap system, details Convergence orbs and their multiplier mechanic, and provides school progression recommendations.

## Prerequisites

- Completed Second Dream quest (unlocks operator mode)
- At least one Focus Lens installed on a warframe or weapon
- Selected a primary Focus school via the operator menu

## Steps

For Stealth Affinity farming, equip a warframe suited for undetected kills -- Equinox Prime with Rest and Rage, or Banshee Prime with Sonar -- and run Adaro on Sedna. Stealth kills at Adaro grant 500% bonus affinity to the equipped lens items. Each stealth kill with a weapon that has an Eidolon Lens equipped converts 2.25% of the inflated affinity value into focus. At 10+ stealth kills per run, focus per minute at Adaro far exceeds any other non-Eidolon source.

For Eidolon Focus farming, participate in Tridolon hunts (Teralyst, Gantulyst, Hydrolyst) on the Plains of Eidolon at night. Capturing (not killing) each Eidolon grants focus bundles directly without requiring lens conversion. Hydrolyst capture grants the largest bundle. See the [Eidolon Hunting guide](/guides/strategy/eidolon-hunting) for preparation requirements.

For Sanctuary Onslaught, run Elite Sanctuary Onslaught with high-damage frames (Saryn Prime, Mesa Prime) to maintain efficiency scores. Efficiency drops over time if kill rate is insufficient; reaching efficiency threshold before zone transition grants bonus affinity. At the daily focus cap, ESO provides the best pure affinity-per-minute ratio, useful later for leveling secondary school nodes.

## Tips

Convergence orbs spawn on a timer during missions, visible as glowing orange orbs on the minimap. Picking up a Convergence orb activates an 8x focus multiplier for 45 seconds. Prioritize kills during the Convergence window to maximize focus conversion. At Adaro with stealth kills and Convergence active, a single 45-second window can generate 50,000+ focus.

Focus lens placement matters for efficiency. Equipping a primary weapon with an Eidolon Lens alongside a warframe with a Greater Lens maximizes total conversion from a single run. Weapons generally generate more affinity than warframes in kill-focused missions, so front-loading the best lens on the primary weapon is the optimal placement for most builds.

School node unlock priority affects early progression speed. Zenurik players should prioritize Energizing Dash first; Naramon players should prioritize Shadow Step; Madurai players should prioritize Void Strike. These tier-1 nodes provide the highest mission impact per focus point invested.

The Eidolon Lens blueprint drops from Eidolon Teralyst captures. Craft and install Eidolon Lenses on at least one weapon before committing to extended focus farming sessions.

## Common mistakes

Exceeding the daily focus cap wastes lens affinity conversion. Track cap progress via the operator menu between missions. Spreading focus farming across multiple days is more efficient than running past the cap in a single session.

Using standard lenses rather than Greater or Eidolon lenses halves the focus yield per run. Standard lenses are acceptable early but should be upgraded as soon as Eidolon runs and craft resources allow.
