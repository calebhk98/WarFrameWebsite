## Overview

Focus is the operator progression currency that powers the five Focus schools: Madurai, Naramon, Unairu, Vazarin, and Zenurik. Each school provides unique passive and active abilities for the operator and warframe. Focus is earned by converting affinity gained during missions, using Focus Lenses equipped on warframes and weapons. The conversion rate depends on lens tier: standard lenses convert 1.25% of affinity, Greater Lenses convert 1.75%, and Eidolon Lenses convert 2.25%.

This guide covers the three most efficient methods for focus farming, explains the daily cap system, details Convergence orbs and their multiplier mechanic, and provides school progression recommendations.

## Prerequisites

- Completed Second Dream quest (unlocks operator mode)
- At least one Focus Lens installed on a warframe or weapon
- Selected a primary Focus school via the operator menu

## Steps

For Stealth Affinity farming, equip a warframe suited for undetected kills -- Equinox Prime with Rest and Rage, or Banshee Prime with Sonar -- and run Adaro on Sedna. Stealth kills at Adaro grant 500% bonus affinity to the equipped lens items. Each stealth kill with a weapon that has an Eidolon Lens equipped converts 2.25% of the inflated affinity value into focus. At 10+ stealth kills per run, focus per minute at Adaro far exceeds any other non-Eidolon source.

For Eidolon Focus farming, participate in Tridolon hunts (Teralyst, Gantulyst, Hydrolyst) on the Plains of Eidolon at night. Capturing (not killing) each Eidolon grants focus bundles directly without requiring lens conversion. Hydrolyst capture grants the largest bundle. See the [Eidolon Hunting guide](/guides/strategy/eidolon-hunting) for preparation requirements.

For Sanctuary Onslaught, run Elite Sanctuary Onslaught with high-damage frames (Saryn Prime, Mesa Prime) to maintain efficiency scores. Efficiency drops over time if kill rate is insufficient; reaching efficiency threshold before zone transition grants bonus affinity. At the daily focus cap, ESO provides a strong affinity-per-minute ratio, useful later for leveling secondary school nodes.

## Tips

Convergence orbs spawn on a timer during missions, visible as glowing orange orbs on the minimap. Picking up a Convergence orb activates an 8x focus multiplier for 45 seconds. Prioritize kills during the Convergence window to maximize focus conversion. At Adaro with stealth kills and Convergence active, a single 45-second window can generate very large focus totals relative to non-Convergence time.

Focus lens placement matters for efficiency. Equipping a primary weapon with an Eidolon Lens alongside a warframe with a Greater Lens maximizes total conversion from a single run. Weapons generally generate more affinity than warframes in kill-focused missions, so front-loading the best lens on the primary weapon is the optimal placement for most builds.

School node unlock priority affects early progression speed. Zenurik players should prioritize Energizing Dash first; Naramon players should prioritize the Shadow Step passive node; Madurai players should prioritize Void Strike. These high-priority nodes provide the greatest mission impact per focus point invested.

The Eidolon Lens blueprint drops from Eidolon Teralyst captures. Craft and install Eidolon Lenses on at least one weapon before committing to extended focus farming sessions. Using standard lenses rather than Greater or Eidolon lenses reduces the focus yield per run substantially. Standard lenses are acceptable early but should be upgraded as soon as Eidolon runs and craft resources allow.

Adaro on Sedna is Infested faction and has a relatively low enemy level compared to other stealth-affinity options. The combination of low enemy health (making stealth kills faster), high enemy density, and the Sedna node's accessibility makes it the default choice for focus farming over other exterminate nodes with stealth potential.

The daily focus cap resets at midnight server time (UTC). Players who reach the cap early in the session should switch activities rather than continuing to run focus-farming missions, since additional kills beyond the cap generate zero focus. The cap counter is visible in the operator menu under the active school's display.

Secondary school node investment: after maxing a primary school, unlocking passive nodes in secondary schools (the outer ring of the focus tree) provides cross-school benefits at reduced cost. Naramon's combat-combo passives and Vazarin's healing passives are commonly chosen as secondary investments for players whose primary school is Zenurik or Madurai.

## Convergence orb spawn behavior

Convergence orbs spawn on a recurring timer during missions where a Focus Lens is equipped. The first orb appears approximately 2 minutes into the mission; subsequent orbs appear approximately every 2 minutes after the previous orb is collected or expires. The 8x multiplier window lasts 45 seconds per orb. In a 20-minute Adaro run, approximately 10 Convergence windows are available.

Convergence orbs display as orange glowing markers on the minimap. They are visible from a substantial distance and do not disappear quickly; the orb window persists for approximately 30 seconds if not collected before expiring. Positioning the run route through the minimap-visible area increases Convergence collection rates in stealth runs where detouring to the orb is possible without breaking stealth.

In Sanctuary Onslaught, Convergence orbs spawn inside the active combat zone. ESO efficiency benefits from prioritizing kills during Convergence windows because the multiplied affinity per kill during the window scales both mission progress toward efficiency thresholds and focus generation simultaneously.

## Common mistakes

Exceeding the daily focus cap wastes lens affinity conversion. Track cap progress via the operator menu between missions. Spreading focus farming across multiple days is more efficient than running past the cap in a single session.

Equipping lens items on weapons that are not the primary source of kills in a mission reduces total focus conversion. Lens placement should match kill source. In ESO, the weapons generate more affinity from kills than the warframe, so lens priority belongs on the primary or secondary weapon over the warframe.

Ignoring Convergence orbs entirely during a farming session cuts effective focus yield in half or more compared to actively picking them up. Convergence orbs appear roughly every two minutes during active missions; even brief detours to collect them pay off significantly over a 20-minute run.

## Focus school primer

Zenurik is the most popular school for general content. Energizing Dash restores energy to the warframe passively over time, eliminating the need for Energy Restore consumables in most missions. Zenurik is effective on high-energy-cost frames (Wisp, Rhino, Octavia) and pairs well with Primed Flow for maximum energy economy.

Naramon is the school of choice for melee builds. Its primary benefit is the Shadow Step passive node, which maintains combo multiplier between kills during stealth. At Adaro, Naramon players benefit from both the stealth affinity bonus and combo multiplier uptime, enabling melee weapons with Body Count or Drifting Contact to maintain maximum combo during extended runs.

Madurai is the recommended school for Eidolon hunting. Void Strike accumulates charges during operator mode and releases them as amplified Void damage on the next amp shot. For Tridolon players, Void Strike is the primary Synovia-breaking tool, and the Madurai school provides additional Void Strike stacks compared to other schools.

Vazarin is the support school, providing the Protective Dash ability that generates overshields and healing on dash-through for allies. Most effective in coordinated squad play where the Vazarin operator circles the team between Void Slings.

Unairu is the armor-stripping school, with the Wisp ability projecting a field that reduces enemy armor. Useful against heavily-armored Grineer targets in Steel Path when combined with Corrosive-status weapons.

## Lens crafting and acquisition

Focus Lenses are items installed in the upgrade menu of a warframe or weapon to enable focus conversion. Standard Lenses are sold in Cetus and Fortuna from syndicate standing vendors. Greater Lenses are sold by Cephalon Simaris for standing or crafted with Simaris crafting blueprints. Eidolon Lenses are crafted using Eidolon Shards from Teralyst captures.

Install the highest available lens tier on the equipment that generates the most affinity. For most kill-focused missions, a primary weapon generates more affinity than the warframe. For ability-focused missions (Saryn spreading spores, Mesa using Peacemaker), the warframe generates more affinity from kills.
