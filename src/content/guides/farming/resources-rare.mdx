## Overview

Rare resources -- Orokin Cells, Argon Crystals, Tellurium, and Nitain Extract -- are gating materials for high-tier crafting recipes including Prime warframes, archwing components, and endgame equipment. Unlike common resources that drop in large quantities across many missions, rare resources have limited drop sources, low per-enemy drop rates, and (in Argon Crystal's case) a daily decay mechanic. Planning resource farm sessions for these materials is more important than for standard resources.

This guide covers the optimal missions and methods for each of the four most commonly needed rare resources.

## Prerequisites

- Star chart access to the required planet for each resource
- Nekros available for Desecrate loot duplication (recommended for all non-Archwing resource farms)
- Archwing and Archwing Launcher segment installed (for Tellurium farming)

## Steps

**Orokin Cells** drop from enemies on Saturn, Orokin Derelict/Deimos, and Ceres. The highest density source is the Orokin Derelict Survival (available via Deimos mission nodes after Heart of Deimos quest completion). Infested enemies have higher per-kill Orokin Cell rates than Grineer or Corpus. Run with Nekros Prime using Desecrate to apply a 54% chance per corpse to generate additional resource drops.

**Argon Crystals** drop exclusively from enemies in Void missions. Argon Crystal has a unique decay mechanic: each crystal decays at the rate of one per day beginning the day after the mission is completed. Stockpiling Argon Crystals is therefore inefficient -- farm Argon Crystal only when needed for an active crafting recipe. Europa Mobile Defense missions provide dense enemy counts and short mission durations, making them efficient for targeted Argon runs.

**Tellurium** drops exclusively from Archwing enemies and Sharkwing enemies (underwater Archwing sections on Uranus). No surface mission drops Tellurium. Uranus Sharkwing missions and any Archwing mission on Eris or Uranus are the standard Tellurium sources. The drop rate is low per enemy; runs of 10-15 minutes accumulate the 1-5 Tellurium typically needed for single crafting recipes.

**Nitain Extract** has no direct mission drop source in standard gameplay. It is obtained through Nightwave Intermission cred exchange (15 cred for 3 Nitain, repeated weekly), Ghoul Purge bounties on the Plains of Eidolon, and Ghoul Purge alerts when they are active. Plan Nitain acquisition around active Nightwave seasons rather than attempting to farm it directly.

## Tips

Resource Booster items (purchasable for platinum or occasionally given via Twitch drops and login rewards) double all resource drop quantities for their duration. Activating a Resource Booster before a scheduled rare resource farm session doubles the effective yield of all Desecrate procs, Pilfering Strangledome drops, and direct enemy drops.

Nekros Prime's Desecrate ability checks each enemy corpse individually. In dense survival missions, Nekros can trigger dozens of Desecrate checks per minute, with each 54% check independently applying to each corpse in range. On a high-enemy-density Deimos survival run, a single Nekros contributes substantially more total resource drops than any other single-frame choice.

Khora Prime with Pilfering Strangledome adds a 65% additional resource chance per kill inside the dome. When paired with Nekros (Desecrate) in the same squad, the two systems check independently per corpse, creating expected total multipliers above 2x from the combination alone.

## Common mistakes

Farming Argon Crystal in advance and storing it wastes farm time. Argon Crystal decays daily; any surplus beyond immediate crafting needs disappears within days. Check the exact Argon Crystal requirement before starting a farm and collect only that amount.

Attempting to farm Tellurium on ground missions does not produce drops. Tellurium is strictly an Archwing resource -- equip the Archwing Launcher and enter an Archwing-capable mission (including Uranus Sharkwing) to access Tellurium drops.
