## Overview

Riven Mods are weapon-specific mods with randomized stats generated when the Riven is revealed through challenge completion. Each weapon type (rifle, shotgun, pistol, melee, etc.) has its own Riven mod pool. A Riven's stats are randomly determined from a pool of possible attributes (critical chance, damage, multishot, fire rate, etc.) and scaled by the weapon's Riven Disposition -- a 1-5 star value assigned per-weapon by Digital Extremes based on weapon usage statistics.

Riven farming focuses on obtaining Rivens from guaranteed sources (sorties, Archon Hunts, Circuit) and either using them directly or rerolling with kuva to chase desired stat combinations. High-value Rivens for meta weapons trade on warframe.market for significant platinum amounts.

## Prerequisites

- Mastery Rank 8+ (required to unlock Rivens from sortie)
- The War Within completed (required for kuva access for rerolling)
- Sufficient kuva for intended reroll volume (each roll costs 900-3500 kuva)

## Steps

Complete the daily sortie three-mission chain to earn one sealed Riven Mod as the final reward. Sorties refresh at midnight UTC daily. Each sortie consists of three missions with condition modifiers (Augmented Enemy Armor, Eximus Stronghold, etc.) that require specific preparation. Plan the loadout around the sortie modifiers before entering.

Complete the weekly Archon Hunt (three missions culminating in an Archon boss fight) for an additional guaranteed Riven. Archon Hunts refresh weekly and require more endgame-capable loadouts than sorties due to Archon boss mechanics and increased enemy density.

Obtain additional Rivens through the Circuit in Duviri by selecting Riven Mods when offered as a reward choice. Circuit runs provide the most Rivens per week outside of trading, as each run that offers a Riven is a free extra beyond the daily sortie allotment.

Reveal sealed Rivens by completing the displayed challenge. Common challenges include getting kills with a melee weapon while undetected, reaching a kill count without taking damage, completing a mission with a companion alive, and similar conditions. Complete challenges in appropriate missions: stealth challenges suit spy or exterminate missions on low-level nodes; companion challenges suit any mission with low enemy damage.

Reroll Rivens using kuva at the Riven mod interface. Each roll randomizes all stats. Track the roll history to avoid accidentally rolling past a desirable result. Reroll cost increases per reroll count: the first five rolls cost 900 kuva each; costs increase incrementally to 3500 kuva at roll 100+.

## Tips

Riven Disposition for a specific weapon can be checked in the codex or on community tools like riven.market. High-disposition weapons (disposition 5, shown as five filled circles) generate stronger stats per mod capacity. Low-disposition weapons like the Kuva Bramma (disposition 0.5) produce Rivens with minimal stat bonuses that rarely justify the reroll investment.

The expected value of a Riven on the secondary market depends on both weapon disposition and the specific stats rolled. For most weapons, the practical value of a Riven is maximized by targeting two positive stats with no negatives, or three positives with an acceptable negative (typically magazine size or ammo max). Consult riven.market for current trading prices before committing kuva to rerolling.

Kuva Lich weapon Rivens and Tenet weapon Rivens are capped at disposition 1.0, reflecting the built-in bonus damage percentage of those weapon types. Rolling a Riven for a Kuva Weapon is typically lower-priority compared to a high-disposition base variant of the same weapon type.

## Common mistakes

Completing Riven challenges on star chart nodes with lower-level enemies and then expecting the challenge to carry over to the main game does not work -- challenges must be completed in missions that meet the level or condition requirement exactly as written. Read each challenge description carefully before selecting the mission.

Rerolling a Riven without tracking roll history can result in passing a desirable combination without realizing it. Enable the roll history display in the Riven interface to review the last 20 results before committing to another roll.
