## Overview

Endo is the upgrade currency for all mods in Warframe, required to rank mods from rank 0 to their maximum rank. High-rank mods (rank 10 legendaries like Primed mods) require hundreds of thousands of Endo to fully upgrade. Arbitrations are the primary endgame Endo source, providing thousands of Endo per rotation alongside Arbitration-exclusive mods and vitus essence.

Arcanes are a separate equipment slot that provides passive bonuses triggered by specific conditions. They are obtained primarily from Elite Sanctuary Onslaught rotations, Zariman Cascade missions, Eidolon Tridolon hunts, and the Arcane market on warframe.market. This guide covers both Endo and Arcane farming strategies together, as both are locked behind endgame content and require similar frame investments to access efficiently.

## Prerequisites

- Complete star chart (all nodes activated) for Arbitration access
- Completed Second Dream for operator mode (for Zariman and Eidolon content)
- Mastery Rank 10+ recommended for ESO efficiency

## Steps

For Endo farming via Arbitrations, access the Arbitration mission from the navigation screen. Arbitrations cycle through all star chart nodes on a one-hour timer. The mission type varies (Survival, Defense, Interception, etc.). Each rotation reward includes a fixed Endo amount: Survival rotation A provides 3,000 Endo, rotation B provides 3,000 Endo, and rotation C provides up to 15,000 Endo. Staying through rotation C maximizes Endo per mission, but requires 20+ minutes in Survival variants.

Arbitration Shield Drones spawn alongside standard enemies and provide invulnerability to specific enemy types -- all enemies marked by a drone's effect are immune to damage until the drone itself is killed. Prioritize drone kills over enemy kills to maintain efficient clear speed. Drones are vulnerable to all damage types.

For Arcane farming via Elite Sanctuary Onslaught, access ESO from the navigation's Special Mission tab. ESO waves scale rapidly in enemy level; maintain efficiency above 25% per zone to receive bonus rewards. Each zone completion has a chance to award Arcanes from the ESO drop table. Running with high-damage frames (Saryn Prime for Infested zones, Mesa Prime for mixed) maintains efficiency through the highest-reward zones (zones 8-10).

For Zariman Arcane farming, run Void Cascade missions in the Zariman ten-zero. Exoframe units drop Arcanes directly on kill, with rare arcanes (Arcane Blessing, Arcane Avenger) appearing at low per-kill rates but accumulating over long runs. Zariman Arcanes include the most impactful frames arcanes for general Steel Path.

## Tips

Arbitrations have a rotation C that awards Endo bundles and exclusive mods. The Arbitration mod pool includes powerful mods not available elsewhere: Adaptation (90% damage reduction scaling), Rolling Guard (brief invulnerability on roll), and Arcane Strike (attack speed on arcane trigger). These mods are also the primary currency for the Arbitration shop vitus essence exchange.

Vitus Essence drops from Arbitration enemies at low rates and functions as a secondary currency exchangeable at the Arbitration vendor for Arbitration-exclusive mods and Galvanized mod variants. Galvanized mods provide weapon buffs that scale on kill count, offering significantly higher damage output than base Serration or Point Strike at high kill counts.

Selling max-ranked Arcanes purchased or farmed at low value on warframe.market and replacing them with crafted or purchased BiS (best in slot) arcanes is a common route for players who lack time to farm ESO extensively. Track Arcane prices weekly on warframe.market and buy during price dips.

## Common mistakes

Leaving Arbitration before rotation C wastes the highest Endo reward per time investment. Plan Arbitration runs to include at least rotation C (20 minutes for Survival) before extracting.

Ranking Arcanes partially and selling them discards the investment made in combining duplicates. Always rank an Arcane to maximum before selling or trading.
