## Overview

Endo is the upgrade currency for all mods in Warframe, required to rank mods from rank 0 to their maximum rank. High-rank mods (rank 10 legendaries like Primed mods) require hundreds of thousands of Endo to fully upgrade. Arbitrations are the primary endgame Endo source, providing thousands of Endo per rotation alongside Arbitration-exclusive mods and Vitus Essence.

Arcanes are a separate equipment slot that provides passive bonuses triggered by specific conditions. They are obtained primarily from Elite Sanctuary Onslaught rotations, Zariman Cascade missions, Eidolon Tridolon hunts, and the Arcane market on warframe.market. This guide covers both Endo and Arcane farming strategies together, as both are locked behind endgame content and require similar frame investments to access efficiently.

## Prerequisites

- Complete star chart (all nodes activated) for Arbitration access
- Completed Second Dream for operator mode (for Zariman and Eidolon content)
- Mastery Rank 10+ recommended for ESO efficiency

## Steps

For Endo farming via Arbitrations, access the Arbitration mission from the navigation screen. Arbitrations cycle through all star chart nodes on a one-hour timer. The mission type varies (Survival, Defense, Interception, etc.). Staying through multiple rotations maximizes Endo per mission. Rotation C provides the highest Endo bundles, but requires 20+ minutes in Survival variants.

Arbitration Shield Drones spawn alongside standard enemies and provide invulnerability to specific enemy types -- all enemies marked by a drone's effect are immune to damage until the drone itself is killed. Prioritize drone kills over enemy kills to maintain efficient clear speed. Drones are vulnerable to all damage types.

For Arcane farming via Elite Sanctuary Onslaught, access ESO from the navigation's Special Mission tab. ESO waves scale rapidly in enemy level; maintain efficiency above 25% per zone to receive bonus rewards. Each zone completion has a chance to award Arcanes from the ESO drop table. Running with high-damage frames (Saryn Prime for Infested zones, Mesa Prime for mixed) maintains efficiency through the highest-reward zones (zones 8-10).

For Zariman Arcane farming, run Void Cascade missions in the Zariman ten-zero. Exoframe units drop Arcanes directly on kill, with rare arcanes (Arcane Blessing, Arcane Avenger) appearing at low per-kill rates but accumulating over long runs. Zariman Arcanes include some of the most impactful warframe arcanes for general Steel Path content.

## Tips

Arbitrations feature a rotating mission pool and exclusive reward pool. The Arbitration exclusive mod pool includes powerful mods not available elsewhere: Adaptation (a warframe mod providing scaling damage reduction up to 90%), Rolling Guard (brief invulnerability on roll), and Vigorous Swap (weapon damage bonus on weapon switching), among others. These mods are also exchangeable via Vitus Essence at the Arbitration vendor.

Vitus Essence drops from Arbitration enemies at low rates and functions as a secondary currency exchangeable at the Arbitration vendor for Arbitration-exclusive mods and Galvanized mod variants. Galvanized mods provide weapon buffs that scale on kill count, offering significantly higher damage output than base rifle damage mods like Serration at high kill counts.

Selling max-ranked Arcanes purchased or farmed at low value on warframe.market and replacing them with crafted or purchased best-in-slot arcanes is a common route for players who lack time to farm ESO extensively. Track Arcane prices weekly on warframe.market and buy during price dips.

When farming Endo at Arbitrations, Survival missions offer the most reliable extended runs. The one-hour mission cycle means the Arbitration node can change before the session ends; plan for at least 20 minutes (rotation C) to maximize Endo yield per session.

Endo also drops from rare containers, ayatan sculptures, and enemy loot in standard missions at lower rates. Ayatan Sculptures found during missions can be traded to Maroo in Maroo's Bazaar for Endo; a completed Ayatan Sculpture with all stars installed yields significantly more Endo than an empty one. Fill found Ayatan Sculptures with Ayatan Stars before trading for maximum Endo return.

## Endo yield comparison across sources

Ayatan Sculptures: a filled Ayatan Amber Star grants 900 Endo when traded to Maroo. A filled Ayatan Cyan Star grants 150 Endo each. Sculptures with multiple star slots can yield 1500-4000 Endo per sculpture depending on the star configuration. Maroo's Bazaar weekly treasure hunt mission rewards one Ayatan Sculpture guaranteed per completion, making it a reliable secondary Endo source during the early-game before Arbitration access.

Junk transmutation: four unwanted mods can be combined in Transmutation at a relay to produce a random higher-rarity mod. While not directly an Endo source, transmuting low-value common mods avoids their eventual conversion to Endo at below-optimal rates. Selling fully-ranked common mods for Endo at the Void Trader (Baro Ki'Teer) or through Mod conversion provides more Endo per mod than base sell values.

Arbitrations remain the gold standard for Endo efficiency above MR 15. No other source provides consistent thousands of Endo per 5-minute increment without the complete star chart requirement.

## Common mistakes

Leaving Arbitration before reaching the rotation C reward wastes the highest Endo reward per time investment. Plan Arbitration runs to include at least rotation C (20 minutes for Survival) before extracting.

Ranking Arcanes partially and selling them discards the investment made in combining duplicates. Always rank an Arcane to maximum before selling or trading.

Missing the Arbitration Shield Drone in a fight and unloading damage into an invulnerable enemy wastes time and ammo. Scan the minimap for drone indicators and prioritize them over anything else in the area.

## Endo cost overview for max-ranking common mods

Understanding total Endo investment requirements helps prioritize what to rank first. Common mods at max rank: Serration (rank 10) requires approximately 40,000 Endo total. Vitality (rank 10) requires approximately 40,000 Endo. Primed mods at max rank (rank 10 Legendary mods such as Primed Continuity or Primed Flow) each require hundreds of thousands of Endo -- these represent the highest Endo sinks in the game and are the primary motivator for sustained Arbitration farming.

Rare mods at their lower max ranks (rank 3-5) require substantially less Endo than Legendary mods. A rare rank-5 mod costs approximately 4,000-16,000 Endo depending on rank progression. Budget Endo investment around Legendary mod upgrades first, as common and rare mods can be maxed in early-mid game quantities while Legendary mod max-ranking requires dedicated Arbitration investment.

## Arcane upgrade mechanics

Arcanes stack in rank by combining duplicate copies. Rank 0 Arcane Energize is one unit; rank 1 requires two combined units; rank 2 requires four total; rank 3 requires seven total; rank 4 requires twelve total; rank 5 requires 21 total. Each rank tier requires combining the previous rank plus additional rank-0 copies.

The practical implication: farming rank 5 of a high-value Arcane requires 21 separate Arcane drops. At ESO with low per-drop rates, this represents significant farming investment. Purchasing rank-5 Arcanes from other players on warframe.market eliminates the farming requirement but costs platinum. Track individual Arcane prices across warframe.market to identify when farming is more efficient than purchasing.

Arcanes from different sources drop at different rates. Eidolon Hydrolyst captures provide higher-tier Arcanes (Arcane Energize, Arcane Blessing) at relatively predictable rates across multiple runs. ESO at high zones provides a broader drop table including rarer Arcanes alongside common ones. Zariman Cascade provides specific Arcane types (Arcane Fury, Arcane Avenger) at rates competitive with other high-end sources.
