## Overview

Steel Essence is the Steel Path exclusive currency, obtained by killing Acolyte mini-bosses that spawn during Steel Path missions. It is exchanged at Teshin (found in relays) for cosmetics, Arcane Adapters, kuva, and other Steel Path exclusive rewards. Steel Essence farming is the primary motivation for running Steel Path endless missions beyond the initial node completion bonus.

Acolytes are variants of the Stalker faction with high health, damage, and unique abilities. In Steel Path endless missions, one Acolyte spawns every 5 minutes. Each Acolyte kill grants 2 Steel Essence. A 20-minute survival run will encounter approximately 4 Acolytes, yielding 8 Steel Essence per session.

## Prerequisites

- Completed all standard star chart nodes (required to unlock Steel Path nodes)
- Access to Steel Path via the in-game navigation toggle
- Endgame-capable warframe build (enemy levels start at +100 above base)

## Steps

Activate Steel Path mode via the toggle in the navigation screen. The Steel Path star chart is a parallel overlay of all existing nodes. Open each Steel Path node by completing it -- the first completion grants a bonus 100 Vitus Essence per node, which is a substantial one-time steel essence income during the initial node clear phase.

After completing all nodes, focus on Steel Path endless missions for sustained Steel Essence income. Survival missions provide the most consistent Acolyte spawns because Acolytes appear on a strict 5-minute timer tied to life support cycles. Defense missions also spawn Acolytes but on a wave-based timer that is more variable.

Kill Acolytes immediately on spawn. Acolytes prioritize attacking the player who has the highest enemy kill count, so dedicated damage dealers attract Acolyte aggression naturally. Acolytes are immune to crowd control abilities (sleep, stasis, temporal) but are vulnerable to all damage types. High-fire-rate weapons that can maintain critical hits deal effective damage against their high health pools.

Exchange Steel Essence at Teshin in any relay. The most valuable exchange in early Steel Path progression is the Arcane Adapter (10 Steel Essence), which allows equipping arcanes on melee weapons. Kuva exchange at 1000 kuva per essence is less efficient than Kuva Fortress Survival but is a secondary outlet when Arcane Adapters are fully stocked.

## Tips

Acolyte spawn rate is influenced by active mission time, not kill count. Staying in mission for 20+ minutes ensures maximum Acolyte spawns per session. Short steel path runs (under 10 minutes) miss the majority of Acolyte spawns and are inefficient for Steel Essence income.

The Steel Path honors daily rotation provides a free Steel Essence from completing a single Steel Path mission each day. This daily task takes under 5 minutes with a fast-clear frame and provides passive Steel Essence accumulation for casual players.

Teshin's shop rotates its offerings weekly. Check the current rotation before exchanging essence on items that may not be priority targets for the current week.

## Common mistakes

Extracting from endless missions before the second or third Acolyte spawn significantly reduces per-run Steel Essence yield. At minimum, run to the second Acolyte spawn (10 minutes) before extracting.

Ignoring the initial Steel Path node completion bonus leaves 100 Vitus Essence per node unclaimed. Clear all Steel Path nodes before focusing on endless farming sessions.
