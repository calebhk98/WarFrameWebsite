## Overview

Steel Essence is the Steel Path exclusive currency, obtained by killing Acolyte mini-bosses that spawn during Steel Path missions. It is exchanged at Teshin (found in relays) for cosmetics, Arcane Adapters, Kuva, and other Steel Path exclusive rewards. Steel Essence farming is the primary motivation for running Steel Path endless missions beyond the initial node completion bonus.

Acolytes are variants of the Stalker faction with high health, damage, and unique abilities. In Steel Path endless missions, one Acolyte spawns approximately every 5 minutes. Each Acolyte kill grants 2 Steel Essence. A 20-minute survival run will encounter approximately 4 Acolytes, yielding 8 Steel Essence per session.

## Prerequisites

- Completed all standard star chart nodes (required to unlock Steel Path nodes)
- Access to Steel Path via the in-game navigation toggle
- Endgame-capable warframe build (Steel Path enemies have significantly higher health and damage than base star chart)

## Steps

Activate Steel Path mode via the toggle in the navigation screen. The Steel Path star chart is a parallel overlay of all existing nodes. Open each Steel Path node by completing it -- first completions grant bonus Steel Essence per node, which is a substantial one-time income during the initial node clear phase.

After completing all nodes, focus on Steel Path endless missions for sustained Steel Essence income. Survival missions provide the most consistent Acolyte spawns because Acolytes appear on a strict 5-minute timer tied to life support cycles. Defense missions also spawn Acolytes but on a wave-based timer that is more variable.

Kill Acolytes immediately on spawn. Acolytes prioritize attacking the player who has the highest enemy kill count, so dedicated damage dealers attract Acolyte aggression naturally. Acolytes are resistant to crowd control abilities but are vulnerable to all damage types. High-fire-rate weapons that can maintain critical hits deal effective damage against their high health pools.

Exchange Steel Essence at Teshin in any relay. The most valuable exchange in early Steel Path progression is the Arcane Adapter (10 Steel Essence), which allows equipping arcanes on melee weapons. Kuva exchange is available as a secondary outlet when Arcane Adapters are fully stocked, though the rate is less efficient than dedicated Kuva Siphon farming.

## Tips

Acolyte spawn rate is influenced by active mission time, not kill count. Staying in mission for 20+ minutes ensures maximum Acolyte spawns per session. Short steel path runs (under 10 minutes) miss the majority of Acolyte spawns and are inefficient for Steel Essence income.

The Steel Path Honors daily rotation provides a free Steel Essence from completing a single Steel Path mission each day. This daily task takes under 5 minutes with a fast-clear frame and provides passive Steel Essence accumulation for casual players without requiring dedicated farming sessions.

Teshin's shop rotates its offerings weekly. Check the current rotation before exchanging essence on items that may not be priority targets for the current week. Some weeks feature exclusive cosmetics or limited-time rotation items; prioritize those before spending on standard shop items.

Mesa Prime and Saryn Prime are reliable Steel Essence farming frames for Survival and Defense missions respectively. Mesa Prime's Peacemaker provides consistent high-damage output against Acolytes without requiring ability management during the Acolyte fight. Octavia Prime's Mallet passive can maintain kills during AFK-adjacent play, though active farming is always more efficient.

Disruption missions on Steel Path offer an alternative to Survival for Steel Essence farming. Each conduit activation has a chance to spawn bonus enemies including Acolytes. Completing conduit chains quickly keeps the pace of the mission high and maintains Acolyte spawn frequency. Some players prefer Disruption over Survival because the mission structure prevents monotony across long farming sessions.

## Steel Path map clearing strategy

The Steel Path overlay reuses all existing star chart nodes with increased enemy scaling. Clearing every node grants bonus Steel Essence per node and reveals the full scope of available Steel Path missions for ongoing farming. The recommended clearing approach:

Start with planets near the star chart start point (Earth, Mars, Venus) where Steel Path enemy levels are lower and the missions are shorter. Capture and Exterminate missions provide the fastest individual node completions during the clearing phase.

Mid-game planets (Saturn, Europa, Uranus) have more mission variety and slightly higher enemy levels. Defense and Interception missions take longer per completion but provide more Acolyte spawn opportunities during the clearing phase.

Late planets (Sedna, Eris, Pluto, Ceres Dark Sector) and the Kuva Fortress nodes complete the clearing phase. Once all nodes are cleared, the player retains the Steel Path node bonuses permanently and switches to endless farming for ongoing Steel Essence income.

## Common mistakes

Extracting from endless missions before the second or third Acolyte spawn significantly reduces per-run Steel Essence yield. At minimum, run to the second Acolyte spawn (10 minutes) before extracting. Runs shorter than 10 minutes yield only 2 Steel Essence and are inefficient compared to longer sessions.

Ignoring the initial Steel Path node completion bonus misses a large one-time Steel Essence income. Clear all Steel Path nodes before focusing on endless farming sessions to claim the full node bonus.

Spending Steel Essence on Kuva as the primary use case when Arcane Adapters are still needed is a priority error. Arcane Adapters provide more value per essence than Kuva for most builds, especially in the early Steel Path phase when melee arcane slots are not yet filled.

## Teshin's shop inventory and priority

Teshin's shop at the relays exchanges Steel Essence for a rotating selection of Steel Path exclusive items. Core permanent offerings include:

Arcane Adapter (10 Steel Essence): Adds an arcane slot to a melee weapon. Melee weapons without an Arcane Adapter cannot equip arcanes. First-time buyers should prioritize the adapters for all active melee weapons before spending essence elsewhere.

Kuva (1 Steel Essence per 1000 Kuva): Available as a secondary use for excess essence after Arcane Adapters are purchased. Less efficient than dedicated Kuva Siphon farming but useful when kuva is needed without a separate farming session.

Riven Slivers (1 Steel Essence per sliver): Riven Slivers can be combined (20 slivers) at Palladino in the Iron Wake to form a Veiled Riven Cipher, which converts to a random sealed Riven Mod. The per-essence value compared to other exchanges depends on the trade value of the resulting Riven.

Teshin also sells cosmetics and exclusive Steel Path-only decorations. These have no build-performance impact and should be purchased last when all functional items are acquired.

## Acolyte mechanics and farming efficiency

Acolytes in Steel Path are named variants from the Stalker's retinue (Angst, Violence, Malice, Torment, Mania, Misery). Each has distinct abilities: Angst uses Ash-like abilities, Violence uses heavy attack slams, Malice uses Trinity-like abilities, and others. Understanding each Acolyte's attack patterns reduces the damage taken during encounters.

Acolytes are not immune to all damage reduction. Despite their high health pools, burst-damage weapons and critical hit setups are effective. Weapons with high base damage multiplied by critical hits (Kuva Bramma, Phenmor, Felarx) can eliminate Acolytes quickly. The priority should be consistent damage output without overloading survivability at the expense of kill speed, since Acolytes on a timer mean faster kills equate to more ground enemies killed before the next spawn.
