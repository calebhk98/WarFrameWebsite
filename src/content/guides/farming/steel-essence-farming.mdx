## Overview

Steel Essence is the exclusive currency of the Steel Path game mode, earned primarily by killing Acolyte mini-bosses that spawn during Steel Path missions. Teshin, located in any relay, accepts Steel Essence in exchange for Arcane Adapters, Kuva, Riven Slivers, and rotating cosmetic items. Understanding Acolyte spawn mechanics is the foundation of efficient Steel Essence income.

Steel Path is a parallel star chart overlay available once every standard node is completed. Enemies in Steel Path have 250% bonus health, 250% bonus shields, and 250% bonus armor on top of base values. This scaling makes standard mid-game builds insufficient; dedicated endgame builds are required for efficient Acolyte kills.

Acolytes are named members of the Stalker's retinue. In Steel Path endless missions, one Acolyte spawns approximately every five minutes of mission time. Each Acolyte kill yields exactly 2 Steel Essence. A 30-minute survival run yields approximately 12 Steel Essence from Acolyte kills, plus any bonus Steel Essence from mission completion rewards.

The initial completion of each Steel Path node also grants a one-time bonus of Steel Essence per node cleared. Clearing the entire Steel Path star chart before settling into endless farming adds a meaningful lump sum of Steel Essence to the player's reserves.

## Prerequisites

- All standard star chart nodes completed (required to unlock the Steel Path toggle)
- At least one endgame-capable warframe with a complete mod build suitable for Steel Path enemy levels
- Access to a relay to reach Teshin for Steel Essence exchanges
- Completed The War Within quest (required to unlock the Steel Path star chart)

## Steps

Activate Steel Path mode from the navigation screen using the toggle at the top of the star chart interface. The Steel Path overlay mirrors all standard star chart nodes but applies the 250% health, shield, and armor multiplier to all enemies.

Begin with the star chart clearing phase. Visit every Steel Path node and complete it at least once. The order does not matter for bonus purposes, but starting from planets with lower Steel Path enemy levels (Earth, Mars, Venus) eases the transition into the mode. Each node cleared grants a one-time Steel Essence bonus. Completing the full star chart in this phase is the fastest single source of Steel Essence available to a new Steel Path entrant.

After clearing all nodes, identify the best endless mission node for ongoing farming. Survival missions are the most consistent choice because Acolyte spawn timers are tied to life support cycles, making timing predictable. Select a high-enemy-density survival node such as Mot in the Void or Selkie on Sedna.

Run each session for at least 20 minutes to capture four Acolyte spawns per run. Extracting before the 10-minute mark yields only 2 Steel Essence from Acolytes, which is inefficient. The marginal Steel Essence per additional five minutes remains constant, so longer runs are strictly more efficient.

Kill Acolytes as the top priority when they spawn. Acolytes prioritize the player with the highest kill count, so frames with broad-area kill capabilities attract and hold Acolyte aggression. Acolytes are not immune to any damage type; high crit-damage weapons eliminate them quickly.

Exchange accumulated Steel Essence at Teshin after each farming session. Prioritize Arcane Adapters before spending on other items, as Arcane Adapters unlock the arcane slot on melee weapons and provide the highest per-essence utility for most builds.

## Tips

Acolyte spawn timing is based on active mission time, not enemy kill count. Pausing to pick up resources or waiting for squadmates does not affect spawn timing. The timer runs continuously from mission start.

Running Steel Path Survival with a squad of four speeds up kill rates on non-Acolyte enemies but does not accelerate Acolyte spawn timers. Squads provide survivability and faster clears of non-Acolyte enemies, which keeps life support stable and removes threats during Acolyte encounters.

The Steel Path Honors system provides one free Steel Essence per day for completing any Steel Path mission. This daily reward accumulates passively for players who run Steel Path for other reasons. Set a reminder to claim it by running even a short Steel Path mission each day.

Teshin's shop rotates its cosmetic and limited offerings weekly. Check the current rotation before spending Steel Essence on standard shop items, as some weeks feature exclusive cosmetics that are only available during their rotation window.

Acolytes share resistance profiles with the Stalker. They have no damage-type immunity, making them viable targets for any well-modded weapon. High burst damage is preferable to sustained fire; the goal is eliminating the Acolyte before it repositions or applies its ability effects.

Bringing a frame with large hitbox crowd control (Rhino's Roar, Harrow's Covenant) helps teammates focus Acolyte damage effectively. While crowd control does not stun Acolytes reliably, area denial abilities prevent surrounding enemies from interrupting the Acolyte fight.

## Common mistakes

Extracting after the first Acolyte spawn (at the five-minute mark) limits each run to 2 Steel Essence, making farming extremely slow. The Steel Essence per hour rate approximately doubles for each additional 10-minute segment completed.

Spending Steel Essence on Kuva before acquiring all needed Arcane Adapters is a priority error for players still filling melee arcane slots. The Arcane Adapter provides permanent build value; Kuva can be acquired efficiently through dedicated Kuva Siphon or Kuva Flood runs.

Ignoring the initial node clearing phase misses a large one-time Steel Essence reward. New Steel Path players often immediately begin endless farming without first completing every node, forgoing the per-node bonuses.

Attempting Steel Path with an undersized build leads to slow Acolyte kills and high risk of mission failure. Steel Path enemy health requires builds capable of delivering several hundred thousand damage per second to clear Acolytes within a practical time window.

Using frames without passive survivability in Steel Path leads to frequent deaths that reduce run efficiency. Adaptation, high shield gating capability, or damage-absorbing abilities are necessary for extended Steel Path sessions.

## Acolyte names and abilities

The six Acolytes encountered in Steel Path have distinct ability sets. Angst mirrors Ash abilities and uses Shuriken and Teleport. Violence uses Excalibur Radial Javelin and Slash Dash. Malice uses Trinity Well of Life and Energy Vampire. Torment uses Nyx abilities including Chaos. Mania uses Loki Invisibility and Radial Disarm. Misery uses a combination of Shadow abilities.

Understanding each Acolyte's behavior allows players to position appropriately. Mania's Invisibility makes it temporarily untargetable; wait for it to drop invisibility before committing high-damage shots. Malice's Energy Vampire can drain energy rapidly; maintaining energy independence (Arcane Energize, Hunter Adrenaline) prevents energy starvation during the encounter.

## Teshin shop priority guide

| Item | Cost (Steel Essence) | Priority |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Arcane Adapter | 10 | Highest -- buy first for each melee slot |
| Kuva | 1 per 1,000 Kuva | Secondary -- lower efficiency than Siphon runs |
| Riven Sliver | 1 per sliver | Situational -- 20 slivers combine into Riven Cipher |
| Cosmetics | Varies | Last -- after functional items acquired |

Riven Slivers require 20 to combine into one Riven Cipher at Palladino in Iron Wake. The resulting Veiled Riven can be traded or revealed; platinum value varies by weapon. This exchange is a valid secondary use of excess Steel Essence once Adapters are fully stocked.
