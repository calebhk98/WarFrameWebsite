## Overview

The Command Intrinsic tree addresses the core challenge of solo Railjack operation: a warship designed for a crew of four is difficult to run alone. Command ranks allow the player to recruit NPC crew members -- called Crew -- from Ticker at Fortuna or from the Railjack Dry Dock interface. These recruits can be assigned to pilot, gunner, engineer, or combat defender positions, filling roles that would otherwise require human squadmates.

Command is the most recently added Intrinsic tree and substantially changed the solo Railjack experience. Before Command, solo Railjack pilots managed all ship functions manually -- piloting, forge, repairs, turrets -- in a demanding multi-task loop. With maxed Command, a solo player can assign NPC crew to three of the four available crew positions and focus personally on the most strategically important role.

## Mechanics

Early Command ranks unlock the ability to hire a single NPC crew member. The recruited NPC has a stat profile covering Combat, Endurance, and Mechanical skills, which govern their effectiveness in combat roles, their survival under fire, and their speed at engineering tasks respectively. Higher-stat crew recruits are available at higher Command ranks.

Mid-tier Command ranks increase the maximum number of NPC crew from one to three. Three NPC crew members can fill the gunner, engineer, and pilot positions, allowing the player to focus entirely on the strategic layer -- boarding runs, tactical map management, or simply piloting themselves while NPC crew handle turrets and repairs.

High Command ranks improve the baseline stats of available NPC recruits and unlock the ability to assign crew to the Railjack's combat defense role, where they engage Grineer and Corpus boarders who enter the ship during missions rather than the player needing to handle this manually.

The Command tree also governs the Railjack's Cephalon -- the AI navigation and combat intelligence overlay -- which provides targeting data and situational alerts at higher ranks.

## Progression

Command follows the 10-rank structure. Investment priorities depend heavily on playstyle. Players who always have a full human crew have little practical need for Command investment. Players who frequently solo or duo Railjack content should treat Command as their primary investment, reaching rank 5 quickly to unlock the second and third NPC slots before distributing points elsewhere.

## Recommended setup

For solo Railjack operators, Command ranks 1 through 7 represent the most impactful investment window. This unlocks three crew slots and access to recruits with higher base stats. Ranks 8 through 10 continue to improve recruit quality and provide minor passive bonuses.

Crew configuration for solo play typically places the highest Mechanical stat recruit in the engineer role, the highest Combat stat recruit in the defender role, and a balanced recruit in the pilot or gunner position based on which the player wants automated coverage on.

## Lore

Command Intrinsic ranks were framed in the narrative as the player's Railjack developing a command culture -- a relationship between the Tenno and the people they choose to crew with them. The NPC crew recruits available from Ticker represent survivors and refugees from the conflict zones across the Origin System who sought purpose aboard a Tenno warship. Teshin has no commentary on Command specifically, but Cephalon Cy notes that Old War crews developed deep operational bonds that exceeded the formal rank structure -- a dynamic the Command system attempts to replicate through long-term crew investment.
