## Overview

Volatile missions are a Railjack mission type focused on sabotage objectives. The crew must clear a path through enemy fighters and crewships, board the target capital ship, locate and destroy its reactor cooling systems, and extract before the capital ship's reactor breaches and destroys the vessel. This introduces an extraction timer to the Railjack mission structure, a constraint absent from Skirmish and Void Storm missions.

Volatile missions require coordination between crew members more explicitly than Skirmish. The boarding team must work efficiently inside the capital ship while the remaining crew defends the Railjack against the continued fighter response and any crewships launched as reinforcements. The Railjack cannot be abandoned for long without hull damage accumulating.

## Mechanics

The exterior phase of a Volatile mission resembles standard Skirmish -- navigate to the capital ship while eliminating the fighter escort and any crewships that would threaten the boarding party's approach. Once the capital ship exterior defenses are neutralized or sufficiently reduced, the boarding team deploys via Archwing to an airlock entry point.

Inside the capital ship, the team navigates to the reactor chamber. Corpus capital ships require destroying cooling modules around the reactor; Grineer Galleons require destroying specific generator nodes. After sufficient damage to the cooling or generation system, the reactor enters breach mode and the extraction timer begins. The boarding team must reach the exit point before the timer expires. Players still aboard the ship when it detonates are downed.

The Railjack crew outside must maintain the ship's hull integrity through the boarding phase, as reinforcement fighter waves arrive after the boarding team deploys. An engineer maintaining Revolite production for repairs is particularly valuable in this phase.

## Progression

Volatile missions drop the same Proxima-region resources and component rewards as Skirmish. The sabotage nature of the mission makes it slightly faster to complete than Skirmish at equivalent difficulty levels, as the mission ends when the capital ship detonates rather than requiring full fighter clearance. This makes Volatile a competitive choice for resource and component farming per unit of time.

## Recommended setup

Boarding teams should include players with strong close-quarters Warframe builds. The interior of a Grineer Galleon or Corpus capital ship is a ground combat environment, not a space combat environment. Frames suited for rapid elimination of armored enemies -- high-damage AoE, armor stripping, or status spam -- complete the interior objective faster and reduce the exposure time for the exterior Railjack crew.

The pilot should maintain an escape route trajectory during the extraction phase, positioning the Railjack near the capital ship's exit airlock so the boarding team can board as soon as they exit.

## Lore

Volatile in Old War doctrine referred to the surgical removal of an enemy capital ship from the battlefield without extended engagement -- a priority for Orokin command that wanted to preserve Void energy resources and avoid collateral damage from extended fleet battles. Destroying a Sentient capital ship in a controlled reactor breach released less ambient Void energy than a prolonged engagement with full weapons discharge. The technique carried over into post-Old War operations against Grineer and Corpus infrastructure, where speed and precision remained the Tenno's defining strategic advantages.
