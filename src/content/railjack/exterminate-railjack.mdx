## Overview

Exterminate missions in Railjack share the basic structure of ground Exterminate: eliminate a target count of enemies to complete the mission. In Railjack context, this count includes enemy fighters destroyed by the ship's turrets and ordnance, crewship crew eliminated during boarding operations, and enemies encountered on ground-side objectives accessed from capital ships.

The hybrid nature of Exterminate Railjack missions means that the kill count is fulfilled across multiple engagement contexts. A crew that focuses exclusively on space combat without boarding may find the fighter count alone insufficient; boarding operations that clear crewship interiors and ground objectives contribute meaningfully to reaching the required elimination total.

Exterminate Railjack nodes exist across the Proxima regions and offer an alternative structure for players who prefer cleaner objective framing -- eliminate a number, mission ends -- over the more complex objective trees of Volatile or the sustained engagement of Skirmish.

## Mechanics

The kill counter increments for each enemy destroyed by the crew, regardless of whether the kill happened from turret fire, ordnance, Archwing combat, or inside a boarded vessel. All four combat contexts contribute to the same pool.

Fighter waves in Exterminate nodes are continuous until the kill count is reached. Unlike Skirmish, where the mission ends after the capital ship is destroyed, Exterminate missions end on kill count alone. There is no fixed final objective outside the kill target, making the mission length primarily a function of the crew's combined DPS across all combat contexts.

Hull breach events, Void Storm conditions if present, and crewship reinforcements all occur under the same mechanics as other Railjack mission types. The Exterminate structure does not simplify these elements.

## Progression

Exterminate Railjack missions grant Intrinsic points and region-specific resource drops on the same formula as other Railjack mission types. Component drops occur from crewships and capital ship interior objectives. The mission type does not have a unique reward track -- it draws from the same Proxima reward pool as Skirmish and Volatile.

## Recommended setup

Exterminate missions favor high combined-damage crew configurations. The fastest kill-count accumulation comes from efficient turret gunners handling the fighter kill count simultaneously with boarding parties clearing crewship and capital interiors. An uninterrupted dual-track operation -- space combat and boarding simultaneously -- minimizes mission time.

Frames optimized for efficient enemy clearing are the preferred boarding option. AoE-capable builds that clear multiple targets per ability activation accumulate boarding kills faster than single-target precision builds.

Solo operators with high Command Intrinsic can assign NPC gunners to turrets while personally handling boarding objectives, creating an effective split-operation even without a human crew.

## Lore

The Exterminate mission type in its Railjack iteration reflects the Orokin military objective category of total-area denial: eliminate all hostile presence within a defined space volume. This was used in Old War scenarios where Sentient forward elements had established a presence in a Proxima zone that would allow them to threaten nearby civilian habitats or Orokin logistics corridors. Removing every enemy asset from the zone, not just the command element, ensured no residual threat remained.
