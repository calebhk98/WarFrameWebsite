## Overview

The Engineering Intrinsic tree governs the maintenance and resupply functions aboard the Railjack. Engineering ranks improve the efficiency of the Forge -- the Railjack's onboard fabrication station -- which produces ammunition, battle payloads, and repair tools from raw Railjack resources. Engineering also governs the speed and effectiveness of hull breach repairs, which occur when the ship takes sufficient damage to crack its hull.

The Engineering role is often the least glamorous but most stabilizing crew position. An effective engineer keeps ammunition flowing, patches hull breaches before they cascade into fires or system failures, and monitors resource reserves to ensure the Forge can sustain production through prolonged engagements. Without Engineering investment, missions that run long see ammunition depletion, unrepaired breaches, and eventual ship loss.

## Mechanics

Early Engineering ranks reduce Forge crafting time and resource consumption, allowing more frequent production cycles within the same resource budget. Each production cycle at the Forge generates a stack of the selected item -- ammunition for turrets and the forward guns, Revolite for hull repairs, or battle payloads for ordnance.

Mid-tier Engineering ranks introduce Forge efficiency multipliers that produce more output per input. This is the Engineering equivalent of a damage multiplier -- the same resource input yields more product, extending the sustainable engagement duration of the ship.

Higher Engineering ranks provide passive bonuses to fire extinguishing speed and the ability to repair hull breaches more quickly while combat is ongoing around the player. Fires aboard the Railjack deal ongoing hull damage; an engineer who can clear fires faster than they deal cumulative damage is functionally maintaining the ship's effective health.

## Progression

Engineering follows the 10-rank structure. Points invested in Engineering have the clearest return for solo operators who must manage both pilot duties and ship maintenance simultaneously. In a full crew, a dedicated engineer with high Engineering ranks frees other players from monitoring the forge and breach panels entirely.

## Recommended setup

Engineering ranks 1 through 5 provide the most impactful Forge efficiency improvements. Ranks 6 through 10 continue to reduce production time and resource cost but with diminishing returns relative to lower ranks. Dedicated engineers in full crews often invest through rank 7 or 8 and distribute remaining points toward Tactical for the multi-role flexibility it enables.

Solo Railjack operators should treat Engineering as a co-priority with Piloting. The Forge must sustain ammo and Revolite production throughout a mission, and without sufficient Engineering rank, solo resource management becomes a significant time constraint.

## Lore

Engineering officers in Old War Railjack doctrine held a status parallel to combat crew, not subordinate to it. Orokin fleet command recognized that a Railjack's combat lifespan was bounded entirely by its engineering crew's ability to keep pace with battle damage. The Intrinsic tree's structure, which can develop an engineering specialist as fully as a pilot or gunner, reflects that doctrinal equivalence.
