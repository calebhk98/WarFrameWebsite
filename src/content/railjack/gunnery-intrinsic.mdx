## Overview

The Gunnery Intrinsic tree governs the effectiveness of Railjack turret operators and the use of the Railjack's forward artillery. Gunnery ranks provide improvements to turret damage multipliers, targeting lock-on speed, heat dissipation for sustained fire, and the forward artillery's ability to destroy crewships and fighters at range. It is the primary tree for players who crew turret positions during Railjack missions.

High Gunnery Intrinsic rank transforms turret operation from a supplementary role into the primary fighter-elimination method for a crew. At maximum rank, a skilled gunner can clear entire waves of fighters before they reach attack range of the Railjack, dramatically reducing incoming hull damage and the frequency of hull breach repair events.

Gunnery Intrinsic is one of the first trees that solo pilots invest in, because the forward artillery -- operated by the pilot -- benefits directly from Gunnery ranks through improved artillery effectiveness and reduced artillery overheat.

## Mechanics

Early Gunnery ranks provide passive damage bonuses to all turrets and improvements to the lock-on assist system, which helps players acquire moving fighter targets. Lock-on assist in Railjack functions similarly to aim assist -- it pulls the reticle toward the nearest valid target within a cone, reducing the manual tracking effort required to maintain sustained fire on small, fast fighters.

Mid-tier Gunnery ranks introduce the ability to operate Archwing weapons while inside Archwing with improved effectiveness, linking Archwing deployment to the Gunnery tree thematically. The transition between Archwing and turret roles is common in active Railjack play.

The highest Gunnery ranks provide access to the Pilot Gunnery mode, where the pilot themselves can engage forward-mounted turrets at improved effectiveness rather than relying on a dedicated gunner crew member. This is particularly valuable in solo or duo operation.

## Progression

The Gunnery tree follows the 10-rank structure shared by all Intrinsic trees. Points are allocated from the same shared Intrinsic point pool as Tactical, Piloting, Engineering, and Command. The early ranks of Gunnery have the largest per-rank combat impact for turret gunners, making it efficient to invest to rank 5 or 6 quickly before spreading points to other trees.

## Recommended setup

For players who fill the gunner role in an organized crew, Gunnery ranks 1 through 6 cover the most significant accuracy and damage improvements. Ranks 7 through 10 continue to improve turret output but have diminishing returns relative to the cost in Intrinsic points.

Solo pilots should invest enough Gunnery to access improved forward artillery targeting, which enables effective crewship targeting at long range without maneuvering into close gun range.

## Lore

The Gunnery Intrinsic reflects the specialized role of Railjack weapons officers in Old War doctrine. Orokin military theory assigned turret positions to crew members with specific attunement to the ship's energy weapons, believing that a bonded gunner -- one with accumulated operational experience with a specific weapon system -- outperformed even technically superior equipment operated by an unfamiliar operator. The Intrinsic system embeds this philosophy as a progression mechanic.
