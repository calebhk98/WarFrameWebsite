## Overview

Skirmish is the foundational Railjack combat mission type. The objective is to destroy a specific number of enemy fighters and crewships, then board or destroy the capital ship at the mission's center. Skirmish missions are available across all Proxima regions and scale in difficulty by region, from Earth Proxima at the low end to the Steel Path variants of the Veil Proxima at the high end.

Skirmish provides the most direct expression of Railjack's design intent: a crew managing space combat from the ship, with boarding runs against crewships and the capital ship to handle heavy targets. The mission completes when the final objective is eliminated, which is typically the capital ship at the center of the engagement zone.

Skirmish missions are the recommended entry point for new Railjack pilots because the objectives are straightforward -- destroy enemies, then destroy the primary target -- without the additional mechanics of Void Storm Relics or the specific engineering challenge of Volatile missions.

## Mechanics

The combat loop in Skirmish involves the pilot navigating between fighter clusters and crewships while gunners handle incoming fire and turret operators eliminate fighters. Crewships have too much health to destroy with turret fire alone at most Railjack configurations; they require either direct ordnance hits (missiles) or boarding. Boarding a crewship means deploying from the Railjack via Archwing or Slingshot, entering the crewship, killing the interior crew, and sabotaging the reactor to destroy the vessel from inside.

The capital ship at the center of the mission zone functions as the final boss of the Skirmish. Depending on the region and faction, this is either a Grineer Galleon or a Corpus Obelisk. The capital ship typically requires a boarding party to reach and destroy an interior objective while the Railjack holds the perimeter against the remaining fighters.

## Progression

Skirmish missions grant Intrinsic points, region-specific resources (Titanium, Dirac, Carbides, Isos), and component drops that include Railjack avionics, archwing parts, and faction-specific weapon components. Higher Proxima region Skirmish missions drop higher-tier components with better stat ranges.

Completing Skirmish missions is the primary way to build up the Intrinsic point pool for a new Railjack operator.

## Recommended setup

For Earth and Saturn Proxima Skirmish, a Railjack with even starter-tier components performs adequately. The pilot should have at least Piloting rank 3 for reliable boost management. A second player covering a turret significantly reduces the time required to clear fighter waves.

For Veil Proxima Skirmish and Steel Path variants, a fully optimized Railjack with max-tier components and high Intrinsic investment in Piloting and Gunnery is necessary to sustain through the extended fighter and crewship counts.

## Lore

Skirmish in Old War context referred to the forward-contact engagements that Railjacks were built to handle -- rapid interception of Sentient forward elements before they could close to range of heavier Orokin capital ships. The current Skirmish missions against Grineer and Corpus forces adapt this doctrine to the factional conflicts of the modern Origin System, with the Railjack used as a strike asset against logistics convoys and patrol elements rather than the existential Sentient threat it was originally built for.
