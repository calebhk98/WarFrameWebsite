## Overview

Void Storm missions are a Railjack mission type that integrates the standard Void Relic system into space combat. Players bring Void Relics to Void Storm nodes and open them during the mission to receive Void Trace bonuses and Prime part rewards, combining the Railjack gameplay loop with the Prime farming loop that occupies a large portion of Warframe's endgame economy.

Void Storm nodes exist across multiple Proxima regions. The Void energy that permeates these nodes creates an environmental hazard during missions -- periodic Void Storm surges that temporarily reduce visibility, interfere with ship systems, or expose the crew to Void-energy effects that increase Operator capability during the surge. These hazards add a navigational layer to the base Railjack mission objectives.

The integration of Relics into Railjack was designed to make Railjack a viable Prime farming method and to give players who enjoy the space combat loop a path toward the same rewards as traditional Void Fissure missions.

## Mechanics

Void Relics equipped by squad members become active during the Void Storm mission. As the mission progresses and the Void energy level builds through combat and objective completion, fissure cracks appear in the mission space and on boarding objectives within enemy vessels. Destroying or interacting with these fissures grants Void Traces to the squad and ultimately resolves into a Relic opening event at mission end, following the same reward selection logic as ground-based Void Fissure missions.

The Void Storm's environmental hazard manifests as periodic pulses that affect the Railjack and its crew. Hull damage from Void energy surges is real and compounds with combat damage. Managing the ship's hull integrity during heavy Void Storm periods while simultaneously completing the mission objective is the defining challenge of the mission type.

## Progression

Void Storm missions do not have their own progression track. Completing them generates Railjack Intrinsic points, mission-specific resource rewards from the Proxima region, and Prime parts from Relic openings. Players progress their Railjack's overall capability through standard Intrinsic investment and component upgrades.

The Relic tier accessible at a given Void Storm node corresponds to the Proxima region's difficulty level. Lower Proxima regions host Lith and Meso Relics; higher regions offer Neo and Axi Relics. Selecting a Void Storm node in the appropriate region is the primary way to target specific Relic tiers.

## Recommended setup

A Railjack configured for sustained engagement -- high hull integrity, efficient forge output, and turret coverage -- handles Void Storm missions effectively because the Void Storm itself adds ongoing damage events to the baseline combat damage. Engineering Intrinsic investment pays dividends in Void Storm contexts because Revolite consumption for hull repairs is higher than in standard missions.

Bringing a full squad of four with all members carrying the same-era Relics maximizes the chance of receiving a desirable reward at mission end, as Relic sharing during the reward selection phase applies the same rules as ground Void Fissures.

## Lore

Void Storms in Railjack's lore are not passive phenomena. They are understood as consequences of the damage done to the Void's structural stability during the Old War -- particularly during the mass Transference events and Sentient incursions that destabilized Void space. The anomalies that persist in Proxima space are residual fractures, slowly widening as the Void's internal topology continues to degrade. Operating within them is dangerous but provides unique access to Void energy concentrations that nowhere else in the Origin System offers.
