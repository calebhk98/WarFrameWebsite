## Overview

The Railjack is a large Tenno warship originally developed during the Old War as a mobile strike platform for Tenno operatives. The Empyrean update introduced Railjack as a playable space combat system, allowing players to pilot, crew, and customize their own warship for missions in the Proxima regions -- space zones surrounding the planets of the Origin System.

Each player or clan builds their own Railjack in the Dojo's Dry Dock room. The construction process requires specific components recovered through the Rising Tide quest and contributed resources. Once built, the Railjack can be piloted by the player and crewed by up to three additional teammates, each taking roles at the turrets, in the forge, or on away missions to enemy crewships and objectives.

The Railjack serves as both a combat vessel and a transportation hub. Missions launched from the Railjack navigate to objectives within the Proxima zone, which include destroying enemy fighters, boarding crewships, securing objectives on enemy capital ships, and completing ground-side missions accessed through enemy vessel boarding.

## Mechanics

The Railjack is controlled from the cockpit by the pilot. Piloting involves three-dimensional navigation within the Proxima space environment, avoiding hazards, maneuvering around debris fields, and positioning for engagement with enemy fighters and crewships. The ship has its own health pool, forward artillery for large targets, and hull breach mechanics where damage can cause onboard fires, hull damage panels, and systems failures that crew members must repair.

Crew roles during a mission are distributed naturally. One player pilots; others operate side turrets mounted on the hull exterior, use the forge to replenish ammunition and forge battle consumables, staff the tactical map for strategic ability use, or pilot Archwing to conduct boarding operations. Solo Railjack operation is supported -- the player controls all critical systems with additional crew AI available through Command Intrinsic progression.

The Railjack has four primary systems: the Reactor (energy pool for abilities and systems), the Engines (speed and maneuverability), Shields (hull protection), and the Hull (base health). Each system has its own stat profile and can be upgraded through component installation.

## Progression

Railjack progression operates through the Intrinsics system, which has five trees: Tactical, Gunnery, Piloting, Engineering, and Command. Each tree has 10 ranks and provides passive and active improvements to the corresponding role. Intrinsic points are earned by completing Railjack missions.

Ship components -- Reactors, Engines, Shield arrays, and the Plating (hull equivalent) -- are acquired through mission rewards and can be installed to change the Railjack's stat profile. Armaments include forward guns, turrets, and ordnance (missiles), each with their own slot and upgradeable components.

The Avionic system, later revised through game updates, provides passive bonuses to the ship through installed modules. These function similarly to mods on Warframes and weapons, allowing players to customize the ship's performance characteristics toward speed, firepower, or survivability.

## Recommended setup

A well-equipped Railjack for endgame Proxima operations requires high-tier components in all four system slots and a full Intrinsic complement in the operator's chosen role. For pilots, Piloting Intrinsic ranks 6 through 10 provide the most impactful maneuverability improvements. For crew-focused operators, Command Intrinsic progression enables effective NPC crew assignment.

Ordnance selection depends on the mission type. Crewship-heavy missions benefit from high-damage ordnance capable of one-shotting or two-shotting enemy crewships. Fighter-dense missions benefit from turret configurations with high fire rate and wide coverage arcs.

## Lore

The Railjack was built during the Old War as a direct counter to Sentient capital ships that proved resistant to conventional Tenno strike team tactics. Its original design team included Cephalon Cy, an Orokin-era military intelligence Cephalon who survived the Old War in fragments and whose reconstruction forms a central part of the Rising Tide quest. Cy now serves as the Railjack's tactical intelligence, providing mission briefings and in-mission commentary.

The specific Railjack recovered through the Rising Tide quest was derelict, its crew lost, its systems dormant for an unknown span of time following the Old War. Its restoration by the player represents the Tenno reactivating a capability that had been dormant since before the Collapse.
