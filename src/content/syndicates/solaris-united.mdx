## Overview

Solaris United is an open-world faction based in Fortuna on Venus, composed of Solaris workers who have rebelled against indentured servitude under Nef Anyo and the Corpus. The Solari are human-machine hybrids engineered to work in the underground processing facilities of Venus, tasked with refining Orokin terraformation coolant. Solaris United offers players access to unique equipment, cosmetics, and mission content, with the highest ranks granting access to profitable Void Fissure heist operations.

Unlike the classic six standing syndicates, Solaris United uses a Debt-Bond system for ranking and offers a wider range of customization tools and weaponry. The faction emphasizes commerce, crafting, and community building, with ranks unlocking access to specialized vendors like Smokefinger (gems), The Business (fish), Rude Zuud (Kitguns), and Legs (MOA companions).

## Standing rewards

Solaris United ranks are titled differently from the classic six syndicates and progress through five tiers before reaching the maximum rank of Old Mate. Each rank tier grants access to increasingly powerful and specialized items. The faction emphasizes exotic weaponry and open-world conveniences: fishing rod upgrades, Kitgun and Zaw blueprints, MOA Pet components, and the ability to manufacture advanced Archwing gear.

At the neutral standing level, players can perform introductory bounties and conservation activities. Outworker rank (first tier) unlocks basic cosmetics and Void Fissure mission access through the Syndicate console. Rapscallion tier introduces themed weapons and equipment. Doer tier (third) expands fishing upgrades and grants advanced blueprint access. Cove rank (fourth) provides Archwing and pet equipment components. The highest tier, Old Mate, permits access to the lucrative Void Fissure heist operations and unlocks the final vendor inventories.

Rank progression requires Debt-Bonds, which are earned through bounties and purchased from Ticker with credits. Unlike the classic syndicates where standing is earned per mission, Solaris United ties progression directly to Debt-Bond expenditure, creating a more deliberate pacing system.

## Daily cap and grind notes

Solaris United does not impose a hard daily standing cap like the classic six syndicates. Instead, players are limited by the availability of daily bounties and the rate at which Debt-Bonds can be farmed. Each bounty grants between 100 and 1,000 standing depending on difficulty, with higher-level bounties offering greater rewards. The Business accepts fish for standing, and Smokefinger accepts gems; both provide flexible grinding opportunities outside the bounty structure.

Debt-Bond farming is the primary limiting factor for rapid ranking. Tickets can exchange credits for Debt-Bonds at a rate of 500 credits per bond. A single rank up costs between 2 and 5 Debt-Bonds of mixed types (Training, Shelter, Medical, Advances, Familial). This creates a soft cap where progression is tied to playtime in Fortuna's mines and lakes rather than a fixed standing threshold.

To maximize grinding efficiency, players should prioritize the highest-level bounties (level 4 Heist-prep) available to them, farm resources for Debt-Bond trading with Ticker, and use the Granum Void minigame (accessible via Void Fissure tiles on Venus) for additional standing bursts. The Granum Void provides standing equal to the bounty level completed within it, making it an excellent supplement to regular bounty grinding.

## Recommended order to rank

Begin at neutral standing and complete low-to-medium bounties to farm initial Debt-Bonds. Once Outworker is reached, purchase additional Debt-Bonds from Ticker and prioritize Rapscallion ranking, as this unlocks access to the second-tier bounty pool with higher standing rewards.

Progress toward Doer rank to unlock Void Fissure Mission access (3-4 level bounties), which significantly accelerates both standing gains and resource accumulation. From Doer onward, players should run the highest available bounty tier continuously while collecting Debt-Bonds from bounty rewards and Ticker.

The jump to Old Mate (rank 5) requires 5 Familial, 5 Advances, 3 Medical Debt-Bonds in addition to the cumulative costs of prior ranks. This final tier grants full Heist access, which offers the highest bounty standing (2,000 per run) and access to exclusive Void Fissure rewards. Experienced players often set a goal of reaching Old Mate before farming endgame open-world content, as the heist bounties provide superior standing-per-hour rates.

## Notes

Solaris United stands apart from other factions due to its open-world setting and connection to Venus-based crafting (Kitguns, Zaws, Amp components). Ranking Solaris United unlocks access to multiple vendors, each specializing in unique equipment: The Business handles fish, Smokefinger handles gems and blueprints, Rude Zuud crafts Kitguns, and Legs supplies MOA Pets.

The Debt-Bond system is less punitive than syndicate sigil negative standing but requires active credit spending to accelerate ranking. New players should not neglect Solaris United if they wish to access Kitgun blueprints and fishing rod upgrades early, as these tools simplify later open-world farming significantly.

The Void Fissure Heist mission line (accessible at Old Mate) offers some of the best profit margins in the game, granting 2,000 standing per mission plus significant credit and void fissure rewards. This makes reaching Old Mate a strategic priority for long-term Venus farming.
