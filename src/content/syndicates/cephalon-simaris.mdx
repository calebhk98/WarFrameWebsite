## Overview

Cephalon Simaris operates the Sanctuary Enclave, a research facility accessible within all Tenno Relays. Unlike the six traditional standing-tier syndicates, Simaris operates as an independent entity focused solely on Synthesis—a process of deconstructing creatures and preserving them as data within the Sanctuary database. Simaris maintains neutrality toward all other syndicates and charges standing exclusively for synthesis research and equipment rewards. This unique position allows players to progress Simaris standing alongside any other faction without alignment penalties or bonuses.

The syndicate functions without rank restrictions. Players accumulate standing indefinitely with no maximum cap, though daily earning is limited by a daily cap mechanic. This accessibility makes Simaris an attractive choice for newer players seeking progression without the complex faction politics of the standing-tier syndicates.

## Standing acquisition and synthesis

Standing with Cephalon Simaris derives exclusively from Synthesis tasks conducted in the field. Synthesis is the act of scanning enemies with a specialized Synthesis Scanner, a one-time-use tool. The scanner deconstructs the target at the cellular level, preserving all genetic and behavioral data within the Sanctuary database.

Two task types exist. Personal Target Synthesis tasks are unique per-enemy missions issued once per enemy type. Completing a target task yields a Research Imprint, a tradeable item, plus standing. Daily Synthesis tasks are randomized missions offering either a single high-level target or two to four common targets. Daily task completion grants standing and Endo (an upgrade material). The standing gain from synthesis scales with enemy difficulty level—higher-level Steel Path enemies yield substantially more standing than level-five enemies.

Stealth gameplay provides a standing multiplier. Enemies scanned while the player remains undetected award more standing than conventional scans. Frames like Ash, Loki, Ivara, and Octavia excel at maximizing these multipliers when paired with invisibility duration mods.

## Daily cap and standing mechanics

Cephalon Simaris imposes a daily standing cap of 125,000, substantially higher than the 25,000 cap shared by the traditional six syndicates. This cap resets daily at the same time as other syndicate caps, allowing players to pursue major standing gains if they dedicate time to synthesis hunting.

Notably, daily quest completion bonuses—rewards granted after completing a daily task—do not count toward the daily cap. This design allows players to complete synthesis tasks, hit the cap, and still claim the daily reward, effectively gaining additional standing beyond the nominal cap by executing careful timing. Skilled players can exploit this mechanic to accelerate standing progression significantly.

Standing is earned with Simaris regardless of other active syndicate selection. Players do not need to switch to Simaris to earn standing with him; synthesis provides standing automatically. This contrasts sharply with the six standing-tier syndicates, where only one active selection receives affinity conversion.

## Rewards and offerings

Simaris offers a narrow but powerful selection of items. Synthesis Scanners (5,000 standing each) and Kinetic Siphon Traps (5,000 standing) are foundational tools for progression. Transmute Cores (5,000 standing) are crafting components used to transform unwanted relics.

Mid-tier rewards include mods: Looter (increases resource drops), Detect Vulnerability (reveals weak points on scanned enemies), and Reawaken (revives fallen companions). These cost between 75,000 and 100,000 standing.

High-value items include the Simulor blueprint (75,000 standing), a purely functional yet niche primary weapon, and the Heliocor blueprint (75,000 standing), a dual-status melee weapon. The Exilus Warframe Adapter (75,000+ standing, depending on rank) unlocks an additional mod slot on any warframe, making it valuable for build optimization. The Simulacrum Access Key (50,000 standing) grants entry to a solo practice arena where players can test builds without consequence, essential for endgame optimization.

Notably, most Simaris rewards are not available through other syndicates or world drops, making them vendor-locked. Synthesis Scanners and Exilus Adapters are particularly sought-after for mid-game progression.

## How to earn standing efficiently

Efficient synthesis farming requires selecting high-level enemies in dangerous maps. Steel Path mission nodes provide the highest standing returns per scan. Pairing stealth frames (Ash, Loki, Ivara) with invisibility duration mods maximizes the standing multiplier—enemies scanned while the frame remains undetected consistently yield more standing than conventional scans.

Prioritize daily synthesis tasks for guaranteed rewards, then hunt personal targets in high-level nodes to exceed the daily cap before quest reset. Some players farm the Sanctuary Enclave itself by spawning enemies within the Simaris practice arena (unlocked via the Simulacrum Access Key), allowing for controlled, repeatable high-yield scans without risk of mission failure.

Resource boosters (weekend events granting doubled rewards) double Simaris standing gains, making boost weekends ideal for grinding synthesis ranks. A player combining stealth play, high-level enemies, and active resource boosts can accumulate 250,000+ standing in a single day despite the nominal cap, by completing daily quests that grant bonus standing outside the rolling cap.

## Neutrality and strategic advantage

Cephalon Simaris remains neutral to all syndicate factions. Progressing Simaris standing causes no standing loss with any other syndicate and grants no bonuses to allied factions. This neutrality allows players to pursue Simaris simultaneously with any combination of the six standing-tier syndicates without diplomatic consequence.

Many players prioritize Simaris early for Synthesis Scanners and Exilus Adapters, then focus on the six traditional syndicates. Alternatively, experienced players maintain active Simaris synthesis grinding throughout their account progression, since the high daily cap (125,000) and quest-bonus workaround allow steady accumulation of rare items like Exilus Adapters for builds across multiple frames.

## Notes

Helios' Investigator mod, an archwing sentinel ability, scans enemies passively but does not contribute standing to Cephalon Simaris. Only active Synthesis Scanner usage triggers standing gains.

The Synthesis Scanner is consumable—each scan uses one scanner. Simaris sells scanners at 5,000 standing each, creating a standing-to-resource conversion loop. This cost is intentional balancing to prevent unlimited farming; high-efficiency scanners are more expensive than low-efficiency ones.

Some of Simaris' unique mods (Looter, Detect Vulnerability) are not available on the player market, making Simaris the only source for builds requiring these specific effects. Account-wide access to these mods makes Simaris standing investment worthwhile even for casual players.
