## Overview

Vent Kids represent the entrepreneurial youth culture of Fortuna, the underground Corpus colony on Venus. Composed of resourceful children and young adults from the Solaris United population, Vent Kids operate outside traditional faction hierarchies by organizing K-Drive hoverboard races and stealing Corpus components to resell or repurpose. Their leadership spans multiple personalities including Roky, Boon, and Legs, each running different racing circuits and side operations across the Orb Vallis open-world region.

Unlike formal syndicates, Vent Kids function as an informal collective united by the desire for freedom and self-determination. Progression with this faction comes through participation in K-Drive races, which serve as both entertainment and a method of accumulating Vent Credits—a currency distinct from traditional standing. Membership grants access to K-Drive customizations, cosmetics, and exclusive hoverboard blueprints unavailable through other factions.

## Standing and daily cap

Vent Kids standing operates distinctly from standing-tier syndicates. Rather than affinity conversion or alert systems, standing is earned through K-Drive racing activities in Orb Vallis. Each race participation grants Vent Credits on completion, with rewards scaling based on race difficulty and placement. Unlike the 25,000 daily cap shared by standing-tier syndicates, Vent Kids impose no explicit daily cap, allowing players to race repeatedly for continuous standing accumulation.

Standing acquisition is tied directly to player engagement with K-Drive mechanics. Winning or simply completing races accumulates standing toward the next rank tier. The more challenging race circuits—particularly those in restricted regions of Orb Vallis—yield substantially higher rewards per race completion, encouraging players to develop K-Drive mastery.

## Ranks and progression

Vent Kids employ a five-tier ranking system reflecting increasing status within their racing circuit community. Rank 0 (Friend) opens basic race participation and K-Drive blueprint access. Rank 1 (Respected) represents minor standing accumulation, granting cosmetic K-Drive skins and basic hoverboard mods for performance enhancement. Rank 2 (Trusted) unlocks mid-tier K-Drive blueprints and archwing component blueprints, reflecting Vent Kids' secondary focus on Corpus technology salvage.

Rank 3 (Distinguished) grants access to exotic K-Drive cosmetics and flying-themed cosmetics that reflect the faction's emphasis on aerial freedom. These cosmetics are visually distinct from other faction offerings, featuring neon Solaris aesthetics and hoverboard-inspired designs. Rank 4 (Legend), the maximum standing tier, provides the most exclusive K-Drive customizations, including rare blueprint variants and legendary hoverboard skins.

## Offerings and rewards

Vent Kids' reward structure centers entirely on K-Drive customization and hoverboard-related cosmetics. The Friend tier grants access to the foundational K-Drive blueprint—the starting hoverboard—and the Vent Kids sigil cosmetic. These items provide the baseline equipment necessary to participate in racing circuits.

Respected rank unlocks a variety of K-Drive cosmetics including body kits, paint schemes, and engine covers styled after Corpus and Solaris aesthetics. Hoverboard mods become available, providing mechanical enhancements to K-Drive performance such as increased acceleration, higher top speed, or improved trick maneuvers.

Trusted rank provides advanced K-Drive blueprints for performance variants, such as the Springer K-Drive model with enhanced stability or the Velocitous K-Drive variant optimized for speed. Archwing component blueprints sourced from Corpus salvage become available, providing alternative acquisition paths for archwing equipment alongside traditional void-fissure farming.

Distinguished rank offers exotic K-Drive skins styled after premium Corpus technology and flying-themed cosmetics including operator armor pieces, syandanas with wing-like designs, and custom emotes referencing aerial tricks. These cosmetics appeal primarily to players seeking visual distinction within the Solaris United open-world community.

Legend rank, the pinnacle of Vent Kids progression, grants exclusive K-Drive cosmetics unavailable elsewhere, including holographic skin variants and legendary paint schemes. Rare hoverboard blueprint variants optimized for specific race track types become available. Syndicate cosmetics including badges, decals, and sigils reflecting the highest status within Vent Kids culture complete the reward lineup.

## How to earn standing

Standing with Vent Kids derives exclusively from K-Drive racing participation. Each race in Orb Vallis grants standing on completion, with rewards scaling based on race difficulty and circuit type. Simple races yield baseline standing, while advanced races—those requiring precise handling or traversal through hazardous terrain—grant substantially higher rewards.

Placement bonuses further enhance standing gains; first-place finishes yield bonus standing compared to mid-pack completions. The most efficient standing farming path involves repeatedly running the highest-difficulty race circuits where the player can consistently place first or second. These races are located in restricted areas of Orb Vallis accessible only after completing the Venus open-world region.

Racing as a group generates no additional standing bonuses—Vent Kids tracks individual pilot performance rather than squad achievements. This faction structure encourages players to develop personal K-Drive proficiency and mastery of race-specific mechanics, making standing progression accessible but rewarding to skilled pilots.

## Alignment with Solaris culture

Vent Kids represent the younger, more rebellious element of Solaris United. While both factions operate within the Venus colony, their progression systems and philosophies diverge. Solaris United pursues debt forgiveness and worker liberation through bounty and trading systems. Vent Kids pursue freedom and self-determination through racing culture and technology theft.

Progressing simultaneously with both Solaris United and Vent Kids requires balancing time between bounty farming (for Solaris standing) and K-Drive racing (for Vent Kids standing). No standing penalties or bonuses apply when progressing both factions—they represent separate career paths within the same open-world region rather than opposing ideologies.

## Notes

K-Drive racing skill directly impacts standing farming efficiency. Players who master advanced race mechanics—shortcuts, trick maneuvers, and precise line execution—can complete races faster and achieve higher placements, yielding greater standing per unit time. Investing in K-Drive customizations unlocks performance enhancements that make future races easier and more profitable.

The Vent Kids sigil and cosmetics are exclusive to this faction; no alternative acquisition methods exist. Players seeking to fully customize their operator appearance with Vent Kids-themed cosmetics must dedicate time to racing circuits. Unlike other syndicates where standing can be purchased with premium currency, K-Drive racing remains the sole standing-acquisition method for Vent Kids.
