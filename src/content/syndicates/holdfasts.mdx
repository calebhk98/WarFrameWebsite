## Overview

The Holdfasts comprise four survivors from the legendary Zariman Ten Zero, an experimental void-ship that disappeared eighty years prior. Restored through the mysterious power of the Void itself, these crew members struggle to maintain their sanity and the integrity of their vessel while gathering void-touched resources. They operate from the Zariman Ten Zero location, accessible through void-fissure mechanics and represent an open-world syndicate unique to void-space exploration.

Unlike Tenno-aligned syndicates or faction-specific groups, the Holdfasts exist as isolated survivors offering collaboration to Tenno who venture into their domain. Their perspective on existence—shaped by decades of void-exposure and isolation—creates a faction focused on survival, adaptation, and understanding the Void itself. Players seeking void-themed cosmetics, augment mods, and challenging content find the Holdfasts rewarding.

## Standing and daily cap

Holdfasts reputation comes exclusively through Voidplume currency collected from Zariman void-fissure missions. Five Voidplume types exist, each converting to standing at different rates: Voidplume Down (500 standing), Voidplume Vane (1,000 standing), Voidplume Crest (2,000 standing), Voidplume Quill (2,500 standing), and Voidplume Pinion (5,000 standing). Players trade these directly to Archimedean Yonta, the Holdfasts' merchant, at will—no daily standing cap exists.

This flexibility allows unlimited standing gain per session, though practical mission design limits hourly yields. Zariman missions support longer endless variants (survival, waves, etc.), enabling extended grinding for committed players. Unlike bounty-based syndicates, Holdfasts progression combines fissure-mission participation with resource management—efficient players accumulate high-rarity Voidplumes and batch-convert them for ranking efficiency.

## Ranks and progression

Holdfasts features five progression ranks: Fallen (5,000-22,000 standing), Watcher (22,000-44,000), Guardian (44,000-70,000), Seraph (70,000-99,000), and Angel (99,000-132,000). Each rank unlock requires standing investment plus credits (10,000 to 200,000 depending on tier), creating a dual-currency bottleneck distinct from other syndicates.

Progression pace depends on Zariman mission efficiency and Voidplume drop luck. Endless variants (survival, disruption) provide highest Voidplume yields per mission, while finite missions (exterminate, rescue) offer faster completion time. Skilled players estimate progression to Angel rank requires sixteen to twenty-five hours of focused Zariman grinding, depending on playstyle preferences and squad efficiency.

## Offerings and rewards

Fallen rank grants access to the Holdfasts sigil. Watcher introduces Holdfasts-specific mods and foundational blueprints reflecting void-adaptation themes. Guardian rank unlocks warframe augment mods and void-aligned equipment pieces, expanding tactical diversity for void-content specialists.

Seraph rank provides rare weapons—typically Zariman-exclusive designs unavailable elsewhere—and advanced blueprints for specialized components. Angel rank, the maximum tier, offers rank 5 (maximum-power) versions of all family augment mods and exclusive cosmetics including operator ephemeras, emotes, and armor pieces reflecting void-touched origins.

Holdfasts rewards emphasize cosmetic customization and thematic consistency for void-enthusiasts. The exclusive cosmetics—unavailable through market purchase or alternative syndicates—provide visual distinction for dedicated players committing significant playtime to Zariman content.

## How to earn standing

Voidplume collection occurs naturally during Zariman missions; players loot them alongside standard mission rewards. Rarity distribution favors Down and Vane drops, with Pinion drops (highest value) being relatively rare. To optimize standing gain, prioritize endless missions granting extended play time—thirty-minute survival or ten-wave disruption runs accumulate substantially more Voidplumes than five-minute exterminate missions.

Squad composition affects drop rates; some frame abilities increase resource acquisition indirectly through faster enemy clears (which enable longer endless runs before squad fatigue). Pilfering-effect mods (Pilfering Swarm, Despoil variants) exist for Warframes but not Holdfasts specifically; standard mission farming applies.

Convert accumulated Voidplumes to standing whenever standing reserves deplete. Batch conversion—accumulating Pinion drops and converting them together—improves psychological satisfaction compared to frequent small conversions.

## Strategy tips

Dual-currency requirements (standing plus credits) create progression bottlenecks. Ensure credit reserves exist before pushing ranking—an Angel rank unlock costs 200,000 credits beyond standing investment. Combine Zariman content with void-fissure mission participation; cracked void fissure missions grant void traces (valuable for relic farming) alongside Voidplume drops, doubling objective value per mission.

Time-efficient players alternate between Zariman void-mission focuses (when pursuing Holdfasts ranking) and index credit-farming (when credits run low), optimizing session structure for multiple syndicate goals.

## Notes

The Holdfasts lack competitive pressure for ranking compared to standing-tier syndicate politics—progression is purely self-paced and resource-based. This makes them ideal for players preferring grinding flexibility over scheduled alert participation or faction-aligned competition. Weekly Kuva rewards (available through Yonta conversations) provide consistent high-end crafting currency, creating secondary incentive for regular Zariman engagement beyond syndicate ranking.
