## Overview

New Loka is one of six major faction syndicates, led by Priestess Amaryn. This faction advocates for "a pure and whole Earth to which humanity can return," opposing genetic tampering and technological corruption. The syndicate favors supportive Warframe augments and maintains their signature "Sancti" weapon line, embodying their philosophy of natural purity and resistance to technological excess.

## Standing rewards

New Loka offers progressive rewards across six ranks. Each rank threshold unlocks new vendor offerings and special items. Progression through the ranks requires accumulating Standing within each tier before advancing to the next level. The syndicate specializes in supportive and restorative items, including specter blueprints, cosmetics, and augment mods that enhance protective or supportive gameplay.

**Rank advancement:** Standing is shared among all six classic faction syndicates. The daily cap applies globally across your entire syndicate standing earnings, requiring strategic prioritization of which syndicate to invest in daily.

## Daily cap and grind notes

The daily standing cap for all faction syndicates is 25,000. This cap is fixed and applies to the combined standing earned from all activities in a single day. Reaching this cap typically takes 2–3 hours of focused syndicate alert farming. The cap resets daily, allowing consistent progression without the need for marathon grinding sessions.

Standing can be earned through multiple methods: syndicate alerts award standing directly upon completion, medallions found within alerts grant 500–5,000 Standing each, and affinity from missions converts at a 15% rate when pledged to the faction. The 15% affinity conversion means every mission played contributes passively to standing growth, making New Loka accessible even during regular gameplay.

## Recommended order to rank

New Loka is allied with The Perrin Sequence, meaning gaining standing with New Loka grants bonus standing to Perrin Sequence as well (50% bonus). However, pledging to New Loka reduces standing gains with Steel Meridian and Cephalon Suda. Players seeking to avoid standing penalties should prioritize New Loka and Perrin Sequence together, or delay commitment until ready to accept the opposing faction losses.

Ranking up unlocks progressive benefits—plan standing expenditure carefully. The earliest worthwhile purchase is the Ancient Healer Specter at Humane rank, useful for difficult missions. Higher-rank augment mods represent long-term goals that significantly enhance specific Warframe playstyles. Weapon augments unlock at Pure rank and provide multiplicative benefits to syndicate weapons.

## Notes

All numeric standing values and daily cap reflect current syndicate mechanics. Reaching Flawless rank (the maximum) requires substantial grinding due to each tier's escalating standing requirements. The actual standing cost to rank up within each tier scales, with later ranks demanding 10,000+ standing per tier advancement. Players committing to New Loka should expect 50–100 hours of focused grinding to reach Flawless without relying solely on passive affinity conversion.

Cosmetic sigils unlock at multiple ranks and change your appearance while displaying your syndicate allegiance. Most veteran players recommend rushing to rank 3 or 4 for access to key augment mods, then grinding higher ranks as secondary long-term goals. New Loka pairs well with farming-focused squads that benefit from the supportive augment mods available at higher ranks.
