## Overview

The Arbiters of Hexis represent one of six major Tenno syndicates, dedicated to the belief that Tenno possess "infinite potential." This faction emerged from a philosophy that the Orokin historically constrained Tenno development and that only through discipline, study, and rigorous trial can the Tenno culture be rebuilt as a beacon of hope across the Origin System. Membership grants access to exclusive weapons, mods, and resources that align with their vision of enlightenment and self-perfection.

The Arbiters share headquarters in Relays across all planets and operate under a structured rank system that rewards consistent engagement. Like the other standing-tier syndicates, they reward players who consistently contribute to their cause through combat affinity conversion and syndicate alert participation.

## Standing and daily cap

Standing gain in the Arbiters of Hexis operates through two primary mechanisms. The first is affinity conversion, where fifteen percent of all affinity earned in combat automatically converts to standing with your currently-selected syndicate. This passive gain encourages players to continue existing mission routines without deviation.

The second mechanism involves syndicate alerts, which spawn three times daily in the alert panel. Each alert grants standing and drops syndicate medallions—items that can be delivered to the syndicate for additional standing (500, 1,000, or 5,000 standing depending on rarity). All syndicate medallion gains count toward a shared daily standing cap of 25,000 across all six standing-tier syndicates combined, meaning efficient players must decide how to distribute their alert runs throughout the day.

## Ranks and progression

The Arbiters operate a six-tier ranking system from rank 0 (Initiation) through rank 5 (Maxim). Each rank unlock requires accumulating standing within a cumulative total band. Rank 0 begins at zero standing and caps at 5,000. Rank 1, "Principled," opens at the moment you hit 5,000 standing and allows up to 22,000 total. Subsequent ranks increase these thresholds: rank 2 (Authentic) to 44,000, rank 3 (Lawful) to 70,000, rank 4 (Crusader) to 99,000, and rank 5 (Maxim) to 132,000.

Each rank unlock grants immediate access to that tier's offerings at the syndicate shop. Lower ranks remain unlocked permanently, so players can purchase from any tier they have achieved once they have sufficient standing within that tier's band.

## Offerings and rewards

The Arbiters reward progression with a strong focus on weapons, warframe augments, and Orokin resources. At the Initiation tier, players gain access to the faction sigil, a cosmetic that displays allegiance. The Principled tier introduces the Corrupted Lancer Specter, a deployable combat ally. Authentic rank unlocks void relic packs (useful for endgame content farming) and archwing component blueprints for building archwing equipment.

Lawful rank provides the Orokin Reactor blueprint, a critical resource for upgrading warframe and weapon mod capacity. Crusader rank offers weapon augment mods for frames like Ash, Excalibur, and Volt, each providing a unique bonus to their abilities. The highest rank, Maxim, unlocks the most powerful offerings: warframe augment mods that significantly enhance frame mechanics, and syndicate melee weapons (such as Arbiters-specific blade designs) that merge aesthetic appeal with combat utility.

Arbiters augments pair well with endurance-focused builds and those requiring increased ability duration or cost reduction, making them valuable for players pursuing Steel Path and void-fissure content.

## How to earn standing

Consistent standing growth comes primarily from regular combat. Since fifteen percent of combat affinity converts automatically, players who run void fissures, defense, or survival missions accumulate standing passively. To accelerate progression, prioritize syndicate alerts spawning three times daily. Each alert guarantees standing on completion plus a medallion drop, turning alert runs into major standing gains—especially if you secure rare-tier medallions (5,000 standing each).

Standing is earned with only one syndicate at a time. Players must manually switch their active syndicate at the relay to earn standing with a different faction. Once switched, all subsequent affinity converts to the newly-selected syndicate.

## Faction alignments and strategy

The Arbiters maintain specific relationship with other syndicates. They gain a fifty-percent standing bonus (allied stance) with Cephalon Suda, making it possible to progress both syndicates simultaneously at an accelerated rate. Conversely, the Perrin Sequence stands as opposed, causing standing losses with Perrin if you gain standing with Arbiters. The Red Veil is outright enemy faction, imposing a negative standing conversion that actively erodes Red Veil ranks as you progress Arbiters.

Strategic syndicate selection revolves around these alignments. Many players progress Arbiters and Cephalon Suda first, leveraging the fifty-percent bonus, then later switch to opposing syndicates once the first pair are maxed. This approach requires careful standing management but yields faster progression for completionists aiming to unlock all augments and weapons across all factions.

## Notes

Arbiters augment mods are tradeable on the market, allowing players who do not wish to grind standing to purchase them from other players. However, tradeable augments are limited to those that do not require mastery rank access. Some high-level augments remain account-bound and untradeable.

Syndicate weapon blueprints require resources beyond just standing—typically plastids, alloys, or void fissure materials—making standing a necessary but not sufficient condition for acquisition. Always check the market for cheaper acquisition paths if the grinding feels protracted.
