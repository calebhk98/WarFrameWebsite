## Overview

The Hex represents a unique open-world faction aligned with the mysterious 1999 parallel-universe civilization discovered during the Zariman Ten Zero update. This faction consists of Protoframes—sentient manifestations from an alternate dimensional timeline where the Orokin perfected consciousness transfer and collective identity. The Hex operates outside traditional Origin System politics, serving as intermediaries between the Zariman's void-touched crew and the modern Tenno.

Unlike the standing-tier syndicates, The Hex employs an open-world progression model tied to activities within the Zariman itself. Members gain access to unique void-touched cosmetics, experimental weapon blueprints, and lore insights into the nature of parallel dimensions and Orokin technological secrets preserved across timelines.

## Standing and daily cap

The Hex operates without a traditional daily standing cap, reflecting their open-world faction status. Standing is earned through participation in Zariman missions, bounties, and void-fissure activities scattered across the tileset. Unlike the standing-tier syndicates where affinity conversion is passive, The Hex standing derives from active mission completion and environmental challenges.

The standing system employs a cumulative progression model where completing missions grants standing based on difficulty level and completion conditions. Void-touched enemies within the Zariman yield enhanced standing gains when defeated, encouraging players to engage with the faction's thematic combat encounters.

## Ranks and progression

The Hex operates a five-tier ranking system reflective of open-world faction progression. Advancement requires accumulating standing through repeated Zariman engagement. Rank 0 (Initiate) opens faction access. Rank 1 (Ally) unlocks basic cosmetics and intel. Rank 2 (Trusted) grants access to weapon blueprints and warframe components. Rank 3 (Champion) provides void-amplification mods enhancing operator and warframe void abilities. Rank 4 (Guardian) represents maximum standing and grants the most exclusive offerings.

Progression is non-linear—players may repeat missions multiple times to accumulate standing toward the next tier without strict daily restrictions, though reputation gain may diminish with repeated runs of identical bounties.

## Offerings and rewards

The Hex's reward structure centers on void-touched aesthetics and experimental weaponry. The Initiate tier grants access to The Hex sigil and glyph—cosmetic displays aligned with the 1999 faction's visual identity. These items serve primarily as cosmetic displays of faction allegiance without mechanical benefit.

Ally rank unlocks Zariman void-fissure intel, which provides targeted information about void-resonance nodes and temporal anomalies within the ship. Cosmetic armor pieces and syandanas styled after prototype Orokin designs become available, allowing players to adopt the visual aesthetic of the 1999 civilization.

Trusted rank provides blueprints for experimental prototype weapons inspired by parallel-timeline engineering. These weapons blend Orokin design principles with 1999 technological modifications, offering unique status effects and scaling mechanics unavailable through other factions. Warframe component blueprints become available for crafting void-enhanced warframe variants.

Champion rank offers void-amplification mods that enhance operator void abilities and warframe transference efficiency. These mods are exclusive to The Hex and provide substantial bonuses to void-strike charge rate, void-beam damage, and operator invulnerability duration. Exotic cosmetic armor pieces styled after Zariman crew uniforms further customize visual appearance.

Guardian rank, the maximum progression tier, unlocks the most exclusive weapon blueprints and exotic cosmetics. These offerings include weapons with intrinsic void-beam properties and cosmetics styled after Protoframe Void-touched aesthetics. Access to void resonance tools—rare blueprints for Zariman-specific gear—remains exclusive to this rank.

## How to earn standing

Standing with The Hex derives from active Zariman engagement rather than passive affinity conversion. Completing bounties issued within the Zariman tileset grants standing on completion. Higher-difficulty bounties yield proportionally greater standing rewards, encouraging players to engage with endgame Zariman content.

Void-fissure activities scattered across the Zariman—environmental puzzles and temporal anomalies—grant standing upon successful completion. These activities are repeatable, allowing players to farm standing indefinitely without strict daily restrictions, though diminishing returns apply after multiple repeats within a short timeframe.

Defeating void-touched enemies grants standing on kill, with multipliers based on enemy difficulty level and weapon void-resonance compatibility. Steel Path Zariman variants yield substantially higher standing than base-difficulty runs, enabling endgame players to accumulate standing efficiently.

## Notable mechanics and lore context

The Hex functions as a gateway to understanding the 1999 timeline and its relationship to the Orokin Empire. Progression through ranks reveals lore entries detailing the Zariman's twin-timeline nature and the Protoframe consciousness network. This faction represents a thematic bridge between the old Orokin lore and newer void-touched narrative expansions.

Unlike other factions, The Hex does not impose standing penalties or bonuses when progressing simultaneously with other open-world syndicates. Players may pursue The Hex alongside Solaris United, Ostron, Entrati, or other open-world factions without diplomatic consequence, though the Zariman's isolation from other open-world hubs means active faction selection may be necessary to earn standing efficiently.

## Notes

The Hex's standing is earned exclusively through Zariman engagement; no affinity conversion or alert-based standing gains apply. This faction-specific progression system encourages players to develop builds and strategies optimized for Zariman mission types rather than general star-chart farming.

Weapons and cosmetics exclusive to The Hex cannot be obtained through other factions or the player market, making standing investment mandatory for completionists. Void-amplification mods are particularly valuable for operator-focused builds and eidolon-hunting strategies, making this faction a priority for advanced endgame progression.
