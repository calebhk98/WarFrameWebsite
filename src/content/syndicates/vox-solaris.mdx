## Overview

Vox Solaris is the shadow operations wing of the Solaris United syndicate operating within Fortuna, Venus. Led by Eudico under her undercover identity, Vox Solaris represents the covert resistance movement against Nef Anyo's corporate control over Venus. Unlike the public-facing Solaris United faction focused on worker liberation through bounties and commerce, Vox Solaris handles clandestine operations and black-market activities. Access to Vox Solaris requires completion of the mainline Vox Solaris quest, which deepens the player's involvement in Fortuna's underground resistance.

The syndicate represents the radical wing of Solaris' movement, recruiting Tenno operatives for increasingly dangerous missions. Vox Solaris offers unique cosmetics, weapon blueprints including the tenet weapon series, and other restricted-access items. The faction is tied to post-Solaris United progression, serving as an endgame standing destination for players who have completed Fortuna's narrative arc.

## Standing and daily cap

Vox Solaris operates on the standard open-world daily cap of 25,000 standing per day. Standing gains are earned through various Fortuna activities including bounties, heists, and special void-fissure operations. The syndicate's progression mirrors Solaris United's tier system but with distinct offerings and cosmetic rewards exclusive to covert operations.

The daily cap resets at the same time as other open-world syndicates. Players can efficiently farm standing by combining Vox Solaris activities with other Fortuna factions, though some high-tier bounties and heist missions provide substantial standing rewards per mission completion.

## Ranks and progression

Vox Solaris uses a four-tier rank system reflecting the secretive nature of the organization. Each rank requires standing thresholds and is tied to exclusive vendor unlocks:

**Neutral** -- Starting rank; no standing required. Players begin at this tier upon accessing Fortuna.

**Friend** -- Requires progression through initial Solaris United bounties and story content. Unlocks cosmetic items including the Vox Solaris Sigil and cosmetic cosmetics reflecting membership in the shadow faction.

**Flawless** -- Higher-tier rank unlocking gravimag blueprints for converting standard arch-guns to gravitic weapons, and Arch-Gun Deployer blueprints allowing portable arch-gun deployment outside Archwing mode.

**Shadow** -- Final rank requiring significant standing investment. Shadow tier unlocks the most restricted items: Tenet weapon blueprints (Tenet Melee weapons and arch-guns), cosmetics themed to Zariman void-fissure aesthetic, and Archon Shards used for endgame Warframe enhancement.

Each rank threshold escalates in standing cost, with total progression from Neutral to Shadow requiring approximately 300,000+ standing.

## Offerings and rewards

Vox Solaris vendors offer a curated selection of items reflecting the syndicate's focus on advanced technology and covert operations. Low-tier offerings include cosmetics and basic blueprints, while higher tiers unlock powerful weapons and transformation systems.

Key reward categories include arch-gun and arch-melee weapon blueprints, cosmetic items with Solaris/void-fissure theming, operator armor pieces, and Archon Shards for Warframe transcendence enhancement. The Tenet weapon series represents exclusive high-tier rewards available only through Vox Solaris vendors, providing unique weapon archetypes unavailable elsewhere in Warframe's economy.

## How to earn standing

Standing is earned through Fortuna's primary activity system: bounties offered by Eudico and other Fortuna vendors. Higher-level bounties (5-30 recommended) provide larger standing rewards and better resource drops. Heist missions unlocked in later tiers also grant standing alongside valuable credits and resources.

Secondary standing sources include trading fish and other Fortuna resources to The Business, mining activities, and completing Zariman void-fissure missions which provide crossover standing with Vox Solaris. Mining raw gems for Smokefinger, while primarily generating Solaris United standing, can be combined with other activities for efficient farming.

Conservation activities capturing animals in the Orb Vallis also contribute standing. Players should prioritize bounties as the most efficient standing farm, selecting missions that align with their desired resource drops (void relics, rare gems, or credits) while maximizing standing gain per mission time.

## Strategy and progression tips

New players accessing Vox Solaris should first complete the mainline Vox Solaris quest to unlock full syndicate access. Early progression focuses on low-to-mid tier bounties farming standing while gathering resources for crafting and trading. Combining Fortuna activities with Void Fissure missions creates synergy for faster rank advancement.

High-tier players prioritize bounty selection based on void relic availability and standing rewards. Using a fast movement Warframe with survival mods enables efficient bounty completion. Carrying a variety of Warframes helps match mission modifiers and enemy types encountered in higher-difficulty bounties.

Coordinate Vox Solaris progression with Solaris United if pursuing both factions simultaneously; they share the same standing cap and vendor hubs. Plan standing allocation strategically since both factions compete for the daily cap. Prioritize Tenet weapon blueprints available only through Vox Solaris, then pursue cosmetics and other items based on personal build needs.

Advanced players working toward Archon Shards should prepare for substantial standing grinding, as Shadow tier costs scale significantly. The investment pays off through permanent Warframe enhancement; plan ahead for specific Warframes requiring shard upgrades. Consider pub squads for faster bounty completion and trading cosmetics or redundant blueprints to other players for credits or resources.

## Notes

Vox Solaris represents the endgame standing progression for Fortuna content. The syndicate's thematic focus on covert operations and restricted technology is reflected in its exclusive weapon offerings and cosmetics. Complete the Vox Solaris quest before serious standing farming to unlock full syndicate merchant access and unlock higher-tier bounties.
