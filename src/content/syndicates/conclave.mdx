## Overview

Conclave stands as the neutral syndicate dedicated to player-versus-player competitive gameplay, led by Teshin Dax from the Conclave Hall present in each Relay. Unlike faction-aligned syndicates driven by philosophy or ideology, Conclave exists purely to organize and reward combat skill in PvP formats. This unique position makes Conclave valuable for players seeking cosmetics, exilus adapters, and mod variety without committing to factional allegiances.

Teshin operates Conclave as a neutral ground where Tenno of all backgrounds compete. The syndicate rejects favoritism and ideological purity, rewarding players based solely on competitive performance and participation in multiple PvP game modes. Membership attracts skill-focused players and those pursuing completionist goals across all syndicate offerings.

## Standing and daily cap

Conclave operates differently from standing-tier syndicates. Standing gain comes exclusively from participating in PvP matches across multiple game modes: Annihilation (free-for-all deathmatch), Team Annihilation (squad-based deathmatches), Cephalon Capture (objective control), Lunaro (territorial sport mode), and Faceoff (one-versus-one duels). Unlike passive affinity conversion, Conclave standing requires active competitive engagement; casual players rank slowly without consistent PvP participation.

Standing gain amounts vary by game mode, match performance, and whether the player achieves victory. Winning teams earn substantially more standing than losing squads, creating incentive for skilled play. Unlike open-world or standing-tier syndicates, Conclave uses no daily standing cap—dedicated PvP players can earn unlimited standing if they play continuously, though practical time constraints limit most players' daily gains to similar ranges as capped factions.

## Ranks and progression

Conclave features six ranks beyond initiation: Rank 0 (Initiation), Rank 1 (Mistral), Rank 2 (Whirlwind), Rank 3 (Tempest), Rank 4 (Hurricane), and Rank 5 (Typhoon). Progression mirrors standard syndicates with cumulative standing bands: Rank 0 extends from zero to 5,000 standing; Rank 1 from 5,000 to 22,000; Rank 2 to 44,000; Rank 3 to 70,000; Rank 4 to 99,000; and Rank 5 to 132,000 total standing.

Reaching higher ranks requires substantial PvP time investment, especially for players without extensive competitive experience. Skilled PvP participants progress faster through consistent victories, while casual participants rank slowly. Many players treat Conclave as secondary to PvE syndicates due to the time commitment required for meaningful progression.

## Offerings and rewards

Conclave rewards emphasize cosmetics and utility items unavailable elsewhere. At Initiation, players gain the Conclave Sigil. Mistral rank unlocks Conclave-specific mods and uncommon competitive variants. Whirlwind introduces rare Conclave mods and weapon skins, allowing cosmetic customization of primary and secondary weapons.

Tempest rank grants access to warframe skins and Conclave-exclusive weapons that function only in PvP contexts, providing cosmetic appeal and competitive loadout diversity. Hurricane rank offers Syandanas (shoulder decorations) and orbiter sculptures. The highest rank, Typhoon, provides the Exilus Adapter Blueprint—a critical resource allowing installation of exilus mods on warframes for additional tactical flexibility—plus elite Conclave cosmetics.

The Exilus Adapter availability at Typhoon rank makes Conclave valuable even for non-PvP players, though acquiring it demands significant competitive time investment. Most players purchase exilus adapters from other players using plat rather than grinding Conclave to Typhoon.

## How to earn standing

Standing gain in Conclave requires consistent PvP participation. Play any available game mode, prioritizing team-based formats where coordination increases win rates and standing multipliers. Team Annihilation rewards more standing per match than Annihilation due to squad coordination value. Cephalon Capture (objective mode) similarly rewards strong tactical play.

Winning matches grants standing multipliers and acceleration bonuses, making skilled play exponentially more efficient. Experienced PvP players progress substantially faster than newcomers. Faceoff (one-versus-one) offers high risk/reward for dueling specialists. Dedicate focused playtime to modes offering standing rewards, avoiding casual matches if progression is the goal.

Matchmaking groups players by skill level; newer competitors face similar opponents and progress at reasonable rates despite lower absolute standing per match. As skill improves, standing gains accelerate.

## PvP game modes

**Annihilation:** Free-for-all deathmatch supporting up to eight players. Last person standing on the scoreboard wins. Highly chaotic; rewards individual fragging skill.

**Team Annihilation:** Squad-based deathmatch (typically 4v4) where the team with highest combined kills wins. Requires communication and tactical coordination.

**Cephalon Capture:** Objective mode requiring teams to secure and hold a central point against opposing squads. Combines fragging skill with positional awareness and map knowledge.

**Lunaro:** Territorial sport mode using ball mechanics similar to lacrosse. Complex coordination requirements; specialized playstyle distinct from traditional deathmatches.

**Faceoff:** One-versus-one duel format for players seeking individual skill validation. High stakes; single mistake determines outcome.

## Notes

Conclave cosmetics are cosmetic-only and offer no mechanical advantage, maintaining PvP balance. Conclave mods function exclusively in Conclave matches and provide no benefit in PvE content, preventing power creep in cooperative missions. Most players focus on Conclave for Exilus Adapter blueprints rather than ranking to Typhoon purely for cosmetics. PvP experience varies significantly based on playerbase size and regional availability; some platforms experience long matchmaking queues, while others maintain healthy populations.
