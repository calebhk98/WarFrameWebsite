## Overview

Kahl's Garrison is a unique resistance movement established by Kahl-175 and his followers to fight against Narmer across the Origin System. Unlike the traditional standing-based syndicates, Kahl's Garrison operates through an alternative progression system tied to weekly Break Narmer missions. The faction represents the evolution of the Grineer's struggle for freedom, blending both former enemies and Tenno cooperation in a shared cause against tyranny.

Access to Kahl's Garrison requires completion of the Veilbreaker quest. The syndicate serves as an introduction to Zariman-adjacent content and parallel storylines that expand the narrative beyond traditional faction warfare.

## Standing and progression

Kahl's Garrison does not operate on a traditional standing system. Instead, players advance through the ranks automatically by completing weekly Break Narmer missions. Progression moves through five fixed ranks over four weeks, ensuring all players reach maximum tier without grinding or seasonal resets.

Advancement unlocks new NPCs at each rank: Chipper joins at Encampment, bringing the first shop access; Horrek arrives at Fort with Archon Mods; Olvar D enables Archon Hunts at Settlement; and Jarka provides full access at Home rank. This structured tier system encourages continued engagement with weekly content rather than open-ended standing farming.

Players earn Stock, a unique currency separate from traditional standing, by completing Break Narmer bonus challenges. Stock is used exclusively at Chipper's shop to purchase exclusive items unavailable through other syndicates.

## Ranks and unlocks

The five-tier progression system guides players through narrative beats and content access simultaneously.

**Shelter** is the starting rank. Players gain access to Kahl's story and initial missions, establishing the foundation for the resistance campaign.

**Encampment** brings Chipper to the camp, unlocking the first set of purchasable wares. Basic weapon blueprints and early-game items become available here, supporting players building their Zariman arsenal.

**Fort** rank arrives with Horrek and introduces Archon Mods to the rotation. Archon Mods represent advanced modularity exclusive to Zariman content, granting unique passive bonuses unavailable from other sources. Early-tier Archon Mods such as Continuity and Stretch become purchasable.

**Settlement** activates Olvar D and unlocks Archon Hunts—endgame activities requiring coordinated teams and optimized builds. These hunts serve as gated content requiring both progression and mechanical skill.

**Home** is the maximum rank, bringing Jarka to the garrison and opening all available wares. This final tier represents the full militarization of Kahl's forces and access to top-tier weapons and warframe blueprints from the syndicate.

## Offerings and rewards

Kahl's Garrison offers a curated selection of weapons, warframes, and utility items tied to Grineer resistance themes and Zariman lore.

**Warframes:** Styanax is the signature warframe available through Kahl's Garrison, obtainable at higher ranks. This Grineer-aligned frame represents the pinnacle of resistance engineering.

**Weapons:** Afentis (melee), Aegrit (secondary), and Slaytra (primary) form the core weapon line, each thematically rooted in anti-Narmer design. These weapons scale well through mid-game content and serve as bridges to prime variants.

**Archon Mods:** The exclusive Archon Mod suite—including Continuity, Stretch, Intensify, Vitality, and Flow—grants passive bonuses to warframe survivability and duration without taking mod slots. These mods are exclusive to Kahl's Garrison and Zariman vendors.

**Cosmetics:** Ephemeras such as Fog Of War, Shard Hex, and Shard Bane provide visual customization. The Skaut Landing Craft offers a Grineer-themed mobile base, and Veilbreak Captura scenes enable custom cinematography in themed environments.

## How to earn Stock and progress

Break Narmer missions are the sole source of progression and currency. Completing the standard mission grants automatic rank advancement once per week; bonus objectives yield Stock for Chipper's shop.

Efficient progression requires weekly engagement. Since advancement is time-gated rather than grind-gated, missing a week delays rank-up by one week. Bonus objectives reward Stock proportional to completion complexity—harder challenges yield more currency.

Stock does not decay and carries between seasons, allowing players to bank currency for planned purchases. The separation of rank progression from currency farming reduces pressure to repeat content excessively while maintaining incentives for perfecting bonus objectives.

## Strategy tips

Prioritize Archon Mods over weapons early, as their passive benefits improve all future builds. Continuity and Vitality are universally useful; choose based on current warframe.

Bond with Chipper early to unlock basic wares, then plan purchases around long-term builds. The progression timeline is forgiving; there is no penalty for strategic hoarding of Stock.

Coordinate with squads on Archon Hunts to ensure role coverage. Tanks and supports are valued equally; individual frame strength matters less than team composition.
