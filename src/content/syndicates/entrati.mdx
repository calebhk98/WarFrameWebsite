## Overview

The Entrati syndicate operates within Zariman void space, consisting of an ancient Orokin family seeking knowledge and redemption through collaboration with the Tenno. Unlike closed relays housing standing-tier syndicates, Entrati interactions occur in-mission through bounty mechanics and token trading with family members. This decentralized structure creates a unique progression system where multiple NPCs (Mother, Father, Daughter, Son, Otak, and Grandmother) each accept different resources in exchange for standing tokens.

Entrati represents an open-world syndicate distinct from classical standing-tier factions. The family has survived void exposure and technological degradation through cooperation and adaptation, now passing knowledge and resources to Tenno allies willing to support their research and expansion. Their rewards emphasize void-touched equipment, fishing tools, and Necramech-related components reflecting Entrati's mastery over Zariman technology.

## Standing and daily cap

Unlike classical syndicates, Entrati uses a token-based system rather than direct standing allocation. Players earn tokens from bounties and resource trading, then convert tokens to standing at specific family members. Token types include Mother Tokens (100 standing each), Father/Daughter/Otak Tokens (500 standing each), Son Tokens (1,000 standing), Grandmother Tokens (3,000 standing), and Universal Medallions (1,000 standing).

This system provides multiple simultaneous pathways to standing gain. Mother's bounties reward high quantities of Mother Tokens, making tier 5 bounty runs (requiring high entry level) efficient for standing accumulation. Father, Daughter, and Otak accept different resource types--each available in various quantities--allowing players to leverage their preferred resource farming methods. Son accepts conservation targets, encouraging species hunting rotations. This flexibility grants Entrati the highest practical standing-cap efficiency among open-world syndicates, as skilled players complete two to three tier 5 bounties to reach daily caps.

## Ranks and progression

Entrati operates six ranks: Neutral (no minimum standing), Stranger (0-5,000), Acquaintance (0-22,000), Associate (0-44,000), Friend (0-70,000), and Family (0-99,000). Notably, Entrati's maximum rank extends only to 99,000 standing versus 132,000 for other syndicates, reflecting its more specialized role. Each rank unlock grants immediate access to that tier's merchandise.

Progression speed depends on player resource availability and bounty farming efficiency. High-level players running tier 5 bounties progress substantially faster than lower-level content participants. Fishing and conservation require outdoor activity in Zariman; some players find these alternatives to combat-focused bounties engaging, while others consider them slower standing sources.

## Offerings and rewards

Entrati rewards emphasize void-adapted equipment and self-sufficient farming tools. Neutral tier offers basic bounty completion rewards. Stranger rank grants Entrati-specific mods and foundational blueprints for fishing and conservation tools. Acquaintance tier unlocks rare mods and access to Isolation Vault bounties, which drop Necramech reactors and specialized void lenses.

Associate rank introduces warframe augment mods and advanced fishing blueprints for specialized void-fish species. Friend tier grants rare weapons and expanded Isolation vault access with higher void energy yields. The highest rank, Family, provides rank 5 augment mods (maximum-power versions of family-wide offerings) and direct Necramech part blueprints including chassis, systems, and weapons.

Entrati's offerings are particularly valuable for players building Necramechs or seeking void-fissure augmentation options. Family-rank access provides equipment unavailable through other syndicates, making Entrati a specialist faction for void-content enthusiasts.

## How to earn standing

Standing gain begins with bounty participation. Mother's bounties provide highest Mother Token yields and are the primary standing source for most players--completing tier 5 runs grants sufficient tokens for daily standing caps in roughly two to three bounty cycles (approximately thirty to forty-five minutes with strong efficiency).

Diversify standing gain by trading resources with Father (alloys, neural sensors, plastids), Daughter (fish parts), Otak (gems, minerals), and Son (conservation tags). This approach bypasses bounty completion and provides downtime variety--players can farm fish, hunt conservation, or mine while accumulating tokens offline. Combining resource trading with bounty participation optimizes standing gains for time-committed players.

Grandmother Tokens (3,000 standing each) appear rarely as bounty rewards; acquiring extras accelerates standing gain substantially.

## Strategy tips

Prioritize unlocking higher bounty tiers immediately upon reaching Entrati; tier 5 bounties (requiring level 60+ enemies access) offer substantially superior standing economics compared to tier 1-3 variants. Leverage token diversity: if running low on bounty enthusiasm, pivot to fishing or conservation for sustained progression without mission monotony.

Combining Entrati and Necraloid progression leverages both syndicates' void-technology focus; many endgame players progress both simultaneously. Plan void-fissure content participation around standing cap usage--void-void mission rewards include void traces useful across both syndicates.

## Notes

Entrati lacks a daily standing cap like open-world syndicates, though practical time limitations effectively cap most players at 50,000-100,000 standing per session. Token currencies persist indefinitely; players can accumulate massive token inventories over time and convert them to standing episodically when capping out remaining ranks. This flexibility makes Entrati friendly for casual players managing competing content calendars.
