## Overview

The Ostrons are inhabitants of Cetus settlement on Earth's Plains of Eidolon, described as "a tight knit band of merchants, hucksters and survivors" who live under protection of the Orokin tower called the Unum. This open-world syndicate provides access to Zaw melee weapons, arcane enhancements, and operator cosmetics. Unlike faction syndicates, standing with Ostrons does not penalize other open-world syndicates, allowing parallel ranking across multiple regions.

## Standing rewards

Ostron standing unlocks vendor access that progresses across six ranks. Early ranks provide cosmetic sigils and foundational Zaw components, while higher ranks unlock arcane enhancement blueprints and advanced weapon components. Each rank grants permanent access to that rank's vendor inventory at Cetus, allowing unlimited purchases of desired items once standing requirements are met.

Ostron specializes in customization through Zaw components—modular melee weapons constructed from blade, handle, and linkage parts. Ranking up unlocks progressively rarer component tiers, enabling creation of more powerful Zaws. Arcane blueprints unlock at Trusted rank and higher, providing access to stat-boosting enhancements for operators and companions.

## Daily cap and grind notes

Ostron standing lacks a hard daily cap, differentiating it from faction syndicates. Standing is earned through bounties (via Konzu), fishing trades, conservation activities, mining trades, and Shrine Defense runs. Bounties reward the most standing per time investment, typically granting 1,000–2,500 standing for completion depending on bounty tier.

A focused player can farm approximately 10,000–15,000 Ostron standing daily through bounties and supplementary fishing. Fishing provides passive standing accumulation: trading baited species grants 200–500 standing per fish. Conservation activities yield 400–800 standing per successful capture. Mining provides lower standing yields (100–300 per ore) but serves as an alternative for players seeking material harvests alongside standing gains.

Progression from Neutral to Kin rank requires approximately 367,000 total standing. With efficient bounty farming, reaching Kin rank takes 30–50 hours of focused effort. Many players interleave Ostron farming with other Plains activities (Eidolon hunts, void fissures) to maintain engagement while accumulating standing.

## Recommended order to rank

Reach Offworlder (rank 1) to access Zaw blueprints—the primary reason most players engage with Ostrons. This rank requires only 10,000 standing and unlocks fundamental weapon crafting. Visitor rank unlocks advanced Zaw component blueprints and fishing unlockers, making progression to rank 2 worthwhile.

Trusted rank (rank 3) opens arcane blueprint access, a major utility unlock for operator builds. Veterans prioritize reaching Trusted rank quickly, then pace higher ranks based on cosmetic or component preferences. Reaching Kin rank (rank 5, the maximum) is optional but worthwhile for players building multiple Zaw variants or collecting arcanes.

## Notes

Zaw construction requires trading completed, max-ranked Zaws to Hok for standing—a secondary and slower earning method. Most players reserve Zaw trading for prestige once primary bounty/fishing methods are optimized. Conservation activities require specific lures and bait crafted from open-world materials, making them time-intensive early on but rewarding for dedicated farmers.

Shrine Defense (a cooperative mini-mission protecting the Unum from Grineer invaders) grants 800 standing per run and is accessible once unlocked through Ostron progression. Veterans use Shrine Defense during lulls in bounty availability or when seeking a playstyle break from typical farming. The activity scales with squad strength and becomes increasingly difficult, providing optional challenge for experienced players.

Ostron standing pairs exceptionally well with Eidolon hunting, as Plains bounties naturally lead hunters to endgame open-world content. Many endgame players maintain multiple Zaw variants for different mission types, requiring sustained Ostron engagement even after ranking up. Operator cosmetics from Ostron vendors are purely cosmetic and offer no mechanical benefit, allowing players to deprioritize ranks if aesthetics are not a priority.
