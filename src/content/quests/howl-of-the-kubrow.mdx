## Overview

Howl of the Kubrow is the quest that introduces Kubrow companions to the game. It walks players through the full pipeline of acquiring, incubating, and deploying a Kubrow -- from stealing the Incubator Segment from a Corpus survival mission to watching the hatched animal earn its place in combat. The quest is relatively brief, spanning four stages that collectively serve as a tutorial for the companion and breeding systems.

Kubrows are biological companions with distinct breeds, each offering different passive abilities and combat behaviors. Howl of the Kubrow is the gateway to accessing them: until the Incubator Segment is installed in the Orbiter and an egg is incubated, no Kubrow can be raised. The quest makes the process explicit step by step, ensuring that players understand the incubation loop before the system is left to them.

Unlike most Warframe quests, Howl of the Kubrow has no narrative weight beyond its premise. The Corpus are attempting to militarize Kubrows; the Lotus intervenes; players steal the necessary technology and do the same thing for their own purposes. It is a systems tutorial dressed as a quest, and it succeeds at that goal by connecting each mechanical beat to an in-mission objective.

## Prerequisites

Players must complete the Junction on Venus before Howl of the Kubrow becomes available in the Codex. Once the junction is cleared, the quest can be started manually from the Codex. There is no Mastery Rank requirement specific to the quest, though the Venus Junction may require a minimum equipment level to complete.

The quest involves missions on Venus and Earth, both of which are low-level areas. A starter Warframe and basic weapons are sufficient for all four stages. The final stage -- a Defense mission on Earth -- scales to beginner-friendly enemy levels and is designed to be completable even with an untested loadout.

## Stages

### Acquire Incubator Segment

The first stage sends players to Unda, a Survival mission on Venus populated by Corpus enemies. Players must survive for 5 minutes to complete the objective and receive the Incubator Segment blueprint as a quest reward. The mission uses the Corpus Ship tileset. Life support capsules spawn regularly during the survival timer, making resource management straightforward at the mission's enemy level range.

The Incubator Segment blueprint is not immediately usable -- it must be built in the Foundry before the Kubrow incubation system activates in the Orbiter.

### Find a Kubrow Egg

With the Incubator Segment built and installed, the next requirement is a Kubrow Egg. Players are directed to Earth's forest tileset to hunt Kubrow Dens, which are burrow mounds that appear in the environment. Destroying a den during the quest has a guaranteed egg drop rate, ensuring that players do not need to grind the same objective repeatedly.

For players who prefer not to run the Earth missions, a Kubrow Egg can be purchased directly from the in-game Market for platinum. Both methods are equally valid for progression purposes.

### Incubate the Kubrow

Once the egg is in inventory, players install it in the newly built Incubator using an Incubator Power Core as the energy source. Power Cores are craftable from the Foundry or purchasable from the Market. During the quest, the incubation timer is shortened to 60 seconds rather than the standard multi-hour wait that applies in normal gameplay. This acceleration exists specifically to keep the tutorial flowing without forcing players to log out and return the next day.

The breed of Kubrow produced depends on the genetic makeup of the egg, which is partially random. Players cannot select a specific breed from a standard egg; breed selection via Genetic Code Templates is a post-quest feature.

### Defend the Kubrow

The final stage is a 5-wave Defense mission on Gaia, Earth. Players deploy their newly hatched Kubrow as an active companion and must protect the defense objective while the Kubrow participates in combat alongside them. This stage exists to establish the Kubrow as a capable companion rather than a passive decoration. Enemy levels are appropriate for Earth and do not require optimized builds.

Completing the defense concludes the quest and awards the cosmetic and mod rewards.

## Rewards

Completing Howl of the Kubrow awards three items:

- Incubator Segment -- this is built during the quest, not awarded at the end; completing the quest permanently establishes the incubation system in the Orbiter.
- Balanced Posture -- a Kubrow stance mod that affects how the companion moves and attacks.
- Taichen Kubrow Collar -- a cosmetic accessory for the Kubrow, equippable from the companion's appearance menu.

The core long-term reward is access to the Kubrow system itself. After completing the quest, players can breed additional Kubrows, acquire Genetic Code Templates to target specific breeds, and use the Incubator for imprinting and offspring management.

## Lore

The Corpus, known for monetizing biological assets as readily as mechanical ones, had identified Kubrows as viable weapons platforms. Their research into forced breeding and combat conditioning prompted Lotus to intervene before the program could mature. The Incubator Segment recovered from the Venus facility represents Corpus investment in controlled reproduction -- technology the Tenno repurpose for their own companion program.

Kubrows themselves are native to Earth's forest regions, where they roam in packs and have coexisted with the planet's landscape since before the Orokin era. Their domestication by Tenno is presented as a partnership rather than ownership; the companion system's loyalty and bond mechanics reinforce this framing throughout the game.

The Taichen breed referenced by the cosmetic reward is one of several standard Kubrow breeds, each associated with distinct behavioral traits and combat roles. The quest does not specify which breed the player incubates, leaving that outcome to chance.

## Tips

- The Survival mission on Unda only requires 5 minutes of survivability. There is no benefit to staying longer; extract as soon as the objective is met.
- Kubrow Dens on Earth have a guaranteed egg drop during the quest. Run any Earth mission on the forest tileset, destroy the first den found, and collect the egg.
- Build the Incubator Power Core in the Foundry before starting the incubation stage to avoid interrupting the tutorial flow.
- After completing the quest, Kubrow breed selection requires Genetic Code Templates. These can be purchased from the Market or found as drops. Plan ahead if a specific breed -- such as the Huras for stealth or Sahasa for resource gathering -- is the goal.
- The Kubrow's combat effectiveness scales with investment in Companion mods. The default loadout it ships with is minimal; upgrading its mod slots should be a priority after completing the quest.
