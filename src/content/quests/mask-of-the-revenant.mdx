## Overview

Mask of the Revenant is a short quest tied closely to the Plains of Eidolon open-world zone and the Quills faction. It tells the story of Revenant, a Warframe unlike others -- not a Tenno-controlled frame in the conventional sense, but a Warframe that was captured and corrupted by a Sentient Eidolon, then transformed into something hybrid and strange. The quest unfolds in three stages, all set on the Plains of Eidolon at night, and each requires the player to don a special mask that allows their Operator to perceive energy traces invisible to the naked eye.

The quest is unusual in its pacing: it does not involve combat-heavy missions in the conventional sense. Instead, players visit specific locations on the Plains, interact with glowing residue left by the entity imprisoned in Gara Toht Lake, and then complete bounties to obtain component blueprints as rewards. The final stage adds a direct confrontation with a Specter version of Revenant himself.

Because the quest requires standing with The Quills, it is gated behind significant progression in the Plains of Eidolon content. Players who have not invested time in Cetus and Eidolon hunts will need to do so before this quest is accessible.

## Prerequisites

Two requirements must be met before Mask of the Revenant can begin:

The first requirement is reaching Observer rank with The Quills. The Quills are a secretive faction based in Cetus whose offerings center on Amp construction and Operator equipment. Observer is the second rank in their progression ladder, which requires earning and spending a significant amount of Quills standing. Standing is earned primarily through completing Eidolon hunts and depositing Intact, Exceptional, Flawless, or Brilliant Eidolon Shards at the Quills vendor.

The second requirement is purchasing the Mask of the Lost One from Nakak, a vendor in Cetus. The mask costs 1 Cetus Wisp, a resource obtained by fishing and exploring the Plains of Eidolon. Once both conditions are met, the quest begins automatically when the player speaks to Nakak.

## Stages

### A New Threat Looms

Players must enter the Plains of Eidolon at night with the Mask of the Lost One equipped on their Operator. Night on the Plains is the period when Eidolons emerge from Gara Toht Lake, and the mask's function -- revealing energy traces associated with Sentient corruption -- only works during this window. Players need to locate energy blotches on the southwest shore of Gara Toht Lake and interact with them. These glowing residue points are visible while the mask is worn.

After interacting with the traces, players must complete a Plains bounty to receive the first Revenant component blueprint. Bounties are available from Konzu at the gates of Cetus, and any tier-appropriate bounty can fulfill the objective.

### A Terror Long Forgotten

The second stage follows the same pattern as the first but directs players to the northeast side of Gara Toht Lake. Night entry is again required, and the Mask of the Lost One must remain equipped for the Operator to perceive the energy blotches. A second interaction is made, and another bounty must be completed to earn the second component blueprint.

The narrative context deepens here: the traces carry fragmented impressions of a Warframe that entered the lake and did not emerge. The precise nature of what happened there is revealed across all three stages rather than delivered in a single exposition block.

### The Lost One

<Callout type="warn">Spoilers for the final stage of Mask of the Revenant follow.</Callout>

The final energy trace is located between Gara Toht Lake and Renthi Spring. Interacting with it triggers a confrontation with a Revenant Specter -- a boss encounter using the Revenant Warframe model, armed with the Phantasma shotgun. The Specter is aggressive and uses Revenant's abilities against the player, including his Enthrall mechanic, which can mind-control enemies into fighting alongside the Specter.

Defeating the Revenant Specter awards the Revenant main blueprint, completing the quest.

## Rewards

Mask of the Revenant awards the Revenant Warframe blueprint across its three stages:

- Stage 1 (bounty reward): first Revenant component blueprint.
- Stage 2 (bounty reward): second Revenant component blueprint.
- Stage 3 (boss defeat): Revenant main blueprint.

The third component blueprint is not awarded through the quest itself but is available through standard Plains of Eidolon bounty rotation. Players must check the available bounties in Cetus for the remaining component or obtain it through trading.

Revenant requires the standard Foundry build process: components must be built individually before the main Warframe can be assembled, with appropriate credit and resource costs at each step.

## Lore

The Unum is the intelligence that inhabits the Orokin Tower visible on the Plains of Eidolon -- an ancient Sentient that chose to fuse with the Orokin structure rather than destroy it. Its motivations and nature remain among the more opaque elements of Warframe lore, but it communicates through chosen intermediaries, one of whom is Nakak, the masked vendor in Cetus.

The mask Nakak provides channels the Unum's awareness of the Sentient energy locked within Gara Toht Lake. The Warframe known as Revenant was one of the Tenno-piloted frames that encountered the great Eidolon there and was captured rather than destroyed. Unlike Eidolons that fragment on the Plains, this one transformed its host: the Revenant that emerges from the lake during the quest is neither fully Warframe nor fully Sentient, but a fusion that serves as both prisoner and jailer.

Revenant's kit reflects this hybrid nature. His abilities involve Sentient energy mechanics -- enthralling enemies, generating Sentient tendrils, and draining energy from those he controls. His passive resistance to Sentient damage mirrors the lore explanation that his Sentient corruption makes him difficult for other Sentients to harm effectively.

## Tips

- Reaching Observer rank with The Quills requires completing multiple Eidolon hunts. The most efficient path is farming Teralyst captures solo or in coordinated squads.
- Cetus Wisps spawn on the edges of water bodies on the Plains during both day and night. They are visible as small floating lights and can be collected without fishing.
- All three stages require entering the Plains at night. Day-night cycles on the Plains last approximately 4 real-time minutes each. Timing a squad entry at the start of a night cycle maximizes available time.
- The Revenant Specter in Stage 3 uses Enthrall, which can turn nearby enemies against the player. Keeping enemies cleared from the area before engaging the Specter reduces the risk of fighting both simultaneously.
- Revenant's third component blueprint is not awarded by the quest. Check Konzu's bounty board regularly for the Neuroptics or Chassis blueprint depending on which component the bounties are currently offering.
