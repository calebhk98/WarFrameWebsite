## Overview

Natah is a main story quest introduced with Update 17: Echoes of the Sentient (July 2015). It serves as the gateway into the Warframe narrative's larger arc, transforming the Lotus from an uncomplicated mission controller into one of the most consequential figures in the game's lore. The quest begins when Tenno detect unusual Oculyst drones surveying the Origin System, searching for ruins left over from the Old War.

The Lotus initially misidentifies the drones as unusual Corpus machinery. When she examines them more closely, the truth becomes apparent: these are Sentient constructs, and their presence signals that something buried and dormant has begun to stir. Teshin, the Conclave master, steps in to provide guidance as the Lotus withdraws into uncharacteristic silence. The investigation takes players into the depths of Uranus, where the Grineer commander Tyl Regor has been conducting illegal archaeological excavations -- digging toward something he does not fully understand.

Completing Natah is a prerequisite for The Second Dream and, by extension, the entire mainline quest chain that follows. It also permanently changes how players perceive the Lotus, providing context that resonates throughout the rest of the game's story.

## Prerequisites

Players must meet the following requirements to begin Natah:

- **Uranus Junction completion** -- The star chart must be progressed to the point where Uranus is accessible. The Uranus Junction requires completion of specific challenges on Sedna and Uranus-adjacent nodes.
- **Mastery Rank 2** -- A basic mastery threshold that most players will have reached well before arriving at Uranus.

The quest key is obtained automatically through server reward upon completing the Uranus Junction requirements, rather than through crafting or purchase. As with most main quests, Natah must be set as the active quest in the Codex before its objectives appear in the navigation interface.

## Stages

Natah unfolds across five stages, moving from detective work to direct confrontation.

**1. Strange Drones**

The first stage establishes the threat. Oculyst drones have been spotted near Uranus, and the Lotus needs players to intercept one for analysis. The mission involves pursuing and scanning one of the drones across a Grineer-controlled node. The encounter is brief and tense -- Oculysts do not engage directly, but their evasive behavior and the Lotus's increasingly anxious transmissions signal that the stakes are higher than a routine recon mission.

**2. Seek Teshin**

The Lotus falls silent without explanation, and Teshin steps in with a rare direct communication. He has information about the drones and their origin, and he wants the Tenno to hear it without the Lotus present. Players visit Teshin's Conclave relay to receive his account of Sentient history and learn that the drones originate from a faction that once waged war on the Orokin -- the same faction that the Orokin created the Tenno to fight.

**3. Tyl Regor's Excavation**

The trail leads to Uranus, where the Grineer scientist Tyl Regor has been running a covert excavation project. His digs have uncovered ruins from the Old War sealed in the ocean trenches. Players infiltrate his sealab complex, fighting through Grineer troops to reach the excavation site and understand what Regor has found. The environment mixes Grineer utilitarian architecture with increasingly strange Orokin and Sentient relics emerging from the depths.

Tyl Regor himself communicates throughout the mission, offering characteristically theatrical monologues that simultaneously provide exposition and obscure it. He knows something is sealed beneath the ocean floor, but he does not fully grasp what he is awakening.

**4. Intercept the Signal**

An interception mission on Uranus focuses on harvesting data being transmitted from the buried site. Players hold multiple relay nodes while fighting off increasingly determined Grineer forces who have realized the nature of the intrusion. The data gathered begins to outline the identity of the entity below -- an ancient Sentient destroyer who was sealed here at the end of the Old War.

**5. Reseal the Tomb**

The final stage brings players into direct conflict with the situation that has been building across the entire quest. The entity sealed on Uranus is Hunhow, a Sentient destroyer of worlds who fought against the Orokin during the Old War. Tyl Regor's excavations have weakened the seals confining him, and he is reaching out -- calling for the one who was sent to destroy the Tenno and never completed the mission.

That one is the Lotus.

Players must act to prevent Hunhow's full awakening while the Lotus confronts her own history. The stage ends not with a conventional boss fight but with a revelation. The Lotus reveals that she was once called Natah, a Sentient mimic created to infiltrate the Orokin and, ultimately, to find and eliminate the Tenno. She crossed through the Void to complete her mission, and the crossing left her sterile -- unable to bear the young that Sentients pass through the Void to propagate. Stripped of that capacity, she made a different choice. She hid the Tenno, raised them, guided them, and became something she was never designed to be.

Hunhow knows the truth. He calls out to his daughter from his prison, and his voice echoes through the Lotus even as she pushes him back and reseals the tomb.

## Rewards

Completing Natah grants the following:

- **Exilus Adapter Blueprint** -- A blueprint used to craft an Exilus Adapter, which unlocks the Exilus slot on a Warframe and allows equipping a utility-type mod without consuming one of the eight standard mod slots. The Exilus Adapter Blueprint has been available through Cephalon Simaris's offerings as well, but the quest completion version is the earliest accessible route for most players.
- **Narrative progression** -- Natah completion is the gating requirement for The Second Dream, the quest that dramatically expands the scope of the game's story and introduces Operator gameplay. Players who have not completed Natah cannot begin The Second Dream regardless of other progression.

## Lore

<Callout type="warn">The following section contains major story spoilers for Natah and The Second Dream.</Callout>

The Sentients are a faction created by the Orokin to serve as autonomous terraforming machines, sent beyond the Origin System to prepare new worlds for habitation. The Void destroyed their ability to reproduce biologically -- anything that passed through it became sterile. Over generations, the Sentients developed a collective resentment toward the Orokin and eventually turned against their creators, launching a war that nearly destroyed the Origin System.

Natah was engineered for a specific purpose: to infiltrate the Orokin from within by mimicking their trusted Tenno operative. She crossed the Void, which stripped her of reproductive capacity as it does all Sentients. She found the Tenno -- children traumatized by their own exposure to the Void, dormant in the Second Dream state, capable of immense destructive power but dependent on guidance.

She did not carry out her mission. The maternal instinct she developed toward the sterile void-touched children replaced her original directive. She became the Lotus, their guide and protector, hiding her Sentient nature behind the comfortable fiction of a human-like advisor. Hunhow, her father, was sealed away before he could learn of her betrayal. His awakening in Natah represents the first crack in the long concealment.

The full consequences of Hunhow's knowledge extend through The Second Dream, The War Within, The Sacrifice, and The New War. Natah is the first chapter in that arc, and its final revelation fundamentally reframes everything that came before it.
