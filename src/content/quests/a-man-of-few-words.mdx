## Overview

A Man Of Few Words is a short quest that introduces Clem, a Grineer defector who works as a contractor for Darvo, the arms dealer found at Relays. The WFCD data describes the quest concisely: "Darvo needs your help again. Save one of his best contractors from Grineer execution." Despite the brief runtime, the quest establishes one of the game's most beloved supporting characters and unlocks a recurring weekly mission tied to Clem.

The quest is accessible relatively early in the star chart progression. Players who have completed Howl of the Kubrow and reached the required Mastery Rank will find Darvo at any Relay, ready to ask for a favor. The tone is lighter than many main storyline quests -- Darvo's concern for Clem and Clem's distinctive manner of speech make the pair memorable even without extensive dialogue.

Completing A Man Of Few Words does more than finish a short story arc. It unlocks the "Help Clem" weekly mission, a repeatable mission available through Darvo that provides Orokin Cell rewards and credits. For players who regularly run weekly bounties, the Clem mission becomes a reliable routine -- a reason to revisit Darvo long after the quest key is spent.

## Prerequisites

Before starting A Man Of Few Words, the following must be in place:

- **Howl of the Kubrow** -- This earlier quest must be completed. It is a prerequisite chain that ensures basic star chart and Relay access are established.
- **Mastery Rank 2** -- A minimum Mastery Rank of 2 is required to undertake this quest.
- **Relay access** -- Players must be able to visit a Relay, where Darvo maintains his shop. Relays unlock through early star chart progression.

No special equipment loadout is required, though a combat-capable frame and weapons appropriate to the node's enemy level are recommended.

## Stages

The quest unfolds across a small number of stages, each taking place in Grineer-controlled territory.

**1. Contact Darvo**

The quest begins with a conversation at Darvo's shop inside any Relay. Darvo explains that one of his most trusted contractors -- a Grineer named Clem -- has been captured and is being held for execution due to his defection from Grineer ranks. Darvo cannot act openly, so he asks the Tenno to handle the rescue.

**2. Locate Clem**

Players deploy to a Grineer mission node. The objective is to navigate the facility and locate Clem before Grineer forces carry out the execution. The environment matches the standard Grineer tileset, populated with Lancers, Troopers, and heavier units depending on the node level.

**3. Rescue Clem**

Once located, Clem must be freed from Grineer custody. This stage functions similarly to a Rescue mission: players must reach the holding area and neutralize threats in the immediate vicinity. Clem, once freed, will accompany the player toward extraction. He is capable of fighting alongside Tenno but must survive the remainder of the mission.

**4. Extract with Clem**

With Clem freed, the priority shifts to extraction. Grineer reinforcements arrive in greater numbers as the alert level rises. Clem moves toward the extraction point alongside the player. Keeping him alive is mandatory -- if Clem is downed before extraction, the mission fails. Players with crowd-control abilities will find this stage significantly more manageable.

<Callout type="warn">Minor story details follow regarding Clem's background and characterization.</Callout>

Clem's defining trait -- described even in the WFCD quest description -- is that he is a contractor of few words. When he speaks, he tends to repeat his own name. This in-universe quirk is explained by the Grineer cloning process, which causes degraded cognition over successive generations. Clem is notable among Grineer because he defected voluntarily, suggesting a level of self-awareness uncommon in the faction.

## Rewards

Completing A Man Of Few Words grants the following:

- **Clem Clone blueprint** -- A blueprint used to create a Clem Clone, which functions as a quest reward item acknowledging the mission's outcome.
- **Help Clem weekly mission unlock** -- After quest completion, Darvo offers a repeatable weekly mission titled "Help Clem." This mission sends players back into Grineer territory alongside Clem to accomplish a secondary objective. Successful completion rewards Orokin Cells and credits on a weekly cooldown.

The quest rewards are modest compared to major story quests, but the weekly mission provides consistent Orokin Cell income -- a resource in high demand for crafting high-tier blueprints. Orokin Cells are used in the construction of many Warframe components and weapons, making the Clem weekly a worthwhile routine for mid-game players.

## Lore

Clem is a Grineer clone who chose to defect from the Grineer Empire and work with Darvo as a contractor. The Grineer, as a faction, rely on mass cloning -- a process that degrades genetic fidelity over successive generations, resulting in widespread cognitive and physical deterioration across their ranks. Clem's limited vocabulary and distinctive speech pattern reflect this degradation.

Despite this, Clem exhibits loyalty, initiative, and fighting capability. His defection is portrayed as a genuine act of will rather than happenstance, which sets him apart from the bulk of Grineer units encountered across the star chart. Darvo's investment in rescuing him -- rather than simply finding a replacement contractor -- suggests that Clem's value extends beyond his combat utility.

The quest's title is a reference to Clem's speech pattern. He speaks very few distinct words, primarily repeating his own name in various inflections to convey meaning. This characteristic became a source of affection among the Warframe community, and Clem grew into one of the game's most recognized supporting characters as a result. The weekly mission that follows from this quest was added in response to player enthusiasm for the character.

He represents a lighter narrative thread within Warframe's generally darker lore -- a small story about loyalty and unlikely kinship set against the backdrop of faction warfare and cosmic horror.

## Tips

The following notes are useful for players attempting A Man Of Few Words for the first time.

- **Treat the rescue stage as a standard Rescue mission.** The mechanics are familiar: reach the target, eliminate immediate threats, and escort the rescued unit to extraction without letting them die.
- **Use crowd-control abilities during extraction.** Frames with abilities that slow, freeze, or displace enemies are effective at protecting Clem during the final push. Vauban, Nova, and Frost are solid choices if available, though any functional frame will suffice at this stage's enemy levels.
- **Do not let Clem take sustained fire.** Clem will engage enemies independently, which can draw attention. If his health drops, prioritize nearby enemies over distant ones to reduce incoming damage.
- **Complete the quest before repeating Darvo's weekly.** The weekly Help Clem mission will not appear in Darvo's offerings until A Man Of Few Words is finished. Players who encounter Darvo at Relays without seeing the weekly should check whether the quest has been completed.
- **Bring an Orokin Cell booster on weekly runs.** The Help Clem weekly mission is a reliable Orokin Cell source, and resource boosters double the yield. Timing the weekly with an active booster improves farming efficiency.
- **Clem can be equipped in some Tactical Alert variants.** In certain event missions, Clem functions as an ally with unique abilities tied to his favored weapon type. These appearances are not part of the base quest but are enabled by its completion.
