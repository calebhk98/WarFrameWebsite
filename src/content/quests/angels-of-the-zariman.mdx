## Overview

Angels Of The Zariman is a main story quest released in Update 31.5 (April 2022) that marks the Tenno's formal return to the Zariman Ten Zero -- the Orokin colony ship where the first Tenno were transformed by a catastrophic Void accident during the reign of the Orokin Empire. For years the vessel drifted between dimensions, partially hidden by the Void's ever-shifting topology. Its reemergence puts it squarely in the crosshairs of Grineer forces intent on claiming its power source, the Reliquary Drive.

The WFCD description captures the scope: "From depths beyond reason a Voice had spoken, and its echoes would not be denied. No longer fully concealed by the ever-shifting Void, the Zariman now rests between worlds." The quest is solo-only and serves as both a conclusion to the arc established by The New War and a gateway to the Zariman as an ongoing mission hub with its own syndicate, the Holdfasts.

At the heart of the quest is the discovery of the Holdfasts: ghostlike survivors of the original Zariman crew who exist in a liminal state between life and death, sustained by the Void energy that permeates the vessel. Among them is Kira, a former Holdfast who succumbed to Void corruption and transformed into a Void Angel -- one of the game's new elite enemy types. Defeating Kira is the quest's climactic confrontation.

Completing the quest does not merely close a narrative chapter. It grants access to the Chrysalith, the Zariman's hub area, and unlocks the Holdfasts syndicate and Chrysalith Bounties that provide parts for the Gyre Warframe. The quest is a prerequisite for engaging with the entirety of the Zariman mission pool.

## Prerequisites

Angels Of The Zariman requires the following before it can be initiated:

- **The New War** -- The preceding main story quest must be completed. The New War is a lengthy, climactic quest that reshapes the Warframe narrative; Angels Of The Zariman follows directly from its events.
- **Mastery Rank 5 (recommended)** -- No hard Mastery Rank gate is documented in WFCD data for this quest, but the Holdfasts and wiki community strongly recommend an Operator with meaningful Void damage output. Underpowered Amps make certain Thrax Centurion encounters significantly more difficult.
- **Solo play** -- The quest must be started solo. Squad play is not permitted during the questline itself, though post-quest Zariman missions support group play.

Players are advised to upgrade their Amp before entering, as Void Shard-based damage is required to interact with Void Cascade Exolizers and to damage Thrax Centurions in their spectral phase.

## Stages

The quest unfolds across seven stages aboard the Zariman, introducing the ship's new mechanics and enemy types progressively.

**1. Find the Song's Source**

A haunting voice draws the Tenno to the Zariman. Upon boarding, players encounter Grineer forces already occupying sections of the vessel and make first contact with the Holdfasts. Quinn, the de facto leader among the survivors, establishes the stakes: the Void energy coursing through the ship is destabilizing, and something must be done before the Zariman becomes completely inhospitable.

**2. Stop the Void Cascade**

The Void Cascade is one of the Zariman's rotating mission types and is introduced here as a tutorial encounter. Players must locate four Exolizers -- devices that have been contaminated by Void energy -- and cleanse them using Operator Void damage. Thrax Centurions guard the Exolizers; they shift between physical and spectral states and can only be damaged in their spectral phase using Operator attacks or Operator-augmented Warframe abilities.

**3. Talk to Quinn**

Between mission stages, players return to the Chrysalith hub area and confer with Quinn. The Holdfasts leader elaborates on the crew's condition and explains the duty they have undertaken to maintain the ship's integrity. This stage deepens the lore context and introduces Quinn as the primary quest NPC.

**4. Stop the Void Flood**

The Void Flood mechanic, another of the Zariman's mission types, is introduced in this stage. Players collect Vitoplast -- a resource dropped by enemies -- and deposit it into three Void Ruptures to seal breaches in the Albrecht Membrane, the metaphysical boundary separating the Zariman's interior from uncontrolled Void space. Failure to seal the ruptures in time allows Void Flood levels to rise, increasing enemy density and difficulty.

**5. Void Flood Debrief**

Returning to the Chrysalith, Quinn reveals that a more serious threat is converging on the Zariman's Reliquary Drive. The Drive powers the ship and, if lost or compromised, would doom the remaining Holdfasts and destabilize the vessel beyond recovery.

**6. Protect the Reliquary Drive**

This stage introduces Void Armageddon, the quest's final mission type. Players construct Auto-Fabricating Armaments using Argozene collected from enemies to establish defensive emplacements around the Reliquary Drive. Grineer assault forces arrive in waves while the Armaments are built and maintained.

The stage culminates in a confrontation with Kira, a former Holdfast who has fully transformed into a Void Angel -- a towering spectral entity sustained by uncontrolled Void energy. Kira must be defeated across two phases: first depleting her physical health bar using Warframe damage, then engaging her spectral form using Operator Void attacks. The fight is one of the more mechanically layered encounters in the game at the time of release.

**7. Retrieve Quinn's Tablet**

After Kira is defeated, the final stage tasks players with recovering a memorial tablet honoring the fallen crew of the Zariman. This quieter conclusion reinforces the Holdfasts' theme of remembrance and duty before the quest resolves.

## Rewards

Completing Angels Of The Zariman grants the following:

- **1x Voidplume Pinion** -- A Holdfasts syndicate currency item used in standing transactions within the Zariman.
- **Operator Voidshell Skin Set** -- A cosmetic set for the Operator character.
- **Drifter Voidshell Skin Set** -- A cosmetic set for the Drifter character.
- **Dormizone access** -- Personal quarters aboard the Zariman that can be customized as an alternative to the Orbiter.
- **The Holdfasts Syndicate access** -- Unlocks the Zariman's primary syndicate, enabling standing accumulation and access to exclusive offerings.
- **Chrysalith Bounties** -- Bounty missions offered by the Holdfasts that reward Gyre Warframe component blueprints, among other items.

The Holdfasts syndicate and Chrysalith Bounties together make this quest one of the more mechanically consequential in the game -- quest completion gates a significant body of endgame content.

## Lore

The Zariman Ten Zero looms large in Warframe's foundational mythology. The vessel departed for the Tau system carrying a generation of Orokin colonists and their children, but a navigational catastrophe sent it deep into the Void. The Void's exposure transformed the children aboard -- those who would become the first Tenno -- while the adult crew members were killed, driven mad, or altered in ways the Orokin could not fully explain.

The Holdfasts represent those crew members who persisted in a corrupted form: sustained by Void energy but no longer fully alive in any conventional sense. Their continued existence aboard the Zariman is described as a liminal state -- present in the physical world through force of will and the Void energy binding them to the vessel they devoted their lives to protecting.

Kira's transformation into a Void Angel represents the catastrophic endpoint of that corruption: a Holdfast who could no longer maintain the balance between Void exposure and self-identity. The quest frames her not as a villain but as a tragedy -- a crew member who held on as long as she could before the Void claimed her entirely. Quinn's grief over confronting Kira underscores this reading.

The Zariman as a post-quest hub serves as a reminder of this history. Every mission undertaken there takes place aboard a ship marked by decades of Void saturation, populated by the ghosts of a crew that died trying to reach a destination they never found.

## Tips

The following notes apply to players attempting the quest for the first time.

- **Upgrade your Amp before starting.** Void damage from the Amp is required for Exolizer cleansing and Thrax Centurion damage. The Mote Amp -- the starter Amp -- is functional but slow. Craft or acquire a stronger Amp through Quills or Vox Solaris before proceeding.
- **Learn the Void Cascade mechanic patiently.** The first Exolizer encounter is a tutorial. Take time to understand the interaction between Operator mode and the Exolizer interface; later stages assume this knowledge.
- **Prioritize Thrax Centurions in spectral phase.** When a Thrax Centurion shifts to its spectral form, it becomes invulnerable to Warframe weapons. Switch to Operator mode immediately to deal with it, then return to your Warframe for standard combat.
- **Collect Vitoplast aggressively in Void Flood stages.** The resource despawns if left uncollected. Staying mobile and picking up Vitoplast drops before depositing at ruptures is more efficient than letting it accumulate on the ground.
- **Use crowd-control during the Reliquary Drive defense.** Warframes with area denial or slow effects protect the Armaments during construction phases. Any frame capable of suppressing a large incoming wave is well-suited to this stage.
