## Overview

The War Within is a main-line cinematic quest released in Update 19.0 in November 2016. It is the direct sequel to The Second Dream and significantly expands the Operator system introduced there. Where The Second Dream revealed the existence of Operators, The War Within teaches players to use them in active combat through a series of missions that frequently remove Warframe access entirely. The quest introduces the Void Sling ability (replacing Void Dash in later game versions), Void Mode, and Operator Transference on enemies -- collectively forming the foundation of endgame Operator play.

The War Within centers on Teshin, the Conclave grandmaster who was a peripheral figure in earlier content. His role is reframed by the quest as far more complicated, tying directly to the nature of the Dax warrior caste and the Orokin's method of binding servants absolutely to their will. The antagonists are the Twin Grineer Queens, who occupy the Kuva Fortress in the asteroid belt and who have their own reasons for wanting to locate and capture a Tenno.

Completing The War Within unlocks a substantial amount of endgame infrastructure. Sorties, daily challenge mission chains offering Riven Mods and unique rewards, require this quest's completion. The Kuva Fortress becomes a mission node on the star chart. The Kuva Siphon and Kuva Flood mission types, used to level Kuva Liches, also unlock. The Quills syndicate, which sells advanced Amp components, becomes accessible from Cetus.

<Callout type="warn">Spoilers for The War Within follow. The sections below describe major revelations about the Orokin, Teshin, and the nature of Kuva.</Callout>

## Prerequisites

The War Within requires completion of The Second Dream and the Sedna Junction on Pluto. The Sedna Junction unlocks after completing Pluto node prerequisites and typically represents late-mid-game progression on the star chart. Players also need Mastery Rank 5 to fulfill the requirement, though by the time the Sedna Junction is available, most players will exceed this threshold.

The quest's Operator combat segments -- particularly the Mountain Pass and Yuvan Theater sections -- benefit significantly from having developed Focus school abilities. Players who have invested in the Zenurik or Naramon focus schools will find the sustained Operator encounters more manageable, though any school's passive bonuses provide some benefit.

## Stages

The War Within progresses through eight distinct mission segments, several of which are solo-only and some of which deny Warframe access entirely.

### Investigate the Reservoir and Track Teshin

The quest opens with a disturbance in the Somatic Link, the connection between the Operator and the Warframe. The Lotus dispatches the player to Lua, where Teshin's Specter is encountered and captured. A second Specter appears at Carpo on Jupiter. These early missions establish that Teshin has been acting against the Tenno's interests, though his motivations are not yet clear.

### Pursue into Asteroid Field

Naga on Sedna is a solo mission requiring players to navigate through an asteroid field environment while avoiding Grineer scanner detection. Disrupting the security matrix involves a timed defend sequence. This stage is the first of several that remove external support, setting up the more isolated Operator segments later.

### Infiltrate the Kuva Fortress

Dakata on the Kuva Fortress requires collecting three Elevator Keys to progress deeper into the fortress architecture. The mission concludes with a cutscene revealing the nature of the Queens' plan and showing Teshin's conflicted position. The Kuva Fortress tileset introduced in this quest features its own aesthetics and enemy composition distinct from standard Grineer infrastructure.

### The Purge Precept

The Orbiter-set mission cuts access to the Warframe and requires the player to use the Operator's Void Beam to interact with and recharge the Somatic Link under time pressure. An escape sequence follows with hazards timed to match the pressure. This segment directly tests the basic Void Beam mechanics introduced in The Second Dream.

### Mountain Pass and Yuvan Theater

The Mountain Pass section is the longest sustained Operator sequence in the quest. Set on a snow-covered mountain on Earth, it strips the Warframe away entirely and forces the player through a series of Void-powered traversal challenges. Void Mode -- a brief state of invisibility and Void energy absorption that must be managed against a limited energy meter -- is introduced here as a defensive tool. Void Sling replaces Void Dash as the primary movement ability, allowing targeted directional launches across gaps.

Alignment choices appear throughout the Mountain Pass and Yuvan Theater segments. These choices assign the player's character to a Sun, Neutral, or Moon alignment path based on selected dialogue options. The alignment system has minor effects on cosmetic outcomes and certain NPC interactions, but does not affect quest rewards or mechanical capabilities.

The Yuvan Theater, an ancient Orokin ceremonial space, culminates the traversal segments with a mirror barrier puzzle solved through Void Beam interaction.

### Confront the Grineer Queens

The final confrontation takes place in the Kuva Fortress throne room. Players battle Kuva Guardians -- elite Grineer warriors immune to conventional Warframe damage but vulnerable to Operator Void attacks -- using Void Sling to approach and Void Beam to disable them. The Twin Queens direct the combat from a raised dais. Each Queen's braids become vulnerable in sequence, and defeating them requires sustained Void Beam fire against the exposed positions.

Teshin appears as a combat encounter near the end of the sequence. His defeat transitions into a dialogue sequence where the final alignment choice is made regarding the Elder Queen's fate and the disposition of Kuva as a resource.

## Rewards

The War Within provides direct item rewards and unlocks a large amount of end-game infrastructure.

Direct rewards include the Broken Scepter staff weapon, delivered at quest completion. The Orvius glaive Blueprint is also rewarded, requiring separate Foundry crafting. A Riven Mod -- a randomly stat-rolled mod that can be applied to a specific weapon type -- is granted; Riven Mods are otherwise primarily obtained through Sorties. The Mountain Pass Captura scene and the Grineer Queens Glyph are cosmetic inclusions.

The Personal Quarters Segment Blueprint is sent by Ordis after completing the quest; this segment unlocks a room in the Orbiter where Operator customization and Focus abilities can be accessed in a dedicated space.

The unlock list extends further: Sortie missions become available, providing daily high-difficulty missions with valuable Riven Mod and Prime part rewards. The Kuva Fortress node appears on the star chart. Kuva Siphon and Kuva Flood missions, which drop Kuva for upgrading Kuva Weapons and feeding Kuva Liches, are gated behind this quest. The Quills faction in Cetus unlocks, providing access to advanced Amp crafting that dramatically improves Operator combat capability.

## Lore

<Callout type="warn">Major spoilers for The War Within and Orokin history follow.</Callout>

The Twin Grineer Queens, despite their decrepit appearance, are among the oldest surviving beings in the Origin System. Grineer cloning degrades over generations, causing physical and cognitive decline. The Queens circumvented this through the Yuvan ceremony -- an Orokin process that allows an elder consciousness to transfer into a younger body. Kuva, a substance produced in the Kuva Fortress and related to the Orokin's Void experiments, is central to this process as both a fuel and a catalyst.

Teshin is a Dax warrior -- one of the Orokin's genetically engineered soldier caste, bound to obey Orokin commands through a biological conditioning called Continuity. This conditioning transfers through the Orokin bloodline; the Grineer Queens, being Orokin-descended, retain the ability to command Dax soldiers regardless of the soldier's own will. Teshin cannot disobey them even when working against the Tenno, whom he has served as Conclave master for generations. The quest makes explicit that his apparent betrayal has never been a choice.

Kuva's significance extends beyond the Yuvan ceremony. It is described as the "blood of the Void" and is tied to a specific Orokin process of extracting and stabilizing Void energy in physical form. Kuva Siphons and Floods extract it from ambient Void rifts, and the Queens' primary concern is maintaining their supply to sustain the Yuvan cycle. The Elder Queen's plan involves using the Operator's young body for the ceremony, which would effectively end the Operator's existence.

The alignment choices made during the quest reflect the Operator's emerging moral framework: Sun choices trend toward direct action and decisive use of power, Moon choices toward restraint and longer-term thinking, and Neutral toward pragmatic balance. These choices accumulate across quests into a system with small cosmetic and narrative effects, though they carry no permanent mechanical penalties.

## Tips

- The Kuva Guardian fights in the throne room require Void Sling to close distance quickly after stunning them with Void Beam. Practice the sling-into-melee rotation in the Mountain Pass section before reaching the throne room.
- Void Mode drains energy while active. Managing energy pickup frequency during the Mountain Pass section prevents running dry during extended traversal sequences.
- The solo restriction on Naga, Sedna applies even in a Squad session; the game will force the mission to be run alone when the quest step is active.
- Sorties unlock immediately upon quest completion and reset daily; beginning them promptly maximizes the long-term Riven Mod and Prime part accumulation.
- The Quills in Cetus offer Mote Amp components by default and progressively better parts as standing increases. Investing in Quills standing after this quest significantly improves Operator combat effectiveness for the quests that follow.
