## Overview

Sands of Inaros is an intermediate-tier quest that serves as the primary acquisition path for the Inaros Warframe. Unlike most quest-locked Warframes, Inaros is not unlocked through Junctions or story progression -- the quest blueprint must be purchased directly from Baro Ki'Teer, the Void Trader who visits relays every two weeks. This makes timing a key part of the acquisition chain, since players must wait for Baro's appearance and bring 100 Orokin Ducats and 25,000 Credits to the trade.

The quest is thematically distinct from most Warframe quests. Rather than heavy narrative dialogue or cutscenes, Sands of Inaros is structured as a series of ritual combat trials conducted inside an ancient Martian tomb. The Lotus plays a minimal role; most of the quest's atmosphere comes from environmental storytelling and the escalating difficulty of the enemy assignments. The setting draws on an Egyptian-adjacent aesthetic consistent with Inaros's design as a mummified, desert-themed Warframe.

The quest blueprint crafts in the Foundry over six hours and requires Nano Spores, Oxium, and one Nitain Extract -- a gated resource available primarily through Nightwave Cred Offerings. Players who do not have Nitain Extract stockpiled may need to purchase it before the quest key can be built. Once the key is crafted and selected in the Codex, the Tomb missions become accessible on the star chart.

Mechanically, Sands of Inaros is notable for its vessel-carrying system: the player holds a Sacred Vessel throughout each trial, and enemy kills only count toward the quest objective while the vessel is equipped. This mechanic is not explained prominently in the mission briefings, so players unfamiliar with it may waste time completing missions without making progress. Selecting the quest as the active quest in the Codex is a prerequisite for progress to register.

Sands of Inaros cannot be replayed after completion. Once Inaros has been obtained and the quest is marked complete, the entry in the Codex is archived. If Inaros is sold or accidentally dismantled, the player must purchase a new copy with Platinum or through Cephalon Simaris, rather than repeating the quest.

## Prerequisites

Before starting Sands of Inaros, the following requirements must be met:

- **Mastery Rank 5** -- the minimum rank required to equip and interact with the quest's enemies at the intended difficulty level.
- **Baro Ki'Teer visit** -- the quest blueprint is sold exclusively by Baro Ki'Teer during his bi-weekly relay appearances for 100 Orokin Ducats and 25,000 Credits. Orokin Ducats are obtained by trading Prime weapon and Warframe parts at the Ducat Kiosks located in relays.
- **Foundry resources** -- crafting the quest key requires 5,000 Credits, 5,000 Nano Spores, 400 Oxium, and 1 Nitain Extract. The build time is six hours.

Players who miss a Baro visit must wait until his next appearance. His rotation is approximately every two weeks, with a 48-hour window at each relay. Checking the in-game alert system or community resources for Baro's scheduled arrival is recommended.

## Stages

Sands of Inaros consists of four stages, all set within or near the ancient Martian tomb complex. The central mechanic throughout is the Sacred Vessel, which the player must carry while completing each trial's kill objectives.

**1. Explore the Tomb**

The quest begins with entry into the ancient Martian tomb. The player navigates the tomb interior, following waypoints to a shrine where the first Sacred Vessel is located. This opening stage is largely non-combat and serves as environmental introduction to the tomb's aesthetic -- sand-covered chambers, Orokin-influenced architecture, and an atmosphere of deliberate isolation. Retrieving the vessel triggers the first trial.

**2. First Vessel Challenge**

With the first vessel in hand, the player must kill 60 enemies from a specific enemy type assigned to their account. The assigned type varies per player and may include Detron Crewmen, Seekers, or Volatile Runners. Kills outside of active vessel-carrying do not count. After reaching the kill threshold, the player returns the vessel to the tomb's shrine, triggering a wave of approximately 50 Feral Kavats. Once the Kavats are cleared, the second vessel becomes accessible.

**3. Second Vessel Challenge**

The second trial reduces the required kill count but targets more resilient enemies. The assigned type for this stage is drawn from a pool including Railgun MOAs, Hyekka Masters, and Brood Mothers. After fulfilling the kill quota and depositing the second vessel, a Tomb Guardian boss spawns. The Tomb Guardian is notable for its splitting mechanic: when its health is depleted, it divides into smaller copies that must also be eliminated. Using weapons with area-of-effect damage is effective against the split forms. Defeating the Tomb Guardian reveals the third vessel.

**4. Third Vessel Challenge**

The final trial introduces a time limit of approximately 15 minutes, adding pressure to the kill requirement. The assigned enemy type includes Denial Bursas, Manics, and Juggernauts. Upon completing the kills and depositing the third vessel, the Tomb Protector appears alongside multiple Effigy statues. The Effigy statues must be destroyed during the encounter; leaving them active prolongs the fight. Completing this stage concludes the quest and delivers all four Inaros component blueprints.

## Rewards

Completing Sands of Inaros awards all the materials needed to craft Inaros:

- **Inaros Warframe blueprint** -- the main blueprint used in the Foundry to assemble the finished frame, requiring all three component pieces.
- **Inaros Neuroptics blueprint** -- one of three component blueprints awarded on completion.
- **Inaros Chassis blueprint** -- the second component blueprint.
- **Inaros Systems blueprint** -- the third component blueprint.
- **Sacred Urn Orbiter decoration** -- a cosmetic item placed in the orbiter as a trophy from the tomb trials.

Because the quest cannot be replayed, selling Inaros after construction means no free recovery path exists. The Warframe can be repurchased with Platinum from the in-game Market or obtained from Cephalon Simaris's Offerings after completing the quest.

## Lore

Inaros draws on the aesthetic and mythology of ancient Egypt, and Sands of Inaros frames the Warframe as a sleeping guardian sealed within a tomb awaiting a worthy successor. The Sacred Vessels function as keys to ritual chambers, each one requiring proof of combat ability before the next can be claimed. This structure mirrors the initiatory logic of the original Orokin civilization, which frequently employed tests of strength and endurance as prerequisite for access to restricted knowledge or power.

Baro Ki'Teer's role as the quest's gatekeeper is consistent with his characterization elsewhere in Warframe: the Void Trader operates as a broker of rare and exotic goods, indifferent to the Tenno's narrative concerns. That the quest to claim one of Warframe's most durable and self-sustaining frames is mediated through a merchant rather than a faction or story event is thematically appropriate -- Inaros belongs to no current power structure, but to a pre-Orokin past.

The Martian tomb complex in which the quest takes place is a remnant of a civilization that predates even the Orokin's height of power. Sand Shadow enemies encountered during the trials are spectral echoes of warriors who failed previous challenges. The final Tomb Protector represents the most recent guardian: an entity that has maintained the wards on Inaros's resting place for an unspecified duration, awaiting a challenger capable of meeting all three trial conditions.

## Tips

- Confirm the quest is set as the active quest in the Codex before entering any trial mission. Kills made while the wrong quest is active will not count toward the vessel objectives.
- The Sand Shadow enemy that appears in certain encounters mirrors the player's equipped loadout. Equipping low-damage weapons before entering makes Sand Shadow encounters significantly easier.
- Area-of-effect weapons or abilities that hit multiple targets simultaneously are recommended for the Tomb Guardian stage. When the Guardian splits, rapid area damage clears the smaller copies before they spread across the arena.
- Denial Bursas, one of the possible assignments in the final stage, are mechanically complex enemies that shut down Warframe abilities. Bringing a frame that does not rely heavily on active abilities helps maintain combat effectiveness during that trial.
- The 15-minute timer in the final stage begins when the vessel is deposited, not when the mission starts. Use the time before depositing to stockpile energy and position before triggering the boss encounter.
