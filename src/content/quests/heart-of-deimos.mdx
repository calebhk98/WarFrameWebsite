## Overview

Heart of Deimos is a solo main quest that introduces the Cambion Drift open-world zone on Deimos, the Infested moon of Mars. The quest centers on the Entrati, a dysfunctional Orokin family preserved in Infestation who have been maintaining a relic of enormous importance -- the Heart of Deimos -- for centuries. When a new and particularly dangerous strain of Infestation called the Grey Strain begins spreading across the moon, Loid, the Cephalon servant of the Entrati, broadcasts a distress call that reaches the Tenno.

The quest serves as the tutorial and introduction to everything Deimos has to offer: the Necralisk hub, the Entrati syndicate, Cambion Drift open-world gameplay, Necramech vehicles, and the Mirror Defense mission type. Completing it is required before any of these systems become accessible.

Unlike many Warframe quests, Heart of Deimos places the Tenno directly in the new environment from the very first stage. There are no off-world detours. Every stage takes place on Deimos itself, ensuring that players become familiar with the landscape, traversal tools, and bounty structure as a natural consequence of progressing the story.

## Prerequisites

To trigger Heart of Deimos, players must complete the Earth to Mars Junction on the star chart. Once that junction is cleared, Loid sends an inbox message that automatically begins the quest. No Mastery Rank requirement exists beyond what the junction demands, making this quest accessible relatively early in the game's progression.

The quest is solo-only. Squad play is not supported for any of its stages.

## Stages

The quest unfolds across six stages set on the Cambion Drift and the Necralisk.

**Visit Cambion Drift** opens with the Tenno arriving at the Necralisk gate and meeting Loid and his eccentric Cephalon counterpart Otak. To earn entry into the Entrati family's trust, two Infested Hives must be destroyed in the surrounding Cambion Drift landscape. Hive locations are marked on the map, and the open-world environment becomes immediately explorable beyond those waypoints.

**Locate Father** sends the Tenno deeper into the Cambion Drift. Father, the Entrati family member who oversees Necramech construction, is found in a deteriorating state. His cooperation is critical to repairing the failing Heart, but convincing him requires navigating the fragile family politics that define the Entrati dynamic.

**The Exocrine Expedition** introduces two of Deimos's core gathering activities: Fass Residue collection and fishing. Daughter, the family's naturalist, requires specimens to continue her work. This stage acts as a hands-on tutorial for the Exocrine fluid cycle and the Deimos fishing system.

**Descent to the Heart** marks the quest's first narrative peak. The Tenno travel through an Esophage -- a biological tunnel unique to Deimos -- to reach the Heart chamber. A Requiem Cipher puzzle bars the way forward, testing the player's familiarity with Orokin symbol logic.

After solving the puzzle, a hostile Necramech destroys the Heart, setting the stage for the climax.

**Through and Beyond** places the Tenno inside the Voidrig Necramech, piloting the heavy exosuit in combat for the first time. The objective is to defend the damaged Heart and defeat the enemy Necramech. This stage introduces the Necramech's weapon systems and ability kit before the player has any Necramech of their own.

**Epilogue** concludes with a meeting with Grandmother, the matriarch of the Entrati family, whose role in the preceding events is more complex than it initially appears. The Xaku blueprint is awarded here, and the Entrati syndicate opens for standing progression.

## Rewards

Completing Heart of Deimos grants several items and unlocks:

- Xaku Warframe blueprint -- Xaku is assembled from three component blueprints also obtainable through Entrati bounties on Cambion Drift.
- Voidrig Necramech blueprints -- the chassis, neuroptics, and systems components are awarded or become available through Deimos activities.
- Deimos Necralisk Captura Scene -- a photography environment depicting the Necralisk interior.
- Full access to the Entrati syndicate and its offering tiers.
- Mirror Defense -- a new mission type exclusive to Deimos that becomes playable after the quest.

## Lore

The Heart of Deimos is an Orokin artifact that acts as a conduit for Void energy, distributing it across the Origin System in a manner that sustains both Warframe systems and the Necramech technology maintained by the Entrati. The Entrati themselves were an Orokin family who voluntarily infested themselves with Technocyte to survive the fall of the Orokin Empire, a decision that fractured them psychologically and physically.

Son, the youngest male member of the Entrati family, deliberately released the Grey Strain of Infestation in an attempt to draw attention and concern from his neglectful parents. The plan endangered everyone on the moon. Grandmother, whose precise motivations remain deliberately ambiguous, appears to have anticipated or even guided the crisis to force her family to confront one another and work together for the first time in centuries.

The quest establishes Deimos as a layered location: simultaneously a living Infested organism, an Orokin ruin, and a fragile ecosystem maintained by a family in permanent psychological crisis. This context enriches every subsequent bounty and interaction with the Entrati syndicate.

## Tips

- Bring a Warframe with survivability on the open-world stages. The Cambion Drift's hazards include Infested enemies, Vome and Fass environmental hazards, and roaming Necramechs.
- Fass Residue collection is required in Stage 3. The glowing golden fluid pools appear on the Cambion Drift surface during the Fass cycle (daytime equivalent). If arriving during a Vome cycle, wait for the next cycle transition.
- The Requiem Cipher puzzle in Stage 4 involves matching Orokin symbols in a specific order. Take a moment to observe the pattern before interacting to avoid resetting the attempt.
- During the Necramech combat stage, the Voidrig's Arquebex heavy weapon deals high damage to the enemy Necramech's weak points. Use it when charged rather than saving it.
