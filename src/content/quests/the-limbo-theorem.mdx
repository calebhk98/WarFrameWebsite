## Overview

The Limbo Theorem is a side quest centered on recovering the scattered technical legacy of the Limbo Warframe. Limbo is a reality-bending Warframe capable of existing simultaneously in the material plane and the Rift -- a parallel dimension that renders occupants immune to physical damage while also cutting them off from certain interactions. The quest's premise is that Limbo's original theoretical framework, the "Limbo Theorem," exists as fragmentary data scattered across star chart nodes.

The quest was introduced in Update 15.0 in October 2014 and is non-replayable. It awards the three component blueprints needed to build Limbo in the Foundry, making it the primary acquisition route for the Warframe. The quest requires Archwing access for its first stage and completion of the Jupiter-to-Europa Junction, which gates the final stage's location.

The Limbo Theorem follows a pattern of Excavation missions interspersed with crafting steps. Players collect Proof Fragments as a recurring currency, use them to craft Theorem Keys that unlock subsequent stages, and ultimately assemble all three component blueprints.

## Prerequisites

Two prerequisites must be satisfied before The Limbo Theorem can begin. The Archwing quest must be completed, since the first stage takes place in an Archwing Interception mission format. Without Archwing, the opening mission is inaccessible.

The Jupiter-to-Europa Junction must also be completed to unlock the final quest stage on Europa. The Junction requires clearing specific objectives on Jupiter, including mission completions and challenge conditions. Players who have progressed to Europa through normal star chart advancement will already meet this requirement.

The WFCD description for the quest key notes explicitly: "The Limbo Theorem quest cannot be completed without an Archwing." This warning applies to anyone attempting to start the quest before The Archwing quest is finished.

## Stages

**Nonsense** opens on Mars at the Syrtis node in an Archwing Interception format. The mission requires defending aerial interception points against incoming Corpus or Grineer units while using the Archwing's flight capabilities. During the mission, enemies drop Proof Fragments -- small collectibles that resemble energy orbs. Collecting at least one Proof Fragment and returning to the Orbiter allows the player to craft the Limbo Neuroptics Theorem Key.

**Rift Plane** moves to Cambria on Earth for an Excavation mission. Excavation requires defending mobile drills as they extract Cryotic from the ground; each drill cycle yields 100 Cryotic. The stage requires accumulating 300 Cryotic before extracting. Completing the stage awards the Limbo Neuroptics Blueprint. Another Proof Fragment is needed here to craft the Limbo Systems Theorem Key.

**Magician of Math** returns to Mars, this time to Martialis, for another Excavation mission. The same Cryotic-gathering loop applies. Completing the stage awards the Limbo Systems Blueprint. A third Proof Fragment allows crafting of the Limbo Chassis Theorem Key.

**Final Jump** concludes the quest at Ose on Europa in a final Excavation mission. This stage awards the Limbo Chassis Blueprint. With all three component blueprints in hand, players can begin crafting the complete Limbo Warframe in the Foundry.

## Rewards

The three component blueprints -- Limbo Neuroptics, Limbo Systems, and Limbo Chassis -- are the quest's direct rewards. Together with the Limbo main blueprint, available for purchase from the in-game Market, these components allow the full construction of Limbo in the Foundry.

No additional rewards are granted beyond the blueprints. The quest does not unlock a new mission node or syndicate, making the blueprints the sole mechanical incentive.

## Lore

Ordis narrates The Limbo Theorem through the lens of a Cephalon confronting disturbing historical data. The Limbo Theorem documents a catastrophic experiment -- Limbo, apparently a Warframe with an unusually theoretical approach to combat, attempted to cross the boundary between the material world and the Rift permanently. The attempt ended badly. The fragmented state of the Theorem suggests either deliberate obfuscation or the physical destruction of Limbo himself in the incident.

Ordis's response is characteristic: he processes the information, judges it unsettling, and urges the Tenno to be more careful with the Warframe than its original operator was. The quest treats Limbo as a figure defined by reckless intellectual ambition -- someone who understood the Rift deeply enough to build a Warframe around its principles, but not deeply enough to survive his final experiment.

The Rift itself, as both a mechanic and a narrative element, is presented as genuinely dangerous rather than simply a tactical tool. Limbo's design externalizes this danger as an ability set: the same powers that make Limbo valuable in specific situations are the powers that destroyed him if the Theorem's warnings are accurate.

Proof Fragments -- the currency that gates each stage -- are presented as surviving pieces of Limbo's calculations. Physically collecting them during missions implies that the Theorem was encoded in material form and scattered rather than stored in a Cephalon archive, which raises unanswered questions about why Limbo wanted his work dispersed rather than preserved.

## Tips

- Proof Fragments spawn as collectibles during the designated missions. They appear as small glowing items dropped by enemies or found in containers. The required count per stage is low (one fragment per key), so focused looting rarely takes long.
- Excavation missions benefit from a team composition that includes a Warframe capable of defending drills independently, such as Frost (Snow Globe) or Limbo himself (Rift Shelter -- ironic but functional). Solo players should prioritize mobile defense setups.
- The Limbo Chassis Theorem Key requires Argon Crystals. As with all Argon Crystal crafting, plan the build to minimize decay time: run Void missions, collect the crystals, and craft immediately.
- After completing the quest, the Limbo main blueprint can be purchased from the Market. Limbo Prime, the Prime variant, provides improved base stats and is sourced through Void Relics.
