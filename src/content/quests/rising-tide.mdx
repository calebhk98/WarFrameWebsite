## Overview

Rising Tide is the quest that introduces the Railjack -- Warframe's large-scale space combat system -- by tasking players with reconstructing one from scattered components across the Origin System. The quest was released in Update 27.0 (December 2019) alongside the Empyrean update, which added Railjack space combat as a fully realized game mode. Rising Tide functions as the mandatory gate for participating in any Railjack mission.

The narrative is guided by Cephalon Cy, an Orokin-era artificial intelligence originally designed to serve as the tactical mind of a Railjack crew. Cy survived his ship's destruction during the Old War at the cost of his crew's lives -- an event that left him with what the quest frames as a form of post-traumatic stress, expressed through terse, militaristic communication and an unwillingness to dwell on the past. His reactivation begins with the Dry Dock installation and continues across every repair phase as he processes the implications of returning to service.

The quest alternates between field missions to recover components and Dry Dock repair phases where gathered resources are spent to rebuild each piece. It is one of the longer quests in terms of total mission count, spanning six components each requiring both a recovery mission and a repair sequence. The field missions follow a Defense-adjacent structure, requiring players to hold a scanning position against waves of enemies. All recovery missions can now be completed with a squad following updates that removed the original solo requirement.

Players who do not wish to complete the quest can bypass it entirely by purchasing a completed Railjack directly from the in-game Market for Platinum, though doing so forfeits the narrative experience with Cephalon Cy.

## Prerequisites

Rising Tide requires two specific prerequisites:

- **Complete The Second Dream** -- The quest is gated behind [The Second Dream](/quests/the-second-dream), a requirement that ensures players have encountered the Sentient threat and Operator gameplay before engaging with content that prominently features Sentient enemies. The connection between The Second Dream and Rising Tide is thematic as well as mechanical: both deal with the consequences of the Old War and the Tenno's role in what comes next.
- **Access to a Dry Dock** -- The Dry Dock is a specialized Dojo room where the Railjack is housed and repaired. Players who belong to a Clan can use their Clan's Dry Dock if one has been built. Players without a Clan or whose Clan lacks a Dry Dock can access a Dry Dock through Tier 2 and Tier 3 Tenno Relays. A personal Dry Dock segment added in later updates also allows solo players to maintain a private one.

The quest key is obtained from the Codex upon meeting these prerequisites. No crafting is required to begin the quest itself.

## Stages

Rising Tide alternates between field recovery missions and Dry Dock repair phases across fourteen stages, building the Railjack component by component.

**1. Install Cephalon Cy**

The quest begins in the Dry Dock. Players install a Cephalon data module into the central terminal, activating Cephalon Cy. His first transmissions are clipped and guarded -- he acknowledges the situation and provides instructions without elaborating on his history or how long he has been dormant. The introduction establishes him as a contrast to the Lotus's warmer communication style: Cy speaks in operational language, focused on objectives and resources.

**2-13. Component recovery and repair cycles**

Six components must be recovered and repaired in sequence: Fuselage (Mars), Propulsion System (Earth), Port Nacelle (Lua), Starboard Nacelle (Venus), Tail Section (Sedna), and Engine Cowling (Europa). Each component follows the same two-phase structure.

The recovery missions require players to deploy to a star chart node and defend a scanning beacon while Cy's sensors locate the component's position. Enemy waves attack the beacon for a set duration, and defending it successfully triggers the component's extraction. The enemies encountered vary by planet: Grineer forces on Mars, Earth, and Sedna; Corpus forces on Venus and Europa; Sentient presence on Lua and appearing alongside Grineer and Corpus forces at later stages.

Sentient enemies encountered during the Lua and later missions reflect the narrative context -- Sentients are drawn to the Railjack's components because Old War technology resonates with them. Operator Void abilities remain effective against Sentients throughout these encounters.

After each recovery mission, players return to the Dry Dock and spend gathered resources to repair the component. Repair times range from one to several minutes of real time per component, with the time requirement reducing if players have Rush Drones or equivalent accelerants. The repair phases are not passive waiting periods -- Cy uses them to communicate, providing fragments of the Old War history attached to the components and offering indirect glimpses into what the Railjack's original crew experienced.

**14. Integrate Cephalon Cy**

<Callout type="warn">The following contains narrative details about Cephalon Cy's history and the emotional resolution of Rising Tide.</Callout>

The final stage requires no additional combat. With all six components repaired and assembled, Cy undergoes full integration into the Railjack's command systems. The integration process is the emotional center of the quest: Cy, connecting fully to the ship, must process the memories encoded in the components he has been repairing. Those memories include the circumstances of his original crew's deaths.

Cy's transmissions during this phase drop some of the operational clipped-speech patterns he has maintained throughout the quest. He does not dramatize the revelation, but the weight of it surfaces in the cadence of what he says and what he does not say. When the integration completes, he confirms operational status with characteristic brevity, but the exchange registers as something he has earned rather than simply performed.

The Railjack is ready.

## Rewards

Rising Tide grants one primary reward:

- **Fully operational Railjack** -- The Railjack is the spacecraft that players use for all Empyrean-mode missions. Equipped with forward artillery, mounted turrets, and the capacity for boarding enemy vessels, it functions as both a mobile combat platform and a navigational hub for the Railjack mission type. Cephalon Cy's integration means the ship is continuously operable, with Cy providing tactical information during missions and managing ship systems between runs.

The Railjack itself is not static at completion. Players can upgrade its components (reactors, shields, engines, weapons) through Railjack-specific progression systems, improving its combat effectiveness over time. Rising Tide provides the foundation; subsequent Railjack content builds on it.

## Lore

Cephalon Cy's origin places him in the Old War period, when the Orokin deployed Railjack vessels as part of their military response to the Sentient uprising. A Railjack crew was a self-contained combat unit: the Cephalon provided tactical coordination and navigation while the crew handled flight and direct combat. Cy's original assignment ended when his Railjack was destroyed, the crew killed in a battle he survived in the ship's data systems while they did not.

The nature of his survival -- present for the deaths of everyone he was integrated with, unable to act, carrying the record of the event -- informs everything about how he interacts with the Tenno during Rising Tide. His terse, operational communication style is not coldness; it is controlled management of something he has been maintaining alone for a very long time. The repair process forces him to revisit encoded memories he has likely been suppressing: each component of the ship carries residual data from its previous installation aboard the original vessel.

The Sentient presence during the later recovery missions connects Rising Tide to the larger narrative arc. The Sentients' interest in Old War technology reflects their awareness that such vessels represent a renewed strategic threat -- the same threat that the Tenno are preparing to face in the events that follow the game's main story quests. Rising Tide is positioned not just as a functional prerequisite for Railjack gameplay but as the beginning of a chapter in which the Tenno must operate at a larger scale than individual star chart nodes allow.

## Tips

- The recovery missions are now squad-enabled. Bringing two or three squadmates significantly reduces the difficulty of defending the scanning beacon, particularly on later stages where Sentient enemies appear.
- Repair times can be reduced with Rush Drones from the Market. If completing Rising Tide on a time-sensitive schedule, stockpiling a few Rush Drones before starting reduces the total clock time considerably.
- Cephalon Cy's dialogue during the Dry Dock repair phases is missable if players leave the Dojo before each repair completes. Staying in the Dry Dock to hear his transmissions provides the full emotional context of the quest.
- After completing Rising Tide, the Railjack can be upgraded progressively. Early Railjack missions are completable with the base components from the quest; survivability and efficiency improve substantially as Intrinsics and component upgrades accumulate.
- Players who complete Rising Tide alone can still invite others to their Railjack for Empyrean missions. The completed Railjack functions as a shared platform for squad play even if built by a single player.
