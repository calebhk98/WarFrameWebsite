## Overview

The Archwing is a mid-game quest that unlocks the Archwing system -- the propulsion-and-weapon harness enabling open-space and underwater movement in Warframe. The quest is required to access any Archwing mission type, including Railjack's Empyrean mode, making it a mandatory progression step for players pursuing the full scope of the game's content. Completion grants the Odonata Archwing, the Imperator archgun, the Veritux arch-melee weapon, and the Plexus, which is the modding interface for Railjack gear.

The quest is structured as a build-and-deploy narrative: the Lotus identifies the Balor Fomorian, a Grineer capital ship, as an imminent threat and tasks the player with recovering Orokin blueprints for an ancient space-flight harness before the fleet reaches populated space. Each stage of the quest advances either the lore around the threat or the collection of components needed to build the Odonata.

The Archwing quest is notable in Warframe's design history as the introduction of the game's third movement mode. Ground-based movement, K-Drive/Yareli aquatic movement, and Archwing open-space flight each represent distinct locomotion systems. The quest's final stage, the Balor Fomorian encounter, is the player's first experience operating a fully assembled Archwing in open vacuum -- an environment with six-axis movement, no ground to stand on, and weapons calibrated for large-scale space combat.

Build times for Odonata components were significantly reduced in post-launch updates. The Wings, Harness, and Systems now each build in one minute, and the main Odonata blueprint builds in one minute as well. This change removed a multi-day wait that previously existed between completing the final Excavation stage and being able to deploy in the Balor Fomorian mission. Players can now complete the entire quest in a single session once all blueprints are obtained.

The quest also introduces the Plexus, which is the modding interface for Railjack equipment. While the Plexus becomes relevant primarily during Empyrean missions rather than solo Archwing play, receiving it at quest completion means players can immediately begin modding it in preparation for Railjack content.

## Prerequisites

The Archwing becomes available after completing the Mars Junction. The Mars Junction requires completing several Mars missions including the Specter challenge, which involves defeating a copy of the player's loadout. By the time Mars Junction is cleared, players are typically equipped to handle the quest's enemy levels.

There is no explicit Mastery Rank requirement for The Archwing quest beyond what is implied by reaching the Mars Junction through normal star chart progression.

## Stages

The Archwing consists of five stages covering Sabotage, Excavation, Exterminate, and finally a dedicated Archwing space mission.

**1. The Time is Now**

Upon activating the quest in the Codex, the Lotus transmits the main Odonata blueprint to the player's Foundry. This stage serves as the narrative setup: the Grineer Balor Fomorian has been detected on an intercept course, and the Lotus explains that an ancient Orokin archwing design was recovered and can be reconstructed if the component parts are gathered. The stage is informational and requires no mission.

**2. The Archive**

The first mission sends the player to Tessera, Venus, a level 10-12 Orokin Sabotage node on a Corpus tileset. The objective is to retrieve an Orokin Archive containing information vital to the Archwing project. The mission involves defending a generator to power a door mechanism, then navigating a Void portal. Up to three Orokin caches can be found in the tileset for bonus rewards, though finding all three is optional for quest progression.

**3. Excavation for Odonata Components**

Three Excavation missions must be completed to obtain the Odonata's component blueprints. Each mission drops one blueprint upon completion:

- **Aphrodite, Venus** (level 10-12, Corpus) -- Odonata Wings blueprint
- **Mantle, Earth** (level 11-13, Grineer) -- Odonata Harness blueprint
- **E Gate, Venus** (level 12-14, Corpus) -- Odonata Systems blueprint

Excavation missions require powering Excavators using Power Cells dropped by Excavation Carrier enemies. Each Excavator takes 100 seconds to complete its dig cycle. Only the first Excavator needs to complete its cycle to satisfy the quest objective and trigger the blueprint reward; additional Excavators provide bonus resources and relics but are not required for progression.

Once all three component blueprints are collected, they must be built in the Foundry along with the main Odonata blueprint before the next stage unlocks. With current one-minute build times, this is not a meaningful delay.

**4. Intel Espionage**

Before the final space deployment, the player completes one additional ground mission at Montes, Venus -- a level 12-14 Exterminate on a mixed Corpus/Grineer tileset. The objective is to eliminate all enemies within the mission timer before extraction becomes unavailable. This stage serves as a final intelligence-gathering step in the narrative and a combat warm-up before the Archwing encounter.

**5. Balor Fomorian**

The final stage is the first Archwing mission in the quest and the player's first Archwing experience in open space. The Odonata deploys automatically via a brief cutscene. The mission objective involves evading the Balor Fomorian capital ship's defensive weapons while destroying Zeplen units -- Grineer fighter craft -- and reaching the extraction marker. The Balor Fomorian's main weapons fire in predictable patterns; maintaining movement and avoiding direct lines of fire is the primary survival skill. The stage ends with the player extracting, and the Fomorian is repelled rather than destroyed, establishing it as a recurring world-state threat.

## Rewards

Completing The Archwing grants all items needed for initial space combat:

- **Odonata Archwing** -- the first and most versatile Archwing, usable in all Archwing mission types. Odonata's abilities include an energy shield, a burst attack, and a wing damage boost. It remains useful for newer players and in specific mission contexts.
- **Imperator archgun** -- a heavy machine gun for Archwing loadouts. Effective at close-to-mid range against most Archwing enemies and Railjack fighters.
- **Veritux arch-melee** -- a large greatsword-style melee weapon designed for Archwing use. Delivers constant heavy damage in close quarters and performs well in zero-gravity melee engagements.
- **Plexus** -- the modding interface for Railjack gear. The Plexus holds Railjack-specific mods affecting ship combat, repair, and battle avionic abilities. It is equipped in the Archwing slot in the Arsenal.

If Odonata, the Imperator, or the Veritux are sold after the quest is completed, replacement blueprints can be obtained from Cephalon Simaris's Offerings.

## Lore

The Archwing quest establishes several narrative foundations important to later content. The Orokin's military technology extended to individual space combat, and archwings were part of that legacy rather than a Tenno invention. The Lotus's urgency in the quest's opening transmissions frames the Fomorian threat as existential, which connects to the recurring world-state mechanic where Fomorian capital ships appear periodically in the game's live event system and must be destroyed by coordinated player action using Archwing.

<Callout type="warn">Spoilers for the broader Archwing and Empyrean lore context follow.</Callout>

The Orokin Archive retrieved in Stage 2 contains records from the Old War, the historical conflict between the Orokin and the Sentients. Archwings were used in that war for rapid deployment in space environments where Warframes alone could not reach. The quest positions the current Balor Fomorian threat as an echo of that conflict: Grineer capital ships deploying tactics derived from their Orokin-era predecessors, and the Tenno countering with recovered Orokin technology. The Plexus awarded at completion connects to the Railjack storyline in which the player eventually recovers and operates an entire Orokin warship, extending the individual-scale Archwing concept to fleet-scale combat.

## Tips

- In the Excavation stages, prioritize protecting Excavators over killing enemies. Excavators at low health are harder to replace than enemies are to defeat, and a destroyed Excavator requires a new Power Cell and full 100-second cycle.
- Each Excavation stage requires only the first successful Excavator completion. Extracting as soon as the blueprint is awarded is valid; extended runs yield additional resources but are not required for quest progression.
- During the Balor Fomorian stage, the Veritux is not recommended for this encounter -- the Fomorian's Zeplen fighters are best engaged at range with the Imperator. The arch-melee weapon is more effective in later Archwing missions where close-range combat is viable.
- The Fomorian's direct-fire weapon deals severe damage in a narrow cone directly ahead of the ship. Moving laterally relative to the Fomorian's orientation avoids the most dangerous fire patterns.
- After completing the quest, the Archwing Launcher item must be crafted and equipped in the gear wheel to deploy an Archwing during ground missions that include Archwing segments. The Launcher blueprint is available from the Market for credits and its components drop from Sabotage missions.
