## Overview

Veilbreaker is a solo quest released in Update 32 that serves as a direct sequel to [The New War](/quests/the-new-war). The quest places the player in control of Kahl-175, a Grineer Lancer who has broken free from Narmer's Veil conditioning. Unlike most Warframe quests, Veilbreaker tasks players with playing as a fully human soldier rather than a Warframe or Operator. Kahl fights with a Grakata and can acquire a Hellion jetpack mid-mission, offering a grounded, infantry-focused experience that contrasts sharply with the supernatural power of the Warframes.

The central narrative follows Kahl's effort to rescue his surviving squad members from Narmer-controlled Corpus and Grineer forces. Daughter Entrati, stationed on the Necraloid syndicate's listening post, detects Kahl's distress beacon and coordinates with the Tenno to support him. On completion, the quest unlocks Kahl's Garrison -- a new syndicate-like structure that provides ongoing weekly missions called Break Narmer, the Archon Hunt activity, and access to unique rewards including components for the Styanax Warframe.

The quest is not replayable through the Codex but can be reviewed in cinematic form.

## Prerequisites

Veilbreaker requires completion of [The New War](/quests/the-new-war), the game's central cinematic questline. Because The New War itself has a long prerequisite chain stretching back through much of the Warframe narrative, Veilbreaker is effectively a late-game quest accessible only to players who have progressed through the majority of the main story.

No Mastery Rank requirement is imposed beyond what The New War demands. The quest is locked to Solo mode to preserve the cinematic pacing.

## Stages

Veilbreaker takes place entirely aboard a Narmer-controlled Murex starship hovering in Deimos orbit. The entire mission is played as Kahl-175 rather than a Warframe, and the design reflects this: Kahl has auto-regenerating health, infinite Grakata ammunition, and no access to Warframe abilities. The Command Mode mechanic -- accessed by default on the G key -- allows Kahl to issue simple orders to any Grineer or Corpus allies he frees during the mission.

### Investigate the Murex

The quest begins with Daughter Entrati intercepting Kahl-175's distress signal after he breaks free from a Medusa Hub, a Narmer device that projects the Veil conditioning field over the ship. The first objective is to disable a new Medusa Hub by shooting nine floating energy nodes in a specific sequence before a sixty-second timer expires. Missing the sequence resets the nodes. This puzzle is the most mechanically demanding segment of the quest for new players, as it requires target identification under pressure.

Once the hub is destroyed, the player transitions fully into Kahl's perspective and the rescue operation begins.

### Free Kahl's Brothers

The majority of the quest involves moving through the Murex's interior, locating trapped Grineer and Corpus soldiers held captive under Narmer control, and liberating them. Liberation requires players to stand inside dissipation fields -- slow, channeled interactions that drain a gauge while Sentient enemies attempt to interrupt the process.

After freeing the first wave of allies, Command Mode becomes available. The interface allows Kahl to direct freed soldiers to targets, specific locations, or downed allies needing revival. Freed units cannot die permanently; if defeated, they simply regroup. Mid-mission, Kahl can acquire a Hellion jetpack from a downed enemy, dramatically increasing mobility in the Murex's vertical interior. Toward the final segments, a Tusk Bolkor becomes pilotable, providing the Grattler heavy weapon and greater firepower for clearing Sentient clusters.

During the escape sequence, Kahl and his freed squad move toward the Murex's hangar to board the Tenno's Railjack. Before the escape completes, Kahl glimpses an entity bearing Erra's body but displaying the behavior and voice of a different consciousness -- the Archon Pazuul, who has apparently subsumed Erra as a host. This brief scene establishes Pazuul as the primary antagonist for the Veilbreaker campaign and the Archon Hunt system.

### Return to the Drifter Camp

After the Murex escape, Kahl takes shelter at the Drifter Camp. He describes Pazuul and insists that the mission is not complete -- other brothers remain under Narmer's control. This stage is primarily narrative but establishes Kahl's motivation and confirms the ongoing campaign structure.

### Talk to Kahl

The quest concludes with a conversation confirming the alliance between Kahl-175, the Tenno, and Daughter Entrati. Kahl's Garrison opens as a functional syndicate, and the Break Narmer weekly missions become available on a rotating schedule.

## Rewards

Completing Veilbreaker grants access to several persistent systems:

- **Archon Hunt** -- a weekly activity replacing Sorties in depth, offering Archon Shards as rewards for slotting into Warframe ability augment sockets.
- **Kahl's Garrison** -- a syndicate providing Styanax Warframe component blueprints, Grineer-themed weapons, Archon mod variants, and cosmetic items earned through Break Narmer missions.
- **Break Narmer missions** -- weekly missions that expand Kahl's story and provide Standing for Kahl's Garrison.
- **Helminth Archon Shard Segment Blueprint** -- allows the Helminth system to consume Archon Shards as an infusion material.

Kahl's Garrison standing is earned by completing Break Narmer missions and spending earned Stock currency at Kahl's personal shop.

## Lore

<Callout type="warn">Spoilers for Veilbreaker and The New War follow.</Callout>

The Narmer Veil technology, introduced in The New War, allowed the Orokin-aligned Sentient Erra to brainwash Corpus and Grineer soldiers by suppressing their autonomy and forcing servile compliance. Kahl-175 -- a recurring background character in several prior Warframe quests including Predasite containment operations and Lavos-adjacent Deimos briefings -- is established as a soldier of unusual resilience and loyalty to his brothers rather than to the typical Grineer command hierarchy.

When the Veil breaks down on the Murex where Kahl is held, he exploits the gap to fight back. His liberation mission is framed not as heroism in a traditional sense but as an act of stubborn fraternal loyalty. Kahl repeatedly emphasizes that he fights for his brothers rather than for the Tenno or any grand ideological cause.

The revelation that Pazuul has taken Erra's body as a vessel expands the post-New War narrative. Erra, previously depicted as a Sentient general corrupted by grief, appears to have become a shell occupied by a more calculating Archon intelligence. The Archon Hunt system frames each weekly boss encounter as a confrontation with one of Pazuul's Archon lieutenants, extending the Veilbreaker storyline across ongoing play.

## Tips

- Study the Medusa Hub node pattern before the sixty-second timer starts; the nodes appear in a visible spatial arrangement and the sequence follows a logical path around the hub.
- Command Mode is most useful for directing allies to detonation charges; ordering two or three soldiers to a charge simultaneously speeds up the activation.
- The Hellion jetpack dramatically improves mobility and should be picked up as soon as it becomes available; vertical traversal in the Murex is otherwise slow.
- The Tusk Bolkor's Grattler deals heavy area damage and is especially effective against clustered Sentient squads in the final approach to the hangar.
- After completing the quest, prioritize Break Narmer missions weekly to accumulate Kahl's Garrison Standing, as Styanax component blueprints require reaching a mid-tier rank.
