## Overview

Mutalist Alad V Assassinate is a repeatable assassination mission on Eris that serves as the primary source for Mesa Warframe component blueprints. Introduced in Update 15.5 (November 2014), the mission requires players to possess a Mutalist Alad V Assassinate Key, which is consumed on mission entry (only the host's key is used in a squad). The encounter takes place on the Infested Ship tileset, a uniquely organic and hostile environment covered in Infested growth.

This mission differs from traditional quests in that it is not a one-time narrative experience. After completing [Patient Zero](/quests/patient-zero) to unlock access, players return to fight Mutalist Alad V as many times as needed to collect all three Mesa blueprint components. The boss fight itself tests players on dealing with an Infested enemy that combines Corpus technology with biological mutation.

The mission represents the culmination of the Infestation storyline that begins with [Once Awake](/quests/once-awake) and escalates through Patient Zero. Fighting Mutalist Alad V closes that arc by confronting the source of the Infested plague head-on.

## Prerequisites

To access the Mutalist Alad V Assassinate mission, players must meet these requirements:

- **Complete Patient Zero** -- This quest concludes with the Mutalist Alad V Assassinate Key Blueprint as its reward. Patient Zero itself requires Once Awake completion and access to Eris via the Pluto-Eris Junction.
- **Craft or obtain an Assassinate Key** -- The Blueprint obtained from Patient Zero must be taken to the Foundry and crafted into the actual key. The key blueprint is repeatable and can also be traded between players. Resource requirements for crafting the key are standard mid-game materials found on Eris.
- **Access to Eris** -- Eris is unlocked through the Pluto Junction, which requires progressing the star chart to Pluto first. Players arriving at this point in the main quest chain will have Eris accessible.

Each run into the Mutalist Alad V arena consumes one key from the host's inventory. Squad members who are not the host do not lose a key upon entry. Coordinating with other players to share key usage is a common and accepted approach to farming efficiently.

## Stages

The Mutalist Alad V Assassinate mission follows a straightforward structure typical of assassination nodes, though the unusual key requirement sets it apart from standard star chart navigation.

**1. Enter the Infested Ship**

Players activate the mission by selecting the Assassinate Key from their Gear wheel or from the Codex if it is set as the active quest. The mission takes place on Eris. The Infested Ship tileset is one of the most atmospheric environments in the game -- corridors coated in biological growth, ambient creature sounds, and irregular lighting create a distinctly oppressive mood compared to Grineer or Corpus levels.

Upon entering, players must navigate through Infested-controlled corridors toward the boss chamber. Standard Infested units, including Runners, Crawlers, and Ancients, populate the approach. The Infested at this level range between 30 and 35 on the standard difficulty setting (130 to 135 on Steel Path), so players should bring appropriate damage output and survivability.

**2. Eliminate Mutalist Alad V**

The boss chamber confrontation with Mutalist Alad V is the mission's central challenge. Mutalist Alad V is not the clean, calculating Corpus executive players may have encountered earlier in the game. He has been thoroughly transformed by the Infestation, his body fused with biological mass and his behavior reflecting the Infested collective's aggression alongside fragmented echoes of his former personality.

<Callout type="warn">The following contains details about the boss fight mechanics and the story context for Mutalist Alad V's transformed state.</Callout>

Mutalist Alad V attacks with a combination of Infested biological projectiles, a debilitating aura that drains Warframe energy and applies a "radiation" status-like confusion effect on nearby players, and an ability that temporarily possesses a squadmate if not countered quickly. His possession mechanic targets one player at a time and forces the possessed Warframe to attack allies, making it the most disruptive element of the encounter.

Countering the possession requires the other squadmates to damage the possessed player enough to break the effect without killing them, or to use abilities that cleanse or interrupt the effect. Warframes with crowd control or defensive abilities can reduce the windows in which possession is dangerous.

Corrosive or Viral damage is effective against Mutalist Alad V's Infested flesh, though the encounter is survivable with most damage types at appropriate power levels. Players running this mission for component blueprints typically optimize for kill speed rather than survivability, since the drop table is the primary objective.

## Rewards

Mutalist Alad V drops one Mesa component blueprint per kill, drawn from his dedicated drop table. The drop rates as documented by the community and WFCD data are:

- **Mesa Chassis Blueprint** -- drops at approximately 38.72% chance
- **Mesa Neuroptics Blueprint** -- drops at approximately 38.72% chance
- **Mesa Systems Blueprint** -- drops at approximately 22.56% chance

Players must collect all three component blueprints plus the Mesa Blueprint (available from the in-game Market) and then craft each component before assembling the final Warframe. The relatively low Systems Blueprint drop rate means most players will farm this mission multiple times before obtaining a complete set.

The mission completion itself provides a standard credit reward. No unique mission-exclusive item other than the Mesa blueprints makes this node notable for farming.

## Lore

Alad V's transformation into Mutalist Alad V is one of the more dramatic character arcs in the early Warframe story. Players who completed the main Corpus questline will have fought Alad V in a standard form during the Ties That Bind quest chain and during Vor's Prize progression. He was a scheming, self-interested Corpus merchant who had been conducting forbidden experiments on captured Warframes -- attempting to reverse-engineer Tenno technology for profit and status within the Corpus Board.

His downfall came from overconfidence. In his experiments, Alad V exposed himself to Infestation-contaminated material, and the Infestation claimed him. Patient Zero reveals that he did not simply fall victim to an accident -- he embraced the Infestation as an opportunity to build a Mutalist Empire, deliberately spreading the Infested plague across ships and stations in the Origin System and creating hybrid Infested Warframes to serve as weapons.

By the time players confront him on Eris, Alad V's transformation is complete. His dialogue in the boss arena retains traces of his former personality -- the theatrical self-promotion, the condescending tone toward the Tenno -- but filtered through an Infested collective consciousness. He refers to himself in terms of propagation and corruption rather than profit.

The Mutalist Alad V arc closes here, but Alad V himself returns in later content in an altered state. The Infestation is not the end of his story, though what he becomes afterward carries the weight of this transformation with it.

## Tips

- Bring a Warframe with reliable crowd control or high damage output to minimize the duration of possession attempts. Keeping Mutalist Alad V interrupted or staggered reduces the frequency of possession.
- Corrosive damage strips the Infested armor variants efficiently. Viral damage amplifies damage taken and is effective against flesh-type enemies.
- Coordinate with squadmates on who is hosting to cycle key consumption. The host's key is the only one consumed, so one player can host a run, then a different player hosts the next.
- Nekros with Despoil and Pilfering augment on Hydroid do not increase blueprint drops since boss blueprints use a fixed drop table, not loot multipliers. Save specialized farming frames for resource drops instead.
- The Infested Ship tileset includes environmental hazards such as spore bursts from wall growth. Staying mobile and avoiding standing in Infested pools prevents unnecessary status procs.
