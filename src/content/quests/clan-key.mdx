## Overview

The Clan Key is a foundational item that every new clan member must build before gaining entry to their clan's Dojo. While it is catalogued under the Quests category in the game's data, it functions less as a narrative mission and more as a crafting prerequisite -- a gatekeeping mechanic introduced in Update 8.0 (May 23, 2013) that ensures players invest a small amount of time and resources before accessing the social hub of their clan.

The Dojo itself is the heart of clan life in Warframe. It is a customizable, multi-room complex that hosts research laboratories, trading posts, dueling rooms, obstacle courses, and much more. None of that content is accessible until a player holds a valid Clan Key in their inventory. Because the key is personal and non-tradeable, every clan member must build their own copy individually.

Understanding the Clan Key process is one of the first organizational steps a new player takes after joining a group, making it an important early milestone on the road to unlocking the full breadth of Warframe's cooperative content.

## Prerequisites

Before the Clan Key blueprint becomes available, the following conditions must be met.

- **Clan membership** -- A player must receive and accept an invitation from an existing clan, or found a new clan through the Contacts panel in their Orbiter's Communication menu.
- **Foundry access** -- The Foundry segment must be installed in the Orbiter, which is unlocked during the early stages of the Vor's Prize introductory quest. Players who have completed Vor's Prize will always have the Foundry available.

No Mastery Rank requirement exists for the Clan Key itself, nor is any prior quest completion needed beyond having the Foundry segment. Any player who has passed through the initial tutorial content and joined a clan is eligible to build it.

Once a player joins a clan, the Clan Key blueprint appears in the Foundry automatically -- no purchase from the Market is necessary, and the blueprint is not listed there for sale. It is exclusively granted through clan membership.

## Stages

The process of obtaining the Clan Key involves four sequential actions rather than traditional story missions with waypoints. These steps take place entirely within the Orbiter and the Star Chart interface.

1. **Receive the blueprint** -- Upon joining a clan, check the Foundry. The Clan Key blueprint is delivered automatically to the player's inventory. If it does not appear immediately, waiting a short time or logging out and back in typically resolves the delay, which is a known minor bug noted in the wiki.

2. **Gather the required resources** -- The build calls for common early-game materials. None of the required components are rare, but a new player may need to run several missions on Earth or Mars to accumulate sufficient quantities of Polymer Bundle and Ferrite. Morphics are uncommon but drop reliably from enemies on Mars.

   | Resource       | Quantity required |
   |----------------|------------------:|
   | Credits        |             1,500 |
   | Morphics       |                 1 |
   | Polymer Bundle |               500 |
   | Ferrite        |               500 |

3. **Initiate construction** -- Open the Foundry, navigate to the Keys category, and start the Clan Key build. The construction timer is 12 hours. Players who do not wish to wait can spend 10 Platinum to rush the build to instant completion. There is no way to reduce the timer through mods or Warframe abilities.

4. **Enter the Dojo** -- Once the key is collected from the Foundry, the Clan Dojo becomes selectable from the Star Chart as a special destination. Selecting it and launching will transport the player into the Dojo's entrance hall. From that point on, the key passively enables Dojo access without any further interaction.

## Rewards

The direct reward for completing this process is the Clan Key item itself -- a permanent addition to the player's inventory that grants ongoing access to the Dojo. No experience, no credits, and no additional items are awarded by the completion event.

The real reward, however, is everything the Dojo unlocks:

- **Research laboratories** -- The Energy Lab, Chem Lab, Tenno Lab, Bio Lab, and Orokin Lab each contain weapon blueprints and mod research projects that cannot be obtained anywhere else. Contributing clan resources to these labs and waiting for research to complete yields some of the strongest weapons available before endgame content.
- **Trading Post** -- The Dojo's Trading Post allows players to exchange items, mods, platinum, and prime parts with other players in a direct peer-to-peer system. Trading is one of the primary methods for obtaining rare items and building a platinum reserve without spending real money.
- **Dueling rooms** -- Clan members with Architect rank can construct sparring rooms where players test builds against one another in friendly PvP. Dueling is separate from the Conclave game mode and carries no stakes.
- **Obstacle courses** -- Some Dojos include obstacle courses with leaderboards, offering a relaxed parkour challenge within a clan-controlled environment.

## Lore

The Clan Dojo carries no narrative quest dialogue. Unlike story quests such as [The Second Dream](/quests/the-second-dream) or [The War Within](/quests/the-war-within), the Clan Key process has no cutscenes, no voiced characters, and no plot advancement.

The lore of the Dojo is environmental rather than scripted. The architecture blends Tenno minimalism with Orokin grandeur -- corridors of pale stone, golden accents, and the recurring geometric motifs found throughout the Origin System's Orokin ruins. The space communicates the history of the Tenno as warriors shaped by an empire they ultimately destroyed, now repurposing its aesthetic for a home of their own choosing.

The Trading Post within the Dojo echoes the broader economy of the Origin System, where alliances form around material exchange as much as ideology. Clans that invest heavily in research and construction accumulate influence not unlike the syndicates, though their power is expressed through weapons and community rather than standing ranks and sigils.

For players new to the game, the Dojo represents a first step into Warframe's social layer. Clans vary enormously -- from small groups of friends running the same content together to large organizations coordinating Eidolon hunts, Railjack operations, and clan-tier events. The Clan Key is the token that grants entry to whichever version of that community a player chooses.

## Tips

- **Timing the build** -- Starting the 12-hour build before logging off for the night ensures the key is ready by the next session. There is no benefit to rushing it unless access to the Dojo is time-sensitive for a clan research project.
- **Resource farming** -- Ferrite and Polymer Bundle drop from enemies on Earth, Mars, and Mercury respectively. Both are common drops and most players accumulate the required amounts passively within a few missions. Morphics drop from mid-level Grineer and Corpus enemies; Mars is the standard recommendation for new players due to level-appropriate enemies.
- **Clan dissolution** -- If a player leaves a clan or the clan disbands, the Clan Key is destroyed automatically. Rejoining a clan or founding a new one provides a fresh blueprint. The new key must be built again from scratch.
- **Guest access** -- Players who have been invited to a Dojo but have not yet built their key can still enter if escorted by an existing member. This allows new recruits to tour the Dojo and see what research is underway before committing their resources to the build.
- **Multiple clans** -- Warframe allows a player to belong to only one clan at a time. The Clan Key corresponds to that single clan's Dojo. Leaving and joining another clan invalidates the old key and requires building a new one for the new Dojo.
