## Overview

Vox Solaris is the introductory quest for Fortuna and the Orb Vallis open world on Venus. Released as part of the Fortuna update, the quest establishes the Solaris United faction -- a community of Corpus-indentured workers whose bodies are repo'd by Nef Anyo when debts go unpaid. The player arrives at Fortuna and is swept into a resistance effort led by Eudico, a former agitator who has spent years suppressing her activist identity to protect her crew.

The quest requires completion of [Saya's Vigil](/quests/sayas-vigil) and access to Fortuna via the Venus Junction. It is replayable through the Codex. A notable update in Update 36.1 reduced enemy spawn counts in the Spy segment to ease the experience for newer players.

Unlike many quest chains, Vox Solaris is paced around a cast of named Solaris characters -- Eudico, The Business, Ticker, Zuud, Biz, Rude Zuud, and Little Duck -- each of whom represents a distinct personality within the Fortuna community. Their interactions give the quest more character depth than a typical mission-chain structure.

On completion, Vox Solaris unlocks the full Orb Vallis open world, the Solaris United syndicate, the K-Drive personal hoverboard system, and the blueprint for the Garuda Warframe.

## Prerequisites

Vox Solaris requires:

- Completion of [Saya's Vigil](/quests/sayas-vigil), which establishes the Cetus and Plains of Eidolon open world on Earth.
- Access to Venus via the Venus Junction on Earth.
- The quest must be started from within Fortuna itself by speaking with the NPC at the entrance.

No Mastery Rank requirement is specified beyond what the Venus Junction unlock implies for new players. The quest is solo-playable and set in a mix of Fortuna's indoor hub and the open Orb Vallis exterior.

## Stages

Vox Solaris moves through a series of Orb Vallis operations that escalate from intelligence gathering to open confrontation with Corpus forces. The early stages focus on stealth and rescue; the final stages involve defending key infrastructure and direct action against the Profit-Taker Orb spider mech -- though the Profit-Taker itself is not fought during the quest; it serves as an environmental threat the player must evade.

### Talk to Thursby -- Fortuna and Orb Vallis

The quest opens in Fortuna's main hub, where the player encounters Eudico posing as a non-threatening operations manager. After the initial conversation establishes the Solaris debt system, the first field objective sends the player into Orb Vallis to collect three supply caches scattered across the open world. These caches are part of a Corpus cargo drop that the Solaris workers have been monitoring.

After collecting the caches, the player returns to Fortuna and is pointed toward defending Eudico while she hacks a Corpus spy drone, requiring twenty enemy kills to suppress Corpus response teams. This stage introduces both the open-world traversal of the Vallis and the basic combat encounter structure the area uses for its bounties.

### Investigate the Repo Hit List

The second major operation shifts toward intelligence work. Eudico asks the player to infiltrate a Corpus installation and stealth-hack a terminal containing Nef Anyo's latest repo hit list -- the names of Solaris workers targeted for body repossession. The encounter is reduced in enemy density following Update 36.1 changes, but it still rewards players who use the Vallis terrain to approach without triggering alerts.

After recovering the hit list, the player investigates multiple sites in the Vallis where squad members from Eudico's network have been killed. The investigation reveals the scope of Corpus surveillance and the immediate danger facing the Solaris community.

### Rescue the Field Squad

The third stage tasks the player with locating and rescuing a captured Solaris field squad held at a Corpus holding site. This involves hacking a second terminal while defending against Corpus response forces. The squad's situation illustrates the personal stakes in Eudico's resistance: these are not abstract political prisoners but named workers whose families live in Fortuna.

After the rescue, Eudico's tone shifts. The community's faith in peaceful appeals to Nef Anyo collapses. The Business -- a conservation-focused Solaris who runs the Orb Vallis wildlife tracking program -- argues that direct action is now necessary.

### Defend the Excavator

Eudico's plan requires Amarast, a rare energy crystal, to power the operation's next phase. The player must protect a single excavator drilling in the Vallis while Corpus forces attack it. Excavation mechanics apply here: the drill needs power cells to continue, which drop from Corpus Power Carrier units the player must intercept.

Once the Amarast is retrieved, the player delivers it to a coolant tower. This first attempt fails; Nef Anyo detects the operation and uses the opportunity to issue a public threat against Fortuna.

### Detonate the K-Bomb

With Nef Anyo's Corpus forces mobilizing for an assault on Fortuna, the player must plant and detonate a kinetic bomb in the Vallis before the assault wave reaches the settlement. The detonation buys time. As wave defenses hold against Corpus attackers, Eudico reclaims her Vox Solaris identity in a public broadcast, openly defying Nef Anyo for the first time in years.

The K-Drive is introduced here as a quest-mandated traversal tool; riders must cover the distance across the Vallis at high speed to position the bomb in time. This segment functions as the player's introduction to K-Drive mechanics before the system opens fully in the Orb Vallis.

### Infiltrate the Coolant Tower

The final stage requires the player to ride a K-Drive through the Orb Vallis past the Profit-Taker Orb spider mech, which patrols the Vallis exterior as an unkillable environmental threat during the quest. The objective is to reach the coolant tower control terminal without being caught by the Profit-Taker's detection radius.

After hacking the terminal, the player defends the tower while Eudico initiates an overheating cascade. Delivering the Amarast completes the cascade, disabling Corpus coolant infrastructure and forcing Nef Anyo to publicly halt the repo operations against Fortuna to prevent catastrophic equipment loss. Eudico's Vox Solaris broadcast is heard across Fortuna, and the Solaris community celebrates a fragile but real victory.

## Rewards

Completing Vox Solaris grants:

- **K-Drive Launcher** -- enables K-Drive use in the Orb Vallis and Cambion Drift.
- **Garuda Warframe Blueprint** -- the main blueprint for Garuda, a blood-magic-themed Warframe whose components are farmed from Orb Vallis bounties.
- **Solaris United syndicate access** -- allows the player to earn Standing and rank up with the Fortuna factions, unlocking Fortuna-specific mods, weapons, and K-Drive components.
- **Full Orb Vallis access** -- all bounty tiers and world activities become available.
- **K-Drive feature** -- the personal hoverboard system is permanently unlocked for all open worlds that support it.

Garuda's component blueprints (Neuroptics, Chassis, Systems) drop from Orb Vallis bounties at various tiers and must be crafted separately. The quest provides the main blueprint only.

## Lore

The Solaris people are a working underclass created by the Corpus to maintain the thermal infrastructure on Venus. Their bodies are cybernetically augmented with heavy Corpus hardware. When a Solaris worker falls behind on debt payments -- debt that can span multiple generations due to the compound interest Corpus creditors apply -- Nef Anyo's repo agents arrive to physically repossess the hardware, leaving workers in reduced biological states until the debt is restructured.

Eudico was once the voice of the Solaris resistance, known by the call sign Vox Solaris. After a prior confrontation with Nef Anyo ended catastrophically, she accepted silence as the price of her crew's safety. The events of the quest force her to recalculate: Nef Anyo's repo operations have grown aggressive enough that silence offers no protection.

The Business, whose real backstory is expanded in later syndicate reputation dialogues, represents the intellectual conscience of the resistance. Little Duck, a young and intensely pragmatic Solaris operative, represents its tactical edge. Their different temperaments create tension that gives the quest's supporting cast unusual texture for a Warframe side questline.

The Profit-Taker Orb -- an enormous spider mech visible throughout the Vallis and encountered as an unkillable hazard during the quest -- becomes a farmable endgame boss after completing the Vox Solaris quest and reaching the highest rank with Solaris United's inner circle.

## Tips

- During the Spy segment, approach the terminal from the western side of the installation to minimize patrol contact.
- In the Excavation stage, prioritize killing Power Carrier enemies before they reach the drill; a carrier that delivers its cell extends the enemy's damage window on the excavator.
- The Profit-Taker Orb during the coolant tower infiltration follows a patrol loop; wait at cover near the tower entrance until the mech moves to the far side of its route before crossing open ground.
- Equip a fast K-Drive for the detonation segment; Boon in the Ventkids Clubhouse sells K-Drive components with higher top speed.
- After the quest, Eudico's daily briefing in Fortuna provides the fastest route to earning Solaris United Standing through bounty chains.
