## Overview

The Lotus Eaters is a short mainline quest that functions as a prologue to The Hex. It was released in Update 36.1 on August 21, 2024, and is a solo-only experience that is not replayable. The quest requires completion of Whispers in the Walls before it becomes accessible, placing it in the late-game narrative sequence that follows the Entrati family storyline established on Deimos.

Where Whispers in the Walls concluded with a confrontation involving the Man in the Wall's Vessel in the Sanctum Anatomica, The Lotus Eaters begins immediately in that aftermath. The Operator and the Drifter both appear at the threshold of a Void expanse, but only the Drifter is summoned forward. The experience is brief by quest standards, serving as a scene-setting prelude rather than a full mission chain.

The quest's title references the Homeric lotus-eaters, who consumed a plant that induced blissful forgetfulness and made them indifferent to returning home. The thematic resonance is with states of comfortable stasis and the disruption that comes from a call that cannot be ignored.

## Prerequisites

The Lotus Eaters requires completion of Whispers in the Walls, the mainline quest introduced with the Whispers in the Walls update. That quest takes place primarily in the Sanctum Anatomica on Deimos and involves confronting the Entrati family's connections to the Man in the Wall, the Void-dwelling entity that has shadowed the Tenno throughout the late-game narrative.

No Mastery Rank gate is listed for The Lotus Eaters, but the Whispers in the Walls prerequisite implies substantial star chart progression, including access to Deimos, completion of Heart of Deimos, and the extended Entrati questline.

## Stages

**Answer the Call** opens in the Sanctum Anatomica, the location where Whispers in the Walls reached its conclusion. The Lotus waits at the edge of a Void expanse, and nearby, a KinePage -- a communication device from the 1999 setting -- emits an insistent signal. Both the Operator and the Drifter are present, but the call is addressed specifically to the Drifter. This distinction between the two aspects of the player character is narratively significant: the Operator represents the Warframe-piloting side of the Tenno, while the Drifter is the identity forged through the Duviri experience.

Answering the KinePage transports the Drifter to an abandoned mall in what is revealed to be Hollvania in 1999. The setting is abrupt and disorienting -- the Void-mediated transition deposits the Drifter in a location that has clearly seen better days, rendered in the aesthetic of a city caught between its last prosperous moment and the chaos that followed.

**Follow Kalymos** centers on locating and following Albrecht Entrati's Kavat companion. Kalymos is a genetically engineered cat companion belonging to Entrati, and its presence in 1999 Hollvania establishes that the temporal connection predates the events of The Hex. Kalymos leads the Drifter through the mall's interior to another KinePage device, completing the loop. The Drifter then returns to the present, carrying the knowledge that a direct line exists between the Orbiter's timeline and the Hollvania of 1999.

The Lotus, standing vigil at the Void expanse throughout the quest, represents continuity and watchfulness. Loid is elsewhere completing preparations with Albrecht's Vessels, and the Drifter's brief excursion into the past is framed as a preparation step rather than a full intervention. The Lotus Eaters ends with all parties aware that the path to Hollvania is open -- the question of what will be done with that access is left for The Hex to answer.

The KinePage device reappears in the quest reward as "Arthur's KinePage," a decoration named for Arthur -- one of the six Hex members -- suggesting the specific line of contact the Drifter followed was Arthur's signal.

## Rewards

The sole reward for completing The Lotus Eaters is Arthur's KinePage, an Orbiter decoration. KinePages are communication devices used within the 1999 setting, and receiving Arthur's specifically suggests a personal connection that will be developed in the subsequent quest. The decoration can be placed within the Orbiter as a display item.

No gameplay unlocks or warframe blueprints are awarded. The quest's reward is deliberately minimal, reinforcing its nature as a transitional prelude rather than a standalone content piece.

## Lore

The Lotus Eaters operates within the late-game narrative web built across Heart of Deimos, The New War, The Duviri Paradox, and Whispers in the Walls. Each of these quests has examined a different aspect of the Tenno's dual identity -- the Operator who pilots Warframes and the Drifter who endured Duviri.

The quest's central gesture -- only the Drifter can answer the call -- is a significant narrative beat. The Operator-Drifter distinction has been treated throughout the post-New War content as philosophically rich: who is the "real" version of the player character, and what does each aspect represent? The Lotus Eaters asserts that the path to 1999 requires specifically the Drifter, the identity shaped by cyclical isolation and the search for meaning within repetition. The Duviri Paradox established the Drifter as capable of navigating and ultimately breaking temporal loops. That capacity is precisely what the 1999 storyline requires.

Albrecht Entrati, the Orokin scholar whose research underlies much of the late-game lore, appears only through his Kavat companion in this quest. Kalymos functions as a proxy, a familiar presence made strange by its displacement in time. The Kavat's ability to navigate between 1999 and the present -- or at least to serve as a guide for someone who can -- raises questions about the nature of the temporal connection and how Albrecht arranged for it.

The Void expanse in the Sanctum Anatomica, where the quest begins, was the site of the final confrontation in Whispers in the Walls. Returning to this location and finding the Lotus waiting there links the two quests tightly: the Void threat that concluded Whispers in the Walls opened a gap through which the signal from 1999 could be received.

## Tips

- The Lotus Eaters is short and does not require any specific loadout or preparation. It is primarily a cinematic quest with minimal combat.
- Complete the quest before attempting to access The Hex, as it is a hard prerequisite for the Hollvania storyline.
- Arthur's KinePage can be placed in the Orbiter using the decoration system. Finding the right placement in the Orbiter's layout rewards the time invested in the quest's narrative context.
- The quest's brevity is intentional. Approaching it as a standalone experience rather than a full quest will calibrate expectations appropriately.
