## Overview

The Sacrifice is a main-line cinematic quest released in Update 23.0 on June 15, 2018. It follows directly from the Apostasy Prologue and requires players to work alone throughout; the quest is solo-only from start to finish. The central goal is to locate and understand an unknown Warframe radiating Sentient energy, which leads the Operator on a journey through some of the most emotionally resonant content in the game.

The quest introduces Excalibur Umbra, a variant of Excalibur that differs from the standard model in both gameplay and narrative weight. Unlike blueprints obtained from bosses or the Market, Excalibur Umbra is granted directly upon completing The Sacrifice and arrives pre-built at Mastery Rank 30 with a full set of unique mods called Umbral Mods. These mods form a set that amplifies health, armor, and ability strength together when equipped as a trio, making Excalibur Umbra one of the most immediately powerful Warframes given to a player at the moment of acquisition.

The quest also continues the thread left by the Apostasy Prologue, where the Lotus departed with Ballas. Players who worked through the Natah, The Second Dream, and The War Within storylines will find The Sacrifice resolving many of the questions raised by those quests about the origin of Warframes and the nature of the Operator's bond with them.

<Callout type="warn">Spoilers for The Sacrifice and its preceding quests follow. The sections below describe major plot revelations.</Callout>

The Sacrifice reveals that Warframes are not machines. They are human beings who were infused with a Helminth parasite -- a Grineer biological weapon repurposed by the Orokin -- and subjected to a traumatic process that destroyed their original personality while preserving some fragment of memory or will. Excalibur Umbra, in particular, retains a burning grief tied to a specific act he was forced to perform, which the quest makes central to its story.

## Prerequisites

The Sacrifice requires completion of the Apostasy Prologue, a brief cinematic quest that unlocks after finishing The War Within. The Apostasy Prologue itself requires no additional prerequisites beyond what was needed for The War Within. Because The Sacrifice is a solo-only quest, players cannot bring squadmates to assist with combat encounters.

The quest is accessible at any Mastery Rank. Players who complete The Second Dream and The War Within will have the Operator combat tools necessary for the Umbra encounter sequences. An Amp capable of dealing meaningful damage helps in the final stage, though the quest's combat requirements are less demanding than those of The New War.

## Stages

The Sacrifice unfolds across six missions tied to the Operator's investigation of Sentient energy and Ballas's recorded confessions.

### Investigate Sentient Energy

The quest begins on Earth at the Lith node. The Operator discovers scattered remains of a Warframe in an ancient Orokin courtyard alongside recognizable objects: a helmet, a body, and a weapon identified as the Skiajati. Scanning these fragments triggers a sequence that identifies the Warframe as having a Sentient resonance unlike any normal Warframe.

### Explore Lua

The investigation continues at Pavlov on Lua, where Orokin Memory Ciphers are embedded in the architecture. Solving these ciphers requires using Operator Void powers to activate the Sekhara devices scattered through the tileset. Completing them unlocks the Vitruvian, a recorded device left by Ballas that contains confessions about his actions before and during the Old War. The Vitruvian is a central narrative device throughout the quest, revealing information in fragments.

### Search for Umbra

The Warframe, identified as Excalibur Umbra, has become active and is operating autonomously. Players pursue him to Ceres, where he must be subdued rather than destroyed. The encounter teaches the Operator to use Void Dash and Void Beam aggressively while managing the awareness that attacking Umbra is attacking something that retains a degree of consciousness. Accessing his memories via Transference reveals flashes of his human past.

### Pursue Umbra

On Neptune, the pursuit continues with a more extended combat sequence. Umbra's memories emerge more clearly in this stage, depicting a Dax soldier -- the warrior caste bound by complete loyalty to the Orokin -- and the crime Ballas forced him to commit. The Void Sling ability is used to close distance during the fight.

<Callout type="warn">The following stage description contains major plot details specific to Excalibur Umbra's backstory.</Callout>

The memories show Isaah, Umbra's son, and the act Ballas compelled before the Helminth transformation. This segment of the quest is deliberately confrontational in tone, using the Transference mechanic not just as a gameplay loop but as a narrative device for forcing the Operator to share in Umbra's experience.

### Confront Umbra

At Tycho on Lua, the Operator faces Umbra alone -- no Warframe -- as a direct test of Operator combat capability. The goal is not to kill but to reach the memory's end. Completing the memory and accepting the full Transference bond stops Umbra's violent state and grants the Operator control of him.

### Return to Earth

The final mission returns to Earth. Players control Excalibur Umbra for the first time and must fight through a Grineer-occupied environment. The stage culminates in a confrontation with Ballas, whose fate is determined by a brief sequence involving the Skiajati. The transformed Lotus -- her Sentient nature now visible -- appears at the conclusion.

## Rewards

The Sacrifice provides a substantial set of rewards upon completion.

- Excalibur Umbra arrives pre-built at rank 30, requiring no crafting time.
- The Skiajati Nikana is the Umbra-themed melee weapon encountered during the quest. It has a unique passive that makes the Operator invisible briefly when performing a finisher.
- The five Umbral Mods -- Umbral Intensify, Umbral Fiber, Umbral Vitality, Sacrificial Steel, and Sacrificial Pressure -- form a named set. Equipping multiple Umbral Mods together activates a stacking set bonus, making them significantly stronger than their individual values suggest.
- A Captura scene depicting Umbra's courtyard is included.
- Animation sets (Agile and Noble) for Excalibur Umbra are unlocked.

## Lore

<Callout type="warn">Major spoilers follow for The Sacrifice and the Warframe origin story.</Callout>

Ballas's Vitruvian recordings, played across the quest's stages, reveal the true origin of Warframes. The Orokin, facing the Sentient threat during the Old War, developed the Helminth strain -- an infective biological system that could transform human subjects. Ballas personally oversaw the process, selecting subjects for transformation based on criteria that remain partly unclear. His recordings carry a tone of justification and guilt in equal measure.

Excalibur Umbra was originally a Dax soldier -- one of the Orokin's elite warrior class, bound by Orokin will through a biological conditioning called Continuity. Dax soldiers could not resist Orokin commands regardless of the moral nature of those commands. Ballas exploited this by ordering Umbra to execute Isaah, his own son, as punishment for Umbra having witnessed Ballas's treachery. Immediately after, Ballas subjected Umbra to the Helminth transformation, ensuring that the act of killing Isaah would be the defining memory carried into Warframe form.

The result is a Warframe that never fully lost its human consciousness. Unlike other Warframes whose identities were erased by the transformation, Umbra retained enough selfhood to act autonomously when Transference-free. The Operator's choice to share Umbra's grief through an empathetic Transference -- rather than simply overwriting his will -- represents the moral core of the quest. The bond formed in this way differs from the standard Operator-Warframe relationship.

The Lotus's appearance at the end of the quest links The Sacrifice directly to the events that follow in the Chimera Prologue and eventually The New War.

## Tips

- Bring a fully upgraded Amp for the Operator-only stage at Tycho, Lua. The encounter requires sustained Void Beam fire against an armored target.
- Excalibur Umbra's passive allows him to act semi-autonomously when the Operator leaves his body, continuing to fight enemies. This passive is unique among Warframes.
- The Umbral Mod set bonus activates when two or more Umbral Mods are equipped on the same Warframe. Three Umbral Mods on Umbra specifically yields the strongest synergy.
- The Skiajati's stealth passive applies during normal gameplay, not just during the quest. Its invisibility on finisher can be useful for sustained engagement in Steel Path content.
