## Overview

Vor's Prize is the second quest in Warframe and the true tutorial sequence. It follows directly from [Awakening](/quests/awakening), the combat-only introduction that teaches basic movement and shooting. Where Awakening covers moment-to-moment combat, Vor's Prize introduces the systems that define the game's broader loop: the Orbiter, the Arsenal, the Foundry, the Mod system, and the Market.

The quest is set entirely on Earth and its orbit, placing the player in escalating conflict with Captain Vor -- a Grineer general who implanted an Ascaris control device in the Tenno's Warframe during their dormancy. Vor views the Tenno as divine instruments of the Orokin era and seeks to harness them rather than destroy them outright. His pursuit makes him a persistent antagonist across the quest's missions. The questline culminates in Vor's assassination, freeing the Tenno from his control and opening the full star chart.

Vor's Prize is the only quest in the game where the player cannot replay individual missions after completion. Once finished, the missions become standard star chart nodes on Earth.

## Prerequisites

Vor's Prize begins automatically after completing [Awakening](/quests/awakening). No Mastery Rank or prior content is required. New accounts start the game at the beginning of Awakening and proceed directly into Vor's Prize. The quest is part of the new player experience and cannot be skipped.

## Stages

The quest is structured around a series of Interlude segments aboard the Orbiter interspersed with field missions on Earth. The Interlude stages serve as guided tutorials for navigating the ship's systems, while the field missions introduce different mission types. The first two field missions have an invincibility modifier that prevents the player from being permanently downed, giving new players room to learn without being blocked by failure.

### Outreach -- E Prime, Earth

The first mission sends the player to a Grineer-controlled node called E Prime to run a Spy mission. The objective is to hack a single console inside a Grineer Data Vault to retrieve a Communications module. The mission introduces the basic hacking interface -- a rotary lock puzzle -- and the concept of silent infiltration, though the penalty for triggering alarms at this stage is minimal.

Completing the mission awards the player four essential mods: Point Blank, Hornet Strike, Pressure Point, and Serration. These mods represent the base damage upgrade for shotguns, secondaries, melees, and rifles respectively, and all four remain relevant throughout much of the early-to-mid game.

### Install Communications Module

Back aboard the Orbiter, the player installs the recovered module into the ship's communication array. The Interlude segments throughout the quest use this installation loop to teach the Orbiter's layout -- Navigation, Arsenal, Foundry, Market, and Mod workbench -- before each is unlocked. The Communications installation specifically unlocks the Market and Matchmaking interfaces, allowing the player to trade and play with others for the first time.

### Familiar Faces -- Mariana, Earth

The second field mission is a Rescue operation to free Darvo, a Corpus black marketeer who has been captured by the Grineer. Darvo is introduced here as a recurring vendor character who appears in later quests and sells limited-time items in a daily rotation. The rescue introduces the extraction mechanic and the concept of freeing a target and escorting them to the extraction point.

Completing the mission rewards five defensive and utility mods: Continuity, Intensify, Stretch, Redirection, and Vitality. These form the foundation of a basic Warframe survival build and introduce the concept of ability-enhancing mods to new players.

### Oppression -- Mantle, Earth

The third field mission is a combined objective: first retrieve the Foundry module from a Grineer installation, then exterminate a force of Grineer enemies. The exterminate segment introduces the kill-counter HUD element and the experience of clearing a tilesets room-by-room.

The Foundry module, once installed in the Interlude following this mission, unlocks the crafting system. Darvo provides the blueprint for an Ascaris Negator -- a device designed to counteract the control chip Vor planted in the Warframe. Crafting the Negator requires materials collected in subsequent missions.

### Loot and Pillage -- Gaia, Earth

The fourth mission introduces Cache collection. Three Supply Caches are scattered across the Gaia tile set, and the player must locate all three. This is a lighter version of the Sabotage mission type and teaches players to explore tilesets beyond the direct path to the extraction point. The caches contain materials needed for the Ascaris Negator blueprint.

Rewards include Steel Fiber and Enemy Sense, both useful early mods for survivability and enemy detection.

### Navigation Recovery -- Pacific, Earth

The fifth field mission places the player aboard a Grineer Galleon for the first time to recover the Navigation segment. The Galleon tileset is a significant upgrade in visual complexity compared to the earlier Earth tiles. Within the mission, an optional objective presents itself: sabotaging the ship's reactor to save colonists held aboard. This optional action has no mechanical penalty but introduces the concept of secondary objectives that exist beyond the primary marker.

The Interlude following this mission installs Navigation, unlocking the full star chart for exploration.

### Faceoff -- Cambria, Earth

The final mission sends the player to a mining asteroid to confront Captain Vor directly. Vor is the quest's first true boss encounter. He wields a Janus Key weapon, teleports during the fight, and periodically deploys shockwave attacks. His health pool is manageable for a player who has been equipping the mods received through prior stages.

Defeating Vor ends his control over the Ascaris and completes the quest. The Orbiter's systems are now fully operational, and the player gains unrestricted access to the star chart.

## Rewards

Completing Vor's Prize grants:

- Burston Rifle Blueprint, with accompanying Morphics, Polymer Bundle, and Alloy Plate resources needed to craft it in the Foundry.
- 25,000 Credits.
- All starter Orbiter segments needed to operate the ship.
- A substantial mod collection distributed across missions: Point Blank, Hornet Strike, Pressure Point, Serration, Continuity, Intensify, Stretch, Redirection, Vitality, Steel Fiber, Enemy Sense, and broken variants of Stretch and Intensify from the Navigation mission.

The Burston is an Earth-tier burst-fire rifle suitable for new players progressing through the early star chart. Its blueprint can be crafted in the Foundry using the resources provided by the quest.

## Lore

Captain Vor is a high-ranking Grineer general with an obsessive reverence for the Orokin era. He refers to the Tenno as divine beings and believes the Warframes are empty vessels -- what he calls "glass" -- through which the Tenno's Void energy flows. Rather than simply killing the awakened Tenno, Vor implanted the Ascaris device to assert control, viewing this as a way to harness a divine force for the Grineer Empire.

The Tenno's rescue of Darvo is the first indication that the solar system contains factions and individuals who operate outside the Grineer-Corpus binary. Darvo's Corpus background and his willingness to side against the Grineer when imprisoned establishes the recurring theme of flexible allegiances that runs through much of the Warframe storyline.

The Ascaris Negator, which the player must craft, represents the Tenno's first act of technological self-sufficiency -- using knowledge gained from rescuing a black marketeer to neutralize a device implanted by a tyrant. The thematic thread of regaining agency after long dormancy connects Vor's Prize to later quests that explore the Tenno's identity more deeply.

Captain Vor reappears after his apparent death in Vor's Prize. In the Void, he becomes fused with a Janus Key and is encountered by players running Void missions at higher levels. The character's obsession with the Orokin divine is recontextualized in later narrative quests that reveal more about the Tenno's actual nature.

## Tips

- Equip Serration and Hornet Strike immediately after the Outreach mission; the base damage boost is significant at the earliest mission levels.
- The Ascaris Negator has a short crafting time; start it in the Foundry as soon as Darvo provides the blueprint.
- In the Navigation Recovery mission, the optional reactor sabotage requires locating a secondary terminal; the route is clearly marked on the mini-map but is easy to miss if following only the primary objective.
- Vor in the Faceoff mission is susceptible to knockdown effects; any weapon with an Impact damage component that triggers a stagger can interrupt his teleport cycle.
- After the quest ends, the Earth nodes unlock as standard repeatable missions, allowing farming of Earth-exclusive resources.
