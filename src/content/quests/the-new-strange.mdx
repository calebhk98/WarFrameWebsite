## Overview

The New Strange is a quest that serves as both the primary acquisition route for the Chroma Warframe and an introduction to Cephalon Simaris and the Synthesis system. Simaris is the Cephalon of the Sanctuary, a digital repository dedicated to preserving permanent copies of organic and mechanical life through a scanning process called Synthesis. The quest tasks the Tenno with working alongside Simaris to track a mysterious signal, which turns out to originate from a rogue, uncontrolled Chroma Warframe operating autonomously across multiple star chart locations.

Completing The New Strange awards the Chroma Blueprint and unlocks Sanctuary Onslaught, a wave-based survival mode accessible from any Relay. The quest spans five stages across Ceres, Mars, and Europa, requiring a Synthesis Scanner item and access to the Jupiter-to-Europa Junction.

The WFCD description is direct: "Work with Cephalon Simaris to find the source of a mysterious voice."

## Prerequisites

Two prerequisites govern The New Strange. Stolen Dreams must be completed first -- this quest introduces Simaris and the Synthesis scanning mechanic, ensuring players have at least basic familiarity with the Synthesis Scanner before being asked to use it as a quest tool. Without Stolen Dreams, the Simaris storyline does not open.

Europa Junction access is required for the third and fifth quest stages, both of which take place on Europa. The Jupiter-to-Europa Junction requires clearing specific conditions on Jupiter, typically including several mission completions and objective-based challenges.

Synthesis Scanners must be purchased from Simaris at a Relay before undertaking the Synthesis-focused stages. The standard Codex Scanner cannot substitute for the Synthesis Scanner in quest objectives.

## Stages

**Missing Sentinels** begins on Ceres at Nuovo, a Grineer-controlled node. Simaris's Sentinel units -- small drone probes he uses to gather data -- have been captured by Grineer forces. The stage combines a Rescue mission to free the Sentinels with a Spy component: the Tenno must also infiltrate a Grineer data vault and extract security logs that contain information about a biological signature Simaris has been tracking. The logs reveal that something unusual is transmitting from deep within Corpus and Infested space.

**Paying with Synthesis** moves to Mars and introduces Synthesis as a quest mechanic. Cephalon Simaris requires synthesized data on Elite Arid Lancers, which are Grineer soldiers specifically found in the Arid tileset. Synthesizing an enemy requires the Synthesis Scanner -- gear equipment purchased from Simaris -- and scanning the target four times during a mission to complete the synthesis entry. Three Elite Arid Lancers must be fully synthesized across multiple Mars nodes before the stage concludes.

**Investigate the Source** takes the Tenno to Morax on Europa for a Mobile Defense mission. Three terminals must be defended against Corpus assault while the Tenno extracts transmission data. Upon completion, the stage yields the Chroma Signal Blueprint -- the first direct evidence that a Warframe is connected to the mysterious voice.

**Revisit the Derelict** returns to Mars at Alator, a Grineer Settlement with access to an Orokin Derelict portal. Fighting through Grineer forces leads to a confrontation with a rogue Chroma -- a Warframe operating without an operator, attacking hostile and neutral forces alike. The Chroma cannot be permanently captured in this stage; the Tenno can scan it once using the Synthesis Scanner before it escapes, yielding the Chroma Mark Blueprint.

**Defeat Chroma** is the final stage at Ose on Europa, a Corpus base. The Tenno must defend an Arcane Machine against escalating Corpus waves while Simaris completes his analysis. After the fifth wave concludes, the rogue Chroma appears and must be scanned five additional times using the Synthesis Scanner. The scanning process serves as both the combat encounter's resolution and the final data collection Simaris needs to complete his study of the entity.

The rogue Chroma has been systematically destroying evidence connected to Arcane Machines and Codices -- artifacts that hint at connections between the Sentients and earlier Warframe-era technology. Whether Chroma's destructive behavior stems from a corrupted directive, an operator's last command, or something more sinister is left partially ambiguous. Simaris gains insight into the mysterious Tenno-origin signatures but cannot fully resolve what the Chroma was protecting or destroying.

## Rewards

The Chroma Blueprint is the quest's primary mechanical reward. Chroma is an elemental Warframe whose abilities shift based on the player's active energy color at the time of casting, covering Heat, Cold, Electric, and Toxin variants for different elemental effects. His component blueprints (Neuroptics, Systems, Chassis) are distributed as Junction rewards across the star chart rather than from a dedicated boss node.

Sanctuary Onslaught is unlocked through the quest and becomes available at Relays. This is an endless wave-based mode that rewards Endo, Riven mods, and other valuable resources. It operates under Simaris's auspices and uses the Sanctuary framework introduced in this quest.

## Lore

Cephalon Simaris occupies a unique niche among the Cephalon personalities introduced in Warframe. Where Ordis is personally devoted to the Tenno and Cephalon Suda pursues knowledge for its own sake, Simaris is motivated by a drive to preserve and catalog all forms of life. His Sanctuary functions as a digital afterlife of sorts -- synthesized creatures continue to exist in its archives even after their physical counterparts die.

The New Strange establishes Simaris as a pragmatic partner rather than an ally. He needs the Tenno's field capabilities; the Tenno need access to the Synthesis system and its rewards. The transactional quality of the relationship is part of the quest's texture: Simaris is not particularly interested in the Tenno's wellbeing, only in their utility.

The rogue Chroma's behavior -- eliminating evidence connected to Arcane Machines and Codices -- fits into the broader late-game narrative about Sentient interference in Orokin-era archaeology. Arcane Machines represent knowledge the Sentients might prefer remain undiscovered or destroyed. The quest does not fully resolve this thread, leaving it open for the player to draw connections to subsequent content, including The Second Dream and The New War.

Chroma himself is an unusual Warframe design: a creature wrapped in elemental scales, with an exoskeleton that can be shed and wielded as a separate entity. The lore embedded in his origin -- an operator-less frame operating on some residual directive -- positions him as a mirror of what the Tenno's Warframes might become if permanently separated from their operators.

## Tips

- Purchase Synthesis Scanners from Simaris at any Relay before beginning the quest. Running out of scanners during a synthesis stage requires returning to a Relay to restock, which can interrupt mission flow.
- Elite Arid Lancers appear in Arid-tileset missions on Mars. Missions with higher enemy density, such as Survival or Defense, provide more opportunities to locate synthesis targets.
- The final stage requires scanning Chroma five times after the fifth wave. Chroma moves aggressively, so prioritize positioning that keeps the scanner's line of sight open rather than attempting to stand still in a fixed location.
- After completing the quest, Simaris's daily synthesis targets provide an ongoing source of standing. Standing earned from Simaris can be exchanged for unique augment mods, Simaris's own weapons, and utility items.
- Chroma's component blueprints come from Junction rewards rather than a boss assassination. Check the Junction path to identify which Junctions award each component.
