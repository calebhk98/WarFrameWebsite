## Overview

The Hex Finale is a follow-up quest to The Hex that requires players to fulfill specific relationship thresholds before it becomes available. Unlike the main quest, which progresses through a fixed linear sequence, the Finale is gated behind syndicate progression and interpersonal Chemistry milestones. The WFCD description frames it simply: "A chance to rewrite history and avert disaster."

The Finale expands on the emotional resolution begun in The Hex by asking whether the Drifter's investment in the six Protoframes is deep enough to forge a genuinely different outcome. It is not accessible immediately after completing The Hex; players must return to Hollvania and work through The Hex Syndicate's standing system and each member's individual Chemistry track.

<Callout type="warn">Spoilers for The Hex Finale follow. The section below describes the conditions and outcomes of the alternate timeline resolution.</Callout>

The Finale represents the "good ending" branch of the 1999 story arc. In the base quest, the Drifter witnesses a timeline where all six Hex members die. In the Finale, the bonds forged during free-roam activity provide the narrative and mechanical foundation for a different conclusion. The quest is marked as non-replayable and is excluded from the standard Codex once completed, suggesting it is intended as a definitive narrative capstone rather than repeatable content.

## Prerequisites

The Hex Finale requires three conditions to be met simultaneously. First, the main quest The Hex must be completed. Second, the player must reach Rank 3, referred to as "2-for-1," with The Hex Syndicate -- the standing-based organization unlocked upon finishing the main quest. Third, the player must achieve "Liked" Chemistry status with all six individual Hex members.

Chemistry is a relationship metric specific to the 1999 content, tracked separately for each Protoframe. It increases through targeted interactions during Hollvania bounties, dialogue choices, and cooperative combat scenarios. Advancing all six members to the Liked threshold requires sustained engagement with the open zone rather than rushing through bounties with a single preferred companion.

The standing requirement for Rank 3 means players must also spend time accumulating and spending The Hex Syndicate currency, earned through bounties and activities in Hollvania.

## Stages

The Hex Finale proceeds in two broad phases.

**Strengthen the Bonds** is not a traditional mission stage but rather the prerequisite activity period. Players must bring all six Hex members to Liked Chemistry through repeated engagement in Hollvania. Each member responds to different interaction types -- some value combat partnerships, others respond to dialogue sequences, and others track completion of specific bounty categories. This phase exists in the open zone rather than in a dedicated quest instance.

**Avert the Disaster** is the culminating mission sequence. With maximum Chemistry across all members and sufficient Syndicate standing, the Drifter returns to the critical moment from the main quest. The bonds formed across the Finale's preparation phase provide the force necessary to shift the outcome of the reactor event, saving the Hex members who were lost in the original timeline.

## Rewards

The primary cosmetic reward for completing The Hex Finale is the Hex Noggle Collection, a set of decorative figurines representing each of the six Protoframes. Noggles are orbital display items that can be placed within the Orbiter.

Beyond cosmetics, the Finale delivers narrative closure for each Hex member's arc. The six Protoframes carry unresolved histories with Albrecht Entrati and with each other; the Finale sequences provide resolution that the main quest, constrained by its urgent pacing, cannot fully develop.

## Lore

<Callout type="warn">Major spoilers for the resolution of the 1999 storyline follow.</Callout>

The Hex Finale is built on the premise that personal bonds are a force capable of resisting the Indifference's influence. The Indifference, which thrives on detachment, resignation, and the repetition of inevitable outcomes, cannot fully account for genuine interpersonal investment. The Finale's gating mechanism -- Chemistry requirements -- functions as a diegetic representation of this: the Drifter cannot simply arrive at the final moment with raw power; they must have actually formed the relationships the story requires.

This connects thematically to the broader Duviri arc. In Duviri, the Drifter experienced cycles of repetition defined by emotional states beyond their control. In Hollvania, the loop is also repetitive, but the Finale argues that agency expressed through connection can produce a different kind of cycle -- one with a real end.

Each Hex member represents a different aspect of the Protoframe experience: the tension between the human identity retained within the Warframe body and the role those bodies were engineered to serve. The Finale's resolution implies that the Orokin's decision to suppress that human identity in standard Warframes was a loss as much as it was a design choice.

The exclusion of the Finale from the standard Codex, noted in the WFCD data, may reflect an in-universe framing: this branch of events is not the "default" record of what happened, but rather the path the Drifter chose to create.

## Tips

- Track Chemistry progress for all six members from the moment Hollvania unlocks. Waiting until standing Rank 3 to begin Chemistry work will extend the time needed to meet both requirements simultaneously.
- Bounties that involve a specific Hex member as an ally tend to award Chemistry with that individual faster than standard bounty types. Check the bounty board for member-tagged missions.
- The Hex Syndicate standing cap per day applies normally. Plan standing expenditure across multiple sessions rather than attempting to rush from Rank 1 to Rank 3 in a single session.
- Chemistry with some members may require completing their personal story threads in addition to bounty participation. Exhaust all available dialogue options during open-zone exploration.
