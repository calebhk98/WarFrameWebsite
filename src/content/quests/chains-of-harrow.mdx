## Overview

Chains of Harrow is a solo-only main quest released in Update 21 (June 2017) as part of the Harrow Warframe update. The quest centers on the Red Veil syndicate, the medium Palladino, and the mystery of a Tenno named Rell who disappeared long ago. Players receive a strange transmission from an abandoned Steel Meridian vessel in Earth orbit, setting off an investigation that leads deep into Red Veil mysticism, Orokin-era history, and the hidden nature of a supernatural entity known as the Man in the Wall.

The quest is notable for its horror-inflected atmosphere. Environments grow increasingly unsettling as players move from a blood-streaked derelict ship to Void temples adorned with the iconography of the Red Veil's devotion. The narrative also introduces Palladino, a recurring character associated with the Steel Meridian outpost Iron Wake, who serves as the quest's guide and emotional anchor.

Completing Chains of Harrow rewards the Harrow Warframe blueprint, a support-oriented frame built around restoring shields, energy, and overshields. The quest also unlocks permanent access to Iron Wake, the Steel Meridian base of operations on Earth, which serves as a staging point for syndicate-related activities.

## Prerequisites

Players must satisfy the following conditions before beginning Chains of Harrow:

- **The War Within** -- The preceding main quest must be completed. Chains of Harrow builds on mechanics and lore established in The War Within, including Operator Transference and the broader Void-based story arc.
- **Mastery Rank 4** -- A minimum Mastery Rank of 4 is required to accept the quest key.
- **Steel Meridian outpost access** -- Players should have at least nominal contact with the Steel Meridian syndicate, as the quest's narrative closely involves the Red Veil, a syndicate allied with Steel Meridian.

The quest is solo-only and cannot be run in co-op. Players should bring a combat-capable loadout appropriate for enemy levels in the mid-twenties to low thirties range.

## Stages

The quest unfolds across eight stages spanning multiple tilesets and solar system locations.

**1. Investigate the Message**

A distress transmission leads to an abandoned Steel Meridian vessel in the Pacific node on Earth. The ship's crew is dead, and the walls are marked with symbols tied to the Red Veil. Players must search the derelict, discover the scope of what happened, and rescue Palladino -- a Red Veil medium -- from fanatics who have turned on her. The tileset is a Grineer Galleon, and enemy levels run from 20 to 25.

**2. Meet Palladino at Iron Wake**

After extraction, players travel to Iron Wake, a Steel Meridian outpost hidden on Earth. Palladino explains the crisis: a Tenno named Rell, long thought simply absent, is disintegrating. She speaks of the Donda -- a sacred relic that serves as the focal point for Rell's connection to the Red Veil and to the Harrow Warframe he inhabits.

**3. Find Rell's Relic**

Players deploy to the Hydra node on Pluto, a Corpus Ship tileset, to retrieve the Donda before Rell's fragmenting consciousness can destroy it. The mission involves combat and navigation through a Corpus interior while intermittent manifestations of Rell's presence create environmental disruptions. Enemy levels are approximately 20 to 25.

**4. Return the Donda**

With the Donda recovered, players return to Iron Wake. Palladino conducts a ritual in which players must identify and respond to emotional states drawn from Rell's fragmented memories. This is a non-combat stage focused on dialogue and memory sequences.

<Callout type="warn">Stages 5 through 8 contain story spoilers for Chains of Harrow.</Callout>

**5, 6, 7. Capture Rell's Manifestations**

Rell's disintegrating consciousness has scattered into three emotional fragments, each manifesting as a hostile presence in a different environment. Players must hunt each manifestation and trap it using Kinetic Siphon Traps, items dispensed at the start of each mission. The three locations are Exequias on Deimos (Orokin Derelict tileset), Stephano on Uranus (Grineer Sealab tileset), and Everest on Earth (Grineer Forest tileset). Enemy levels range from 25 to 35 across these missions.

The trapping mechanic requires players to locate the manifestation's anchor point and deploy the Siphon Trap in its vicinity. Enemies protect the anchor and must be cleared before the trap can be set and maintained. Each mission concludes when the manifestation is successfully captured and sealed.

**8. Enter the Temple**

The final mission takes place in the Tiwaz node, a Void-adjacent Orokin Derelict tileset. Players must navigate the Red Veil's inner temple, defeat waves of Red Veil Fanatics, and destroy a series of blue-lit chains that bind the Harrow Warframe -- and Rell within it. Operator Transference is required at specific points in this stage, making familiarity with Operator combat important.

After destroying the chains and engaging with a final memory sequence, the Harrow Warframe is freed and Rell's bound consciousness is released. The quest concludes with an epilogue encounter aboard the Orbiter.

## Rewards

Completing Chains of Harrow grants the following:

- **Harrow Warframe blueprint** -- The full blueprint for the Harrow Warframe, a support-focused frame with abilities centered on restoring shields, generating energy, and providing overshields to allies. Crafting Harrow requires additional component blueprints dropped from specific mission types; the quest grants only the main blueprint.
- **Iron Wake access** -- Permanent access to Iron Wake, the Steel Meridian outpost on Earth. Iron Wake functions as an alternative navigation point and social space linked to the Steel Meridian syndicate. Palladino remains available there for ongoing interactions.

Harrow's component blueprints -- Neuroptics, Chassis, and Systems -- are obtained through separate activities rather than quest stages. The Neuroptics drop from Rotation C of Defection missions, the Chassis from Corrupted Void enemies, and the Systems from the Pago node on Kuva Fortress. Players who wish to build the full Warframe must complete these additional farming steps after the quest ends.

## Lore

<Callout type="warn">The following section contains major story spoilers for Chains of Harrow.</Callout>

Rell was a Tenno who bore a heightened sensitivity to emotion -- a trait that made him exceptional in some respects and deeply burdened in others. Unlike most Tenno, Rell perceived the feelings of those around him with unusual clarity, and this perception made him an outcast. During the Orokin era, he was isolated and rejected by his peers, never properly accepted into the framework that governed Tenno society.

When the time came for the Tenno to enter cryosleep, Rell refused. He had a different purpose: he had detected the presence of an entity that would later be known as the Man in the Wall -- an anomalous consciousness of uncertain origin that attached itself to Tenno through the Void. Rather than leave this entity uncontained, Rell chose to act as a permanent jailer. He bound his own consciousness into the Harrow Warframe, sacrificing continued existence to serve as a living prison.

The Red Veil formed around this sacrifice. The syndicate's rituals, their iconography, and their extreme methods all trace back to reverence for Rell and the vigil he maintains. Palladino, as a Red Veil medium, served as a spiritual intermediary -- a voice for Rell's fragmented consciousness.

When Rell begins to dissolve, the consequences ripple outward through the Red Veil. His fragmented emotions manifest as hostile presences because the containment is failing. The player's role in the quest is ultimately to gather these fragments and restore enough coherence for Rell to be properly released -- not saved, but given the rest he was denied for so long.

The entity Rell was containing, the Man in the Wall, is not resolved in this quest. It remains a persistent and unsettling presence in the broader Warframe narrative, appearing again in subsequent story content.
