## Overview

The New War is a cinematic main-line quest released alongside Update 31.0 in December 2021. It represents the culmination of a storyline that began with the Natah quest and escalated through The Second Dream, The War Within, and the Prelude to War cinematic. The scale of the quest is unlike any other in Warframe: players take control of three non-Tenno characters -- a Grineer soldier, a Corpus technician, and the Dax warrior Teshin -- before resuming control of their own Operator and the newly introduced Drifter.

The quest demands completion of several prior story quests and requires ownership of both a Railjack and an Amp. The Railjack is used for a full space-combat segment early in the quest, and the Amp is necessary for Operator combat against Sentient forces. Players who do not own a Railjack can acquire one through the Rising Tide quest or purchase one using Platinum.

At its conclusion, The New War fundamentally alters the state of the Origin System and introduces the Drifter as a permanent playable identity, distinct from the Operator. Completing the quest also unlocks Narmer Bounties from both Cetus and Fortuna, which are the primary path to obtaining the Caliban Warframe.

<Callout type="warn">Spoilers for The New War follow. The sections below describe major plot developments.</Callout>

The narrative follows the Sentient armada's full-scale invasion of the Origin System, led by Natah -- the entity players knew as the Lotus -- who operates under the influence of Ballas and her brother Erra. Players witness the fall of Cetus, the brainwashing of civilians through Narmer Veil technology, and the desperate efforts of Kahl-175, Veso, and Teshin to resist the occupation. The Drifter, revealed to be an alternate-timeline version of the Operator who refused the Void powers offered by the Man in the Wall, emerges as a key figure in organizing the resistance.

## Prerequisites

The New War requires completion of the Prelude to War cinematic quest, which itself is unlocked through completing the Erra quest. Players must also have completed the Rising Tide quest or otherwise have access to a functional Railjack. An Amp is required to deal meaningful damage to Sentient enemies during Operator combat segments.

The breadth of prerequisite quests makes The New War a late-game undertaking. Players who have not worked through the Natah and Second Dream story arc will lack the narrative context to understand the quest's central conflicts. The recommended completion order mirrors the main-line quest progression: Natah, The Second Dream, The War Within, The Sacrifice, Chimera Prologue, Apostasy Prologue, Erra, and finally the Prelude to War cinematic before starting The New War.

## Stages

The quest proceeds across multiple distinct phases, each with its own playstyle and mechanical focus.

**For My Brothers** places players in control of three non-Tenno characters in sequence. Kahl-175, a Grineer soldier, fights through the initial Sentient assault using heavy Grineer weapons and unique squad mechanics. Veso, a Corpus robotics technician, relies on deployable MOA units rather than direct combat. Teshin, the Conclave grandmaster and a Dax warrior of immense skill, wields twin swords against Sentient forces. Each character segment is a self-contained mission with its own controls and abilities.

**The Rise of Narmer and Drifter's Camp** reframe the story from the perspective of the Drifter, operating in an Origin System now under Narmer occupation. Unlike the Operator, the Drifter does not use Warframes or Transference in the conventional sense. The Drifter's Camp serves as a hub where allies are recruited and plans are formed.

**The Wild Hunt** requires players to defeat at least two of three Archons -- powerful Sentient entities deployed by the armada -- across different mission nodes. Archons function as boss encounters with unique mechanics tied to their elemental affinities.

**The Aftermath** presents a narrative choice with lasting consequences: whether the player continues as the Operator or the Drifter. Both options are preserved in the long term; the choice primarily determines which character model appears in certain contexts.

**Approach Praghasa** is the final mission, culminating in a confrontation with Ballas aboard the Sentient mothership.

## Rewards

Completing The New War grants a significant array of items. The Sirocco Amp blueprint is provided alongside a cosmetic skin for it. The Nataruk, an Orokin bow once wielded by Hunhow's forces, is obtained during the quest itself. Completing the quest unlocks Narmer Bounties from both Konzu in Cetus and Eudico in Fortuna; these bounties award standing toward acquiring Caliban Warframe parts. The quest also awards a three-day Affinity Booster and a three-day Credit Booster.

## Lore

<Callout type="warn">Major spoilers follow for The New War and its preceding quests.</Callout>

The Sentient invasion is orchestrated by Ballas, the treacherous Orokin Executor last seen in the Apostasy Prologue, who has been manipulating Natah. Natah, the Tenno's guide known as the Lotus, is revealed to be a Sentient who was sent to infiltrate and destroy the Tenno but instead developed a parental bond with them. Ballas exploits her original nature, reasserting her Sentient identity as "Natah" and using the Sentient armada to subjugate the Origin System.

The Drifter's origin is revealed through the Temporal Axioms segment: they are an alternate version of the player's Operator who refused the Void powers offered by the entity known as the Man in the Wall during the Zariman Ten Zero incident. This creates a temporal paradox that the quest uses to justify both characters coexisting. Ballas is ultimately defeated through the combined efforts of the Tenno, the Drifter, and Hunhow -- once a primary antagonist -- who forms an alliance of necessity.

The quest ends with the Lotus recovering consciousness, though her identity remains fractured among her roles as Natah, the Lotus, and the Orokin-imprinted entity known as the Margulis echo. The Man in the Wall's continued presence at the quest's conclusion signals ongoing narrative stakes beyond The New War's resolution.

## Tips

- Complete the quest on Normal mode first; Steel Path modifiers apply to the character segments and make them significantly more difficult.
- Kahl-175's segment controls differently from standard Warframe; take time to learn his abilities before engaging large groups.
- Archon fights require switching damage types mid-encounter. Bringing a diverse arsenal ensures options when Archon shields or resistances activate.
- The Amp used during Operator segments should be at least a Mote Amp equivalent or better; low-tier amps struggle significantly against Sentient armor.
- Narmer Bounties become accessible immediately after completion and do not require additional standing thresholds to begin.
