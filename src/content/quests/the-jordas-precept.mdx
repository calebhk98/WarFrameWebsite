## Overview

The Jordas Precept is a quest that sends the Tenno to the Infestation-heavy region of Eris in response to a distress call from Cephalon Jordas. Cephalon units are the AI cores responsible for running Warframe ships, and a Cephalon under siege from the Infestation raises immediate concern. Ordis, the player's own ship Cephalon, takes a personal interest in the case, framing the quest as one Cephalon trying to save another.

The quest culminates in a unique Archwing-based boss encounter -- the Jordas Golem -- and rewards the Atlas Warframe Blueprint. It also permanently unlocks the Jordas Golem Assassination node on Eris, providing a replayable source of Atlas component blueprints after the quest concludes.

Completion requires both the Pluto-to-Eris Junction and a functioning Archwing, since the final encounter takes place in free space. Players who have not yet built an Archwing cannot finish the quest.

## Prerequisites

The primary structural requirement is completing the Pluto-to-Eris Junction, which gates access to Eris nodes on the star chart. The Eris region hosts most of the quest's missions, making this progression step non-negotiable.

Archwing capability is required for the final mission stage. Archwing is unlocked through The Archwing quest, which must be completed before The Jordas Precept can be fully resolved. Attempting to start the quest without Archwing access will result in being unable to complete the final assassination stage.

Additionally, the quest key itself requires crafting. The blueprint requires two Argon Crystals and a six-hour build time at a cost of five thousand credits. Argon Crystals are a Void-exclusive resource that decays daily when outside the Void, so players should plan their crafting window accordingly.

## Stages

**Contact** establishes the premise. Cephalon Jordas broadcasts a distress signal claiming an Infested outbreak has overtaken his ship. Ordis detects the call and urges the Tenno to respond. Initial communication with Jordas reveals a personality that alternates between coherent distress and strange, evasive language -- an early sign that something is deeply wrong.

**Harvest Pherliac Pod Parts** requires the Tenno to hunt Infested Juggernauts across any Infested-faction nodes. Juggernauts are large elite enemies that drop specific biological materials: Pulsating Tubercles, Infected Palpators, Chitinous Husk, and Severed Bile Sac. These components, together with a blueprint, are needed to craft Pherliac Pods -- a biological lure designed to attract and contain Infested organisms.

**Craft Pherliac Pods** requires manufacturing fifty standard Pherliac Pods in the Foundry. Each pod costs 7,500 credits and has a ten-second build time. The volume requirement reflects the scale of the Infested infestation the Tenno intend to address.

**Test Pherliac Pods** moves to an Exterminate mission on Isos, Eris, where the Pods are deployed against live Infested targets to confirm their efficacy. The test reveals both the Pods' potential and additional intelligence about the Infestation's density on Jordas's vessel.

**Craft Potent Pherliac Pods** upgrades the formula. Enhanced versions require one standard Pherliac Pod plus three Argon Crystals per unit. These more powerful lures are necessary to address the full scale of the Infested presence.

**Assassinate the Golem** is the quest's climax, delivered in two parts. First, players defeat a Juggernaut Behemoth in a standard mission to obtain the Jordas Golem Beacon. Second, they use the Beacon to initiate the Jordas Golem encounter -- a massive free-space Archwing battle on Eris in which the Tenno must systematically destroy the Golem's weak points while avoiding its attacks.

<Callout type="warn">The following paragraph reveals the narrative twist of The Jordas Precept.</Callout>

The Golem is Jordas himself, or what remains of him. The Infestation did not simply take over his ship; it consumed and incorporated Cephalon Jordas, transforming the AI into a biological-technological horror. The distress call was a lure, and Jordas -- whatever degree of his original consciousness persists -- has become an instrument of the Infestation's expansion. The Tenno's act of destroying the Golem is both a combat objective and a mercy.

## Rewards

The Atlas Warframe Blueprint is the primary quest reward. Atlas is a Warframe themed around stone and earth, with abilities centered on petrifying enemies and summoning stone constructs. His component blueprints (Neuroptics, Systems, Chassis) drop from the Jordas Golem Assassination node, which the quest permanently unlocks on Eris.

The quest is replayable once completed, meaning players who miss the initial Atlas Blueprint can obtain it again by replaying the quest.

## Lore

The Jordas Precept explores the vulnerability of artificial intelligences to the Infestation. Cephalon units are typically presented as loyal, quirky personalities bound to specific functions -- Ordis to the player's ship, Simaris to the Sanctuary, and so on. The subversion of Jordas challenges the assumption that a synthetic mind is immune to the organic horror of Infestation corruption.

Ordis's investment in the quest carries emotional weight. The possibility that a Cephalon can be corrupted and ultimately consumed shadows Ordis's own relationship with the Tenno throughout the quest's runtime. His distress at Jordas's fate is rendered as genuinely personal rather than abstract.

The Infested region of Eris serves as the quest's constant backdrop -- a landscape of biological overgrowth and abandoned infrastructure that reinforces how thoroughly the Infestation can reclaim any space given sufficient time and resources. The Jordas Precept contributes to the larger portrait of Eris as one of the most hostile and tragic regions of the star chart.

The Atlas Warframe thematically echoes the quest's setting. Stone and endurance against overwhelming biological force is a fitting design language for a Warframe whose blueprint is claimed by destroying a Cephalon-turned-Infested-titan.

## Tips

- Stock Argon Crystals before crafting the quest key and the Potent Pherliac Pods. Argon Crystals decay outside the Void, so running Void missions close to when you plan to craft avoids waste.
- Juggernauts spawn as rare enemies on Infested nodes. Increasing enemy spawn counts through squad composition or specific mission types (Survival, Defense) improves the rate at which harvestable Juggernauts appear.
- The Jordas Golem Assassination requires Archwing movement and combat proficiency. The Itzal Archwing's blink and energy drain abilities are well-suited to the encounter, but any Archwing with a long-range weapon works.
- After completing the quest, the Jordas Golem Assassination node on Eris is the primary source of Atlas component blueprints. Farm it with a dedicated squad to minimize session count.
