## Overview

Saya's Vigil is a short introductory quest tied to the Plains of Eidolon open-world zone and serves as the primary acquisition path for the [Gara](/warframes/gara) Warframe. The quest is accessible early in the game's progression and is designed to introduce players to the Plains environment, Cetus as a hub, and the Ostron community that lives beneath the shadow of Eidolons. It is not a prerequisite for most other quests, but completing it unlocks the Shrine Defense mission type on the Plains and opens access to the Quills faction.

The quest centers on Saya, an elderly Ostron woman living in Cetus who has spent five years searching for answers about her disappeared husband. The player is enlisted to track down fragments that Saya's husband left behind -- a task that takes them from a Grineer dig site on the Plains surface to an orbital Grineer galleon, and finally back to the Plains during the dangerous nighttime hours when Eidolons are active. This structure serves as a natural introduction to the Plains' day-night cycle, a core mechanic of the open-world zone.

The quest's pacing is lighter than most story quests. There are no major cutscenes and dialogue is delivered through transmissions and brief NPC interactions. The narrative weight is carried by the environmental details and the mystery of Onkko's true fate, which is revealed only in the final epilogue stage. Players completing Saya's Vigil will encounter The Quills faction for the first time, which is relevant for later Operator progression.

Mechanically, the most noteworthy aspect of Saya's Vigil is its reliance on Codex Scanners. The quest cannot proceed without them, and players who have never equipped a scanner before may be confused by the requirement. Both Codex Scanners and the more versatile Synthesis Scanners function for the quest objectives. Helios companions fitted with the Investigator mod can auto-scan items within proximity, reducing the manual burden.

The quest is accessible to newer players. Enemy levels on the Plains stages range from 4 to 8, and the Grineer galleon infiltration involves manageable foes. The nighttime stage involves the presence of an Eidolon Teralyst patrolling the Plains, but the shard can be scanned without directly engaging it. Maintaining distance from the Teralyst is the primary survival concern in that stage.

## Prerequisites

The following conditions must be met before Saya's Vigil becomes available:

- **Completion of Vor's Prize** -- the tutorial quest that opens the star chart and basic navigation.
- **At least one bounty completed in the Plains of Eidolon** -- the quest becomes available in the Codex only after the player has entered the Plains at least once via a Cetus bounty. Simply visiting Cetus is not sufficient; the player must accept and complete a bounty assignment.

Players gain access to Cetus by reaching Earth and completing the Earth Junction, which opens the Plains of Eidolon landing craft option from the Navigation panel. Cetus itself is a free-roam hub; no additional quest is required to enter it.

## Stages

Saya's Vigil unfolds in six stages, each building on the previous through investigation and retrieval. The quest forces solo instances on the Plains during its story stages, so squad play is not possible for these missions.

**1. A Personal Favor**

The quest begins with a conversation prompt in Cetus. Konzu, the bounty master, directs the player to speak with Saya near the outer walls of the settlement. Saya explains that her husband Onkko disappeared five years ago under circumstances the Grineer covered up. She asks the player to investigate a Grineer dig site where Onkko was last known to have been working. Agreeing triggers the first Plains mission.

**2. Find Onkko**

On the Plains of Eidolon during daytime, the player locates a Grineer excavation camp operating over a sealed cave entrance. A drilling machine blocks access to the cave. After destroying the drilling machine and defeating the defending Grineer forces, the player enters the cave and retrieves an Ostron Coffer, a locked container that Onkko concealed before his disappearance.

**3. Locate the Shatter-Lock Pieces**

Back on the Plains, the player must locate three glass shards scattered around a Grineer camp. These shards are not visible to the naked eye and require a Codex Scanner or Synthesis Scanner to detect and scan. Equipping the scanner in the gear wheel and activating it while near the shard locations registers the scan. Enemy reinforcements arrive during the search, and a Tusk Bolkor gunship must also be destroyed to complete the stage's combat requirements. This is the stage most likely to halt progress for players who have not equipped scanners before starting the quest.

**4. Board Hek's Galleon**

The trail leads to a Grineer galleon in Earth's orbit. The player infiltrates the ship, encounters a Nox enemy -- a heavy Grineer unit whose head is a sealed environmental suit vulnerable to precision fire -- and accesses a secured vault. Hacking the vault console reveals another shard, which is scanned to add to the collection.

**5. Saya's Vigil**

The most atmospheric stage of the quest, set on the Plains of Eidolon during nighttime. The final shard is located in the open Plains, but the region is patrolled by an Eidolon Teralyst during night hours. The player must navigate to the shard's location, scan it, and extract without being destroyed. The Teralyst targets players who enter its detection radius, and its attacks are lethal to unprepared builds. The shard can be reached by moving carefully and avoiding the Teralyst's patrol path. Extraction after scanning ends the stage.

**6. Epilogue**

Returning to the Orbiter triggers a message about the coffer's lock mechanism. The player then travels to The Quills, a secretive Ostron faction accessible through a hidden passage in Cetus. Inside, the coffer is opened and the contents reveal the circumstances of Onkko's disappearance and the connection to Gara, an ancient Warframe whose history is intertwined with the Plains themselves.

<Callout type="warn">Spoilers for Onkko's fate and the Gara lore follow.</Callout>

The coffer contains fragments that reveal Onkko has become the leader of The Quills, a faction dedicated to observing and studying the Eidolons. His disappearance was not a death but a transformation: Onkko now exists partly outside normal time, perceiving events in a non-linear sequence as a side effect of his prolonged Eidolon research. He has chosen to remain with The Quills and cannot return to Saya in any conventional sense. The shards themselves are fragments of Gara's crystallized body, a remnant of a battle in which Gara sacrificed herself to contain an Eidolon threat and became the glass Warframe now retrievable through the quest's completion.

## Rewards

Completing Saya's Vigil grants:

- **Gara Warframe blueprint** -- the main blueprint needed to begin crafting Gara. The three component blueprints (Neuroptics, Chassis, Systems) are obtained separately through Shrine Defense missions on the Plains of Eidolon, which unlock upon quest completion.
- **Access to Shrine Defense missions** -- a mission type on the Plains where additional Gara components and other rewards drop.

Because Gara's component blueprints are locked behind Shrine Defense missions rather than awarded directly, completing the quest is only the beginning of the crafting process. Shrine Defense missions run at night on the Plains and require defeating waves of Grineer enemies while protecting a crystal shrine.

## Lore

Saya's Vigil occupies a precise narrative position in Warframe's lore: it explains the origin of Gara while simultaneously introducing players to the Eidolon phenomenon that defines the Plains of Eidolon open world. The quest implies that Gara was one of the original Warframes deployed by the Orokin during a period when Eidolons first became a threat to human settlements on Earth.

The glass aesthetic running through the quest -- the shards, the coffer, the scanning mechanic -- maps onto Gara's identity as a glass-elemental Warframe. Her abilities involve creating and shattering glass constructs, and the fragments found during the quest are literally pieces of her form preserved after her sacrifice. The Plains of Eidolon's landscape, marked by craters, scars, and the ruin of old structures, reflects the aftermath of whatever conflict resulted in Gara's petrification.

The Quills faction introduced in the epilogue becomes more significant during the Eidolon hunting endgame content accessible after The Second Dream quest line. Onkko's role as the Quills' leader provides context for why that faction holds specialized knowledge about Eidolons and can provide upgrades to the Operator's Void Amp -- the weapon used to strip Eidolon synovia during hunts.

## Tips

- Equip Codex Scanners or Synthesis Scanners in the gear wheel before starting Stage 3. The quest will not progress until the shards are scanned, and entering the mission without scanners requires returning to the Orbiter.
- Helios sentinel equipped with the Investigator mod will auto-scan nearby objects including quest shards, reducing the need for manual scanning in close proximity.
- During the nighttime stage, the Eidolon Teralyst's patrol route can be observed from a distance before committing to a path toward the shard. Sprinting between cover points away from the Teralyst's line of sight minimizes exposure time.
- The Nox enemy in the galleon stage is most efficiently defeated by shooting the glass dome on its head with precision weapons. Direct hits on the dome deal significantly increased damage compared to body shots.
- Enemy levels throughout the quest are low, making it suitable for players who have just unlocked the Plains. Any Warframe and weapon combination effective in early-game Earth missions is sufficient for all stages.
