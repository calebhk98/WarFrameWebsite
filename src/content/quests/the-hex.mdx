## Overview

The Hex is a mainline quest that immerses the Drifter in the city-state of Hollvania on the night of December 31st, 1999. The Scaldra -- a militaristic faction enforcing a harsh lockdown -- and the mutating Techrot threat have plunged the city into crisis even as its citizens attempt to celebrate the new year. At the center of the chaos is Albrecht Entrati, whose capture sets off a chain of events the Drifter must unravel by working alongside a group of six Protoframes known as The Hex.

The quest serves as the primary introduction to the Year of 1999 content, establishing Hollvania as an explorable open zone, introducing The Hex syndicate, and unlocking the Protoframe system. It runs approximately ninety minutes from start to finish and is a solo-only experience. Once started, standard Warframe activities are suspended until completion. To begin, players must type the code "KALYMOS" in the relevant interface.

<Callout type="warn">Spoilers for The Hex follow. The sections below describe plot details including the nature of the time loop and the fate of The Hex members in the initial timeline.</Callout>

The quest's central tension is a predestination loop: in the unaltered timeline, all six Hex members perish defending a nuclear reactor. The Drifter, wielding the reality-bending abilities cultivated in Duviri, rewinds time and enters a new loop with the knowledge needed to change outcomes. The antagonist force, the Indifference -- manifesting through Major Rusalka -- actively works to prevent any deviation from the catastrophic original sequence.

## Prerequisites

To begin The Hex, players must have completed both The Duviri Paradox and The Lotus Eaters. The Duviri Paradox unlocks the Drifter's time-manipulation abilities, which are central to the quest's mechanics, while The Lotus Eaters establishes the narrative thread leading to Hollvania and introduces Albrecht Entrati's Kavat companion, Kalymos.

No specific Mastery Rank requirement is listed, but access to both prerequisite quests implicitly assumes meaningful progression through the star chart.

## Stages

The Hex proceeds through a structured sequence of missions inside Hollvania and, briefly, aboard the Zariman Ten Zero.

**Happy Y2K!** opens in the city streets as the Drifter makes contact with the six members of The Hex. Players acquire the Atomicycle, a rideable vehicle central to navigation within Hollvania, and face their first major combat encounter against the H-04 Efervon Tank guarding key districts.

**Exterminate** tasks the Drifter with fighting through Scaldra military units to locate the facility where Albrecht Entrati is being held. The Scaldra's occupation of Hollvania is portrayed through dense environmental storytelling -- checkpoints, propaganda, and evidence of forced population displacement.

**Hell-Scrub** is a survival-style stage: the Drifter must hold a position against continuous Techrot waves for ten minutes. The Techrot represent the mutating biological threat spreading through Hollvania's lower districts, and this stage underscores the scale of that crisis.

**Rescue** centers on accessing a POM-2 terminal that reveals the nature of the loop. The Drifter confronts the full scope of the disaster -- the original sequence in which every Hex member dies -- and secures Entrati's release.

**Who's On Point** introduces a player choice: the Drifter selects one Hex member to take point on the push toward the reactor. Different members offer different tactical approaches, and the choice has narrative weight for the Finale.

**Zariman Visions** briefly shifts the setting to the Zariman Ten Zero, where the Indifference forces the Drifter to witness earlier events -- a reminder that the threat extends beyond 1999.

**Final Stand** is the climactic sequence. The Hex hold corridors and chokepoints while the Drifter works to prevent the reactor detonation. In this loop, the outcome is not predetermined.

**Conclusion** closes out the main quest, delivers rewards, and unlocks Hollvania for free-roam missions and bounties.

## Rewards

Completing The Hex grants the Cyte-09 Warframe Blueprint, the Magnetic Might mod, a Forma, and several cosmetic items including the Atomicycle vehicle with its Rust Belt and Standard Liveries and the Protokol Longsword Skin. Structurally, the quest unlocks The Hex Syndicate and access to Hollvania's open-zone bounty system.

The Atomicycle Summon resource is also awarded and is used to call the vehicle during Hollvania traversal.

## Lore

<Callout type="warn">Major story spoilers for The Hex and its narrative connection to The Duviri Paradox follow.</Callout>

The Hex exists within a wider arc examining what it means to be a Protoframe -- an earlier form of Warframe that retained a distinct human identity rather than the warrior-template standardization of the Orokin era. Each of the six members carries unresolved history with Albrecht Entrati, and their willingness to fight for Hollvania is bound to personal loyalty as much as duty.

The Indifference, which players encountered during The Duviri Paradox, returns here as the adversarial force. Its control over Major Rusalka allows it to direct Scaldra operations and actively counter the Drifter's timeline interventions. The quest argues that the Indifference feeds on detachment and resignation -- that genuine bonds between people represent its only true weakness.

The reactor catastrophe, if unremedied, represents the permanent death of all six Protoframes. The loop structure means the Drifter carries the burden of knowing this outcome while working against it. This narrative device connects the emotional stakes of 1999 to the Drifter's Duviri experience of endless repetition and the search for meaningful change.

Albrecht Entrati's role bridges The Lotus Eaters and this quest: his research into the Void and the nature of time has made him both a target of the Scaldra and a resource the Drifter needs to break the cycle. His survival is necessary for later narrative developments.

## Tips

- Invest standing in The Hex Syndicate after completing the quest. Reaching rank three (the "2-for-1" threshold) while also achieving "Liked" Chemistry with all six Hex members unlocks The Hex Finale, the follow-up quest that provides additional cosmetic rewards including the Hex Noggle Collection.
- The Atomicycle can be used throughout Hollvania's open zone after the quest concludes. Familiarizing yourself with its handling during the quest stages reduces traversal time in later bounties.
- The survival stage (Hell-Scrub) rewards players who bring high-damage builds. Techrot enemies have specific weaknesses; consult the Hollvania faction notes before selecting a loadout.
- Chemistry levels with Hex members increase through specific interactions during free-roam bounties. Track all six members to maximize Finale eligibility.
