## Overview

The Waverider is a solo quest centered on K-Drive culture and the Ventkids faction in Fortuna on Venus. Players help Roky, a member of the Ventkids, recover a rare Graphica -- a comic-book-style collectible -- that depicts Yareli, the legendary inspiration behind K-Drive riding. The quest has no combat-heavy sequences; instead, it challenges the player to complete a series of K-Drive trick and race objectives spread across five pages of the Graphica. Completing all five pages unlocks the blueprint for Yareli, a water-themed Warframe who rides a personal K-Drive called Merulina.

The quest was introduced alongside Yareli in Update 31.1 and is one of the more unconventional quest structures in the game, requiring genuine mechanical proficiency with K-Drive controls. Players who have not yet developed comfort with grinding, tricking, and racing on the K-Drive will find the later pages more demanding than the earlier ones.

## Prerequisites

Before starting The Waverider, the following conditions must be met:

- Completion of [Vox Solaris](/quests/vox-solaris), which unlocks Fortuna access and the Ventkids faction.
- Mastery Rank 3 or higher.
- The quest must be started and completed in Solo mode.

Because Vox Solaris itself requires completion of [Saya's Vigil](/quests/sayas-vigil) and access to the Venus Junction, new players will encounter The Waverider only after working through at least part of the star chart.

## Stages

The Waverider is structured around five pages of a Graphica discovered by Roky. Each page requires fulfilling a trio of feats that escalate in complexity. All outdoor feats take place in the Orb Vallis open world.

### Reading the comic and meeting Roky

The quest begins when an inbox message from Roky arrives describing an old Graphica she found. Traveling to the Ventkids Clubhouse inside Fortuna triggers a cutscene that sets up the narrative: Roky injured herself trying to unlock the comic, and the Tenno must unlock its pages by performing the feats depicted within.

### Page 1

The first page introduces basic K-Drive mechanics. Required feats include performing five Copter tricks, finishing one K-Drive race anywhere in the Orb Vallis, and killing five enemies with a secondary weapon while mounted on the K-Drive.

Copter tricks are performed by spinning the board using lateral inputs during jumps. They are straightforward to execute near any elevated surface or natural ramp in the Vallis.

### Page 2

The second page increases the difficulty modestly. Players must perform five nose planks or tail planks, maintain continuous airtime for five seconds in a single jump, and earn five headshot kills while riding.

Airtime is easier to accumulate by finding tall geological formations in the Vallis rather than relying on small ramps. The Archwing can be deployed to reach height quickly, then dismissed to drop back onto the board mid-descent, which counts toward the airtime requirement.

### Page 3

Page 3 tests sustained trick chains. Players must maintain an unbroken trick chain for ten seconds, complete five frontside rollouts, and complete five backside rollouts.

Trick chains can be extended by linking moves without touching the ground. Extended pipes and railings near the Fortuna entrance are well-suited for long grinding sessions that keep the chain counter running.

### Page 4

The fourth page introduces combination challenges. Players need to reach two x10 trick combo milestones, finish two distinct K-Drive races in the Vallis, and score five slam kills -- kills caused by the slam attack performed when landing from height while on the K-Drive.

Slam kills require positioning over grouped enemies and timing the descent carefully. The enemy camps near the Spaceport or Temple of Profit work well for this.

### Page 5

The final page has the most demanding aggregate requirements. Players must kill five enemies with a secondary weapon while airborne (not just mounted but mid-jump), accumulate ten thousand total race points across any races, and score five hundred trick-chain points in a single chain or across multiple runs.

Ten thousand race points may sound substantial but accumulates quickly across several races; finishing a race awards points proportional to time and route. Trick-chain points require consistent trick execution without breaking the chain on a surface landing.

### Return to Fortuna

After completing all five pages, returning to the Ventkids Clubhouse triggers the quest's conclusion. Roky hands over the Yareli blueprint. The narrative reveals that Grandmother, an Entrati elder on Deimos, purchased the comic to help revive Yareli's legacy and free the Ventkids from debt.

## Rewards

Completing The Waverider grants:

- Yareli Warframe Blueprint (main blueprint; components must be crafted separately)
- Yareli Noggle Statue
- K-Drive Booster Decoration
- Yareli Prex card (Orbiter decoration)
- Waverider issue 1 (comic decoration)

Players who already own Yareli at the time of quest completion receive a bonus Riven Mod instead of the Warframe blueprint.

## Lore

<Callout type="warn">Spoilers for The Waverider follow.</Callout>

Roky discovers a Graphica -- an in-universe illustrated serial -- hidden in the Ventkids Clubhouse. The comic depicts a character named Yareli defeating Vulgran, a slaver, using a K-Drive and water-based abilities. Roky is injured in the process of retrieving the Graphica, so the Tenno must complete each page's depicted feats to unlock the story further.

The Ventkids, who owe a significant debt to Nef Anyo's Corpus organization, find in Yareli's story a symbol of resistance and hope. Grandmother, contacted through means not fully explained in the quest itself, arranges to purchase the Graphica outright, freeing the Ventkids from the debt and allowing the legacy of Yareli to be carried forward. The quest's resolution confirms that Yareli is not merely a fictional character but a historical figure whose essence can be revived as a Warframe.

The Waverider is one of the few quests that ties Warframe lore directly to the Ventkids and K-Drive subculture, expanding on the Fortuna storyline introduced in Vox Solaris.

## Tips

- Deploy Archwing to gain altitude, then dismiss it mid-air to extend airtime for Page 2 and Page 5 challenges.
- The circular platform called The Pearl in the Vallis is a reliable location for accumulating trick points due to its raised edges and open layout.
- Equip the Inertia Dampeners mod on the K-Drive to reduce knockback on trick landings, which helps maintain longer chains for Page 3 and Page 5.
- Slam kills require landing directly on top of enemies; clustering enemies near structures and using Archwing drops increases the kill rate for Page 4.
- K-Drive races in the Vallis respawn at the same locations after each completion, so racing the same circuit twice fulfills the two-race requirement for Page 4.
- Secondary weapons can be fired from the K-Drive at any time; equip a high-damage secondary with splash or punch-through damage for faster kills on Pages 1 and 5.
