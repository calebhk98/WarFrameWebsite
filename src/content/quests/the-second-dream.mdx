## Overview

The Second Dream is a landmark cinematic quest that fundamentally redefines the game's core identity. Released in Update 18.0 in December 2015, it is the first quest to deliver what the Warframe community refers to as "the reveal" -- a piece of information about the player's character that reframes everything that came before it. New players are encouraged to avoid spoilers for this quest and to experience it without prior knowledge of its ending.

Before The Second Dream, players controlled Warframes as the apparent protagonists. The quest establishes that the Warframes are not autonomous beings but are remotely piloted by Tenno -- children who survived the Zariman Ten Zero void disaster and possess dangerous Void energy -- through a mind-link process called Transference. The Tenno's true physical forms, called Operators, have been kept safe and dormant in a hidden Reservoir somewhere in the solar system.

The Second Dream is required before any subsequent main-line quests, including The War Within, The Sacrifice, and eventually The New War. The Operator system, the Focus ability framework, and access to Lua as a mission node are all gated behind its completion. It is also the quest that causes the Shadow Stalker to emerge as a distinct and more threatening version of the Stalker, building on the Natah quest's revelations about Hunhow.

<Callout type="warn">Spoilers for The Second Dream follow. The sections below describe the quest's central revelation.</Callout>

The quest's antagonist setup involves the Sentient Hunhow, confined beneath Uranus, reaching out through the Lotus to locate the Reservoir. Hunhow partners with the Stalker, transforming him into the Shadow Stalker and providing him with the greatsword War. The Lotus, whose mind is partially compromised by Hunhow's infiltration, works to protect the Reservoir's secret as long as possible.

## Prerequisites

The Second Dream unlocks after completing the Natah quest, which itself becomes available after clearing the Neptune Junction. Natah introduces Hunhow and establishes the Sentient threat in the current timeline. Players who complete Natah will receive a message or inbox notification directing them toward The Second Dream.

No Mastery Rank requirement applies. However, the missions in the quest include Uranus and Lua content, so having reasonably leveled gear simplifies the combat sections. The final stage at Grimaldi on Lua is notably demanding, as it requires the player to function without a Warframe for a segment.

## Stages

The Second Dream spans five missions across Uranus, Neptune, and Lua, concluding with a sequence aboard the player's Orbiter.

### Infiltrate the Grineer Archives

The quest begins at Umbriel on Uranus, a Spy mission requiring players to breach three data vaults. The intelligence gathered confirms that Hunhow has embedded himself within the Lotus's mind and has learned the general location of the Reservoir. The mission establishes Alad V as an unexpected ally; he provides information on the Sentient in exchange for access to Sentient tissue samples, a detail that foreshadows his continued involvement.

### Find the Sentient Fragment

At Cressida on Uranus, players recover a Sentient relic from an underwater cavern. This Archwing mission serves as a transition point for the narrative: obtaining the fragment triggers a trap Hunhow laid in the Lotus's mind, giving him the precise location of the Reservoir. The mission signals that Lua -- the Moon, long hidden from the star chart -- is where the Reservoir was concealed.

### Follow the Stalker

The Stalker, now empowered as the Shadow Stalker, moves to locate the Reservoir. Chasing him through Neso on Neptune leads to a Void Gate embedded in Corpus infrastructure. The mission concludes by disabling a Void mask that kept Lua's position cloaked, bringing the Moon back into detectable space. Lua then becomes accessible on the star chart as a new planet node.

### Protect the Moon

Lua's sudden reappearance attracts both Grineer and Corpus forces. The Defense and Puzzle hybrid mission at Plato on Lua requires activating three Void compasses scattered through the tileset and shutting down four Pendula -- large Orokin resonators that were maintaining the Void collapse. The Shadow Stalker appears during this mission as a narrative encounter, though the full confrontation is reserved for the next stage.

### Save the Reservoir

The final mission, Grimaldi on Lua, is a solo escort sequence. Players must physically carry the Operator -- the Tenno's true form -- to extraction while repelling Sentient drones. The Somatic Link, a device connecting the Operator to the Warframe, must be filled three times by interacting with points along the route. The Shadow Stalker attacks in force during these phases.

At the conclusion, the Operator speaks for the first time. The camera shifts to reveal the Tenno's physical body inside a pod in the Reservoir. This scene ends the reveal sequence.

After the mission, players return to the Orbiter for a customization sequence. The Operator's appearance -- face, hairstyle, skin tone -- can be adjusted. The Lotus presents five Focus school options through a brief dialogue: Madurai, Vazarin, Naramon, Unairu, and Zenurik. The chosen school determines the first Focus ability available to the Operator.

## Rewards

The Second Dream grants several significant unlocks alongside its direct item rewards.

The Broken War is a two-handed Nikana received as a direct drop during the quest; it represents a fragment of the greatsword War that the Shadow Stalker wields. The full War blade is only obtainable by collecting Broken War and crafting the rest separately.

The Focus system becomes active, allowing Operators to develop passive and active abilities in the chosen school by channeling Affinity earned during missions. This system expands substantially in The War Within with Void-mode combat capabilities.

Lua becomes a permanent mission location with unique Grineer and Corpus mission nodes, several of which offer distinct gameplay challenges tied to the Orokin architecture.

Access to the Rising Tide quest unlocks, which is required to build a Railjack and ultimately a prerequisite for The New War.

## Lore

<Callout type="warn">Major spoilers for The Second Dream follow.</Callout>

The Zariman Ten Zero was an Orokin colonization vessel that passed through the Void during transit. The adults aboard died in the crossing; the children survived, absorbing Void energy in a way that left them with uncontrollable and destructive power. The Orokin, fearful of what the children could do, considered executing them until Margulis -- a researcher and healer -- argued for a different approach.

Margulis created the Transference system and the dream-state Reservoir as a way to keep the Tenno's Void energy channeled into the Warframes while their physical forms remained safe and dormant. The Lotus, who players knew as their mission controller and guide, is revealed to be a caretaker who took on the role of protecting the Tenno after Margulis was executed by the Orokin for refusing to weaponize them.

Hunhow is a Sentient -- an artificial life form created by the Orokin to construct things in environments hostile to biological life, but which the Orokin eventually turned against during the Old War. Sentients can absorb and adapt to damage types used against them, making conventional Warframe combat ineffective. The Tenno's Void energy is one of the few things that can reliably damage Sentients, which is what made the Tenno valuable as weapons during the Old War and what now makes Hunhow determined to destroy them.

The quest establishes that the relationship between the Operator and the Warframe is the central mystery of Warframe's lore: a sleeping child controlling a living weapon through a dream.

## Tips

- The Spy vaults at Umbriel use standard Uranus tileset layouts; Warframes with stealth abilities simplify the approach.
- Grimaldi on Lua includes Sentient enemies that adapt to damage types. Switching between damage types on the fly prevents full immunity from forming.
- Choosing a Focus school at quest completion locks that school as the primary, but additional schools can be unlocked later through the Focus menu with Void Traces.
- The Shadow Stalker drops the Broken War during the final stage; this item is necessary to craft the full War blade later.
- After completion, Lua missions offer Axi relics and special challenge rooms with unique Arcane rewards not found elsewhere.
