## Overview

The Silver Grove is a side quest released in Update 19.5 in January 2017. It is initiated through New Loka, one of the six Tenno syndicates, though no standing requirement with New Loka is needed to begin. The quest centers on protecting a sacred grove on Earth from Grineer desecration by crafting three ritual offerings called Apothics from botanical specimens harvested across the Origin System. The primary reward is the complete blueprint set for Titania, a fairy-themed Warframe introduced alongside the quest.

The quest structure is distinct from most main-line content. Rather than navigating a series of linear missions, players spend significant time between each stage gathering botanical samples from various planets. Each sample type grows in specific environments on specific planets, and several are found only during day or night cycles in open-world areas or in particular Earth biome tiles. This scanning-and-crafting loop gives The Silver Grove a slower, more methodical pace compared to combat-heavy quests.

Because each Apothic also requires Morphics and Credits alongside the botanical materials, players should ensure they have raw material supplies before beginning. The quest cannot be paused mid-Apothic; once the botanical crafting window opens, the materials should be ready.

## Prerequisites

The Silver Grove requires completion of [The Second Dream](/quests/the-second-dream) and reaching Mastery Rank 7. Mastery Rank 7 is a relatively early mid-game milestone, achievable by leveling a handful of Warframes and weapons to rank 30. Players who have completed The Second Dream and are progressing through the main star chart will generally encounter this quest within a reasonable period.

The quest begins via a message from Amaryn of New Loka, received once the prerequisites are met. No alliance with New Loka or any minimum New Loka standing is needed; the message arrives regardless of syndicate choices.

## Stages

The Silver Grove divides into three main stages, each structured around crafting an Apothic and then using it at the shrine located at E Prime on Earth.

### Nightfall Apothic

The first Apothic requires gathering Dusklight Sarracenia from the Grineer Settlement tileset on Ceres, Moonlight Dragonlilies from the Grineer Forest tileset on Earth during night, and Sunlight Threshcones from Earth during day. Beyond the botanicals, the recipe calls for Morphics and Credits.

Once crafted, the Nightfall Apothic is used at the shrine in E Prime. Anointing the shrine summons a Specter -- a Grineer-model Loki variant called the Loki Knave Specter, accompanied by clone support units. Defeating the Specter grants the Titania Systems Blueprint, the first of the four component blueprints.

### Twilight Apothic

The second Apothic requires Moonlight Jadeleaf from the Grineer Forest tileset on Earth at night, Ruk's Claw from the Grineer Settlement tileset on Mars, and Lunar Pitcher from Lua. The Lunar Pitcher is notable because it only grows on the Lua tileset, requiring players to run Lua missions specifically to find it. Morphics and Credits round out the recipe.

Using the Twilight Apothic at the shrine summons a Saryn Orphid Specter, a more mobile and poison-oriented encounter than the first. Defeating it grants the Titania Chassis Blueprint.

### Sunrise Apothic

The final Apothic calls for Sunlight Jadeleaf from Earth's Grineer Forest at dawn, Frostleaf from Uranus's Grineer Galleon tileset, and Vestan Moss from the Grineer Settlement tileset on Ceres or Mercury. Morphics and Credits are again required.

The Sunrise Apothic stage is more demanding than the first two. Anointing the shrine triggers a two-wave encounter: first, an Oberon Feyarch Specter must be defeated. Then a sustained two-minute defense sequence begins against a mixed Grineer assault force including Napalm units, Flameblade melee fighters, Elite Lancers, and Hyekka Masters with their flamethrower Hyekka companions. Surviving the defense and defeating all enemies grants the Titania Neuroptics Blueprint.

The full Titania Blueprint is awarded at quest completion, separate from the component blueprints earned during each stage.

## Rewards

Completing all three stages and the final quest resolution provides the complete manufacturing set for Titania:

- Titania Warframe Blueprint (main, awarded at quest completion)
- Titania Systems Blueprint (awarded at Nightfall Apothic stage)
- Titania Chassis Blueprint (awarded at Twilight Apothic stage)
- Titania Neuroptics Blueprint (awarded at Sunrise Apothic stage)

Each component still requires Credits and materials to build in the Foundry after the blueprints are obtained. Titania does not come pre-built; players must manufacture all four components and then assemble the Warframe as with any crafted frame.

After the quest is completed, the Silver Grove tile in Earth missions becomes a persistent encounter location. Players who return to that specific grove tile can anoint the shrine again using additional Apothics to summon the Specters for their Arcane drops. The Specters each carry unique Arcanes not available elsewhere, which provides a long-term reason to revisit the quest's crafting loop even after Titania is obtained.

## Lore

<Callout type="warn">Spoilers for The Silver Grove follow.</Callout>

The Silver Grove is revealed to be an artificially maintained ecosystem engineered by Silvana, an Orokin Archimedean -- a rank denoting an advanced researcher -- who diverted resources earmarked for the war effort into restoring Earth's devastated ecology. Silvana was motivated by a refusal to accept that everything should be subordinated to conflict, a stance that put her in direct opposition to the Orokin's priorities during the Old War.

Silvana created Titania as the grove's protector, binding a Warframe to the location through a form of permanent Transference that anchored Titania's consciousness there. When the Orokin sent forces to reclaim the diverted resources and punish Silvana, Titania died defending both Silvana and the grove. The result was a Warframe whose consciousness was dissolved into the grove itself rather than surviving in a coherent Transference state -- which is why Titania must be recovered through the Apothic ritual rather than found as a straightforward blueprint.

New Loka's interest in the grove stems from their belief in the sanctity of unmodified Earth ecology. They see the grove as evidence of what Earth was before Orokin and Grineer interference, though Silvana's grove was itself a deliberate artificial creation rather than a natural remnant. The quest leaves this irony implicit; the New Loka representative Amaryn presents the grove as natural without acknowledging that it was engineered.

The Specter encounters at the shrine represent echoes of the grove's defenders over time. The Loki, Saryn, and Oberon Specters in Grineer-corrupted forms suggest that the grove has been attacked before and that its history involves layers of violence the Apothic ritual is repeating in condensed form.

## Tips

- Use a Warframe with a codex scanner augment or the Helios Sentinel equipped with Investigator to automate scanning. Helios scans enemies automatically; for plants, a manual scanner or the Sol-Battery Widget from Cetus is required.
- The Lunaro Widget purchased from Quills in Cetus allows players to see plant scan locations on the minimap, significantly reducing search time in Earth tiles.
- Moonlight and Sunlight variants of the same plant (Jadeleaf, Threshcone) require different times of day. The Earth day/night cycle is approximately four real-time minutes per phase, so players may need to wait in a mission briefly for the cycle to shift.
- Vestan Moss is small and grows on rock surfaces; scan the lower levels of Ceres and Mercury Grineer Settlement tiles near pipes and structural supports.
- The Hyekka Masters in the Sunrise Apothic defense bring Hyekkas that apply fire damage rapidly. Bringing a cold or toxin-dominant weapon helps manage the fire status stacks during the survival window.
- After completing the quest, Apothics can be re-crafted and used at the shrine to farm the Arcane drops from each Specter, which are distinct rewards not tied to quest completion.
