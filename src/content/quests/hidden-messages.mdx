## Overview

Hidden Messages is a short quest that tasks players with solving three cryptic riddles, each pointing toward a specific mission node on the star chart. The prize waiting at the end is Mirage, an agile and high-damage Warframe whose component blueprints are distributed one per riddle solved. The quest is notable for its puzzle structure: rather than directing players to a waypoint, each stage presents a riddle written in verse, and the player must identify the correct mission location from contextual clues.

The riddles draw on Greek mythology, with mission locations named after figures and places from classical antiquity. Solving them without external assistance requires careful reading of the verse and cross-referencing the star chart. Community resources have documented all three solutions, but the riddles are designed to be solvable through the in-game map alone.

Hidden Messages was introduced in Update 14.0, which simultaneously added Mirage, her associated augments, and the Mirage Prime variant that followed later. It remains one of the few quests in the game that requires active intellectual engagement rather than pure combat or traversal, making it a distinct entry in the quest catalog.

## Prerequisites

To access Hidden Messages, players must complete the Pluto to Sedna Junction. Once that junction is cleared, the quest becomes available in the Codex under the Quests tab. It must be manually started from the Codex; no inbox message triggers it automatically. There is no Mastery Rank requirement beyond whatever rank the junction demands.

The three missions that make up the quest -- Olympus on Mars, Calypso on Saturn, and Charybdis on Sedna -- vary in level and mission type. Players should have a reasonably leveled Warframe and weapons before attempting Charybdis in particular, since it sits in a higher level range than the earlier stages.

## Stages

### Solve Riddle One

The first riddle points to Olympus, a level-30 Exterminate mission on Mars played on an Infested Ship tileset. The riddle contains references consistent with the mythological Mount Olympus and the planet it names. The objective is a straightforward exterminate: locate and eliminate the required number of Infested enemies. Upon completion, Mirage Neuroptics Blueprint is awarded automatically.

### Solve Riddle Two

The second riddle directs players to Calypso, a Survival mission on Saturn also set inside an Infested Ship. Players must survive for 15 minutes to complete the stage objective. Calypso references the mythological nymph of the same name, and the Saturn location narrows the guess considerably for players who know the star chart layout. Completing the survival timer awards the Mirage Systems Blueprint.

### Solve Riddle Three

The third and final riddle leads to Charybdis, a Hive Sabotage mission on Sedna. The mission type is more involved than the previous two: players must locate and destroy Infested Hives scattered across the tileset rather than completing a linear objective. Charybdis is the name of the mythological sea monster featured in Homer's Odyssey, fitting thematically with the quest's classical motif. Completing this stage grants the Mirage Chassis Blueprint and concludes the quest.

## Rewards

Hidden Messages awards the three component blueprints needed to build the Mirage Warframe:

- Mirage Neuroptics Blueprint -- awarded upon completing Stage 1 (Olympus, Mars).
- Mirage Systems Blueprint -- awarded upon completing Stage 2 (Calypso, Saturn).
- Mirage Chassis Blueprint -- awarded upon completing Stage 3 (Charybdis, Sedna).

The Mirage main blueprint must be purchased separately from the in-game Market for credits. Once all four blueprints are collected and the components are built, Mirage can be assembled in the Foundry. Build times apply to each component separately, followed by the final assembly.

Mirage is not otherwise obtainable outside of trading with other players for her parts. The quest is non-replayable; after completion the stages disappear from the Codex, and the reward blueprints cannot be obtained again through this quest.

## Lore

<Callout type="warn">Spoilers for Hidden Messages follow.</Callout>

The transmissions received by Lotus during the quest reveal that Mirage was not lost through combat but through betrayal. She was sealed away by her own faction -- ostensibly because her actions, however effective, were deemed too dangerous or uncontrollable. The transmissions are fragmented and poetic, matching the riddle format of the quest itself, suggesting that Mirage encoded her own location deliberately, leaving a trail for whoever might come looking.

Her imprisonment predates the fall of the Orokin Empire, placing her confinement during the Old War -- the same conflict that shaped most Warframe lore. The nature of her offense and the identity of those who sealed her are left deliberately vague, consistent with the quest's theme of incomplete truths and riddles without full answers.

Mirage's kit reflects her personality: illusory, unpredictable, and visually chaotic. Her abilities revolve around creating copies of herself, distorting light, and overwhelming enemies with spectacle. The contrast between her playful kit and the grave circumstances of her imprisonment is intentional.

## Tips

- The riddles are solvable without external help. Read each verse carefully and match the mythological and geographic clues to star chart locations.
- Olympus and Calypso are low-to-mid level missions. A Warframe capable of handling level-30 enemies comfortably will clear them without difficulty.
- Charybdis on Sedna is the hardest stage. The Hive Sabotage mission type requires locating multiple objectives in an Infested environment with denser enemy spawns. Mobility helps considerably.
- The Mirage main blueprint is not a quest reward. It must be purchased from the Market for credits after completing the quest.
- All three component blueprints have standard Foundry build times. Plan accordingly if rushing toward a specific Mastery goal.
