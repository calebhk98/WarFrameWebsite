## Overview

Whispers in the Walls is a story quest released in Update 35 (December 2023). It is set entirely within Albrecht Entrati's hidden laboratory beneath Deimos -- the Sanctum Anatomica. The quest centers on uncovering the final research of Albrecht Entrati, the Orokin scientist who founded the Entrati family and secretly studied a boundary between the known universe and a realm of strange entities he called the Murmur. As the narrative unfolds, the Murmur faction -- Void-twisted humanoids spawned from Albrecht's failed experiments -- are revealed as a direct consequence of his research, and the quest tasks players with navigating the Sanctum Anatomica to piece together what he discovered.

The quest introduces several new systems: the Sanctum Anatomica functions as a new open-world hub with its own vendors and progression; Tome mods, a new mod category granting passive bonuses from ancient Entrati research texts, become accessible upon completion; and the Murmur faction appears as a recurring enemy type with unique resistances and behaviors. The warframe Dante, who embodies themes of Entrati scholarship, was introduced in connection with the Whispers in the Walls update.

Completing this quest is a prerequisite for engaging with the Sanctum Anatomica hub and its associated content.

## Prerequisites

The sole quest prerequisite is completion of Heart of Deimos, which establishes the Entrati family, grants access to the Cambion Drift open world, and introduces players to Deimos as a location. Without Heart of Deimos completed, the Whispers in the Walls quest node is not visible.

Players should also have sufficient mastery and gear to navigate moderately difficult Infested and Murmur combat encounters within the Sanctum Anatomica. The quest itself does not impose a Mastery Rank floor, but enemies encountered during stages are tuned higher than standard Deimos content.

## Stages

Whispers in the Walls unfolds across five stages set within the Sanctum Anatomica and surrounding Cambion Drift.

### The Anomaly

Strange signal disturbances are detected beneath the Cambion Drift. Loid, the Entrati family's Cephalon, contacts the Operator with evidence that activity is originating from a sealed portion of Deimos that the Entrati family never fully mapped. Investigation reveals a concealed entrance to Albrecht Entrati's private research laboratory -- the Sanctum Anatomica -- sealed since Albrecht's disappearance centuries ago. The stage introduces the Sanctum Anatomica tileset and establishes Albrecht as the central figure of the quest.

### Descent

The Operator descends into the Sanctum Anatomica and begins reading Albrecht Entrati's personal research journals, scattered throughout the laboratory. These logs detail his experiments with a phenomenon he described as the rift -- a boundary between the material plane and a secondary realm populated by entities he called the Murmur. The journals reveal he was attempting to communicate with these entities, convinced they held knowledge of the Void. The stage alternates between exploration of the lab and reading of journal fragments that reconstruct Albrecht's increasingly desperate research.

### The Murmur

Hostile Murmur faction enemies emerge from deeper within the Sanctum Anatomica -- Void-twisted humanoids that are the direct result of Albrecht's experiments destabilizing the rift boundary. The Operator must repel multiple Murmur assault waves while continuing to gather research data from the laboratory. This stage introduces the Murmur as a combat faction, showing their abilities and resistances. The scale of Albrecht's mistake becomes clear: his research did not merely open a theoretical door but created a sustained breach, spawning an ongoing source of Murmur incursions.

### Albrecht's Legacy

With sufficient journal fragments collected, the Operator assembles Albrecht's final research conclusions. The rift he opened between worlds was not an accident of carelessness but an intentional gamble -- Albrecht believed the entities on the other side possessed transformative knowledge about the Void and the nature of Warframe biology. His final entries express both the weight of what he unleashed and his conviction that the research was worth the cost. The stage requires the Operator to access Albrecht's sealed data core at the center of the Sanctum Anatomica.

### Through the Rift

The Operator navigates a partial incursion into the rift boundary itself to seal the most dangerous active breach within the laboratory. The encounter draws on Void abilities and involves direct confrontation with Murmur leadership-class entities. The rift is not fully closed -- the breach is too deeply embedded in the Sanctum Anatomica's architecture -- but the immediate threat is contained. The quest concludes with the Sanctum Anatomica stabilized enough for use as a permanent hub, and Loid begins cataloguing Albrecht's research for future study.

## Rewards

Completing Whispers in the Walls grants access to the Sanctum Anatomica as a new hub area within Deimos. The Sanctum Anatomica contains dedicated vendors, a new progression track tied to Murmur research standing, and access to Tome mods -- a category of mods encoding Albrecht Entrati's accumulated research into passive gameplay bonuses.

Introductory Tome mod rewards are granted directly upon completion as a first reward for accessing the new system. Additional Tome mods are earned through Sanctum Anatomica activities and vendor standing.

The Murmur faction becomes accessible as a recurring enemy type in repeatable Sanctum Anatomica missions, enabling farming of Murmur-specific drops including materials unique to the new hub. Players also gain narrative context that informs subsequent Entrati-related content updates tied to the Whispers expansion.

## Lore

<Callout type="warn">Spoilers for Whispers in the Walls follow.</Callout>

The core revelation of Whispers in the Walls is the true scope of Albrecht Entrati's research. Albrecht was not merely an Infested biologist -- he was investigating the boundary between the material universe and a secondary realm he believed held ancient Orokin secrets about the origin of the Void and the biological basis of Warframe construction. His research journals document a scientist increasingly consumed by the conviction that communication with entities in the rift would yield transformative knowledge.

The Murmur are not natural Infested. They are Void-twisted humanoids -- former Entrati researchers and subjects who were caught in the rift boundary when Albrecht's experiments destabilized it. Over centuries of exposure, these individuals were transformed into something neither fully Infested nor Void-touched but a hybrid state with its own emergent behaviors and faction identity. Their aggression toward the living is not simple hostility but a distorted reflection of their original purpose: protecting the research within the Sanctum Anatomica and preventing unauthorized access.

Albrecht's final journal entries suggest he understood what he had done and chose to seal himself within the Sanctum Anatomica rather than risk the breach spreading further. His fate is left ambiguous by the quest; his absence is confirmed, but whether he survived in some form within the rift boundary, or was consumed by the Murmur, is not resolved in this installment. This ambiguity positions Albrecht as a recurring mystery for future content in the Entrati narrative arc.

The quest also establishes that Albrecht's research touched directly on the Warframe design process, implying that some Warframe biological traits may have been derived from his rift experiments. This connects the Whispers storyline to the broader questions about Warframe origin that run throughout the game's lore.

## Tips

- Murmur enemies have unusual damage resistances -- standard Infested-effective loadouts do not translate directly. Void damage from Operator Amps is effective against Murmur leadership-class enemies.
- Reading all available journal fragments during the Descent and Albrecht's Legacy stages is not required for progression but provides narrative context that makes subsequent Sanctum Anatomica content more legible.
- The Sanctum Anatomica hub vendors use a standing system independent of existing Entrati standing. Prioritize early standing activities after quest completion to unlock Tome mod slots faster.
- The rift navigation segment in Through the Rift is mechanically distinct from standard combat. Bring sufficient Operator Focus school bonuses (Zenurik for energy recovery, Vazarin for survivability) for this sequence.
