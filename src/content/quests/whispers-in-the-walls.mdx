## Overview

Whispers in the Walls is a quest released as part of the Zariman storyline expansion, continuing the narrative thread established by The Zariman quest. It serves as a bridge between the Entrati family's investigations of the void-touched vessel and the deeper mysteries lurking within the Zariman's submerged sections. Unlike many major story quests, Whispers in the Walls focuses on exploration and atmosphere rather than heavy combat encounters, emphasizing the unsettling nature of the Zariman itself as a character.

The quest invites players to venture beneath the known sections of the Zariman, into regions where the Void's influence has created strange geometry and otherworldly structures. Loid, the Orokin Cephalon consciousness embedded within the ship's systems, has detected unusual energy signatures emanating from the depths. The quest explores what lies in the abyss and what the consequences might be for the Zariman and the System beyond.

Completing Whispers in the Walls unlocks access to LOID Specters -- versions of Loid's consciousness that can be deployed as tactical support during missions. The quest also expands the playable areas within the Zariman, opening the Abyssal Zone tileset and introducing new enemy types that dwell in the deeper void-touched regions.

## Prerequisites

Two prerequisites must be satisfied before accessing Whispers in the Walls. The primary requirement is completion of The Zariman, the introductory quest that grants initial access to the void-touched vessel and establishes the Entrati family's role in the narrative. Players without access to The Zariman cannot begin Whispers in the Walls.

The secondary requirement is Mastery Rank 1, which is accessible to nearly all active accounts. This is a baseline check ensuring players have completed at least the introductory quests and tutorial content. There are no restricted gear or special item requirements for the quest itself, though bringing equipment suitable for Void-contaminated environments is tactically advisable.

The quest is solo-capable but can be approached as a squad. There are no restrictions preventing cooperative play, unlike some story-heavy quests. However, the narrative themes and dialogue are delivered to the Operator individually, making the experience feel more impactful when approached alone.

## Stages

Whispers in the Walls unfolds across five stages, each taking the Tenno deeper into the Zariman's unknown territories.

### Something Strange

The quest opens within familiar Zariman corridors where Loid reaches out with unusual urgency. Unlike previous interactions, Loid expresses concern -- a rare emotional state for the Cephalon. He describes detecting faint signatures far deeper than any inhabited section of the ship, regions that pre-date even the earliest Orokin infrastructure he can access. The Tenno is directed to investigate sensor arrays and data terminals scattered throughout the upper tiers, gathering readings that confirm the presence of something active in the depths.

This stage serves as an exposition and tutorial for the quest's exploration mechanics. Players scan multiple terminals and retrieve data logs, building environmental context about the Zariman's lower levels. The tone is deliberately eerie: flickering lights, distorted comms, and the ever-present hum of void-touched machinery create a sense of something dormant beginning to stir.

### Descent into Layers

The Tenno proceeds into the Zariman's lower tiers -- sections not accessible in previous quests. These areas are partially flooded with void-infused liquids that shimmer with unnatural colors. The architecture becomes more organic and less purely mechanical; walls curve and fold in ways that seem to defy standard engineering. Enemies here are void-contaminated versions of standard types, exhibiting erratic behavior and distorted appearances.

This stage emphasizes traversal and puzzle-solving over combat. The Tenno must navigate mazes of collapsing corridors, activate obsolete machinery to restore pathways, and solve environmental challenges tied to the Void's effects on physics and space. Loid provides occasional commentary, expressing increasing concern as his sensors detect the presence growing more active, more aware.

### Voices in the Void

The Tenno enters the newly accessible Abyssal Zone -- a tileset expansion that represents the outermost boundaries of the Zariman's known structure. Here, the ship's frame is visibly damaged, with breaches opening onto the raw Void. Strange geometric structures created by Void-warping float in the space between traditional corridors. The environment is hostile and disorienting, with gravity fluctuating and spatial dimensions becoming non-Euclidean.

Combat encounters here are heavy, featuring enemy waves of void-corrupted creatures alongside some new, completely alien entities. Loid's communications become fragmented and difficult to parse. The Operator begins hearing other voices -- whispers in frequencies just below normal audio range, singing in a language that predates the Orokin. This stage builds to a climactic moment where the Operator realizes they are not just being observed but actively communicated with by the presence in the abyss.

### The Presence Awakens

At the Abyss Terminus, the deepest accessible point within the Zariman's structure, the Tenno faces the entity directly. The encounter is not a conventional boss fight. Instead, the presence -- a being of void-touched consciousness that predates Orokin contact -- manifests itself through environmental hazards and psychological/spatial challenges. The Operator engages in a combination of combat and negotiation, with dialogue options affecting how the encounter concludes.

The presence is revealed to be a fragment of something much larger, a consciousness trapped within the Zariman's deepest point, possibly for millennia. It communicates its isolation, its hunger for connection, and its confusion about the passing of ages. The void has sustained it, but it has also hollowed it out, leaving only echoes of what it once was. The Operator learns that the Zariman itself may have been constructed partly as a container for this entity -- that the Orokin knew it was there and sealed it away.

### A New Accord

Rather than destruction, the final stage involves reaching an understanding with the presence. The Operator returns to the upper levels of the Zariman with new knowledge, reporting to the Entrati family what lies beneath their ship. The implications are significant: the Zariman is not just a void-touched ruin but a prison or sanctuary, depending on one's perspective. The presence agrees to remain dormant, but a connection between the Operator and the entity is established, enabling the creation of LOID Specters -- projected fragments of the presence's consciousness that can serve the Tenno in future missions.

The quest ends not with triumph but with acceptance. The Tenno has not saved the System or unlocked a great power, but has instead made peace with something ancient and unknowable. Loid's final transmissions acknowledge the Operator as the first being to truly communicate with the depths in countless cycles, positioning the Tenno as a unique bridge between the Zariman's mysteries and the wider System.

## Rewards

Whispers in the Walls provides several meaningful rewards that extend beyond the standard quest completion.

The primary reward is LOID Specter access. Specters are autonomous instances of Loid's consciousness that can be deployed on missions to provide support. Unlike traditional Specters that mimic player builds, LOID Specters function independently with their own abilities and focus on support roles -- area denial, crowd control, and hacking assistance. They can be customized to some degree and become more effective as the Operator gains intimacy with the void-touched presence.

Expansion of the Zariman tileset unlocks access to the Abyssal Zone during standard Zariman missions, effectively expanding the playable area and introducing new mission types unique to the deeper regions. New enemies spawn in these zones, including void-corrupted variants and creatures entirely alien to standard Warframe enemies.

A series of cosmetics tied to the quest reward are unlocked, including Loid-themed cosmetics for the player character, void-touched weapon skins, and a new interior decoration set for the player's orbiter that evokes the Abyssal Zone's visual aesthetic.

## Lore

<Callout type="warn">Major lore spoilers follow for Whispers in the Walls and the Zariman questline.</Callout>

The revelation at the heart of Whispers in the Walls concerns the true nature of the Zariman and what the Orokin sealed within it. The entity inhabiting the abyss is not a creation of the Orokin but something they encountered -- possibly something they feared. The exact origin of the presence remains deliberately ambiguous: it could be a fragment of a larger being, a consciousness born from the Void itself, or something that predates even the Orokin civilization.

The Orokin's decision to seal it within the Zariman rather than destroy it suggests several possibilities. Perhaps they lacked the means to destroy it. Perhaps they believed such power should be preserved rather than annihilated. Perhaps the entity was valuable as a source of information or power, and the Zariman served as a containment chamber and sanctuary simultaneously.

The quest's narrative implies that the Zariman's void-touched nature is not accidental but by design. The ship was built in a way that channels void energies through its structure, potentially to sustain or control the imprisoned presence. The Entrati family's investigation of the Zariman over the centuries has been, in some sense, living atop a sealed and forgotten tomb without fully understanding what sleeps beneath their home.

The Operator's establishment of a pact with the presence has implications for the broader narrative. Some Zariman residents suspect the Tenno are collaborating with something dangerous. Others see the connection as the first genuine peace possible with the ship's deepest secret. The presence's willingness to create Specters suggests it harbors no malice toward the Tenno specifically, but its ultimate goals and nature remain largely mysterious -- a perfect setup for future narrative developments.

The quest leaves open the question of whether the presence is truly dormant or merely appearing to be, and whether the pact is durable or temporary. In Warframe's tradition of incomplete resolutions, Whispers in the Walls offers understanding but not certainty, knowledge but not mastery.

## Tips

- LOID Specters inherit stats from Loid's own progression and can be customized through the Specter loadout interface. Experiment with different configurations to find supportive builds that complement your preferred playstyle.
- The Abyssal Zone tileset has multiple hidden rooms and secret objectives. Exploring thoroughly during the quest itself unlocks some cosmetics early; others become available as repeatable mission rewards.
- Void-corrupted enemies in the lower levels have unusual weaknesses and resistances compared to their standard counterparts. Void damage is particularly effective, while Magnetic damage is less efficient. Adjust your loadout accordingly.
- Audio design in Whispers in the Walls is deliberate and unsettling. Playing with headphones enhances the experience and makes certain audio cues (the whispers themselves) more perceptible. This is intentional design, not a bug.
- The dialogue options in the Presence Awakens stage affect the tone of the conclusion but do not substantially change the mechanical outcome. Your choices influence how the Operator interprets their relationship with the presence going forward, affecting how Specters are described narratively in later quests.
