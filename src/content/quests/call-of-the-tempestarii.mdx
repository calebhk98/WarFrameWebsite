## Overview

Call of the Tempestarii is a Railjack-integrated quest released in Update 30.0 (April 2021) that introduces the Warframe Sevagoth and ties Void Storm activity to a ghost ship drifting through Corpus space. The quest requires a functional Railjack and completion of The Deadlock Protocol, anchoring it firmly in the mid-to-late game Railjack progression arc.

The central antagonist in the quest's frame is Corpus captain Vala Glarios, who commands the Parvos Granum-aligned forces attempting to salvage or destroy the mysterious vessel known as the Tempestarii. The Tenno, guided by Cephalon Cy aboard their Railjack, must investigate the source of Void Storms that are disrupting Corpus supply lines in the Proxima regions. What they find is not an enemy weapon but an ancient ship crewed only by a fractured shadow -- the disembodied essence of a Warframe pilot who could not bring himself to leave the post he had sworn to keep.

The quest is notable for its presentation of Sevagoth's backstory through environmental exploration and data vault retrieval rather than direct cutscenes. Players piece together the tragedy of the Tempestarii's last mission through hacked archives and the behavior of Sevagoth's Shadow, which alternates between defending the ship and pleading for release. Call of the Tempestarii is a relatively short quest by major-content standards, comprising three mission nodes, but it delivers a self-contained story arc with strong atmospheric design.

Players who already own Sevagoth at the time of completion will receive a Riven Mod in place of the Sevagoth Blueprint. All cosmetic and Captura rewards are granted regardless of Sevagoth ownership status.

## Prerequisites

Before starting Call of the Tempestarii, the following conditions must be met:

- **The Deadlock Protocol completed** -- The Deadlock Protocol introduces Parvos Granum and the Corpus Proxima region. Completing it is required to unlock access to Pluto Proxima, where two of the quest's three missions take place.
- **Railjack ownership** -- The quest missions are Railjack missions and cannot be completed without a built Railjack. Players who have not completed Rising Tide and built their Railjack cannot start this quest. A Mastery Rank 4 minimum is also required to own a Railjack.
- **Mastery Rank 4** -- The minimum Mastery Rank required to equip and operate the Railjack systems involved in the quest missions.

Players should also ensure their Railjack has a reasonable complement of weapons and avionics before beginning, as the mission nodes scale to Proxima-level enemies. The quest itself is designed around Railjack skirmish gameplay with boarding sequences, so familiarity with the Railjack combat loop is beneficial.

## Stages

Call of the Tempestarii proceeds through three main mission nodes, each building on the previous stage's discoveries.

**1. Intercept Corpus Shipping Convoy (Arc Silver, Veil Proxima)**

Cephalon Cy identifies unusual Void Storm activity clustered around Corpus shipping routes in the Veil Proxima. The Tenno intercept a convoy and encounter Vala Glarios commanding Corpus forces. During the engagement, a spectral Railjack-class vessel appears amid the Void disturbance -- the Tempestarii -- and demonstrates hostile behavior before vanishing. This first contact establishes the mystery at the heart of the quest and marks Vala Glarios as a recurring obstacle.

**2. Infiltrate Corpus Archives (Seven Sirens, Pluto Proxima)**

Following leads recovered from the convoy, the Tenno travel to a Corpus archive vessel in the Pluto Proxima. The mission requires boarding the enemy ship and completing a spy objective, hacking three data vaults to extract historical records. The recovered archives reveal the Tempestarii's origin as an Old War-era rescue vessel and identify its pilot as a Warframe operator who sacrificed himself to the Void to save colonists stranded during an emergency. The ship has been drifting, crewed only by its pilot's Shadow, for centuries. The Shadow's bond to the rescue mission it could never complete is generating the Void Storm anomalies.

**3. Signal the Tempestarii (Obol Crossing, Pluto Proxima)**

Armed with the Tempestarii's transponder codes recovered from the archives, the Tenno broadcast a distress signal designed to draw the ghost ship out. When the Tempestarii responds, the Tenno board it and must defend the vessel against a Corpus assault led by Vala Glarios, who intends to capture the ship for Parvos Granum. The Tenno destroy Corpus beam generators trying to immobilize the Tempestarii, then locate Sevagoth's physical remains preserved aboard the ship. Reuniting the body with his Shadow releases Sevagoth from his centuries-long vigil. Sevagoth's remains -- now reconstituted as a viable Warframe -- are taken aboard the orbiter. The Tempestarii itself is set adrift into the Void, completing the burial rite that Sevagoth had sought.

## Rewards

Completing Call of the Tempestarii grants the following:

- **Sevagoth Blueprint** -- The main blueprint for crafting the Sevagoth Warframe. Component blueprints (Neuroptics, Chassis, Systems) are obtained separately from Void Storm missions across the Proxima regions. Players who already own Sevagoth receive a Riven Mod instead.
- **Tempestarii Railjack Skin** -- A cosmetic skin for the Railjack styled after the ghost ship Tempestarii. Applied from the Railjack customization menu in the Dry Dock.
- **Sevagoth Prex Card** -- A collectible card item stored in the Codex.
- **Sevagoth Statuette** -- A Captura scene prop item.
- **The Cold Below Captura Scene** -- A Captura environment representing the frozen void interior associated with Sevagoth's story.

Sevagoth's component blueprints are not quest rewards; they drop from Void Storm missions in the Veil Proxima, Neptune Proxima, and Pluto Proxima. Each component has a drop chance distributed across mission completion rewards, making Sevagoth a farm-after-quest acquisition rather than a direct story reward.

## Lore

Sevagoth's history predates the modern era of the Origin System. During the Old War, when the Orokin civilization still commanded the system and deployed Warframes against the Sentient threat, the Tempestarii served as a rescue vessel tasked with recovering civilian and military casualties from contested zones. The Warframe pilot who crewed it -- the being who would become Sevagoth -- made a fateful choice during a rescue operation that left his ship stranded at the edge of a Void rift.

The colonists he was transporting could not survive a Void transit, but the rift was closing and no conventional escape route remained. Sevagoth's pilot chose to draw the ship into the Void, expending himself in the process to cushion the transit enough for the colonists to survive. The colonists escaped. The pilot did not. His body remained aboard the Tempestarii, locked in the Void, while his Shadow -- the part of the Warframe's psyche that persisted as a semi-autonomous entity -- continued to perform its rescue protocols, broadcasting distress signals and intercepting passing vessels, for centuries.

This is the source of the Void Storms. Sevagoth's Shadow, separated from its body and unable to complete either its rescue mission or its own death, generated cascading Void energy disturbances that spread into Corpus territory as the game's current era began. The storms themselves served as Sevagoth's unconscious call for help -- a signal pattern that Cephalon Cy recognized as anomalous, triggering the quest's opening.

Vala Glarios represents the Corpus response to the storms: an opportunist who saw the Void disturbances as a potential energy or weapons source to be claimed for Parvos Granum's network. Her repeated interference throughout the quest frames the conflict as a race between the Tenno seeking to honor the dead and a Corpus faction seeking to exploit them.

The conclusion of the quest -- physically returning Sevagoth's body to his Shadow and launching both into the Void -- is framed as a final act of rescue, completing the mission Sevagoth started. The Warframe that the Tenno receive is described as reconstituted from this reunion, a dark-aspected frame whose abilities reflect both the Shadow's predatory nature and the original pilot's drive to protect others.
