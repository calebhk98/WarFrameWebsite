## Overview

Once Awake is an introductory quest that introduces players to the Infested faction and two core mission types: Spy and Defense. It is designed as an early-game experience, accessible immediately after completing the Mercury Junction from the starting star chart nodes unlocked during [Vor's Prize](/quests/vors-prize). The quest positions itself as a natural next step for players who have completed the tutorial chain and want to explore the narrative content available in the early star chart.

The story follows intercepted Grineer communications that reveal an illegal bioweapon program being conducted by Dr. Tengus, a Grineer scientist operating outside standard military protocols. His research involves deliberately cultivating the Infestation as a weapon. When the containment fails, the Infestation spreads through Mercury facilities, transforming Grineer soldiers and threatening to expand further into the Origin System.

The Lotus directs the Tenno to investigate, contain, and ultimately destroy the outbreak using a specialized weapon: a Cascade Bomb enhanced with Void energy, capable of eliminating Infested biological matter at scale. The quest concludes with the immediate threat neutralized, though Tengus himself survives, leaving the door open for further Infested-related storylines that develop in subsequent quests like [Patient Zero](/quests/patient-zero).

Once Awake is replayable following completion, having been updated in Update 22.20 (January 2018) to allow new players and veterans alike to experience the quest multiple times. It also plays a role in the broader Cetus introduction, as completing it unlocks access to Saya in Cetus on Earth.

## Prerequisites

Once Awake has minimal requirements, making it accessible to players at a very early point in their progression:

- **Complete Vor's Prize** -- The starter quest that introduces core game systems and opens the initial star chart nodes. Vor's Prize is mandatory and precedes all other quest content.
- **Complete the Mercury Junction** -- The Mercury Junction connects the starting Earth nodes to Mercury's star chart region. Completing it grants access to Mercury's missions, including the nodes where Once Awake takes place.

The quest key is obtained automatically after these prerequisites are met and is accessible from the Codex. No crafting is required to start the quest. Players who are new to the game will encounter this quest very early, often before developing specialized loadouts, so the mission difficulty is tuned accordingly.

## Stages

Once Awake consists of three missions on Mercury, each introducing a distinct mission type and escalating the Infested threat.

**1. Investigate the bio weapon (M Prime, Mercury)**

The first stage is a Spy mission set inside a Grineer asteroid base. Players must recover three data units from secured vault terminals without triggering permanent alarms. Spy missions reward stealth and environmental awareness: each vault is guarded by security systems including laser grids, cameras, and patrolling Grineer soldiers. Triggering a vault's alarm does not necessarily fail the mission, but it locks down the terminal and prevents extraction of that data unit, reducing the potential reward.

For new players, this mission introduces the Spy mechanic and demonstrates that Warframe offers varied objective types beyond simple combat. The intel recovered reveals Dr. Tengus's bioweapon research program and the catastrophic failure that has already occurred at other facilities. The Infested begin to break free from quarantine during the mission itself, overlapping with the primary Spy objective and creating a sense of urgency.

**2. Infestation outbreak (Terminus, Mercury)**

With the scope of the outbreak confirmed, the second stage is an Extermination mission on a Grineer Galleon. The Infested have fully overrun this vessel, and the objective is to eliminate all of them. Extermination missions require players to kill a set number of enemies before extraction becomes available. This stage is a straightforward combat encounter designed to familiarize players with how Infested units behave -- their movement patterns, their lack of projectile weapons compared to Grineer and Corpus, and their tendency to close distance aggressively.

Completing this mission grants the Molten Impact mod as a direct reward. Molten Impact adds Heat damage to melee weapons and is a useful early-game mod for players building their first melee loadout.

**3. Cascade Bomb (Lares, Mercury)**

The final stage is a Defense mission requiring players to protect a power core through eight waves of Infested attackers. The core is a staging platform for the Cascade Bomb -- a device infused with Void energy designed to eliminate Infested biological matter at the cellular level. Players must hold position and repel Infested forces while the bomb arms and detonates.

Defense missions at this stage in the game introduce the concept of holding ground against escalating enemy pressure. Each wave increases enemy count and the aggression of the Infested units, building toward a conclusion that rewards persistence over mobility. Completing the defense successfully triggers the bomb's detonation, destroying the Infested colony on Lares and ending the immediate outbreak.

The Heat Sword Blueprint is awarded upon completing this stage. The Heat Sword is a melee weapon that deals Heat damage alongside physical impact, making it a functional early-game option for players who want a melee weapon with built-in elemental properties.

## Rewards

Once Awake grants two items upon progression through its stages:

- **Molten Impact** -- A melee mod awarded at the end of Stage 2. It adds a flat amount of Heat damage to melee weapons. Heat damage deals Ignite procs to enemies, reducing their movement speed and dealing damage over time. For early-game players, any elemental addition to melee output is meaningful, and Molten Impact fills that role reliably.
- **Heat Sword Blueprint** -- Awarded at the end of Stage 3. The Heat Sword must be crafted in the Foundry using the Blueprint and standard early-game resources. It is not a top-tier weapon in the long run, but it provides players with a craftable melee option at a point where gear options are still limited.

Neither reward is a rare item -- both are accessible through other means as players progress -- but receiving them during a quest ensures early-game players have them without needing to specifically farm their sources.

## Lore

Dr. Tengus occupies an unusual position in Grineer society. The Grineer military is predominantly focused on physical combat and territorial expansion, but Tengus represents the faction's scientific wing -- the same strand of Grineer intellect that produces figures like Tyl Regor and the various Grineer researchers who appear in later quests. His bioweapon program was not officially sanctioned; his research into weaponizing the Infestation was conducted outside standard Grineer military channels, suggesting either independent ambition or backing from a faction within the Grineer leadership that operated outside the Queens' immediate oversight.

The Infested themselves are not a naturally occurring phenomenon. They originate from Technocyte, a Orokin-era biological agent that was developed as a weapon during the Old War. The Orokin created it; the war spread it beyond containment; the Infestation that now occupies segments of the Origin System is the long-decayed and self-propagating remnant of that original weapon. Dr. Tengus was attempting to recapture what the Orokin built -- to harness the Infestation as a controlled military tool rather than a spreading plague.

His failure to maintain containment is the inciting event of Once Awake, but his survival means the research continues. The Patient Zero quest takes up the thread of Infested experimentation with Alad V as the new subject, and the broader Infested storyline remains an ongoing element of Warframe's world. Once Awake establishes the foundational premise: the Infestation is dangerous not because it acts with intelligence of its own, but because it spreads without limit when carelessly handled.

The Cascade Bomb represents the Tenno's pragmatic approach to Infestation threats at this stage in the story. Void energy disrupts Infested biology at a fundamental level -- the same Void that created the Tenno's unique relationship with Warframes also provides tools for destroying threats that conventional weapons struggle to eliminate cleanly.

## Tips

- Spy missions reward stealth, but Once Awake's Spy stage is designed for new players. If a vault's alarm triggers, proceed to the other vaults and accept the reduced reward. The mission does not fail from alarm triggers alone.
- Infested units have no shields, making Slash, Puncture, and Toxin damage particularly effective. Heat damage also works well and creates burning procs. The Heat Sword Blueprint earned from this quest is a natural pairing with the Molten Impact mod.
- Defense missions benefit from controlling enemy approach vectors. The power core at Lares has defined chokepoints; standing near the core rather than spreading out prevents Infested from reaching it from unexpected angles.
- The quest is replayable. Players who want to experience the story a second time can replay it from the Codex without losing any progress or rewards from the initial completion.
