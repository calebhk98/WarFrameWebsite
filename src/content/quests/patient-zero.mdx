## Overview

Patient Zero is a mid-game quest set on Eris that directly follows the Infested storyline introduced in [Once Awake](/quests/once-awake). Where Once Awake established the Infestation as a contained threat on Mercury, Patient Zero reveals that the situation has escalated dramatically: Alad V, a Corpus merchant and recurring antagonist, has been infected and is now actively weaponizing the Infestation to build a Mutalist Empire across the Origin System.

Introduced in Update 14 (July 2014), the quest sends players on a series of intelligence-gathering and sabotage missions across Eris to locate and destroy Alad V's hidden laboratory. The investigation involves intercepting transmissions, triangulating signal sources, and ultimately confronting an Infested pseudo-boss created from a hollow Warframe shell animated by Infested biology.

Completing Patient Zero rewards the Mutalist Alad V Assassinate Key Blueprint, which unlocks access to the [Mutalist Alad V Assassinate](/quests/mutalist-alad-v-assassinate) mission -- the encounter where players fight Alad V directly and farm Mesa Warframe component blueprints. Patient Zero is therefore both a narrative quest and a functional gate that controls access to one of the game's significant boss farms.

The quest is replayable following completion, having been updated in Update 25.7 (September 2019) to support that functionality.

## Prerequisites

Patient Zero requires two prior accomplishments:

- **Complete Once Awake** -- The introductory Infested quest establishes the context for the Infested storyline. Patient Zero continues that narrative directly, and the Lotus's awareness of Dr. Tengus's earlier bioweapon program informs her analysis of Alad V's activities.
- **Eris Junction completion (via Pluto)** -- Eris is a late-midgame star chart region accessible through a Junction on Pluto. Reaching this Junction requires substantial progression through the star chart, including the Neptune and Pluto regions. Players who arrive at Patient Zero will typically be in the mid-game phase of their star chart progression, with access to a range of Warframes and weapons.

The quest key is obtained through the Codex upon meeting these prerequisites. No additional crafting is required to begin. The Eris access requirement naturally filters out newer players, ensuring Patient Zero functions as a mid-game experience rather than an early introduction.

## Stages

Patient Zero unfolds across five missions on Eris, building a picture of Alad V's operations before culminating in direct confrontation.

**1. Mutalist Empire (Naeglar, Eris)**

The quest opens with a Spy mission. The Lotus intercepts communications suggesting that Alad V is coordinating something significant from Eris, and players must infiltrate a Corpus ship to extract intelligence data. Naeglar is a Corpus node despite being in Eris's Infested-dominated region -- the Corpus presence reflects their ongoing attempts to salvage resources and technology from the Infestation zone.

The data recovered confirms what the Lotus suspected: Alad V has been deliberately spreading the Infested plague to ships and stations, creating what he calls a Mutalist Empire. His transmissions reveal a mind still recognizable as Alad V's -- theatrical, self-aggrandizing, convinced of his own vision's grandeur -- but filtered through something deeply wrong. The Infestation has not simply infected him; he has embraced it as a path to power.

**2. First Triangulation (Brugia, Eris)**

Three consecutive Hive Sabotage missions triangulate the position of Alad V's laboratory. Hive Sabotage is an Eris-specific mission type in which players must locate and destroy Infested Hive structures embedded in the environment. Each Hive serves as a biological broadcasting node, and destroying them narrows the signal that has been masking Alad V's lab location.

Brugia is the first of the three triangulation nodes. The Hive Sabotage mechanic requires identifying the Hive chambers -- heavily Infested rooms with distinctive visual architecture -- and destroying the growth pods inside each one. Enemy resistance in these missions is drawn primarily from standard Infested units that spawn in larger numbers as the Hives take damage.

**3. Second Triangulation (Saxis, Eris)**

The second triangulation mission on Saxis mirrors the first in structure but reflects an increased Infested concentration. Alad V has noticed the pattern of interference with his network. The transmissions between missions acknowledge that his systems have detected probes, and his tone shifts from monologue to something more urgent.

**4. Third Triangulation (Kala-azar, Eris)**

The final triangulation on Kala-azar completes the pinpointing of Alad V's laboratory. With all three Hive networks disrupted, the signal masking the lab collapses and the Lotus can trace the exact location. Alad V's communications at this point confirm that he understands what the Tenno are doing and has prepared the lab accordingly.

**5. Destroy the Laboratory (Xini, Eris)**

<Callout type="warn">This stage contains major story spoilers for Patient Zero, including the nature of Alad V's experiments and the boss encounter.</Callout>

The final mission is a Sabotage on Xini, the Eris Interception node that anchors Alad V's operation. Players must navigate to the laboratory's reactor and destroy it. Along the way, the environment reveals the full scope of what Alad V has been creating: Warframe shells stripped of their Tenno operators and reanimated by Infested biology.

The Lotus identifies the threat clearly during the mission: "That's a hollow Warframe being puppeted by Infested flesh." The pseudo-boss is not a Warframe in any meaningful sense -- the original Transference connection has been severed, and what remains is a biological puppet shaped like a Warframe and animated by the Infestation's collective coordination.

The encounter tests players on dealing with a highly mobile and aggressive target that combines Warframe-like movement with Infested attack patterns. The pseudo-boss can be staggered and interrupted with knockdown effects and melee finishers. Destroying it and sabotaging the reactor completes the mission and awards the quest reward.

## Rewards

Patient Zero grants one primary reward upon completion:

- **Mutalist Alad V Assassinate Key Blueprint** -- This Blueprint is taken to the Foundry and crafted into a single-use key that grants access to the Mutalist Alad V Assassinate mission on Eris. The mission is the only source for Mesa component blueprints. The Assassinate Key Blueprint is repeatable: it can be crafted multiple times to farm the boss as many times as needed. The Blueprint itself can also be traded between players, allowing those who have not completed Patient Zero to participate in the boss mission if hosted by someone who has.

The Assassinate Key Blueprint is the entirety of the functional reward. Patient Zero does not grant experience, credits, or other items beyond the standard mission completion bonuses.

## Lore

Alad V's history makes his Infestation arc one of the more developed character arcs in the early Warframe narrative. He appeared first as a Corpus antagonist connected to the illegal Warframe experimentation program that served as backstory in earlier quests and transmissions. His scientific interest in Warframe biology and his willingness to operate outside the Corpus Board's official directives are consistent character traits that carry through his transformation.

The Infestation did not find him randomly. His laboratories were conducting Technocyte research when containment failed -- or when he allowed it to fail. The question of whether Alad V was a victim of the Infestation or a willing participant in his own transformation is left deliberately ambiguous in Patient Zero's dialogue. His transmissions suggest the latter: he speaks of the Mutalist Empire not as something imposed on him but as a vision he has chosen, a new form of power that transcends the profit-driven constraints of Corpus society.

His creation of Infested pseudo-Warframes represents a profound transgression in the game's world. Warframes are not simply weapons; they carry significance tied to the Tenno's relationship with the Void and with the figures the Warframes were built from. Animating hollow Warframe shells with Infested biology treats that significance as a material resource to be exploited -- a very Alad V approach to the situation, filtered through an Infested lens.

Patient Zero does not conclude Alad V's story. His later appearances across Warframe's content show the consequences of his Infested period and introduce subsequent transformations. The Mesa farm that follows from this quest connects players to his story through every run of the Assassinate mission.

## Tips

- Hive Sabotage missions on Eris can spawn large numbers of Infested simultaneously when a Hive takes damage. Area-of-effect abilities and weapons help manage the surge. Warframes effective in crowd control situations perform well across all three triangulation stages.
- The pseudo-boss in Stage 5 is susceptible to finisher attacks. Getting a knockdown or a stagger opens finisher windows that deal significant damage. Melee-focused players can expedite the encounter considerably this way.
- Corrosive and Viral damage types are effective against the Infested enemy types throughout this quest. Corrosive strips armor from Infested Ancients, while Viral amplifies the effective damage dealt to any Infested unit.
- After completing Patient Zero, the Mutalist Alad V Assassinate Key Blueprint can be crafted repeatedly in the Foundry. Batch-crafting several keys before a farming session reduces interruptions between runs.
- The quest is replayable. Players who want to experience the narrative again can do so through the Codex without losing their existing progression or the previously earned Blueprint.
