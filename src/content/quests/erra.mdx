## Overview

Erra is a short main quest released in Update 27.0 on December 13, 2019. It forms the second act of the Prelude to War narrative arc, a sequence of cinematic quests that bridge the earlier mysteries introduced in [Chimera Prologue](/quests/chimera-prologue) with the full-scale conflict of The New War. The quest does not involve combat or traversal in the traditional sense; it consists almost entirely of cinematic sequences that deliver substantial lore payoff for players who have followed the story to this point.

The quest's central subject is Erra himself -- a Sentient warlord and the biological brother of Natah, the entity who took on the identity of the Lotus and served as the Tenno's guiding voice for years. Where the Chimera Prologue ended with the revelation that Ballas had formed an alliance with the Sentients and had seemingly taken the Lotus captive, Erra expands on the scope of that betrayal and shows the Tenno the face of the adversary they are about to face.

Because the quest is structured as a vision witnessed by the Operator rather than a mission the player actively completes, it functions as extended storytelling rather than gameplay. Its runtime is brief -- under fifteen minutes for most players -- but the narrative density is high, and the visual design of the Old War flashback is among the most technically elaborate sequences produced for Warframe at the time of its release.

<Callout type="warn">Spoilers for Erra and The New War follow.</Callout>

The events depicted carry major implications for every story thread that preceded them: Natah's true identity, the fate of Ballas, the Sentients' long-term objectives, and the status of Tenno as a species are all addressed or recontextualized within the quest's short runtime.

## Prerequisites

Completing Erra requires the following:

- **Chimera Prologue** -- The direct narrative predecessor. The Chimera Prologue ends with Ballas's apparent defection to the Sentients, establishing the crisis that Erra continues. Players who have not completed Chimera Prologue will not have access to the Erra quest entry in the Codex.
- **The Second Dream and The War Within** -- While not technically gated by the system at the Erra stage, the quest's dialogue and cinematics assume full familiarity with the Operator reveal and the nature of the Tenno's relationship with the Lotus. Players who jump to Erra without that context will lack the emotional and narrative grounding the quest depends on.
- **Access via Codex** -- Erra is initiated from the Codex's Quest tab rather than from a Star Chart node. Once the prerequisites are met, the quest appears automatically.

No Mastery Rank requirement is enforced. The Paracesis weapon, which was required to trigger a specific activation mechanic in earlier versions of the quest, is no longer mandatory as of Update 27.2 (March 5, 2020), when the unlock condition was simplified.

## Stages

The quest proceeds through three distinct sequences.

1. **Investigate Personal Quarters** -- The Operator is prompted to visit the Personal Quarters aboard their Orbiter. The Lotus's headgear, left behind after the events of Chimera Prologue, radiates an unusual purple aura. Interacting with it triggers a vision that pulls the Operator into a shared memory.

2. **Old War Flashback** -- A cinematic depicts a large-scale battle from the Old War fought on Lua. Four Prime Warframes -- Excalibur Prime, Mag Prime, Nova Prime, and a black-armored Mesa Prime variant -- engage a wave of Sentient forces. The sequence shows Excalibur Prime deploying Exalted Blade and using a Burston Prime while Mag Prime intervenes to prevent an Aerolyst from finishing off a downed ally. A Dax soldier is rescued as part of the combat, and the Lotus is heard issuing orders for Tenno Void Beam attacks against a humanoid Sentient target.

   <Callout type="warn">The humanoid Sentient in this sequence is revealed to be Erra, Natah's brother. The Lotus ordering strikes against her own family is one of the emotional cores of the quest's lore.</Callout>

3. **Vision on the Praghasa** -- The present-day portion of the vision takes place aboard Praghasa, the Sentient Mothership. Erra and Natah stand surrounded by assembled Sentient forces. Ballas appears as Erra's prisoner. The exchange between the siblings reveals that Natah has experienced significant memory loss from her long years as the Lotus, and Erra is pressing her to reclaim her Sentient identity and use her knowledge of the Tenno against them. Natah releases a pulse that forcibly ends the vision, returning the Operator to the Orbiter.

## Rewards

Completing the Erra quest grants the blueprint for the Shedu, an Arch-gun that can also be used in normal ground combat via the Operator's Void Sling. The Shedu fires tendrils of Sentient energy at its targets and is notable for recharging via enemy Overguard and health pools rather than using conventional ammunition. It represents the primary gameplay reward attached to this otherwise narrative-focused quest.

No credits, experience, or secondary items are awarded beyond the Shedu blueprint.

## Lore

The quest's core revelation is the relationship between Erra and Natah, and the cost of the transformation that turned a Sentient operative into the Lotus.

The Sentients are a machine intelligence originally dispatched by the Orokin to terraform the Tau system. They evolved beyond their initial programming, developed the capacity to adapt to Void-based attacks -- the Tenno's primary weapon -- and returned to the Origin System to destroy the civilization that created them. Erra was one of the Sentients' most powerful commanders during the Old War, and the Old War flashback shows him fighting in a form recognizable as humanoid, setting him apart from the more alien Sentient drones and constructs.

Natah was sent to the Origin System as a Sentient infiltrator, tasked with subverting the Tenno from within. The Orokin recognized this and subjected her to a procedure that suppressed her Sentient nature and implanted a new identity: the Lotus, a mother-figure who would guide and protect the Tenno. The procedure worked well enough that Natah genuinely became the Lotus over time, losing access to her memories and forming real bonds with the Tenno she was meant to destroy.

Erra's appearance represents the Sentients reclaiming what they view as their agent. The vision shows him leveraging Natah's fractured state -- her lapses of memory, her uncertainty about who she is -- to reassert her original mission. Ballas, whose defection from the Orokin and alliance with the Sentients was established in Chimera Prologue, appears as a willing collaborator in this plan, though his motivations extend beyond simple allegiance to either side.

The confirmation in this quest that the Orokin are extinct -- stated plainly by Erra as context for why the Tenno remain the primary threat -- closes a narrative loop that had been building since the early story quests. The Tenno are no longer fighting a war in service of an empire. The empire is gone. What they do next, and whether they can prevent the New War, becomes the question that The New War quest answers.

## Tips

- The quest has no combat encounters and cannot be failed. Players experiencing technical issues (freezing cinematics, audio desync) can restart the mission from the Codex without losing progress.
- Watching the quest again after completing The New War adds considerable retroactive context. Natah's behavior in the Praghasa vision, which appears conflicted on first viewing, reads differently once her full arc is understood.
- The Shedu blueprint is granted immediately upon completion. Building the weapon requires additional Sentient-origin components that drop from Sentient encounters, including those found in the Veil Proxima during Railjack missions.
