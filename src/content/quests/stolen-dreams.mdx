## Overview

Stolen Dreams is an early-to-mid-game quest built almost entirely around the Spy mission type. It introduces Maroo, a recurring non-player character who becomes a permanent fixture in the game as the host of the weekly Ayatan Treasure Hunt. The quest follows the player's pursuit of mysterious Arcane Codices across three factions -- Corpus, Grineer, and Infested -- before culminating in a confrontation with an ancient Orokin machine whose message raises more questions than it answers.

The quest is notable as one of the first to foreground Spy missions as its core gameplay loop. Three of the five stages require successfully hacking all Data Vaults without triggering alarms. Unlike many quest missions where stealth is optional, the Spy stages in Stolen Dreams penalize detection by triggering data destruction countdowns, making undetected vault access the expected method. Players unfamiliar with Spy mechanics before starting this quest will encounter their first significant test of stealth technique.

Stolen Dreams is entirely replayable after completion, making it one of the few quests in the game that can be run again for practice or enjoyment. The Orokin Catalyst awarded on completion is a valuable universal upgrade resource that doubles the mod capacity of any weapon or companion, making it a meaningful reward for newer players who may be approaching their first resource of this type.

After completing the quest, Maroo begins offering her weekly Ayatan Treasure Hunt missions, which send players to locate a specific Ayatan Sculpture hidden somewhere in a randomized mission tileset. Ayatan Sculptures can be filled with Ayatan Stars and sold to Maroo for Endo, the resource used to rank up mods. This weekly activity is directly unlocked by completing Stolen Dreams, making the quest mechanically relevant beyond its own narrative.

The quest was introduced as an early showcase for the revised Spy mission system, which was reworked alongside the quest's original launch. Data Vaults in Corpus, Grineer, and Corpus Ship tilesets each have distinct puzzle layouts, and the quest's three Spy stages use one of each major faction's vault designs. This gives players exposure to a variety of Spy room mechanics before they encounter the harder Spy mission variants required for later junction tasks.

## Prerequisites

Stolen Dreams becomes available after completing the Phobos Junction. The Phobos Junction is accessed from Mars and requires completing several Mars objectives, including defeating Sargas Ruk. Players will typically encounter the quest during their first playthrough of the inner solar system star chart progression.

There is no Mastery Rank requirement beyond what is naturally met by reaching Phobos Junction through normal progression. By the time the junction is accessible, most players will have sufficient mod pool and Warframe options to handle the quest's mission types.

## Stages

Stolen Dreams consists of five stages involving a Capture, three Spy missions, and a final combined Exterminate-upload mission.

**1. Capture Maroo**

The quest opens on Mars, where intelligence points to a thief named Maroo hiding in a Corpus facility. The mission is a Capture -- the player must locate Maroo before she can escape and bring her down to zero health, at which point she is restrained rather than killed. Maroo carries dual pistols and can activate an invisibility cloak when pressured, making her more mobile than standard Capture targets. Once downed, Maroo explains that she was hired to retrieve the Arcane Codices by parties on multiple sides, and she proposes cooperation with the player rather than captivity.

**2. Arcane Codex I**

The first Spy mission takes place in a Corpus Outpost on Venus. Three Data Vaults must be accessed and their data extracted without triggering the 30-second data destruction sequence that activates on alarm. Each vault room has a distinct layout with patrolling Corpus security drones and laser grid obstacles. Successfully hacking all three vaults provides the first set of codex fragments.

**3. Arcane Codex II**

The second Spy mission moves to a Grineer Galleon in Mercury orbit. Grineer vault rooms differ significantly from Corpus designs: magnetic locks on doors, roving Sensor Regulators that activate wall-mounted alarms, and heavier guard patrols create a more active threat environment. Two codices are housed in three vaults across this tileset. Completing all three without triggering persistent alarms yields the second codex set.

**4. Arcane Codex III**

The final Spy mission is aboard a Corpus Ship at Phobos. Corpus Ship vault layouts introduce elevated guard density and more complex laser configurations than the earlier Corpus Outpost stage. Three vaults contain the remaining codex fragments, completing the full set needed to operate the Arcane Machine.

**5. Arcane Machine**

The final stage takes place on Mars, where the player fights through Infested enemies to locate an Orokin Derelict portal. The mission type shifts from stealth to combat: the Infested do not use standard alarm mechanics, and the objective is simply to reach the portal's location while clearing obstacles. Upon reaching the Arcane Machine through the portal, the player uploads the assembled codices. The upload triggers a response wave of enemies that must be held off while the transmission completes. The machine broadcasts its message -- "All is silent...the womb of the sky" -- before the mission ends. The origin and meaning of the message are not explained within the quest.

## Rewards

Completing Stolen Dreams grants:

- **Ether Daggers blueprint** -- a melee weapon blueprint usable in the Foundry. The Ether Daggers are a dual-wield secondary melee option, though they are generally considered a foundational item rather than a competitive endgame choice.
- **Orokin Catalyst blueprint** -- a high-value upgrade resource that doubles the mod capacity of any weapon or companion it is applied to. Players receive one Orokin Catalyst blueprint that can be built in the Foundry. Using an Orokin Catalyst on a frequently-used weapon early in progression provides lasting benefit.
- **Weekly Ayatan Treasure Hunt** -- completing the quest permanently unlocks the ability to receive weekly Ayatan Treasure Hunt missions from Maroo at her Bazaar, reachable via the Navigation panel. Each week's mission provides one Ayatan Sculpture whose type varies weekly.

## Lore

Stolen Dreams operates on the boundary between early-game and mid-game narrative. The Arcane Codices themselves are not explained beyond their apparent importance to multiple factions: Maroo was hired by a Corpus client, Grineer interests also pursue them, and the Orokin built the machine that reads them. The transmission produced at the quest's climax -- a phrase about silence and a sky womb -- is a deliberate narrative fragment, one of several early signals that larger mysteries about the Origin System's past are embedded in accessible early quests.

The codices reference a transmission source associated with the Orokin's history of outer-system exploration. The phrase "the womb of the sky" is interpreted by some community analysis as a reference to the Void itself, or to the conditions that led to the Zariman Ten Zero incident explored in later quests. Stolen Dreams does not resolve this thread; it exists as a fragment of a larger narrative about what the Orokin knew and built before the fall of their civilization.

Maroo's introduction as a morally ambiguous character -- neither ally nor enemy, operating for pay rather than ideology -- establishes a recurring archetype in Warframe. She reappears in the Conclave social space as a permanent vendor and mission provider, her past as a thief absorbed into a pragmatic ongoing arrangement with the Tenno.

## Tips

- Before entering each Spy stage, access the Star Chart mission preview to identify the specific tileset. Different factions use vault rooms with distinct alarm systems; understanding the layout reduces time spent searching for vault entrances.
- The 30-second data destruction timer that activates on Corpus alarm is long enough to complete a vault hack if the player acts immediately after detection. However, avoiding detection entirely is safer and leaves no risk of failure.
- Warframes with innate invisibility -- such as Ash, Ivara, or Loki -- simplify Spy missions significantly by allowing free movement past security drones and guards. Ivara's Prowl ability in particular prevents alarms from triggering even when moving through laser fields.
- The Capture stage targeting Maroo does not require killing surrounding Corpus troops. Moving directly toward Maroo's location and engaging her specifically is faster than clearing the room.
- The Ayatan Treasure Hunt unlocked after completion is best run weekly without fail. Ayatan Sculptures filled with Ayatan Stars provide significant Endo yields when traded to Maroo, and Endo is the primary bottleneck for maxing high-drain mods in the mid-game.
