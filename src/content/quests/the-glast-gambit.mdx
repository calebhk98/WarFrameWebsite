## Overview

The Glast Gambit is a late-game quest that introduces The Index, Warframe's competitive point-accumulation arena operated by the Corpus, and serves as the primary acquisition path for the [Nidus](/warframes/nidus) Warframe. The quest is initiated through Ergo Glast, a representative of the Perrin Sequence syndicate, and involves escalating wagers in Corpus gambling arenas in order to free kidnapped colonists from a settlement of Ostron offshoot called the Mycona Colony. Nef Anyo serves as the primary antagonist, deploying economic leverage rather than direct combat as his weapon.

The Glast Gambit requires completion of The War Within, making it a late-game progression gate. By the time players reach it, they are expected to have a working knowledge of mission types and Warframe builds. The Index itself is a mechanically demanding mode: enemies scale with the credit wager placed before each match, and the Financial Stress system penalizes players for holding too many Index Points by draining health continuously. Understanding how to play The Index efficiently -- knowing when to bank points, how to handle the health drain, and when to sacrifice a run -- is essential to completing the quest's multiple Index stages.

The quest contains one stage in which the player is intentionally unable to win. The fifth Index match in the quest is scripted to end in defeat regardless of performance, demonstrating that Nef has been cheating throughout the entire contest. This moment serves as a narrative pivot: what appeared to be a fair (if high-stakes) wager is revealed as a rigged system. The revelation informs the final alignment choice, which has limited but meaningful consequences for the quest's ending.

Nidus's design as an Infestation-themed Warframe is tied directly to the quest's narrative. The Mycona Colony is revealed to maintain a symbiotic relationship with a controlled Infestation strain, and the Triuna -- the child at the center of Nef's scheme -- is the living nexus of that relationship. Nidus emerges from the Orokin Derelict after the quest's conclusion as a product of studying the Mycona's relationship with the Infestation.

## Prerequisites

The Glast Gambit requires:

- **Completion of The War Within** -- the preceding main story quest, which grants access to the Operator's full combat capabilities and the inner solar system's late-game nodes.
- **Speaking with Ergo Glast** -- the quest is not automatically available after completing The War Within. The player must physically travel to a Relay and enter The Perrin Sequence room, then interact with Ergo Glast to trigger the quest's availability in the Codex.

The War Within prerequisite means players will have completed The Second Dream and have access to the Operator before starting this quest. The credit reserves needed for the Index wagers are also a practical prerequisite: the quest's three wager stages require 100,000, 120,000, and 140,000 credits respectively, held as stakes for each match. Players with limited credit reserves may need to farm credits in standard missions before starting.

## Stages

The Glast Gambit has six stages spread across Defense, multiple Index matches, a Spy mission, and a final Index encounter.

**1. Help Secure Mycona Colony**

The quest opens on a defense mission at Monolith, Phobos. The Mycona Colony is under Infestation assault, and three Infested Tumors are blocking the gates that would allow survivors to shelter. The player destroys the tumors while Hunters -- colonist fighters -- close each gate via security consoles. After the gates are secured, six Infestation Spawn Pods must be destroyed to prevent reinforcement. Enemy levels here are 25-30 on an Infested Ship tileset. The Infestation is not the usual mindless variant but one the Mycona have a complex relationship with, which the quest gradually explains.

**2-4. Challenge Nef Anyo on The Index (Three Matches)**

Three successive Index matches against Corpus agents on Neptune's arena tileset. Each match is preceded by a wager selection screen:

- **Match 1:** Wager of 100,000 credits; win three 2-minute rounds
- **Match 2:** Wager of 120,000 credits; win three rounds
- **Match 3:** Wager of 140,000 credits; win three rounds

The Index operates as follows: enemies drop Index Points when killed, and players must collect those points and carry them to a bank terminal. Carrying multiple points increases the player's Financial Stress level, which drains health at an increasing rate until the points are banked or the player is killed (which causes the held points to be dropped at the death location). Enemy agents also collect points and bank them against the player's score. Winning requires banking more points than the Corpus agents before the round timer expires.

Enemy levels in the Index during these stages range from 15 to 22, scaling with the wager amount. Players who find the Index challenging can recover credit losses by completing standard credit-farming missions before re-attempting a failed match, as the wager is only lost on a match loss, not on individual round failures.

**5. Find Nef's Cheats**

A Spy mission at Oceanum, Pluto (level 24-26, Corpus Ship tileset). Two vault hacks retrieve hidden communications from an entity named Neewa revealing information about the Triuna -- the young person at the center of the Mycona's ritual. The transmissions expose that Nef knew about the Triuna's significance and has been exploiting the Mycona Colony's vulnerability rather than operating in good faith. Two of the three vaults contain quest-critical data; the third is optional.

**6. Win Back Neewa**

The final Index match is entered without a credit wager, meaning no credits are at stake. The match is played to force the confrontation. During this stage's Index run, the match is scripted to conclude with Nef activating a cheat that invalidates the player's point total, demonstrating that every previous match was similarly rigged. The revelation is presented through dialogue rather than combat.

After the scripted defeat, the quest reaches its alignment choice: the player must decide the fate of the Triuna, who has been revealed as the living node of the Mycona's Infestation bond. Three options are presented, each with a Sun/Moon/Neutral alignment implication and a different ending sequence for the Triuna's fate. The choice does not affect the Nidus reward, only the narrative outcome.

<Callout type="warn">Spoilers for the Triuna's nature and the Mycona Colony's Infestation relationship follow.</Callout>

The Triuna is not simply a hostage. The Mycona Colony exists in a controlled symbiotic relationship with the Infestation, and the Triuna is the ritual successor figure who absorbs a portion of the Infestation's directive intelligence to maintain the colony's safety. The three alignment choices correspond to: curing the Triuna of the Infestation bond (Sun -- removes the burden), allowing the Triuna to decide their own fate (Neutral -- respects autonomy), or sending the Triuna back to fulfill the role (Moon -- preserves the colony's tradition). Each choice has proponents within the quest's characters and none is presented as objectively correct.

## Rewards

Completing The Glast Gambit grants:

- **Nidus Warframe blueprint** -- the main blueprint for Nidus. Unlike many quest-reward Warframes, Nidus's component blueprints (Neuroptics, Chassis, Systems) are not awarded by the quest directly. They must be farmed separately from Rotation C of Orokin Derelict Survival (post-update: Orokin Derelict Survival nodes on the updated star chart). This makes Nidus farming a post-quest activity rather than a simple Foundry queue.

The Orokin Derelict Survival runs for Nidus components require extended sessions to reach Rotation C, which occurs every 20 minutes of continuous mission time. Survival-focused Warframes and energy-sustaining builds are recommended for these farm sessions.

## Lore

The Glast Gambit situates Nidus within the broader Infestation narrative: the Warframe is not derived from the Corpus or Grineer's weaponized Infestation strains but from the Mycona Colony's ancient controlled variant -- one that predates the militarized Infestation forms seen elsewhere. This gives Nidus a unique position in the Warframe roster: a frame built from cooperation with the Infestation rather than a pure extraction of its destructive elements.

Ergo Glast's involvement positions the Perrin Sequence syndicate as a faction with genuine ethical concerns alongside its mercantile identity. The Perrin Sequence prizes fair exchange and rejects exploitation; Nef Anyo's rigged Index represents exactly the kind of corrupt practice they oppose. The quest functions partly as a character study for Ergo Glast as someone willing to spend significant resources to defend principles rather than profit margins.

## Tips

- Ensure sufficient credit reserves before starting the Index stages. Entering a match with 100,000, 120,000, or 140,000 credits as wager and losing means those credits are forfeited. Running Profit-Taker or Index standard missions to build credits before the quest is a useful precaution.
- The Financial Stress mechanic drains health based on how many Index Points are currently held. Banking points frequently in small batches keeps health drain low. Only hold multiple points simultaneously when the bank terminal is immediately accessible.
- The Index enemies use Corpus weaponry at levels 15-22. Warframes effective against Corpus (high shield damage -- magnetic, electricity, or radiation) perform well here. Warframes with health regeneration are also practical for countering Financial Stress drain.
- The Spy stage at Oceanum requires only two of three vault completions for quest progression. If one vault triggers an alarm, completing the other two is still sufficient.
- The fifth Index stage ends in mandatory defeat; do not spend consumables or prepare extensively for it. The loss is scripted and no credit penalty applies.
