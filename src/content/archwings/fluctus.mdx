## Overview

The Fluctus is a sweeping energy arch-gun that fires a wide horizontal arc of pure physical force. Its defining characteristic is the arc-shot attack pattern -- rather than a projectile or beam, the Fluctus fires a crescent-shaped energy wave that damages all enemies within its arc simultaneously. This makes it one of the best arch-guns for clearing tightly grouped fighter formations where a single shot can hit four to six enemies across a wide horizontal band.

Introduced alongside the Itzal Archwing in Update 15.7 (December 2014), the Fluctus was designed as a crowd-control oriented complement to the Itzal's mobility kit. Its relatively slow fire rate (5 rounds per second) is compensated by the wide arc coverage. In practice, the effective DPS against groups can exceed single-target weapons because each shot hits multiple enemies.

The Fluctus is available at Mastery Rank 2 and is researched in the Clan Dojo Tenno Lab. Its physical damage composition (50 impact, 175 puncture, 25 slash, total 250) emphasizes puncture, giving it natural effectiveness against Grineer health pools that have lower puncture resistance.

## Acquisition

Fluctus blueprint and components (Barrel, Receiver, Stock) are researched in the Clan Dojo Tenno Lab as part of the same research branch as the Itzal Archwing. Clans that have progressed far enough in Tenno Lab research to unlock the Itzal can also unlock the Fluctus. Build time is approximately 12 hours for the assembled weapon after all components are crafted.

Resources required are standard Archwing materials available from early to mid star chart planets. No rare components are needed, making the Fluctus one of the more accessible Dojo-researched arch-guns.

## Playstyle

The Fluctus excels in dense fighter formations where its arc shot connects with multiple targets per fire. The optimal positioning is perpendicular to a line of enemy fighters -- facing the formation broadside maximizes the number of enemies hit per shot. In Archwing Exterminate missions where fighter squads fly in loose formations, one Fluctus shot often kills or cripples an entire group simultaneously.

The slow fire rate and 5-second reload time demand deliberate pacing. Players who continuously pull the trigger against single targets waste the Fluctus's design. Against single high-health targets (Crewships, commanders), direct-fire weapons like the Imperator Vandal or Velocitus are more efficient.

In combination with [Itzal](/archwings/itzal)'s Cosmic Crush, the Fluctus's crowd-control potential increases significantly: use Cosmic Crush to group enemies into a tight formation, then fire the Fluctus into the concentrated cluster for amplified hit count per arc.

## Recommended builds

Hollowed Point (critical chance) and Sabot Rounds (critical multiplier) bring the 22% base crit chance into reliable territory. Rubedo-Lined Barrel increases raw damage per shot, multiplying its effect across all targets hit. Thermite Rounds or Cryogenic Leakage add elemental procs for faction-specific damage bonuses.

For pure crowd clearing in Archwing missions, Pared Turret (fire rate) bridges the gap between the 5 rps base rate and a more responsive feel, though the arc nature of the shots means accuracy and positioning matter more than raw fire rate.

## Notes

The Fluctus does not have a Prime variant as of May 2026. Its arc-wave projectile is unique among arch-guns and does not behave like traditional beam or projectile weapons -- it passes through enemies rather than stopping on first contact, meaning the full arc hits all enemies in the wave's path. This punch-through mechanic is inherent to the weapon design, not a mod effect.

Reload time of 5 seconds is among the longest for arch-guns. Plan reloads during movement gaps or while repositioning between enemy groups.
