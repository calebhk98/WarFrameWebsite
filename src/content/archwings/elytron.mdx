## Overview

Elytron is the heavy-artillery Archwing, designed from the ground up for maximum destructive output in space combat. Its high health of 765 and shield pool of 745 make it the most durable Archwing in terms of raw effective hit points, and its four abilities all deliver explosive area damage. Elytron rewards an aggressive playstyle: close into Grineer fighter formations, saturate them with explosions, and let the high defensive stats absorb counter-fire.

Introduced in the original Archwing update (Update 15, October 2014), Elytron was one of two launch Archwings alongside Odonata. It established the design pattern of a high-EHP frame that trades utility for raw damage throughput. Players who prefer Warframes like Saryn or Ember often gravitate toward Elytron for Archwing missions because the playstyle translates: sustain through stats, kill through area-of-effect.

## Abilities

<AbilityCard

Bloomer is a precision tool for priority target elimination. Fire it onto a Grineer Crewship hull or a high-value target, then trigger detonation remotely. The latch mechanic ensures the explosion occurs at a chosen location rather than on contact, allowing players to delay detonation until clustered enemies move into the blast radius. This gives Bloomer more burst potential than it first appears.

<AbilityCard
  name="Core Vent"
  key="2"
  energyCost={50}
  description="Vents the Archwing propulsion core, emitting a heat trail that confuses incoming guided missiles and causes them to detonate away from Elytron."

Core Vent is Elytron's only defensive ability. Against Grineer Crewship missile volleys and torpedo attacks in Railjack missions, Core Vent provides reliable missile diversion. Activate it when a tracking warning appears on screen and the incoming guided munitions will veer away and detonate harmlessly. Its relatively modest energy cost makes it practical to use preemptively when entering areas with known missile-equipped enemy spawns.

<AbilityCard
  name="Thumper"
  key="3"

Thumper is Elytron's area-denial tool and its most interesting mechanic. Stacking up to four Thumpers in overlapping locations multiplies the damage output significantly. In Archwing Interception missions, pre-positioning Thumper stacks on objective capture points before enemy waves arrive creates denial zones that eliminate incoming fighters without needing active ability use.

Warhead is the signature ability: a massive, slow projectile that deals the highest single-hit area damage in Elytron's kit. Its slow flight speed is its main drawback -- fast-moving targets can dodge it. However, against stationary Crewship modules, docked fighters, or grouped enemy spawns in Exterminate missions, Warhead consistently clears large numbers of enemies with a single cast.

## Acquisition

Elytron's blueprint components are researched in the Clan Dojo Tenno Lab. The Harness, Systems, and Wings components each require separate research and crafting time before the main blueprint can be built. Total build time including all components is 36 hours without rushing. The primary resource requirement includes Control Modules and standard Archwing materials available from Europa, Neptune, and the Void.

## Playstyle

Elytron performs best in Archwing Exterminate and Interception missions where mass enemy destruction is the objective. The optimal approach: engage at medium range with Thumper to stack AoE denial, fire Warhead into clustered enemy formations, and use Bloomer for any high-priority targets that survive the area bombardment. Core Vent on cooldown when missile warning indicators appear preserves the high shield and health pool for sustained engagements.

In Railjack missions, Elytron's missile defense via Core Vent becomes particularly valuable when dealing with Crewship torpedo salvos. The combination of high EHP and reliable missile interception lets Elytron hold objective areas longer than other Archwings before needing to disengage.

## Modding notes

Elytron benefits from Ability Strength to maximize Warhead and Thumper damage, and Ability Range to widen Thumper's area. Redirection and Vitality lean into the existing defensive stats. The energy economy is tight at 120 base -- Primed Vigor or Endurance Drift help, as does bringing a Zenurik-focused Operator to maintain energy during missions. Rush (sprint speed) is secondary but helps close distance to use Warhead in its effective range.

## Lore

Elytron's design takes its name and visual inspiration from insect wing cases -- the hardened outer wings that protect a beetle's flight wings. This aesthetic and the emphasis on heavy armor and explosive power reflect a Tenno engineering philosophy of maximum destruction in open-space theater. WFCD documentation notes that Elytron was the first Archwing explicitly built for offense, with the support and maneuverability roles left to the Odonata and Itzal respectively.
