## Overview

Itzal Prime is the Orokin-era Prime variant of the [Itzal](/archwings/itzal) Archwing. As with all Prime variants, it improves upon the base frame's stats while retaining the same ability set. Numeric stats are not present in the WFCD warframe-items dataset for Itzal Prime as of this writing and are therefore listed as unknown -- when WFCD adds the entry, this page will be updated.

Itzal Prime retains the same stealth-and-mobility identity as the base Itzal: the grapple hook for traversal, cloaking field for evasion, gravitational singularity for burst area clearance, and autonomous drone escort. Players who enjoy the base Itzal's playstyle will find the Prime variant a direct improvement once its stats become available.

The Prime variant carries the Orokin gold-and-platinum visual treatment on the dark swept-back wing design of the original, producing a visually striking contrast between the shadow-oriented color scheme and the ornate Prime accents.

## Acquisition

Itzal Prime components (Blueprint, Harness, Systems, Wings) are obtained via Void Relic farming. Specific relics rotate with Prime Access releases and Vault cycling. Check the Fissure mission board in-game for currently active relics containing Itzal Prime components, or consult warframestat.us for live relic rotation data.

Components not yet obtained can be traded with other players via [warframe.market](https://warframe.market). Itzal Prime sets typically trade at moderate platinum values relative to the convenience of skipping relic farming.

## Playstyle

The playstyle is identical to [Itzal](/archwings/itzal). Any stat improvements in the Prime version amplify the existing strengths: additional health extends the margin before dying in the low-armor frame, and any energy improvements reduce the frequency of energy management decisions. The core mobility-stealth-burst loop remains unchanged.

## Modding notes

All modding considerations from [Itzal](/archwings/itzal) apply. Once WFCD publishes the Prime stats, the specific stat totals may shift the relative value of Redirection versus Vitality. The ability kit is identical so the same ability-focused mods (Strength, Range for Cosmic Crush) remain optimal.

## Lore

Itzal Prime predates the base Itzal in the Orokin timeline, as with all Prime variants -- the base variants are reconstructions built from recovered Orokin blueprints after the Collapse. The original Itzal Prime was an infiltration and denial platform designed for operations where detection could not be risked. The Orokin's enhancements over whatever original design was eventually reconstructed as the base Itzal likely focused on endurance and power density -- allowing the cloaking systems and gravitational equipment to operate longer at higher output.
