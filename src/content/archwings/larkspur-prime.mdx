## Overview

The Larkspur Prime is the Prime variant of the Larkspur, a heavy energy arch-gun introduced with the Railjack update. Where the Larkspur already distinguished itself with its 50% status chance -- the highest among all arch-guns -- the Prime version improves upon the critical chance (from 10% to 14%), critical multiplier (from 1.5x to 1.6x), and magazine size while keeping the same exceptional status chance. The result is the most status-focused arch-gun on the roster.

The Larkspur fires a continuous beam of radiation and impact energy, making it naturally effective against Alloy Armor enemies (Grineer Heavy units, Orokin Sentients) where radiation damage receives a damage bonus. The 50% status chance means that approximately half of all ticks against a target will apply radiation, viral, or other procs depending on elemental combinations -- making it effective for disrupting enemy targeting and grouping Grineer units via radiation confusion.

Mastery Rank 8 is required to use the Larkspur Prime.

## Acquisition

Larkspur Prime components rotate through Void Relics as part of the Prime Access and Vault cycling program. Check the current Fissure mission board in-game or warframestat.us to find which relics contain specific Larkspur Prime components (Blueprint, Barrel, Receiver, Stock). Unvaulting events periodically make retired Prime content available again for a limited time.

Larkspur Prime components can be traded between players in unbuilt form. The full set typically trades at moderate platinum values on warframe.market, with the rarer components (often Blueprint or Receiver) commanding higher prices.

## Playstyle

The Larkspur Prime rewards a status-first build philosophy. The 50% status chance means status mods (Contamination Casing, Venomous Clip equivalents) push it toward near-guaranteed proc application on every burst. The primary use case for this is:

1. Radiation confusion: applying radiation to Grineer infantry causes them to attack each other, effectively removing them from combat without requiring kills.
2. Combined element builds: mixing radiation (base damage) with heat or viral via mods creates dual-element combinations effective against specific factions.

The 14% critical chance is below the threshold where heavy critical investment pays off reliably, so building primarily for status and raw damage is more efficient than splitting mods between both. Fire rate at 12 rps is moderate, providing a comfortable balance between DPS and ammunition economy over the 100-round magazine.

Reload time at 4.5 seconds is on the slower side. Position and movement during reloads matter; use arch-gun dodge animations or boost away from enemy fire during the reload cycle.

## Recommended builds

For Archwing content: Rubedo-Lined Barrel (damage), Contamination Casing (status chance), Venomous Clip (toxin + status), and Cryogenic Leakage (cold + status) create a high-status radiation base with viral or corrosive secondary elements. Status-focused builds benefit from Pariticle Munitions (radiation bonus) to increase the base damage type further.

In Railjack heavy-weapon mode (via Gravimag), the radiation base damage is particularly useful against Sentient Anomaly targets and Corpus Crewships. Building for viral secondary on top of the radiation base creates a damage-amplification combination for high-health priority targets.

## Notes

The base Larkspur (MR 8, same mastery requirement) has a 10% critical chance versus 14% on the Prime and a 1.5x versus 1.6x multiplier. The status chance is identical at 50%. For most practical purposes the Prime is preferred, but the base Larkspur is a solid weapon in its own right during the MR progression period. Both variants share the heavy energy beam visual effect and the same damage composition.
