## Overview

The Knux is a powered gauntlet arch-melee weapon -- effectively two large mechanical fists attached to the Archwing's arms. It deals the highest total damage among the arch-melee weapons covered here (445 total), with almost all of that damage concentrated in impact (400.5 of 445). This extreme impact-heavy damage profile makes the Knux naturally effective against shields (impact deals bonus damage to shields) and against enemies with low impact resistance.

The Knux trades the critical-chain style of the [Centaur](/archwings/centaur) for a status-forward approach. Its 30% status chance (compared to the Centaur's 20%) makes it the better platform for status-focused elemental builds. The lower critical chance (13%) means critical mods are less efficient, but the high status chance rewards Viral, Magnetic, or Corrosive combination builds that amplify the raw impact damage further.

Mastery Rank 7 is required to equip the Knux. Its visual design -- large pneumatic gauntlets -- matches the impact-dominant damage profile and aggressive close-quarters use case.

## Acquisition

Knux component blueprints (and the main blueprint) are researched in the Clan Dojo Tenno Lab. As with the [Centaur](/archwings/centaur), component blueprints are sourced from Syndicate vendors at specific standing tiers. Total build time across all components is approximately 12 hours.

Players who prefer to skip the research path can purchase the Knux directly from the in-game Market for platinum. This is a viable option for players who want to progress through Archwing content quickly without waiting for research timers.

## Playstyle

The Knux is a brawler arch-melee weapon. The impact-dominant damage profile and high status chance reward getting into close range with enemy fighters and landing rapid successive hits to apply status effects. Each hit has a 30% chance to proc impact status, which temporarily reduces the enemy's damage output -- particularly useful when surrounded by multiple fighters simultaneously.

Against Corpus shielded units, the 400.5 impact damage per swing deals bonus damage to shields. This makes the Knux one of the faster shield-stripping arch-melee options when the Archwing engages at close quarters. Against Grineer, the impact damage is less efficient due to Grineer health scaling with armor, but the status chance helps by building crowd-control effects.

Slam attacks with the Knux deal radial impact damage, consistent with the brawler identity. In tight Archwing corridors where enemies are close together, a well-timed slam clears multiple opponents simultaneously.

## Modding notes

The Knux's 30% status chance makes it one of the best platforms for status-elemental arch-melee builds. Adding North Wind (cold) and Shocking Touch (electricity) creates a Magnetic secondary element (effective against Corpus shields) on top of the base impact damage. Alternatively, Volcanic Edge (heat) and North Wind (cold) produce a Blast combination for crowd control through knockdown procs.

Because critical chance is only 13%, investing in critical mods (True Steel equivalent) has diminishing returns. Prioritize status chance, attack speed (Coiling Viper), and damage mods over critical investment. The 2.0x critical multiplier means the occasional critical hit is still worthwhile, but it should not be the primary build focus.

## Notes

The Knux's extreme impact-skew (90% of total damage is impact) is unusual among melee weapons. No other arch-melee weapon has this degree of damage-type concentration, which makes its faction matchup very binary: very effective against shields (Corpus, all shielded enemies), less efficient against Grineer armor where impact is partially mitigated. Carrying a secondary arch-gun handles the Grineer-specific encounters where the Knux underperforms relative to puncture or slash weapons.

The arch-melee slot supports only one weapon at a time. The Knux and [Centaur](/archwings/centaur) serve different roles -- Centaur for critical and anti-armor (puncture), Knux for status and anti-shield (impact) -- making the choice between them faction-dependent.
