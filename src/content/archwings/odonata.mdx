## Overview

Odonata is the starter Archwing, awarded to players who complete the Archwing quest. It represents the well-rounded middle ground of the Archwing roster: balanced health (425) and shield (430) with moderate armor (100), and an ability set that mixes defensive shields, missile countermeasures, area attacks, and crowd control. For players new to Archwing missions, Odonata provides all the tools needed to handle every core mission type without requiring specialist knowledge.

Introduced in Update 15 (October 2014) as the second launch Archwing alongside Elytron, Odonata was designed as the approachable, tutorial-friendly option. Its abilities are intuitive -- shield yourself, decoy missiles, shoot homing rockets, push enemies away -- and its balanced stats mean it tolerates mistakes that would destroy the fragile Itzal. The prime variant, Odonata Prime, improves upon every base stat while retaining the same ability set.

Odonata is named after the order of insects that includes dragonflies, reflecting the frame's aerodynamic visual design and its combination of speed and attack capabilities.

Energy Shell provides a burst damage buffer. It is most useful at the start of a high-damage engagement: pop Energy Shell before entering a Grineer Crewship defense perimeter to absorb the opening salvo, then engage once the immediate threat is identified. The shell does not regenerate -- once depleted, it requires a second cast to restore. Managing Energy Shell timing is one of the core skills for Odonata players.

<AbilityCard
  name="Disarray"

Disarray is Odonata's countermeasure ability, functionally similar to Elytron's Core Vent but operating through beacon drones rather than exhaust. The beacons remain active for several seconds and draw multiple volleys of guided munitions. In any Archwing content featuring Grineer missile boats or Corpus drone swarms, Disarray prevents shield chip damage from guided fire and lets players focus on priority targets.

<AbilityCard

Seeking Fire is Odonata's offensive workhorse. The auto-tracking nature of the missiles removes the aiming requirements that direct-fire weapons demand in the fast movement of Archwing combat. Against small fighters that weave through formations, Seeking Fire connects reliably where manual shots miss. The volley damages multiple targets simultaneously, making it effective for thinning out fighter escorts protecting larger objectives.

<AbilityCard

Repel is a defensive panic button. When surrounded by enemies in tight formations -- common in the corridors of Grineer Galleons during Archwing Sabotage missions -- Repel clears breathing room by knocking all nearby enemies away and briefly stunning them. The damage component is secondary to the displacement effect. Repel also serves as a reliable interrupt when enemy ability users are winding up powerful attacks.

## Acquisition

The Odonata blueprint is a guaranteed reward upon completing the Archwing quest, which becomes available after completing the Earth to Mars Junction. The three component blueprints (Harness, Systems, Wings) are researched in the Clan Dojo Tenno Lab. Because the Archwing quest is a prerequisite for all space-based content, Odonata is effectively the first Archwing every player builds.

Build time for the complete Odonata is listed as nearly instant in WFCD (buildTime: 60 seconds) for the main frame, but the components require standard Archwing crafting times. Resource costs are low-tier planets materials accessible from Earth and early star chart nodes.

## Playstyle

Odonata plays as a generalist Archwing: adapt the ability rotation to the mission type. For defense missions, prioritize Energy Shell and Disarray to maintain durability. For exterminate, spam Seeking Fire to clear fighter groups while using Repel as a reset button when enemies surround. The balanced stats and modest energy cost on Energy Shell allow an efficient loop without demanding precision energy management.

Newer Archwing pilots benefit from building into Odonata before farming more specialized frames. Understanding the defensive mechanics -- when to shield, when to decoy -- carries forward to every other Archwing.

## Modding notes

The low base energy of 85 is Odonata's main limitation. Endurance Drift from the Drift mods or Vigilante Pursuit (Exilus) help, as does Zenurik focus. Ability Strength increases Energy Shell absorption capacity and Seeking Fire damage. Redirection improves the already solid shield pool. For players using Odonata in sustained missions, Primed Flow significantly extends the ability uptime.

## Lore

The Odonata was the first Archwing operational model distributed to active Tenno by the Lotus, serving as the standard-issue space-combat harness before specialized variants were developed. Its design philosophy -- reliability over specialization -- reflects the practical constraints of early Archwing technology. The ordnance systems (Seeking Fire, Energy Shell) were later refined and enhanced in the Odonata Prime, which represents the Orokin-era gold standard of the original design.
