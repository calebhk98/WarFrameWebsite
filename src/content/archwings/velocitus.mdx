## Overview

The Velocitus is a magnetic accelerator arch-gun that fires supercharged slugs dealing the highest single-shot damage of any arch-gun in the game. Each shot deals 1600 total damage split evenly between impact, puncture, slash, and magnetic (400 each), giving it a balanced physical and elemental damage profile. Its 30% critical chance and 2.0x multiplier, combined with the raw per-shot damage, make it the premier choice for eliminating high-value single targets such as Crewships and commanders.

Unlike the automatic weapons in the arch-gun lineup, the Velocitus fires as a semi-automatic weapon with a small 10-round magazine. This demands accurate shot placement -- each trigger pull must count. The 1.4-second reload time is the fastest of any arch-gun, however, which compensates for the small magazine by allowing rapid magazine cycling.

Magnetic damage (400 per shot) is especially effective against Corpus shields, dealing 75% bonus damage to shields before armor damage reduction. In Corpus-heavy Archwing content, the Velocitus strips shields extremely fast, then continues dealing full physical damage to health.

## Acquisition

The Velocitus blueprint and its three components (Barrel, Receiver, Stock) are researched in the Clan Dojo Tenno Lab. Research prerequisites include the standard Archwing research tier. Components require Argon Crystals among the resource costs -- Argon decays out of the inventory if not used promptly, so timing the research to build immediately after farming Argon from Void missions is advisable.

Total build time is approximately 12 hours for all components and the main weapon. Resources are otherwise standard mid-tier Archwing materials.

## Playstyle

The Velocitus is a precision arch-gun for players comfortable with semi-automatic fire discipline in fast-moving Archwing combat. The core technique is identifying priority targets -- Crewships, Corpus Ospreys, or boss-tier enemies -- and landing accurate shots rather than spraying. Each of the 10 rounds in the magazine should be directed at a single, high-health target for maximum efficiency.

Against Grineer in Railjack missions, the mixed physical damage (impact, puncture, slash) avoids any faction-specific penalty. Against Corpus, the magnetic component deals bonus shield damage, eliminating Corpus shielded units faster than any other arch-gun. Against the Sentients in Anomaly missions, the physical damage components bypass Sentient adaptation more reliably than pure elemental builds.

The 1.4-second reload is fast enough that a depleted magazine does not feel punishing. Experienced players carry the Velocitus for anti-capital-ship work (Crewship elimination) and swap to a secondary arch-gun for fighter clearance.

## Recommended builds

Hollowed Point (critical chance) and Sabot Rounds (critical multiplier) push the already solid 30% crit base into consistent territory. Rubedo-Lined Barrel is the highest-priority damage mod, multiplying the high base 1600 damage significantly. Pariticle Munitions (radiation addition) or Thermite Rounds (heat) provide faction-specific bonuses on top of the base magnetic damage.

For Railjack Crewship destruction, a full damage build (Rubedo-Lined Barrel, Hollowed Point, Sabot Rounds) with faction-appropriate elemental mods is optimal. The goal is one-to-two-magazine kills on Crewships, which the Velocitus can achieve with this build in missions up to level 40.

## Notes

The Velocitus does not have a Prime variant as of May 2026. It was introduced in Update 15.0 (October 2014) as part of the original Archwing launch content. Its design -- a charged particle accelerator -- is reflected in the magnetic base damage type and the visual effect of the projectile trail.

The 10-round magazine is the smallest of any arch-gun. Resist the temptation to spray fire; each shot represents 10% of the total magazine. In sustained engagements, managing the reload timing around enemy movement patterns is more important than raw fire rate.
