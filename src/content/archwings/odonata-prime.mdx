## Overview

Odonata Prime is the Orokin-era Prime variant of the [Odonata](/archwings/odonata) Archwing. It improves every defensive stat of the base frame while retaining identical abilities and energy. Health increases from 425 to 650, shields jump from 430 to 640, and armor remains constant at 100. These improvements make Odonata Prime one of the most durable Archwings available, combining the generalist ability set of the original with greatly increased survivability.

Odonata Prime's visual design adds the signature Prime aesthetic -- gold and platinum Orokin accents against the base Odonata silhouette -- while maintaining the aerodynamic profile of the original. The increased bulk of the health and shield pools reflects the Orokin tendency to engineer Prime variants as significantly superior to standard models.

Unlike Prime Warframes, Odonata Prime does not carry different polarity slots than its base version. Players upgrading from Odonata to Odonata Prime can carry over the same mod builds with minimal adjustment.

## Acquisition

Odonata Prime components are obtained through Void Relic farming. The specific relics containing Odonata Prime Blueprint, Harness, Systems, and Wings rotate with Prime Access and unvaulting events. Check the current Fissure mission drop tables in-game or on warframestat.us to identify which relics are currently active.

Odonata Prime components can also be traded between players via warframe.market if specific component blueprints are the limiting factor. The full Prime set typically sells for low to moderate platinum values due to Odonata Prime's regular availability in unvaulting rotations.

## Playstyle

Odonata Prime plays identically to [Odonata](/archwings/odonata) with the added margin of error that the improved health and shield totals provide. New Archwing players who are learning ability rotations will find the extra buffer allows more time to react to incoming damage. Veterans transitioning from Itzal or Amesha will appreciate the increased durability for content where consistent uptime matters more than specialization.

The same low energy pool (85) that constrains base Odonata applies here. Energy management remains the primary skill expression point for Odonata Prime pilots.

## Modding notes

All modding advice from [Odonata](/archwings/odonata) applies directly. The higher base health and shield values give more headroom before investing in Redirection and Vitality, allowing some mod slots to go toward offensive or utility options without sacrificing survivability. Ability Strength and Range remain the primary scaling mods for the ability kit.

## Lore

Odonata Prime was the Orokin's refined and enhanced version of the standard Odonata combat harness. The Orokin's characteristic excess -- gold filigree, polished alloys, significantly increased resource expenditure in the manufacturing process -- resulted in a platform with the same tactical profile as its successor but with substantially more resilience. The Lotus distributed Odonata Prime components via void relics recovered from Orokin caches, making them available to Tenno who proved themselves in Fissure missions.
