## Overview

The Imperator Vandal is the Vandal-variant upgrade of the base Imperator arch-gun. While the standard Imperator is the starter arch-gun awarded by the Archwing quest, the Vandal version significantly improves critical chance, fire rate, and magazine capacity, making it one of the strongest sustained-fire Archwings in the game for players who can obtain it.

The Vandal designation appears on weapons distributed through limited events or Invasion rewards, indicating a Corpus-manufactured variant with enhanced specifications. The Imperator Vandal's statistics reflect this: compared to the base Imperator, critical chance rises from 24% to 28%, critical multiplier improves from 2.0x to 2.4x, fire rate nearly doubles from 16.7 to 25.0 rounds per second, and the magazine expands from 200 to 300 rounds. Per-shot damage (50 total: 20 impact, 12.5 puncture, 17.5 slash) is identical to the base model.

Mastery Rank 5 is required to equip the Imperator Vandal, compared to MR 0 for the base Imperator.

## Acquisition

The Imperator Vandal's three components (Barrel, Receiver, and Blueprint) are obtained from Invasion mission rewards. When Grineer and Corpus factions clash in Invasion conflicts on the star chart, players can complete missions for either side in exchange for rewards. Imperator Vandal components appear in the reward pool when the conflict involves the relevant parties.

Because Invasion conflicts rotate unpredictably, farming Imperator Vandal components requires patience and monitoring the Invasion board regularly. Warframestat.us lists active Invasions in real time. The components can also be traded between players, though Vandal weapon parts cannot be traded in assembled form -- only unbuilt components are tradable.

## Playstyle

The Imperator Vandal is a sustained-fire arch-gun built for continuous DPS. Its high fire rate (25 rps) and large magazine (300 rounds) allow extended engagements without reloading. The 28% critical chance and 2.4x multiplier make it responsive to critical-focused mods -- Point Strike and Vital Sense equivalents in the arch-gun mod pool pay off reliably.

The mixed physical damage (impact, puncture, slash) gives it moderate effectiveness against all factions without requiring specialized elemental combinations. For mixed-faction Archwing content like Railjack missions, this generalist damage profile prevents the need to switch weapons between different enemy types.

Reload time at 4.15 seconds is moderate -- fast enough to not feel punishing but long enough that players should aim to reload during movement gaps rather than in the middle of enemy contact. The 12% status chance is below the threshold where status-focused builds make sense; stick to critical and damage mods.

## Recommended builds

For Archwing and Railjack missions the Imperator Vandal performs well with: Hollowed Point (critical chance), Sabot Rounds (critical multiplier), and Rubedo-Lined Barrel (damage). Pared Turret (fire rate) is useful if the base rate still feels insufficient for the content tier.

In Railjack settings where the arch-gun is used as a heavy weapon (via Gravimag), the same critical-focused build applies. The large magazine size means Primed Rubedo-Lined Barrel's damage increase translates to more total damage per reload cycle.

## Notes

The base Imperator (MR 0) is a viable stepping stone for new Archwing players. The Vandal version is a direct upgrade for all use cases and is worth farming once the player is regularly running Archwing missions. Invasion reward pools cycle, so checking the Invasion board for Imperator Vandal components during off-meta farming sessions is the most efficient acquisition strategy.
