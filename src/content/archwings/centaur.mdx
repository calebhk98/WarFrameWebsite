## Overview

The Centaur is a sword-and-shield arch-melee weapon introduced in Hotfix 15.7.2 (December 2014). Its hybrid design -- one arm holds a slashing blade, the other holds a large circular shield -- reflects its defensive-aggressive identity. Arch-melee weapons serve a niche role in Warframe's combat system, used when an Archwing player closes to within melee range of enemy fighters, but the Centaur's high critical chance (25%) and respectable total damage (376) make it competitive when that situation arises.

The Centaur's damage composition strongly emphasizes puncture (263.2 of 376 total), with slash (75.2) and impact (37.6) providing secondary physical damage types. Puncture deals bonus damage against Ferrite Armor and reduces the target's damage output via the Puncture status effect, making the Centaur particularly effective against armored Grineer fighter units in space combat.

Its 20% status chance paired with 25% critical chance means the Centaur has viable paths into either status-focused or critical-focused mod builds. Mastery Rank 8 is required to equip it.

## Acquisition

The Centaur is available through two paths. Players can purchase the fully built weapon from the in-game Market for 195 platinum, making it accessible without farming. Alternatively, the blueprint and component blueprints (Aegis, Blade, Handle) are researched in the Clan Dojo Tenno Lab at a total research cost of 35,000 credits plus standard Archwing materials. Component blueprints drop from Syndicate offerings: Arbiters of Hexis (Aegis), The Perrin Sequence (Blade), and New Loka (Handle).

## Playstyle

Arch-melee weapons in general are situational -- they come into play when an Archwing player approaches within melee range of an enemy fighter, which typically happens during aggressive charge maneuvers or when navigating tight interior sections of Archwing missions. The Centaur's sword-and-shield configuration is designed for charging headlong into formations, as the in-game description notes.

In practice, the most common use case for the Centaur is as a secondary option when ammunition management for the primary arch-gun becomes a concern in extended missions. The high 25% critical chance makes it reliable for triggering critical hits on melee attacks, and the puncture damage deals with Grineer armor effectively.

Slam attacks with the Centaur deal additional radial damage around the impact point, making them useful for finishing off weakened fighters clustered near the player.

## Modding notes

Arch-melee mods follow similar patterns to ground melee. North Wind (cold), Shocking Touch (electricity), and Volcanic Edge (heat) add status-proc elemental damage. For the Centaur's critical-friendly stats, Organ Shatter (critical multiplier) and True Steel (critical chance) in their arch-melee equivalents are the primary scaling path. The high puncture base damage also benefits from Coiling Viper (attack speed) to increase how fast the 263.2 puncture damage connects per second.

Because arch-melee is used situationally rather than as a primary weapon system for most missions, a balanced build that adds one or two relevant mods without over-investing is typically more cost-effective than a fully optimized configuration.

## Notes

The Centaur's shield component does not function as a blocking mechanic in Archwing mode -- the visual is cosmetic to the weapon design. Blocking mechanics as understood from ground melee do not apply in Archwing combat. The WFCD `blockingAngle: 90` value refers to the weapon's physical geometry, not a functional gameplay mechanic in space.

Centaur shares the arch-melee slot with other arch-melee weapons including the [Knux](/archwings/knux), Agkuza, Veritux, and others. Only one arch-melee weapon can be equipped at a time.
