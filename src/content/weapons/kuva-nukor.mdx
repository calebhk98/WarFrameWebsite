## Overview

Kuva Nukor is a beam-based secondary weapon offering continuous radiation damage ideal for status-application focused builds. Unlike projectile weapons, the Nukor fires an instant-hit beam that maintains connection with targets as long as the trigger is held, enabling zero-travel-time accuracy and persistent damage application. The 50 percent status chance is among the highest for any secondary weapon, providing near-guaranteed status application even without status-focused mods. The fire rate at 10 rounds per second rapidly accumulates status procs, making it a cornerstone weapon for status-heavy damage strategies. The base radiation damage element combines well with cold or electric mods for additional combined elements. The beam mechanics provide exceptional consistency compared to projectile weapons, removing the need for lead-time calculations and enabling precise medium-range control.

The Kuva Nukor represents the Kuva-variant of the original Nukor, acquired through the Sister of Parvos system with a selectable elemental bonus that adds on top of the base radiation damage. The extreme 5.0x critical multiplier rewards the rare but powerful critical hits despite the low 7% base critical chance. The high status chance combined with instant-hit mechanics creates an efficient status application platform that synergizes with frames focused on status amplification. The weapon excels in organized squad play where status coordination maximizes team damage output.

## Acquisition

Kuva Nukor is acquired as a potential reward from Sisters of Parvos encounters in void fissure missions. Players must locate and defeat a Sister of Parvos, then break the weapon reward node during the encounter to unlock the blueprint. The weapon is not tradable, requiring direct gameplay acquisition. The 12-hour build time allows queue management during farming sessions.

## Variants

The original Nukor serves as the base electrical weapon with identical mechanics and lower damage output. The Tenet Kuva Nukor variant exists as an alternative endgame option with different stat weighting. Kuva Nukor bridges these variants by offering customizable element types through forma investment, enabling rapid transition between electricity, magnetic, and radiation builds depending on mission requirements.

## Recommended builds

Barrel Diffusion provides foundational secondary weapon damage while supporting the weapon's high fire rate. Lethal Torrent amplifies fire rate further, pushing status application rates toward guarantee. Pistol Pestilence and Thermite Rounds enable status builds, with Cold Rounds or Toxin combining to create popular viral damage outputs. The high base status chance means status-building mods are essential for proper weapon expression. Critical mods like Pistol Gambit provide minor value given the 16 percent base chance; focus on status application instead. Magazine Warp extends the 40-round capacity for extended engagements. The beam weapon nature allows precision targeting with instant feedback, rewarding accurate positioning and trigger discipline.

## Notes

Kuva Nukor's strength lies in status application rather than raw damage output, making it exceptional in squad contexts where status coordination amplifies team effectiveness. The instant-hit beam mechanics eliminate travel time considerations, enabling zero-compensation aiming compared to projectile weapons. The radiation base element provides natural synergy against armored targets and causes confusion procs that turn enemies against each other. The low 7% base critical chance means investment should focus on status application and fire rate rather than critical scaling, despite the impressive 5.0x crit multiplier being a useful bonus when crits do occur. Its role as a dedicated status applicator elevates it beyond typical secondary weapons, making it a strategic choice for endgame and coordinated mission contexts.
