## Overview

The [Aklex](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Aklex) is the dual version of the Lex semi-automatic pistol, firing at 1.58 rounds per second. Each shot deals 130 total damage: 13 impact, 13 puncture, 104 slash. The slash-dominant profile (80% of total) makes the Aklex effective at generating bleed procs against armored targets. The 20% critical chance and 2.0x multiplier support functional critical builds. The deliberate fire rate rewards precision placement over volume fire.

Riven disposition at 3/5 is neutral. The Aklex Prime offers significantly improved stats for endgame use.

## Acquisition

The Aklex blueprint is available from the Market for credits. As an MR4 weapon, it is accessible in early-mid progression. The completed weapon can also be purchased with platinum.

## Variants

The Aklex Prime (Mastery Rank 10) improves critical chance, multiplier, and magazine size substantially. Players with access to Prime relics should pursue the Prime variant. No Vandal or Wraith version exists.

## Recommended builds

**Critical slash build:** Hornet Strike, Barrel Diffusion, Primed Pistol Gambit, Primed Target Cracker, Hydraulic Crosshairs, Hornet Strike, and two elemental mods for viral. At 20% base critical chance, Primed Pistol Gambit pushes the Aklex into reliable yellow-crit territory. The 104 base slash combined with crits delivers strong burst damage per trigger pull.

**Heavy shot build:** Capitalize on the high per-shot damage with Hornet Strike, Barrel Diffusion, and four elemental mods targeting faction weaknesses. The 130 base damage per shot amplified by elemental mods delivers concentrated burst against priority targets.

**Headshot precision:** Add Hydraulic Crosshairs for headshot-triggered crit bonus. At the Aklex's deliberate fire rate, aimed headshots are achievable per shot, consistently triggering the headshot bonus for elevated crit frequency.

## Synergies

- **Slash proc + armor bypass:** The 104 base slash combined with Hunter Munitions (secondary equivalent) or natural bleed proc triggers bypasses Grineer Ferrite armor mitigation. High-damage-per-shot slash procs deal substantial percentage bleed damage.
- **Banshee's Sonar:** The Aklex's high per-shot damage is ideal for Sonar weak-point targeting. Precision single shots into highlighted Sonar weak points deliver maximum amplified burst damage.
- **Mesa's Peacemaker:** Mesa's passive bonuses apply to all gunplay. The Aklex's deliberate single-shot fire pattern receives full benefit from Peacemaker's accuracy and damage bonuses.

## Notes

- The 3.0-second reload is the longest among basic dual secondaries. At 12 rounds and 1.58 fire rate, the magazine lasts approximately 7.6 seconds. Reload discipline -- initiating between engagements -- is essential.
- The deliberate 1.58 fire rate makes the Aklex unsuitable for area suppression but ideal for single-target elimination. Use in missions where target selection matters more than raw fire volume.
- Riven disposition at 3/5 offers neutral roll potential. A riven with multishot converts the Aklex's slow fire rate into a pseudo-shotgun output, significantly amplifying burst damage per trigger pull.
