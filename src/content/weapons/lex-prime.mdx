## Overview

The Lex Prime stands as the defining high-damage secondary weapon, delivering devastating single-shot power through precise puncture-focused damage application. This semi-automatic pistol rewards accurate aim through exceptional damage scaling on critical hits. The weapon enables powerful secondary-only playstyles where pistol mastery determines mission success. Lex Prime excels in solo endurance content and challenging assassination missions where concentrated burst damage eliminates priority targets rapidly. The weapon's relatively low fire rate demands trigger discipline and tactical positioning, creating engaging gameplay centered on accuracy-based rewards.

## Stats

Lex Prime delivers 144 puncture damage per shot, overwhelmingly favoring armor penetration over impact spread. Impact and slash components each contribute 18 damage, with puncture dominance enabling exceptional armor-type targeting. Fire rate of 2.08 rounds per second sustains reasonable offensive pressure while maintaining reload frequency management. Magazine capacity of just 8 rounds necessitates frequent reload cycles at 2.35 seconds, creating natural engagement pacing. Critical chance of 25% with 2x multiplier enables powerful critical builds through modest mod investment, transforming accurate shot playstyles into high-burst scenarios.

## Acquisition

Lex Prime components distribute across advanced Axi void relics (Axi L4, Axi M2, Axi N7), making acquisition require high-level void fissure participation. Axi relic rarity creates longer farming expectations compared to earlier-era relic pools, compensating through commonality of void fissure mission frequency in late-game rotations. Each component requires 6 forma and approximately 6 hours crafting time, with total construction demand of 18,000 credits. Unvaulted status maintains current market availability, though Axi relic frequency fluctuates with content rotation cycles.

## Damage and Performance

The 144 puncture-dominant damage profile creates specialized performance against heavily armored targets, particularly Grineer units with proportional armor scaling. The 25% crit chance baseline combines excellently with Point Strike and Vital Sense, enabling 55%+ crit rates through modest mod investment. Status chance of 25% enables reliable proc application, particularly valuable for Corrosive combinations eliminating armor-based advantages. The semi-automatic fire mechanics reward precision aiming, creating skill-based damage scaling tied directly to player accuracy.

## Mod Recommendations

Hornet Strike and Barrel Diffusion establish secondary weapon damage foundation, with these mods determining primary DPS scaling relative to other pistol platforms. Point Strike and Vital Sense enable critical-focused builds where 25% base crit chance achieves 60%+ through stacking. Elemental damage mods like Heated Charge and Pistol Pestilence provide status application scaling, particularly Corrosive for armor stripping. Mag size extensions through Lethal Force enable extended engagement between reloads. Reload speed mods reduce downtime, critical quality-of-life improvement.
