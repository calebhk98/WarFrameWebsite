## Overview

Kronen Prime is the Prime variant of the Kronen dual blades, delivering improved attack speed and critical output over its base counterpart. Dual blades in Warframe combine fast multi-hit attack strings with wide sweep coverage, and the Kronen Prime version pushes this archetype with a 1.17 attack speed well above the standard melee baseline. The puncture-dominant damage distribution -- 21.2 impact, 169.6 puncture, and 21.2 slash -- emphasizes armor penetration against Grineer targets, while the 34% status chance enables consistent elemental proc application with appropriate mod combinations.

This weapon sits at Mastery Rank 13, positioning it firmly in the endgame tier alongside other Prime melee weapons that demand significant progression to unlock. The combination of attack speed, 22% critical chance, and 34% status chance gives Kronen Prime the statistical foundation for both critical and status-focused builds, making it a versatile platform across a range of content types.

The 169.6 puncture damage constitutes the majority of each hit's damage output and provides a natural advantage against heavily armored Grineer units, where puncture damage is amplified by armor damage modifiers. The 22% base critical chance is solid for a dual-blade weapon and scales well with Blood Rush as combo counter builds. The 34% status chance allows two combined elemental mods to produce procs consistently even before Weeping Wounds contributes additional scaling.

The 1.167 attack speed is above the standard melee baseline and produces more hits per second than slower polearm or heavy blade weapons. More hits per unit of time means faster combo counter buildup, which accelerates the effectiveness of scaling mods like Blood Rush and Weeping Wounds simultaneously.

## Acquisition

Kronen Prime components -- Blueprint, Blade, and Handle -- are obtained from Void Relics tied to the Kronen Prime Access rotation. When the associated Prime Access is active, components appear in Lith, Meso, Neo, or Axi relics depending on the component. When Prime Access concludes, the weapon enters the Prime Vault and its relics are removed from the standard drop table. The weapon returns during unvault events or when its rotation cycles back.

The base Kronen is available from the in-game Market for credits and serves as a lower-stat entry point for dual-blade builds.

## Variants

The base Kronen is an accessible dual-blade option from the Market, with lower attack speed and reduced status chance compared to the Prime. Kronen Prime improves the overall stat package substantially, particularly in the status chance category, making it the preferred choice for endgame builds.

No Kuva, Tenet, or Wraith variant of the Kronen exists. The Kronen Prime is the highest-tier version in the weapon family.

## Recommended builds

A critical build for Kronen Prime uses Blood Rush as the primary scaling mod, combined with Organ Shatter for critical multiplier and Primed Pressure Point for base damage amplification. Because the 1.167 attack speed generates combo counts quickly, Blood Rush produces high effective critical chance at medium combo with minimal downtime. Adding Condition Overload alongside status-applying elemental mods converts each proc into additional damage multiplication.

A status build leverages Weeping Wounds alongside two elemental mods -- commonly Primed Fever Strike and Virulent Scourge for Viral -- to exploit the 34% base status chance. At higher combo counts, Weeping Wounds pushes effective status chance well above 100%, guaranteeing multiple procs per swing and enabling Condition Overload to reach its maximum multiplier quickly.

For endgame Steel Path survival missions, a hybrid approach combining Blood Rush, Weeping Wounds, Condition Overload, and Viral damage provides both consistent proc application and strong crit damage output. This configuration benefits from Kronen Prime's dual-stat spread without requiring specialization into a single damage mode.

## Notes

Dual-blade stance mods for Kronen Prime include Carving Mantis and Dividing Blades. Carving Mantis provides gap-closing directional combos that allow rapid repositioning while maintaining damage output. The stance influences which combo strings produce the highest hit counts, which affects combo counter buildup speed and therefore the effectiveness of scaling mods.

Kronen Prime's attack strings hit multiple times per swing animation, which multiplies status proc chances per attack sequence beyond what the 34% base status chance alone implies. In practice, a full combo string against a group of enemies produces numerous proc opportunities, making Kronen Prime particularly effective in dense enemy environments.
