## Overview

Karak is a Primary weapon in Warframe with a focus on delivering consistent physical damage through a balanced distribution of impact, puncture, and slash components. The weapon's design emphasizes reliability, making it suitable for a broad range of mission types and enemy compositions. Whether tackling early-game content or supplementing endgame builds, Karak provides a solid foundation for players seeking a dependable damage source.

The weapon's characteristics make it particularly valuable for newer players establishing weapon diversity while simultaneously offering viable damage scaling for experienced players willing to invest proper modding resources. Its learning curve remains gentle, allowing new Tenno to focus on enemy positioning and ability usage rather than complex weapon mechanics.

## Stats

Karak delivers a total damage output of 29 per hit, distributed across 13 impact, 7 puncture, and 9 slash. This balanced physical damage breakdown means the weapon performs adequately against all armor types without requiring extensive elemental augmentation, though such additions significantly amplify its effectiveness.

Critical stats show a 9.0% chance to trigger critical hits with a 1.5x damage multiplier on success. This translates to consistent critical damage when paired with crit-enhancing mods, enabling viable crit-focused build paths. Status chance sits at 15.0%, providing moderate baseline proc application that scales substantially with status-boosting mods and elemental damage additions.

The weapon's range characteristics allow for standard engagement distances typical of its weapon class. Attack speed and damage per swing make it viable as both a primary damage source and a utility tool in mixed damage compositions. Attack animations are relatively standard for the weapon class, facilitating smooth weapon transitions during combat rotations.

## Acquisition

Karak is available as a blueprint from the in-game Market for Credits. Components are standard resources that newer players can gather from early planetary nodes. The weapon requires 24 hours to build in the Foundry. It is an appropriate choice for players in the Mastery Rank 1 range who want a balanced rifle before investing in harder-to-obtain options.

## Variants

The Karak has two additional variants. Karak Wraith, obtained through time-limited Operation events, improves critical chance to 13%, critical multiplier to 2.0x, and status chance to 25%. No Prime variant of the Karak exists as of the current game version.

## Recommended builds

Successful Karak builds follow several established archetypes depending on mission type and squad composition:

**Crit-focused builds** prioritize critical chance and critical multiplier mods to maximize per-hit damage on headshots and weak points. Stack Point Strike, True Steel, and potentially Blood Rush for scaling critical chance. This build path scales well into higher-level content where precision placement becomes increasingly valuable. Heavy attack builds benefit significantly from this approach, converting consistent crits into devastating heavy attack strikes.

**Status-focused builds** emphasize status chance modding paired with elemental damage to reliably trigger status effects on enemies. Combining multiple elemental mods creates combined damage types like Corrosive against armored units or Toxin for raw damage output. This path particularly benefits players targeting specific enemy types where status application provides meaningful tactical advantages.

**Balanced utility builds** combine modest crit investment with elemental damage, attack speed, and damage mods for consistent all-around performance. Utility mods like Heavy Impact or elemental damage conversion mods expand tactical options without sacrificing damage output. This approach performs well across diverse mission objectives without requiring precision or perfect status application timing.

**Elemental builds** layer elemental damage types for effective coverage against specific enemy factions. Corrosive dominates against Grineer armor, Toxin provides unmodified health damage, and Magnetic strips Corpus shields effectively. Customizing elemental combinations based on mission faction or planetary tileset maximizes damage efficiency.

## Notes

The Karak's 15% status chance is one of the higher values available in the early-access weapon range, making it useful for new players exploring status proc mechanics. Its fire rate of 11.67 rounds per second applies procs frequently, which partially compensates for modest per-shot damage. The 9% critical chance is too low to support reliable crit builds without heavy mod investment, so status-focused or hybrid builds perform better at the mastery ranks where this weapon is typically used.
