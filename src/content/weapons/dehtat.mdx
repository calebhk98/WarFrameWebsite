## Overview

The Dehtat is a Zaw Strike component available from Hok's Anvil at Cetus on the Plains of Eidolon. As a Strike, it defines the base weapon archetype and initial damage profile of the assembled Zaw. WFCD reports zeros for all standalone stats because the Dehtat is an unassembled part, not a functional weapon on its own. When combined with a compatible Grip and Link component at Hok's Anvil, the resulting Zaw inherits the Strike's contribution to the final stat pool.

In the three-part Zaw construction system, the Strike determines the fundamental weapon class: whether the assembled Zaw functions as a dagger, polearm, heavy blade, or another melee archetype. The Dehtat Strike in particular enables specific weapon types that benefit from particular stance mods. Selecting the correct Grip pairing adjusts the assembled Zaw's attack speed and alters how the Strike's stats scale into the full configuration. Consulting Hok's interface before committing to a purchase is essential, as assembled stats are visible in a preview before spending Ostron Standing.

Zaw weapons assembled with the Dehtat Strike can be gilded at Mastery Rank 8 or higher to unlock Arcane slots and allow Forma installation for polarity customization. Gilding also allows the Zaw to be named by the player.

## Acquisition

Purchase from Hok's Anvil at Cetus on the Plains of Eidolon. Ostron Standing is required; higher-tier Strike components unlock at higher Ostron rank. Earn Ostron Standing through fishing, mining, and bounty completion in the Plains of Eidolon. Check [the Dehtat Fandom wiki page](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Dehtat) for the exact Ostron rank requirement and standing cost.

## Variants

Zaw Strikes do not have Prime, Kuva, or Tenet versions. The Dehtat is one of several Strike options available from Hok's Anvil, each contributing differently to the assembled Zaw configuration. Compare the full selection of Strikes at Hok's interface to determine which best suits the desired weapon class and stat profile.

## Recommended builds

When incorporating the Dehtat Strike into a Zaw build:

- Choose a Grip that produces the desired weapon category and attack speed. Heavy Grips favor two-handed configurations with higher damage per swing; light Grips increase attack speed for faster combo generation.
- Select a Link component based on whether the build prioritizes critical chance, status chance, or a balance of both. Ekwana-family links skew toward critical; Ruhang-family links skew toward status.
- After assembly, preview the final Zaw stats in Hok's interface before purchasing.
- Gild the Zaw at MR8 or higher to access Arcane slots, then apply Forma to match desired polarity configurations.
- Install a Stance mod matching the assembled Zaw's weapon category to unlock the full combo set.

## Synergies

The assembled Zaw's final performance depends on the full Strike-Grip-Link combination. High crit-weighted configurations benefit from Blood Rush and Organ Shatter. Status-weighted builds suit Weeping Wounds and Condition Overload. Frames that generate combo (Mirage's Hall of Mirrors, Equinox's Rest and Rage) amplify any assembled Zaw regardless of the chosen Strike.
