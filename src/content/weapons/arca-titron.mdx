## Overview

The Arca Titron is a Mastery Rank 10 hammer-type melee weapon manufactured by the Corpus Arca series. It deals 360 total damage split between Impact (234) and Puncture (126), with 24% Critical Chance at 2.0x multiplier and an impressive 38% Status Chance. The Arca Titron charge passive triggers when enemies are killed with electricity-based damage -- upon a full charge, the weapon next heavy attack deals significantly amplified damage.

The 38% base Status Chance is particularly noteworthy for a melee weapon, enabling rapid status application especially when combined with elemental mods. Electricity is a natural elemental fit for the Arca Titron and activates the weapon charge mechanic synergistically when used as an added elemental mod.

## Acquisition

The Arca Titron blueprint is available from the in-game Market for credits. Components are crafted in the Foundry. No special prerequisites beyond Mastery Rank 10 are required.

## Variants

The Arca Titron does not have a Prime or Kuva variant as of this writing. Check the [Arca Titron wiki page](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Arca_Titron) for updates.

## Recommended builds

The 38% Status Chance is the primary build driver. Fever Strike and Volcanic Edge add Toxin and Heat for Radiation or Gas combinations. Condition Overload multiplies damage per unique status type active on an enemy, synergizing with the multiple procs the Titron applies quickly. Primed Pressure Point and Berserker Fury are core damage mods for any melee build.

The charge mechanic rewards an Electricity mod (Shocking Touch) to activate charges from electrically enhanced kills. A fully charged heavy attack can delete high-level enemy health in endgame content.

## Notes

- The Arca Titron charge passive requires kills with electricity damage; equipping an Electricity mod accelerates charge generation.
- Hammers have a slightly lower attack speed than swords; Heavy Attack builds benefit from this weapon power spike.
- 2.6m range is average for hammers; wide swing arcs cover multiple enemies per hit in dense groups.
- The Impact-heavy damage profile procs the Impact status (stagger) frequently with high status chance.
- Condition Overload synergy is especially effective on the Arca Titron because the weapon can simultaneously apply Electricity (from the mod), Impact, and Puncture procs in a single combo sequence, rapidly reaching three or more unique status types for maximum Condition Overload bonus.
- The Arca series of Corpus weapons -- Arca Titron, Arca Plasmor, Arca Scisco -- are designed with energy cell aesthetics and passive electricity mechanics. The Titron is the melee representative of this series, distinguishing it from standard hammers that lack an active charge mechanic.
- Heavy Attack builds pair well with Amalgam Organ Shatter (bonus heavy attack wind-up speed) and Life Strike (health on hit) to sustain the player through endgame content while maximizing charge mechanic output.
