## Overview

The Fragor Prime is the Prime variant of the Fragor heavy hammer weapon, with an Impact-dominant damage profile: 189 Impact, 40.5 Puncture, and 40.5 Slash. The 40% critical chance is one of the highest base crit values among heavy melee weapons, and combined with Blood Rush scaling, the Fragor Prime reliably reaches orange and red crits at high combo multipliers. The 2.5x multiplier amplifies each crit significantly at these high base values. The 18% status chance is secondary. Attack speed of 0.8 is slow -- expected for a heavy weapon -- and the 2.6 m range provides wide swing coverage. Mastery Rank 12 is required.

Heavy-hitting Impact damage is most effective against Corpus Shields, where Impact receives bonus modifier damage. The Fragor Prime's 189 Impact per swing, amplified by Blood Rush orange crits, delivers substantial per-hit shield-strip damage against Corpus units. Heavy blade stance mods (Cleaving Whirlwind, Rending Crane, Tempo Royale) provide the Fragor Prime with wide-arc sweeping combos suited for crowd-clearing. Slam attacks from heavy weapons apply radial damage that benefits from the Impact's stagger effect on grouped enemies. Mastery Rank 12 positions the Fragor Prime as an endgame primary melee weapon.

## Variants

The base Fragor is the standard heavy hammer, available at lower mastery rank. The Fragor Prime significantly improves crit chance, multiplier, and base damage. No Kuva or Tenet variant exists for the Fragor family.

## Recommended builds

**Blood Rush orange-crit build.** At 40% base crit with Blood Rush, the Fragor Prime enters orange-crit territory by combo x2 and reaches red crit at higher multipliers. Equip Organ Shatter for the multiplier boost, Sacrificial Steel for additional crit chance scaling, and two Viral 60/60 mods. The 189 Impact base delivers very high per-swing orange-crit damage. Berserker Fury compensates for the slow 0.8 attack speed after each kill.

**Heavy-slam Impact/Corpus build.** The Impact base combined with a Magnetic elemental combination (Cold + Electric 60/60 mods) creates a Corpus-specialized build. Magnetic procs drain Corpus shields, and each Impact hit against drained shields delivers the full 189 Impact damage without shield absorption. Killing Blow enhances heavy slam finisher damage, and Amalgam Organ Shatter provides attack speed after slams.

**Cleaving Whirlwind crowd build.** Equip Cleaving Whirlwind stance for wide-arc sweeping combos that cover the 2.6 m range fully per swing. Combine with Primed Pressure Point, Blood Rush, and Condition Overload. Despite low status, the 18% base status applies Viral and elemental procs alongside the high-crit physical hits.

## Synergies

Mag's Magnetize concentrates enemy damage from all sources; the Fragor Prime's sweeping heavy attacks within a Magnetize bubble amplify damage due to the proc concentration effect. Wisp's Haste Mote compensates for the 0.8 attack speed with frame-level speed augmentation. Wukong's passive combo preservation allows Blood Rush to sustain orange crits across extended engagements.

## Notes

- The 40% critical chance is the Fragor Prime's defining stat; always equip Blood Rush and Organ Shatter to maximize the orange/red crit potential.
- The 0.8 attack speed is slow; compensate with Berserker Fury, Arcane Strike, or Wisp's Haste Mote.
