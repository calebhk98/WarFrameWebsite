## Overview

The [Coda Pox](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Coda_Pox) is the Jade Shadows enhanced version of the Pox secondary at Mastery Rank 17. It fires canisters that release a cloud of toxic gas dealing 90 pure toxin damage per hit. The 45% status chance enables consistent toxin proc application across the gas cloud's affected area. Toxin bypasses Corpus shields, dealing damage directly to health. The 10% critical chance and 2.2x multiplier are secondary considerations.

## Acquisition

Obtained through the Jade Shadows content update. Check the [Coda Pox Wiki page](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Coda_Pox) for acquisition sources tied to Jade Shadows mission rewards.

## Variants

The Coda Pox is the enhanced form of the base Pox (MR5). The base version has lower status chance and damage. The Coda form is the endgame upgrade for toxin-cloud secondary builds.

## Recommended builds

**Toxin status build:** Hornet Strike, Barrel Diffusion, Lethal Torrent, Pistol Pestilence (additional toxin), Frostbite (cold for viral with the toxin), Scorch (heat), Jolt (electric), and Primed Quickdraw. The 45% status chance combined with the gas cloud mechanic (multiple hits per cloud) applies viral and heat procs reliably. Toxin bypass on Corpus shields makes this effective in Corpus endless missions.

**Shield bypass Corpus build:** Focus on toxin and magnetic (toxin + cold... wait, cold + electric = magnetic) combinations. Toxin bypasses shields; magnetic drains shields simultaneously. This combination eliminates shields from both bypass and drain effects. Add heat for damage-over-time against Corpus flesh.

**Area denial:** Throw canisters into chokepoints to create persistent toxic cloud zones. Enemies passing through take multiple hits from the cloud's duration, accumulating toxin procs. This is effective in Defense, Mobile Defense, and Excavation missions.

## Synergies

- **Corpus bypass:** Toxin's shield bypass combined with the Coda Pox's cloud mechanic delivers multiple bypassing hits per canister, eliminating Corpus health rapidly.
- **Saryn's Spores:** Toxin procs from the Coda Pox spread through Spores, extending the cloud's effective range across enemy groups.
- **Area chokepoint control:** Clouds persist for a duration. In missions with defined enemy paths, thrown canisters create denial zones that damage and slow enemies without requiring player attention.

## Notes

- The gas cloud mechanic differs from direct-fire weapons. Each canister creates an AOE cloud that strikes enemies multiple times during its duration. Status procs accumulate from multiple cloud hits against the same target.
- The 1.0-second reload and 8-round magazine make the Coda Pox one of the most manageable reload cycles among secondary weapons. Reload between canister deployments is rarely disruptive.
- Mastery Rank 17 gates this weapon to veterans. The 45% status chance at this tier is competitive with top-performing status secondaries.
