## Overview

Itzal is an agile Archwing introduced in Update 18.4, designed for mobility and utility rather than raw firepower. With the lowest health (310) and shield (240) of the classic Archwing trio but the highest sprint speed, Itzal excels in mission types prioritizing speed: mobile rescues, data extractions, and timed objectives. Its utility abilities provide mobility boosts, damage buffs, and crowd control rather than offensive firepower.

Itzal represents the fragile speedster archetype: piloting it demands positioning discipline and active dodge mechanics. Experienced players find Itzal superior to bulkier alternatives because mobility prevents damage more effectively than armor scaling. For new players, Itzal punishes positioning mistakes more severely than balanced Archwings.

## Abilities

Arch Line launches a tethered grappling hook that pulls enemies close or allows the Warframe to swing toward fixed objects. This mobility tool enables short-range repositioning without relying on flight acceleration, useful for tight tileset navigation and rapid target engagement.

Penumbra activates a cloaking field that renders Itzal and nearby allies invisible while allowing unrestricted movement. Unlike invisibility frames, Penumbra breaks on ability activation rather than weapon fire, enabling invis-casting builds with proper energy management.

Accelerant increases sprint speed for Itzal and nearby allies, enabling rapid engagement and escape. The buff stacks with existing speed mods, allowing compositions built entirely around movement speed for trivializing mobility-dependent mission types.

Soar transfers damage received to the immediate surroundings, converting incoming fire into area denial. Enemies attacking Itzal trigger reciprocal damage pulses, creating incentive structures where tanking fire paradoxically inflicts damage.

## Acquisition

Itzal is available through Void Fissure Archwing Void Cascade missions, with the blueprint dropping from rotation C at escalating void levels. The rarity of Void Cascade rotation C drops (typically 2-6% per run) makes Itzal acquisition time-intensive. Clan Dojo research unlocks component crafting once the blueprint is obtained.

Build time is 12 hours, standard for endgame Archwing crafting. Resource costs reflect endgame availability, requiring void fissure farming for Void Traces and standard mission farming for exotic crafting materials.

Itzal has been the #1 speed-running Archwing since its introduction. Competitive void fissure farming groups prioritize Itzal for objective speedrun records.

## Modding Notes

Itzal benefits heavily from ability duration (extending Penumbra invisibility and Accelerant duration). Sprint speed scaling on mods like Vigilante Pursuit compounds Accelerant benefits, enabling 3.0+ sprint multipliers on optimized builds. Energy efficiency reduces invisibility spam drain during sustained invis phases. Health/shield mods provide band-aid scaling for the fragile frame, but proper positioning remains the primary defense.

Optimal Itzal builds prioritize duration and efficiency, with secondary scaling on speed mods. Umbral mods provide no value because Itzal's abilities do not leverage strength scaling.

## Playstyle

Abuse Penumbra invisibility for positioning and damage avoidance. Use Arch Line for tactical repositioning and crowd control. Activate Accelerant before objective callouts to enable rapid deployment. Spam Penumbra when overwhelmed. Managing invisibility uptime and energy is the core skill for Itzal players -- understanding energy regeneration rates and ability cooldowns separates masterful pilots from button-mashers.

Newer pilots struggle with Itzal's low durability. Experienced players leverage mobility to avoid damage entirely, making Itzal feel invincible compared to durable frames that rely on armor scaling.

## Synergies

Itzal pairs exceptionally well with Volt's speed buffs in squad compositions. Harrow's invisibility synergy enables indefinite unseen firing. Trinity provides energy refunds, enabling permanent Penumbra uptime when built for full efficiency. Wisp's mobility buffs stack with Accelerant for movement-focused endgame compositions.

Itzal does not provide external buffs, so squad building focuses on enabling Itzal's mobility rather than leveraging Itzal's utility contributions.

## Lore

Itzal was conceptualized as the scout Archwing, reflecting the Tenno philosophy of information gathering and rapid deployment. The mobility toolkit prioritizes intel collection and objective extraction over battlefield control. Itzal's cloaking technology is theorized to be Orokin void-based rather than conventional stealth, explaining the immunity to sensor detection systems.

## Variants

Itzal Prime is a vaulted variant offering improved stats across all categories. Prime variants of other Archwings share equivalent stat improvements, maintaining Itzal's role as the speedy frame despite numeric enhancements.
