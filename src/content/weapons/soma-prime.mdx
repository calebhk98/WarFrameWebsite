## Overview

Soma Prime is the prime variant of the Soma rifle, delivering exceptional fire rate combined with a massive magazine capacity of 100 rounds. This weapon excels at sustained fire and crowd suppression, making it ideal for endless missions and high-level content. The prime variant improves upon the standard Soma with enhanced critical statistics and overall reliability, cementing its position as one of the most popular assault rifles in Warframe.

## Damage

Soma Prime delivers balanced damage with slash emphasis, making it effective for sustained fire and crowd clearing. The 30 percent critical chance combined with 8.58 fire rate allows consistent critical hits even without specialized mods. The massive magazine capacity enables extended combat before reload is necessary. Each shot scales dramatically with elemental mods and critical enhancements.

## Acquisition

Soma Prime components are obtained from void relics across all difficulty tiers. The weapon has been unvaulted multiple times, ensuring accessibility. Blueprint assembly requires credits and an orokin catalyst.

## Builds

A Soma Prime critical build combines serration, point strike, vital sense, and elemental mods. The exceptional fire rate makes critical scaling extremely valuable. Magazine extensions allow sustained fire for indefinite periods in endless missions. For endgame content, riven mods with positive damage or critical chance rolls transform this into a sustained damage powerhouse. The weapon's fire rate synergizes exceptionally well with attack speed buffs.

## Synergies

Soma Prime pairs exceptionally well with warframes that provide weapon scaling or fire-rate bonuses, such as Mirage (for multishot multiplication), Mesa (for weapon scaling), or Volt (for speed synergies). Arcane bonuses that trigger on critical hits amplify the weapon's exceptional fire rate. The weapon's sustained fire nature makes it ideal for endless missions and coordinated group content.

## Lore

Soma Prime represents the pinnacle of assault rifle design, combining rapid fire with reliable damage output. The prime variant's superior statistics make it one of the most iconic weapons in Warframe, favored by both casual players and endgame enthusiasts.
