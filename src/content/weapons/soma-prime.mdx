## Overview

The Soma Prime is the primed variant of the Soma assault rifle and one of the most recognizable weapons in Warframe. Its 15 rounds-per-second fire rate and 200-round magazine combine to produce extended suppressive fire unmatched in its class. The 30% critical chance and 3x critical multiplier define the weapon as a critical-focused platform: with proper mod investment it delivers consistent orange or red crits at the speed of a full-auto rifle.

Per-round damage is intentionally low at 12 total (1.2 impact, 6 puncture, 4.8 slash). The weapon's design philosophy is volume-of-fire scaling: the 3x multiplier multiplied across 15 rounds per second yields significantly higher effective damage per second than high-damage, low-fire-rate alternatives when the critical threshold is met. The 10% status chance is modest; the Soma Prime is not a status weapon and functions best when treated purely as a critical DPS platform.

The Soma Prime has remained viable across multiple content expansions due to the universality of critical builds and the flexibility of its large magazine, which allows extended engagements without a reload interrupting sustained damage.

## Acquisition

Soma Prime components drop from void relics across all four tiers. The weapon is currently unvaulted, meaning its relics are in the active drop pool and can be obtained from Void Fissure missions. Blueprint, Barrel, Stock, and Receiver each appear in different relic reward slots. Running Void Fissures with radiant relics maximizes rare drop chances for any single component.

## Variants

The base [Soma](/weapons/soma) offers the same fire rate profile at lower critical values (25% chance, 2.5x multiplier) and mastery rank 6. There is no Kuva or Tenet Soma. The Soma Prime represents the final and strongest form of the weapon family. Players looking for a comparable sustained-fire primary with higher per-shot damage can consider the Stradavar Prime or Braton Prime.

## Recommended builds

**Standard critical build.** Point Strike, Vital Sense, and Serration form the foundation. The 30% base critical chance reaches 90% with Point Strike, ensuring consistent orange crits on every shot. Vital Sense at maximum rank pushes the effective multiplier above 7x. Two 90% elemental mods (Corrosive for Grineer, Radiation for Corpus) complete the primary damage path.

**Galvanized critical build.** Galvanized Chamber replaces Split Chamber for players with Galvanized mods. Galvanized Aptitude adds bonus damage per status type, incentivizing one or two status procs on target even in a primarily critical build. Arcane Avenger (triggering on damage taken) or Arcane Acceleration (triggering on critical hits) both synergize with the Soma Prime's fire rate.

**Concentrated fire endgame build.** Heavy Caliber reduces accuracy but increases damage; at 15 rounds per second and a 200-round magazine, the spray pattern at mid-range remains viable since most rounds still connect. This build prioritizes raw damage multiplication over accuracy, effective in close-quarters or defense-style missions.

## Notes

- The 3-second reload is the most significant tactical limitation; leaving around 20-30 rounds in the magazine and reloading during a cover break avoids a full reload mid-combat.
- Arcane Momentum (unlocked from Warframe) reduces reload time on heavy attack kills; it does not apply to rifles, but Arcane Acceleration on critical hits does and directly benefits this weapon.
- Mastery rank 7 is low relative to the weapon's performance tier, making it accessible to mid-game players who have completed the Void relic system introduction.
- The 200-round magazine is large enough that Rifle Ammo Mutation in the exilus slot is rarely necessary even in ammo-scarce missions.
