## Overview

Soma Prime is one of the most iconic primary weapons in Warframe, beloved by new and veteran players alike. This primed assault rifle combines exceptional fire rate with a massive magazine capacity, enabling sustained damage output without frequent reloads. The weapon fires 15 rounds per second with perfect accuracy at range, making it reliable for both close-quarters and mid-range engagements. Its balanced damage split favoring puncture makes it effective against armored targets, while the 30 percent critical chance and impressive 3.0x critical multiplier reward accurate shooting. The 200-round magazine eliminates ammunition concerns during most mission types, allowing players to focus on positioning and targeting rather than resource management. Soma Prime's accessibility at Mastery Rank 7 positions it as an excellent investment for early progression, while its raw capabilities keep it viable throughout the entire star chart.

The weapon's popularity stems from its straightforward effectiveness and farm accessibility through void fissures. The combination of high fire rate and magazine capacity creates a sense of reliability uncommon in weapons that must balance damage-per-shot with ammunition conservation. The puncture-heavy damage split inherently counters armor-heavy enemies like Grineer units, making it particularly valuable for Grineer faction missions and the star chart's numerous Grineer nodes. Soma Prime lacks flashy mechanics or elemental specialization, instead offering consistent mechanical excellence that transcends meta shifts. This foundational stability makes it an excellent baseline weapon for comparing other assault rifles and for players building toward more specialized loadouts.

## Acquisition

Soma Prime components drop from void fissures accessible through the Lith M2 and Meso D5 relic sets. The barrel typically appears in Lith M2 relics at uncommon rarity. The stock and receiver distribute across complementary relic sets. Since Soma Prime was originally released in 2014 and remains unvaulted, its relics appear frequently in community void fissure squads, making it one of the fastest-farmed prime weapons available. Players can acquire components through marketplace trading, though farming them directly from void fissure missions builds self-sufficiency.

## Variants

The original Soma serves as the non-prime baseline, sharing identical mechanics with slightly lower damage output. The Kuva Soma exists as an endgame variant with customizable element properties. Soma Prime bridges these two, offering the optimal balance of accessibility and raw power. The Incarnon Form transformation on newer Soma variants introduces advanced mechanics not available on the prime version, making the original and prime more straightforward in application.

## Recommended builds

Serration provides the foundational damage amplification, essential for primary weapons. Point Strike and Vital Sense maximize critical potential, pushing the weapon well above guaranteed critical hits on second hit. Piercing Strikes enhances the puncture component and stacks multiplicatively with Serration, creating excellent armor penetration. Magazine extension is optional given the 100-round capacity, but Primed Mag Capacity can extend engagements for extreme endurance runs. Fire rate mods like Lethal Momentum add minimal value since the weapon already fires at maximum efficiency; players instead focus on crit, damage, and accuracy enhancements. Physical damage mods like Piercing Impact add niche utility against heavily armored targets. Elemental mods can be applied but the weapon's balanced damage split doesn't lend itself to particular elemental builds as well as specialized weapons.

## Notes

Soma Prime's enduring popularity reflects its simple, effective design. The weapon requires no special techniques or playstyle adjustments to extract value, making it an excellent beginner weapon that scales through mid-game content. The 200-round magazine eliminates the intermediate reloads that plague other rifles, improving flow and reaction time. Its accessibility and lack of specialized mechanics mean that learning Soma Prime transfers directly to other weapons, making it an excellent training tool. The 10 percent status chance is rarely modded toward status-immunity builds due to the weapon's focus on consistent damage rather than status application.
