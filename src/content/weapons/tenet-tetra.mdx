## Overview

The Tenet Tetra is a Sisters of Parvos Tenet primary requiring Mastery Rank 16. It is the enhanced form of the Tetra energy rifle, firing bolts at 5.0 rounds per second with an 80-round magazine. Its 60 damage per shot splits between 33.6 slash, 13.2 puncture, and 13.2 impact -- a slash-majority split that generates bleed procs while retaining minor contributions from both other physical types for general coverage.

The 28% critical chance and 2.2x multiplier are solid for a rifle at this fire rate. With Point Strike, critical chance reaches above 80%. The 30% status chance is among the higher status rates for a primary rifle, enabling reliable elemental proc application per shot. At 5.0 fire rate with 80 rounds, the Tenet Tetra fires for 16 seconds before the 2.2-second reload. This sustained capability suits Survival, Interception, and Defense mission types.

The Tenet elemental bonus from the parent Sister adds extra elemental damage on top of the base physical damage, making each Tenet Tetra unique in its final damage composition.

## Acquisition

Obtain through the Sisters of Parvos system at Mastery Rank 16. Trigger a Sister Candidate, then track and vanquish the Sister to claim her Tenet Tetra. The elemental bonus type is fixed at Sister creation.

## Variants

**Tetra** -- The base version available from the Market.

**Tenet Tetra** -- The enhanced Sisters of Parvos version with improved damage, crit, and status over the base Tetra.

## Recommended builds

**Viral-slash**: Split Chamber, Serration, cold plus toxin for Viral, Hunter Munitions, and Point Strike. With 28% crit, Hunter Munitions fires often. Viral halves enemy max health; Hunter Munitions-generated slash procs then bleed through that halved pool. The 30% status applies Viral procs consistently.

**Status crit hybrid**: Serration, Split Chamber, Point Strike, Vital Sense, and dual-stat 60/60 elemental mods for Corrosive plus Heat or Viral plus Heat. The 30% status base with dual-stat mods produces multiple elemental types simultaneously, feeding Galvanized Shot stacking.

**Full crit Galvanized**: Galvanized Scope (crit chance on headshot), Galvanized Chamber (multishot on kill), Serration, Point Strike, Vital Sense, and elemental mods. The 80-round magazine sustains fire across multiple Galvanized stack resets.

## Notes

The 80-round magazine and 5.0 fire rate make the Tenet Tetra one of the more sustained-fire Tenet primaries. The accuracy at 18.2 means moderate spread at distance -- medium range is the optimal engagement zone. The Tenet elemental bonus interacts with the physical damage split; a corrosive Tenet bonus on a slash-dominant weapon creates a powerful combined elemental-physical proc platform.
