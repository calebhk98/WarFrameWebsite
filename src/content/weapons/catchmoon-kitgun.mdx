## Overview

The Catchmoon represents a popular kitgun secondary assembly combining the Catchmoon barrel (energy projectile launcher), Haymaker grip (rate of fire), and Zippered link (status chance). This modular configuration creates a radiation-focused secondary excelling in controlled burst damage against grouped targets. The kitgun platform enables extensive customization through component swapping, making any specific assembly one of many viable configurations. The described Catchmoon variant provides balanced status and critical performance, supporting hybrid builds without specialization toward either stat. Perfect accuracy ensures every projectile reaches its target regardless of range. The modular nature means individual players can create entirely different stat profiles through alternative component selection, making kitguns highly personalized weapons reflecting individual playstyle preferences.

## Damage

The Catchmoon's radiation focus provides elemental utility against unarmored and lightly-armored targets. The 23 radiation damage combines with 17% status chance to enable confusion procs, disorienting affected enemies. The balanced critical stats of 25% chance and 1.8x multiplier support hybrid builds, though neither stat reaches specialization tiers. Against heavily armored Grineer, radiation damage becomes less effective without supporting corrosive mods, requiring elemental scaling through mod combinations. The modular weapon platform allows complete stat restructuring through barrel swapping, enabling players to optimize damage profiles toward specific mission requirements.

## Acquisition

Catchmoon kitgun construction requires three modular components: a Catchmoon barrel, a grip frame, and a link assembly. Barrel and grip options are available from Hok at Cetus (Plains of Eidolon) or Rude Zuud at Fortuna (Orb Vallis) in exchange for standing and credits. Component prices vary based on rarity (common components cost less than rare variants). Assembly at the kitgun crafting bench requires 5,000 credits and one hour of crafting time. Alternative component choices create entirely different weapons, giving players agency in performance optimization.

## Variants

The Catchmoon barrel appears as a component option for kitgun secondary construction, not a standalone weapon. Different barrel, grip, and link combinations create completely different weapons with different names (Catchmoon-Haymaker-Zippered represents one specific assembly). Players experimenting with kitgun crafting can create unique configurations, including the Tombfinger barrel as an alternative heavy-impact option. This flexibility enables personal weapon customization beyond fixed weapon choices.

## Recommended builds

The Catchmoon's balanced stats support hybrid critical-status modding. Hornet Strike and Pistol Grip form core damage amplifiers, while True Puncher boosts critical chance toward 50%+. Elemental mods like Heat Damage or Cold Damage enhance radiation procs through multiproc chains. Magazine extension via Increased magazine capacity extends the modest 40-round capacity. Reload-reduction mods like Trick Mag accelerate the 1.2-second reload time. Forma investment unlocks exilus slots for utility mods like ammo mutation.

## Synergies

Frames benefiting from consistent secondary fire synergize well with the Catchmoon. Trinity benefits from the perfect accuracy and reasonable fire rate to maintain passive energy generation. Baruuk's melee-focused playstyle integrates kitgun support fire seamlessly. Grendel absorbs confused targets automatically, enabling synergistic crowd control. Saryn converts radiation status into passive damage scaling through her abilities. Support frames appreciate balanced performance without extreme specialization, enabling flexible playstyle adaptation.

## Lore

Kitguns emerged from open-world faction engineering, blending Ostron (Cetus) and Corpus (Fortuna) design philosophies into customizable weapons. The Catchmoon barrel specifically reflects radiation specialization against specific threats encountered in Plains of Eidolon farming. The modular construction philosophy enables players to experiment with component combinations, reflecting faction commitment to personalized equipment. Within player communities, kitgun crafting represents weapon mastery advancement, teaching stat optimization and build theory through practical experimentation.
