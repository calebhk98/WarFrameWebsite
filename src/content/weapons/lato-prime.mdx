## Overview

Lato Prime is a semi-automatic pistol distributed exclusively to players who backed the original Warframe Founders program before it closed. Unlike other Prime weapons that cycle in and out of the Void Relic reward tables, Lato Prime is permanently unavailable through in-game acquisition for players who did not participate in the Founders program. This makes it one of a small group of items in Warframe that remain strictly legacy exclusive, alongside Excalibur Prime and Skana Prime.

From a performance standpoint, Lato Prime is a strong contender for secondary slot use in endgame content. Its puncture-dominant damage breakdown -- 6 impact, 42 puncture, and 12 slash per shot -- provides good armor penetration against Grineer targets. The 30% critical chance and 2.6x critical multiplier enable consistent and powerful critical hits, placing it firmly in the crit-focused pistol tier. The 20% status chance supports hybrid builds that combine elemental damage with critical scaling through mods like Arcane Pistoleer or Galvanized Diffusion.

At Mastery Rank 14, Lato Prime targets experienced players, which aligns with its historical role as a reward for early community supporters who played during the game's earliest access period.

The 30% base critical chance reaches reliably high crit rates with Primed Pistol Gambit and Hydraulic Crosshairs. The 2.6x critical multiplier provides meaningful burst damage on crit triggers, and when both are applied together the Lato Prime outputs damage well above its unmodded total. The relatively modest 60 base damage per shot benefits substantially from Primed Hornet Strike and elemental damage mods.

The 20-round magazine is adequate for burst-fire engagements but demands discipline in full-auto scenarios. The 1.0-second reload is among the fastest reload times in the pistol category, which reduces downtime during extended combat. The 5.0 fire rate supports steady trigger-pull cadence without over-extending the magazine.

## Acquisition

Lato Prime is not obtainable through any current in-game system. It was granted permanently to players who purchased a Founders Pack during Warframe's early access period before the program closed. Because it is account-bound, Lato Prime cannot be traded between players via the Trading Post, which makes it permanently inaccessible to players who did not participate in the Founders program.

Players who own Lato Prime retain it permanently on their accounts. Digital Extremes has confirmed the weapon will not be re-released through any future in-game method, preserving its status as a legacy exclusive.

## Variants

The standard Lato is available to all players from the Market for credits and serves as an introductory secondary weapon. The Lato Vandal was distributed as an event reward and later became obtainable through Invasions, representing an intermediate tier between the base Lato and the Founders-exclusive Prime. Lato Prime stands as the top-tier variant with the highest critical chance and multiplier among the three versions.

## Recommended builds

A critical damage build for Lato Prime uses Primed Hornet Strike as the base damage multiplier, Primed Pistol Gambit and Lethal Torment for critical chance, and Primed Target Cracker and Hollow Point for critical multiplier. Adding two elemental damage mods -- typically Primed Heated Charge and Convulsion for Heat or Pathogen Rounds and Pistol Pestilence for Corrosive -- rounds out the mod loadout for most mission types.

A hybrid build that combines status and critical chance can exploit the 20% status chance alongside the 30% critical chance. Galvanized Diffusion with two elemental mods and Arcane Pistoleer for sustained fire supports aggressive room-clearing in Steel Path Survival missions.

The 20-round magazine pairs well with Magazine Warp or Primed Slip Magazine to extend capacity before reload becomes necessary in extended engagements.

## Notes

Lato Prime's Founders exclusivity means its rarity affects perceived prestige more than in-game performance relative to Meta weapons. In terms of raw statistics, several freely obtainable pistols -- including the Epitaph, Laetum, and Pyrana Prime -- offer comparable or superior performance in specific contexts. However, Lato Prime's balanced crit and status spread allows it to function well in generalist builds without demanding heavy specialization.

Players who own Lato Prime should apply a full set of forma to maximize the available mod capacity and unlock all polarity slots needed for endgame builds.
