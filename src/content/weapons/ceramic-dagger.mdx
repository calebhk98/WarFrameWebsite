## Overview

The [Ceramic Dagger](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Ceramic_Dagger) is a dagger melee weapon with 140 total damage per attack. Uniquely, all physical damage is slash (126) with a small impact component (14) and no puncture. The slash-dominant profile makes the Ceramic Dagger effective at applying bleed procs that bypass armor mitigation. The 20% status chance is moderate, and the 10% critical chance with 1.5x multiplier does not support critical builds. Riven disposition at the maximum tier (5/5) means strong riven rolls are available.

Dagger weapons have access to the Covert Lethality mod -- a unique dagger-exclusive mod that sets the finisher damage of a dagger to a flat 2000 (unaffected by other mods). This enables guaranteed one-shot finisher kills on most enemies up to mid-game content, making daggers particularly powerful when combined with abilities that generate finisher opportunities (Ash's Shuriken, Ivara's Sleep Arrow, Excalibur's Radial Blind).

At Mastery Rank 3, the Ceramic Dagger is accessible to early-game players.

## Acquisition

The Ceramic Dagger blueprint is available from the Market for credits. Crafting requires standard early-game materials. As a permanently available Market weapon, it is not vaulted. The completed weapon can also be purchased with platinum.

## Variants

No Prime, Vandal, or Wraith variant of the Ceramic Dagger exists. Players who want a dagger with a Prime upgrade should consider the Rakta Dark Dagger (syndicate weapon with lifesteal) or the Dark Dagger as alternatives. Covert Lethality works on all daggers, so weapon choice often comes down to personal stat preference.

## Recommended builds

**Covert Lethality finisher build:** Primed Pressure Point, Covert Lethality, Gleaming Talon (lifesteal), Body Count, and elemental mods for faction-specific damage. Combine with a Warframe ability that blinds, sleeps, or staggers enemies (Excalibur's Radial Blind, Ivara's Sleep Arrow, Ash's Shuriken) to trigger finishers. Each finisher deals 2000 damage from Covert Lethality, instantly killing most standard enemies.

**Slash proc build:** Primed Pressure Point, Weeping Wounds, Condition Overload, Berserker Fury, and elemental mods for viral. The 90% slash composition generates bleed procs reliably through Weeping Wounds scaling, feeding Condition Overload damage amplification.

**Riven-enhanced build:** With a 5/5 disposition riven (status chance or critical chance), the Ceramic Dagger becomes a functional status or hybrid platform. Combining a strong riven with Covert Lethality creates a weapon that excels at both finisher kills and status-based combat.

## Synergies

- **Ivara's Sleep Arrow:** Sleeping enemies are vulnerable to finisher attacks. The Ceramic Dagger with Covert Lethality one-shots sleeping enemies up to high-level content, enabling efficient stealth kill chains.
- **Ash's Shuriken with Seeking Shuriken augment:** Shuriken strips enemy armor and puts them in a vulnerable state. Finisher the stripped enemy with Ceramic Dagger for guaranteed elimination.
- **Covert Lethality (mod):** This dagger-exclusive mod is the defining reason to use any dagger. It sets finisher damage to 2000, bypassing all enemy armor and scaling. The Ceramic Dagger's low mod cost allows Covert Lethality to fit comfortably in the build.

## Notes

- Dagger range (1.8 m) is among the shortest of any melee weapon category. Finisher-focused play naturally closes to point-blank range, but open combat requires careful positioning to avoid misses.
- The Ceramic Dagger's slash-only physical damage has no advantage against specific armor types beyond the standard slash bonuses (Flesh, Infested health). Adding elemental mods covering corrosive or viral compensates for the lack of puncture against heavily armored targets.
- Spin attacks on daggers deliver a longer-range hit than standard attacks. In open-area content, slide attacks can reach enemies that standing attacks miss due to the short base range.
- Riven disposition at 5/5 makes even a basic riven roll worth considering. Range, critical chance, or damage additions all meaningfully improve the Ceramic Dagger's effective ceiling in finisher or status builds.
