## Overview

The Telos Akbolto is the Arbiters of Hexis-enhanced version of the Akbolto dual pistols. Available at Mastery Rank 11, it is distributed through the Arbiters of Hexis Syndicate and shares the Akbolto's dual automatic pistol format while improving on its core stats. Its 70 total damage per shot is heavily concentrated in slash (63), with a small impact component (7) and no puncture. That slash-dominant profile produces consistent bleed procs against any faction.

At 13% critical chance and a 2.0x multiplier, the Telos Akbolto is not a critical-focused weapon. Its strength is the 29% status chance, which is high for a dual secondary with a 5.0 fire rate. The combination of fire rate and status chance means elemental procs apply multiple times per second. With a 30-round magazine and a 2.0-second reload, the Telos Akbolto sustains fire through most engagements before requiring a reload pause.

The Arbiters of Hexis version carries the unique passive Entropy Burst, granting a chance to generate an energy sphere on kills during Syndicate missions. This bonus rewards aggressive play in Arbiter-aligned nodes. The Telos Akbolto is direct upgrade material for players who favor the Akbolto's dual-secondary format but have invested in the Arbiters faction.

## Acquisition

The Telos Akbolto requires Arbiters of Hexis Syndicate rank and standing to purchase. Check the Arbiters of Hexis vendor in any Relay for current cost in Syndicate standing. No blueprint or crafting is required -- the weapon is purchased directly as a complete item.

## Variants

**Akbolto** -- The base version available from the Market. The Telos version improves slash damage weighting and status chance over the base.

**Akbolto Prime** -- A Prime variant from Void Relics with further improved stats across all categories.

## Recommended builds

**Viral status**: Combine cold and toxin mods for Viral. The 29% status at 5.0 fire rate ensures Viral procs apply within the first few shots, halving enemy max health. Barrel Diffusion and Lethal Torrent boost multishot and fire rate for rapid proc stacking.

**Slash bleed**: The 63 slash base supports Hunter Munitions or direct slash-status builds. Galvanized Chamber for multishot, Primed Pistol Gambit for critical chance, and Primed Target Cracker for critical multiplier build on the slash foundation. Slash bleeds bypass armor and scale with Viral.

**Beginner dual pistol**: Hornet Strike, Barrel Diffusion, Heated Charge, and Lethal Torrent. The dual-pistol format with 30 rounds sustains fire while building status through natural proc accumulation.

## Notes

The 29% status chance is measured per pellet (or per hit), so multishot mods like Barrel Diffusion add additional proc chances per trigger pull. Magazine capacity of 30 rounds is generous by dual-pistol standards. The 2.0-second reload is faster than most primaries. Arbiters of Hexis standing requirements mean this weapon is gated behind mid-to-late-game faction progress.
