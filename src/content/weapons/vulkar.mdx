## Overview

The Vulkar is a Grineer-designed sniper rifle available at Mastery Rank 3. Its most distinctive feature is a heavily impact-weighted damage profile: 225 total damage per shot splits into 180 impact, 11.25 puncture, and 33.75 slash. The impact dominance makes the Vulkar unusual among snipers, as impact damage is most effective against shields rather than armor. This profile excels against Corpus enemies, whose shield-heavy defenses take full benefit from impact's shield bypass.

At 20% critical chance and 2.0x critical multiplier, the Vulkar has acceptable critical scaling for a base-tier sniper. Its 25% status chance is high for a sniper rifle, enabling consistent elemental proc application per shot. The 6-round magazine gives the Vulkar more sustained fire than single-round snipers like the Vectis, though the 3.0-second reload time creates a meaningful penalty when the magazine is exhausted.

The Vulkar is the Grineer counterpart to the Corpus-aligned Snipetron. The Vulkar's impact weighting makes it a natural choice for Corpus-dominated missions on Jupiter, Europa, or Corpus Ship tilesets.

## Acquisition

The Vulkar blueprint is purchasable from the in-game Market for 20,000 credits. Build time is 12 hours. No quest or event unlock is required.

## Variants

**Vulkar Wraith** -- A Wraith variant with improved damage, critical chance, and status chance. The Wraith version was distributed through event rewards and alerts. Check in-game for current availability.

## Recommended builds

**Impact-focused Corpus build**: Against Corpus shields, impact's shield-stripping efficiency shines. Equip Serration, Split Chamber, Vital Sense, Point Strike, and a magnetic elemental combination (cold + electric) to strip shields and deal bonus damage. The 25% status chance ensures magnetic procs apply reliably.

**Viral status sniper**: Use cold + toxin for Viral to halve enemy max health. The 25% status chance applies Viral within two to three shots, after which all subsequent damage is amplified. Add Serration, Split Chamber for base damage scaling.

**Standard crit sniper**: Point Strike, Vital Sense, Serration, Split Chamber, Heavy Caliber, and two elemental mods. With Point Strike, critical chance reaches near 60%, producing reliable orange crits.

## Notes

The 3.0-second reload is the Vulkar's principal weakness. In missions with continuous enemy pressure, maintaining position and cover during reloads is important. Sniper ammo is rarer than rifle ammo; bring Sniper Ammo Mutation for extended survival or defense missions.


The Vulkar's place in early-game progression is as an accessible sniper rifle for players who prefer precise, deliberate engagement. At MR 3, most players have limited mod access, and the Vulkar's 25% status chance enables elemental proc application with only one or two elemental mods -- a significant advantage when mod capacity is constrained. A single corrosive mod pair or Viral pair on the Vulkar produces reliable status output without requiring a full 8-mod build.

The Grineer design aesthetic of the Vulkar -- heavy, industrial, and angular -- contrasts with Corpus energy weapons and Tenno bio-organic designs. Players who roleplay faction alignment within their loadout often pair the Vulkar with other Grineer weaponry (Grakata, Ack and Brunt) for thematic consistency.
