## Overview

The Euphona Prime is a Prime secondary with an extremely high pure-Impact damage profile: 292.5 Impact versus 16.25 each of Puncture and Slash. The 30% critical chance and 2.5x critical multiplier make the Euphona Prime a dedicated crit-impact secondary with minimal status application (2% status). Fire rate of 1.5 rounds/second and a 2.0-second reload indicate a slow, deliberate firing pattern appropriate for a high-impact precision secondary. The 100 accuracy ensures all shots connect at range. Mastery Rank 14 is required.

The 292.5 Impact base is among the highest per-shot Impact values available in the secondary category, making the Euphona Prime a precision critical platform that delivers concentrated damage per trigger pull. The Euphona functions as a sawed-off shotgun-style secondary with dual alternate fire modes -- a standard shot and an alternate charged mode. The WFCD stats capture the primary-fire values. Impact damage is most effective against Corpus Shields, making the Euphona Prime naturally suited to Corpus missions where Impact bonus damage against shields delivers maximum per-hit value. The Prime version improves on the base Euphona with higher critical chance and damage.

## Variants

The base Euphona is the standard version of this secondary. The Euphona Prime improves on the base weapon with higher critical chance and enhanced base damage. No Kuva or Tenet variant exists for the Euphona family.

## Recommended builds

**Critical Impact/Magnetic Corpus build.** The 292.5 Impact base against Corpus Shields delivers high per-shot Shield damage. Combine with Pistol Gambit, Vital Sense, and Barrel Diffusion for critical scaling. Add Jolt (Electric + Cold 60/60 mods) for Magnetic, which drains and bypasses Corpus Shields. At 30% base crit, orange crits occur regularly with Galvanized Crosshairs stacking.

**Pure critical high-damage build.** Equip Hornet Strike, Pistol Gambit, Vital Sense, Barrel Diffusion, Lethal Torrent, and Galvanized Crosshairs. The 30% base crit and 2.5x multiplier deliver heavy per-crit damage on each of the slow 1.5 rounds/second shots. The 100 accuracy ensures all hits connect at range for maximum crit value delivery.

**Heavy-Impact slam build.** The Euphona's alternate fire mode changes the shot pattern. Equip base damage and crit mods to amplify the Impact damage per shot mode. Combine with frames that benefit from Impact procs: Mag's Polarize strips shields further after Impact proc application, while Wisp's Reservoirs provide accuracy and health regen for sustained precision fire.

## Synergies

Mag's passive increases secondary damage on magnetized targets, compounding with the Euphona Prime's Magnetic elemental mods for Corpus-specific burst. Mesa's Shooting Gallery buffs secondary fire rate, compensating for the 1.5 rounds/second base. Ash's passive secondary damage bonus applies on airborne approach shots for target entry.

## Notes

- The 2% status chance makes status mods ineffective; commit to crit and damage mods exclusively.
- The 292.5 Impact base against Corpus Shields makes each orange-crit hit deliver exceptional shield-damage values.
