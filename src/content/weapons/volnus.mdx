## Overview

The Volnus is a mace-category melee weapon combining a reasonable attack speed (1.2) with a 30% status chance. Its 220 total damage per hit splits into 48.4 impact, 101.2 puncture, and 70.4 slash -- with puncture dominant and slash providing secondary proc opportunity. The high status chance is the Volnus's most compelling feature: at 30% per hit and 1.2 attack speed, status procs accumulate quickly.

The 18% critical chance and 1.6x critical multiplier are modest. The Volnus is primarily a status weapon; critical builds yield less return than weapons with 25%+ critical chance. Condition Overload synergizes directly with the high status chance, providing a damage multiplier for each unique status type active on the target. With corrosive + heat + the physical procs potentially active, Condition Overload triples or more the effective damage per hit.

At Mastery Rank 9 and with a 2.9-meter range, the Volnus reaches groups of enemies effectively with mace sweep attacks.

## Acquisition

The Volnus blueprint is purchasable from the in-game Market for 20,000 credits. Build time is 12 hours. No quest unlock is required.

## Variants

**Volnus Prime** -- A Prime variant with improved damage, critical chance, critical multiplier, and status chance. It drops from Void Relics and requires a higher Mastery Rank.

## Recommended builds

**Condition Overload status build**: Equip Condition Overload, Weeping Wounds, Primed Pressure Point, and elemental mods for corrosive (electric + toxin) plus heat. The 30% base status with Weeping Wounds at x2 combo reaches very high proc rates. Three unique status types active simultaneously triple the Condition Overload multiplier.

**Viral-slash utility**: Weeping Wounds, cold + toxin for Viral, Hunter Munitions (slash on crit), and Blood Rush. The combination of Viral amplification and Hunter Munitions-generated slash procs produces sustained true-damage output through bleed ticks.

**Beginner status build**: Pressure Point, Fever Strike (toxin), and North Wind for Viral. The 30% status chance reliably applies procs even with basic mods.

## Notes

Mace weapons use a different set of stance mods than swords or polearms. Check the in-game mod codex for the applicable stance. The Volnus's 30% status chance compares favorably to many higher-Mastery-Rank melees, making it a reliable status platform even without heavy mod investment.


The Volnus's 30% status chance without any mod investment means the weapon begins applying elemental procs reliably from the first swing. Unlike weapons that require Weeping Wounds to achieve meaningful status rates, the Volnus reaches useful proc frequency through base stats alone, freeing one mod slot for additional damage or utility. Players who are still building their melee mod collection benefit from not requiring Weeping Wounds as a mandatory inclusion.

Mace weapons often receive less attention than swords or polearms in community tier lists despite competitive status stats at their Mastery Rank tier. The Volnus's 220 damage per hit with 30% status is a competent combination at MR 9, performing reliably in mid-level Star Chart content. Players who progress to the Volnus Prime at MR 14 find the same platform with improved numbers, validating the mace-category investment.
