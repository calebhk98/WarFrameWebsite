## Overview

The Daikyu is a Tenno bow that fires a single, powerful arrow with exceptional damage per shot. With a 34% critical chance and 46% status chance on a single-shot magazine, it rewards patient, deliberate marksmanship. The damage profile splits roughly equally between Impact (210) and Puncture (210), with a heavier Slash component (280), making it effective against unarmored and shielded targets. The weapon demands full draw before releasing -- partial draws reduce damage output -- so positioning and timing are essential to effective use.

The Daikyu's 0.6-second reload is unusually fast for a single-shot bow, allowing confident follow-up after each arrow. Accuracy is limited at 16.67, reflecting the weapon's projectile arc; aim for center-mass at medium range rather than relying on pixel-perfect headshots. Mastery Rank 10 is required to equip the Daikyu.

## Variants

The Daikyu has no confirmed Prime or Kuva/Tenet variant as of this writing. The base Daikyu remains the only available version. Any future prime release would be obtained through Void Relics in the standard prime acquisition system.

## Recommended builds

**Status/Slash build.** The 46% base status chance enables consistent Slash procs with mods such as Primed Shred and Hunter Munitions. Add two 60/60 elemental mods (e.g., Malignant Force and Infected Clip) to reach Viral, which amplifies Slash bleeding damage against health. Fill remaining slots with Serration and Split Chamber for raw damage.

**Crit/status hybrid.** Pair Point Strike (crit chance) and Vital Sense (crit multiplier) with two 60/60 elemental mods. The 34% base crit chance reaches yellow-crit territory reliably with Point Strike. Hunter Munitions converts crits to Slash procs for additional scaling.

**Bane of faction build.** Bane of Grineer or Bane of Corrupted amplifies all damage against the target faction. Combine with Serration and a split of crit and elemental mods for efficient Steel Path clearing.

## Synergies

Frames that increase critical chance (Mirage with Eclipse) or general damage amplification (Rhino's Roar) multiply the Daikyu's per-shot output significantly. Valkyr's Warcry provides no direct benefit but increases melee combo counter speed if mixing melee with the Daikyu.

## Notes

- Full draw is required for maximum damage; release too early and output drops substantially.
- The single-shot magazine punishes missed shots heavily -- prioritize cover and deliberate aim over aggressive pacing.
- The Daikyu pairs well with a fast-switching melee weapon for close-range encounters.
