## Overview

The Dual Coda Torxica is a high-tier secondary weapon requiring Mastery Rank 17, featuring a 160-round magazine -- among the largest secondary magazines in the game. The damage profile is Puncture-dominant (32.5 Puncture, 17.5 Slash, zero Impact) with 25% critical chance, 2.4x multiplier, and 28% status chance. The fire rate of 6.0 rounds per second combined with 160 rounds creates an exceptionally long sustained fire window before reload is needed. The 2.33-second reload and 30.77 accuracy indicate a spread-fire mechanic at close-to-medium range.

The MR17 requirement positions the Dual Coda Torxica as an endgame secondary for players with extensive mod collections. The balanced 25% crit and 28% status values allow either a crit or status build path depending on the target faction. Against Corpus, the Puncture base combined with elemental Magnetic procs (from Cold + Electric mods) provides a combined approach: Puncture against Robotics, Magnetic stripping shields, and status proc accumulation. The large 160-round magazine means fewer reload interruptions during extended engagements.

## Variants

The Dual Coda Torxica has no Prime, Kuva, or Tenet variant as of this writing. It is a standalone secondary weapon at the high end of the mastery rank spectrum.

## Recommended builds

**Status/Viral sustained fire build.** The 28% status and 6.0 fire rate deliver consistent proc application over the 160-round magazine. Equip Barrel Diffusion, Lethal Torrent, Pistol Pestilence, Frostbite for Viral, and Hornet Strike. Viral weakens enemy health, and the Puncture base ensures armor-penetrating damage on proced targets.

**Critical hybrid build.** At 25% base crit, Pistol Gambit and Galvanized Crosshairs push effective crit chance high enough for consistent yellow and orange crits. Add Vital Sense for the 2.4x multiplier amplification. Use two 60/60 mods for secondary element application alongside the crit stack.

**Galvanized Aptitude status build.** The 28% status enables consistent proc application for Galvanized Aptitude's damage-per-unique-status-type scaling. Stack Viral, Corrosive or Heat, and Slash procs via the weapon's Slash component. Galvanized Shot provides additional condition-gated damage. This build leverages the Dual Coda Torxica's large magazine to sustain proc cycling.

## Synergies

Mesa's Shooting Gallery buffs secondary fire rate, compounding the already-high 6.0 rounds/second with additional shots per second. Ash's passive increases secondary damage on airborne enemies; use aerial approaches on deployment. The 30.77 accuracy indicates spread fire; Frames that keep enemies grouped at close range (Mag, Khora) maximize hit count per shot.

## Notes

- Zero Impact means no Impact proc (Shield Disruption) against Corpus shields; use Magnetic elemental mods to compensate against shielded targets.
- The 160-round magazine is the Dual Coda Torxica's primary quality-of-life advantage; use the long mag to sustain fire through entire encounter waves.
