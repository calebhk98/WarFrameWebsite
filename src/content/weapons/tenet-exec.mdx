## Overview

The Tenet Exec is a Sisters of Parvos Tenet melee weapon requiring Mastery Rank 16. Its 190 total damage per hit divides between impact (102.6) and puncture (87.4) with no slash component. The dual physical type split means the Exec avoids single-faction overspecialization -- impact is effective against Corpus shields while puncture penetrates Grineer armor. The lack of slash means no bleed procs, so raw damage delivery and elemental status are the primary damage vectors.

The 38% critical chance is among the highest available for melee weapons and is the Exec's defining stat. At 38% base with Blood Rush at x3 combo, critical chance approaches 100%. The 2.4x critical multiplier scales those crits into high-damage territory. The 22% status chance supports elemental proc application and Condition Overload builds. At 1.0 attack speed with 2.8-meter range, the Exec is a mid-pace weapon suited to methodical combo building rather than rapid spam.

Tenet weapons receive a unique elemental bonus from the Sister's element, adding extra elemental damage beyond the base stats.

## Acquisition

Obtain through the Sisters of Parvos system. Trigger a Sister Candidate through Granum Void encounters, then track, confront, and vanquish the Sister to claim her Tenet weapon. The weapon type and elemental bonus are determined at Sister creation.

## Variants

No base non-Tenet Exec exists. No Prime, Vandal, or Wraith version is available.

## Recommended builds

**Blood Rush critical**: Blood Rush, Organ Shatter, Primed Pressure Point, Weeping Wounds, and dual-stat 60/60 elemental mods. At x3 combo, Blood Rush combined with 38% base crit produces near-guaranteed crits. Organ Shatter maximizes the 2.4x base multiplier. Weeping Wounds adds status proc support without sacrificing crit capacity.

**Condition Overload hybrid**: Condition Overload, Primed Pressure Point, Blood Rush, and elemental mods for corrosive plus heat or viral. Multiple unique status types from dual-stat mods and the Tenet elemental bonus stack Condition Overload multipliers rapidly.

**Heavy attack build**: Killing Blow, Corrupt Charge (12x combo opener), Primed Pressure Point, Sacrificial Steel. Heavy attacks at 12x combo from Corrupt Charge deal massive burst damage. Sacrificial Steel raises the already high 38% base crit into higher territory for critable heavy attacks.

## Notes

The absence of slash damage is the Tenet Exec's principal limitation for armor bypass: slash bleeds are the primary true-damage source in melee builds, and the Exec lacks them. Hunter Munitions partially compensates by converting crits into slash procs at the cost of a mod slot. The high crit chance makes Hunter Munitions activations frequent.


Heavy blades in the Tenet Exec's category have access to wide sweep stance attacks that hit multiple enemies per swing at the 2.8-meter range. These sweeping attacks apply the 22% status chance to every enemy hit, potentially distributing corrosive or other elemental procs across an entire group with each swing. In missions where enemies cluster in chokepoints or rooms, the Tenet Exec's sweep efficiency maximizes its status density output per swing.
