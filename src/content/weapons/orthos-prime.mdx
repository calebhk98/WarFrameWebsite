## Overview

The Orthos Prime is a balanced polearm weapon combining respectable reach with rapid attack cadence and high status proc frequency. The weapon excels through its 36% status chance, enabling frequent physical and elemental status procs when properly modded. The damage profile distributes evenly between impact, puncture, and slash, making it effective against all armor types without specialization. The 1.17 attack speed provides aggressive swing pace while maintaining practical charge and slam attack damage. The three-meter reach rivals or exceeds many other melee options, enabling wide area coverage without sacrificing damage concentration. The Orthos Prime serves as an excellent hybrid status-damage platform for players seeking versatility and area control.

## Damage

The Orthos Prime's balanced damage profile distributes across physical and elemental channels effectively. Slash comprises 67% of base damage, while impact and puncture each account for 14%, creating consistent performance against light armor and unarmored targets. The 36% status chance ensures frequent proc application, synergizing excellently with elemental mods for multiproc chains. The 24% critical chance supports critical-hybrid builds through mod enhancement, enabling secondary critical scaling without prioritizing critical-first strategies. Against varied enemy compositions, the balanced approach provides consistent performance across mission types without requiring stat adjustments.

## Acquisition

Orthos Prime components drop from Axi O1 and Axi O2 void relics as common rewards during void fissure missions. Construction requires 15,000 credits and twelve hours of Foundry time after gathering all components. The weapon remains unvaulted and regularly available through current fissure rotations, ensuring consistent farm accessibility. Trading of Orthos Prime blueprints and components occurs regularly in player markets, offering quick acquisition through platinum investment.

## Variants

The standard Orthos exists as a non-prime variant with reduced attack speed (1.0 instead of 1.17) and lower critical multiplier. The Orthos Prime represents the optimal polearm choice, combining superior attack speed with balanced damage distribution. No Kuva variant exists currently, though riven disposition allows riven mods to improve endgame performance.

## Recommended builds

Status-focused modding capitalizes on the Orthos Prime's 36% base status chance. Pressure Point and Piercing Strikes form the core damage amplifiers, while True Strike pushes status proc frequency toward guaranteed application. Elemental mods like Fever Strike or Molten Impact create multiproc chains amplifying crowd control. Berserker further accelerates the already-fast 1.17 attack speed, creating blur-tier weapon performance during extended engagements. Bloodletting provides self-sustaining playstyle through lifesteal scaling. Condition Overload stacks status effects into exponential damage scaling when paired with multiproc builds.

## Synergies

Frames benefiting from fast attack speed synergize excellently with the Orthos Prime. Wukong's enhancements to melee attack speed stack directly with Berserker, creating devastating swing rates. Nidus scales off consecutive hits through his stacking mechanics, rewarding the rapid attack cadence. Rhino's roar multiplies both normal and critical strikes, amplifying the weapon's balanced damage profile. Saryn converts weapon status procs directly into passive ability damage through her scaling. Ash's blade storm synergizes with melee damage rolls, creating ability-weapon hybrid damage chains.

## Lore

The Orthos Prime originates from Orokin-era polearm engineering, designed for balanced performance across varied Old War battlefield scenarios. The weapon's attack speed and reach suggest optimization for formation combat and coordinated unit tactics. The balanced damage approach reflects Orokin philosophy that adaptability serves strategy better than specialization. Within player communities, the Orthos Prime stands as an iconic polearm, introducing many players to fast-attack melee gameplay and status-focused scaling. Its popularity endures through accessibility and proven effectiveness across playstyles.
