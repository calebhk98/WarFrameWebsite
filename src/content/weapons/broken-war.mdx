## Overview

The Broken War is a signature sword-and-shield melee weapon granted as a quest reward from The Second Dream quest line. Despite its fractured aesthetic, the weapon functions as a complete and effective melee platform combining critical damage scaling with balanced physical performance. The weapon's 35% critical chance makes it one of the highest-crit swords available, enabling consistent critical-focused builds that scale exceptionally well with critical-multiplier mods. The slash-dominant damage profile of 149.6 points ensures effective bleed proc chains against soft targets while the balanced impact and puncture provide armor-type versatility. The 2.5-meter range offers slightly shorter reach than other swords but compensates through superior critical damage potential. The Broken War serves as an excellent introduction to high-crit melee weapons for players progressing through the main questline.

## Damage

The Broken War concentrates approximately 79% of its damage in the slash component, optimizing for bleed proc chains on biological targets. The balanced impact and puncture values provide reasonable performance against light armor when slash efficiency fails. The 35% critical chance stands among the highest for swords, enabling consistent critical-focused builds through mods like True Steel and Organ Shatter. The 20% status chance supports hybrid builds, though critical scaling typically dominates optimization. Against high-armor enemies, the weapon requires elemental support or frame damage buffs to maintain endgame effectiveness, but against the majority of mission content the pure damage output remains competitive without special adjustments.

## Acquisition

The Broken War is granted as a quest reward upon completion of The Second Dream, making it accessible to all players regardless of progression level. The weapon does not require farming or blueprint collection beyond quest completion. Construction at the Foundry requires 15,000 credits and twelve hours of build time. The quest-reward status makes the Broken War unique among high-tier melee options, ensuring accessible acquisition for dedicated players.

## Variants

No prime or alternate variants of the Broken War currently exist. The standard weapon remains the only version available. The weapon's unique design and quest-reward status prevent variants from being developed, keeping the Broken War distinctive within the weapon roster. No Kuva variant exists currently, though riven disposition allows riven mods to improve the weapon's already-respectable endgame performance.

## Recommended builds

Critical-focused modding maximizes the Broken War's exceptional 35% base critical chance. Pressure Point and Fury form the core damage multipliers, while True Steel and Organ Shatter boost critical damage scaling dramatically. Bloodletting provides self-sustaining playstyle through lifesteal scaling. Elemental mods like Heat Damage create slash-heat procs for improved crowd control. Berserker accelerates the standard 1.0 attack speed during extended engagements. Condition Overload adds bonus damage when status effects are active, creating hybrid critical-status damage. Primed mods unlock superior scaling for veteran players willing to invest forma.

## Synergies

Frames providing critical bonuses synergize excellently with the Broken War's 35% base critical chance. Harrow's invulnerability provides guaranteed critical strikes, eliminating variance from the weapon's already-high critical chance. Mesa's accuracy buff makes the sword even more reliable for precise strikes. Ash benefits from the weapon's critical strikes enabling faster finisher accumulation for blade storm resets. Valkyr's damage immunity pairs well with close-range sword engagement, enabling aggressive positioning without survivability concerns. Rhino's roar multiplies both normal and critical strikes, scaling the weapon's already-exceptional damage.

## Lore

The Broken War is a signature blade belonging to the Tenno and discovered during the Second Dream quest narrative. Its shattered aesthetic belies complete functionality, suggesting the weapon survived ancient Old War conflicts through superior Orokin engineering. The design reflects early Tenno combat capabilities before advanced warframe technology became available. Within player communities, the Broken War represents a milestone achievement—proof of quest progression and Operator-awakening in the lore. The weapon's narrative integration makes it particularly valued beyond pure mechanical performance, serving emotional significance alongside practical combat value.
