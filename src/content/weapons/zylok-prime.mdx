## Overview

Zylok Prime is a precision semi-automatic pistol built for crit-focused combat at mid-range. With 2.8x critical multiplier and 29 percent crit chance, the weapon demands accurate shot placement but rewards headshots with devastating damage spikes. The Zylok Prime excels as a finisher secondary for players who already carry a strong primary, enabling quick execution of priority targets and flexible burst damage when primary ammo is depleted.

The weapon's impact-weighted damage profile makes it effective against shields and light armor. The semi-automatic fire rate (3.33 shots/second) allows precise trigger control for headshot chaining, particularly effective with frames that amplify crit damage or accuracy. Experienced players appreciate the Zylok Prime's skill-based design; casual players find its slow fire rate and low status chance less forgiving than spray-and-pray alternatives.

Zylok Prime's crit stats are exceptional for a secondary; the 2.8x multiplier rivals many melee weapons and surpasses most pistols. The 29 percent base crit chance is the highest among non-special-interest secondaries, making viable crit builds possible without every mod slot dedicated to crit scaling.

## Damage and effectiveness

Base damage per shot (90 total) is modest but multiplies quickly through crit scaling. A single headshot with optimal crit mods (Primed Pistol Gambit, Primed Pressure Point) can deal 2000+ damage, making priority target elimination trivial. Against grouped enemies, however, the low fire rate and status chance make the Zylok Prime less effective than higher-fire-rate alternatives like Akstiletto or Atomos.

The weapon shines in mission types that reward precision: assassination targets, bombard-heavy defense waves, and solo void fissures where single-target focus is viable. Against hordes of weak enemies (void cascade, void fissure waves), the Zylok Prime's strengths are less relevant unless modded for area effects.

## Acquisition

Zylok Prime blueprints drop from void fissure void relics, with the barrel and receiver scattered across various relic tiers. Expected farm time is 10-15 void fissure runs for a complete set. The weapon is currently vaulted, but becomes available during Prime Resurgence events on a rotating schedule.

Crafting requires 24500 credits and standard secondary components. New players at MR 12 can acquire the blueprint immediately upon reaching the requirement, making Zylok Prime accessible once vault schedules permit.

## Recommended builds

Optimal Zylok Prime builds prioritize crit damage and rate. Primed Pressure Point, Primed Pistol Gambit, and Organ Shatter form the core, reaching 80-90 crit chance with 4-5x multiplier. Adding Primed Wet Grip or Augur Pact provides secondary scaling (elemental damage or ability efficiency). Ammo economy mods like Pistol Gambit or Terminal Velocity enable longer engagement windows. A baseline crit build for mid-tier content includes: Hornet Strike, Primed Pistol Gambit, Primed Pressure Point, and Organ Shatter. This reaches 2-3k crit damage per headshot, sufficient for clearing level 50-70 enemies in a single trigger pull.

Advanced Primed builds swap Hornet Strike for Primed Power Donation and add Augur Pact, pushing crit multiplier to 5-6x and reaching 5-8k damage per headshot in Steel Path. A single magazine of perfectly-placed headshots can eliminate entire heavy armor units without reloading.

For endgame content, replacing a crit mod with a status mod (Wet Grip) creates hybrid builds viable against heavily armored targets, though this sacrifices crit consistency. Pure crit remains superior for Steel Path assassination and void fissure speed-running where raw damage per shot matters most. Some hybrid builds use viral elemental mods (Cold + Toxin) to scale health pools while maintaining crit focus, creating a balance between pure damage and sustained proc application.

## Synergies

Zylok Prime synergizes exceptionally well with frames that amplify crit chance or damage. Rhino with Roar provides raw damage buff for all crit hits. Octavia with Metronome buff crit chance and fire rate, transforming the Zylok Prime's semi-automatic pacing into rapid-fire burst windows. Mesa with Shooting Gallery grants guaranteed crit chance after dodging, enabling consistent crits even without crit mods.

Companion weapons with crit focus (Vulpaphyla with Hunter Synergy, Moas with specific precepts) layer additional crit chance. A Riven mod with crit chance/multiplier/headshot damage can push Zylok Prime crits into 5-6k territory with proper setup.

## Lore

Zylok Prime is an artifact of Orokin craftsmanship, representing refinement and precision in sidearm design. The Zylok lineage suggests a legacy of careful engineering prioritized accuracy and crit potential over volume, reflecting Orokin cultural values around technical superiority. The Prime variant is among the vaulted weapons, suggesting its retirement from active service and re-emergence through Void fissure phenomena.

In the Warframe universe, semi-automatic pistols evoke tactical operations and sniper-adjacent marksmanship. The Zylok Prime appeals to players who see secondary weapons as precision tools rather than spray-and-pray insurance, embodying the philosophy of calculated strikes over overwhelming firepower.
