## Overview

The Sicarus Prime is the primed variant of the Sicarus burst-fire pistol. It fires a three-round burst per trigger pull at a combined effective rate of 9.375 rounds per second, placing it among the fastest-firing secondaries when sustained over a magazine. The 25% critical chance and 2x multiplier give it a solid crit foundation, and the 20% status chance supports elemental status builds without specialized investment.

The damage profile distributes relatively evenly across impact (20), puncture (15), and slash (15), giving no single enemy type a pronounced advantage or disadvantage based on physical damage type alone. This balance makes the Sicarus Prime a flexible general-purpose secondary without the specialized niche that heavily skewed damage profiles provide.

Accuracy of 25 reflects the burst-fire mechanic: the first shot of each burst is precise, but successive rounds in the three-round volley climb due to recoil. Players who tap the trigger at distance rather than holding for continuous bursts get better accuracy on the first pellet. The weapon is primarily designed for mid-range engagement where the full burst has time to converge.

## Acquisition

The Sicarus Prime is vaulted and cannot be obtained from the current active relic pool. Components were distributed across Lith and Meso era relics during its active period. Acquisition options include trading with other players via [warframe.market](https://warframe.market/items/sicarus_prime_set) or waiting for an announced Prime Vault rotation that includes the Sicarus Prime.

## Variants

The base [Sicarus](/weapons/sicarus) offers the same burst-fire mechanics at lower stats (15% critical chance, 1.5x multiplier) and mastery rank 5, accessible to players before reaching mid-game. No Tenet or Kuva variant exists for the Sicarus family.

## Recommended builds

**Critical burst build.** Point Strike raises the 25% crit chance to 75%, delivering reliable yellow crits across most bursts. Hornet Strike and Barrel Diffusion form the damage baseline. Lethal Torrent adds fire rate and multishot simultaneously, improving burst frequency and pellet count. The 2x multiplier is the primary ceiling; Hollow Point can extend it at the cost of status chance.

**Viral status build.** The 20% base status chance reaches functional proc rates with Pathogen Rounds and Pistol Pestilence equipped for Viral damage. Each burst applies status independently on multiple rounds, accelerating the Viral stack buildup. This pairs well with Condition Overload-adjacent secondaries in a two-secondary setup or as the primary crowd softener.

**Galvanized build.** Galvanized Shot replaces Barrel Diffusion for players with access to it, providing conditional multishot that stacks on kills. The Sicarus Prime's burst-fire enables fast kill confirmation, keeping the Galvanized buff active in missions with consistent enemy flow.

## Notes

- The 24-round magazine accommodates eight full three-shot bursts; mid-magazine reloads before the final two or three bursts avoid mid-fight reload interruptions.
- Mastery rank 14 is the highest rank requirement among common burst-fire secondaries, restricting this to experienced players.
- Burst-fire weapons in Warframe do not have the fire-rate cap limitation of full-auto secondaries, making the Sicarus Prime one of the faster effective fire-rate secondaries despite the burst mechanic.
- The vaulted status means market trading is the primary path; set prices fluctuate based on the time since the last unvaulting event.
