## Overview

A charge-based flak cannon delivering devastating single shots of Impact and Heat damage. Uncharged shots fire at 2 rounds per second, while fully charged shots gain a significantly higher critical multiplier and additional falloff range. Introduced in Update 15.0 (2014-10-24, Archwing), the Corvas is notable for charge mechanic dramatically improving critical multiplier on charged shots; one of the original Archwing weapons from Update 15.

The Corvas draws on Orokin-era flak technology adapted by Tenno engineers for the extremes of space combat. Its capacitor-based charge system was novel when first fielded in the earliest Archwing deployments, providing burst capability against armored Corpus fighter craft.

## Acquisition

Component parts available from Syndicates: Barrel from Arbiters of Hexis at Authentic rank, Receiver from Cephalon Suda at Intriguing rank, Stock from Steel Meridian at Valiant rank. Blueprint from Market for 35,000 credits. Build costs 45,000 credits. The Corvas requires Mastery Rank 1 to equip.

Arch-gun weapons require either standard crafting resources, Syndicate standing for component parts, or specialized mission drops. Once crafted, equipping the Corvas in Archwing uses the standard Arch-gun slot. To use it as a Heavy Weapon in ground missions or Railjack combat, a Gravimag must be installed. Gravimags are available from Clan Dojos via Energy Lab research, or from Arbitrations as rotation rewards.

## Variants

Corvas Prime was released with Garuda Prime Access in March 2022, offering improved critical chance (44%), critical multiplier (3.0x), status chance (30%), and significantly reduced reload time (2.0s vs 8.0s).

The Arch-gun family spans weapons from Tenno engineering (Corvas, Fluctus, Velocitus), Grineer design (Grattler, Kuva line), and Entrati craftsmanship (Cortege, Mausolon, Morgha), each with distinct stat profiles suited to different engagement contexts.

## Recommended builds

Critical chance of 40% makes crit builds highly effective. Charged shots benefit from Critical Focus and Hollowed Bullets. The 8-second reload is the primary drawback -- Charged Bullets and Superior Defenses address magazine and reload limitations respectively.

**Core Arch-gun mods to consider:**

- Rubedo-Lined Barrel -- +165% Arch-gun damage (primary damage mod for all builds)
- Hollowed Bullets -- +120% critical chance (essential for any crit-focused build)
- Critical Focus -- +60% critical multiplier while aiming
- Modified Munitions -- +90% status chance (for status-focused builds)
- Arch-gun Ace -- +165% fire rate (useful on slow-fire weapons to improve feel)
- Parallax Scope -- +30% critical chance while aiming
- Charged Bullets -- +60% magazine capacity
- Superior Defenses -- reduces reload time
- Conductive Coils -- adds Electricity damage
- Thermite Rounds -- adds Heat damage

Prioritize Rubedo-Lined Barrel as the first mod in any Arch-gun build. Follow with critical or status mods based on the weapon's stat profile. Elemental mods provide faction-specific damage type flexibility.

## Notes

The Corvas operates in the Arch-gun slot, used in Archwing missions and as a Heavy Weapon on the ground via Gravimag. In Railjack (Empyrean) missions, the Arch-gun serves as the primary armament during space combat against fighters and Crewships.

Effective in Railjack ground assault for single-target elimination. Charged shots defeat armored Grineer crew efficiently in boarding scenarios.

Archwing combat system notes: Archwing missions require completing The Archwing quest. Four Archwings are available -- Itzal (mobility/stealth), Amesha (tank/support), Elytron (AoE damage), and Odonata (balanced starter). Best deployed from Itzal for stealth approach before burst fire. Elytron's Afterburner bridges positioning gaps for optimal engagement range before charging shots.

The weapon's Riven disposition is 4/5 (high). Riven dispositions range from 0 (absolute minimum, strong weapon) to 5 (maximum, weak weapon). DE adjusts dispositions quarterly based on weapon usage rates.
