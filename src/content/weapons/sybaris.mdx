## Overview

Sybaris is a primary rifle weapon that serves as burst-fire rifle delivering two rounds per trigger pull; even physical damage distribution makes it adaptable with elemental mods, and the deliberate fire rhythm suits accurate players. Mastery Rank 5 is required to equip the weapon. Players who invest in building it properly will find it reliable across the star chart, with scaling potential on the Steel Path when supported by appropriate mods and Warframe ability synergies.

The primary slot is competitive in Warframe, and Sybaris occupies a distinct position within it. Understanding its damage profile -- the distribution across physical damage types and any elemental components -- is the first step toward optimizing it for specific mission types and enemy factions.

## Recommended builds

Build direction for Sybaris depends on its stat profile. Status-oriented builds stack the Primed variant of elemental mods alongside dual stat mods (such as Infected Clip for toxin plus status, or Rime Rounds for cold plus status) to maximize proc frequency. Critical builds layer critical chance and damage multiplier mods to amplify each hit that lands as a critical strike.

For primary weapons, the standard mod capacity baseline after zero forma accommodates roughly four to five max-rank mods.
