## Overview

The Skana is one of the original swords in Warframe and the default melee weapon for Excalibur. It is a single-handed sword with straightforward attack animations and a reach of 2.5 meters -- modest by melee standards but consistent with the one-handed sword archetype. At mastery rank 0, the Skana is available to every player regardless of progression, making it the most accessible melee weapon in the game.

The weapon's damage profile is puncture-heavy at 84 puncture per strike, which benefits against armored Grineer enemies. Impact and slash contribute 18 each, giving a total per-hit damage of 120. Critical output is limited with only 5% critical chance and a 1.5x multiplier, putting critical builds far below optimal for this weapon. Status builds are more viable at 16% status chance, enabling puncture, impact, or slash procs at a reasonable rate.

The Skana is primarily a starter and mastery-feed weapon. Players equip it to level it for mastery points and then replace it with higher-tier swords. However, for early-game content through the inner star chart, a modded Skana is entirely functional. Several variants of the Skana lineage represent meaningful upgrades for players who want to stay within the sword archetype.

## Acquisition

The Skana blueprint is purchasable from the Market for 15,000 credits. Components require Ferrite, Polymer Bundle, and Alloy Plate -- all available from early star chart planets. Build time is 12 hours. Players who chose Excalibur as a starter frame receive a Skana at the start of the game without crafting.

## Variants

The sword archetype has several notable upgrades:

- [Skana Prime](/weapons/skana-prime) -- the Prime variant with higher critical chance (10%) and a 2.0x multiplier. Obtainable from void relics.
- Prisma Skana -- an enhanced version distributed through Baro Ki'Teer with improved status and damage. Requires a prime access or Baro appearance window.
- Dex Skana -- a Tenno anniversary weapon with moderate improvements and exclusive aesthetics, available during anniversary events.

## Recommended builds

**Status melee build.** The 16% status chance is the most reliable damage path for the Skana. Primed Fever Strike and Virulent Scourge build Viral status, which halves enemy effective health. Condition Overload (or its Primed variant) then multiplies damage with each unique status type active on the target, turning even the Skana into a capable early-game tool.

**Basic damage build.** Pressure Point, Spoiled Strike, and two 90% elemental mods of complementary types (Viral or Corrosive depending on faction) provide straightforward damage scaling. This is the easiest setup to construct early in the game when specialized mods are unavailable.

**Stance-centric build.** Excalibur's Exalted Blade does not use the Skana directly, but the Vengeful Revenant stance fits the Skana and provides long combo chains that extend its practical melee reach through slide attacks.

## Notes

- The Skana grants 100 mastery points at rank 30 like all standard weapons; leveling it is worthwhile for early-game progression even if the weapon is not kept.
- Attack speed at 0.83 is moderate; Fury or Primed Fury mods bring it to standard speed comparable to faster one-handed swords.
- Excalibur players often use the Skana as a companion piece for thematic cohesion, particularly in focused Excalibur-centric loadouts.
- Stance polarity is Naramon, which most common melee stances share, keeping polarity costs manageable.
