## Overview

The Sybaris Prime is the primed variant of the Sybaris, a semi-automatic double-tap rifle that fires two rounds per trigger pull. The double-tap mechanic places the effective shot count between a standard semi-auto and a burst rifle: each trigger pull delivers two rounds simultaneously, doubling the raw damage per action compared to a single-fire semi-auto with identical per-shot stats.

The damage profile distributes evenly across impact (29), puncture (30), and slash (29), totaling 88 damage per round or 176 per trigger pull before multishot. The even spread means no faction type benefits disproportionately from the physical damage type alone, making elemental selection the primary driver of faction-specific damage tuning. The 30% critical chance and 2x multiplier provide a serviceable but not dominant critical build path, while the 25% status chance supports elemental build strategies that complement the double-tap application rate.

The Sybaris Prime is vaulted, adding an acquisition challenge relative to its performance tier.

## Acquisition

The Sybaris Prime is vaulted. Its components were distributed through Meso and Neo era relics during its active vault cycle. Player-to-player trading via [warframe.market](https://warframe.market/items/sybaris_prime_set) or direct in-game trading is the current acquisition path. Periodic Prime Vault events may restore access to its relics; DE typically announces these several weeks in advance.

## Variants

The base [Sybaris](/weapons/sybaris) offers the same double-tap mechanic at lower stats (15% critical chance, 1.5x multiplier) and mastery rank 0. A Dex Sybaris was distributed as a Tenno anniversary reward with moderate improvements. No Kuva or Tenet variant exists. The [Tiberon Prime](/weapons/tiberon-prime) occupies a comparable precision-rifle niche for players seeking an alternative.

## Recommended builds

**Critical build.** The 30% base critical chance reaches consistent yellow-crit territory with Point Strike and Vital Sense. The double-tap mechanic means each trigger pull processes two rounds through the crit calculation independently, maintaining reliable crits per trigger action. Speed Trigger or Arcane Acceleration increases fire cadence without sacrificing the crit benefit.

**Viral slash build.** Cold and Toxin mods yield Viral, which halves effective enemy health. The balanced physical damage distribution means slash procs and puncture procs both contribute meaningfully. At 25% base status chance, Rifle Aptitude raises status coverage enough for consistent proc application per burst sequence. Hemorrhage converts impact procs to slash on trigger, adding further bleed stacking.

**High-accuracy precision build.** The 25 accuracy value improves considerably with Stabilizer and Rifle Aptitude in the exilus slot. This build focuses on reliable headshot damage through precision engagement: the double-tap fires both rounds to the same point at short to medium range, amplifying headshot multipliers per pull.

## Notes

- Fire rate of 4.72 represents the rounds-per-second value; in practice, the trigger pull rate determines engagement rhythm since both rounds fire simultaneously.
- The 20-round magazine accommodates 10 full trigger pulls; the 2-second reload is fast enough to avoid significant downtime between engagements.
- Mastery rank 12 gates this weapon to mid-to-late-game accounts; players at this mastery typically have access to the mod investments the weapon benefits from most.
- The Sybaris Prime's riven disposition is worth checking before investing heavily in one; moderate disposition levels respond well to damage or critical chance riven rolls.
