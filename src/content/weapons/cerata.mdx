## Overview

The [Cerata](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Cerata) is a glaive-type melee weapon with 183 total damage per attack. Its damage includes 76 innate toxin damage alongside 19 impact, 52 puncture, and 36 slash physical damage. The built-in toxin component bypasses Corpus shields entirely, dealing damage directly to health -- a significant advantage in Corpus missions where shield stripping would otherwise require weapon swaps or ability use. The 30% status chance allows reliable secondary elemental proc application.

As a glaive, the Cerata can be thrown as a heavy attack, detonating on impact for area damage. The thrown detonation retains the toxin damage, applying toxin procs to multiple enemies within the blast radius. This unique delivery mechanic distinguishes the Cerata from standard melee weapons and enables room-clearing potential beyond the melee swing range. Riven disposition at 4/5 provides good roll potential.

Glaives can also be detonated mid-air by pressing the melee button during the throw, adding tactical flexibility to the attack.

## Acquisition

The Cerata blueprint is available from the Market for credits. Build time is 12 hours in the Foundry. As a standard Market weapon, it is permanently available and never vaulted. The completed weapon can also be purchased with platinum.

## Variants

No Prime, Vandal, or Wraith variant of the Cerata exists. Players seeking glaive alternatives should compare the Glaive Prime (higher crit stats) or Pathocyst (innate viral component) for different elemental innate combinations.

## Recommended builds

**Toxin status build:** Primed Pressure Point, Condition Overload, Weeping Wounds, Fever Strike, Shocking Touch (electric, forming viral with the toxin damage), and Volcanic Edge. The built-in 76 toxin damage combines with an additional toxin mod to boost total toxin output. Adding electric forms viral (toxin + electric procs create viral), enabling health reduction alongside the toxin bypass. Condition Overload amplifies damage with each unique proc.

**Throw build:** Corrupt Charge, Amalgam Organ Shatter, Killing Blow, and elemental mods targeting Corpus shields (magnetic). The thrown detonation delivers area toxin damage, poisoning multiple grouped Corpus enemies simultaneously. Charge-accelerated heavy throws increase the detonation damage output significantly.

**Hybrid melee-throw:** Alternate between melee swings to accumulate combo multiplier and heavy throw detonations to deal burst area damage. This pattern leverages both the melee combo scaling and the glaive's unique thrown-explosive mechanic within the same engagement.

## Synergies

- **Corpus missions:** The 76 innate toxin bypasses shields on all Corpus targets, making the Cerata naturally strong in Corpus-faction missions without needing specific elemental mod investment.
- **Condition Overload:** Innate toxin procs plus additional elemental types from mods rapidly accumulate unique status types, feeding Condition Overload's multiplicative damage bonus.
- **Volt's Speed:** The glaive throw speed increases with Volt's Speed ability, enabling faster delivery of thrown detonations to targets at range.

## Notes

- Glaive throw detonation is triggered by pressing the heavy attack button while the glaive is in flight. Precision timing allows detonation near enemy clusters for maximum AOE damage.
- Range of 1.25 m is among the shortest for melee weapons. The weapon's throw mechanic compensates for the limited swing reach in open areas, but indoor close-quarters combat benefits from the throw more than outdoor wide-area fights.
- The toxin damage component combines with cold mods (via Frostbite) to form viral (not corrosive). Players targeting viral procs should use cold mods alongside the built-in toxin, not electricity or heat.
- Riven disposition at 4/5 makes range, multishot (for the throw's splash), or critical chance rivens particularly valuable for expanding the Cerata's offensive ceiling.
