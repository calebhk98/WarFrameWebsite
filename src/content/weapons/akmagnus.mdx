## Overview

The [Akmagnus](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Akmagnus) is the dual version of the Magnus revolver secondary. At Mastery Rank 12, it delivers 98 total damage per shot: 44.1 impact, 26.95 puncture, 26.95 slash. The 22% critical chance and 2.0x multiplier support functional critical builds, while the 22% status chance enables moderate proc application. Both critical and status values are identical, positioning the Akmagnus as a hybrid weapon with balanced offense capability.

Riven disposition at 5/5 is the maximum tier, making Akmagnus rivens exceptionally potent.

## Acquisition

The Akmagnus blueprint is available from the Market for credits. As an MR12 weapon, it is accessible to mid-to-late progression players. Crafting requires standard materials. The completed weapon can be purchased with platinum.

## Variants

No Prime, Vandal, or Wraith variant exists. The base Akmagnus is the only form. Players who want a dual revolver with higher damage output should compare the Pyrana Prime or Stubba alternatives.

## Recommended builds

**Hybrid crit-status build:** Hornet Strike, Barrel Diffusion, Lethal Torrent, Primed Pistol Gambit, Primed Target Cracker, Pistol Pestilence, and Frostbite. At 22% base crit, Primed Pistol Gambit pushes into reliable yellow-crit. The 22% status chance combined with Lethal Torrent's fire rate increase applies viral procs consistently. The impact-dominant physical damage adds bonus effectiveness against Corpus shielded targets.

**Full critical:** Primed Pistol Gambit, Primed Target Cracker, Hydraulic Crosshairs, and three elemental damage mods. This configuration maximizes crit output on the Akmagnus's balanced damage profile. Headshot precision sustains Hydraulic Crosshairs uptime.

**Riven-powered:** With a 5/5 disposition riven adding multishot, critical chance, or status chance, the Akmagnus becomes a competitive mid-to-late-game secondary. The dual revolver style combined with strong riven bonuses delivers reliable performance across most mission tiers.

## Synergies

- **Hydraulic Crosshairs:** Headshot-triggered crit bonus pairs well with the Akmagnus's moderate fire rate, which allows deliberate headshot placement between trigger pulls.
- **Impact vs Corpus:** 44% impact damage bias deals bonus damage against Corpus shields. In Corpus missions, the Akmagnus requires fewer shots to strip shields before following up with elemental damage to health.
- **5/5 riven disposition:** The maximum riven tier means any Akmagnus riven is worth evaluating. Multishot rivens are particularly impactful, effectively doubling per-shot output of both damage types and proc chances.

## Notes

- The 3.2-second reload is longer than most dual secondaries. With only a 16-round magazine at 5.0 fire rate, the magazine empties in 3.2 seconds -- exactly equal to the reload time. This creates a near-constant fire-then-reload rhythm in extended combat.
- The balanced 22% crit / 22% status makes the Akmagnus equally suited to crit or status builds. Choose based on available mods and current mission needs rather than attempting to optimize both simultaneously without riven investment.
- Riven disposition at 5/5 is a significant asset. Even a mediocre riven roll provides above-average improvements compared to rivens for higher-use weapons.
