## Overview

The Kuva-enhanced version of the Grattler autocannon. Doubles the magazine capacity from 30 to 60 rounds, improves fire rate slightly, and adds an innate elemental bonus from the Kuva Lich. The Riven disposition of 1 (minimum) reflects its strong baseline performance in the Arch-gun meta. Introduced in Update 30.5 (2021-07-06, Sisters of Parvos), the Kuva Grattler is notable for doubled magazine (60 vs 30 base Grattler); Kuva elemental bonus 25-60%; Riven disposition 1 indicating peak-tier power level.

The Kuva Grattler inherits the base weapon's Grineer autocannon lineage, amplified by Kuva infusion from Liches who specialized in explosive ordnance. The weapon's doubled magazine reflects the Kuva enhancement process optimizing feed mechanisms that were a limitation in the original Grineer design.

## Acquisition

Obtained by defeating a Kuva Lich wielding the Grattler. Kuva Liches spawn with randomly assigned weapons in the Kuva Fortress. After defeating the Lich (using correct Requiem sequence), vanquish it to claim the weapon. Blueprint built for 10,000 credits. The Kuva Grattler requires Mastery Rank 15 to equip.

Arch-gun weapons require either standard crafting resources, Syndicate standing for component parts, or specialized mission drops. Once crafted, equipping the Kuva Grattler in Archwing uses the standard Arch-gun slot. To use it as a Heavy Weapon in ground missions or Railjack combat, a Gravimag must be installed. Gravimags are available from Clan Dojos via Energy Lab research, or from Arbitrations as rotation rewards.

## Variants

Superior Kuva variant of the base Grattler. Riven disposition 1 is the absolute minimum, indicating DE has assessed this weapon as top-tier within its category.

The Arch-gun family spans weapons from Tenno engineering (Corvas, Fluctus, Velocitus), Grineer design (Grattler, Kuva line), and Entrati craftsmanship (Cortege, Mausolon, Morgha), each with distinct stat profiles suited to different engagement contexts.

## Recommended builds

Riven disposition of 1 makes Rivens provide minimal benefit -- avoid spending Kuva on Rivens for this weapon. Focus on fixed mods: Rubedo-Lined Barrel, Hollowed Bullets, and elemental pairings. 27% crit and 27% status create a genuine hybrid. Choose Toxin as the Lich element to enable Viral (Cold + Toxin) for maximum health damage amplification.

**Core Arch-gun mods to consider:**

- Rubedo-Lined Barrel -- +165% Arch-gun damage (primary damage mod for all builds)
- Hollowed Bullets -- +120% critical chance (essential for any crit-focused build)
- Critical Focus -- +60% critical multiplier while aiming
- Modified Munitions -- +90% status chance (for status-focused builds)
- Arch-gun Ace -- +165% fire rate (useful on slow-fire weapons to improve feel)
- Parallax Scope -- +30% critical chance while aiming
- Charged Bullets -- +60% magazine capacity
- Superior Defenses -- reduces reload time
- Conductive Coils -- adds Electricity damage
- Thermite Rounds -- adds Heat damage

Prioritize Rubedo-Lined Barrel as the first mod in any Arch-gun build. Follow with critical or status mods based on the weapon's stat profile. Elemental mods provide faction-specific damage type flexibility.

## Notes

The Kuva Grattler operates in the Arch-gun slot, used in Archwing missions and as a Heavy Weapon on the ground via Gravimag. In Railjack (Empyrean) missions, the Arch-gun serves as the primary armament during space combat against fighters and Crewships.

Among the strongest Arch-guns for Railjack crew suppression. Doubled magazine and explosive rounds allow extended suppression of boarding threats without reload interruptions.

Archwing combat system notes: Archwing missions require completing The Archwing quest. Four Archwings are available -- Itzal (mobility/stealth), Amesha (tank/support), Elytron (AoE damage), and Odonata (balanced starter). Elytron's Warhead provides supplementary AoE, complementing the autocannon's sustained explosive output. Itzal enables approach for optimal range where Blast radius is most effective.

The weapon's Riven disposition is 1/5 (very low). Riven dispositions range from 0 (absolute minimum, strong weapon) to 5 (maximum, weak weapon). DE adjusts dispositions quarterly based on weapon usage rates.
