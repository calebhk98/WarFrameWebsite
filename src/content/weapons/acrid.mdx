## Overview

The Acrid is a toxin-based pistol that synergizes with condition overload builds. Its high status chance and magazine capacity make it effective for applying viral and toxin procs. The weapon specializes in status application for augmenting other damage sources. This support-focused design creates interesting build opportunities unavailable with pure damage weapons.

The Acrid's 5.0 fire rate is moderate, but sufficient for status application at medium range. The 15 round magazine is modest but typical for poison-focused weapons. The 1.2 reload time is quick, enabling rapid magazine cycling. Despite low magazine capacity, the quick reload minimizes practical limitations.

With 41% status chance (matching embolist, suggesting balance across status weapons), the Acrid provides reliable status application. The weapon's pure toxin damage makes it less viable as primary damage source, instead serving as status enabler for melee builds. Players use the Acrid to apply viral effects before switching to melee weapons.

This weapon serves specialized roles in condition overload-focused builds. Veterans appreciate the Acrid's reliability for applying viral effects while managing ammunition scarcity through elemental procs. Some players invest multiple forma creating optimized Acrid platforms.

The weapon's specialization encourages creative build construction and synergistic planning. Players must consider overall loadout compatibility rather than individual weapon strength. This design philosophy creates engaging endgame optimization challenges.

The Acrid exemplifies specialized weapons that excel through smart build integration.
