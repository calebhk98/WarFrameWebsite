## Overview

The [Acrid](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Acrid) is a Corpus-derived secondary pistol that fires hypodermic needles loaded with pure toxin. Its entire 43 base damage output comes from the Toxin element, bypassing shields entirely and dealing damage directly to enemy health -- a property unique to toxin damage that makes it exceptionally dangerous against Corpus targets, where shields typically demand significant time-to-kill investment.

The Acrid was introduced as a Bio Lab research weapon and predates many modern secondary options, yet retains a loyal following for its focused toxin delivery and high accuracy. Riven disposition at the maximum tier (5/5) signals that rivens for this weapon tend to roll with strong multipliers, offering potential for significant improvement beyond the base stats.

At Mastery Rank 7, the Acrid gates players who are mid-progression through the star chart. The weapon's bio-lab origin means players without a clan must either join one or purchase the built weapon from other players on warframe.market.

## Acquisition

The Acrid blueprint is researched in the Bio Lab of a Clan Dojo. Solo-clan or ghost-tier players can access it without group coordination, but the Bio Lab itself must first be constructed, which requires clan resources. Once researched, the blueprint is reusable and buildable in the Foundry using the required components.

Players without clan access can purchase a built Acrid from other players through trading or warframe.market using platinum. The weapon does not drop from enemies or missions directly.

## Variants

No Prime, Wraith, or Vandal variant of the Acrid exists. The base Acrid is the only form available. Players seeking a pure-toxin upgrade in the secondary slot should consider the Embolist, which trades fire rate for a slightly different delivery mechanism, or the Pox, which provides an area-of-effect toxin application.

## Recommended builds

**Viral + toxin build:** Pair Infected Clip (toxin) with two cold mods to create the viral + toxin combination. Viral halves enemy health, and toxin bypasses shields, creating a two-step kill pattern effective against all enemy factions. Add Hornet Strike, Barrel Diffusion, and Lethal Torrent to boost base damage and fire rate.

**Condition Overload support build:** Apply the Acrid's toxin procs as a status primer for a melee Condition Overload build. Switch weapons after landing several toxin hits to capitalize on the damage multiplier on the primary melee. The 10% status chance requires a few trigger pulls per status application, so sustained trigger discipline is necessary.

**Raw toxin build:** Maximize toxin damage with Pistol Pest (or Primed Pistol Pest), Infected Clip, and Hornet Strike. This configuration delivers the highest raw toxin output per bullet, ideal for bypassing Corpus shields quickly and dealing consistent health damage in high-level missions.

## Synergies

- **Saryn:** The Acrid's toxin procs pair naturally with Saryn's Spores, which spread the toxin condition to nearby enemies without additional player input.
- **Nidus:** Nidus benefits from consistent status procs to trigger Parasitic Link interactions. The Acrid's consistent toxin output supports ability uptime.
- **Slash-heavy melee:** Toxin bypasses shields; slash procs bypass armor mitigation. Combining the Acrid as a Corpus primer with a slash-heavy melee creates a highly efficient two-weapon setup.

## Notes

- Toxin damage bypasses Corpus shields completely and deals 50% bonus damage to Infested flesh. Against Grineer, it deals no bonus or penalty, making it a neutral choice for mixed-faction missions.
- The 10% status chance is low by modern standards. Expect approximately one proc every two to three trigger pulls at base stats without additional status mods.
- The 15-round magazine limits sustained engagements. Reload discipline -- initiating reloads between encounters rather than mid-fight -- keeps the Acrid's time-to-kill consistent.
- Riven disposition at 5/5 means a well-rolled status chance or toxin damage riven can meaningfully change how the Acrid performs, potentially pushing its status application into reliable single-pull territory.
