## Overview

The Nikana Prime is a prestigious sword melee weapon excelling in critical damage builds. The prime variant boasts exceptional critical chance and multiplier stats, positioning it as a top-tier crit-scaling melee option. Its slender profile and elegant animations appeal to players seeking both performance and aesthetics. The weapon dominates Steel Path content when paired with critical-focused mods and properly planned forma layouts. As an unvaulted prime weapon, accessibility through void fissure farming remains reasonable, making it a standard investment for endgame melee combatants.

## Damage

The Nikana Prime's damage distribution heavily favors puncture at 178.2 with modest impact and slash components at 9.9 each. The puncture-dominant profile is effective against armored targets where puncture damage reduces enemy armor. The 28% critical chance paired with 2.4x multiplier enables builds achieving guaranteed crit status through elemental and buff combinations. Status chance of 28% supports hybrid approaches that leverage both critical and status scaling simultaneously.

## Acquisition

Nikana Prime components drop exclusively from Axi-era void relics. Players must farm void fissures and accumulate components across multiple missions. Currently unvaulted means regular rotation through fissure rewards, making farming accessible. Building requires 15,000 credits and 12 hours of foundry time. Trading is available for component acquisition, allowing players to purchase pieces directly rather than farming if preferred. Extensive farming typically supplies components for multiple copies within reasonable timeframes.

## Variants

The standard Nikana exists as a non-prime variant with significantly lower base damage and critical stats. The prime variant's superior stats justify the farming effort. Cosmetic skins offer visual customization post-acquisition. No other mechanical variants exist.

## Recommended builds

Nikana Prime builds center entirely on critical damage scaling given its exceptional stats. Organ Shatter and Sacrificial Pressure amplify critical multiplier beyond the base 2.4x. Gladiator mods add conditional critical chance stacking. Combo multiplier from stance builds compounds critical damage further. Elemental mods enable status application as secondary damage source, though status scaling remains minimal. Riven mods with critical damage, critical chance, and elemental bonuses unlock peak performance. Berserker Status and similar mods amplify attack speed, reducing engagement time. The weapon's balanced attack speed of 1.0 suits most stances and playstyles equally.

## Notes

Nikana Prime's mastery requirement of 12 necessitates progression through lower-tier content before acquisition. Investment is justified given the weapon's endgame performance. The weapon's sleek design and satisfying critical hit animations appeal to dedicated melee users. Stance selection significantly impacts playstyle: Blind Justice offers reliable damage, Shimmering Blight excels in crowd control, Tranquil Cleave provides utility. Steel Path performance requires proper modding and enemy awareness; the weapon enables solo endless missions with proper planning. The weapon represents significant forma investment given critical dependency, but returns on investment justify the expense through years of viable performance. As a standard prime sword, many players own multiple copies for different build purposes and forma configurations.

## Long-term investment

The Nikana Prime justifies extensive forma investment and riven placement through sustained performance in endgame content. Its critical stats remain competitive even compared to newer weapons, demonstrating how well-designed weapons age gracefully. Players who own Nikana Prime typically maintain multiple copies with different forma distributions, enabling rapid adaptation to mission requirements. The weapon's timeless design and reliable performance make it a staple in many veteran arsenals.
