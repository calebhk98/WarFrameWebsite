## Overview

The Dual Ichor is a pair of notched melee blades with built-in Toxin damage (47), alongside Puncture (45), Slash (11), and Impact (19). The innate Toxin element allows it to combine with Heat mods to produce Gas damage, or with cold mods to produce Viral, without occupying both elemental mod slots with the same element type. The 25% critical chance and 3.0x multiplier make it a strong crit platform when paired with Organ Shatter.

Mastery Rank 6 is required. Attack speed of 1.083 is slightly above average for dual swords, and the 2.3 m range is standard. The 15% status chance is modest; the weapon favors crit builds over status builds. Dual Sword stance mods (Swirling Tiger, Carving Mantis) apply to unlock the full combo potential.

## Variants

The Dual Ichor has no Prime, Kuva, or Tenet variant. It is a standalone weapon in the dual-sword category with a unique innate Toxin element.

## Recommended builds

**Gas/Crit build.** The innate Toxin combines with any Heat mod (Primed Heated Charge mod equivalent for melee: Volcanic Edge or Primed Fever Strike) to produce Gas. Gas procs deal area-of-effect Toxin damage, effective against Infested and unarmored enemies. Add Organ Shatter and Blood Rush for crit escalation.

**Viral crit build.** Add a Cold 60/60 mod alongside the innate Toxin to produce Viral. The 3.0x multiplier with Blood Rush reaches orange-crit territory at x4-x6 combo. Viral amplifies health damage for sustained scaling against health-heavy enemies.

**Condition Overload hybrid.** Use a Cold and a Heat 60/60 mod to create both Viral (Toxin + Cold) and have additional elemental coverage. The Gas cloud from the innate Toxin + any Heat mod provides area DoT while crit builds handle burst damage.

## Synergies

Saryn's Spores pair excellently with Gas proc area-of-effect Toxin damage, spreading the cloud effect to nearby enemies. Nidus's Virulence interacts with the Toxin/Gas combination during Infested missions. Equinox's Mend/Maim augments melee damage during night form, stacking with the Dual Ichor's crit output. The innate Toxin makes the Dual Ichor particularly effective against Corpus units, as Toxin bypasses Corpus shields entirely and deals damage directly to health, making each Toxin proc -- and the Gas clouds derived from it -- highly efficient against shielded targets. Valkyr's Warcry accelerates the 1.083 attack speed further for faster combo accumulation and more frequent crit triggers at the 25% base critical chance, while Rhino's Roar multiplies all damage types including the innate Toxin component.

## Notes

- The innate Toxin element counts as one elemental type for combination purposes; adding one more matching element creates the combined status.
- The 3.0x critical multiplier is the primary draw; prioritize crit scaling mods over status.
