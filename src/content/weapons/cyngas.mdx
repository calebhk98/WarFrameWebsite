## Overview

A rapid-fire Arch-gun firing bursts of projectiles with an even distribution across all three physical damage types. Its balanced Impact, Puncture, and Slash damage makes it effective against multiple enemy factions without requiring elemental mod specialization. Introduced in Specters of the Rail (2016-07-08), the Cyngas is notable for balanced physical damage split across all three damage types; rapid fire rate with very short 1.25s reload; 30% status chance.

The Cyngas emerged from post-Archwing research addressing the need for a versatile all-faction weapon. Its triple physical damage type design was a deliberate engineering choice to ensure effectiveness regardless of targeted enemy faction affiliation during multi-theater operations.

## Acquisition

Component parts available from Syndicates: Barrel from Arbiters of Hexis at Lawful rank, Receiver from Cephalon Suda at Intelligent rank, Stock from The Perrin Sequence at Executive rank. Blueprint from Market for 35,000 credits. The Cyngas requires Mastery Rank 4 to equip.

Arch-gun weapons require either standard crafting resources, Syndicate standing for component parts, or specialized mission drops. Once crafted, equipping the Cyngas in Archwing uses the standard Arch-gun slot. To use it as a Heavy Weapon in ground missions or Railjack combat, a Gravimag must be installed. Gravimags are available from Clan Dojos via Energy Lab research, or from Arbitrations as rotation rewards.

## Variants

No Prime or Vandal variant currently exists. High Riven disposition (4) reflects its position as a balanced but not top-tier weapon.

The Arch-gun family spans weapons from Tenno engineering (Corvas, Fluctus, Velocitus), Grineer design (Grattler, Kuva line), and Entrati craftsmanship (Cortege, Mausolon, Morgha), each with distinct stat profiles suited to different engagement contexts.

## Recommended builds

Balanced physical damage allows flexible elemental combinations. Corrosive (Electricity + Toxin) targets Grineer armor. Viral (Cold + Toxin) amplifies health damage against Corpus. Status chance of 30% enables reliable proc application -- Modified Munitions pushes this toward 55% for consistent status stacking.

**Core Arch-gun mods to consider:**

- Rubedo-Lined Barrel -- +165% Arch-gun damage (primary damage mod for all builds)
- Hollowed Bullets -- +120% critical chance (essential for any crit-focused build)
- Critical Focus -- +60% critical multiplier while aiming
- Modified Munitions -- +90% status chance (for status-focused builds)
- Arch-gun Ace -- +165% fire rate (useful on slow-fire weapons to improve feel)
- Parallax Scope -- +30% critical chance while aiming
- Charged Bullets -- +60% magazine capacity
- Superior Defenses -- reduces reload time
- Conductive Coils -- adds Electricity damage
- Thermite Rounds -- adds Heat damage

Prioritize Rubedo-Lined Barrel as the first mod in any Arch-gun build. Follow with critical or status mods based on the weapon's stat profile. Elemental mods provide faction-specific damage type flexibility.

## Notes

The Cyngas operates in the Arch-gun slot, used in Archwing missions and as a Heavy Weapon on the ground via Gravimag. In Railjack (Empyrean) missions, the Arch-gun serves as the primary armament during space combat against fighters and Crewships.

A solid all-rounder in Railjack ground assault due to faction-agnostic damage distribution. The short reload ensures minimal downtime during prolonged crew combat.

Archwing combat system notes: Archwing missions require completing The Archwing quest. Four Archwings are available -- Itzal (mobility/stealth), Amesha (tank/support), Elytron (AoE damage), and Odonata (balanced starter). Odonata's Energy Shell ability adds Electricity for Corpus shield interaction. Amesha's Warding Grace provides the survivability to sustain fire in dense engagement zones.

The weapon's Riven disposition is 4/5 (high). Riven dispositions range from 0 (absolute minimum, strong weapon) to 5 (maximum, weak weapon). DE adjusts dispositions quarterly based on weapon usage rates.
