## Overview

The Embolist is a status-focused secondary that relies entirely on toxin damage. Its high status chance and magazine capacity make it effective for applying viral and toxin procs. The weapon specializes in hybrid damage builds where status effects matter more than raw damage. This specialization creates interesting build opportunities unavailable with balanced-damage secondaries.

The Embolist's 8.0 fire rate provides steady output suitable for medium-range toxic cloud generation. The 33 round magazine enables extended status application without frequent reloading. The 1.3 reload time is quick, minimizing downtime between magazines. The generous magazine combined with quick reload enables sustained status generation.

With 41% status chance, the Embolist prioritizes status application over raw damage. This weapon shines in builds focused on condition overload, where status effects amplify melee weapon damage. The weapon's pure toxin specialization requires proper mod selection and build synergy but creates powerful hybrid damage builds. Players combining Embolist with melee-focused frames create multiplicative damage scaling.
