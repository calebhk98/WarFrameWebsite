## Overview

The Embolist is a pure-Toxin secondary with 41% status chance -- among the highest status values available for a secondary weapon. The damage profile is entirely elemental: 35 Toxin with zero physical damage types (zero Impact, Puncture, and Slash). Toxin bypasses Corpus Shields entirely, dealing its damage directly to Corpus health pools, and generates Toxin procs that deal additional damage over time. At 41% status chance with 8.0 fire rate and a 33-round magazine, the Embolist applies Toxin procs rapidly and consistently. Mastery Rank 9 is required. The 3% critical chance is negligible; this weapon does not benefit from critical investment.

As a primary status-application tool, the Embolist is most effective when used to apply Toxin procs before switching to a Condition Overload melee. Toxin added to another elemental mod creates Gas (Toxin + Heat) or Viral (Toxin + Cold) depending on the combination. Viral -- combining Cold and Toxin -- multiplies the damage that subsequent hits deal to enemy health pools, making it the dominant secondary element for most enemy types. The Embolist's innate Toxin base can serve as half of a Viral combination when paired with Cold elemental mods. The 1.3-second reload is fast, minimizing downtime.

## Variants

The Embolist has no Prime, Kuva, or Tenet variant. It is a standalone secondary weapon in the pistol category with a unique pure-Toxin damage profile.

## Recommended builds

**Viral status primer build.** The 41% status enables rapid Viral proc application. Equip Hornet Strike, Barrel Diffusion, Lethal Torrent, Frostbite (Cold 60/60), and a second status mod. Combined Toxin + Cold creates Viral. Fire a half-magazine into a target to establish Viral stacks, then switch to a Condition Overload melee for amplified damage. The 100 accuracy ensures all rounds connect at range.

**Gas cloud build.** Adding a Heat 60/60 mod (Pistol Pestilence with Heat) instead of Cold creates Gas (Toxin + Heat). Gas applies Toxin procs in an area-of-effect cloud, spreading to nearby enemies. Equip Augur Seeker for increased status chance. This is most effective against grouped Infested where Gas cloud applies rapidly.

**Sustained Toxin primer.** Against Corpus, the innate Toxin bypasses shields and deals damage to health directly. Equip Hornet Strike, Barrel Diffusion, Lethal Torrent, and two Toxin-boosting 60/60 mods to increase Toxin proc damage per tick. Galvanized Shot rewards proc accumulation with additional damage. The 33-round magazine sustains fire through entire waves.

## Synergies

Saryn's Spores spread Toxin procs from enemies affected by the Embolist; the secondary's rapid Toxin application accelerates Spore spreading. Frames that strip shields (Mag's Polarize) expose Corpus health directly, allowing the Embolist's Toxin to proc on the now-unshielded health pool. The Embolist's status delivery role complements any Condition Overload melee in the loadout.

## Notes

- Zero physical damage means the Embolist deals no Impact, Puncture, or Slash; all damage and procs are purely elemental Toxin.
- Toxin bypasses Corpus Shields; the Embolist is particularly efficient against Corpus health pools without needing shield-stripping support.
