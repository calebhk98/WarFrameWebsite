## Overview

The [Dual Cestra](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Dual_Cestra) is a secondary pistol pair researched through the Tenno Lab in a Clan Dojo. Wielding two Cestras simultaneously doubles the single weapon's fire rate to 18.75 rounds per second while providing a 120-round magazine for extended sustained fire. The weapon's Slash-dominant damage profile (20.8 slash per shot vs 5.2 impact, zero puncture) and 20% per-shot status chance make it a reliable status delivery platform rather than a burst-damage weapon.

The 6% critical chance is low and makes the Dual Cestra ill-suited to crit-focused builds. Its strength lies in consistent status proc application at high fire rate -- at 20% status and 18.75 rounds per second, elemental status effects accumulate rapidly, making it effective for status-synergy setups. The 3.5-second reload is a meaningful drawback that players must account for, though the large magazine minimizes how often it triggers in average engagements.

At Mastery Rank 7, the Dual Cestra is accessible mid-game but requires Tenno Lab access through a Clan Dojo, gating it behind clan membership and research investment.

## Acquisition

The Dual Cestra blueprint is researched through the Tenno Lab in a Clan Dojo. Players must join a clan with an active Tenno Lab and wait for the Dual Cestra research to complete before purchasing and crafting the blueprint. Crafting requires 25,000 credits and standard mid-tier materials; build time is 24 hours.

Players without Clan Dojo access can acquire the Dual Cestra through player trading via Trade Chat or warframe.market, as the built weapon is tradeable.

## Variants

**Secura Dual Cestra** -- A Perrin Sequence syndicate variant at Mastery Rank 10 with significantly improved stats: 16% critical chance, 28% status chance, and a unique credit-income aura proc on status application. The Secura variant is obtained through Perrin Sequence standing at the Exalted rank. It is the preferred choice for endgame builds due to the critical chance improvement enabling hybrid build viability.

No Prime, Kuva, or Tenet variant of the Dual Cestra exists.

## Recommended builds

**Status-Viral-Slash build**: Apply Barrel Diffusion and Lethal Torrent for additional multishot and fire rate. Add Infected Clip and Thermite Rounds for Viral damage combination. At high fire rate, Viral procs accumulate rapidly and amplify subsequent Slash proc damage from the base damage type. This build excels against high-health enemies in Grineer and Corpus missions.

**Corrosive armor strip**: Use Convulsion (Electric) and Infected Clip (Toxin) to produce Corrosive damage. High fire rate drives rapid Corrosive proc accumulation, stripping up to 80% armor from Grineer heavy units at 10 stacks.

**Synergies with status frames**: The Dual Cestra pairs naturally with Saryn (Spores spread procs), melee builds using Condition Overload (status count from secondary weapon adds to the melee multiplier), and Nidus (Mutation stacks benefit from reliable proc generation in nearby enemies). The weapon's consistent status delivery makes it an effective secondary for any build that rewards status-type diversity or proc count.

## Notes

The 3.5-second reload is the Dual Cestra's most significant weakness. Plan reloads during movement phases, behind cover, or while companions or abilities handle nearby enemies. Pistol Ammo Mutation is recommended for Survival and other endless missions where ammunition may run short at sustained fire rates.

The Dual Cestra is a standard secondary Pistol weapon and is affected by Pistol mods, not Melee mods -- despite its name following a naming convention associated with melee weapon pairs.
