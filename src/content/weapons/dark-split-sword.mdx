## Overview

The Dark Split-Sword is a unique melee weapon that functions as both a Heavy Blade and a Dual Sword depending on which stance mod is equipped. This mechanic allows a single weapon to occupy two distinct playstyle niches: the Heavy Blade configuration swings slowly with high damage per hit, while the Dual Sword configuration attacks rapidly with shorter reach. Both modes use the same damage pool and mod build.

WFCD reports base damage of approximately 3.3 per physical type per attack phase, but the weapon's actual per-hit output depends on the active stance and combo multipliers. Status chance is 10% and critical chance is 0% in base configuration, which is low for either build path. Mastery Rank 5 is required. The weapon is obtained through Wraith/Vandal-style methods or direct market purchase.

The dual-stance mechanic works by reading which stance mod is installed: if the equipped stance is a Heavy Blade stance (Cleaving Whirlwind, Tempo Royale, Rending Crane), the Dark Split-Sword attacks as a Heavy Blade with the corresponding animation set and range. If a Dual Sword stance is installed (Swirling Tiger, Crossing Snakes, Gemini Cross), it switches to the faster dual-sword attack pattern. This flexibility allows players to adapt the same weapon to different mission contexts by swapping the stance mod between loadouts without needing to own two separate weapons.

## Variants

The Dark Split-Sword has no Prime, Kuva, or Tenet variant. Its dual-stance mechanic is unique among Warframe melee weapons; no other weapon shares this functionality.

## Recommended builds

**Heavy Blade stance -- damage build.** Equip a Heavy Blade stance such as Cleaving Whirlwind or Tempo Royale. Focus on Pressure Point, Primed Fever Strike, Volcanic Edge, and Organ Shatter. The slower swing speed suits long, wide-arc attacks that hit multiple enemies.

**Dual Sword stance -- speed build.** Switch to Swirling Tiger or Carving Mantis stance. Add Berserker Fury and Primed Fury for rapid combo generation. Status-oriented elemental combinations like Viral benefit from high hit frequency.

**Condition Overload hybrid.** Either stance benefits from layering multiple status types. Equip two 60/60 elemental mods for Viral or Corrosive and include Condition Overload for scaling with each unique status applied.

## Synergies

Warframes with melee buffs (Valkyr's Warcry, Chroma's Vex Armor, Rhino's Roar) amplify both stance configurations equally. Mirage's Eclipse doubles the applicable damage multipliers in light. The Heavy Blade configuration pairs well with Rhino for consistent melee damage in tight quarters.

## Notes

- Switching stances changes the weapon category for the purpose of stance mods -- equip the correct stance before a mission.
- The 0% base crit chance makes crit builds unviable; invest in status and damage scaling instead.
