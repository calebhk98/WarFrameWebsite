## Overview

The Fang is a dual-dagger melee weapon with a Slash-dominant damage profile: 75.6 Slash versus 16.2 each of Impact and Puncture. Available at Mastery Rank 0, the Fang is an early-accessible dual-dagger that provides a Slash-heavy attack pattern for players who prioritize bleed proc application from the outset. The 8% critical chance and 16% status chance are modest; neither crit nor status investment will yield strong returns without significant mod resources. Attack speed of 0.917 and 1.65 m range are typical for dual daggers. The Fang Prime is a substantial upgrade with 26% critical chance, 2.6x multiplier, and 26% status chance.

Dual-dagger weapons use stance mods (Gnashing Payara, Brutal Tide) for their multi-hit combo animations. At MR0, the Fang provides an accessible platform for learning dual-dagger combos and applying early-game Slash procs. The 75.6 Slash base means each hit has a meaningful chance to apply bleed procs even at low status chance, which scales into higher-level play as mod investment improves. Players who enjoy the dual-dagger weapon class should pursue the Fang Prime as their endgame upgrade target.

## Variants

The Fang Prime is the Prime variant with substantially improved critical chance (26%), critical multiplier (2.6x), and status chance (26%), making it one of the strongest dual-dagger weapons available. Pursue Void Relics for the Fang Prime components when ready to upgrade. No Kuva or Tenet variant exists for the Fang family.

## Recommended builds

**Early-game Slash build.** For MR0 players, equip whatever mods are available: Pressure Point, Fever Strike, Voltaic Strike (for Radiation against Grineer armor), and Organ Shatter. The Fang's 75.6 Slash base generates bleed procs at low status chance, which compound over time against enemies. This is an effective early-star-chart approach.

**Upgraded status build (mid-game).** As mod resources increase, equip Primed Pressure Point, Condition Overload, two Viral 60/60 mods, and Weeping Wounds. Despite the low 16% base status, Weeping Wounds at x4 combo reaches usable proc rates. The Slash base ensures bleed procs alongside elemental procs for Condition Overload stacking.

**Bridge weapon build.** Use available mods from earlier weapons -- Pressure Point, Fever Strike, Voltaic Strike, Organ Shatter, Fury -- to maximize the Fang's functional performance until the Fang Prime is accessible. The Fang's MR0 requirement makes it immediately available; treat it as a training ground for dual-dagger mastery.

## Synergies

Frames that group enemies (Khora, Vauban) compensate for the 1.65 m range limitation by bringing targets within reach for multi-target dual-dagger combos. Rhino's Roar amplifies all physical damage equally, benefiting the Slash-heavy base. Volt's Speed increases attack speed to compensate for the moderate 0.917 base.

## Notes

- At MR0 with limited mods, the Fang provides accessible dual-dagger combat; upgrade to Fang Prime as soon as Void Relics become accessible.
- The 8% critical chance makes crit mods ineffective; prioritize damage and status mods.
