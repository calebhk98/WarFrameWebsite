## Overview

The Tenet Grigori is a Sisters of Parvos Tenet melee weapon requiring Mastery Rank 14. Its 228 total damage per hit distributes across 136.8 puncture, 82.08 slash, and 9.12 impact. The puncture-dominant profile makes the Grigori effective against Grineer armor, where puncture penetration reduces effective armor damage mitigation. The 82 slash component generates bleed procs that bypass armor and tick true damage independently.

The 38% status chance is the Tenet Grigori's primary combat feature -- the highest status chance of Tenet melee weapons at this stat tier. At 1.08 attack speed with 38% status, procs accumulate rapidly. The 24% critical chance is functional but the 1.6x multiplier is below average for melee weapons, meaning critical output provides modest returns. This is a status platform: Condition Overload and Weeping Wounds benefit far more from the 38% status than Blood Rush or Organ Shatter would benefit from the 1.6x crit scaling.

Tenet weapons carry a unique elemental bonus from the parent Sister, adding a second elemental damage source to the physical base. The Grigori's bonus element interacts with modded elemental additions to potentially form three distinct elemental status types on a single weapon.

## Acquisition

Obtain through the Sisters of Parvos system at Mastery Rank 14. Trigger a Sister Candidate through Granum Void encounters on Corpus-controlled nodes, then track and vanquish the resulting Sister to claim her Tenet Grigori. The elemental bonus type and magnitude are determined at Sister creation.

## Variants

No base non-Tenet Grigori or Prime version exists.

## Recommended builds

**Condition Overload status**: Condition Overload, Weeping Wounds, Primed Pressure Point, and dual-stat 60/60 elemental mods for corrosive (electric + toxin) plus heat. The 38% base status with Weeping Wounds at x2 combo reaches very high proc rates. Three unique status types (corrosive, heat, and the Tenet bonus element) stack Condition Overload multipliers substantially.

**Viral-slash**: Cold plus toxin for Viral, Weeping Wounds, Condition Overload, Primed Pressure Point. Viral halves enemy max health. The 82.08 slash base generates bleed procs that tick through the halved health pool, bypassing all armor. At 38% status, slash procs apply frequently.

**Corrosive armor strip**: Electric plus toxin for Corrosive, Weeping Wounds, Primed Pressure Point. Six corrosive procs remove all Grineer armor. With 38% status and Weeping Wounds at x2, six procs accumulate within the first 3-4 swings. After full armor strip, 136.8 puncture deals full value against exposed health.

## Notes

The 1.6x critical multiplier is below most melee weapons of this tier -- do not invest heavily in critical mods. Blood Rush and Organ Shatter provide far less return here than they would on a weapon with 2.4x+ multiplier. Invest the mod slots in Weeping Wounds, Condition Overload, and elemental damage instead. The 38% status base essentially guarantees Condition Overload activation within the first combo cycle.
