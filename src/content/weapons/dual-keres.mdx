## Overview

The Dual Keres are twin blades with a Puncture-heavy damage distribution (66.7 Puncture vs 34.5 Slash and 13.8 Impact) and a 1.25 attack speed -- faster than most dual swords. The 28% critical chance and 2.6x multiplier establish a strong crit foundation, while the 14% status chance keeps status builds secondary. Mastery Rank 7 provides access to a weapon that punches above its tier with the right mod investment.

The Dual Keres Prime is the natural upgrade, improving all stats. The base Dual Keres serves as the intermediate dual-sword option for players who have not yet unlocked or farmed the Prime variant. The 1.25 attack speed enables faster combo counter accumulation than slower alternatives, benefiting Blood Rush-based builds that scale with combo multiplier.

## Variants

The Dual Keres Prime is the prime variant, available through Void Relics. The Prime improves critical chance (to 32%), critical multiplier (unchanged at 2.6x), status chance (to 24%), and attack speed. Upgrading to the Prime as soon as Void Relics allow is recommended.

## Recommended builds

**Blood Rush crit build.** The 28% base crit with 2.6x multiplier scales well with Blood Rush at high combo. Equip Organ Shatter and Sacrificial Steel, then Berserker Fury for attack-speed boost on crit. Two 60/60 elemental mods for Viral or Corrosive round out the build.

**Hybrid Weeping Wounds build.** The 14% status chance is low, but Weeping Wounds scales it alongside Blood Rush-scaling crit at high combo. Apply two different elemental pairs to ensure multiple unique status types for Condition Overload scaling.

**Entry-level pre-Prime build.** Before the Dual Keres Prime is accessible, use basic mods: Primed Pressure Point, Organ Shatter, True Steel, and two elemental mods for the desired element. This configuration handles mid-star-chart missions.

## Synergies

Naramon Void Sling reduces combo counter decay, maintaining Blood Rush stacks between kills. Valkyr's Warcry adds attack speed, compounding with the already-high 1.25 base. Rhino's Roar applies a flat damage multiplier across the physical damage profile. The Dual Keres's 1.25 attack speed paired with the Dual Sword stance arcs also benefits from Wisp's Haste Mote, which pushes the attack cadence past the natural ceiling and generates combo counter increments more rapidly. For Steel Path content, Arcane Fury (bonus melee damage on crit) reliably activates at 28% crit chance during sustained engagement, providing a consistent damage multiplier without requiring ability usage.

## Notes

- The 1.25 attack speed is the primary differentiator from slower dual swords; this enables faster combo generation.
