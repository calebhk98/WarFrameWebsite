## Recommended builds

**Pure Slash Crit Build:** With a pure Slash damage type, the Ax-52 benefits massively from Slash-boosting elements. Galvanized Chamber (multishot) and Galvanized Aptitude (status damage scaling) form the core, supported by Vigilante Armaments for additional multishot rolls. The 26% critical chance responds well to Point Strike, enabling reliable yellow and orange crits when using Vital Sense as well.

**Viral-Slash Synergy:** Slash procs deal bleed damage that bypasses armor, and Viral status doubles the bleed damage applied to health. Equip Hellfire (Heat) and a Cold mod for Viral alongside base damage mods. The combination of high fire rate (10 rps) and solid status chance (18%) ensures consistent Slash procs that each receive the Viral damage multiplier. This is among the most effective build archetypes for pure-Slash weapons in higher difficulty content.

The 133.3 accuracy is exceptional for a rifle at this fire rate, enabling reliable hit rates at medium-to-long engagement distances.

## Notes

Pure Slash damage weapons occupy a specialized position in the meta -- they are less versatile against shielded enemies (where Impact is preferred for Magnetic proc) but excel against health and armor through bleed proc mechanics. Ax-52 is well-suited for Infested content where targets lack shields, and against Corrupted enemies with bleed amplification.

The 40-round magazine at 10 rounds per second provides 4 seconds of sustained fire, requiring reloads every 4-second burst cycle. Magazine Warp or Primed Magazine Warp extend this window.
