## Overview

The Pandero is a Secondary weapon in Warframe classified as a Pistol. As a secondary weapon, it provides a reliable fallback option during reload sequences or when ammo constraints demand weapon switching. Requiring Mastery Rank 8, the Pandero bridges the gap between primary weapons and melee combat. The weapon's critical chance of 30.0% and multiplier of 2.8x create opportunities for burst damage. Its damage profile emphasizes balanced physical distribution across impact (18), puncture (36), and slash (18). This versatility makes the weapon effective against varied enemy armor profiles when modded appropriately. The weapon's availability from the Market ensures accessibility for all players once mastery requirements are met.

The Pandero represents a solid investment for players seeking a practical pistol option. Unlike signature weapons requiring prime farming or void fissure progression, it remains a reliable choice throughout mid-game star chart exploration. Its balanced stats serve as a foundation for specialization through modding rather than innate specialization.
