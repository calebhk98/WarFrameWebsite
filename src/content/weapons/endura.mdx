## Overview

The Endura is a rapier melee weapon with a heavily Slash-dominant damage profile: 140 Slash, 50 Puncture, and 10 Impact. The 36% status chance is exceptionally high for a melee weapon, making the Endura one of the strongest status-platform rapiers available. The 10% critical chance and 2.0x multiplier make critical investment unrewarding; this weapon's strength is in status proc accumulation and Condition Overload scaling. Attack speed of 0.917 is slightly below average, and the 2.5 m range is standard for the rapier class. Mastery Rank 7 is required.

The Slash base of 140 combined with 36% status ensures rapid bleed proc application. Slash bleed procs deal a percentage of the total physical damage as damage over time, bypassing armor -- making the Endura effective against high-armor targets despite not having explicit armor-penetrating Puncture as its primary type. The rapier weapon class uses Pointed Wind as its stance mod, providing forward-thrust combo animations at medium range. With the high 36% status and Weeping Wounds, the Endura reaches very high status chance at combo multiplier x4, enabling rapid proc cycling for Condition Overload stacks.

## Variants

The Endura has no Prime, Kuva, or Tenet variant. For a Prime rapier option, the Destreza Prime provides improved critical stats. The Endura's status specialization distinguishes it from the Destreza's crit focus.

## Recommended builds

**Slash/Viral status build.** The 140 Slash and 36% status make the Endura an ideal Slash proc platform. Equip Primed Pressure Point, Condition Overload, Weeping Wounds, and four 60/60 elemental mods (Viral + additional element). At x4 combo with Weeping Wounds, status chance reaches very high levels, applying multiple proc types rapidly for Condition Overload's multiplicative damage.

**Status primer for Condition Overload.** Use the Endura to apply Slash, Viral, and additional element procs quickly before engaging with heavy melee combos. The 36% base status ensures consistent proc generation even without Weeping Wounds investment. Primed Pressure Point and Condition Overload are the core mods; elemental 60/60 mods for Viral provide the health-damage amplification from the Viral status effect.

**Pointed Wind stance combo build.** Equip Pointed Wind for the rapier's forward-thrust combo chain. The Endura's high status per hit combined with a fast combo chain applies proc types faster than single-hit heavy weapons. Berserker Fury increases attack speed after kills, compounding the proc generation rate across extended engagements.

## Synergies

Saryn's Spores spread Slash bleed procs from enemies affected by the Endura's high-status output. Khora's Strangledome groups enemies for multi-target rapier thrust hits, amplifying proc application across clustered groups. Frames with armor-stripping abilities (Nyx, Wisp) expose enemy health for unmitigated Slash bleed proc damage, bypassing the need for Corrosive procs as an armor-removal mechanism.

## Notes

- At 36% status, the Endura is one of the highest-status-chance rapiers; invest in Weeping Wounds and Condition Overload rather than crit mods.
- Slash bleed procs from the 140 Slash base deal armor-bypassing damage over time; this makes the Endura effective against armored targets even without Corrosive elemental procs.
