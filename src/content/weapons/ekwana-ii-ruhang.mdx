## Overview

The Ekwana II Ruhang is a Zaw Link component available from Hok's Anvil at Cetus on the Plains of Eidolon. As a Link, it does not have standalone combat statistics; WFCD reports zeros because the item is an unassembled weapon part. The Ekwana II Ruhang modifies the status chance and critical chance of the assembled Zaw, with the Ruhang naming convention indicating a bias toward status chance in the Ekwana Link family.

Within the Zaw construction system, Link components occupy the third and final slot in the Strike + Grip + Link assembly. The Ekwana series of Links offers different weightings of critical and status modifiers, with "Jai" variants biased toward critical chance and "Ruhang" variants biased toward status chance. The "II" designation typically indicates an upgraded version of the base Ekwana Ruhang with further enhanced stat modifiers. Comparing Link options within Hok's interface before committing to a purchase is essential, as the preview shows assembled Zaw stats before spending Ostron Standing.

Zaw weapons assembled with the Ekwana II Ruhang can be gilded at Mastery Rank 8 or higher to unlock Arcane slots and allow Forma installation for polarity customization. Status-biased assembled Zaws benefit from Weeping Wounds and Condition Overload to exploit the proc accumulation from higher status chance.

## Acquisition

Purchase from Hok's Anvil at Cetus on the Plains of Eidolon. Ostron Standing is required; higher-tier Link components unlock at higher Ostron community rank. Earn Ostron Standing through fishing, mining, and bounty completion in the Plains of Eidolon. Check [the Ekwana II Ruhang Fandom wiki page](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Ekwana_Ii_Ruhang) for the exact Ostron rank requirement and standing cost.

## Variants

The Ekwana Link family includes multiple variants with different critical and status weightings. Compare Ekwana Jai, Ekwana II Jai, Ekwana Ruhang, Ekwana II Ruhang, Ekwana Jai II, and Ekwana Ruhang II to select the modifier that best complements the chosen Strike and Grip combination. No Zaw component has a Prime or Kuva version.

## Recommended builds

When incorporating Ekwana II Ruhang into a Zaw build:

- Select a Strike first to establish the weapon archetype and base damage profile.
- Select a Grip to determine the weapon category (polearm, sword, etc.) and swing speed.
- Add Ekwana II Ruhang as the Link to apply its status-biased modifier to the assembled configuration.
- Preview the final assembled stats in Hok's interface before purchasing.
- For status-biased assembled Zaws, equip Weeping Wounds, Condition Overload, and 60/60 elemental mods to maximize the benefit of higher status chance.
- After gilding at MR8 or higher, install Stance mod matching the weapon category and apply Forma as needed.

## Synergies

Zaw weapons assembled with Ruhang-family Links and biased toward status benefit from Weeping Wounds (scales status chance with combo) and Condition Overload (amplifies damage per unique status type active on target). Frames that sustain combo duration (Wukong, Naramon school) extend Weeping Wounds scaling windows.
