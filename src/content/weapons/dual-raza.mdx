## Overview

The Dual Raza are twin blades with a Puncture-heavy damage distribution (66 Puncture vs 33 Slash and 11 Impact) and a notable 25% critical chance with a 3.0x multiplier. The 10% status chance is low, making this a dedicated crit weapon. Attack speed of 0.917 and range of 2.4 m are standard for the dual-sword class. Mastery Rank 6 is required.

The 3.0x critical multiplier is the defining feature of the Dual Raza. At 25% base crit, mods like Sacrificial Steel combined with Blood Rush quickly reach orange-crit territory during sustained combat. The low status chance discourages status-heavy builds; instead, stack critical scaling and use elemental mods primarily for the base damage bonus.

## Variants

The Dual Raza has no Prime, Kuva, or Tenet variant. Players seeking a Prime dual-sword with similar crit emphasis should consider the Dual Zoren Prime (3.2x multiplier) or Dual Keres Prime (2.6x multiplier with higher crit chance).

## Recommended builds

**Blood Rush crit build.** Sacrificial Steel and Blood Rush bring the 25% base crit to consistent orange-crit levels by x4-x6 combo. The 3.0x multiplier makes each crit hit substantially higher than equivalent weapons with lower multipliers. Add Organ Shatter and Berserker Fury, with two elemental 60/60 mods for Viral or Radiation.

**High-multiplier burst build.** Equip Primed Pressure Point, True Steel (crit chance), Organ Shatter, and a single elemental 60/60 pair. Focus on reaching the highest single-hit damage possible per crit during specific kill windows (e.g., Hydrolyst limb shots or Eidolon synovia).

**Sustained Steel Path build.** Use Amalgam Organ Shatter for attack-speed bonus on slams alongside Blood Rush. Apply Arcane Fury on melee crit for additional damage amplification. Two different elemental pairs ensure Condition Overload bonus from multiple unique statuses even at low 10% base status.

## Synergies

The Dual Raza's 3.0x multiplier benefits proportionally more from damage-multiplying Warframe abilities (Rhino's Roar, Mirage's Eclipse) than weapons with lower multipliers. Volt's Speed increases melee attack animation, compounding Berserker Fury acceleration. Chroma's Vex Armor scales melee damage with received damage, enabling exponential burst windows. Naramon's Power Spike extends combo counter duration between kills, sustaining Blood Rush stacks for consistent orange-crit output even in lower-density missions. The Dual Raza's fast-enough attack speed (0.917, close to standard) also benefits from Arcane Fury, which grants additional melee damage on crit, ensuring that the high-multiplier crits continually feed the Arcane's own damage amplification cycle across extended survival or defense mission encounters.

## Notes

- The 10% status chance means status builds are not viable without Weeping Wounds scaling; invest in crit instead.
- The 3.0x critical multiplier is this weapon's primary selling point; every mod choice should support it.
