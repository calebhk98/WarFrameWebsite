## Overview

The Dragon Nikana is a Nikana-class melee weapon with a heavily Puncture-dominant damage profile: 159.8 Puncture versus 18.8 Slash and 9.4 Impact. Nikana weapons use the unique Nikana stance mods (Decisive Judgement, Tranquil Cleave, Blind Justice) for their combat animations, delivering precise, flowing slash-step combos distinct from standard sword movesets. The 22% critical chance and 22% status chance create a balanced hybrid foundation. The 2.0x critical multiplier is modest but functional with Blood Rush scaling. Mastery Rank 8 is required, and the 2.5 m range is standard for a single-handed melee.

The Dragon Nikana is crafted from the base Nikana, requiring it as a component. This creates a two-stage crafting path: first craft or acquire a Nikana, then use it as an ingredient in the Dragon Nikana blueprint. The result is a meaningfully upgraded stat profile. Players who intend to pursue the Dragon Nikana should craft the Nikana first and hold it rather than building out the base weapon for use. The Nikana Prime is the ultimate upgrade, offering substantially improved critical multiplier and attack speed over the Dragon Nikana.

## Variants

The Nikana Prime is the Prime variant for the Nikana family, with significantly higher critical multiplier, improved attack speed, and enhanced base damage. It is acquired via Void Relics and does not require the Dragon Nikana as a component. The Dragon Nikana has no Kuva or Tenet version.

## Recommended builds

**Blood Rush crit build.** At 22% base crit with Blood Rush, the Dragon Nikana reaches orange-crit at x4 combo. Equip Organ Shatter for the multiplier bonus, Sacrificial Steel for crit chance scaling, and two 60/60 elemental mods for Viral. Blind Justice stance provides fast finisher-setup combos for high-crit single-target burst.

**Hybrid status/crit.** The equal 22% crit and status values support a hybrid approach. Equip Weeping Wounds alongside Blood Rush to scale both simultaneously at higher combos. Four 60/60 elemental mods for Corrosive (armor strip on Puncture-heavy builds) and Viral create multiple proc types for Condition Overload.

**Puncture/Corrosive armor-strip build.** The 159.8 Puncture base pairs with Corrosive elemental mods to strip Grineer Ferrite Armor. After armor strips to zero, the full Puncture damage lands without mitigation. Combine with Primed Pressure Point, Condition Overload, and Blood Rush for scaling damage across the engagement.

## Synergies

Decisive Judgement stance's finisher setup opens stealth multiplier hits; Ash's smoke screen provides a guaranteed stealth finisher window. Rhino's Roar amplifies all physical damage, benefiting the Puncture-dominant base proportionally. The Nikana's flowing combo animations pair well with Naramon's Power Spike focus school for extended combo duration.

## Notes

- Crafting the Dragon Nikana requires an intact Nikana as an ingredient; do not build out the base Nikana for combat before converting it.
- Upgrade to Nikana Prime for the highest-tier Nikana performance, with improved critical multiplier and attack speed.
