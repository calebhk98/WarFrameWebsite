## Overview

The Twin Rogga is a dual shotgun secondary available at Mastery Rank 9, costing 30,000 credits from the Market. As a double-barrel shotgun, each trigger pull fires a pellet spread, and the 2-round magazine means two shots before the 1.5-second reload. The 47 total damage per shot divides across 18.8 impact, 4.7 puncture, and 23.5 slash. The slash majority generates bleed procs on status. The impact component provides stagger potential.

The 10% critical chance and 6.6% status chance are both low. The Twin Rogga is not a stat-focused weapon -- its character is the double-barrel shotgun experience, requiring deliberate trigger discipline given the 2-shot magazine. At 2.5 fire rate, the two shots discharge in quick succession, creating a brief burst of damage before the 1.5-second reload.

At accuracy 8, the Twin Rogga has wide pellet spread, functioning at close range. The double-barrel format at close range delivers both barrels point-blank for full pellet cluster impact. Medium and long range scatter the pellets too widely for reliable target acquisition.

## Acquisition

The Twin Rogga blueprint is purchasable from the in-game Market for 30,000 credits. Build time is 24 hours.

## Variants

No Prime, Vandal, or Wraith variant exists for the Twin Rogga.

## Recommended builds

**Point-blank pellets**: Hornet Strike, Barrel Diffusion, Lethal Torrent, two elemental 60/60 mods. Barrel Diffusion increases pellets per shot, and at point-blank range all additional pellets impact. Both shots fired within the 2-round magazine benefit equally from multishot mods.

**Viral close range**: Cold plus toxin for Viral, Barrel Diffusion, Hornet Strike. Two shots with Viral at close range against any target apply the health-halving status quickly. Reload and repeat.

**Basic shotgun experience**: Hornet Strike and Barrel Diffusion only. The Twin Rogga at base with these two essential mods delivers its double-barrel character without advanced farming requirements.

## Notes

The 2-round magazine requires adapting gameplay around the constant 1.5-second reload rhythm. In continuous enemy waves, the reload cadence disrupts sustained engagement. Pair the Twin Rogga with a high-sustained-fire primary that covers the Twin Rogga's reload windows. The 24-hour build time is longer than typical Market weapons -- plan accordingly. The Twin Rogga is primarily a novelty and roleplay weapon; players seeking competitive secondary performance should consider higher-stat alternatives.


The Twin Rogga's double-barrel design means both barrels fire simultaneously on each trigger pull. While this creates a 2-round maximum magazine, both barrels contribute to the same trigger-pull damage value. The 47 total damage reflects the combined per-shot output of both barrels firing together. This is different from weapons where a 2-round burst fires sequentially -- the Twin Rogga's simultaneous discharge concentrates both barrels' damage into a single brief impact.
