## Overview

The Orokin Prime refinement of the Corvas flak cannon, featuring superior critical chance (44%), a 3.0x critical multiplier, improved status chance (30%), and -- most critically -- a drastically reduced reload time of 2.0 seconds versus the base weapon's 8.0 seconds. Introduced in Update 31.3 (2022-03-28, Garuda Prime Access), the Corvas Prime is notable for 3.0x critical multiplier on charged shots; reload reduced from 8.0s to 2.0s eliminates the base Corvas's primary weakness.

The Orokin Prime design elevates the Corvas's capacitor-charge system to its theoretical maximum, refining the flak mechanism with superior alloys and Void-channeling techniques lost after the Old War. The Prime variant's instantaneous capacitor recharge was the most significant engineering achievement.

## Acquisition

All four components obtainable from Void Relics across Lith, Meso, Neo, and Axi tiers. Blueprint available from Axi K7, Lith W3, Meso B8, Meso H8, Neo D5, and Neo L2 relics. The Corvas Prime requires Mastery Rank 14 to equip.

Arch-gun weapons require either standard crafting resources, Syndicate standing for component parts, or specialized mission drops. Once crafted, equipping the Corvas Prime in Archwing uses the standard Arch-gun slot. To use it as a Heavy Weapon in ground missions or Railjack combat, a Gravimag must be installed. Gravimags are available from Clan Dojos via Energy Lab research, or from Arbitrations as rotation rewards.

## Variants

The Prime version of Corvas. Released alongside Garuda Prime and Nagantaka Prime as part of the Garuda Prime Access package.

The Arch-gun family spans weapons from Tenno engineering (Corvas, Fluctus, Velocitus), Grineer design (Grattler, Kuva line), and Entrati craftsmanship (Cortege, Mausolon, Morgha), each with distinct stat profiles suited to different engagement contexts.

## Recommended builds

Critical multiplier of 3.0x on charged shots is among the highest in the Arch-gun category. The 2.0s reload enables aggressive charge cycling. Combine Hollowed Bullets and Critical Focus for devastating output. The reduced reload eliminates the base weapon's defensive window, enabling sustained charged-shot pressure.

**Core Arch-gun mods to consider:**

- Rubedo-Lined Barrel -- +165% Arch-gun damage (primary damage mod for all builds)
- Hollowed Bullets -- +120% critical chance (essential for any crit-focused build)
- Critical Focus -- +60% critical multiplier while aiming
- Modified Munitions -- +90% status chance (for status-focused builds)
- Arch-gun Ace -- +165% fire rate (useful on slow-fire weapons to improve feel)
- Parallax Scope -- +30% critical chance while aiming
- Charged Bullets -- +60% magazine capacity
- Superior Defenses -- reduces reload time
- Conductive Coils -- adds Electricity damage
- Thermite Rounds -- adds Heat damage

Prioritize Rubedo-Lined Barrel as the first mod in any Arch-gun build. Follow with critical or status mods based on the weapon's stat profile. Elemental mods provide faction-specific damage type flexibility.

## Notes

The Corvas Prime operates in the Arch-gun slot, used in Archwing missions and as a Heavy Weapon on the ground via Gravimag. In Railjack (Empyrean) missions, the Arch-gun serves as the primary armament during space combat against fighters and Crewships.

Strictly superior to base Corvas in all Railjack scenarios. Fast reload and higher per-shot damage make it the definitive choice for players who have unlocked it.

Archwing combat system notes: Archwing missions require completing The Archwing quest. Four Archwings are available -- Itzal (mobility/stealth), Amesha (tank/support), Elytron (AoE damage), and Odonata (balanced starter). Pairs with any Archwing due to improved reload speed. Itzal's Cosmic Crush ability can group targets for area charged shots. Elytron provides AoE support between single-target charged engagements.

The weapon's Riven disposition is 3/5 (moderate). Riven dispositions range from 0 (absolute minimum, strong weapon) to 5 (maximum, weak weapon). DE adjusts dispositions quarterly based on weapon usage rates.
