## Overview

The Tenet Envoy is a Sisters of Parvos Tenet primary weapon requiring Mastery Rank 16. It is a rocket launcher that fires guided missiles. Its 740 total damage per rocket is cold-dominant: 640 cold elemental and 100 impact. Cold damage deals bonus damage to Corpus shields and slows targets on proc; multiple cold procs eventually freeze the target. At 0.83 fire rate, the Envoy fires deliberately -- one rocket approximately every 1.2 seconds.

The 28% critical chance and 2.6x multiplier are strong for a launcher. Point Strike pushes the 28% base toward 85% with occasional orange crits on large targets. The 24% status chance enables cold proc and elemental proc application per rocket hit. Each rocket impact applies both the cold direct damage and an area-of-effect detonation, meaning group targets in the blast radius receive damage and proc chances from a single shot.

The 8-round magazine and 4.0-second reload create meaningful reload pauses after sustained fire. Eight rockets at 0.83 fire rate provides approximately 9.6 seconds of continuous engagement before the 4-second reload window. Like all Tenet weapons, the Envoy carries a unique elemental bonus from its parent Sister.

## Acquisition

Obtain through the Sisters of Parvos system at Mastery Rank 16. Trigger a Sister Candidate via Granum Void encounters, track and vanquish the Sister to claim her Tenet Envoy. The elemental bonus type from the Sister modifies the total cold-plus-impact damage profile.

## Variants

No base non-Tenet Envoy counterpart or Prime version exists.

## Recommended builds

**Cold freeze cluster**: Serration, Split Chamber, Point Strike, Vital Sense, Thermite Rounds (heat), and Cold Hazard or additional cold mods. Heat plus cold on the same weapon does not combine -- they deal separate elemental procs. The cold base stacks cold procs toward freeze while heat status adds independent burn ticks. Point Strike with 28% base reaches high crit rates.

**Magnetic Corpus**: Add electric mods to the cold base for Magnetic elemental (cold + electric = Magnetic). Magnetic deals 75% bonus damage to Corpus shields. The 640 cold base amplified by Magnetic mod combination devastates Corpus defensive units. Status procs reduce shield capacity further.

**Radiation**: Add heat mods to the cold base. Cold plus heat creates Blast (not Radiation). For Radiation, use the Sister's bonus element if it provides heat, then combine with electric mods added through modding. Radiation confuses enemies and deals bonus damage against Alloy Armor.

## Notes

The Tenet Envoy fires guided missiles that track toward the aimed target. This homing capability improves hit rate at longer ranges compared to unguided rockets. The 4-second reload is long -- reload in cover between engagements. The 640 cold damage creates natural freeze-stagger synergy: multiple hits from the Envoy in rapid succession accumulate cold procs toward a frozen target, providing passive crowd control through status accumulation.
