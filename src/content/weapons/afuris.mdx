## Overview

The [Afuris](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Afuris) is the base dual machine pistol secondary. It fires 12.5 rounds per second from a 70-round magazine, making it one of the fastest-firing secondaries accessible at Mastery Rank 4. Total damage per bullet is 20 (3 impact, 3 puncture, 14 slash), with the slash component dominant at 70% of output. The 12% status chance is low; the 5% critical chance is minimal. The Afuris's strength is the volume of bullets delivered per second rather than per-shot damage or proc reliability.

Riven disposition at the maximum tier (5/5) means Afuris rivens roll with excellent multipliers, giving the weapon surprising endgame potential when properly riven-invested. The Afuris Prime at MR12 provides a significant upgrade in status chance and magazine size.

## Acquisition

The Afuris blueprint is available from the Market for 20,000 credits. As a Mastery Rank 4 weapon, it is accessible in early-mid progression. Crafting requires standard materials. The Afuris can also be purchased built with platinum.

## Variants

The [Afuris Prime](/weapons/afuris-prime) (Mastery Rank 12) upgrades status chance to 30% and magazine to 80 rounds, substantially improving proc output for status-focused builds. Riven disposition on the Prime drops to 3/5. Players who prioritize status over riven investment should use the Prime; those investing in a riven for volume-fire play should compare both versions.

## Recommended builds

**Status rapid-fire build:** Hornet Strike, Barrel Diffusion, Lethal Torrent, Pistol Pestilence, Frostbite, Jolt, Scorch, and Primed Quickdraw. At 12.5 fire rate and 12% status chance, procs accumulate roughly every 1-2 seconds against a single target. Multiple elemental mods ensure procs apply across useful faction-specific types. This is the standard status build for the Afuris at this mastery tier.

**Riven-powered build:** With a 5/5 disposition riven adding status chance, the Afuris reaches 24-30% effective status chance, dramatically improving proc rates. Add multishot or damage for additional scaling. A strong riven converts the Afuris into a competitive mid-game secondary.

**Mastery progression build:** Equip any available mods and level to 30 for Mastery Experience. The Afuris's high fire rate makes leveling fast in high-density missions.

## Synergies

- **Afuris Prime upgrade:** For players who invest forma into the Afuris and then progress to MR12, the Afuris Prime is the natural upgrade with compatible stat values. Mod configurations transfer well between the two versions.
- **Condition Overload primer:** Despite the low status chance, the high fire rate means multiple bursts quickly apply 2-3 unique proc types. Brief Afuris fire before switching to melee enables Condition Overload setups in fast-paced combat.
- **High-density missions:** Survival, Defense, and Exterminate with large enemy counts benefit from the Afuris's sustained suppressive fire, keeping constant pressure on groups while primary weapons reload.

## Notes

- Accuracy of 15.4 is low. The Afuris's dual-pistol spread pattern widens beyond 15-20 meters. Effective use is at close range where all bullets connect.
- The 2.0-second reload with 70 rounds at 12.5 fire rate means the magazine lasts 5.6 seconds. Reload management in extended encounters requires triggering reload during movement between clusters.
- The Afuris is a budget-accessible, straightforward rapid-fire secondary that pairs well with learning the game's dual-pistol mechanics. It does not compete with top-tier secondaries at endgame without riven investment, but riven potential at 5/5 disposition means the ceiling exists if desired.
