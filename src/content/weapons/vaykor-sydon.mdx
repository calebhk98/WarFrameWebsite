## Overview

The Vaykor Sydon is the Steel Meridian-enhanced variant of the Sydon polearm, requiring Mastery Rank 11. Its 213 total damage per hit is overwhelmingly slash: 202.35 slash and 10.65 impact, with no puncture. A 202-slash weapon generates bleed procs on nearly every hit, as slash procs bypass armor and tick true damage over time. Polearms reach 2.9 meters -- among the longest melee ranges -- enabling strikes that hit multiple targets in a line or arc.

The 33% status chance ensures slash procs activate frequently. Combined with elemental mod additions (corrosive or viral), the Vaykor Sydon produces mixed physical and elemental procs per swing. The 21% critical chance and 2.5x multiplier are a useful crit bonus on top of the status base. At x3 combo with Blood Rush, critical chance exceeds 60%, producing regular orange crits alongside the slash bleed output.

The Vaykor Sydon carries the Steel Meridian Justice passive, generating healing and area damage on kills during Syndicate missions. The polearm format with its wide sweep stance moves distributes these kills across enemy groups, activating the Justice proc frequently.

The Vaykor Sydon requires Steel Meridian Syndicate rank and standing. Purchase directly from the Steel Meridian vendor at any Relay without crafting.

## Recommended builds

**Slash-Viral**: Cold plus toxin for Viral, Weeping Wounds, Blood Rush, Primed Pressure Point, Organ Shatter. Viral halves enemy max health. The 202.35 slash base with Weeping Wounds at x2 combo produces slash procs reliably -- bleed ticks deal true damage against the halved health pool. Blood Rush at x3 combo pushes the 21% crit toward 60% for additional output.

**Condition Overload**: Condition Overload, Weeping Wounds, Primed Pressure Point, corrosive (electric + toxin) plus heat mods. Three unique status types (slash proc, corrosive proc, heat proc) stack Condition Overload multipliers substantially.

**Heavy attack burst**: Killing Blow, Corrupt Charge, Primed Pressure Point, Sacrificial Steel. The 1065 base heavy attack at 12x combo from Corrupt Charge generates massive one-shot burst damage with the slash dominant base.

## Notes

Polearm stance mods with wide sweep attacks maximize the 2.9-meter range. Shimmering Blight and Bleeding Willow are common polearm stances -- check their sweep patterns to match the playstyle. The Justice passive healing rewards fighting in melee range, which polearm range enables safely. In Steel Path content, the combination of slash bypass, Viral amplification, and Justice healing creates a self-sustaining melee loop.


The Justice passive healing from the Steel Meridian Syndicate proc is most valuable in high-enemy-density missions where kill frequency is high enough to consistently trigger the passive. Survival and Defense missions on Syndicate-aligned nodes provide the conditions where the Vaykor Sydon's kill speed and Justice proc interact most effectively -- rapid melee kills from the slash-dominant polearm trigger healing repeatedly, sustaining health through sustained combat without needing a dedicated healing warframe ability.
