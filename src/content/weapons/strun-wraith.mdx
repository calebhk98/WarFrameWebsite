## Overview

Strun Wraith is a primary shotgun weapon that serves as Wraith variant of the Strun shotgun; improved over the base Strun with higher critical chance and tighter spread; obtained through time-limited event rewards. Mastery Rank 10 is required to equip the weapon. Players who invest in building it properly will find it reliable across the star chart, with scaling potential on the Steel Path when supported by appropriate mods and Warframe ability synergies.

The primary slot is competitive in Warframe, and Strun Wraith occupies a distinct position within it. Understanding its damage profile -- the distribution across physical damage types and any elemental components -- is the first step toward optimizing it for specific mission types and enemy factions.
