## Overview

A continuous-beam Arch-gun dealing Radiation damage with the highest status chance of any Arch-gun at 50%. Its beam fires across a wide arc, applying Radiation procs that inflict Confusion -- causing enemies to attack one another and disrupting AI targeting systems. Introduced in Update 24.4 (2019-03-08, Buried Debts), the Larkspur is notable for 50% status chance -- highest among Arch-guns; innate Radiation damage causing Confusion (enemy infighting); Fortuna open-world resources required for crafting.

Developed using reverse-engineered Corpus beam technology discovered during the Buried Debts investigation on Fortuna, the Larkspur represents a Tenno adaptation of Corpus energy projection systems. Its Radiation core was a deliberate design choice to disrupt Corpus and Sentient targeting systems through electromagnetic saturation.

## Acquisition

Blueprint from Market for 15,000 credits. Components require Fortuna resources from Orb Vallis: Crisma Toroid (from Orb Vallis Toroid farming), Radiant Zodian (mining), Synathid Ecosynth Analyzer (Fortuna Bounty rewards), and Venerdo Alloy (from Fortuna resources). Build cost 20,000 credits. The Larkspur requires Mastery Rank 8 to equip.

Arch-gun weapons require either standard crafting resources, Syndicate standing for component parts, or specialized mission drops. Once crafted, equipping the Larkspur in Archwing uses the standard Arch-gun slot. To use it as a Heavy Weapon in ground missions or Railjack combat, a Gravimag must be installed. Gravimags are available from Clan Dojos via Energy Lab research, or from Arbitrations as rotation rewards.

## Variants

Larkspur Prime released in Hotfix 32.3.6 (2023-03-15) improves critical chance from 10% to 14% and critical multiplier from 1.4x to 1.6x, enabling hybrid builds while retaining the 50% status identity.

The Arch-gun family spans weapons from Tenno engineering (Corvas, Fluctus, Velocitus), Grineer design (Grattler, Kuva line), and Entrati craftsmanship (Cortege, Mausolon, Morgha), each with distinct stat profiles suited to different engagement contexts.

## Recommended builds

The 50% status chance is the defining feature -- maximize it with Modified Munitions for near-guaranteed Radiation Confusion on hit. Radiation Confusion causes enemies to attack each other, dramatically reducing incoming damage in dense fights. Critical stats (10%/1.4x) are low, making pure status builds superior to hybrid approaches. Larkspur Prime improves critical stats if hybrid builds are desired.

**Core Arch-gun mods to consider:**

- Rubedo-Lined Barrel -- +165% Arch-gun damage (primary damage mod for all builds)
- Hollowed Bullets -- +120% critical chance (essential for any crit-focused build)
- Critical Focus -- +60% critical multiplier while aiming
- Modified Munitions -- +90% status chance (for status-focused builds)
- Arch-gun Ace -- +165% fire rate (useful on slow-fire weapons to improve feel)
- Parallax Scope -- +30% critical chance while aiming
- Charged Bullets -- +60% magazine capacity
- Superior Defenses -- reduces reload time
- Conductive Coils -- adds Electricity damage
- Thermite Rounds -- adds Heat damage

Prioritize Rubedo-Lined Barrel as the first mod in any Arch-gun build. Follow with critical or status mods based on the weapon's stat profile. Elemental mods provide faction-specific damage type flexibility.

## Notes

The Larkspur operates in the Arch-gun slot, used in Archwing missions and as a Heavy Weapon on the ground via Gravimag. In Railjack (Empyrean) missions, the Arch-gun serves as the primary armament during space combat against fighters and Crewships.

Radiation Confusion disrupts Railjack crew coordination during boarding events. Interior mission use against grouped boarders reduces threat level significantly through enemy infighting.

Archwing combat system notes: Archwing missions require completing The Archwing quest. Four Archwings are available -- Itzal (mobility/stealth), Amesha (tank/support), Elytron (AoE damage), and Odonata (balanced starter). Odonata's Energy Shell adds Electricity for Corpus shield interaction while the Radiation output handles targeting disruption. Amesha's Warding Grace provides damage reduction during the stationary beam engagement.

The weapon's Riven disposition is 3/5 (moderate). Riven dispositions range from 0 (absolute minimum, strong weapon) to 5 (maximum, weak weapon). DE adjusts dispositions quarterly based on weapon usage rates.
