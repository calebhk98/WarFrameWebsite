## Overview

The [Amanata](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Amanata) is a polearm melee weapon introduced in a Warframe update. It deals 126 total damage per attack: 88.2 puncture and 37.8 slash with no impact component. The 24% critical chance and 2.0x multiplier support functional critical builds, while the 24% status chance allows moderate proc application. Both critical and status values are identical, positioning the Amanata as a naturally balanced hybrid weapon.

At Mastery Rank 2 and with a 3.0 m range (generous for a polearm), the Amanata is accessible early and provides excellent reach for sweeping multi-enemy attacks from the start of progression.

## Acquisition

The Amanata blueprint is available from the Market for 30,000 credits. Crafting requires standard early-game materials. As a Market weapon, it is permanently accessible and never vaulted.

## Variants

No Prime, Vandal, or Wraith variant exists for the Amanata. The base weapon is the only available form.

## Recommended builds

**Hybrid critical-status build:** Primed Pressure Point, Berserker Fury, Blood Rush, Organ Shatter, Weeping Wounds, Condition Overload, and two elemental mods for viral. At 24% base critical chance, Blood Rush reaches yellow-crit consistently at x4 combo. Weeping Wounds parallels status scaling simultaneously. The 3.0 m range ensures combo-building polearm sweeps connect against multiple enemies per swing.

**Early game accessible build:** Pressure Point, North Wind, Fever Strike, Shocking Touch, Volcanic Edge, and a polearm stance. This entry-level configuration delivers functional elemental damage and status procs within budget-accessible mod parameters.

**Status polearm:** Weeping Wounds, Condition Overload, and four elemental mods. The 24% base status chance scales rapidly with Weeping Wounds stack growth, enabling rapid proc accumulation per combo cycle.

## Synergies

- **Blood Rush:** 24% base crit makes Blood Rush reach orange-crit at moderate combo multipliers. The Amanata's 3.0 m range and polearm AOE sweeps build combo quickly against enemy groups.
- **Condition Overload:** Polearm sweeping combos apply procs to multiple enemies simultaneously. Each target receives independent status accumulation, maximizing Condition Overload's value in group fights.
- **Shimmering Blight stance:** Provides sweeping wide-arc combos ideal for the Amanata's long reach. The stance's forward-momentum combos close distance while delivering multi-hit proc applications.

## Notes

- Riven disposition at 1/5 is the lowest tier, reflecting high community use. Rivens for the Amanata roll with minimal multipliers; the weapon performs well without riven investment.
- The 3.0 m range is above average for polearms. Use this reach advantage to engage enemy groups from slightly beyond their melee attack range, reducing incoming damage while landing multiple targets per swing.
- Zero impact damage means the Amanata has no bonus against Corpus shields. Add magnetic (cold + electric) elemental mods in Corpus missions to compensate for the absent impact damage type.
