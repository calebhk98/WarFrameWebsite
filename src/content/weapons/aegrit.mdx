## Overview

The Aegrit is a Mastery Rank 11 secondary throwing weapon that launches sticky grenades. Each thrown grenade adheres to a surface or enemy and detonates after a brief delay, dealing the majority of its damage as Blast. The weapon carries two grenades per magazine and reloads quickly at 1.3 seconds, enabling a rapid two-round burst followed by immediate reload cycling. Its high Critical Chance of 37% with a 1.9x multiplier makes it effective in critical-focused builds, and its 19% Status Chance supports Blast status procs that knock down and ragdoll nearby enemies.

The Aegrit is primarily used in close-to-mid range combat where grenades can be placed precisely on high-value targets or choke points. The Blast damage type is effective against machinery and light armor, though it is less effective against heavily armored Grineer. The two-round magazine encourages deliberate target selection rather than sustained fire.

## Acquisition

The Aegrit blueprint is available from the in-game Market for credits. Components are crafted in the Foundry. The weapon does not require any special prerequisite missions beyond Mastery Rank 11.

## Variants

The Aegrit does not have a Prime, Kuva, or Tenet variant as of this writing. Check the [Aegrit wiki page](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Aegrit) for any updates.

## Recommended builds

Critical builds leverage the 37% base Critical Chance with Primed Pistol Gambit and Hollow Point to reach near-guaranteed crits with orange-tier multipliers on each throw. Pair with Pistol Gambit and Target Cracker for consistent critical output.

Status builds invest in Pistol Pestilence and Pathogen Rounds to add Toxin to the Blast base, creating a Gas combination. Blast status itself knocks down enemies; adding Gas enables poison stacking for sustained damage over time.

## Notes

- Grenades stick to enemies and surfaces; they will not detonate on contact.
- The Blast status proc ragdolls enemies, useful for crowd control in narrow corridors.
- Avoid using the Aegrit in close proximity; the blast radius can damage the user.
- At 37% base crit chance, Argon Scope provides a meaningful crit chance spike when aiming.
- The two-round magazine means reload discipline is critical; reload immediately after the second shot to minimize downtime.
- The Aegrit's high base crit chance (37%) is unusual for a secondary; it rivals some dedicated critical-focus weapons. This makes it viable both as a standalone critical secondary and as a complement to a primary weapon focused on status or area denial.
- Blast damage is most effective against Machinery (Corpus Moas and Ospreys) and Corrupted enemies encountered in Void missions; plan faction matchups accordingly.
