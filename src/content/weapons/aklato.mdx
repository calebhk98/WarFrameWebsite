## Overview

The [Aklato](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Aklato) is the dual-wield version of the starter Lato secondary pistol, available at Mastery Rank 3. Each bullet deals 40 total damage: 6 impact, 24 puncture, 10 slash. At 5.0 fire rate and 30-round magazine, the Aklato provides steady sustained fire. Critical chance (10%) and multiplier (1.8x) are modest; status chance (6%) is very low. Riven disposition at the maximum tier (5/5) offers the best possible riven roll potential.

The Aklato is primarily a Mastery Rank progression weapon and new-player learning tool. Its dual-pistol category introduces players to the mechanics of dual secondaries.

## Acquisition

The Aklato blueprint is available from the Market for credits. As an MR3 weapon, it is accessible early in progression. Crafting requires minimal materials available from the inner star chart.

## Variants

No Prime variant of the Aklato exists (the Lato Prime is a starter frame entitlement, not a standard Prime). No Vandal or Wraith version exists. Players progressing past the Aklato should consider the Aklex (higher crit and damage) or Akmagnus (better balanced stats).

## Recommended builds

**Early game build:** Hornet Strike, Barrel Diffusion, Lethal Torrent, Pistol Pestilence, and Frostbite. This budget configuration delivers viral proc potential and improved fire rate within the mod access of an early-game player.

**Riven-enhanced build:** A 5/5 disposition riven with critical chance, multishot, or status chance converts the Aklato into a competitive secondary. Combine with Hornet Strike, Barrel Diffusion, and elemental mods. A strong riven can make the Aklato viable in mid-game content.

**Mastery leveling:** Equip any four available mods and run missions efficiently. The Aklato's fire rate makes leveling quick in high-density missions.

## Synergies

- **Puncture bonus:** The 60% puncture distribution provides a modest advantage against Grineer Ferrite Armor, making the Aklato slightly more effective against Grineer than against Corpus when unmodded.
- **Dual pistol mechanics:** The Aklato introduces players to dual secondary handling -- shared magazine, dual-aim animations, and the Barrel Diffusion interaction. Skills developed with the Aklato transfer to higher-tier dual secondaries.
- **5/5 riven:** At maximum disposition, even a partial riven stat is meaningful. Damage, multishot, or critical chance additions all improve the Aklato noticeably at this tier.

## Notes

- The Aklato's 6% status chance makes status builds non-functional without riven investment. Prioritize raw damage mods rather than elemental status mods for baseline performance.
- Reload time of 2.4 seconds is above average for secondaries at this mastery level. Trigger discipline and reload timing are important habits to develop.
- This weapon is primarily encountered during new-player progression before better options become available. The 5/5 riven disposition means a riven can extend its utility into mid-game, but most players retire it after obtaining a higher-tier secondary.
