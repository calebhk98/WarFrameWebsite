## Overview

The Ignis Wraith is a hitscan flamethrower designed to engulf enemies in continuous streams of superheated flame. Unlike its predecessor the standard Ignis, the Wraith variant features improved ammo efficiency and raw damage output, making it significantly more effective at crowd control. The weapon fires in a cone pattern, automatically hitting all enemies within its spread radius without requiring precision aiming. This makes it exceptionally forgiving for new players while remaining competitive in higher-level content when properly modded.

The Ignis Wraith's primary strength lies in its simplicity and area denial capabilities. It does not require critical hit stacking or complex status synergies; instead, it burns through enemy ranks through sustained application of heat damage and the occasional status proc. The weapon excels at maintaining pressure on grouped enemies and is particularly effective on tileset maps where corridors naturally funnel enemy movement into the flame cone.

## Damage and status

All damage from the Ignis Wraith manifests as heat, making it inherently effective against armor-heavy targets vulnerable to thermal effects. The weapon's 29 percent status chance translates to frequent heat procs, each of which applies a damage-over-time burn effect that can quickly accumulate on tightly grouped enemies. In coordinated groups where other players apply different status types (corrosive for armor strip, viral for health scaling), the Ignis Wraith's heat damage pairs well by exploiting both raw thermal vulnerability and synergistic status combinations.

The lack of critical chance means crit-focused mods are not viable, and pure damage scaling must come from fire-rate and elemental mods. This keeps the damage ceiling lower than weapons with high crit multipliers, but it also makes the Ignis Wraith less dependent on perfect hit placement and RNG-driven crit procs.

Heat damage itself is one of the more straightforward damage types in Warframe's status system. Heat procs apply burning damage that ticks for 6 seconds, dealing a percentage of the triggering hit's damage as DoT. Multiple heat procs do not stack in duration but refresh the timer, meaning rapid application against single targets creates near-permanent burning status. The Ignis Wraith's flame cone nature ensures that every enemy hit during the stream receives heat damage, naturally stacking procs across grouped targets.

Against mechanical enemies (Corpus), heat deals 25 percent increased damage, making the Ignis Wraith particularly nasty in Void Fissure void missions dominated by Corpus units. Against organic enemies (Grineer), heat deals reduced damage, but the status proc remains effective for control and sustained pressure.

## Acquisition

The Ignis Wraith blueprint is obtained through Clan Dojo Chem Lab research (requires the clan to be rank 10) or from Veil Proxima Railjack cache missions at Rotation B. Since Update 32, the blueprint is available to all rank-10 or higher clans without the prior event-exclusivity restriction. Crafting costs 30,000 credits, 3 Argon Crystals, 5 Detonite Injectors, 10,000 Ferrite, and 5,000 Rubedo, making it accessible to players at mastery rank 9 or higher. The relatively low resource cost and credit fee mean multiple copies can be built cheaply if desired for different elemental builds.

## Recommended builds

The ideal Ignis Wraith build prioritizes flat damage scaling and heat amplification. Serration, Cosmetic Expulsion, and Heat mods form the backbone, pushing the weapon's heat damage into the hundreds per shot. Adding utility mods like Multishot or a fire-rate booster extends the flame cone's reach and applies status procs more frequently. A baseline beginner build for new players looks like: Serration (damage), Multishot (projectile count), Heat Damage (elemental scaling), and Rifle Ammo Mutation (ammo sustain). This keeps the weapon's heat damage respectable while maintaining reasonable ammo economy for extended missions.

For higher-difficulty content (Sortie, Steel Path), combining the Ignis Wraith with an elemental mod that converts its base damage into a combined heat-viral or heat-corrosive status is standard. Paired with a frame that provides damage amplification (Xata's Whisper, Roar, etc.), it becomes viable even against level 100+ enemies through sustained pressure and proc stacking.

Advanced players optimize the Ignis Wraith through combination with Primed mods. Primed Serration, Primed Multishot, and Thermite Rounds push the weapon's heat damage to 300+ per hit. Adding a fire-rate booster like Primed Speed Trigger enables flame stream spam that applies heat status nearly every tick against clustered enemies. In survival missions with resource boosters active, the Ignis Wraith becomes a weapon of mass crowd dissolution.

## Synergies

The Ignis Wraith pairs exceptionally well with frames that amplify elemental damage or provide damage buffs. Ember benefits from the weapon's inherent heat scaling via her passive. Xorias with infused status or Napalm procs chain elemental effects across the map when applied by the flame. Trinity with Well of Life can sustain teammates through passive healing while they focus fire with the Ignis Wraith.

The weapon also synergizes well with Heat-based status synergies on larger tiles. When heat procs stack on a target (especially a heavily armored enemy), the cumulative DPS from multiple burns becomes substantial. Companion weapons with heat damage further amplify this effect if modded identically.

Wukong is an excellent frame pairing for the Ignis Wraith because his Cloud Walker ability grants both mobility and damage reduction, enabling aggressive mid-range play with a flame weapon without constant cover-to-cover movement. Gyre's shield cascades interact favorably with the Ignis Wraith's sustained fire pattern, creating overlapping defensive bubbles around the player. Hildryn with Aegis Storm converts the weapon's ammo drain into shield generation, creating a nearly infinite sustain loop where firing the Ignis Wraith actively increases defensive capabilities.

Moa companions with elemental mods (particularly heat-based precepts) chain attacks with the Ignis Wraith's heat procs, creating layered status application. A Taxon Moa with a heat elemental damage precept will apply additional heat burns to enemies already burning from the primary weapon, stacking DoT effects substantially.

## Lore

The Ignis Wraith is a variant of the original Ignis flamethrower, refined through prolonged use in Void Fissure incidents. The "Wraith" designation typically indicates a weapon enhanced or reforged in the Void's influence, granting it improved efficiency and resilience. The Ignis Wraith first became available during Void Fissure events as a reward for players brave enough to farm convergence void missions, cementing its status as an endgame grind for dedicated players seeking a superior flamethrower.

In the Warframe universe, heat-based weapons are associated with aggressive, direct combat styles. The Ignis Wraith exemplifies this philosophy through its relentless flame stream and refusal to reward precision aiming. Veterans appreciate its simplicity; newer players value its forgiving hitbox and lack of micro-management.
