## Overview

A charged electromagnetic rail cannon firing projectiles with an equal split across all three physical damage types and innate Magnetic damage -- 400 damage in each category for 1600 total per charged shot. Magnetic damage is especially effective against Corpus shields, making the Velocitus the definitive Corpus Archwing weapon. Introduced in Update 15.2 (2014-11-13), the Velocitus is notable for unique equal-split 400 Impact/Puncture/Slash/Magnetic (1600 total); innate Magnetic devastates Corpus shields; no Mastery Rank requirement.

The Velocitus was among the earliest specialized Arch-guns, designed specifically to engage Corpus capital ships by exploiting their Magnetic-vulnerable shield systems. Its electromagnetic rail mechanism was adapted from Corpus cannon technology captured during early Archwing deployments in the Outer Terminus region.

## Acquisition

Component parts from Syndicates: Barrel from Cephalon Suda at Intriguing rank, Receiver from Steel Meridian at Valiant rank, Stock from Red Veil at Honored rank. Blueprint from Market for 15,000 credits. Build cost 30,000 credits. The Velocitus requires Mastery Rank 0 to equip.

Arch-gun weapons require either standard crafting resources, Syndicate standing for component parts, or specialized mission drops. Once crafted, equipping the Velocitus in Archwing uses the standard Arch-gun slot. To use it as a Heavy Weapon in ground missions or Railjack combat, a Gravimag must be installed. Gravimags are available from Clan Dojos via Energy Lab research, or from Arbitrations as rotation rewards.

## Variants

No Prime or Vandal variant exists. One of the original Archwing weapons from Update 15. Mastery Rank 0 requirement makes it accessible at the start of Archwing content.

The Arch-gun family spans weapons from Tenno engineering (Corvas, Fluctus, Velocitus), Grineer design (Grattler, Kuva line), and Entrati craftsmanship (Cortege, Mausolon, Morgha), each with distinct stat profiles suited to different engagement contexts.

## Recommended builds

Magnetic innate damage strips Corpus shields in a single charged shot against most fighter craft. The 400-damage-per-type profile (1600 total) makes charged shots exceptional against single high-health targets. Very small magazine (10 rounds) demands precise shot selection -- each round must count. 30% critical chance benefits from Hollowed Bullets. Best modded as a precision single-target weapon.

**Core Arch-gun mods to consider:**

- Rubedo-Lined Barrel -- +165% Arch-gun damage (primary damage mod for all builds)
- Hollowed Bullets -- +120% critical chance (essential for any crit-focused build)
- Critical Focus -- +60% critical multiplier while aiming
- Modified Munitions -- +90% status chance (for status-focused builds)
- Arch-gun Ace -- +165% fire rate (useful on slow-fire weapons to improve feel)
- Parallax Scope -- +30% critical chance while aiming
- Charged Bullets -- +60% magazine capacity
- Superior Defenses -- reduces reload time
- Conductive Coils -- adds Electricity damage
- Thermite Rounds -- adds Heat damage

Prioritize Rubedo-Lined Barrel as the first mod in any Arch-gun build. Follow with critical or status mods based on the weapon's stat profile. Elemental mods provide faction-specific damage type flexibility.

## Notes

The Velocitus operates in the Arch-gun slot, used in Archwing missions and as a Heavy Weapon on the ground via Gravimag. In Railjack (Empyrean) missions, the Arch-gun serves as the primary armament during space combat against fighters and Crewships.

Exceptional for Corpus-faction Railjack missions. Magnetic damage is particularly effective against Profit-Taker Orb during its shield phase when used as an Arch-gun via Gravimag in ground combat. One of the most specialized Arch-guns for specific faction matchups.

Archwing combat system notes: Archwing missions require completing The Archwing quest. Four Archwings are available -- Itzal (mobility/stealth), Amesha (tank/support), Elytron (AoE damage), and Odonata (balanced starter). Itzal's stealth and mobility allow repositioning between charged shots without exposing to retaliatory fire. Against Corpus fighter wings, Velocitus's Magnetic damage decimates shields before transitioning to hull damage.

The weapon's Riven disposition is 3/5 (moderate). Riven dispositions range from 0 (absolute minimum, strong weapon) to 5 (maximum, weak weapon). DE adjusts dispositions quarterly based on weapon usage rates.
