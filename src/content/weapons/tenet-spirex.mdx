## Overview

The Tenet Spirex is a Sisters of Parvos Tenet secondary requiring Mastery Rank 14. Its 200 total damage per shot is split between 140 heat, 40 impact, and 20 slash. Heat is the dominant damage type: it deals bonus damage against Infested and applies fire status procs that tick additional burn damage over time. The impact component provides stagger on proc, while slash generates bleed ticks. The heat plus impact combination is particularly effective against Corpus, where heat amplifies against flesh and impact strips shields.

The 40% status chance is the Tenet Spirex's most distinctive attribute. At 2.33 fire rate with 40% status per shot, fire procs apply reliably on nearly every other shot, generating independent burn damage that stacks. Combined with the Tenet elemental bonus (adding a second elemental type), the Spirex can trigger multiple different status effects per magazine, setting up Condition Overload synergies for melee builds or self-contained elemental proc stacking.

The 26% critical chance and 2.4x multiplier are secondary to the status focus but enable useful crit output. An 8-round magazine at 2.33 fire rate lasts about 3.4 seconds before the 2.4-second reload.

## Acquisition

Obtain through the Sisters of Parvos system at Mastery Rank 14. Create a Sister Candidate, track and vanquish the Sister to claim her Tenet Spirex. The Sister's additional elemental bonus adds to the base heat damage profile.

## Variants

No base non-Tenet counterpart or Prime version exists for the Spirex.

## Recommended builds

**Status fire stacking**: Hornet Strike, Barrel Diffusion, Galvanized Shot, Thermite Rounds, and Galvanized Aptitude. The 40% status at 2.33 fire rate produces fire procs on most shots. Fire procs stack independently; multiple stacks deal accumulating burn damage. Galvanized Shot and Galvanized Aptitude both provide stacking damage bonuses on status-affected enemies.

**Radiation Corpus**: Add electric mods to heat base for Radiation elemental combination. Radiation deals 75% bonus damage against Alloy Armor (Grineer Commanders, Corpus Techs), confuses enemies, and causes enemies to attack each other. The 40% status proc rate applies Radiation confusion reliably.

**Viral supplemental**: Replace Thermite Rounds with cold mod to create Viral from cold plus toxin. Keep heat from base damage. The Spirex then outputs heat procs and Viral from added elements, plus the Tenet bonus elemental.

## Notes

The 8-round magazine limits sustained engagements. With the semi-auto trigger, controlled shooting extends the magazine duration but the small size still requires frequent reloads in longer fights. Accuracy at 12.5 is low for a secondary -- close range is the effective engagement zone. The high heat damage makes the Spirex a natural choice for Infested and Corpus missions where heat deals bonus damage.
