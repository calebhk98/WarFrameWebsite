## Overview

Pyrana Prime is a high-impact dual-pistol secondary weapon that provides exceptional close-to-mid-range damage output and tactical flexibility. The weapon's puncture-heavy damage profile makes it surprisingly effective against armored targets for a secondary weapon class, enabling it to function as a primary weapon substitute in many mission contexts. The 26 percent critical chance and 2.6x multiplier reward headshot accuracy, while the 16 percent status chance supports status-application builds without requiring extreme modding. The five-round-per-second fire rate balances raw damage per shot with practical fire rate, avoiding the extreme spray behavior of fully automatic secondaries while maintaining impressive burst damage capacity. Pyrana Prime's rapid reload at 1.3 seconds enables reactive combat pacing that rarely leaves players vulnerable during engagement gaps.

The dual-pistol presentation creates a unique aesthetic and mechanical feel compared to single-weapon secondaries. The weapon's puncture emphasis naturally counters armor-heavy factions like the Grineer, positioning it as an excellent complement to frames lacking armor-shred capabilities. The 28-round magazine capacity exceeds many secondaries, providing extended engagement windows without requiring intermediate reloads. This combination of accessibility, damage, and practical fire rate makes Pyrana Prime an excellent secondary choice for mid-tier players and a viable endgame option when properly modded. Its role extends beyond simple backup weapon, functioning as a competent primary in many scenario types.

## Acquisition

Pyrana Prime components appear in void fissure relics, particularly Neo T2 and Axi K1 sets. The weapon's moderate age (released 2017) positions it in established void fissure rotations, making the farm reasonably efficient. Component trading through the marketplace provides rapid acquisition. The 12-hour build time integrates well into typical progression schedules.

## Variants

The original Pyrana serves as the non-prime baseline with reduced damage output. The Kuva Pyrana offers elemental customization as an alternative endgame option. Pyrana Prime bridges these variants, offering the best combination of raw power and accessibility. Each variant maintains the distinctive dual-pistol mechanics and fire rate behavior.

## Recommended builds

Barrel Diffusion provides the foundational secondary weapon damage boost and benefits the rapid-fire nature. Lethal Torrent amplifies fire rate, pushing the weapon toward higher bullet output per second. Pistol Gambit and Ravage maximize critical potential, while Hollow Point and Shallow Grave add flat damage multipliers. The 16 percent status chance supports status builds through Cold Rounds and Pathogen Rounds, creating the popular viral build for status-dependent strategies. Magazine Warp extends the 28-round capacity for extreme endurance missions, though the rapid reload mitigates the typical magazine size concerns. Single-shot effectiveness mods like Steady Hands improve accuracy during extended fire.

## Notes

Pyrana Prime excels as a complement to primary weapons focused on crowd control or utility rather than raw damage. The puncture-heavy damage enables it to handle armored targets more effectively than typical secondaries. The weapon's dual-pistol presentation creates excellent responsiveness and quick reaction times compared to larger secondary platforms. Its role as a true secondary backup weapon elevates it above weapons functioning purely as aesthetic choices, making it a practical investment for most loadout configurations. The reasonable fire rate prevents the ammo-starvation problems that plague fully automatic secondaries while maintaining impressive burst potential.
