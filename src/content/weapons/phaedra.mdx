## Overview

A rapid-fire Arch-gun emphasizing Slash damage with the second-highest fire rate among Arch-guns at 18.75 rounds per second and the largest magazine capacity at 240 rounds. Its 30% status chance enables consistent Slash bleed proc stacking that bypasses armor. Introduced in Update 17.5 (2015-10-01), the Phaedra is notable for 18.75 fire rate (second-highest among Arch-guns); 240-round magazine (largest among classic Arch-guns); 30% status for reliable Slash bleed procs.

The Phaedra was designed to sustain fire through extended deep-space engagements where ammunition resupply was impossible. Tenno engineers prioritized magazine capacity and fire rate over per-shot power, creating a weapon that excels in wars of attrition against fighter wings and fortified positions.

## Acquisition

Component parts from Syndicates: Barrel from Arbiters of Hexis at Authentic rank, Receiver from The Perrin Sequence at Senior Associate rank (also drops from Mars/Syrtis, Mercury/Caduceus, Venus/Montes Exterminate missions), Stock from New Loka at Bountiful rank. Blueprint from Market for 35,000 credits. The Phaedra requires Mastery Rank 3 to equip.

Arch-gun weapons require either standard crafting resources, Syndicate standing for component parts, or specialized mission drops. Once crafted, equipping the Phaedra in Archwing uses the standard Arch-gun slot. To use it as a Heavy Weapon in ground missions or Railjack combat, a Gravimag must be installed. Gravimags are available from Clan Dojos via Energy Lab research, or from Arbitrations as rotation rewards.

## Variants

No Prime or Vandal variant exists. High Riven disposition (4) indicates strong Riven potential for players who acquire one. One of the original mid-tier Archwing weapons.

The Arch-gun family spans weapons from Tenno engineering (Corvas, Fluctus, Velocitus), Grineer design (Grattler, Kuva line), and Entrati craftsmanship (Cortege, Mausolon, Morgha), each with distinct stat profiles suited to different engagement contexts.

## Recommended builds

30% status with Slash-dominant damage enables reliable Slash bleed proc stacking. Slash procs bypass armor, dealing true damage to health -- effective against all enemy types regardless of faction. Very high fire rate (18.75) amplifies proc accumulation speed. Adding Toxin or Heat creates Gas or Heat combinations for mixed faction scenarios. High Riven disposition (4) means Rivens provide significant stat bonuses.

**Core Arch-gun mods to consider:**

- Rubedo-Lined Barrel -- +165% Arch-gun damage (primary damage mod for all builds)
- Hollowed Bullets -- +120% critical chance (essential for any crit-focused build)
- Critical Focus -- +60% critical multiplier while aiming
- Modified Munitions -- +90% status chance (for status-focused builds)
- Arch-gun Ace -- +165% fire rate (useful on slow-fire weapons to improve feel)
- Parallax Scope -- +30% critical chance while aiming
- Charged Bullets -- +60% magazine capacity
- Superior Defenses -- reduces reload time
- Conductive Coils -- adds Electricity damage
- Thermite Rounds -- adds Heat damage

Prioritize Rubedo-Lined Barrel as the first mod in any Arch-gun build. Follow with critical or status mods based on the weapon's stat profile. Elemental mods provide faction-specific damage type flexibility.

## Notes

The Phaedra operates in the Arch-gun slot, used in Archwing missions and as a Heavy Weapon on the ground via Gravimag. In Railjack (Empyrean) missions, the Arch-gun serves as the primary armament during space combat against fighters and Crewships.

240-round magazine ensures extended Railjack engagements without reloading. High fire rate and status application make Phaedra effective in sustained crew suppression scenarios.

Archwing combat system notes: Archwing missions require completing The Archwing quest. Four Archwings are available -- Itzal (mobility/stealth), Amesha (tank/support), Elytron (AoE damage), and Odonata (balanced starter). Amesha's Warding Grace provides the survivability needed during the 5.05s reload vulnerability window. Odonata's Disarray augment synergizes with the sustained status proc output.

The weapon's Riven disposition is 4/5 (high). Riven dispositions range from 0 (absolute minimum, strong weapon) to 5 (maximum, weak weapon). DE adjusts dispositions quarterly based on weapon usage rates.
