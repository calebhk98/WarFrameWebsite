## Overview

The Phenmor is an Incarnon-evolved sniper rifle that combines precision long-range damage with tactical versatility. As an Incarnon weapon, the Phenmor can be transformed through Omnia during missions, granting it alternate fire modes and enhanced capabilities. In its base form, it fires slow, heavy rounds with exceptional crit multiplier and puncture-slash split damage. The weapon shines in missions where you can position safely at range and pick off high-value targets with deliberate, powerful shots.

The Phenmor's transformation mechanic makes it unique among sniper rifles. Players can toggle between its standard configuration and an alternate form mid-combat, adapting to shifting engagement distances and enemy compositions. This flexibility elevates it beyond traditional sniper rifles that demand static positioning and rewards tactical decision-making about when to transform.

## Damage and variants

In its base form, the Phenmor deals pure puncture and slash damage with no elemental component. This makes it adaptable to any elemental build direction without inherent bias. Adding Serration plus Split Chamber provides raw single-target devastation; adding elemental mods opens paths to viral-slash or corrosive hybrid builds suitable for Steel Path. The puncture weighting (70 percent) makes the weapon particularly effective against armor, while the 30 percent slash component grants armor-stripping potential when viral is applied.

When transformed via Incarnon evolution, the Phenmor gains alternate fire capabilities and increased ammo efficiency, making it viable for longer engagements without relying on ammo pickups. The transformed version typically provides sustained damage output improvements and QoL benefits like faster reload or ammo efficiency per WFCD documentation. The transformation is triggered by leveling the weapon during void fissure missions, accumulating Zariman void insights that eventually unlock permanent Incarnon form availability in all missions.

## Acquisition

The Phenmor blueprint and components drop from Zariman Ten Zero void missions in rotation A and B rewards. Expected farm time is 5-10 void fissure runs with proper void relic refinement. Crafting requires 39000 credits and moderate primary weapon resources, making it accessible once mastery rank 14 is achieved.

Incarnon transformation materials are obtained through continued void fissure farming or can be purchased with void cascade rewards from the Zariman tileset's primary puzzle encounter. The resource investment is significant but manageable for dedicated players.

## Recommended builds

Phenmor builds divide into two categories: crit-focused and status-focused. A crit build stacks Serration, Point Strike, and Vital Sense, pushing the crit multiplier to 4-5x and landing near 80-90 percent crit chance with melee-applicable mods like Tactical Reload. Paired with elemental mods (Cryo Rounds, Thermite Rounds), this generates reliable procs while maintaining headshot one-shots on most units below level 60. The advantage of crit-focused builds is raw burst damage; landing a single headshot crit on a heavy unit effectively kills it instantly, making assassination targets trivial.

For Steel Path and Arbitrations, a hybrid approach using Serration, Split Chamber, Vital Sense, and Cold/Toxin mods (for Viral scaling) outperforms pure crit because the sustained damage from viral procs on missed/non-lethal shots improves reliability. Adding mods like Tactical Reload or Primed Chamber increases ammo sustain and first-shot burst damage. This hybrid philosophy prioritizes consistency over peak damage, enabling steady performance across varying enemy compositions and engagement ranges.

A pure status build replacing Point Strike with Weeping Wounds reaches 100+ percent status chance, applying viral procs so frequently that armor becomes irrelevant within seconds. This approach pairs excellently with frames that reward sustained DoT, such as Saryn with Toxic Lash or Mirage with damage multiplication.

## Synergies

The Phenmor synergizes with any frame that extends range or improves positioning. Mesa's ability buffs shooting speed and crit damage, amplifying the Phenmor's already-high multiplier. Ivara's cloaking and tactical lens mods enable sneaky long-range plays where precision kills feel impactful. Hildryn with a Primed Fury mod setup provides survivability while the Phenmor handles damage.

Companion weapons with synergistic damage profiles (secondary with elemental damage matching the Phenmor's mod build) stack procs effectively. A Companion set to attack aerial enemies keeps ground enemies grouped, making Phenmor follow-up shots simpler.

## Lore

The Phenmor is among the first wave of Incarnon weapons, tools of the New War that evolved from past combat gear into something unprecedented. The Incarnon evolution feature reflects the Void's influence on ancient Tenno weaponry, granting them adaptive capabilities unknown to conventional arms. Phenmor's designation suggests a legacy name preserved through transformation, tying it to the broader Incarnon quest line and the Void fissure phenomenon.

Sniper rifles in the Warframe universe represent precision, patience, and calculated strikes. The Phenmor modernizes this archetype through Incarnon evolution, making it a bridge between traditional long-range damage and the adaptive, transformative weaponry of the post-New War era.
