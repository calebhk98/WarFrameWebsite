## Overview

Ekwana Jai is a Zaw link component available from Hok's Anvil in Cetus on the Plains of Eidolon. As a Zaw component, the Ekwana Jai does not have standalone combat statistics; its contribution to the finished weapon depends on the full three-part Zaw assembly (Strike, Grip, and Link). The Ekwana Jai link component provides specific critical chance and status chance bonuses that modify the base Strike values in the final assembled Zaw.

Link components are the third slot in Zaw construction. They fine-tune the balance between critical chance, status chance, and other secondary weapon attributes. Selecting the Ekwana Jai over other link options pushes the assembled Zaw toward a particular stat weighting; preview the assembled stats with Hok before committing Ostron standing to the purchase.

## Stats

The Ekwana Jai reports zero standalone stats in WFCD because it is an unassembled component, not a combat-ready weapon. Final assembled stats depend on the Strike and Grip selections. Contact Hok at Cetus with at least Visitor-rank Ostron standing to purchase and preview assembled configurations.

## Acquisition

Purchase from Hok's Anvil at Cetus on the Plains of Eidolon. Ostron standing is required; higher-rank link components unlock at higher Ostron tiers. Gifting Nakak with fish, mining gems, or bounty rewards earns Ostron standing toward the required threshold.

## Variants

Zaws are inherently modular; the Ekwana Jai link can be paired with any compatible Strike and Grip combination. Each combination yields a distinct Zaw with unique base stats. No Prime or separate Kuva variant of Ekwana Jai exists.

## Recommended builds

Determine the target weapon type (dagger, scythe, sword, etc.) from the Strike selection first, then choose the Grip for desired attack speed, and finally select Ekwana Jai as the Link if its critical/status weighting matches the intended build goal. Consult the Fandom wiki Zaw calculator to preview final assembled stats before purchasing.
