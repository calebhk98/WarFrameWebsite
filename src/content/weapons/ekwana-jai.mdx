## Overview

Ekwana Jai is a Zaw Link component available from Hok's Anvil in Cetus on the Plains of Eidolon. As a Zaw component, the Ekwana Jai does not have standalone combat statistics; its contribution to the finished weapon depends on the full three-part Zaw assembly (Strike, Grip, and Link). The "Jai" naming convention in the Ekwana Link family indicates a bias toward critical chance in the stat modifiers applied to the assembled Zaw. WFCD reports zeros for all standalone stats because the item is an unassembled component, not a combat-ready weapon.

Link components are the third slot in Zaw construction, added after the Strike and Grip are selected. They fine-tune the balance between critical chance and status chance in the assembled configuration. Selecting the Ekwana Jai over a Ruhang-family Link pushes the assembled Zaw toward higher critical chance at the expense of lower status chance. This tradeoff is meaningful: critical-biased assembled Zaws scale better with Blood Rush (combo-scaling critical chance) and Organ Shatter (critical multiplier amplification), while status-biased Zaws benefit more from Weeping Wounds and Condition Overload.

Previewing the assembled stats within Hok's interface before committing Ostron Standing to the purchase is the recommended approach. The preview shows exact numerical values for the assembled Zaw, allowing direct comparison between Link options without guessing.

## Acquisition

Purchase from Hok's Anvil at Cetus on the Plains of Eidolon. Ostron Standing is required; higher-tier Link components unlock at higher Ostron community rank. Earn Ostron Standing through bounty completion, fishing, and mining in the Plains of Eidolon. Check [the Ekwana Jai Fandom wiki page](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Ekwana_Jai) for the exact Ostron rank requirement and standing cost.

## Variants

Zaws are inherently modular; the Ekwana Jai Link can be paired with any compatible Strike and Grip combination. Each combination yields a distinct assembled Zaw with unique base stats. The Ekwana Link family includes Ekwana, Ekwana Jai, Ekwana II Jai, Ekwana Jai II, Ekwana Ruhang, Ekwana II Ruhang, and Ekwana Ruhang II -- each offering different critical and status weightings. No Prime or Kuva version of any Zaw component exists.

## Recommended builds

When incorporating the Ekwana Jai Link into a Zaw build:

- Select a Strike first to establish weapon archetype (dagger, scythe, heavy blade, etc.) and base damage type weighting.
- Select a Grip to determine weapon category and swing speed; heavy Grips favor high damage per swing, light Grips favor faster attack.
- Add Ekwana Jai as the Link to apply its critical chance bias to the assembled Zaw.
- Preview the final assembled stats in Hok's interface before purchasing.
- For critical-biased assembled Zaws, equip Blood Rush, Organ Shatter, and Sacrificial Steel to maximize crit output through combo scaling.
- Gild the assembled Zaw at Mastery Rank 8 or higher to unlock Arcane slots.

## Synergies

Critical-biased Zaws assembled with Ekwana Jai benefit from Naramon's Power Spike focus school, which extends combo duration for sustained Blood Rush scaling. Wukong's combo-preservation passive further sustains high-combo phases. Frames that apply enemy vulnerability (Rhino, Wisp) amplify critical hit damage from the assembled Zaw's elevated crit chance.
