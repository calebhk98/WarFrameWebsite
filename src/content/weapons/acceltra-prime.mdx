## Overview

The Acceltra Prime stands as one of the fastest-firing launcher platforms in the Warframe arsenal. This projectile-based weapon combines rocket acceleration technology with explosive area damage, excelling in crowd elimination and boss phases. The weapon's exceptional fire rate at 10 rounds per second allows rapid target acquisition and eliminate multiple objectives in sequence. Unlike conventional slow-firing launchers, Acceltra Prime enables aggressive playstyle through sustained high-volume rocket output. The weapon balances raw projectile speed with impact detonation mechanics, creating engagement flexibility across varied content. Its 34% critical chance and 3x multiplier enable high-burst critical builds for maximum damage optimization.

## Stats

Acceltra Prime delivers 44 impact damage per rocket impact, with explosion radius providing secondary area damage to nearby targets. Fire rate of 10 rounds per second sustains continuous rocket stream, requiring proper ammo management through extended encounters. Magazine capacity of 48 rockets supports extended suppression fire before reload becomes necessary. Critical chance of 34% compounds with crit mods into exceptional burst opportunities, while 3x critical multiplier transforms lucky shots into massive single-target elimination. Status chance of 18% enables reliable proc application when paired with elemental damage mods.

## Acquisition

Acceltra Prime components distribute across multiple Lith void relics (Lith A2, Lith M3, Lith T4), making early-phase void fissure farming the primary acquisition path. Lith relic availability ensures relatively frequent component acquisition compared to later-era relic pools. Each component requires 6 forma in build construction, consuming approximately 18 hours total crafting time. Materials include 6,000 ferrite and 600 plastids per component, requiring resource farming alongside relic opening. Unvaulted status maintains market availability for trading-focused players prioritizing platinum-based acquisition over time investment.

## Damage and Performance

The impact-only damage model concentrates force into explosive radius effects, making the weapon exceptionally effective against clustered enemies and objectives. Explosion radius extends effective engagement distance beyond hitscan limitations, enabling accurate fire even against fast-moving targets. The 34% crit chance baseline combines with Point Strike and Vital Sense to enable consistent critical hits, particularly valuable for boss-phase focus-fire scenarios. Status application through elemental mods enables Blast procs for guaranteed knockdown, significantly improving crowd control relative to raw damage alternatives.

## Mod Recommendations

Serration and Heavy Caliber provide foundational raw damage scaling essential for competitive DPS output. Point Strike and Vital Sense enable critical build approaches, where 34% base crit chance achieves 65%+ with mod stacking. Multishot mods like Hell's Chamber amplify rocket count, effectively scaling damage linearly. Elemental damage mods contribute to explosion scaling, particularly Heat for area denial and Blast for crowd control. Magazine extension through Ammo Chain enables sustained suppression fire during endurance content.

## Builds and Synergies

Acceltra Prime pairs exceptionally well with Mesa's Peacemakers for synchronized high-damage output phases. Mirage's Hall of Mirrors multiplies rocket output through reflected projectiles, creating exponential damage scaling. Volt's Speed buff accelerates reload animations, partially mitigating reload downtime. Saryn's Spores create additional explosion triggers through impact detonations, stacking status effects dramatically. Trinity's Energy Vampire sustains continuous fire through energy management, enabling unlimited rocket suppression.

## Performance Notes

The weapon's exceptional fire rate creates unique ammo consumption patterns, requiring frequent Heavy Ammo farming or Ammo Chain mods to sustain endurance content. Projectile travel time necessitates leading mobile targets, increasing skill ceiling for consistent accuracy. However, explosion radius provides forgiving aim, enabling area-based targeting when precision becomes challenging. The weapon excels in open-area missions where enemy clusters justify explosive area damage applications.

The weapon emerged during periods demanding overwhelming suppressive fire against entrenched positions, favoring volume over precision targeting. Prime-grade construction enhances acceleration efficiency, enabling unprecedented fire rates unmatched by modern manufactured alternatives.
