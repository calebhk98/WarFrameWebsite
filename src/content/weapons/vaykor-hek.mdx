## Overview

The Vaykor Hek is the Steel Meridian-enhanced variant of the Hek shotgun, requiring Mastery Rank 12. Its 75 damage per pellet divides into 48.75 slash, 15.0 puncture, and 11.25 impact -- slash-dominant, unlike the base Hek which is more evenly spread. The slash weighting generates bleed procs against any enemy type. As a shotgun, the Vaykor Hek fires multiple pellets per shot, and each pellet rolls its own crit and status chances.

The 25% critical chance per pellet, with multishot producing many pellets per trigger pull, gives the Vaykor Hek a high aggregate crit output. The 2.0x multiplier is baseline for shotguns. The 10.7% status chance per pellet is moderate, but with 5-7 pellets per shot from multishot mods, aggregate status procs become frequent. The 8-round magazine at 3.0 fire rate provides about 2.7 seconds of sustained fire.

The Vaykor Hek includes the Justice Syndicate effect, granting an area blast and healing on kills, which is the Steel Meridian Syndicate's signature proc. This passive supports aggressive front-line combat in Steel Meridian missions.

## Acquisition

The Vaykor Hek requires Steel Meridian Syndicate rank and standing. Purchase directly from the Steel Meridian vendor at any Relay. No crafting is required.

## Variants

**Hek** -- The base shotgun from the Market.

**Corinth Prime** -- An unrelated shotgun of the prime tier but in the same shotgun category.

## Recommended builds

**Viral shotgun**: Chilling Grasp (cold) plus Contagion (toxin, or any toxin mod) for Viral. Viral halves enemy max health. With multishot and the 10.7% per-pellet status chance, Viral procs apply within the first trigger pull. Point Strike raises the 25% base crit for more reliable orange crits.

**Critical slash**: Point Strike, Vital Sense, Serration, Shotgun Savant (if Primed), Hell's Chamber, and two elemental mods. The 25% base crit with Point Strike and multiple pellets from Hell's Chamber produces consistent crits on several pellets per shot. Slash bleeds from the dominant slash type bypass armor.

**Justice passive build**: Steel Meridian members can exploit the Justice proc with warframes that have sustain ability needs. The healing on kill rewards aggressive close-range combat with the Justice passive active.

## Notes

Shotgun accuracy at 9.1 means significant pellet spread -- the Vaykor Hek is a close-to-medium-range weapon. At long range, pellets scatter such that only a fraction impacts the target. Use within 20-30 meters for consistent pellet cluster impact. The 8-round magazine with a 2.25-second reload is manageable for a shotgun. Heavy Caliber does not apply to shotguns -- use the shotgun-specific damage mods (Ravage, Shotgun Spazz).
