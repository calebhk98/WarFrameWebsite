## Overview

The Fang Prime is the Prime variant of the Fang dual-dagger melee with a Slash-dominant damage profile: 124.6 Slash versus 26.7 each of Impact and Puncture. The equal 26% critical chance and 26% status chance make the Fang Prime a genuine hybrid platform, scaling meaningfully in both crit and status paths simultaneously with Blood Rush and Weeping Wounds. The 2.6x critical multiplier amplifies orange-crit hits during Blood Rush phases. Attack speed of 1.0 and 1.75 m range are standard for dual daggers. Mastery Rank 10 is required.

Dual-dagger weapons use stance mods (Gnashing Payara, Brutal Tide) that deliver fast, multi-hit combo animations. The Slash dominance combined with 26% status ensures bleed procs apply consistently, and at higher combo levels with Weeping Wounds active, proc rates reach sufficient thresholds for Condition Overload's full damage multiplier. The Fang Prime's equal 26%/26% stat distribution is one of the most balanced hybrid profiles among dual-dagger weapons, making it a versatile choice rather than a narrow specialist in one direction. The 1.75 m range is the primary limitation; enemy-grouping frame abilities maximize multi-target hit count at this short range.

## Variants

The base Fang is the standard version, available at Mastery Rank 0. The Fang Prime significantly improves all stats, including critical multiplier and status chance. No Kuva or Tenet variant exists for the Fang family.

## Recommended builds

**Blood Rush/Weeping Wounds hybrid build.** Equip Blood Rush, Weeping Wounds, Organ Shatter, Primed Pressure Point, Condition Overload, and two Viral 60/60 mods. At x4 combo, both crit and status scale simultaneously. Orange crits from Blood Rush combined with Condition Overload's proc-type multiplier create strong sustained DPS. The Slash base generates bleed procs alongside elemental procs for Condition Overload stacking.

**Pure crit build.** At 26% base crit with Blood Rush and Sacrificial Steel, the effective crit chance reaches orange-crit territory at x4 combo. Equip Organ Shatter for the 2.6x multiplier amplification. Add two Viral 60/60 mods for health-damage amplification. Berserker Fury increases attack speed after kills, compounding combo generation.

**Slash/Condition Overload status build.** Prioritize Weeping Wounds and Condition Overload with four 60/60 elemental mods for Corrosive + Viral. The 26% base status with Weeping Wounds at x4 combo reaches very high proc rates. Condition Overload multiplies per-hit damage as proc types accumulate; the Slash base adds bleed procs alongside elemental procs.

## Synergies

Khora's Strangledome groups enemies within reach of the 1.75 m range for multi-target combo generation. Naramon's Power Spike extends combo duration, sustaining Blood Rush and Weeping Wounds scaling without losing the combo multiplier between waves. Wukong's passive combo preservation complements the aggressive close-quarters engagement required at 1.75 m range.

## Notes

- The 1.75 m range is short; stay in close quarters and use enemy-grouping abilities to maximize hit count per swing.
- The equal 26% crit and status provides genuine flexibility; choose Blood Rush or Weeping Wounds as the primary scaling mod based on the target faction.
