## Overview

The Vastilok is a Corpus gunblade that fires ricocheting energy shots via alternate fire while functioning as a melee weapon in primary use. Its 279 total melee damage (19.5 impact, 234.4 puncture, 25.1 slash) is defined by extremely high puncture, which reduces armor mitigation against Grineer targets more effectively than balanced physical damage. The 31% status chance is the Vastilok's most prominent feature, enabling reliable elemental proc application to prime enemies for damage amplification.

Gunblades function differently from standard melees: normal attacks fire an energy shot rather than a traditional swing, while the ground slam produces a visible melee arc. Players familiar with other gunblades like the Redeemer will find the mechanics immediately recognizable. The ricocheting shot can connect with multiple enemies in enclosed tilesets.

The 19% critical chance and 1.7x multiplier are modest; the Vastilok rewards status-focused play more than critical stacking. Condition Overload synergizes strongly with the 31% status chance, multiplying damage for each unique status type active on a target.

## Acquisition

The Vastilok blueprint is available from the in-game Market. Build time is 12 hours. No quest or event unlock is required.

## Variants

No Prime, Vandal, or Wraith variant exists for the Vastilok. It is a standalone Corpus-themed gunblade without enhanced counterparts in the current WFCD dataset.

## Recommended builds

**Status-Condition Overload build**: Equip Condition Overload (damage per unique status), Weeping Wounds (status chance on combo), Primed Pressure Point, and elemental mods for corrosive (electric + toxin) or viral (cold + toxin). With three unique status types active, Condition Overload triples the weapon's damage output.

**Heavy attack build**: Use Killing Blow, Corrupt Charge (start at x12 combo), Organ Shatter, and Sacrificial Steel. Each heavy attack deals 1395 base damage before mods, amplified dramatically by the combo multiplier.

## Notes

The gunblade shot from normal attacks travels and can ricochet off surfaces in enclosed mission tiles, hitting multiple enemies. Gunblade-specific stances are limited; confirm the available stance mod from the WARFRAME Wiki or in-game codex.


The Vastilok's gunblade mechanics make it unique in the melee category. Unlike conventional melee weapons, the normal attack fires a projectile rather than performing a traditional melee swing. This means the Vastilok can engage targets at ranges beyond its 1.0-meter effective melee range using its shot. The shot applies melee damage type and critical stats, scaling with melee mods rather than firearm mods. Players who master the shot timing and positioning leverage the Vastilok's full potential across both melee and ranged engagement distances.

At Mastery Rank 9, the Vastilok is available to mid-game players who have progressed through the standard Corpus mission nodes. Its Corpus aesthetic and gunblade format fit players who prefer unique hybrid weapon playstyles over conventional primary-plus-melee loadouts.
