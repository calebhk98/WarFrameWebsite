## Overview

The Lanka is a Corpus charge-type beam sniper that fires a concentrated energy beam with devastating impact. Despite missing fire rate documentation in WFCD database, it is well-known in-game for efficient charge mechanics and high single-shot damage output. The weapon specializes in precise, long-range engagements and serves excellently for anti-armor applications.

The Lanka's 25% status chance supports hybrid builds combining raw damage with elemental effects for diverse applications. The weapon's core identity revolves around charge mechanics: holding down the trigger accumulates energy before releasing a devastatingly powerful shot. This mechanic encourages planning and positioning rather than continuous trigger pulling. Players must wait for charge completion before firing, creating distinct gameplay rhythm compared to traditional rapid-fire weapons.

Building the Lanka emphasizes raw damage through mods like Serration, Heavy Caliber, and Cosmetic to maximize each charged shot. The weapon's 10 mastery requirement provides accessibility early in player progression. Experienced players recognize the Lanka's potential in high-level content when built properly and used by players with precise aim. The charge mechanic rewards patience and map awareness, allowing players to prepare powerful shots while sheltered from direct fire.

The Lanka rewards mastery of charge mechanics and careful timing. Players must anticipate enemy movement and ensure clear sightlines before committing to charged shots. The weapon's unique gameplay loop makes it appealing to veterans seeking alternatives to traditional hitscan weapons.

The weapon's pure beam nature without projectile travel time distinguishes it from other snipers. Beams fire instantly upon release, eliminating projectile travel time calculations and enabling snap-shooting when enemies appear. This instant-hit nature pairs well with frame abilities that disable or slow enemies, maximizing charge-shot consistency.

Despite lower fire rate making it demanding in fast-paced missions, the Lanka excels in controlled engagements.
