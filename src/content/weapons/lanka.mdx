## Overview

Lanka is a Corpus-designed sniper rifle that fires a fully charged electromagnetic beam rather than a conventional projectile. The weapon requires the player to hold the trigger to accumulate charge before releasing a single high-damage shot. All damage is delivered as electricity, making Lanka one of the rare primary weapons with a pure elemental damage profile -- 525 electricity with zero physical damage components. This base electricity damage combines with a second elemental mod to form a combined damage type, such as Radiation (Electricity + Heat) for use against Alloy Armor or Magnetic (Electricity + Cold) for Corpus shields.

Lanka is closely associated with Eidolon hunting on the Plains of Eidolon. Sentient units and Eidolons are vulnerable to Radiation damage and have reduced effectiveness against Void damage, making Lanka's electricity base an efficient starting point for constructing Radiation builds. For this reason, Lanka is frequently cited as the preferred Eidolon-hunting primary weapon alongside Corinth and Rubico Prime alternatives.

The charge mechanic distinguishes Lanka's gameplay from bolt-action sniper rifles like Rubico or Vectis. Players must anticipate enemy movement, time the charge release, and commit to the shot before the enemy repositions. This creates a deliberate, positioning-focused cadence that rewards map awareness and target prediction over reaction speed.

Lanka has no fire rate entry in WFCD because its output is charge-dependent rather than a repeating cycle. The effective fire rate is determined by how quickly a player completes the charge animation, which scales with Charged Chamber mods rather than fire rate mods.

The 525 electricity base damage is substantial for a single-shot weapon, and adding a Heat mod produces Radiation damage at the full combined total. With Serration, Split Chamber, and a critical chance mod, Lanka can deliver very high single-shot damage values at full charge. The 20% critical chance is reliable with critical-focused builds and scales to consistent crits under Primed Sniper Scavenger's bonus.

## Acquisition

The Lanka blueprint is available from the in-game Market for credits. Components use standard resources obtainable through mid-tier star chart progression. Mastery Rank 10 is required to equip Lanka. No Void Relic farming is required; the weapon is fully accessible through solo play and credit accumulation. Construction takes place in the Foundry and requires standard build time.

## Variants

No Prime, Vandal, or Kuva variant of the Lanka has been released. Lanka exists in a single version. The Arca Plasmor and Opticor are thematically similar charge-type primary weapons but function differently and occupy different statistical niches.

## Recommended builds

An Eidolon hunting build centers on Primed Cryo Rounds or Primed Charged Shell combined with Hellfire to produce Radiation damage. Serration, Split Chamber, and Vigilante Armaments add base damage and multishot. Critical Delay boosts critical chance at the cost of fire rate -- which is largely irrelevant on a charge weapon -- providing a net positive trade. A scope zoom mod like Ambush Optics extends effective engagement range for distant Eidolon synovias.

A general-purpose sniper build for star chart content replaces Radiation mods with Corrosive (Electricity + Toxin) to penetrate Grineer armor. Argon Scope provides substantial critical chance on headshots, rewarding precise aim against stationary or slow-moving targets.

Players running Lanka in squad Eidolon hunts should coordinate elemental damage types with squadmates to maximize damage against specific Eidolon limb types.

## Notes

Lanka's charge-type mechanic means that fire rate mods have no effect on its shooting rhythm. Charged Chamber reduces the time required to reach full charge, effectively increasing practical fire rate and rewarding its use over standard fire rate mods. Zoom level affects both critical chance and headshot multiplier on Lanka through the sniper zoom mechanic, incentivizing players to engage from maximum effective range.

Because Lanka's damage is purely electrical, it does not benefit from physical damage type interactions such as slash bleed procs. Builds relying on Slash for damage-over-time against armored targets should use a different platform.
