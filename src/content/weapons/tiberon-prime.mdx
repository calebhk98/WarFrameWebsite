## Overview

The Tiberon Prime represents a premium precision rifle combining exceptional damage output with flexible firing modes and robust critical scaling. It features a unique semi-automatic to burst fire toggle mechanic, allowing users to switch between measured single shots and aggressive three-round bursts depending on tactical circumstances. The weapon's high slash component of 104 points ensures consistent proccing of bleeding effects on lighter enemies while maintaining respectable performance against armored targets. The 3.0x critical multiplier rivals the Baza Prime in scaling potential, though the lower fire rate of five rounds per second creates a more deliberate playstyle emphasizing accuracy over suppressive volume. The weapon's Mastery Rank 14 requirement positions it as an endgame primary for players seeking versatile, consistent performance.

## Damage

The Tiberon Prime's damage profile heavily emphasizes slash at 104 points, making it optimized for bleed proc chains on grouped targets. Impact at 39 and puncture at 19.5 provide balanced physical damage across armor types. The 3.0x critical multiplier enables significant scaling through critical-focused mods, transforming the 28% base critical chance into a reliable critical-strike platform. The 20% status chance supports elemental and physical status builds, though critical scaling typically dominates optimization paths. The lower fire rate compared to other primaries means each shot carries greater weight, rewarding precise trigger discipline.

## Acquisition

Tiberon Prime components drop from Axi T2 and Axi T3 void relics as common rewards during void fissure void fissure missions. The blueprint construction requires 30,000 credits and twelve hours in the Foundry after gathering all components. The Prime remains unvaulted and regularly available through current fissure rotations. Trading of Tiberon Prime blueprints and components occurs regularly in the player market, providing alternative acquisition paths for those preferring platinum investment over relic farming.

## Variants

The standard Tiberon exists as a non-prime variant with reduced damage and critical multiplier (2.8x instead of 3.0x). A Zenith variant exists as a reward from reaching Mastery Rank 30, featuring special projectile mechanics. The Tiberon Prime represents the most balanced and powerful standard variant. No Kuva variant currently exists, though high-level riven disposition allows riven builds to improve endgame scaling.

## Recommended builds

Critical-focused modding maximizes the Tiberon Prime's potential. Serration and Vital Sense form the core damage amplification, while Point Strike elevates critical chance above 50%. Shred provides through-shot penetration synergizing with the semi-burst mechanics. Heavy Caliber reduces accuracy but the weapon's loose aim tolerance absorbs the penalty. Magazine extension through Tactical Reload or Increased magazine capacity overcomes the tight 40-round capacity during sustained engagements. Forma investment unlocks Umbral mod slots for superior endgame scaling with Umbral Intensify and other Umbral variants.

## Synergies

Frames benefiting from critical bonuses or semi-automatic weapon mechanics pair excellently with the Tiberon Prime. Harrow's invulnerability phases sync perfectly with the weapon's methodical fire rate, enabling guaranteed critical strikes. Rhino's roar applies directly to weapon damage, amplifying both normal and critical strikes. Ash benefits from the weapon's burst mechanics when integrating gunplay with melee combo chains. Volt's speed buff makes the lower fire rate feel more responsive while improving overall mission completion time.

## Lore

The Tiberon Prime originates from Orokin-era weapon refinement, its toggle fire mechanism reflecting Old War battlefield versatility. The design suggests weapons that adapted to multiple engagement ranges, combining precision with suppressive capability. Its particular effectiveness against biological targets hints at counter-development against ancient threats now lost to time. Within the player community, the Tiberon Prime represents a refined approach to assault rifle design, balancing damage, accuracy, and tactical flexibility in a single platform.
