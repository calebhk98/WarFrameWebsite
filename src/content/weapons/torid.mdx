## Overview

The Torid is an Incarnon primary weapon specializing in toxin damage and status application. This rifle excels in missions requiring crowd control and sustained damage over time. The extremely high status chance of 36% guarantees toxin procs on nearly every shot, allowing even unmodded fire to poison enemies effectively. As an Incarnon weapon, it evolves through use in void fissure activities, unlocking additional mechanics and visual upgrades. The weapon's slow fire rate suits tactical gameplay where controlled bursts outperform spray-and-pray approaches.

## Damage

The Torid's damage distribution emphasizes slash and toxin. Slash damage at 7 is the highest component, making the weapon particularly effective against flesh-heavy enemies. Inherent toxin damage of 8 combines with high status chance to create reliable poison application. Impact and puncture round out coverage against armor and shields. The low critical stats suggest the weapon is designed for status damage scaling rather than crit-heavy builds. Poison damage-over-time stacks multiplicatively with additional applications, making the weapon devastating when combined with status mods.

## Acquisition

The Torid is farmed exclusively from Zariman Ten Zero void fissure missions. Players must accumulate void traces and open relics containing Torid components. The blueprint requires 15,000 credits and 12 hours of crafting time. Unlike market weapons, Torid acquisition rewards engagement with endgame content, positioning it as a milestone weapon for veterans. Multiple runs typically supply components for several copies, allowing players to forma-farm or trade duplicates.

## Variants

The Torid Prime exists as an upgraded variant with enhanced stats and a higher disposition. Torid Prime is vaulted as of 2024 but remains obtainable through relic farming of unvaulted rotations during anniversary void fissure events. No non-prime variants exist.

## Recommended builds

Torid builds revolve around status application and condition overload synergy. Mods like Virulent Scourge and Toxic Barrage amplify base toxin damage while increasing status chance. Dual stat elemental mods combining toxin with heat or electricity create powerful proc chains. Multishot mods like Split Chamber increase proc frequency, essential for status-focused gameplay. Magazine size mods ensure sustained fire without frequent reloads. In squad-based builds, coordinating with condition overload melee users amplifies overall damage output. The weapon scales poorly with critical mods due to low crit chance; status investment yields better returns.

## Notes

Incarnon evolution unlocks at mastery rank 5, providing a gameplay unlock that encourages progression. The evolution process requires dedication to Zariman content but grants visual and mechanical improvements. The weapon's accuracy penalty of 15.6% is offset by its slow fire rate and magazine size, making spray patterns manageable. Torid fills a niche role as a pure status-damage weapon, excelling in team compositions that abuse condition overload mechanics. Solo players benefit less from status stacking but can still leverage poison damage-over-time for wave clearing. The weapon remains underutilized in public squads despite its potential, making it a choice pick for theorycrafters exploring status synergies.

## Playstyle and synergies

The Torid shines in coordinated squads leveraging condition overload mechanics. Melee teammates applying high-damage weapons benefit exponentially from status stacking. The weapon's slow fire rate encourages tactical positioning and careful target selection rather than spray-and-pray gameplay. Experienced players use Torid as a support weapon in endgame missions, softening high-priority targets for teammates while maintaining map control through poison application.
