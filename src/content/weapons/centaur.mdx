## Overview

The [Centaur](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Centaur) is an Archwing melee weapon used exclusively in Archwing missions, Empyrean (Railjack) missions, and Sanctuary Onslaught (Archwing variant). It deals 376 total damage per attack with a heavy puncture emphasis (263.2 puncture, 75.2 slash, 37.6 impact). The 25% critical chance and 2.0x multiplier support functional critical builds for an Archwing melee weapon.

Archwing melee weapons function differently from standard melee: attacks are delivered in broad sweeps in zero-gravity environments, and combo mechanics operate without stances. The Centaur's 376 total damage is competitive in Archwing content, where enemy scaling and engagement ranges differ significantly from ground missions.

At Mastery Rank 8, the Centaur targets players who have progressed into Archwing content -- accessible after unlocking the Archwing flight segment.

## Acquisition

The Centaur blueprint is available from the Market for credits. Crafting requires standard components. As a permanently available Market weapon, it is not vaulted and can be built at any progression stage after unlocking Archwing.

## Variants

No Prime, Vandal, or Wraith variant of the Centaur exists. Players who want Archwing melee alternatives should compare the Veritux, Elytron Melee, and Prisma Veritux for different stat profiles. Prisma variants offer generally superior performance.

## Recommended builds

**Critical Archwing build:** Primed Reach, Astral Slash, Vicious Spiral, and Buzzing Strike. Archwing melee mods differ from ground melee -- the Archwing-specific critical mods (Vicious Spiral, Astral Slash) replace Blood Rush and Organ Shatter. At 25% critical chance, Vicious Spiral pushes the Centaur into reliable critical hit territory during sustained Archwing combat.

**Status build:** Equip Archwing-specific elemental mods targeting the primary enemy faction in the mission. Puncture's 20% critical chance combined with 20% status chance supports a balanced approach. Against Grineer Archwing enemies, adding corrosive or heat improves effective damage output.

**Heavy attack burst:** Archwing heavy attacks deliver wide-arc sweeps that hit multiple enemies in zero-gravity. Combine damage mods with the Centaur's generous 376 base damage for burst eliminations against grouped Archwing enemies.

## Synergies

- **Itzal Archwing:** Itzal's Cosmic Crush gathers nearby enemies into a cluster. Centaur heavy attacks during Cosmic Crush hit all gathered targets simultaneously.
- **Aviator mod:** Reduces damage taken while airborne in Archwing mode. Pairing survivability mods with the Centaur allows extended close-quarters engagement against enemy Archwing units.
- **Railjack crew missions:** In Empyrean missions, Archwing is used for ship boarding and exterior combat. The Centaur's high per-hit damage is effective against boarding targets.

## Notes

- Archwing melee has no stance mod system. Combat is guided by the player's aim direction in zero-gravity, delivering sweeping attacks rather than combo-based sequences.
- The Centaur's puncture dominance (70% of total damage) favors encounters with Ferrite-armored enemies. In Archwing content, Grineer fighters have Alloy Armor, where puncture also deals favorable bonus damage.
- Mastery Rank 8 requirement is moderate. Players typically unlock Archwing content earlier in progression, but the Centaur provides a meaningful upgrade from the starter Archwing melee options.
- Archwing weapons do not benefit from ground melee mods. Ensure all equipped mods are Archwing-compatible (visible in the Archwing weapon mod screen) before building.
