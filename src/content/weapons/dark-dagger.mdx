## Overview

The Dark Dagger is a Tenno melee dagger with an unusual damage profile: it deals no Impact damage, splitting instead between Puncture (36), Slash (58), and a built-in Radiation element (60). The innate Radiation damage makes it immediately effective against Alloy Armor enemies such as Grineer Heavy Gunners and Corrupted Bombards, without requiring elemental mod investment. At Mastery Rank 2, it is accessible to early-game players who want elemental priming capability in the melee slot.

Status chance sits at 22%, which is serviceable for applying the built-in Radiation proc alongside additional elemental combinations from mods. Critical chance is low at 8%, discouraging crit-focused builds. The 0.92 attack speed and 1.75 m range are typical for dagger class weapons. The Dagger stance mods (Pointed Wind, Homing Fang) modify the combo moveset and should be equipped to maximise damage per combo step.

## Variants

The Dark Dagger has no Prime, Kuva, or Tenet variant. The base version is the only option. Its sister weapon the Dark Sword shares the Dark weapon aesthetic and the same Radiation element, filling the single-handed sword slot.

## Recommended builds

**Radiation/status build.** The innate Radiation element counts as one elemental type, freeing mod slots. Add Primed Fever Strike and Voltaic Strike to layer Viral or Magnetic depending on the target faction. Fill with Pressure Point, Fury, and Organ Shatter for scaling.

**Corrosive-focused build.** Replace one 60/60 mod pairing with Infected Clip and Voltaic Strike to produce Corrosive, stripping Grineer armor quickly. Combine with Lasting Sting for high status duration.

**Dagger stance combo build.** Equip Pointed Wind or Homing Fang. These stances enable forward-sliding attacks that chain status procs efficiently. Include Condition Overload for damage multiplication per unique status applied.

## Synergies

Nidus, Saryn, or Rhino provide damage amplification through Virulence, Spores, or Roar respectively. The Dark Dagger's moderate status chance benefits from Warframe abilities that lower enemy armor before engagement. Equipping a Radiation-themed Warframe like Chroma (with Heat/Electricity conversion to Radiation) creates thematic synergy.

## Notes

- The innate Radiation element does not stack additively with additional Radiation mods -- adding a Radiation mod produces no combined element, just more Radiation.
- Range of 1.75 m is below average for melee; close quarters or confined corridors favour this weapon.
