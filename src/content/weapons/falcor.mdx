## Overview

The Falcor is a glaive-type melee weapon with an innate Electricity element (84) alongside Puncture (92) and Impact (36). As a thrown melee weapon, the Falcor can be thrown in a spinning arc that hits multiple enemies along its flight path before returning to the player. The innate Electricity is immediately useful without occupying two elemental mod slots, enabling Magnetic (Electricity + Cold) or Radiation (Electricity + Heat) combinations from a single additional elemental mod. The 34% status chance is very high for a thrown melee and ensures consistent elemental proc application both on direct hits and on the return arc. Mastery Rank 8 is required. The 12% critical chance and 1.6x multiplier are secondary concerns.

Glaive weapons use dedicated stance mods (Gleaming Talon) that enable thrown-arc combos and charged throw heavy attacks. The charged throw is the Falcor's primary tool: holding and releasing the heavy attack button launches the glaive on a ricocheting arc that hits all enemies in its path. Combined with the 34% status chance, a fully-charged throw applies Electricity, Puncture, and potential elemental proc hits to every enemy caught in the arc. The innate Electricity's Magnetic combination (adding Cold mods) is particularly effective against Corpus shield units, as Magnetic drains and bypasses shields. The short 1.3 m range applies only to direct melee swings; thrown attacks cover much greater distances.

## Variants

The Falcor has no Prime, Kuva, or Tenet variant. It is a standalone glaive melee weapon. The Glaive Prime is the Prime option for the glaive weapon class.

## Recommended builds

**Magnetic/Corpus build.** The innate Electricity + Cold 60/60 mod creates Magnetic, which drains and bypasses Corpus Shields. Equip Primed Pressure Point, Condition Overload, a Magnetic elemental mod (Frostbite equivalent), and Volcanic Edge for secondary Heat element. The 34% status ensures Magnetic and Heat proc application on each thrown arc hit against grouped Corpus targets.

**Status/Viral thrown arc build.** Equip Weeping Wounds, Condition Overload, two Viral 60/60 mods (one Toxin, one Cold), and Primed Pressure Point. At x4 combo with Weeping Wounds, the 34% status reaches very high levels. The charged-throw arc applies multiple elemental procs to every enemy in the flight path simultaneously, creating rapid Condition Overload stacking.

**Heavy-attack Electricity spam.** Use the Falcor's charged heavy attack throw to apply Electricity procs in rapid succession. Equip Volcanic Edge for Radiation (Electricity + Heat) as a secondary element. Combine with Primed Pressure Point and Corrupt Charge (starts at higher combo) for higher heavy-attack damage from the initial throw. Arcane Fury increases attack speed after heavy-attack kills.

## Synergies

Mag's Magnetize funnels enemy projectiles and melee contacts in a concentrated area; the Falcor's thrown-arc path through Magnetize multiplies hit count. Volt's Electric Shield enhances all Electricity damage for shots fired through it -- thrown glaive arcs passing through Electric Shield receive this damage amplification. Frames that group enemies create ideal conditions for multi-target thrown-arc coverage.

## Notes

- The 1.3 m range applies only to direct melee swings; thrown arc attacks bypass this limitation entirely.
- The innate Electricity frees one mod slot; a single Cold mod creates Magnetic without requiring a second Electricity mod.
