## Overview

Kuva Karak is the Kuva Lich variant of the Karak assault rifle, significantly improving on the base weapon with a raised critical chance of 23%, a 2.1x critical multiplier, and a 70-round magazine. Like all Kuva weapons, it rolls with an additional elemental bonus damage type determined by the Kuva Lich's element, ranging from 25% to 60% bonus elemental damage. The Kuva Karak retains the Karak's reliable fire rate of 11.67 rounds per second, which combines with its improved stats to produce strong sustained DPS for a primary rifle.

The per-shot damage of 23 is low but compensated by the high fire rate, which applies damage and status procs continuously. With 31% status chance and two elemental mods, Corrosive and Heat can be reliably proc'd to strip armor and ignite targets simultaneously.

## Acquisition

Kuva Karak is obtained by vanquishing a Kuva Lich that has a Karak as its weapon. Kuva Liches are created in Grineer-controlled missions by allowing a Kuva Larvling to become a Lich. The Lich's weapon is randomly assigned, so players may need to create multiple Liches before finding one with the Kuva Karak. The elemental bonus is determined when the Larvling absorbs Kuva from a nearby Kuva Siphon, so choosing the Larvling's exposure element is possible with planning. A higher Kuva bonus (closer to 60%) requires Larvlings at higher-level nodes.

## Variants

The base Karak is available from the Market at Mastery Rank 1 for Credits. Karak Wraith improves stats for players who have obtained it through Operation events. Kuva Karak represents the strongest variant with the addition of a customizable elemental bonus and improved critical and status stats.

## Recommended builds

The combination of 23% crit chance and 31% status chance supports a crit-status hybrid approach. Serration and Split Chamber provide base damage and multishot, while Primed Bane of Grineer adds faction-specific damage for the primary use case against Grineer. Point Strike raises crit chance toward 46%, enabling regular critical hits. Two elemental mods combine with the weapon's inherent Kuva element bonus for strong elemental coverage.

A pure status build using four elemental mods (two pairs for Viral and Heat) maximizes proc application rate and performs well in endurance Steel Path missions against all factions.

## Notes

The Kuva Karak's elemental bonus is inherited by Lich-converted copies shared with allies, making it useful for squadmates who have not farmed their own. Lich weapon fusion allows combining two copies of the same Kuva weapon to increase the elemental bonus toward the 60% cap, which is worth pursuing for weapons used in endgame builds.
