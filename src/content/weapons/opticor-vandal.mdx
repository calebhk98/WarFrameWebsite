## Overview

The Opticor Vandal is a Corpus beam rifle that fires a concentrated beam of energy, delivering sustained high damage at medium to long ranges. It excels at long-range engagements with moderate fire rate but significant damage output per shot. The weapon requires charging time between shots and benefits substantially from mods that increase charge rate and beam damage output. Unlike rapid-fire rifles, the Opticor Vandal emphasizes precision and sustained beam mechanics, rewarding careful aiming and positioning.

The Opticor Vandal represents a middle ground between raw firepower and practical usability in mission scenarios. Unlike its standard counterpart, the Vandal variant improves reload speed and magazine capacity, making it more forgiving for sustained combat and reducing downtime between volleys. The weapon's high slash damage output makes it particularly effective against flesh-based enemies, while the balanced damage profile allows flexibility in mod selection and enables diverse build approaches.

In practical use, the Opticor Vandal performs best at medium to long ranges where its charge-up time becomes less of a tactical liability. Players should build for critical damage or status effects depending on mission requirements and enemy compositions. The weapon's 0.3 status chance suggests supplementation with elemental mods for environmental control and crowd management. For dedicated crit builds, a 2.0 critical multiplier provides decent scaling with mods like Critical Damage or Point Strike, though the zero critical chance means users must invest significantly in crit chance augmentation.
