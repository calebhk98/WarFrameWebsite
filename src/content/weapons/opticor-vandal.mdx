## Overview

Opticor Vandal is a Vandal-tier variant of the Opticor beam rifle, awarded through limited Operations and tradeable between players. The damage profile is slash-dominant with 280 slash far exceeding the 80 puncture and 40 impact per charge. The weapon fires a charged beam delivering high per-shot damage, distinguishing it from automatic rifles by its deliberate, high-impact shot cadence. With 24% critical chance and 2.6x critical multiplier alongside 30% status chance, the Opticor Vandal supports both critical and status build directions, with the slash-heavy base making slash proc builds particularly effective through Hunter Munitions.

The Vandal variant improves on the base Opticor with higher base damage, improved reload, and a larger magazine. At mastery rank 14 and 100 accuracy, the Opticor Vandal is one of the most precise primary weapons available -- its beam travels to the aimed point without dispersion. The 2.0 rounds per second fire rate reflects the charge-fire cycle, and the 8-round magazine with 1.4-second reload provides reasonable sustained engagement capability.

The slash-dominant damage profile enables Hunter Munitions synergy. At 24% crit chance, critical hits apply slash procs through Hunter Munitions at a predictable rate, adding armor-bypassing bleeding damage over time. Combined with the high base 280 slash, physical slash procs deal meaningful damage even without elemental amplification.

## Acquisition

The Opticor Vandal is a limited-availability weapon obtained through participation in specific Operations events when active. After the event period, the weapon and its blueprint become available through player trading. Check [warframe.market](https://warframe.market) for current availability and pricing. The weapon cannot be crafted independently outside of event access or trading.

## Variants

The base [Opticor](/weapons/opticor) is available through the Market and is the standard form of this beam rifle. The Vandal variant provides meaningfully higher damage, better reload, and a larger magazine. The Opticor does not have a Prime variant as of the current game version.

## Recommended builds

The Opticor Vandal's 280 slash, 24% crit, and 30% status create multiple viable build paths.

Critical plus Hunter Munitions:
- Serration for base damage
- Split Chamber for multishot (applies to charged beam shots)
- Point Strike for critical chance
- Vital Sense for critical damage
- Hunter Munitions to convert crits into slash procs
- Galvanized Scope for headshot-stacking damage bonus on consecutive precision kills
- Elemental mod (Heat, Cold, or Toxin) for additional status proc type

Status-forward Viral build:
- Serration and Split Chamber for base scaling
- Rime Rounds (cold) and Infected Clip (toxin) for Viral combination
- Galvanized Aptitude for damage stacking per status type applied
- The high slash base with Viral amplification reduces effective enemy health, enabling bleeding procs to deal more damage per tick

For missions requiring sustained fire at range, the 100 accuracy ensures precise beam placement at any distance within visual range without spread management.

## Notes

The charge-fire cycle makes the Opticor Vandal less effective in scenarios requiring rapid target acquisition against multiple small enemies at close range. The weapon is most valuable in situations where deliberate, high-damage shots against medium-to-large targets are appropriate: boss encounters, heavy unit prioritization in survival, and sortie missions with specific faction compositions.

Magazine management is important given the 8-round capacity. At 2.0 rounds per second effective fire rate, the magazine depletes in four seconds of continuous fire. The 1.4-second reload is relatively fast, so reloading during target transitions maintains high practical uptime.

As a limited-availability weapon, the Opticor Vandal is not always accessible through farming. Players without event access must trade to acquire it. Its practical performance is competitive with regularly available beam weapons, and the trading market reflects its limited supply.
