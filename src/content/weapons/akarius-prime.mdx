## Overview

The [Akarius Prime](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Akarius_Prime) is the Prime variant of the Akarius secondary launcher. It fires explosive rockets that deal 577 total damage per shot: 68 impact and 509 blast. The 34% status chance combined with blast damage creates reliable blast procs (knockdown and stagger) on every hit. The 18% critical chance and 2.0x multiplier support modest crit builds alongside the status focus. At Mastery Rank 14, it targets experienced players building into late-game explosive secondary builds.

Blast damage deals area-of-effect damage on impact, hitting all enemies within the explosion radius. The Akarius Prime's large blast component means each rocket delivers both direct and splash damage, effective against enemy groups. Riven disposition at 1/5 reflects very high community use.

## Acquisition

Akarius Prime components drop from Void relics. Check the [Akarius Prime Wiki page](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Akarius_Prime) for current relic locations and vault status, as the relic pool changes with each Prime Vault rotation.

## Variants

The base Akarius (Mastery Rank 8) has lower blast damage and lower status chance. The Prime is the significant upgrade for explosive secondary users. No Vandal or Wraith version exists.

## Recommended builds

**Blast crowd control:** Hornet Strike, Barrel Diffusion, Lethal Torrent, Pistol Pestilence, Jolt, Frostbite, Scorch, and Primed Heated Charge. Blast procs from the 509 base blast stagger and knockdown enemies reliably at 34% status chance. Additional elemental procs (viral, heat) compound the damage over time. In crowd-control scenarios, blast staggers delay enemy advance while elemental procs reduce health.

**Explosive critical:** Hornet Strike, Primed Pistol Gambit, Primed Target Cracker, Barrel Diffusion, Lethal Torrent, and elemental mods. At 18% base crit, Primed Pistol Gambit pushes into reliable yellow-crit on each rocket. The 509 blast base amplified by 2.0x crit multiplier delivers burst AOE damage against groups.

**Status maximum:** Replace crit mods with Galvanized Crosshairs and Galvanized Shot. Galvanized status mods provide escalating status chance per kill, pushing the Akarius Prime's already high 34% base into guaranteed-proc territory at full stacks.

## Synergies

- **Blast knockdown:** Blast procs knock enemies down, creating windows for finisher attacks or uninterrupted ability activation. In Interception or Defense missions, consistent knockdown from the Akarius Prime slows enemy capture progress.
- **Condition Overload melee:** Blast (from the rocket) plus two additional elemental types from mods creates three distinct status types. Switching to melee after two rocket hits enables full Condition Overload damage bonus immediately.
- **Rhino's Roar:** Multiplicatively amplifies the 509 blast damage, converting each rocket into a massive AOE burst against enemy clusters.

## Notes

- Riven disposition at 1/5 is the minimum, reflecting the weapon's high use rate. Rivens roll with minimal multipliers, making riven investment less impactful than for low-disposition weapons.
- Self-damage from explosions applies when firing at close range. Maintain at least 3-5 meters of separation from explosion targets. In enclosed corridors, use the minimum safe distance consistently.
- Accuracy of 26.7 reflects the projectile arc and spread pattern. At medium range, rockets travel reliably to the aim point; at long range, the arc trajectory requires lead angle compensation.
