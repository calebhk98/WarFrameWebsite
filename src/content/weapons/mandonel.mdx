## Overview

A heavy Arch-gun introduced with the Whispers in the Walls update, featuring mixed physical damage and innate Radiation. Its extremely low accuracy (1.43) indicates a wide-spread scatter pattern designed for area coverage rather than precision targeting. Introduced in Whispers in the Walls update, the Mandonel is notable for Riven disposition 0 (absolute minimum, lowest possible); innate Radiation from Entrati-influenced design; acquired exclusively from Deimos Entrati Lab Bounties.

The Mandonel carries the hallmarks of Entrati weapon-craft, blending their signature Radiation output with a spread pattern designed for Deimos's enclosed corridors and open excavation zones. Its acquisition through Albrecht's Laboratories connects it directly to the secrets uncovered in the Whispers in the Walls storyline.

## Acquisition

Blueprint from Deimos/Albrecht's Laboratories Entrati Lab Bounties at Rotation C (Rare). Component parts (Barrel, Receiver, Stock) from Deimos mission rotations: Munio Defense, Cambire Alchemy, and Persto Survival missions at Rotation B and C. The Mandonel requires Mastery Rank 10 to equip.

Arch-gun weapons require either standard crafting resources, Syndicate standing for component parts, or specialized mission drops. Once crafted, equipping the Mandonel in Archwing uses the standard Arch-gun slot. To use it as a Heavy Weapon in ground missions or Railjack combat, a Gravimag must be installed. Gravimags are available from Clan Dojos via Energy Lab research, or from Arbitrations as rotation rewards.

## Variants

No Prime or Vandal variant exists. Among the newest Arch-guns, added in the Whispers in the Walls update.

The Arch-gun family spans weapons from Tenno engineering (Corvas, Fluctus, Velocitus), Grineer design (Grattler, Kuva line), and Entrati craftsmanship (Cortege, Mausolon, Morgha), each with distinct stat profiles suited to different engagement contexts.

## Recommended builds

Riven disposition of 0 makes Riven mods provide zero benefit -- do not invest Kuva or Riven Slivers on this weapon. Focus entirely on fixed mods: Rubedo-Lined Barrel for primary damage, then elemental combinations. The 25% critical chance supports Hollowed Bullets. Radiation innate pairs with Heat for Radiation + Heat double-elemental application.

**Core Arch-gun mods to consider:**

- Rubedo-Lined Barrel -- +165% Arch-gun damage (primary damage mod for all builds)
- Hollowed Bullets -- +120% critical chance (essential for any crit-focused build)
- Critical Focus -- +60% critical multiplier while aiming
- Modified Munitions -- +90% status chance (for status-focused builds)
- Arch-gun Ace -- +165% fire rate (useful on slow-fire weapons to improve feel)
- Parallax Scope -- +30% critical chance while aiming
- Charged Bullets -- +60% magazine capacity
- Superior Defenses -- reduces reload time
- Conductive Coils -- adds Electricity damage
- Thermite Rounds -- adds Heat damage

Prioritize Rubedo-Lined Barrel as the first mod in any Arch-gun build. Follow with critical or status mods based on the weapon's stat profile. Elemental mods provide faction-specific damage type flexibility.

## Notes

The Mandonel operates in the Arch-gun slot, used in Archwing missions and as a Heavy Weapon on the ground via Gravimag. In Railjack (Empyrean) missions, the Arch-gun serves as the primary armament during space combat against fighters and Crewships.

Radiation innate benefits Grineer-heavy Railjack encounters. Wide spread suits close-quarters Railjack corridor combat where accuracy limitations are minimized.

Archwing combat system notes: Archwing missions require completing The Archwing quest. Four Archwings are available -- Itzal (mobility/stealth), Amesha (tank/support), Elytron (AoE damage), and Odonata (balanced starter). Amesha's defensive abilities compensate for the commitment required to use a wide-spread weapon at effective range. Elytron for AoE supplementation in dense engagements.

The weapon's Riven disposition is 0/5 (minimum). Riven dispositions range from 0 (absolute minimum, strong weapon) to 5 (maximum, weak weapon). DE adjusts dispositions quarterly based on weapon usage rates.
