Staticor is a secondary pistol weapon that serves as radiation-damage secondary that fires energy blasts; radiation procs cause confusion, turning enemies against each other -- useful in dense groups for self-generated crowd control. Mastery Rank 10 is required to equip the weapon. Players who invest in building it properly will find it reliable across the star chart, with scaling potential on the Steel Path when supported by appropriate mods and Warframe ability synergies.
