## Acquisition

Argo & Vel is obtained through the Duviri Paradox content. The weapon's components are acquired from the Duviri circuit or Duviri open world activities. Players access Duviri through the Star Chart after completing the prerequisite quest. The Mastery Rank 0 requirement makes it immediately accessible to new players who reach Duviri content, though the content itself requires some progression.

## Variants

Argo & Vel does not have a Prime or standard variant in the traditional sense. As a Duviri origin weapon, it stands as its own entry in the sword-and-shield category. Players seeking alternatives may consider the Cobra & Crane (MR10, impact and slash focus) or the Magistar (hammer class) for heavy impact alternatives. The Ack & Brunt offers a shield/blade combination with different stat distribution.

## Recommended builds

**Puncture Corrosive Build:** The 80% puncture damage weighting synergizes with Corrosive elemental combination (Toxic + Electric) to stack Corrosion procs stripping enemy armor. Pressure Point, Primed Fever Strike, and Voltaic Strike form the core. Add Condition Overload for damage scaling with multiple status types, and Weeping Wounds to escalate status chance during combos.

**Crit Build:** With 32% critical chance and 2.4x multiplier, dedicated crit investment returns strong results. Organ Shatter (critical multiplier) plus Blood Rush (scaling CC with combo counter) creates escalating crit damage. The sword-and-shield stance -- Eleventh Storm or Final Harbinger -- provides both combo building and defensive blocking capability.

The shield component provides a blocking angle that reduces frontal melee damage taken while attacking. Positioning to leverage the frontal block improves survivability in high-density melee situations.

## Notes

Sword-and-shield weapons occupy a unique niche in Warframe's melee system, providing inherent blocking capability integrated into the weapon's combat mechanics. The shield allows manual blocking in block-specific stances, and certain combos incorporate the shield for slam attacks.

The 0.8 attack speed is slower than the melee average, so players should plan combo rotations around this pacing. Berserker Fury or Primed Fury mods increase attack speed but do not change the fundamental deliberate rhythm of shield weapon combos.

At MR0, Argo & Vel represents exceptional potential for new players who access Duviri content early, offering stats that compete with mid-tier melee weapons without a mastery requirement.
