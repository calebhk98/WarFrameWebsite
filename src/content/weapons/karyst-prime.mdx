## Overview

Karyst Prime is the Prime variant of the Karyst dagger, adding built-in toxin elemental damage to the weapon's base physical damage profile. The base Karyst is a Tenno dagger with a toxin-themed design; Karyst Prime retains this identity while substantially improving the damage numbers and adding 96 toxin elemental damage as a permanent mod-independent component. This built-in toxin damage is notable because it combines with Cold mods to form Viral or with Heat mods to form Gas without requiring a dedicated Toxin mod slot.

As a dagger, Karyst Prime uses the dagger stance system, which produces fast single-target attacks at relatively short reach. The 2.2 m range is modest compared to polearms or heavy blades, making the weapon suited for close-range engagements. The 24% critical chance and 2.2x multiplier provide a functional crit platform, while the 30% status chance enables reliable elemental proc builds alongside the weapon's intrinsic toxin damage.

Mastery Rank 12 positions Karyst Prime in the mid-to-upper tier of the Prime melee weapons.

The 96 toxin elemental damage is the key differentiator from most melee weapons. Because toxin bypasses Corpus shields entirely, the intrinsic toxin component deals direct health damage to shielded Corpus units. This gives Karyst Prime a natural advantage in Corpus-heavy missions without requiring the player to sacrifice a mod slot to add Toxin separately.

The puncture-dominant physical damage split (102 puncture, 96 slash, 50 impact) performs well against armor, complementing the toxin damage against mixed-faction enemies.

## Acquisition

Karyst Prime components are obtained from Void Relics tied to its Prime Access rotation. The Blueprint, Blade, and Handle drop from specific Lith, Meso, Neo, or Axi relics depending on the component. When Karyst Prime is in the active Prime Access window, its relics are available in the standard drop table. Between rotations, it enters the Prime Vault and its relics must be obtained through unvault events or trading.

Players can trade individual Karyst Prime components or the completed weapon directly via the Trading Post.

## Variants

The base Karyst is available from the Market for credits and shares the toxin-themed identity but lacks the Prime variant's improved stats and higher intrinsic toxin damage. Karyst Prime is the highest-tier version of this dagger family.

No Kuva, Tenet, Wraith, or Vandal version of the Karyst exists.

## Recommended builds

Because Karyst Prime already provides intrinsic Toxin damage, adding a Cold mod creates Viral damage without spending an additional mod slot on Toxin. A Viral build using appropriate elemental mods and Condition Overload creates a strong combination that reduces enemy maximum health (Viral proc) while dealing raw damage each hit. The intrinsic toxin damage applies independently of other elemental combinations.

A Blood Rush-based crit build using the 24% base critical chance can reach reliable crit rates at moderate combo counts. Organ Shatter adds multiplier, and Primed Pressure Point establishes base damage scaling. The intrinsic toxin damage applies independently and does not dilute the crit build's output.

For Corpus missions specifically, the intrinsic Toxin damage bypasses shields and deals direct health damage, making Karyst Prime particularly efficient against shielded Corpus units compared to weapons without elemental damage.

## Notes

Dagger stance mods compatible with Karyst Prime include Pointed Wind and Homing Fang. Pointed Wind provides quick multi-hit directional combos effective for single-target burst damage. Homing Fang allows gap-closing attacks that reposition the player toward the target mid-combo, which partially compensates for the weapon's 2.2 m range in moving enemy encounters.

The 0.667 attack speed is below average for a dagger, which affects how quickly Blood Rush builds combo and how often Weeping Wounds scales status chance. Players who prefer faster combo buildup should consider whether the intrinsic toxin advantage justifies the lower attack speed for their playstyle.
