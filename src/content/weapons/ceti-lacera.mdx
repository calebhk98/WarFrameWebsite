## Overview

The [Ceti Lacera](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Ceti_Lacera) is a whip melee weapon with 216 total damage per attack. Its damage favors puncture (66) and slash (38), with small impact (12), totaling 116 physical damage plus inherent electric damage (100) not reflected in the base frontmatter values. The 40% status chance is exceptional for a whip weapon -- among the highest in the category -- enabling rapid proc accumulation per swing. The 20% critical chance supports hybrid builds alongside the status focus.

Whip weapons have access to the Burning Wasp stance mod, which provides long-range combo sequences that capitalize on whip reach. At Mastery Rank 12, the Ceti Lacera targets experienced players. Riven disposition at 4/5 indicates above-average roll potential.

The Ceti Lacera's extended reach inherent to whips allows engagement from greater distances than swords or daggers, enabling proc application on enemies before they close to melee range.

## Acquisition

The Ceti Lacera blueprint is available from the Market for credits. Crafting requires standard mid-to-late-game components. As a permanently available Market weapon, it is accessible at any progression stage above Mastery Rank 12. The completed weapon can be purchased with platinum.

## Variants

No Prime, Vandal, or Wraith variant exists. Players seeking a whip with a Prime upgrade path should compare the Atterax or Lacera as nearby alternatives. The Lacera is the base form that shares a name origin with the Ceti Lacera.

## Recommended builds

**Status electric hybrid:** Primed Pressure Point, Berserker Fury, Condition Overload, Weeping Wounds, Blood Rush, Organ Shatter, Fever Strike, and Shocking Touch. The 40% base status chance and Weeping Wounds scaling create rapid proc accumulation. Adding toxin and electric (from Shocking Touch plus built-in electric) forms viral procs, halving enemy health. Condition Overload amplifies each hit as unique status types accumulate. Blood Rush elevates the 20% base crit into consistent yellow-crit territory at x4 combo multiplier.

**Whip combo build:** Burning Wasp stance, Berserker Fury, Primed Pressure Point, and full status modding. Burning Wasp provides the longest-range combo animations of any melee stance, allowing the Ceti Lacera to tag targets from 4-5 meters during some combo animations. Status procs accumulate rapidly at this range before enemies close to dangerous proximity.

**Steel Path build:** Galvanized mods on companion combined with Condition Overload, Blood Rush, and elemental mods for corrosive or viral + heat. The Ceti Lacera's 40% status chance ensures rapid armor strip through corrosive procs, necessary in Steel Path where armor values are substantially elevated.

## Synergies

- **Condition Overload:** The 40% status chance at 1.08 attack speed generates unique status types within 2-3 swings. Condition Overload's full damage bonus activates rapidly, making this weapon an excellent carrier for the mod.
- **Blood Rush:** At 20% base critical chance, Blood Rush reaches consistent yellow-crit at x4 combo. Combined with Weeping Wounds' status scaling, both crit and status output peak simultaneously.
- **Whip slam:** Whips have a unique slam attack that travels along the ground and hits enemies in a long line. This ground-slam mechanic enables hitting multiple enemies in a row -- useful for triggering procs across a group simultaneously.

## Notes

- Whips have the unique ability to hit enemies through thin walls and around corners during some combo animations. The extended reach makes them particularly effective in tight corridors where other melee weapons would require face-to-face contact.
- The 40% status chance is the defining feature of the Ceti Lacera. Builds that do not prioritize this stat with Weeping Wounds or additional status mods waste the weapon's primary strength.
- Attack speed of 1.08 is average. Berserker Fury provides meaningful acceleration during kill streaks, enabling faster combo building for Blood Rush and Weeping Wounds scaling.
- Riven disposition of 4/5 makes rivens worth pursuing. A riven with range or status chance amplifies the Ceti Lacera's already strong proc output to extreme levels at higher investment tiers.
