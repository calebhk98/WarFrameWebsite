## Overview

Paracesis is a greatsword imbued with Orokin technology and specialization against Sentient enemies, awarded through The Zariman Ten Zero quest. The weapon distinguishes itself through innate Sentient damage that applies regardless of modding, making it the mandatory choice for Sentient-focused missions like The New War quest. The puncture-heavy damage profile makes it effective against armored targets, while the 20 percent critical chance and 2.2x multiplier enable moderate critical builds. The standard one-second attack speed provides consistent, predictable damage application. Paracesis functions as a capable melee weapon throughout the star chart, with its specialized Sentient damage transforming from niche utility into endgame necessity when Sentient encounters become mandatory. The quest reward nature eliminates marketplace acquisition, ensuring every Warframe can access this unique weapon.

The psychological impact of possessing a dedicated Sentient-killer cannot be overstated in the Warframe narrative context. Paracesis represents the culmination of anti-Sentient technology, granting players an iconic blade with unique mechanical grounding in the game's lore. Beyond the narrative resonance, the weapon's balanced stat distribution enables it to function as a reliable melee option for new players lacking better alternatives. The puncture emphasis positions it well against Grineer targets while the Sentient damage bonus applies against all faction types, creating universal utility within its specialized role. Its immediate availability at Mastery Rank 10 positions it as a natural progression weapon for mid-game players.

## Acquisition

Paracesis is awarded directly upon completing The Zariman Ten Zero quest, making it one of the few weapons with guaranteed progression acquisition. The blueprint and components are immediately available upon quest completion, with instant assembly granting immediate access without build time. This direct quest reward system ensures every player has access to the Sentient-killer weapon before confronting significant Sentient encounters.

## Variants

No variants of Paracesis exist, as the weapon's unique Sentient-damage property is exclusive to its singular form. Other greatswords in the melee category function as alternatives with different damage distributions and special properties, but no Paracesis Prime or Kuva variant has been released.

## Recommended builds

Pressure Point provides foundational melee damage applicable to all weapons. Organ Shatter amplifies critical hit effectiveness, while True Steel increases critical chance toward guaranteed-hit territory. Relentless Combination adds attack speed, accelerating damage application rates. Elemental mods can be added through Heat or Cold Rounds, creating inflammation or freeze conditions depending on tactical preference. Cleaving Whirlwind provides movement benefits during combat, enabling aggressive playstyle without penalization. Reach extends the weapon's effective engagement range, compensating for the three-meter baseline. The 10 percent status chance suggests pure damage builds rather than status application strategies.

## Notes

Paracesis fills a specialized role as the mandatory Sentient-killer without direct marketplace alternatives. Its strength lies not in maximum damage output compared to meta melee weapons, but in universal Sentient effectiveness that scaling and modding cannot replicate elsewhere. The weapon's balanced stat distribution enables casual use throughout progression without requiring intensive optimization. Its narrative importance as a technology representing anti-Sentient resistance adds emotional weight beyond mechanical statistics. The puncture emphasis makes it particularly effective against Grineer armor-heavy units, positioning it as a reasonable default melee choice for balanced gameplay.
