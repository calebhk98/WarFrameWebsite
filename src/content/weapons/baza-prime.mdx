## Overview

The Baza Prime represents the premium evolution of the standard Baza rifle, combining exceptional accuracy, pinpoint weapon handling, and devastating critical strike potential. Unlike its standard counterpart, the Prime variant boasts a 3.0x critical multiplier that scales dramatically with critical-focused mods, enabling consistent one-shot elimination of priority targets in mid-level content. The weapon's perfect accuracy stat ensures every projectile reaches its intended target, eliminating spray patterns common in other automatic rifles. Its tight fire rate of 16.67 rounds per second and 40-round magazine create a sustained damage platform ideal for both hitscan gameplay and mobility-oriented builds. The Baza Prime stands as one of the most refined assault rifles available, serving both new and veteran players seeking precision weapon platforms.

## Damage

Baza Prime's damage skews toward slash at 76.8 points, making it effective against unarmored and lightly-armored enemies. The 28.8 impact and 14.4 puncture components provide versatility against varied armor types. Critical scaling transforms the modest per-shot damage into devastating sustained output when paired with multiplier mods. The 3.0x critical multiplier ensures that critical strikes deal nearly triple damage, making critical-focused builds the preferred approach. The low status chance means status procs remain secondary to raw damage output, though elemental mods still provide utility for specific mission types.

## Acquisition

Baza Prime drops from Neo Z2 and Neo Z3 void relics as a common reward, making farming straightforward during Void Fissure void fissure missions. The blueprint construction requires 20,000 credits and twelve hours of Foundry time after component acquisition. The Prime remains unvaulted and actively available through current void relic rotations, ensuring consistent access for dedicated farmers. Players can trade Baza Prime blueprints and components through the market, accelerating acquisition for those preferring platinum investment.

## Variants

The standard Baza exists as a non-prime variant with reduced critical multiplier (2.5x instead of 3.0x) and slightly lower overall damage values. The Baza Prime represents the optimal choice for critical-focused gameplay, though the standard version remains viable for budget builds and early-mastery completion. No Kuva or Prisma variants currently exist.

## Recommended builds

The Baza Prime's 3.0x critical multiplier demands critical-focused modding. Serration provides the foundational damage multiplier, while Vital Sense and Point Strike maximize critical scaling. Shred or Piercing Shots extend armor penetration through critical mechanics. Hammer Shot or other critical-boosting mods push the natural 28% critical chance into comfortable 50%+ territory. For endgame content, Primed mods like Primed Bane of Grineer or Primed Target Cracker amplify critical effectiveness against specific enemy factions. Magazine extension via Tactical Reload is optional given the tight 40-round capacity.

## Synergies

Frames providing critical bonuses synergize perfectly with the Baza Prime. Mesa's Peacemaker scales weapon critical strikes through her accuracy buff, making the Prime's perfect accuracy irrelevant but allowing ability-first playstyles. Harrow provides critical chance bonuses during his invulnerability windows, synchronizing perfectly with the weapon's rapid fire rate. Ash benefits from the weapon's tight hitscan properties when combining gun usage with blade melee combos. Baruuk's defensive stance pairs well with the sustained fire rate for steady crowd control.

## Lore

The Baza Prime belongs to an ancient Orokin weapons line, predating Corpus industrialization. Its precision engineering reflects Orokin commitment to weapon refinement and critical efficiency. The exceptional accuracy suggests advanced ballistic modeling and targeting systems incorporated during the Old War. Within the player community, the Baza Prime represents a benchmark for what refined automatic rifle design should achieve, influencing expectations for weapon precision and handling consistency.
