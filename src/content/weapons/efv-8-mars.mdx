## Overview

The Efv-8 Mars is a secondary weapon with a Slash-dominant damage profile (55 Slash vs 20 Impact and zero Puncture). The 27% critical chance and 2.1x multiplier provide a functional crit platform, while the 17% status chance is moderate. Fire rate of 6.5 rounds per second with a 15-round magazine creates a burst-style secondary suited to close-quarters rapid fire. Mastery Rank 14 is required.

The low 10 accuracy reflects a spread-fire or shotgun-style mechanic at range. The 1.67-second reload is fast, minimizing downtime between magazines. Zero Puncture means the Efv-8 Mars is weakest against heavily armored Grineer targets; it performs best against Corpus and Infested where Slash and Impact are effective.

## Recommended builds

**Slash/Viral build.** The 55 Slash base combined with Viral (Toxin + Cold 60/60 mods) and multishot (Barrel Diffusion, Lethal Torrent) ensures Slash bleed procs stack quickly through Viral-weakened health. Add Hornet Strike for base damage scaling.

**Critical/Slash.** Use Pistol Gambit and Lethal Torrent to reach effective crit with the 27% base. Combine with Hunter Munitions (available via Secondary Deadhead or another mod if applicable) for Slash proc generation on crits. Vital Sense adds multiplier amplification.

**Status primer build.** Use the Efv-8 Mars at close range to apply Slash procs rapidly, then switch to a Condition Overload melee. The fast 6.5 fire rate and 15-round magazine generate multiple procs quickly before the magazine empties.

## Synergies

Ash's passive increases secondary weapon damage on airborne enemies; pair with an aerial approach on deployment. Mesa's Shooting Gallery buffs secondary fire rate, compounding the already-high 6.5 rounds/second. Frames that group enemies at close range (Mag, Khora) enable maximum hit coverage despite the 10 accuracy spread. Wisp's Haste Mote increases fire rate, helping empty the 15-round magazine faster and extending the Efv-8 Mars's burst damage window before reload. Because the Efv-8 Mars excels at close range, Warframes with mobility augments -- Nova's Worm Hole, Nezha's Fire Walker -- help close the gap to deliver the weapon's spread at effective range.

## Notes

- The 10 accuracy means the Efv-8 Mars is a close-range secondary; accuracy degrades rapidly at medium distance.
- Zero Puncture reduces effectiveness against Grineer Alloy and Ferrite Armor; pair with Corrosive mods or armor-stripping Warframe abilities.
