## Overview

The Vitrica is a Corpus heavy blade with 333 total base damage per hit -- split into 99.9 impact, 133.2 puncture, and 99.9 slash. The near-equal spread across all three physical types makes the Vitrica effective against any enemy type without a single physical damage weakness. This balanced profile is unusual for a heavy blade and provides versatility across faction encounters.

The 33% status chance is among the highest available for a heavy blade, enabling reliable elemental proc application. Combined with the 23% critical chance and 2.3x multiplier, the Vitrica occupies a strong hybrid position. Heavy blades characteristically have slower attack speeds (0.83 for the Vitrica) offset by very high per-hit damage. At Mastery Rank 13, the Vitrica is a late-game weapon that rewards players with a full mod collection.

## Acquisition

The Vitrica blueprint is purchasable from the in-game Market for 20,000 credits. Build time is 12 hours. No quest unlock is required.

## Variants

No Prime, Vandal, or Wraith variant exists for the Vitrica. Players seeking additional heavy blade options should examine the Gram Prime or Galatine Prime through Void Relics.

## Recommended builds

**Status-Condition Overload**: Equip Condition Overload (damage per unique status), Weeping Wounds (status chance on combo), Primed Pressure Point, and elemental mods for corrosive plus heat for a third status type. The 33% base status rises sharply with Weeping Wounds at x2+ combo. Three unique status types multiply Condition Overload's bonus significantly.

**Crit-focused heavy blade**: Blood Rush, Organ Shatter, Primed Pressure Point, and elemental mods. At x2 combo with Blood Rush, the 23% base crit chance approximately doubles. Hunter Munitions converts crits to slash procs that bypass armor and synergize with Viral.

**Heavy attack endgame**: Killing Blow, Corrupt Charge (12x combo opener), Sacrificial Steel, and Organ Shatter. The 1665 base heavy attack at 12x combo exceeds 19,000 before mods.

## Notes

Heavy blades have slower combo accumulation than lighter weapons due to the 0.83 attack speed. Players who rely on combo-scaling builds must invest more time to reach peak effectiveness. Pairing with a warframe ability that groups enemies (such as Undertow or Bastille) maximizes each wide heavy blade sweep.


The Vitrica's balanced damage distribution (99.9 impact, 133.2 puncture, 99.9 slash) with near-equal impact and slash components means it avoids the large damage penalties other heavy blades receive against specific enemy health types. Most faction-specialized heavy blades deal heavily reduced damage against the opposing faction's damage resistance; the Vitrica's equal split minimizes this vulnerability while puncture handles armor penetration.

At Mastery Rank 13 with a 33% status chance and 23% critical chance, the Vitrica is most effective in late-game Steel Path content where the combination of Condition Overload and Weeping Wounds creates a multiplicative damage loop that scales with enemy level. Players who have progressed to MR 13 typically have access to the full range of melee mods needed to maximize the Vitrica's hybrid potential.
