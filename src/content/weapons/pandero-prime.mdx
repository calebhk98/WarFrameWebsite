## Overview

Pandero Prime is the prime variant of the Pandero revolver-style pistol, featuring a high 30% critical chance and an exceptional 2.8x critical multiplier that place it among the top-tier crit secondaries in the game. The damage distribution is puncture-heavy at 52 per shot, with 26 impact and 26 slash. While the base 24% status chance is below average for a dedicated status build, the crit statistics create compelling builds around pure critical or crit-hybrid configurations.

At 3.0 rounds per second with an 8-round magazine and 1.0-second reload, the Pandero Prime is a fast-firing revolver-style sidearm that rewards aggressive trigger use with rapid critical hits. The 1.0-second reload means effective uptime is high -- the reload completes quickly enough to maintain sustained pressure. Accuracy at 16 is consistent with most pistols, suited for close-to-medium range secondary use. Mastery rank 14 is required.

The 2.8x critical multiplier is one of the highest available on secondary weapons. At 30% base crit chance, roughly one in three shots crits without mods -- adding a moderate crit chance mod pushes this into highly reliable crit territory where the multiplier applies consistently.

## Acquisition

Pandero Prime components are obtained from Void Relics. The blueprint, barrel, and receiver drop from specific relics at varying rarities. Trading through [warframe.market](https://warframe.market) is a faster alternative. Check the current relic pool for which relics carry Pandero Prime components.

## Variants

The base [Pandero](/weapons/pandero) provides a lower mastery rank entry point with reduced crit multiplier. The Prime variant's 2.8x multiplier represents a substantial damage increase for crit builds and is worth the additional investment.

## Recommended builds

Given the 30% critical chance and 2.8x multiplier, critical builds are the most efficient use of Pandero Prime's mod slots.

Pure crit build:
- Hornet Strike for base damage
- Barrel Diffusion for multishot -- each additional projectile independently crits at the full base crit rate
- Lethal Torrent for combined fire rate and multishot bonus
- Primed Pistol Gambit or Pistol Gambit for critical chance
- Hollow Point for critical damage amplification
- Convulsion or Heated Charge for elemental damage

Because the 2.8x multiplier scales multiplicatively with Hollow Point, damage output on critical hits is substantially higher than non-crit hits. At 60%+ crit chance (achievable with a moderate crit mod), the majority of shots deal crit damage, making this interaction consistently impactful.

Hybrid builds using the 24% status chance benefit from dual-stat elemental mods like Convulsion (electricity + crit) or Frostbite (cold + crit), adding crit chance while enabling elemental status procs. Viral (Cold + Toxin) or Corrosive (Electric + Toxin) elemental pairs boost effective damage against faction-specific enemy types.

## Notes

The Pandero Prime functions best as a precision secondary rather than a spray weapon, given its 8-round magazine. Players who target high-value enemies or shield-gated Corpus units get more value from each magazine than those using it indiscriminately. The 1.0-second reload allows frequent magazine swaps without significant downtime.

The puncture-dominant damage profile makes the Pandero Prime naturally aligned against armored Grineer targets, where puncture procs reduce enemy damage output. Against Corpus, the relatively low impact component is less effective at cycling through shields quickly compared to impact-heavy secondaries.
