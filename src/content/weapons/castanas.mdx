## Overview

The [Castanas](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Castanas) are secondary throwing weapons that attach to surfaces and enemies, detonating on a separate command or on a timer. They deal 160 pure electric damage per detonation with no physical damage component. The 22% status chance enables reliable electric procs (Tesla proc), which chain electricity to nearby enemies and stagger them. The 8% critical chance and 1.5x multiplier do not support critical builds.

Castanas can be thrown and detonated manually, allowing players to place multiple charges before triggering them simultaneously. This "daisy chain" detonation pattern enables high burst damage against groups. As a throwing weapon, Castanas benefit from unique mechanics not available to standard firearms.

Riven disposition at 5/5 maximizes riven roll potential.

## Acquisition

The Castanas blueprint is available from the Market for credits. As a Mastery Rank 3 weapon, it is accessible to early-to-mid progression players. Crafting requires standard materials available from the inner star chart.

## Variants

The Sancti Castanas (Steel Meridian syndicate weapon) improves critical chance, critical multiplier, and status chance while adding a lifesteal passive on detonation. Players allied with Steel Meridian who have invested in syndicate standing should pursue the Sancti Castanas as the primary upgrade path.

## Recommended builds

**Tesla chain build:** Hornet Strike, Barrel Diffusion, Lethal Torrent, Jolt (electric), Pistol Pestilence (toxin for corrosive), Frostbite (cold), and Scorch. Electric procs from the 160 base damage create Tesla chains that jump to nearby enemies, applying electric status across groups. The 22% status chance reliably triggers Tesla chains per detonation, creating cascading crowd control across enemy clusters.

**Burst detonation build:** Place four to six Castanas on a dense enemy group before detonating simultaneously. Each detonation applies electric procs independently, creating chain lightning from multiple sources. This burst pattern delivers AOE crowd control and damage in one coordinated trigger press.

**Status primer:** Use Castanas as a status primer before switching to melee. Electric and corrosive (from toxin mods) procs accumulate from each detonation, setting up Condition Overload melee follow-ups.

## Synergies

- **Tesla proc chaining:** Each electric status (Tesla) proc chains to nearby enemies. In dense groups, a single Castanas detonation can propagate through 6-8 enemies via Tesla chains, applying electric status across the entire group from a single throw.
- **Volt's Electric Shield:** Bouncing Castanas throws through Volt's Electric Shield converts the electric damage to true electricity, boosting its damage in concert with Volt's amplification effect.
- **Manual detonation:** Unlike timed explosive weapons, Castanas detonate only when the player presses the alternate fire button (or when an attached enemy dies). This control allows strategic detonation timing.

## Notes

- The 2-round magazine means each "volley" consists of two Castanas. Reloading provides 2 more. In the daisy-chain burst pattern, throw all available Castanas before detonating.
- Pure electric damage pairs with toxin mods to form corrosive (electric + toxin) or with cold to form magnetic. Against Corpus, magnetic strips shields; against Grineer, corrosive strips armor.
- Castanas can attach to walls, floors, ceilings, and enemy bodies. Attached-to-enemy detonation deals bonus finisher-style damage when the enemy is in a vulnerable state. This interaction is limited but useful in specific stealth or finisher-focused builds.
- Riven disposition at 5/5 makes multishot (additional Castanas per throw) or status chance rivens particularly valuable. Additional throws per magazine increase the effective AOE burst potential dramatically.
