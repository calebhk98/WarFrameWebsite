## Overview

Kuva Tonkor is the Kuva Lich variant of the Tonkor grenade launcher, significantly upgrading the original weapon's critical chance and adding the Kuva system's bonus elemental damage type. The Tonkor platform fires bouncing grenades that detonate after a short delay, differentiating it from direct-detonation launchers like the Ogris. Kuva Tonkor raises the critical chance substantially while retaining the high-damage grenade mechanic that made the base Tonkor popular.

The damage breakdown includes 204 puncture, 227 slash, and 302 blast elemental damage per grenade, with no impact component. The blast elemental damage applies the Blast status effect -- a knockdown -- on proc, adding crowd control alongside the grenade's area-of-effect detonation. The 30% critical chance is the weapon's defining stat and enables a functional crit build in the launcher category, which is rare among grenade weapons. The 2.5x critical multiplier is moderate but provides meaningful burst output on critical hits.

Mastery Rank 13 is required to equip Kuva Tonkor. The single-round magazine demands reload discipline -- every shot requires a 1.5-second reload cycle -- but the 3.17 fire rate reflects the quick single-shot cadence rather than the per-reload timing.

The 30% critical chance supports a dedicated crit build in a weapon type where status builds are more common. With appropriate crit scaling mods, Kuva Tonkor reaches reliable critical rates that scale meaningfully with the 2.5x multiplier. At the same time, the 17% status chance is low for status-exclusive builds, making crit or hybrid approaches more efficient than pure status.

The 733 total damage per grenade before mods is high for a primary launcher and compounds with multishot mods that fire additional grenades per shot.

## Acquisition

Kuva Tonkor is obtained by vanquishing a Kuva Lich whose assigned weapon is the Kuva Tonkor. The Lich's weapon is determined at larvling creation. Once vanquished, the weapon is credited to the player with a randomly assigned damage bonus (25-60%) and a randomly selected bonus elemental type. The bonus element adds a flat percentage of total damage as additional elemental damage, which can be combined with modded elemental damage for improved procs.

Players can trade Kuva Lich weapons on the Trading Post, making it possible to acquire a preferred element and high bonus percentage without repeating the Lich acquisition loop personally.

## Variants

The base Tonkor is available from the Dojo's Bio Lab through clan research. It offers a lower base critical chance and no bonus elemental damage compared to the Kuva variant. Kuva Tonkor represents a substantial upgrade for players who invest in the Kuva Lich system.

No Tenet or Prime version of the Tonkor exists. Kuva Tonkor is the highest-tier version available.

## Recommended builds

A critical build uses Split Chamber or Vigilante Armaments for multishot alongside a secondary elemental mod for a combined damage type. With 30% crit chance and multishot producing additional grenade hits, the Kuva Tonkor reaches high burst potential in open areas where grenades can detonate freely. Vital Sense adds critical multiplier to amplify each crit trigger.

A hybrid crit-status build adds Malignant Force or Rime Rounds to push status chance higher while maintaining the crit core. At 17% base status chance, this requires several status mods to reach reliable proc rates, which compresses the available mod slots for crit scaling.

Cautious Shot is strongly recommended due to the grenade mechanic and close-range detonation risk, particularly in corridor-heavy tile sets.

## Notes

Kuva Tonkor grenades bounce off surfaces before detonating, which differentiates their trajectory from direct-detonation rockets. This bounce behavior enables indirect fire around corners in some mission layouts but requires awareness of grenade physics to avoid misfires. Grenades that fail to contact an enemy before detonating still deal area damage centered on the explosion point.

The 1-round magazine means every shot is followed immediately by a reload. Players should position carefully before firing and use the 1.5-second reload window to reposition or take cover between engagements.
