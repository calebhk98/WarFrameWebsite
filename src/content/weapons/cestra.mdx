## Overview

The [Cestra](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Cestra) is a secondary automatic pistol at Mastery Rank 4. It fires 8.33 rounds per second from a 60-round magazine, delivering 26 total damage per bullet: 5.2 impact and 20.8 slash (80% slash). The slash-dominant profile makes the Cestra effective at generating bleed procs, which bypass armor mitigation. The 20% status chance enables moderate proc application; the 6% critical chance and 1.6x multiplier are minimal.

Riven disposition at the maximum tier (5/5) provides excellent riven roll potential. The Supra Vandal and other alternatives offer more competitive endgame stats, but the Cestra with a strong riven can perform adequately in mid-game content.

## Acquisition

The Cestra blueprint is available from the Market for credits. As an MR4 weapon, it is accessible to early-mid game players. Crafting requires standard materials. The completed weapon can be purchased with platinum.

## Variants

The Secura Cestra (Perrin Sequence syndicate weapon) improves critical chance and multiplier, and adds a credit reward passive. No Prime or Vandal variant exists.

## Recommended builds

**Slash status build:** Hornet Strike, Barrel Diffusion, Lethal Torrent, Pistol Pestilence, Frostbite, Jolt, Scorch, and Primed Heated Charge. At 20% status chance and 8.33 fire rate, procs apply frequently. The 80% slash distribution generates bleed procs reliably, bypassing armor. Additional elemental procs create viral (toxin + cold) and heat for health reduction and damage-over-time.

**Condition Overload primer:** Use the Cestra as a status primer for melee Condition Overload. The 8.33 fire rate applies two to three distinct status types within 1-2 seconds, enabling immediate Condition Overload bonus on the first melee hit after the brief burst.

**Riven-enhanced build:** With a 5/5 disposition riven adding status chance or multishot, the Cestra becomes a competitive mid-game secondary. Multishot doubles bullet volume per second; status chance pushes proc reliability into near-guaranteed territory.

## Synergies

- **Slash bleed bypass:** The 80% slash composition generates bleed procs at 20% status chance per bullet (8.33 fire rate = approximately 1.67 procs per second). Against Grineer armored targets, sustained Cestra fire creates continuous bleed accumulation bypassing armor calculations.
- **Lethal Torrent + Barrel Diffusion:** Essential dual secondary mods that significantly improve the Cestra's effective output. Adding both improves bullet volume and fire rate simultaneously.
- **5/5 riven disposition:** The maximum disposition means any Cestra riven is worth evaluating. Multishot or status chance rivens provide the most impactful improvements to the weapon's baseline.

## Notes

- The 6% critical chance makes crit builds ineffective without significant riven investment. The Cestra is strictly a status-and-damage secondary.
- Accuracy of 33.3 is moderate. At close to medium range, bullets connect reliably. At long range, spread widens. Use within 20-25 meters for consistent hit registration.
- The 60-round magazine at 8.33 fire rate provides approximately 7.2 seconds of continuous fire. Combined with a 2.0-second reload, the Cestra sustains 78% uptime in fully-sustained fire scenarios.
- Mastery Rank 4 places the Cestra in early-mid game territory. Players at this level are typically building their first set of foundational mods -- the Cestra's straightforward stat profile makes it a good platform for learning status-focused secondary builds.
