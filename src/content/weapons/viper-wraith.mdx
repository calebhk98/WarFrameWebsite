## Overview

The Viper Wraith is the Wraith variant of the Viper auto-pistol, available at Mastery Rank 4. It is an improvement over the base Viper with higher damage per round (18 vs 12), a better critical profile (19% vs 15%, 2.0x vs 1.5x), and larger magazine (20 vs 15). At 14.38 fire rate with a 0.8-second reload, the Viper Wraith fires rapidly and reloads almost instantly. The 18 total damage per shot splits into 14.4 impact, 1.8 puncture, and 1.8 slash -- overwhelmingly impact.

The impact dominance means the Viper Wraith is most effective against Corpus shields, where impact deals bonus damage. The 19% critical chance and 2.0x multiplier produce useful crit output with Primed Pistol Gambit support. The 9% status chance is very low and procs are incidental rather than reliable. This is a high fire-rate impact secondary best suited for Corpus missions and early-to-mid game content.

The 0.8-second reload is the Viper Wraith's defining feature after its fire rate. The near-instant reload means the 20-round magazine at 14.38 fire rate (1.4-second magazine) empties and reloads in a near-seamless cycle -- the reload penalty is among the lowest of any secondary.

## Recommended builds

**Impact Corpus rapid fire**: Hornet Strike, Barrel Diffusion, Lethal Torrent, magnetic (cold + electric), and two more elemental mods. Magnetic deals bonus damage to Corpus shields and reduces shield capacity on proc. At 14.38+ fire rate with Lethal Torrent, the Viper Wraith strips Corpus shields in under a second.

**Fast reload sustained**: Hornet Strike, Barrel Diffusion, Lethal Torrent, Primed Pistol Gambit, and elemental mods. The 0.8-second reload means the weapon is almost always firing. Cycle through magazines rapidly during extended fights.

**Early game starting build**: Hornet Strike, Barrel Diffusion, and two elemental damage mods. Effective at low-difficulty content without requiring rare or primed mods. The Viper Wraith outperforms the base Viper with only basic modding.

## Notes

The 0.8-second reload is fast enough that proactively reloading when the magazine reaches 5-6 rounds costs very little time relative to the sustained fire benefit of entering the next engagement at full magazine. The 9% status chance does not justify status-enhancing mods -- allocate those slots to damage and fire rate. The Viper Wraith is an excellent early-to-mid game secondary that remains viable with good mods even into mid-difficulty Steel Path content.
