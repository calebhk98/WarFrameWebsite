## Overview

The Despair is a Stalker-themed secondary throwing weapon with a Slash-dominant damage profile: 46 Slash versus 8.7 Puncture and 2.9 Impact. This heavy Slash weighting makes the Despair effective at applying bleed procs against all enemy factions where Slash is effective, particularly Corpus and Infested. The 16% critical chance and 1.6x multiplier are modest, placing the Despair firmly in the status-primary camp rather than a crit platform. Fire rate of 3.33 rounds per second with a 10-round magazine and an exceptionally fast 0.75-second reload creates a burst-then-reload throwing pattern. Mastery Rank 4 is required.

The Despair is associated with Stalker, the recurring assassin enemy who hunts players marked for killing boss targets. As a throwing weapon, the Despair functions similarly to the Kunai, delivering individual projectile hits rather than full-auto spray. The Slash dominance means each hit has a chance to apply a bleed proc, and with 16% status chance, procs accumulate steadily over a 10-round magazine. The fast reload minimizes downtime, making the Despair a usable status-delivery tool between primary weapon engagements.

## Acquisition

The Despair drops from Stalker encounters. Stalker appears when players kill boss targets (assassination mission targets) and mark the player for retribution. Stalker has a chance to drop his signature weapons on death: the Despair (secondary), Dread (primary bow), and Hate (melee scythe). Farm Stalker by repeatedly completing assassination missions to trigger encounters. Check [the Despair Fandom wiki page](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Despair) for the current drop rate and encounter details.

## Variants

The Despair has no Prime, Kuva, or Tenet variant. Stalker's weapon set consists of Despair, Dread, and Hate -- none have Prime versions. Players seeking an upgrade path should look to other high-status throwing secondaries or the Hikou Prime for a more versatile throwing weapon.

## Recommended builds

**Slash/Viral build.** The 46 Slash base is the Despair's strongest asset. Equip Hornet Strike, Barrel Diffusion, Lethal Torrent, and two 60/60 status mods (Pistol Pestilence and Frostbite) for Viral. The 16% status ensures regular bleed and Viral procs per magazine. Slip Magazine increases ammo count.

**Status primer build.** Use the Despair to rapidly apply Slash procs to a target before switching to a Condition Overload melee. The fast 0.75-second reload and 10-round magazine generate enough procs to maximize Condition Overload stacks efficiently. Augur Seeker increases status chance further.

**Critical hybrid.** With Pistol Gambit and True Steel equivalent, the 16% base crit can reach functional levels, producing yellow crits regularly. Combine with Vital Sense for multiplier. The Slash damage still generates bleed procs even without dedicated status investment, providing persistent damage alongside crit hits.

## Synergies

The Despair's Slash weighting pairs well with frames that apply enemy vulnerability. Ash's Blade Storm augment increases all damage on marked targets; the Despair's bleed procs extend this window. Mesa's Shooting Gallery buffs secondary fire rate, compounding the Despair's throwing cadence.

## Notes

- Stalker encounters trigger from assassination mission boss kills -- run any assassination mission repeatedly to increase encounter frequency.
- The 0.75-second reload is one of the fastest among secondary weapons; use the brief reload window to close distance or reposition.
- Zero Impact means no shield-disruption procs; the Despair is weakest against heavy Corpus shielded units without elemental supplementation.
