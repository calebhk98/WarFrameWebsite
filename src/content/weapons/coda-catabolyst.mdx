## Overview

The [Coda Catabolyst](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Coda_Catabolyst) is the Jade Shadows enhanced version of the Catabolyst at Mastery Rank 17. It fires a corrosive stream dealing 56 corrosive damage per hit at 12 rounds per second. The 50% status chance is the highest available for a corrosive-stream secondary, enabling near-guaranteed corrosive proc application every other hit. The 2.9x critical multiplier rewards critical builds when combined with the 11% critical chance. Like the base Catabolyst, an empty-magazine explosion occurs on full magazine depletion.

Obtained through the Jade Shadows content update. Check the [Coda Catabolyst Wiki page](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Coda_Catabolyst) for current acquisition sources associated with Jade Shadows progression rewards.

## Variants

The Coda Catabolyst is the enhanced form of the base [Catabolyst](/weapons/catabolyst) (MR11). The base version has 43% status chance versus the Coda's 50%. Both share the empty-magazine explosion mechanic.

## Recommended builds

**Maximum armor strip:** Hornet Strike, Barrel Diffusion, Lethal Torrent, Pistol Pestilence (additional toxin), Jolt (electric), Scorch (heat), Frostbite (cold for viral formation). The 50% status chance at 12 fire rate applies corrosive procs multiple times per second. Steel Path Grineer armor drops within 1-2 seconds of sustained fire on a target.

**Explosion maximizer:** Add mods that boost corrosive elemental damage to amplify the empty-magazine explosion. Empty the 37-round magazine deliberately before each reload to trigger the AOE explosion, using the stream to soften targets and the explosion for burst group damage.

**Critical corrosive:** Primed Pistol Gambit and Primed Target Cracker push the 11% base crit toward yellow-crit frequency. The 2.9x multiplier generates substantial crit damage on armor-stripped targets where full base damage applies.

## Synergies

- **Armor strip for team:** Rapidly stripping Grineer armor benefits all damage-dealing teammates. In group Steel Path, a Coda Catabolyst user provides essential armor strip that enables the entire squad's damage to apply without armor mitigation.
- **Condition Overload melee:** Apply corrosive and heat (from elemental mods) within the first second of fire, then switch to melee for amplified Condition Overload damage.
- **Empty explosion timing:** Coordinate the explosion with enemy cluster positioning. Empty the magazine when multiple enemies are grouped near a primary target to maximize explosion AOE coverage.

## Notes

- Mastery Rank 17 is in the veteran range. The Coda Catabolyst's 50% status chance is the highest available for this weapon category and justifies the high mastery requirement.
- The explosion at magazine depletion scales with corrosive damage mods. Investments in elemental damage mods amplify both stream and explosion simultaneously.
- Accuracy of 6.25 reflects the stream's spray pattern. Effective range is close-to-medium -- maintain within 15 meters of targets for reliable hit confirmation.

The Coda Catabolyst shares the base Catabolyst's empty-magazine explosion mechanic but with improved elemental values. The Coda version's higher status chance (50%) ensures the explosive nova on magazine emptying reliably applies multiple procs to nearby enemies, amplifying the area denial utility of the weapon's signature gimmick.
