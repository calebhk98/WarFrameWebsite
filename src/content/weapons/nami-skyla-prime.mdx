## Overview

Nami Skyla Prime is the prime variant of the Nami Skyla dual swords, offering a fast attack speed combined with above-average status chance. The weapon's damage distribution favors puncture heavily, with 126 puncture dominating the 18 impact and 36 slash per hit. This profile provides solid performance against armor (puncture reduces armor value on proc) while the 34% status chance makes elemental builds practical. At mastery rank 11, it sits in the mid-tier prime range and is a competitive choice for status-oriented melee builds.

The 1.33 attack speed is a defining characteristic. Dual sword animations chain rapidly, making it straightforward to accumulate melee combo counter stacks and stack elemental status procs on groups of enemies. The 22% critical chance sits at a point where investing in crit chance mods brings it above the effective threshold; combined with the 2.0x critical multiplier, crit builds are possible but less efficient than status or hybrid approaches.

Status chance at 34% means applying Viral (Cold + Toxin) or Corrosive (Electric + Toxin) requires only moderate status investment to reliably proc on most attacks, especially with high attack speed driving rapid hits.

## Variants

The base [Nami Skyla](/weapons/nami-skyla) offers lower stats with identical dual-sword handling. The prime variant improves damage output and status chance significantly, making it the preferred version once relics are accessible.

## Recommended builds

Given the 34% status chance and 1.33 attack speed, status and hybrid builds outperform pure critical setups in most scenarios.

Status-focused build priorities:
- Pressure Point for base damage scaling
- Primed Fever Strike and Voltaic Strike for Corrosive combination (Electricity + Toxin)
- Condition Overload as primary damage multiplier once multiple status types are applied
- Drifting Contact to extend combo duration and maintain Condition Overload stacks
- Berserker Fury to amplify attack speed further on critical hits

Alternatively, a Viral build (Cold + Toxin) pairs with Slash procs if mods are arranged to add Slash to the elemental distribution, enabling bleeding procs that bypass armor scaling on health-type enemies.

For critical hybrid builds, adding Blood Rush benefits from the high attack speed and combo accumulation, pushing effective crit chance well above the base 22% after a few seconds of sustained combat.

## Notes

Dual swords in Warframe use stances that affect both damage multipliers and attack patterns. The two primary stances compatible with this weapon category -- Crossing Snakes and Swirling Tiger -- influence how the weapon interacts with groups of enemies. Swirling Tiger's wider sweeping attacks benefit status builds that want to apply procs to multiple targets simultaneously. Crossing Snakes offers more targeted attack chains with higher per-strike multipliers.

Nami Skyla Prime performs well in mid-game star chart content and scales reasonably into Steel Path with sufficient forma investment. Players seeking further melee options should review other prime dual swords based on available relics and desired damage profiles.
