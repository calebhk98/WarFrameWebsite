## Overview

Panthera Prime is the prime variant of the Panthera sawglaive launcher, a primary weapon that fires disc-shaped projectiles capable of ricocheting between enemies. The 90 puncture per disc dominates the damage profile over the 20 impact and 10 slash. The weapon's defining mechanic is the ricochet behavior -- discs bounce between enemies applying damage and status procs to multiple targets per shot, making it significantly more efficient than its single-target statistics suggest in densely packed groups.

At 3.67 rounds per second, the Panthera Prime fires rapidly for a projectile weapon. The 80 accuracy rating is high, meaning discs travel precisely to the aimed point before beginning their ricochet chain. The 30% status chance applied per enemy hit (including ricochets) produces substantial proc output in groups. Critical performance at 18% chance and 2.0x multiplier is modest, making status and base damage builds outperform crit-focused configurations. Mastery rank 14 is required.

Each disc that ricochets off an enemy applies a fresh damage and status check to the next target in its path. Against tight groups, a single disc can hit multiple enemies in sequence, multiplying effective status application rate well beyond the 30% per-hit figure in single-target encounters.

## Acquisition

Panthera Prime components are obtained through Void Relic farming. The blueprint, blade, and receiver components drop from specific relics. Trading through [warframe.market](https://warframe.market) is an alternative acquisition route. Check the current relic drop tables to identify active farming locations.

## Variants

The base [Panthera](/weapons/panthera) is available at a lower mastery rank with reduced damage and stats. The Prime version improves overall damage and the ricochet mechanic functions identically, making the Prime the clear upgrade for any serious investment.

## Recommended builds

The 30% status chance per hit combined with ricochet mechanics hitting multiple enemies makes status builds effective even at moderate investment levels.

Status and base damage build:
- Serration for base damage
- Split Chamber for multishot -- additional discs per shot, each ricochets independently
- Vigilante Armaments for further multishot
- Infected Clip (toxin) and High Voltage (electricity) for Corrosive against Grineer
- Galvanized Aptitude for stacking damage bonus as status types are applied across targets

The multishot interaction is particularly strong: each additional disc from Split Chamber or Vigilante Armaments ricochets independently, multiplying the effective number of enemy hits per trigger pull and accelerating status proc accumulation in tight groups.

For Viral builds targeting Grineer health:
- Cold and Toxin mods for Viral combination
- Viral halves effective enemy health for 6 seconds; with ricochet applying procs across groups, this debuffs entire rooms simultaneously

Critical builds are less optimal given the 18% base and 2.0x multiplier, but a basic crit mod (Point Strike) improves consistency for single-target scenarios when groups are not available for ricochet chaining.

## Notes

The Panthera Prime's effectiveness scales sharply with enemy density. In hallways and tight rooms, ricocheted discs chain through groups efficiently. In open-area missions with spread-out enemies, the weapon performs closer to its individual-target statistics. Mission type selection influences performance relative to alternatives.

The 2.4-second reload is moderate for the fire rate. Players burning through magazine capacity quickly in high-density encounters should consider reload speed mods if ammunition management becomes limiting.

Panthera Prime suits players who enjoy projectile-based weapons with multi-target mechanics. Its puncture profile and ricochet interaction suit Grineer-heavy content particularly well, and it performs above its stat sheet in any mission type featuring tightly clustered enemies.
