## Overview

Kesheg is a polearm-category melee weapon with a standout 19% critical chance and 2.5x critical multiplier, making it one of the stronger crit platforms at Mastery Rank 7. Its long range of 2.9 m is typical of polearms and enables reliable multi-target coverage with wide swing arcs. The puncture-dominant damage distribution makes it effective against armored Grineer enemies, while the 23% status chance allows elemental proc builds when Weeping Wounds is equipped.

The 19% base critical chance reaches reliable crit rates with Blood Rush at moderate combo, and the 2.5x multiplier provides high burst damage on critical strikes. The low attack speed is offset by the wide polearm swing arc, which hits multiple enemies per swing.

## Acquisition

Kesheg is available as a blueprint from the in-game Market for Credits. Components require standard resources obtainable from mid-tier planetary nodes. Construction takes 12 hours in the Foundry. The Mastery Rank 7 requirement gates access until players have ranked up several earlier weapons.

## Variants

No Wraith, Vandal, or Prime variant of the Kesheg has been released as of the current game version.

## Recommended builds

A crit-focused build centers on Blood Rush, True Steel, and Primed Pressure Point for scaling crit chance and base damage. Condition Overload adds significant damage multiplication against enemies affected by status procs from Weeping Wounds or a secondary weapon. Organ Shatter adds the crit multiplier boost that complements Blood Rush's crit chance scaling.

A status-hybrid build using Weeping Wounds with two elemental mods produces Corrosive or Viral reliably at moderate combo counts. Weeping Wounds' status scaling combined with the 23% base status chance produces reliable procs even before the combo reaches high levels.

## Notes

Kesheg's polearm stance mods (Shimmering Blight or Bleeding Willow) influence the weapon's combo strings and spin attack range. Shimmering Blight provides a stagger effect on its main combo, which can interrupt enemy attacks in close quarters. The 2.9 m range is sufficient to hit enemies through doorways and around minor cover, making it practical for corridor-heavy mission types like Survival and Defense.
