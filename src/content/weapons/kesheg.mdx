## Overview

Kesheg is a polearm melee weapon with a puncture-dominant damage profile and above-average critical multiplier for its mastery rank tier. Polearms as a weapon class deliver wide sweeping attacks that cover a broad arc, making them effective against multiple enemies simultaneously. Kesheg's 2.9 m range is consistent with the polearm category and enables reliable multi-target coverage in tight mission corridors.

The 19% critical chance and 2.5x critical multiplier give Kesheg the statistical foundation for a crit-focused build at Mastery Rank 7, which is relatively early in the game's progression. Most crit-focused polearms at this tier offer lower multipliers, making Kesheg's 2.5x figure notable. The 23% status chance allows elemental proc builds to function alongside Weeping Wounds, which scales status chance multiplicatively as the melee combo counter increases.

The puncture-dominant physical distribution -- 96.4 impact, 120.5 puncture, 24.1 slash -- provides the best single-hit performance against heavily armored Grineer targets, where puncture damage bypasses a portion of armor damage reduction.

The 2.5x critical multiplier is the weapon's primary competitive advantage over similarly ranked polearms. With Blood Rush adding combo-scaling crit chance, the 19% base chance reaches reliable rates by the second rotation of a standard melee combo string.

The 0.833 attack speed is typical for polearms. Polearm wide-swing animations hit multiple enemies in a single swing arc, compensating for the below-standard attack speed with effective multi-target damage per animation cycle.

## Acquisition

The Kesheg blueprint is available from the in-game Market for credits. After purchasing, the weapon is crafted in the Foundry using standard resources available through mid-tier star chart planetary nodes. Mastery Rank 7 is required to equip Kesheg. The weapon is fully obtainable through solo play without Void Relic farming or Trading Post transactions.

Construction takes standard Foundry build time. No special research or syndicate standing is required.

## Variants

No Wraith, Vandal, Kuva, Tenet, or Prime variant of the Kesheg has been released. Kesheg exists in a single version as of the current game state.

## Recommended builds

A crit-focused build centers on Blood Rush and Primed Pressure Point as the damage core, with Organ Shatter adding critical multiplier and True Steel contributing additional critical chance. At moderate combo counts, Blood Rush brings effective critical chance well above the 19% base, and the 2.5x multiplier delivers high burst damage on each crit. Condition Overload can be added alongside a status-applying secondary weapon to multiply damage further.

A status-hybrid build uses Weeping Wounds alongside two elemental mods to construct Corrosive (Electricity + Toxin) or Viral (Cold + Toxin). The 23% base status chance with Weeping Wounds' combo-scaling reaches reliable proc rates by mid-combo. Corrosive is effective against high-armor Grineer in Steel Path missions; Viral reduces enemy maximum health and synergizes well with faction-specific damage types.

For newer players, a simpler build using Primed Pressure Point, Pressure Point, and two elemental mods with a basic crit chance mod provides consistent performance across normal star chart missions without requiring rare mods.

## Notes

Polearm stance mods compatible with Kesheg include Shimmering Blight and Bleeding Willow. Shimmering Blight provides a stagger effect on its main combo sequence, which interrupts enemy attacks in close quarters and reduces the risk of taking hits during extended melee engagements. Bleeding Willow emphasizes wider spin attacks for increased multi-target coverage. Stance choice depends on whether single-target control or multi-target sweeping is the priority.

Kesheg's 2.9 m range allows it to connect hits through minor cover and reach enemies in adjacent doorways without requiring the player to advance to point-blank range. This practical reach advantage is particularly useful in Defense mission types where maintaining positioning is important.
