## Overview

The Gotva Prime is a Prime primary with a unique pure-Slash damage profile: 25 Slash per round, zero Impact, zero Puncture. The extremely fast 13.33 rounds-per-second fire rate combined with 27% status chance creates rapid Slash bleed proc accumulation -- among the fastest bleed proc generation rates available in the primary category. The 23% critical chance and 2.5x multiplier provide a functional crit platform alongside the status output. The 2.0-second reload and 100 accuracy round out the profile. Mastery Rank 14 is required.

Zero physical damage types other than Slash means the Gotva Prime deals only Slash damage and generates only Slash procs alongside elemental procs from elemental mods. Slash bleed procs deal armor-bypassing damage over time based on a percentage of the total physical damage, making the Gotva Prime highly effective against high-armor targets where Slash bypass mitigates armor scaling. At 13.33 rounds/second with 27% status, Slash procs and elemental procs apply extremely rapidly, quickly stacking Condition Overload and Galvanized Aptitude to high levels for damage amplification. Mastery Rank 14 positions this as an endgame primary for players with established mod collections.

## Variants

The base Gotva is the standard version of this primary, available at lower mastery rank through earlier means. The Gotva Prime improves on the base weapon with higher damage, crit, and status values. No Kuva or Tenet variant exists for the Gotva family.

## Recommended builds

**Slash/Viral Galvanized build.** The pure Slash base and 27% status make the Gotva Prime the ideal Galvanized Aptitude platform. Equip Serration, Galvanized Chamber, Galvanized Aptitude, and four 60/60 elemental mods for Viral + Slash-boosting combination. At 13.33 rounds/second, multiple proc types apply within the first second of fire, rapidly stacking Galvanized Aptitude damage. Viral amplifies health damage alongside Slash bleeds.

**Hunter Munitions crit/Slash build.** At 23% base crit with Point Strike and Vital Sense, Hunter Munitions converts each crit into an additional Slash proc. The existing 27% status already generates Slash procs from the Slash-only damage type; Hunter Munitions doubles the proc rate on critical hits. Equip Serration, Split Chamber, Vital Sense, Point Strike, Hunter Munitions, and two Viral mods.

**Galvanized Aptitude sustained status.** The Gotva Prime's fastest-rate Slash proc generation makes it ideal for sustained Galvanized Aptitude stacking across extended engagements. Equip Galvanized Aptitude, two Viral 60/60 mods, Serration, and Galvanized Chamber. The rapid proc accumulation means full Aptitude stacks establish within 2-3 seconds of sustained fire.

## Synergies

Saryn's Spores spread Toxic procs from affected targets; Gotva Prime's rapid Slash procs trigger Spore spreading synergy across grouped enemies. Rhino's Roar amplifies Slash damage proportionally, benefiting each round's bleed proc source damage. Wisp's Haste Mote further amplifies the already-high fire rate, compounding proc application speed.

## Notes

- Zero Impact and Puncture means the Gotva Prime generates only Slash and elemental procs; all damage mitigation comes only from elemental proc and armor interaction.
- At 13.33 rounds/second, the Gotva Prime exhausts its magazine quickly; note the magazine size and plan reload windows.
