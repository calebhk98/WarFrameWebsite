## Overview

Dual-wielded Arch-gun pistols offering a high fire rate and large 64-round magazine. The paired configuration doubles the original Decurion's sustained output, accepting reduced accuracy as the trade-off for dramatically increased damage per second. Introduced in Update 15.5 (2014-11-27), the Dual Decurion is notable for dual pistol configuration; 64-round magazine for sustained engagement; fast 1.53s reload minimizes downtime.

The single Decurion design was deemed insufficient for sustained space engagements. Tenno engineers paired two units, accepting the reduced accuracy inherent in dual-wielded platforms as a trade-off for dramatically increased sustained damage output and continuous suppression capability.

## Acquisition

Blueprint from Market for 35,000 credits. Component parts crafted from standard Archwing resources including Orokin Cells, Alloy Plate, and Rubedo. Build cost 45,000 credits. The Dual Decurion requires Mastery Rank 1 to equip.

Arch-gun weapons require either standard crafting resources, Syndicate standing for component parts, or specialized mission drops. Once crafted, equipping the Dual Decurion in Archwing uses the standard Arch-gun slot. To use it as a Heavy Weapon in ground missions or Railjack combat, a Gravimag must be installed. Gravimags are available from Clan Dojos via Energy Lab research, or from Arbitrations as rotation rewards.

## Variants

Prisma Dual Decurions is the Baro Ki'Teer variant with improved critical chance (30%), status chance (24%), and fire rate. No Prime variant exists.

The Arch-gun family spans weapons from Tenno engineering (Corvas, Fluctus, Velocitus), Grineer design (Grattler, Kuva line), and Entrati craftsmanship (Cortege, Mausolon, Morgha), each with distinct stat profiles suited to different engagement contexts.

## Recommended builds

Large magazine and high fire rate favor sustained-fire builds. Hollowed Bullets and Critical Focus maximize the 28% critical chance. For Corpus missions, Magnetic damage (Cold + Electricity) leverages the Impact-dominant physical distribution against Corpus shields.

**Core Arch-gun mods to consider:**

- Rubedo-Lined Barrel -- +165% Arch-gun damage (primary damage mod for all builds)
- Hollowed Bullets -- +120% critical chance (essential for any crit-focused build)
- Critical Focus -- +60% critical multiplier while aiming
- Modified Munitions -- +90% status chance (for status-focused builds)
- Arch-gun Ace -- +165% fire rate (useful on slow-fire weapons to improve feel)
- Parallax Scope -- +30% critical chance while aiming
- Charged Bullets -- +60% magazine capacity
- Superior Defenses -- reduces reload time
- Conductive Coils -- adds Electricity damage
- Thermite Rounds -- adds Heat damage

Prioritize Rubedo-Lined Barrel as the first mod in any Arch-gun build. Follow with critical or status mods based on the weapon's stat profile. Elemental mods provide faction-specific damage type flexibility.

## Notes

The Dual Decurion operates in the Arch-gun slot, used in Archwing missions and as a Heavy Weapon on the ground via Gravimag. In Railjack (Empyrean) missions, the Arch-gun serves as the primary armament during space combat against fighters and Crewships.

Effective in Railjack corridor combat where accuracy limitations are minimized by proximity. The large magazine suits sustained suppression during boarding defense.

Archwing combat system notes: Archwing missions require completing The Archwing quest. Four Archwings are available -- Itzal (mobility/stealth), Amesha (tank/support), Elytron (AoE damage), and Odonata (balanced starter). Itzal's mobility allows rapid repositioning to compensate for the reduced accuracy at longer ranges. Elytron pairs for area denial while Dual Decurions handle priority targets.

The weapon's Riven disposition is 4/5 (high). Riven dispositions range from 0 (absolute minimum, strong weapon) to 5 (maximum, weak weapon). DE adjusts dispositions quarterly based on weapon usage rates.
