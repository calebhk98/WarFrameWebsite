## Overview

The [Afentis](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Afentis) is a Duviri bow primary weapon that fires a single arrow per shot, dealing 100 total damage: 60 slash, 20 impact, and 20 puncture. The 26% critical chance and 2.2x multiplier support effective critical builds, while the 24% status chance provides moderate proc application. Bows deliver maximum damage at full charge, with the Afentis's 0.83 draw rate providing fast charge speed compared to many bows.

The 4-arrow magazine and 1.8-second reload are standard for a bow. As a Duviri weapon, the Afentis is obtained through the Circuit or Duviri Experience rather than the Market.

## Acquisition

The Afentis is obtained through the Duviri Paradox game mode. It appears as a Circuit reward or Duviri Experience bounty reward. Players must complete the introductory Duviri quest to access these reward sources. Check the [Afentis Wiki page](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Afentis) for the current drop table and rotation.

## Variants

No Prime, Vandal, or Wraith variant of the Afentis exists. Duviri weapons do not currently have Prime forms. Players seeking bows with higher damage should compare the Cinta (also Duviri, 800 per shot) or Cernos Prime for different stat profiles.

## Recommended builds

**Critical slash build:** Serration, Split Chamber, Hunter Munitions, Point Strike, Vital Sense, and two elemental mods for viral or heat. At 26% critical chance, Hunter Munitions reliably converts crits to slash procs, generating armor-bypassing bleed on a proportion of shots. Vital Sense amplifies crit multiplier to deliver meaningful damage per critical arrow.

**Headshot critical:** Add Argon Scope for headshot-triggered critical chance boost. At 100 accuracy, precision headshot delivery is consistent in aimed mode. Argon Scope's headshot bonus pushes effective crit into orange-crit territory during sustained targeted fire.

**Status hybrid:** Replace Hunter Munitions with Galvanized Aptitude. The 24% status chance combined with Galvanized's conditional multishot stacks provides increasing proc rates per kill. Viral and heat procs accumulate reliably at full Galvanized stacks.

## Synergies

- **Wisp's Haste Mote:** Increases attack speed, which for bows translates to faster draw speed. The Afentis's 0.83 base draw becomes even faster with Haste Mote, enabling rapid sequential shots.
- **Hunter Munitions:** At 26% crit, Hunter Munitions generates consistent slash procs per crit hit. Slash procs from a 60-base-slash weapon with Hunter Munitions deal strong bleed damage against armored enemies.
- **Volt's Electric Shield:** Arrows passing through Volt's shield gain bonus electric damage. Combined with the Afentis's existing elemental mods, this bonus elevates per-arrow damage significantly.

## Notes

- Bows require full charge for maximum damage. The 0.83 draw speed is fast by bow standards -- a slight hold before release ensures full-charge delivery without significant time investment per shot.
- The 4-arrow magazine is small. Prioritize reloading between engagements rather than mid-fight, as the 1.8-second reload window is vulnerable if started during active enemy fire.
- Slash at 60% of total physical damage benefits from bleed proc mechanics. Against armored Grineer, slash procs bypass Ferrite armor mitigation, making the Afentis competitive against armored targets despite not having puncture dominance.
