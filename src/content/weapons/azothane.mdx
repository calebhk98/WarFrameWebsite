## Overview

The Azothane is a two-handed nikana (heavy sword) melee weapon in Warframe that requires no mastery rank to equip, making it accessible from early progression while offering statistics competitive into mid-game content. Its total base damage of 170 per swing is distributed across impact (51), puncture (85), and slash (34), with puncture as the primary component at 50% of total damage.

The Azothane's standout statistics are its 34% critical chance and 3.0x critical multiplier -- a combination that places it among the stronger critical-oriented melee weapons in its class. Heavy attacks in particular scale dramatically with these numbers, as heavy attack mods further amplify the multiplier. The 3.2-meter range is generous, enabling sweeping strikes that consistently hit multiple grouped enemies.

As a two-handed nikana, the Azothane uses two-handed nikana stances: Wise Razor and Blind Justice. These stance mods provide unique combo sequences with long reach and powerful charged strikes. Blind Justice in particular is known for its heavy attack cancel technique, which maximizes DPS on single high-health targets.

## Acquisition

The Azothane blueprint is purchasable from the in-game Market using credits. The Foundry build time is 12 hours. Component resources are standard mid-game materials. The weapon is also available for Platinum as a fully built purchase. At MR0, any player can begin gathering materials immediately after account creation.

## Variants

The Azothane does not have a Prime variant documented at this time. Players seeking two-handed nikana alternatives may consider the Tatsu Prime or Dragon Nikana Prime for different stat profiles and Prime aesthetics. The Gram Prime offers similar heavy sword handling with different crit/status balance.

## Recommended builds

**Heavy Attack Crit Build:** Organ Shatter (critical multiplier) and Killing Blow (heavy attack damage) are mandatory. Blood Rush (combo-scaling CC) is the cornerstone mod on two-handed nikanas, as the combo counter multiplies the base 34% CC to reliable orange and red crit territory at combo tiers above 5x. Add Corrupt Charge for an instant 5x combo at the start. Drifting Contact maintains the combo counter between attacks.

**Sustained Crit Melee Build:** Equip Primed Pressure Point, Blood Rush, Organ Shatter, Weeping Wounds, and two elemental mods for Viral or Corrosive. The combination of escalating CC and status chance during combos makes sustained strings increasingly effective. The 3.2m range sweeps entire groups, applying stacked Viral procs to multiple targets simultaneously.

The 22% status chance enables meaningful status application and pairs well with Condition Overload for additional damage scaling with each unique status type on the target.

## Notes

The Azothane's MR0 requirement combined with high crit stats makes it an unusually powerful early-game weapon, capable of handling mid-game content without Forma investment when properly modded with basic crit mods.

Two-handed nikanas use a unique heavy attack cancel technique where performing a heavy attack and immediately canceling the animation with a dodge maintains the damage output while skipping the recovery frames. This technique significantly increases practical DPS on the Azothane.

At 1.17 attack speed, the Azothane is faster than most two-handed heavy weapons, making stance combos flow smoothly.
