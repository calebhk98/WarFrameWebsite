## Overview

The Vericres is a claw-category melee weapon with a strong puncture bias. Its 180 total damage per hit divides into 21.6 impact, 129.6 puncture, and 28.8 slash -- the puncture component exceeds 70% of total damage. Claw weapons with high puncture excel against Grineer and armored targets where puncture's armor-penetration modifier reduces effective armor value before damage calculation.

At Mastery Rank 8, the Vericres has sufficient mods available to push its 22% critical chance and 20% status chance toward meaningful build synergies. The 2.0x critical multiplier is baseline for the claw category but scales effectively with Blood Rush in extended combo builds. The 1.8-meter range is typical of claws, requiring very close engagement.

Claw weapons deliver rapid multi-strike combos where the hit rate compensates via many proc opportunities per second. The Vericres benefits from this multi-hit pattern by applying corrosive or other elemental procs rapidly across a target.

## Acquisition

The Vericres blueprint is available from the in-game Market. Build time is 12 hours. No quest unlock is required.

## Variants

No Prime, Vandal, or Wraith variant currently exists for the Vericres. The Venka Prime offers a similar claw-puncture profile with improved critical stats as an upgrade path.

## Recommended builds

**Corrosive crit build**: Equip Blood Rush, Organ Shatter, Primed Pressure Point, and elemental mods for corrosive with Weeping Wounds. The 22% base crit chance reaches 60-80% at x2-x3 combo with Blood Rush. Corrosive strips Grineer armor to zero in six procs, after which the raw puncture damage deals full value against exposed health.

**Viral-slash approach**: Weeping Wounds for status, cold + toxin (Viral) elemental, and Hunter Munitions for slash proc generation from crits. Viral halves enemy max health; slash bleeds bypass armor. This performs reliably in Steel Path content.

**Beginner foundation**: Pressure Point, Fury (attack speed), Fever Strike (toxin), and North Wind (cold) for Viral. No rare mods required.

## Notes

Claw stance mods shape the Vericres's combo string significantly. The specific combo inputs and timing of multi-hit bursts affect proc density. Bullet jump into melee range, then execute full combos and use the combo duration window to maintain the counter between consecutive enemy groups.


Claw weapons require the player to be at very close quarters to enemies -- typically 1.8 meters or less for consistent contact. The Vericres's melee engagement requires either bullet-jumping into groups, using warframe abilities to pull enemies close, or navigating cover effectively to intercept enemies. In missions where enemies are spread out, the short claw range creates positioning challenges that longer-reach weapons avoid.

The Vericres's 22% critical chance with Blood Rush creates substantial crit potential at maintained combos. Players who build around sustained combo uptime -- using combo duration mods like Drifting Contact and maintaining engagement without breaking the counter -- unlock the Vericres's full crit scaling. The weapon is best in missions with continuous enemy flow (Survival, Defense) rather than discrete-encounter missions where combo resets frequently.
