## Overview

Paris Prime is the prime variant of the Paris bow, delivering an exceptional 45% critical chance with a slash-dominant damage profile that makes it one of the strongest critical bows in the game at its mastery level. The 288 slash damage per arrow dwarfs the 63 puncture and 9 impact, establishing slash procs as the primary damage mechanism in status-enabled builds. Combined with 45% base crit, the Paris Prime reaches near-guaranteed critical hits with minimal mod investment, leaving mod slots for damage scaling rather than crit chance.

Bows operate differently from automatic or semi-auto guns: arrows must be charged to deal full damage, and full charge produces the listed statistics. The 0.7-second nock time between arrows is fast by bow standards, enabling a cadence of approximately one arrow per second at full draw speed. The 20% status chance is below the level needed for reliable status proc application without additional mods, but Hunter Munitions fills this role by converting critical hits into slash procs, bypassing armor scaling at high crit rates. Mastery rank 8 provides notably early access for a prime weapon of this capability.

The 45% critical chance means at base, nearly half of all arrows deal critical damage. Adding a single crit chance mod like Point Strike pushes effective crit chance to approximately 67%, and Primed versions push beyond 80%. At these levels, nearly every shot crits without additional setup.

## Acquisition

Paris Prime components drop from Void Relics. The blueprint, lower limb, and upper limb components are distributed across multiple relic types. Trading through [warframe.market](https://warframe.market) or in-game trade chat provides an alternative. The Paris Prime has been in the game since early access, so components are generally available and reasonably priced.

## Variants

The base [Paris](/weapons/paris) is accessible at mastery rank 0 with substantially lower statistics, serving as the introductory bow. Paris Prime is the recommended form once Void Relic farming is accessible.

## Recommended builds

The 45% critical chance makes crit builds the natural fit, with Hunter Munitions enabling slash proc synergy.

Critical plus Hunter Munitions build:
- Serration for base damage
- Split Chamber for multishot -- additional arrows per shot, each independently crits and procs
- Vigilante Armaments for further multishot and set bonus critical chance
- Point Strike for critical chance
- Vital Sense for critical damage multiplier
- Hunter Munitions to convert critical hits into slash procs, bypassing armor
- Galvanized Scope for stacking headshot damage bonus on kills

With Split Chamber and Vigilante Armaments providing two or three additional arrows per shot, and each arrow critting at 60%+ and proccing slash through Hunter Munitions, the effective damage per trigger pull is substantially multiplied beyond the single-arrow stat block.

Elemental additions like Rime Rounds (cold) plus Infected Clip (toxin) for Viral further amplify damage against health pools debuffed by Viral's health-halving effect. Heat and Cold are also options depending on content requirements.

## Notes

The Paris Prime requires a full charge for maximum damage. Partial draws deal reduced damage. Players new to bows should practice full-charge timing before committing mod resources, as the weapon's stats assume full charge performance.

Arrow travel time is a consideration at range. Unlike hitscan weapons, arrows travel at finite velocity and require leading moving targets. At close range this is negligible; at distances above 30 meters against fast-moving enemies, leading becomes a skill factor.

The Paris Prime is a strong contender for early-to-mid game players who can access the relics, as mastery rank 8 is significantly lower than many prime weapons with comparable output. Veterans sometimes use it as a reference standard for bow comparisons against later-release bows in the same category.
