## Overview

The Dera Vandal is an assault rifle variant focusing on puncture damage with high fire rate and magazine capacity. It excels in sustained damage scenarios and works well for clearing mid-level missions. The 6.4 impact, 1.6 puncture, and 24 slash damage distribution emphasizes slash with respectable impact components. The unusual damage profile provides interesting building opportunities focused on different primary components.

The Dera Vandal's 11.25 fire rate is high for an assault rifle, enabling rapid target suppression and burst-focused gameplay. The 60 round magazine is generous, allowing extended fire without reloading. The 1.8 reload time is reasonable, encouraging mobile playstyle with occasional reload intervals. The combination of high fire rate and substantial magazine creates sustained suppression platform.

With 30% status chance, the Dera Vandal supports diverse mod configurations. Players can build for pure damage amplification, status effects, or hybrid approaches. The weapon's high fire rate and magazine capacity combine well with status mods, allowing rapid application of environmental effects across enemy groups. Elemental mod combinations multiply the weapon's effectiveness.

This weapon remains accessible and effective throughout early-to-mid game content. The Dera Vandal's emphasis on slash damage makes it particularly valuable when facing high-armor Grineer units. Many veterans keep a Dera Vandal forma'd for specific mission types where sustained, high fire-rate support proves tactically valuable. The weapon remains competitive for players who prefer suppression-focused builds.

The weapon's straightforward mod path and accessible mastery requirement make it educational for players learning weapon building. The Dera Vandal's consistent performance encourages experimentation with different mod loadouts and damage type combinations. Some players specialize in status-focused Dera Vandal builds.

Forma investment creates capable mid-game and sortie-level weapon platform.
