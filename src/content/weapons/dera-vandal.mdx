## Overview

The Dera Vandal is a Vandal variant of the Dera assault rifle with a Slash-dominant damage profile: 24 Slash, 6.4 Impact, and 1.6 Puncture. The defining upgrade over the base Dera is the 30% status chance, placing the Dera Vandal firmly in the status-primary camp among automatic rifles. An 11.25 rounds-per-second fire rate with a 60-round magazine and 1.8-second reload delivers sustained suppression capability. The 8% critical chance is low; this weapon's strength is status proc accumulation rather than crit scaling. Mastery Rank 7 is required, and the 100 accuracy value indicates pinpoint precision at range.

The Vandal suffix in Warframe typically indicates a weapon obtained through Invasion rewards or event-limited distribution rather than standard Market purchase, and the Dera Vandal follows this pattern. The high fire rate combined with 30% status ensures Slash bleed procs accumulate rapidly, while two 60/60 elemental mods push the status toward elemental proc application alongside the physical Slash ticks. The accuracy advantage over many status-focused rifles makes the Dera Vandal usable at longer ranges than comparable high-fire-rate options.

## Acquisition

The Dera Vandal is obtained through Invasion rewards or alert missions. Vandal weapons are not available from the standard Market. Check active Invasions for Corpus-aligned rewards, as Vandal weapons appear as Invasion completion prizes on applicable missions. Check [the Dera Vandal Fandom wiki page](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Dera_Vandal) for the current acquisition method and any updates to the distribution system.

## Variants

The base Dera is the standard version of this rifle, purchasable from the Market. The Dera Vandal improves on the base weapon with higher status chance and magazine size. No Prime, Kuva, or Tenet variant exists for the Dera family.

## Recommended builds

**Slash/Viral status build.** The 30% status and 11.25 fire rate deliver rapid proc accumulation. Equip Serration, Split Chamber, Vigilante Armaments, Galvanized Aptitude, Pistol Pestilence equivalent (Rime Rounds + Thermite Rounds for Viral). Viral multiplies health damage while Slash bleed ticks chip through enemy health. High fire rate generates enough procs per second for consistent Viral stacking.

**Galvanized Aptitude hybrid.** The 30% status ensures multiple proc types active simultaneously. Equip Galvanized Aptitude, Galvanized Chamber, and two sets of 60/60 elemental mods for Viral. Each unique status type on the target grants Galvanized Aptitude a damage stack; at high status diversity the Dera Vandal's sustained DPS climbs significantly.

**Corrosive sustained fire.** Against Grineer Steel Path enemies, equip Infected Clip + Stormbringer for Corrosive alongside Serration and Heavy Caliber. The 11.25 fire rate applies Corrosive procs rapidly, stripping Ferrite Armor. Add a status duration mod to extend proc windows. This build sacrifices the Slash component but maximizes armor stripping efficiency.

## Synergies

Wisp's Fused Reservoir provides a fire rate boost that compounts the Dera Vandal's already-high 11.25 rounds/second. Banshee's Sonar marks weak points where the 100 accuracy allows precise targeted fire. Corrosive Projection aura from teammates accelerates armor strip alongside the Dera Vandal's own Corrosive procs.

## Notes

- The Vandal version is strictly superior to the base Dera in status chance and magazine; upgrade when Invasion opportunities arise.
- The 100 accuracy is exceptional for a high-fire-rate automatic rifle; the Dera Vandal maintains tight groupings at medium-to-long range.
