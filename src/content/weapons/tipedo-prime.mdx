## Overview

The Tipedo Prime is the Prime variant of the Tipedo staff, available at Mastery Rank 10. Its 170 total damage per hit is heavily weighted toward puncture (136), with minor impact (17) and slash (17) components. Staff weapons combine the puncture-heavy Tipedo Prime's faction strength against Grineer with the extended 3.0-meter reach characteristic of polearms and staves, enabling sweeping attacks that hit multiple enemies in a line.

The 24% critical chance and 2.4x multiplier are in line with prime-grade melee weapons. With Blood Rush at x3 combo, the critical chance exceeds 70%, producing reliable orange crits at maintained combos. The 24% status chance enables dual-stat mod use for corrosive or viral elemental combinations. At 1.17 attack speed, the Tipedo Prime is faster than heavy melee weapons and generates combo counter ticks rapidly.

The 3.0-meter range is the Tipedo Prime's tactical advantage: it reaches enemy groups that shorter melees cannot, and sweep attacks from staff stances hit multiple targets simultaneously. This makes it especially efficient in enemy-dense mission types like Survival or Defense.

## Acquisition

The Tipedo Prime blueprint and components drop from Void Relics. Check the current relic drop table in-game for containing relics. Build time is 12 hours per component and 12 hours for the main weapon assembly.

## Recommended builds

**Corrosive-crit**: Blood Rush, Organ Shatter, Primed Pressure Point, Weeping Wounds, electric plus toxin (Corrosive) mods. Corrosive strips Grineer armor in six procs; after full strip, the 136 puncture base deals full damage. Blood Rush at x3 combo reaches above 70% crit chance. The 3-meter range hits multiple Grineer per swing, applying corrosive procs widely.

**Viral-slash at range**: Cold plus toxin for Viral, Hunter Munitions (slash on crit), Blood Rush, Primed Pressure Point, Organ Shatter. Viral halves max health across the reach of each swing. Hunter Munitions generates slash bleeds from crits. The sweep patterns of staff stances apply Viral and bleeds to clustered enemies simultaneously.

**Status sweep**: Weeping Wounds, four 60/60 dual-stat elemental mods, Condition Overload, Primed Pressure Point. The 3-meter range with Weeping Wounds at x2 combo produces high status density on groups, feeding Condition Overload.

## Notes

Staff weapons use the Clashing Forest or Flailing Branch stance mods (or others available in the Staff weapon group). The specific stance determines sweep patterns and combo strings. Stances with wide sweep attacks maximize the 3-meter range against enemy groups. Confirm which stance is equipped before entering missions to ensure the swing pattern fits the playstyle.
