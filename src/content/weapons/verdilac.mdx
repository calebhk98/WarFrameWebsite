## Overview

The Verdilac is an Orokin-influenced whip with exceptional critical stats for a melee weapon at Mastery Rank 13. Its 30% critical chance and 2.5x critical multiplier place it among the strongest critical platforms in the whip category. The 213 total damage per hit (21.3 impact, 106.5 puncture, 85.2 slash) provides a mixed physical damage spread, with puncture and slash sharing the majority. The 21% status chance is workable but secondary to the critical focus.

Whips have extended reach compared to most other melee categories. The Verdilac's 2.996-meter effective range enables striking enemies at distances where shorter melee weapons would miss. This makes whips useful when maintaining some distance from dangerous enemies is tactically valuable, particularly against enemies with dangerous melee retaliations.

With 30% base critical chance, Blood Rush at x2+ combo counter pushes critical chance past 90%, producing near-constant orange and red crits. The 2.5x base multiplier ensures each crit deals significantly elevated damage.

## Acquisition

The Verdilac blueprint is purchasable from the in-game Market for 20,000 credits. Build time is 12 hours. No quest unlock is required.

## Variants

No Prime, Vandal, or Wraith variant exists for the Verdilac. The Atterax is a slash-focused whip alternative if the Verdilac's critical focus does not fit a specific build goal.

## Recommended builds

**Blood Rush crit build**: Equip Blood Rush, Organ Shatter, Primed Pressure Point, Condition Overload, and Weeping Wounds. With Blood Rush at x4 combo, critical chance reaches near 150%. Organ Shatter pushes the multiplier toward 3.3x+ territory. Hunter Munitions converts crits to slash procs, synergizing with Condition Overload when viral is also active.

**Heavy attack whip**: Combine Killing Blow, Corrupt Charge, Sacrificial Steel, and Organ Shatter. At 12x combo from Corrupt Charge, heavy attacks deal enormous burst damage at extended whip range.

**Stealth multiplier**: The Verdilac's critical stats synergize with the 8x stealth damage multiplier when attacking unalerted enemies.

## Notes

Whip stances (Burning Wasp and Coiling Viper) dramatically affect range and attack shape. Burning Wasp provides wider sweeping attacks; Coiling Viper offers more directional combos. Primed Reach can extend the already-generous range toward 5 meters, which is especially useful in wide-open Infested tilesets.


The Verdilac's whip category creates important tactical considerations beyond just damage output. Whips have longer wind-up animations on certain combo strings compared to faster claw or dual-blade weapons. The 3.0-meter reach compensates for this -- each swing covers a larger cone and can tag enemies the player has not yet directly targeted. In narrow corridors or when surrounded, the wide arc of whip sweeps is a survival advantage rather than just a damage consideration.

Building around the Verdilac's 30% crit base is highly rewarding because combo-scaling with Blood Rush begins from a higher base than most weapons. At x2 combo with Blood Rush, the Verdilac's 30% base already approaches 60%, producing consistent orange crits without the sustained combo buildup that lower-crit-base weapons require.
