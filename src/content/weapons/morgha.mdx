## Overview

An Entrati-designed Necramech Arch-gun firing explosive projectiles with high Blast and Impact damage. Its wide spread pattern (low accuracy 6.67) saturates an area with explosive detonations. With 28% status chance, it applies Blast Knockdown procs reliably across multiple targets simultaneously. Introduced in Update 29.5 (2020-11-19, Deimos Arcana), the Morgha is notable for Entrati explosive weapon with 28% status chance for consistent Blast Knockdown; NecraLoid Syndicate acquisition; wide spread pattern for area saturation.

The Morgha was engineered by the Entrati alongside the Cortege, serving as a longer-range explosive suppression weapon for Necramechs operating in Deimos's open excavation zones. Its low accuracy was deliberate -- the Entrati designed it to saturate an area with explosive detonations rather than target individual Infested bioforms.

## Acquisition

Blueprint from NecraLoid Syndicate (Loid) at Clearance: Odima standing rank. Both Cortege and Morgha share the same acquisition rank requirement. Component parts crafted using Deimos resources. Market purchase for Platinum also available after initial community unlock. The Morgha requires Mastery Rank 15 to equip.

Arch-gun weapons require either standard crafting resources, Syndicate standing for component parts, or specialized mission drops. Once crafted, equipping the Morgha in Archwing uses the standard Arch-gun slot. To use it as a Heavy Weapon in ground missions or Railjack combat, a Gravimag must be installed. Gravimags are available from Clan Dojos via Energy Lab research, or from Arbitrations as rotation rewards.

## Variants

No Prime or Vandal variant exists. Companion piece to Cortege (Entrati flamethrower) and Mausolon (Voidrig default). All three are Entrati-origin Arch-guns acquired from NecraLoid or crafted via Entrati Syndicate.

The Arch-gun family spans weapons from Tenno engineering (Corvas, Fluctus, Velocitus), Grineer design (Grattler, Kuva line), and Entrati craftsmanship (Cortege, Mausolon, Morgha), each with distinct stat profiles suited to different engagement contexts.

## Recommended builds

28% status with Blast as the primary element enables consistent Knockdown crowd control across grouped enemies. Adding Radiation (Heat + Electricity) provides combined Grineer armor damage and enemy Confusion. 30% critical chance opens crit-hybrid builds with Hollowed Bullets alongside Modified Munitions for status optimization.

**Core Arch-gun mods to consider:**

- Rubedo-Lined Barrel -- +165% Arch-gun damage (primary damage mod for all builds)
- Hollowed Bullets -- +120% critical chance (essential for any crit-focused build)
- Critical Focus -- +60% critical multiplier while aiming
- Modified Munitions -- +90% status chance (for status-focused builds)
- Arch-gun Ace -- +165% fire rate (useful on slow-fire weapons to improve feel)
- Parallax Scope -- +30% critical chance while aiming
- Charged Bullets -- +60% magazine capacity
- Superior Defenses -- reduces reload time
- Conductive Coils -- adds Electricity damage
- Thermite Rounds -- adds Heat damage

Prioritize Rubedo-Lined Barrel as the first mod in any Arch-gun build. Follow with critical or status mods based on the weapon's stat profile. Elemental mods provide faction-specific damage type flexibility.

## Notes

The Morgha operates in the Arch-gun slot, used in Archwing missions and as a Heavy Weapon on the ground via Gravimag. In Railjack (Empyrean) missions, the Arch-gun serves as the primary armament during space combat against fighters and Crewships.

Blast Knockdown procs keep Railjack boarders suppressed during objective completion. Wide spread suits interior corridor combat where precision matters less than area coverage.

Archwing combat system notes: Archwing missions require completing The Archwing quest. Four Archwings are available -- Itzal (mobility/stealth), Amesha (tank/support), Elytron (AoE damage), and Odonata (balanced starter). Voidrig Necramech pairs naturally with Morgha for explosive suppression while the Mausolon handles precision targets. As an Arch-gun via Gravimag, Elytron's AoE abilities synergize with the spread-fire pattern.

The weapon's Riven disposition is 3/5 (moderate). Riven dispositions range from 0 (absolute minimum, strong weapon) to 5 (maximum, weak weapon). DE adjusts dispositions quarterly based on weapon usage rates.
