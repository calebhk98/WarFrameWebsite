## Overview

The [Akzani](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Akzani) is a dual secondary pistol firing at 20 rounds per second -- the fastest fire rate among standard secondary weapons available at Mastery Rank 4. Each bullet deals 12 total damage: 1.8 impact, 1.8 puncture, 8.4 slash. The extremely high fire rate combined with the 100-round magazine provides over 5 seconds of uninterrupted fire before reload. The 14% critical chance, 2.0x multiplier, and 14% status chance are modest but functional at this fire rate's volume.

Riven disposition at 5/5 is the maximum tier, making Akzani rivens among the most powerful in the secondary category for players who invest in them.

## Acquisition

The Akzani blueprint is available from the Market for credits. As an MR4 weapon, it is accessible to early-mid game players. Crafting requires basic materials. The completed weapon can also be purchased with platinum.

## Variants

No Prime, Vandal, or Wraith variant of the Akzani exists. The base version is the only form available.

## Recommended builds

**Status spray build:** Hornet Strike, Barrel Diffusion, Lethal Torrent, Pistol Pestilence, Frostbite, Jolt, Scorch, and Primed Heated Charge. At 14% status chance and 20 fire rate, procs accumulate at approximately 2.8 procs per second per elemental type. Multiple elemental mods create overlapping proc application from different damage types simultaneously. Viral (toxin + cold) and heat procs accumulate within seconds of sustained fire.

**Slash proc volume:** Hornet Strike, Barrel Diffusion, Lethal Torrent, Hemorrhage, and elemental mods. The 8.4 base slash combined with Hemorrhage generates bleed procs at a rate of 14% per bullet times 20 fire rate = approximately 2.8 slash procs per second. Sustained slash bleed accumulates against armored Grineer targets, bypassing armor mitigation continuously.

**Riven burst:** With a 5/5 disposition riven adding multishot or damage, the Akzani's 20 fire rate becomes overwhelming. Multishot doubles effective bullet volume per second; damage amplification multiplies per-hit output. Combined with the standard status mod stack, the Akzani becomes a competitive mid-game secondary at riven-invested levels.

## Synergies

- **Condition Overload primer:** At 20 fire rate and 14% status chance, three unique proc types accumulate within 1 second of fire. Brief Akzani bursts before switching to melee ensure Condition Overload maximum bonus activates immediately.
- **Saryn's Spores:** Toxin procs from the Akzani spread via Spores across nearby enemies. At 20 fire rate, the Akzani applies toxin procs to the primary target fast enough to seed multiple Spore spread events per second.
- **Lethal Torrent + Barrel Diffusion:** These dual secondary mods are critical for the Akzani. Lethal Torrent's fire rate bonus on an already 20-fire-rate weapon creates exceptionally rapid bullet delivery; Barrel Diffusion's multishot effectively doubles output.

## Notes

- The 100-round magazine at 20 fire rate empties in 5 seconds. At Lethal Torrent's bonus, this window shortens. Plan reloads between enemy clusters to avoid the 2-second gap during sustained combat.
- The Akzani's low per-bullet damage (12) is compensated entirely by volume. Against tanky enemies, sustained fire time is required to accumulate meaningful total damage. Elemental procs and status effects -- rather than raw per-bullet damage -- are the primary damage source.
- Riven disposition at 5/5 is the key to unlocking the Akzani's endgame potential. Without a riven, the weapon serves best as a Mastery Experience source and status primer at lower mastery levels.
