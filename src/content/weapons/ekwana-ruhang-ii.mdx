## Overview

The Ekwana Ruhang II is a Zaw Link component available from Hok's Anvil at Cetus on the Plains of Eidolon. As a Link, it does not have standalone combat statistics; WFCD reports zeros because the item is an unassembled weapon part. The "Ruhang" designation in the Ekwana naming convention indicates a bias toward status chance in the assembled Zaw's stat modification. The "II" suffix typically indicates an upgraded version of the Ekwana Ruhang with enhanced stat contribution. This Link occupies the third slot in the Strike + Grip + Link Zaw assembly.

Status-biased Zaw Links like the Ruhang family are chosen when the intended assembled Zaw build focuses on status proc accumulation rather than critical hits. Status-biased Zaws perform optimally with Weeping Wounds (which scales status chance with the melee combo multiplier) and Condition Overload (which multiplies damage output per unique status type active on the target). Comparing the Ekwana Ruhang II against the Ekwana II Ruhang within Hok's interface will reveal which offers higher status weighting; the assembled preview shows exact numerical values before spending Ostron Standing.

Zaw weapons assembled with the Ekwana Ruhang II Link can be gilded at Mastery Rank 8 or higher to unlock Arcane slots. After gilding, Forma can be installed to customize polarity slots, and the weapon can be given a custom player-chosen name.

## Acquisition

Purchase from Hok's Anvil at Cetus on the Plains of Eidolon. Ostron Standing is required; higher-tier Link components unlock at higher Ostron community rank. Earn Ostron Standing through fishing, mining, and bounty completion in the Plains of Eidolon. Check [the Ekwana Ruhang II Fandom wiki page](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Ekwana_Ruhang_Ii) for the exact Ostron rank requirement and standing cost.

## Variants

The Ekwana Link family includes multiple variants: Ekwana Jai, Ekwana II Jai, Ekwana Jai II, Ekwana Ruhang, Ekwana II Ruhang, and Ekwana Ruhang II. Jai variants bias toward critical chance; Ruhang variants bias toward status chance. Compare all available options within Hok's interface to select the modifier best suited to the chosen Strike and Grip. No Zaw component has a Prime or Kuva version.

## Recommended builds

When incorporating the Ekwana Ruhang II Link into a Zaw build:

- Select a Strike to establish the weapon archetype and base damage profile.
- Select a Grip to determine weapon category and swing speed.
- Add Ekwana Ruhang II as the Link to apply its status chance bias to the assembled Zaw.
- Preview assembled stats in Hok's interface to verify status chance meets the desired threshold.
- Equip Weeping Wounds and Condition Overload on the assembled Zaw to maximize the benefit of elevated status chance at higher combo.
- Gild the Zaw at Mastery Rank 8 or higher and install relevant Arcane slots (Arcane Fury, Arcane Tanker, etc.).

## Synergies

Status-biased Zaws assembled with Ruhang-family Links pair well with Saryn's Spores for Toxin proc distribution across groups. Frames that sustain combo (Wukong, Naramon school) extend Weeping Wounds scaling for consistent high-status proc generation. Condition Overload's multiplicative damage scales with each unique proc type; pair with elemental 60/60 mods for Viral, Corrosive, or Heat procs in addition to physical procs.
