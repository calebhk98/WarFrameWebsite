## Overview

Seer is a secondary pistol weapon that serves as early secondary pistol with a balanced physical damage profile; historically notable as a classic Grineer-designed sidearm with a slow fire rate and deliberate feel. Mastery Rank 0 is required to equip the weapon. Players who invest in building it properly will find it reliable across the star chart, with scaling potential on the Steel Path when supported by appropriate mods and Warframe ability synergies.

The secondary slot is competitive in Warframe, and Seer occupies a distinct position within it. Understanding its damage profile -- the distribution across physical damage types and any elemental components -- is the first step toward optimizing it for specific mission types and enemy factions.

## Recommended builds

Build direction for Seer depends on its stat profile. Status-oriented builds stack the Primed variant of elemental mods alongside dual stat mods (such as Infected Clip for toxin plus status, or Rime Rounds for cold plus status) to maximize proc frequency. Critical builds layer critical chance and damage multiplier mods to amplify each hit that lands as a critical strike.

For secondary weapons, the standard mod capacity baseline after zero forma accommodates roughly four to five max-rank mods. Most competitive loadouts require at least two forma to fit all desired mods without capacity overflow. Specific forma placement should target the polarities of the highest-capacity mods in the planned loadout.
