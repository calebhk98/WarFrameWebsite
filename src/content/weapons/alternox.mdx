## Overview

The Alternox is a Mastery Rank 8 primary rifle that deals pure Electricity damage with no base physical damage. Its 62 Electricity damage per shot combines with a 30% Status Chance and 14% Critical Chance to form a balanced hybrid weapon. The 2.2x Critical Multiplier and strong status chance enable both elemental combination builds and sustained critical output. The 28-round magazine reloads in 1.8 seconds, providing comfortable sustained fire capability.

Pure Electricity base damage means the Alternox naturally creates Magnetic damage when combined with Cold mods, effective against Corpus shields, or Corrosive when paired with Toxin mods for stripping Grineer armor. The absence of physical damage types keeps elemental combination options clean without unintended proc types.

## Acquisition

The Alternox blueprint is available from the in-game Market for credits. Components are crafted in the Foundry. No special prerequisites beyond Mastery Rank 8 are required.

## Variants

The Alternox does not have a Prime or Kuva variant as of this writing. Check the [Alternox wiki page](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Alternox) for any updates.

## Recommended builds

Magnetic builds add Cold via Chilling Grasp or similar mods to combine with the base Electricity for Magnetic damage, highly effective against Corpus shields. Pair with Rifle Aptitude and High Voltage for increased status and Electricity damage.

Corrosive builds add Toxin via Infected Clip to create Corrosive damage, which strips Grineer armor efficiently. At 30% base status, a single Toxin mod reliably applies Corrosive procs without needing dedicated status stacking.

## Notes

- Electricity status procs chain damage to nearby enemies, valuable in clustered enemy groups.
- The low accuracy of 18.18 means the Alternox is most effective at short to medium range despite being a rifle.
- 30% status chance enables consistent proc application even without dedicated status mods.
- Riven mods with damage or multishot bonuses substantially increase this weapon's output given its clean elemental profile.
- Pure Electricity damage means the Alternox has no physical proc types (no Slash, Impact, or Puncture). This is both an advantage (no unintended physical procs in elemental combination builds) and a limitation (cannot exploit enemy-specific vulnerabilities to physical damage).
- The Electricity base can be combined with two additional elements in a four-slot build: Toxin for Corrosive, Cold for Magnetic, or Heat for Radiation -- all relevant combinations for the three major factions (Grineer, Corpus, Infested). Plan the elemental combination based on the target mission faction rather than locking into a single setup.
- At 4.33 fire rate with a 28-round magazine, the Alternox empties in approximately 6.5 seconds; factor reload time into sustained damage calculations for endurance runs.
