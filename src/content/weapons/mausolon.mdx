## Overview

The signature Arch-gun of the Voidrig Necramech and one of the most powerful Arch-guns available. Features a 300-round magazine for sustained normal fire and a devastating charged mode delivering a high-damage laser blast with 50% critical chance, 3.5x critical multiplier, and 50% status chance. Introduced in Update 29.0 (2020-08-25, Heart of Deimos), the Mausolon is notable for default Voidrig Necramech weapon; 300-round magazine; charged mode with exceptional 50% crit chance and 3.5x multiplier; Entrati origin.

The Mausolon was designed by the Entrati as the primary armament for Voidrig Necramechs defending Deimos from the Infested tide. Its charged fire mode was reserved for defeating the most formidable Infested bioforms -- the Jugulus and Saxum variants that threatened the Entrati family's deeper vaults.

## Acquisition

Automatically provided as the default weapon when a Voidrig Necramech is constructed from Entrati Syndicate (Grandmother) blueprints on Deimos. Cannot be purchased or traded separately. Requires Entrati Syndicate standing and Deimos resources to build the Voidrig itself. The Mausolon requires Mastery Rank 14 to equip.

Arch-gun weapons require either standard crafting resources, Syndicate standing for component parts, or specialized mission drops. Once crafted, equipping the Mausolon in Archwing uses the standard Arch-gun slot. To use it as a Heavy Weapon in ground missions or Railjack combat, a Gravimag must be installed. Gravimags are available from Clan Dojos via Energy Lab research, or from Arbitrations as rotation rewards.

## Variants

No variants exist. The Mausolon is the Voidrig's default and only weapon -- it cannot be removed or swapped. Cortege is the Bonewidow's equivalent Entrati Necramech weapon.

The Arch-gun family spans weapons from Tenno engineering (Corvas, Fluctus, Velocitus), Grineer design (Grattler, Kuva line), and Entrati craftsmanship (Cortege, Mausolon, Morgha), each with distinct stat profiles suited to different engagement contexts.

## Recommended builds

The charged mode's stats (50% crit, 3.5x multiplier, 50% status) are exceptional and should drive build choices. Hollowed Bullets, Critical Focus, and Modified Munitions benefit both fire modes. Normal fire provides sustained 300-round suppression. Riven disposition of 1 indicates fixed mods outperform Riven investment for this weapon.

**Core Arch-gun mods to consider:**

- Rubedo-Lined Barrel -- +165% Arch-gun damage (primary damage mod for all builds)
- Hollowed Bullets -- +120% critical chance (essential for any crit-focused build)
- Critical Focus -- +60% critical multiplier while aiming
- Modified Munitions -- +90% status chance (for status-focused builds)
- Arch-gun Ace -- +165% fire rate (useful on slow-fire weapons to improve feel)
- Parallax Scope -- +30% critical chance while aiming
- Charged Bullets -- +60% magazine capacity
- Superior Defenses -- reduces reload time
- Conductive Coils -- adds Electricity damage
- Thermite Rounds -- adds Heat damage

Prioritize Rubedo-Lined Barrel as the first mod in any Arch-gun build. Follow with critical or status mods based on the weapon's stat profile. Elemental mods provide faction-specific damage type flexibility.

## Notes

The Mausolon operates in the Arch-gun slot, used in Archwing missions and as a Heavy Weapon on the ground via Gravimag. In Railjack (Empyrean) missions, the Arch-gun serves as the primary armament during space combat against fighters and Crewships.

Top-tier Railjack Arch-gun. The 300-round magazine enables extended engagement without reloading, and the charged mode provides burst-damage capability for high-priority Crewship targets.

Archwing combat system notes: Archwing missions require completing The Archwing quest. Four Archwings are available -- Itzal (mobility/stealth), Amesha (tank/support), Elytron (AoE damage), and Odonata (balanced starter). Designed for and most naturally used on the Voidrig Necramech. Via Gravimag in Archwing mode, its 300-round magazine and charged mode make it compatible with any Archwing's engagement style.

The weapon's Riven disposition is 1/5 (very low). Riven dispositions range from 0 (absolute minimum, strong weapon) to 5 (maximum, weak weapon). DE adjusts dispositions quarterly based on weapon usage rates.
