## Overview

The [Akbolto](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Akbolto) is a dual secondary crossbow that fires bolts at 4.5 rounds per second. Its damage is slash-dominant: 57.6 slash out of 64 total per shot, with 6.4 impact and negligible puncture. The 16% critical chance and 2.4x multiplier support functional critical builds, making the Akbolto one of the few dual secondaries with genuine crit potential at Mastery Rank 8. Status chance at 2.2% is very low and not a meaningful build consideration.

The Akbolto Prime offers substantially improved stats. The base version is primarily valuable as a Mastery source or as a platform for riven investigation given its 4/5 disposition.

## Acquisition

The Akbolto blueprint is available from the Market for credits. As a standard Market weapon, it is permanently available. The completed weapon can be purchased with platinum.

## Variants

The Akbolto Prime (Mastery Rank 12) significantly improves critical chance (28%), status chance (16%), and multiplier (2.6x). Players who can access Prime relics should pursue the Prime variant. No Vandal or Wraith version exists.

## Recommended builds

**Critical slash build:** Hornet Strike, Barrel Diffusion, Lethal Torrent, Primed Pistol Gambit, Primed Target Cracker, Hydraulic Crosshairs (headshot crit bonus), and two elemental mods. At 16% base crit, Primed Pistol Gambit pushes effective crit into reliable yellow-crit territory. The 2.4x multiplier delivers notable per-hit bonus on each crit. The slash-dominant damage generates bleed procs on critical hits when combined with Hemorrhage.

**Riven-enhanced build:** A 4/5 disposition riven with critical chance or multishot can elevate the Akbolto's performance meaningfully. Combine with Point Strike and Primed Target Cracker for a crit-focused secondary that performs beyond its base stat expectations.

**Mastery leveling build:** Equip four basic mods and run high-density missions. The 4.5 fire rate and 30-round magazine provide adequate kill rates for Mastery Experience farming.

## Synergies

- **Critical Hydraulic Crosshairs:** Headshots trigger the conditional crit bonus. At range where headshots are accessible, Hydraulic Crosshairs elevates the Akbolto's effective crit chance to sustained high levels.
- **Slash + Hemorrhage:** The 57.6 base slash combined with the Hemorrhage mod adds additional slash proc sources per hit. Slash procs bypass armor on Grineer targets, improving effective DPS against armored enemies.
- **Lethal Torrent + Barrel Diffusion:** Dual secondaries benefit from multishot mods. Adding both increases bolt volume per trigger pull, improving overall damage output and proc rates simultaneously.

## Notes

- The 2.2% status chance is effectively negligible without riven investment. Build the Akbolto as a critical weapon rather than a status platform.
- Reload time of 2.6 seconds is above average for secondaries. The 30-round magazine at 4.5 fire rate empties in approximately 6.7 seconds -- plan reloads during brief disengagements.
- Riven disposition at 4/5 offers good roll potential. For players interested in the Akbolto specifically, a riven can close the performance gap with higher-tier alternatives.
