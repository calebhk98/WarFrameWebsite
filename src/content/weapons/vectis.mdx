## Overview

The Vectis is a Tenno-engineered sniper rifle introduced in Update 10.6. It fires one round per trigger pull and auto-reloads, which eliminates the conventional reload animation and makes it one of the fastest-cycling single-shot snipers in the game. With 225 total base damage split between impact (90), puncture (78.75), and slash (56.25), the Vectis delivers substantial per-shot output well suited to precision target elimination across all content tiers.

The weapon's 25% critical chance paired with a 2.0x critical multiplier rewards headshot discipline. Its 30% status chance is high for a sniper rifle, enabling reliable elemental proc application with appropriate mods. The Vectis has Mastery Rank 2 as its requirement, making it accessible well before mid-game progression. Its single-round magazine is the defining constraint: every shot must count, and players must plan around the auto-reload cadence during extended engagements.

The Vectis occupies an important role in the Tenno arsenal as the introductory sniper rifle. It rewards precision, punishes missed shots, and provides a clear progression path toward the Vectis Prime for players who master single-shot sniping.

The weapon supports three zoom levels (unzoomed, 3x, 4.5x); each zoom level maintains the same base damage, critical, and status values per WFCD data.

## Acquisition

The Vectis blueprint is purchasable from the in-game Market for 25,000 credits. Crafting requires Nano Spores (3,000), Neurodes (4), Orokin Cells (4), and Rubedo (2,500). Build time is 24 hours. The blueprint itself drops from Duviri Endless Tier 6 runs as a rare reward (1.03% chance per WFCD drop tables) for players preferring to skip the Market purchase.

The Vectis is not tradeable between players, so each Tenno must acquire and craft their own copy. No quest or event is required; it is a standard Market weapon accessible from the beginning of the star chart.

## Variants

**Vectis Prime** -- The Prime variant requires Mastery Rank 14 and delivers substantially improved performance: 350 total damage (Impact 140, Puncture 52.5, Slash 157.5), 30% critical chance, 30% status chance, a 2-round magazine, and a significantly faster 0.85-second reload. The higher slash damage profile favors Viral-Slash builds in endgame. Vectis Prime components drop from Void Relics.

**No Vandal or Wraith variant exists** for the Vectis. The Prime is the only enhanced version available to standard players.

## Recommended builds

**Critical-focused build**: Equip Vital Sense (critical damage), Point Strike (critical chance), Heavy Caliber (base damage), Split Chamber (multishot), and a Primed or standard Serration (base damage). Pair with a heat or viral elemental combination for Steel Path viability. The 25% base critical chance becomes reliable with Point Strike applied; aim for headshots to consistently trigger orange critical hits.

**Status-Viral build**: Prioritize status chance mods to reach 100% status. Combine Infected Clip (viral component) with Thermite Rounds (heat) to produce Viral procs. Add Serration and Split Chamber for damage scaling. This build excels against armored Grineer and high-health targets where Viral amplifies all subsequent damage.

**Hybrid build**: Balance critical and status using Galvanized Aptitude and Hunter Munitions. This approach leverages the Vectis's naturally strong base critical and status values without fully committing to either extreme, performing reliably across mixed content.

## Notes

The Vectis benefits from the Depleted Reload mod, which reduces the magazine to 0 but dramatically shortens the already-fast reload time to near-zero. With Depleted Reload at max rank, the Vectis cycles so quickly it approaches automatic fire cadence for a sniper. This is a well-known advanced modding choice that sacrifices the small magazine for raw fire rate.

Sniper Rifle-specific mods apply fully: Sniper Ammo Mutation, Vigilante mods for ammo economy, and the Sharpshooter focus way passive improve sustained use. The Vectis uses sniper ammo, which is scarcer than rifle ammo; bringing an ammo mutation mod is advisable for extended runs.
