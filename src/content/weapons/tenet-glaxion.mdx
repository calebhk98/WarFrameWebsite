## Overview

The Tenet Glaxion is a Sisters of Parvos Tenet beam weapon requiring Mastery Rank 16. It fires a continuous cold beam at 12.0 ticks per second with 100% accuracy. Its 34 damage per tick is entirely cold elemental -- no physical damage components. Cold damage is especially effective against Corpus shields: it deals 50% bonus damage to shields, slowing and eventually freezing shielded Corpus targets. The continuous beam format means all ticks apply to the same target, rapidly stacking cold procs.

The 40% status chance is the Tenet Glaxion's primary attribute. At 12.0 ticks per second with 40% status per tick, cold procs apply multiple times per second. Six cold procs freeze the target. Frozen enemies take increased damage from follow-up attacks -- creating a chill, slow, freeze, and vulnerability cycle. The 20% critical chance and 2.2x multiplier are secondary; this is a status-focused beam.

With 90-round magazine capacity and 2.0-second reload, the Glaxion sustains continuous beam fire for 7.5 seconds before reload. The Tenet version carries an additional elemental bonus from the parent Sister, modifying the total elemental composition beyond pure cold.

## Acquisition

Obtain through the Sisters of Parvos system. Trigger a Sister Candidate at Mastery Rank 16+ via Granum Void encounters. Vanquish the Sister to receive her Tenet weapon. The cold base element of the Glaxion combines with the Sister's chosen element for a unique elemental profile.

## Variants

No base Glaxion counterpart. The original Glaxion (non-Tenet) is a separate weapon. The Tenet version is the enhanced form.

## Recommended builds

**Cold status freeze**: Serration, Split Chamber, four 60/60 dual-stat elemental mods for magnetic (cold plus electric -- bonus against Corpus shields), Thermagnetic Shells augment if available. The 40% status at 12 ticks stacks cold procs rapidly, freezing Corpus targets before blasting them with magnetic.

**Viral secondary element**: Combine the inherent cold with toxin mods to create Viral damage as the secondary elemental combination. Viral halves enemy max health. The cold primary element slows and freezes while Viral amplifies all subsequent damage.

**Status density**: Primed Firestorm for beam-weapon reach, Galvanized Aptitude for status-kill stacking, Thermagnetic Shells. The beam format allows continuous fire without reacquiring; Galvanized Aptitude stacks damage bonuses on each status-effect kill.

## Notes

Beam weapons drain the magazine based on ticks rather than discrete shots. At 12 ticks per second, the 90-unit magazine lasts 7.5 seconds of continuous fire -- shorter than it appears numerically. Position accordingly and plan reloads during movement between enemy groups. Cold procs from beam fire stack to six before triggering a freeze; this is the primary crowd-control mechanic of the Glaxion platform.
