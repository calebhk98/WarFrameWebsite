## Overview

The Afuris Prime is the primed dual machine pistol with exceptional fire rate. It provides consistent damage output through continuous suppression, ideal for close-quarters support. The weapon dual-wields rapid-fire pistols, combining practical magazine capacity with high sustained fire rate. Each trigger pull fires both pistols, creating substantial projectile volume from single magazine.

With a 12.5 fire rate, the Afuris Prime maintains nearly continuous fire at close to medium range. The 80 round magazine enables extended suppression without reloading, a significant advantage in team missions. The 1.2 reload time is quick, minimizing downtime between magazines. The combination of high fire rate and substantial magazine creates effective suppression platform.

The Afuris Prime's 30% status chance supports status-focused builds. Many players prefer viral and heat combinations for crowd control and weakening. The weapon works well in group content where suppressive fire from secondaries supports team objectives. Elemental status mods multiply the weapon's effectiveness.

This weapon appeals to players preferring close-range, high-fire-rate playstyles. The Afuris Prime shines in missions with dense enemy clusters where sustained suppression proves tactically valuable. Veterans appreciate the weapon's effectiveness at point-blank range despite moderate individual shot damage. The high fire rate enables rapid application of status effects.

The dual-pistol mechanic fires both weapons simultaneously, requiring only magazine capacity for one weapon despite dual wielding. This efficient ammunition usage combined with high fire rate creates effective suppression. Some players combine the Afuris Prime with abilities providing ammunition generation.

Forma investment creates capable close-range secondary platform.
