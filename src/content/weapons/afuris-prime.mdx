## Overview

The Afuris Prime is the primed dual machine pistol with exceptional fire rate. It provides consistent damage output through continuous suppression, ideal for close-quarters support. The weapon dual-wields rapid-fire pistols, combining practical magazine capacity with high sustained fire rate. Each trigger pull fires both pistols, creating substantial projectile volume from single magazine.

With a 12.5 fire rate, the Afuris Prime maintains nearly continuous fire at close to medium range. The 80 round magazine enables extended suppression without reloading, a significant advantage in team missions. The 1.2 reload time is quick, minimizing downtime between magazines. The combination of high fire rate and substantial magazine creates effective suppression platform.

The Afuris Prime's 30% status chance supports status-focused builds. Many players prefer viral and heat combinations for crowd control and weakening. The weapon works well in group content where suppressive fire from secondaries supports team objectives. Elemental status mods multiply the weapon's effectiveness.

This weapon appeals to players preferring close-range, high-fire-rate playstyles. The Afuris Prime shines in missions with dense enemy clusters where sustained suppression proves tactically valuable. Veterans appreciate the weapon's effectiveness at point-blank range despite moderate individual shot damage. The high fire rate enables rapid application of status effects.

The dual-pistol mechanic fires both weapons simultaneously, requiring only magazine capacity for one weapon despite dual wielding. This efficient ammunition usage combined with high fire rate creates effective suppression. Some players combine the Afuris Prime with abilities providing ammunition generation.

Forma investment creates capable close-range secondary platform.

## Acquisition

The Afuris Prime is a Prime weapon available through the Void Relic system. Its component blueprints drop from applicable Void Relics (Axi and Neo tiers historically). It enters and exits the Vault on a rotation schedule; when vaulted, Relics containing Afuris Prime parts are unavailable from standard drop tables but can be obtained through Prime Resurgence events or player trading on warframe.market. Players can also trade completed parts or the full set directly with other players.

## Variants

The base **Afuris** is available from the Market as a craftable weapon with lower stats, requiring lower Mastery Rank. The Afuris Prime improves on all major stats, primarily the increased status chance (30% vs lower on the base) and higher magazine capacity. Players seeking fire-rate secondaries may also consider the Aksomati Prime for a critical-focused alternative, or the Akstiletto Prime for a mix of accuracy and performance.

## Recommended builds

**Viral Status Build:** With 30% status chance at 12.5 rounds per second, the Afuris Prime procs elemental effects very reliably. Stack Pistol Pestilence (Toxin) and Frostbite (Cold) for Viral, supplemented by Hornet Strike and Barrel Diffusion for base damage and multishot. Add Hollow Point for crit to leverage the moderate 16% CC. Primed Heated Charge or Convulsion rounds out the elemental combination.

**Sustained Suppression Build:** Prioritize magazine management with Lethal Momentum or Magazine Warp alongside Gunslinger for reduced fire delay. The 80-round magazine combined with fast reload allows near-continuous operation in sustained missions. This approach works well in survival and defense scenarios where downtime from reloads costs DPS uptime.

The Afuris Prime excels in close-to-medium range content. At longer ranges, its accuracy (28.6) limits reliable hit rates, so players should position aggressively or pair it with frames offering teleportation or movement amplification.

## Notes

The dual-pistol animation locks players out of ability casts briefly during the full auto fire sequence. Brief pauses between magazine cycles allow ability usage without significant DPS loss. The weapon's high fire rate depletes ammo reserves quickly in extended runs -- bringing Ammo Mutation or energy-based reload alternatives reduces supply pressure.

Afuris Prime's slash-heavy damage distribution means Slash procs deal bleed damage bypassing armor, which makes it surprisingly effective even against high-armor targets when status procs land consistently.
