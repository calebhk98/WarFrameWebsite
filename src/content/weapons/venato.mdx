## Overview

The Venato is a scythe-category melee weapon with a slash-dominant damage profile: 220 total damage per hit with 110 slash, 77 puncture, and 33 impact. The slash weighting makes the Venato a natural candidate for bleed-proc strategies. Slash procs bypass armor and shields, dealing true damage equal to a percentage of the proc-trigger hit per tick.

With 27% critical chance and a 2.1x multiplier, the Venato also functions as a capable critical platform. Hunter Munitions pairs naturally with the high base slash and critical stats, creating a build where criticals generate additional bleed stacks on top of the naturally high slash proc rate. At 24% status chance, the Venato can apply both physical and elemental status types with reasonable reliability.

Scythes provide wide, sweeping attacks that cover large arcs, making them effective against grouped enemies. The 2.7-meter range is above average and ensures scythe arcs connect with multiple targets in each swing.

## Acquisition

The Venato blueprint is purchasable from the in-game Market for 20,000 credits. Build time is 12 hours. No quest unlock is required.

## Variants

No Prime, Vandal, or Wraith variant currently exists for the Venato. Players looking for upgraded scythe options should explore the Reaper Prime through Void Relics, which offers higher critical chance and a larger slash component.

## Recommended builds

**Slash-bleed crit build**: Equip Primed Pressure Point, Blood Rush (critical chance scales with combo), Organ Shatter (critical multiplier), Hunter Munitions (slash procs on critical hit), and Weeping Wounds (status chance on combo). With Blood Rush active at x2+ combo, critical chance exceeds 60%, and Hunter Munitions converts most crits to additional bleed stacks. Viral on elemental mods doubles bleed tick damage.

**Condition Overload hybrid**: Swap Hunter Munitions for Condition Overload and stack diverse status types (slash, viral, heat). Each unique status type multiplies Condition Overload's damage bonus, rewarding multitype proc application.

**Heavy attack scythe**: Combine Killing Blow, Corrupt Charge, Sacrificial Steel, and Organ Shatter for burst damage on priority targets with the extended-range heavy attack animation.

## Notes

Scythe stances (Reaping Spiral is common for this category) affect the arc shape and combo strings significantly. Primed Reach extends the 2.7-meter range, enabling strikes at greater distances. This is especially effective in defense missions where enemies approach from predictable directions.


Scythe weapon stances typically feature wide arc sweeps that cover 180 degrees or more in front of the player. The Venato's 2.7-meter range combined with the arc sweep of Reaping Spiral stance creates a large cone of damage that hits every enemy in the arc simultaneously. This enables the Venato to apply status procs to multiple targets per swing, which feeds Condition Overload and Galvanized Shot stack accumulation across an entire enemy group rather than per-target.

The Venato's 9 mastery requirement ensures players have a sufficient mod collection to take advantage of its hybrid crit-status stats. Players who acquire the Venato at MR 9 typically have access to Blood Rush, Organ Shatter, and Weeping Wounds from earlier melee investments, enabling immediate full-build deployment.
