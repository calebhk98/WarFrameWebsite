## Overview

Zakti is a Grineer-origin injector pistol that fires toxic darts capable of leaving poison clouds on impact surfaces. The weapon's defining mechanic is its ability to deposit lingering hazard zones rather than dealing immediate burst damage, making it a crowd-control tool rather than a conventional damage secondary. Each dart fired embeds into surfaces or enemies, releasing a gas cloud on detonation that poisons nearby targets.

The damage per dart is low -- 12 impact and 18 slash per shot -- but the cloud damage accumulates over time from multiple darts detonating simultaneously. A three-dart burst into a doorway or chokepoint creates sustained area denial. This playstyle differs fundamentally from most secondaries and rewards players who invest in status-oriented builds over raw damage amplification.

Zakti's 2% critical chance makes critical builds irrelevant. The 20% status chance per dart, however, scales with multi-dart detonations and status mods. The 3-round magazine cycles quickly given the 0.8-second reload, enabling rapid cloud creation before enemies reach a defensive position. At Mastery Rank 10, Zakti serves players who want a utility secondary for crowd control rather than damage output.

## Acquisition

Zakti's blueprint is available from the in-game Market for 15,000 credits. The weapon crafts in 12 hours in the Foundry. No rare resources are required.

## Variants

Zakti has a prime variant: [Zakti Prime](/weapons/zakti-prime), which offers higher status chance and magazine capacity.

## Recommended builds

**Area-denial build:** Combine Barrel Diffusion (multishot) with Lethal Torrent to increase dart count per shot. More darts per burst means more cloud sources, increasing the total poison damage in the area. Add Pistol Pestilence and Scorch for Corrosive damage on the darts, turning the gas clouds into armor-stripping hazards. This build suits Survival and Defense missions where enemies funnel through chokepoints.

**Status-hybrid build:** Equip Seeking Fury (Pistol) and Rime Rounds to combine Viral with Toxin procs. Viral halves enemy health pools, and the lingering toxin cloud extends proc application over several seconds. This pairs well with a high-damage primary that benefits from the Viral debuff, making Zakti a force-multiplier secondary rather than a standalone damage source.

Zakti rewards positioning knowledge. Placing clouds at door frames, near objective consoles, or on approach paths to defensive positions applies maximum value from each magazine.

## Notes

- Zakti's cloud mechanic is distinct from direct-impact status effects. The cloud lingers after the dart detonates, and enemies passing through the cloud after initial detonation continue to receive proc stacks.
- The 3-dart magazine cycles fast enough that players can maintain near-continuous cloud coverage in a small area with minimal reload downtime.
- Zakti Prime offers meaningful improvements to status chance (42% vs. 20%) and magazine size (8 vs. 3), making the prime version a direct upgrade for status-focused builds.
- The low accuracy value (26.7) reflects the dart spread over distance. At close to medium range, all three darts typically cluster within a small area; at long range, spread increases and the cloud coverage expands.
