## Overview

The Arca Plasmor is a unique shotgun that fires a continuous plasma discharge rather than traditional pellets. This weapons stands apart with its radial damage application and exceptional crowd control capabilities. The weapon excels in mid-range engagements where its large impact radius can eliminate multiple enemies simultaneously. Unlike conventional shotguns requiring precise aim, the Arca Plasmor's spread pattern makes it forgiving in dynamic combat scenarios. The weapon's relatively quick fire rate combined with moderate magazine capacity allows sustained fire during extended engagement windows.

## Stats

The Arca Plasmor operates with a fire rate of 1.1 rounds per second, delivering pure radial damage without impact, puncture, or slash breakdown. With a magazine capacity of 10 rounds and reload time of 2.8 seconds, the weapon balances ammo efficiency with downtime between magazine changes. Critical hit chance of 22% and multiplier of 1.6x provide consistent bonus damage on well-placed shots. Status chance of 28% enables reliable proc application when paired with elemental mods, particularly effective with Heat for area denial or Corrosive for armor stripping.

## Acquisition

The Arca Plasmor is obtained as a rare drop from Void Cascade enemies encountered during Void Cascade missions. Alternatively, the weapon may appear as a rotation reward in certain void-fissure mission variants. The weapon requires a build time of 12 hours and costs 15,000 credits to construct from its blueprint. Component farming typically requires multiple runs through void-fissure content, making patient farming sessions necessary for acquisition.

## Damage and Performance

Without traditional physical damage split, the Arca Plasmor relies entirely on its radial damage model and elemental augmentation through mods. The continuous discharge pattern creates overlapping damage zones, allowing multiple hits on clustered targets. The 22% crit chance pairs effectively with crit-boosting mods like Point Strike and vital sense, enabling strong burst damage opportunities. The 28% status chance baseline allows reliable status application, making Corrosive Projection or Heat applicable for specific mission types.

## Mod Recommendations

Serration and Heavy Caliber provide essential raw damage scaling for this weapon. Point Strike and Vital Sense enable critical damage builds, transforming occasional headshots into major damage spikes. Multishot mods like Hell's Chamber amplify the radial spread, creating denser damage zones. Status-focused players should prioritize Infecton and Heated Charge for elemental damage stacking. Split Ammo magazine mods extend engagement time between reloads, critical for sustained operations.

## Builds and Synergies

The weapon performs exceptionally well in crowd-control oriented builds where AoE damage trumps single-target precision. When equipped alongside abilities like Rhino's Stomp or Equinox's Sleep, the Plasmor converts incapacitated targets into guaranteed damage amplification opportunities. Trinity's Energy Vampire synergizes via energy management, keeping the weapon firing continuously. Saryn's Spores amplify radial damage application further. Grimoire decks enabling projectile speed bonuses improve accuracy falloff compensation at range.

## Performance Notes

The Arca Plasmor's radial damage model creates natural grouping incentives for squad play. The weapon becomes increasingly effective in higher-difficulty content where enemy clustering occurs around defensive positions. Reload speed becomes critical in endurance missions, making Quick Reload or Carrion Scavenger considerations. Fall-off damage reduction beyond 20 meters limits effective range, encouraging mid-range positioning rather than sniper-style engagement distances.
