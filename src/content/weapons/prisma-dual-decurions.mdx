## Overview

The Baro Ki'Teer exclusive Prisma enhancement of the Dual Decurion. Improves critical chance from 28% to 30%, status chance from 14% to 24%, and fire rate from 10.42 to 12.5 rounds per second, transforming the base weapon's crit-leaning profile into a genuine critical/status hybrid. Introduced in Hotfix 29.5.8 (2020-12-11), the Prisma Dual Decurions is notable for Baro Ki'Teer exclusive Prisma variant; significantly improved status chance (24% vs 14% base); balanced 30%/24% crit and status for hybrid builds.

Baro Ki'Teer acquired Prisma Dual Decurions from Orokin vaults during one of his Void expeditions, recognizing the enhanced variants' superior engineering. The Prisma refinement process -- applied using Orokin techniques preserved in Baro's collection -- elevates standard Tenno craftsmanship to near-Orokin quality.

## Acquisition

Available periodically from Baro Ki'Teer (the Void Trader) at any Relay. Baro appears every two weeks and his stock rotates. When available, the Prisma Dual Decurions blueprint costs Ducats and Credits. Trade via Warframe Market if missed during a Baro appearance. The Prisma Dual Decurions requires Mastery Rank 1 to equip.

Arch-gun weapons require either standard crafting resources, Syndicate standing for component parts, or specialized mission drops. Once crafted, equipping the Prisma Dual Decurions in Archwing uses the standard Arch-gun slot. To use it as a Heavy Weapon in ground missions or Railjack combat, a Gravimag must be installed. Gravimags are available from Clan Dojos via Energy Lab research, or from Arbitrations as rotation rewards.

## Variants

Enhanced Prisma variant of the Dual Decurion. Both share Mastery Rank 1. Monitor community trackers (Hub, Warframe Market, or degenerate-gamers.com) for upcoming Baro Ki'Teer rotations.

The Arch-gun family spans weapons from Tenno engineering (Corvas, Fluctus, Velocitus), Grineer design (Grattler, Kuva line), and Entrati craftsmanship (Cortege, Mausolon, Morgha), each with distinct stat profiles suited to different engagement contexts.

## Recommended builds

30% critical chance and 24% status enable a true hybrid build unlike the base model's 28%/14% crit-leaning profile. Hollowed Bullets, Critical Focus, and Modified Munitions form the core. The status improvement from 14% to 24% dramatically increases proc frequency -- Modified Munitions pushes this above 45% for consistent procs.

**Core Arch-gun mods to consider:**

- Rubedo-Lined Barrel -- +165% Arch-gun damage (primary damage mod for all builds)
- Hollowed Bullets -- +120% critical chance (essential for any crit-focused build)
- Critical Focus -- +60% critical multiplier while aiming
- Modified Munitions -- +90% status chance (for status-focused builds)
- Arch-gun Ace -- +165% fire rate (useful on slow-fire weapons to improve feel)
- Parallax Scope -- +30% critical chance while aiming
- Charged Bullets -- +60% magazine capacity
- Superior Defenses -- reduces reload time
- Conductive Coils -- adds Electricity damage
- Thermite Rounds -- adds Heat damage

Prioritize Rubedo-Lined Barrel as the first mod in any Arch-gun build. Follow with critical or status mods based on the weapon's stat profile. Elemental mods provide faction-specific damage type flexibility.

## Notes

The Prisma Dual Decurions operates in the Arch-gun slot, used in Archwing missions and as a Heavy Weapon on the ground via Gravimag. In Railjack (Empyrean) missions, the Arch-gun serves as the primary armament during space combat against fighters and Crewships.

Outperforms base Dual Decurion in all Railjack scenarios. Limited availability through Baro means acquiring when available is a priority for long-term Arch-gun investment.

Archwing combat system notes: Archwing missions require completing The Archwing quest. Four Archwings are available -- Itzal (mobility/stealth), Amesha (tank/support), Elytron (AoE damage), and Odonata (balanced starter). Works with all Archwings. Itzal's close-approach mobility compensates for lower accuracy at extended range. The improved stats over base make the Prisma version viable in higher-tier Archwing and Railjack content.

The weapon's Riven disposition is 4/5 (high). Riven dispositions range from 0 (absolute minimum, strong weapon) to 5 (maximum, weak weapon). DE adjusts dispositions quarterly based on weapon usage rates.
