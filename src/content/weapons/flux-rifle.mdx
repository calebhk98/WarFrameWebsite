## Overview

The Flux Rifle is a continuous beam primary rifle with a Puncture-dominant profile: 17.16 Puncture and 4.84 Slash per tick at 12.0 ticks per second. Beam weapons in Warframe apply their status at a separate rate from their damage ticks; the 24% status chance per tick, combined with 12 ticks per second, creates a rapid status application rate that accumulated procs within a second of sustained fire. The 10% critical chance and 2.0x multiplier are secondary. The 50-round magazine depletes in approximately 4.2 seconds of continuous fire; the 1.75-second reload is fast. Mastery Rank 6 is required. The 100 accuracy is precise for a beam weapon.

Beam (continuous) weapons use a different targeting model than projectile weapons: they hit instantly and can be swept across groups of enemies. The Flux Rifle's beam delivers 12 damage ticks per second, each with an independent 24% status chance roll. This means Puncture procs (Impair) and elemental procs from elemental mods apply multiple times per second, rapidly stacking to Condition Overload thresholds. The high tick rate also benefits Galvanized Aptitude, as each damage tick benefits from accumulated status stacks. At MR6, the Flux Rifle is an unusually strong status delivery tool compared to other accessible primary options.

## Variants

The Flux Rifle has no Prime, Kuva, or Tenet variant. It is a standalone beam primary weapon. For an upgraded continuous-beam option, players may consider the Opticor Vandal or other high-tier beam primaries at higher mastery ranks.

## Recommended builds

**Galvanized Aptitude status build.** The 24% status per tick at 12 ticks/second creates very rapid proc accumulation. Equip Serration, Galvanized Aptitude, and four 60/60 elemental mods for Viral + Corrosive (Infected Clip + Cryo Rounds + Stormbringer + Toxic Barrage). Each unique proc type active on target grants a Galvanized Aptitude damage stack. Within 0.5 seconds of beam contact, multiple proc types are active simultaneously.

**Viral/status sweep build.** Equip Serration, Split Chamber, two Viral 60/60 mods (Cryo Rounds + Infected Clip equivalent), and Galvanized Chamber. Sweep the beam across groups of enemies to apply Viral procs to multiple targets simultaneously. Viral's health-damage amplification compounds across subsequent hits for sustained DPS on each target in the sweep.

**Corrosive sustained beam.** Equip Infected Clip + Stormbringer for Corrosive. At 12 ticks/second with 24% status, Corrosive procs stack rapidly, stripping Grineer Ferrite Armor within 1-2 seconds of sustained beam contact. Add Serration and Heavy Caliber for base damage amplification. Point Strike for the 10% crit contribution is optional.

## Synergies

Wisp's Haste Mote increases primary weapon fire rate, compounding the already-high tick rate for even faster status application. Banshee's Sonar marks enemy weak points; the Flux Rifle's precise beam can target these for amplified damage. Rhino's Roar amplifies the Puncture-heavy damage proportionally, benefiting each tick in the sustained beam.

## Notes

- Beam weapon status applies per tick, not per second; the 24% status at 12 ticks/second applies procs far faster than the listed percent suggests for non-beam weapons.
- The 50-round magazine depletes in approximately 4 seconds of continuous fire; position reload windows carefully.
