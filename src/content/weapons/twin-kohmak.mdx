## Overview

The Twin Kohmak is a dual shotgun-pistol secondary available at Mastery Rank 10. It fires blasts of shotgun pellets at 6.67 bursts per second with an 80-round magazine. Its 30 damage per shot divides across 6 impact, 18 puncture, and 6 slash per pellet -- the puncture majority gives the Twin Kohmak better performance against armored Grineer targets than unarmored enemies. As a shotgun, the Twin Kohmak fires multiple pellets per trigger pull, and each pellet has its own proc chance.

The 11% critical chance and 13.8% status chance are both low. The Twin Kohmak is not a high-performance stat weapon in either category -- its appeal is the dual-shotgun fantasy of rapid spread fire from dual wielded explosives at close range. With a 80-round magazine and 2.2-second reload, it sustains fire adequately for its medium damage output.

At accuracy 5.7, the Twin Kohmak has very wide spread. It is a close-range weapon by design; pellets scatter at medium range such that only a portion strikes the target. Point-blank engagement maximizes pellet cluster impact.

## Acquisition

The Twin Kohmak blueprint is purchasable from the in-game Market for 20,000 credits. Build time is 12 hours. No quest or event unlock is required.

## Variants

No Prime, Vandal, or Wraith variant exists for the Twin Kohmak.

## Recommended builds

**Pellet count multishot**: Barrel Diffusion, Lethal Torrent, Hornet Strike, and two elemental 60/60 mods. Barrel Diffusion increases pellets per shot, which multiplies total proc opportunities. At close range with maximum pellet count, the Twin Kohmak delivers high total proc density per burst.

**Viral close range**: Cold plus toxin for Viral, Barrel Diffusion, Lethal Torrent, Hornet Strike. Viral procs from multiple pellets per burst apply the health-halving status rapidly within point-blank engagements.

**Basic build**: Hornet Strike, Barrel Diffusion, and two single elemental mods. The Twin Kohmak is effective as a backup or fallback secondary at moderate difficulty. It does not require advanced mods to function for its intended close-range role.

## Notes

The accuracy of 5.7 is among the lowest for secondary weapons. Do not attempt medium or long range engagements -- the pellet spread guarantees poor damage delivery beyond close range. In close-range mission types (Capture, low-level Exterminate, Rescue) the Twin Kohmak performs better than the stat numbers suggest due to full pellet impact on targets at point-blank. Carry a precise primary for any situations requiring ranged engagement.


The Twin Kohmak's low accuracy (5.7) makes it a dedicated close-range weapon. In missions where enemies must be engaged at close quarters -- Rescue, Spy, capture-point objectives -- the Twin Kohmak's effective range matches the tactical requirement. Players who pair the Twin Kohmak with a precision primary weapon can use the Kohmak for close-range engagement while reserving the primary for any targets requiring medium or long-range fire.
