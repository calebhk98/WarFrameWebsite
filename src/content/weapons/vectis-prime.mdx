## Overview

The Vectis Prime is the Prime variant of the Vectis bolt-action sniper rifle, available at Mastery Rank 14. Its 350 total damage per shot splits across 157.5 slash, 140 impact, and 52.5 puncture -- slash-dominant, unusual for a sniper which typically focuses on puncture. The high slash weighting produces bleed procs that bypass armor, making each Vectis Prime shot effective at stacking true damage over time regardless of enemy armor type.

The 30% critical chance and 2.0x multiplier, combined with 30% status chance, make the Vectis Prime a genuine hybrid crit-status weapon. With Point Strike, crit chance reaches near 90%. The 2-round magazine and 0.85-second reload create the Vectis Prime's signature rhythm: fire two shots, reload in under a second, fire two more. This extremely fast reload for a sniper means sustained fire is surprisingly consistent.

The Vectis Prime drops from Void Relics and is one of the most popular Prime sniper options due to its combination of high damage, balanced stats, and rapid reload.

## Acquisition

The Vectis Prime blueprint and components drop from Void Relics. Check the current relic drop table in-game. Build time is 12 hours per component plus 12 hours for the main assembly.

## Recommended builds

**Crit slash sniper**: Serration, Split Chamber, Point Strike, Vital Sense, Hunter Munitions, and Viral (cold + toxin) elemental mods. Point Strike at 30% base reaches near-90% crit. Vital Sense amplifies the 2.0x base multiplier. Hunter Munitions converts crits to slash procs. Viral halves max health; slash bleeds then bypass armor against the halved pool.

**Status sniper**: Dual-stat 60/60 elemental mods for Corrosive plus Heat or Viral plus Heat, Point Strike, Vital Sense, Serration, Split Chamber. The 30% status at 2.67 fire rate produces consistent proc application per shot. Galvanized Scope (crit on headshot) rewards precise aim.

**Headshot focus**: Galvanized Scope (stacking crit on headshots), Primed Shred (punch-through), Point Strike, Vital Sense, Serration, Split Chamber, Viral elemental. Galvanized Scope provides a massive crit bonus after consecutive headshots -- the Vectis Prime's 2-round magazine and fast reload support the rhythm of aimed headshot pairs.

## Notes

The 0.85-second reload is the Vectis Prime's defining advantage over other snipers. The effective fire cadence is two quick shots plus a brief reload, much faster than 6-round snipers that reload after the full magazine. Sniper ammo is scarce -- bring Sniper Ammo Mutation for extended missions. Punch-through mods (Primed Shred) allow sniper rounds to pass through multiple targets in line, multiplying the damage of each shot against clustered enemies.
