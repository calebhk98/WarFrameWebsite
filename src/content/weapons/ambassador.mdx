## Overview

The Ambassador is a Mastery Rank 10 primary rifle that fires high-velocity Electricity rounds. With a large 96-round magazine and 13.33 rounds per second fire rate, it sustains continuous pressure on groups of enemies. Its 26% Status Chance supports elemental combination builds, while the 14% Critical Chance and 2.8x Critical Multiplier enable critical-focused configurations. Pure Electricity base damage makes elemental combination choices straightforward.

The weapon features an alternate fire mode that launches a single charged electromagnetic shot. The primary auto-fire handles sustained damage output across multiple targets, while the alternate fire concentrates damage for tougher single targets. This dual mode makes the Ambassador adaptable across mission types where both AoE suppression and targeted damage matter.

## Acquisition

The Ambassador blueprint is available from the in-game Market for credits. Components are crafted in the Foundry. No special prerequisites beyond Mastery Rank 10 are required.

## Variants

The Ambassador does not have a Prime variant as of this writing. Check the [Ambassador wiki page](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Ambassador) for any future variant information.

## Recommended builds

Magnetic builds add Cold damage via Chilling Grasp to combine with base Electricity for Magnetic damage, highly effective against Corpus shields. High Voltage adds additional Electricity damage and raises status chance further.

Corrosive builds swap Cold for Toxin using Infected Clip, stripping Grineer armor through repeated Corrosive procs. At 26% base status, a single Toxin mod reaches reliable proc frequency per burst sequence.

Critical builds with Argon Scope and Point Strike leverage the high 2.8x multiplier when scoped.

## Notes

- Electricity status procs arc to nearby enemies, making the Ambassador valuable in tight corridors.
- The alternate fire charges an electromagnetic burst; timing matters for efficient magazine usage.
- At 96 rounds the Ambassador can sustain entire enemy waves without reloading in shorter missions.
- The 2.8x crit multiplier is above average for rifles, making Argon Scope synergy strong.
- The Ambassador was introduced with the Infested Liches system and has loose thematic ties to that content arc; it is not gated behind that system but the design aesthetic reflects it.
- Fire rate of 13.33 rounds per second is among the highest for primary weapons; multishot mods (Split Chamber) are extremely powerful on this weapon because each of the rapid-fire rounds can proc a status and each can crit independently.
- Combining the Ambassador's high fire rate with Galvanized Aptitude (bonus status damage stacking per unique status type) rewards builds that apply multiple elemental combinations simultaneously. Target a three-status build (Electricity, Magnetic, plus one more) to maximize Galvanized Aptitude stacks during sustained fire.
