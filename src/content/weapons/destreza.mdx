## Overview

The Destreza is a rapier melee weapon with a strongly Slash-dominant damage profile: 134 Slash versus 19.75 Puncture and 3.95 Impact. Rapiers in Warframe use a thrust-based attack animation distinct from standard swords, with a dedicated rapier stance mod (Pointed Wind) that delivers quick, forward-thrust combos effective at single-target damage. The 28% critical chance and 2.0x multiplier provide a functional crit platform, while the 14% status chance is secondary. Mastery Rank 7 is required, and the 2.5 m range is average for the melee category. Attack speed of 1.0 is standard.

The Slash dominance makes the Destreza well-suited to applying bleed procs, which bypass armor and deal damage based on a percentage of the total raw damage inflicted. Against high-armor enemies such as Grineer Heavy Gunners, bleed procs from the Slash component continue to deal damage at a fixed rate regardless of armor values. This makes Slash-heavy melee weapons like the Destreza more effective in Steel Path content than their crit stats alone would suggest. The Destreza Prime is a direct upgrade with improved critical and status values for players who reach its Void Relic acquisition.

## Variants

The Destreza Prime improves on the base with higher critical chance, a 3.0x critical multiplier, and enhanced status chance, making it the preferred end-game option. The Destreza Prime is acquired through Void Relics. No Kuva or Tenet variant exists for the rapier class.

## Recommended builds

**Blood Rush critical/Slash build.** At 28% base crit, Blood Rush reaches orange-crit territory by combo level x4. Equip Organ Shatter for the critical multiplier, Sacrificial Steel (or True Steel) for additional crit chance, and two 60/60 elemental mods for Viral. The Slash base ensures bleed proc stacking alongside crit hits.

**Slash/Condition Overload status build.** Equip Primed Pressure Point, Condition Overload, Weeping Wounds, and four elemental 60/60 mods for Viral plus one additional element. At x4 combo, the 14% status reaches sufficient proc rates for Condition Overload stacks. The 134 Slash base amplifies bleed damage.

**Pointed Wind stance combo build.** Equip the Pointed Wind stance to use the rapier's thrust combo, which chains hits effectively at medium range. Add Berserker Fury for attack-speed acceleration after kills, enabling faster combo generation and Condition Overload cycling.

## Synergies

Wukong's Primal Fury stance does not apply to the Destreza, but his passive ability to avoid death complements any aggressive melee style. Khora's Strangledome groups enemies for multi-target rapier thrust combos. Frames that apply strip-armor abilities (Corrosive Projection aura, Saryn's Corrosive proc) enhance the bleed proc damage from the Destreza's Slash base.

## Notes

- The rapier attack animation differs from standard sword combos; practice the Pointed Wind stance transitions for optimal combo flow.
- Upgrade to Destreza Prime for a 3.0x critical multiplier, which significantly increases per-hit damage during Blood Rush orange-crit phases.
