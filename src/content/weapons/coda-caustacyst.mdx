## Overview

The [Coda Caustacyst](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Coda_Caustacyst) is an exclusive Jade Shadows scythe melee weapon requiring Mastery Rank 17. It deals 285 total damage per attack including 120 innate corrosive damage alongside 20 impact, 70 puncture, and 75 slash physical damage. The 41% status chance is one of the highest among Jade Shadows weapons, enabling rapid proc accumulation. The 19% critical chance and 2.3x multiplier allow for hybrid crit-status approaches.

As with all Jade Shadows Coda weapons, this represents a Coda-tier version with enhanced stats compared to any base variant. Mastery Rank 17 gates these weapons behind significant player investment, positioning them as endgame melee options for experienced players.

## Variants

The Coda Caustacyst is itself the enhanced variant of the Caustacyst. The base [Caustacyst](/weapons/caustacyst) (Mastery Rank 7) has lower status chance (37%), critical chance (9%), and no rarity-enhanced damage values. The Coda version is the superior endgame form.

## Recommended builds

**Full status corrosive strip:** Primed Pressure Point, Berserker Fury, Condition Overload, Weeping Wounds, Blood Rush, Organ Shatter, and two additional elemental mods (heat and another to form a second element). The built-in corrosive eliminates the need for a corrosive elemental pair. The freed slots accommodate viral (Fever Strike plus North Wind) or heat, enabling three distinct status types per engagement for maximum Condition Overload stacking. At 41% status chance plus Weeping Wounds, procs accumulate within the first two or three swings.

**Endgame armor strip build:** Combine the built-in corrosive with Corrosive Projection aura and elemental corrosive mods. The layered corrosive proc application strips Grineer armor in Steel Path faster than any single-element approach, enabling subsequent hits to deal full unmitigated damage.

**Blood Rush critical hybrid:** Blood Rush, Weeping Wounds, Condition Overload, Organ Shatter, and elemental mods. At 19% base crit, Blood Rush reaches yellow-crit territory at x4 combo. Both crit and status scale simultaneously with combo multiplier.

## Synergies

- **Condition Overload:** Built-in corrosive plus two additional elemental types (viral and heat) from mods creates three unique status types, reaching Condition Overload's full damage bonus within the first engagement.
- **Corrosive Projection:** Reduces enemy armor percentage-wise. Combined with the Coda Caustacyst's innate corrosive proc strip, Grineer armor in Steel Path drops faster per engagement cycle.
- **Saryn's Spores:** Corrosive procs from the weapon spread through Spores across enemy groups, delivering armor strip at scale without individual targeting.

## Notes

- Mastery Rank 17 places the Coda Caustacyst firmly in veteran territory. The weapon's enhanced stat profile reflects this gating, delivering performance competitive with the best endgame melee options.
- The 41% status chance at attack speed 1.0 generates consistent procs without requiring Weeping Wounds acceleration. Adding Weeping Wounds pushes the proc rate into near-guaranteed territory within the second swing.
- Scythe stance mods (Reaping Spiral, Stalking Fan) provide wide sweeping combos that hit multiple enemies simultaneously, multiplying the value of each proc application across grouped targets.
- The built-in 120 corrosive damage is a fixed elemental component. Adding additional corrosive mods stacks multiplicatively on the base value, but the returns diminish compared to diversifying into a second elemental type for additional Condition Overload stacks.
