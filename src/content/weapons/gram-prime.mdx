## Overview

The Gram Prime is the Prime variant of the Gram great-sword, featuring a Puncture-dominant damage profile: 225 Puncture, 60 Impact, and 15 Slash. The equal 32% critical chance and 32% status chance make the Gram Prime one of the few melee weapons in the game with a perfectly balanced hybrid stat profile at this high a value for both metrics simultaneously. The 2.6x critical multiplier amplifies orange-crit hits significantly. Attack speed of 0.8 is slow for a great-sword, and the 2.9 m range compensates by covering a wide area per sweep. Mastery Rank 14 is required.

The Gram Prime is classified as a two-handed sword (great-sword) weapon category. Great-swords use Rending Crane, Cleaving Whirlwind, or Tempo Royale stance mods for wide-arc sweep combos that cover the 2.9 m range fully per swing. The equal 32%/32% crit and status values mean either Blood Rush or Weeping Wounds is a valid primary scaling investment, and running both simultaneously creates a genuine dual-scaling build where both stats improve at x4 combo. This flexibility distinguishes the Gram Prime from most weapons that skew heavily toward one stat or the other.

## Variants

The base Gram is the standard great-sword, available at lower mastery rank. The Gram Prime significantly improves critical chance, multiplier, status chance, and base damage. No Kuva or Tenet variant exists for the Gram family.

## Recommended builds

**Blood Rush/Weeping Wounds dual-scaling build.** The equal 32% crit and status is the Gram Prime's defining trait. Equip Blood Rush, Weeping Wounds, Organ Shatter, Primed Pressure Point, Condition Overload, and two Viral 60/60 mods. At x4 combo, both stats scale simultaneously -- orange crits compound with Condition Overload's proc-type multiplier for exceptional damage output. This is the Gram Prime's most powerful build path.

**Pure critical heavy-blade build.** At 32% base crit with Blood Rush and Sacrificial Steel, the Gram Prime reaches red-crit territory at high combo. Equip Organ Shatter, Primed Pressure Point, and two Viral 60/60 mods. The 2.9 m range allows wide-arc sweeps that deliver orange/red crit hits to multiple enemies per swing.

**Tempo Royale crowd-clearing build.** Equip Tempo Royale stance for the great-sword's widest-arc combo animations. Combine with Primed Pressure Point, Condition Overload, Blood Rush, and Weeping Wounds. The 2.9 m range ensures each Tempo Royale sweep covers clustered enemy groups fully, applying both crit and status proc benefits simultaneously.

## Synergies

The Gram Prime's 2.9 m range pairs with enemy-grouping abilities (Khora, Vauban) for maximum per-swing hit count. Naramon's Power Spike extends combo duration for sustained Blood Rush and Weeping Wounds scaling. Wukong's passive combo preservation maintains the dual-scaling at peak combo through extended engagements without resets.

## Notes

- The equal 32% crit and status is the Gram Prime's most notable feature; always run Blood Rush and Weeping Wounds together to exploit this unique dual scaling.
- The 0.8 attack speed is slow; compensate with Berserker Fury (after kill) or Arcane Strike for speed augmentation.
