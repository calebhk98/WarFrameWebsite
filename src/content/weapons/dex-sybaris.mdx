## Overview

The Dex Sybaris is a Dex-series anniversary burst-fire rifle with a 35% critical chance -- one of the highest base crit values among burst primaries. The damage profile distributes across Puncture (33.75), Impact (22.5), and Slash (18.75), with no single damage type dominant. Dex weapons are distributed annually by Digital Extremes as a community anniversary gift, typically in March. Fire rate of 4.17 rounds/second reflects burst-fire mechanics where two rounds fire per trigger pull. The 14-round magazine (seven bursts) and 1.5-second reload emphasize precision over sustained suppression. Mastery Rank 7 is required.

Burst-fire rifles in Warframe operate differently from automatic weapons: each trigger pull fires a set burst, and the fire rate describes the cadence of bursts rather than individual rounds. The 28.57 accuracy reflects the burst spread at medium range. The 35% critical chance with 2.0x multiplier creates strong critical hit consistency with Galvanized Scope or Hunter Munitions, as each burst has multiple opportunities to proc critical hits against the target.

## Acquisition

The Dex Sybaris is a Warframe anniversary gift distributed annually by Digital Extremes. Log in during the anniversary event period (typically March) to claim the Dex Sybaris and other Dex weapons. Check [the Dex Sybaris Fandom wiki page](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Dex_Sybaris) for the current anniversary date and distribution schedule.

## Variants

The base Sybaris is the standard version of this burst rifle, available from the Market. The Sybaris Prime is the Prime variant with higher crit multiplier and status chance. The Dex Sybaris is the anniversary edition with a high crit chance but modest multiplier compared to the Prime. No Kuva or Tenet variant exists for the Sybaris family.

## Recommended builds

**Critical/Galvanized Scope build.** The 35% base crit reaches consistent yellow-crit territory without Blood Rush. Equip Serration, Split Chamber, Vital Sense, Point Strike, and Galvanized Scope for head-focused critical scaling. Hunter Munitions converts crits into Slash proc generation for bleed damage supplementation. This is the primary build path for the Dex Sybaris.

**Hunter Munitions proc build.** With 35% crit, Hunter Munitions generates reliable Slash procs on most bursts. Equip Serration, Split Chamber, Hunter Munitions, Vital Sense, and two Viral 60/60 mods. Viral weakens health while Slash bleed ticks compound the burst's physical damage. Galvanized Chamber replaces Split Chamber for ramping damage.

**Faction-specific Bane build.** Equip a Bane of [faction] mod alongside Serration, Split Chamber, Vital Sense, and Point Strike. The multiplicative Bane modifier amplifies both the burst crit hits and any Slash bleed procs. This builds the Dex Sybaris into a faction-specialized precision tool effective for sortie-tier assassination targets.

## Synergies

Banshee's Sonar highlights weak points where the Dex Sybaris's precision bursts can target for additional damage multipliers. Rhino's Roar amplifies all physical damage proportionally, benefiting the balanced Impact/Puncture/Slash distribution. Octavia's Resonator grouping allows efficient burst fire against clustered enemies despite the 28.57 accuracy spread.
