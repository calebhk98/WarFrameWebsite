## Overview

The [Catabolyst](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Catabolyst) is a corrosive-elemental secondary pistol that fires a continuous stream of corrosive liquid. It deals 53 corrosive damage per hit with no physical damage component. The 43% status chance is among the highest for secondary weapons -- combined with the 12 rounds-per-second fire rate, the Catabolyst applies corrosive procs at an extreme rate, stripping enemy armor faster than almost any other secondary. The 2.9x critical multiplier rewards critical builds despite the modest 11% critical chance.

A unique mechanic: when the Catabolyst's magazine is emptied completely, the explosion on the empty-chamber deals significant area damage. This intentional "reload explosion" rewards exhausting the full magazine before reloading, adding a burst AOE damage source to an otherwise stream-based weapon.

## Acquisition

The Catabolyst blueprint is available from the Market for credits. Crafting requires standard mid-game materials. As a permanently available Market weapon, it is never vaulted. The completed weapon can be purchased with platinum.

## Variants

The Coda Catabolyst ([Coda Catabolyst](/weapons/coda-catabolyst)) provides enhanced stats for MR17 players. No Vandal or Wraith variant exists.

## Recommended builds

**Armor strip status build:** Hornet Strike, Barrel Diffusion, Lethal Torrent, Pistol Pestilence (toxin to stack corrosive), Scorch (heat), Jolt (electric), Frostbite (cold), and Primed Heated Charge. The 43% status chance at 12 fire rate applies corrosive procs multiple times per second, stripping Grineer Ferrite armor in 2-3 seconds. Additional elemental procs (heat, viral from toxin + cold) stack Condition Overload bonuses for follow-up melee.

**Explosion harvest build:** Hornet Strike, Barrel Diffusion, elemental mods, and an explosive-damage bonus mod (if available). Empty the full 31-round magazine before every reload to trigger the empty-chamber explosion. The explosion deals substantial AOE corrosive damage that benefits from the same elemental bonuses applied to the stream damage.

**Critical corrosive build:** Replace some elemental slots with Primed Pistol Gambit and Primed Target Cracker. At 11% base critical chance, these mods push the Catabolyst into viable yellow-crit territory. The 2.9x multiplier makes each critical hit deal notable burst damage against already-weakened (armor-stripped) targets.

## Synergies

- **Rapid armor strip:** The 43% status chance at 12 fire rate strips Grineer armor within 2-3 seconds. Following up with a high-base-damage primary or melee against armor-stripped targets delivers dramatically amplified damage output.
- **Coda Catabolyst upgrade path:** Players at MR17 can transition directly to the Coda Catabolyst for enhanced stat performance, maintaining the same mechanical familiarity.
- **Empty-magazine explosion:** Coordinate the explosion timing with grouped enemies for AOE impact. The explosion applies corrosive to all enemies within range, providing immediate armor strip on newly-arrived enemies entering the radius.

## Notes

- Accuracy of 6.25 reflects the spray pattern of the liquid stream. Effective range is close-to-medium; at long range, the stream disperses significantly. The Catabolyst is best used at within 15 meters of targets.
- The magazine empties in approximately 2.6 seconds at 12 fire rate. The 1.7-second reload means the weapon spends 1.7s inactive between each 2.6s active window. Intentionally emptying for the explosion turns this constraint into a tactical feature.
- The empty-chamber explosion damage scales with mods applied to the weapon's corrosive damage. Mods that increase elemental damage (Pistol Pestilence, Hornet Strike) also amplify the explosion.
- Riven disposition at 4/5 provides above-average roll potential. A riven with multishot or critical chance significantly amplifies both the stream's proc rate and the explosion's AOE effectiveness.
