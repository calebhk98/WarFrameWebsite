## Overview

The [Coda Tysis](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Coda_Tysis) is the Jade Shadows enhanced version of the Tysis secondary at Mastery Rank 17. It deals 135 total damage per shot: 18 impact, 26 puncture, 32 slash, and 59 innate corrosive. The 50% status chance is among the highest for secondary weapons, enabling near-guaranteed proc application per shot. The 13% critical chance and 2.0x multiplier are secondary. The 2.5 fire rate and 18-round magazine support sustained fire.

Obtained through the Jade Shadows content update. Check the [Coda Tysis Wiki page](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Coda_Tysis) for current acquisition methods.

## Variants

The Coda Tysis is the enhanced form of the base Tysis (MR7). The Coda delivers 50% status chance versus the base's lower output. Both have innate corrosive; the Coda version is the endgame upgrade.

## Recommended builds

**Armor strip secondary:** Hornet Strike, Barrel Diffusion, Lethal Torrent, Pistol Pestilence (additional toxin for more corrosive), Jolt (electric to add to existing corrosive), Scorch (heat), and Frostbite (cold for viral). At 50% status chance, corrosive procs apply on every other shot at minimum. In 2-3 shots, Steel Path Grineer armor is significantly reduced. Viral from cold further halves remaining health.

**Multi-faction status build:** Configure for viral (toxin + cold) using Pistol Pestilence and Frostbite alongside the innate corrosive. This provides corrosive for Grineer armor strip and viral for health reduction simultaneously.

**Heavy caliber support:** In high-level missions, the Coda Tysis's rapid armor strip enables teammates' primary weapons to deal full damage. Use it as a support secondary focused on enemy debuffing rather than primary damage output.

## Synergies

- **Corrosive strip + melee:** Apply corrosive procs to strip armor, then switch to melee Condition Overload for amplified damage on the stripped target.
- **Viral + corrosive combination:** Innate corrosive plus viral from mods creates two distinct proc types, feeding Condition Overload stacking in addition to armor strip and health reduction.
- **Team content support:** In Steel Path group play, the Coda Tysis's rapid armor strip benefits the entire squad's damage output against Grineer units.

## Notes

- 50% status chance at 2.5 fire rate means one to two status procs per second, depending on elemental mod distribution. Each shot reliably applies at least one proc type.
- The 1.2-second reload is fast. The 18-round magazine at 2.5 fire rate lasts 7.2 seconds -- long enough for most engagement cycles before reload is needed.
- Mastery Rank 17 investment is appropriate given the weapon's exceptional status output. Players at this mastery level have access to Primed mods that amplify the Coda Tysis's already strong proc foundation.
