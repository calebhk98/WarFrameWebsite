## Overview

The Burston is a Grineer-manufactured burst-fire assault rifle available from the earliest stages of Warframe. Firing in three-round bursts, it delivers a balanced mix of impact, puncture, and slash damage per burst with a combined total of roughly 30 damage across all three types. The burst-fire mode rewards deliberate trigger pulls and precise aim, making it an excellent platform for players learning rifle mechanics and mod theory.

Its design philosophy centers on versatility. With approximately equal distribution across impact, puncture, and slash, the Burston does not hard-specialize against any single enemy armor class. This even spread allows players to apply whichever elemental combination is appropriate for a given faction without sacrificing effective physical base damage.

As a mastery rank 0 weapon, the Burston is among the most accessible rifles in the game. It serves not just as a starting weapon but as a benchmark for understanding how burst rifles behave compared to automatic and semi-automatic alternatives.

The Burston's 18% status chance per burst makes it a capable status applicator when paired with elemental mods. Its critical stats (6% CC, 1.6x CM) are low, so pure crit builds are inefficient -- status and hybrid approaches outperform. The 45-round magazine allows sustained engagement before a 2-second reload interrupts the flow of combat.

## Acquisition

The Burston blueprint is purchasable from the in-game Market using credits. Component blueprints can be obtained as mission rewards across the Star Chart, particularly from early Grineer missions. Crafting requires standard resources available at low Mastery levels: Ferrite, Circuits, and Polymer Bundle. Build time is 24 hours in the Foundry. The base weapon can also be purchased fully built from the Market using Platinum.

## Variants

The **Burston Prime** is the Orokin-era prime variant, offering substantially improved statistics including higher base damage, better critical chance, a larger magazine, and faster fire rate. It is available through Prime Resurgence, Void Relic drops, or trading with other players. The Prime variant is the preferred choice for players who want to invest Forma into a burst rifle platform.

## Recommended builds

**Status/Hybrid Build:** Equip Infected Clip and Stormbringer to create Viral -- the dominant faction-neutral combination -- alongside Serration and Split Chamber for base damage scaling. The Burston's 18% status chance is enough to reliably proc Viral with two elemental mods at high fire rate. Add Thermite Rounds or High Voltage for a secondary status type.

**Budget Starter Build:** Serration, Split Chamber, and two elemental mods of choice cover the essential damage scaling. The Burston's balanced damage distribution means no physical damage type is wasted; all three scale evenly with Serration.

Avoid heavy investment in crit mods (Point Strike, Vital Sense) as the 6% base critical chance returns poor value compared to damage or status investment.

## Notes

The Burston holds nostalgic significance for long-time players, as it was among the first weapons many encountered on the Star Chart. Despite power creep over multiple updates, it remains a competent early-game rifle capable of clearing standard Star Chart content when properly modded.

The burst-fire mechanic requires adjusted aiming habits -- leading targets and timing bursts beats holding the trigger. Players transitioning from automatic rifles often need a short adaptation period.

The Burston is an efficient Mastery Rank fodder weapon for new players, granting MR experience quickly due to low investment requirements.
