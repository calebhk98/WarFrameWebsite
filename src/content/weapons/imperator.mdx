## Overview

The starter Arch-gun awarded upon completing The Archwing quest. A balanced physical damage rifle with a high fire rate (16.67 rounds/s), large 200-round magazine, and no Mastery Rank requirement, making it accessible to all players beginning Archwing content. Introduced in Update 15.0 (2014-10-24, Archwing), the Imperator is notable for starter Arch-gun free from The Archwing quest; no Mastery Rank required; 200-round magazine for extended sustained fire.

The Imperator was the first Arch-gun made available to Tenno upon their initial Archwing deployment. Cephalon Simaris preserved the design as part of his cataloguing of Tenno technology, ensuring new Archwing pilots always have access to a functional primary weapon.

## Acquisition

Blueprint obtained as a reward for completing The Archwing quest through Cephalon Simaris. No credit cost for the blueprint itself. Component parts (Circuits, Ferrite, Morphics, Polymer Bundle) built for 25,000 credits. The Imperator requires Mastery Rank 0 to equip.

Arch-gun weapons require either standard crafting resources, Syndicate standing for component parts, or specialized mission drops. Once crafted, equipping the Imperator in Archwing uses the standard Arch-gun slot. To use it as a Heavy Weapon in ground missions or Railjack combat, a Gravimag must be installed. Gravimags are available from Clan Dojos via Energy Lab research, or from Arbitrations as rotation rewards.

## Variants

Imperator Vandal is a significantly improved Vandal variant with 25 fire rate, 300-round magazine, and improved critical and status stats. Obtained through Fomorian Sabotage event missions.

The Arch-gun family spans weapons from Tenno engineering (Corvas, Fluctus, Velocitus), Grineer design (Grattler, Kuva line), and Entrati craftsmanship (Cortege, Mausolon, Morgha), each with distinct stat profiles suited to different engagement contexts.

## Recommended builds

Balanced physical damage across Impact, Puncture, and Slash suits broad elemental combinations. Standard damage mods -- Rubedo-Lined Barrel and Parallax Scope -- improve output significantly for new players. The Imperator Vandal strictly outperforms this weapon in every stat. Replace when Fomorian Sabotage events provide Vandal components.

**Core Arch-gun mods to consider:**

- Rubedo-Lined Barrel -- +165% Arch-gun damage (primary damage mod for all builds)
- Hollowed Bullets -- +120% critical chance (essential for any crit-focused build)
- Critical Focus -- +60% critical multiplier while aiming
- Modified Munitions -- +90% status chance (for status-focused builds)
- Arch-gun Ace -- +165% fire rate (useful on slow-fire weapons to improve feel)
- Parallax Scope -- +30% critical chance while aiming
- Charged Bullets -- +60% magazine capacity
- Superior Defenses -- reduces reload time
- Conductive Coils -- adds Electricity damage
- Thermite Rounds -- adds Heat damage

Prioritize Rubedo-Lined Barrel as the first mod in any Arch-gun build. Follow with critical or status mods based on the weapon's stat profile. Elemental mods provide faction-specific damage type flexibility.

## Notes

The Imperator operates in the Arch-gun slot, used in Archwing missions and as a Heavy Weapon on the ground via Gravimag. In Railjack (Empyrean) missions, the Arch-gun serves as the primary armament during space combat against fighters and Crewships.

A serviceable Arch-gun for early Railjack missions before acquiring higher-tier weapons through Syndicate or Lich routes. Its large magazine reduces reload interruptions during learning phases.

Archwing combat system notes: Archwing missions require completing The Archwing quest. Four Archwings are available -- Itzal (mobility/stealth), Amesha (tank/support), Elytron (AoE damage), and Odonata (balanced starter). Suitable for all Archwings while learning space combat. Odonata's passive shield generation compensates for the weapon's lower raw power in early Archwing content.

The weapon's Riven disposition is 3/5 (moderate). Riven dispositions range from 0 (absolute minimum, strong weapon) to 5 (maximum, weak weapon). DE adjusts dispositions quarterly based on weapon usage rates.
