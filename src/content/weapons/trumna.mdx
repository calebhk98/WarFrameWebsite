## Overview

The Trumna is the Entrati signature automatic rifle with an alternate fire mode that launches an explosive projectile upon reaching a kill count threshold. Its primary fire delivers 82 damage per shot: 29 impact with 53 innate heat damage. The heat elemental component enables reliable Ignite procs without requiring a mod slot, leaving room for players to stack additional elemental types for combination damage.

The Trumna's defining mechanic is its alternate fire. After accumulating enough kills with primary auto fire, the alternate fire launches a large explosive orb that detonates after a short delay, dealing massive area-of-effect damage. This secondary fire resets the kill counter. Experienced players alternate between sustained primary fire and opportunistic explosive rounds to maintain momentum in dense enemy areas.

With 30% status chance and a 24% critical chance with 2.2x multiplier, the Trumna occupies a strong hybrid position. The 200-round magazine is enormous but the 5-second reload is correspondingly long. The weapon is obtained through the Entrati syndicate on Deimos.

## Acquisition

The Trumna is obtained through the Entrati syndicate on the Cambion Drift (Deimos). Players must reach sufficient standing rank with Entrati to access the blueprint offering. Build time is 12 hours after the blueprint is acquired.

## Variants

**Trumna Prime** -- A Prime version drops from Void Relics and offers improved base damage, critical stats, and status chance. It retains the alternate fire mechanic. Mastery Rank 15 is required for the Prime variant.

## Recommended builds

**Heat-viral combo**: Keep the innate heat and add cold + toxin for Viral on top. Heat procs strip shields and provide crowd control; Viral amplifies all subsequent damage. Equip Serration, Split Chamber, Vital Sense, and Point Strike. The 30% status chance ensures reliable Viral procs within a few rounds.

**Full heat status**: Stack multiple heat mods with the innate heat for maximum Ignite proc frequency. High heat proc stacks deal significant damage over time and strip Corpus shields rapidly. Add Galvanized Aptitude to amplify status chance further after kills.

**Explosive alternate build**: Focus on kill speed to charge the alternate fire quickly using damage mods that emphasize per-kill stacking (Galvanized Chamber, Primary Merciless). Reserve alternate fire for tight enemy clusters. Primed Sure Footed prevents self-knockdown from nearby explosions.

## Notes

The alternate fire charges from kills, not time. Tracking the kill counter and planning the explosive shot timing around enemy spawns maximizes the explosive round's impact. In endless missions, the alternating rhythm of primary kills into explosive payoff creates a satisfying damage loop for high-density waves.
