## Overview

The [Coda Hirudo](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Coda_Hirudo) is an exclusive Jade Shadows sparring (fist) melee weapon requiring Mastery Rank 17. It deals 175 total damage per attack with a slash-dominant (140) profile and minor impact (26.25) and puncture (8.75) components. The 34% critical chance and 3.1x critical multiplier are outstanding for a fist weapon, making dedicated crit builds extremely effective. Status chance at 18% is secondary.

Fist weapons have some of the fastest attack speed available in the melee category. The Coda Hirudo's crit stats combined with fist-category speed create a rapid crit-proc machine that excels at building and maintaining combo multiplier.

## Variants

The Coda Hirudo is the enhanced Jade Shadows variant of the Hirudo. The base Hirudo has lower critical chance and multiplier but includes a lifesteal passive on crit. The Coda version trades the lifesteal for superior crit stats. Players who rely on health sustain should compare the two versions before committing.

## Recommended builds

**Blood Rush crit build:** Primed Pressure Point, Berserker Fury, Blood Rush, Organ Shatter, Weeping Wounds, Condition Overload, and two elemental mods for viral. At 34% base critical chance, Blood Rush reaches consistent orange-crit at x3 combo multiplier -- faster ramp-up than most weapons. The 3.1x multiplier ensures each crit delivers substantial damage. Berserker Fury amplifies the already-fast fist attack speed further during kill streaks.

**Pure critical build:** Blood Rush, Organ Shatter, Berserker Fury, Gladiator Vice, and four damage-focused mods. This configuration maximizes crit output without status investment, relying on the sheer damage of 3.1x crits at high combo multipliers.

**Slash proc hybrid:** Weeping Wounds, Hemorrhage, and Condition Overload alongside Blood Rush. The 140 base slash generates bleed procs through Hemorrhage, which bypass armor mitigation. Combining armor bypass with the high crit damage creates an effective Steel Path configuration.

## Synergies

- **Blood Rush:** The 34% base crit reaches orange-crit at x3 combo, making the ramp-up window short. In fast-paced fist combat, x3 combo is typically achieved within 10-15 seconds.
- **Berserker Fury:** Fist weapons already have high base attack speed. Berserker Fury stacks on top, creating the fastest effective attack speed of any melee category when kills maintain the buff.
- **Sparring stances:** Gaia's Tragedy or Seismic Palm provide rapid combo sequences suited to fist weapons. The Coda Hirudo's short range (1.25 m) is partly compensated by the forward-mobility animations in these stances.

## Notes

- Range of 1.25 m is among the shortest available for melee weapons. Position within arm's reach of targets before engaging -- or use slide attacks, which extend effective range slightly.
- The 3.1x critical multiplier is exceptionally high. At orange-crit (2x critical), effective damage per hit reaches 6.2x the base value, and at red-crit (3x critical, achievable with companion Gladiator mods), 9.3x.
- Riven disposition of 1/5 reflects the weapon's high use rate (or Jade Shadows weapon-tier disposition system). Rivens for this weapon roll with lower multipliers, making them less impactful than for low-disposition weapons.
- The Coda Hirudo does not have the base Hirudo's lifesteal passive. Players who relied on the Hirudo's self-heal mechanic in difficult content should account for this difference when building for sustained combat.
