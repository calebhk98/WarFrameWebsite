## Overview

The Twin Krohkur is a dual sword melee weapon available at Mastery Rank 10, crafted from the Market blueprint for 20,000 credits with a 24-hour build time. Its 250 total damage per hit is puncture-dominant: 175 puncture, 45 slash, and 30 impact. Puncture damage penetrates Grineer armor, and at 175 puncture per hit, the Twin Krohkur is one of the stronger puncture-based dual swords. The 45 slash component generates bleed procs for armor-bypassing damage.

The 33% status chance is the Twin Krohkur's primary combat advantage. Combined with the dual sword attack speed and combo strings, status procs accumulate rapidly across a target. The 19% critical chance and 1.7x multiplier are modest -- not worth heavy investment in critical mods. This is a status-focused weapon where Condition Overload and elemental proc stacking deliver more return than critical investment.

At 0.92 attack speed and 2.5-meter range, the Twin Krohkur is slower than lighter dual-blade weapons but compensates with higher per-hit damage. Dual sword stances with wide sweeping moves extend the effective engagement area beyond the 2.5-meter base range.

## Acquisition

The Twin Krohkur blueprint is purchasable from the in-game Market for 20,000 credits. Build time is 24 hours -- longer than most weapons.

## Variants

No Prime, Vandal, or Wraith variant exists for the Twin Krohkur.

## Recommended builds

**Corrosive Condition Overload**: Condition Overload, Primed Pressure Point, Weeping Wounds, electric plus toxin (Corrosive) mods. Corrosive strips Grineer armor -- 6 corrosive procs remove armor entirely. With 33% status and Weeping Wounds at x2 combo, corrosive procs apply within a few swings. After armor strip, the 175 puncture plus slash bleed deals full damage.

**Viral-slash hybrid**: Cold plus toxin for Viral, Weeping Wounds, Condition Overload, Primed Pressure Point, Hunter Munitions. Viral halves enemy max health. The 45 slash combined with Hunter Munitions (slash procs from crits) stacks bleed damage through the halved health pool.

**Status platform**: Four 60/60 elemental dual-stat mods, Weeping Wounds, Condition Overload. Three unique elemental status types from dual-stat combinations plus the inherent physical procs create multiple Condition Overload stacks per target.

## Notes

The 24-hour build time is the longest single-component build in the Market weapons list. Plan ahead and start the build before farming completes. The 1.7x critical multiplier is below most melee weapons of this tier; prioritize status and Condition Overload over critical scaling when allocating mod slots.


The Twin Krohkur's 24-hour build time is one of the longer weapon crafting times in the game. Planning ahead is essential: start the build before beginning the resource farming, not after. Players who collect all necessary components before placing the blueprint in the Foundry can minimize the waiting period. The extended build time does not affect the weapon's in-game performance, only the time investment before first use.
