## Overview

The Evensong is a bow-type primary weapon with an exceptionally high damage profile: 455 Puncture and 195 Slash per shot, with zero Impact. As a single-shot bow (magazine 1, fire rate 1, reload 0.6 seconds), each arrow delivers this combined physical damage in a single hit. The 38% status chance is high for a bow and enables reliable elemental proc application on each arrow. The 25% critical chance and 2.2x multiplier provide a functional crit platform. Mastery Rank 10 is required. The 16.67 accuracy reflects the bow's arrow spread characteristic, which differs from hitscan accuracy.

Bow weapons in Warframe are charged-shot primaries: holding the fire button charges the draw, and releasing at full draw fires the fully charged arrow with maximum damage. The Evensong's 455 Puncture base at full draw represents one of the highest per-arrow Puncture values available among bow primaries. Puncture penetrates armor, making the Evensong particularly effective against Grineer Ferrite Armor targets. The 38% status chance per arrow ensures elemental procs apply consistently with every charged shot, enabling Viral proc application with Cold + Toxin mods for health-damage amplification.

## Variants

The Evensong has no Prime, Kuva, or Tenet variant. It is a standalone bow primary weapon. Players seeking bow upgrades may consider Daikyu or other endgame bow options.

## Recommended builds

**Puncture/Viral status build.** The 455 Puncture combined with 38% status allows consistent Viral proc application. Equip Serration, Split Chamber, Rime Rounds (Cold) and Thermite Rounds (Heat) -- or other Cold/Toxin elemental mods for Viral. Each charged shot with Viral applied amplifies subsequent health damage. Hunter Munitions converts crit hits to Slash procs for armor-bypass bleed damage.

**Critical/Hunter Munitions build.** At 25% base crit with Point Strike and Vital Sense, the Evensong generates reliable yellow crits. Hunter Munitions applies on crit: the resulting Slash procs deal armor-bypassing bleed damage over time alongside the Puncture hit. This is most effective against high-armor Grineer targets where both Puncture and Slash procs compound their damage.

**Status/Galvanized Aptitude build.** The 38% status enables multiple proc types per shot with elemental mod combinations. Equip Galvanized Aptitude alongside Viral and Heat mods. Each unique proc type active on target grants a Galvanized Aptitude damage stack. The single-shot nature means each fully charged arrow applies multiple procs simultaneously.

## Synergies

Ivara's Prowl ability generates stealth damage multipliers on all bow shots. Combined with the Evensong's 455 Puncture base, stealth-multiplied hits deliver exceptional per-arrow damage. Banshee's Sonar highlights weak points where the Evensong's high per-arrow damage can concentrate critical hits. Rhino's Roar amplifies all physical damage proportionally.

## Notes

- As a single-shot bow, the Evensong requires full draw for maximum damage; fire at full charge for the 455 Puncture / 195 Slash damage values.
- Zero Impact means no Impact proc (shield disruption) against Corpus shields; use Magnetic elemental mods to compensate for Corpus engagements.
