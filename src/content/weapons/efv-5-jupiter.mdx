## Overview

The Efv-5 Jupiter is a primary automatic rifle with an innate Corrosive element (7) alongside Slash (31) and Puncture (23) physical damage. The built-in Corrosive damage is particularly noteworthy: it strips Grineer Ferrite Armor and Corpus Robotic armor on each proc, without requiring players to dedicate two mod slots to build Corrosive from scratch. The 25% status chance enables consistent Corrosive proc application during sustained fire. Mastery Rank 14 is required.

The 7.83 fire rate and 65-round magazine provide sustained automatic fire capability. The 133 accuracy is high for a rifle, delivering precise hits at medium range. The 2.5-second reload is standard for a large-magazine primary. The innate Corrosive elemental frees up mod slots for additional elements or damage amplification, enabling efficient builds for Steel Path Grineer and Corrupted content.

## Recommended builds

**Viral/Corrosive combination.** The innate Corrosive frees one elemental slot. Use Primed Fever Strike (Toxin) and Cryo Rounds (Cold) for Viral alongside the innate Corrosive. Corrosive strips armor each proc while Viral amplifies health damage from the Slash component. Add Serration and Split Chamber.

**Galvanized Aptitude build.** The 25% status ensures consistent proc application. Galvanized Aptitude adds damage per unique status type active on the target. Apply Corrosive (innate), Viral (two 60/60 mods), and potentially a third element through Augur mods for maximum Aptitude stacks.

**Critical hybrid.** At 21% crit, add Hunter Munitions alongside Point Strike for simultaneous Slash proc generation and crit scaling. Vital Sense adds crit multiplier. The innate Corrosive strips armor to expose health for Slash bleed proc damage.

## Synergies

Rhino's Roar and Wisp's Fused Reservoir amplify primary weapon damage. Banshee's Sonar highlights weak points where the Efv-5 Jupiter's precise accuracy (133) enables targeted critical headshots. Corrosive Projection aura from teammates combined with the innate Corrosive procs accelerates armor stripping for group play. Saryn's Spores can spread Toxic status across enemy groups when paired with the Efv-5 Jupiter's Viral mod combination, enabling a multi-frame status stack approach for Condition Overload melee follow-through. Cephalon Suda's Entropy Spike augment or any status-spreading Warframe ability compound the high base status chance of 25%, stacking unique proc types rapidly for maximum Galvanized Aptitude damage multipliers when transitioning between groups of enemies in mobile missions.

## Notes

- The innate Corrosive counts as one element for combination purposes; adding a Corrosive mod enhances the innate Corrosive rather than creating a new element.
- At MR14 the Efv-5 Jupiter is a late-game weapon suited to players with endgame mod collections.
