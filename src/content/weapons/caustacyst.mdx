## Overview

The [Caustacyst](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Caustacyst) is a scythe melee weapon with 260 total damage per attack. Its damage has a notable elemental component: 103 corrosive damage in addition to 17 impact, 69 puncture, and 71 slash physical damage. The built-in corrosive damage makes the Caustacyst intrinsically effective against Grineer armored targets, where corrosive removes a percentage of Ferrite and Alloy Armor per proc, bypassing the need to combine cold and toxin mods for the corrosive element. The 37% status chance enables rapid corrosive strip alongside secondary elemental procs.

The 9% critical chance and 2.0x multiplier do not support crit-focused builds. The Caustacyst's strength lies entirely in its status output and intrinsic corrosive damage. Riven disposition at 4/5 indicates above-average roll potential.

Scythes have access to Reaping Spiral and Stalking Fan stance mods, providing wide sweeping combos that hit multiple enemies simultaneously and complement the Caustacyst's status-focused approach.

## Acquisition

The Caustacyst blueprint is available from the Market for credits. Build time is 12 hours in the Foundry. Required resources include standard mid-game materials -- Orokin Cells, Plastids, and common alloys. As a permanently available Market weapon, it is accessible at any progression stage.

## Variants

No Prime, Vandal, or Wraith variant exists. Players seeking scythes with stronger critical stats should consider comparing the Caustacyst against the Reaper Prime, which offers higher critical chance and multiplier without the built-in elemental component.

## Recommended builds

**Status corrosive strip build:** Primed Pressure Point, Berserker Fury, Condition Overload, Weeping Wounds, and two additional elemental mods (heat and another corrosive pair or blast). The built-in corrosive eliminates the need to dedicate two mod slots to forming corrosive, freeing them for additional elemental types. More unique status types active simultaneously means higher Condition Overload damage multipliers per hit.

**Viral + corrosive hybrid:** Replace one elemental pair with viral (Fever Strike plus North Wind). The built-in corrosive handles armor strip; viral halves enemy health. This combination works across all enemy factions without needing separate mod configurations.

**Steel Path farm build:** Add Primed Smite (faction-specific) and use the freed elemental slots for heat and toxin combinations. The 37% status chance ensures armor strip and damage procs accumulate rapidly, which is critical in Steel Path where enemy armor values are significantly elevated.

## Synergies

- **Condition Overload:** The built-in corrosive proc plus any two additional elemental status types from mods quickly reaches the maximum Condition Overload damage bonus, making this mod exceptionally effective on the Caustacyst.
- **Corrosive Projection (aura):** Stacking aura with the corrosive procs from the weapon strips armor faster, reducing the number of hits needed to neutralize Grineer heavy units.
- **Saryn's Spores:** Corrosive procs spread through Spores across enemy groups, multiplying armor strip across the entire room rather than target-by-target.

## Notes

- The built-in corrosive damage is a fixed component of the weapon's damage output. Adding additional corrosive mods stacks on top of the base value, increasing total corrosive damage but offering diminishing returns compared to adding a different elemental type.
- Scythe combos from Reaping Spiral include wide horizontal sweeps that hit enemies at range. At 2.9 m range, the Caustacyst's arcs cover a substantial area per combo activation.
- The 37% status chance at 1.0 attack speed means status procs accumulate steadily rather than explosively. Weeping Wounds accelerates this as combo multiplier climbs.
- Mastery Rank 7 places the Caustacyst in mid-game territory. Players at this rank are typically building their first forma-invested weapon; the Caustacyst's built-in elemental reduces the mod investment needed for a functional build, making it a good candidate for an early forma project.
