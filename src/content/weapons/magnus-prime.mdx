## Overview

Magnus Prime is the primed form of the Magnus heavy pistol, offering high per-shot damage with a notable 28% critical chance and 2.8x critical multiplier that make it one of the more crit-capable secondary weapons in the game. The damage distribution leans toward impact at 44.1 per shot, with puncture and slash at roughly 27 each. Impact damage primarily affects shields, making the Magnus Prime effective at cycling through shielded Corpus targets.

The 5.0 fire rate combined with the small 8-round magazine creates a secondary that demands deliberate trigger control. Players who fire indiscriminately will find themselves reloading frequently; the 1.6-second reload is not punishing, but managing the 8 rounds efficiently is part of effective use. Accuracy at 16.0 suits close to medium range engagements. The mastery rank 14 requirement is appropriate given the weapon's competitive statistics.

## Acquisition

Magnus Prime parts drop from Void Relics. The blueprint, barrel, and receiver drop across multiple relic types at varying rarities. Trading with other players through the in-game trade chat or [warframe.market](https://warframe.market) is an alternative route. Check current relic pools for the active drop locations.

## Variants

The base [Magnus](/weapons/magnus) provides a lower-MR entry point with reduced stats. The Magnus Prime represents the recommended form once relics are accessible, as the improved critical multiplier substantially increases damage ceiling in crit builds.

## Recommended builds

The 28% critical chance and 2.8x multiplier support pure crit builds effectively. Critical Delay in the exilus slot can push base crit chance toward 50%+ before additional mods, though the fire rate penalty requires evaluation based on playstyle.

Critical build priorities:
- Hornet Strike for base damage
- Barrel Diffusion for multishot (doubles effective damage output)
- Lethal Torrent for combined fire rate and multishot
- Pistol Gambit or Critical Delay for critical chance
- Primed Pistol Gambit if available
- Hollow Point for critical damage

For status-hybrid builds, Convulsion, Frostbite, and other dual-stat elemental mods allow simultaneous crit chance gains and elemental damage stacking. Viral (Cold + Toxin) combined with crit mods produces a versatile loadout that scales against most faction types.

The 8-round magazine benefits from Trick Mag or Primed Slip Magazine to reduce reload frequency in sustained engagements. Prioritize magazine mods only after damage scaling is established.

## Notes

The Magnus Prime's lower accuracy value of 16.0 is characteristic of many pistols and reflects practical performance rather than per-shot precision. At typical engagement ranges inside missions (under 30 meters), the spread is manageable. At longer ranges, the shot dispersion becomes noticeable, which reinforces the weapon's role as a close-to-medium range secondary.

The high critical multiplier makes the Magnus Prime respond strongly to headshots in modes where aim is practical. Some players use it as an on-demand execution tool for downed enemies or priority targets rather than as a primary source of sustained damage. In that role its 8-round magazine is rarely a constraint.
