## Overview

The Magnus Prime is the primed heavy pistol combining impact and puncture damage. It offers strong damage per shot and critical potential, serving as a reliable secondary for demanding content. The 44.1 impact, 26.95 puncture, and 26.95 slash damage represents balanced distribution with emphasis on impact. The high impact damage makes it particularly effective against shields and certain armor types.

The Magnus Prime's 2.0 critical multiplier provides excellent scaling for critical-focused secondary builds. The 28% status chance supports hybrid damage builds. The 5.0 fire rate is moderate for a pistol, encouraging deliberate trigger pulls rather than spray-and-pray tactics. This moderate fire rate combined with high damage per shot creates consistent, predictable damage output.

With 8 round magazine capacity, the Magnus Prime encourages ammunition conservation and precision. The 1.6 reload time is respectable, allowing players to maintain engagement without excessive downtime. The weapon's 14 mastery requirement reflects its prime status and strong performance characteristics. The modest magazine size encourages shot discipline.

The Magnus Prime stands as one of the most reliable secondaries available, useful across mission levels when properly modded. The weapon's solid damage per shot and critical potential make it excellent for focused targets or emergency backup during primary weapon reloads. Some players dedicate significant forma investment to Magnus Prime critical builds.

The weapon's balanced damage profile and straightforward mod path enable quick optimization without specialized knowledge. Crit-focused mods like Critical Damage and Vital Sense transform the Magnus Prime into a devastating secondary. The weapon performs admirably in sorties and moderate-to-high level missions.

Veterans appreciate the Magnus Prime's reliability as trustworthy backup secondary.
