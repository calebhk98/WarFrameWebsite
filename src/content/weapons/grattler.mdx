## Overview

A Grineer-designed explosive autocannon firing bursting rounds with high Blast and Slash damage. The Blast damage type procs Knockdown, providing crowd control capability alongside damage output. Balanced at 25% critical chance and 25% status chance, it supports hybrid builds. Introduced in Update 17.0 (2015-07-31), the Grattler is notable for Grineer-origin design with high Blast damage (310) enabling Knockdown crowd control; balanced 25%/25% crit and status profile.

Captured from Grineer Archwing units during early Void expeditions, the Grattler's autocannon design was reverse-engineered by Tenno weapon-smiths. The original Grineer design prioritized hull-breaching capability against fortified positions, which translates to exceptional Blast damage in direct combat.

## Acquisition

Blueprint from Market for 15,000 credits. Component parts (Forma, Oxium, Salvage, Tellurium) crafted as Grineer-themed resources. Build cost 65,000 credits -- the highest of the classic Archwing weapons. The Grattler requires Mastery Rank 4 to equip.

Arch-gun weapons require either standard crafting resources, Syndicate standing for component parts, or specialized mission drops. Once crafted, equipping the Grattler in Archwing uses the standard Arch-gun slot. To use it as a Heavy Weapon in ground missions or Railjack combat, a Gravimag must be installed. Gravimags are available from Clan Dojos via Energy Lab research, or from Arbitrations as rotation rewards.

## Variants

Kuva Grattler is a significantly improved Kuva Lich variant with a doubled magazine (60 rounds), improved stats, and an innate elemental bonus from the Lich. Mastery Rank 15 required for Kuva Grattler.

The Arch-gun family spans weapons from Tenno engineering (Corvas, Fluctus, Velocitus), Grineer design (Grattler, Kuva line), and Entrati craftsmanship (Cortege, Mausolon, Morgha), each with distinct stat profiles suited to different engagement contexts.

## Recommended builds

Equal 25% crit and 25% status create a genuine hybrid build platform. Blast procs Knockdown for crowd control. Adding Radiation (Heat + Electricity) creates an effective Grineer counter -- Radiation procs Confusion while Blast Knockdowns suppress groups. The Kuva Grattler is a strictly superior variant with doubled magazine capacity.

**Core Arch-gun mods to consider:**

- Rubedo-Lined Barrel -- +165% Arch-gun damage (primary damage mod for all builds)
- Hollowed Bullets -- +120% critical chance (essential for any crit-focused build)
- Critical Focus -- +60% critical multiplier while aiming
- Modified Munitions -- +90% status chance (for status-focused builds)
- Arch-gun Ace -- +165% fire rate (useful on slow-fire weapons to improve feel)
- Parallax Scope -- +30% critical chance while aiming
- Charged Bullets -- +60% magazine capacity
- Superior Defenses -- reduces reload time
- Conductive Coils -- adds Electricity damage
- Thermite Rounds -- adds Heat damage

Prioritize Rubedo-Lined Barrel as the first mod in any Arch-gun build. Follow with critical or status mods based on the weapon's stat profile. Elemental mods provide faction-specific damage type flexibility.

## Notes

The Grattler operates in the Arch-gun slot, used in Archwing missions and as a Heavy Weapon on the ground via Gravimag. In Railjack (Empyrean) missions, the Arch-gun serves as the primary armament during space combat against fighters and Crewships.

Explosive rounds and Knockdown procs are effective against Railjack crew during boarding events. Interior corridor combat maximizes the AoE radius of each detonation.

Archwing combat system notes: Archwing missions require completing The Archwing quest. Four Archwings are available -- Itzal (mobility/stealth), Amesha (tank/support), Elytron (AoE damage), and Odonata (balanced starter). Elytron's Warhead provides additional AoE, complementing Grattler's sustained explosive output. Itzal enables approach for optimal engagement range where AoE effectiveness peaks.

The weapon's Riven disposition is 3/5 (moderate). Riven dispositions range from 0 (absolute minimum, strong weapon) to 5 (maximum, weak weapon). DE adjusts dispositions quarterly based on weapon usage rates.
