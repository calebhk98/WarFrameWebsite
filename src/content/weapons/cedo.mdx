## Overview

The [Cedo](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Cedo) is a shotgun primary weapon designed for heavy status application. The Cedo's primary fire deals 30 slash damage per pellet at 3.83 fire rate with a 40-round magazine, but its most notable feature is an extremely low 0.33% individual pellet status chance. The weapon compensates for this with a secondary fire mode that shoots a specialized bolt: hitting an enemy with the secondary fire causes all subsequent primary fire pellets that hit the same enemy to proc status effects. This interaction enables the Cedo to serve as both status primer and follow-up damage dealer.

The 20% critical chance and 2.4x multiplier support critical builds alongside the status mechanic. The Cedo was crafted through the Entrati laboratory and represents mid-to-late game investment at Mastery Rank 8.

## Acquisition

The Cedo blueprint and components are available from the Entrati Syndicate in the Necralisk on Deimos. Players must have progressed through the Heart of Deimos quest and built sufficient Entrati standing (Surah rank) to access the blueprint. Components require Necralisk resources including Thaumic Distillate and other Deimos-specific materials. Check the [Cedo Wiki page](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Cedo) for current standing requirements.

## Variants

No Prime, Vandal, or Wraith variant of the Cedo exists. The base version is the only available form.

## Recommended builds

**Status shotgun build:** Point Blank, Hell's Chamber, Scattered Justice, Blaze, Contagious Spread, Frigid Aperture, Jolt, and Seeking Fury. Fire the secondary mode at priority targets to trigger status-on-hit. Follow with primary fire to apply proc types from all equipped elemental mods. The 20% critical chance combined with Hell's Chamber's multishot creates reliable critical-status hybrid damage.

**Critical slash build:** Primed Ravage, Point Blank, Hell's Chamber, Shrapnel Shot (slash), Hemorrhage, and elemental mods. The Cedo's 30 base slash damage generates Hemorrhage bleed procs. Secondary fire triggers the status conversion on subsequent primary pellets, turning the low-proc-chance primaries into guaranteed proc deliveries.

**Armor strip primer:** Configure primarily for corrosive (electric + toxin mods) to rapidly strip armor after secondary-fire tagging. Once armor is stripped via secondary-triggered corrosive procs, switch to melee or primary for follow-up damage on the armor-stripped target.

## Synergies

- **Secondary fire + primary combo:** The defining Cedo mechanic. Tag a target with secondary fire, then dump primary fire pellets to apply all elemental status procs simultaneously. Each pellet's 0.33% status chance becomes 100% for tagged targets.
- **Galvanized Savvy:** Provides escalating status chance per kill. For the Cedo, this reduces the dependency on secondary fire tagging as the bonus stacks, making primary fire alone more proc-reliable in high-kill-rate situations.
- **Viral/corrosive combination:** Tag with secondary, proc viral (toxin + cold) to halve enemy health, then add corrosive for armor strip. Three unique status types activate on a single target within one secondary-primary sequence.

## Notes

- Riven disposition at 1/5 is the lowest available, reflecting the Cedo's exceptionally high community use rate. Rivens for this weapon roll with minimal multipliers, making riven investment less impactful compared to low-disposition weapons.
- The secondary fire consumes a secondary projectile resource, not the primary magazine. Monitor secondary fire ammunition separately from the main magazine count.
- At Mastery Rank 8, the Cedo is accessible relatively early compared to its Entrati Standing requirement. Players may reach MR8 before they have sufficient standing to purchase the blueprint. Plan Entrati standing farming alongside other Deimos objectives.
- The 2.2-second reload is slightly above average for shotguns. With a 40-round magazine at 3.83 fire rate, the magazine lasts approximately 10 seconds, so reload opportunities are frequent but not immediate.
