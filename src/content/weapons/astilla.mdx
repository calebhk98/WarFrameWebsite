## Overview

The Astilla is a single-projectile shotgun requiring Mastery Rank 10 that functions more like a precision semi-automatic rifle than a conventional scatter-shot weapon. Unlike most shotguns that fire multiple pellets per shot, the Astilla fires a single glass slug that deals full impact, puncture, and slash damage on impact -- totaling 190 base damage per shot -- and then shatters on contact to generate a secondary area-of-effect burst. This dual-hit mechanic rewards accuracy while providing splash damage for grouped enemies.

The combined base damage of 190 (70 impact, 78 puncture, 42 slash) delivers substantial per-shot punch compared to traditional shotguns whose per-pellet damage is divided across a spread pattern. The result is a weapon that maintains damage consistency at ranges where conventional shotguns fall off. The Astilla's 25 accuracy rating reflects how shotguns report accuracy -- the slug itself flies in a consistent arc from the barrel without pellet spread, confirming its status as a slug thrower rather than scatter weapon.

At 33% status chance and a 4.33 round-per-second fire rate, the Astilla applies elemental procs at a meaningful rate for a shotgun, making status builds a viable path alongside the moderate 17% critical chance.

## Acquisition

The Astilla blueprint is available from the in-game Market for 25,000 credits. Component blueprints are obtained through mission drops or Nightwave Intermission offerings. Crafting requires resources available at mid-game progression, with a standard 24-hour Foundry build timer. The completed weapon is also purchasable for Platinum directly from the Market.

## Variants

The **Astilla Prime** is the enhanced Orokin variant with improved statistics across the board, including higher base damage, better critical chance, and an expanded magazine. As a Prime weapon, it is available through Void Relic drops from applicable Void missions, Prime Resurgence events, or direct player trading. The Prime variant is the long-term investment platform for players who enjoy the Astilla's slug mechanics.

## Recommended builds

**Status Shotgun Build:** The Astilla's 33% status chance enables reliable elemental proc stacking. Equip Hell's Chamber (multishot -- note: multishot on a slug weapon generates additional projectiles that each apply the shatter effect), Contagious Spread and Charged Shell for Corrosive, plus Vigilante Armaments for additional multishot. This dramatically increases DPS and status proc rate. Add Blaze for base damage and Heat proc stacking.

**Hybrid Crit-Status Build:** At 17% CC, Point Strike brings the Astilla to usable crit territory. Combine with Vital Sense, two elemental mods for a strong damage combination, and Primed Ravage or Ravage for multiplier scaling. The slug mechanic means every shot has potential for a critical hit unlike spread weapons where individual pellets crit independently.

**Precision Engagement:** The Astilla's single-slug accuracy allows effective engagement at ranges most shotguns cannot reliably cover. Builds with high base damage and flight-path awareness take advantage of the secondary shatter AOE -- hitting enemies slightly above ground level maximizes shatter splash coverage.

## Notes

The Astilla requires awareness of its unique slug mechanics to maximize effectiveness. Shots must connect with a target or surface to trigger the shatter burst; missed shots waste the secondary AOE damage component entirely.

The glass aesthetic and shatter visual effects of the Astilla make it a popular cosmetic choice among players who enjoy distinctive weapon appearances alongside functional performance. The impact combined with shatter creates satisfying visual feedback on kills.

In Corpus missions, the combined impact and puncture weighting is well-suited against Shielded and Robotic enemies, respectively, and the status chance supports Magnetic proc stacking against shields when appropriate elemental mods are equipped.
