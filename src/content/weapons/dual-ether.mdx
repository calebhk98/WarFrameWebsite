## Overview

The Dual Ether is a pair of energy swords with a Puncture-heavy damage distribution (126) split by equal Impact (27) and Slash (27) components. The 28% status chance is the weapon's strongest attribute, enabling reliable elemental proc application at the 1.0 attack speed over the 2.5 m range. The 20% critical chance provides a functional but secondary crit layer. Mastery Rank 8 is required.

Dual Ether uses Dual Sword stance mods (Swirling Tiger, Crossing Snakes, Gemini Cross), which produce multi-hit arcing combos. These wide sweeps benefit from the 28% status chance, applying procs across multiple enemies in a single combo step. The Dual Ether is a mid-game weapon that transitions players from basic dual swords into the higher-tier dual-sword family.

The 28% status chance combined with Weeping Wounds scales to very high effective status at x4 combo, enabling rapid multi-elemental proc cycling for Condition Overload's multiplicative damage stacking. The Puncture dominance pairs well with Corrosive elemental mods against armored Grineer targets; Corrosive procs strip Ferrite Armor through proc accumulation, and the 126 Puncture base benefits from the armor-penetration advantage against partially stripped targets.

## Variants

The Dual Ether has no Prime or Kuva/Tenet variant. Players seeking an upgraded dual-sword option should explore the Dual Keres Prime or Dual Zoren Prime as natural successors.

## Recommended builds

**Corrosive status build.** The 28% status makes Corrosive reliable after a few hits. Use Infected Clip and Voltaic Strike. Add Primed Pressure Point, Organ Shatter, and Berserker Fury for damage and speed amplification. This configuration works well against Grineer Ferrite Armor.

**Viral/Slash combo.** Use Primed Fever Strike (Toxin) and Cryo Rounds (Cold) for Viral. While Slash is not the dominant type, Viral amplifies health damage from any source, including Puncture. Add Weeping Wounds for scaling status and Blood Rush for crit scaling simultaneously.

**Condition Overload build.** Equip two different 60/60 elemental pairs alongside Condition Overload. The Dual Ether's 28% status with multishot quickly applies multiple unique status types, generating multiplicative Condition Overload damage bonuses.

## Synergies

Frame abilities that slow or group enemies (Nova's Molecular Prime, Khora's Strangledome) give the Dual Ether's wide combo arcs time to hit multiple targets. Condition Overload rewards Warframe abilities that apply additional status procs, such as Saryn's Spores spreading Toxic.

## Notes

- The 28% status chance is the primary reason to use the Dual Ether; build around this strength.
- The Puncture-heavy physical damage underperforms against Infested units; consider faction-specific elemental combinations.
