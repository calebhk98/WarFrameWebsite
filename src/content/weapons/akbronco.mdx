## Overview

The [Akbronco](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Akbronco) is the dual version of the Bronco secondary shotgun, firing 24 pellets per shot (12 from each weapon). Each pellet deals 42 damage: 33.6 impact, 4.2 puncture, 4.2 slash. At point-blank range, all 24 pellets connect, delivering 1,008 total impact damage per shot -- sufficient to strip Corpus shields instantly. The 2-round-per-trigger magazine and short 2.0-second reload encourage rapid burst-and-reload cycling in close quarters.

Critical chance (6%) and multiplier (2.0x) are minimal. Status chance at 3.1% per pellet is low but across 24 pellets per shot, status proc chances accumulate per volley. Riven disposition at 5/5 is the maximum.

## Acquisition

The Akbronco blueprint is available from the Market for credits. As an MR2 weapon, it is immediately accessible to most players. Crafting requires minimal materials. The Akbronco can also be purchased built with platinum.

## Variants

The Akbronco Prime (Mastery Rank 6) improves critical chance, critical multiplier, status chance, and pellet count substantially. Players with access to Prime relics should pursue the Prime variant for endgame secondary use.

## Recommended builds

**Shotgun pellet status:** Hornet Strike, Barrel Diffusion, Lethal Torrent, Pistol Pestilence, Frostbite, Scorch, Jolt, and Primed Quickdraw. Across 24 pellets, elemental status chance accumulates per volley even at the low 3.1% per-pellet base. Multiple pellets hitting simultaneously create cascading proc application from combined probability.

**Point-blank shield strip:** Hornet Strike, Barrel Diffusion, Lethal Torrent, and elemental mods for magnetic (cold + electric) to shred Corpus shields. At zero range, 24 pellets deliver concentrated impact and magnetic damage, instantly stripping most Corpus unit shields.

**Riven-powered:** With a 5/5 disposition riven adding multishot, status chance, or critical chance, the Akbronco becomes a competitive close-range secondary. Multishot adds additional pellets per trigger pull, exponentially increasing point-blank damage density.

## Synergies

- **Point-blank engagement pattern:** In close quarters, 24 pellets connecting simultaneously deliver exceptional burst damage. Warframes that close gaps quickly (Ash, Volt, Excalibur) maximize Akbronco effectiveness.
- **Magnetic Corpus strip:** All 24 pellets apply independent magnetic proc chances. Against Corpus, even the 3.1% per-pellet status from magnetic mods can trigger multiple shield drain procs per volley.
- **Primed Quickdraw:** Fast holster speed enables quick transition from Akbronco burst to primary or melee follow-up. The burst-and-swap pattern maximizes Akbronco's close-range value.

## Notes

- Accuracy of 3.7 reflects the extreme pellet spread. At any range beyond 5 meters, significant pellet scatter results in missed hits. Strictly a close-quarters weapon.
- The 4-round (2 shots) magazine empties in 0.4 seconds at 5.0 fire rate. The 2.0-second reload is effectively constant during sustained combat. Internalize the fire-fire-reload rhythm.
- Riven disposition at 5/5 is exceptional. Any riven roll is worth inspecting for multishot, critical chance, or status chance improvements.
