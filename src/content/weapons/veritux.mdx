## Overview

The Veritux is an Archwing melee weapon available from Mastery Rank 0, making it the entry-level option for players who have unlocked Archwing. Its 470 total damage per hit is dominated by puncture (329), with balanced impact (70.5) and slash (70.5) components. The puncture profile is effective against Grineer and Corpus enemies that populate Archwing missions.

Archwing melee weapons function in Archwing combat mode -- space and underwater tileset missions where the standard loadout is replaced by Archwing-specific gear. The Veritux's 3-meter range enables sweeping attacks against single targets in the often-sparse Archwing engagement zone. The 0.83 attack speed is appropriate for the Archwing category, where combat pacing differs from ground missions.

With 5% critical chance and 10% status chance, the Veritux is not a proc-heavy weapon. Its role is straightforward: deliver high puncture damage to enemy craft and units in Archwing missions using the entry-level Archwing melee option.

## Acquisition

The Veritux blueprint is purchasable from the in-game Market for 25,000 credits. Build time is 24 hours. No quest unlock is required.

## Variants

**Prisma Veritux** -- An enhanced version available from the Void Trader (Baro Ki'Teer) with improved critical chance and status chance while retaining the Veritux's core damage profile. Check when Baro Ki'Teer visits with Prisma Veritux in stock.

No standard Prime, Vandal, or Wraith variant exists. Players seeking improved Archwing melee options should also look to the Knux.

## Recommended builds

**Damage-focused Archwing build**: Equip Entropy Spike (Archwing melee damage), Meteor Crash (Archwing melee damage), and Seeking Talons (Archwing melee damage). Archwing-specific mods are fewer in number than ground melee mods.

**Anti-Corpus (radiation)**: Add electric and heat adapters for radiation damage, which deals bonus damage against Corpus units and confuses enemies.

**Anti-Grineer (corrosive)**: Combine electric and toxin adapters for corrosive to strip Grineer armor. The puncture base of the Veritux synergizes with corrosive.

## Notes

Archwing melee is used less frequently than Archwing guns in most missions because gun range often exceeds what melee provides. However, in dense close-quarters scenarios like Railjack boarding actions, melee has tactical value. Archwing melee mods are distinct from ground melee mods and must be collected separately.


The Veritux's role within Archwing mission types is shaped by the broader Archwing combat context. Archwing missions occur in space and underwater environments where movement is three-dimensional. At 0.83 attack speed and 3-meter range, the Veritux requires approaching enemy fighters and drones closely for each swing. In missions with sparse enemy positioning, the travel time between targets limits the Veritux's sustained damage compared to Archwing guns that can engage at long range.

The Prisma Veritux available from Baro Ki'Teer offers improved critical and status stats over the base version, making it the preferred choice for players who invest in Archwing melee. Players who encounter Baro Ki'Teer with Prisma Veritux in stock should consider acquiring it for the improved stats, as Baro rotates his inventory and the Prisma version is not always available.
