## Overview

The Twin Vipers Wraith is the Wraith variant of the Twin Vipers dual automatics, requiring Mastery Rank 7. It improves on the base Twin Vipers with higher damage per round, better critical stats (19% versus 15%, and 2.0x versus 1.5x multiplier), and a larger 40-round magazine -- while retaining the extreme 25.0 rounds-per-second fire rate. At 18 damage per shot split across 14.4 impact, 1.8 puncture, and 1.8 slash, the Wraith version is more impact-concentrated than the base and hits harder per individual round.

The 19% critical chance with 2.0x multiplier is a meaningful upgrade from the base 15% and 1.5x. With Primed Pistol Gambit, critical chance reaches above 60%, producing reliable crits during sustained fire. The 9% status chance is low -- elemental procs remain possible through volume but are not the primary damage vector. The 40-round magazine at 25 fire rate lasts 1.6 seconds before the 2-second reload.

The Twin Vipers Wraith was distributed through in-game alerts and Nightwave offerings historically. Availability varies; check current in-game event and Nightwave offerings.

## Recommended builds

**Impact-crit Corpus**: Hornet Strike, Barrel Diffusion, Primed Pistol Gambit, Lethal Torrent, magnetic (cold plus electric), and Primed Target Cracker. Impact at 14.4 per round combined with magnetic deals bonus damage to Corpus shields. Primed Pistol Gambit at 19% base reaches near 60% crit chance for consistent crits.

**Sustained fire crit**: Hornet Strike, Barrel Diffusion, Primed Pistol Gambit, Lethal Torrent, and two elemental mods. Maximize damage per round and fire rate. The 40-round magazine allows slightly longer burst windows than the base before the 2-second reload.

**Ammo mutation**: Ammo Drum or secondary ammo mutation mods for extended field use. At 25 fire rate, the Twin Vipers Wraith consumes ammo at a rate that warrants ammo efficiency consideration in longer missions.

## Notes

Accuracy at 11.1 is low -- spread is significant at medium range. Point-blank engagement is where the Wraith's fire rate advantage matters most. At distance, spread diffuses the projectile cluster, reducing effective DPS. The 2-second reload combined with the 1.6-second magazine duration means roughly equal fire and reload times. Burst the magazine deliberately and anticipate the reload window to avoid being caught mid-reload in enemy contact.
