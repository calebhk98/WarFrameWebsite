## Overview

The [Coda Pathocyst](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Coda_Pathocyst) is a Jade Shadows infested whip melee weapon at Mastery Rank 17. It deals 270 total damage per attack including 92 innate viral damage alongside 58 impact, 65 puncture, and 55 slash physical damage. The 35% status chance combined with the built-in viral proc provides immediate health reduction upon the first hit. The 20% critical chance and 2.3x multiplier support hybrid builds. Attack speed of 0.67 is notably slow, requiring berserker or speed mods to compensate.

Built-in viral damage means every hit applies a viral-forming component without requiring two elemental mods to create the combination. This frees mod slots for additional elemental types, enabling more diverse proc accumulation for Condition Overload stacking.

## Variants

The Coda Pathocyst is the Jade Shadows enhanced form of the Pathocyst. The base Pathocyst is an infested-themed whip with similar viral innate but lower overall stats. The Coda version delivers superior damage and status output at the cost of a higher mastery requirement.

## Recommended builds

**Viral dominant status build:** Primed Pressure Point, Berserker Fury, Condition Overload, Weeping Wounds, Blood Rush, Organ Shatter, and two additional elemental mods (heat and electric or toxin). Built-in viral frees two mod slots that would otherwise form the viral combination. Adding heat and corrosive (electric + toxin from mods) creates three distinct status types simultaneously: viral, corrosive, and heat. Condition Overload reaches full bonus within the first several hits. Blood Rush uses the 20% base crit to contribute crit damage as combo climbs.

**Berserker-compensated build:** Add Berserker Fury and Gladiator Vice to counteract the 0.67 attack speed. With these mods active, the effective attack speed approaches 1.0-1.2 range, making the Coda Pathocyst competitive in sustained combat.

**Heavy attack whip build:** Whip heavy attacks perform a ground-traveling strike that hits enemies in a line. Corrupt Charge plus Killing Blow creates burst damage whip slams that apply viral procs across multiple targets simultaneously.

## Synergies

- **Built-in viral:** Every attack applies a viral-component hit, reducing target health by 50% on proc. Combined with subsequent physical and elemental damage from mods, the health-halved target dies much faster.
- **Condition Overload:** Viral (built-in) plus heat plus corrosive from mods creates three unique proc types. Condition Overload's full bonus activates rapidly, amplifying all subsequent hits multiplicatively.
- **Whip ground slam:** The traveling ground slam hits multiple enemies in sequence. Built-in viral procs on each hit in the slam line reduce the health of multiple targets simultaneously.

## Notes

- Attack speed of 0.67 is the weapon's primary limitation. Berserker Fury is effectively mandatory in any combat-focused build -- without it, the slow swing rate reduces effective DPS below comparable status weapons.
- Range of 1.3 m is extremely short for a whip. This appears to reflect the base melee reach rather than the whip's full extension. Whip combo animations extend effective range significantly beyond the listed value during specific moves.
- The Mastery Rank 17 requirement ensures players have access to Primed mods and advanced mod configurations before encountering this weapon. Builds without forma investment will underperform relative to the weapon's potential.
- Riven disposition data for Coda weapons may differ from their base counterparts. Check current disposition before investing in a riven for this weapon.
