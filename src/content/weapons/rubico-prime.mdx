## Overview

The Rubico Prime is the primed variant of the Grineer sniper rifle, combining excellent base damage with respectable fire rate characteristics. It provides reliable long-range damage output with strong critical multiplier scaling. The 149.6 impact damage and 28 slash damage make the Rubico Prime one of the higher-damage sniper rifles available. This damage profile ensures effectiveness against diverse enemy armor types through impact-focused strikes.

The Rubico Prime's 2.0 critical multiplier is notably high for a sniper, providing strong scaling when combined with critical damage mods. The 16% base critical chance establishes a foundation for critical builds that become truly devastating with proper modding. This weapon represents one of the most forgiving sniper rifles for newer players while remaining competitive for veterans. The combination of high base damage and respectable critical multiplier creates natural critical scaling that benefits from standard crit-focused mod loadouts.

The 3.67 fire rate for a sniper is unusually high, allowing the Rubico Prime to maintain a faster firing rhythm than most alternatives. The 5 round magazine is tight, requiring players to be ammunition-conscious in extended missions. However, the higher fire rate partially compensates by enabling more frequent shots before reload requirements become pressing. This combination of high fire rate and low magazine creates interesting tactical considerations where quick reloading becomes necessary.

The Rubico Prime's accessibility and performance make it a popular choice across skill levels. The weapon performs excellently in sorties, void fissures, and moderate-to-high level content. Modding typically focuses on critical amplification through Critical Damage and Vital Sense, creating reliable crit builds that scale well into endgame.

The weapon's combination of high base impact damage and good critical multiplier makes it versatile for both crit-focused and hybrid builds. Elemental effects add nicely to crit chains, creating varied build options depending on mission requirements.

The Rubico Prime's reputation as a reliable, user-friendly sniper rifle makes it an excellent choice.
