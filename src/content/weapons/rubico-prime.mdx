## Overview

The Rubico Prime is the primed variant of the Rubico sniper rifle and is widely recognized as one of the strongest sniper platforms in Warframe for critical-focused play. Its 38% base critical chance and 3x critical multiplier represent a rare combination that allows consistent orange or red critical hits with minimal mod investment. At 149.6 impact damage per shot, it deals the bulk of its damage through a single high-impact hit, making it effective against shielded Corpus enemies and general armored targets.

Where many sniper rifles demand scope zoom and patient positioning, the Rubico Prime's 3.67 fire rate is high enough for semi-rapid follow-up shots. This makes it adaptable to aggressive close-to-medium-range play that other snipers cannot manage. The trade-off is a 5-round magazine, which demands reload discipline, especially in extended high-level runs where targets require multiple shots to drop.

The Rubico Prime has become a standard choice for Eidolon hunts due to its high damage-per-shot potential and synergy with Volt's Electric Shield, which doubles the damage of hitscan shots passing through it.

## Acquisition

Rubico Prime components drop from void relics across multiple eras. The set has been accessible outside of the vault for extended periods. Blueprint, Stock, and Barrel each appear in different relic tier reward tables. The Warframe Market is the fastest route to a full unranked set for players with platinum.

## Variants

The base [Rubico](/weapons/rubico) shares the same sniper archetype at lower critical values (24% chance, 2.4x multiplier) and mastery rank 6. The Prime retains the same base damage profile but significantly improves the critical ceiling, making it substantially stronger in end-game scenarios.

## Recommended builds

**Eidolon critical build.** Critical Delay and Vital Sense are the core mods, pushing critical chance above 80% and multiplier to roughly 8x with Primed Chamber included. Matching your elemental mod to the Eidolon's weak point (radiation for Hydrolyst synovia) maximizes per-shot damage during time-sensitive shield phases.

**General crit build.** Point Strike and Vital Sense deliver consistent orange crits in standard missions. Argon Scope adds a conditional bonus that activates immediately on headshots, which sniper playstyle naturally facilitates. Serration and a 90% elemental mod round out the build for damage scaling.

**Speed-fire build.** Replacing Critical Delay with Vigilante Fervor increases fire rate to around 5.5 rounds per second. This sacrifices some critical consistency for faster follow-up shots, which is useful in missions where immediate multi-target response matters more than maximum single-shot output.

## Notes

- The 5-round magazine requires a reload after every other small enemy encounter; Fast Hands or Primed Fast Hands reduces the 2.0-second reload notably.
- Headshots trigger Argon Scope reliably; a scoped headshot on the first kill activates the buff for the duration of the engagement.
- Rubico Prime's combination of high fire rate and strong crit makes it competitive even without a riven mod, which is uncommon for weapons at this power level.
- Volt's Electric Shield buffs hitscan shots by 100% damage and adds a 50% critical chance bonus; positioning shots through the shield is a core Eidolon technique.
