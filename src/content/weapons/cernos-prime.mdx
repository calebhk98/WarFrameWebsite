## Overview

The [Cernos Prime](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Cernos_Prime) is the Prime variant of the Cernos tri-bow, firing three simultaneous arrows per shot. Its damage is impact-dominant: 165.6 impact per arrow with minor puncture (9.2) and slash (9.2). The 35% critical chance and 2.0x multiplier make it a strong critical platform, and the 30% status chance enables reliable proc application across the three arrows per shot. The 0.65-second reload (nocking next three-arrow volley) is the fastest of any bow variant.

Three arrows per shot at 35% critical chance means multiple simultaneous crit opportunities per trigger pull. With appropriate mods, the Cernos Prime delivers high burst damage from consistent yellow-crit triple-arrow volleys.

## Variants

The base Cernos (Mastery Rank 0) fires a single arrow per shot and has lower critical chance (20%). The Mutalist Cernos fires a single arrow that releases spores. The Cernos Prime is the superior endgame variant for triple-arrow critical builds. No Vandal or Wraith version exists.

## Recommended builds

**Triple-arrow critical build:** Serration, Split Chamber, Point Strike, Vital Sense, Hunter Munitions, and three elemental mods for viral or corrosive. At 35% crit chance per arrow and three arrows per shot, each volley delivers multiple critical hits simultaneously. Hunter Munitions converts crits to guaranteed slash procs. Split Chamber adds additional arrows per shot, further multiplying the volley output.

**Status blast build:** Replace Hunter Munitions with Galvanized Aptitude. The 30% status chance per arrow across three arrows delivers high proc accumulation per volley. Viral (toxin + cold) and heat combination provides health reduction and ongoing damage from each volley.

**Headshot precision:** Add Argon Scope for headshot-triggered crit bonus. With three arrows per shot and 35% base crit, consistent critical headshots maintain Argon Scope activation. This configuration sustains elevated crit chance over extended engagements.

## Synergies

- **Split Chamber:** Each arrow from Split Chamber has 35% independent crit chance. A single Split Chamber trigger pull at full Galvanized stacks fires 6 arrows, all independently rolling critical hits at 35% chance, delivering consistently high burst damage.
- **Hunter Munitions + triple arrows:** Three arrows per shot, each with 35% crit chance, creates approximately 1 Hunter Munitions slash proc per volley (probability), accumulating rapidly across multiple volleys. Against armored Grineer, the sustained bleed proc rate is highly effective.
- **Banshee's Sonar:** The Cernos Prime's high per-volley damage pairs well with Sonar's weak-point amplification. Triple arrows into a Sonar weak point deliver three amplified hits simultaneously.

## Notes

- The three-arrow pattern creates a fan spread. At close range, all three arrows hit the same target; at long range, arrows disperse to hit different targets or miss. The Cernos Prime is optimized for medium range where fan spread connects multiple arrows to a single large target.
- Impact dominance at 90% of total damage provides bonus damage against Corpus shields at the cost of no natural armor bypass via slash. Adding viral or corrosive elemental mods compensates effectively.
- Riven disposition at 4/5 provides above-average roll potential. A multishot riven adds a fourth arrow per volley, significantly amplifying burst output per trigger pull.
