## Overview

The Dark Sword is a Tenno longsword with innate Radiation damage alongside Puncture (60) and Slash (120). Its 40% status chance is the weapon's defining trait -- among the highest for longsword-class weapons -- making it a strong platform for status-focused builds, particularly Radiation + another element to create Magnetic or other combined types. The 5% critical chance is negligible, so crit investment is counterproductive.

The Slash component of 120 represents the dominant physical type, which combined with high status chance enables effective Slash bleed procs against enemies with large health pools. Mastery Rank 8 is required. Attack speed of 0.83 is on the slower end for a longsword, but the 2.5 m range compensates by reaching enemies slightly farther away.

## Variants

The Dark Sword has no Prime, Kuva, or Tenet variant. It is thematically paired with the Dark Dagger, which fills a similar high-status, Radiation-elemental role in the secondary melee slot. Players seeking a Prime-tier longsword alternative should consider the Dragon Nikana or Dakra Prime.

## Recommended builds

**Viral/Slash status build.** With 40% base status, apply two 60/60 mods (Primed Fever Strike + Voltaic Strike) to produce Viral. The high Slash component ensures bleed procs tick through Viral-amplified health pools. Include Organ Shatter and Pressure Point for damage scaling.

**Radiation + Toxin (Corrosive alternative).** Add Primed Fever Strike and a Heat 60/60 mod to create Radiation-infused Corrosive. This strips Grineer armor while the innate Radiation applies bonus damage to Alloy Armor. Combine with Lasting Sting for extended proc duration.

**Condition Overload build.** Equip the Shattering Impact mod alongside standard elemental pairs to strip armor on every hit. Each unique status type active on the enemy multiplies Condition Overload's damage bonus. The Dark Sword's 40% status makes it reliable at stacking multiple procs quickly.

## Synergies

Saryn's Spores pair with the Slash bleed procs triggered by the Dark Sword; Saryn spreads the bleed to nearby enemies. Equipping the longsword with Crimson Dervish stance unlocks powerful diagonal swing combos that hit multiple enemies in a line. Melee-amplifying Warframes such as Rhino and Valkyr further increase output.

## Notes

- The Dark Sword's innate Radiation element is pre-combined; adding additional Radiation mods increases Radiation damage rather than triggering a new element.
- At 40% status chance, two hits will nearly guarantee a status proc -- pair this with melee combo counter for accelerating proc stacking.
