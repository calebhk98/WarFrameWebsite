## Overview

The Dex Furis is a Dex-series anniversary akimbo secondary with an exceptional 20.0 rounds-per-second fire rate and a 100-round magazine. The damage profile is Slash-dominant (11.2 Slash) with minor Impact and Puncture (2.4 each), creating a status-delivery platform at extreme fire rates. The 28% status chance combined with 20 rounds per second generates the fastest secondary proc accumulation rate among anniversary weapons. Critical chance of 14% and 2.0x multiplier are secondary concerns. Mastery Rank 10 is required, and the 15.38 accuracy indicates spread fire appropriate to close-range combat.

Dex weapons are distributed annually by Digital Extremes as a community anniversary gift, typically in March. The Dex Furis is not available through standard Market purchase or crafting. The 2.0-second reload interrupts the sustained-fire cycle for a brief window; the 100-round magazine minimizes how frequently this occurs. The Dex Furis excels as a close-range status primer, applying multiple proc types in seconds before switching to a melee weapon to exploit Condition Overload stacks.

## Acquisition

The Dex Furis is a Warframe anniversary gift distributed annually by Digital Extremes. Log in during the anniversary event period (typically March) to claim the Dex Furis and other Dex weapons. Check [the Dex Furis Fandom wiki page](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Dex_Furis) for the current anniversary date and any updates to the distribution schedule.

## Variants

The base Furis is the standard version of this secondary, available from the Market. The Dex Furis is the anniversary variant with higher fire rate and magazine. No Prime, Kuva, or Tenet variant exists for the Furis family.

## Recommended builds

**Status primer build.** The 28% status and 20 rounds/second make the Dex Furis the fastest status-primer secondary in its class. Equip Barrel Diffusion, Lethal Torrent, Pistol Pestilence, Frostbite for Viral, Hornet Strike, and Slip Magazine. Empty the magazine into a target to apply maximum Slash and Viral procs, then switch to a Condition Overload melee.

**Slash/Viral close-quarters build.** Use the Dex Furis as a primary damage dealer at close range. Equip Hornet Strike, Barrel Diffusion, Lethal Torrent, two Viral 60/60 mods, and Galvanized Shot. The 100-round magazine sustains fire through an entire wave. The 15.38 accuracy is manageable at close range against grouped enemies.

**Galvanized Aptitude status build.** Equip Galvanized Aptitude alongside two Viral 60/60 mods and Slash-boosting mods. Each unique status type active on the target grants a Galvanized Aptitude stack; the extreme fire rate maximizes how quickly multiple proc types are applied. The Slash component generates bleed procs alongside elemental procs for rapid Aptitude stacking.

## Synergies

Mesa's Shooting Gallery further boosts the already-extreme fire rate, making the Dex Furis virtually continuous at full proc application. The low accuracy of 15.38 makes frames that group enemies (Mag, Khora) ideal partners, ensuring maximum pellet contact at close range. Ash's secondary damage passive applies on airborne approaches for initial burst phases.

## Notes

- The 15.38 accuracy means the Dex Furis is a close-range weapon; accuracy degrades significantly at medium and long distance.
- The 20 rounds/second fire rate exhausts the 100-round magazine in 5 seconds; position reload windows carefully during encounters.
