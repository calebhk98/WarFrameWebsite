## Overview

The Ballistica Prime is the primed explosive crossbow combining high puncture damage with significant critical potential. It excels at mid-range engagements and benefits from critical and reload mods. The 3.8 impact, 30.4 puncture, and 41.8 slash damage emphasizes puncture with strong slash components. The emphasis on puncture makes it particularly effective against armored enemies.

The Ballistica Prime's 2.0 critical multiplier provides strong scaling for critical-focused builds. The 15% status chance supports hybrid damage approaches. The 3.33 fire rate is moderate for a crossbow, encouraging deliberate aim and positioning. The moderate fire rate combined with critical potential creates precision-focused secondary platform.

The Ballistica Prime's 8 round magazine requires ammunition management but supports burst-fire combat. The 1.2 reload time is quick, enabling rapid magazine cycling between volleys. The 14 mastery requirement reflects its prime status. The explosive nature enables area damage when combined with proper mods.

This weapon serves well in mid-level missions and sorties where precision and critical damage matter. The Ballistica Prime works particularly well for players preferring explosive, projectile-based secondaries with strong damage per shot. The weapon's unique mechanics appeal to players seeking alternatives to traditional pistol secondaries.

The explosive projectile nature enables creative usage patterns where understanding environment layout multiplies weapon effectiveness. Skilled players position shots to ricochet or explode in optimal patterns. Some players specialize in ballistic-focused builds.

Forma investment creates capable mid-range secondary platform.

## Acquisition

The Ballistica Prime is a Prime weapon obtained through the Void Relic system. Its blueprint and component blueprints drop from specific Void Relics across the Neo and Axi tiers. During active vault cycles, the relevant Relics are obtainable from Void missions; when vaulted, they are accessible only through Prime Resurgence events or direct player trading on warframe.market. The MR14 requirement limits acquisition to experienced players with substantial progression.

## Variants

The base **Ballistica** is available as a non-prime version with lower stats (2.5% CC, 10% SC) and a 2-second reload. The Prime version substantially improves critical chance, status chance, and reload speed. The Ballistica line's crossbow mechanic is unique among secondaries -- it fires timed-detonation explosive bolts that make the weapon play differently from conventional pistols or automatics. Players who enjoy projectile weapons may also evaluate the Athodai (beam secondary) or Opticor (charged primary) for different high-damage-per-shot playstyles.

## Recommended builds

**Slash Proc Build:** The 41.8 slash base makes slash proc bleed damage a reliable strategy. Equip Primed Pistol Gambit (critical chance) and Primed Target Cracker (critical multiplier) for crit scaling, then add Viral via Pistol Pestilence and Frostbite. The combination of slash procs multiplied by Viral status makes each crit-triggered bleed powerful against high-health targets.

**Explosive Build:** The Ballistica Prime's bolts explode on impact or detonation. Area radius mods like Ruinous Extension (if applicable) expand the blast area, allowing damage to multiple targets per shot. At 3.33 fire rate, each shot must be placed deliberately -- accuracy and positioning determine output more than fire rate.

The 1.2-second reload is fast despite the 8-round magazine, enabling quick cycling in sustained encounters. Shotgun Savant or Magazine Warp mods extend the magazine if frequent reloads disrupt engagement flow.

## Notes

The Ballistica Prime fires secondary bolts that home toward nearby enemies after the primary bolt is released in certain fire modes. This homing behavior makes the weapon forgiving against moving targets despite the low accuracy rating. Players should read the weapon's specific secondary fire mechanic on the Fandom wiki for current mechanic details, as patch updates occasionally adjust how the homing projectiles function.

At MR14, the Ballistica Prime is positioned as an end-progression secondary that rewards understanding its unique firing mechanics over raw spray-and-pray approaches.
