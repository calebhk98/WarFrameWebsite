## Overview

Cyath is a Zaw strike component available from Hok the Anvil in Cetus, the Plains of Eidolon hub. As a Zaw strike, Cyath is not a complete weapon -- it is one part of a modular melee system where players combine a Strike (the head), Grip (the handle), and Links (connectors) to craft a custom Zaw with a final stat profile determined by the combination of chosen parts.

The Cyath strike is classified as a dagger-type strike, meaning it creates a dagger Zaw when paired with a Dagger Grip. When combined with a Staff Grip, it produces a polearm-type Zaw instead. This dual-grip compatibility gives Cyath flexibility: players can create either a compact, fast dagger or a longer-reaching polearm depending on which Grip they select. The final weapon type determines which stance mods can be equipped.

The stats shown in the WFCD dataset (all zeros) reflect that strike components have no independent stats -- the damage, critical chance, status chance, and attack speed emerge from the combination formula applied during Zaw assembly at Hok's workbench. Players must test different part combinations using the Zaw preview interface before committing resources.

## Stats

Cyath's stats as a standalone component are undefined; all values are 0 until assembled into a complete Zaw. The assembled Zaw's final stats depend on the Strike, Grip, and Link combination selected. Typical dagger Zaw outputs (Cyath + Plague Bokwin grip, for example) deliver balanced crit/status profiles. Polearm Zaw outputs from Cyath generally favor either status or crit depending on the Link selected.

Refer to the Warframe Zaw builder on the Fandom wiki or community tools like Overframe.gg to model specific Cyath combinations and preview final assembled stats before purchasing components from Hok.

## Acquisition

Cyath is purchased from Hok the Anvil in Cetus using Ostron Standing. Ostron Standing is earned through Plains of Eidolon bounties, fishing, mining, and conservation activities. The Standing cost varies by community events and rank thresholds. Players must reach sufficient Ostron rank (typically Neutral or higher) before Hok unlocks Cyath for purchase. Once the strike component is bought, it is kept in inventory and combined with other Zaw parts at Hok's bench.

## Variants

All Zaw builds using Cyath as the strike are technically variants -- each grip-and-link combination produces a distinct weapon with different stats, mastery credit, and visual appearance. Players frequently craft multiple Cyath Zaws with different grips to compare playstyles (dagger vs polearm) and optimize for different content tiers.

The Zaw system allows Gilding a completed Zaw at Hok once it reaches max rank (30). Gilding permanently applies a custom name and grants Mastery Rank experience, equivalent to ranking a normal weapon. Gilded Zaws can also receive Arcane slots via Arcane adapters.

## Recommended builds

Build recommendations depend entirely on the assembled Zaw variant. For a **dagger Cyath Zaw**, Covert Lethality is the single most important mod -- it adds 100% total base damage on finisher kills and applies to all daggers, making daggers exceptionally powerful in stealth finisher contexts. Pair with Primed Fever Strike and Voltaic Strike for Corrosive status, and maiming/crit mods for combo-based play.

For a **polearm Cyath Zaw**, standard polearm modding applies: Primed Pressure Point, a status or crit mod setup based on the assembled stats, stance mod (Shimmering Blight or Bleeding Willow), and elemental combination targeting the primary mission faction.

## Notes

Zaw building provides a rewarding customization layer for players who prefer crafting their ideal weapon rather than farming drops. Cyath's dual compatibility with both dagger and polearm grips makes it a versatile strike investment -- a single purchase unlocks both weapon types for exploration.

New players should note that Plains of Eidolon bounties are the primary Standing source, and multiple sessions are typically required to accumulate sufficient Standing for component purchases. Hok's inventory resets daily with some components; availability should be confirmed before farming Standing specifically for Cyath.
