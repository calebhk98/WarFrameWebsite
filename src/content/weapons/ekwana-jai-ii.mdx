## Overview

The Ekwana Jai II is a Zaw Link component available from Hok's Anvil at Cetus on the Plains of Eidolon. As a Link, it does not have standalone combat statistics; WFCD reports zeros because the item is an unassembled weapon part. The "Jai" designation in the Ekwana naming convention indicates a bias toward critical chance in the assembled Zaw's stat modification, while "II" indicates an enhanced version of the base Ekwana Jai Link. The Ekwana Jai II occupies the Link slot in the Strike + Grip + Link Zaw assembly.

Within the Zaw crafting system, the Link component is the final selection before the Zaw is assembled. Jai-family Links provide critical chance weighting to the assembled Zaw; comparing Ekwana Jai versus Ekwana Jai II within Hok's interface will reveal the numerical difference in how much critical chance is added to the assembled configuration. Critical-chance-biased assembled Zaws benefit most from Blood Rush and Organ Shatter, which scale the effective critical chance with the melee combo multiplier and amplify the critical hit bonus respectively.

Zaw weapons assembled with the Ekwana Jai II Link can be gilded at Mastery Rank 8 or higher. Gilding unlocks Arcane slots and allows Forma installation for polarity customization. After gilding, the assembled Zaw can receive a custom player-chosen name.

## Acquisition

Purchase from Hok's Anvil at Cetus on the Plains of Eidolon. Ostron Standing is required; higher-tier Link components unlock at higher Ostron community rank. Earn Ostron Standing through fishing, mining, and bounty completion in the Plains of Eidolon. Check [the Ekwana Jai II Fandom wiki page](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Ekwana_Jai_Ii) for the exact Ostron rank requirement and standing cost.

## Variants

The Ekwana Link family includes multiple variants: Ekwana Jai, Ekwana II Jai, Ekwana Jai II, Ekwana Ruhang, Ekwana II Ruhang, and Ekwana Ruhang II. Jai variants bias toward critical chance; Ruhang variants bias toward status chance. Compare all options in Hok's interface to identify the modifier that best complements the Strike and Grip combination. No Zaw component has a Prime or Kuva version.

## Recommended builds

When incorporating the Ekwana Jai II Link into a Zaw build:

- Select a Strike first to establish the weapon archetype and base damage profile.
- Select a Grip to determine weapon category and swing speed.
- Add Ekwana Jai II as the Link to apply its critical chance bias to the assembled configuration.
- Preview the final assembled stats in Hok's interface before purchasing.
- For critical-chance-biased assembled Zaws, equip Blood Rush and Organ Shatter to exploit the elevated crit chance through combo scaling.
- Gild the assembled Zaw at MR8 or higher to access Arcane slots, then install a Stance mod and apply Forma as needed.

## Synergies

Critical-biased Zaws assembled with Jai-family Links benefit from Sacrificial Steel and Blood Rush combo scaling. Frames that sustain combo duration (Wukong, Naramon focus school) extend Blood Rush scaling windows for maximum critical chance efficiency. Organ Shatter combined with a high critical multiplier from the chosen Strike delivers increased per-crit damage.
