## Overview

The Kuva Bramma is a Kuva variant bow that stands as one of the most powerful crowd-control weapons in Warframe. It fires clusterbomb-tipped arrows that detonate either on impact or mid-air when manually detonated. The weapon excels in high-level content by combining instant impact damage with devastating area-of-effect explosions. Its low magazine capacity of one arrow requires strategic management, but experienced players leverage this limitation into a strength by careful positioning and arrow recovery. The weapon's unique arrow recovery mechanic and scaling blast damage make it a signature choice for players seeking explosive utility and area denial capabilities in endgame missions.

## Damage

The weapon's damage split heavily favors blast procs. Impact damage from the charged shot itself is modest at 187, but the radial explosion contributes 839 blast damage with significant falloff reduction (90% at 8.3 meters). Cluster bombs add 49 impact + 57 blast each, making sustained bombardment capable of clearing entire rooms. Blast procs create knockdown and heat buildup, synergizing with status-focused builds.

## Acquisition

The Kuva Bramma is obtained exclusively from Kuva Liches. When a Kuva Lich is defeated with an elemental damage bonus applied to the mission, players have a chance to obtain its signature weapon as a reward. Building the weapon requires the blueprint, 10,000 credits, and one hour of build time at the Foundry. Arrows are recovered singly from the environment, encouraging careful resource management during missions.

## Variants

The Prisma Bramma exists as a cosmetic variant with identical stats, obtained from Void Fissure void trace farming. No prime variant has been released.

## Recommended builds

The Kuva Bramma benefits from mods that enhance its already-high status chance and critical damage. Mod recommendations include Serration for base damage, Multishot mods to overcome the single-arrow limitation, and Blast damage mods to amplify its elemental strength. Heavy Caliber reduces accuracy but minimal accuracy already limits the weapon's effectiveness at range. Elemental mods synergize well: Vile Acceleration increases fire rate to improve arrow spam, while Vigilante Armaments and other multishot sources can turn the single arrow into a volley.

## Notes

Arrow recovery mechanics are crucial to Kuva Bramma gameplay. In solo missions, players must physically pick up arrows after recovering them from fallen enemies or standing still for approximately 10 seconds. In squad play, teammates can trigger the recovery remotely. The weapon's charge time of 0.4 seconds is often overlooked but becomes significant when stacking reload-reduction mods. The disposition value of 1.0 indicates no riven balancing malus, making it rivens' weapon of choice. Against high-armor enemies, the blast procs provide excellent utility by knocking down targets and allowing repositioning. Its role shifts from single-target deletion in lower levels to crowd-control artillery in steel path endless missions.

## Build fundamentals

The Kuva Bramma's effectiveness multiplies exponentially with proper mod selection and forma investment. Players targeting Steel Path or unlimited void fissures should prioritize blast damage scaling over status application. Adaptation mods and shield-gating mechanics become crucial for survivability when standing still to maximize damage output. The weapon pairs excellently with frames providing damage buffs or damage resistance, creating synergistic loadouts that amplify the weapon's already-impressive crowd control capabilities.
