## Overview

The Quartakk is a four-barreled Ostron rifle that fires a simultaneous burst of four rounds per trigger pull. Unlike traditional burst-fire rifles that release shots sequentially, the Quartakk's four rounds all leave the barrels at the same instant, functioning effectively as a quad-pellet weapon rather than a burst weapon. This behavior means multishot mods add additional simultaneous pellets rather than additional burst shots, significantly amplifying total per-trigger-pull damage.

The balanced damage distribution across impact (18.1), puncture (16.7), and slash (14.2) gives the Quartakk flexibility against multiple faction types. The 27% status chance per trigger pull, combined with four simultaneous pellets, results in a high effective status application rate in practice. The 19% critical chance and 2.3x critical multiplier provide a secondary damage path that complements rather than dominates the status-oriented playstyle.

The Quartakk is tied to the Plains of Eidolon open world and requires Ostron standing to purchase its blueprint, making it a mid-game goal rather than a freely available weapon.

## Acquisition

The Quartakk blueprint is purchased from Hok's Anvil on the Plains of Eidolon after reaching Master standing rank with the Ostron syndicate. Component parts -- Barrel, Receiver, Stock -- each require separate purchases or crafting from plains-exclusive resources such as Iradite, Grokdrul, and Tepa. Total standing investment is significant; plan for several days of Plains bounties if starting from Neutral standing.

## Variants

The Quartakk has no Prime or Kuva variant. It is a standalone Plains of Eidolon weapon with no direct family upgrades. Players who want a similar four-barrel or burst-fire experience can look at the Fulmin or Stradavar for comparable automatic-style alternatives.

## Recommended builds

**Viral status build.** The simultaneous four-pellet fire means all four pellets apply status independently per trigger pull. With two 90% elemental mods (Cold and Toxin for Viral), effective status application per trigger pull exceeds the listed 27% significantly. Viral halves target health, compounding the Quartakk's multishot damage efficiently. Primed Bane of Grineer or equivalent faction mods further multiply output.

**Hybrid crit-status build.** Adding Point Strike raises the 19% crit chance to 57%, pushing into yellow-crit territory frequently. Vital Sense and a Galvanized mod complement the multishot nature of the weapon. The status procs from elemental mods continue to apply on every pellet even when the focus shifts to crits.

**Slash hemorrhage build.** Replacing one elemental mod with Hemorrhage converts impact procs to slash procs, benefiting from the weapon's balanced physical damage distribution. Combined with Viral, slash procs apply bleed that ignores armor, making this build effective against heavy Grineer at high levels.

## Notes

- The four simultaneous pellets mean the Quartakk behaves mechanically similar to a shotgun for status and multishot calculation purposes.
- The 84-round magazine is generous; the 1.9-second reload is fast enough that ammunition management is rarely an issue in standard missions.
- Accuracy of 90.9 is high for a multi-pellet weapon, keeping pellet spread tight at medium range without accuracy mods.
- Ostron standing is earned through bounties on the Plains of Eidolon; the Quartakk represents approximately 3-5 days of dedicated bounty farming for a fresh account.
