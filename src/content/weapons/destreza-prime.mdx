## Overview

The Destreza Prime is the Prime rapier melee weapon with a Slash-dominant damage profile: 119 Slash, 30.6 Puncture, and 20.4 Impact. The 32% critical chance and 3.0x critical multiplier make the Destreza Prime one of the highest-multiplier rapiers available. At Blood Rush orange-crit phases, the 3.0x multiplier delivers very high per-hit damage on Slash-based attacks. The 20% status chance supports supplementary elemental proc application. Attack speed of 0.917 is slightly below average for a rapier, and the 2.5 m range is standard. Mastery Rank 10 is required.

Rapier stance mods (Pointed Wind) deliver forward-thrust combo animations that benefit from the Destreza Prime's high critical multiplier by chaining fast, direct hits in sequence. Bleed procs from the 119 Slash base bypass armor, delivering damage-over-time regardless of target armor values. Combined with the high crit chance and 3.0x multiplier, the Destreza Prime performs as a high-crit Slash delivery platform: each orange-crit hit generates significant immediate damage and a bleed proc that compounds the total damage output. The upgrade from the base Destreza (2.0x multiplier) to the Prime's 3.0x represents a 50% increase in per-crit multiplied damage.

## Variants

The base Destreza is the standard rapier with a 2.0x multiplier at MR7. The Destreza Prime upgrades to 3.0x multiplier, higher crit chance, and improved base damage at MR10. No Kuva or Tenet variant exists for the Destreza family. The Destreza Prime is the endgame option for the rapier weapon class.

## Recommended builds

**Blood Rush/3.0x multiplier critical build.** At 32% base crit with Blood Rush and Sacrificial Steel, the Destreza Prime enters orange-crit at x3 combo. The 3.0x multiplier produces very high per-hit damage at orange crit. Equip Organ Shatter, Primed Pressure Point, Blood Rush, Sacrificial Steel, and two Viral 60/60 mods. Berserker Fury after kills accelerates attack speed for faster combo generation.

**Pointed Wind finisher-setup build.** Equip Pointed Wind for its finisher-setup dash mechanic. The forward-dash combo positions behind enemies for finisher inputs; finisher hits are multiplied by stealth/finisher bonuses regardless of stealth state on subsequent uses. Combine with Primed Pressure Point, Blood Rush, and Organ Shatter. High 3.0x multiplier finisher-multiplied crits deliver exceptional per-hit burst damage.

**Slash/Viral status build.** Despite crit being the primary path, Weeping Wounds at 20% base status and x4 combo reaches sufficient proc rates for Condition Overload. Equip Primed Pressure Point, Condition Overload, Weeping Wounds, Blood Rush, Organ Shatter, and two Viral mods. The Slash base generates bleed procs alongside Viral for Condition Overload stacking.

## Synergies

Ash's smoke screen provides stealth multiplier hits on all attacks during the ability; combined with Destreza Prime's finisher-setup stance, this creates extended windows of finisher-multiplied 3.0x crit hits. Naramon's Power Spike extends combo duration for sustained Blood Rush at peak levels. Rhino's Roar amplifies all physical damage proportionally, benefiting the Slash-dominant base.

## Notes

- The 3.0x critical multiplier is the Destreza Prime's key upgrade over the base Destreza; it is the highest multiplier among rapiers.
- At orange crit with Blood Rush and Organ Shatter, per-hit Slash damage values are very high; bleed procs from these crits also deal proportionally high DoT.
