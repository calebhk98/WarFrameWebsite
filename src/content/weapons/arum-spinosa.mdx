## Overview

The Arum Spinosa is a polearm melee weapon requiring Mastery Rank 11. Its total damage per swing reaches approximately 297, composed of 35.6 impact, 201.9 puncture, and 59.4 slash. The heavy puncture weighting -- nearly 68% of base damage -- makes the Arum Spinosa one of the stronger options when facing heavily armored Grineer enemies where puncture bypasses ferrite armor at favorable modifiers.

As a polearm, the Arum Spinosa benefits from the polearm stance mods, which extend reach and alter the combo flow. With a base range of 1.7 meters and the inherent multi-hit sweeping arcs of polearm combos, this weapon strikes multiple enemies per swing in dense rooms, multiplying its effective damage output in Horde-style missions.

The 37% status chance stands out as the defining characteristic for build strategy. While the 9% critical chance and 1.9x multiplier offer little for pure critical builds, the high status rate enables aggressive elemental proc stacking -- making status-oriented builds and hybrid approaches the optimal paths.

## Acquisition

The Arum Spinosa blueprint is purchasable from the in-game Market for 25,000 credits. Component blueprints are obtained through mission rewards or Nightwave offerings. The weapon requires standard crafting resources and a 24-hour Foundry build time. The completed weapon may also be purchased for Platinum from the Market.

## Variants

The Arum Spinosa does not currently have a Prime variant. Players seeking higher-tier polearms with improved critical statistics may consider the Orthos Prime or Guandao Prime, which trade status focus for stronger crit scaling. The Arum Spinosa occupies the status-dominant niche among polearms.

## Recommended builds

**Status Corrosive Build:** With 37% status chance, the Arum Spinosa procs elemental effects frequently. Pairing Volcanic Edge and Voltaic Strike creates Corrosive, which strips ferrite armor from Grineer -- synergizing with the weapon's puncture damage type that already benefits against armored foes. Add Condition Overload for damage scaling with stacked status effects.

**Viral Slash Build:** Swap elemental mods to Drifting Contact and Weeping Wounds to maintain combo counter and escalate status chance. Use Virulent Scourge (Toxin) and a Cold mod for Viral, paired with Slash-boosting mods. The slash component at 59.4 is sufficient for Slash procs to deal meaningful bleed damage multiplied by Viral debuff.

**Stance Choice:** Shimmering Blight and Bleeding Willow are the primary polearm stances. Shimmering Blight provides sweeping multi-target combos; Bleeding Willow emphasizes forward momentum and slide attacks. Either works with status builds, though multi-target sweeps maximize status applications per second in dense groups.

Heavy attacks on this weapon deal amplified puncture damage and benefit from Amalgam Organ Shatter or Killing Blow investment.

## Notes

The MR11 requirement reflects a mid-game weapon that rewards players already familiar with melee modding and faction-specific builds. The Arum Spinosa is particularly effective in Grineer missions on Steel Path where heavy armor makes puncture damage reduction significant and Corrosive procs are essential.

Polearm range synergizes with frame abilities that group enemies -- Limbo's Rift, Vauban's Bastille, or Nova's Molecular Prime make landing multiple-enemy sweeps with the Arum Spinosa trivially consistent.

The 1.0 attack speed is the polearm baseline, leaving room for Fury or Berserker Fury mods to push toward faster combos.
