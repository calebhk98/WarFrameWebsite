## Overview

The Tipedo is a Tenno-designed staff with a balanced combination of critical chance and status chance that makes it a genuine hybrid weapon at Mastery Rank 3. Its 124 total damage per hit is heavily weighted toward puncture (99.2), with smaller contributions from impact (12.4) and slash (12.4). Puncture-dominant melee weapons perform best against armored Grineer targets, where puncture reduces damage mitigation more effectively than other physical types.

With 20% critical chance, a 2.0x critical multiplier, and 20% status chance, the Tipedo is a flexible platform that rewards either a crit build or a status build. The 1.33 attack speed is above average for the melee category, enabling more hits per second than slower polearms. The 3-meter range is generous, allowing the Tipedo's sweep attacks to connect with several grouped enemies simultaneously.

Staff weapons use stances that provide wide horizontal sweep attacks ideal for crowd management. The Tipedo's speed and reach combination makes it effective at managing multiple enemies, applying elemental procs across a group with each swing.

## Acquisition

The Tipedo blueprint is available from the in-game Market. Build time is 12 hours. No quest unlock is required. The weapon is immediately accessible after reaching Mastery Rank 3.

## Variants

**Tipedo Prime** -- The Prime variant requires Mastery Rank 12 and offers substantially improved damage, critical chance, critical multiplier, and status chance. Tipedo Prime parts drop from Void Relics.

## Recommended builds

**Corrosive critical build**: Equip Blood Rush (critical chance scales with combo counter), Weeping Wounds (status chance scales with combo), Primed Pressure Point (base damage), and elemental mods for corrosive (electric + toxin). With Blood Rush at x2 combo, critical chance far exceeds the 20% base, producing consistent orange crits. Organ Shatter adds critical multiplier. This build strips Grineer armor rapidly.

**Viral status build**: Use Primed Pressure Point, Condition Overload (damage per unique status), and cold + toxin for Viral. Condition Overload's damage scaling rewards stacking diverse status types -- the Tipedo's hybrid crit/status profile accumulates stacks efficiently. Add heat for a third status type to maximize the Condition Overload multiplier.

**Heavy attack burst**: Combine Sacrificial Steel, Organ Shatter, and Killing Blow with Corrupt Charge for an opener heavy attack. Each heavy attack lands enormous burst damage against priority targets.

## Notes

Staff stances compatible with the Tipedo include Clashing Forest and Flailing Branch. Stance mods significantly affect combo strings and attack pattern. Primed Reach extends the 3-meter range further and is particularly valuable on staves, where the arc sweeps even wider groups of enemies.
