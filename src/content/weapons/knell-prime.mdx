## Overview

Knell Prime is the Prime variant of the Knell, a scoped secondary pistol with a unique headshot mechanic. Landing a headshot causes the weapon to enter a temporary rapid-fire state that consumes no ammo, rewarding precise aim with burst damage potential that far exceeds its stated stats. The Prime variant raises critical chance to 40%, making crit chains easier to sustain. The weapon is particularly effective on Harrow, whose passive grants a 2-round magazine and headshot bonuses that feed directly into the Knell's mechanic.

The Knell Prime fires as a scoped pistol with a headshot mechanic that temporarily grants unlimited ammo and increased fire rate. Its damage distribution is slash-heavy, which favors bleed proc builds. The 40% critical chance is particularly strong for a secondary, enabling reliable yellow crits with minimal mod investment. Status chance of 10% is modest, so crit-focused builds outperform status-focused ones.

## Acquisition

The Knell Prime blueprint and components are obtained from Void Relics. The weapon was originally released alongside Harrow Prime. It enters and exits the vault periodically; check the current unvault status before farming. Building requires Credits and standard crafting resources available through Foundry construction.

The base Knell is available from the in-game Market for Credits, providing access to the headshot mechanic before farming Prime Relics.

## Variants

Knell is the base variant, purchasable directly from the Market. Knell Prime improves base stats significantly, raising critical chance from 20% to 40% and improving overall damage distribution. When used by Harrow or Harrow Prime, the magazine size expands to 2 rounds, adding a small buffer for the headshot-chain mechanic.

## Recommended builds

The Knell Prime's 40% base critical chance supports a crit-focused build using Hornet Strike, Barrel Diffusion, Lethal Torrent, Pistol Gambit, and Target Cracker as the crit core. Augur Pact and a Primed Heated Charge add damage and elemental coverage. The headshot mechanic rewards precise aim: landing a headshot grants unlimited ammo briefly, so mixing in a Magazine Warp can help during the downtime between chains.

For status builds, Primed Pistol Gambit combined with two elemental mods creates Viral or Corrosive, pairing well with the slash-heavy damage to strip health effectively against armored enemies.

## Notes

The Knell Prime's defining mechanic triggers on headshots: a confirmed headshot briefly enables rapid-fire, free-ammo shooting. This makes it especially powerful in the hands of Harrow, whose passive and abilities synergize with headshot gameplay. The weapon's performance degrades significantly when targets lack clear headshot zones, such as some infested units.
