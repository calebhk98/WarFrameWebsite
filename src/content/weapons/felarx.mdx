## Overview

The Felarx is an Incarnon primary rifle that bridges accessibility and power progression. As an Incarnon weapon, it evolves through combat, unlocking new capabilities as players use it in missions. The weapon features a balanced damage profile with strong status chance and solid crit multiplier, making it effective across all mission types. Its Incarnon form transforms the weapon into a more potent variant with additional mechanics, appealing to players seeking growth alongside their equipment. The low mastery requirement of 3 makes it viable even for new players capable of reaching the market or farming Zariman relics.

## Damage

The Felarx's damage composition leans toward impact and puncture, beneficial against most enemy types. Impact damage is highest at 5.4, useful against organic units and certain armor types. Puncture at 4.5 provides consistent application. The inherent heat damage of 1.2 sets up fire procs, which combine with status mods to create elemental chains. The weapon's high status chance of 26% ensures reliable elemental application even without additional mods. Damage values remain modest compared to riveted weapons, but the Incarnon evolution path makes up for this through gameplay mechanics rather than raw numbers.

## Acquisition

The Felarx blueprint is available from the Star Chart market for 20,000 credits and 155 platinum. Alternatively, players can farm Zariman Ten Zero void fissure missions to obtain relic components, offering a free-to-play path. Building the weapon requires 12 hours of foundry time and 20,000 credits. The Incarnon weapon system requires players to perform specific mission objectives to unlock weapon evolution, adding a progression layer beyond acquisition.

## Variants

No prime variant of the Felarx has been released. The Incarnon evolution is the primary upgrade path, triggered through repeated use in void fissure missions and Zariman Ten Zero activities. Players who ignore Incarnon mechanics miss out on significant mechanical upgrades and form aesthetic variety.

## Recommended builds

Felarx builds benefit from status and reload-reduction mods given its high status chance and rapid fire rate. Serration provides essential base damage, while Cosmetic Rounds and Split Chamber increase effective output. Cryo Rounds and Heat mods chain elements for status stacking, particularly effective in organized groups. Magazine capacity mods extend engagement duration without requiring constant reloads. For early-game players, the weapon performs acceptably with just Serration and Elemental mods before Orokin catalyst placement. Late-game builds leverage Berserker Status and Adrenaline builds to maximize fire rate while maintaining damage output.

## Notes

Incarnon evolution progress persists across all instances of the weapon. Once evolved, the visual appearance changes, and weapon behavior adapts. The exact evolution requirements vary but typically involve collecting resonite from void fissure activities. The Felarx's accessibility makes it excellent for new players learning optimal build structures. Its moderate stats avoid overwhelming newcomers while still performing decently in early star chart content. The weapon transitions poorly to high-level endgame without significant forma investment and riven use, but serves admirably in moderate-difficulty sorties and standard void fissures.

## Endgame viability

While the Felarx never becomes an endgame staple, creative players exploit its rapid fire rate and decent crit multiplier in hybrid builds. Pairing with frames offering crit bonuses like Kairos Augments elevates performance significantly. The weapon's role as a training tool shifts to niche option as players progress, but dedicated enthusiasts demonstrate viable Steel Path performance through extensive optimization.
