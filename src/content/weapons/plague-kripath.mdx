## Overview

The Plague Kripath is a custom zaw staff (melee weapon) composed of a Plague Strike Polearm strike modular component and Kripath staff handle. This particular assembly excels through inherent viral damage of 64 points, providing unconventional elemental focus unusual among standard melee weapons. The combination creates a status-oriented platform optimized for spreading viral procs, which reduce enemy health pools and enable health-stripping tactics against biological targets. The balanced physical damage of 70 puncture, 49 slash, and 30 impact provides versatility against varied armor types when paired with modding. The zero mastery rank requirement makes this zaw accessible to new players willing to farm constituent components. The Plague Kripath serves as an excellent introduction to modular zaw construction and viral-status specialization.

## Damage

The Plague Kripath's viral damage component creates a unique specialization: health reduction through status application. The 70 puncture damage forms the physical baseline, while 49 slash and 30 impact provide balanced coverage. The inherent 64 viral damage combines with 18% status chance to enable frequent health-stripping procs against biological targets. Against Infested and other biological enemies, the weapon excels, while against armor-heavy Grineer the viral becomes less valuable without supporting corrosive mods. The 22% critical chance enables moderate critical scaling when hybrid-built, though status builds typically dominate optimization paths. The modular construction allows component swapping to create entirely different stat profiles, making this configuration one option among many viable zaw assemblies.

## Acquisition

The Plague Kripath requires separate component acquisition and assembly through Hok at Cetus (Plains of Eidolon) or the Jerkin equivalent at Fortuna (Vallis). The Plague Strike Polearm strike component costs around 20,000 standing with ostrons, while the Kripath staff handle costs similar amounts. Assembly at the zaw crafting bench requires 10,000 credits and one hour of crafting time. The entire acquisition path requires faction standing progression and component farming, making the weapon accessible only to established players rather than new accounts. The modular nature ensures high reusability of individual components across multiple zaw assemblies.

## Variants

The Plague Kripath represents one specific assembly of zaw components. Alternative polearm assemblies exist with different strike and handle combinations, each creating unique stat profiles and specializations. Players experimenting with zaw crafting can create entirely different weapons by swapping the strike component (plague, toxic, etc.) or handle type. This flexibility enables personal customization of weapon stats toward player-specific build goals, unlike fixed non-modular weapons.

## Recommended builds

Viral-status builds maximize the Plague Kripath's inherent advantage. Pressure Point and Fury form core damage amplification, while True Strike pushes the base 18% status chance toward guaranteed application. Viral mod stacking through Pathogen Rounds and similar mods ensures rapid health-pool reduction against biological targets. Elemental mods like Heat Damage create viral-heat multiprocs for improved crowd control. Berserker acceleration compensates for the modest 0.95 base attack speed, turning the weapon into practical speed-tier performance. Bloodletting lifesteal mods provide self-sustaining gameplay through melee healing mechanics.

## Synergies

Frames specializing in viral damage synergize excellently with the Plague Kripath. Nidus converts health-stripping into stacking bonuses through his passive, multiplying the weapon's effectiveness proportionally. Saryn benefits from viral status procs to amplify her passive miasma damage output. Infant Inaros converts health damage into personal overshield generation, creating synergistic defensive scaling. Hydroid pairs well with the weapon's crowd control focus, stacking control effects. Mesa concentrates single-target elimination capabilities when the weapon's health stripping enables finisher execution.

## Lore

Zaws represent modular melee weapon construction originating from Ostron and Corpus engineering collaboration. The Plague Kripath specifically combines Plague component design with Kripath staff architecture, creating weapons optimized for specific playstyles. The inherent viral damage suggests counter-development against Infested biological threats prevalent in open-world farming. Within player communities, zaw crafting represents an advancement in weapon mastery, teaching players about modular optimization and stat customization beyond fixed weapon choices. The Plague Kripath serves as an approachable entry point to zaw experimentation for players seeking to escape standard weapon limitations.
