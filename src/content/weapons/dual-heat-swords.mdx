## Overview

The Dual Heat Swords are a pair of energy blades with a strong Puncture component (102.9) compared to equal Impact (22.1) and Slash (22.1). The 20% status chance supports elemental proc builds, while the 5% critical chance effectively eliminates crit as a build path. Mastery Rank 3 makes the Dual Heat Swords accessible early in the game, filling the dual-sword melee slot before more powerful options become available.

Attack speed of 0.917 and range of 2.5 m are standard for dual swords. The Dual Sword stance mods (Swirling Tiger, Crossing Snakes, Gemini Cross) apply here. The Dual Heat Swords serve as a learning platform for the dual-sword playstyle, teaching combo animation timing and elemental mod interaction before players invest heavily in higher-tier weapons.

The Puncture dominance makes the Dual Heat Swords naturally suited to Grineer Ferrite Armor targets, where Puncture's armor-penetration advantage reduces effective damage mitigation. Combining the 20% status chance with Corrosive elemental mods (Fever Strike + Voltaic Strike) strips Ferrite Armor through proc accumulation, enabling unmitigated Puncture damage delivery against fully stripped targets. At MR3 with limited mod resources, this Corrosive plus Puncture combination provides the most efficient anti-Grineer approach available without expensive mod investments.

## Variants

The Dual Heat Swords have no Prime, Kuva, or Tenet variant. Players seeking upgraded dual-sword performance should transition to the Dual Keres, Dual Ether, or eventually the Dual Keres Prime.

## Recommended builds

**Status/Corrosive build.** Use Infected Clip and Voltaic Strike for Corrosive. The 20% status with combo multi-hit applies armor strip reliably. Add Primed Pressure Point and Organ Shatter for amplified damage per hit.

**Viral Puncture build.** Pair Primed Fever Strike and Cryo Rounds for Viral. The Puncture-dominant physical damage benefits from Viral's health amplification, especially against armored targets after any armor strip. Include Berserker Fury for attack-speed acceleration.

**Entry-level damage build.** For early-game progression, equip Pressure Point, Fever Strike (Toxin), and whatever elemental mods are available. The Dual Heat Swords do not require heavy investment to clear star-chart content up to mid-tier planets.

## Synergies

Any Warframe with melee damage boosts (Rhino's Roar, Chroma's Vex Armor) amplifies the Dual Heat Swords. For new players, frames with crowd-control abilities (Frost, Limbo) reduce incoming damage while using the dual swords at close range.

## Notes

- The 5% crit chance means crit mods are wasted slots; invest entirely in status and damage.
- Upgrade to Dual Ether or Dual Keres when available for meaningful stat improvements.
