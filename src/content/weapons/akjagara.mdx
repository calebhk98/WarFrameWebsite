## Overview

The [Akjagara](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Akjagara) is a dual automatic secondary pistol firing at 10 rounds per second. Each bullet deals 36 total damage: 5.4 impact, 25.2 puncture, 5.4 slash. Puncture dominates at 70% of total damage, making the Akjagara effective against Grineer armored targets where puncture provides bonus damage. The 28% status chance enables consistent proc application at the weapon's high fire rate, while the 6% critical chance and 2.0x multiplier are not meaningful for crit builds.

Riven disposition at 3/5 is neutral, providing moderate riven roll potential.

## Acquisition

The Akjagara blueprint is available from the Market for credits. As a standard Market weapon, it is permanently available. Crafting requires mid-game materials.

## Variants

The Akjagara Prime (Mastery Rank 12) substantially improves status chance (38%), critical chance (18%), and critical multiplier (2.4x). Players who can access Prime relics should pursue the Prime variant for endgame use. No Vandal or Wraith version exists.

## Recommended builds

**Puncture status viral build:** Hornet Strike, Barrel Diffusion, Lethal Torrent, Pistol Pestilence, Frostbite, Jolt, Scorch, and Primed Heated Charge. At 28% status chance and 10 fire rate, proc application is rapid -- approximately once per 0.3 seconds per type. Viral (toxin + cold) halves enemy health while corrosive from Jolt and toxin strips Grineer armor. The naturally high puncture adds physical bonus damage against Ferrite Armor.

**Condition Overload primer:** Use the Akjagara to rapidly apply three distinct status types before switching to melee. At 10 fire rate and 28% status chance, three unique procs typically apply within 1-2 seconds of sustained fire.

**Full status elemental:** Configure all remaining mod slots with elemental status mods. With 28% base chance and 10 fire rate, the Akjagara delivers sustained proc accumulation even without Galvanized mods, effective across the mid-game star chart.

## Synergies

- **Condition Overload:** The Akjagara's high fire rate and 28% status chance create rapid status accumulation. Three unique proc types activate Condition Overload's full damage bonus within 1-2 seconds of fire.
- **Saryn's Spores:** Procs from the Akjagara spread via Spores, extending effective proc range across enemy groups from a single target engagement.
- **Puncture vs Grineer:** The 70% puncture distribution provides bonus damage against both Ferrite and Alloy armor types. In Grineer missions, the Akjagara's physical damage component is naturally effective without additional armor-targeting mod investment.

## Notes

- The 6% critical chance is not worth building around. Status-focused builds outperform any attempt at critical scaling given the limited base crit chance and multiplier.
- Magazine of 36 at 10 fire rate empties in 3.6 seconds. The 2.25-second reload is above average. Time reloads carefully to minimize downtime during engagement chains.
- The Akjagara Prime is a meaningfully superior upgrade in all combat-relevant stats. If Prime relics are accessible, use the base Akjagara for Mastery Experience and transition to the Prime for sustained play.
