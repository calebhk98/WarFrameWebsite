## Overview

The [Cyanex](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Cyanex) is a homing secondary pistol that fires guided needles at enemies. It carries 91 total damage per hit: 50 impact and 41 innate gas elemental damage. Gas damage deals bonus damage against Infested enemies and applies a gas proc (cloud AOE toxin damage) that persists briefly. The 32% status chance enables reliable gas proc application, making the Cyanex particularly effective in Infested missions. The 8% critical chance and 1.4x multiplier do not support critical builds.

The homing mechanic allows needles to curve toward locked-on enemies, enabling accurate delivery in moving-target scenarios without precise aiming. This auto-aim characteristic makes the Cyanex accessible to players who struggle with precision targeting.

Riven disposition at 3/5 is neutral.

## Acquisition

The Cyanex blueprint is available from the Market for credits. Crafting requires standard mid-game materials. As a permanently available Market weapon, it is not vaulted. The completed weapon can be purchased with platinum.

## Variants

No Prime, Vandal, or Wraith variant of the Cyanex exists. Players seeking alternative gas-elemental secondaries should compare the Pox (gas on impact with area cloud) for different delivery mechanics.

## Recommended builds

**Gas Infested build:** Hornet Strike, Barrel Diffusion, Lethal Torrent, Pistol Pestilence (toxin to amplify gas formation), Jolt (electric for radiation), Scorch (heat for toxin viral proc), and Frostbite. Gas deals 75% bonus damage against Infested Flesh and Infested Sinew. The Cyanex's 32% status chance applies gas clouds reliably, creating persistent AOE toxin damage zones ideal for Infested survival missions.

**Status primer build:** Configure for gas, viral, and heat status types. The homing mechanic ensures needle delivery even during fast-moving Infested enemies, making the Cyanex reliable for status application without precision aiming requirements.

**Crowd control build:** Rely on gas procs' AOE cloud effect to create persistent damage zones that slow and damage groups of Infested simultaneously. Multiple needles fired in succession create overlapping gas cloud areas, effective in Infested Defense or Survival rotation missions.

## Synergies

- **Infested missions:** Gas damage's 75% bonus to Infested Flesh makes the Cyanex a natural choice for Deimos Survival, Orokin Derelict missions, or any Infested-faction endless run.
- **Saryn's Spores:** Gas clouds from Cyanex procs spread toxin components through Spores, amplifying the effective AOE damage area across Infested groups.
- **Equinox's Maim:** Gas cloud toxin accumulation from Cyanex procs feeds Maim's damage stacks, generating substantial stored damage for Maim's area release against Infested groups.

## Notes

- Gas damage forms from toxin + heat elemental combination. The Cyanex carries innate gas, which functions as the combined type. Adding additional toxin mods stacks on the gas output, amplifying the cloud's toxin damage component.
- The accuracy value of 1.82 is extremely low, reflecting the homing mechanic's target-acquisition design rather than traditional aim scatter. The homing seeks the locked target rather than firing in a straight line, making accuracy less relevant to practical performance.
- The 11-round magazine empties in approximately 2.4 seconds at 4.67 fire rate. The 2.2-second reload is longer than the magazine duration, meaning careful fire rate management avoids frequent full magazine empties.
- Homing needles require a locked-on target within reasonable range. Against enemies beyond the lock-on distance, needles travel in a more direct path without strong homing. Learn the lock-on range to maximize homing accuracy.
