## Overview

The Ekhein is a melee weapon with a pure Impact damage profile (340 Impact, zero Puncture and Slash). This makes it one of the most Impact-specialized melee weapons in the game, with the damage type most effective against Corpus Shields and Corpus units generally. The 30% critical chance and 2.4x multiplier provide a strong crit platform, while the 15% status chance is secondary. Attack speed of 0.767 is slow for a melee weapon, reflecting the heavy Impact-focused archetype. Mastery Rank 10 is required.

The 2.8 m range is wider than average, compensating for the slower attack speed by covering more enemies per swing. The pure Impact profile means the Ekhein deals reduced damage against Grineer Alloy Armor (where Puncture is preferred) and Infested Flesh (where Slash excels), but Corpus enemies receive bonus Impact damage that synergizes with the high base damage pool.

## Variants

The Ekhein has no Prime, Kuva, or Tenet variant. It is a standalone weapon in its melee category.

## Recommended builds

**Impact/Magnetic Corpus build.** The pure Impact base maximizes damage against Corpus Shields. Add Volcanic Edge and Jolt (Heat + Electric = Radiation, or alternatively Electric + Cold = Magnetic) for Magnetic, which bypasses and drains shields. Primed Pressure Point and Blood Rush scale the crit and raw damage output.

**Critical heavy-hit build.** Equip True Steel, Organ Shatter, Blood Rush, and Organ Shatter. At 30% base crit with Blood Rush at high combo, orange crits per swing deliver high per-hit Impact damage. The 2.8 m range ensures wide-sweeping single attacks connect to multiple enemies.

**Heavy-slam focused build.** Combine Killing Blow (finisher damage amplification) and Amalgam Organ Shatter (swing speed on slam). The heavy Impact damage amplifies slam attacks that benefit from Impact's stagger mechanic against grouped enemies.

## Synergies

Mag's Magnetize concentrates enemies and causes bullets (and melee impacts) to orbit and hit a target repeatedly; the Ekhein's high per-hit Impact damage amplifies this interaction. Wisp's Reservoirs provide Haste (attack speed) and Motes (Health regeneration), compensating for the slow 0.767 attack speed with an external speed boost. Rhino's Roar multiplies the 340 Impact base damage proportionally, making each slow swing hit significantly harder during the ability's duration. The 2.8 m sweep range of the Ekhein combined with Vauban's Bastille grouping means a single swing can reach clustered enemies for efficient area clearance. Arcane Fury (bonus melee damage on crit) activates reliably at the 30% base critical chance, providing a sustained damage amplifier during prolonged combat without requiring specific ability casting or combo counter prerequisites.

## Notes

- Zero Puncture and Slash means no Slash bleed or armor-penetrating procs; the Ekhein is faction-specific and best used against Corpus.
- The 340 Impact base is extremely high; each hit delivers significant damage without elemental proc reliance.
