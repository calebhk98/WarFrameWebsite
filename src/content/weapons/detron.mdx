## Overview

The Detron is a pure-radiation secondary pistol that delivers concentrated bursts of energy projectiles. Unlike the Nukor's continuous beam, the Detron fires discrete shots with significant area-of-effect falloff, making it effective for controlled burst damage against grouped targets. The weapon's low accuracy of 7.1% requires close-range positioning for consistent hits, creating an aggressive secondary playstyle that rewards exposure management over distant support fire. The radiation element combines with modest status chance to enable confusion procs, though the lower proc rate makes elemental scaling less dominant than the Nukor. The Detron excels in close-quarters combat scenarios where accuracy limitations transform into tactical advantages through environmental density.

## Damage

The Detron deals purely radiation damage at 40 points per shot, delivering roughly double the Nukor's output per hit. The radiation element enables confusion procs at 12.86% per shot, creating disorientation opportunities though less reliably than higher status weapons. The critical multiplier of 1.5x remains impractical given the 4% base critical chance, making critical scaling negligible. The weapon's accuracy limitation actually enables superior area-of-effect performance at close range, spreading projectiles across clustered targets. Against heavily armored enemies, radiation damage without supporting mods remains modest, requiring elemental builds or frame abilities for high-level content.

## Acquisition

The Detron blueprint is available from the market for 18,000 credits plus additional 18,000 credit construction cost and twelve hours of Foundry time. Additionally, Detron components drop from Lith D1 void relics as uncommon rewards, providing an alternative farm path for those preferring relic missions over market purchases. Trading of Detron blueprints and components occurs regularly, enabling quick acquisition through platinum investment. The multiple acquisition paths ensure flexible access regardless of player preference.

## Variants

The Detron Prime exists as a superior variant with higher damage and improved accuracy, obtained through void relic farming from Neo A2 and Axi D1 relics. The Prime variant becomes available after reaching Mastery Rank 6, making it a natural upgrade path for players investing in Detron gameplay. No Kuva variant currently exists, though riven disposition allows riven mods to improve endgame performance.

## Recommended builds

Elemental mods amplify the Detron's radiation output and status proc frequency. Heat Damage or Cold mods create radiation combinations, multiplying effective damage through status chains. Pistol Pestilence or other status mods push the 12.86% base chance higher, though achieving guaranteed procs remains difficult compared to higher-status weapons. Magazine extension through Increased magazine capacity supports the low fire rate during extended engagements. Reload-reduction mods become valuable given the 2.0-second reload time between magazine cycles. Forma unlocks exilus slots for utility mods like ammo mutation.

## Synergies

Frames benefiting from close-range crowd control synergize perfectly with the Detron. Ash gains disruption opportunities from confusion procs for finisher executions. Mesa's Peacemaker amplifies Detron damage through her accuracy buff, though the weapon's natural loose spread means mesa's focus becomes less relevant. Rhino's roar applies directly to Detron output, multiplying both normal and critical strikes. Vauban creates crowd control synergies through his crowd-focus abilities, complementing the Detron's close-range requirements. Grendel absorbs confused targets automatically, enabling synergistic crowd control chains.

## Lore

The Detron emerged from Orokin energy weaponry, its radiation focus suggesting counter-development against heavily shielded threats. The area-of-effect spread pattern indicates battlefield-optimized design from the Old War era, prioritizing clustered-target elimination. Within player communities, the Detron represents an aggressive secondary option for players preferring close-quarters combat over distant support roles. Its eventual Prime variant improved its practical utility while maintaining the core aggressive playstyle identity established by the standard version.
