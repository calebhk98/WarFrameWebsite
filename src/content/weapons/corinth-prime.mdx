## Overview

Corinth Prime is the Prime variant of the Corinth shotgun. It fires in a two-stage pattern: a primary shotgun blast and a timed airburst grenade released by pressing and holding the alternate fire button. The grenade detonates on contact or after a short fuse, dealing area-of-effect damage independent of the pellet spread. Mastery Rank 14 is required to equip Corinth Prime.

The high critical multiplier of 2.8x makes Corinth Prime one of the harder-hitting shotguns on critical hits. Its 30% base critical chance on the primary buckshot attack gives crit-focused builds a strong platform. The 9% base status chance on buckshot is modest but scales well with status mods given the multi-pellet nature of the shot.

Corinth Prime is a multi-pellet shotgun; the damage values above represent per-pellet figures. Multishot mods increase total pellets per shot and multiply overall damage output substantially. The Air Burst Projectile and Air Burst Explosion attacks have a separate 4% critical chance, 1.6x critical multiplier, and 50% status chance, and deal Impact and Blast damage respectively.

## Acquisition

Corinth Prime is obtained exclusively through Void Relic fissure missions. There is no Market credit purchase route for any Prime weapon. The weapon requires farming its Blueprint, Barrel, Receiver, and Stock components from specific Void Relics, then assembling the complete weapon in the Foundry.

Components appear in Axi and Meso tier relics. The Barrel is a rare drop in Axi C-series relics. As an unvaulted Prime, all relevant relics are available in standard Void fissure reward pools without requiring Prime Resurgence access. Check the Warframe drop tables or [warframe.market](https://warframe.market) to identify which specific relics carry each component at any given time.

## Variants

Corinth Prime is the Prime variant of the Corinth. The base Corinth is craftable using a blueprint from the in-game Market and standard resources. No Vandal or Wraith version of the Corinth line exists. The Prime variant is the highest-tier version of this weapon family.

## Recommended builds

The 30% base critical chance and 2.8x critical multiplier make Corinth Prime well-suited to crit-focused shotgun builds. Primed Point Blank increases base shotgun damage, while Hunter Munitions adds Slash procs on critical hits against armored targets. Seeking Fury or Seeking Force can be added for punch-through to hit multiple enemies per pellet.

For status-hybrid approaches, Shotgun Spazz and Accelerated Blast increase fire rate, enabling faster application of the 9% per-pellet status chance across the full pellet count. Elemental combinations such as Corrosive (Cold + Electric) strip Grineer armor reliably. The alternate fire Air Burst has 50% base status chance, making it effective for Blast proc crowd control as a secondary engagement tool.

## Notes

All stats sourced from WFCD warframe-items. The alternate fire grenade deals area damage on detonation and can be aimed at enemy clusters above cover for effective crowd suppression. The buckshot primary fire has damage falloff starting at 18 m and ending at 36 m, reducing to 35.6% of base damage at maximum falloff range. Corinth Prime is best used at close-to-mid range where full pellet counts connect and falloff does not apply.
