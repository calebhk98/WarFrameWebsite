## Overview

The Dakra Prime is a Prime longsword with a Puncture-dominant damage profile: 136 Puncture versus 17 each of Impact and Slash. The 36% critical chance is one of the higher base crit values among Prime longswords, and combined with Blood Rush scaling, the Dakra Prime reaches orange-crit territory at x3-x4 combo. The 2.4x multiplier amplifies these crit hits substantially. The 18% status chance is secondary, supporting supplementary elemental proc application rather than a dedicated status build. Attack speed of 1.0 and 2.5 m range are standard for the longsword class. Mastery Rank 10 is required.

Longsword stance mods (Iron Phoenix, Crimson Dervish, Vengeful Revenant) provide the Dakra Prime with flowing, medium-range combo animations. Vengeful Revenant in particular enables parry mechanics with finisher-setup opportunities, which pair well with the high crit chance for finisher-multiplied critical hits. The Puncture dominance makes the Dakra Prime effective against Grineer Ferrite Armor when combined with Corrosive elemental mods, as Corrosive strips armor for unmitigated Puncture damage delivery. At MR10, the Dakra Prime serves as an endgame longsword option before further crit-specialized longswords become available.

## Variants

The Dakra has no non-Prime standard version; the Dakra Prime is the base entry for this weapon design. No Kuva or Tenet variant exists. Players seeking a comparable longsword archetype at lower mastery rank may consider the Dark Sword or Skana Prime.

## Recommended builds

**Blood Rush critical build.** At 36% base crit, Blood Rush reaches orange-crit by x3 combo. Equip Organ Shatter for the 2.4x multiplier amplification, Sacrificial Steel for additional crit chance scaling, Primed Pressure Point, and two Viral 60/60 mods. Berserker Fury increases attack speed after kills for faster combo accumulation.

**Crimson Dervish burst combo build.** Equip Crimson Dervish stance for spinning, multi-hit combo animations. Combine with Primed Pressure Point, Blood Rush, Organ Shatter, and Condition Overload. Despite the low 18% status, Corrosive 60/60 mods provide armor stripping procs. At x4 combo, the 36% base crit delivers consistent orange crits through each Crimson Dervish combo spin.

**Corrosive/Puncture Grineer build.** The 136 Puncture base combined with Corrosive elemental mods creates an anti-armor build for Steel Path Grineer content. Equip Primed Pressure Point, Condition Overload, two Corrosive 60/60 mods, Blood Rush, and Organ Shatter. After Corrosive procs strip Ferrite Armor, each orange-crit Puncture hit delivers full unmitigated damage.

## Synergies

Frames that generate stealth multipliers (Ash, Ivara) compound finisher-multiplied crit hits from Vengeful Revenant stance parries. Rhino's Roar amplifies all physical damage proportionally, benefiting the Puncture-dominant base. Naramon's Power Spike extends combo duration for sustained Blood Rush orange-crit output.

## Notes

- The 36% critical chance is the Dakra Prime's primary strength; always equip Blood Rush and Organ Shatter for maximum orange-crit output.
- Vengeful Revenant stance's parry mechanics enable finisher-multiplied crit hits; learn the parry timing for maximum damage windows.
