## Overview

The Trumna Prime is the Prime variant of the Trumna automatic rifle, available at Mastery Rank 15. Like the base Trumna, the Prime version fires a combination of impact physical bullets and innate heat elemental rounds, but with improved statistics across all categories. Its 85 damage per shot divides between 32 impact and 53 heat. The heat component deals bonus damage to Infested and Corpus flesh, and generates fire procs that burn targets over time independently of the primary hit.

The 34% status chance is the Trumna Prime's most significant asset. At 4.67 fire rate, status procs apply approximately every three shots on average, enabling rapid heat proc accumulation alongside whatever elemental mods are installed. The 24% critical chance and 2.4x multiplier provide reliable crit output with Point Strike support. The 250-round magazine with a 4.0-second reload provides very long sustained fire windows -- approximately 53 seconds before reload.

The Trumna's alt-fire mode launches a grenade-type projectile when enough kills have been accumulated in the primary fire mode. The Trumna Prime retains this mechanic with improved grenade damage. The grenade is a free burst of area damage rewarded for sustained primary fire engagement.

## Acquisition

The Trumna Prime blueprint and components drop from Void Relics. Check the in-game Void Relic drop tables for containing relics. Build time is 12 hours per component plus 12 hours for the main assembly.

## Variants

**Trumna** -- The base non-Prime version available from the Market (blueprint cost 15,000 credits).

## Recommended builds

**Heat status**: Serration, Split Chamber, Thermite Rounds, Galvanized Aptitude, Galvanized Shot, and additional elemental mods. The 34% status at 4.67 fire rate with Split Chamber multishot produces multiple fire procs per burst. Fire stacks accumulate, dealing burn damage over time independently. Galvanized Aptitude and Shot both stack damage bonuses.

**Viral plus heat**: Cold plus toxin for Viral -- cold interacts with heat as the primary element and creates Blast (not viral). Use the Tenet elemental bonus instead for Viral, or add toxin mods and allow heat plus toxin to merge into Gas. Gas deals area toxin damage on proc, spreading to nearby enemies.

**Gas status**: Thermite Rounds (heat) plus toxin mods. Heat and toxin combine to Gas elemental. Gas procs deal area toxin damage on application, spreading the toxin cloud to nearby enemies. High status weapons that combine heat and toxin deal consistent Gas proc area denial.

## Notes

The 4.0-second reload is the Trumna Prime's principal weakness. With the 250-round magazine, empty reloads are infrequent during normal play, but when they occur, they create a long vulnerability window. Position in cover before reloading in high-difficulty content. The grenade alt-fire charges by kills with primary fire; in kill-dense missions, the alt-fire becomes available frequently.
