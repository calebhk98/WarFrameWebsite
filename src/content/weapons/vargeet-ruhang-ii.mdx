## Overview

The Vargeet Ruhang Ii is a Zaw Strike component crafted through the Ostron faction's Zaw system in Cetus, the Plains of Eidolon hub. Zaw weapons are fully custom melee weapons assembled from three interchangeable components: Strike, Grip, and Link. The Vargeet Ruhang Ii is a Strike -- the blade or head component that determines the weapon's base damage profile and weapon type. As a component, it has no combat stats on its own; its characteristics only emerge when assembled into a complete Zaw.

All Zaw Strikes have zero standalone stats by design. The final assembled weapon's impact, puncture, slash, critical chance, critical multiplier, and status chance are calculated from the combination of all three selected components. Choosing different Grips changes the range and attack speed of the assembled weapon. Choosing different Links adjusts the critical or status values. The Strike chosen determines the fundamental weapon type (sword, polearm, dagger, etc.) and base damage distribution.

The Vargeet Strike family produces Zaws in the scythe weapon type. Scythe weapons feature wide sweeping attacks with stance mods that emphasize spinning combo strings, covering broad areas per swing. Players crafting a Vargeet-based Zaw select from the various Vargeet variants (Jai, Ruhang, and their Roman-numeral versions) to access different base stat weightings before accounting for Grip and Link modifications.

## Acquisition

Vargeet Strike components are purchased from Hok, the Zaw artisan in Cetus, using Ostron Standing. Ostron Standing is earned through Plains of Eidolon bounties, Eidolon-related activities, and selling Zaw and Kitgun parts. Each Standing purchase requires a minimum Ostron rank (Visitor or higher for most common components). Build time at the forge is 1 hour.

## How to Build a Complete Zaw

1. Purchase all three components from Hok in Cetus: Strike, Grip, and Link.
2. Visit the Foundry and craft each component separately (1 hour each).
3. Return to Hok and select "Forge Zaw" to assemble the three crafted components into a complete weapon.
4. Optionally, Gild the finished Zaw (after reaching max rank) to unlock custom naming and an additional mod slot.

## Notes

Gilding a completed Zaw unlocks a dedicated slot for Exodia arcanes, which can provide powerful unique effects not available to standard melee weapons. Many endgame melee builds use Gilded Zaws specifically because of Exodia arcane access. The Vargeet Strike family's scythe type enables wide sweep attacks that hit multiple targets per swing -- an advantage in enemy-dense missions. Experiment with different Grip and Link combinations in Hok's interface to preview the assembled stats before committing materials.
