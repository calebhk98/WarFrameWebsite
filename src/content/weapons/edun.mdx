## Overview

The Edun is a polearm melee weapon with a broadly distributed damage profile: Impact (44), Puncture (110), and Slash (66). The 34% critical chance and 2.6x multiplier make it a strong crit platform, while the 20% status chance supports a secondary hybrid approach. Available at Mastery Rank 0, the Edun provides strong stats for early-game players, particularly given its 3.0 m range -- the longest reach among starter-tier polearms.

Polearm stance mods (Shimmering Blight, Bleeding Willow, Crushing Ruin) apply to the Edun, providing wide-arc sweep attacks at 1.0 attack speed. The broad range and polearm stances make the Edun effective at crowd-clearing during lower-tier missions, while the 34% crit chance scales into later content with Blood Rush investment.

## Variants

The Edun has no Prime, Kuva, or Tenet variant. It is a standalone entry in the polearm category. Players seeking a polearm Prime should consider the Guandao Prime as a natural upgrade path.

## Recommended builds

**Blood Rush crit build.** The 34% crit chance is excellent for a MR0 weapon. Equip Blood Rush, Organ Shatter, and Sacrificial Steel. By x4 combo the effective crit chance reaches orange-crit territory. Add two 60/60 elemental mods for Viral and Berserker Fury for attack-speed acceleration.

**Polearm sweep build.** Equip Shimmering Blight or Bleeding Willow stance for wide-arc combos. The 3.0 m range ensures hits on multiple enemies per swing. Include Primed Pressure Point and Condition Overload, with two elemental procs from 60/60 mods. This exploits the Edun's crowd-clearing strength through the polearm moveset.

**Early-game progression build.** Use whatever mods are available: Pressure Point, Fever Strike, Voltaic Strike, Organ Shatter, Fury. The Edun's 34% crit and solid base damage carry players through the early star chart without heavy mod investment.

## Synergies

The Edun's 3.0 m range pairs with frame abilities that group enemies (Vauban's Bastille, Khora's Strangledome) to maximize polearm sweep hit count. Rhino's Roar amplifies all physical damage types equally, benefiting the balanced Impact/Puncture/Slash distribution. Volt's Speed augments the polearm's already-efficient combo generation. The Edun's MR0 accessibility means it pairs well with starter frames (Excalibur, Volt, Mag) that new players commonly use during early-game progression. Excalibur's Exalted Blade stance buffs give way to melee-focused frames later, but the Edun's high 34% crit chance remains effective through all stages of the star chart when Blood Rush is added. Chroma's Vex Armor scales with damage received, providing an exponentially increasing damage buff during sustained combat that amplifies the already-strong Edun crit output further.

## Notes

- The MR0 requirement combined with 34% crit makes the Edun one of the best accessible early-game melees.
- The 3.0 m range distinguishes the Edun from shorter polearms; use this advantage to hit enemies at the edge of the sweep arc.
