## Overview

A wave-projection Arch-gun firing wide arcing energy waves. Its heavy Puncture bias (175 of 250 total physical damage) shreds armor effectively. The sweeping wave pattern can hit multiple targets simultaneously, making it effective against fighter swarms and grouped enemies. Introduced in Hotfix 15.7.2 (2014-12-19), the Fluctus is notable for fires sweeping wave projectiles that arc across a wide area hitting multiple targets; heavy Puncture bias at 175 damage per wave.

The Fluctus emerged from Corpus energy-wave research, later adapted for Tenno Archwing deployment after early encounters revealed the need for area-coverage weapons against fighter wings. Its sweeping wave mechanism was initially a defensive Corpus technology, repurposed for offensive space combat.

## Acquisition

Component parts from Syndicates: Barrel from Cephalon Suda at Intelligent rank, Stock from Steel Meridian at Defender rank. Blueprint from Market for 15,000 credits. Build cost 45,000 credits. The Fluctus requires Mastery Rank 2 to equip.

Arch-gun weapons require either standard crafting resources, Syndicate standing for component parts, or specialized mission drops. Once crafted, equipping the Fluctus in Archwing uses the standard Arch-gun slot. To use it as a Heavy Weapon in ground missions or Railjack combat, a Gravimag must be installed. Gravimags are available from Clan Dojos via Energy Lab research, or from Arbitrations as rotation rewards.

## Variants

No Prime or Vandal variant exists. One of the original Archwing weapons introduced in Update 15. Riven disposition of 3 indicates moderate balance standing.

The Arch-gun family spans weapons from Tenno engineering (Corvas, Fluctus, Velocitus), Grineer design (Grattler, Kuva line), and Entrati craftsmanship (Cortege, Mausolon, Morgha), each with distinct stat profiles suited to different engagement contexts.

## Recommended builds

Puncture-dominant damage (175 base) shreds armor and Grineer heavy units. Adding Radiation (Heat + Electricity) or Corrosive (Electricity + Toxin) augments the armor penetration. The wave pattern benefits from Arch-gun mods that increase projectile size. Status chance of 16% is lower than ideal for status builds.

**Core Arch-gun mods to consider:**

- Rubedo-Lined Barrel -- +165% Arch-gun damage (primary damage mod for all builds)
- Hollowed Bullets -- +120% critical chance (essential for any crit-focused build)
- Critical Focus -- +60% critical multiplier while aiming
- Modified Munitions -- +90% status chance (for status-focused builds)
- Arch-gun Ace -- +165% fire rate (useful on slow-fire weapons to improve feel)
- Parallax Scope -- +30% critical chance while aiming
- Charged Bullets -- +60% magazine capacity
- Superior Defenses -- reduces reload time
- Conductive Coils -- adds Electricity damage
- Thermite Rounds -- adds Heat damage

Prioritize Rubedo-Lined Barrel as the first mod in any Arch-gun build. Follow with critical or status mods based on the weapon's stat profile. Elemental mods provide faction-specific damage type flexibility.

## Notes

The Fluctus operates in the Arch-gun slot, used in Archwing missions and as a Heavy Weapon on the ground via Gravimag. In Railjack (Empyrean) missions, the Arch-gun serves as the primary armament during space combat against fighters and Crewships.

Wave fire covers Railjack exterior engagement zones effectively. Especially potent against clustered fighter formations in proximity defense scenarios.

Archwing combat system notes: Archwing missions require completing The Archwing quest. Four Archwings are available -- Itzal (mobility/stealth), Amesha (tank/support), Elytron (AoE damage), and Odonata (balanced starter). Amesha's Warding Grace suits defensive positioning while Fluctus sweeps incoming fighters. Odonata's Energy Shell adds Electricity for Corpus shield interaction while maintaining the wave's anti-armor output.

The weapon's Riven disposition is 3/5 (moderate). Riven dispositions range from 0 (absolute minimum, strong weapon) to 5 (maximum, weak weapon). DE adjusts dispositions quarterly based on weapon usage rates.
