## Overview

Masseter Prime is the prime variant of the Masseter jaw sword, a single-handed melee weapon with high total base damage split between impact and puncture. The 145.6 impact and 114.4 puncture per hit totals to a substantial physical damage pool before mods, making this weapon a strong foundation for builds focused on physical damage amplification or combined elemental effects. There is no base slash damage, which limits the weapon's effectiveness in builds relying on Slash procs to bypass armor scaling -- players seeking armor-bypass should consider adding Slash through the Sacrificial Steel or Organ Shatter mod pairs.

At 1.08 attacks per second, the Masseter Prime has moderate attack speed -- competitive for a single sword. The 2.9-meter reach is above average for its category, providing range on par with many polearms. The 26% critical chance and 2.2x multiplier offer meaningful crit scaling, while the 36% status chance supports elemental builds. Mastery rank 14 reflects the weapon's prime tier positioning.

The absence of slash damage means the weapon's baseline physical damage is entirely impact and puncture. Impact procs stagger enemies; puncture procs reduce enemy damage output. Neither proc provides the armor bypass of slash bleeding procs, so builds should plan accordingly when facing high-armor Grineer on Steel Path.

## Variants

The base [Masseter](/weapons/masseter) is the non-prime version with reduced damage and critical statistics. The Prime variant's higher total damage and better critical chance make it the preferred option for endgame content once relics are accessible.

## Recommended builds

The Masseter Prime's 36% status chance and 26% crit chance enable both status and hybrid approaches. Given the lack of slash damage, Corrosive or Viral builds that bypass armor through chemical damage types are recommended over relying on physical proc scaling.

Corrosive status build:
- Pressure Point and Primed Pressure Point for base damage
- Fever Strike (toxin) and Voltaic Strike (electric) for Corrosive combination
- Condition Overload as the scaling multiplier with multiple status types active
- Drifting Contact for combo duration
- Berserker Fury to leverage the moderate attack speed into higher sustained damage

Hybrid crit-status build:
- Replace Condition Overload with Blood Rush to scale critical chance with combo counter
- Add Organ Shatter or Primed Smite for critical damage amplification
- Retain elemental mods for Viral proc (Cold + Toxin) to amplify damage taken by enemies

The 2.9-meter range allows the weapon to hit targets slightly beyond typical sword reach, which makes it comfortable for groups without requiring stance-specific sweeping combos.

## Notes

Jaw swords in Warframe use a limited selection of compatible stances. Confirm stance compatibility before purchasing or crafting this weapon to ensure the correct stance mod is available for build optimization.

The Masseter Prime's lack of slash damage in the base profile is a meaningful design limitation compared to other prime melee weapons that carry all three physical damage types. Players who prioritize Slash proc builds -- which deal damage through the bleed effect that scales with level-independent mechanics -- should evaluate whether adding Slash through mods fits the available slot budget. Sacrificial Steel provides both critical chance and slash damage, but the mod requires umbral forma to polarize effectively.
