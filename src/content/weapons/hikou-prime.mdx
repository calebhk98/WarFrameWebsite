## Overview

Hikou Prime is the Prime variant of the Hikou throwing stars, a thrown secondary weapon that fires in rapid bursts and reloads quickly from a reserve pool rather than a standard magazine. The thrown weapon mechanic makes the Hikou platform behave differently from pistols: projectiles are physical thrown items that travel in an arc, and the "reload" is a rapid retrieval animation rather than a clip swap. Hikou Prime improves the base Hikou's status chance to 28%, making it one of the more status-capable thrown secondaries available.

The slash-dominant damage profile -- 3.6 impact, 1.8 puncture, and 30.6 slash per star -- provides a foundation for bleed proc builds. With 28% status chance and rapid fire rate of 5.83 throws per second, Hikou Prime applies status effects quickly across multiple targets. The 0.5-second reload is among the fastest in the secondary category, minimizing downtime between throw bursts.

The low mastery rank requirement of 4 makes Hikou Prime unusually accessible for a Prime weapon. Players who obtain it through Void Relics gain access to a capable status secondary early in their progression.

The 6% critical chance makes crit builds impractical; Hikou Prime's value lies in its status chance and fire rate. At 28% status chance with 5.83 throws per second, the weapon applies elemental procs rapidly enough to maintain active status effects on priority targets. Additional status chance mods push reliable multi-proc application even at moderate engagement ranges.

The 36 base damage per star is low, and per-shot damage scaling is not Hikou Prime's strength. Its role is status application and proc stacking rather than raw damage output.

## Acquisition

Hikou Prime components are obtained from Void Relics associated with its Prime Access rotation. Blueprint, Stars, and Pouch drop from specific relics by tier. When Hikou Prime is in active Prime Access, its relics appear in the standard drop table. Between rotations it enters the Prime Vault. Players can trade components and the completed weapon on the Trading Post regardless of vault status.

The base Hikou is available from the Market for credits and provides a lower stat entry point for the thrown weapon archetype.

## Variants

The base Hikou is a Market-available secondary with lower status chance and slower fire rate compared to the Prime version. Hikou Prime is the highest-tier version in the throwing star family.

No Kuva, Tenet, Wraith, or Vandal variant of the Hikou exists. Hikou Prime is the sole improved variant.

## Recommended builds

A status build for Hikou Prime uses Barrel Diffusion and Lethal Torrent for fire rate and multishot, Augur Pact for base damage, and two elemental mods for a combined damage type. Viral (Cold + Toxin) is a common choice for general-purpose status proc application. At 28% status chance with multishot and high throw rate, the weapon maintains Viral stacks on grouped enemies efficiently.

Raising status chance further allows more consistent proc application even against faster-moving enemies at mid range. The short 0.5-second reload makes Seeker (punch-through) a valuable mod for engaging clustered enemies through cover and walls without interrupting throw cadence.

## Notes

Hikou Prime projectiles follow a slight arc trajectory rather than firing in a straight line. At close to mid range this arc is negligible, but at longer ranges it requires adjustment. The thrown weapon category also benefits from Concealed Explosives, which adds a small area-of-effect blast on impact at the cost of a mod slot.

Thrown secondaries are silent by default, giving Hikou Prime practical utility for stealth builds. Enemy bodies created by silent kills do not trigger alert status in nearby enemies, making Hikou Prime suitable for solo stealth runs through spy or sabotage mission types.
