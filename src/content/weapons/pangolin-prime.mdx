## Overview

Pangolin Prime is the prime variant of the Pangolin sword, a single-handed melee weapon dominated by puncture damage. The 198.4 puncture per hit dwarfs the 37.2 slash and 12.4 impact, creating one of the highest puncture concentrations among swords in the game. This extreme puncture weighting makes Pangolin Prime particularly effective against Grineer units, where puncture damage reduces enemy damage output on proc and the raw physical force benefits from Corrosive amplification against Ferrite Armor.

At 0.92 attacks per second, Pangolin Prime is slower than many sword-category competitors. The 2.5-meter range compensates by maintaining reach on targets at the edge of typical melee engagement distance. The 26% critical chance and 2.2x multiplier are adequate for hybrid builds, while the 30% status chance enables elemental proc application on most hits. Mastery rank 14 reflects its prime status.

The near-total puncture focus means that standard slash-proc builds are less viable than on weapons with balanced or slash-dominant distributions. Builds should lean into puncture's natural Grineer synergy or use elemental damage types to bypass armor altogether through combined elemental procs.

## Acquisition

Pangolin Prime components drop from Void Relics. The blueprint, blade, and handle components are distributed across multiple relic types. Player trading through [warframe.market](https://warframe.market) provides an alternative. Check active relic drop tables to identify which farming nodes contain the relevant relics.

## Variants

The base [Pangolin Sword](/weapons/pangolin-sword) has lower statistics and a lower mastery rank requirement. The Prime variant's substantially higher puncture damage and better crit chance make it the clear choice for endgame use once relic farming is viable.

## Recommended builds

The extreme puncture weighting and 30% status chance suggest Corrosive builds targeting Grineer heavy units, where Corrosive strips Ferrite Armor and puncture procs reduce incoming enemy damage.

Corrosive puncture build:
- Pressure Point and Primed Pressure Point for base damage multiplication
- Voltaic Strike (electricity + status) plus Fever Strike (toxin + status) for Corrosive combination
- Condition Overload as the primary scaling mod with multiple status types active
- Drifting Contact for combo sustain
- Gladiator Might for critical damage at high combo levels

Against Corpus (shield-focused enemies), Magnetic (Cold + Electric) or Viral (Cold + Toxin) is preferable over Corrosive. Corpus Alloy Armor responds differently than Grineer Ferrite and Corpus shields are more effectively countered through Magnetic.

Blood Rush builds are also viable given the 26% base crit chance. At 12x combo multiplier, Blood Rush combined with a crit chance mod pushes effective crit chance above 100%, enabling guaranteed crits. The lower attack speed of 0.92 slightly reduces combo accumulation rate; Berserker Fury can offset this if the build includes crits to trigger its bonus.

## Notes

Single swords in Warframe use Crimson Dervish, Swooping Falcon, and Iron Phoenix stances. Each changes the attack pattern, range of cleaving hits, and combo window timings. Iron Phoenix provides consistent DPS-focused attack chains for sustained combat; Swooping Falcon excels at repositioning around groups.

Pangolin Prime's narrow damage type focus is both a strength and a limitation. Against Grineer specifically, few other swords in the category deliver as much raw puncture damage per swing. Against factions where puncture provides less benefit -- Infested, for example -- the weapon's narrow damage distribution shows its limits. Players alternating between faction types should consider different weapon loadouts rather than trying to build Pangolin Prime as a universal option.
