## Overview

The Furis is a rapid-fire dual-wield secondary pistol designed for sustained mid-range firepower. Available immediately to new players through the market, the Furis serves as an entry-level secondary that teaches weapon mechanics without demanding high mastery rank. Despite its low base damage and cost, the Furis has evolved through Incarnon transformation into a viable tool for higher-difficulty content. Its high fire rate makes it exceptional at triggering status effects, and the dual-wield design grants passive multishot benefits unavailable to single-handed pistols.

The weapon's primary appeal lies in its accessibility and forgiving learning curve. New players using the Furis learn trigger discipline and sustained fire mechanics without losing credibility in mid-tier missions. Veterans appreciate its Incarnon variant as a situational tool for status-stacking builds where raw damage matters less than consistent proc application.

The Furis's defining characteristic is its extreme fire rate relative to damage per shot. At 13.33 rounds/second, the weapon fires bullets so rapidly that spread and inaccuracy (14.29 accuracy) matter less than expected. The high volume compensates for low individual-shot damage, making the Furis viable for suppressing fire and elemental proc application.

## Damage and effectiveness

Base damage per shot is negligible (~5 total), but multiplying by fire rate yields ~67 damage per second against unarmored targets. This is sufficient for early-star-chart missions and adequate for crowd control in higher-content if properly modded. The weapon's true strength emerges when status mods are applied; 13 percent base status chance becomes 80-100 percent with dual-weapon and elemental mods, enabling rapid viral or slash procs on entire enemy groups.

Against heavily armored targets, the Furis cannot deliver raw damage comparable to higher-tier secondaries. Instead, pairing it with corrosive mods to strip armor, then switching to a primary for damage, leverages the Furis's strength in enabling rather than finishing.

## Acquisition

The Furis blueprint is available immediately from the market for 10000 credits (no platinum required). New players often receive it as part of starting inventory in some regions. Crafting requires only 12 hours and minimal components, making the Furis the most accessible secondary in the game.

An Incarnon variant of the Furis can be unlocked through the Zariman void mission tileset, granting enhanced fire rate and alternate firing modes when evolved. The transformation makes the standard Furis competitive in higher-level content through its alternate-form benefits.

## Recommended builds

Furis builds for new players focus on raw fire rate and magazine capacity: Hornet Strike (damage boost), Lethal Momentum (fire rate), and elemental mods (Heat, Cold) create a learning-friendly loadout. Players upgrade to Primed Pistol Gambit and Primed Pressure Point as they progress, pushing the weapon toward 80+ crit chance and critical-viral hybrid scaling.

For veteran players using the Incarnon variant, the build shifts toward status stacking: Hornet Strike, Primed Wet Grip, Primed Lethal Momentum, Augur Pact, and dual-elemental mods (Cold + Toxin for Viral) transform the Furis into a rapid proc machine. Paired with a frame that converts procs into buffs or debuffs (Saryn, Sevagoth), the Furis becomes a catalyst for team-wide effects.

## Synergies

The Furis pairs exceptionally well with frames that amplify fire rate or status effects. Mirage's Hall of Mirrors triples projectile count, transforming the Furis's high fire rate into overwhelming volume. Octavia's Metronome can buff reload speed and fire rate for the entire squad while the Furis spins in sync. Saryn with spore proliferation chains infected enemies together, multiplying Furis procs into exponential damage scaling.

Secondary-focused companions (Moa, Hound, Adarza Kavat with critical-boosting precepts) layer additional damage or crit chance benefits. A Moa with multishot mods effectively doubles the Furis's projectile output through companion weaponry, creating a minigun-like experience.

## Lore

The Furis is a foundational weapon in the Tenno arsenal, available to new Operators as they first venture into the Origin System. Its accessibility and reliability have made it a cultural touchstone for players starting their Warframe journey. The weapon reflects Tenno practical engineering: high volume, rapid application, minimal complexity.

The Incarnon evolution of the Furis suggests that even the most basic Tenno weapons can be refined through exposure to Void fissure phenomena. This narrative reinforces that power in Warframe is not solely determined by rarity or cost, but by mastery and adaptation. A skilled operator with a Furis can outperform a new player with a Kuva weapon.
