## Overview

The Tenet Plinx is a Sisters of Parvos Tenet secondary requiring only Mastery Rank 6 -- the lowest Mastery Rank of any Tenet weapon, making it accessible to mid-game players who have progressed through the Sister system. Its 70 damage per shot splits between 40 slash and 30 heat (elemental). The absence of impact and puncture means all physical damage is slash, which generates bleed procs. The intrinsic heat base produces heat status, which has a fire proc that burns targets independently of the slash bleed.

The 44% critical chance and 3.0x multiplier are exceptional by secondary standards. At 44% base with Primed Pistol Gambit, crit chance exceeds 100%, guaranteeing orange or red crits every shot. The 3x multiplier means crits deal triple base damage. At 3.33 fire rate with a 10-round magazine, the Plinx empties in 3 seconds and then requires the fast 1.3-second reload. The low magazine demands awareness of ammunition count during engagements.

The Tenet Plinx carries an additional elemental bonus from its parent Sister, modifying the total damage profile beyond the base 40 slash + 30 heat.

## Acquisition

Obtain through the Sisters of Parvos system. Trigger a Sister Candidate via Granum Void, then track and vanquish the Sister to receive the Tenet Plinx. At MR 6, this is reachable before most Tenet weapons. The Sister's elemental bonus is fixed at creation.

## Variants

No base non-Tenet Plinx version. The Tenet Plinx is a standalone weapon design.

## Recommended builds

**Full critical**: Hornet Strike, Primed Pistol Gambit, Primed Target Cracker, Barrel Diffusion, Lethal Torrent, and Galvanized Chamber. With Primed Pistol Gambit and 44% base, the Plinx crits every shot. The 3x multiplier amplified by Primed Target Cracker produces devastating individual shot damage.

**Slash-crit synergy**: Primed Pistol Gambit, Primed Target Cracker, Hornet Strike, Barrel Diffusion, Galvanized Aptitude, and Augur Pact. The slash base combined with crits generates frequent bleed procs that bypass armor. Galvanized Aptitude stacks further damage on status-proc kills.

**Viral**: Cold mod added for Viral (cold plus toxin). The heat base prevents full cold-toxin Viral without replacing heat. Instead, use Viral as a supplemental element through the Tenet elemental bonus if it is a cold or toxin type.

## Notes

The 10-round magazine is the Tenet Plinx's most significant limitation. With the semi-auto trigger, fast manual clicking can deplete the magazine in 3 seconds or fewer. The 1.3-second reload is fast by secondary standards, partially compensating. In sustained fights, plan reloads proactively. The 50% accuracy rating causes moderate spread at longer ranges -- the Plinx is a close-to-medium-range secondary.
