## Overview

The Dokrahm is a Zaw Strike component available from Hok's Anvil at Cetus on the Plains of Eidolon. As a Strike, it provides the foundation for the assembled Zaw, contributing to the base damage profile and determining the weapon category of the finished configuration. WFCD reports zeros for all standalone stats because the Dokrahm is an unassembled part; actual combat statistics emerge only after combining Strike, Grip, and Link at Hok's Anvil.

The Dokrahm is one of several heavy weapon Strikes available in the Zaw system, enabling wide-arc melee archetypes such as heavy blades or scythes depending on the chosen Grip. Heavy-archetype Zaws assembled with heavy Grips tend to have lower attack speed but higher base damage per swing, while light Grips shift the assembled weapon toward faster, lighter weapon categories. Comparing configurations within Hok's interface before purchasing is essential, as the preview shows the assembled stats before spending Ostron Standing.

Zaw weapons incorporating the Dokrahm Strike can be gilded at Mastery Rank 8 or higher. Gilding unlocks Arcane slots and allows Forma installation for polarity optimization. After gilding, the assembled Zaw can also receive a custom player-chosen name.

## Acquisition

Purchase from Hok's Anvil at Cetus on the Plains of Eidolon. Ostron Standing is required; higher-tier Strikes become available at higher Ostron community rank. Earn Ostron Standing through bounty completion, fishing, and mining in the Plains of Eidolon. Check [the Dokrahm Fandom wiki page](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Dokrahm) for the exact Ostron rank requirement and standing cost.

## Variants

Zaw Strikes do not have Prime, Kuva, or Tenet versions. The Dokrahm is one of multiple Strike options at Hok's Anvil, each suited to different Grip and Link pairings. Reviewing the full selection of Strikes available at your current Ostron rank will help identify the best configuration for the desired weapon archetype.

## Recommended builds

When incorporating the Dokrahm Strike into a Zaw build:

- Select a heavy Grip to create a heavy blade or scythe archetype with high per-swing damage. A lighter Grip produces a faster weapon at reduced damage per hit.
- Choose a Link component based on the desired stat weighting: Ekwana-family Links increase critical chance; Ruhang-family Links increase status chance.
- Preview the assembled Zaw stats inside Hok's interface before spending Ostron Standing.
- Gild the assembled Zaw at MR8 or higher to unlock Arcane slots for Arcane Guardian or Arcane Strike.
- Install a Stance mod matching the assembled weapon category (Cleaving Whirlwind for heavy blades, Reaping Spiral for scythes).

## Synergies

Heavy Zaw configurations built with the Dokrahm Strike benefit from Blood Rush, which scales critical chance with the melee combo multiplier. Organ Shatter amplifies the critical multiplier for each heavy hit. Frames that increase combo duration (Wukong, Arcane Fury) sustain the Blood Rush scaling across longer engagements.
