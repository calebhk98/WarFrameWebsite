## Overview

Corufell is a heavy axe designed around raw impact and slash damage with balanced impact-slash weighting. As a heavy melee weapon, Corufell emphasizes charge attacks and devastating area sweeps over rapid combo chains. The weapon's 616 base damage, balanced physical split, and 26 percent status chance make it exceptionally effective at crowd control while maintaining single-target viability. Heavy weapons reward patient, positioning-aware gameplay; mastering Corufell involves timing charges and reading enemy spacing to maximize area-of-effect impacts.

The weapon's primary strength lies in its charge attack damage and impact knockback procs. Combining high-damage slams with impact status creates natural enemy crowd control without requiring external frame abilities. This independence from frame-specific synergies makes Corufell viable across virtually every loadout, though certain frames (Hildryn, Atlas, Inaros) amplify its effectiveness dramatically.

## Damage and effectiveness

Base damage of 616 is among the highest for melee weapons without Kuva modifiers. When paired with Primed Power Donation and heavy attack mods, individual charge slams can exceed 5000 damage to multiple enemies simultaneously. The weapon's attack speed (0.967) is slightly below average for heavy weapons, but this is offset by its high individual-hit damage and large area-of-effect radius on charged attacks.

Corufell excels at wave-defense missions, endless survival, and any mission type where maintaining space against grouped enemies is critical. The heavy slam attacks create knockback that disrupts enemy fire while dealing significant damage, effectively creating breathing room for the player and squadmates. Against isolated high-level targets (heavy gunners, elite heavy units), Corufell's high damage ensures quick elimination without relying on status procs or team synergies.

## Acquisition

Corufell blueprint drops from Zariman Ten Zero void mission rewards in rotation A or B, similar to Phenmor. Expected farm time is 5-10 void fissure runs with appropriate void relic refinement. Crafting requires 54000 credits and moderate heavy weapon resources (plastids, alloy plate, neural sensors), making it accessible to players at MR 16 or higher.

The Zariman tileset's puzzle encounter may drop additional blueprints or components, creating opportunities for faster farming if players engage with the tileset's secondary objectives.

## Recommended builds

Corufell builds emphasize raw damage and attack speed stacking. Primed Power Donation, Organ Shatter, and Primed Pressure Point form the core damage foundation. Fury or Primed Fury increases attack speed, reducing charge attack recovery time and enabling faster slam-spam rotations. For crowd control, adding heavy-attack mods (Drifting Contact, Weeping Wounds for status) prioritizes impact and slash procs over pure damage. A baseline Corufell build for mid-tier content includes: Primed Pressure Point, Primed Fury, Organ Shatter, and Rending Strike (slash bonus). This enables consistent 1.5-2k crit damage per slam against level 50-70 enemies.

Advanced builds swap elemental mods for Cold + Toxin (Viral scaling) while maintaining crit focus. Reaching 80+ crit chance with 4-5x multiplier allows slam attacks to one-shot heavy units below level 80. The addition of status procs (impact for knockdown, slash for open wounds) creates compound crowd control where enemies are simultaneously knocked down and bleeding.

For endgame Steel Path content, combining Primed mods with elemental damage (Molten Impact for heat, North Wind for cold) generates synergistic effects; heat causes burning DoT, while impact knockdown chains prevent enemy recovery. This hybrid approach maintains crowd control while dealing respectable damage to heavily armored units. A pure status build replacing Organ Shatter with Weeping Wounds reaches 100+ percent status chance, ensuring nearly guaranteed impact/slash procs that create overlapping knockdowns and bleeds.

## Synergies

Corufell synergizes exceptionally well with frames that amplify heavy attack damage or crowd control effects. Hildryn with Balefire provides raw damage buff and shield-based durability, enabling Corufell swing-spam without fear of one-shots. Atlas with Petrify creates stone-armored enemies that take increased damage and become grouped, perfect for Corufell's area-of-effect charge attacks.

Companion weapons with impact or slam focus (heavy swords, other heavy axes) layer additional knockback and crowd control. A Moa or Kavat with bonuses to heavy attack damage further amplifies Corufell's charge-attack devastation. Spirit Shield mods and lifesteal through finishers provide on-demand sustain against high-level enemy fire.
