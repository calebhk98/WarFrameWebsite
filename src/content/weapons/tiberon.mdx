## Overview

The Tiberon is a versatile burst-fire rifle that fires three-round bursts per trigger pull. Its fire mode can be toggled between full-auto and semi-auto (for the base model), giving players flexibility to adapt their engagement range and target priority. With 44 total damage per shot -- 11 impact, 11 puncture, and 22 slash -- each round in a burst delivers a balanced physical damage spread, and the slash-dominant component generates reliable bleed procs over time.

At 26% critical chance and a 2.4x critical multiplier, the Tiberon is a strong candidate for critical builds. The 2.4x multiplier is notably high for a base-tier assault rifle, meaning critical hits deal substantially more damage than average. Combined with a 16% status chance, the Tiberon occupies a hybrid position: critical builds work best but status elements are not wasted.

The 30-round magazine and 2.26-second reload time reflect the burst-fire trade-off -- each burst consumes three rounds, so a 30-round magazine provides 10 full bursts between reloads. Effective fire rate calculation should account for burst cadence and inter-burst gap. Mastery Rank 10 positions the Tiberon in the transition from mid-game to late-game content.

## Acquisition

The Tiberon blueprint is available in the in-game Market. Build time is 12 hours. No quest or event is required. The weapon is also purchasable fully built for platinum from the Market.

## Variants

**Tiberon Prime** -- The Prime variant offers improved damage, critical chance, critical multiplier, and status chance, with a larger magazine. Tiberon Prime requires Mastery Rank 16 and is obtained through Void Relics. The Prime model retains the same burst-fire mechanic with the fire mode toggle between burst and semi-auto.

## Recommended builds

**Critical burst build**: Maximize the Tiberon's high critical multiplier with Vital Sense and Point Strike. Add Serration, Split Chamber, and an elemental combination such as heat + cold (Blast) or cold + toxin (Viral). With Point Strike, critical chance reaches consistent orange crit territory, and the 2.4x multiplier ensures substantial burst damage per trigger pull. Hunter Munitions is viable given the meaningful slash component.

**Galvanized build for sustained content**: Equip Galvanized Chamber and Galvanized Aptitude for stacking bonuses that grow with kill count. Add Primary Dexterity or Primary Merciless for kill-stacking base damage. The Tiberon's burst-fire cadence allows precise kill confirmation to maintain stacks reliably.

**Semi-auto precision**: Switch to semi-auto mode with Argon Scope for critical chance on headshot and Bladed Rounds for additional critical damage after a kill. This playstyle uses the Tiberon's toggle mode for ranged target elimination, rewarding precise headshot discipline.

## Notes

The Tiberon's fire mode toggle (default V on PC) allows adapting to different engagement distances within the same mission. Use burst for medium-range targets to land full bursts reliably, and switch to semi-auto for long-range targets where full burst accuracy may degrade. Managing the toggle deliberately maximizes damage per magazine.

The Tiberon holds a fan-favorite reputation as an accessible mid-game critical rifle before Prime variants become obtainable. Its 2.4x base multiplier compares favorably to many higher-mastery weapons.
