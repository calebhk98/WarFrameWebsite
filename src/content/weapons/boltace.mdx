## Overview

The [Boltace](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Boltace) is a dual-tonfas melee weapon with 176 total damage per attack. The damage is predominantly slash (140.8), with minor impact (17.6) and puncture (17.6) contributions. This slash-heavy profile makes the Boltace excellent at inflicting bleed procs, which bypass armor mitigation entirely -- a strong advantage against Grineer Ferrite and Alloy Armor. The 28% status chance supports reliable proc application, though the 6% critical chance and 1.5x multiplier do not support critical builds.

Tonfa weapons have access to the Sovereign Outcast stance mod, which provides flowing combo animations with sweeping arc attacks that can hit multiple nearby enemies. The dual-tonfas category is known for fast, mobile combat patterns. Riven disposition at 4/5 indicates the Boltace is moderately underused, giving riven rolls above-average multiplier potential.

At Mastery Rank 4, the Boltace is accessible to early-mid game players. The slash focus and status chance make it a functional platform for learning status-based melee building principles.

## Acquisition

The Boltace blueprint is available from the Market for credits. Crafting requires standard mid-game resources. As a non-event, non-prime weapon, it is permanently available. Players can also purchase the completed weapon with platinum.

## Variants

No Prime, Vandal, or Wraith variant of the Boltace exists. The base Boltace is the only form. Players who prefer dual-tonfas with superior stats should consider the Kronen Prime, which offers higher critical chance and multiplier alongside competitive status output.

## Recommended builds

**Slash status build:** Primed Pressure Point, Berserker Fury, Weeping Wounds, Condition Overload, Blood Rush, Organ Shatter, Fever Strike, and North Wind. The 140.8 base slash damage generates bleed procs reliably through Weeping Wounds scaling. Condition Overload amplifies each subsequent hit as status types accumulate. Despite the low 6% base crit, Blood Rush reaches effective crit rates at higher combo multipliers due to combo meter synergy with Weeping Wounds.

**Viral bleed build:** Replace Organ Shatter with a second viral-forming mod. Viral halves enemy health pools; slash procs then bleed through the reduced health. This two-layer approach is highly effective against heavily armored Grineer in Steel Path content.

**Beginner status build:** Pressure Point, Fever Strike, Shocking Touch, Jagged Edge, and Drifting Contact. This configuration delivers consistent slash procs and elemental procs within budget-friendly mod access, suitable for the mid star chart where the Boltace is most naturally encountered.

## Synergies

- **Condition Overload:** The slash-primary damage and 28% status chance create rapid bleed proc accumulation. Adding elemental mods for two additional proc types (viral and heat) quickly maxes Condition Overload's bonus.
- **Nekros's Despoil and Shadows of the Dead:** In farming missions, using the Boltace to trigger multiple status procs increases Desecrate roll opportunities with each kill.
- **Hemorrhage (mod):** This melee mod converts a portion of base damage to additional slash procs, stacking with the Boltace's already high base slash. The combined slash proc rate accelerates Condition Overload stacking.

## Notes

- The 0.917 attack speed is average for dual melee weapons. Berserker Fury compensates effectively, especially during high-density encounters where quick kills maintain the speed buff stack.
- Dual tonfas have a unique slide attack that propels the player forward while dealing area damage. Incorporating slide attacks into combat rotation increases mobility and adds AOE damage beyond the standard combo sequences.
- The Boltace's slash dominance (80% of total physical damage) means it is neutral to slightly favorable against most enemy armor types, since slash deals bonus damage against Flesh and Infested health types.
- Riven disposition at 4/5 makes a range or status chance riven meaningful. Range increases the effective arc width of tonfa combos, connecting with more targets per swing.
