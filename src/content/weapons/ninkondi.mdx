## Overview

Ninkondi is a nunchaku-style melee weapon that attacks at 1.0 attacks per second with a 2.3-meter range. Its 10% critical chance and 2.0x multiplier are moderate for the melee category. The nunchaku stance Flailing Branch provides wide sweep attacks that can hit multiple targets simultaneously, making the weapon more effective in crowd scenarios than its raw stats suggest. Available at mastery rank 0, Ninkondi is an accessible crowd-clearing melee option.

## Acquisition

Ninkondi's blueprint is available from the in-game Market. Players at mastery rank 0 can equip it after Foundry crafting.

## Variants

Ninkondi Prime is the prime variant with improved critical chance and status chance. See [Ninkondi Prime](/weapons/ninkondi-prime) for details.

## Recommended builds

Given the modest crit chance, Ninkondi performs better in status or elemental-focused configurations. Viral (Cold + Toxin) or Corrosive (Electric + Toxin) elemental combinations work well against Grineer and Infested respectively. The Flailing Branch stance's wide arc attacks benefit from status mods that apply procs to all targets hit per swing, making Condition Overload a strong damage multiplier as status stacks accumulate.

Mod recommendations:
- Pressure Point for base damage
- Elemental mods for status and combined damage types
- Condition Overload for multi-status scaling
- Drifting Contact for combo duration

## Notes

Nunchaku weapons use the Flailing Branch stance, which is the only stance mod for this category. The stance's sweep patterns hit in wide horizontal arcs, making Ninkondi stronger against groups than isolated single targets. Ninkondi Prime outperforms the base version significantly and should be a priority upgrade once accessible through relics.
