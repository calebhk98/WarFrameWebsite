## Overview

Ninkondi is a nunchaku melee weapon that provides an unusual damage profile compared to most early-game options: its 90 impact and built-in 100 electricity damage create a consistent source of Electric procs, which temporarily disable robotic and Corpus enemies by stunning them. With 35% status chance and 1.0 attack speed, Ninkondi applies status reliably on most hits, making it well suited to status-focused builds without requiring heavy mod investment in elemental mods.

The weapon's critical performance is modest -- 10% critical chance and 2.0x multiplier -- which makes crit builds inefficient. The emphasis belongs on status and elemental builds that exploit the built-in electricity and high status chance. At mastery rank 8, Ninkondi is accessible to mid-game players beginning to encounter Corpus-heavy missions where electric procs provide genuine crowd control value.

The 2.3-meter range is competitive for a nunchaku weapon, and the Flailing Branch stance's wide horizontal sweep attacks hit multiple targets across that arc. Against groups, this distributes electric procs broadly, which is more efficient than single-target melee weapons requiring repositioning.

The innate electricity damage combines with any added toxin to produce Corrosive (Electric + Toxin), or with cold mods to produce Magnetic (Electric + Cold) for anti-shield builds against Corpus. Players choosing to add heat instead create no combination, so heat functions as a standalone damage type alongside the electric base.

## Acquisition

The Ninkondi blueprint is available from the in-game Market for credits. Players at mastery rank 8 or above can craft the weapon in their Foundry once the blueprint is purchased. All components are crafted from common resources available across the star chart.

## Variants

[Ninkondi Prime](/weapons/ninkondi-prime) is the prime variant with improved critical chance, critical multiplier, and status chance, as well as a mixed damage distribution that adds puncture and slash alongside the retained electric base. The Prime version scales substantially better into high-level content.

## Recommended builds

Given the low crit chance, elemental and status builds are more mod-efficient than critical approaches.

Corrosive build (armor strip):
- Pressure Point for base damage
- Voltaic Strike for additional electricity and status chance
- Fever Strike for toxin (creates Corrosive combined with built-in electricity)
- Condition Overload as the primary damage multiplier once three or more status types are active
- Drifting Contact to maintain combo counter between engagements

Viral alternative:
- Replace the Corrosive elements with Frostbite (cold + status) and Fever Strike (toxin) to create Viral, then allow the built-in electricity to apply a second status type -- Viral plus Electric reduces health recovery and stuns, effective against Grineer and Infested

The Flailing Branch stance is the only compatible stance for nunchaku weapons. Its wide arc attacks on charged combos hit multiple enemies simultaneously, spreading status effects efficiently. Prioritize finding and equipping this stance before investing forma in the weapon.

## Notes

Ninkondi's built-in electricity makes it one of the few base-game melee weapons with an innate elemental type. This reduces the number of mod slots needed to reach combined elemental effects compared to weapons starting from only physical damage. Players building for Corrosive against Grineer or Magnetic against Corpus need only one additional elemental mod to reach the combined element.

The weapon requires mastery rank 8, placing it at a stage when players are beginning to engage with forma investment and dedicated modding. Given Ninkondi Prime's availability and substantially improved statistics, consider whether leveling the base Ninkondi for mastery points and then transitioning to the prime variant is more efficient for long-term progression.
