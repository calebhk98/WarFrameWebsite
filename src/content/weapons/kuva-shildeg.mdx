## Overview

Kuva Shildeg is a Kuva variant hammer obtained exclusively through the Kuva Lich system. Players must identify and vanquish a Kuva Lich that spawned using the Kuva Shildeg as its weapon, which triggers a random damage bonus (between 25% and 60%) and a bonus elemental damage type applied to the weapon permanently. The hammer category gives Kuva Shildeg wide sweep attacks and access to heavy slam mechanics, while its Kuva-enhanced stats push it well beyond comparable standard melee hammers.

At 31% critical chance and 2.7x critical multiplier, Kuva Shildeg occupies strong ground in the crit-melee tier. The 3.0 m range is generous for a hammer, enabling coverage of multiple enemies per swing. The damage split -- 75.8 impact, 28.7 puncture, and 100.4 slash -- is mixed, with no extreme weighting toward a single physical type, allowing the weapon to function reliably across most enemy factions. The bonus elemental damage from the Kuva Lich assignment adds a substantial flat damage layer on top of the base WFCD stats.

Mastery Rank 13 is required to equip Kuva Shildeg, consistent with the upper-tier positioning of Kuva weapons within the game's progression hierarchy.

## Acquisition

Kuva Shildeg is obtained by vanquishing a Kuva Lich that spawned with the Shildeg as its assigned weapon. Lich weapons are determined at the moment the Lich is created through the Kuva Larvling assassination step. Once vanquished, the weapon is delivered to the player with a fixed elemental bonus damage percentage (25-60%) and a randomly assigned bonus element. Players can trade vanquished Kuva Lich weapons via the Trading Post if they own the weapon but prefer a different element or bonus percentage.

Repeating the Kuva Lich acquisition loop for a preferred element and high damage bonus (55-60%) is common among players who intend to use the weapon in endgame content.

## Variants

Kuva Shildeg is itself a Kuva variant of the standard Shildeg, which was released as a clantech weapon from the Orokin Lab. The base Shildeg is craftable through Dojo research and offers lower overall stats without the bonus elemental damage. The Kuva version supersedes it in all performance categories.

No Tenet, Wraith, or Prime version of the Shildeg exists. Kuva Shildeg is the highest-tier variant in the weapon family.

## Recommended builds

A crit-focused build for Kuva Shildeg uses Primed Pressure Point, Blood Rush, Organ Shatter, and Amalgam Organ Shatter for heavy attack critical multiplier. Combining Blood Rush's combo-scaling crit chance with the 2.7x multiplier produces high burst damage output at maintained combo counts. Heavy Attack builds using Killing Blow leverage the weapon's high multiplier for single-strike burst damage at the cost of combo management.

A status build benefits from the 27% base status chance and Weeping Wounds scaling. Pairing two combined elemental mods -- such as Viral (Cold + Toxin) -- with Condition Overload creates a damage loop where each proc multiplies subsequent hits. The mixed damage split provides reasonable Slash proc values for bleed damage-over-time against armored targets.

The bonus elemental damage from the Kuva Lich assignment should inform which elemental mods are added to avoid redundant combinations. A Kuva Shildeg with a Heat bonus, for example, pairs well with Cold mods to form Blast or with Toxin to form Gas.

## Notes

Hammer stance mods compatible with Kuva Shildeg include Crushing Ruin, which adds directional slamming combos effective at knocking back grouped enemies. Slam attacks from hammers have amplified AoE radius compared to other melee types, making ground slams useful for clearing clustered enemy groups in Defense and Survival missions.

Because Kuva Shildeg is obtained through the Kuva Lich system, it cannot be blueprinted or purchased through the Market. Players without the weapon must engage the full Lich progression loop to acquire it.
