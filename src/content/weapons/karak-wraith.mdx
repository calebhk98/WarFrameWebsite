## Overview

Karak Wraith is a Primary weapon in Warframe with a focus on delivering consistent physical damage through a balanced distribution of impact, puncture, and slash components. The weapon's design emphasizes reliability, making it suitable for a broad range of mission types and enemy compositions. Whether tackling early-game content or supplementing endgame builds, Karak Wraith provides a solid foundation for players seeking a dependable damage source.

The weapon's characteristics make it particularly valuable for newer players establishing weapon diversity while simultaneously offering viable damage scaling for experienced players willing to invest proper modding resources. Its learning curve remains gentle, allowing new Tenno to focus on enemy positioning and ability usage rather than complex weapon mechanics.

## Stats

Karak Wraith delivers a total damage output of 31 per hit, distributed across 14 impact, 8 puncture, and 9 slash. This balanced physical damage breakdown means the weapon performs adequately against all armor types without requiring extensive elemental augmentation, though such additions significantly amplify its effectiveness.

Critical stats show a 13.0% chance to trigger critical hits with a 2.0x damage multiplier on success. This translates to consistent critical damage when paired with crit-enhancing mods, enabling viable crit-focused build paths. Status chance sits at 25.0%, providing moderate baseline proc application that scales substantially with status-boosting mods and elemental damage additions.

The weapon's range characteristics allow for standard engagement distances typical of its weapon class. Attack speed and damage per swing make it viable as both a primary damage source and a utility tool in mixed damage compositions. Attack animations are relatively standard for the weapon class, facilitating smooth weapon transitions during combat rotations.

## Acquisition

Karak Wraith is obtained from the Grineer-themed Operation event, where it is periodically distributed as a reward. It is not craftable from a blueprint; the weapon itself is awarded directly. Check the Warframe official site or in-game alerts for current availability.

## Variants

The base Karak is available from the Market for Credits. Karak Wraith improves critical chance from 9% to 13%, increases critical multiplier from 1.5x to 2.0x, and raises status chance from 15% to 25%, making it a substantial upgrade for status-focused builds.

## Recommended builds

A status-focused Karak Wraith build uses Serration, Split Chamber, Heavy Caliber, two elemental mods for Corrosive or Viral, and Vigilante Armaments for additional multishot. The 25% base status chance scales well with these mods, producing reliable proc application at standard fire rate.

A crit-status hybrid build substitutes one elemental mod for Point Strike, exploiting the 13% base crit chance alongside the 2.0x multiplier for consistent critical hits. This hybrid approach performs well against mixed enemy compositions where neither pure crit nor pure status dominates.

Bring an Armor-stripping secondary or rely on Corrosive procs from the Karak Wraith itself when facing heavily-armored Grineer units above level 40.

## Notes

Karak Wraith's improved stats over the base Karak make it a capable mid-tier primary for players who have not yet unlocked stronger options. Its high fire rate of 11.67 rounds per second compensates for modest per-shot damage figures by applying damage and procs continuously. Reload time of 2.0 seconds is standard for the category; Vigilante Supplies is recommended if ammo economy becomes a concern in long missions.
