## Overview

The Velox is a high-fire-rate automatic pistol offering 15 rounds per second with a generous 62-round magazine. Its 24 total damage per shot (4.3 impact, 11.5 puncture, 8.2 slash) is modest individually, but the sustained damage-per-second from uninterrupted fire is competitive. The 22% status chance is the weapon's primary strength: at 15 rounds per second, status procs accumulate rapidly across a target, enabling fast buildup of elemental debuffs.

The 14% critical chance and 1.8x multiplier are unremarkable but add meaningful damage variance when combined with multishot mods. The 2.6-second reload is on the slower side for a pistol, but the 62-round magazine means players spend far less time reloading than the reload time suggests -- a full magazine at 15 rounds per second lasts over four seconds.

The Velox suits aggressive, mobile combat styles where players stay in continuous firing while moving through enemy groups, applying status procs with high frequency.

## Acquisition

The Velox blueprint is purchasable from the in-game Market for 20,000 credits. Build time is 12 hours. No quest or event unlock is required.

## Variants

**Velox Prime** -- The Prime variant offers improved critical chance, status chance, damage, and magazine capacity. It requires a higher Mastery Rank and is obtained through Void Relics.

## Recommended builds

**Status saturation build**: Equip Barrel Diffusion (multishot), Lethal Torrent (fire rate + multishot), Hornet Strike (base damage), and elemental status mods. At 15 rounds per second plus multishot, the Velox applies status procs at a rate that saturates targets within seconds. Gas damage (heat + toxin) works well given the target-saturation model.

**Viral primer support**: Equip cold + toxin for Viral and focus on multishot mods. Use the Velox to quickly prime targets with Viral before switching to a primary weapon for high-damage follow-up.

## Notes

The Velox's high fire rate chews through ammo faster than most pistols. Pistol Ammo Mutation is advisable for extended survival missions. Pre-reload at safe moments rather than waiting for the magazine to empty during active engagements.


The Velox's status chance of 22% at 15 rounds per second means that on average, a status proc applies every 0.3 seconds with a single target -- significantly faster than most secondaries. Against targets where elemental status types accumulate, this creates rapid Galvanized Shot and Galvanized Aptitude stack building. In kill-dense missions like Defense or Survival, the Velox's status output enables very rapid Galvanized bonus resets and rebuilds.

The Velox Prime offers an improved version of this same platform with higher status and critical stats, accessible to players who have progressed further in Mastery Rank. The base Velox remains a strong option at its Mastery Rank tier and benefits from the same build principles.
