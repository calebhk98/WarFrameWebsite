## Overview

The Tigris Prime represents the ultimate evolution of shotgun engineering in the Warframe arsenal. This powerful hitscan shotgun delivers devastating single-target damage through overwhelming burst potential. The weapon's twin-barrel design requires careful trigger discipline due to its minimal magazine capacity, but compensates through exceptional raw output per shot. Tigris Prime serves as the reference point for high-damage shotgun gameplay, excelling in steel path endurance missions where armor scaling demands concentrated firepower. The weapon functions as a precision instrument despite its shotgun classification, rewarding careful shot placement and tactical positioning.

## Stats

Tigris Prime fires at 2 rounds per second with a magazine capacity of just 2 rounds, requiring frequent reload cycles of 1.8 seconds. The damage breakdown emphasizes slash at 156 units per shot, with 19.5 impact and 19.5 puncture creating balanced physical damage. Critical chance sits at 10% with 2x multiplier, providing occasional burst when crits land. Status chance of 11.25% remains relatively low, encouraging raw damage stacking over status application. The hitscan fire type delivers instant damage without travel time, eliminating projectile compensation requirements at any range.

## Acquisition

Tigris Prime components distribute across multiple Axi void relics (Axi V6, Axi G4, Axi K3), making acquisition require persistent void fissure farming. All three components (blueprint, barrel, stock) appear as rare drops within their respective relics, necessitating Radiant refinement or high fissure frequency for efficient collection. Each component requires 6 forma in build time totaling 72 hours, consuming approximately 20,000 void traces for full acquisition through relic opening. The weapon remains unvaulted as of 2026, keeping current market prices stable through live relic availability.

## Damage and Performance

The 156 slash damage provides exceptional status proc buildup potential when paired with status chance mods, enabling bleed stacking on tanky targets. The split physical damage distribution ensures consistent performance against varied armor types. While 10% crit chance seems low, it compounds meaningfully with Point Strike and Vital Sense, enabling critical builds that exceed raw damage approaches in endgame content. The hitscan delivery guarantees accuracy without aiming compensation, making the weapon consistent across engagement distances within effective shotgun range.

## Mod Recommendations

Serration and Heavy Caliber form the core raw damage foundation, with these mods determining primary DPS scaling. Point Strike and Vital Sense transform occasional crits into substantial damage multipliers, particularly synergistic given the weapon's 2x crit multiplier base. Corrosive Projection and Hellfire status mods enable armor stripping and proc application respectively. Split Ammo provides magazine extension, critical given the tiny 2-round capacity. Reload speed mods become mandatory quality-of-life improvements reducing downtime between magazine changes.

## Builds and Synergies

Tigris Prime excels in Banshee-built teams where Sound Quake enables guaranteed crits for massive burst damage. Rhino's Iron Skin provides durability for sustained close-range engagement where shotguns operate optimally. Limbo's Rift Walk creates safe positioning zones enabling uninterrupted aim with predictable target phases. Volt's Speed Buff increases reload animation speed, partially mitigating the weapon's magazine capacity limitation. Mesa's Peacemakers pair tactically as secondary high-damage options for boss phases.

## Performance Notes

The Tigris Prime demands high-level piloting skills compared to automatic alternatives. Managing 2-round magazines requires constant attention in extended firefights, making it unsuitable for newer players unfamiliar with reload discipline. Effective engagement distance remains limited by shotgun falloff, requiring frequent repositioning in sprawling tilesets. However, within optimal range the weapon delivers unmatched instant burst damage unrivaled by any other primary weapon platform in 2026.

## Lore

The Tigris Prime emerged during the Ancient War as Orokin personal armament for close-quarters battlefield supremacy. Its dual-barrel configuration reflects Orokin preferences for overwhelming force application rather than ammunition efficiency. Prime craftsmanship endows the weapon with superior damage scaling through void-infused materials, distinguishing it from its non-prime counterpart. The weapon represents peak shotgun design from the Orokin golden age, adapted for modern Tenno tactical application.
