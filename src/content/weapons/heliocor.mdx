## Overview

Heliocor is a research hammer with a built-in scanner mechanic that generates holographic spectral duplicates of defeated Sentient enemies. Each kill with Heliocor has a chance to produce a spectral copy of the slain enemy that fights alongside the player for a short duration. This dual-purpose design makes Heliocor both a functional melee weapon and a convenient tool for Cephalon Simaris Synthesis tasks, since enemies killed by Heliocor are automatically scanned for the Codex without consuming a Synthesis Scanner charge.

As a hammer, Heliocor uses wide sweeping heavy attack animations with a generous hit area, useful for clearing grouped enemies. Its 38% base critical chance is one of the highest among standard melee weapons in the hammer category. The impact-weighted damage profile performs well against Corpus shield pools, and the 12% status chance provides a baseline for building toward elemental procs with appropriate mods.

The 280 base damage total is heavily impact-weighted at 238 impact. This damage distribution is effective against shields and some Corpus health units, but less efficient against Grineer armor compared to puncture-dominant or slash-dominant weapons. The 38% critical chance is the weapon's primary stat strength and drives most of its effective mod combinations.

## Acquisition

The Heliocor blueprint is available from the in-game Market for credits. After purchasing, the weapon is constructed in the Foundry using standard components available through mid-game star chart progression. Mastery Rank 9 is required to equip Heliocor. No Void Relic farming or Trading Post purchase is required; the weapon is fully obtainable through solo play and credit acquisition.

## Variants

No Prime, Vandal, or Wraith version of the Heliocor exists as of the current game version. The Synoid Heliocor is a Cephalon Suda syndicate offering with improved stats, adding Cold damage and higher critical multiplier over the base model. The Synoid variant is purchased with syndicate standing from Cephalon Suda at the appropriate standing tier.

## Recommended builds

The 38% critical chance responds well to Blood Rush, which scales crit chance multiplicatively with the melee combo counter. Combining Blood Rush with Weeping Wounds enables simultaneous critical and status scaling at higher combo counts. Organ Shatter adds critical multiplier, and Primed Pressure Point establishes the base damage foundation.

For a heavy attack-focused build, Killing Blow and Amalgam Organ Shatter convert Heliocor's large per-hit damage into burst damage on heavy attacks. The impact-heavy damage profile can be supplemented with Hunter Munitions, which adds Slash procs on critical hits, effectively converting impact damage into bleed damage-over-time against armored targets.

The spectral copy mechanic is cosmetic in function and does not significantly affect combat efficiency. Its primary practical use is against Synthesis targets for Cephalon Simaris without consuming Synthesis Scanner charges.

## Notes

Heliocor's scanner integration works with the Cephalon Simaris Synthesis system. Codex scans from Heliocor kills accumulate without consuming Scanner charges, making it a convenient companion weapon for players working toward Synthesis Standing or completing daily targets.
