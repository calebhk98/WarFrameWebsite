## Overview

The Epitaph Prime is the Prime variant of the Epitaph secondary weapon, with a damage profile split across Impact (126), Puncture (141.75), and Slash (47.25). The Puncture weighting slightly dominates, which combined with a 14% status chance and very low 4% critical chance confirms this as a status-leaning secondary -- though the status chance is not high enough for dedicated status builds without mods. The fire rate of 1.75 rounds/second indicates a slow-firing secondary; the 0.6-second reload is fast. The 80 accuracy at that fire rate suggests a precision secondary that fires individual projectiles rather than spread-shot. Mastery Rank 14 is required.

The Epitaph's distinctive mechanic is a charged shot that launches a frost projectile creating a freezing area-of-effect cloud. This is not reflected in WFCD's standard stat fields, which capture the uncharged shot values. The Prime version improves on the base Epitaph's stats with increased damage and enhanced performance. For full mechanic details including the charged-shot behavior and cloud duration, consult the Fandom wiki, as these mechanics are not captured in WFCD numeric data.

## Acquisition

The Epitaph Prime blueprint is available from the Market. As a Prime weapon, its component blueprints (Barrel, Receiver, Stock) are obtained from Void Relics. The Market blueprint alone does not allow crafting without the relic-sourced components. Check [the Epitaph Prime Fandom wiki page](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Epitaph_Prime) for current relic availability and vault status.

## Variants

The base Epitaph is the standard version of this secondary, available through earlier Void Relics or trading. The Epitaph Prime improves on it with higher base damage values. No Kuva or Tenet variant exists for the Epitaph family.

## Recommended builds

**Status/Viral utility build.** The 14% status serves a utility role for proc application. Equip Hornet Strike, Barrel Diffusion, Lethal Torrent, and two Viral 60/60 mods (Pistol Pestilence + Frostbite). Use the charged-shot mechanic to deliver area-of-effect Cold procs before switching to a primary or melee for follow-up damage. The charged-shot's cold cloud utility supplements the uncharged shot damage application.

**Charged-shot AoE utility.** The Epitaph Prime's primary value is the charged shot's area-of-effect freeze mechanic rather than raw DPS. Equip mods that enhance elemental proc duration and strength, including Augur Seeker for status chance and Primed Heated Charge for heat combination if desired. Use the charged shot for crowd control and proc priming between engagements.

**Impact/Puncture damage build.** The 126 Impact + 141.75 Puncture base creates a viable physical damage combination for Corpus and Grineer Robotics targets. Equip Hornet Strike, Barrel Diffusion, Lethal Torrent, and two faction-specific Bane mods if applicable. Galvanized Chamber replaces Split Chamber for ramping damage during extended encounters.

## Synergies

The Epitaph Prime's cold-cloud charged shot pairs with Nova's Molecular Prime (also a Cold/Radiation mechanic) for Frozen target follow-up damage. Frames that can sustain enemies in area denial benefit from the freeze cloud as a crowd control bridge. Frost's Freeze and the Epitaph Prime's charged shot stack Cold proc duration for extended crowd control windows.

## Notes

- The 4% critical chance is negligible; do not invest in critical mods. Status and raw damage mods are the priority.
- The charged-shot mechanic is the primary differentiator between the Epitaph Prime and other secondaries; practice the charge timing to maximize the cold cloud's area coverage.
