## Overview

The [Kuva Kohm](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Kuva_Kohm) is a Grineer-originated automatic shotgun obtained exclusively through the Kuva Lich system. Unlike standard Market or crafted weapons, the Kuva Kohm is always acquired with a randomized bonus elemental damage type (between 25% and 60% of base damage) inherited from the Lich that held it. This bonus damage is added on top of the weapon's base Impact, Puncture, and Slash values, making each Kuva Kohm statistically unique.

The Kohm and its Kuva variant fire a multishot spread that starts at a single projectile and expands to a full spread as the trigger is held continuously. This spin-up mechanic means effective DPS increases with sustained fire. At full spin-up, the combination of multishot and 10% per-pellet status chance results in a very high number of status procs per second, making Kuva Kohm a premier status-delivery platform for elemental build strategies.

Mastery Rank 13 is required to equip Kuva Kohm.

## Acquisition

Kuva Kohm is obtained by defeating a Kuva Lich that holds a Kohm. Kuva Liches are created by killing a Kuva Larvling in a Kuva Fortress or standard Grineer mission while the Kuva Lich candidate appears. The Lich is then pursued and defeated through the Requiem Parazon system across multiple missions. On vanquish, the Lich surrenders its weapon with the randomized bonus elemental type to the player.

The weapon can also be traded between players via Trade Chat or warframe.market, allowing players to acquire a specific bonus elemental type without Lich hunting. The Kuva Kohm is not purchasable from the Market or craftable via blueprint.

## Variants

The base **Kohm** (non-Kuva) is available from the Market at Mastery Rank 5. It shares the same spin-up mechanic but lacks the bonus elemental damage of the Kuva variant and has lower base stats. The Kuva Kohm represents a significant improvement over the base Kohm in all statistical categories.

No Prime, Tenet, Vandal, or Wraith variant of the Kohm exists.

## Recommended builds

**Viral-Status (standard endgame)**: Stack viral (Infected Clip + Thermite Rounds for the viral combination) with Galvanized Aptitude and Galvanized Chamber. The high per-pellet status chance at full spin-up delivers Viral procs consistently, amplifying all subsequent damage. Hunter Munitions provides Slash procs on critical hits for additional health damage.

**Full status spread**: Maximize total status chance with additional status mods. At full multishot and spin-up, Kuva Kohm can apply multiple status types simultaneously, ideal for frames running Condition Overload or similar status-count-based multipliers.

**Corrosive armor strip**: Use Heat + Electric (Corrosive combination) against Grineer heavy units. Corrosive reduces armor by up to 80% at 10 stacks; Kuva Kohm's rapid status delivery reaches this threshold quickly on high-armor targets.

## Notes

The bonus elemental type from Lich conversion significantly influences the optimal build direction. A Kuva Kohm with Radiation bonus is strong against Grineer heavy units; one with Toxin bonus enables Corrosive or Viral builds without a second elemental mod. Players should determine the bonus type before committing to a build.

Kuva Kohm uses rifle ammo. The 209-round magazine accommodates extended engagements, but the spin-up mechanic means players should hold the trigger through sustained encounters rather than releasing between shots.
