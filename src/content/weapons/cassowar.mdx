## Overview

The Cassowar is a polearm melee weapon that distributes physical damage across all three types, making it one of the more versatile melee options available at Mastery Rank 5. Its combination of a solid total damage of 188 and a 28% status chance positions it as a reliable status-focused polearm. Unlike many melee weapons that lean heavily into one damage type, the Cassowar splits its output across impact, puncture, and slash, allowing elemental mods to apply procs broadly across enemy types.

The polearm stance category grants the Cassowar access to stance mods such as Shimmering Blight and Bleeding Willow, both of which dramatically expand its combo potential and effective range. Polearms benefit from naturally extended reach compared to swords or daggers, enabling the Cassowar to engage multiple enemies simultaneously during combo strings.

Riven disposition sits at 5 out of 5, the highest tier, indicating that the weapon is underused relative to the broader playerbase. This makes Cassowar rivens statistically likely to roll strong values, benefiting players who invest in riven hunting or trading.

## Acquisition

The Cassowar blueprint is available through the [WARFRAME Wiki](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Cassowar) for crafting via the Foundry. The Market also lists the completed weapon for platinum. As a non-prime, non-event weapon, it remains permanently available and is never vaulted, making it accessible to players at any stage of progression.

Crafting costs are standard for a Mastery Rank 5 weapon. Players should expect to farm common resources such as Ferrite, Circuits, and Alloy Plate alongside a blueprint purchase from the Market.

## Variants

The Cassowar has no prime, vandal, or wraith variant as of the current update. The base version is the only form of this weapon available. Players looking for a primed polearm should consider the [Bo Prime](/weapons/bo-prime), which offers significantly higher critical chance and multiplier at the cost of a lower status profile.

## Recommended builds

**Status hybrid build:** Equip Primed Pressure Point, Berserker Fury, and Condition Overload as the primary damage drivers. Add four elemental mods (two pairs for viral or corrosive plus heat) to maximize the 28% status chance. Fill remaining slots with Organ Shatter and a stance mod. This configuration exploits the Cassowar's broad physical damage split to apply multiple status types simultaneously, feeding Condition Overload stacks rapidly.

**Pure status build:** Replace Organ Shatter with Drifting Contact to extend combo duration. Prioritize viral and heat for the most universally effective damage profile against armored and unarmored targets. Corrosive pairs well when facing high-armor Grineer content.

**Beginner status build:** Use Pressure Point, Fever Strike, Shocking Touch, Molten Impact, and North Wind alongside a polarity-appropriate stance. This entry-level configuration delivers adequate performance in star chart content without requiring rare mods.

## Synergies

- **Saryn:** Saryn's Spores spread toxin procs. Adding corrosive through the Cassowar multiplies the strip potential against Grineer armored units.
- **Condition Overload (mod):** Each unique status type active on an enemy multiplies the weapon's damage. The Cassowar's physical split ensures that unmodded hits apply at least one type, while elemental additions stack further.
- **Gladiator set mods:** Combining Gladiator mods on the Cassowar and Warframe increases critical chance per active combo multiplier, compensating for the weapon's modest 6% base critical chance.

## Notes

- The Cassowar's low critical chance (6%) and low multiplier (1.4x) make pure crit builds inefficient. Status and condition-based approaches are consistently superior for this weapon.
- Polearm stance mods are relatively common drops. Shimmering Blight drops from Arid Butchers on Mars; Bleeding Willow has a wider drop pool including several Grineer assassination targets.
- Riven disposition at the maximum tier means that any Cassowar riven is worth examining for status chance, status duration, or damage rolls before discarding.
- The weapon was introduced as part of the Tenno-themed melee expansion and does not have a signature frame synergy.
