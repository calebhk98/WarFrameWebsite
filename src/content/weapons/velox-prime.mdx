## Overview

The Velox Prime is the Prime variant of the Velox automatic pistol, available at Mastery Rank 13. It fires at 17.0 rounds per second with a 70-round magazine and 27 total damage per shot (6.48 impact, 12.96 puncture, 7.56 slash). The high fire rate and large magazine make the Velox Prime a sustained-fire secondary oriented toward status proc accumulation rather than burst crit damage.

The 32% status chance is the Velox Prime's most valuable stat. At 17.0 fire rate with 32% status per shot, elemental procs apply multiple times per second. The 14% critical chance and 2.0x multiplier are below crit-focused secondary weapons -- this is a status platform. The 70-round magazine at 17 fire rate provides approximately 4.1 seconds of continuous fire before the 2.6-second reload.

The Velox Prime is the prime-tier equivalent of the Velox and drops from Void Relics, making it a late-game weapon for players who have progressed through the relic farming system.

## Recommended builds

**Viral status**: Cold plus toxin for Viral, Hornet Strike, Barrel Diffusion, Lethal Torrent, Galvanized Aptitude. At 17 fire rate with 32% status, Viral applies within the first 2 seconds. Galvanized Aptitude stacks damage bonuses on each proc-kill, rapidly building a damage multiplier during the 70-round magazine window. Lethal Torrent adds further fire rate.

**Galvanized Shot stack**: Galvanized Shot (bonus damage per status type on enemy), Galvanized Aptitude, Galvanized Chamber, Hornet Strike, and dual-stat 60/60 elemental mods. Multiple elemental status types applied by dual-stat mods at 32% status each stack Galvanized Shot bonuses rapidly.

**Corrosive sustained fire**: Electric plus toxin (Corrosive) dual-stat mods, Hornet Strike, Barrel Diffusion, Lethal Torrent. Corrosive strips Grineer armor through proc accumulation. At 17 fire rate, six corrosive procs apply within approximately 1 second, stripping all Grineer armor layers before the remainder of the magazine deals full damage.

## Notes

The 70-round magazine is generous for a secondary. Combine with Vigilante Armaments or Lethal Torrent to extend the effective fire window. Ammo mutation mods help in long survival or defense missions where secondary ammo is scarce. The 32% status chance does not need supplemental status mods to achieve reliable proc application at 17 fire rate -- invest the mod slots in elemental damage and fire rate instead.
