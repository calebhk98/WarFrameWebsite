## Overview

Jai is a Zaw strike component sold by Hok the Artisan in Cetus on the Plains of Eidolon. It is not a standalone weapon. As a strike, Jai defines the blade shape and the base physical damage profile of the assembled Zaw melee weapon. The full weapon only exists once a player combines the Jai strike with a compatible grip and a link at Hok's forge. Zaw assembly is the Ostron approach to modular melee construction: the three components -- strike, grip, and link -- each contribute to the final weapon's stats, stance type, and appearance.

The WFCD database records Jai with zero standalone damage values because Zaw components have no combat function outside of a completed assembly. This is expected behavior for a crafting component. The slug and page exist here to document the component's role, acquisition requirements, and how it interacts with the Zaw system.

## Stats

Jai contributes no independent combat stats. Its role in the assembled Zaw is to define the blade type and influence the damage ratio between physical damage types on the finished weapon. The table below reflects the WFCD-recorded component values.

| Stat | Value |

The assembled Zaw inherits damage, critical chance, critical multiplier, status chance, and range from the combination of all three chosen components. Two players using the same Jai strike but different grips and links will produce Zaws with different attack speeds, stances, and stat totals. The [Fandom wiki Zaw page](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Zaw) provides full stat formulas for each strike and grip combination.

## Acquisition

Hok the Artisan in Cetus sells Jai for Ostron Standing. Ostron Standing is earned by completing Plains of Eidolon bounties and by trading fish parts and ore at Cetus vendors. Strike components are unlocked progressively as a player increases their Ostron Standing rank. Jai does not drop from enemies, cannot be purchased from the in-game Market for credits, and does not appear in Trade Chat because Zaw components are untradable.

To forge a Zaw using Jai, visit Hok and select "Forge Zaw." Select Jai as the strike, then choose a compatible grip and a link. The grip determines the weapon's stance category (one-handed, two-handed, nikana, and others) and has a direct impact on attack speed. The link modifies critical chance, critical multiplier, and status chance. After assembly, the Zaw can be ranked to 30 and then gilded at Hok to receive a custom name, enable Exodia arcane installation, and apply a cosmetic polish.

## Variants

Zaw strikes do not have Prime, Vandal, or Wraith variants. The Zaw system as a whole is the modular melee alternative to standard manufactured weapons. Individual component pages (strike, grip, link) document the component in isolation; complete weapon stat results depend on the full assembly combination.

## Recommended builds

Because Jai is a component and not a complete weapon, build recommendations apply to the assembled Zaw rather than to Jai itself. The assembled weapon's base stats determine the most effective mod loadout. A Zaw with high status chance from its link component favors Condition Overload and Weeping Wounds. A Zaw with high critical chance benefits from Blood Rush at elevated combo counters.

After gilding, the Zaw can receive an Exodia arcane. Exodia Contagion and Exodia Devastation are popular choices for Zaw melee builds that rely on heavy attack patterns. Exodia Brave and Exodia Hunt serve utility roles for energy management and enemy grouping, respectively.

## Notes

Ostron Standing has a daily cap that scales with the player's Mastery Rank. Purchasing higher-tier Zaw components may require multiple farming sessions. Bounty rewards are the fastest method for accumulating large amounts of Ostron Standing in a short session. Hok also accepts fish parts and ore traded by Hai-Luk and Konzu vendors as secondary Standing sources. Check the current Ostron Standing cap before planning farming sessions to avoid diminishing returns after the daily limit is reached.
