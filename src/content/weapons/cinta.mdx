## Overview

The [Cinta](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Cinta) is a Duviri bow primary weapon with exceptional per-shot damage: 800 total per shot split between 640 slash and 160 puncture. The 20% critical chance and 2.0x multiplier support functional critical builds, while the 32% status chance enables reliable proc application. As a bow, the Cinta fires one arrow per shot with a 0.65-second reload between shots.

Bows in Warframe charge their shots for increased damage -- releasing at full charge delivers maximum per-shot damage. The Cinta's 800 base damage at full charge is among the highest single-shot damage values for bows. Mastery Rank 6 makes it accessible to mid-progression players who have unlocked the Duviri Paradox game mode.

## Acquisition

The Cinta is obtained through the Duviri Paradox game mode -- via The Circuit (Duviri's roguelike) or the Duviri Experience bounties. Players must complete the introductory Duviri quest to access these reward sources. Check the [Cinta Wiki page](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Cinta) for current drop tables and reward rotation.

## Variants

No Prime, Vandal, or Wraith variant exists. Duviri weapons do not currently have Prime forms. Players seeking bow alternatives should compare the Cernos Prime (higher critical stats) or the Dread (innate slash bonus) for different profiles.

## Recommended builds

**Slash critical build:** Serration, Split Chamber, Hunter Munitions, Vital Sense, Point Strike, and two elemental mods for viral or heat. Hunter Munitions converts critical hits to guaranteed slash procs, feeding bleed stacks that bypass armor. At 20% critical chance, Hunter Munitions triggers frequently. The 640 base slash already generates bleed procs at 32% status chance; Hunter Munitions adds guaranteed slash procs on every critical hit.

**Headshot sniper build:** Add Vigilante Supplies for ammo efficiency and Argon Scope for headshot critical chance bonus. The Cinta's high base damage and single-shot accuracy make precision headshots reliable for triggering headshot-dependent bonuses.

**Status hybrid:** Equip Galvanized Aptitude (status-on-kill conditional multishot) alongside elemental mods for viral and heat. The 32% status chance at full charge delivers procs on most shots, with viral halving health and heat adding ongoing damage.

## Synergies

- **Hunter Munitions:** Converts critical hits to guaranteed slash procs. At the Cinta's 800 base damage and 20% crit chance, Hunter Munitions generates bleed ticks that deal substantial percentage-of-health damage, effective in Steel Path.
- **Wisp's Reservoirs:** Damage and attack speed buffs from Wisp's Breach Surge and Haste Mote improve the Cinta's draw speed, increasing effective fire rate and damage output simultaneously.
- **Banshee's Sonar:** The Cinta's precision single-shot delivery makes it ideal for targeting Sonar's amplified weak points. A fully charged arrow into a Sonar weak point delivers devastating per-hit damage.

## Notes

- Bows require full-charge shots for maximum damage. Releasing early delivers partial damage proportional to charge completion. Full-charge timing is weapon-specific and should be practiced in the Simulacrum before deployment.
- The 0.6-second reload (nocking next arrow) is the fastest among bows, enabling a higher effective fire rate than longer-reload bows like the Lenz.
- Slash at 640 (80% of total) means the Cinta's physical damage is biased toward flesh-vulnerable targets (Infested, Corpus Flesh) and armor-bypass bleed procs (from slash procs) against Grineer.
- As a Duviri weapon, the Cinta may appear as a random reward in Circuit runs before players actively pursue it. If encountered as a Circuit reward, selecting it provides an immediate copy without further farming.
