## Overview

The Tenet Agendus is a Sisters of Parvos Tenet melee weapon requiring Mastery Rank 14. Its 120 total damage per hit is entirely impact -- no puncture, no slash. Pure impact damage is unusual for a melee weapon and has a specific tactical niche: impact procs apply the Knockdown or Stagger status, which interrupts enemy actions. This makes the Agendus an effective crowd-control melee option for disrupting Corpus enemies whose shield mechanics benefit less from slash's bleed damage.

The 28% status chance generates impact procs reliably over the course of a combo. The 24% critical chance and 2.0x multiplier are serviceable for hybrid builds but not high enough for a pure crit approach. At a slower 0.92 attack speed, each individual hit carries more weight than lighter weapon classes. The 2.6-meter range covers clustered enemy groups well for a single-target melee pattern.

Tenet weapons are acquired through Sisters of Parvos -- fighting and vanquishing a Sister yields her Tenet weapon with a unique elemental bonus. The bonus element and its magnitude vary per Sister, making each Agendus unique. Players who prefer a specific elemental bonus may need to run multiple Sister hunts.

## Acquisition

Obtain through the Sisters of Parvos system. Create a Sister Candidate by completing Granum Void challenges (Corpus Railjack proxima nodes or Corpus ship missions with Granum Crown offers). Vanquish the resulting Sister to claim her Tenet weapon. Convert the Sister instead to gain her as an ally, sacrificing the weapon. The specific weapon type and elemental bonus depend on which Lich seed you triggered.

## Variants

Tenet weapons are themselves unique -- they are the enhanced form, not a variant of a base weapon. No Prime, Vandal, or Wraith version of the Tenet Agendus exists. The Agendus does not have a base non-Tenet form with the same design.

## Recommended builds

**Impact crowd control**: Primed Pressure Point, Weeping Wounds, Condition Overload, and status elemental mods. The 28% base status with Weeping Wounds at x2+ combo reaches high impact proc rates. Repeated staggering and knockdowns disrupt enemy attack patterns, creating windows for follow-up attacks.

**Hybrid crit-status**: Blood Rush for combo-scaling crits, Organ Shatter for crit multiplier, Primed Pressure Point, and dual-stat 60/60 elemental mods. At x3 combo, Blood Rush pushes the 24% crit chance toward 60-70%, producing regular crits alongside status procs.

**Condition Overload endgame**: Stack unique status types with corrosive plus heat plus the inherent impact procs. Each unique status type active on the enemy multiplies Condition Overload damage. Three unique types triple the bonus.

## Notes

The purely impact-based damage profile is most effective against Corpus enemies whose shields amplify impact damage. Against Grineer with heavy armor, puncture or corrosive elemental is more effective -- the Agendus relies on its status procs and Condition Overload to overcome armor rather than inherent armor-penetrating damage types.
