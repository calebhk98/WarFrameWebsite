## Overview

The Vandal-tier upgrade of the Imperator featuring a dramatically increased fire rate (25 rounds/s), 300-round magazine, and improved critical statistics. Limited availability through Fomorian Sabotage event missions makes it a sought-after sustained-fire Arch-gun. Introduced in Update 15.8 (2014-12-19), the Imperator Vandal is notable for 25 rounds/s fire rate -- among the fastest Arch-guns; 300-round magazine; Vandal limited availability through Fomorian Sabotage events.

Vandal-class weapons were originally Corpus prototypes captured and repurposed by the Tenno. The Imperator Vandal represents a battlefield upgrade of the standard design, enhanced with superior manufacturing and improved feed mechanisms, originally captured during Corpus attempts to sabotage Fomorian fleet assets.

## Acquisition

All three components (Blueprint, Barrel, Receiver) drop as Uncommon rewards from Fomorian Sabotage alert missions. These missions appear intermittently and are triggered when a Fomorian fleet approaches a relay. Monitor Warframe alerts for availability. The Imperator Vandal requires Mastery Rank 5 to equip.

Arch-gun weapons require either standard crafting resources, Syndicate standing for component parts, or specialized mission drops. Once crafted, equipping the Imperator Vandal in Archwing uses the standard Arch-gun slot. To use it as a Heavy Weapon in ground missions or Railjack combat, a Gravimag must be installed. Gravimags are available from Clan Dojos via Energy Lab research, or from Arbitrations as rotation rewards.

## Variants

Upgraded Vandal form of the base Imperator. No Prime variant exists for Vandal-class weapons. The Imperator Vandal is itself the pinnacle of this weapon line.

The Arch-gun family spans weapons from Tenno engineering (Corvas, Fluctus, Velocitus), Grineer design (Grattler, Kuva line), and Entrati craftsmanship (Cortege, Mausolon, Morgha), each with distinct stat profiles suited to different engagement contexts.

## Recommended builds

Fire rate of 25 rounds per second with a 300-round magazine enables exceptional sustained DPS. Critical chance of 28% and multiplier of 2.4x benefit from Hollowed Bullets and Critical Focus. Status chance of 12% is relatively low -- focus on critical output rather than status procs. The large magazine significantly reduces reload frequency.

**Core Arch-gun mods to consider:**

- Rubedo-Lined Barrel -- +165% Arch-gun damage (primary damage mod for all builds)
- Hollowed Bullets -- +120% critical chance (essential for any crit-focused build)
- Critical Focus -- +60% critical multiplier while aiming
- Modified Munitions -- +90% status chance (for status-focused builds)
- Arch-gun Ace -- +165% fire rate (useful on slow-fire weapons to improve feel)
- Parallax Scope -- +30% critical chance while aiming
- Charged Bullets -- +60% magazine capacity
- Superior Defenses -- reduces reload time
- Conductive Coils -- adds Electricity damage
- Thermite Rounds -- adds Heat damage

Prioritize Rubedo-Lined Barrel as the first mod in any Arch-gun build. Follow with critical or status mods based on the weapon's stat profile. Elemental mods provide faction-specific damage type flexibility.

## Notes

The Imperator Vandal operates in the Arch-gun slot, used in Archwing missions and as a Heavy Weapon on the ground via Gravimag. In Railjack (Empyrean) missions, the Arch-gun serves as the primary armament during space combat against fighters and Crewships.

Among the best sustained-fire options for Railjack missions. The 300-round magazine minimizes reload interruptions during extended Railjack assault sequences and crew suppression.

Archwing combat system notes: Archwing missions require completing The Archwing quest. Four Archwings are available -- Itzal (mobility/stealth), Amesha (tank/support), Elytron (AoE damage), and Odonata (balanced starter). Works with any Archwing. Itzal's stealth approach allows sustained-fire positioning. Odonata's area healing supports extended engagements without retreating.

The weapon's Riven disposition is 3/5 (moderate). Riven dispositions range from 0 (absolute minimum, strong weapon) to 5 (maximum, weak weapon). DE adjusts dispositions quarterly based on weapon usage rates.
