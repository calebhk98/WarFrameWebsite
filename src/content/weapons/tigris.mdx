## Overview

The Tigris is a double-barreled shotgun with a distinctive break-action design. It fires two shells in rapid succession before reloading, delivering 210 damage per shot (21 impact, 168 puncture, 21 slash) across multiple pellets. The puncture-dominant damage profile makes it highly effective against Grineer armor, as puncture bypasses a meaningful portion of ferrite and alloy armor damage reduction.

The Tigris fires its two-round magazine without reloading between shots. Both barrels fire before a 1.8-second reload initiates, creating an effective combat rhythm of two rapid shots followed by a brief pause. The 10% critical chance is low for a critical build, directing Tigris players toward status-based modding instead. Its 16.8% status chance is moderate but can be pushed to reliable proc territory with shotgun-specific status mods.

Mastery Rank 7 gives the Tigris solid mid-game availability, filling the role of a high-per-shot damage shotgun that excels at close to medium range. Against unarmored targets, the impact and slash components still contribute to overall damage output, making the Tigris versatile beyond strict anti-armor engagements.

## Acquisition

The Tigris blueprint is purchasable from the in-game Market for 20,000 credits. Build time is 24 hours. No quest or event is required.

## Variants

**Tigris Prime** -- The Prime version requires Mastery Rank 13 and dramatically improves on the base with 495 total damage per shot, 22% status chance, 20% critical chance, and a 3-round magazine. Tigris Prime drops from Void Relics.

**Sancti Tigris** -- The Syndicates variant from New Loka at max standing, offering improved fire rate and damage over the base model.

## Recommended builds

**Corrosive status build**: Combine Hell's Chamber (multishot), Shotgun Savvy (status chance), Charged Shell (electric), and Contagious Spread (toxin) to reach corrosive. Add Point Blank (base damage) and Blaze (base damage + heat) for additional damage layers. Against Grineer, corrosive removes armor stacks and the underlying puncture damage deals full value against exposed health.

**Viral-slash build**: Use cold + toxin for Viral to halve enemy max health. Equip Vigilante Armaments for multishot and Seeking Fury for reload speed. The Tigris's slash component delivers bleeds at modest frequency, and Viral amplifies the bleed damage substantially.

## Notes

The two-shot break-action mechanic rewards deliberate shooting over rapid trigger use. Players who master positioning can fire both barrels into a clustered group and reload behind cover, maintaining a safe engagement cycle. Shotgun Ammo Mutation is advisable for extended survival or defense missions.
