## Overview

The Telos Boltace is the Arbiters of Hexis-enhanced variant of the Boltace tonfas. At Mastery Rank 11, it requires Arbiters of Hexis Syndicate standing to acquire. Its 210 total damage per hit is dominated by slash (178.5), making it one of the stronger slash melee weapons in the secondary tier. Tonfas are known for quick attack animations that enable rapid combo buildup, and the Telos Boltace follows this pattern with its 1.08 attack speed.

The 35% status chance is the Telos Boltace's defining offensive feature. Combined with the tonfa attack speed and multi-hit combo strings, status procs accumulate rapidly. Slash bleed procs generated from the high slash base bypass armor and tick true damage. The 20% critical chance and 2.0x multiplier are secondary to this status platform but still produce meaningful crits during extended combos.

The Telos Boltace carries the Entropy Spike Syndicate proc, which grants a chance to generate energy spheres during Syndicate mission kills. Players running Arbiters of Hexis missions benefit from sustained ability usage funded by these energy drops.

## Acquisition

The Telos Boltace is sold by the Arbiters of Hexis Syndicate vendor in any Relay. Syndicate standing and appropriate rank with the Arbiters are required. No blueprint or crafting is involved.

## Variants

**Boltace** -- The base tonfa from the Market. The Telos version improves slash weighting, status chance, and the Syndicate passive.

No Prime or Wraith variant exists for the Telos Boltace.

## Recommended builds

**Condition Overload slash**: Weeping Wounds (status on combo), Condition Overload (damage per unique status), Primed Pressure Point, and elemental mods for corrosive or heat. The 35% base status with Weeping Wounds produces reliable multi-type procs that feed Condition Overload's damage multiplier.

**Viral-slash hybrid**: Cold plus toxin for Viral, Hunter Munitions for slash-on-crit conversion, Blood Rush for combo-scaling crits. The slash dominant base and Hunter Munitions combine to stack slash bleeds during crit-heavy combo phases.

**Basic status build**: Pressure Point, Fury, Fever Strike, and North Wind for Viral. The 35% status without any augmentation produces procs on many hits at normal play.

## Notes

Tonfa weapons use a distinct stance pool. Check the in-game mod codex for the Telos Boltace's compatible stances, which determine combo strings and the overall hit pattern. Slash bleed procs generated by this weapon bypass all armor types, making it consistent in Steel Path and late-game content.


Tonfa weapons in Warframe have a unique stance set that emphasizes spinning combo strings and rapid multi-hit sequences. The Telos Boltace's 1.08 attack speed feeds these multi-hit combos well, accumulating combo counter ticks quickly. Players who sustain combo uptime through Drifting Contact or Brief Respite allow the combo counter to reach x4 or higher, at which point Weeping Wounds pushes the 35% status chance toward near-100% effective proc rates per hit -- every swing reliably applies elemental procs. This makes the Telos Boltace most powerful during extended engagements where the combo counter can build and maintain.
