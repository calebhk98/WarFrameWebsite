## Overview

The Exergis is a single-shot shotgun primary weapon with a potent innate Radiation element (140) alongside Puncture (260) and Slash (120). The innate Radiation is immediately useful without requiring two mod slots to build the element from scratch, providing bonus damage against Alloy Armor (Grineer heavy units) and the Sentient faction. The 36% status chance is high for a shotgun, enabling consistent elemental proc application. The 8% critical chance is low; this is a status-primary shotgun rather than a crit platform. Magazine of 1 and fire rate of 3.33 rounds/second means each shot must be followed by a reload cycle (1.6 seconds). Mastery Rank 8 is required. The 15.38 accuracy indicates pellet spread.

The Exergis fires a single, crystalline shard projectile that shatters on impact, applying all pellet hits simultaneously to the target and nearby enemies in the impact radius. The innate Radiation combined with Puncture creates an effective anti-armor profile: Radiation strips Alloy Armor through procs while Puncture penetrates remaining armor. At 36% status chance with the high per-shot pellet count, multiple proc types apply on each trigger pull. The single-shot magazine creates a rhythm of fire-reload-fire that suits deliberate, aimed engagement at medium range.

## Variants

The Exergis has no Prime, Kuva, or Tenet variant. It is a standalone shotgun primary weapon. The innate Radiation element is a unique characteristic that distinguishes it from other shotguns.

## Recommended builds

**Radiation/Viral status build.** The innate Radiation frees two elemental mod slots. Equip Hellfire (Heat) and Cryo Rounds (Cold) for a Blast combination, or Pistol Pestilence equivalent (Toxic Barrage, Frostbite) for Viral alongside the Radiation. The 36% status ensures consistent Radiation and Viral proc application on each shot. Galvanized Aptitude amplifies damage per unique status type.

**Corrosive supplemental build.** Adding Corrosive mods (Toxic Barrage equivalent for shotguns + Charged Shell) alongside the innate Radiation provides armor strip on multiple armor types simultaneously. Serration, Split Chamber equivalent (Shotgun Savagery), and Full Contact (Heavy Caliber equivalent) maximize the per-shot physical damage. This suits Steel Path Grineer encounters.

**Galvanized status shotgun.** Equip Galvanized Aptitude, Hell's Chamber (multishot), and multiple 60/60 shotgun status mods alongside base damage mods. The 36% status with multishot creates multiple proc rolls per shot. Innate Radiation generates Radiation procs free, and additional elements create diverse proc types for Galvanized Aptitude stacking.

## Synergies

Wisp's Reservoirs provide Haste (improving fire-reload cadence) and Breach (health) for the Exergis's single-shot sustained fire rhythm. Banshee's Sonar highlights weak points where the Puncture-dominant pellets concentrate damage. Frames that group enemies (Khora, Vauban) allow the Exergis's shatter mechanic to hit multiple grouped targets per shot.

## Notes

- The innate Radiation means two mod slots that would normally be consumed by Radiation (Hellfire + Stormbringer) are available for other elements or damage mods.
- Magazine of 1 requires discipline in reloading; preload before engagements rather than reloading mid-fight.
