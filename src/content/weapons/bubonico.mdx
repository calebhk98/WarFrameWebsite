## Overview

The Bubonico is a Mastery Rank 13 Infested-themed primary rifle with two fire modes. The primary fire launches goo-covered rounds that deal Toxin damage and apply status procs, while the alternate fire launches a burst of Viral projectiles. The base 41 damage per shot includes 2 Impact, 19 Puncture, 13 Slash, and 7 Toxin, with a 25% Critical Chance and 2.3x multiplier making it viable in critical configurations. At 9% Status Chance the weapon benefits more from critical builds than status stacking.

The Bubonico is crafted from Infested Dojo research components, giving it a distinct acquisition path. Its Toxin base damage makes it naturally effective against Corpus shields (Toxin bypasses them), and the alternate fire Viral burst applies Viral procs that amplify damage from all sources by 100% for six seconds.

## Acquisition

The Bubonico is crafted using Infested components obtainable through Infested Research in the Dojo. No blueprint is sold on the Market; players must join a clan with Infested Research or research it themselves. Requirements include Mastery Rank 13.

## Variants

The Bubonico does not have a Prime or Kuva variant as of this writing. Check the [Bubonico wiki page](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Bubonico) for any future variant information.

## Recommended builds

Critical builds use Point Strike and Vital Sense to leverage the 25% crit chance and 2.3x multiplier. At 25% base crit, Galvanized Scope and Argon Scope quickly push the weapon to reliable yellow and orange crits when aiming.

Elemental builds add Cold (Cryo Rounds) to combine with the Toxin base for Viral damage, applying Viral status procs that increase all incoming damage by 100% for 6 seconds.

## Notes

- The Toxin damage type bypasses Corpus shields entirely, making the Bubonico effective in Corpus missions without elemental mods.
- The alternate fire Viral burst has its own magazine pool; manage both ammunition types during extended missions.
- The 4.5-second reload is among the longer reloads for primary weapons; Primed Fast Hands is a strong investment.
- Infested Research requires Antiserum Injectors and Mutagenic samples obtained from Infested missions.
- The Bubonico was introduced alongside the Helminth system and has stylistic ties to Infested-themed crafting, though it is not directly part of Helminth mechanics.
- WFCD reports magazine size as 27, which is an unusual count for a primary weapon; this reflects the dual-fire mode design where the magazine is shared between the primary goo-round and alternate Viral burst ammunition pools.
- At Mastery Rank 13, the Bubonico is a late-mid-game acquisition. Players pursuing it should complete their Infested Research tree in their clan Dojo, which requires building the Orokin Laboratory and progressing through the Infested tech chain before the Bubonico blueprint becomes researchable.
