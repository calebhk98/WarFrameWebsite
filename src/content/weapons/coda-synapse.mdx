## Overview

The [Coda Synapse](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Coda_Synapse) is the Jade Shadows enhanced version of the Synapse continuous-beam rifle at Mastery Rank 17. It fires an electric corrosive beam dealing 26 corrosive damage per hit at 12 fire rate. The exceptional 40% critical chance and 2.7x critical multiplier make the Coda Synapse one of the premier critical-beam primary weapons available. Status chance at 15% is secondary, supporting occasional procs between crit hits.

## Variants

The Coda Synapse is the enhanced form of the base Synapse (lower critical stats). The Coda version delivers peak critical beam output at MR17.

## Recommended builds

**Critical beam build:** Serration, Split Chamber, Point Strike, Vital Sense, Hunter Munitions, and three elemental mods. At 40% critical chance, the Coda Synapse consistently lands yellow crits, with orange crits achievable through Argon Scope headshots or companion Gladiator mods. The 2.7x multiplier on each crit at 12 fire rate delivers outstanding sustained DPS.

**Argon Scope headshot build:** Add Argon Scope for headshot-triggered critical chance boost. The beam's perfect accuracy (100) and continuous fire makes headshot contact consistent in aimed mode, enabling frequent Argon Scope activations for elevated crit chance.

**Hunter Munitions slash conversion:** Hunter Munitions converts critical hits to guaranteed slash procs. At 40% crit chance, Hunter Munitions fires reliably, generating continuous bleed procs that bypass armor mitigation on Grineer targets.

## Synergies

- **Argon Scope:** The beam's 100 accuracy makes precise headshot contact reliable. Argon Scope's headshot bonus dramatically elevates the already-high 40% crit to near-guaranteed yellow or orange crit during sustained aimed fire.
- **Hunter Munitions:** Converts criticals to slash procs. At 40% base crit, Hunter Munitions fires frequently, accumulating bleed stacks on enemies efficiently.
- **Volt's Electric Shield:** The Coda Synapse's corrosive beam benefits from electric amplification through Volt's Shield. This adds electric damage multiplication to each beam tick, significantly elevating DPS.

## Notes

- The 76-round magazine at 12 fire rate provides approximately 6.3 seconds of sustained beam fire. The 1.5-second reload is fast, enabling quick return to fire.
- Corrosive damage at 26 per hit is low in absolute terms, but the 40% critical chance at 2.7x multiplier means effective damage per critical hit is 70.2 per tick -- and at 12 fire rate, this accumulates rapidly.
- Mastery Rank 17 beam weapons are among the most powerful in their category. The 40% crit combined with 2.7x multiplier is the defining stat pair that makes the Coda Synapse exceptional.

The Coda Synapse shares the base Synapse's beam mechanics -- continuous fire with 100% accuracy at close-to-medium range. Beam weapons in Warframe have distinct ammo consumption (draining a pool per second rather than per shot), and the Coda Synapse's enhanced critical chance (40%) makes sustained beam application reward prolonged trigger holding more than burst firing.
