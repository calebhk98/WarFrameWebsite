## Overview

The Vasto is a semi-automatic revolver-styled secondary with high per-shot damage for its mastery tier. Its 90 total damage per shot -- 22.5 impact, 45 puncture, and 22.5 slash -- delivers reliable output against armored and unarmored targets alike. The puncture component provides meaningful damage against Grineer armor types, while the slash portion contributes bleed procs at low frequency.

At 20% critical chance and a 1.8x multiplier, the Vasto has sufficient critical scaling to reward Point Strike investment. Its 8% status chance is low, directing build focus squarely toward critical damage. The 6-round magazine and 1.0-second reload create a distinctive combat rhythm: six deliberate shots, then a quick reload before continuing. The 2.5 fire rate rewards players who prefer deliberate, precise shooting over spray tactics.

The Vasto is a competent secondary for players at Mastery Rank 4 who prefer careful target acquisition. It remains useful into mid-game content with appropriate mods.

## Acquisition

The Vasto blueprint is purchasable from the in-game Market for 30,000 credits. Build time is 12 hours. No quest unlock is required.

## Variants

**Vasto Prime** -- The Prime variant requires Mastery Rank 13 and offers substantially better critical chance, critical multiplier, and total damage. Vasto Prime is obtained through Void Relics and is periodically vaulted.

## Recommended builds

**Critical revolver build**: Equip Hornet Strike (base damage), Barrel Diffusion (multishot), Point Strike (critical chance), Pistol Gambit (critical chance), Lethal Torrent (fire rate + multishot), and Vital Sense (critical multiplier). With Point Strike at max rank, critical chance reaches near 60%. Add heat + cold (Blast) elemental combination to knock down lighter enemies.

**Hybrid precision**: Use Augur Pact (damage) and Expel faction mods for damage bonuses on priority targets. This intermediate build works without maxed rare mods and still meaningfully improves performance.

## Notes

The 1.0-second reload is one of the fastest available for a revolver-type secondary. Players who predict when approaching their final rounds can pre-emptively reload during movement between targets, maintaining near-constant firing availability.

Comparing the Vasto to the Lex reveals trade-offs: the Lex deals more damage per shot but fires more slowly; the Vasto offers better fire rate and faster reload at slightly lower per-shot damage. Both fill the precision semi-auto secondary role.


The Vasto's balanced physical damage distribution -- 50% puncture, 25% impact, 25% slash -- is useful in mixed-faction missions where enemies from multiple factions appear simultaneously (invasion missions, Railjack boarding, Crossfire missions). Without a single-faction physical damage overweighting, the Vasto avoids the significant penalties that impact-dominant or slash-dominant weapons receive against specific health types.

Players progressing from the base Vasto toward the Vasto Prime benefit from understanding the revolver's fire rhythm at the base level first. The deliberate semi-auto cadence and 6-round cycle are the same between versions; the Prime's improved stats reward the same playstyle with greater damage output.
