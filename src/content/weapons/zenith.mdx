## Overview

Zenith is a Corpus assault rifle with an alternate fire mode that transforms it into a semi-automatic scoped configuration with increased per-shot damage. This dual-mode design gives it unusual flexibility: full-auto primary fire sprays 90 rounds rapidly at close to medium range, while the alternate mode switches to a scoped, high-precision semi-auto configuration suited for longer-range targets. The ability to switch modes on the fly suits missions with varied engagement distances.

In full-auto mode, the 10.8 rounds per second fire rate and 90-round magazine allow sustained suppression of enemies at close range. The 34% status chance per bullet enables frequent proc application at this fire rate, with procs landing multiple times per second during prolonged bursts. Individual bullet damage is low at 30 per shot at base, but the high fire rate compensates for this through accumulated proc stacks and sustained damage.

Zenith's 10% critical chance and 2.0x multiplier make crit builds marginal without significant mod investment. Status builds take full advantage of the high fire rate and 34% status chance, applying multiple proc stacks per second. At moderate status levels, maintaining viral or corrosive procs across groups of enemies is straightforward. The alternate fire mode applies the same stat profile to each semi-auto shot, but fires at a much lower rate with a zoom advantage for distant targets.

The 90-round magazine means sustained fire without frequent reloads, and the 1.6-second reload is fast for a magazine of this size. Zenith suits players who want a versatile mid-game primary that handles both close-quarters crowd control and occasional long-range precision work.

## Acquisition

The Zenith blueprint is available from the in-game Market for 30,000 credits. Build time is 24 hours. The weapon requires standard mid-game crafting resources obtainable from Corpus and mid-tier planets.

## Variants

Zenith has no prime variant.

## Recommended builds

**Status-spray build:** Combine Infected Clip (Toxin) and Stormbringer (Electric) for Corrosive damage. At 34% base status chance with Rifle Aptitude added, status chance exceeds 50% per bullet. At 10.8 rounds per second, Corrosive stacks accumulate on targets within seconds, stripping armor rapidly. This build performs well in Steel Path Survival and Defense against armored Grineer units where sustained armor removal matters more than burst damage.

**Viral-slash build:** Equip Cryo Rounds (Cold) and Malignant Force (Toxin) for Viral. Viral's 200% health damage amplification applies after the first two procs, making subsequent bullets significantly more lethal against stripped health pools. Add Split Chamber for multishot, doubling effective fire rate and status proc frequency. This build excels against Corpus and Infested, which lack heavy armor and are vulnerable to Viral amplification.

The alternate fire mode benefits from mods that increase accuracy and per-shot damage, but most builds serve the full-auto mode since that is where the high fire rate and status application shine most effectively.

## Notes

- The alternate fire mode is toggled with the alternate-fire key and activates a scope. Switching modes mid-engagement allows adapting to sudden long-range targets without changing weapons.
- The accuracy value of 33.3 in full-auto mode reflects spray spread; the alternate fire mode improves accuracy significantly while scoped.
- Zenith's large magazine (90 rounds) means reload interruptions are infrequent during normal combat at the standard fire rate.
- Mod synergies with Vigilante Armaments (bonus multishot on sentinel kill) complement Zenith's spray playstyle if using a Carrier or Taxon sentinel.
- The 1.6-second reload is fast for a 90-round magazine, keeping downtime low between engagements and allowing players to maintain continuous fire on priority targets.
