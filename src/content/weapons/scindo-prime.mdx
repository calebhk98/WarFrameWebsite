## Overview

The Scindo Prime is the primed variant of the Scindo, a heavy two-handed axe built around punishing single-strike damage. The puncture-dominant damage profile at 200 puncture per hit is the most notable aspect of its stat card: this makes it unusually effective against Grineer heavy armor, where puncture damage deals bonus damage to Ferrite Armor. At 2.7 meters, the Scindo Prime extends slightly further than average for its archetype, adding crowd-clearing utility across its wide horizontal swing arcs.

The 26% critical chance and 2.4x critical multiplier form a competent critical build foundation without requiring heavy investment to reach reliable crit rates. The 24% status chance on a per-hit basis is similarly capable: one or two status-oriented mods push it into meaningful proc rates for elemental or slash conditions. The weapon supports both crit and status playstyles, with crit providing the higher damage ceiling.

Attack speed at 0.967 is on the slower side for the axe category, which is expected given the two-handed swing mechanics. Heavy attack builds can leverage the Scindo Prime's high per-hit damage further, since multiplied impact through charged attacks scales the already-high puncture values dramatically.

## Acquisition

Scindo Prime is currently vaulted, meaning its component relics are not in the active drop pool. Players can obtain it by trading with other players through [warframe.market](https://warframe.market/items/scindo_prime_set) or by waiting for a Prime Vault unvaulting event. When unvaulted, components appear in Axi and Neo era relics. The Scindo Prime was originally released alongside Valkyr Prime.

## Variants

The base [Scindo](/weapons/scindo) shares the axe category at lower stats (15% crit chance, 2.0x multiplier) and mastery rank 0, making it a freely accessible early-game alternative. No Kuva or Tenet variant exists for the Scindo family.

## Recommended builds

**Puncture anti-armor build.** The 200 base puncture synergizes directly with Primed Fever Strike (or Fever Strike) and Primed Bane of Grineer. Puncture procs reduce enemy damage output, adding a defensive layer on top of the raw damage advantage against Ferrite Armor. Volcanic Edge and Vicious Spread complete a corrosive build to strip armor layers before puncture damage is applied.

**Critical heavy-attack build.** Blood Rush in combination with the Scindo Prime's 2.4x multiplier creates an escalating crit build through combo stacking. Heavy attacks reset the combo counter but deal amplified base damage; players who prioritize heavy attacks use Sacrificial Steel and Organ Shatter to maximize per-heavy-strike output without relying on combo counter scaling.

**Condition Overload status build.** Pairing three elemental mods (Corrosive + Heat or Viral + Heat) with Condition Overload turns each accumulated status proc into a compounding damage multiplier. The Scindo Prime's moderate attack speed means Condition Overload stacks accumulate across successive enemy encounters rather than within a single rapid combo.

## Notes

- The vaulted status means acquiring a Scindo Prime requires either market trading or waiting for an unvault announcement.
- Stance mods for the axe category include Cleaving Whirlwind, which improves the reach of horizontal sweeps and adds spin-attack combos particularly effective against grouped enemies.
- Mastery rank 8 is a reasonable mid-game threshold; players at that point typically have access to sufficient mods to build the weapon effectively.
- The Scindo Prime carries a riven disposition that supports meaningful improvement through good riven rolls, though the weapon is capable without one.
