## Overview

The Tysis is an Infested-bioengineered secondary weapon that fires needle-like projectiles with an exceptional 50% base status chance and built-in corrosive elemental damage. The combination of 27 innate corrosive damage with a 50% status chance makes the Tysis one of the most reliable status-proc secondaries available at Mastery Rank 9. Each shot has a near-certain chance of applying a status proc, and with corrosive already built in, armor stripping begins immediately without elemental mods consuming slots.

The Tysis's 76 total damage per shot (9 impact, 17 puncture, 23 slash, 27 corrosive) is modest but sufficient for a utility-focused secondary. The 3% critical chance makes it unsuitable for critical builds; this is purely a status weapon. Its 1.2-second reload and 11-round magazine create small pauses, but at 2.5 fire rate those 11 rounds take roughly 4.4 seconds.

The Tysis functions best as a debuffing tool -- priming enemies with corrosive stacks while a primary weapon provides bulk damage. With six corrosive procs, armor is fully stripped and the primary weapon's damage scales dramatically.

## Acquisition

The Tysis blueprint is purchasable from the in-game Market for 30,000 credits. Build time is 24 hours. No quest unlock is required.

## Variants

No Vandal, Wraith, or Prime variant exists for the Tysis. It is a standalone Infested-themed weapon without upgrade counterparts in the current WFCD dataset.

## Recommended builds

**Corrosive stack support**: The Tysis naturally excels with its built-in corrosive. Add Barrel Diffusion (multishot) and Lethal Torrent (fire rate + multishot) to increase proc frequency. Pistol Pestilence adds additional toxin for Gas, allowing the Tysis to prime multiple status types simultaneously.

**Pure utility debuffer**: Use the Tysis specifically to strip armor from priority targets (heavy units, boss phases) before switching to the primary weapon. Magazine Warp improves magazine capacity. Target Cracker adds critical chance, though its value is marginal given the 3% base.

**Viral supplement**: Modify elemental combination to achieve Viral (cold + toxin) to halve enemy max health while retaining the slash component that delivers bleeds. This combination synergizes with the slash-heavy base damage.

## Notes

The Tysis projectiles have slight arc trajectory at longer ranges. At close to medium range this is negligible, but players must adjust aim at distance. Condition Overload on a paired melee weapon with the Tysis applying corrosive and slash creates a strong two-weapon synergy.


The Tysis's 100% accuracy is notable for a secondary weapon -- the needle projectile travels true to the aimed point at all ranges without spread or bloom. This accuracy combined with the 50% status chance means every carefully aimed shot at a distant target still delivers its proc. Unlike shotguns or spread-fire secondaries, the Tysis can serve as a precise debuffing tool at ranges where pellet spread would otherwise reduce the aggregate status output.

Players who use the Tysis as a primer weapon pair it with primary or melee weapons that deal large amounts of burst damage to corrosive-stripped targets. The Tysis's six corrosive procs to full armor strip are achievable within 4-5 shots at the base 50% status chance, after which switching to the primary weapon or melee delivers full damage against the exposed health pool.
