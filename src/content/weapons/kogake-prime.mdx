## Overview

Kogake Prime is the Prime variant of the Kogake sparring-type melee weapon. Sparring weapons deliver rapid unarmed-style strikes optimized for high combo counter buildup and status proc application. The Prime version improves the base Kogake's crit multiplier to 1.8x and substantially raises status chance to 38%, enabling reliable elemental proc builds. The impact-heavy damage distribution makes Kogake Prime effective against shielded Corpus enemies, where impact damage is amplified against shield pools.

The 38% status chance is the defining stat. At this base rate, applying Weeping Wounds through combo scaling pushes effective status chance above 100% at moderate combo counts, guaranteeing multiple elemental procs per strike. Sparring weapons like Kogake Prime execute fast multi-hit attack strings, which accelerates both combo counter buildup and the frequency of status proc triggers.

The short 1.25 m range is the primary trade-off of the sparring category. Kogake Prime requires close engagement and paired survivability from the equipped warframe to function optimally, but within that close range it applies status effects at high velocity.

The impact-dominant damage split (169.4 impact out of 242 total) performs best against shields. Corpus shielded units take increased damage from impact, and the Kogake Prime's heavy impact weighting means each hit strips significant shield capacity. Once shields are removed, the residual puncture and slash components continue to deal damage to exposed health pools.

The 38% base status chance is high enough that two combined elemental mods reliably produce status procs per strike even before Weeping Wounds scales the rate further. At maximum Weeping Wounds scaling, nearly every strike applies one or more elemental effects.

## Acquisition

Kogake Prime components -- Blueprint, Gauntlet, and Boot -- are obtained from Void Relics tied to the Kogake Prime Access rotation. When in active Prime Access, relics appear in the standard drop table. In vault periods, components are obtained through unvault events or by trading with other players via the Trading Post.

The base Kogake is available from the in-game Market for credits and uses the same sparring framework at lower stat values.

## Variants

The base Kogake is a Market-accessible sparring weapon with lower status chance (25%) and reduced critical multiplier (1.5x). Kogake Prime improves both figures substantially, making the Prime version the preferred choice for status-focused melee builds. No Kuva, Tenet, Wraith, or Vandal variant of the Kogake exists.

## Recommended builds

A status-focused build centers on Primed Fever Strike and Virulent Scourge for Viral damage, combined with Weeping Wounds and Condition Overload. At moderate combo counts, Weeping Wounds pushes effective status chance well past 100%, enabling consistent double-proc application per strike and maximizing Condition Overload's damage multiplier. Primed Reach extends the 1.25 m range for improved multi-target coverage in grouped enemy encounters.

A crit-status hybrid adds Blood Rush alongside the status core to build crit chance at high combo. The 16% base critical chance is modest, but Blood Rush's multiplicative scaling delivers usable crit rates at sustained combo counts. The 1.8x multiplier provides meaningful burst damage when crits trigger.

For Steel Path missions, Primed Pressure Point as the base damage amplifier combined with Viral procs and Condition Overload creates a reliable damage loop against high-health enemies regardless of faction.

## Notes

Sparring weapons use the sparring stance system, with Grim Fury and Brutal Tide as the primary stance options for Kogake Prime. Grim Fury provides a high-hit-count combo that maximizes both combo counter buildup and status proc opportunities per second. The fast multi-hit nature of sparring combos means Weeping Wounds reaches effective proc rates much earlier in the combo counter progression than on slower weapon types.

The 1.25 m range requires the player to be adjacent to enemies. Frames with reliable survivability, crowd control, or invisibility allow Kogake Prime to operate safely at close range. Frames like Ash, Loki, or Inaros are natural pairings for close-quarters sparring weapon playstyles.
