## Overview

Kogake Prime is the Prime variant of the Kogake sparring-type melee weapon. As a sparring weapon, it executes rapid unarmed strikes optimized for combo buildup and status proc application. The Prime version improves the base Kogake's crit multiplier to 1.8x and substantially raises status chance to 38%, enabling reliable elemental proc builds. The impact-heavy damage distribution makes Kogake Prime effective against shielded Corpus enemies, and its high status chance creates excellent synergy with Weeping Wounds for scaling status in extended missions.

Weeping Wounds scales status chance multiplicatively with the combo counter, making Kogake Prime a strong choice for status-stacking melee builds.

## Acquisition

Kogake Prime components are obtained from Void Relics associated with its Prime Access or unvault period. Check the current relic availability; the weapon vaults when its associated Prime is retired from the standard reward table. The base Kogake is available from the Market for Credits.

## Variants

The base Kogake is a starter-accessible sparring weapon available from the Market. Kogake Prime improves crit multiplier from 1.5x to 1.8x and raises status chance from 25% to 38%, making it a meaningful upgrade for endgame status builds.

## Recommended builds

A status-focused build for Kogake Prime centers on Primed Fever Strike and Virulent Scourge for Viral, combined with Weeping Wounds and Condition Overload for cascading damage scaling. At high combo, Weeping Wounds pushes status chance far beyond 100%, enabling consistent multi-proc application per strike.

A crit-status hybrid using Blood Rush alongside two elemental mods produces strong performance in Steel Path by combining Viral procs with crit-scaling damage. Primed Reach extends the weapon's short 1.25 m range for improved multi-target coverage.

## Notes

Sparring weapons like Kogake Prime execute fast multi-hit attacks, which accelerates combo counter buildup and makes scaling mods like Blood Rush and Weeping Wounds more effective than on slower weapons. The Kogake Prime's relatively modest range of 1.25 m requires close engagement to connect hits, so pairing with a frame that has reliable survivability or crowd-control is recommended.
