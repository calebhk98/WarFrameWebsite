## Overview

The Supra is a minigun-style weapon with exceptional fire rate and large magazine capacity. Its sustained damage output makes it effective for suppression and crowd control, though accuracy suffers at range without mods. The weapon's 4 impact, 6 puncture, and 30 slash damage provides solid distributed output against most enemy types. Despite modest individual damage components, the high fire rate translates to substantial total damage output.

With a 12.5 fire rate, the Supra represents one of the highest fire-rate primary weapons. The 180 round magazine enables extended suppression without reloading, a tactical advantage when holding positions or clearing high-density enemy areas. The 3.0 reload time is reasonable given the large magazine, providing balance between firepower and reload burden. Players can engage multiple targets without ammunition pressure, enabling confident trigger discipline.

The Supra's 30% status chance supports status-focused builds with elemental mods. Many players prefer status builds using viral and heat to crowd-control and weaken enemies. The weapon benefits from mods that improve accuracy or reduce recoil, making it more effective at range where status applications prove valuable. Accuracy improvements prove particularly valuable for the Supra given its modest per-shot damage.

This weapon excels in mid-level mission content and provides excellent support in team-based missions where suppressive fire proves valuable. The Supra's accessibility and straightforward mod path make it popular among players seeking sustained-damage weapons. Veterans appreciate its effectiveness in specific mission types where ammunition quantity and sustained fire matter more than precision.

The weapon's high fire rate enables rapid status proc application through status-focused mod builds. Environmental hazards created through status effects multiply the weapon's effectiveness despite modest individual damage. Some players combine the Supra with warframe abilities that generate ammunition or provide fire-rate buffs.

Forma investment transforms the Supra into a respectable mid-game platform.
