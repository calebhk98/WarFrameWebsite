## Overview

The Supra is a Corpus-manufactured minigun chambered in a high-capacity rotating barrel configuration. It rewards sustained fire over precision: the 12.5 round-per-second fire rate and 180-round magazine let it run continuously for nearly 15 seconds before reload. Individual shot damage is modest, with slash as the dominant type at 30 per projectile, but volume of fire compensates for per-shot weakness against dense enemy groups.

Accuracy is deliberately limited at 14.3, reflecting the minigun's wide spray pattern. At medium and close range this rarely matters in practice, but long-range precision shooting demands accuracy mods or careful burst management. The 30% status chance is the weapon's most notable combat stat -- it applies elemental procs reliably even at high fire rate, making the Supra a natural platform for status-focused elemental builds.

The Supra Vandal, an event-exclusive variant, improves on the base model across every statistic. The standard Supra remains a valid mid-game option and an accessible path to understanding sustained-fire primary playstyles.

## Acquisition

The Supra blueprint is purchasable from the in-game Market for credits. Components require standard planetary resources available from mid-game star chart nodes. Build time is 24 hours.

## Variants

The [Supra Vandal](/weapons/supra-vandal) was distributed through The Jordas Verdict trial and intermittent alerts. It carries substantially higher critical chance and improved accuracy alongside additional damage. Players without access to the Vandal should treat the standard Supra as an interim weapon.

## Recommended builds

**Viral slash build.** Combining Cold and Toxin elemental mods produces Viral. Viral halves effective enemy health while slash procs deal true damage over time, bypassing armor. The 30% status chance reaches reliable proc rates with Thermite Rounds and Infected Clip even at high fire rate, making this the most broadly effective damage path.

**Heat crowd-control build.** Replacing one Viral component with Heat yields a weapon that continuously applies the stagger-and-DoT from burning. Heat status stacks its damage bonus multiplicatively with each successive proc, rewarding prolonged target engagement. This approach works particularly well in enclosed mission environments.

**Accuracy correction build.** For players wanting effective range, Rifle Aptitude and Stabilizer add status chance and reduce spread respectively. This converts the Supra into a more controlled platform that applies status at distance without wasting ammunition on missed shots.

## Notes

- Spin-up delay is minimal compared to other minigun-type weapons; the Supra reaches full fire rate within the first few shots.
- The large magazine makes it unsuitable for precise ammo conservation but ideal for holding a position during defense missions.
- Mastery rank 12 restricts this to players with some progression; the Soma or Karak cover the same fire-rate niche at lower rank requirements.
- Equipping a Speed Trigger or Arcane Acceleration pushes the fire rate above 15 rounds per second, maximizing status proc accumulation speed.
