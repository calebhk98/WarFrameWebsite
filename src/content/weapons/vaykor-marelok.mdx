## Overview

The Vaykor Marelok is the Steel Meridian-enhanced version of the Marelok pistol, available at Mastery Rank 10. Its 160 total damage per shot divides into 96 impact, 48 puncture, and 16 slash -- impact-dominant. The heavy impact weighting deals bonus damage to Corpus shields, which are highly vulnerable to impact. At Mastery Rank 10 with a steel Meridian standing requirement, the Vaykor Marelok is a mid-game secondary with strong Corpus-faction targeting.

The 35% status chance is the Vaykor Marelok's defining stat. At 2.0 fire rate with 35% status per shot, elemental procs apply consistently. The impact component procs Knockback, and the high status chance produces mixed physical plus elemental procs per shot. The 20% critical chance and 1.5x multiplier are below effective crit thresholds; this is a status platform, not a crit weapon.

The 10-round magazine at 2.0 fire rate and 1.67-second reload supports methodical, deliberate shooting. At accuracy 10, the Vaykor Marelok is a close-range sidearm -- spread reduces effective damage at medium and long range.

## Acquisition

The Vaykor Marelok requires Steel Meridian Syndicate rank and standing. Purchase directly from the Steel Meridian vendor at any Relay without crafting.

## Recommended builds

**Magnetic Corpus**: Cold plus electric for Magnetic elemental. Magnetic deals 75% bonus damage to Corpus shields. The Vaykor Marelok's 96 impact base combined with Magnetic against shields creates rapid shield depletion. The 35% status applies Magnetic procs that reduce enemy shield capacity further.

**Viral status**: Cold plus toxin for Viral. With 35% status and 2.0 fire rate, Viral applies on average every three shots. Viral halves max health for the remainder of the engagement. Follow up with the weapon's impact damage against stripped health pools.

**Radiation Corpus tech**: Heat plus electric for Radiation. Radiation deals bonus damage to Alloy Armor and confuses enemies. Confused enemies attack each other, providing indirect crowd control. At 35% status, Radiation confusion procs apply reliably.

## Notes

The 35% status chance without any status-enhancing mods is high for a secondary. Players do not need Stunning Speed or status-specific mods to achieve reliable proc application -- the base stat handles consistent proc generation. Invest mod slots in elemental damage mods rather than status chance mods. The Steel Meridian Justice passive procs on kills, providing healing and area blasts during Syndicate missions.


The Steel Meridian's Justice Syndicate passive procs on kills, producing an area blast and providing healing. The Vaykor Marelok's 35% status chance means kills accomplished against status-affected enemies trigger the Justice proc more consistently, as status procs often accelerate kill timing. In Steel Meridian Syndicate missions where the Justice bonus is active, the Marelok's aggressive close-range status output supports self-sustain through the healing component of each Justice proc.
