## Overview

The [Cernos](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Cernos) is a Tenno bow with exceptional critical chance and high per-shot impact damage. With 380 total base damage heavily weighted toward Impact (342), the Cernos delivers crushing force against enemies susceptible to Impact procs. Its 36% critical chance is among the highest base values in the bow category, enabling reliable orange-critical hits without heavy mod investment in critical chance.

The bow mechanic requires holding the trigger to draw and releasing to fire. The Cernos fires a single arrow per shot with a 0.6-second nock time. This single-shot rhythm rewards players who time their shots between other actions -- bullet jump, aim, release -- rather than sustained firing. The Cernos is suited to players comfortable with the Warframe sniper/bow playstyle of high-damage individual shots over automatic fire.

At Mastery Rank 6, the Cernos is accessible in mid-early progression and serves as a strong bridge toward the Cernos Prime for players who develop a preference for bows.

The Impact-dominant damage profile is most effective against enemy shields and unarmored flesh.

## Acquisition

The Cernos blueprint is available from the in-game Market for 25,000 credits. Crafting requires standard mid-tier materials: Nano Spores, Rubedo, Polymer Bundle, and Circuits (quantities per WFCD component data). Build time is 12 hours. The built weapon is not tradeable.

No quest is required. The Market blueprint is immediately accessible once credits are available.

## Variants

**Cernos Prime** -- The Prime variant has 35% critical chance, 2.0x critical multiplier, 30% status chance, and notably higher slash damage. Cernos Prime is obtained through Void Relics and is vaulted periodically. It applies a triple-arrow burst fire pattern distinct from the base Cernos single-shot.

**Mutalist Cernos** -- An Infested variant firing toxic projectiles that leave a cloud on impact. Available through a Research blueprint from an Infested Clan Lab; requires Infested research investment.

**Rakta Cernos** -- A Red Veil syndicate variant with enhanced critical and unique aura proc. Obtained through Red Veil standing at the Flawed rank threshold.

## Recommended builds

**Critical headshot build**: Prioritize Point Strike (critical chance), Vital Sense (critical damage), and Primed Shred or natural talent for draw speed. At 36% base critical chance, Point Strike ensures frequent orange crits. Combine with a hunter munitions-style setup once crits are reliable for Slash proc delivery.

**Impact-Magnetic build**: Pair base damage mods (Serration, Split Chamber with multishot) with Magnetic damage (Frost + Electric) to amplify shield damage. Impact procs stagger enemies momentarily, providing brief crowd control. This build excels against Corpus shielded units.

**Status hybrid**: Use elemental mods alongside status chance boosters. At 18% base status, two elemental mods and a status multiplier can reach reasonable status rates for proc application. Heat or Corrosive combinations complement impact damage against armored targets.

## Notes

The Cernos benefits from all Sniper Rifle mods that apply to bows in Warframe. Arrow mutations (Primed Arrow Mutation) address ammo economy for extended missions. The 0.6-second nock time is fast for a bow and allows aggressive shot cadence with practice. Headshot bonuses apply fully; precision aiming significantly increases per-shot output beyond the listed damage values.
