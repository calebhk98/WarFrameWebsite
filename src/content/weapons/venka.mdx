## Overview

The Venka is a claw-type melee weapon with a puncture-dominant damage profile. Its 140 total damage per hit splits into 7 impact, 98 puncture, and 35 slash. The extreme puncture weighting mirrors other claw-class weapons designed for anti-armor effectiveness, targeting the ferrite and alloy armor types worn by Grineer and heavy Infested units.

Claw weapons have a distinctive rapid, multi-hit combo attack pattern. Individual hits in a claw combo deal fraction-of-base damage, but the combo string delivers many hits per second. The per-attack 15% critical chance and 15% status chance accumulate across all hits in a combo string, making effective proc rates per second higher than the listed values suggest.

At Mastery Rank 4, the Venka is accessible early in progression. Its 1.75-meter range is shorter than polearms or greatswords, reflecting the claw category's close-quarters nature. Players must close to melee distance consistently, but the combo speed compensates for the positioning requirement.

## Acquisition

The Venka blueprint is purchasable from the in-game Market for 20,000 credits. Build time is 12 hours. No quest or mission unlock is required.

## Variants

**Venka Prime** -- The Prime version offers substantially improved critical chance, critical multiplier, and damage. It drops from Void Relics and requires a higher Mastery Rank.

## Recommended builds

**Crit-corrosive build**: Equip Primed Pressure Point, Blood Rush (critical chance on combo), Organ Shatter (critical multiplier), and corrosive mods (electric + toxin). The high puncture base works well with corrosive as corrosive reduces the armor that would otherwise reduce puncture damage. At x2 combo with Blood Rush, critical chance surpasses 45%.

**Viral-slash approach**: Use Weeping Wounds, cold + toxin for Viral, and Hunter Munitions for slash proc generation from crits. Viral halves enemy max health; slash bleeds bypass armor.

**Beginner build**: Pressure Point, Fury (attack speed), Fever Strike (toxin), and North Wind (cold) for Viral. This four-mod approach delivers significant improvement over unmodded performance without requiring rare mods.

## Notes

Claw stance mods shape the Venka's combo string significantly. The specific combo inputs and timing of multi-hit bursts affect proc density per second. The 1.75-meter range requires closing to melee distance via bullet jumping or warframe abilities.


Claw-category weapons like the Venka feature distinctive forward-dash attacks in many of their stance combos, which close the gap to distant enemies quickly. This reduces the positional disadvantage of the 1.75-meter range compared to longer melee categories. Players who use bullet jump to close distance and then execute a claw forward-dash attack chain can engage enemies at medium range effectively.

The Venka's accessibility at Mastery Rank 4 makes it an early-game stepping stone toward the Venka Prime at MR 14. Players who invest in building a strong claw foundation on the base Venka develop the positioning instincts and combo timing that translate directly to the Venka Prime's more powerful crit output.
