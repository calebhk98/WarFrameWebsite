## Overview

The Tenet Ferrox is a Sisters of Parvos Tenet primary weapon requiring Mastery Rank 16. Its 200 total damage per shot is slash-dominant: 140 slash, 40 puncture, and 20 impact. The slash weighting produces consistent bleed procs that bypass armor and tick true damage -- one of the most reliable damage sources in high-level content. The 26% status chance supports proc application across the 2.67 fire rate.

The 34% critical chance and 3.0x critical multiplier make the Tenet Ferrox a powerful critical weapon. A 3x multiplier is high by primary standards; crits deal triple base damage, and with Point Strike, critical chance reaches near 100%. The 20-round magazine is small for an auto-fire primary at 2.67 fire rate, lasting roughly 7.5 seconds of sustained fire before the fast 1.8-second reload. The Ferrox is best used in controlled bursts.

Like all Tenet weapons, the Ferrox receives a unique elemental bonus from the Sister whose connection was severed. This bonus adds extra elemental damage on top of the base stats, making each Ferrox a somewhat distinct weapon based on its element.

## Acquisition

Obtained through the Sisters of Parvos system at Mastery Rank 16. Trigger a Sister Candidate by completing Granum Void encounters on Corpus-controlled nodes. Track and vanquish the resulting Sister to claim her Tenet Ferrox. The elemental bonus type and magnitude are fixed at Sister creation.

## Variants

The Tenet Ferrox has no base counterpart or Prime version.

## Recommended builds

**Crit slash endgame**: Serration, Split Chamber, Point Strike, Vital Sense, Hunter Munitions, and corrosive or viral plus heat elemental mods. Point Strike at 34% base crit achieves near-100% crit rate. Vital Sense amplifies the 3.0x multiplier further. Hunter Munitions converts crits to slash procs, stacking bleed damage that ignores armor scaling.

**Viral-slash**: Cold plus toxin for Viral, Split Chamber, Serration, Point Strike, Hunter Munitions. Viral halves enemy max health. Slash bleeds from both the base damage and Hunter Munitions procs deal amplified true damage against halved health pools.

**Status primary**: Four 60/60 dual-stat mods for 26% status base with additional elemental layers. The 2.67 fire rate with 26% status produces frequent procs for Condition Overload or Galvanized Shot stacking.

## Notes

The 20-round magazine requires discipline at the 2.67 fire rate -- full-auto fire empties the magazine in 7-8 seconds. The fast 1.8-second reload mitigates this. Accuracy at 16.7 is low for a rifle; close-to-medium range is the Ferrox's natural engagement zone where spread does not impair target acquisition.
