## Overview

The Epitaph is an Incarnon secondary pistol with an emphasis on critical damage and radiation procs. The weapon boasts the highest critical multiplier of its tier at 2.2x, enabling powerful crit-focused builds. Inherent radiation damage adds utility against mechanical enemies and sets up confusion procs. As an Incarnon weapon, it evolves through use to unlock additional mechanics and visual upgrades. The low mastery requirement of 3 makes it accessible to virtually all players, while its performance scales into endgame with proper modding and forma investment.

## Damage

The Epitaph's physical damage favors impact and slash with balanced puncture contribution. The 1.5 radiation damage, while modest, triggers confusion procs that temporarily turn enemies against each other. This utility effect scales in value as enemy levels increase. The 21% critical chance paired with 2.2x multiplier makes the weapon crit-viable without external critical chance boosting. Builds leveraging Organ Shatter and crit-damage mods can achieve significant per-shot values. Status chance of 19% suggests the weapon prioritizes critical scaling over proc stacking.

## Acquisition

The Epitaph blueprint is available from the Star Chart market for 25,000 credits and 165 platinum. Building requires 12 hours of foundry time and another 25,000 credits. This market accessibility allows players to obtain the weapon early in progression. Incarnon evolution requires farming specific void fissure objectives, extending the weapon's value beyond initial acquisition.

## Variants

Epitaph Prime exists as an upgraded variant with higher base damage and critical stats. The prime variant is currently vaulted but available through relic farming during void fissure events. No other weapon variants exist.

## Recommended builds

Epitaph builds prioritize critical damage enhancement given the weapon's 2.2x multiplier. Hornet Strike provides essential base damage. Pistol Gambit and Trigger Discipline increase critical chance, approaching the crit threshold for reliable headshot kills. Organ Shatter amplifies critical multiplier beyond the base 2.2x. Crit-focused builds leverage Body Count and Berserker Status for scaling. Radiation damage mods add utility; Heated Charge adds fire proc on top of radiation for enhanced status flexibility. The weapon shines in critical builds paired with headshot discipline, rewarding accurate play with explosive damage output.

## Notes

Accuracy of 14% requires aimed fire for consistent application at range. Hip-fire spreads bullets, making optimal builds emphasize aimed engagement. The weapon transitions from general-use secondary to specialized tool depending on build investment. Incarnon evolution unlocks mechanical upgrades rewarding sustained play. The higher magazine capacity of 45 exceeds many secondaries, reducing reload frequency during engagement. As a market weapon, acquisition is trivial, making Epitaph an excellent early-game secondary choice. The weapon's radiation damage provides utility in factions vulnerable to confusion procs, particularly effective in Grineer-heavy missions where disorienting enemies turns the tide of engagement.

## Critical focus philosophy

Unlike status-focused secondary weapons, Epitaph rewards accurate shooting and critical positioning. Players who practice headshots and target priority benefit exponentially from the weapon's critical scaling. The learning curve transitions new players from spray patterns to tactical accuracy-focused gameplay, building foundational skills applicable to all crit-heavy weapons. The high critical multiplier of 2.2x encourages disciplined aim and careful target selection rather than spray-and-pray approaches that waste ammunition.
