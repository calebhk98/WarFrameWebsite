## Overview

Boltor Prime is a projectile-based rifle prized for its balance between hitscan accuracy and impactful bolts. Unlike beam weapons, Boltor Prime fires physical projectiles that travel to targets, allowing it to damage enemies around obstacles while maintaining clear line-of-sight advantages. The weapon emphasizes slash damage, making it particularly effective against unarmored and lightly-armored enemies like Corpus units and Infested. The 28 percent critical chance and 2.8x multiplier reward accurate aiming, especially against weakpoints. Boltor Prime's relatively high fire rate at 9.17 rounds per second combined with its instant reload speed creates excellent tactical pacing, allowing players to maintain sustained fire with minimal downtime. The weapon remains highly effective throughout the star chart and retains viability in higher-difficulty content through careful modding and elemental build selection.

The projectile nature of Boltor Prime creates interesting tactical opportunities unavailable to hitscan weapons. Players can predict enemy positions and bank shots around cover, especially in tightly confined environments. The 1.8-second reload time is among the fastest in the rifle category, enabling reactive playstyle without ammunition anxiety. The slash-heavy damage profile lends itself naturally to viral builds, where adding toxin mods amplifies the slash damage through synergistic scaling. This specialization positions Boltor Prime as an excellent complement to frames focusing on crowd control or status effects, as the weapon's consistent application of status conditions amplifies team effectiveness.

## Acquisition

Boltor Prime components distribute across multiple void fissure relic sets, with primary drops in Axi A5 and Meso S7 relics. The weapon's long history in the game (originally released in 2013) means it appears frequently in community void fissure squads. Component trading through the marketplace provides rapid acquisition for impatient players, though the farm difficulty remains minimal compared to newer prime weapons. The 12-hour build time allows queue-management alongside other crafts.

## Variants

The original Boltor shares identical mechanics with lower damage output. Kuva Boltor offers elemental customization but sacrifices the prime's superior statistics. Boltor Prime represents the optimal accessibility-to-power ratio, making it the recommended variant for the majority of playstyles. The weapon's design lineage extends to the Projectile archetype, separating it from hitscan beams and slug-based shotguns.

## Recommended builds

Serration provides foundational damage, while Point Strike and Vital Sense maximize critical contribution. Slash mod enhancements like Organ Shatter or Piercing Strikes dramatically amplify the weapon's natural slash emphasis. For viral builds, adding Cold and Toxin mods through Cryo Rounds and Pathogen Rounds creates a multiplicative damage bonus against all targets. Fire rate mods like Lethal Momentum add minor value; focus on damage and critical investment instead. Magazine extension mods are optional given the 60-round capacity and rapid reload, but Primed Mag Capacity benefits extreme endurance missions. The weapon's low status chance means building toward guaranteed criticals rather than status application, as the base 6 percent status requires extreme modding to reach relevance.

## Notes

Boltor Prime's projectile nature introduces a learning curve compared to hitscan weapons, requiring lead-time adjustment at range. This minor inconvenience is offset by the tactical advantages of banking shots and predicting high-priority targets. The slash-heavy damage makes it particularly valuable against Corpus Crewmen and Infested units, though Grineer armor reduces effectiveness unless combining with mods or status builds. The weapon's speed and reload consistency enable reactive playstyle, making it excellent for players who value responsive combat mechanics. Its continued viability through endgame content validates the weapon's fundamental design despite the prevalence of more specialized alternatives.
