## Overview

Soma is a primary rifle weapon that serves as high fire rate automatic rifle with one of the highest critical multipliers in the rifle class; a benchmark crit rifle for mid-game players that scales well with critical damage mods. Mastery Rank 6 is required to equip the weapon. Players who invest in building it properly will find it reliable across the star chart, with scaling potential on the Steel Path when supported by appropriate mods and Warframe ability synergies.

The primary slot is competitive in Warframe, and Soma occupies a distinct position within it. Understanding its damage profile -- the distribution across physical damage types and any elemental components -- is the first step toward optimizing it for specific mission types and enemy factions.

## Stats

Per WFCD warframe-items data, Soma deals 1.2 impact, 6.0 puncture, 4.8 slash per bullet per attack. The critical chance of 30% and critical multiplier of 3.0x define its critical strike ceiling. Status chance of 7% determines how reliably it applies elemental or physical status effects per attack. Fire rate is 15.0 rps.

These numbers establish the weapon's identity. High status chance weapons benefit from status-stacking mods and condition overload synergies. High critical chance weapons respond well to critical damage multiplier mods. Balancing both directions through dual stat mods -- which add elemental damage and status chance simultaneously -- is the standard approach for intermediate and advanced builds.

## Recommended builds

Build direction for Soma depends on its stat profile. Status-oriented builds stack the Primed variant of elemental mods alongside dual stat mods (such as Infected Clip for toxin plus status, or Rime Rounds for cold plus status) to maximize proc frequency. Critical builds layer critical chance and damage multiplier mods to amplify each hit that lands as a critical strike.

For primary weapons, the standard mod capacity baseline after zero forma accommodates roughly four to five max-rank mods. Most competitive loadouts require at least two forma to fit all desired mods without capacity overflow. Specific forma placement should target the polarities of the highest-capacity mods in the planned loadout.

Pair Soma with Warframe abilities that amplify damage output. Rhino's Roar, Wisp's Haste Mote, and Mirage's Eclipse are faction-agnostic multipliers that scale every weapon's damage. Frames that generate energy pickups -- such as Trinity or a Zenurik-focused Operator -- enable sustained use of energy-expensive buffing abilities during long missions.
