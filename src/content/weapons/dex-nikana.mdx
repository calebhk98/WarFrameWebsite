## Overview

The Dex Nikana is a Dex-series anniversary Nikana with a Puncture-dominant damage profile: 126 Puncture, 25.2 Slash, and 16.8 Impact. Dex weapons are distributed annually by Digital Extremes as a community anniversary gift, typically in March. The 24% critical chance and 2.6x critical multiplier make the Dex Nikana one of the stronger crit platforms in the Nikana weapon family, comparable to the Dragon Nikana in critical chance but with a notably higher 2.6x multiplier. The 18% status chance supports supplementary elemental proc application. Attack speed of 1.0 and 2.5 m range are standard for the Nikana class. Mastery Rank 8 is required.

Nikana weapons use dedicated Nikana stance mods (Decisive Judgement, Tranquil Cleave, Blind Justice) that deliver flowing, step-based combos. Blind Justice in particular provides efficient finisher-setup opportunities and rapid hit chains that interact well with Blood Rush scaling. The 2.6x multiplier significantly amplifies orange-crit hits when Blood Rush pushes the effective crit chance above the orange-crit threshold at higher combo levels.

## Acquisition

The Dex Nikana is a Warframe anniversary gift distributed annually by Digital Extremes. Log in during the anniversary event period (typically March) to claim the Dex Nikana and other Dex weapons. Check [the Dex Nikana Fandom wiki page](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Dex_Nikana) for the current anniversary date and distribution schedule.

## Variants

The Dex Nikana is an anniversary variant without a Prime or Kuva version. The Nikana Prime is the Prime option for the Nikana family, available through Void Relics. The Dragon Nikana is a crafted upgrade to the base Nikana with a different stat profile. The Dex Nikana sits between these as an event-exclusive option with competitive stats.

## Recommended builds

**Blood Rush crit build.** The 24% crit and 2.6x multiplier are the Dex Nikana's defining strengths. Equip Blood Rush, Organ Shatter, Sacrificial Steel, and two Viral 60/60 mods. At x4 combo, orange crits deliver substantial per-hit damage amplified by 2.6x. Berserker Fury accelerates attack speed after kills.

**Blind Justice finisher build.** Equip Blind Justice stance and use the forward-dash heavy attack sequence to position behind enemies for finisher kills. Combine with Primed Pressure Point, Blood Rush, and a Covert Lethality mod (if dagger-applicable) equivalent for heavy-attack damage on finisher-eligible targets.

**Hybrid crit/status build.** The 18% status supports Weeping Wounds investment. Equip Blood Rush and Weeping Wounds simultaneously; at high combo both stats scale up. Add Corrosive 60/60 mods for armor strip against Grineer targets. Condition Overload amplifies per-hit output as proc types accumulate.

## Synergies

Naramon's Power Spike extends combo duration for sustained Blood Rush scaling. Ash's stealth passive allows finisher-multiplier hits on every target during Blade Storm, synergizing with Nikana finisher-setup combos. Frames that strip armor (Corrosive Projection aura, Wisp) improve the Puncture damage efficiency against Grineer.
