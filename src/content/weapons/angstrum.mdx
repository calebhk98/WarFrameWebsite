## Overview

The Angstrum is a Mastery Rank 4 secondary rocket launcher that fires explosive rockets dealing 450 Blast damage per shot. At 16% Critical Chance with a 2.0x multiplier and 22% Status Chance, the Angstrum sits in a hybrid territory where both critical and status builds are viable. Rockets detonate on impact with surfaces and enemies, dealing area-of-effect damage in a radius around the detonation point.

The Angstrum is one of the earliest rocket launchers available in the game, accessible at Mastery Rank 4. Despite its early rank, its raw Blast damage per shot is substantial enough to carry through mid-game content. The weapon scales well with elemental mods that convert the base Blast damage into Radiation or Gas combinations against specific enemy factions.

## Acquisition

The Angstrum blueprint is available from the in-game Market for credits. Components are crafted in the Foundry. No special prerequisites beyond Mastery Rank 4 are required.

## Variants

The Prisma Angstrum is a Void Trader (Baro Ki Teer) variant with improved stats. Check the [Angstrum wiki page](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Angstrum) for variant details and Baro availability windows.

## Recommended builds

At 16% base crit, Primed Pistol Gambit and Hollow Point can push critical chance high enough for reliable yellow crits. The 2.0x multiplier rewards crit investment efficiently. Convulsion and Heated Charge add Cold and Heat to convert Blast to Radiation, effective against Alloy Armor, Corpus Robots, and Sentients.

Status builds use Pistol Pestilence and Pathogen Rounds to add Toxin, creating a Gas combination with the Blast base for area-of-effect poison clouds. The 22% status chance benefits from status mod investment for persistent damage over time.

## Notes

- Rockets detonate on contact with any surface including allies; avoid firing at close range.
- The Blast status proc ragdolls enemies, providing crowd control alongside damage.
- A single rocket damages all enemies within the blast radius; crowded groups amplify total damage output.
- Mastery Rank 4 makes this one of the most accessible explosive secondaries for new players.
- Blast damage deals bonus damage to Machinery (Corpus Moas, Ospreys) and Corrupted enemies; it is less effective against Grineer heavy armor. Account for faction matchups when selecting this weapon.
- The Prisma Angstrum is a direct upgrade available from Baro Ki Teer; farm Ducats and credits before each Void Trader visit to obtain it if it appears in the rotation.
- Multishot mods for secondaries (Barrel Diffusion, Lethal Torrent) increase effective rocket damage output; they are worthwhile investments on the Angstrum even though they function differently on rocket launchers than on pistols with multiple pellets.
