## Overview

A Kuva Lich variant of the Grineer Ayanga grenade launcher. Fires arcing explosive grenades dealing Impact and Blast damage. An innate elemental damage bonus from the Kuva Lich (ranging 25% to 60%) supplements the base damage profile, with the element type determined at Lich spawn. Introduced in Update 26.0 (2019-10-31, The Old Blood), the Kuva Ayanga is notable for Kuva Lich weapon with randomized innate elemental bonus (25-60%); high critical chance (35%) with very fast 1.2s reload; grenade arc trajectory.

The Ayanga was a signature Grineer weapon before Kuva Liches began claiming it as their own. Bathed in Kuva and empowered by the Lich's personal element, each Kuva Ayanga carries traces of its former wielder's essence -- a weapon reborn through Kuva-fueled resurrection.

## Acquisition

Obtained by defeating a Kuva Lich that wields the Ayanga. The Lich weapon is randomly assigned at spawn in the Kuva Fortress. After defeating the Lich, convert it to gain the weapon without trading it, or vanquish it to add the weapon to your arsenal. Blueprint then built for 10,000 credits. The Kuva Ayanga requires Mastery Rank 13 to equip.

Arch-gun weapons require either standard crafting resources, Syndicate standing for component parts, or specialized mission drops. Once crafted, equipping the Kuva Ayanga in Archwing uses the standard Arch-gun slot. To use it as a Heavy Weapon in ground missions or Railjack combat, a Gravimag must be installed. Gravimags are available from Clan Dojos via Energy Lab research, or from Arbitrations as rotation rewards.

## Variants

Kuva variant of the base Ayanga design. No Prime exists. The Lich element is randomized at spawn and cannot be changed -- selecting a preferred Lich before vanquishing is important.

The Arch-gun family spans weapons from Tenno engineering (Corvas, Fluctus, Velocitus), Grineer design (Grattler, Kuva line), and Entrati craftsmanship (Cortege, Mausolon, Morgha), each with distinct stat profiles suited to different engagement contexts.

## Recommended builds

35% critical chance enables effective crit builds even without Riven investment. Blast procs Knockdown for crowd control. Choose the Lich's element deliberately: Toxin enables Viral combinations, Radiation handles Grineer armor, Heat provides additional burn procs. Fast reload (1.2s) means magazine size is rarely limiting.

**Core Arch-gun mods to consider:**

- Rubedo-Lined Barrel -- +165% Arch-gun damage (primary damage mod for all builds)
- Hollowed Bullets -- +120% critical chance (essential for any crit-focused build)
- Critical Focus -- +60% critical multiplier while aiming
- Modified Munitions -- +90% status chance (for status-focused builds)
- Arch-gun Ace -- +165% fire rate (useful on slow-fire weapons to improve feel)
- Parallax Scope -- +30% critical chance while aiming
- Charged Bullets -- +60% magazine capacity
- Superior Defenses -- reduces reload time
- Conductive Coils -- adds Electricity damage
- Thermite Rounds -- adds Heat damage

Prioritize Rubedo-Lined Barrel as the first mod in any Arch-gun build. Follow with critical or status mods based on the weapon's stat profile. Elemental mods provide faction-specific damage type flexibility.

## Notes

The Kuva Ayanga operates in the Arch-gun slot, used in Archwing missions and as a Heavy Weapon on the ground via Gravimag. In Railjack (Empyrean) missions, the Arch-gun serves as the primary armament during space combat against fighters and Crewships.

Grenade arc trajectory suits Railjack boarding and interior combat scenarios. Blast Knockdown keeps enemy crew suppressed during objective completion. One of the better Kuva Arch-guns for ground-phase Railjack missions.

Archwing combat system notes: Archwing missions require completing The Archwing quest. Four Archwings are available -- Itzal (mobility/stealth), Amesha (tank/support), Elytron (AoE damage), and Odonata (balanced starter). Works across all Archwings. Itzal enables safe positioning for grenade arc optimization. The Kuva element bonus grants flexibility to complement any Archwing's ability set.

The weapon's Riven disposition is 3/5 (moderate). Riven dispositions range from 0 (absolute minimum, strong weapon) to 5 (maximum, weak weapon). DE adjusts dispositions quarterly based on weapon usage rates.
