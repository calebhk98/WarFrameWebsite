## Overview

The Ether Daggers are a dual-dagger melee weapon with a Puncture-dominant damage profile: 156.8 Puncture versus 33.6 each of Impact and Slash. The 30% status chance is high for a dagger-class weapon, positioning the Ether Daggers as a status-delivery platform within the dual-dagger archetype. The 10% critical chance and 1.8x multiplier are low; critical investment would waste mod slots. Attack speed of 0.833 is slightly below average, and the 1.75 m range reflects the short reach characteristic of dagger weapons. Mastery Rank 6 is required.

Dual-dagger weapons use the Gnashing Payara or Brutal Tide stance mods (depending on the configuration), which deliver rapid multi-hit combos from the dual-weapon animation set. The Puncture dominance combined with 30% status makes the Ether Daggers most effective against armored Grineer targets, where Puncture provides an armor-penetration advantage and Corrosive elemental procs from elemental mods further strip Ferrite Armor. The short 1.75 m range requires close-quarters engagement; positioning within melee range is essential for reliable hit connection.

## Variants

The Ether Daggers have no Prime, Kuva, or Tenet variant. For higher-tier dual-dagger options, players should consider other melee dual-dagger weapons with improved critical or status values available at higher Mastery Rank requirements.

## Recommended builds

**Corrosive/Puncture Grineer build.** The 156.8 Puncture base combined with Corrosive elemental mods (Fever Strike + Voltaic Strike for melee) strips Grineer Ferrite Armor through Corrosive procs. After armor strips to zero, the Puncture damage lands at full value. Add Primed Pressure Point and Condition Overload. The 30% status ensures consistent Corrosive proc generation.

**Status/Viral close-range build.** The 30% status supports Weeping Wounds scaling at higher combos. Equip Weeping Wounds, Condition Overload, four 60/60 elemental mods (Viral + Corrosive), and Primed Pressure Point. At x4 combo with Weeping Wounds active, multiple elemental proc types generate rapidly for Condition Overload's multiplicative damage bonus.

**Sustained status primer.** Use the Ether Daggers to rapidly apply Puncture, Viral, and Corrosive procs at close range, then switch to a higher-damage melee for Condition Overload-amplified follow-up. The fast multi-hit dual-dagger combos apply procs more quickly per second than slower heavy weapons, making the Ether Daggers effective as a proc-primer before weapon swapping.

## Synergies

Rhino's Roar amplifies all physical damage proportionally, benefiting the Puncture-dominant base. Frames that group enemies at close range (Khora, Vauban) maximize the 1.75 m range limitation by keeping targets within reach. Naramon's Power Spike extends combo duration for sustained Weeping Wounds scaling across extended engagements.

## Notes

- The 1.75 m range is short; engage at close quarters and use enemy-grouping frame abilities to connect with multiple targets simultaneously.
- The 10% critical chance makes crit investment unrewarding; prioritize status and Condition Overload mods instead.
