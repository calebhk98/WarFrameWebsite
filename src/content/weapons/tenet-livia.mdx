## Overview

The Tenet Livia is a Sisters of Parvos Tenet melee weapon available at Mastery Rank 14. Its 198 total damage per hit is puncture-dominant: 178.2 puncture, 9.9 slash, and 9.9 impact. Puncture damage has a damage reduction debuff that reduces enemy damage output and, more importantly, penetrates Grineer armor at a higher effective rate than other physical types. The Livia's puncture concentration makes it a natural Grineer-fighting weapon.

The 28% critical chance and 2.2x multiplier position the Livia as a hybrid crit-status weapon. With Blood Rush at x3 combo, critical chance reaches 70-80%, producing regular orange crits. The 28% status chance enables corrosive or viral elemental proc application through dual-stat mods, stripping Grineer armor before the puncture damage applies at full value against exposed flesh. The 1.08 attack speed and 2.5-meter range support methodical target engagement.

Tenet weapons carry a unique elemental bonus from the Sister whose link was severed, adding an extra elemental damage layer beyond the base stats. The elemental bonus type is determined when the Sister is created.

## Acquisition

Obtain through the Sisters of Parvos system. Trigger a Sister Candidate via Granum Void encounters on Corpus-controlled nodes, then track and vanquish the resulting Sister to claim her Tenet Livia. The elemental bonus is fixed at Sister creation.

## Variants

No base Livia counterpart or Prime version exists. The Tenet Livia is the only form of this weapon.

## Recommended builds

**Corrosive-crit Grineer build**: Blood Rush, Organ Shatter, Primed Pressure Point, Weeping Wounds, electric plus toxin (Corrosive) mods. Corrosive strips Grineer armor -- 6 corrosive procs remove all armor. After armor is stripped, the 178.2 puncture base deals full damage against exposed Grineer health pools. Blood Rush at x3 combo reaches high crit rates.

**Viral puncture**: Cold plus toxin for Viral, Blood Rush, Primed Pressure Point, Organ Shatter. Viral halves enemy max health; puncture's natural armor-penetration function means less damage loss through Grineer armor scaling.

**Condition Overload**: Condition Overload, Weeping Wounds, Primed Pressure Point, and three elemental 60/60 mods for multiple status types. Unique status types from corrosive (counts as two: electric + toxin), heat, and the Tenet elemental bonus stack Condition Overload multipliers.

## Notes

The Tenet Livia's puncture dominance means it is faction-specialized toward Grineer. Against Corpus enemies, the lack of impact (which deals bonus damage to shields) and no slash means average faction performance -- corrosive plus the Tenet elemental bonus compensates. In Corpus-heavy missions, consider a more balanced damage-type melee or supplement with warframe abilities.


The Tenet Livia's 2.5-meter range and its weapon type (likely a tonfas or similar dual-blade category) determine the specific stance combos available. Wider sweep stances enable the Livia to apply its 28% status chance across multiple targets per attack animation, multiplying its effective status density in grouped combat scenarios. Players who select stances with broad sweep attack patterns leverage the Livia's range most effectively in wave-based content.
