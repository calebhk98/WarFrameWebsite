## Overview

The [Akvasto](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Akvasto) is the dual version of the Vasto revolver secondary at Mastery Rank 8. Each shot deals 90 total damage: 22.5 impact, 45 puncture, 22.5 slash. The puncture-dominant profile (50% of total) makes it effective against Grineer armored targets. The 16% critical chance and 1.8x multiplier support functional critical builds; 12% status chance is too low for reliable proc strategies. Riven disposition at 4/5 offers good roll potential.

The Akvasto Prime (MR12) improves critical chance to 28% and multiplier to 2.0x with additional quality-of-life improvements.

## Acquisition

The Akvasto blueprint is available from the Market for credits. As a standard Market weapon at MR8, it is accessible to mid-game players. The completed weapon can be purchased with platinum.

## Variants

The Akvasto Prime (Mastery Rank 12) improves crit chance (28%), multiplier (2.0x), magazine, and adds additional stats. Players with Prime relic access should pursue the Prime variant. No Vandal or Wraith version exists.

## Recommended builds

**Critical puncture build:** Hornet Strike, Barrel Diffusion, Lethal Torrent, Primed Pistol Gambit, Primed Target Cracker, and three elemental mods. At 16% base crit, Primed Pistol Gambit elevates to viable yellow-crit frequency. The puncture-dominant physical damage bonus against Grineer armor complements a corrosive elemental configuration.

**Fast critical:** Add Hydraulic Crosshairs alongside Pistol Gambit. The 3.33 fire rate allows aimed fire between shots for headshot-triggered bonuses. Dual-revolvers provide a distinctive fire pattern that rewards precision per-shot targeting.

**Mastery + riven investigation:** The 4/5 riven disposition makes Akvasto rivens worth evaluating for players who plan to use the Akvasto Prime later. Stats and build patterns transfer from base to Prime.

## Synergies

- **Grineer missions:** 50% puncture damage deals bonus against both Ferrite and Alloy armor. Adding corrosive elemental mods (electric + toxin) compounds the physical bonus with armor strip procs.
- **Hydraulic Crosshairs:** The moderate fire rate (3.33) enables deliberate headshot timing between shots. Sustained headshots trigger Hydraulic Crosshairs consistently, keeping the crit bonus active during extended engagement.
- **Barrel Diffusion + Lethal Torrent:** The most impactful dual secondary mods. Adding both doubles effective bullet volume per trigger pull -- critical for a revolver with moderate base fire rate.

## Notes

- Status chance of 12% is below the minimum for reliable proc builds. The Akvasto is a crit-or-damage secondary, not a status primer.
- The 1.6-second reload is fast for a dual secondary. With 12 rounds and 3.33 fire rate, the magazine lasts approximately 3.6 seconds. The fast reload makes the fire-reload cycle smooth and low-disruption.
- Riven disposition at 4/5 provides above-average roll potential. A multishot riven combined with critical chance converts the Akvasto into a capable mid-game secondary.
