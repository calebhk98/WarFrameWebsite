## Overview

The Ether Reaper is a scythe melee weapon with a Puncture-dominant damage profile: 126 Puncture, 27 Impact, and 27 Slash. Scythes are a rare melee category in Warframe, using the Reaping Spiral or Stalking Fan stance mods for sweeping, wide-arc combo animations suited to crowd-clearing at range. The 20% critical chance and 2.0x multiplier create a functional crit base, while the 20% status chance supports supplementary elemental proc application in a hybrid build. Attack speed of 1.083 is slightly above average for a long-reach weapon, and the 2.7 m range is generous -- scythes are among the longer-range melee categories. Mastery Rank 4 is required.

The equal 20% critical chance and 20% status chance make the Ether Reaper a genuine hybrid platform, benefiting from both Blood Rush scaling for critical and Weeping Wounds for status. The 2.7 m range combined with scythe sweep combos creates efficient multi-target hit coverage. At MR4, the Ether Reaper provides unusually strong range for an early-accessible melee weapon, enabling effective crowd clearing during initial star chart progression.

## Variants

The Ether Reaper has no Prime, Kuva, or Tenet variant. For higher-tier scythe options, players should consider other scythe weapons available at higher Mastery Rank requirements.

## Recommended builds

**Blood Rush hybrid build.** At 20% crit and 20% status, the Ether Reaper suits Blood Rush and Weeping Wounds simultaneously. By x4 combo, both stats scale meaningfully. Equip Primed Pressure Point, Blood Rush, Organ Shatter, Weeping Wounds, and two Viral 60/60 mods. Condition Overload amplifies each hit as proc types accumulate. The 2.7 m range ensures wide-arc sweep combos connect to multiple targets.

**Reaping Spiral stance crowd build.** Equip Reaping Spiral for the scythe's multi-hit sweep animation. Combine with Primed Pressure Point, Condition Overload, and four 60/60 elemental mods for Viral + Corrosive. The 2.7 m reach hits multiple enemies per swing, applying procs across groups simultaneously.

**Early-progression crit build.** With available early mods -- Pressure Point, Fever Strike, Voltaic Strike, Organ Shatter -- the Ether Reaper at MR4 outperforms many alternatives. The 20% crit produces yellow crits regularly without Blood Rush. The 2.7 m range covers larger areas than typical MR4 swords, making the Ether Reaper a meaningful early-game choice.

## Synergies

The Ether Reaper's 2.7 m range pairs with enemy-grouping frame abilities (Vauban's Bastille, Khora's Strangledome) for maximum multi-target hit count per swing. Rhino's Roar amplifies all physical damage proportionally, benefiting the Puncture-dominant base. Volt's Speed increases attack speed for faster combo generation, amplifying Blood Rush and Weeping Wounds scaling.

## Notes

- The 2.7 m range makes the Ether Reaper one of the longest-reaching MR4-accessible melee weapons; use sweep combos to hit multiple enemies per attack.
