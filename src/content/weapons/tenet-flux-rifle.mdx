## Overview

The Tenet Flux Rifle is a Sisters of Parvos Tenet primary weapon requiring Mastery Rank 16. It is a beam weapon that fires a continuous energy beam at 15.0 ticks per second with 200 accuracy -- the highest accuracy rating of any primary weapon, equivalent to perfect precision at all ranges. Its 22 damage per tick divides between 17.16 puncture and 4.84 slash -- both physical, with no elemental base. The puncture component penetrates Grineer armor; the slash component generates bleed procs.

The 26% status chance at 15.0 ticks per second means elemental procs from added mods apply multiple times per second. The beam format allows continuous application of all modded elemental types simultaneously -- adding corrosive (electric + toxin) elemental mods creates corrosive procs at 15 tick rate with 26% status per tick. The 20% critical chance and 1.8x multiplier are secondary; this is a status-focused beam platform.

The 120-round magazine at 15 ticks per second drains in 8 seconds of sustained fire. The 1.8-second reload recovers quickly. The Tenet Flux Rifle carries a unique elemental bonus from its parent Sister, adding a base elemental type to the physical beam.

## Acquisition

Obtain through the Sisters of Parvos system at Mastery Rank 16. Trigger a Sister Candidate via Granum Void encounters on Corpus nodes, then track and vanquish the Sister to claim her Tenet Flux Rifle. The elemental bonus adds to the physical puncture-slash beam base.

## Variants

**Flux Rifle** -- The base beam primary. The Tenet version provides improved stats and the Tenet elemental bonus.

## Recommended builds

**Corrosive sustained beam**: Serration, Split Chamber, dual-stat 60/60 electric plus toxin (Corrosive) mods, Galvanized Aptitude, Galvanized Scope. At 15 ticks/s with 26% status, corrosive procs apply approximately 4 times per second. Six corrosive procs strip all Grineer armor within 1.5 seconds. After strip, the 17.16 puncture deals full value. Galvanized Aptitude stacks damage bonuses on proc kills.

**Viral plus Heat**: Cold plus toxin for Viral, and Thermite Rounds for Heat as a separate second element. Viral halves enemy max health; heat procs burn independently. The beam applies both simultaneously. Viral accumulates from cold-toxin and heat accumulates from Thermite Rounds on separate proc tracks.

**Precision status**: Primed Shred (punch-through, beam extends through enemies), Galvanized Scope (crit on headshot), Serration, and elemental status mods. The 200 accuracy and beam format enable threading through lined-up enemies with punch-through, applying status to multiple targets on each fire line.

## Notes

Beam weapons interact with punch-through differently than projectile weapons -- the beam extends continuously through pierced enemies rather than discrete projectiles punching through. Primed Shred significantly extends the effective engagement area of the Tenet Flux Rifle by allowing the beam to damage multiple enemies in a line simultaneously. The 8-second sustained fire window before reload is long by beam standards; the 1.8-second reload recovers quickly.
