## Overview

The Twin Basolk is a dual melee weapon available at Mastery Rank 7 from the Market for 30,000 credits. Its 210 total damage per hit divides across 55 impact, 55 puncture, 15 slash, and 85 heat. The heat elemental base is the most significant component: at 85 heat per hit with 40% status chance, fire procs apply reliably each engagement. The balanced impact and puncture physical split provides coverage against both Corpus shields (impact) and Grineer armor (puncture) without single-faction specialization.

The 40% status chance is the Twin Basolk's defining feature. Critical chance at only 5% means this is a pure status weapon -- building for critical output yields minimal returns. Instead, the 40% status base with Weeping Wounds at combo produces rapid multi-type elemental proc stacking. The inherent heat combined with added toxin from mods creates Gas elemental damage, which procs area toxin clouds on application.

At 1.0 attack speed and 2.4-meter range, the Twin Basolk covers medium melee engagement zones. Dual melee weapons generate combo counter ticks rapidly due to their multi-hit combo strings, feeding Weeping Wounds and Condition Overload quickly.

## Acquisition

The Twin Basolk blueprint is purchasable from the in-game Market for 30,000 credits. Build time is 12 hours. No quest or event unlock is required.

## Variants

No Prime, Vandal, or Wraith variant exists for the Twin Basolk.

## Recommended builds

**Gas area denial**: Primed Pressure Point, Weeping Wounds, Condition Overload, toxin mod (heat + toxin = Gas). Gas procs deal AoE toxin damage on proc, spreading damage to nearby enemies. The 40% base status with Weeping Wounds produces Gas procs almost every hit at x2 combo.

**Heat plus electric Radiation**: Electric plus existing heat base creates Radiation elemental. Radiation confuses enemies and deals bonus damage against alloy armor. The 40% status applies Radiation confusion reliably, causing enemies to attack each other.

**Condition Overload corrosive**: Add electric plus toxin mods on top of the heat base to produce two elemental types -- corrosive (electric + toxin) and heat separately. Each unique status type active on the enemy (heat proc, corrosive proc, physical procs) multiplies Condition Overload damage.

## Notes

The 5% critical chance makes building for crits nearly futile -- even with maxed critical chance mods, consistent crits are not achievable. Avoid Point Strike and Blood Rush; invest those slots in status enhancement (Weeping Wounds) and Condition Overload. The dual nature of the weapon produces rapid combo accumulation that Weeping Wounds converts into very high effective status chance within seconds.
