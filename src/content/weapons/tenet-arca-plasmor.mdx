## Overview

The Tenet Arca Plasmor is a Corpus tenet weapon that represents a weaponized evolution of the original Arca Plasmor. This destructive shotgun fires projectiles that ricochet off surfaces, making it effective in cramped indoor environments. The weapon's primary strength lies in its exceptional radiation damage output and the utility of its bouncing projectiles, which allow skilled players to hit enemies around corners. The Arca Plasmor's lineage traces back to Corpus engineering principles, and the Tenet variant enhances this pedigree with improved projectile behavior and raw damage. Its relatively low fire rate is offset by the devastating per-shot damage and the potential for multiple hits from ricocheting projectiles.

The Tenet Arca Plasmor excels in medium-range combat scenarios where its projectile behavior can be exploited. The pure radiation damage type makes it particularly effective against shields and certain faction types. The high critical chance at 22 percent rewards careful aiming, especially when targeting weak points or setting up ricochet sequences. The weapon's accuracy allows for controlled bursts and tactical positioning, distinguishing it from fully automatic crowd-control shotguns. Its role in the weapon meta centers on tactical placement and understanding enemy positioning rather than pure raw damage per second.

## Acquisition

The Tenet Arca Plasmor is acquired as a potential reward from Sisters of Parvos encounters in void fissure missions. Players must defeat a Sister of Parvos and unlock her weapon drop by breaking the weapon node during the encounter. The blueprint is not tradable, making this weapon a direct reward from gameplay rather than marketplace acquisition. The 12-hour build time is standard for Tenet weapons, allowing players to queue it while actively farming other content.

## Variants

The original Arca Plasmor serves as the base model, sharing the same weapon class and general mechanics. The Kuva equivalent, Kuva Arca Plasmor, exists as an alternative with similar behavior but distinct damage output and element customization. The Tenet variant specifically emphasizes the ricochet mechanic and radiation specialization, making it distinct in the weapon ecosystem.

## Recommended builds

The Tenet Arca Plasmor benefits from mods that enhance its critical chance and elemental damage potential. Serration provides the foundational damage boost for primary weapons, while Point Strike pushes the critical chance above 50 percent with proper investments. Barrel Diffusion increases the projectile count per shot, though this requires careful positioning to maximize. Radiation damage can be amplified through mods like Heat and Cold combinations, or players can hybrid with viral builds by adding toxin elements. Vigilante Armaments and similar critical-enhancing mods synergize well with the weapon's natural critical nature. Magazine size modding is optional given the modest magazine capacity, as players typically reload between major engagement phases.

## Notes

The Tenet Arca Plasmor occupies a unique niche in the shotgun arsenal by emphasizing projectile behavior over damage-per-second metrics. Its ricochet mechanic rewards environmental awareness and tactical play. The radiation damage type, while specific, aligns well with common endgame faction threats and shielded targets. Players expecting a traditional close-range shotgun experience may find the slower fire rate and projectile-based mechanics require adjustment. The weapon's viability scales well with mod investments targeting critical chance and damage amplification.
