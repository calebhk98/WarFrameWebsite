## Overview

The Cobra & Crane is a sword-and-shield melee weapon requiring Mastery Rank 10. Its damage per swing totals 296 -- split between 207.2 impact and 88.8 slash, with zero puncture -- making it heavily impact-dominant at 70% of total damage. This physical damage profile is best suited against Corpus shields and Robotic enemies where impact receives positive modifiers.

As a sword-and-shield weapon, the Cobra & Crane provides a 2.6-meter reach and access to the sword-and-shield stance pool: Eleventh Storm and Final Harbinger. These stances incorporate the shield into both offensive combos and blocking mechanics, giving the weapon a distinctive dual-function character that rewards players who practice the stance combo sequences.

The 36% status chance is the defining build stat on this weapon. While the 15% critical chance and 1.5x multiplier offer limited returns for dedicated crit investment, the status chance enables reliable elemental proc stacking at the 0.917 attack speed. Status builds are the primary competitive approach for the Cobra & Crane in mid-to-high content.

## Recommended builds

**Status Viral/Corrosive Build:** The 36% status chance makes the Cobra & Crane an excellent status applicator. For Corpus missions, equip Convulsion (Electric) and Shocking Touch for Magnetic to strip shields, paired with Condition Overload and Weeping Wounds. Against Grineer, switch to Corrosive (Toxic + Electric) to strip Ferrite armor. The impact weighting adds Knockdown proc potential from Impact status effects.

**Hybrid Status-Crit Build:** Blood Rush brings the 15% CC up to usable territory at high combo counters. Pair with Organ Shatter and Condition Overload for scaling damage, Weeping Wounds to escalate status chance during combos, and Primed Pressure Point for base damage. This hybrid approach is more consistent than pure crit due to the low base CC.

Eleventh Storm stance provides good multi-target coverage with sweeping combos. Final Harbinger emphasizes mobility and directional attacks, better suited for single-target precision.

## Notes

Sword-and-shield weapons block frontal melee attacks when the shield-side hand faces the damage source. Practiced players incorporate blocking into combat rhythm to reduce damage taken in dense melee situations -- particularly useful in Steel Path Infested missions where melee attacks are frequent.

The 0.917 attack speed is slower than the standard 1.0 baseline, so Fury or Berserker Fury mods are valuable for improving combo fluidity. At slow attack speed, Berserker Fury (which triggers on critical hits) requires some crit chance investment to activate reliably.
