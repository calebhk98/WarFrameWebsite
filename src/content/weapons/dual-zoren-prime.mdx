## Overview

The Dual Zoren Prime is the primed variant of the Dual Zoren, featuring a 30% critical chance and a 3.2x critical multiplier -- among the highest multipliers for dual swords. The attack speed of 1.25 and Puncture-heavy damage (126 Puncture vs 7 Impact and 7 Slash) make it a fast, high-crit weapon suited to priority-target combat. Status chance of 22% adds flexibility for hybrid builds. Mastery Rank 13 is required.

The 3.2x critical multiplier is the weapon's primary appeal. At 30% base crit with Blood Rush scaling at high combo multiplier, the Dual Zoren Prime delivers orange or red critical hits with reliable frequency. The 1.25 attack speed ensures the combo counter builds quickly, feeding Blood Rush stacks. The low Slash (7) and Impact (7) components reduce physical proc diversity, focusing damage output entirely through Puncture and elemental mods.

## Acquisition

The Dual Zoren Prime blueprint and components are obtained through Void Relics. Check [the Dual Zoren Prime Fandom wiki page](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Dual_Zoren_Prime) for current relic availability and vault status. Player trading is an alternative acquisition path.

## Variants

The Dual Zoren Prime is the primed variant of the base Dual Zoren. The Prime improves critical chance, multiplier, status chance, and attack speed. No Kuva or Tenet variant exists.

## Recommended builds

**Blood Rush high-crit build.** The 3.2x multiplier makes Blood Rush scaling extraordinarily effective. Equip Sacrificial Steel, Organ Shatter, and Blood Rush. Add Berserker Fury for attack-speed acceleration. At x8 combo, effective crit chance with Sacrificial Steel and Blood Rush approaches guaranteed red-crit. Include two 60/60 elemental mods for Viral to amplify Puncture damage.

**Weeping Wounds hybrid.** Add Weeping Wounds alongside Blood Rush for simultaneous status scaling. At high combo, Viral procs stack from the 22% status alongside the crit burst, enabling Condition Overload damage multiplication.

**Speed-focused build.** The 1.25 attack speed with Berserker Fury and Valkyr's Warcry creates a very high hit rate. Combine with Organ Shatter and the base 3.2x multiplier for sustained high-damage output. This approach sacrifices some combo maintenance for raw hits-per-second.

## Synergies

Naramon Void Sling is near-mandatory for Blood Rush builds; maintaining the combo counter between enemy clusters is critical for the build's effectiveness. Valkyr's Warcry provides additional attack speed. Arcane Fury (melee damage on crit) complements the high crit rate to boost output during sustained combat.

## Notes

- The 3.2x multiplier is one of the highest in the dual-sword class; prioritize mods that exploit this.
- The range of 2.3 m is slightly shorter than average; aggressive positioning is required for full combo hits.
