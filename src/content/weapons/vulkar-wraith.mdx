## Overview

The Vulkar Wraith is the Wraith variant of the Vulkar sniper rifle, available at Mastery Rank 7. It improves on the base Vulkar with higher damage per shot -- 273 total (245.7 impact, 27.3 slash) versus 225 for the base. Like the base Vulkar, the Wraith version is heavily impact-weighted: over 90% of physical damage is impact. This impact concentration makes the Vulkar Wraith most effective against Corpus enemies, whose shields absorb impact damage efficiently.

The fire rate increases to 2.0 rounds per second (versus 1.5 for the base), making the Wraith version a noticeably faster sniper. The 8-round magazine (versus 6 for the base) extends sustained fire before reload. Critical chance and status chance both remain at 20% and 25% respectively -- the same as the base Vulkar -- but the higher damage per shot means each stat-adjusted shot deals more total output.

The 3.0-second reload is unchanged from the base Vulkar. The Vulkar Wraith was historically distributed through Nightwave and in-game Alert rewards.

## Recommended builds

**Impact Corpus sniper**: Serration, Split Chamber, Point Strike, Vital Sense, magnetic (cold + electric) elemental mods, and Vigilante Armaments. Magnetic deals 75% bonus damage to Corpus shields and reduces shield capacity on proc. The 245.7 impact base with magnetic devastates Corpus shields on every hit. Point Strike raises the 20% crit to near 60%.

**Status sniper**: Viral (cold + toxin) plus Split Chamber, Serration, Point Strike. The 25% status chance at 2.0 fire rate applies Viral within the first few shots. Viral halves max health; subsequent shots deal amplified damage. The 8-round magazine provides enough sustained fire to apply and exploit Viral.

**Standard crit**: Serration, Split Chamber, Point Strike, Vital Sense, Heavy Caliber, and two elemental mods. At 20% base with Point Strike, the Vulkar Wraith reaches near-60% crit, generating reliable crits at 2.0 fire rate. Higher per-shot damage than the base Vulkar amplifies each crit.

## Notes

The 3.0-second reload remains the Vulkar Wraith's primary weakness. In continuous combat, the 8-round magazine empties in 4 seconds at 2.0 fire rate, followed by a 3-second reload gap. Use cover during the reload window. Sniper ammo scarcity in long missions is addressed by Sniper Ammo Mutation. The Vulkar Wraith's impact dominance is its faction specialization -- against Grineer or Infested, consider other sniper platforms or adjust elemental mods to compensate for the mismatched physical damage type.
