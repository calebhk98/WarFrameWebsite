## Overview

A dual-mode Necramech weapon that combines a primary flamethrower with a secondary grenade launcher. Primary fire siphons life essence from targets to fuel a devastating secondary fire mode. Secondary fire launches three explosive projectiles in a fan pattern that leave a damaging area on detonation. Introduced in Update 29.0 (2020-08-25, Heart of Deimos), the Cortege is notable for dual fire modes combining sustained flamethrower with explosive grenade burst; high status chance (40%) and Heat innate damage.

Designed by the Entrati for use by their Necramechs, the Cortege predates the Infestation's takeover of Deimos. Its life-siphon mechanism once powered Necramechs through battlefield attrition across the moon's labyrinthine tunnels.

## Acquisition

Blueprint purchased from NecraLoid Syndicate (Loid) at Clearance: Odima standing rank. Component parts (Barrel, Receiver, Stock) are crafted using Deimos resources. The weapon comes with a Gravimag pre-installed. The Cortege requires Mastery Rank 14 to equip.

Arch-gun weapons require either standard crafting resources, Syndicate standing for component parts, or specialized mission drops. Once crafted, equipping the Cortege in Archwing uses the standard Arch-gun slot. To use it as a Heavy Weapon in ground missions or Railjack combat, a Gravimag must be installed. Gravimags are available from Clan Dojos via Energy Lab research, or from Arbitrations as rotation rewards.

## Variants

No Prime or Vandal variant exists. Morgha is the complementary Entrati Necramech explosive weapon, while Mausolon is the Voidrig's default sidearm.

The Arch-gun family spans weapons from Tenno engineering (Corvas, Fluctus, Velocitus), Grineer design (Grattler, Kuva line), and Entrati craftsmanship (Cortege, Mausolon, Morgha), each with distinct stat profiles suited to different engagement contexts.

## Recommended builds

High status chance (40%) paired with Heat innate damage makes Thermite Rounds or Heat-Radiation builds effective. The explosive secondary fire mode benefits from area-damage setups. Primary fire sustained DPS pairs with Modified Munitions for reliable Heat proc application.

**Core Arch-gun mods to consider:**

- Rubedo-Lined Barrel -- +165% Arch-gun damage (primary damage mod for all builds)
- Hollowed Bullets -- +120% critical chance (essential for any crit-focused build)
- Critical Focus -- +60% critical multiplier while aiming
- Modified Munitions -- +90% status chance (for status-focused builds)
- Arch-gun Ace -- +165% fire rate (useful on slow-fire weapons to improve feel)
- Parallax Scope -- +30% critical chance while aiming
- Charged Bullets -- +60% magazine capacity
- Superior Defenses -- reduces reload time
- Conductive Coils -- adds Electricity damage
- Thermite Rounds -- adds Heat damage

Prioritize Rubedo-Lined Barrel as the first mod in any Arch-gun build. Follow with critical or status mods based on the weapon's stat profile. Elemental mods provide faction-specific damage type flexibility.

## Notes

The Cortege operates in the Arch-gun slot, used in Archwing missions and as a Heavy Weapon on the ground via Gravimag. In Railjack (Empyrean) missions, the Arch-gun serves as the primary armament during space combat against fighters and Crewships.

Equip via Gravimag for Railjack ground assault phases. Especially effective in Deimos-sector missions where the weapon's Entrati origin provides thematic and mechanical synergy with Infested-heavy encounters.

Archwing combat system notes: Archwing missions require completing The Archwing quest. Four Archwings are available -- Itzal (mobility/stealth), Amesha (tank/support), Elytron (AoE damage), and Odonata (balanced starter). Excels on the Voidrig Necramech for combined arms with the Mausolon. As an Arch-gun via Gravimag, pairs with Itzal for stealth approach and burst-mode delivery.

The weapon's Riven disposition is 3/5 (moderate). Riven dispositions range from 0 (absolute minimum, strong weapon) to 5 (maximum, weak weapon). DE adjusts dispositions quarterly based on weapon usage rates.
