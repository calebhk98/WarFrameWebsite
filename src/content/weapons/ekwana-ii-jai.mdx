## Overview

Ekwana II Jai is a Zaw Link component available from Hok's Anvil at Cetus on the Plains of Eidolon. As a Link component, it does not have standalone combat statistics; WFCD reports zeros because the item is an unassembled weapon part. The Ekwana II Jai modifies the critical chance and status chance of the assembled Zaw according to which Strike and Grip are selected during the three-part Zaw construction process.

Link components occupy the third slot in Zaw assembly (Strike + Grip + Link). The Ekwana series of links (Ekwana, Ekwana Jai, Ekwana II Jai, Ekwana Ruhang, Ekwana II Ruhang, and their variants) each offer different weightings of critical and status modifiers. Comparing link options within Hok's interface before committing to a purchase is essential.

## Acquisition

Purchase from Hok's Anvil at Cetus on the Plains of Eidolon. Ostron standing is required; higher-tier link components unlock at higher Ostron ranks. Earn Ostron standing through fishing, mining, and bounty completion in the Plains of Eidolon. Check [the Ekwana II Jai Fandom wiki page](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Ekwana_Ii_Jai) for the exact Ostron rank requirement and standing cost.

## Variants

The Ekwana Link family includes multiple variants offering different critical and status weightings. Compare Ekwana Jai, Ekwana II Jai, Ekwana Ruhang, and Ekwana II Ruhang to select the modifier that best complements the chosen Strike and Grip combination. No Zaw component has a Prime or Kuva version.

## Recommended builds

When incorporating Ekwana II Jai into a Zaw build:

- Select a Strike first to establish the weapon archetype and base damage profile.
- Select a Grip to determine the weapon category (polearm, sword, etc.) and swing speed.
- Add Ekwana II Jai as the Link to apply its critical and status modifiers to the assembled configuration.
- Preview the final assembled stats in Hok's interface before purchasing.
- After gilding, install a Stance mod matching the weapon category and apply Forma to the desired polarity slots.

## Synergies

The Ekwana II Jai's exact modifier contribution depends on the assembled Zaw configuration. In general, Zaw weapons assembled with Link components biased toward critical chance benefit from Blood Rush and Organ Shatter, while status-biased links benefit from Weeping Wounds and Condition Overload. Within the Ekwana Jai family, the II designation signifies an improved or second-tier version compared to the base Ekwana Jai, offering enhanced critical modifiers for high-performance crit-focused Zaw builds. Players who have outgrown the base Ekwana Jai can substitute the Ekwana II Jai into the same Zaw assembly to upgrade the critical contribution without changing the Strike or Grip selection. Naramon's Power Spike further complements crit-biased Zaw assemblies by sustaining combo counter uptime for Blood Rush scaling.
