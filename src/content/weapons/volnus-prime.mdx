## Overview

The Volnus Prime is the Prime variant of the Volnus mace, available at Mastery Rank 14. Its 250 total damage per hit distributes across 115 puncture, 80 slash, and 55 impact -- puncture-dominant with significant slash secondary. This damage mix provides faction coverage: puncture penetrates Grineer armor while slash generates bleed procs that bypass armor. The 2.9-meter range is long for a mace and enables wide sweep attacks against clustered enemy groups.

The 34% status chance is the Volnus Prime's strongest attribute, improved over the base Volnus's already high 30%. At 1.2 attack speed with 34% status, procs accumulate very quickly. The 20% critical chance and 1.8x multiplier are below critical-focused melees but serve adequately as secondary sources of damage. Condition Overload synergy is the primary build path: each unique status type active on the target provides a damage multiplier.

The Volnus Prime drops from Void Relics and represents the top tier of the Volnus weapon line, intended for late-game players with a full mod collection.

## Recommended builds

**Condition Overload status**: Condition Overload, Weeping Wounds, Primed Pressure Point, and elemental mods for corrosive (electric + toxin) plus heat. Three unique status types from corrosive, heat, and physical procs stack Condition Overload multipliers significantly. The 34% base status with Weeping Wounds at x2 combo ensures all three status types apply reliably.

**Viral-slash**: Cold plus toxin for Viral, Weeping Wounds, Condition Overload, Primed Pressure Point. Viral halves enemy max health. The 80 slash base generates bleed procs that bypass armor and tick into the halved health pool. At x2 combo with Weeping Wounds, slash procs apply on most hits.

**Corrosive armor strip**: Electric plus toxin dual-stat 60/60 mods for Corrosive, Weeping Wounds, Primed Pressure Point. Six corrosive procs remove all Grineer armor. With 34% status and Weeping Wounds, six procs accumulate within the first few swings. After armor strip, the 115 puncture deals full damage against exposed Grineer health.

## Notes

The 1.8x critical multiplier is below average for mace weapons -- avoid over-investing in critical mods. The 34% status base with Weeping Wounds is more productive than a crit build for this weapon. The 2.9-meter range combined with mace sweep stances hits multiple enemies per swing, applying status procs to several targets simultaneously and feeding Condition Overload against the nearest target.
