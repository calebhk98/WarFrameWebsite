## Overview

The Dorrclave is a melee weapon with a uniquely pure Puncture damage profile: 222 Puncture and zero Impact or Slash. Among melee weapons, pure-physical builds are unusual; most weapons distribute damage across multiple types. The Dorrclave's complete commitment to Puncture makes it factionally specialized -- Puncture is most effective against Grineer Ferrite Armor and is the damage type of choice for enemies where physical penetration matters. The 26% critical chance and 2.2x multiplier provide a solid crit platform, while the 24% status chance is high enough to support Puncture proc (Impair) application and elemental procs from mod combinations. Mastery Rank 8 is required.

The 0.833 attack speed is slightly below average, reflecting the weapon's heavier archetype. The 2.5 m range is standard for a one-handed melee. With no Slash, the Dorrclave generates no bleed procs, making it less effective against high-health Infested and armor-stripped enemies where Slash typically excels. Against armored Grineer targets, however, the pure Puncture profile can be advantageous when Corrosive procs from elemental mods strip Ferrite Armor to zero, allowing the full Puncture base damage to land without mitigation.

## Recommended builds

**Corrosive/Puncture Grineer build.** The pure Puncture base combined with Corrosive mods (Fever Strike + Voltaic Strike or equivalent 60/60 mods) strips Grineer Ferrite Armor on Corrosive proc, then delivers unmitigated Puncture damage. Add Primed Pressure Point and Blood Rush. This is the Dorrclave's strongest faction-specific application.

**Critical hybrid build.** At 26% base crit, Blood Rush reaches orange-crit by combo x4. Equip Organ Shatter, Sacrificial Steel, and two 60/60 Viral mods. The 24% status ensures Viral procs on health-exposed targets after armor strip, amplifying the Puncture damage output.

**Status/Weeping Wounds build.** The 24% status supports a dedicated status path. Equip Weeping Wounds, four 60/60 elemental mods (Corrosive + Viral), Primed Pressure Point, and Condition Overload. Corrosive procs strip armor; Viral weakens health; Condition Overload multiplies damage per unique status type.

## Synergies

Corrosive Projection aura from teammates, combined with the Dorrclave's own Corrosive procs, accelerates armor strip on Grineer Heavy Gunners and Bombards. Rhino's Roar amplifies all physical damage, benefiting the 222 pure Puncture base proportionally. Frames that strip armor (Nyx's Psychic Bolts, Wisp's Motes) create optimal conditions for the Dorrclave's unmitigated Puncture output.

## Notes

- Zero Slash means no bleed procs; the Dorrclave is weakest against Infested and armor-stripped targets where Slash typically dominates.
- The 222 Puncture base is exceptionally high for a single physical type, making each hit deliver significant damage to Puncture-vulnerable targets.
