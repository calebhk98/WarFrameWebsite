## Overview

Xoris is a Corpus-designed glaive introduced alongside the Deadlock Protocol quest. It occupies an unusual position in the Warframe arsenal: the weapon was specifically designed without a finite combo counter, meaning its melee combo never expires. This property has significant build implications -- Xoris cannot build infinite combo stacks with Garuda's Talons interaction or similar infinite-scaling mechanics, but it does maintain consistent combo count throughout a mission without requiring active upkeep.

The damage profile combines 55 puncture with 41 slash, making it effective against medium-armor targets while still generating slash bleed procs on direct hits. The 20% critical chance and 2.4x multiplier give it credibility as a crit platform, particularly when paired with Blood Rush, which scales crit chance with combo count.

Glaives can be thrown at enemies for ranged melee attacks and detonate on heavy attack, dealing area damage to groups. Xoris's throw mechanics make it useful in corridors and enclosed spaces where enemies cluster within the detonation radius. The 1.3-meter ground range understates its true effective range since thrown glaives travel considerable distances before returning.

## Acquisition

The Xoris blueprint is obtained by completing the Deadlock Protocol quest, which also drops the Protea Warframe components. Players who missed the quest can purchase the blueprint from the Market for 15,000 credits. No rare resources are required for crafting.

## Variants

Xoris has no prime variant.

## Recommended builds

**Crit-throw build:** Pairing Xoris with Blood Rush, Organ Shatter, and Primed Pressure Point creates a crit-focused glaive setup that scales with the permanent combo count. Because Xoris's combo never resets, Blood Rush's scaling applies continuously throughout a mission, reaching reliable orange-crit territory after the first few kills. Add Volatile Quick Return for faster detonation timing.

**Area-control build:** Equip Volatile Quick Return with Amalgam Organ Shatter and focus on heavy-attack detonations. The detonation radius benefits from range-increasing mods (Reach), and Melee Arcane Fury on kill accelerates heavy attack charge rate. This build clears tightly packed enemy groups efficiently in Defense and Interception missions.

Players who want to preserve Xoris's infinite-combo property should avoid swapping to a different melee mid-mission, as the combo counter resets on sheathing in some configurations.

## Notes

- Xoris's infinite combo counter is a design choice by Digital Extremes to prevent the weapon from trivializing certain Helminth mechanics. Players who seek infinite-scaling combo strategies use other glaives.
- Glaive-class weapons equip the Gleaming Talon or Xata's Whisper stance, both of which include throw combos.
- The floating decimal values in WFCD (55.199997 puncture, 40.799999 slash) are floating-point artifacts from the data export; effective in-game values are 55.2 and 40.8 respectively.
- Xoris can trigger elemental procs on throw, making status mods situationally useful even at 18% base status chance when paired with a multi-hit combo.
- The Deadlock Protocol quest is a prerequisite for obtaining Xoris through the quest reward path. Players who skip the quest can still purchase the blueprint from the Market.
