## Overview

The Dual Cleavers are a dual-sword melee weapon with a notably high 3.0x critical multiplier, one of the more impressive multiplier values available among early dual-sword options at Mastery Rank 5. The damage profile favors Puncture (109.9) with equal Impact and Slash (23.55 each). With 25% critical chance and a 3.0x multiplier, a dedicated Blood Rush build can reach devastating orange-crit hits by mid-combo. The 10% status chance is low, firmly placing the Dual Cleavers in the critical rather than status camp. Attack speed of 0.833 is slightly below the dual-sword average, and the 1.7 m range is shorter than polearms or heavy blades.

Dual sword weapons use dual-sword stance mods (Crossing Snakes, Swirling Tiger, Gemini Cross) that deliver multi-hit spinning combos, which synergize with the high critical multiplier by amplifying each individual hit within the combo sequence. At a moderate investment level, the Dual Cleavers outperform many higher-mastery-rank weapons in raw critical burst damage due to the 3.0x multiplier.

## Variants

The Dual Cleavers have no Prime, Kuva, or Tenet variant. Players seeking a higher-tier dual sword option should consider the Dual Keres Prime or Dual Zoren Prime, which offer competitive critical stats with better base damage values.

## Recommended builds

**Blood Rush high-multiplier build.** The 3.0x multiplier is the Dual Cleavers' defining asset. Equip Blood Rush, Organ Shatter, Sacrificial Steel, and two Viral 60/60 mods. By combo x4, orange-crit hits amplified by 3.0x multiplier deliver heavy per-hit damage. Berserker Fury increases attack speed after kills, accelerating combo generation further.

**Critical melee burst build.** Equip Primed Pressure Point, Blood Rush, Organ Shatter, Sacrificial Steel, Arcane Strike, and Berserker Fury. This maximizes raw crit output from the 3.0x multiplier with minimal status investment. Use a single Viral 60/60 mod if two mod slots remain after core crit mods.

**Swirling Tiger combo build.** Equip the Swirling Tiger stance for wide-arc spinning attacks that hit multiple enemies per hit. Combine with Primed Pressure Point, Blood Rush, and Weeping Wounds (despite low base status, Weeping Wounds at high combo still contributes procs). The 1.7 m range is short, so group enemies using Vauban or Khora before engaging.

## Synergies

Naramon's Power Spike focus school extends melee combo duration, sustaining Blood Rush at higher multipliers for longer. Wukong's passive (immortality on combo) pairs well with the aggressive close-range melee playstyle the Dual Cleavers demand. Volt's Speed augments the below-average 0.833 attack speed with a frame-level boost.

## Notes

- The 3.0x critical multiplier is the primary reason to use Dual Cleavers over other MR5-range options; build exclusively around maximizing it.
- The 1.7 m range is short; stay close to enemies and use enemy-grouping frame abilities to maximize multi-target hit count.
