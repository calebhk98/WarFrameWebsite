## Overview

The Nukor stands as a pure-radiation secondary weapon firing continuous beam projectiles rather than discrete bullets. It specializes in status-focused builds, leveraging a 29% status chance to create radiation procs that cause confusion and temporary enemy disorientation. The weapon's perfect accuracy ensures every beam finds its target regardless of range, making it reliable for pinpoint secondary support fire. The inherent radiation element provides natural synergy with elemental mods and faction-specific builds targeting Corpus units. Despite its low critical chance, the Nukor excels in crowd control through status procs rather than raw damage scaling, filling a supporting role in squads while maintaining respectable solo performance.

## Damage

The Nukor deals purely radiation damage at 22 points per shot, making it elementally focused. The radiation element triggers confusion procs at 29% per shot, causing affected enemies to attack their own allies. The critical multiplier of 4.0x appears high but becomes secondary due to the 3% base critical chance, making critical scaling impractical. Instead, status-focused builds dominate, with elemental mods amplifying radiation procs to maximize crowd control utility. Against radiation-vulnerable enemies like the Corpus, the weapon performs exceptionally well, while against heavily armored Grineer the damage output without supporting mods remains modest.

## Acquisition

The Nukor blueprint is available directly from the market for 15,000 credits, with construction requiring an additional 15,000 credits and twelve hours of Foundry time. The weapon's low mastery requirement makes it accessible early, providing new players with a reliable status-focused secondary platform. The Nukor is tradable in blueprint form, allowing player-to-player commerce for those preferring platinum investment. The straightforward acquisition path removes farming barriers, ensuring consistent access.

## Variants

No prime variant of the Nukor currently exists. A variant weapon does not yet have been released, keeping the standard Nukor the only option. Players seeking higher-damage radiation secondaries must look to alternatives or invest in heavy modding.

## Recommended builds

Status-focused modding maximizes the Nukor's utility. Adding elemental damage mods like Heat Damage or Cold mods creates multiproc chains through radiation combinations, amplifying crowd control effectiveness. Pistol Pestilence or other status-enhancing mods push the 29% base chance toward 100% for guaranteed procs. Magazine extension through Increased magazine capacity supports the high fire rate during sustained engagements. Critical mods remain ineffective given the 3% base chance, so resources are better spent on elemental synergy. Forma investment enables exilus polarity mods for situational utility like reload speed.

## Synergies

Frames capitalizing on enemy disorientation synergize perfectly with the Nukor's confusion procs. Loki benefits from the crowd control to maintain stealth and infiltration objectives. Ash receives consistent openings from confused enemies for finisher executions. Nyx converts confusion into Psychic Bolts for additional crowd control layers. Trinity uses the confusion to enable defensive abilities without overextending. Support frames appreciate the Nukor's ability to incapacitate threat clusters while maintaining their primary focus on ally buffs.

## Lore

The Nukor emerged from ancient Orokin energy weapon research, its continuous beam design reflecting advanced plasma generation technology. The radiation focus suggests counter-development against metallic armored threats prevalent during the Old War. Within player communities, the Nukor represents an accessible introduction to status-focused secondary builds, teaching the value of crowd control over raw damage output. Its popularity among support frame pilots reinforces its role as a defensive, utility-first secondary platform.
