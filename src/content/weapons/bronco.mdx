## Overview

The [Bronco](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Bronco) is a secondary shotgun pistol that fires 12 pellets per shot, each dealing 42 total damage. The Bronco's impact-dominant pellets (33.6 impact, 4.2 puncture, 4.2 slash) make it particularly effective against Corpus shielded enemies at close range. The 2-round magazine and 1.05-second reload encourage deliberate shot timing rather than continuous fire. Each shot covers a wide spread pattern that guarantees all pellets connect at point-blank range.

At Mastery Rank 0, the Bronco is immediately accessible. Riven disposition at 5/5 is the maximum tier, meaning any Bronco riven rolls with excellent multipliers. The Bronco Prime offers substantially improved stats for mid-to-late game players.

## Acquisition

The Bronco blueprint is available from the Market for credits. As an MR0 weapon, it is immediately accessible to all players. The Bronco can also be purchased built for platinum.

## Variants

The Bronco Prime significantly improves critical chance, critical multiplier, status chance, and pellet count. Players who can access Prime relics should pursue the Bronco Prime for endgame secondary use. No Vandal or Wraith variant exists.

## Recommended builds

**Shotgun status build:** Hornet Strike, Barrel Diffusion, Lethal Torrent, Flechette (puncture to slash for proc improvement), Pistol Pestilence, Frostbite, Jolt, and Scorch. At 12 pellets per shot, status procs from multiple pellets accumulate rapidly even at the 9.4% per-pellet status chance. Elemental mods create viral, heat, or corrosive procs per volley.

**Beginner build:** Hornet Strike, Barrel Diffusion, and two elemental mods covering a single combination (viral or corrosive). This entry-level configuration delivers functional damage across star chart content without requiring rare mods.

**Point-blank burst:** At zero range (touching an enemy), all 12 pellets connect simultaneously, delivering 504 total impact damage per shot (12 x 42). Against shielded Corpus targets, where impact deals bonus damage, this creates instant shield strip potential from a single trigger pull.

## Synergies

- **Viral/slash combination:** Adding viral (toxin + cold) converts pellet status procs into health reduction, pairing well with the Bronco's natural impact for rapid Corpus shield stripping (impact) followed by health reduction (viral).
- **Close-quarters Warframes:** Ash, Excalibur, and Rhino -- frames that regularly close to melee range -- benefit from the Bronco's point-blank effectiveness during ability execution sequences.
- **Arcane Deadhead:** Landing headshots with any weapon grants a damage bonus. The Bronco's wide spread pattern includes pellets that can register as headshots, potentially triggering the arcane consistently.

## Notes

- Accuracy of 3.7 reflects the extreme spread pattern. At any range beyond 5 meters, pellets begin to miss targets. The Bronco is strictly a close-quarters weapon.
- The 2-round magazine requires near-constant reloading in sustained combat. Thankfully, the 1.05-second reload is among the fastest in the game. Build a rhythm of fire-fire-reload that maintains continuous pressure.
- The Bronco's impact bias provides no benefit against Grineer Ferrite Armor. Add heat or corrosive elemental mods to compensate when running Grineer missions.
- Riven disposition at 5/5 is excellent. A riven with multishot (additional pellets per shot), critical chance, or status chance dramatically improves the Bronco's output at point-blank range.
