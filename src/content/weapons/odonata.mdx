## Overview

Odonata is the starter Archwing, awarded to players completing the Archwing quest. It represents the well-rounded middle ground of the Archwing roster: balanced health (425) and shield (430) with moderate armor (100), and an ability set mixing defensive shields, missile countermeasures, area attacks, and crowd control. For players new to Archwing missions, Odonata provides all tools needed to handle every core mission type without requiring specialist knowledge.

Introduced in Update 15 (October 2014) as the second launch Archwing alongside Elytron, Odonata was designed as the approachable, tutorial-friendly option. Its abilities are intuitive -- shield yourself, decoy missiles, shoot homing rockets, push enemies away -- and balanced stats mean it tolerates mistakes that would destroy fragile Itzal. The prime variant, Odonata Prime, improves upon every base stat while retaining the same ability set.

Odonata is named after the order of insects that includes dragonflies, reflecting the frame's aerodynamic visual design and combination of speed and attack capabilities.

## Abilities

Energy Shell forms a temporary energy shield around the Warframe that absorbs incoming damage up to a fixed threshold before breaking. Most useful at the start of high-damage engagements: pop Energy Shell before entering a Grineer Crewship defense perimeter to absorb the opening salvo, then engage once the immediate threat is identified. The shell does not regenerate -- once depleted, it requires a second cast to restore. Managing Energy Shell timing is one of the core skills for Odonata players.

Disarray deploys flare beacons that attract and detonate incoming guided missiles before they reach the Warframe. Functionally similar to Elytron's Core Vent but operating through beacon drones rather than exhaust. The beacons remain active for several seconds and draw multiple volleys of guided munitions. In any Archwing content featuring Grineer missile boats or Corpus drone swarms, Disarray prevents shield chip damage from guided fire and lets players focus on priority targets.

Seeking Fire launches a volley of homing missiles that automatically track and damage the nearest enemy targets within range. The auto-tracking nature removes aiming requirements that direct-fire weapons demand in the fast movement of Archwing combat. Against small fighters that weave through formations, Seeking Fire connects reliably where manual shots miss. The volley damages multiple targets simultaneously, making it effective for thinning out fighter escorts protecting larger objectives.

Repel releases a radial energy burst that stuns and physically knocks back all enemies within range of the detonation. This defensive panic button clears breathing room when surrounded by enemies in tight formations -- common in Grineer Galleon corridors during Archwing Sabotage missions. Repel also serves as a reliable interrupt when enemy ability users wind up powerful attacks.

## Acquisition

The Odonata blueprint is guaranteed upon completing the Archwing quest, available after completing the Earth to Mars Junction. The three component blueprints (Harness, Systems, Wings) are researched in the Clan Dojo Tenno Lab. Because the Archwing quest is a prerequisite for all space-based content, Odonata is effectively the first Archwing every player builds.

Build time for complete Odonata is listed as nearly instant in WFCD (buildTime: 60 seconds) for the main frame, but components require standard Archwing crafting times. Resource costs are low-tier planets materials accessible from Earth and early star chart nodes.

## Modding Notes

Low base energy of 85 is Odonata's main limitation. Endurance Drift from Drift mods or Vigilante Pursuit (Exilus) help, as does Zenurik focus. Ability strength increases Energy Shell absorption capacity and Seeking Fire damage. Redirection improves the already solid shield pool. For sustained mission players, Primed Flow significantly extends ability uptime.

Optimal Odonata builds prioritize duration and efficiency over raw strength, with flexibility to add strength scaling where energy availability permits. Umbral mods provide limited value because Odonata's abilities do not leverage strength scaling effectively.

## Playstyle

Use Energy Shell preemptively before known damage spikes. Deploy Disarray against guided missile barrages. Spam Seeking Fire for consistent area damage. Use Repel as reset button when surrounded. Odonata's gameplay loop balances offensive output (Seeking Fire) with defensive management (Energy Shell, Disarray), creating engaging decision-making for tactical pilots.

Newer Archwing pilots benefit from building into Odonata before farming specialized frames. Understanding the defensive mechanics -- when to shield, when to decoy -- carries forward to every other Archwing.

## Synergies

Odonata pairs well with Trinity or Amesha support frames in squad compositions because they provide energy refund mechanics enabling sustained ability spam. Volt's speed buffs enhance Odonata's mobility for repositioning. Harrow provides invisibility synergy for reduced incoming damage requirements.

Odonata's balanced design enables flexibility: it supports damage dealers through missile countermeasures and crowd control without requiring dedicated support frame support.

## Lore

Odonata was the first Archwing operational model distributed to active Tenno by the Lotus, serving as standard-issue space-combat harness before specialized variants were developed. Its design philosophy -- reliability over specialization -- reflects practical constraints of early Archwing technology. The ordnance systems (Seeking Fire, Energy Shell) were refined and enhanced in Odonata Prime, representing the Orokin-era gold standard of the original design.

## Variants

Odonata Prime is an unvaulted variant offering improved stats across health, shield, and armor while retaining the same ability set. Prime status reflects Orokin optimization of the original Tenno design.
