## Overview

The Ferrox is a speargun primary with a Slash-dominant damage profile: 245 Slash, 70 Puncture, and 35 Impact. The 32% critical chance and 2.8x critical multiplier make it one of the stronger crit-platform primaries in the speargun class. Fire rate of 1.33 rounds/second and a 10-round magazine (7.5 seconds of sustained fire) with a 1.8-second reload create a deliberate, semi-automatic firing rhythm. The 10% status chance is low; this weapon is not a status platform. Mastery Rank 14 is required. The 16.67 accuracy indicates projectile spread appropriate to a thrown-spear mechanic.

Speargun weapons have a unique throw mechanic: hitting a surface with the embedded spear creates a tether that draws enemies toward the impact point. This crowd-control function complements the Ferrox's high per-shot damage, as grouped enemies pulled toward the tether point become easy targets for follow-up shots or ability use. The 245 Slash base combined with 32% crit and 2.8x multiplier means orange-crit hits deliver very high per-shot Slash damage. At MR14, this is an endgame primary suitable for players with established crit mod collections.

## Variants

The Ferrox has no Prime, Kuva, or Tenet variant. It is a standalone speargun primary weapon with a unique crowd-control tether mechanic.

## Recommended builds

**Critical/Hunter Munitions Slash build.** At 32% base crit with Point Strike and Vital Sense, the Ferrox generates reliable orange crits. Hunter Munitions converts each crit into a Slash proc, compounding the already-high 245 Slash base with additional bleed procs. Serration and Split Chamber maximize the base damage. Galvanized Scope stacks crit multiplier after headshots.

**High-damage per-shot crit build.** Equip Serration, Split Chamber, Point Strike, Vital Sense, Galvanized Chamber, and two elemental mods for Viral. At 32% base crit, the 2.8x multiplier delivers very high per-shot damage at point-blank range or with the tether grouping enemies into the impact point. This suits assassination-style engagements against single high-value targets.

**Tether crowd-control utility build.** Throw a Ferrox spear into cover near enemy groups to create a tether pull point. Equip a Bane-of-faction mod alongside Serration, Split Chamber, and Vital Sense. Use the crowd-control tether to concentrate enemies for follow-up AoE primary damage or Warframe ability use, then transition to the Ferrox for crit hits against the grouped targets.

## Synergies

Khora's Strangledome and the Ferrox's tether create combined crowd control that stacks grouping effects. Banshee's Sonar marks weak points where the Ferrox's precision shot delivers amplified crit damage. Rhino's Roar proportionally amplifies the Slash-heavy damage profile, benefiting each orange-crit Slash hit.

## Notes

- The 2.8x critical multiplier is the Ferrox's primary strength; commit fully to crit mods and Hunter Munitions for maximum output.
- The spear-tether mechanic provides crowd control independent of the damage build; use it to group enemies before high-damage follow-up.
