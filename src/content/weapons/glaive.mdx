## Overview

The Glaive is the foundational thrown-melee weapon in Warframe, available at Mastery Rank 1. Its damage profile: 73.5 Puncture, 15.75 each of Impact and Slash. As a glaive-type weapon, it can be thrown in a spinning arc that hits multiple enemies in its flight path before returning to the player. The 12% critical chance and 12% status chance are low by endgame standards, but the Glaive's primary value is its throwing mechanic rather than raw damage stats. The 1.25 m range applies to direct melee swings; thrown arc attacks are not limited by this range and can cover entire corridors.

The Glaive uses the Gleaming Talon stance mod, which enables direct-throw combos and charged heavy attack throws. The charged throw is the Glaive's most powerful attack: holding the heavy attack button and releasing launches the glaive on a ricocheting arc that bounces off surfaces, hitting everything in its path. Combining the thrown arc with elemental mods creates a mobile status-delivery tool effective at hitting groups from safety. The Glaive Prime is the definitive upgrade for players who enjoy the thrown melee archetype, offering substantially better crit and status stats. The base Glaive teaches the weapon class mechanics before committing resources to the Prime version.

## Acquisition

The Glaive blueprint is available from the Market. It requires a 12-hour crafting time in the Foundry after purchasing the blueprint. Check [the Glaive Fandom wiki page](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Glaive) for current credit cost and crafting resource requirements.

## Variants

The Glaive Prime is the Prime variant with significantly higher critical chance (25%), critical multiplier (2.0x), status chance (20%), and base damage. The Glaive Prime is acquired through Void Relics and is the recommended end-goal for players who enjoy the thrown-melee archetype. No Kuva or Tenet variant exists for the Glaive.

## Recommended builds

**Thrown-arc status primer.** Equip Primed Pressure Point, four 60/60 elemental mods for Viral + Corrosive, and Condition Overload. Use the charged heavy attack throw to deliver elemental procs across entire enemy groups simultaneously. Despite the 12% base status, the arc hits multiple enemies per pass, accumulating Condition Overload stacks for follow-up damage.

**Gleaming Talon direct-throw combo.** Equip Gleaming Talon stance and use the throw-into-combo sequence. The multi-hit direct throw chains between enemies in the combo rather than sweeping a single arc. Combine with Primed Pressure Point and two elemental mods for supplementary damage. This is the Glaive's core combat pattern at MR1.

**Early-game progression build.** For new players, equip Pressure Point, Fever Strike, Voltaic Strike, and Organ Shatter as soon as available. The 73.5 Puncture base carries early missions adequately, and the thrown-arc mechanic allows engagement from cover. Upgrade to Glaive Prime when Void Relics become accessible.

## Synergies

Volt's Electric Shield amplifies Electricity damage for shots (and glaive arcs) passing through it; thrown Glaive arcs passing through Electric Shield receive the damage bonus. Frames with grouping abilities (Khora, Vauban) ensure the Glaive's thrown arc hits the maximum number of targets per pass. Mag's Magnetize bubble concentrates the ricocheting arc's bounces for increased hit repetition on grouped targets.

## Notes

- Upgrade to Glaive Prime for substantially improved crit and status stats; the base Glaive is a starting point for learning the thrown-melee archetype.
- The 1.25 m melee range is short; rely primarily on the thrown arc for multi-target engagement rather than direct swings.
