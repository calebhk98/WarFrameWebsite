## Overview

The Burston Prime exemplifies balanced rifle design through moderate damage output and reliable accuracy across engagement ranges. This burst-fire weapon delivers consistent performance through three-round bursts, balancing magazine longevity with continuous damage application. The weapon serves intermediate players effectively, requiring moderate tactical positioning rather than extreme precision or overwhelming firepower. Burston Prime enables solo progression through star chart content while maintaining competitive viability in squad-based endurance missions. The balanced damage distribution across impact, puncture, and slash provides consistent performance against varied armor types without requiring elemental augmentation.

## Stats

Burston Prime fires in five-round bursts at 5 rounds per second, delivering balanced physical damage with 13.8 impact, 13.8 puncture, and 18.4 slash per round. Magazine capacity of 45 rounds sustains extended engagements with reasonable reload frequency at 2 seconds. Critical chance of 18% with 1.8x multiplier provides occasional bonus damage opportunities without requiring crit-focused builds. Status chance of 30% enables reliable proc application when status mods are applied, particularly effective for armor stripping through Corrosive combinations. The hitscan delivery ensures consistent accuracy regardless of engagement distance or target mobility.

## Damage and Performance

The balanced damage distribution creates consistent performance against armor types across faction variety. The 30% status chance baseline enables status-focused builds through elemental mod stacking, particularly Corrosive Projection for armor removal on Grineer and Cold for slow effects on Corpus units. Critical chance of 18% pairs moderately well with crit mods, enabling 40%+ crit rates through basic Point Strike application. The three-round burst mechanics encourage controlled trigger discipline, rewarding accurate players while maintaining rapid fire capability during sustained engagement.

## Mod Recommendations

Serration and Heavy Caliber establish core raw damage foundation for competitive DPS output. Mod Capacity investment towards status effects proves highly rewarding given the 30% baseline, with Corrosive Projection and Heat providing distinct tactical advantages. Split Chamber increases projectiles per burst, while Extended Clips magnifies magazine utility. Point Strike enables moderate critical builds, while Thermite Rounds or Hellfire provide elemental scaling. Reload mods optimize quality of life, reducing downtime between engagements.
