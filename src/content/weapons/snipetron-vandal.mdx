## Overview

The Snipetron Vandal is the Vandal variant of the Snipetron, a Corpus-manufactured sniper rifle no longer available in standard play. The Vandal revision raises it to a competitive tier: its 180 slash damage per shot dominates unarmored and flesh targets, and the 28% critical chance with a 2x multiplier provides a capable crit ceiling for a mastery rank 5 weapon.

The Snipetron family distinguishes itself from Grineer-pattern snipers by its Corpus lineage and an entirely different aesthetic. In terms of raw performance, the Vandal holds its own well into mid-game star chart content. The low fire rate of 2 shots per second is typical for bolt-action-style snipers; each shot is intended to be deliberate, with the player reacquiring the scope between pulls.

Slash as the primary damage type is a meaningful advantage. Slash procs apply bleeding damage that ignores armor, making the Snipetron Vandal effective against armored Grineer targets even without corrosive elemental mods, unlike many other snipers that rely on impact or puncture to penetrate heavy armor directly.

## Acquisition

The Snipetron Vandal is an intermittent reward from Nightwave series and historical alerts. It is not available on the Market or through standard crafting, making it a time-limited acquisition. Players who missed its availability windows should monitor Nightwave store rotations and DE-announced re-release events. Trading with other players on [warframe.market](https://warframe.market/items/snipetron_vandal_set) is the most reliable current option.

## Variants

The base Snipetron is no longer obtainable through normal means and was functionally discontinued before the Vandal variant was introduced. There is no Prime variant. The Snipetron Vandal stands alone as the only viable form of this weapon family.

## Recommended builds

**Slash-status build.** The 180 slash base pairs naturally with Bladed Rounds, which adds 60% slash damage. Pairing with a 90% elemental and Hammer Shot generates reliable slash procs that bypass Grineer armor. At 16% base status chance, adding a second elemental mod through Thermite Rounds or Infected Clip reaches roughly 40% status chance in a four-mod elemental setup.

**Critical slash build.** Point Strike and Vital Sense push the critical ceiling into orange-crit range. The slash procs triggered on critical hits scale with the multiplier, amplifying bleed damage that ticks afterward. This build performs especially well in sorties and missions with level 40-60 enemies.

**General-purpose build.** For players who want versatility without specialist investment, Serration, Heavy Caliber, and two elemental mods cover most content. The Snipetron Vandal's slash bias means it naturally benefits from even basic elemental setups. Ammo Scavenger in the exilus slot prevents the 6-round magazine from becoming a liability in extended missions.

## Notes

- The 2x critical multiplier is modest compared to Rubico Prime (3x) or Lanka (2.5x), meaning crit investment reaches a lower ceiling; raw damage and slash procs are often more reliable paths.
- Headshot multipliers stack multiplicatively with crit and elemental; the Snipetron Vandal rewards accurate aim more than spray-and-pray at range.
- Mastery rank 5 makes this one of the lowest-requirement Vandal weapons, useful for mid-progression accounts that want a step up from standard snipers.
- The Vandal skin distinguishes it visually from standard Corpus weapons with a darker metallic finish and ornamental plating.
