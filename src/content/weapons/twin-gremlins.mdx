## Overview

The Twin Gremlins are dual pistols available at Mastery Rank 5 from the Market for 25,000 credits. Their 37 total damage per shot divides exactly equally across all three physical damage types: 12.33 impact, 12.33 puncture, and 12.33 slash. This perfect three-way split is unusual and provides the most balanced faction performance possible from physical damage -- no faction receives a large bonus or penalty from the damage mix. Impact hits Corpus shields, puncture penetrates Grineer armor, and slash bleeds through any faction's defenses.

The 15% critical chance and 1.5x multiplier are low -- the Twin Gremlins are not a crit weapon and building for crits is not recommended. The 15% status chance, similarly, is below the threshold for reliable proc application without extensive mod investment. The Twin Gremlins are best understood as a starter secondary that performs consistently without excelling in any specific area.

At 5.0 fire rate with a 30-round magazine and a fast 1.1-second reload, the Twin Gremlins provide sustained output without extended reload pauses. The accessibility of MR 5 makes them appropriate for early-game players who have not yet acquired advanced secondaries.

## Acquisition

The Twin Gremlins blueprint is purchasable from the in-game Market for 25,000 credits. Build time is 24 hours. No quest or event unlock is required.

## Variants

No Prime, Vandal, or Wraith variant exists for the Twin Gremlins.

## Recommended builds

**Elemental conversion starter**: Hornet Strike, Barrel Diffusion, Heated Charge, Pathogen Rounds, Lethal Torrent. The balanced physical base takes well to elemental conversion. Heat and toxin combine to Gas elemental. The 30-round magazine sustains elemental proc application during engagements.

**Viral beginner**: Hornet Strike, Barrel Diffusion, North Wind (cold), Pathogen Rounds (toxin). Cold plus toxin creates Viral. At 15% status with multishot from Barrel Diffusion, Viral applies within a few shots to halve enemy max health.

**Basic damage**: Hornet Strike, Barrel Diffusion, and two single elemental mods. The dual-pistol format performs adequately at early-game difficulty with simple mod loadouts. The fast reload of 1.1 seconds keeps uptime high.

## Notes

The Twin Gremlins are an entry-level secondary and will be outpaced by most mid-game alternatives in terms of raw damage output. They serve well as a bridging weapon while farming or purchasing stronger secondaries. The equal physical distribution is a useful property for players who run varied mission types without faction-specific loadout adjustments. Replace with a higher-stat option as Mastery Rank and mod access improve.
