## Overview

The [Aeolak](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Aeolak) is a Duviri semi-automatic rifle with 60 total damage per shot (15 impact, 17 puncture, 23 slash, 5 radiation). The 21% critical chance and 2.3x multiplier support viable critical builds, and the 33% status chance enables reliable elemental proc application. At Mastery Rank 10, the Aeolak targets mid-game players progressing through the Duviri Paradox content.

The Aeolak's semi-automatic fire mechanic rewards deliberate shot placement over spray. The 6.0 fire rate allows reasonably rapid follow-up shots while maintaining the precision advantages of the semi-auto trigger. The 40-round magazine and 2.0-second reload provide comfortable sustained fire without frequent interruptions.

## Acquisition

The Aeolak blueprint is available from the Market for 25,000 credits. As a Duviri-introduced weapon, it may also appear as a Circuit reward in the Duviri Paradox game mode. Crafting requires standard mid-game materials plus any Duviri-specific components. Check the [Aeolak Wiki page](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Aeolak) for current acquisition details.

## Variants

No Prime, Vandal, or Wraith variant of the Aeolak exists. The base weapon is the only available form.

## Recommended builds

**Critical status hybrid:** Serration, Split Chamber, Galvanized Aptitude, Point Strike, Vital Sense, Hunter Munitions, and two elemental mods for viral or heat. At 21% critical chance, Hunter Munitions triggers reliably, converting crits to slash procs that bypass armor. Galvanized Aptitude adds stacking multishot from kills, amplifying proc rate in high-density missions.

**Status armor strip:** Serration, Split Chamber, Galvanized Aptitude, and four elemental mods targeting corrosive (electric + toxin) and heat. The 33% status chance per shot applies corrosive procs within 3-4 shots on a single target, stripping Grineer armor for follow-up damage.

**Radiation faction build:** The innate 5 radiation damage is minor but represents the starting point for a radiation-focused build targeting Corrupted enemies (Void missions). Add electric and heat mods to form additional radiation elemental damage, amplifying the innate 5 to a significant radiation component against Corrupted Tier enemies.

## Synergies

- **Galvanized Aptitude:** Conditional multishot stacking per kill escalates the Aeolak's proc rate rapidly in missions with frequent kills, making the 33% status chance deliver near-guaranteed procs per trigger pull at full stacks.
- **Banshee's Sonar:** The Aeolak's 100 accuracy makes aimed Sonar weak-point delivery reliable. Semi-auto firing allows deliberate targeting of each highlighted weak point.
- **Rhino's Roar:** Each shot's 60 base damage benefits from multiplicative amplification. Combined with the semi-auto deliberate fire pattern, Roar converts each well-placed shot into substantial burst damage.

## Notes

- Accuracy of 100 is perfect -- the Aeolak hits the exact point of aim with no spread, making it effective at any range the semi-auto fire rate allows. Long-range precision work is viable without accuracy-penalty concerns.
- Semi-auto fire rewards single-target precision over area spray. Use in missions where enemy density is manageable and deliberate shot placement yields higher crit and proc returns per ammunition spent.
- Riven disposition at 4/5 means above-average roll potential. A riven adding multishot converts the semi-auto to effectively a burst weapon, significantly amplifying DPS.
