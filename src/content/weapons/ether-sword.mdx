## Overview

The Ether Sword is a one-handed longsword melee weapon with a Puncture-dominant damage profile: 134.4 Puncture, 28.8 Impact, and 28.8 Slash. The 34% status chance is exceptionally high for a longsword, positioning the Ether Sword as one of the strongest status-platform swords in the MR7 tier. The 10% critical chance and 1.5x multiplier are low; this weapon gains nothing from crit investment. Attack speed of 1.0 and 2.5 m range are standard for the longsword class. Mastery Rank 7 is required.

Longswords use single-sword stance mods (Iron Phoenix, Crimson Dervish, Vengeful Revenant) that deliver balanced, swift combo sequences. The 34% status chance combined with Weeping Wounds scales to very high status proc rates at x4 combo, enabling rapid multi-elemental proc cycling for Condition Overload stacking. The Puncture dominance pairs well with Corrosive elemental mods against Grineer Ferrite Armor, as Corrosive procs strip armor while Puncture's armor-penetration value interacts favorably with partially stripped targets. At MR7, the Ether Sword provides an unusually high status chance for accessible content.

## Variants

The Ether Sword has no Prime, Kuva, or Tenet variant. It is a standalone longsword weapon. Players seeking upgrade paths should consider higher-tier longswords with improved stat profiles.

## Recommended builds

**Status/Condition Overload build.** The 34% status is the Ether Sword's primary asset. Equip Primed Pressure Point, Condition Overload, Weeping Wounds, and four 60/60 elemental mods for Corrosive + Viral. At x4 combo with Weeping Wounds active, the effective status chance reaches very high levels. Condition Overload multiplies damage per unique status type active on target.

**Corrosive armor-strip Grineer build.** The Puncture base combined with Corrosive elemental mods strips Grineer Ferrite Armor through proc accumulation. At 34% base status with Weeping Wounds, Corrosive procs apply rapidly. After full armor strip, Puncture damage lands without mitigation. Add Primed Pressure Point and Condition Overload as the primary damage multipliers.

**Vengeful Revenant finisher build.** Equip Vengeful Revenant stance for its parry-into-finisher mechanics. On parried attacks, follow with the finisher combo for high-damage finisher multiplier hits. The Ether Sword's 34% status ensures Puncture procs (Impair) and elemental procs apply during the finisher animation, generating Condition Overload stacks.

## Synergies

Frames that generate invulnerability windows (Rhino's Iron Skin, Frost's Snow Globe) create safe parry opportunities for Vengeful Revenant stance finisher combos. Saryn's Spores spread Toxic procs from enemies affected by the Ether Sword's status procs, amplifying Condition Overload stacks across groups. Frames with armor-strip abilities (Corrosive Projection aura, Nyx) reduce the dependency on Corrosive procs for armor removal.
