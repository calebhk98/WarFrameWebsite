## Overview

The [Coda Hema](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Coda_Hema) is an Infested-themed primary rifle obtained through the Technocyte Coda Adversary system on Deimos. Unlike most weapons, it deals its damage entirely as Viral (52 per shot) with zero physical damage components. This makes it an exceptional status-delivery weapon -- every shot applies Viral procs directly without requiring elemental mod combinations, freeing mod slots for other damage amplifiers or utility mods.

The Coda Hema's 20% critical chance and 2.3x critical multiplier position it for hybrid critical-status builds. Its 30% status chance ensures consistent Viral proc delivery from primary fire alone, while the 6.15 rounds-per-second fire rate sustains high proc frequency. At Mastery Rank 17, it is a late-game weapon targeting experienced players who have progressed through Deimos Adversary content.

The Coda Hema parallels the base Hema in Infested aesthetic and design, but the Coda prefix marks it as an Adversary-system weapon with enhanced stats and unique damage typing.

## Acquisition

The Coda Hema is obtained by defeating a Technocyte Coda Adversary on Deimos. Coda Adversaries are Infested nemesis enemies that players create and pursue through the Deimos mission system, analogous to Kuva Liches and Sisters of Parvos on their respective factions. Defeating the Coda Adversary yields its associated weapon, in this case the Coda Hema.

Each Coda Hema carries a random bonus attribute from the Adversary conversion (damage type bonus, status, or critical), making some individual weapons statistically superior to others. The weapon is tradeable between players.

## Variants

The base **Hema** (non-Coda) is a craftable Infested primary with a health-cost self-damage reload mechanic and different stats. The Coda Hema shares the Hema's Infested visual design but differs in acquisition, stats, and damage type. No Prime or other variant of the Coda Hema exists.

## Recommended builds

**Pure Viral stacking**: Because Viral damage is already native to the Coda Hema, applying additional Heat via mods produces Corrosive (for armor strip) rather than stacking Viral. Players wanting maximum Viral buildup should instead focus on Serration (base damage), Galvanized Chamber (multishot), and status chance mods to rapidly stack Viral procs. At 30% base status and high fire rate, Viral reaches 10-stack cap quickly without additional status mods.

**Viral-Slash hybrid**: Add Hunter Munitions to convert critical hits into Slash procs. Viral's 50% damage-to-health amplification stacks multiplicatively with Slash procs dealing damage over time, creating a powerful combination for high-health-pool enemies in Steel Path endgame.

**Heat combination**: Add Thermite Rounds to create a Corrosive combination (Viral base + Heat mod = Corrosive by elemental combination rules -- Heat combines with Viral's Toxin component). This is effective against Grineer heavy armor. Note: this changes the damage type from pure Viral; verify the combination against current elemental rules before committing.

## Notes

Mastery rank 17 is among the highest weapon requirements in the game, limiting Coda Hema to late-stage players. The weapon's Adversary system acquisition mirrors the Kuva Lich loop; players familiar with Lich hunting will find the Deimos Coda system structurally similar. The bonus attribute from conversion influences optimal build direction, so target specific bonus types (critical chance or multishot bonus being generally most valuable) when farming.
