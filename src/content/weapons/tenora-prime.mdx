## Overview

The Tenora Prime is the Prime variant of the Tenora, Octavia's signature rifle. Available at Mastery Rank 14, it fires at a sustained 12.67 rounds per second with a 200-round magazine -- among the largest magazine capacities in the primary weapon category. Its 28 damage per round distributes across slash (11.2), impact (8.4), and puncture (8.4) in near-equal proportions, giving the Tenora Prime broad faction viability rather than a single-faction specialization.

The 30% critical chance and 2.2x multiplier place the Tenora Prime in high-functioning critical territory for an automatic rifle. With Point Strike, crits occur on nearly every shot. At 12.67 fire rate, the Tenora Prime spools through its critical output rapidly. The 24% status chance is significant at this fire rate: status procs apply multiple times per second, enabling consistent elemental proc accumulation.

The Tenora Prime retains the Tenora's signature warm-up mechanic: fire rate builds to maximum over approximately 2 seconds of sustained fire. The initial burst-fire mode at lower fire rates gives way to full-auto as the spooling completes. This mechanic rewards commitment to sustained fire over target-switching.

## Acquisition

The Tenora Prime blueprint and components drop from Void Relics. Check the current relic drop table in-game for which relics contain Tenora Prime parts. Components require crafting with Orokin resources; build time is 12 hours per component plus 12 hours for the main assembly.

## Variants

**Tenora** -- The base non-Prime version, Octavia's signature primary with lower stats.

## Recommended builds

**Crit spray**: Serration, Split Chamber, Point Strike, Vital Sense, Heavy Caliber, and two 60/60 elemental mods. The 30% base crit with Point Strike reaches near-full crit. At 12.67 fire rate, the volume of crits generates massive sustained DPS. Heavy Caliber reduces accuracy but is acceptable at the Tenora Prime's medium engagement range.

**Viral sustained fire**: Cold plus toxin for Viral, Split Chamber, Serration, Point Strike, Galvanized Aptitude. Viral at 24% status applies within a few shots. Galvanized Aptitude stacks damage bonuses on each proc kill during the sustained fire of the 200-round magazine.

**Status density**: Four 60/60 dual-stat mods for Viral plus Heat or Corrosive plus Heat. The 24% status at 12.67 fire rate with multishot applies procs several times per second, rapidly building Galvanized Shot kill-stacks.

## Notes

The spooling mechanic means maximum damage output takes 2 seconds to reach. Do not release the trigger mid-engagement if possible. The 200-round magazine -- at 12.67 fire rate -- provides roughly 15.8 seconds of sustained fire before the 2.5-second reload interrupts output. This sustained capacity is the Tenora Prime's signature advantage in long-wave mission types.
