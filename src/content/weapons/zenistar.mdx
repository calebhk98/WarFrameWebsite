## Overview

Zenistar is a Tenno-crafted throwing disc weapon with a unique area-denial mechanic. Unlike conventional melee weapons, Zenistar can deploy its disc at a target location by holding the melee throw button, leaving it spinning in place and dealing continuous heat damage to enemies within the disc's area of effect. This deployed-disc capability makes it distinct from all other melee weapons and gives it significant tactical versatility in Defense, Excavation, and Survival missions.

The base damage profile includes 80 impact, 68 puncture, and 150 heat per strike, for a combined 298 damage on direct melee contact. The built-in heat damage enables Ignite procs on direct strikes without requiring elemental mods, freeing mod slots for other damage types or utility options. The disc's deployed form continues dealing heat damage in its area, applying heat procs to enemies who linger nearby.

Zenistar's 30% status chance enables reliable heat proc application, and the disc's persistent nature means multiple procs stack over the disc's duration. This area-denial output is difficult to replicate with other melee weapons and suits a defensive playstyle where controlling objective space matters more than maximizing per-swing damage.

The 0.83 attack speed is below average for melee, and the 10% critical chance makes crit-focused builds poor investments. Zenistar rewards modding for status and heat damage amplification over conventional crit or raw damage approaches. Players who position the disc at objectives and then use other weapons to finish off enemies entering the kill zone extract the most value from this weapon.

## Acquisition

Zenistar is obtained through the Daily Tribute login reward system. Players who log in consecutively accumulate tribute milestones, and Zenistar appears as a milestone reward at specific login intervals. This makes it a time-gated weapon tied to account age rather than resource farming or Market purchase. Players cannot craft or buy Zenistar through conventional means -- only the Daily Tribute system grants it. No Foundry build is required; the weapon is delivered directly as a completed item.

## Recommended builds

**Area-denial disc build:** The deployed disc's damage scales with the duration the disc remains active. Lengthening disc deployment duration maximizes area-denial uptime. Combine Volcanic Edge and Thermite Rounds to push heat damage higher, increasing disc tick damage. Add Condition Overload for melee combo synergy when striking enemies directly. This build excels in confined spaces where the disc covers the full engagement area, such as excavator pits and Defense objective rooms.

**Heat status build:** Focus entirely on status proc application. With 30% base status chance and heat mods, status chance exceeds 60% on each strike. Weeping Wounds during active combos pushes this further. The disc passively continues applying heat procs to enemies within range, creating stacked fire stacks that deal significant damage over time. This build performs best in Survival and Defense where sustained pressure on groups outperforms burst damage. Combine with Corrosive mods for dual-status coverage.

Because Zenistar attacks slowly, players who want fast melee combo buildup may prefer to use a companion primary weapon for combat and reserve Zenistar specifically for disc deployment at objectives.

## Notes

- Zenistar's deployed disc duration scales with the player's current combo counter when the disc is thrown. Higher combo counts yield longer disc deployment times, incentivizing maintaining combo throughout the mission.
- The disc can be recalled manually by pressing the heavy attack button, allowing repositioning to a new location when the tactical situation changes.
- Zenistar requires no crafting materials -- it is granted directly as the completed weapon from the Daily Tribute system.
- The weapon's unique disc mechanic has no equivalent in any other melee weapon class, making it a distinctive tool for players who prioritize objective defense strategies.
- Heat built into the base damage profile means fire-focused elemental mods amplify existing damage rather than converting it from physical types, making heat stacking efficient.
