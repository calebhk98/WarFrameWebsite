## Overview

Fulmin Prime is the primed variant of the Fulmin rifle, a unique Corpus weapon featuring dual-fire functionality that adapts to different tactical situations. In full-auto mode, it fires hitscan projectiles at high velocity with respectable critical potential. Switching to semi-auto mode transforms it into a charged projectile weapon, trading fire rate for higher per-shot damage with superior elemental output. This versatility makes Fulmin Prime exceptional for players who value adaptability in their loadouts. The weapon excels in mid-range encounters where both spray and burst mechanics are useful, supporting aggressive playstyles while retaining accuracy at distance. The electricity element provides effective crowd control against shielded enemies and Corpus units, making it a cornerstone weapon for faction-specific missions.

The prime variant improves upon the base Fulmin with superior damage scaling and critical statistics. The 34 percent critical chance in auto mode and 30 percent in semi-auto reward headshot-focused play. The 2.4x multiplier ensures that critical hits deliver meaningful damage spikes. The combination of impact damage and electricity opens build paths involving radiation (electricity plus heat) or magnetic builds (electricity plus cold), depending on mission requirements. Fulmin Prime's high fire rate in auto mode generates excellent status application opportunities, though its base 10 percent status chance in full-auto requires specific modding to maximize status effectiveness.

## Acquisition

Fulmin Prime components drop from Void Fissure void relics in current rotation. The barrel component typically appears in Axi Z2 and Lith G10 relics. The stock and receiver components distribute across other relic sets. Players can farm void fissure missions to acquire relics, then run them in cooperative void fissure public squads. Alternatively, the components can be traded between players in the marketplace. The prime variant launched alongside the Fulmin's introduction and remains in active rotation, making it one of the more accessible prime weapons for newer players who have access to void fissure progression.

## Variants

The original Fulmin serves as the base weapon with identical mechanics but lower damage scaling. The Kuva variant, Kuva Fulmin, exists as an alternative endgame option with selectable element types. Fulmin Prime stands out for its raw damage and critical superiority, making it the preferred choice for critical-focused builds. The weapon category unifies these three around the dual-fire mechanic, meaning all variants share the mode-switching playstyle while differing in numerical output.

## Recommended builds

Serration provides foundational damage for all primary weapons and synergizes excellently with Fulmin Prime's high fire rate. Point Strike and Vital Sense maximize critical effectiveness, pushing the auto mode critical chance above 100 percent with reasonable investments. For elemental builds, High Voltage and Heated Charge create radiation (against shields), or add a toxin mod for viral builds in conjunction with a melee weapon carrying slash emphasis. Magazine mods like Vital Sense or Split Chamber extend kill potential and quality of life. Headshot-focused players benefit from Precision mods and scope overlays that emphasize the weapon's inherent accuracy. The weapon's mode flexibility allows situational modding: stack crit mods for auto-mode spam or reload mods if semi-auto charging appeals to the player's playstyle.

## Notes

Fulmin Prime's dual-fire mechanic creates a learning curve for new users, but the flexibility justifies the mastery requirement. The weapon excels in organized squad play where cover allows mode-switching without penalty. Its electricity element aligns well with Corpus missions, making it a natural choice for void fissure farming. The high fire rate in auto mode generates excellent ammo economy when combined with mods, allowing extended engagements without reloading. The semi-auto mode, while less appealing in raw DPS, provides safety options and precision targeting for skilled players.
