## Overview

Amesha is a support-focused Archwing introduced in Update 22.20. Rather than offensive firepower, Amesha excels at sustaining the squad through protective drones and damage conversion mechanics. It is the primary choice for players seeking to maximize survivability in high-level Archwing missions, particularly void fissure Archwing disruptions and elite squad content.

The frame represents the pinnacle of defensive Archwing design, trading raw damage output for unparalleled battlefield control and ally protection. Health at 650 and armor at 195 provide a robust foundation, while 220 shield and energy allow extended ability uptime. Every ability in Amesha's kit contributes directly to team survival rather than personal damage.

## Abilities

Watchful Swarm deploys interceptor drones that absorb incoming fire, each shot consuming one drone charge until all 20 charges deplete. This ability fundamentally changes enemy engagement: rather than dodging, Amesha can fly directly at targets and let the drones handle incoming projectiles.

Benevolent Decoy creates beacon drones that draw enemy fire and convert absorbed damage into healing pulses for nearby allies. The heal radius of 12 meters ensures squad coverage in most mission layouts. When properly spaced, multiple beacons create redundant healing sources.

Warding Grace renders Amesha and nearby allies immune to status effects while slowing all nearby enemies for the duration. This defensive utility prevents stuns, staggers, and other crowd control from disrupting squad offense. Combined with other frames' crowd control, Warding Grace ensures consistent uptime.

Vengeful Rush converts all damage taken into energy for Amesha while granting enhanced ability range, duration, and strength to the frame and nearby allies. This transforms enemy aggression into team buffs: taking damage heals both Amesha and squad through energy generation.

## Acquisition

Amesha is researched in the Clan Dojo Tenno Lab. The blueprint and component blueprints (Harness, Systems, Wings) drop from Void Fissure Archwing Void Cascade missions at escalating void levels. Individual component drops are time-gated by void fissure rotation, requiring multiple mission runs to collect all three. Clan members must unlock the blueprint research before they can craft their own copy, adding a coordination requirement for newer players joining research-invested clans.

Build time is 12 hours, a standard Archwing craft duration. Nitain Extract cost of 4 units reflects late-game crafting material inflation. Players farming other frames should prioritize Amesha research once void fissure access is available.

## Modding Notes

Amesha benefits from ability range (Stretch, Augur Reach) to maximize healing radius and crowd control range. Ability duration scaling increases Warding Grace immunity window and Vengeful Rush buff uptime. Flow and Primed Flow extend energy pool, enabling longer ability spam before energy starvation. Redirection enhances the shield pool, and Vigor adds health for survivability.

Optimal Amesha builds prioritize range and efficiency over strength, though some strength scaling improves healing numbers and damage conversion. Umbral mods are not recommended; Amesha lacks the ability to leverage strength scaling as effectively as other frames.

## Playstyle

Fly directly into enemy formations with Watchful Swarm active. Deploy Benevolent Decoy beacons in defensible positions where the squad will cluster during objectives. Spam Warding Grace on rotation to prevent status-based disruptions. Use Vengeful Rush as a panic button when overwhelmed: the damage-to-energy conversion sustains the ability loop indefinitely in sufficiently dense enemy populations.

Amesha excels in wave-based content (Defense, Disruption, Void Fissure) where predictable enemy patterns enable positioning for optimal beacon placement. In mobile missions (Capture, Rescue), Amesha's abilities are less impactful because the squad is constantly moving away from beacon placement zones.

## Synergies

Amesha pairs exceptionally well with damage frames (Mesa, Saryn, Protea) in cooperative Archwing content. While Amesha provides no direct damage buffs, the unlimited survivability allows damage dealers to focus entirely on offense without managing defensive resources. Frost, Trinity, and Harrow all stack additional crowd control and healing, creating overstacked protection that trivializes elite Void Fissures.

Void Fissure squad compositions often center around one Amesha support, two damage dealers, and one utility/CC frame. Amesha enables this composition by removing defensive pressure from other squad slots.

## Lore

Amesha was designed as the Orokin's answer to sustained void exposure. The protective drones and energy conversion mechanics reflect Orokin understanding of void corruption: converting hostile energy into benevolent healing. Amesha's presence in void fissures is so iconic that newer players often recognize it before learning the frame's name -- it is the frame that enables their squads to survive Void Fissure Cascade, the endgame equivalent of infinite scaling.
