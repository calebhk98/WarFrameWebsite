## Overview

Okina Prime is the prime variant of the Okina dual daggers, featuring a crit-forward profile with 30% critical chance and 2.6x critical multiplier -- among the stronger crit values in the dual dagger category. The damage distribution emphasizes puncture at 110.4, with meaningful slash at 64.4 and minimal impact at 9.2 per hit. The puncture-heavy profile is effective against Grineer armor, while the slash component enables bleeding proc builds. At 1.17 attack speed and 1.7-meter range, the weapon operates as a fast, close-range melee option suited to aggressive play.

The 24% status chance is below average for dedicated status builds, making elemental mods less efficient per-hit than on higher-status weapons. However, the 30% critical chance pairs well with Blood Rush scaling through the dual dagger stance combo system, and slash procs from the 64.4 slash base trigger reliably with the right mod configuration. Mastery rank 12 provides accessible prime entry for mid-to-late star chart players.

## Acquisition

Okina Prime components drop from Void Relics. The blueprint, blade, and ornament components are distributed across different relic types at varying rarities. Player trading through [warframe.market](https://warframe.market) provides an alternative. Check current relic rotations for the appropriate farming nodes.

## Variants

The base [Okina](/weapons/okina) provides lower mastery rank access with reduced critical and damage statistics. Okina Prime is the recommended version for players prioritizing performance, as the improved crit values meaningfully change how the weapon scales with Blood Rush builds.

## Recommended builds

Given the 30% crit chance and 2.6x multiplier, critical builds are the most efficient use of available mod slots.

Crit-primary build:
- Pressure Point for base damage
- Sacrificial Steel or Primed Fever Strike for critical chance boost
- Blood Rush to scale critical chance with combo counter -- reaches near-100% at moderate combo levels
- Organ Shatter for critical damage multiplier
- Berserker Fury for attack speed scaling on critical hits
- Drifting Contact to sustain combo duration between kills

Slash-proc hybrid build:
- Retain crit mods and add Weeping Wounds for status chance scaling with combo counter
- At high combo levels, both Blood Rush and Weeping Wounds push crit and status into dominant territory simultaneously
- Viral or Corrosive elemental mods benefit slash proc targets where bleeding bypasses armor scaling on health pools

At 1.7 meters, positioning within melee range is more demanding than with longer-range melee options. Companion abilities or warframe crowd-control tools that pull enemies into close range reduce this limitation in practice.

## Notes

Dual daggers use Gnashing Payara and Pointed Wind stances. Both offer rapid strike patterns that build combo counter quickly, important for Blood Rush builds relying on combo multiplier scaling. Pointed Wind generally provides more efficient DPS through faster hit patterns; Gnashing Payara offers more crowd-oriented sweep attacks.

The Okina Prime's 1.7-meter range is shorter than most swords or polearms. The fast attack speed (1.17) compensates by enabling rapid repositioning and target swapping within the short reach window. Players preferring longer reach should examine dual swords or equivalent categories.
