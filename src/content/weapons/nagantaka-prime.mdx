## Overview

Nagantaka Prime is the prime variant of the Nagantaka crossbow, carrying a strong puncture focus with 155.7 puncture per bolt far exceeding the 15.6 slash and 1.7 impact per shot. The 39% status chance is the defining mechanical feature -- combined with 2.5 rounds per second fire rate, the weapon applies status procs at a high cadence, making it one of the more status-efficient crossbows available. The 25% critical chance and 2.3x multiplier support hybrid builds, though status-primary configurations typically produce more consistent output against armored targets.

Crossbows in Warframe fire projectile bolts with finite travel velocity rather than hitscan beams, requiring players to lead moving targets at range. The Nagantaka Prime's 40 accuracy value is moderate, suited to medium-range engagements. The 2.3-second reload applies between shot sequences. At mastery rank 12, it provides prime-tier access earlier than many equivalent primary weapons.

## Variants

The base [Nagantaka](/weapons/nagantaka) provides lower mastery rank access with reduced damage and status chance. The Prime version improves on all key statistics and is the recommended form for serious build investment.

## Recommended builds

The 39% status chance is the weapon's primary build driver. With 2.5 rounds per second, status procs accumulate rapidly, enabling Condition Overload or elemental status synergy builds.

Corrosive status build:
- Serration for base damage scaling
- Split Chamber for multishot -- each additional projectile independently procs status
- Infected Clip (toxin) and High Voltage (electricity) for Corrosive combined element
- Galvanized Aptitude for stacking damage bonus as status types accumulate
- Vigilante Armaments for additional multishot and set bonus

For Viral (Cold + Toxin) against health pools:
- Replace Corrosive elements with cold and toxin mods
- Viral halves effective enemy health for 6 seconds on proc
- Effective against Grineer or Infested when armor stripping is handled separately

Critical hybrid builds benefit from Point Strike pushing the 25% base toward 50%, with Critical Delay in the exilus slot if the fire rate reduction is acceptable at 2.5 rounds per second.

## Notes

Crossbow projectiles travel at finite velocity, requiring leading on fast-moving targets beyond close range. At typical indoor mission engagement distances this is negligible; in open-area exteriors against moving enemies, bolt lead time becomes a practical skill factor.

The Nagantaka Prime performs well in sustained Grineer-heavy content -- Void Armageddon fissures, Arbitrations, and Steel Path survival missions -- where its 39% status chance translates directly into reliable Corrosive proc application and subsequent Condition Overload damage scaling.
