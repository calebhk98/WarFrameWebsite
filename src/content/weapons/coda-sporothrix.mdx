## Overview

The [Coda Sporothrix](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Coda_Sporothrix) is a Jade Shadows primary rifle at Mastery Rank 17 with exceptional status output. It deals 376 total damage per shot (102 impact, 158 puncture, 117 slash) with a 55% status chance -- among the highest available for primary weapons. The 5% critical chance and 3.0x multiplier suggest critical investment is not worthwhile. The 55% status chance at 1.83 fire rate creates consistent, reliable proc application per shot.

## Variants

The Coda Sporothrix is the enhanced Jade Shadows form of the Sporothrix. The base Sporothrix has lower status chance. The Coda version delivers peak status output for this weapon type.

## Recommended builds

**Maximum status build:** Serration, Split Chamber, Vigilante Armaments, Galvanized Aptitude, and four elemental mods for viral, corrosive, or heat combinations. At 55% status chance, each shot delivers at least one guaranteed proc in most cases. Elemental mods ensure the proc type applied is tactically useful rather than random physical.

**Viral corrosive combination:** Fever Strike plus North Wind (viral) and Cryo Rounds plus Rime Rounds (cold to reinforce). Adding Stormbringer (electric) plus Infected Clip (toxin) for corrosive fills remaining slots. With four elemental mods, the 55% status chance distributes procs across three distinct types per shot cycle.

**Armor strip build:** Focus corrosive and heat exclusively. At 55% status, corrosive procs accumulate within 2-3 shots on a single target, stripping Steel Path armor in seconds. Heat adds ongoing damage-over-time. This configuration works across Grineer and Corrupted content.

## Synergies

- **Condition Overload melee:** The Coda Sporothrix's multiple-status delivery primes melee targets rapidly. Switch to melee after landing two shots for full Condition Overload bonus activation.
- **Galvanized Aptitude:** Provides additional status chance stacking with kills. With 55% base, Galvanized pushes effective status into near-guaranteed territory.
- **Rhino's Roar:** Each shot's 376 damage benefits from the multiplicative amplification, pairing high-damage output with consistent status application.

## Notes

- The fire rate of 1.83 is moderate for a primary. Each shot requires deliberate trigger discipline to maximize accuracy and status proc value. Spray firing wastes ammunition and does not improve proc rate proportionally.
- Magazine of 11 rounds at 1.83 fire rate provides approximately 6 seconds of fire before reload. Plan reloads during movement transitions.
- Mastery Rank 17 investment is justified by the 55% status chance -- this level of proc reliability is unmatched in the primary category for semi-auto rifles.

Coda Sporothrix's 55% status chance is exceptionally high among rifles, enabling near-guaranteed proc stacking on every shot. Combined with multishot mods, each trigger pull applies multiple independent status checks, making it among the fastest status applicators in the primary weapon class. This makes it particularly effective in Arbitrations and Steel Path where health scaling favors status-based damage.
