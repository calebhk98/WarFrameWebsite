## Overview

The [Cronus](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Cronus) is a sword melee weapon with 106 total damage per attack. Its damage splits across puncture (58.3), impact (31.8), and slash (15.9). As a Mastery Rank 0 sword, the Cronus serves as an early-game melee weapon that introduces the one-handed sword category and its associated stance mods. The 18% status chance and 6% critical chance are low by modern standards, reflecting the weapon's introductory tier.

The Cronus carries significance as a craft reward or early-game weapon with historical roots in the game. Riven disposition at the maximum tier (5/5) means rivens for this weapon consistently roll with high multipliers, giving the Cronus surprising endgame ceiling if riven-invested. Single-handed swords access stance mods such as Crimson Dervish, Vengeful Revenant, and Iron Phoenix, providing different combat patterns for players to explore.

The Cronus is primarily a progression weapon for players learning the melee system before transitioning to weapons with stronger inherent stats.

## Acquisition

The Cronus is obtained as a crafting reward from completing the Jackal assassination mission on Venus (Fossa). The blueprint drops from the Jackal boss, making it one of the first boss-drop weapons accessible to new players. Crafting the Cronus requires materials available from Venus and Mars. Alternatively, the completed weapon can be purchased from other players or the Market with platinum.

## Variants

No Prime, Vandal, or Wraith variant of the Cronus exists. For players who want to continue with sword melee weapons, the Galatine, Dakra Prime, or Reaper Prime offer substantially stronger stats. The Broken War (see [Broken Scepter](/weapons/broken-scepter)) is a related sword with superior critical and status profiles.

## Recommended builds

**Early game status build:** Pressure Point, Fever Strike, North Wind, Shocking Touch, and Volcanic Edge with a sword stance. This entry-level configuration delivers elemental procs consistently against early star chart enemies and works within the limited mod slot access of a newly-ranked player.

**Riven-powered build:** With a strong riven (critical chance + critical damage, or status chance + damage), the Cronus becomes a functional mid-game weapon. The 5/5 riven disposition means riven rolls are excellent. Combine with Pressure Point, Blood Rush, and Organ Shatter for a compact critical build that leverages the riven's high multipliers.

**Mastery fodder approach:** Equip any four available mods to accelerate leveling. The Cronus reaches rank 30 quickly, contributing Mastery Experience before the player transitions to a higher-tier melee weapon.

## Synergies

- **New player progression:** The Cronus pairs well with early-game Warframes like Volt or Excalibur while players learn the melee system. Its single-handed sword category is the widest sword stance pool in the game, offering multiple stance choices.
- **Riven investment:** A 5/5 disposition riven makes the Cronus a potential endgame melee weapon with the right roll. Critical chance combined with critical damage can push the weapon into reliable crit territory despite its 6% base.
- **Excalibur's Exalted Blade:** Excalibur's signature ability replaces melee weapons, but the Cronus can be equipped to satisfy Mastery Rank requirements while Exalted Blade handles damage.

## Notes

- The puncture-heavy physical split (55% of total damage) provides a modest advantage against Ferrite Armor but offers no benefit against shields or light armor. Elemental additions are essential for efficient damage output.
- Mastery Rank 0 means any player, including accounts in their first session, can equip and build the Cronus. It functions as an effective Mastery Experience source for new accounts.
- The Cronus does not have a signature Warframe or unique passive. Its mechanics follow standard one-handed sword behavior.
- Historical note: The Cronus is among the earliest melee weapons available in the game's progression path, predating many quality-of-life additions to the melee system. Current players typically use it for Mastery Experience before moving to stronger alternatives.
