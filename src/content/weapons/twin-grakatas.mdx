## Overview

The Twin Grakatas is a dual-rifle secondary available at Mastery Rank 9, requiring 30,000 credits from the Market. It fires at 20.0 rounds per second -- one of the highest fire rates available for a secondary weapon. Its 10 damage per round distributes across 4 impact, 2.67 puncture, and 3.33 slash. Per-shot damage is low, but the extreme fire rate means damage-per-second accumulates rapidly. At 20 rounds per second with multishot mods, the Twin Grakatas delivers a staggering volume of projectiles.

The 25% critical chance and 2.7x critical multiplier are the Twin Grakatas's high-value stats. The 2.7x multiplier is above average for secondaries. With Primed Pistol Gambit and 25% base crit, each shot has roughly 75% crit chance -- the high fire rate converts that probability into consistent orange-crit output. The 16.5% status chance is moderate; the fire rate compensates by applying procs through sheer volume.

The 60-round magazine at 20 fire rate lasts 3 seconds before the 3.0-second reload. Magazine-to-reload ratio management is essential -- the Twin Grakatas fires through its ammunition very quickly.

## Acquisition

The Twin Grakatas blueprint is purchasable from the in-game Market for 30,000 credits. Build time is 12 hours. No special unlock is required beyond Mastery Rank 9.

## Variants

No Prime, Vandal, or Wraith variant exists for the Twin Grakatas.

## Recommended builds

**Max crit rate**: Hornet Strike, Barrel Diffusion, Primed Pistol Gambit, Primed Target Cracker, Lethal Torrent, and two elemental mods. Primed Pistol Gambit at 25% base reaches near-full crit. The 2.7x base multiplier with Primed Target Cracker amplifies every crit substantially. Lethal Torrent increases fire rate beyond the already extreme 20 rounds/s.

**Viral sustained**: Cold plus toxin for Viral, Barrel Diffusion, Hornet Strike, Primed Pistol Gambit, Lethal Torrent. At 20 fire rate, Viral procs apply within 1 second. Viral halves max health; the volume of fire at 20+ rounds/s then shreds the halved health pool.

**Ammo efficiency**: Ammo Drum (magazine size) or Wildfire for extended sustained fire. Magazine capacity mods extend the firing window before the 3-second reload becomes necessary.

## Notes

The 3-second reload at a 3-second magazine duration means the Twin Grakatas fires and reloads in equal time intervals. Effective use requires anticipating the reload window -- start the reload before entering the next enemy group rather than reloading mid-engagement. Accuracy at 28.6 is moderate; close to medium range is the effective zone. At 20 fire rate, full-auto spray at long range wastes a significant portion of the magazine.
