## Overview

The [Coda Mire](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Coda_Mire) is a Jade Shadows club melee weapon at Mastery Rank 17. It deals 235 total damage per attack including 79 innate toxin alongside 45 impact, 66 puncture, and 45 slash physical damage. The 40% status chance enables rapid proc accumulation. The 18% critical chance and 2.4x multiplier support hybrid builds. Club weapons provide a concussive mechanic that staggers enemies, adding crowd control alongside the damage output.

The built-in toxin bypasses Corpus shields, delivering damage directly to health in Corpus missions without elemental mod investment for bypass.

## Acquisition

The Coda Mire is obtained through the Jade Shadows update. Check the [Coda Mire Wiki page](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Coda_Mire) for current drop sources and acquisition paths, as Jade Shadows content rewards may be mission-specific or tied to standing progression.

## Variants

The Coda Mire is the enhanced Jade Shadows variant of the Mire. The base Mire (Mastery Rank 5) has lower status chance and no enhanced Jade Shadows stat profile. The Coda version represents the endgame upgrade.

## Recommended builds

**Status viral build:** Primed Pressure Point, Berserker Fury, Condition Overload, Weeping Wounds, Blood Rush, Organ Shatter, and cold mods (Frostbite). Built-in toxin plus cold mods form viral, halving enemy health. The 40% status chance accelerates Condition Overload stacking within the first swing cycle. Blood Rush uses the 18% base crit to reach viable yellow-crit at moderate combo.

**Toxin Corpus build:** Add cold mods (Frostbite) to form viral, bypassing Corpus shields and reducing health simultaneously. The built-in toxin ensures each hit already bypasses shields before the cold component halves health. This combination is highly effective in Corpus endless missions.

**Heavy attack build:** Corrupt Charge, Amalgam Organ Shatter, Killing Blow. Club-category heavy attacks deliver powerful overhead slams that stagger nearby enemies. With high base damage plus innate toxin, single heavy attacks eliminate standard enemies efficiently.

## Synergies

- **Condition Overload:** Built-in toxin plus cold mods (viral) plus heat form three distinct status types rapidly. Condition Overload reaches full bonus within a short engagement, multiplying per-hit damage significantly.
- **Saryn's Spores:** Toxin procs spread through Spores, extending the effective AOE of the Coda Mire's toxin application across entire rooms.
- **Club concussion mechanic:** Club weapons stagger enemies on impact, delaying their attacks. Combined with fast attack speed (1.08), the concussion effect is applied repeatedly, making targets hesitate during engagement.

## Notes

- Built-in toxin pairs with cold mods to form viral (not corrosive). Players targeting corrosive for armor strip should add electric plus toxin mods on top of the built-in toxin, using the built-in for viral and the mod-added toxin for corrosive (with electric). This elemental layering allows the Coda Mire to deliver multiple element types from a single melee weapon.
- The 2.7 m range is competitive for a club-category weapon. Wide-arc combo animations from club stances (Shattering Storm) connect against multiple enemies within this range.
- Attack speed 1.08 is above the club category average. Berserker Fury pushes this further during kill streaks, enabling rapid Weeping Wounds stack buildup.
