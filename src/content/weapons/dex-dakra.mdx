## Overview

The Dex Dakra is a Dex-series anniversary dual sword with a Puncture-dominant damage profile: 113.6 Puncture versus 14.2 each of Impact and Slash. Dex weapons are distributed annually by Digital Extremes as a gift to celebrate the game's anniversary, typically in March. They are not obtainable through standard Market purchase or crafting. The 24% status chance with 16% critical chance positions the Dex Dakra as a status-oriented dual sword, while the 2.0x multiplier limits crit scaling potential. Attack speed of 0.883 is slightly below the dual-sword average; the 2.5 m range is standard. Mastery Rank 6 is required.

Dual sword stance mods (Crossing Snakes, Swirling Tiger, Gemini Cross) apply to the Dex Dakra, delivering multi-hit spinning combo animations. The Puncture dominance benefits from Corrosive elemental pairings against Grineer Ferrite Armor, as Puncture's armor penetration combined with Corrosive proc armor strip creates efficient damage delivery against armored targets. The anniversary origin makes the Dex Dakra a seasonal collectible in addition to a functional weapon.

## Acquisition

The Dex Dakra is a Warframe anniversary gift distributed annually by Digital Extremes. Log in during the anniversary event period (typically March) to claim the Dex Dakra and other Dex weapons. Check [the Dex Dakra Fandom wiki page](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Dex_Dakra) for the current anniversary date and distribution schedule.

## Variants

The Dex Dakra has no Prime, Kuva, or Tenet variant. Dex weapons are standalone anniversary items. Players seeking a stronger dual-sword option may consider the Dual Keres Prime or Dual Zoren Prime for improved critical stats.

## Recommended builds

**Corrosive/Puncture Grineer build.** The 113.6 Puncture base combined with Corrosive elemental mods strips Grineer Ferrite Armor through Corrosive procs, then delivers Puncture damage without mitigation. Add Primed Pressure Point and Condition Overload. The 24% status ensures consistent Corrosive proc application at the dual-sword fire rate.

**Status/Viral sustained build.** The 24% status supports Weeping Wounds scaling at higher combos. Equip two Viral 60/60 elemental mods, Primed Pressure Point, Condition Overload, and Weeping Wounds. Viral weakens enemy health while Condition Overload multiplies damage per unique status active on target.

**Hybrid crit/status build.** Equip Blood Rush alongside Weeping Wounds. At x4 combo, both crit chance and status chance scale simultaneously. Add Organ Shatter for crit multiplier and two Viral 60/60 mods. This maximizes the Dex Dakra's dual scaling without requiring heavy investment in either path exclusively.

## Synergies

Frames that group enemies (Vauban, Khora) maximize dual-sword sweep hit counts against multiple targets. Rhino's Roar amplifies all physical damage equally, benefiting the Puncture-dominant base. Naramon's Power Spike extends combo duration, sustaining Blood Rush and Weeping Wounds scaling across prolonged engagements.
