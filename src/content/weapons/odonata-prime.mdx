## Overview

Odonata Prime is the vaulted prime variant of the Odonata Archwing, released in Update 20.7 (April 2017) alongside Sari's signature weapon. As the Orokin-optimized version of the balanced starter frame, Odonata Prime retains the signature ability set (Energy Shell, Disarray, Seeking Fire, Repel) while improving every baseline stat. Health increased to 468, shield to 473, armor to 110, and energy to 93 enable longer sustained engagements compared to the base Odonata.

Odonata Prime represents the endgame variant of the tutorial Archwing: players mastering base Odonata mechanics find Prime to be a direct upgrade with no fundamental ability changes. The stat improvements prove meaningful in high-level void fissure content and elite archwing sabotage missions where enemy scaling demands maximum durability.

The frame was vaulted on January 4, 2022, placing its relics in the legacy rotation with rarity brackets determined by era (Lith/Meso/Neo/Axi). Current availability depends on void fissure rotation and relic availability in player inventories.

## Abilities

Energy Shell provides an improved absorption threshold compared to base Odonata, handling more incoming damage per cast while maintaining identical mechanics. The increased absorption allows longer defensive windows before recast is required, critical in high-level void fissure disruptions where damage spikes are predictable.

Disarray functions identically to base Odonata but scales with Odonata Prime's improved energy pool, enabling more beacon spam before energy depletion. The defensive value is identical; the efficiency improvement comes from energy quantity, not mechanic optimization.

Seeking Fire missile volley benefits from increased energy availability, enabling more volleys per energy threshold compared to base Odonata. Higher energy capacity supports sustained missile spam in extended engagements.

Repel crowd control remains mechanically identical. The stat improvements provide minimal gameplay variance compared to base Odonata.

## Acquisition

Odonata Prime is obtainable exclusively through void relic farming. Players may find the blueprint in Axi/Neo/Meso/Lith relics corresponding to the frame's vault era. Void Fissure Disruption missions provide the primary avenue for consistent relic acquisition and radiant refinement. Component blueprints (Harness, Systems, Wings) are farmed identically to base Odonata, requiring multiple void fissure runs to collect all four parts.

Build time follows standard Archwing crafting patterns (12 hours per component, 1 hour for main blueprint). Resource costs match base Odonata requirements.

Relic farming efficiency varies based on squad speed and void fissure node selection. Competitive farming groups complete Void Cascade missions in sub-5-minute intervals, enabling 12+ runs per hour and reliable part acquisition over 1-2 farming sessions.

## Modding Notes

Odonata Prime's improved energy pool (93 vs. 85 base) enables longer ability spam uptime. Primed Flow benefits scale additively: 93 + Primed Flow bonus significantly improves sustained rotation efficiency. Ability duration scaling extends defensive ability windows. Ability strength increases Energy Shell absorption and Seeking Fire damage. Efficiency mods provide diminishing returns given the energy pool improvement.

Optimal Odonata Prime builds mirror base Odonata modding with reduced efficiency priority and increased duration/strength secondary scaling. Umbral mods remain low-value due to Odonata's limited strength scaling ceiling.

## Playstyle

Odonata Prime plays identically to base Odonata with extended ability uptime. Energy management remains the core consideration; the increased pool simply extends the rotation before downtime recovery. Newer players transitioning from base Odonata find the gameplay loop familiar, with stat improvements providing quality-of-life enhancements rather than mechanical changes.

## Synergies

Odonata Prime pairs well with energy-providing support frames (Trinity, Harrow, Amesha) in squad compositions. The improved energy pool means Odonata Prime scales better with support enhancements than base Odonata, creating stronger synergies with energy-refund builds.

## Lore

Odonata Prime represents the Orokin refinement of the original Tenno Archwing design. The ordnance systems and shielding technology were optimized through Orokin engineering principles, resulting in a frame that maintains design philosophy while achieving superior numeric performance. Odonata Prime's vault status reflects the update cycle that primes are retired periodically while remaining available through legacy relics.

## Vault Status

Odonata Prime vaulted on January 4, 2022. The frame is available exclusively through void relic farming from player-held relics. Future unvaulting is speculative; Orokin and vaulted timeline rotations determine accessibility.
