## Overview

Wolf Sledge is a Grineer hammer that leans heavily into impact and puncture damage, making it effective against the shield-dependent Corpus and armored Grineer units respectively. The weapon's 259 combined base damage places it among the top-tier hammers at Mastery Rank 7. Its 3.1-meter range, high for a hammer, and a 33% status chance make it a reliable status-focused option for mid-game and Steel Path content.

The Wolf Sledge was introduced as a signature weapon of the Wolf of Saturn Six boss encounter and later became available through the Market. Its Grineer industrial aesthetic -- a heavy alloy head on a reinforced haft -- conveys purpose-built brutality consistent with the faction's design philosophy.

As a hammer, Wolf Sledge benefits from stance mods that include ground-slam attacks. These slams radiate shockwaves dealing impact damage in an area, which synergizes with the weapon's already-high impact component. The 33% status chance makes Corrosive or Viral procs feasible within a small number of hits, providing armor-stripping utility that extends the weapon's effective damage output at higher enemy levels.

The 17% critical chance is relatively modest for a crit build, making this weapon better suited to status and hybrid builds than pure crit stacking. The 1.9x critical multiplier is below the threshold where critical builds truly outperform other approaches at MR 7, reinforcing the status-first philosophy.

## Acquisition

The Wolf Sledge blueprint is available from the in-game Market for 30,000 credits. Foundry build time is 12 hours. No uncommon crafting materials are required. For players who encountered the Wolf of Saturn Six event, the weapon may have been obtained as a direct drop during that limited-time encounter.

## Variants

Wolf Sledge has no prime variant as of the current game version.

## Recommended builds

**Status build:** Status chance of 33% scales effectively with Condition Overload. Combine Corrosive (Electric + Toxin) mods with Volcanic Edge or Thermite Rounds for a dual-proc setup. Apply Weeping Wounds to ramp status chance during combos. At 2x combo multiplier, status chance exceeds 60%, making each swing likely to land at least one proc. This build handles armored Grineer reliably at mid-tier Steel Path.

**Viral-slash approach:** Though Wolf Sledge's slash damage is low (7.8), adding two slash damage mods increases the raw slash component. Viral (Cold + Toxin) amplifies health damage by a factor that makes even modest slash bleeds punishing at scale. Condition Overload multiplies with each unique proc type, so maintaining viral and slash procs simultaneously generates strong sustained DPS. This build is more complex but competitive in extended Survival missions.

Combine either approach with Amalgam Organ Shatter for a minor speed buff, helping counteract the inherent slowness of hammer attacks and enabling faster combo buildup.

## Notes

- Wolf Sledge's best stance mod is Crushing Ruin, which provides wide horizontal swings and heavy slam attacks that use the weapon's range effectively.
- Ground slams from hammer stances also benefit from Seismic Wave arcane, which amplifies heavy attack radial damage.
- The floating decimal values in WFCD (e.g., 119.14001 puncture) are artifacts of floating-point storage in the data export and round to whole numbers in-game.
- Wolf Sledge was notably wielded by the Wolf of Saturn Six NPC, making it a thematic trophy weapon for players who completed the associated Nightwave season content.
