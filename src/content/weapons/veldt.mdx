## Overview

The Veldt is a semi-automatic scout rifle characterized by identical 22% critical chance and 22% status chance -- a genuinely balanced hybrid platform that rewards builds targeting either or both damage vectors. Its 90 total damage per shot (23.4 impact, 43.2 puncture, 23.4 slash) delivers even physical damage distribution with the puncture component providing meaningful Grineer armor mitigation. The 2.2x critical multiplier is high for a base-tier scout rifle.

The 3.67 fire rate is deliberate and controlled, characteristic of a semi-automatic rifle designed for target acquisition rather than spray patterns. Each trigger pull fires a single round, rewarding players who aim carefully. The 26-round magazine and 1.8-second reload provide acceptable sustained fire. Mastery Rank 8 places the Veldt in mid-game availability, after players have built a substantial mod collection.

The Veldt is thematically tied to the Plains of Eidolon open world -- a scout rifle suited for long-range engagements -- but its stats translate effectively to any mission type.

## Acquisition

The Veldt blueprint is purchasable from the in-game Market for 20,000 credits. Build time is 12 hours. No quest prerequisite applies.

## Variants

No Prime, Vandal, or Wraith variant exists for the Veldt. Players seeking a higher-tier scout-style primary should examine the Rubico for sniper upgrades or Tiberon Prime for a multi-fire-mode rifle.

## Recommended builds

**Crit-status hybrid**: Equip Serration, Split Chamber, Point Strike, Vital Sense, and Viral (cold + toxin) elemental combination. Hunter Munitions forces slash procs from critical hits, creating bleed dots that benefit from Viral amplification. Add Argon Scope for headshot-triggered critical chance stacking.

**Pure critical build**: Stack Point Strike, Vital Sense, Heavy Caliber, Serration, Split Chamber, and two elemental mods. With Point Strike, critical chance reaches near 66%, producing mostly orange crits with the 2.2x base multiplier.

## Notes

The Veldt's semi-auto trigger rewards trigger discipline. Firing in controlled singles at medium range eliminates spread accumulation. Argon Scope (critical chance on headshot kill) integrates naturally with the Veldt's deliberate fire cadence and 32 accuracy -- targeted shooting enables consistent headshot access.


The Veldt's design as a balanced crit-status primary at MR 8 means it occupies a flexible role in the loadout. Players who have not yet farmed Rivens or primed mods can build the Veldt effectively with standard mods (Point Strike, Vital Sense, Serration, Split Chamber, and 60/60 elemental dual-stats) and achieve meaningful crit and status outputs within the same build. This versatility is valuable for players who want a single primary capable of performing adequately in both crit-focused and status-heavy scenarios without rebuilding.

The 32% accuracy provides acceptable spread at medium range, making the Veldt more reliable at distance than low-accuracy assault rifles, though short of the precision snipers. Players who engage at 20-40 meters benefit from the 3.67 semi-auto fire rate, which enables deliberate target selection without the scatter of high-fire-rate automatics.
