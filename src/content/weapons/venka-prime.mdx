## Overview

The Venka Prime is the Prime variant of the Venka claws melee weapon, available at Mastery Rank 14. Its 188 total damage per hit is puncture-dominant: 141 puncture, 37.6 slash, and 9.4 impact. Claw weapons deliver rapid multi-strike combos, and the puncture weighting makes each strike effective against Grineer armor, which is the primary faction for puncture's penetration bonus.

The 32% critical chance and 2.6x multiplier are exceptional for a melee weapon. With Blood Rush at x3 combo, critical chance exceeds 90%, and the 2.6x base multiplier amplified by Organ Shatter produces very high crit damage per strike. The 24% status chance supports corrosive or viral elemental procs through dual-stat mods. Claw weapons hit rapidly in multi-strike combos, generating combo counter ticks quickly and enabling rapid ramp-up of Blood Rush's combo-dependent crit scaling.

At 1.8-meter range, claws require close engagement. Claw stances with rapid forward strikes enable quick penetration into enemy groups.

## Recommended builds

**Blood Rush crit**: Blood Rush, Organ Shatter, Primed Pressure Point, Weeping Wounds, and corrosive (electric + toxin) dual-stat mods. At x3 combo with Blood Rush and 32% base crit, the Venka Prime reaches near-100% crit chance. The 2.6x multiplier with Organ Shatter amplification produces very high crit damage per rapid multi-strike hit.

**Viral-crit Hunter Munitions**: Blood Rush, Organ Shatter, Primed Pressure Point, cold + toxin for Viral, Hunter Munitions. Hunter Munitions converts crits to slash procs; at near-100% crit rate, slash procs apply on almost every hit. Viral halves enemy max health while slash bleeds bypass armor.

**Corrosive armor strip**: Electric plus toxin dual-stat mods for Corrosive, Weeping Wounds, Blood Rush, Primed Pressure Point. Corrosive strips Grineer armor in 6 procs. At the claw's rapid hit rate with 24% status base and Weeping Wounds, armor strip completes within 2-3 seconds of engagement. After strip, 141 puncture deals full damage.

## Notes

Claw stance mods determine the combo string patterns and multi-strike timing. Shimmering Blight (if applicable to claws) or the Venka's native stance mod shapes how quickly the combo counter accumulates. The 1.8-meter range is the primary limitation -- positioning very close to targets is required for consistent contact. In content with heavy crowd control or knockback from enemies, maintaining claw range becomes challenging.
