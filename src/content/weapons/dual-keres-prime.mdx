## Overview

The Dual Keres Prime is the primed variant of the Dual Keres dual swords, offering 32% critical chance and 2.6x multiplier alongside 24% status chance and an attack speed of 1.25. The damage profile features Puncture (104.4) as the dominant physical type with meaningful Slash (54) and Impact (21.6) supporting components. Mastery Rank 14 gates access to this high-tier prime.

The 1.25 attack speed is above average for the dual-sword class, enabling faster combo generation and more frequent status application. The combination of 32% crit and 2.6x multiplier makes the Dual Keres Prime one of the stronger crit platforms in the dual-sword family, capable of reaching reliable orange-crit with Blood Rush scaling at high combo multipliers.

## Acquisition

The Dual Keres Prime blueprint and components are obtained through Void Relics. Check [the Dual Keres Prime Fandom wiki page](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Dual_Keres_Prime) for current relic availability and vault status. It may also be traded between players.

## Variants

The Dual Keres Prime is the primed version of the base Dual Keres. The Prime improves critical chance, critical multiplier, status chance, and attack speed across the board. No Kuva or Tenet variant exists.

## Recommended builds

**Blood Rush crit build.** The 32% base crit with 2.6x multiplier scales strongly with Blood Rush at high combo. Add Sacrificial Steel for additional base crit and Organ Shatter for the multiplier. Include Berserker Fury for accelerating attack speed further. Two 60/60 elemental mods for Viral complete the build.

**Weeping Wounds hybrid.** Pair Blood Rush with Weeping Wounds for simultaneous crit and status scaling. At x8 combo both reach high values, applying consistent Slash bleed procs alongside crit bursts. Condition Overload multiplies damage per unique proc applied.

**Steel Path sustained build.** For endgame Steel Path content, prioritize Arcane Fury (melee damage on crit) alongside the crit build. The Dual Keres Prime's fast attack speed and high crit chance trigger Arcane Fury reliably throughout sustained engagements.

## Synergies

Naramon Void Sling extends combo counter uptime between kills, critical for maintaining Blood Rush stacks. Valkyr's Warcry adds 40% attack speed, stacking with Berserker Fury for rapid combo generation. Rhino's Roar multiplies all physical damage types equally across the Dual Keres Prime's mixed profile. Mirage's Eclipse doubles applicable damage multipliers in high-light conditions, amplifying the Dual Keres Prime's per-crit output significantly. For survival missions where combo maintenance across multiple rooms is difficult, Arcane Avenger (guaranteed crit chance on receiving damage) provides crit sustain during periods when the combo counter has not yet scaled to peak Blood Rush values, ensuring consistent orange crits even at low combo stacks.
