## Overview

Hikou is a secondary throwing weapon that fires star-shaped projectiles in rapid succession. As a throwing weapon, it consumes carried ammunition rather than using a magazine in the conventional sense, and reloads quickly by cycling to a fresh set of prepared throwing stars. The low Mastery Rank 2 requirement makes Hikou accessible early in progression, and its puncture-dominant damage profile is effective against lightly armored enemies and unshielded health pools.

The weapon's high fire rate of 6.67 rounds per second and 0.75-second reload make it a continuous-pressure secondary, suited to sustained fire against single targets or small groups. Its 10% status chance can be leveraged with status-focused mods and elemental combinations for proc application, though the low 4% critical chance makes critical builds less rewarding unless heavily invested.

## Acquisition

Hikou's blueprint is available from the in-game Market for 15,000 credits. After purchasing the blueprint, the weapon is crafted in the Foundry using standard materials including Morphics, Plastids, Rubedo, and credits. The blueprint also has a rare drop chance from Duviri Endless Tier 6 (Normal) missions. Mastery Rank 2 is required to equip the weapon.

## Variants

Hikou Prime is the Prime variant, offering substantially improved critical chance and status chance over the base Hikou along with a larger ammo pool. Hikou Prime is obtained through Void Relic fissure missions. No Vandal or Wraith version of Hikou exists.

## Recommended builds

The 10% base status chance responds to investment with status-boosting mods like Pistol Pestilence and Scorch for elemental proc builds. Combining Heat and Electric produces Radiation for a crowd-control angle against armored enemies, while Toxin paired with Cold produces Viral to reduce enemy health pools by half. Steady Hands reduces recoil for more accurate sustained fire at longer distances.

For players focused on maximizing damage output rather than procs, Hornet Strike and Barrel Diffusion add base damage and multishot respectively. Multishot is particularly effective on Hikou given its rapid fire rate, as doubling the projectile count per throw significantly increases total damage application per second.

Lethal Torrent adds both fire rate and multishot, making it a high-value investment for builds that prioritize aggressive suppressive fire over targeted proc application.

## Notes

Hikou's 0.75-second reload is faster than most secondary weapons, making it forgiving in extended engagements where ammo runs low. The weapon is classified as a Throwing secondary and uses Throwing Knives ammo. Carrying extra ammo mutation mods or using the Ammo Drum exilus mod extends mission sustainability. Hikou Prime represents a significant stat upgrade and should be farmed when the Mastery Rank investment in base Hikou is complete.

Hikou was introduced as a Tenno star-based secondary and listed in WFCD as an alternative to Kunai with expanded ammo capacity and higher fire rate at the cost of lower damage per projectile. The Hikou requires Morphics, Plastids, and Rubedo from the Foundry in addition to credits. These materials are obtainable from mid-tier planets such as Mars, Phobos, Saturn, and Pluto. Players targeting efficient resource acquisition can farm Morphics from Phobos assassination missions or Mars survival rotations before returning to craft Hikou.

The low critical chance means Hikou rewards elemental and status investment more than critical scaling. Players who focus on elemental proc builds should pair Hikou with frames that amplify status effects: Saryn's Spores interact multiplicatively with Viral procs, and Nova's Molecular Prime increases all damage taken by affected targets, compounding the weapon's output without requiring the weapon itself to deliver high base damage. Against lower-level content where enemies do not require armor-stripping, Hikou's rapid fire rate maintains consistent pressure with minimal setup.
