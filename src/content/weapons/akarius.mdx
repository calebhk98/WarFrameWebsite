## Overview

The Akarius is a Mastery Rank 8 dual secondary pistol that fires homing rockets. Each rocket tracks nearby enemies after launch, detonating on contact and dealing Blast damage. The weapon combines a relatively low Critical Chance of 6% with a high Status Chance of 28%, making it suited to status-oriented builds rather than critical damage stacking. Its 10-round magazine and 3.4-second reload are longer than typical dual pistols, reflecting the launcher category.

The homing behavior distinguishes the Akarius from other launchers. Rockets actively seek the nearest enemy in their flight path, reducing the aiming precision required. In corridor-heavy tilesets the homing arc can navigate partial cover. The Akarius Prime variant exists and improves on the base statistics significantly.

## Acquisition

The Akarius blueprint is available from the in-game Market for credits. Components are crafted in the Foundry. The Akarius Prime is obtained through void relic farming when unvaulted.

## Variants

The Akarius Prime improves on base statistics across the board and is obtainable through the Void Relic system when unvaulted. Check the [Akarius wiki page](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Akarius) for Prime availability and current vault status.

## Recommended builds

Status builds prioritize Pistol Pestilence, Pathogen Rounds, and Barrel Diffusion to maximize status chance. Blast combined with Toxin creates Gas status procs, which deal area-of-effect poison damage suitable for group enemies. Thermite Rounds adds Heat for Viral combinations.

The homing mechanic means accuracy mods are less important; redirect that slot toward elemental damage or Hornet Strike for base damage scaling.

## Notes

- Rockets from the Akarius have limited range; targets beyond approximately 40m may not be reliably homed.
- Self-damage risk exists if a rocket detonates on a surface very close to the player.
- The Akarius Prime significantly improves status chance and damage; prioritize the Prime for endgame use.
- Status chance of 28% enables reliable proc application; a single elemental mod reaches near-guaranteed procs per shot.
- The homing mechanic is particularly effective in Defense and Mobile Defense mission types where enemies funnel toward a fixed objective; fire ahead of the choke point and rockets redirect automatically onto incoming waves.
- Gas combinations from Toxin plus Blast create lingering poison clouds that affect groups of enemies; this makes the Akarius excellent for corridor-based Corpus missions where enemies cluster tightly.
- At 28% base status chance with 4.25 fire rate, a player can apply multiple status procs per second -- relevant for builds leveraging Condition Overload or similar damage-amplifying mod synergies on the target.
- The Akarius Prime is the primary endgame recommendation for players who enjoy the homing mechanic, as it provides substantially improved base statistics across all damage and status categories.
