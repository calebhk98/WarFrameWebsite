## Overview

Zakti Prime is the Orokin-era upgrade of the Zakti injector pistol. It retains the base weapon's dart-and-cloud mechanic while significantly improving the status profile -- status chance rises from 20% to 42% -- and expanding the magazine from 3 to 8 darts. These changes transform Zakti from a niche crowd-control tool into a competitive status secondary capable of applying multiple elemental procs per engagement without constant reloading.

The 42% status chance per dart is high enough to guarantee at least one proc on most hits even before status mods are applied. When combined with multishot mods, each trigger pull fires multiple darts simultaneously, and the resulting gas clouds stack status procs rapidly across enemy groups. This makes Zakti Prime one of the more efficient status applicators in the secondary slot for players running condition-based damage builds.

The 8% critical chance and 1.8x multiplier remain low, confirming that Zakti Prime's design intent is status application rather than crit scaling. Players seeking a high-crit secondary should look elsewhere; players who want a secondary that generates procs quickly to fuel Condition Overload on their melee or Viral amplification for their primary will find Zakti Prime well-suited to that role.

The Mastery Rank 14 requirement reflects prime status -- higher than the base Zakti's MR 10, consistent with the prime tier requirement convention across the game.

## Acquisition

Zakti Prime components drop from Void relics. Check the current relic pool to identify which relic tiers contain the barrel, receiver, and blueprint. Farming the appropriate relics through Void fissure missions yields prime components. Assembling the full component set and purchasing the blueprint from the Market requires 15,000 credits and 12 hours of build time.

## Variants

Zakti Prime is the prime version of [Zakti](/weapons/zakti). It improves status chance, magazine capacity, and critical chance over the base variant.

## Recommended builds

**High-status area denial:** Barrel Diffusion and Lethal Torrent add multishot, increasing dart count per trigger pull to 4-5 darts. At 42% base status chance with multishot, landing 2-3 procs per burst is typical. Equip Pistol Pestilence and Pathogen Rounds for Corrosive, or Cold and Heated Charge for Viral. The resulting cloud area creates a sustained proc zone that applies multiple status types to enemies moving through it.

**Viral primer build:** Zakti Prime primes efficiently with Viral. Equip Frostbite and Virulent Scourge to apply both Cold and Toxin, combining into Viral on hit. With multishot at 42% base chance, a single burst into an enemy group applies Viral to most targets simultaneously. This primer role synergizes with a high-damage melee that benefits from the 200% health vulnerability that Viral provides against non-boss enemies.

**Corrosive stripping build for Steel Path:** Equip Pistol Pestilence and Pathogen Rounds to convert all damage to Corrosive. At 42% status chance with multishot, stripping armor from Steel Path enemies takes 5-7 stacks of Corrosive, achievable within 2 trigger pulls with multishot. This frees the primary weapon to deal full damage to unarmored health pools.

## Notes

- Zakti Prime's expanded magazine means fewer reloads during sustained engagements, an improvement over the base 3-dart Zakti that required frequent magazine cycling.
- The reload speed (1.0 second vs. 0.8 second for base Zakti) is slightly slower despite the larger magazine, a standard tradeoff in prime versions that increases capacity at a minor reload cost.
- Seeking Fury (or Pistol Gambit) does not meaningfully benefit Zakti Prime given the 8% base critical chance; mod slots are better allocated to status or multishot.
- Cloud lingering duration is unchanged from the base Zakti; the prime version benefits from the same duration and positioning strategies.
