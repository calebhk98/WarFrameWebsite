## Overview

Gunsen Prime is the vaulted prime variant of the Gunsen glaive, featuring enhanced base damage and crit stats compared to the standard version. Glaives are hybrid melee weapons that function as both thrown projectiles and melee arms; the Gunsen Prime inherits this dual nature while adding the polish and stat boosts typical of primes. The weapon excels at mid-range crowd control through thrown projectile harassment combined with close-range slash-heavy melee combos.

The glaive playstyle rewards positioning and timing rather than button-mashing. Throwing the glaive creates distance while damaging enemies, then meleeing fallen opponents with slam attacks or heavy attacks lands devastating slash procs. Gunsen Prime's 2.0x crit multiplier and 20 percent crit chance make it competent at crits without heavy investment, allowing hybrid builds that blend status and critical damage.

## Damage and effectiveness

Base damage (200 total) is moderate for a melee weapon, roughly equivalent to mid-tier heavy swords. However, glaives benefit uniquely from attack-speed mods; increasing attack speed decreases the projectile throw recovery time, enabling more throws per engagement. Pairing Gunsen Prime with attack-speed mods (Fury, Primed Fury) transforms the weapon from occasional thrown harassment into sustained projectile pressure.

The weapon excels at hit-and-run tactics: throw the glaive at an enemy group from range, close distance while the glaive recovers, perform heavy attacks or finisher combos on priority targets, then repeat. This playstyle keeps the player mobile while maintaining high damage output, making Gunsen Prime effective in tile sets with vertical movement or cover-to-cover navigation.

## Acquisition

Gunsen Prime blueprints drop from void relic void fissures, with the full set (blueprint, handle, disc) scattered across various relic tiers. Expected farm time is 15-20 void fissure runs for a complete set assuming reasonable relic luck. Crafting requires 45000 credits and moderate melee weapon resources (plastids, rubedo, etc.).

Alternatively, Gunsen Prime is periodically re-vaulted during Prime Resurgence events, allowing condensed farming windows where drop rates are elevated. Players unable to farm relics can wait for these events or purchase completed copies from player markets on warframe.market.

## Recommended builds

Gunsen Prime builds divide into pure crit, pure status, and hybrid approaches. A crit build stacks Primed Fury, Organ Shatter, Primed Pressure Point, and Bloodlust, reaching 80+ crit chance with 3-4x multiplier. Pure slash mods (Rending Strike, Cleaving Whirlwind) maximize the weapon's inherent slash bias. This configuration generates crits frequently enough that many enemies die from single heavy-attack slams, making Gunsen Prime feel like a one-shot weapon despite its moderate base damage.

A hybrid status-crit build replaces one crit mod with a status mod (Weeping Wounds), reaching 60+ percent status chance while maintaining 50+ crit chance. This approach leverages Gunsen Prime's reasonable stats in both damage types, enabling synergies with frames that amplify either crit or status (Rhino with Roar, Saryn with spore chains). The hybrid philosophy shines in endless missions where both consistent crits and sustainable status procs are valuable.

A pure status build emphasizing slash procs (Weeping Wounds, Bloodlust, all elemental slots filled with Cold/Toxin for Viral) reaches 90+ percent status chance, ensuring nearly every hit applies status. This approach transforms Gunsen Prime into a proc-stacking tool where individual damage matters less than the cumulative DoT from viral bleeds and slash procs.

## Synergies

Gunsen Prime synergizes exceptionally well with frames that amplify crit damage or projectile speed. Rhino with Roar buff provides raw damage amplification for both thrown and melee hits. Volt with Speed buffs attack speed and projectile speed, enabling faster throw cycles and harder-hitting projectiles. Wisp with attack-speed buffs multiplies Gunsen Prime's throw frequency, creating near-continuous projectile pressure on enemies.

Companion weapons with slash focus (whips, glaives themselves) layer additional proc stacking. A Companion set to use its own melee weapon increases the total projectile and melee damage output against grouped enemies.

## Lore

Gunsen Prime represents an older era of Tenno weaponcraft, refined through Orokin manufacturing before the empire's fall. Glaives themselves are hybrid tools that blur the line between melee and ranged combat, reflecting Tenno adaptability and versatility. The Gunsen Prime designation places it among the vaulted relics lost to the Void, making acquisition a hunt through fissure phenomena.

In the Warframe universe, glaives are associated with tactical combat and calculated strikes. The Gunsen Prime exemplifies this through its moderate-damage philosophy; it is not a one-shot weapon but rather a tool for positioning, control, and sustained pressure. Veterans appreciate its skill ceiling and reward-to-effort ratio.
