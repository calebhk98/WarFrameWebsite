Snipetron is a primary sniper rifle weapon that serves as classic Corpus-designed sniper rifle with slash-dominant damage; one of the older sniper rifles, now largely outperformed but historically significant as an early long-range option. Mastery Rank 0 is required to equip the weapon. Players who invest in building it properly will find it reliable across the star chart, with scaling potential on the Steel Path when supported by appropriate mods and Warframe ability synergies.
