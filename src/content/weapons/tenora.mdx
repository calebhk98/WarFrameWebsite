## Overview

The Tenora is Octavia's signature rifle, firing full-auto with a spooling mechanic -- sustained fire increases the rate of fire over time. Its base fire rate of 11.67 rounds per second accelerates after a brief sustained-fire period, rewarding players who hold the trigger through sustained engagements rather than tapping for single shots. This makes the Tenora best suited to mobile, aggressive combat where the trigger stays depressed.

With 24 total damage per round split roughly evenly between impact (7.2), puncture (7.2), and slash (9.6), the per-shot damage is modest. The Tenora's strength lies in volume: 150-round magazine capacity combined with a high fire rate allows sustained fire that benefits greatly from multishot mods. At 28% critical chance and a 2.0x multiplier, the weapon performs reliably in critical builds once Point Strike or similar mods push that chance above 50%.

The 16% status chance is workable for utility procs but not a primary build direction. Players typically modded the Tenora for raw critical output rather than status, though Viral priming via a secondary remains viable in squad play. Mastery Rank 10 positions the Tenora in mid-game, accessible once players have progressed through the second half of the star chart and acquired sufficient mod capacity.

## Acquisition

The Tenora blueprint is available from the in-game Market. The weapon can also be purchased fully built from the Market for platinum. Build time is 12 hours. No quest or mission unlock is required.

## Variants

**Tenora Prime** -- The Prime variant requires Mastery Rank 12 and improves on the base model with higher total damage, an improved critical multiplier, and a larger magazine. Tenora Prime components drop from Void Relics and are traded on the player market.

## Recommended builds

**Critical auto build**: Stack Point Strike (critical chance), Vital Sense (critical multiplier), Serration (base damage), Split Chamber (multishot), and a damage elemental combination such as heat + cold for Blast, or cold + toxin for Viral against Grineer. The 28% base critical chance reaches a reliable orange crit threshold with Point Strike at max rank. Hunter Munitions can be added if the slash-proc payoff justifies the slot.

**Galvanized build**: Equip Galvanized Chamber (multishot, stacks on kill), Galvanized Aptitude (status chance, stacks on kill), and Primary Merciless (damage, stacks on kill). The Tenora's fire rate enables rapid kill accumulation, keeping stacks at maximum during sustained engagements. Combine with heat + toxin for Gas or heat alone for crowd control utility.

## Notes

The Tenora synergizes directly with Octavia's Mallet ability, which scales with nearby sound volume. Sustained Tenora fire near an active Mallet dramatically increases the ability's damage output. Players who run Octavia regularly will find the Tenora's spooling fire rate creates a consistent audio loop that maximizes Mallet performance.

The high magazine capacity means Ammo Stock mods are less necessary than on smaller-magazine rifles. Vigilante Supplies can replace ammo economy mods, contributing to the set bonus that benefits primary weapons with Vigilante mods across the kit.
