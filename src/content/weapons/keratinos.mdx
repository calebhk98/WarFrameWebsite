## Overview

Keratinos is a Corpus-themed melee weapon in the fist weapon category. It is notable for having a built-in 33 Viral damage in addition to its physical damage distribution, making it one of the few weapons with intrinsic elemental damage straight from the foundry. The 29% status chance and 13% critical chance make it viable for status-focused builds that exploit the innate Viral proc alongside additional elemental mods. The puncture-heavy damage split also provides useful damage against armored targets.

The innate Viral damage effectively gives this weapon a three-element head start, as the Viral proc halves enemy health pools on application. Pairing an additional elemental mod for Corrosive or Heat covers two damage types simultaneously.

## Acquisition

Keratinos is crafted from a blueprint available through the in-game Market or dropped via mission rewards. Consult the current drop table for component drop locations. Construction requires Credits and components obtainable from Corpus-faction missions. Build time is standard for the weapon category.

## Variants

No Wraith, Vandal, or Prime variant of the Keratinos exists as of the current game version.

## Recommended builds

A Viral-amplification build uses Primed Fever Strike for additional Toxin and Virulent Scourge for Toxin, combining into Viral on top of the weapon's innate Viral damage. Weeping Wounds scales status further at combo, enabling reliable Viral stacks and a secondary elemental proc per strike. Condition Overload multiplies damage against status-affected enemies, making it the priority damage mod.

A crit-status hybrid using Blood Rush alongside one elemental mod covers Steel Path content well. The 2.1x critical multiplier provides meaningful burst when crits trigger, and the 13% base chance reaches reliable levels with Blood Rush at moderate combo counts.

## Notes

The innate Viral damage on Keratinos does not consume a mod slot, which is the weapon's principal advantage over other fist weapons. Players should not re-apply Viral through elemental mods; the innate Viral proc is sufficient, and mod slots are better used for other elemental types or crit mods. The 1.7 m range is standard for the fist category; Primed Reach can extend it if multi-target coverage is needed.
