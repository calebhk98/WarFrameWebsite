## Overview

Keratinos is an Infested claw melee weapon from the Deimos open world, notable for carrying innate Viral damage in addition to its physical damage distribution. The Viral element is built directly into the weapon without consuming a mod slot, giving Keratinos an immediate advantage over most melee weapons in status-oriented builds. Its 29% base status chance amplifies the value of this innate element, enabling reliable Viral proc application from the first hit of combat.

The weapon belongs to the Claws stance category, using rapid multi-hit attack animations suited to sustained close-range combat. Its puncture-dominant physical profile performs well against Grineer ferrite armor. The heavy attack mechanic extends the weapon's effective range and slam radius at max combo, as described in the in-game weapon description. Mastery Rank 9 is required to equip Keratinos.

The 33 Viral damage is effective at all armor levels. Viral procs reduce enemy maximum health by 50% for a limited duration, scaling with additional Viral proc stacks up to a cap. Combined with the puncture-dominant physical damage, Keratinos applies armor-bypassing pressure while simultaneously reducing health pool totals.

## Acquisition

Keratinos components are purchased from Father in the Necralisk on Deimos. Father is an Entrati syndicate vendor. The Blueprint, Blades, and Gauntlet component blueprints are all available at the Neutral standing tier, meaning no standing investment is required before purchasing them. Components and credits are required for Foundry construction. Mastery Rank 9 is required to equip the finished weapon.

## Variants

No Wraith, Vandal, or Prime variant of the Keratinos exists as of the current game version.

## Recommended builds

A Viral-amplification build adds Primed Fever Strike for Toxin damage and Virulent Scourge for additional Toxin, both combining with Keratinos's innate Viral to produce additional Viral elemental mod stacking. Because Viral procs reduce enemy health pools multiplicatively, stacking multiple Viral proc ranks before applying other damage types maximizes effective damage. Weeping Wounds scales status chance further at combo, enabling consistent Viral proc stacks alongside a secondary elemental type. Condition Overload multiplies base damage for each unique status effect active on the target, making it the priority damage mod.

A crit-status hybrid using Blood Rush alongside one elemental mod covers Steel Path content well. The 2.1x critical multiplier delivers meaningful burst when crits trigger, and the 13% base critical chance reaches reliable levels with Blood Rush at moderate to high combo counts. Pairing Weeping Wounds with Blood Rush creates an effective scaling loop over extended engagements.

For builds targeting specific factions, adding Cold to produce Magnetic against Corpus, or adding Heat for a Viral plus Heat combination against general armor, provides strong elemental coverage with a single additional mod slot.

## Notes

The innate Viral damage on Keratinos does not consume a mod slot, which is the weapon's principal advantage over other claw weapons. Players building for Viral proc application should not re-apply Viral through elemental mods; the innate proc is sufficient for stacking, and the mod slots are better used for other elemental types or critical mods. The 1.7 m range is standard for the claws category; Primed Reach can extend it if multi-target coverage is needed in dense enemy groupings. Stats sourced from WFCD warframe-items.
