## Overview

The Vesper 77 is a secondary pistol available at Mastery Rank 14, purchasable from the Market for 20,000 credits. Its 180 total damage per shot splits between 108 slash and 72 impact -- slash-dominant with a significant impact component. The slash weighting generates bleed procs that bypass armor, while the impact component deals stagger and bonus damage to Corpus shields. This combination covers two distinct faction weaknesses in a single weapon.

The 24% critical chance and 2.6x critical multiplier position the Vesper 77 as a strong crit secondary. At 24% base with Primed Pistol Gambit, critical chance approaches 80%, and the 2.6x multiplier (above the secondary average of 2.0-2.4x) amplifies each crit substantially. The 26% status chance is solid for a high-crit pistol, enabling elemental proc application on many shots. The 8-round magazine at 2.08 fire rate lasts about 3.8 seconds before the 2.35-second reload.

## Variants

No Prime, Vandal, or Wraith variant currently exists for the Vesper 77.

## Recommended builds

**Crit slash**: Primed Pistol Gambit, Primed Target Cracker, Hornet Strike, Barrel Diffusion, Galvanized Chamber, and Viral (cold + toxin) mods. Primed Pistol Gambit at 24% base reaches near 80% crit chance. The 2.6x multiplier with Primed Target Cracker produces strong per-shot crit damage. Viral halves max health; slash bleeds then tick through the halved pool bypassing armor.

**Status hybrid**: Galvanized Aptitude, Galvanized Shot, dual-stat 60/60 elemental mods for Viral plus Heat or Corrosive plus Heat, Hornet Strike, Barrel Diffusion. The 26% status at 2.08 fire rate applies procs reliably per shot. Galvanized Aptitude and Shot stack damage bonuses on proc kills.

**Slash Corpus**: Magnetic (cold + electric) elemental for Corpus shield bonus, Primed Pistol Gambit, Primed Target Cracker, Hornet Strike. The 108 slash plus 72 impact both deal relevant damage to Corpus (slash to health after shield, impact to shield). Magnetic procs reduce shield capacity.

## Notes

The 2.6x critical multiplier is the Vesper 77's primary advantage over similar-tier pistols. At the same crit chance, a higher multiplier means larger individual crit values. This makes the Vesper 77 especially effective when paired with headshot bonuses (Harrow's Covenant, Valence Formation) that further amplify crit damage. The 8-round magazine is small -- use precise semi-auto fire rather than rapid trigger spam to avoid running empty mid-engagement.


The Vesper 77's 2.6x critical multiplier distinguishes it from the majority of pistols with 2.0-2.4x multipliers at comparable stats. When combined with headshot bonuses (certain warframe abilities, arcanes like Arcane Avenger or Arcane Rage) and the Vesper 77's semi-auto trigger that rewards aimed shots, the per-shot damage output against high-value targets exceeds most similarly statted secondaries. This makes the Vesper 77 particularly effective as a boss-killing secondary where individual shot value matters more than sustained rate-of-fire.
