## Overview

The Ogris is a single-shot launcher firing explosive rockets in a slow, methodical pattern. It prioritizes area-of-effect impact over fire rate, excelling in clearing clustered enemies and controlling tight corridors. The weapon's high status chance of 35% enables explosive procs that trigger secondary detonations, turning each shot into a cascading chain of destruction. Its modest critical chance makes it a status-focused weapon, with builds centering on elemental damage multiplication and explosions rather than critical scaling. The Ogris remains a solid choice for crowd control in lower-level content and synergizes well with frames providing explosive-damage amplification.

## Damage

The Ogris concentrates its damage in the puncture component at 140.8, with smaller impact and slash contributions. Explosions generate area-of-effect damage with falloff, making placement crucial for maximum efficiency. The 35% status chance ensures frequent explosive procs that spread damage across grouped targets. Against single high-level enemies, the weapon struggles unless properly modded for elemental scaling. Heavy armor targets reduce explosive effectiveness without dedicated armor-stripping support, but the weapon shines in mid-level content against lighter enemy types.

## Acquisition

The Ogris blueprint is available from the market for 20,000 credits. Building the weapon requires an additional 20,000 credits and one full day of Foundry construction time. Like most market weapons, the Ogris is tradable only before construction, allowing player-to-player commerce of the blueprint component. The straightforward acquisition path makes the Ogris accessible early for players with sufficient credits.

## Variants

The Ogris Prime exists as an improved version with higher damage and better stats, obtained from void relic fissure farming. A Kuva Ogris variant does not currently exist. Players seeking superior performance should target Ogris Prime relics during fissure missions.

## Recommended builds

Elemental mods multiply the Ogris's already-high status chance and explosive potential. Serration provides the foundation, while elemental mods like Heat Damage and Blast Damage scale explosive effects through status procs. Multishot mods like Vigilante Armaments help overcome the single-rocket limitation by converting a single shot into multiple projectiles. Heavy Caliber slightly reduces accuracy but the weapon's loose aim reduces impact of the penalty. Primed mods and forma investments maximize endgame scaling.

## Synergies

Frames providing explosive-damage amplification pair excellently with the Ogris. Chroma's damage buff extends to explosions, significantly increasing each rocket's output during coordinated squad play. Saryn benefits from the high status chance to spread miasma procs across populations struck by explosive detonations. Nidus scales with the crowd control from explosions, stacking power through his parasitic link mechanic and enabling aggressive crowd-control tactics. Wisp provides temporary firepower boosts that synergize with the Ogris's controlled, deliberate pacing, amplifying burst damage during timed offensive windows. Hildryn converts explosive damage into shields through her aegis storm, creating synergistic defensive mechanics through offensive weapon application.

## Lore

The Ogris emerged from military applications of explosive ordinance, designed for use in tactical containment operations. Its slow fire rate reflects the careful logistics of ammunition management in field operations. Within Corpus hierarchies, the Ogris represents an evolution from earlier explosive platforms, emphasizing Area-of-effect coverage over rapid deployment. Players often view the Ogris as a stepping stone to higher-tier explosive weapons, serving as an introduction to status-focused explosive playstyles.
