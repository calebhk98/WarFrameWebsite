## Overview

The Aksomati Prime is the primed akimbo rifle, delivering high fire rate pistols with balanced damage. It combines the accuracy benefits of rifles with the flexibility of dual wielding. The 2 impact, 10 puncture, and 8 slash damage per shot balances physical damage components. The balanced distribution provides flexibility against different enemy armor types.

The Aksomati Prime's 13.33 fire rate enables rapid suppression and target acquisition at medium range. The 80 round magazine accommodates burst fire and sustained engagement without reload pressure. The 1.4 reload time is reasonable, encouraging confident trigger discipline. The combination of accuracy, fire rate, and magazine capacity creates practical secondary platform.

With 18% status chance, the Aksomati Prime supports hybrid builds combining raw damage with elemental effects. The weapon's balanced damage distribution makes it versatile against different armor types. This weapon bridges the gap between pure damage and utility through status application. Some players prefer status-focused builds using viral effects.

The Aksomati Prime represents a middle-ground secondary suitable for diverse mission types. The weapon's accessibility and straightforward effective range make it popular among players seeking reliable, all-purpose secondaries. The dual-rifle mechanic provides excellent accuracy compared to pure pistol secondaries.

The weapon's balanced stat distribution enables smooth scaling into mid-level content without requiring specialized mod knowledge. Forma investment creates capable all-around secondary suitable for diverse builds and mission requirements. Veterans appreciate the weapon's versatility.

The Aksomati Prime's popularity stems from accessible acquisition, practical performance.

## Acquisition

The Aksomati Prime is obtained through the Void Relic system. Relics containing component blueprints are available from Void missions during active vault periods. When the weapon enters the Vault, Relics drop from the standard pool only during Prime Resurgence events. Players can trade complete parts or the assembled weapon on warframe.market or in-game Trade Chat. The Mastery Rank 12 requirement means only mid-to-late progression players can equip it.

## Variants

The base **Aksomati** is a non-Prime variant with lower stats and Mastery Rank 9 requirement. The Prime version improves upon the critical multiplier and magazine capacity while maintaining the same fire-rate-focused character. Players who enjoy the dual rifle-pistol aesthetic may also consider Akstiletto Prime (better accuracy, lower fire rate) or Afuris Prime (higher status chance, similar fire rate).

## Recommended builds

**Crit Hybrid Build:** Point Strike (critical chance) combined with Vital Sense (critical multiplier) maximizes the 3.0x multiplier's value. At 24% base CC with Point Strike, reliable orange crits appear frequently. Hornet Strike and Barrel Diffusion cover base damage and projectile count. A Viral or Corrosive elemental combination rounds out the loadout for faction-specific optimization.

**Crit-Status Hybrid:** Replacing one elemental mod with Primed Pistol Gambit pushes CC to levels where yellow crits cascade into orange and red regularly. At 13.33 fire rate, status effects still apply at meaningful frequency even with 18% base SC. Weeping Wounds or Galvanized Crosshairs synergize well on sustained fire.

The weapon's 12.5 accuracy limits reliable engagement to close-to-medium range. Aggressive positioning or ability-assisted movement is recommended for maximizing hit rate at extended ranges.

## Notes

The "akimbo rifle-pistol" dual-wield animation is unique to the Aksomati line, offering rifle-style accuracy grip on pistol-format weapons. The resulting higher accuracy compared to pure pistol secondaries makes them reliable on moving targets at ranges where dual pistols typically struggle.

Ammo economy requires attention at 13.33 fire rate with standard secondary ammo reserves. Ammo Mutation mods or Carrier Sentinel's Ammo Case can prevent supply exhaustion in extended survival missions.
