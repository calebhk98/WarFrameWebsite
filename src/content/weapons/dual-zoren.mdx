## Overview

The Dual Zoren are twin blades with a sharply Puncture-focused damage profile (63 Puncture vs 3.5 Slash and 3.5 Impact) and a 3.0x critical multiplier at 25% critical chance. The 5% status chance is negligible, making this a pure crit weapon. The 1.167 attack speed and 2.3 m range are on the faster and shorter end for dual swords. Mastery Rank 2 makes this accessible to early-game players who want a crit-focused dual sword before reaching the Prime variant.

The Dual Zoren Prime is the natural upgrade with a 3.2x multiplier and 30% crit chance. The base Dual Zoren serves as the learning platform and Mastery Rank grind weapon for the crit-based dual-sword playstyle.

Dual sword stance mods (Crossing Snakes, Swirling Tiger, Gemini Cross) apply to the Dual Zoren, delivering multi-hit spinning combo animations that provide multiple crit roll opportunities per combo chain. The 3.0x multiplier means each orange-crit hit delivers high per-hit damage, and at higher combo levels with Blood Rush the damage output per swing compounds significantly. The 2.3 m range is above average for dual swords, providing slightly better multi-target sweep coverage than most weapons in this class.

## Variants

The Dual Zoren Prime is the primed version with improved critical multiplier (3.2x), critical chance (30%), and status chance (22%). It is acquired through Void Relics at Mastery Rank 13.

## Recommended builds

**Basic crit build.** True Steel (crit chance) and Organ Shatter (crit multiplier) with Primed Pressure Point and Berserker Fury. At 25% crit with True Steel, yellow crits occur reliably without Blood Rush investment. This simpler build suits early-game content.

**Blood Rush scaling build.** Sacrificial Steel and Blood Rush scale the 25% crit into high-crit territory at combo x6+. Add Organ Shatter and two elemental 60/60 mods. This requires combo counter maintenance but produces significantly higher peak damage.

**Speed-first build.** Berserker Fury and Primed Fury paired with the 1.167 base attack speed create a very rapid attack cadence. Combine with crit mods for consistent burst output. This suits fast-paced exterminate missions.

## Synergies

Valkyr's Warcry adds attack speed on top of the already-fast 1.167 base. Chroma's Vex Armor scales melee damage during sustained combat. Naramon Void Sling maintains Blood Rush stacks between kills in higher-tier content.

## Notes

- At MR2, the Dual Zoren provides a crit-oriented option earlier than most crit-focused melee weapons.
- Transition to the Dual Zoren Prime when Void Relics permit; the stat improvements are substantial.
