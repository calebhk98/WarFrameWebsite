## Overview

The [Balla](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Balla) is a Zaw strike component for dagger-class Zaw weapons, available from Hok the Artisan in Cetus on the Plains of Eidolon. Like all Zaw strikes, the Balla has no standalone stats -- its damage, critical chance, status chance, and attack speed are determined by the complete Zaw assembly (strike + grip + link).

The Balla strike creates a dagger-class Zaw, granting access to dagger-exclusive mechanics including the Covert Lethality mod (sets finisher damage to 2000) and dagger-specific stance mods such as Pointed Wind. Daggers are the premier finisher-build weapon category in Warframe, capable of instantly killing most enemies up to high-level content when combined with finisher-enabling Warframe abilities.

Riven disposition for Balla-based Zaws is 2/5, reflecting moderate use.

## Acquisition

Purchase the Balla strike from Hok the Artisan in Cetus using Ostron Standing. Ostron Standing is earned through Plains of Eidolon bounties and selling fish to Fisher Hai-Luk. Combine the Balla strike with a dagger grip (Peye grip for dagger-length, or Kroostra for a longer reach) and a stat-modifying link (Ekwana Jai for attack speed, Ruhang for damage, etc.). Build the assembled Zaw in the Foundry and gild it at Hok after reaching rank 30 to unlock the Zaw arcane slot and a free Stance Forma.

Check the [Balla Wiki page](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Balla) for current standing costs and link/grip compatibility options.

## Variants

The Balla is one of multiple dagger strikes available from Hok, alongside the Dokrahm (heavy blade) and other strike types. For alternative Ostron dagger strikes, compare the Dehtat strike for different stat contributions. No Prime form of any Zaw component exists.

## Recommended builds

**Covert Lethality finisher build:** Primed Pressure Point, Covert Lethality, Gleaming Talon, and elemental mods for faction weaknesses. Covert Lethality sets finisher damage to 2000 -- paired with a Warframe ability that creates finisher opportunities (Ivara's Sleep Arrow, Ash's Shuriken, Excalibur's Radial Blind), each finisher one-shots standard enemies up to high-level content. The Balla's dagger category grants access to this unique mechanic unavailable to any other melee weapon type.

**Status dagger:** Equip Weeping Wounds, Condition Overload, and elemental mods for viral. Scale the Balla's status chance with link selection (Ekwana II Jai link for status chance bonus) and Weeping Wounds stack growth.

**Exodia Epidemic arcane:** After gilding, equip Exodia Epidemic. This arcane causes finisher kills to release a viral explosion affecting nearby enemies. Combined with Covert Lethality's reliable finisher kills, Exodia Epidemic creates area-of-effect viral procs around every finisher kill.

## Synergies

- **Covert Lethality:** The defining mod for dagger builds. Every finisher performed with a dagger equipped deals exactly 2000 damage, bypassing armor and health scaling. This enables one-shot finisher kills on elite enemies that cannot be killed in one regular melee swing.
- **Ivara's Sleep Arrow:** Sleeping enemies are permanently vulnerable to finishers until they wake or die. Sleep Arrow + Covert Lethality Balla creates an efficient stealth kill loop effective in spy missions and alert-reduction stealth runs.
- **Zaw arcanes (Hok's shop):** Exodia Epidemic (viral explosion on finisher), Exodia Triumph (health orb on finisher), and others provide passive effects uniquely tied to Zaw weapons. These arcanes are unavailable on non-Zaw weapons.

## Notes

- Zaw weapons display the player's chosen custom name after gilding, not "Balla." The WFCD entry uses the strike component name for reference.
- Dagger range (Peye grip) is short (approximately 1.0-1.5 m). Finisher animations close to point-blank range automatically, but open-combat swings require positioning within arm's reach of targets.
- The Balla's dagger category provides access to Pointed Wind stance, which has animations that create brief surges of mobility alongside the attack. For finisher-focused builds, stance choice matters less than Covert Lethality and the finisher-enabling ability setup.
- Gilding is required to unlock the Zaw arcane slot. Rank the Balla-based Zaw to 30 before gilding -- the process resets it to rank 0, granting a free Stance Forma in compensation.
