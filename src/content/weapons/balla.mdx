## Overview

Balla is a Zaw blade component available from Hok's Anvil in Cetus on the Plains of Eidolon. As a Zaw component, the Balla does not have standalone combat statistics; its contribution to the finished weapon depends on the full three-part Zaw assembly (Strike, Grip, and Link). The Balla strike component provides a specific damage split and scaling parameters that combine multiplicatively with the chosen Grip and Link to produce the final assembled Zaw's stats.

The Balla is classified as a short blade strike. Short blade strikes produce daggers when paired with one-handed grips, and sword-and-shield or dual-dagger configurations with specific grip types. Players building a Zaw should consult the Zaw calculator on the Fandom wiki or Overframe to preview combined stats before purchasing from Hok, since Ostron standing is required to purchase components.

## Stats

The Balla reports zero standalone stats in WFCD because it is an unassembled component, not a combat-ready weapon. Final assembled stats vary by Grip and Link selection. Contact Hok at Cetus with at least Visitor-rank Ostron standing to purchase the component and preview assembled options.

## Acquisition

Purchase from Hok's Anvil at Cetus on the Plains of Eidolon. Ostron standing is required; the Balla becomes available once the player reaches the appropriate Ostron rank. Gifting Nakak with fish or resource bundles earns Ostron standing toward the required threshold.

## Variants

Zaws are inherently modular; the Balla strike can be paired with multiple grip and link options to produce different weapon types. Each combination yields a distinct Zaw with unique base stats and damage profiles. No separate Prime or Kuva variant of Balla exists.

## Recommended builds

Determine the target Zaw configuration (dagger, sword, etc.) first, then select Grip and Link to reach desired attack speed and damage split. For status-focused Zaws, the Link component can provide additional status chance. For critical builds, Link components with critical chance bonuses pair with the Balla strike's base swing speed.

## Notes

- Balla assembled as a dagger pairs with the Exodia Prime set for Zaw-exclusive arcane bonuses.
- Ostron standing from Plains of Eidolon activities (fishing, mining, bounties) unlocks higher-rank Zaw components over time.
- WFCD reports zero stats for this entry because it is a component, not a finished weapon; the zeros are not a data error.
- Zaw gilding at Hok's Anvil (after reaching Mastery Rank 8) allows installing Forma and naming the weapon.
