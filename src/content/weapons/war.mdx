## Overview

War is a Grineer-themed greatsword that ranks among the heaviest-hitting melees available at Mastery Rank 10. Its damage profile leans into impact, making it effective against shielded Corpus targets, while the 60 slash damage ensures some bleed proc potential on unarmored enemies. The weapon is notable for its balanced critical and status profile -- both sitting at 26% -- which gives modders a choice between crit-scaling builds or status-proc strategies without either direction feeling compromised.

The 3.2-meter melee range is one of the longest in the greatsword category, allowing War to hit multiple enemies in a single swing arc. The wide reach is particularly valuable in high-density missions such as Defense or Survival, where enemies cluster. Combos that use wide horizontal attacks maximize this range advantage, clearing groups without needing repositioning.

War's association with the Wolf of Saturn Six event gave the weapon additional cultural weight in the Warframe community, and its sinister Grineer aesthetic appeals to players building thematically consistent loadouts. The blueprint is available on the Market for credits, keeping the acquisition process straightforward.

## Acquisition

The War blueprint is available from the in-game Market for credits. After purchasing the blueprint, the weapon requires 12 hours to build in the Foundry. No rare resources are needed, making it one of the more accessible MR 10 melees. The Market blueprint costs 20,000 credits.

## Variants

War has no prime variant as of the current game version.

## Recommended builds

War's dual 26% crit and status chances support two distinct build directions.

**Status-focused:** Pair Condition Overload with two elemental damage mods to maximize status proc diversity. Corrosive (Electric + Toxin) strips Grineer armor, freeing War's impact damage to land at full effectiveness against health pools. Add Weeping Wounds to ramp status chance during extended combos. This build performs well in Steel Path Survival where sustained armor-stripping matters more than burst damage.

**Critical-focused:** Blood Rush combined with Organ Shatter and Primed Pressure Point scales War's crit multiplier with combo count. At 4x combo, crit chance reaches reliable orange-crit territory. Berserker Fury provides a fire-rate buff on kill, accelerating combo buildup further. This direction suits faster mission types such as Disruption or Exterminate where enemies die quickly and restarting the combo counter is not a concern.

Melee Influence (an Arcane) enables electricity procs to cascade between nearby enemies, turning War's wide-range attacks into crowd-clearing tools in dense corridors and tight rooms.

## Notes

- War's greatsword stance is Tempo Royale or Cleaving Whirlwind. Cleaving Whirlwind provides wider sweeping attacks that take better advantage of the 3.2-meter range; Tempo Royale offers faster individual strike speed.
- The floating damage values in WFCD (e.g., 70.000008 puncture) are a display artifact of floating-point precision in the data export. The effective in-game value rounds to 70.0.
- War requires Mastery Rank 10, placing it in the mid-game category. Players farming the Wolf of Saturn Six event historically encountered War as a drop before the Market blueprint existed.
- Heavy attacks with War deal substantial radial damage, useful for breaking enemy formations without targeting individual units.
