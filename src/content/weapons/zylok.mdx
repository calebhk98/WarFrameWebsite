## Overview

Zylok is a semi-automatic pistol designed as a companion to the Sibear melee weapon, both being part of the Tenno-crafted weapon lineup. Its damage profile is dominated by puncture (78 per shot), with moderate impact (45) and low slash (17). This puncture-heavy distribution makes Zylok naturally effective against Grineer armor, where puncture damage applies at full efficiency before armor reduction, and against Corpus robotics where puncture has favorable modifiers.

The 26% status chance at a 1.5 shots-per-second fire rate means Zylok reliably applies one status proc every few shots. With two elemental mods, status type combinations like Corrosive or Viral become consistent, supporting the puncture damage with additional debuffs. The 8-round magazine limits burst before reload, but the 1.2-second reload is fast enough to keep downtime brief.

Zylok's 8% critical chance makes crit builds irrelevant. The 2.0x critical multiplier provides no competitive advantage at that base crit rate. Instead, the weapon rewards status-first builds that leverage the already-high puncture damage against targets weakened by elemental procs.

At Mastery Rank 6, Zylok is accessible relatively early in mid-game progression. It functions as a reliable status secondary during the MR 6-10 phase before higher-tier options become available. Its prime variant, [Zylok Prime](/weapons/zylok-prime), represents a significant upgrade for players who reach later game stages.

## Acquisition

The Zylok blueprint is available from the in-game Market for credits. No rare resources are required for crafting. The weapon becomes available at Mastery Rank 6, making it accessible at the start of mid-game content.

## Variants

Zylok has a prime variant: [Zylok Prime](/weapons/zylok-prime), which improves critical chance, critical multiplier, and status chance significantly over the base version.

## Recommended builds

**Corrosive status build:** Equip Pistol Pestilence and Pathogen Rounds to apply Corrosive (Toxin plus Electric). At 26% base status chance with Barrel Diffusion adding multishot, status proc frequency increases to approximately one proc per 2-3 trigger pulls. Corrosive stacks strip Grineer armor efficiently, freeing the puncture damage to land at full value against exposed health pools. Add Hornet Strike and Primed Pressure Point for damage scaling.

**Viral-puncture build:** Combine Frostbite (Cold) and Virulent Scourge (Toxin) for Viral. Two Viral procs reduce enemy health by 200%, making subsequent puncture damage hit harder. At 1.5 shots/second, applying the two Viral procs needed for the debuff takes 3-4 shots, then all remaining shots benefit from the health amplification. Add Augur Pact or Pistol Gambit for secondary scaling.

The semi-automatic fire rate suits deliberate engagements. Zylok does not reward trigger-mashing and instead rewards measured shot placement to conserve the small magazine.

## Notes

- Zylok is thematically paired with the Sibear heavy sword and shares Tenno crafting aesthetic.
- The accuracy value of 23.5 means some spread at range, consistent with the pistol's intended close-to-medium engagement distances.
- Floating decimal values in WFCD (44.799999, 78.400002) are floating-point artifacts; in-game display rounds these to 44.8 and 78.4 respectively.
- Zylok Prime is a substantial upgrade worth farming once relic access allows, particularly given the higher crit multiplier (2.4x vs. 2.0x) and elevated status chance.
- Seeking Fury (for reload speed) benefits Zylok less than multishot mods given the fast 1.2-second base reload; prioritize damage and status over reload optimization.
