## Overview

Latron Prime is the Prime variant of the Latron semi-automatic rifle, offering improved base damage, critical chance, critical multiplier, and status chance over its standard counterpart. The Latron platform fires one round per trigger pull, rewarding accurate semi-auto trigger technique over sustained full-auto fire. Latron Prime rewards this precision with a 2.8x critical multiplier -- one of the higher values in the semi-automatic rifle tier -- making each critical hit deliver substantial burst damage.

The damage breakdown shows a strong slash weighting: 9 impact, 9 puncture, and 72 slash per shot. Slash-dominant rifles benefit naturally from Hunter Munitions, which converts a percentage of critical hits into Slash procs (bleed damage-over-time). Against armored targets, bleed damage bypasses armor damage reduction entirely, making slash-heavy weapons with Hunter Munitions particularly effective in Steel Path where enemy armor values are substantially elevated.

The 22% critical chance paired with the 2.8x multiplier creates strong crit potential. The 26% status chance allows hybrid builds to incorporate elemental procs alongside crit damage. At Mastery Rank 10, Latron Prime targets mid-game players who have cleared a meaningful portion of the star chart.

The 2.8x critical multiplier is the weapon's key strength. At 22% base critical chance with crit-focused mods, Latron Prime reaches consistent critical trigger rates and high per-crit damage output. The 90 base damage per shot scales meaningfully with Serration and elemental damage mods.

The 4.167 fire rate represents the maximum semi-auto cadence -- achieving this requires rapid trigger input. Typical player fire rates will be lower, which affects sustained DPS but does not change burst potential per shot.

## Acquisition

Latron Prime components are obtained from Void Relics tied to its Prime Access rotation. Blueprint, Stock, Barrel, and Receiver components appear in specific Lith, Meso, Neo, and Axi relics during active rotation. When in the Prime Vault, components must be sourced through unvault events or the Trading Post.

The base Latron is available from the in-game Market for credits and provides access to the semi-auto platform at lower stat values.

## Variants

The base Latron is a Market-available semi-automatic rifle. The Latron Wraith is an improved version distributed through Invasions and offers stats between the base and Prime versions. Latron Prime represents the highest-tier variant with the best critical multiplier in the family.

## Recommended builds

A Hunter Munitions build uses Serration, Split Chamber, Hunter Munitions, alongside two elemental mods for Corrosive or Viral. Hunter Munitions converts critical hits into Slash procs at a percentage rate, and the high per-shot damage means each bleed tick deals significant damage-over-time against armored targets. This approach is effective for Steel Path missions against heavy Grineer and corrupted units.

A crit-focused build adds Vital Sense for critical multiplier alongside a critical chance mod, with Argon Scope providing a substantial critical bonus on headshots. Latron Prime's semi-auto nature rewards headshot accuracy, making Argon Scope more practical than on fully automatic weapons.

A hybrid build combining Viral procs and critical damage uses Cold and Toxin mods for Viral status application alongside the crit core. At 26% base status chance, a few status chance mods can raise proc reliability to practical levels without fully displacing crit-oriented mods.

## Notes

Latron Prime's semi-automatic fire mechanism means damage output scales directly with trigger speed up to the 4.167 rounds per second cap. Players using controllers or who have lower trigger speed will achieve lower effective fire rates. Arcane Precision, which activates on headshots, synergizes well with the accurate semi-auto playstyle and rewards hitting weak points.

The 28.6 accuracy rating is moderate by rifle standards, producing some bullet spread at extended range. This is not a significant limitation for most mission engagement distances but may affect performance at maximum sniper-range distances.
