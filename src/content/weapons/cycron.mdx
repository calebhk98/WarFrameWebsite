## Overview

The [Cycron](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Cycron) is a secondary pistol that fires a continuous radiation beam. Its damage consists of 5 puncture, 8 slash, and 10 innate radiation per hit, totaling 23 per hit. The 30% status chance combined with the 12 fire rate delivers reliable radiation proc application -- radiation procs confuse enemies, causing them to attack allies and each other. The 12% critical chance and 1.8x multiplier support modest crit builds alongside the status focus.

The continuous beam mechanic and innate radiation make the Cycron a crowd control weapon: radiation-confused enemies cease attacking the player and begin targeting their allies, effectively neutralizing them without killing. This is particularly useful in Corrupted faction missions (Void) where radiation is already a damage-type advantage.

## Acquisition

The Cycron blueprint is available from the Market for credits. As a Mastery Rank 8 weapon, it targets mid-game players. The Cycron can also be purchased built for platinum. Crafting requires standard mid-game materials.

## Variants

No Prime, Vandal, or Wraith variant exists. Players seeking alternative radiation-delivery secondaries should compare the Kitgun with a Rattleguts chamber for different firing mechanics with radiation elemental potential.

## Recommended builds

**Radiation crowd control:** Hornet Strike, Barrel Diffusion, Lethal Torrent, Jolt (electric -- combines with heat for radiation or adds independent electric procs), Scorch (heat), Pistol Pestilence (toxin), Frostbite (cold for viral), and Primed Quickdraw. The 30% status chance at 12 fire rate applies radiation, viral, and heat procs simultaneously. Radiation-confused Corrupted enemies attack allies; viral reduces their health; heat adds damage-over-time ticks.

**Void radiation build:** In Void missions, add radiation-amplifying mods and exploit the Corrupted faction's radiation vulnerability. Corrupted enemies take 75% bonus damage from radiation, and confusion procs are particularly disruptive to organized Corrupted unit behavior.

**Status primer beam:** Fire the Cycron briefly to apply two or three unique status types (radiation, viral, heat), then switch to melee for Condition Overload follow-up. The 30% status chance at high fire rate applies procs within 0.5-1 seconds of fire, making the Cycron an exceptionally fast status primer.

## Synergies

- **Condition Overload melee:** Radiation, viral, and heat from Cycron procs feeding Condition Overload on a subsequent melee swing creates an efficient two-weapon damage cycle. The Cycron's rapid proc application makes this the fastest status-primer-to-melee transition available among beam secondaries.
- **Void missions:** Corrupted enemies are weak to radiation, providing a natural faction damage advantage that amplifies the Cycron's already radiation-focused output.
- **Banshee's Sonar:** The Cycron's perfectly accurate beam (accuracy 100) makes it ideal for Sonar weak-point targeting. Precise beam delivery to the highlighted weak spot maximizes each hit's damage amplification.

## Notes

- Radiation procs cause confused enemies to attack the nearest target (ally or enemy) for 12 seconds. In group play, confusing a dangerous enemy (Heavy Gunner, Bombard, Nullifier) effectively removes it from combat temporarily without requiring a kill.
- The 40-round magazine at 12 fire rate provides approximately 3.3 seconds of continuous fire before reload. Manage fire in short bursts rather than sustained streams to extend effective time-on-target.
- Accuracy of 100 is perfect. The Cycron's continuous beam hits the exact point of aim with no spread. This precision makes it effective at range despite being categorized as a secondary weapon.
- Riven disposition at 4/5 is above average. A riven with multishot (fires an additional beam) or critical chance provides meaningful performance improvements.
