## Overview

The Tenet Cycron is a Sisters of Parvos Tenet secondary requiring Mastery Rank 14. It is a beam weapon that fires a continuous heat-damage beam at 12.0 ticks per second with 100% accuracy. Its 22 damage per tick is entirely heat elemental -- no physical damage components. Heat damage is especially effective against Infested and Corpus flesh, and generates fire procs that tick additional burn damage over time independently of the primary beam.

The 40% status chance is the Tenet Cycron's defining attribute. At 12.0 ticks per second with 40% status per tick, fire procs apply multiple times per second. Multiple fire procs stack independently: each proc creates its own burn timer, layering independent burn-tick damage onto the target. The 20% critical chance and 1.8x multiplier are secondary to this status output. With a 40-round magazine and a 1.5-second reload, the Cycron sustains beam fire for approximately 3.3 seconds before a fast reload.

The Tenet Cycron carries an additional elemental bonus from the parent Sister, adding a second elemental type to the heat base. The combination of base heat, modded elements, and the Sister's bonus creates up to three distinct elemental types on a single secondary -- ideal for Condition Overload melee synergy or multi-elemental status stacking.

## Acquisition

Obtain through the Sisters of Parvos system at Mastery Rank 14. Trigger a Sister Candidate via Granum Void on Corpus nodes, then track and vanquish the resulting Sister to claim her Tenet Cycron. The Sister's elemental bonus type combines with the base heat profile.

## Variants

No base non-Tenet Cycron. The standard Cycron is a separate weapon design; the Tenet Cycron is not an upgrade of it.

## Recommended builds

**Heat proc stacking**: Hornet Strike, Barrel Diffusion, Thermite Rounds (heat), Galvanized Aptitude, Galvanized Shot, and toxin mod (heat + toxin = Gas). At 40% status and 12 ticks/s, fire procs apply constantly. Multiple stacks burn simultaneously. Gas from heat plus toxin creates area toxin clouds on proc. Galvanized Aptitude stacks damage on each proc kill.

**Viral secondary status**: Use cold mod with toxin mod. Cold plus toxin creates Viral. The heat base generates heat procs while cold plus toxin mods create Viral procs separately. Both status types apply simultaneously, halving health (Viral) while burning targets (heat).

**Maximum status**: Four 60/60 dual-stat mods for multiple elemental types plus Galvanized Shot. The beam format applies all elemental types simultaneously, each rolling its individual status chance per tick. Three distinct status types on a target build Galvanized Shot's bonus rapidly.

## Notes

Beam weapons drain the magazine based on continuous ticks. At 12 ticks per second, the 40-round magazine lasts approximately 3.3 seconds of sustained beam fire, followed by the 1.5-second reload. The fast reload compensates for the limited sustained duration. At 100% accuracy, the Tenet Cycron hits with full precision at any secondary weapon engagement range -- suitable for mid-range use unlike many high-fire-rate secondaries with significant spread.
