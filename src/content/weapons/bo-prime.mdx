## Overview

The [Bo Prime](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Bo_Prime) is the Prime variant of the Bo polearm, offering markedly improved critical chance, critical multiplier, and status chance compared to its base version. It deals 176 total damage with a heavy emphasis on impact (158.4), making it particularly strong against Corpus shielded enemies and useful for Shield Gating interactions. The 24% critical chance and 2.6x multiplier open up reliable critical builds that the base Bo cannot support.

As a polearm, the Bo Prime inherits access to the Shimmering Blight and Bleeding Willow stance mods, providing sweeping multi-target combos with excellent reach. The polearm category consistently performs well in high-density enemy scenarios due to wide hitbox coverage during combo animations.

Riven disposition at the maximum tier (5/5) reflects the Bo Prime's underuse relative to the playerbase, making rivens for this weapon often roll with substantial multipliers on key stats.

## Acquisition

Bo Prime parts drop from Void relics. The specific relics rotate in and out of the unvaulted pool with each Prime Vault cycle. When not unvaulted, the parts can be purchased from other players via warframe.market. The component list typically includes a Blueprint, Ornament, and Handle, each housed in different relic tiers (Lith, Meso, Neo, or Axi).

Check the [Bo Prime Wiki page](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Bo_Prime) for the current relic list, as this changes with each vault rotation.

## Variants

The base Bo is available from the Market and requires no prime relic farming. It shares the polearm stance pool but has significantly lower critical chance (12.5%) and a 2.0x multiplier. Players who cannot access Prime relics should consider the Bo as a temporary placeholder.

No Vandal or Wraith versions of the Bo exist.

## Recommended builds

**Critical status hybrid:** Primed Pressure Point, Berserker Fury, Organ Shatter, Blood Rush, and Condition Overload form the damage core. Add viral (Fever Strike plus North Wind or Primed North Wind) to capitalize on both the 32% status chance and the 24% critical chance. Gladiator mods from companion builds further boost combo-scaling critical chance.

**Pure critical build:** Blood Rush, Weeping Wounds, Organ Shatter, Berserker Fury, and Primed Pressure Point with four elemental mods focused on heat and viral. At x12 combo multiplier, Blood Rush elevates the effective critical chance into orange-crit territory, delivering consistent high damage output.

**Heavy attack build:** Corrupt Charge, Amalgam Organ Shatter, and Killing Blow generate powerful single-hit heavy attacks suitable for Profit-Taker weak points, Necramech limbs, and eidolon synovia. The high base impact damage aligns with the heavy attack bonus.

## Synergies

- **Rhino's Roar:** Scales multiplicatively with the Bo Prime's base damage, amplifying its already high impact value.
- **Blood Rush + Weeping Wounds:** These companion mods scale with combo multiplier. The 24% base critical chance and 32% base status chance both benefit significantly as combo climbs.
- **Gladiator set:** Combining Gladiator Vice (attack speed) on the Bo Prime and other Gladiator mods on the Warframe or companion provides a powerful combo-scaling critical bonus.

## Notes

- The heavy impact bias (158.4 out of 176 total) means the Bo Prime underperforms against high-armor targets with Impact resistance. Adding corrosive or viral slash procs through elemental combos compensates.
- Polearms cannot equip Heavy Blade stances -- confirm your chosen stance is compatible with the polearm category before purchase.
- The Bo Prime's Mastery Rank 5 requirement is unusually low for a Prime weapon, making it accessible to earlier-progression players who obtain the relics through trading.
- Riven disposition at 5/5 is rare for a Prime polearm. A good riven offering critical chance, critical damage, or status chance can dramatically elevate this weapon's ceiling.
