## Overview

The Ekwana Ruhang is a Zaw Link component available from Hok's Anvil at Cetus on the Plains of Eidolon. As a Link, it does not have standalone combat statistics; WFCD reports zeros because the item is an unassembled weapon part. The "Ruhang" designation indicates a bias toward status chance in the Ekwana Link family, making the Ekwana Ruhang the entry-level status-biased option within this component series.

In the Zaw assembly system, Link components occupy the third slot after the Strike and Grip are selected. The Ekwana Ruhang modifies the critical chance and status chance of the assembled Zaw configuration according to its Ruhang stat weighting. Status-biased Links suit assembled Zaws intended for Condition Overload and Weeping Wounds builds, where higher status chance enables faster elemental proc accumulation on targets, multiplying Condition Overload's damage output per proc type. Comparing the Ekwana Ruhang against the Ekwana II Ruhang and Ekwana Ruhang II in Hok's interface reveals the incremental stat differences; the assembled preview shows exact combined values before purchasing.

Zaw weapons assembled with the Ekwana Ruhang Link can be gilded at Mastery Rank 8 or higher to unlock Arcane slots and allow Forma installation for polarity customization. The completed Zaw can then receive a custom name after gilding.

## Acquisition

Purchase from Hok's Anvil at Cetus on the Plains of Eidolon. Ostron Standing is required; Link components unlock at progressively higher Ostron community ranks. Earn Ostron Standing through bounty completion, fishing, and mining in the Plains of Eidolon. Check [the Ekwana Ruhang Fandom wiki page](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Ekwana_Ruhang) for the exact Ostron rank requirement and standing cost.

## Variants

The Ekwana Link family includes multiple options with different critical and status weightings: Ekwana Jai, Ekwana II Jai, Ekwana Jai II, Ekwana Ruhang, Ekwana II Ruhang, and Ekwana Ruhang II. Jai variants bias toward critical chance; Ruhang variants bias toward status chance. The Ekwana Ruhang is the base status-biased option; the II and enhanced variants provide stronger stat modifiers at higher Ostron rank requirements. No Zaw component has a Prime or Kuva version.

## Recommended builds

When incorporating the Ekwana Ruhang Link into a Zaw build:

- Select a Strike to establish the weapon archetype and base damage type distribution.
- Select a Grip to determine the weapon category and swing speed.
- Add Ekwana Ruhang as the Link to apply its status chance bias to the assembled Zaw.
- Preview the assembled stats in Hok's interface before purchasing to confirm the stat distribution meets the build goal.
- For status-focused assembled Zaws, equip Weeping Wounds, Condition Overload, and multiple elemental 60/60 mods for diverse proc types.
- Gild at Mastery Rank 8 or higher to access Arcane slots.

## Synergies

Status-biased Zaws assembled with Ruhang-family Links benefit from frames that apply vulnerability or grouping. Khora's Strangledome clusters enemies for multi-target status proc application. Condition Overload's multiplicative damage amplifies more quickly when multiple proc types (Physical, Viral, Corrosive, Heat) are simultaneously active on a target.
