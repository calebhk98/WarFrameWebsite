## Overview

The Dual Kamas Prime is the primed variant of the Dual Kamas, featuring a Puncture-heavy damage split (120 Puncture vs 32 Slash and 8 Impact), 20% critical chance, and 25% status chance. The reported critical multiplier from WFCD is 2.0x. The 1.167 attack speed and Mastery Rank 8 requirement make it an accessible prime melee option for mid-game players.

The Dual Kamas Prime uses Dual Sword stance mods (Swirling Tiger, Carving Mantis) for multi-hit arc combos. The 25% status chance supports elemental proc builds, while the 20% crit provides a secondary crit layer. The weapon suits players transitioning from standard dual swords into Prime-tier melee options.

## Acquisition

The Dual Kamas Prime blueprint and components are obtained through Void Relics. Check [the Dual Kamas Prime Fandom wiki page](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Dual_Kamas_Prime) for the current relic list and vault status. It may also be purchased as a set from other players via trading.

## Variants

The Dual Kamas Prime is the primed variant of the base Dual Kamas. Both use the same stance mods and weapon class. The Prime version offers improved stats across critical chance, status chance, and attack speed. No Kuva or Tenet Dual Kamas exists.

## Recommended builds

**Viral/status hybrid.** Use Primed Fever Strike and Cryo Rounds for Viral. At 25% status with the 1.167 attack speed, Viral procs apply regularly. Add Blood Rush and Organ Shatter for crit scaling as the combo counter climbs, then Weeping Wounds to push status chance further.

**Corrosive armor strip.** Pair Infected Clip and Voltaic Strike for Corrosive. The Puncture-heavy base is most effective against armored Grineer enemies, and Corrosive amplifies this further by stripping armor. Include Primed Pressure Point and Berserker Fury.

**Full crit Blood Rush build.** Sacrificial Steel and Blood Rush scale the 20% base crit into orange-crit territory at high combo. Combine with Organ Shatter and two 60/60 elemental mods for both damage and status coverage.

## Synergies

Valkyr's Warcry provides 40% attack speed at base rank, pushing the Dual Kamas Prime's already-fast 1.167 attack speed into rapid-combo territory. Rhino's Roar amplifies all physical damage types equally. Naramon Void Sling extends combo counter uptime between enemy clusters. The Puncture-dominant physical profile makes the Dual Kamas Prime most effective against Grineer Ferrite Armor, where Corrosive stacking compounds this advantage further. Mesa's Shooting Gallery and Wisp's Fused Reservoir further boost melee swing speed when active, enabling faster combo counter accumulation and more frequent status proc delivery with the 25% status base.

## Notes

- The 25% status chance is the primary asset for status-oriented builds; invest in elemental mods that exploit this.
- The Dual Kamas Prime is a reliable mid-game prime melee weapon before higher-tier primes become accessible.
