## Overview

The Orokin Prime refinement of the Larkspur beam weapon. Retains the exceptional 50% status chance while improving critical chance to 14% and critical multiplier to 1.6x, enabling hybrid critical/status builds that apply Radiation Confusion while delivering boosted critical hits. Introduced in Hotfix 32.3.6 (2023-03-15), the Larkspur Prime is notable for retains 50% status chance of base Larkspur; improved critical chance (14%) and multiplier (1.6x) enable hybrid builds; Riven disposition 1.

The Orokin Prime design enhances the Larkspur's energy core with Void-attuned beam arrays, maintaining the Radiation output that makes the weapon invaluable against Corpus and Sentient targets while refining the precision optics that were the original design's weakness. The Prime engineering achieves both status saturation and critical efficiency simultaneously.

## Acquisition

All four components obtainable through Void Relic farming across Lith, Meso, Neo, and Axi tiers. Blueprint from Axi S16, Lith T12, Lith X1, Meso B9, Meso H4, Meso K6, and Meso M4 relics. Barrel from Axi L6, Neo L2, Neo L3, Neo L4 relics. The Larkspur Prime requires Mastery Rank 8 to equip.

Arch-gun weapons require either standard crafting resources, Syndicate standing for component parts, or specialized mission drops. Once crafted, equipping the Larkspur Prime in Archwing uses the standard Arch-gun slot. To use it as a Heavy Weapon in ground missions or Railjack combat, a Gravimag must be installed. Gravimags are available from Clan Dojos via Energy Lab research, or from Arbitrations as rotation rewards.

## Variants

Prime version of the Larkspur. Both share Mastery Rank 8. Riven disposition 1 indicates the weapon's strong competitive standing in DE's balance assessment.

The Arch-gun family spans weapons from Tenno engineering (Corvas, Fluctus, Velocitus), Grineer design (Grattler, Kuva line), and Entrati craftsmanship (Cortege, Mausolon, Morgha), each with distinct stat profiles suited to different engagement contexts.

## Recommended builds

Improved critical stats over base Larkspur allow combining Hollowed Bullets with Modified Munitions for simultaneous critical and status optimization. The 50% status still applies Radiation Confusion reliably. Riven disposition of 1 means fixed mods outperform Riven investment -- prioritize Rubedo-Lined Barrel, Critical Focus, and Modified Munitions as the core three mods.

**Core Arch-gun mods to consider:**

- Rubedo-Lined Barrel -- +165% Arch-gun damage (primary damage mod for all builds)
- Hollowed Bullets -- +120% critical chance (essential for any crit-focused build)
- Critical Focus -- +60% critical multiplier while aiming
- Modified Munitions -- +90% status chance (for status-focused builds)
- Arch-gun Ace -- +165% fire rate (useful on slow-fire weapons to improve feel)
- Parallax Scope -- +30% critical chance while aiming
- Charged Bullets -- +60% magazine capacity
- Superior Defenses -- reduces reload time
- Conductive Coils -- adds Electricity damage
- Thermite Rounds -- adds Heat damage

Prioritize Rubedo-Lined Barrel as the first mod in any Arch-gun build. Follow with critical or status mods based on the weapon's stat profile. Elemental mods provide faction-specific damage type flexibility.

## Notes

The Larkspur Prime operates in the Arch-gun slot, used in Archwing missions and as a Heavy Weapon on the ground via Gravimag. In Railjack (Empyrean) missions, the Arch-gun serves as the primary armament during space combat against fighters and Crewships.

Premium Railjack Arch-gun for Radiation-focused builds. The Confusion proc from Radiation status reduces Railjack boarding threat levels significantly in interior missions.

Archwing combat system notes: Archwing missions require completing The Archwing quest. Four Archwings are available -- Itzal (mobility/stealth), Amesha (tank/support), Elytron (AoE damage), and Odonata (balanced starter). Works with all Archwings. Amesha's supportive abilities complement the sustained beam playstyle. Odonata's Energy Shell adds Electricity status output for combined faction coverage.

The weapon's Riven disposition is 1/5 (very low). Riven dispositions range from 0 (absolute minimum, strong weapon) to 5 (maximum, weak weapon). DE adjusts dispositions quarterly based on weapon usage rates.
