## Overview

Rubico is a primary sniper rifle that combines high impact damage per shot with an excellent critical multiplier of 3.0x, making it one of the more effective sniper platforms for Eidolon hunting and long-range single-target engagements. The weapon requires Mastery Rank 6 and delivers its damage through slow, deliberate semi-automatic fire rather than the sustained output of automatic rifles. Players who invest in its critical build path will find it competitive across the star chart and viable on the Steel Path with full mod investment.

Sniper rifles in Warframe perform best when the player maintains distance and exploits headshot multipliers. The Rubico's impact-biased physical damage profile means it hits hardest against enemies susceptible to impact procs, though its true strength lies in critical strike amplification rather than status accumulation.

## Stats

Per WFCD warframe-items data, Rubico deals 144 impact, 9.0 puncture, and 27.0 slash per shot. The critical chance of 30% and critical multiplier of 3.0x make it a strong crit platform; at 30% base chance, mods such as Point Strike and Vital Sense push it into reliable headshot territory for high-damage single-target output. Status chance of 12% is low for consistent proc application but sufficient for hybrid builds using a single elemental mod. Fire rate is 2.67 rps and accuracy is 13.3, reflecting the low-zoom base profile of the weapon before scope modifiers apply.

## Acquisition

The Rubico blueprint is available from the in-game Market for credits. After purchasing the blueprint, components are crafted in the Foundry with standard materials available from the star chart. The Mastery Rank 6 requirement is accessible early in a player's progression. Consult the [Rubico wiki page](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Rubico) for the full resource cost.

## Variants

Rubico Prime is the significantly improved Prime variant, offering higher critical chance, damage, and magazine size. It is one of the preferred sniper rifles for Eidolon Teralyst hunting due to its crit profile and synergy with Volt's Electric Shield buff. Players who enjoy the base Rubico should prioritize farming or trading for the Prime version once Mastery Rank permits.

## Recommended builds

Critical damage builds are the standard path for Rubico. Stack Point Strike to push critical chance, then Vital Sense for critical damage amplification. Primed Chamber (a rare mod) adds a bonus critical hit on the first shot after reloading, which pairs well with sniper rhythm. For Eidolon hunting specifically, pairing with Volt's Electric Shield and using Radiation or Magnetic damage mods maximizes synovi damage output.

For general star chart and Steel Path use, add a dual-stat elemental mod targeting the primary enemy faction. Corrosive (Heat plus Toxin) handles Grineer armor; Magnetic (Cold plus Electric) strips Corpus shields. Hunters Munitions is also valuable if the intent is applying slash procs to bypass armor rather than relying on pure crit damage.

## Notes

Sniper rifles receive a combo multiplier that increases damage per consecutive hit without missing. Managing the Rubico's combo requires deliberate shot placement rather than holding fire. Frames that provide aim assistance or slow enemies -- such as Nova or Limbo -- extend windows for landing combo-building shots in cluttered missions.

Riven mods with positive disposition for Rubico can significantly improve its output. Check warframe.market for current Riven price ranges before investing kuva in rerolling.
