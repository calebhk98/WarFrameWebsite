## Overview

The Tonkor is a grenade launcher platform that fires slow-moving explosive projectiles with devastating impact damage. It specializes in area damage against grouped enemies with a low fire rate and limited magazine, rewarding careful ammunition management. The weapon excels against grouped enemies and benefits substantially from impact damage mods. The 75 slash damage ensures each grenade deals significant damage on both impact and detonation against any target.

Grenades from the Tonkor are affected by environmental gravity and physics, requiring players to lead targets or predict enemy movement patterns. The single round magazine necessitates disciplined ammunition management, making every shot count strategically. The weapon's 1.7 reload time provides breathing room between shots, allowing time to aim and position for the next volley. This reload pacing is actually quite respectable for a launcher, enabling consistent shot rhythm without excessive downtime.

The 10% status chance provides a baseline for environmental effects, primarily poison and viral procs when combined with appropriate elemental mods. The weapon's 5 mastery requirement makes it accessible early in progression, though practical use at higher mission levels requires significant mod investment and extended practice with grenade mechanics. Grenade launchers reward positioning and map awareness, as understanding enemy movement patterns and chokepoints enables maximum grenade effectiveness.

Tonkor builds emphasize raw explosive damage through Serration-class mods and impact-enhancing modifications like Heavy Caliber. Some players prefer status-focused builds with toxin or electric mods to apply crowd control effects while managing reload timing. The weapon's single magazine round creates interesting tactical options where switching to secondaries between shots becomes tactically optimal and necessary.

The Tonkor represents a unique playstyle within primary weapons, appealing to players who enjoy methodical, high-impact gameplay over sustained fire volume. The weapon's explosion radius and significant damage potential make it excellent for handling clustered enemies and defensive objective missions. In wave-based missions, experienced Tonkor users can thin enemy waves significantly with well-placed grenades.

Veterans appreciate the Tonkor's effectiveness in specific mission contexts where its high per-shot damage and area effects matter more than sustained rate of fire. The weapon remains competitively viable in sortie missions and void fissures when built properly with heavy damage multipliers and capacity extensions.
