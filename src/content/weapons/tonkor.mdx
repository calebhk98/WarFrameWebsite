## Overview

The Tonkor is a Grineer-designed grenade launcher available at Mastery Rank 5. Each shot fires an explosive grenade projectile with 725 total damage per detonation: 650 blast elemental damage and 75 slash. Blast damage deals area-of-effect knockdown against clustered enemies and bonus damage against machinery and Grineer Gorg-type units. The slash component generates bleed procs on direct hits. With 25% critical chance and 2.5x multiplier, the Tonkor has strong crit potential for a launcher.

Grenades fired by the Tonkor are affected by gravity and travel in an arc. This projectile physics requires aiming above the target at range to land shots accurately. The slow-moving grenade trajectory also creates a delay between trigger pull and detonation, requiring target anticipation. In close quarters, the grenade bounces off surfaces before detonating (with a minimum arming distance), providing a small margin of safety compared to instant-detonation launchers.

The single-round magazine is the Tonkor's defining limitation. Each shot requires an immediate 1.7-second reload before the next round can fire. At 3.17 fire rate theoretical, the practical sustained fire rate is determined by the reload -- effectively one round every 1.7 seconds in continuous operation. This pacing demands that each grenade placement be deliberate and accurate. Missed shots waste nearly 2 seconds of combat potential.

## Variants

**Tonkor Gilded** -- Not a separate item; players can mod the standard Tonkor to maximize performance. No Wraith, Prime, or Vandal variant exists.

## Recommended builds

**Blast crit**: Serration, Split Chamber, Point Strike, Vital Sense, Heavy Caliber, and two elemental mods. Point Strike at 25% base crit reaches near 75% with Point Strike. Vital Sense amplifies the 2.5x base multiplier. Heavy Caliber adds damage at no accuracy penalty since the Tonkor is already limited by arc physics rather than spread.

**Radiation for grouped enemies**: Add heat plus electric for Radiation elemental. Radiation confuses Corpus and deals bonus damage against Grineer Alloy Armor. Confused enemies in the detonation area attack each other, amplifying the area impact.

**Viral plus blast**: Cold plus toxin for Viral. At 10% status, Viral application requires several shots, but once applied, the health-halving effect amplifies subsequent blasts against affected survivors.

## Notes

The grenade does not detonate immediately on firing -- it has a minimum travel distance before arming. In very close-range situations, the grenade may not detonate on impact and instead bounces. Maintain engagement range of at least 5-8 meters. Grenades roll on surfaces, making indirect fire around corners possible with practice. The blast area knockdown can disrupt melee enemies approaching from the front, providing reactive crowd control.
