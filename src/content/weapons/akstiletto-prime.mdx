## Overview

Akstiletto Prime is the Prime variant of the Akstiletto dual-pistol secondary weapon. It fires hitscan projectiles at a high rate from twin pistols, emphasizing sustained fire over burst damage. The 30% base status chance is the weapon's defining stat, making Akstiletto Prime a strong platform for elemental status proc builds. Mastery Rank 10 is required to equip it.

The 40-round magazine and 1.1-second reload provide comfortable sustained engagement. Impact-dominant physical damage at 21.6 impact per shot is most effective against Corpus shield pools. The 15% base critical chance is workable but not the weapon's primary strength; status-oriented builds tend to outperform crit-focused configurations given the high base status chance.

The impact-heavy damage profile performs best against shielded Corpus enemies where impact damage strips shields efficiently. Against Grineer armor, elemental combinations such as Corrosive (Cold + Electric) compensate for the lower puncture and slash components.

## Acquisition

Akstiletto Prime components drop from Void Relics across multiple tiers. The Barrel drops as an uncommon reward from Axi A1, Axi H2, Lith A5, Lith H1, and Meso K1 relics. Other components (Blueprint, Link, Receiver) distribute across additional Lith, Meso, and Axi relics. Akstiletto Prime is currently unvaulted, so all relevant relics appear in standard Void fissure reward pools. Check warframe.market or the official drop table site to confirm which relics are available in the current game version.

## Variants

Akstiletto Prime is the Prime variant of the base Akstiletto. The base Akstiletto is craftable from a Market blueprint. No Vandal or Wraith versions of the Akstiletto line exist.

## Recommended builds

The 30% base status chance makes status proc builds the most effective approach. Adding Pistol Pestilence, Scorch, Jolt, or Frostbite for elemental combinations produces reliable proc application at high fire rate. Viral plus Heat is a common combination: Viral halves enemy health pools and Heat procs additional damage over time. Hornet Strike and Barrel Diffusion establish base damage and multishot, while Lethal Torrent adds fire rate and multishot together.

For players who prefer a crit-hybrid approach, Primed Pistol Gambit and Primed Target Cracker raise critical chance and multiplier to more rewarding levels while the status chance provides supplementary proc coverage. This approach works well in content where mixed damage type coverage is useful.

## Notes

Akstiletto Prime's rapid fire rate means ammo consumption can become a concern in extended endurance runs. Ammo Chain in the exilus slot or Pistol Ammo Mutation helps sustain ammunition. The 1.1-second reload is fast enough to avoid significant downtime between magazines during normal play. Stats sourced from WFCD warframe-items.

The base Akstiletto is a craftable secondary weapon from the Market. Akstiletto Prime improves upon the base version with higher critical chance, critical multiplier, and status chance while retaining the same dual-pistol firing pattern. Players reaching Mastery Rank 10 will find the Prime variant's unvaulted status makes relic farming accessible without rare Vault access requirements.

Akstiletto Prime's hitscan fire type means all projectiles connect at the moment of firing, with no travel time to account for. This makes the weapon reliable at any engagement range where targets are visible, unlike projectile weapons that require leading moving enemies. The 23.5 accuracy rating indicates some spread on the hitscan pattern, but at typical secondary engagement distances of 10 to 25 meters this spread does not significantly reduce effective hit rate.

Frames with pistol damage buffs apply directly to Akstiletto Prime. Octavia's Amp buff and Wisp's Haste Mote increase damage and fire rate respectively. Mirage's Hall of Mirrors creates three additional copies of equipped weapons, multiplying effective projectile count during the buff window and substantially increasing status proc application rate on nearby targets.
