## Overview

The Vasto Prime is the Prime variant of the Vasto revolver, available at Mastery Rank 10. Its 110 total damage per shot is puncture-dominant: 77 puncture, 16.5 slash, and 16.5 impact. The heavy puncture weighting makes the Vasto Prime effective against Grineer armor, where puncture deals its highest relative damage. At 2.5 fire rate with a 6-round cylinder and an extremely fast 1.0-second reload, the Vasto Prime fires in controlled deliberate shots followed by rapid reloads.

The 22% critical chance and 2.4x multiplier position the Vasto Prime as a crit-capable revolver. With Primed Pistol Gambit, critical chance reaches near 80%. The 2.4x multiplier is above-average for secondaries and amplifies each crit meaningfully. The 22% status chance enables elemental proc application and supports hybrid builds. The 6-round magazine requires confident trigger control -- each round carries high value when fully modded.

The Vasto Prime drops from Void Relics, making it a mid-late-game acquisition requiring relic farming. Its Prime status means above-average stats across the board compared to the base Vasto.

## Acquisition

The Vasto Prime blueprint and components drop from Void Relics. Check the current relic drop table in-game to find which relics contain Vasto Prime parts. Components require crafting with standard Orokin resources; the main weapon blueprint assembly time is 12 hours.

## Variants

**Vasto** -- The base revolver version available from the Market.

## Recommended builds

**Crit revolver**: Hornet Strike, Primed Pistol Gambit, Primed Target Cracker, Barrel Diffusion, Galvanized Chamber, and two elemental mods for Viral (cold + toxin). At 22% base crit with Primed Pistol Gambit, near-80% crit chance produces reliable orange crits per shot. The 2.4x base multiplier amplified by Primed Target Cracker delivers strong per-shot burst damage.

**Viral puncture**: Cold plus toxin for Viral, Primed Pistol Gambit, Primed Target Cracker, Hornet Strike, Barrel Diffusion. Viral halves enemy max health; the 77 puncture base then deals full armor-penetrating damage against the halved health pool. Effective against both Grineer (armor penetration) and general content (Viral amplification).

**Status hybrid**: Four 60/60 dual-stat mods for Corrosive plus Heat, Hornet Strike, Barrel Diffusion. The 22% status with dual-stat mods produces multiple elemental types per shot, enabling Condition Overload synergies if carrying a melee.

## Notes

The 1.0-second reload is the fastest of any 6-round revolver secondary, allowing quick magazine cycles. Fire 6 shots deliberately (no wasted rounds), reload in 1 second, and repeat. The semi-auto trigger rewards measured aim at 2.5 fire rate -- the Vasto Prime is not a spray-and-pray secondary. High per-shot damage at good crit stats favors sniping single high-value targets rather than sweeping small enemies.
