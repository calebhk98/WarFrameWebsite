## Overview

Hystrix Prime is the Prime variant of the Hystrix pistol, a secondary weapon with a unique multi-mode fire mechanic. The base Hystrix fires different quill-type projectiles depending on the fire mode selected, each delivering a different elemental damage type. Hystrix Prime improves on the base weapon's critical chance and damage output while retaining the mode-switching system that allows players to adapt their elemental delivery on the fly during combat.

The standard shot profile shows 2.76 impact, 3.68 puncture, and 39.56 slash damage, with a 28% critical chance and 2.2x critical multiplier. The 7.0 fire rate enables fast sustained fire, and the slash-dominant base damage provides bleed proc potential when combined with the 20% status chance and appropriate mods. The mode-switching mechanic allows delivery of Heat, Cold, Electricity, or Toxin procs depending on the selected mode, which significantly expands its elemental flexibility beyond the base profile.

At Mastery Rank 12, Hystrix Prime is a mid-to-upper tier secondary suited to players who have progressed through a significant portion of the star chart.

The 28% critical chance is the weapon's primary stat advantage and supports dedicated crit builds. The 2.2x critical multiplier amplifies crit output meaningfully. The 20% status chance is sufficient for a one-proc trigger but requires status-focused mods to reach consistent multi-proc application.

The 14.3 accuracy rating is low by pistol standards, reflecting the spread behavior of the quill projectiles at longer range. Effective engagement range is short to mid distance.

## Acquisition

Hystrix Prime components are obtained from Void Relics during its Prime Access rotation or unvault events. The Blueprint, Barrel, and Receiver drop from relics at various tiers. When in vault, components must be obtained through trading with other players via the Trading Post.

The base Hystrix is available from the in-game Market for credits and provides access to the mode-switching mechanic at lower stat values.

## Variants

The base Hystrix is a Market-available secondary with lower critical chance and damage output. Hystrix Prime improves these stats while retaining the elemental mode-switching system that defines the weapon family.

No Kuva, Tenet, Wraith, or Vandal version of the Hystrix exists. Hystrix Prime is the highest-tier variant.

## Recommended builds

A critical build for Hystrix Prime uses Primed Hornet Strike for base damage, alongside crit chance and crit multiplier mods. The 28% base critical chance responds well to these mods and reaches consistent critical triggers. Two elemental mods -- or one elemental combined with Seeker for punch-through -- fill the remaining slots.

A hybrid build exploiting the mode-switching mechanic allows players to switch elemental types mid-mission to adapt to faction weaknesses. Heat for Infested organic flesh, Cold for Corpus shields, and Toxin for bypassing Corpus shields altogether are the most common tactical mode choices. This flexibility requires planning around which base elemental mods to equip, as the weapon's intrinsic mode-specific elemental damage combines with modded elemental additions.

## Notes

Hystrix Prime's mode-switching mechanic is activated by holding the alternate fire key, cycling through available quill elemental modes. Each mode fires a different quill projectile type with associated elemental damage. This mechanic is the weapon's defining quality and primary reason to choose it over other secondaries with comparable base stats.

The low accuracy (14.3) causes significant projectile spread at range, making Hystrix Prime best suited for close-range encounters where the spread is less significant. Accuracy mods can tighten the spread for moderate range engagements, extending effective targeting distance.
