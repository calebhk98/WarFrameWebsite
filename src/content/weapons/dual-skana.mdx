## Overview

The Dual Skana are the twin-blade variant of the iconic Skana starter longsword, available from Mastery Rank 0. The damage profile features Puncture (84) as the primary component alongside equal Impact (18) and Slash (18). The 5% critical chance and 1.5x multiplier make crit builds nonviable; the 16% status chance is the more productive foundation. The Dual Skana serves as the introductory dual-sword weapon for new players, providing a first exposure to the dual-sword stance system.

Attack speed of 0.833 and range of 2.5 m are standard. Dual Sword stance mods (Swirling Tiger, Carving Mantis) teach the multi-hit arc combo system that characterizes the dual-sword archetype. The Dual Skana is superseded quickly by mid-game dual swords, but remains useful for Mastery Rank leveling and introducing players to weapon-type mechanics.

## Variants

The Dual Skana has no Prime, Kuva, or Tenet variant. The broader Skana family includes the standard Skana longsword and the Skana Prime (accessible through Prime Access or Void Relics). The Dual Skana does not have a direct prime variant.

## Recommended builds

**Early-game status build.** Use basic elemental mods (Fever Strike for Toxin, Voltaic Strike for Electric) to produce Corrosive. At 16% status, proc frequency is low but sufficient for early star-chart content where enemy health pools are modest. Add Pressure Point and Fury for basic damage and speed.

**Mastery leveling build.** The Dual Skana only needs to reach Rank 30 for Mastery. Equip whatever mods are available, prioritize damage, and run medium-level missions (Mars, Jupiter) to reach max rank quickly.

**Dual Sword stance tutorial.** Equip Swirling Tiger or Carving Mantis and practice the neutral combo chain to learn dual-sword animation timing. This knowledge transfers to all dual swords including endgame options.

## Synergies

Any Warframe with crowd-control or damage amplification abilities eases early-game dual-sword use. Excalibur's Radial Blind groups and staggers enemies, creating safe windows for close-range dual-sword engagement. Rhino's Stomp stuns enemies for follow-up melee. For new players learning the game, Nezha's Warding Halo provides continuous damage absorption during melee range, enabling aggressive close-quarters swings without heavy survivability investment. Volt's Electric Shield placed before engaging dense enemy groups provides bonus Electric damage to melee attacks passing through it, adding an elemental layer to the Dual Skana's physical-only damage profile without spending a mod slot.

## Notes

- Upgrade to Dual Heat Swords, Dual Keres, or Dual Ether as soon as resources allow; the Dual Skana is primarily a starting point.
- Crit investment is wasteful at 5%; focus entirely on status and damage amplification.
