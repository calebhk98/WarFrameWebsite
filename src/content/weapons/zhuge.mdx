## Overview

Zhuge is a Tenno-designed automatic crossbow that fires bolts at a moderate rate, combining meaningful slash damage (75 per bolt) with a 35% status chance and 20% critical chance. This combination makes it one of the more stat-dense crossbows in the game at Mastery Rank 10, suitable for both status-proc and hybrid crit-status builds.

The 75 slash component is the weapon's most distinctive feature. Slash procs bypass armor by dealing damage directly to health, making Zhuge effective against heavily armored Grineer enemies even without Corrosive armor-stripping. A single slash proc deals 35% of the proc damage as a bleed over 6 seconds, and multiple overlapping slash procs stack under the right conditions. At 35% status chance and 4.17 rounds per second, maintaining consistent slash bleed stacks is practical without extensive status mod investment.

The 20-round magazine limits sustained burst fire, and the 2.5-second reload can expose players during brief downtime. Zhuge rewards measured firing over holding the trigger continuously, particularly in Steel Path content where resource management and positioning matter more than raw fire volume.

Zhuge has a prime variant (Zhuge Prime) that significantly improves critical chance, fire rate, and magazine capacity, making the base Zhuge primarily an intermediate stepping stone before accessing the prime version.

## Acquisition

The Zhuge blueprint is available from the in-game Market for 20,000 credits. Build time in the Foundry is 12 hours. Component resources are standard mid-game materials from Tenno-associated planets.

## Variants

Zhuge's prime variant is [Zhuge Prime](/weapons/zhuge-prime), which improves critical chance, magazine capacity, and fire rate while retaining the crossbow archetype.

## Recommended builds

**Slash-viral build:** Zhuge's high slash component makes it ideal for viral plus slash synergy. Equip Cryo Rounds and Malignant Force for Viral. With viral active, slash procs apply to the expanded health pool after Viral halves enemy effective health, dramatically increasing bleed tick damage. Add Hunter Munitions to convert a percentage of critical hits to slash procs, further increasing bleed frequency at the 20% base crit chance. This build is highly effective against Steel Path Grineer.

**Status-hybrid build:** Pair Infected Clip and Stormbringer for Corrosive alongside the built-in slash. Status chance reaches 50-60% with Rifle Aptitude added, enabling reliable proc application on most shots. This build handles armored targets through Corrosive strip while slash bleeds continue to damage health pools. Split Chamber increases effective status proc rate by doubling multishot.

Avoid pure crit builds -- the 20% base crit chance and 2.0x multiplier are insufficient for reliable crit damage without dedicating several mod slots to crit scaling, which leaves too few slots for damage amplification.

## Notes

- Zhuge fires projectiles with travel time, unlike hitscan rifles. Leading moving targets at medium-to-long range is necessary for consistent hits.
- The crossbow archetype allows Zhuge to equip Thunderbolt, converting non-crit arrows into explosive projectiles. This adds area damage at the cost of reduced direct-hit damage.
- Hunter Munitions is particularly strong on Zhuge given the high slash base damage -- slash procs from Hunter Munitions benefit from the existing 75 slash per bolt, resulting in higher bleed tick damage than on weapons with lower slash components.
- Zhuge Prime's improved magazine (30 rounds) and fire rate (5.5 shots/s) make it a substantial upgrade over the base version for players who have access to prime relics.
- The floating decimal value (19.999998 puncture) in WFCD is a floating-point artifact; the effective in-game value is 20.0.
