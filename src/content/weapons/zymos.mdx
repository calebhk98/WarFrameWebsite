## Overview

Zymos is a Helminth-design pistol introduced with the Deimos expansion. The weapon fires spores that latch onto enemies and detonate into a cloud of spores on kill, spreading toxic damage to nearby targets. This propagation mechanic makes Zymos distinct from conventional secondaries and gives it unique value in dense enemy scenarios where one kill generates secondary damage to surrounding enemies.

The base damage profile centers on the built-in 61 toxin damage, with minimal impact (9.2) and slash (13.8) components. The toxin is a native damage type, meaning it deals toxin damage directly and combines with Cold to form Viral or with Heat to form Gas. Gas damage generates a cloud that deals toxin damage in an area of effect, synergizing with Zymos's spore-propagation mechanic. Gas clouds spread additional toxic damage, compounding the on-kill spread effect.

The 30% status chance enables reliable proc application, and toxin procs deal damage over time that bypasses shields. Against Corpus targets, the toxin damage reaches health pools directly, making Zymos particularly effective against shield-reliant enemies without requiring separate shield-stripping. The spore-spread mechanic also applies the weapon's elemental damage type to the secondary targets, allowing procs to chain through densely packed groups.

Zymos's 5% critical chance effectively eliminates crit builds as a viable option. The 2.3x multiplier is wasted at 5% base chance. Status and elemental damage strategies are the only build directions that extract meaningful value from this weapon.

## Recommended builds

**Gas-spread build:** Equip Thermite Rounds (Heat) to combine with the built-in toxin, creating Gas damage. Gas procs generate clouds on proc application, and each cloud deals toxin damage per second to enemies within range. Combined with the Zymos on-kill spore spread, this creates cascading damage zones as enemies die. Add Barrel Diffusion and Lethal Torrent for multishot, doubling effective proc frequency. This build excels in crowd-dense missions such as Survival and Defense.

**Viral-toxin build:** Equip Frostbite (Cold) to combine with toxin for Viral. Viral halves enemy effective health pools, then subsequent toxin procs from Zymos shots deal full damage to the reduced health pool. At 30% status chance with multishot, maintaining Viral stacks on priority targets takes 4-6 shots. This primer role synergizes with a high-damage melee that benefits from the 200% health vulnerability Viral provides against non-boss enemies.

Because Zymos fires at 1.33 shots per second, it functions better as a proc applicator and secondary support tool than a primary damage dealer. Pair it with a high-damage primary or melee to exploit the debuffs Zymos applies.

## Notes

- The accuracy value of 9.8 is extremely low, indicating significant spread. Zymos is designed for close-range engagements where the spore spread compensates for imprecise targeting.
- Toxin damage bypasses shields, making Zymos effective against Corpus enemies without requiring specific anti-shield mods.
- The on-kill spore propagation mechanic scales with weapon mods -- higher damage Zymos shots yield proportionally larger spore clouds on kill.
- The 3.2-second reload is slow for a 17-round magazine; players should reload during movement between objectives rather than mid-combat where possible.
- Zymos's Helminth design aesthetic connects it thematically to the Infested and Deimos content where the weapon was introduced, matching the Infested visual language of the Necraloid syndicate.
