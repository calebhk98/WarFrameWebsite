## Overview

The Viper is a Corpus-manufactured automatic pistol notable for its extremely high fire rate of 14.38 rounds per second -- among the fastest of any secondary weapon. This fire rate defines its playstyle: the Viper empties its 14-round magazine in under one second, followed by an extremely fast 0.7-second reload. The cycle repeats rapidly, making the Viper feel like a continuous-fire weapon for practical purposes despite the small magazine.

Each round deals 17 total damage: 10.2 impact, 5.1 puncture, and 1.7 slash. The impact-dominant profile is weakest against armored enemies but reasonable against unarmored Corpus and Infested targets. At 15% critical chance and a 1.5x multiplier, critical builds offer minimal return; status at 11% is also low, directing build focus toward raw volume output via multishot mods.

The Viper's strength is raw shots-per-second. Players who leverage multishot mods (Barrel Diffusion, Lethal Torrent) effectively double the projectile count, dramatically improving damage output while maintaining the same fire cadence.

## Acquisition

The Viper blueprint is purchasable from the in-game Market for 20,000 credits. Build time is 12 hours. No quest or event prerequisite applies.

## Variants

**Viper Wraith** -- A Wraith variant with improved damage, critical chance, and status chance while retaining the same high fire rate. The Wraith version was historically available through alert missions and events.

**Twin Vipers** -- The akimbo version of the Viper, dual-wielding both pistols simultaneously. This doubles effective fire rate further and improves critical chance, making it a popular upgrade choice.

## Recommended builds

**Multishot spray build**: Equip Barrel Diffusion (multishot) and Lethal Torrent (multishot + fire rate) as the foundation. Add Hornet Strike (base damage) and a heat + cold (Blast) elemental combination to knock down enemies. The combined multishot mods roughly double effective DPS.

**Impact proc build**: Stack Maim or Concussion Rounds to amplify impact damage and leverage impact procs to stagger enemies while the Viper's volume of fire accumulates damage.

## Notes

The low accuracy (15.4) reflects the Viper's wide spread at range. It performs best in close-to-medium combat. The 0.7-second reload is among the fastest available, enabling near-continuous fire. Mod combinations that reduce reload time further can reduce it below 0.5 seconds.


The Viper's place in early to mid-game is as a rapid-cycling sidearm that rewards volume of fire over precise placement. Players who prefer secondary weapons as their primary damage dealer can leverage the Viper's exceptional fire rate with multishot mods to produce competitive DPS against unarmored targets. In tilesets with many weaker enemies (initial Grineer Lancers, Corpus Crewmen), the Viper's fire rate clears groups faster than single-shot alternatives.

For players advancing from the Viper toward upgrades, the Twin Vipers offer dual-wielding at the same fire rate, and the Viper Wraith provides improved damage and critical stats. Both maintain the Viper's defining fire rate while improving on its moderate damage and critical profile.
