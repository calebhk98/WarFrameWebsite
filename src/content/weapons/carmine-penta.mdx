## Overview

The Carmine Penta is a Mastery Rank 6 grenade launcher that fires sticky grenades detonatable on command or on a timer. Each grenade deals 425 total damage split between Impact (75) and Blast (350), with a 10% Critical Chance at 2.0x multiplier and 10% Status Chance. The Penta family defining mechanic is that grenades adhere to surfaces and enemies, allowing players to stack multiple grenades before triggering a simultaneous detonation for burst damage.

At Mastery Rank 6, the Carmine Penta is accessible during mid-game progression. The grenade detonation mechanic rewards players who learn grenade placement timing over simply firing at enemies, making it a skill-expressive option compared to most launcher-type weapons. Its Blast damage base is effective against machinery and Corrupted units.

## Acquisition

The Carmine Penta blueprint is available from the in-game Market for credits. Components are crafted in the Foundry. No special prerequisites beyond Mastery Rank 6 are required.

## Variants

The Secura Penta is a Syndicate-enhanced variant available through the Perrin Sequence. The Carmine Penta base model provides a more accessible entry into the Penta playstyle. Check the [Carmine Penta wiki page](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Carmine_Penta) for variant details.

## Recommended builds

The on-demand detonation allows for stacking multiple grenades before triggering. Pair Thunderbolt with Tainted Magazine for longer grenade arming distance and increased detonation blast radius. Thermite Rounds and Rifle Aptitude improve status output from the blast damage type.

Heavier elemental builds add Toxic Barrage and Thermite Rounds to shift the Carmine Penta toward Gas or Radiation damage types through elemental combination.

## Notes

- Grenades from the Carmine Penta stick to enemies and surfaces; they do not explode on initial contact.
- Multiple stacked grenades all detonate simultaneously on the detonation trigger, enabling burst damage spikes.
- The Blast status proc ragdolls enemies; chain with stacked grenades for area crowd control.
- Players should avoid close-range detonations; the blast radius extends toward the user.
- The Carmine Penta is one of the few primary weapons with an on-demand detonation input rather than a fuse-based system; this distinguishes it from the Tonkor (fuse timer) and gives the player precise control over damage timing.
- Stacking 10 grenades (a full magazine) before detonation deals up to 4,250 damage in the blast radius simultaneously -- against clustered enemies in Defense or Survival tilesets, this spike can eliminate entire waves if deployed at a choke point.
- The Secura Penta syndicate variant (Perrin Sequence) features a unique passive that credits kills to the squad, making it particularly popular in Arbitration and Disruption missions where sustained kill counts matter for reward pacing.
