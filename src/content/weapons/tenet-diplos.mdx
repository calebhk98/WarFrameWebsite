## Overview

The Tenet Diplos is a Sisters of Parvos Tenet secondary pistol requiring Mastery Rank 16. It fires at 9.67 rounds per second with 100% accuracy -- a precision auto pistol that delivers consistent damage at range without spread. Its 28 total damage per shot distributes across all three physical types: 11.2 impact, 7.84 puncture, and 8.96 slash. The balanced split means the Diplos does not favor any specific faction weakness but avoids the worst mismatches.

The 36% critical chance is the Tenet Diplos's strongest feature for a secondary. At that base rate, Point Strike pushes it to near 100%, effectively guaranteeing crits on every shot. The 2.2x critical multiplier is above baseline, meaning consistent high-damage crits at scale. The 14% status chance is low -- this is a critical weapon, not a status platform. With a 92-round magazine, the Tenet Diplos fires for extended periods before the 2.8-second reload interrupts output.

Tenet weapons carry a unique elemental bonus from the Sister whose link was broken. The bonus element and magnitude vary, adding additional elemental damage beyond the base stats.

## Acquisition

Obtain through the Sisters of Parvos system. Trigger a Sister Candidate via Granum Void, then track and vanquish the Sister across her escalating confrontations. Vanquishing yields her Tenet weapon. The specific weapon type and elemental bonus are determined at the time the Sister is created.

## Variants

The Tenet Diplos has no base non-Tenet counterpart and no Prime or Wraith version.

## Recommended builds

**Full critical**: Hornet Strike, Barrel Diffusion, Primed Pistol Gambit, Primed Target Cracker, Lethal Torrent, and two elemental mods. With Primed Pistol Gambit and 36% base crit, the Diplos reaches near-100% crit chance. Every shot crits at 2.2x base multiplier. Galvanized Aptitude adds stacking damage on status procs applied by elemental mods.

**Galvanized crit**: Replace base mods with Galvanized variants where available for stacking damage bonuses. Galvanized Chamber (multishot), Galvanized Shot (status-kill bonus damage), and Amalgam Barrel Diffusion. The high fire rate makes kill accumulation rapid.

**Crit plus viral**: Primed Pistol Gambit, Primed Target Cracker, cold plus toxin for Viral, Barrel Diffusion. Viral halves enemy max health while crits deal their amplified damage. The combination is reliable for steel-path enemy scaling.

## Notes

The 100% accuracy at auto fire rate makes the Tenet Diplos effective at longer secondary-weapon ranges where low-accuracy secondaries scatter shots. At max range for secondary weapons, all rounds hit the intended target without spread loss. The 92-round magazine is generous for a secondary -- most encounters do not require a reload mid-fight.
