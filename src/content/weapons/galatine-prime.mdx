## Overview

Galatine Prime is a slash-focused greatsword representing the optimal balance between raw power and practical melee engagement. The weapon's damage distribution heavily favors slash, constituting 50 percent of the total damage output, making it exceptional against unarmored and lightly-armored targets while remaining effective against all faction types. The 210-total-damage output in the main attack mode provides substantial per-hit impact, rewarding deliberate positioning and tactical strikes over rapid button-mashing. The 22 percent critical chance and 2.0x multiplier enable critical-focused builds without requiring extreme modding investments. The 26 percent status chance supports status-application strategies without requiring exotic modding, enabling hybrid critical-status builds. The greatsword attack pattern with 3.4-meter range enables extended reach compared to smaller melee weapons, allowing players to maintain effective distance while delivering powerful strikes.

Galatine Prime excels in organized squad play where slash-procs coordinate with other damage sources. The weapon's high slash emphasis creates natural synergy with frames focusing on slash amplification or status-immunity scaling. The balanced critical and status opportunities enable flexible modding approaches, whether emphasizing guaranteed criticals or rapid status application. The relatively slow 0.92 attack speed emphasizes deliberate combat pacing over rapid-fire mechanics, rewarding players who value positioning and target selection over twitch reflexes. The prime status ensures optimal damage scaling while maintaining accessibility through void fissure farming, making it an excellent endgame melee investment.

## Variants

The original Galatine serves as the non-prime baseline with reduced damage output. The Kuva Galatine offers elemental customization as an endgame alternative. Galatine Prime bridges these variants, offering the best combination of raw power and accessibility. Each variant maintains the distinctive greatsword mechanics and slash-focused damage distribution.

## Recommended builds

Pressure Point provides foundational melee damage enhancement applicable to all weapons. Organ Shatter amplifies critical hit effectiveness, pushing the critical multiplier above 5.0x with proper stacking. True Steel increases base critical chance toward guaranteed hits. Cleaving Whirlwind enables movement during combat without penalty, supporting aggressive engagement patterns. Elemental mods like Primed Fury and Volcanic Edge combine to create radiation builds or additional status procs. For status-focused builds, Pathogen Rounds and Thermite Rounds enable viral damage scaling. Relentless Combination accelerates attack speed, mitigating the baseline 0.92 speed for rapid-fire playstyles. The balanced critical and status baseline enables hybrid builds combining both approaches.

Galatine Prime excels in situations emphasizing slash damage output and melee precision. The 26 percent status chance enables reasonable status procs without requiring exotic modding, making it accessible to mid-game players lacking advanced build knowledge. The 3.4-meter range extends effective engagement distance, reducing the need for repositioning between targets.
