## Overview

The Twin Vipers are dual pistols available at Mastery Rank 5, purchasable from the Market for 20,000 credits. They fire at 25.0 rounds per second -- an extremely high fire rate for a secondary weapon. Each round deals 17 damage: 10.2 impact, 5.1 puncture, and 1.7 slash. The impact dominance means individual shots are most effective against Corpus shields. The slash component is minimal and does not generate reliable bleed procs.

The 15% critical chance and 1.5x multiplier are below useful crit thresholds; this is not a crit weapon. The 11% status chance, similarly, does not support reliable proc application even with the high fire rate providing volume. The Twin Vipers' value is in raw fire rate -- at 25 rounds per second with Lethal Torrent, the fire rate exceeds most primary weapons. The 28-round magazine at 25 fire rate empties in just over 1 second before the 2-second reload.

The Twin Vipers are the base form of the Wraith lineage. Players who have unlocked the Twin Vipers Wraith or have access to higher-tier dual automatics will find those options more efficient, but the Twin Vipers remain accessible at MR 5.

## Acquisition

The Twin Vipers blueprint is purchasable from the in-game Market for 20,000 credits. Build time is 12 hours. No quest or event unlock is required.

## Variants

**Twin Vipers Wraith** -- An improved Wraith variant with higher impact damage, magazine, and stats. Distributed through alerts and events.

## Recommended builds

**Impact Corpus**: Hornet Strike, Barrel Diffusion, Lethal Torrent, and magnetic (cold plus electric) mods. Impact deals bonus damage to Corpus shields; magnetic deals additional bonus damage to shields. The 25 fire rate with Lethal Torrent strips Corpus shields rapidly. High-impact builds are naturally suited to Corpus-heavy missions.

**Fire rate focus**: Hornet Strike, Barrel Diffusion, Lethal Torrent, two elemental mods. Maximize fire rate and per-shot damage. The extremely high fire rate creates a distinctive rapid-fire secondary experience even at low-difficulty content.

**Elemental conversion**: Hornet Strike, Heated Charge plus Pathogen Rounds (Gas), Barrel Diffusion. Convert the impact base to elemental through mods. The high fire rate applies elemental procs rapidly despite the 11% base status, simply through volume.

## Notes

Magazine management is critical for the Twin Vipers. At 25 fire rate with a 28-round magazine, shooting continuously empties the magazine in approximately 1.1 seconds. The 2.0-second reload means the downtime nearly doubles the shooting time per cycle. Use the Twin Vipers in controlled bursts rather than holding the trigger continuously. Short, decisive engagements are more efficient than sustained fire that wastes shots during transitions.
