## Overview

Shedu is a primary launcher weapon that serves as Sentient-designed arm cannon with combined heat and electricity damage; drops from Conculysts and Battalysts in the Eidolon Plains at night. Mastery Rank 13 is required to equip the weapon. Players who invest in building it properly will find it reliable across the star chart, with scaling potential on the Steel Path when supported by appropriate mods and Warframe ability synergies.

The primary slot is competitive in Warframe, and Shedu occupies a distinct position within it. Understanding its damage profile -- the distribution across physical damage types and any elemental components -- is the first step toward optimizing it for specific mission types and enemy factions.

## Stats

Per WFCD warframe-items data, Shedu deals 71 heat, 87 electricity per shot (combined radiation on contact) per attack.
