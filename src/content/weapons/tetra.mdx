## Overview

The Tetra is a Corpus-manufactured rifle that fires energy bolts with a distinctive visual profile. Its damage breakdown is unusual for a rifle: 32 total damage per shot consists of 6.4 impact and 25.6 slash, with zero puncture. This slash-dominant profile means the Tetra naturally generates bleed procs at a reasonable rate, which deal percentage-based true damage and bypass armor.

The weapon features a 20% status chance, which is functional for a low-tier rifle, and a very low 4% critical chance that makes critical builds unproductive. Status is the clear modding direction: bleed from inherent slash damage, combined with elemental status procs, can make the Tetra perform above its tier against enemies that are otherwise hard to kill at this mastery range. At Mastery Rank 3, the Tetra competes against the Braton and Grakata for new-player primary weapon slots.

With a 60-round magazine and 6.67 rounds per second fire rate, ammunition management is simple. The 2-second reload is standard for the category. The Tetra's greatest limitation is its modest base damage, which falls off in mid-game content unless well-modded.

## Acquisition

The Tetra blueprint is purchasable from the in-game Market for 15,000 credits. Build time is 24 hours. The weapon is available without any prerequisite quest or mission unlock. No Vandal version is available through alerts; the base Tetra is the only obtainable form through standard Market purchase.

## Variants

**Tetra Vandal** -- A Vandal version with improved stats appeared in certain event rewards. It offers higher critical chance, increased damage, and overall better performance over the base model. Check the WARFRAME Wiki for current availability and acquisition method, as event weapons cycle in and out of accessibility.

The **Tenet Tetra** is a separate weapon in the Tenet family obtained from Sisters of Parvos and features heat-based innate elemental damage with significantly higher baseline stats.

## Recommended builds

**Status-slash build**: Equip Serration and Split Chamber for base damage, then add Rime Rounds (cold) and Infected Clip (toxin) to achieve Viral. The Tetra's inherent slash damage will produce bleed procs, which Viral amplifies to deal doubled bleed damage. Hunter Munitions can replace a mod slot to force additional slash procs on crit, though with 4% critical chance this is less reliable without stacking Point Strike.

**Elemental variety build**: Use Thermite Rounds (heat) and Stormbringer (electric) for Radiation, effective against Alloy armor worn by most Grineer heavy units. Combine with Flawed Serration and Point Strike to make the most of available mod capacity early in progression.

## Notes

The Tetra is a solid first step into rifle usage for new players. Its slash-first damage profile distinguishes it from more generic rifles like the Braton and teaches players about physical damage type advantages. Status-focused play that learns to exploit Slash's true damage behavior pays dividends on higher-tier weapons later in the game.

The Tetra does not have unique weapon-specific mods or augments. Standard rifle mod compatibility applies: rifle-specific mods like Serration, Split Chamber, and Ammo Mutation function normally.
