## Overview

The Glaive Prime is a throwing melee weapon combining direct impact damage with status application through its thrown mechanics. The prime variant excels in mid-tier to endgame missions, offering versatility through thrown and melee attack modes. Players throw the glaive to hit distant targets, execute a melee combo against nearby enemies, or charge attacks for enhanced effects. The weapon's balanced critical and status stats enable hybrid builds capitalizing on both damage types. As a prime weapon currently unvaulted, accessibility remains reasonable through void fissure farming, making it a staple of many players' arsenals.

## Damage

The Glaive Prime's damage distribution emphasizes slash at 35 with respectable impact and puncture components. Slash procs create bleeds that scale with weapon damage and enemy health, making the weapon increasingly effective against high-health targets. The balanced critical and status stats allow players to choose either damage scaling path. Thrown attacks benefit from weapon throw distance mods, allowing precision engagement beyond melee range. Melee attacks enable combo building and stance-based bonus damage, creating varied playstyles within a single weapon.

## Acquisition

Glaive Prime components drop from void relics across Neo and Axi eras. Players must accumulate three components plus a void trace to open relics containing the weapon. Multiple farming runs supply all necessary components. Building requires 15,000 credits and 12 hours of foundry time. Currently unvaulted means relics rotate through void fissure activities regularly, making acquisition straightforward for dedicated farmers. Trading is available for parts obtained from relics, allowing players to purchase components directly if farming falls short.

## Variants

The original Glaive exists as a non-prime variant with lower base damage and slightly different attack speed. The Glaive Prime is superior in nearly every regard, making the non-prime largely obsolete. Cosmetic skins are available post-acquisition, offering aesthetic variety without mechanical changes.

## Recommended builds

Glaive Prime builds split into thrown-focused and melee-focused variants. Melee-heavy builds prioritize stance mods like Twirling Spire or Sundering Weave for combo damage scaling. Mods like Organ Shatter and Bloodletting enhance critical damage and proc stacking. Condition Overload scales with enemy status effects, making status mods valuable. Thrown-focused builds emphasize throw-distance and velocity mods. Hybrid builds balance both attack modes, using fast-attack stances while carrying throw-utility mods. Elemental mods allow customization: corrosive against armor, magnetic against shields, viral for health scaling.

## Notes

The weapon's throw mechanic creates unique gameplay dynamics absent in other melees. Thrown attacks trigger separately from melee attacks in most mission contexts. Precision throw positioning rewards skilled players with satisfying direct hits. Combo counter builds on both throw and melee hits, encouraging players to alternate between attack modes. The weapon scales excellently with riven mods given its balanced critical and status stats, justifying investment for long-term use. As a prime weapon, building precedence prioritizes more powerful frames and weapons, but the Glaive Prime deserves consideration for its versatility and unique playstyle. Steel Path mission performance depends heavily on proper modding and forma investment, but remains viable as a secondary melee choice complementing primary weapons.
