## Overview

The Laetum is an Incarnon secondary pistol combining balanced damage distribution with rapid fire and solid status potential. As a dual-wield secondary option paired with primaries like the Felarx, it provides consistent damage output and crowd control through high attack speed. The Incarnon system allows the weapon to evolve through use, unlocking enhanced mechanics and form transformations. The weapon bridges mid-tier and endgame content effectively, performing admirably in sorties while remaining accessible to players building their arsenal.

## Damage

The Laetum's damage favors puncture at 96 with significant impact at 64 and no slash component. This distribution favors armored targets where puncture reduces enemy armor. The 22% critical chance and 2.2x multiplier support crit-focused modding. Status chance of 22% enables status-building approaches when paired with elemental mods. The weapon lacks inherent elemental damage, allowing full mod flexibility. The low fire rate of 2.5 rounds per second reflects its Incarnon semi-automatic fire pattern; the Incarnon evolution significantly changes the weapon's behavior.

## Acquisition

Laetum components drop from Zariman Ten Zero void fissure missions. Players must farm relics and accumulate components across multiple runs. The blueprint costs 15,000 credits and requires 12 hours of foundry time. As with other Incarnon weapons, acquisition rewards engagement with endgame fissure content rather than providing purchase options. Trading is prohibited due to the weapon's Incarnon nature, making farming the only acquisition path.

## Variants

No prime variant of the Laetum exists. The Incarnon evolution serves as the primary upgrade path, unlocking through void fissure activities. Variants are limited to cosmetic skins applied post-acquisition. The weapon was designed as an Incarnon-exclusive secondary, differentiating it from earlier weapon designs.

## Recommended builds

Laetum builds emphasize utility through elemental selection and status application. Serration equivalents for secondaries like Hornet Strike provide base damage scaling. Multishot via Barrel Diffusion and Lethal Force improves damage output and proc frequency. Elemental mods allow customization for specific mission targets: Freeze mods against unarmored targets, corrosive mods for armor stripping, magnetic against shield-heavy enemies. Reload reduction mods manage the magazine size, extending engagement time. The weapon's dual-wield nature means using it alongside a strong primary, requiring careful mod distribution to balance resources across both weapons.

## Notes

The Laetum excels as a panic weapon or finishing tool thanks to its rapid fire rate and reasonable accuracy. Secondary weapons in Warframe serve niche roles, and the Laetum succeeds when switching between it and a complementary primary weapon. The Incarnon evolution unlocks specific benefits that reward sustained use. Magazine capacity of 12 limits engagement duration in endless missions without reload-speed investment. The weapon transitions poorly to solo endgame content without extensive forma and rivens but performs admirably in squad-based missions where sustained damage supplements teammates. Its role as a utility secondary alongside status-scaling primaries makes it valuable in organized teams capable of stacking condition overload effects.

## Strategic positioning

Laetum use requires discipline in secondary weapon discipline. Swapping during crucial moments risks losing favorable damage multipliers or combos. Optimal play involves cycling between primary and secondary strategically rather than panic-switching when threatened. Teams coordinating elemental effects benefit significantly from Laetum contributions to status stacking patterns.
