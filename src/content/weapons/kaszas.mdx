## Overview

Kaszas is an Arch-Melee scythe used in Archwing and open-world Archwing segments. It is crafted using components from Steel Meridian and Red Veil syndicates, making it a mid-progression Arch-Melee option tied to syndicate standing. The weapon's slash-dominant damage distribution (80% slash) and 20% status chance support status proc builds against armored Grineer and Corpus units encountered in Archwing missions. Its 2.5 m range is above average for the Arch-Melee category, providing useful multi-target swing coverage in space combat scenarios.

## Acquisition

Kaszas components drop from syndicate rank rewards. The Blade drops at Steel Meridian's Honored rank, and the Handle drops at Red Veil's Honored rank. The blueprint is available from the in-game Market. Building requires Credits, Tellurium, and the two syndicate components. Construction takes 12 hours in the Foundry. Tellurium is most efficiently farmed on Archwing missions in Uranus.

## Variants

No Wraith, Vandal, or Prime variant of the Kaszas exists as of the current game version. It is the sole version of this Arch-Melee scythe.

## Recommended builds

Kaszas is an Arch-Melee weapon used exclusively in Archwing, Railjack, and open-world Archwing segments. Arch-Melee mods differ from standard melee mods. Core mods for Kaszas include Primed Reach for extended range in space combat, Astral Slash for elevated slash damage, and Seismic Wave for heavy slam scaling. Its 20% status chance enables reliable proc builds using Voltaic Strike or Vicious Frost for elemental combinations.

The slash-dominant damage profile (80% slash) favors bleed proc builds when status chance is raised sufficiently. Arch-Melee combo does not function the same as standard melee combo; Kaszas benefits more from direct damage scaling than from combo multiplier mods.

## Notes

Kaszas is one of the few Arch-Melee weapons with a built-in scythe animation set, which influences spin attack range and timing in Archwing combat. It is crafted as a consumable-on-build weapon, meaning the blueprint is consumed after a single use and must be purchased again to build another copy. Its Tenno faction tag gives it access to syndicate proc augment mods from associated syndicates.
