## Overview

Kaszas is a Tenno-designed Arch-Melee scythe used exclusively in Archwing, Railjack, and open-world Archwing segments. It is one of the few Arch-Melee weapons tied to syndicate standing rather than Market purchase or Dojo research, requiring players to earn components through Steel Meridian and Red Veil syndicate ranks. The scythe design gives Kaszas wide swing animations in zero-gravity Archwing combat, suitable for hitting multiple enemy units in a single pass.

The slash-dominant damage distribution is the weapon's defining characteristic: 313.6 slash out of 392 total damage constitutes 80% of the base damage output. Slash damage in Archwing applies bleed procs against high-health enemy units, and at 20% status chance, triggering bleed reliably requires status-focused Arch-Melee mods. The 2.5 m range is above average for Arch-Melee, providing practical multi-target coverage in space combat encounters.

At Mastery Rank 4, Kaszas is accessible earlier in progression than many Arch-Melee options, though the syndicate component requirement adds a standing investment prerequisite for players who have not ranked into Steel Meridian and Red Veil.

## Acquisition

The Kaszas blueprint is available from the in-game Market for credits. Building the weapon additionally requires the Kaszas Blade, obtained as a standing reward from Steel Meridian at the Honored rank tier, and the Kaszas Handle, obtained from Red Veil at their Honored rank tier. Players must be simultaneously enrolled in both syndicates at sufficient rank to collect both components.

Construction takes 12 hours in the Foundry and requires Tellurium alongside standard resources. Tellurium is most efficiently farmed through Archwing missions in the Uranus node cluster or Sharkwing missions on the Uranus tile set.

## Variants

No Wraith, Vandal, Kuva, Tenet, or Prime variant of the Kaszas exists as of the current game version. Kaszas is the sole version of this Arch-Melee scythe family. The blueprint is consumed upon construction and must be repurchased from the Market to build an additional copy.

## Recommended builds

Kaszas benefits from Primed Reach as the primary range-extension mod, increasing effective swing coverage in Archwing zero-gravity environments. Astral Slash raises slash damage output, amplifying the weapon's already-dominant slash base for stronger bleed proc damage. Seismic Wave improves heavy attack performance, useful for targeting grouped enemies during Railjack boarding events.

For status-focused builds, Voltaic Strike (Electricity) combined with the intrinsic slash damage produces Slash procs alongside Electricity procs, applying bleed and shock effects against grouped enemies. Vicious Frost (Cold) pairs with the slash base to create Magnetic combined damage effective against Corpus Archwing targets.

The slash-dominant profile makes bleed-focused mods the natural complement for maximizing damage against heavy Archwing enemies. The 20% status chance should be supplemented by at least one status chance Arch-Melee mod to push proc frequency above casual trigger rates.

## Notes

Kaszas uses a scythe animation set unique within the Arch-Melee category. The wide arc of scythe attacks is well-suited to space combat where multiple enemy units may approach simultaneously from different angles. Spin attack coverage in Archwing is determined partly by the stance animation, and Kaszas's scythe timing provides broad area-of-effect potential in dense enemy groups.

Because Kaszas is faction-tagged as Tenno, it is eligible for syndicate proc augment mods from associated syndicates. These augment mods add faction-specific proc effects to attacks, providing additional utility in missions against corresponding enemy factions.

Arch-Melee combo mechanics differ from standard ground combat combo -- the combo counter builds differently and heavy attacks behave distinctly in zero-gravity. Players transitioning from ground melee should account for these differences when planning Kaszas builds.
