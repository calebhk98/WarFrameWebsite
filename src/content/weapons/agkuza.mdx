## Overview

The [Agkuza](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Agkuza) is an Archwing melee weapon used exclusively in Archwing, Empyrean, and Sanctuary Onslaught Archwing content. It deals 436 total damage per attack: 305 slash, 65 impact, and 65 puncture. The slash-dominant profile makes the Agkuza effective at applying bleed procs against Archwing enemies in zero-gravity combat. The 5% critical chance and 1.5x multiplier are minimal, while the 15% status chance supports moderate proc application.

At Mastery Rank 3, the Agkuza is one of the earlier accessible Archwing melee options. The high base slash damage makes it naturally strong against flesh-type enemies encountered in Archwing missions.

## Acquisition

The Agkuza blueprint is available from the Market for credits. As an Archwing melee weapon, it is crafted in the Foundry using Archwing-specific materials alongside standard resources. Permanently available and never vaulted.

## Variants

No Prime, Vandal, or Wraith variant of the Agkuza exists. The Dual Keres and Onorix are alternative Archwing melee weapons with different damage profiles.

## Recommended builds

**Slash status Archwing build:** Equip Archwing-compatible melee mods -- Astral Slash (slash damage amplifier), Vicious Spiral (critical chance), and elemental Archwing mods. The 305 base slash generates bleed procs at 15% status chance, effective against Archwing Grineer and Corpus fighter types.

**Heavy attack burst:** Archwing heavy attacks from the Agkuza deliver wide-arc slashes covering a broad area in zero-gravity. Combined with the high base slash damage, heavy attacks eliminate groups of lighter Archwing enemies in a single pass.

**Speed-focused build:** Add Archwing melee attack speed mods to compensate for the 1.0 base attack speed. Faster attacks increase slash proc accumulation rate and combo meter buildup during extended Archwing encounters.

## Synergies

- **Itzal's Cosmic Crush:** Pulls nearby enemies into a cluster. Agkuza heavy attacks during Cosmic Crush hit all gathered targets with the wide slash arc simultaneously.
- **Zero-gravity mobility:** Archwing melee combat rewards forward momentum. Bullet-dash attacks and forward-moving combo animations close distance rapidly, enabling the Agkuza's melee range to apply to fast-moving Archwing enemies.
- **Archwing missions -- Exterminate:** High-slash-proc weapons excel in Archwing Exterminate missions where volume of kills is the objective. Bleed procs from the Agkuza continue dealing damage between swings.

## Notes

- Archwing melee has no stance mod system. Combat direction is player-controlled in three dimensions, delivering sweeps based on movement and aim direction.
- The Agkuza's slash dominance (70% of total damage) is well-suited to Archwing enemies, many of which have Flesh or similar unarmored health types.
- Archwing weapon mods differ from ground weapon mods. Ensure all equipped mods have Archwing compatibility before building.

The Agkuza benefits from Arch-Melee mods rather than standard melee mods. Sacrificial Steel and Sacrificial Pressure provide strong crit scaling in Archwing missions, and the Agkuza's slash distribution means Slash proc bleed damage applies to the large health pools of Archwing enemies.
