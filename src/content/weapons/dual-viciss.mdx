## Overview

The Dual Viciss is a dual-sword melee weapon with an unusual damage profile: 51 Puncture, 51 Slash, zero Impact, and a built-in Gas element (153). The innate Gas damage is the weapon's distinguishing feature -- Gas deals area-of-effect Toxin damage on proc, effective against Infested and unarmored enemies. The 23% critical chance and 2.1x multiplier provide a functional but secondary crit layer, while the 29% status chance enables consistent Gas proc application. Mastery Rank 14 is required.

The dual-sword attack speed of 1.0 and 2.4 m range are standard. The equal Puncture/Slash split means the Dual Viciss is versatile across enemy factions without hard resistance penalties on either physical type. The Gas element makes it particularly effective in Infested missions where Gas procs deal area Toxin damage, killing clustered enemies simultaneously.

## Variants

The Dual Viciss has no Prime, Kuva, or Tenet variant. It is a standalone weapon in the dual-sword category with unique innate Gas damage.

## Recommended builds

**Gas/Toxin Infested build.** The innate Gas element provides area Toxin DoT on proc. Against Infested, Gas procs chain to nearby enemies, clearing clusters effectively. Add Primed Pressure Point, Organ Shatter, and Blood Rush for crit scaling alongside the area Gas damage. Include a Heat 60/60 mod (since Toxin + Heat = Gas again, so actually it won't combine; add a different element) for additional elemental damage coverage. Note: adding a Toxin mod enhances the Gas directly; adding non-Heat/non-Toxin elements creates a second independent element.

**Slash/Viral hybrid.** The 51 Slash component enables Slash bleed procs alongside Viral amplification. Use Primed Fever Strike and Cryo Rounds for Viral. Slash procs through Viral-weakened health tick independently from the Gas area damage.

**Condition Overload multi-proc.** The 29% status with innate Gas already provides one consistent proc type. Add two more elemental 60/60 pairs for two additional unique status types, activating Condition Overload scaling from three or more unique procs per target.

## Synergies

Saryn's Spores interact with Gas/Toxin, spreading the Toxin component to nearby enemies and creating chained death. Equinox's Day form damage amplification (Mend/Maim) stacks with the Gas area damage. Frames that group enemies (Khora, Vauban) maximize Gas proc area coverage by concentrating multiple targets.

## Notes

- Adding a Toxin elemental mod enhances the innate Gas damage by increasing the Toxin component feeding into it.
- The Gas proc deals area Toxin damage around the enemy -- position to hit multiple enemies simultaneously.
