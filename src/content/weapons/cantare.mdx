## Overview

The [Cantare](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Cantare) is a secondary throwing weapon that fires explosive projectiles in an arc. Each shot deals 90 total damage: 63 puncture and 27 slash, detonating on impact for area damage. The 18% critical chance and 2.0x multiplier support functional critical builds; the 22% status chance enables moderate proc application. The 6-round magazine and fast 1.2-second reload allow frequent volleys.

At Mastery Rank 10, the Cantare targets mid-to-late game players who appreciate the throwing weapon category's unique arc-trajectory mechanics and area detonation.

## Acquisition

The Cantare blueprint is available from the Market for credits. As a standard Market weapon at MR10, it is permanently available and never vaulted. The completed weapon can be purchased with platinum.

## Variants

No Prime, Vandal, or Wraith variant exists. The base Cantare is the only available form. Players interested in alternative throwing weapons with different elemental properties should compare the Sancti Castanas or Pox.

## Recommended builds

**Critical puncture build:** Hornet Strike, Barrel Diffusion, Lethal Torrent, Primed Pistol Gambit, Primed Target Cracker, and three elemental mods for viral or corrosive. At 18% base crit, Primed Pistol Gambit pushes into reliable yellow-crit. The puncture-dominant physical damage (70%) deals bonus damage against Grineer Ferrite Armor, complementing a corrosive elemental configuration.

**Status explosive build:** Replace crit mods with elemental status mods -- Pistol Pestilence, Frostbite, Jolt, Scorch, and Primed Heated Charge. The 22% status chance across the explosion radius applies procs to multiple enemies per detonation. Viral halves group health; heat adds persistent damage.

**Area detonation build:** Focus on maximizing explosion radius through range-affecting mods (if available for throwing weapons) and elemental combinations that maximize multi-target damage. The arc trajectory allows detonations at corners and behind cover without direct line-of-sight.

## Synergies

- **Arc trajectory utility:** The Cantare's projectile travels in an arc, enabling shots over obstacles. Against entrenched enemies behind barricades (Defense objective sides, Corpus crates, etc.), angled throws deliver detonations without requiring direct exposure.
- **Condition Overload melee:** Puncture and slash procs from the Cantare followed by elemental procs set up three unique status types for Condition Overload follow-up melee strikes.
- **Rhino's Roar:** Each projectile's 90 base damage receives Roar's multiplicative bonus. In the explosion's AOE radius, multiple enemies receive amplified damage simultaneously.

## Notes

- The 6-round magazine at 3.33 fire rate empties in approximately 1.8 seconds. The 1.2-second reload is fast, creating a fire-then-reload rhythm with minimal downtime.
- As a throwing weapon, the Cantare benefits from Barrel Diffusion's additional projectile mod, effectively doubling per-trigger output. Confirm mod compatibility with throwing secondary category before purchase.
- Puncture dominance makes the Cantare effective in Grineer missions where Ferrite and Alloy Armor are the primary defense types. Add corrosive or heat to complement the natural puncture advantage.
- Riven disposition at 3/5 provides neutral roll potential. A multishot riven fires additional projectiles per throw, multiplying AOE coverage per trigger pull.
