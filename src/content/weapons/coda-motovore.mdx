## Overview

The [Coda Motovore](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Coda_Motovore) is a Jade Shadows rapier melee weapon at Mastery Rank 17. It delivers 250 total damage per attack with an equal physical damage distribution across impact (83.3), puncture (83.35), and slash (83.3). The 25% critical chance and 2.3x multiplier support reliable critical builds, while the 30% status chance allows effective status application. This balanced profile makes the Coda Motovore versatile against all enemy factions, as no single physical damage type dominates.

Rapiers have access to the Vulpine Mask stance mod, which provides elegant thrust-based combo sequences with forward momentum, ideal for mobile combat. Rapiers also deal bonus damage on charged attacks to specific enemy types.

## Variants

The Coda Motovore is the Jade Shadows enhanced form of the Motovore rapier. The base Motovore has lower critical and status stats. The Coda version is the endgame upgrade for rapier enthusiasts at MR17.

## Recommended builds

**Critical hybrid:** Primed Pressure Point, Berserker Fury, Blood Rush, Organ Shatter, Weeping Wounds, Condition Overload, and two elemental mods for viral or corrosive. At 25% base critical chance, Blood Rush reaches reliable yellow-crit at x4 combo. Weeping Wounds parallels the status side simultaneously. Condition Overload amplifies each hit as procs accumulate. This is the standard high-performance melee build for weapons with dual critical and status viability.

**Faction-specific build:** Replace one elemental mod with a Primed Smite variant for the target faction. The balanced physical distribution means all three physical damage types apply equally to faction-specific armor, so elemental additions are the primary damage lever.

**Heavy attack:** Rapier heavy attacks perform a powerful lunge thrust dealing concentrated damage to a single target. Corrupt Charge, Killing Blow, and Amalgam Organ Shatter combined with the 2.3x critical multiplier create burst damage useful for elite enemy elimination.

## Synergies

- **Blood Rush:** At 25% base crit, Blood Rush achieves effective yellow-crit territory at x4 combo. The rapier's fast thrust combos build combo meter quickly, accelerating Blood Rush ramp-up.
- **Vulpine Mask stance:** Provides forward-thrusting combos with good range coverage. The Coda Motovore's 2.5 m range pairs with stance animations that extend effective reach beyond the base value during thrust sequences.
- **Condition Overload:** The balanced physical damage distribution plus elemental additions create multiple unique status types efficiently. Three distinct proc types reach Condition Overload's full bonus within the first several hits.

## Notes

- The equal physical damage split (33% each for impact, puncture, slash) means the Coda Motovore is equally effective against all standard enemy armor types without adjustment. This versatility reduces the need for faction-specific reconfiguration between missions.
- Rapiers cannot equip heavy blade stances. The Vulpine Mask stance is the primary and most-used option; confirm it is equipped before min-maxing combo patterns.
- Mastery Rank 17 is in the veteran range. The Coda Motovore's performance reflects this gating, delivering consistent endgame output competitive with top-tier melee options.
