## Overview

Cedo Prime is the Orokin-enhanced Prime variant of the [Cedo](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Cedo), a Tenno shotgun built around a unique disc alt-fire mechanic. Its damage profile is almost entirely Slash (32 per shot with zero impact and puncture), positioning it naturally for Viral-Slash endgame builds. A 24% critical chance and 2.4x critical multiplier provide reliable crit output, while the base 2% status chance on primary fire is intentionally low -- the Cedo's disc alt-fire interacts with each primary shot to amplify status application, rewarding players who integrate disc throws into their firing rhythm.

At Mastery Rank 15, Cedo Prime targets players who have completed substantial star chart and Void Relic progression. Its Slash-dominant damage type pairs exceptionally with Hunter Munitions and Viral mods for health-stripping at high enemy levels.

## Acquisition

Cedo Prime is obtained through the Void Relic system. Neuroptics, Chassis, Systems, and Blueprint components drop from specific Lith, Meso, Neo, and Axi Void Relics obtained in Void Fissure missions. Prime components and the built weapon are tradeable via Trade Chat or warframe.market.

The base Cedo (non-Prime) is researched and crafted in the Tenno Lab of a Clan Dojo, requiring Dojo research investment before blueprint access. Cedo Prime is the recommended version for endgame players and does not require Dojo access.

## Variants

**Cedo (base)** -- Mastery Rank 8, lower stats, but the disc alt-fire mechanic is identical. Accessible earlier through Clan Dojo research.

No Kuva, Tenet, Vandal, or Wraith variant of the Cedo exists as of the last verified date.

## Recommended builds

**Viral-Slash (recommended for Steel Path)**: Combine Hunter Munitions (slash proc on crit), Galvanized Chamber (multishot), Viral damage via Infected Clip and Thermite Rounds, Vital Sense (critical damage), and Point Strike (critical chance). At 24% base critical chance, Point Strike pushes into reliable orange-critical territory, triggering Hunter Munitions consistently against armored enemies.

**Status-sustain build**: Prioritize Galvanized Aptitude and Primed Bane mods. Regular disc alt-fire throws increase proc frequency beyond what base status chance suggests, so alternating disc and primary fire maximizes total status application.

**General star chart**: Serration, Split Chamber, Heavy Caliber, Infected Clip, Stormbringer (Viral combination), Vital Sense, and Point Strike covers most star chart and mid-tier Steel Path content without specialized investment.

## Notes

Cedo Prime has no elemental base damage; all elemental combinations come from installed mods. The purely Slash-based physical profile means Viral -- which increases damage dealt to health -- is the most effective status type to apply first, amplifying subsequent Slash proc damage significantly.

The disc alt-fire consumes magazine rounds, so tracking remaining ammo during disc casts prevents running dry at critical moments. The 40-round magazine accommodates regular disc use without forcing early reloads in most engagements.
