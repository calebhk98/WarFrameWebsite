## Overview

The Guandao Prime is the Prime variant of the Guandao polearm, with a Puncture-dominant damage profile: 168 Puncture, 60 Impact, and 12 Slash. The 32% critical chance and 2.4x multiplier are strong for a polearm, while the 20% status chance supports supplementary elemental proc application. The defining characteristic of the Guandao Prime is its 3.0 m range -- one of the longest reach values among polearm weapons, enabling wide-arc sweep combos that cover substantial ground per swing. Attack speed of 1.0 is standard. Mastery Rank 12 is required.

Polearms use stance mods (Shimmering Blight, Bleeding Willow, Crushing Ruin) that deliver wide-arc, spinning combo animations suited to crowd-clearing. The Guandao Prime's 3.0 m range combined with polearm sweep stances ensures each combo swing hits multiple enemies simultaneously in standard mission density. Blood Rush at 32% base crit reaches orange-crit territory by x4 combo. The Puncture dominance benefits from Corrosive elemental mods against Grineer Ferrite Armor, with Corrosive procs stripping armor for unmitigated Puncture damage delivery.

## Variants

The base Guandao is the standard polearm, available at lower mastery rank. The Guandao Prime substantially improves crit chance, multiplier, and base damage. No Kuva or Tenet variant exists for the Guandao family. Players looking for an entry-level polearm to practice the weapon class before committing to the Prime should consider the Edun at MR0.

## Recommended builds

**Blood Rush crit polearm build.** At 32% base crit, Blood Rush reaches orange-crit at x4 combo. Equip Organ Shatter, Sacrificial Steel, Primed Pressure Point, and two Viral 60/60 mods. The 3.0 m range ensures wide-arc combos connect to multiple enemies per swing, accumulating combo quickly through multi-hit polearm animations.

**Shimmering Blight crowd build.** Equip Shimmering Blight or Bleeding Willow stance for the widest-arc polearm combo sweeps. Combine with Primed Pressure Point, Condition Overload, Blood Rush, and four 60/60 elemental mods for Viral + Corrosive. At x4 combo with both Blood Rush and Weeping Wounds, the Guandao Prime delivers orange crits alongside multiple proc types for Condition Overload amplification.

**Corrosive/Puncture Grineer build.** The 168 Puncture base combined with Corrosive mods creates optimal anti-armor performance against Grineer Steel Path enemies. Equip Primed Pressure Point, Condition Overload, two Corrosive 60/60 mods, Blood Rush, and Organ Shatter. After full armor strip via Corrosive procs, the Puncture damage delivers maximum value to exposed health.

## Synergies

The Guandao Prime's 3.0 m range pairs with enemy-grouping frame abilities (Khora, Vauban) for maximum per-swing hit count. Rhino's Roar proportionally amplifies the Puncture-dominant base across all hits. Naramon's Power Spike extends combo duration for sustained Blood Rush orange-crit phases during extended encounters.
