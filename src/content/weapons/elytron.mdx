## Overview

Elytron is an offensive Archwing introduced alongside Odonata in Update 15 (October 2014). Designed as the heavy weapons specialist of the early Archwing roster, Elytron trades the balanced approach of Odonata for aggressive firepower. High armor (210) and respectable health (475) allow it to tank enemy fire while offensive abilities provide area denial and explosive damage.

Elytron represents the "glass cannon" Archwing philosophy: survive through damage output and positioning rather than shields and evasion. For experienced Archwing pilots, Elytron clears objectives faster than defensive alternatives while maintaining acceptable survivability in high-level content.

## Abilities

Bloomer fires remote-detonated explosive projectiles that latch onto targets and detonated at will. The delayed detonation mechanic enables area-of-effect positioning: fire at a group, time the detonation to hit maximum cluster, then trigger for area denial.

Core Vent vents the propulsion core, releasing exhaust clouds that confuse incoming guided missiles. Similar to Odonata's Disarray but through active emission rather than deployed beacons. Multiple vents in succession create overlapping confusion zones.

Seek deploys seeking missiles that automatically track enemies within range and deal massive area damage on impact. The auto-tracking eliminates precision aiming requirements and allows weapon-free periods for ability spam rotations.

Cannons transforms Elytron into a mobile gun platform, deploying independent turrets that automatically fire on nearby enemies. Cannons provides passive area denial and reduces ability reliance for extended periods, making it useful for sustained engagements where energy management is tight.

## Acquisition

Elytron is awarded as a blueprint after completing the Archwing quest, making it available to players at the same progression point as Odonata. The frame is fundamentally tutorial-friendly despite its offensive design. Component blueprints (Harness, Systems, Wings) research in the Clan Dojo Tenno Lab, identical to other Archwing components. Early access combined with straightforward acquisition means Elytron is one of the first frames most players build.

Build time is instant for the main blueprint (60 seconds in WFCD) but components require standard crafting. Resource costs are low-tier materials accessible from Earth and early star chart nodes, making Elytron accessible to new players immediately upon quest completion.

## Modding Notes

Elytron benefits from ability strength for improved explosion damage and turret firepower. Range scaling on Seek missiles improves tracking coverage. Efficiency reduces energy starvation when ability-spamming Bloomer detonations. Redirection scales the shield pool, and Vigor adds health. Umbral mods provide limited value because Elytron's abilities do not leverage strength scaling as effectively as other frames.

Optimal Elytron builds prioritize strength and efficiency over range, though mobility builds substitute range for extended seek coverage. Exilus polarity mods like Vigilante Pursuit improve speed, complementing Elytron's aggressive playstyle.

## Playstyle

Engage enemy formations aggressively, triggering Bloomer detonations on impact. Use Core Vent reactively when guided missile barrages are detected. Spam Seek for consistent area damage without aiming precision. Deactivate Cannons for downtime recovery or when ability energy is low. Elytron's gameplay loop is higher intensity than defensive Archwings: constant ability use drives energy starvation, requiring efficiency scaling and energy management discipline.

Newer Archwing pilots may struggle with Elytron's energy consumption. Experienced pilots thrive by maintaining rotations that balance offensive output with downtime recovery.

## Synergies

Elytron pairs well with Trinity or Amesha in squad compositions because supporting frames provide energy refund mechanics and defensive coverage. Volt's speed buff enhances Elytron's already-high mobility, enabling tactical repositioning between ability casts. Harrow's crit bonus amplifies Bloomer detonation scaling if modded for critical hits.

Elytron does not provide external buffs, so squad composition focuses on supporting Elytron's aggressive playstyle through defensive scaling and energy management.

## Lore

Elytron was designed as the proactive Archwing, reflecting the Tenno philosophy of aggressive engagement over retreat. The explosive arsenal and automatic tracking weapons represent Orokin advancement in autonomous weapon systems. Elytron's prominence in raid content and elite void fissure scaling demonstrates that early design decisions prioritized raw damage output, a philosophy that resonates with endgame players.
