## Overview

The [Broken Scepter](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Broken_Scepter) is a staff melee weapon obtained as a reward from completing The War Within quest. It deals 179 total damage per attack with an impact-heavy distribution (125.3 impact, 35.8 puncture, 17.9 slash). The 30% status chance and 10% critical chance support status-focused builds. A unique passive mechanic allows kills with the Broken Scepter to create an energy orb from Grineer, health orb from Corpus, and a universal orb from Infested enemies, providing sustained resource generation during combat.

This resource generation mechanic makes the Broken Scepter particularly valuable in long survival, defense, or interception missions where energy and health sustain determine endurance ceiling. Riven disposition at 5/5 is the maximum tier, meaning rivens roll with exceptionally high multipliers for this weapon.

Staff weapons access the Clashing Forest and Crushing Ruin stances, providing wide-area combo sequences suited to the Broken Scepter's 2.9 m reach.

## Acquisition

The Broken Scepter is obtained exclusively by completing The War Within quest. There is no other source for this weapon. The quest is available after completing The Second Dream and reaching the Kuva Fortress junction. The blueprint drops at quest completion, and the weapon is built in the Foundry. The War Within's completion also unlocks the Kuva Fortress region and the Riven Mod system.

## Variants

No Prime, Vandal, or Wraith variant of the Broken Scepter exists. Its unique passive is not available on any other staff weapon. The weapon is exclusive to players who have completed The War Within.

## Recommended builds

**Orb generation utility build:** Primed Pressure Point, Berserker Fury, Condition Overload, Weeping Wounds, and elemental mods for viral plus heat. The 30% status chance generates consistent procs while kills produce energy orbs from Grineer. This build excels in survival missions where energy management is critical, as kills on Grineer enemies continuously replenish energy for ability use.

**Status support build:** Prioritize Condition Overload with four elemental mods. The Broken Scepter becomes a status primer that kills enemies quickly, generating orbs for the entire squad in group play. In endless missions, this sustain contribution adds value beyond the weapon's individual damage output.

**Riven-enhanced build:** With a 5/5 disposition riven adding critical chance and damage, replace Weeping Wounds with Blood Rush for a hybrid crit-status build. A strong riven can push the Broken Scepter into consistent crit territory despite the low 10% base chance.

## Synergies

- **Energy-hungry Warframes:** Saryn, Octavia, Mesa, and Limbo all benefit from the energy orb generation from Grineer kills. In Grineer missions, the Broken Scepter effectively provides passive energy sustain without Energy Siphon or arcanes.
- **Arcane Energize:** Combines with the Broken Scepter's orb generation -- each energy orb pickup has a chance to trigger Arcane Energize's energy burst, creating substantial energy income in Grineer missions.
- **Condition Overload:** The impact-heavy damage distribution and 30% status chance feed Condition Overload stacks effectively when combined with elemental mods.

## Notes

- The Broken Scepter's orb generation scales with kills, not damage dealt. Each enemy killed by the Broken Scepter -- regardless of damage level -- generates one orb. Status proc kills (bleed, toxin, heat) do count toward orb generation when the proc was applied by the Broken Scepter.
- The impact-dominant damage profile (70% of total) favors use against Corpus shielded enemies, where impact deals bonus damage. Against Grineer Ferrite Armor, add corrosive elemental mods to compensate for the absence of strong puncture output.
- The 1.25 attack speed is above average for staff weapons. Berserker Fury combined with the fast base speed creates notably rapid staff attacks during extended kill chains.
- Riven disposition at 5/5 is exceptional for a quest reward weapon. A riven with status chance or damage can make the Broken Scepter viable in Steel Path content with appropriate elemental configuration.
