## Overview

The [Convectrix](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Convectrix) is a shotgun-category primary weapon that fires two crossing laser beams. Unlike pellet-based shotguns, the Convectrix fires continuous beams that damage all enemies within their path. The 12 rounds per second fire rate and 45% status chance make it one of the highest-status-chance primary weapons available at Mastery Rank 7. Each beam can independently trigger status procs, enabling extremely rapid elemental proc accumulation against any enemy within the crossing point.

The crossing beam mechanic means the Convectrix effectively has a multiplied hit rate in practice: two beams crossing means each enemy at the intersection receives hits from both beams simultaneously, doubling effective proc application per second. This is the weapon's defining mechanic and the reason its stated 45% status chance translates to near-guaranteed proc application in real combat.

Riven disposition at 5/5 is the maximum tier, offering exceptional riven roll potential.

## Acquisition

The Convectrix blueprint is available from the Market for credits. As a permanently available Market weapon, it is never vaulted. The completed weapon can also be purchased with platinum. Crafting requires standard mid-game materials available from the star chart.

## Variants

No Prime, Vandal, or Wraith variant exists. Players seeking a beam shotgun alternative should compare the Corinth (pellet-based) or the Synapse (primary beam weapon) for different fire mechanics.

## Recommended builds

**Full status elemental build:** Point Blank, Primed Ravage, Scattered Justice, Blaze (heat), Contagious Spread (viral via toxin), Frigid Aperture (cold), Jolt (electric), and Seeking Fury. With 45% status chance per beam hit and two beams striking simultaneously, each target at the beam intersection receives proc applications at approximately 90% effective status chance per second. Viral and heat together deliver health reduction plus ongoing damage-over-time. Corrosive strips Grineer armor within seconds.

**Status primer build:** Configure the Convectrix as a dedicated status primer for melee Condition Overload builds. Apply two to three unique elemental status types via the dual beams, then switch to melee. The extreme proc rate means two to three unique status types accumulate within 0.5 to 1 second of fire -- faster than any other primary.

**Armor strip build:** Focus on corrosive (electric + toxin) and heat mods. The crossing beams deliver corrosive procs at an exceptional rate, stripping even Steel Path Grineer armor within 2-3 seconds of sustained fire on a single target.

## Synergies

- **Condition Overload melee:** The Convectrix applies multiple unique status types faster than any other primary. A brief burst of fire before switching to melee ensures Condition Overload's maximum damage bonus activates immediately on the first melee hit.
- **Saryn's Spores:** Proc types applied by the Convectrix spread via Spores across nearby enemies, delivering corrosive and heat procs at room scale from a single target hit.
- **Equinox's Maim:** Enemies afflicted by multiple status types take additional damage from Maim's bleed accumulation. The Convectrix's high proc rate accelerates Maim stack buildup across groups.

## Notes

- The Convectrix's damage per hit (12) is low in absolute terms, but the crossing beam mechanic means two hits land per visible pulse against enemies at the intersection. Effective DPS exceeds the listed single-hit stats by the beam-count multiplier.
- Magazine stats are not listed in WFCD data for this weapon. The Convectrix uses a rechargeable power cell mechanic (continuous fire until heat buildup requires cooldown) in the base game rather than a traditional magazine. Check the Fandom Wiki for current heat generation and cooldown values.
- Riven disposition at 5/5 is exceptional. A riven with multishot on a shotgun fires additional beams per trigger pull, doubling effective proc rate further.
- The 45% status chance means a single-beam hit already procs 45% of the time. The dual-beam crossing effectively guarantees status proc application against any target within the crossing zone.
