## Overview

The [Cobra & Crane Prime](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Cobra_%26_Crane_Prime) is the Prime variant of the Cobra & Crane sword and shield weapon. It delivers 300 total damage per attack: 210 impact and 90 slash. The 18% critical chance and 2.0x multiplier provide functional critical builds, while the 38% status chance -- among the highest for sword and shield weapons -- enables rapid elemental proc accumulation for Condition Overload synergy.

As a sword and shield, the Cobra & Crane Prime provides a blocking mechanic that reduces incoming frontal damage when held, unique to this melee category. At Mastery Rank 14, it targets experienced players who have progressed into the later star chart.

Riven disposition at 3/5 is neutral, providing moderate roll potential for rivens.

## Variants

The base [Cobra & Crane](/weapons/cobra-crane) (Mastery Rank 10) has lower status chance (36%), critical chance (15%), and multiplier (1.5x). The Prime version is the superior endgame form for sword and shield enthusiasts.

## Recommended builds

**Status hybrid build:** Primed Pressure Point, Berserker Fury, Condition Overload, Weeping Wounds, Blood Rush, Organ Shatter, and two elemental mods for viral or corrosive. The 38% base status chance and Weeping Wounds scaling create rapid proc accumulation. Condition Overload amplifies each hit as unique status types accumulate. Blood Rush uses the 18% crit to add crit damage as combo climbs.

**Blocking-enhanced build:** Guardian Derision (taunts and boosts attack speed on block) combined with full status modding. Block incoming enemy attacks to trigger Guardian Derision's buff, then transition into rapid combo attacks. The 2.6 m range and impact-heavy damage create efficient encounter-management with the defensive block cycle.

**Corrosive armor strip:** Configure two elemental pairs (electric + toxin for corrosive) alongside heat. The 38% status chance strips Grineer Ferrite armor within 3-4 swings, and heat adds ongoing damage-over-time simultaneously.

## Synergies

- **Condition Overload:** The 38% status chance enables rapid unique status accumulation. Three distinct types (viral, heat, corrosive) from elemental mods reach Condition Overload's full bonus within the first engagement cycle.
- **Guardian Derision:** Blocking triggers the taunt effect, consolidating nearby enemy fire. The resulting Berserker-Fury-triggered speed boost from block-counter sequences elevates effective attack speed significantly.
- **Rhino's Roar:** Multiplicatively amplifies each swing's 300 base damage, compounding with the high status chance to deliver amplified proc-enhanced damage.

## Notes

- Sword and shield blocking requires holding the aim button while stationary or moving. Players who incorporate blocking into their rhythm gain a meaningful survival advantage in solo Steel Path.
- The zero puncture damage means Ferrite Armor receives no physical puncture bonus. Corrosive elemental mods compensate effectively.
- The Cobra & Crane Prime's impact dominance (70% of total) is particularly effective against Corpus shields, where impact deals bonus damage. Corpus missions benefit from this physical type without additional elemental adjustment.
- At Mastery Rank 14, the Cobra & Crane Prime is one of the later-tier accessible Prime melee weapons, justifying investment in forma and Primed mods.
