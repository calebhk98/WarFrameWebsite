## Overview

The Telos Boltor is the Arbiters of Hexis-enhanced version of the Boltor rifle. At Mastery Rank 12, it fires bolt projectiles at 9.33 rounds per second with a 90-round magazine. Its 30 total damage per bolt is concentrated in slash (27), with a minor impact component (3). The slash-dominant profile generates bleed procs that bypass armor, making the Telos Boltor effective against heavily armored Grineer at higher difficulties.

The 30% critical chance and 2.4x multiplier place the Telos Boltor firmly in crit-capable territory. With Point Strike, critical chance reaches near 90%, turning most shots into orange or red crits. The 16% status chance is moderate -- not high enough for a pure status build, but sufficient for elemental proc application on individual rounds given the high fire rate. At 9.33 fire rate, the Telos Boltor fires enough rounds per second that even moderate status chance produces procs reliably.

The Telos Boltor includes the Entropy Burst Syndicate effect, granting a chance to produce an energy orb on kills. This passive enables warframe ability-heavy playstyles during Syndicate missions.

## Acquisition

The Telos Boltor is sold directly by the Arbiters of Hexis Syndicate vendor at any Relay. Syndicate standing and appropriate rank are required. No crafting needed.

## Variants

**Boltor** -- The base rifle from the Market.

**Tenet Boltor** -- Not an existing weapon; no Tenet variant of the Boltor exists.

## Recommended builds

**Critical build**: Serration, Split Chamber, Point Strike, Vital Sense, Heavy Caliber, and two elemental mods. With Point Strike at 30% base, critical chance reaches near 90%. Heavy Caliber adds damage at the cost of accuracy -- acceptable at the Telos Boltor's medium engagement range.

**Viral crit**: Point Strike, Vital Sense, Serration, Split Chamber, Vigilante Armaments, cold plus toxin elemental mods. Viral amplifies all damage taken by halving enemy max health. The slash procs from the base damage type then tick true bleed damage through armor.

**Status-focused**: Four 60/60 dual-stat mods for 120% elemental damage and status layering. The 16% base status with multishot produces consistent procs during the high fire rate.

## Notes

The Telos Boltor fires physical bolt projectiles that travel at a finite speed. Unlike hitscan weapons, leading moving targets is required at longer ranges. The 90-round magazine provides extended sustained fire, making it effective in survival and defense missions before reload pauses are needed.
