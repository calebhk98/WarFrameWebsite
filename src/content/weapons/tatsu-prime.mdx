## Overview

Tatsu Prime is a melee two-handed nikana weapon that serves as Prime two-handed nikana with innate radiation damage; radiation damage is particularly effective against Alloy-armored Grineer elites; the Prime version improves on the base Tatsu with higher critical multiplier and total damage. Mastery Rank 14 is required to equip the weapon. Players who invest in building it properly will find it reliable across the star chart, with scaling potential on the Steel Path when supported by appropriate mods and Warframe ability synergies. It has a reach of 3.0 m.

The melee slot is competitive in Warframe, and Tatsu Prime occupies a distinct position within it. Understanding its damage profile -- the distribution across physical damage types and any elemental components -- is the first step toward optimizing it for specific mission types and enemy factions.
