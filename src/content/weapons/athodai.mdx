## Overview

The [Athodai](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Athodai) is a secondary pistol weapon that fires a continuous energy beam. Its damage consists entirely of 22 slash and 48 heat elemental damage, totaling 70 per hit. The 32% critical chance is notably high for a secondary weapon, making critical builds rewarding. Status chance at 8% is low, limiting effective proc application per individual hit. The 5.0 fire rate and 24-round magazine support sustained beam fire for several seconds before reloading.

The Athodai has a unique spin-to-fire mechanic where spinning increases its fire rate and damage. Bullet jumping and sliding trigger the spin-up, enabling skilled players to maintain elevated fire rate and damage during mobile combat. This mechanic distinguishes the Athodai from static beam weapons and rewards kinetic playstyles.

## Variants

No Prime, Vandal, or Wraith variant of the Athodai exists. The base weapon is the sole available form.

## Recommended builds

**Critical beam build:** Hornet Strike, Barrel Diffusion, Lethal Torrent, Primed Pistol Gambit, Primed Target Cracker, and two elemental mods (heat is already built-in; adding electricity for radiation or toxin for viral). At 32% base critical chance, Primed Pistol Gambit pushes effective crit into reliable yellow-crit territory. The 2.0x multiplier delivers consistent crit damage across the beam's continuous fire.

**Spin-up exploit build:** Equip utility mods (Primed Quickdraw for holster speed, Lethal Torrent for fire rate) to maximize the spin-up mechanic benefit. Bullet jump frequently between engagements to maintain elevated fire rate. This mobile-combat build delivers highest DPS to players who incorporate constant movement.

**Heat status build:** Add Scorch (heat), Pistol Pestilence (toxin), and Frostbite (cold) to complement the built-in heat damage. At 8% base status chance, consistent proc application requires sustained fire -- the 5.0 fire rate and 24-round magazine support this. Heat procs deal damage over time alongside the base heat damage.

## Synergies

- **Bullet jumping:** The Athodai's spin-up mechanic activates on movement abilities. Frequent bullet jumping between targets maintains the elevated fire rate bonus across engagements without requiring reactivation pause.
- **Volt's Speed:** Speed ability increases movement velocity, making bullet jumping and sliding more frequent during natural movement. This indirectly maintains the Athodai's spin-up state more consistently.
- **Mesa's Peacemaker:** Mesa's passive provides bonus damage during gunplay, amplifying the Athodai's critical hits across its fire rate.

## Notes

- The spin-up mechanic requires movement input (bullet jump, slide) to activate. Stationary firing delivers base fire rate; mobile combat delivers the enhanced rate. Build strategies should account for whether the player's combat style incorporates the constant movement this weapon rewards.
- Heat damage at 48 is the dominant component (69% of total). Against enemies with heat resistance (some Grineer variants), the effective damage drops. Adding a second elemental type from mods compensates.
- Reload time of 2.0 seconds at 24-round magazine and 5.0 fire rate means the magazine empties in approximately 4.8 seconds at base fire rate. Reload during cover transitions rather than mid-engagement.
