## Overview

The Tenet Detron is a Sisters of Parvos Tenet secondary shotgun requiring Mastery Rank 16. It is the Tenet-enhanced version of the Detron, firing a burst of radiation pellets. Its base damage is 26 radiation per pellet -- entirely elemental, with no physical impact, puncture, or slash components. Radiation is particularly effective against Corpus units in Alloy Armor and causes enemy confusion on proc, making targets attack allies instead of the player.

As a shotgun, the Tenet Detron fires multiple pellets per trigger pull, each rolling its own status and crit chance. The 10% status per pellet is moderate; multishot mods increase pellet count, proportionally increasing the aggregate status proc opportunities per shot. The 18% critical chance and 2.0x multiplier are below crit-focused secondaries -- this weapon's radiation confusion proc is its primary utility rather than raw crit damage.

The 6-round magazine at 3.33 fire rate empties in 1.8 seconds. The fast 1.0-second reload allows rapid magazine cycling. The Tenet Detron carries a unique elemental bonus from its parent Sister, modifying the total elemental composition beyond the base radiation.

## Acquisition

Obtain through the Sisters of Parvos system at Mastery Rank 16. Trigger a Sister Candidate via Granum Void, track and vanquish the Sister to claim her Tenet Detron. The Sister's elemental bonus type modifies the radiation base.

## Variants

**Detron** -- The base pistol-shotgun. The Tenet version provides radiation base damage and Tenet elemental bonus over the standard Detron.

## Recommended builds

**Radiation confusion**: Hornet Strike, Barrel Diffusion, Primed Pistol Gambit, Lethal Torrent, and additional radiation-enhancing elemental mods. Multiple pellets from Barrel Diffusion each roll status independently, applying radiation confusion procs rapidly. Confused enemies attack allies, providing passive crowd control in crowded engagements.

**Corpus specialist**: The radiation base synergizes naturally with Corpus-heavy missions. Add electric mods for additional radiation scaling or cold plus electric for Magnetic-radiation hybrid damage. Both radiation and magnetic deal bonus damage to Corpus shield systems.

**Status multishot**: Barrel Diffusion, Galvanized Chamber, Galvanized Shot, Hornet Strike, and Lethal Torrent. Maximize pellet count to increase aggregate status proc frequency. Galvanized Shot stacks damage bonuses on status-affected enemies across the shotgun burst.

## Notes

Accuracy at 20.0 means significant pellet spread -- the Tenet Detron is a close-range secondary by design. At medium and long range, spread reduces the number of pellets landing on target. In close-quarters combat (Rescue, Spy, Capture), full pellet cluster impact at point-blank is the effective use case. The 1.0-second reload allows nearly continuous cycling through 6-round magazines in sustained fights.
