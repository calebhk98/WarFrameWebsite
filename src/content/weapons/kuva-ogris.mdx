## Overview

Kuva Ogris is the Kuva Lich variant of the Ogris rocket launcher, substantially upgrading the base weapon's status chance and adding a bonus elemental damage type from the Lich assignment. The defining feature of the Ogris platform is its proximity-detonating rocket projectiles, which explode on contact with enemies or surfaces and deal area-of-effect damage to nearby targets. Kuva Ogris retains this behavior while pushing the status chance to 47%, making it one of the most status-effective launchers available in the primary slot.

The base damage breakdown includes 18.9 impact, 155 puncture, 191.1 slash, and 349.0 blast elemental damage. Blast damage applies a knockdown status effect, adding crowd control alongside the weapon's area-of-effect detonation. The combined damage total before mod scaling is substantial per rocket, though the 3-round magazine limits burst endurance before reload is required.

At Mastery Rank 15, Kuva Ogris is positioned as an endgame primary weapon requiring investment in the Kuva Lich acquisition system. Its high status chance and area damage output make it effective in grouped-enemy scenarios such as Defense, Survival, and Interception missions where clusters of enemies concentrate.

The 9% critical chance makes dedicated crit builds impractical; status-focused builds are the natural path. At 47% status chance per hit, and considering that rocket blasts can hit multiple enemies within the detonation radius, the Kuva Ogris applies status effects rapidly. Combining two elemental mods produces a combined damage type applied alongside the blast proc, enabling Viral or Corrosive status buildup against groups of enemies simultaneously.

The 3-round magazine is the primary limiting factor in sustained engagements, requiring more frequent reloads than most primary weapons. Magazine Warp or similar capacity mods reduces this interruption.

## Acquisition

Kuva Ogris is obtained by vanquishing a Kuva Lich assigned the Kuva Ogris as its weapon. Lich weapon assignment is determined at larvling creation. Once vanquished, the weapon arrives with a random damage bonus percentage (25-60%) and a randomly selected bonus elemental damage type. Players trading the weapon via the Trading Post can seek higher bonus percentages or preferred elements.

A 60% bonus Kuva Ogris with a compatible elemental type is the target for optimized endgame builds and may command a premium on the Trading Post.

## Variants

The base Ogris is a Market-available launcher with significantly lower status chance and no bonus elemental damage. Kuva Ogris improves the status chance from 20% to 47% and adds the Lich-assigned elemental bonus, substantially outperforming the base version for status-focused builds.

No Tenet or Prime variant of the Ogris exists. Kuva Ogris is the highest-tier version in the launcher family.

## Recommended builds

A status-focused build centers on Vigilante Armaments for multishot, and Primed Charged Shell or Primed Cryo Rounds alongside a secondary elemental mod for Viral or Corrosive. Status chance mods like Malignant Force and High Voltage stack the per-hit status chance above 100% against primary targets, ensuring at least one proc per rocket.

For Kuva Ogris with a Heat bonus from the Lich, adding Cold mods forms Blast plus Heat, or Toxin forms Gas -- both effective for large enemy clusters in indoor tile missions. Players should match elemental combinations to the primary faction encountered for maximum efficiency.

Cautious Shot is recommended to prevent self-damage from close-range detonations.

## Notes

Kuva Ogris fires rockets that travel as physical projectiles and detonate on contact or proximity. Unlike hitscan weapons, rockets require leading moving targets at longer ranges. The area-of-effect blast radius means one rocket can apply status to multiple enemies simultaneously, which multiplies the practical status throughput beyond the per-shot percentage.

Kuva Ogris uses the shotgun mod category due to its classification as a launcher in the game's mod system, which means standard rifle mods are not compatible.
