## Overview

Redeemer Prime is the Prime variant of the Redeemer, a melee gunblade that delivers a devastating charged shotgun blast on heavy attacks. It combines the fluid motion of a sword with the burst power of a close-range firearm, creating a weapon that rewards aggressive, forward-pressing playstyles. The Prime iteration improves on the base model with higher critical chance, status chance, and overall damage output, making it one of the more powerful gunblades in the melee category.

The weapon rewards players who understand its dual nature. Light attacks build combo count quickly, while heavy attacks consume combo for amplified shotgun blasts that deal heavy puncture damage to single targets or groups in close range. This makes the Redeemer Prime particularly effective against armored targets, where puncture bonuses provide direct reduction against armor class.

## Stats

Redeemer Prime deals physical damage split across impact, puncture, and slash. Per WFCD data, the base damage per shot distributes with a heavy bias toward puncture at 148.4, with impact at 21.2 and slash at 42.4. This damage profile favors armored Grineer enemies, where puncture directly interacts with armor reduction. The critical chance of 24% and critical multiplier of 2.2x provide a moderate crit ceiling that benefits from standard crit-enhancing mods. Status chance at 30% is sufficient to reliably proc elemental or physical statuses on each swing, enabling condition overload-style builds.

The weapon's fire rate of 0.92 reflects its attack speed as a gunblade melee, and it has a melee range of 2 meters -- standard for the sword and shield category. Heavy attack builds benefit from the high combo multiplier efficiency this weapon generates during normal attack chains.

## Acquisition

Redeemer Prime is a Prime weapon and requires relic farming to obtain its component blueprints. The component parts drop from Void Relics across various tiers. Players should check the current unvaulting schedule and relic drop tables on the Warframe wiki to identify the active relics containing Redeemer Prime parts. Alternatively, the fully built weapon or individual components can be traded between players on [warframe.market](https://warframe.market). The base Redeemer blueprint is available from the Market with credits; the Prime version is relic-locked.

## Variants

The base [Redeemer](/weapons/redeemer) is available from the Market and serves as the entry-level gunblade experience. The Redeemer Prime significantly outperforms the base version in critical chance, status chance, and total damage, making relic investment worthwhile for players who enjoy the gunblade archetype.

## Recommended builds

Heavy attack builds are the primary build path for Redeemer Prime. Equip Amalgam Organ Shatter or Organ Shatter for heavy attack wind-up reduction, combined with Sacrificial Steel or True Steel for critical chance, and North Wind or other elemental mods to add status-applying damage types. Condition Overload synergizes well with the weapon's high status chance, scaling damage as multiple status types accumulate on enemies.

For melee-focused loadouts, pairing the Redeemer Prime with a frame that provides damage amplification -- such as Rhino's Roar or Mirage's Eclipse -- dramatically increases heavy attack output. The Berserker Fury mod can increase attack speed during light attack chains, enabling faster combo building before committing to a heavy blast.

## Notes

The gunblade mechanic means heavy attacks fire a shotgun-style projectile that benefits from melee mods rather than secondary weapon mods. Players sometimes confuse this with a secondary weapon interaction -- all damage scaling is through melee mod slots. Silencer mods and fire rate mods for secondaries do not apply.

Riven mods for the Redeemer Prime can dramatically alter its performance, particularly those with disposition bonuses to heavy attack efficiency or multishot equivalents for the shotgun blast.
