## Overview

Ninkondi Prime is the prime variant of the Ninkondi nunchaku melee weapon, offering substantial improvements over the base version including a more distributed physical damage spread, improved critical chance, and a higher critical multiplier. The weapon retains the built-in 90 electricity damage and the nunchaku's sweeping attack pattern, while adding 50 puncture and 28 slash to complement the 66 impact. This broader damage profile opens more elemental combination options and adds slash proc potential absent in the base weapon.

The 36% status chance pairs with 1.0 attack speed and the wide-arc Flailing Branch stance attacks to spread status effects across groups efficiently. With 22% critical chance and 2.4x multiplier, hybrid builds combining status accumulation with Blood Rush crit scaling become viable at high combo counter levels. At mastery rank 14, Ninkondi Prime is positioned as an endgame-capable nunchaku for players who have invested in the weapon category.

The distributed physical damage and innate electricity mean only one additional elemental mod is needed to trigger a combined damage type. Toxin creates Corrosive (with built-in electricity); Cold creates Magnetic. The added slash component enables slash procs, which deal damage over time to enemy health pools and bypass armor scaling -- a significant buff over the base Ninkondi.

## Acquisition

Ninkondi Prime components are obtained through Void Relic farming. Specific relics contain the blueprint, handle, and chain components at various rarities. Trading through the in-game market or [warframe.market](https://warframe.market) provides an alternative route. Check the current relic pool for the relics containing the relevant components.

## Variants

The base [Ninkondi](/weapons/ninkondi) offers lower mastery rank access with a simpler damage profile (pure impact plus electricity). Ninkondi Prime supersedes it in nearly all respects and is the recommended weapon for dedicated melee builds.

## Recommended builds

The combination of 36% status chance, 22% crit chance, and Blood Rush compatibility supports several build directions.

Status-primary build (Corrosive):
- Pressure Point for base damage scaling
- Fever Strike (toxin) combines with built-in electricity for Corrosive
- Condition Overload for damage scaling with multiple status types
- Drifting Contact for extended combo duration
- Gladiator Might or Organ Shatter for critical damage once Blood Rush is active

Viral plus electric build:
- Frostbite (cold + status chance) combined with Fever Strike (toxin + status chance) for Viral
- Built-in electricity serves as the second status type alongside Viral, applying stun to robotic enemies
- Effective against Grineer heavy units where Viral doubles effective health reduction

Hybrid Blood Rush build:
- Blood Rush scales critical chance per combo multiplier, pushing effective crit chance toward 100% at 12x combo
- Combine with Organ Shatter and Berserker Fury for sustained high-damage output
- Maintain combo with Drifting Contact and aggressive stance combos

## Notes

Flailing Branch is the only compatible stance for nunchaku weapons. Its attack patterns produce wide horizontal sweeps that catch enemies across the 2.3-meter range arc. In groups, this distributes status procs broadly -- each enemy hit has an independent chance to receive a proc at the weapon's full status rate, making group fights efficient for stack-building.

The Ninkondi Prime compares well to other status-focused melee weapons in its tier. Its ranged performance versus comparable nunchaku options is competitive, and the built-in electricity reduces mod slot pressure compared to weapons that require two elemental mods for a combined damage type. Players transitioning from the base Ninkondi will notice the addition of puncture and slash damage as a meaningful quality-of-life improvement for broader content use.
