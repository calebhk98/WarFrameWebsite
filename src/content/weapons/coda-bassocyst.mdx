## Overview

The [Coda Bassocyst](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Coda_Bassocyst) is a Jade Shadows shotgun weapon at Mastery Rank 17. It delivers 808 total damage per shot: 789 blast and 19 radiation elemental damage with no physical component. The 40% status chance combined with the shotgun category's multi-pellet mechanics enables rapid blast and radiation proc application. The 18% critical chance and 2.2x multiplier support hybrid crit builds. Blast procs knockdown and stagger enemies; radiation procs cause confusion.

At 808 total damage per shot, the Coda Bassocyst is one of the highest single-shot damage shotguns available. The 24-round magazine and 1.17 fire rate allow sustained fire without frequent reloads.

Obtained through the Jade Shadows content update. Check the [Coda Bassocyst Wiki page](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Coda_Bassocyst) for current drop sources and reward tables associated with Jade Shadows mission content.

## Variants

The Coda Bassocyst is the enhanced Jade Shadows version of the Bassocyst. The base Bassocyst has lower status chance and damage output. The Coda form is the endgame upgrade at MR17.

## Recommended builds

**Blast crowd control build:** Point Blank, Hell's Chamber, Scattered Justice, Blaze (heat -- forms blast with impact or radiation), elemental mods targeting faction weaknesses, and Primed Ravage. Blast procs stagger and knockdown enemies consistently at 40% status chance per pellet, enabling crowd control alongside damage. Radiation confusion from the innate 19 radiation adds secondary crowd control.

**Critical elemental build:** Point Blank, Primed Ravage, Hell's Chamber, Point Strike equivalent (shotgun), Vital Sense equivalent, and three elemental mods. At 18% critical chance with Primed Ravage, the Coda Bassocyst delivers notable critical hit damage on the 808 base. Each critical applies to the full pellet payload.

**Armor strip support:** Add corrosive mods (electric + toxin) alongside the existing blast and radiation. Blast reduces enemy accuracy; radiation confuses; corrosive strips armor. This three-status configuration creates sustained debuffing at the expense of raw damage optimization.

## Synergies

- **Blast proc knockdown:** Blast stagger applies to enemies within the explosion radius. In defense missions, consistent blast procs from the Coda Bassocyst slow enemy advance by repeatedly knocking down approaching waves.
- **Radiation confusion:** Confused enemies attack their allies, effectively reducing the number of active threats targeting the player. In high-density content, multiple confused enemies cascade into group self-damage.
- **Hellminth Roar or Rhino Roar:** Multiplicatively scales the 808 per-shot damage, turning each blast into a massive AOE burst against grouped enemies.

## Notes

- Pure blast and radiation damage offers no physical base, meaning armor-bypass via slash procs is not available without adding physical mods. Corrosive additions are recommended for Grineer content.
- The 2.5-second reload is among the longer reload times for shotguns. Plan reloads during movement between waves or clusters rather than mid-engagement.
- Mastery Rank 17 gates the Coda Bassocyst firmly in veteran territory. The weapon's performance justifies the investment for players who have reached end-game modding capacity.
