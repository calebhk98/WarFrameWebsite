## Overview

The Spira Prime is the primed throwing weapon with rapid fire and reliable status chance. Its spread pattern and quick reload make it effective for medium-range encounters. The weapon fires star-shaped projectiles that return after striking distant surfaces or enemies. This returning mechanic enables creative usage patterns where ricocheting throws hit multiple targets before returning.

The Spira Prime's 13.3 fire rate provides continuous suppression, allowing rapid volleys against grouped enemies. The 12 round magazine accommodates burst fire patterns without frequent reloading. The 0.75 reload time is quick, supporting dynamic mobile playstyle and repositioning between volleys. The rapid reload encourages magazine cycling and quick repositioning, creating engaging tactical gameplay.

With 14% status chance, the Spira Prime supports elemental builds focused on status application rather than raw damage. The weapon's projectile nature means shots can ricochet off surfaces, hitting multiple enemies or striking targets from unexpected angles. This mechanic rewards creative positioning and environmental awareness. Skilled players exploit ricochets to chain hits across enemy groups.

The Spira Prime appeals to players enjoying projectile-based combat with unique mechanics. The weapon works well in team environments where suppressive fire supports teammates. Veterans appreciate the weapon's visual design and satisfying handling, though pure damage output remains secondary to control and status application.

The returning projectile mechanic enables interesting play patterns where understanding enemy positioning and environmental layout enables multi-target engagement. Some players specialize in Spira Prime builds combining high fire rate with status effects.

The weapon's unique mechanics and satisfying feedback make it enjoyable for diverse mission types.
