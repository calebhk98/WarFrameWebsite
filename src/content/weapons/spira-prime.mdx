## Overview

The Spira Prime is the primed variant of the Spira, a set of throwing blades fired in rapid succession. It holds a commanding critical multiplier of 3x alongside a 30% critical chance, making it one of the more imposing secondary throwing weapons in the game. The dominant slash damage type performs well against unarmored and lightly armored enemies, and the near-instant 0.75-second reload means the weapon rarely forces a tactical pause during combat.

Throwing weapons in Warframe operate differently from pistols: projectiles travel in an arc and can physically pass through multiple enemies when their paths align. The Spira Prime fires individual blades rather than a simultaneous fan, so spacing and target alignment matter more than with shotgun-style secondaries. Players who invest time in understanding projectile travel find the weapon consistently rewarding.

## Acquisition

Spira Prime is a Prime weapon whose components drop from void relics. The set has been vaulted and unvaulted multiple times; check the current relic pool or the [warframe.market listing](https://warframe.market/items/spira_prime_set) for availability. Blueprint, Blade, and Pouch components each appear in different relic tiers.

## Variants

The base [Spira](/weapons/spira) shares the same throwing-blade archetype with lower critical chance (15%) and a smaller multiplier (2.0x). The Prime version also gains two extra magazine capacity and a slightly faster reload, making it strictly superior in combat performance.

## Recommended builds

**Critical strike build.** The 30% base critical chance reaches consistent yellow-crit territory with Point Strike equipped. Pairing Vital Sense and Primed Target Cracker pushes the 3x multiplier into devastating range against any enemy type. The slash bias naturally strips health from unarmored targets even without dedicated slash-proc mods.

**Hybrid status-crit build.** Adding Pistol Pestilence and Jolt to a crit foundation reaches meaningful status coverage. Viral slash combinations proc bleed ticks and halve effective enemy health simultaneously, scaling effectively into Steel Path content where raw damage alone falls short.

**Galvanized build.** Galvanized Crosshairs replaces Point Strike for players with access to Incarnon or Galvanized mods. The conditional critical-chance bonus activates readily with the Spira Prime's fire rate, rewarding accurate aggressive play.

## Notes

- The 0.75-second reload is one of the fastest reloads for any secondary in its damage class, enabling consistent cycling without downtime.
- Projectiles have a small collision radius; lining up throws along densely packed enemy corridors allows a single toss to hit multiple targets.
- Mastery rank 10 is the only gate; no standing or quest completion is required beyond the relic unlock.
- Riven disposition sits at a moderate value, meaning riven mods can meaningfully push builds without requiring near-perfect rolls.
