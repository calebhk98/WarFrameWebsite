## Overview

Zhuge Prime is the Orokin-era upgrade of the Zhuge automatic crossbow. Compared to the base Zhuge, the prime version improves critical chance from 20% to 26%, fire rate from 4.17 to 5.5 shots per second, and magazine capacity from 20 to 30 rounds. The status chance decreases slightly from 35% to 30%, a tradeoff that shifts the weapon's identity toward a more crit-viable platform while retaining meaningful status application.

The 26% critical chance and 2.0x multiplier are respectable for a crossbow and enable crit builds with moderate mod investment. Adding Vital Sense and Point Strike pushes crit chance toward 50-60% and amplifies critical hit damage significantly. Unlike the base Zhuge, where crit builds felt underpowered relative to status builds, Zhuge Prime sits at the threshold where both approaches are competitive options.

The reduced slash per bolt (22.5 vs. 75 on base Zhuge) reflects the class change to Launcher in WFCD categorization. The slash-viral synergy that made the base Zhuge compelling is weaker here. Zhuge Prime's build direction leans toward crit or balanced crit-status approaches rather than pure slash-bleed strategies.

The 30-round magazine and 5.5 shots-per-second fire rate create a sustained damage platform that handles extended engagements well. The 3.0-second reload is slow, but the larger magazine reduces how often reload interruptions occur compared to the base Zhuge. At Mastery Rank 14, Zhuge Prime is accessible once players have progressed through mid-game content.

## Acquisition

Zhuge Prime components drop from Void relics across multiple relic tiers. The blueprint, barrel, and receiver each appear in different relic categories. Running Void fissure missions with the appropriate relics yields components over multiple runs. After completing a full component set, assembling the weapon requires standard crafting resources and time in the Foundry.

## Variants

Zhuge Prime is the prime version of [Zhuge](/weapons/zhuge). It improves critical chance, fire rate, and magazine capacity at the cost of lower slash damage per bolt.

## Recommended builds

**Crit-focused build:** Point Strike, Vital Sense, and Primed Shred form the core of a crit build. At 26% base crit chance with two crit mods, crit chance exceeds 60%, achieving reliable yellow crits. Add Primed Cryo Rounds for bonus cold damage that also contributes status procs to enemies. This build handles single-target priority killing effectively in missions where burst damage per shot matters.

**Crit-status hybrid:** Split Chamber, Vital Sense, and Point Strike provide crit foundation, while Infected Clip and Cryo Rounds generate Viral proc application at 30% status chance. The combination of viral procs and crit damage per bolt maximizes sustained DPS against most enemy types. This build handles both armored Grineer (Corrosive alternative) and shield Corpus (Magnetic alternative).

**Sustained fire build:** The 30-round magazine enables long burst sequences before reloading. Primed Shred penetrates through lined-up enemies, allowing multiple targets to be hit per bolt. In corridors and tightly packed Defense waves, Primed Shred provides crowd-clearing efficiency that compensates for the 3.0-second reload interruption between magazine cycles.

## Notes

- The 3.0-second reload is slow relative to the fire rate and magazine size. Reload speed mods mitigate this limitation during extended engagements.
- Zhuge Prime fires bolts with travel time, requiring lead on moving enemies at medium-to-long range.
- The weapon benefits from the Thunderbolt mod (crossbow-specific), converting non-crit bolts into explosive projectiles for area damage.
- Hunter Munitions converts crit hits into slash procs; at 26% crit chance with mods, this generates consistent bleed stacks even at lower slash base damage.
- Zhuge Prime's magazine and fire rate improvements over the base Zhuge make it a meaningful upgrade for players who farm prime relics.
