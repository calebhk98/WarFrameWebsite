## Overview

The Praedos is an Incarnon sword and shield melee weapon combining respectable damage with defensive mechanics. The shield component provides passive blocking capability, reducing incoming damage and enabling tactical repositioning. As an Incarnon weapon, it evolves through Zariman farming to unlock enhanced mechanics and form transformations. The weapon suits hybrid playstyles blending offense and defense, appealing to players prioritizing survivability alongside damage output. The balanced critical and status stats enable flexible build approaches, making the weapon adaptable across various mission types and squad compositions.

## Damage

The Praedos emphasizes slash damage at 36 with substantial impact and modest puncture components. Slash procs scale with enemy health, providing scaling into higher mission difficulties. Critical stats of 18% chance and 2.0x multiplier support crit scaling through gear and buffs but remain secondary to status or slash-scaling approaches. Status chance of 24% enables meaningful elemental application, particularly valuable in squad compositions leveraging condition overload synergies. The balanced stat distribution allows flexible build direction based on mission requirements.

## Acquisition

Praedos components drop from Zariman Ten Zero void fissure missions. Players must accumulate relics and complete void fissure runs to obtain components. Building requires 15,000 credits and 12 hours of foundry time. As an Incarnon weapon, acquisition requires engagement with endgame fissure content, rewarding sustained play. Multiple component drops from repeated missions allow players to craft several copies for forma experimentation.

## Variants

No prime variant of Praedos exists. The Incarnon evolution serves as the primary upgrade path, unlocking through void fissure farming. Cosmetic skins are available post-acquisition, offering visual customization without mechanical changes. No other weapon variants exist.

## Recommended builds

Praedos builds emphasize slash damage and status application. Organ Shatter amplifies critical output despite modest critical stats. Elemental mods apply reliable status effects; corrosive excels against armor, magnetic against shields, viral against health pools. Condition Overload benefits from status stacking, particularly valuable in organized squads. The weapon's shield component provides passive survivability, reducing the need for excessive defensive modding. Stance selection impacts playstyle: aggressive stances maximize damage output, defensive stances enhance crowd control and blocking effectiveness. Riven mods with slash damage, status chance, and elemental bonuses optimize endgame performance.

The shield component distinguishes Praedos from pure damage-focused melee weapons.
