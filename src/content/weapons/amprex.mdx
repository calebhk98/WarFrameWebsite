## Overview

The Amprex is a rapid-fire rifle that projects a chain-lightning beam capable of hitting multiple enemies at once. It combines reliable base damage with an inherent electric element, making it effective against unarmored targets and particularly strong against corpus units. The weapon excels in crowd control through its piercing chain-lightning mechanic, which can clear entire rooms of weak to moderate enemies. Its high fire rate of twelve rounds per second makes it a sustained-damage platform, though its relatively modest base damage per shot requires proper modding and critical scaling to shine in high-level content. The Amprex remains a consistent choice for players seeking an elemental primary that rewards accuracy and positioning.

## Damage

The Amprex's damage profile splits between physical and electric components. The physical damage totals 100 points (24 impact, 12 puncture, 64 slash), with slash comprising the largest portion for consistent cutting procs. The 76 electric damage provides additional utility through stun effects and synergy with elemental status modding. The weapon's chain-lightning mechanic means damage propagates to nearby enemies, making it effective against grouped targets. Against high-armor enemies, the electricity provides less utility without corrosive mods, requiring build adjustments for heavy armor content.

## Acquisition

The Amprex blueprint is available directly from the market for 25,000 credits. Construction requires 25,000 additional credits and takes twelve hours in the Foundry. The weapon is tradable only in its unbuilt blueprint state, making it accessible to players who acquire it from traders. No void relic components exist for the Amprex, keeping its acquisition straightforward for players with market access.

## Variants

No prime or other variants of the Amprex currently exist in Warframe. The standard Amprex remains the only version available.

## Recommended builds

The Amprex benefits from mods that enhance its critical chance and elemental damage output. Serration provides the foundational damage multiplier, while Vital Sense and Point Strike boost critical scaling to leverage the 2.2x multiplier. For elemental builds, Adding Electric mod enhances the native electric damage, and Splitting Flights or Crescent Talon extend the chain-lightning reach. Magazine extension via Increased magazine capacity helps sustain the high fire rate during extended engagements. Primed mods like Primed Cryo Rounds or Primed Fever Strike offer superior scaling if available.

## Synergies

The weapon pairs excellently with ability-spam frames that benefit from consistent weapon pressure without requiring careful positioning. Volt benefits from the weapon's electric synergy and the stun effects from electric procs during his speed farm runs, making him an ideal companion for endless fissure farming. Mesa's Peacemaker mod converts weapon elements directly, amplifying the Amprex's electric component through her ability, creating multiplicative crowd-control potential. Electric damage scales effectively with frames providing elemental bonuses like Wisp or Volt, turning the weapon into a group-clear platform ideal for mid-level endless missions. Revenant leverages electric procs to maintain his warding halo buff, synergizing perfectly with the weapon's consistent stun output.

## Lore

The Amprex emerged from Corpus research into efficient crowd-control weaponry. Its design prioritizes rate of fire and elemental consistency over raw impact, reflecting Corpus engineering philosophy that favors scalable, repeatable damage patterns. The chain-lightning mechanism suggests reverse-engineered technology from ancient plasma systems, adapted into a handheld platform. Within the player community, the Amprex has been a reliable entry point for players learning to scale elemental damage through modding.
