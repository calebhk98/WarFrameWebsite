## Overview

The Dera is a Corpus-manufactured automatic rifle that fires at 11.25 rounds per second from a 45-round magazine. The Slash-dominant physical damage profile (22.5 Slash vs 6 Impact and 1.5 Puncture) gives the Dera an affinity for proc-based status builds. At 22% status chance, elemental procs apply consistently during sustained automatic fire. The 8% critical chance and 1.6x multiplier make crit builds secondary; the Dera's main strength lies in its rapid status application. Mastery Rank 4 is required.

The Dera is research-craftable from the Tenno Lab in a Dojo or purchasable through the Market. The high fire rate and perfect accuracy (100, meaning no bullet spread) allow consistent mid-range engagement. The Slash component generates bleed procs that bypass armor and deal damage-over-time to health directly, making the Dera useful against armored Grineer targets where Slash procs reach the health pool beneath the armor layer.

The Dera Vandal is the enhanced Invasion-reward variant, substantially improving critical chance, critical multiplier, status chance, and fire rate over the base Dera. The base Dera serves as the accessible introduction to the Corpus automatic rifle archetype and as a Mastery Rank progression weapon before the Vandal becomes available.

## Acquisition

The Dera blueprint is available for research in the Tenno Lab within a Clan Dojo or for purchase directly from the in-game Market with credits. Research requires Tenno Lab resources including Circuits, Polymer Bundle, Alloy Plate, and Orokin Cell. Consult [the Dera Fandom wiki page](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Dera) for the current resource costs and research time.

## Variants

The Dera Vandal is the enhanced variant available as a reward from Invasion missions when a Corpus vs. Grineer invasion appears. The Vandal improves critical chance, critical multiplier, status chance, and fire rate, making it significantly more effective for both crit and status builds. Players should prioritize completing Invasion missions when the Dera Vandal blueprint or components appear as rewards.

## Recommended builds

**Viral/Slash status build.** Use Primed Fever Strike (Toxin) and Cryo Rounds (Cold) for Viral alongside the Slash base. Viral amplifies health damage; each Slash bleed proc deals increased DoT against Viral-weakened health pools. Add Serration and Split Chamber for base damage scaling and multishot. Galvanized Aptitude further amplifies damage when multiple status types are active.

**Corrosive armor strip build.** For Grineer Alloy Armor, pair Infected Clip (Toxin) and Voltaic Strike (Electric) for Corrosive. The 22% status applies Corrosive procs consistently at 11.25 fire rate, rapidly stripping armor for unmitigated physical damage delivery. Add Hunter Munitions for Slash proc generation on the occasional crit hit.

**Entry-level progression build.** Serration, Split Chamber, and two elemental 60/60 mods (Fever Strike + Voltaic Strike for Corrosive, or Fever Strike + Cryo Rounds for Viral). This build covers early and mid-star-chart content without requiring expensive Primed mods, and establishes familiarity with the Dera's automatic fire style before upgrading to the Vandal.

## Synergies

Rhino's Roar applies a flat damage multiplier to all physical damage from the Dera, including the Slash component. Banshee's Sonar highlights enemy weak spots for bonus damage, pairable with the Dera's 100 accuracy for targeted weak-spot fire at medium range. Saryn's Spores synergize with the Toxin component of Viral procs applied by the Dera, spreading status effects to additional nearby enemies in groups.

## Notes

- The 22% status chance at 11.25 fire rate means multiple proc types apply rapidly; choose two complementary elements rather than stacking a single type.
- Upgrade to the Dera Vandal as soon as Invasion opportunities arise; the stat improvements are substantial across every category.
