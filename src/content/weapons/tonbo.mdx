## Overview

The Tonbo is an early-accessible polearm with a heavily puncture-weighted damage profile. Its 184 total base damage per hit is dominated by 138 puncture, making it particularly effective against armored Grineer enemies whose armor type reduces incoming damage from other physical types. The weapon is unlocked at Mastery Rank 3, placing it firmly in early-game territory but giving it more reach and range than swords or daggers.

Polearms as a category offer extended melee reach compared to swords, enabling multi-enemy swipes that can connect with several targets in a single attack. The Tonbo's standard 2.5-meter range is consistent with the polearm archetype. Players new to melee combat will find the polearm's wide arc attack pattern intuitive and forgiving when surrounded by groups of enemies.

The Tonbo's 25% status chance is a genuine strength for a base weapon at this mastery tier. Status-focused builds using corrosive (electric + toxin) or viral (cold + toxin) damage can strip armor or amplify damage against biological enemies respectively. Because the weapon's 5% critical chance offers minimal reliable critical return, status is the more productive modding direction for general use.

## Acquisition

The Tonbo blueprint is available in the in-game Market for 15,000 credits. Crafting materials match the standard entry-level polearm recipe. Build time is 12 hours. The weapon does not require any quest completion or special access, making it straightforwardly obtainable for players reaching the appropriate mastery level.

## Variants

No Vandal, Wraith, or Prime variant exists for the Tonbo. Players looking for an upgraded polearm experience should examine the Guandao (which shares the polearm stance) or wait for higher-tier polearms like Orthos Prime accessible through Void Relics.

## Recommended builds

**Corrosive status build**: Combine Primed Fever Strike (toxin) with Voltaic Strike (electric) to achieve corrosive damage. Add Pressure Point (base damage), Fury or Primed Fury (attack speed), and Rending Strike (impact/puncture multiplier). This build strips armor efficiently from Grineer Lancers and Bombards, exposing their lower base health for rapid elimination.

**Viral-slash build**: Swap to cold + toxin (viral) combined with a slash-enhancing mod like Drifting Contact or Maiming Strike if available. The 27.6 slash damage is modest but viral amplification doubles damage received and stacks to x4 with six procs, significantly increasing effective DPS against high-health enemies.

**Heavy attack build**: Use Sacrificial Steel and Organ Shatter for critical damage amplification on the heavy attack, combined with Corrupt Charge (increases combo to 12x from the start) and Killing Blow (heavy attack multiplier). Each heavy attack consumes the full combo counter but deals enormous burst damage.

## Notes

The Tonbo does not have a dedicated unique stance; it uses standard polearm stances, which are available as drops from various Grineer enemy types on Earth and Mars. Primed Reach extends the 2.5-meter range and significantly increases the effective sweep, enabling attacks to connect with more enemies simultaneously. Condition Overload synergizes well with the high status chance build, multiplying damage for each unique status type afflicting the target.
