## Overview

The Tombfinger represents an alternative kitgun secondary assembly combining the Tombfinger barrel (heavy impact projectile launcher), Ramping grip (scales damage over time), and Haymaker link (status chance multiplier). This modular configuration specializes in raw radiation damage output, excelling in sustained damage against grouped targets through its slower, harder-hitting projectiles. The Tombfinger barrel prioritizes damage concentration over fire rate, creating a methodical secondary playstyle emphasizing accurate burst fire. Like all kitguns, the Tombfinger assembly is highly customizable through component swapping, enabling players to create entirely different weapons reflecting personal preferences. The described configuration trades critical performance for raw damage, making it ideal for status-focused and pure-damage builds.

## Damage

The Tombfinger's radiation damage of 45 points delivers roughly double the Catchmoon output per projectile, compensating for the slower 2.67 fire rate through raw damage concentration. The 16% status chance enables radiation confusion procs, though the lower frequency makes multiproc builds less reliable than high-status variants. The minimal critical stats of 12% chance and 1.4x multiplier make critical scaling impractical, instead requiring pure damage and status-focused modding. Against lightly-armored and unarmored targets, the weapon excels through damage volume and crowd control. Against armor-heavy enemies without elemental mod support, the radiation damage becomes increasingly ineffective in late-game content, requiring build adjustments for high-level play.

## Acquisition

Tombfinger kitgun construction requires three modular components: a Tombfinger barrel, a grip frame, and a link assembly. Component selection and acquisition follow the same process as other kitguns through Hok at Cetus or Rude Zuud at Fortuna. Assembly at the kitgun crafting bench requires 5,000 credits and one hour of construction time. The flexibility enables players to experiment with alternative component combinations, creating entirely different weapons through barrel and grip swapping. Advanced players develop multiple kitgun assemblies for mission-specific optimization.

## Variants

The Tombfinger barrel represents one component option within the extensive kitgun ecosystem. The same barrel can be combined with different grips and links to create weapons with different stat profiles. The Catchmoon barrel serves as an alternative heavier-fire variant using the same construction pathway. Players crafting extensively develop personal collections of kitgun assemblies optimized toward specific playstyles and mission requirements. The modular platform discourages the concept of "variants" because every component combination creates a unique weapon.

## Recommended builds

Raw damage and status-focused modding capitalize on the Tombfinger's damage concentration. Hornet Strike and Pistol Grip form core damage amplifiers, while true puncher boosts critical chance above the pathetically low 12% baseline. Elemental mods like Heated Chamber or Pathogen Rounds create multiproc chains through radiation combinations. Magazine extension via Increased magazine capacity extends the 40-round capacity for sustained fire. Reload-reduction mods like Trick Mag accelerate the reload time during extended engagements. Forma investment unlocks exilus slots for utility like ammo mutation or fire-rate scaling.

## Synergies

Frames providing damage buffs synergize excellently with the Tombfinger's high per-shot damage. Chroma's damage multiplier scales directly with each projectile, creating exceptional burst potential. Wisp's mote buffs improve overall weapon effectiveness through multiplicative stacking. Harrow provides crit chance boosts helping the anemic 12% base chance reach practical tiers. Nidus converts kills into stacking bonuses, multiplying effectiveness over time. Frost's slow effects complement the weapon's methodical fire rate through enhanced positioning control.

## Lore

Kitguns represent personalized weaponry emerging from open-world faction collaboration. The Tombfinger barrel specifically reflects heavy-impact specialization against armored threats encountered in Vallis farming. The modular construction philosophy enables players to experiment extensively with component combinations, reflecting faction values regarding individual customization. Within player communities, the Tombfinger assembly serves as an introduction to heavy-hitter secondary weapons, teaching players about damage concentration versus volume-of-fire tradeoffs through practical experimentation.
