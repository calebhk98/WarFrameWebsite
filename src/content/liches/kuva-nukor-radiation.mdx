## Overview

The Kuva Nukor with a Radiation elemental bonus is widely considered one of the best secondary weapons in Warframe for endgame content. The Kuva Nukor is a beam weapon that chains between nearby enemies, rapidly applying status effects. Its exceptional status chance combined with a Radiation bonus makes it the premier tool for status-based builds that need to apply Radiation procs -- which cause enemies to attack each other -- while simultaneously stacking other elemental status effects through the multichain mechanic.

The Radiation element bonus, at 25%-60% of base damage, adds directly to the Kuva Nukor's damage output while also increasing the probability of Radiation procs per hit. Radiation is especially effective against Alloy Armor (common on Grineer and Corpus heavy units) and causes enemies to turn on their allies, providing crowd control alongside damage.

To spawn a Radiation Larvling, use Chroma or Mirage. Chroma's elemental affinity in its base state is associated with Radiation, making it the most reliable choice for forcing a Radiation Larvling. Mirage may produce Radiation under certain conditions.

## Acquisition

### Preparing for the Larvling

Equip Chroma and head to a level 20+ Grineer node such as Cassini (Saturn) or Tethys (Saturn). Cassini's Defense mission allows multiple Kuva siphon cycles, increasing the chance of a Larvling spawn per run. Once the Larvling appears, verify the element icon (a spiral indicating Radiation) before performing the Mercy kill.

### The murmur grind

The Kuva Nukor is a highly sought weapon, and players often chain multiple Lich spawns specifically for high-percentage Radiation Nukors. Each Lich requires a full murmur cycle. The typical strategy is:

1. Spawn the Lich, note the weapon and element.
2. If the element percentage is low (below 50%), abandon the Lich by re-spawning after allowing the Lich to accumulate influence.
3. Repeat until a high-percentage roll spawns.
4. Farm that Lich's murmurs, identify the Requiem pattern, and vanquish.

The percentage is fixed at spawn and cannot be altered. Valence Fusion allows combining two Kuva Nukors -- the weapon with the higher percentage retains its element, but the raw percentage can be boosted to a cap of 60%.

### Valence Fusion

Valence Fusion is an endgame upgrade system specific to Kuva and Tenet weapons. By consuming a second copy of the same weapon, the percentage increases by 10% up to the 60% cap. This means a player can start with a 40% Radiation Nukor and fuse it with a second Radiation Nukor (any percentage) to reach 50%, then fuse again with a third to reach 60%.

Both weapons used in Valence Fusion must be the same weapon type. The element of the surviving weapon is taken from the one that had the higher percentage.

### Requiem pattern confirmation

Once all three Requiem mod slots are identified through murmur farming, equip the mods in the revealed order on the Parazon. Enter the Lich's lair mission from the Kuva Fortress. Down the Lich three times using the correct sequence to trigger the kill prompt.

## Weapon performance

The Kuva Nukor functions as a beam that bounces between targets within range, hitting up to six enemies simultaneously. Each chain hit applies the weapon's full status chance independently, allowing the Radiation Kuva Nukor to stack multiple different status effects across an entire enemy group in seconds.

The Radiation proc causes affected enemies to prioritize attacking their allies for several seconds. In a group of heavily armored Grineer Bombards, a single Nukor burst can turn the entire squad against itself, effectively neutralizing the encounter without spending significant ammunition.

For critical-path mods:

- Secondary Dexterity (Helminth ability or arcane) extends beam duration
- Primed Heated Charge combined with the Radiation bonus creates a dual-element status explosion
- Galvanized Diffusion increases multishot, multiplying the beam chain hits

The Kuva Nukor is a secondary weapon, freeing the primary slot for a high-damage primary such as the Kuva Zarr or a Tenet weapon from the Sister system.

## Recommended mod setup

Maximize status chance and multishot for the beam-chain effect. A high-percentage Radiation bonus contributes to elemental damage without a dedicated mod slot. Key mods:

- Galvanized Diffusion (multishot)
- Primed Heated Charge (Heat, to combine with Radiation toward desired procs)
- Barrel Diffusion
- Lethal Torrent
- Primed Pistol Gambit (critical chance)
- Primed Target Cracker (critical multiplier)
- Augur Seeker or Creeping Bullseye for further crit investment

## Required Requiem mods

Requiem patterns are unique to each Lich. Maintain a stockpile of all eight Requiem mods and use the murmur system to identify the three correct mods and their slot order. Requiem Relics are the primary source; run Kuva Siphon and Flood missions to gather Relics, then crack them in Requiem Fissure missions to collect the mods.

## Lore

The Kuva Nukor was originally an experimental weapon designed by the Grineer Queens to channel raw Void-infused Kuva energy in a focused beam. When a Lich inherits it with a Radiation affinity, the weapon's beam spectrum shifts into the ionizing range, producing the telltale purplish arc of Radiation damage on impact. Grineer alchemists theorized that Radiation-tuned Kuva could disrupt enemy neural processing -- an effect confirmed in combat by the chaos Radiation procs cause among tightly grouped squads.

The Lich who carries this weapon was likely a Grineer officer involved in weapons testing or energy research, drawn to the Nukor's precision targeting system as a tool befitting a commander rather than a frontline soldier.
