## Overview

The Tenet Tetra is the Sister of Parvos variant of the Corpus assault rifle Tetra. Sisters of Parvos function similarly to Kuva Liches but arise from Corpus Candidates rather than Grineer Larvlings. They are tied to the Parvos Granum narrative and use a different set of weapons -- the Tenet line -- which are Corpus-faction weapons enhanced through Granum Void technology.

The Tenet Tetra combines the Tetra's burst-fire precision with a Tenet-amplified damage profile. An elemental bonus on a Sister weapon functions identically to the Kuva Lich system: the element percentage (25%-60%) multiplies the base damage as a bonus of that element type, stackable with mods. This entry covers the Toxin-element variant; pairing a Toxin-element Tenet Tetra with an Electric mod creates the Corrosive combined element, which is highly effective against Grineer Alloy Armor.

To spawn a Corpus Candidate, complete [The Deadlock Protocol](/quests/the-deadlock-protocol) quest, then enter Corpus Proxima missions or Corpus ground missions on Venus. The Candidate will spawn when a Treasurer Corpus unit appears, at which point the player can attack the Treasurer until the Candidate spawns. The element of the resulting Sister is determined by the Warframe used when the Candidate spawns.

## Acquisition

### Spawning a Candidate

The Sister of Parvos system is triggered via Corpus Treasure missions. After completing The Deadlock Protocol, Treasurer enemies appear in Corpus ground missions. Defeating a Treasurer drops a Granum Crown, which can be used to enter the Granum Void -- a short pocket dimension combat challenge. Completing the Granum Void challenge at Rank 1, 2, or 3 spawns a Candidate of escalating power.

The higher the Granum Void rank completed, the higher the initial weapon bonus percentage range for the resulting Sister. Rank 3 (Gold Granum Crown challenge) gives a higher floor for the percentage roll.

### Corpus Hound companion

Unlike Kuva Liches, Sisters of Parvos have a Corpus Hound companion that accompanies them when they appear in missions. The Hound must be defeated alongside the Sister during the lair confrontation. The player can also obtain the Hound as a companion after converting or vanquishing the Sister, by recovering the Hound's imprint.

### Murmur process

Sisters follow the same murmur accumulation loop as Kuva Liches. Kill Sister Thralls in Corpus missions under the Sister's claimed territory to earn murmurs. Once all three Requiem mod slots are identified, enter the Sister's Lair (accessed via Veil Proxima or by accumulating enough influence for a direct confrontation node) with the correct Parazon mods.

## Weapon performance

The Tenet Tetra fires in bursts, with each burst delivering concentrated damage at mid-to-long range. The Corpus design philosophy emphasizes precision and critical damage, and the Tenet variant improves upon this with enhanced critical chance and status chance relative to the base Tetra.

For general Corpus content, an elemental bonus paired with Electric (from mods) to create Magnetic, or with Toxin (to bypass shields entirely), leverages the Tenet Tetra's per-burst damage effectively. The burst-fire mechanism rewards trigger discipline: allowing each burst to complete before firing again improves accuracy at range.

The Tenet Tetra's critical hit path benefits significantly from Arcane Avenger -- the arcane applies when the player takes damage, pushing critical chance high enough for consistent orange/red crits.

## Recommended mod setup

- Serration or Primed Cryo Rounds for base damage
- Split Chamber for multishot
- Vital Sense and Point Strike for critical path
- Hunter Munitions for Slash proc application on critical hits
- Malignant Force (Toxin) to combine with Electric (from Charged Rounds) for Corrosive

## Required Requiem mods

The Requiem pattern shown is one example. Each Sister generates a unique pattern at spawn. Maintain all eight Requiem mods in supply before initiating the Sister cycle. The same Requiem mods used for Kuva Liches are used for Sisters of Parvos -- the mod pool is shared.

## Lore

Sisters of Parvos serve Parvos Granum, the legendary founder of the Corpus who transcended death by anchoring his consciousness to the Granum Void. Parvos rejected the Tenno and views them as chaos incarnate in a universe that rewards discipline and profit. His Sisters are Corpus businesswomen and warriors who proved their worth through the Granum Void trials, earning Parvos Granum's personal patronage and the Tenet weapon technology that comes with it.

The Tenet Tetra's design reflects the Corpus philosophy of elegant precision -- it is not a brute-force weapon but a calculated-damage delivery system. A Sister bearing this weapon represents Parvos Granum's ideal of the disciplined warrior: skilled, precise, and willing to use any tool to achieve victory.
