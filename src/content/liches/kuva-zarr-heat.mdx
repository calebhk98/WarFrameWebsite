## Overview

The Kuva Zarr is a Grineer cannon-class primary weapon that fires explosive mortar rounds. With a Heat elemental bonus, the Kuva Zarr combines massive area-of-effect blast damage with persistent burning procs, making it one of the most effective primary weapons for crowd-clearing in open-world and corridor missions alike.

Unlike most Kuva weapons, the Zarr fires in two modes: a primary fire that lofts an arcing explosive round, and an alternate fire that launches a spread of cannon shot. Both fire modes benefit from the Heat elemental bonus. The primary fire's large radius means that the Heat proc from a single shot can affect multiple grouped enemies simultaneously.

At 60% Heat bonus, a well-modded Kuva Zarr can apply Heat status in seconds to entire groups, stripping armor contributions when Corrosive procs are added through mods. For Steel Path Grineer content, Heat plus Corrosive is the damage type combination that most efficiently bypasses Ferrite Armor.

## Acquisition

Equip Ember or Nezha and run level 20+ Grineer nodes to spawn a Heat Larvling. Confirm the Heat element icon before performing the Mercy kill. The Zarr is one of the more common Kuva weapon drops, so players farming for a specific element may encounter it frequently.

### Murmur farming strategy

The Kuva Zarr's high area-of-effect damage output also makes the Lich that carries it relatively easy to deal with during the murmur farming phase. Thralls in the Lich's territory die quickly to the weapon, ironically accelerating murmur accumulation if the player re-equips the Kuva Zarr during farming.

Group murmur farming in Kuva Survival missions is particularly efficient for the Zarr-carrying Lich. Survival missions spawn thralls continuously, and the Zarr's splash damage clears groups rapidly, converting each thrall kill into murmur progress.

### Requiem pattern determination

Maintain all eight Requiem mods charged. Once the murmur system identifies all three Requiem slots, arrange them in the correct order on the Parazon and enter the Lich lair from the Kuva Fortress navigation screen.

## Weapon performance

The Kuva Zarr's primary fire has one of the largest explosion radii of any primary weapon in Warframe. The arcing trajectory requires some adjustment for close-quarters engagements, but in open maps the weapon excels. The Heat proc from a 60% bonus adds a meaningful damage-over-time effect to every target caught in the blast.

Combining the Heat bonus with Viral modding (adding Cold + Toxin through mods) creates a powerful three-element setup: the Lich's Heat element, combined with Cold and Toxin mods, creates a weapon that simultaneously applies Viral (Health halving) and Heat (armor reduction through heat stack) status effects. This setup is considered top-tier for Grineer endurance.

Weaknesses of the Kuva Zarr include:

- Self-damage from close-range detonations (reduced but still present)
- Slower fire rate compared to automatic primary weapons
- The arcing projectile requires leading moving targets at range

## Recommended mod setup

For maximum anti-Grineer effectiveness with the Heat Kuva Zarr:

- Primed Firestorm extends explosion radius, increasing the number of targets hit per shot
- Hellfire combined with the Heat Lich bonus creates a high Heat damage output
- Infected Clip for Toxin; Heat + Toxin = Gas, or pair differently for Viral
- Vigilante Armaments for multishot on the cannon-shot alternate fire
- Metal Auger for punch-through, allowing rounds to hit targets behind cover

## Required Requiem mods

The Requiem pattern in frontmatter is an example. Each Lich has a unique randomized sequence. Gather all eight Requiem mods before beginning the murmur cycle for a new Lich to avoid delays.

## Lore

The Zarr is a Grineer artillery piece that predates the modern Star Chart wars, used during the Old War against Sentient forces. Kuva-infused variants were reserved for Lich-class soldiers as a mark of prestige: the weapon's destructive capacity matched the near-immortal resilience of its carrier. A Heat-aligned Lich favored the Zarr for its ability to deny large areas of terrain through persistent fire zones, a scorched-earth tactic reflecting the Grineer Queens' ruthless philosophy that victory through total destruction is preferable to any compromise.

In the post-Queens era following the events of The Sacrifice, Liches operate semi-independently, their loyalty to the player who defeated them replacing their original fealty. A converted Heat Kuva Zarr Lich can be deployed in missions as a powerful allied combatant, raining explosive fire on the same enemies it once protected.
