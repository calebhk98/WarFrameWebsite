## Overview

The Kuva Bramma with a Heat elemental bonus is one of the most sought-after Kuva Lich weapon variants in the game. The Kuva Bramma is a Grineer bow that fires cluster-bomb arrows, each detonating in a large area of effect. When the carrier Lich rolls a Heat bonus, the weapon gains bonus Heat damage ranging from 25% to 60% of the weapon's base damage, applied as a multiplicative increase to the Heat damage component.

A Kuva Lich carrying this weapon spawns after a player performs a Mercy kill on a Heat-element Kuva Larvling. The larvling's element is determined by the Warframe the player uses when it spawns -- each Warframe corresponds to a specific element. Matching the Larvling's element to the desired bonus requires careful Warframe selection before entering the mission.

The Kuva Bramma excels in open areas where its massive cluster explosion can hit multiple enemies. The Heat proc it applies causes targets to burn over time, and when combined with a Toxin mod, Heat and Toxin combine into Gas, a powerful crowd-control element against Infested and grouped enemies.

## Acquisition

### Spawning a Larvling

To obtain a Kuva Bramma with a Heat element, the player must first spawn a Kuva Larvling on any level 20 or higher Grineer mission node after completing [The War Within](/quests/the-war-within) quest. Larvlings spawn when a player kills at least one enemy after a red-lit Kuva siphon drains energy on that node.

The Warframe used during the Larvling spawn determines the elemental bonus. To get a Heat element, use a Warframe associated with Heat: Ember, Nezha, Lavos (when channeling Heat), or any other Heat-affiliated frame. Confirm the element by looking at the Larvling icon that appears when it spawns -- a flame symbol indicates Heat.

### Murmur farming

After converting or vanquishing a Larvling into a Lich, the Lich will begin claiming territory. Murmurs are the currency used to identify the three Requiem mods needed to vanquish the Lich. Murmurs are earned by:

- Killing thralls the Lich spawns in its claimed territory
- Running Kuva Lich-influenced missions, which spawn more thralls per mission
- Converting a different Lich that has the same weapon (rare strategy)

Each Requiem mod slot requires a set number of murmurs to unlock. Once all three Requiem slots are identified, the player can challenge the Lich in its lair. The Lich lair is accessed via the Kuva Fortress node or by accumulating enough influence that the Lich confronts the player directly.

### Requiem mods

Requiem mods drop from Requiem Relics, which in turn drop from Kuva Siphon and Kuva Flood missions. The three required Requiem mods for this specific Lich are determined at spawn and remain fixed -- no two Lich spawns are guaranteed to share the same pattern unless the Requiem Ultimatum is used.

The mods must also be placed in the correct slot order on the Parazon. If the player has the right mods but in the wrong order, the Lich will be temporarily downed but not killed, granting a partial murmur reveal for the sequence. Each failed attempt the Lich gains power and its level increases.

### Vanquish or convert

When the Lich is finally downed with the correct Requiem pattern:

- **Vanquish**: The Lich is killed. The player receives the weapon at the rolled element percentage.
- **Convert**: The Lich becomes an ally. It can be deployed in missions as an NPC helper or traded to another player via Crimson Branch in a Relay.

## Weapon performance

The Kuva Bramma fires a single arrow that, on impact, releases multiple cluster bomblets. These bomblets each deal damage in their own area of effect, creating overlapping damage zones. Against grouped enemies, a single shot can hit the same target multiple times.

With a 60% Heat bonus, the weapon gains a substantial burn damage component. Heat procs strip armor when combined with Corrosive, and the burn-over-time effect ensures enemies continue taking damage after the initial hit. For Steel Path content, a high element percentage Bramma is considered among the top choices for Grineer and Corpus missions.

The bow benefits significantly from the following mod types:

- Point Strike and Vital Sense for critical damage amplification
- Vigilante Fervor or Speed Trigger to reduce the draw time
- Primed Firestorm to extend the bomblet blast radius
- A riven mod, if available, can push the weapon's performance further

## Recommended mod setup

A standard high-damage configuration for the Heat Kuva Bramma combines critical and status path mods. The innate Heat element from the Lich bonus can be paired with a Toxin mod to create Gas, or with a Cold mod to create Blast for knockdown utility.

Arcane Avenger is a strong arcane pairing because it increases critical chance after receiving damage, synergizing with the bow's high critical multiplier.

## Required Requiem mods

The exact Requiem pattern for any given Lich is random and determined at spawn. Run Kuva Siphon missions (any planet) to obtain Requiem Relics, then crack those relics in Requiem Fissure missions to acquire the specific Requiem mods needed. The Parazon Requiem mod slots are filled by identifying each slot through murmur farming.

Keep a full stock of every Requiem mod (Ris, Lohk, Fass, Vome, Netra, Khra, Jahu, Xata) to avoid farming delays when a new Lich spawns.

## Lore

Kuva Liches are born from the Grineer's Continuity program, a grotesque process where loyal Grineer soldiers drink Kuva -- the life essence of the Orokin Queens -- to achieve a form of undying rebirth. When a Tenno performs a Mercy kill on a Larvling rather than finishing it, the Larvling escapes to undergo the Kuva ritual, emerging as a Lich: a powerful Grineer warrior who has partially cheated death and bears a personal grudge against the Tenno who spared them.

The Kuva Lich system was introduced in Update 26, The Old Blood, and represents one of Warframe's most elaborate enemy progression systems. Each Lich is procedurally named, carries a weapon, and occupies territory on the star chart, affecting mission rewards in those nodes until dealt with.

The Kuva Bramma itself is an ancient Grineer siege weapon repurposed as a personal sidearm for the Lich's elite class. Its cluster-explosive design makes it effective against fortified positions, and the Heat variant reflects the Lich's affinity for incendiary weaponry drawn from its Kuva-infused heritage.
