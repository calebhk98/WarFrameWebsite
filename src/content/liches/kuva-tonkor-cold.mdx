## Overview

The Kuva Tonkor is a grenade launcher that fires grenades in a predictable arc, detonating on a short timer. Its dual-grenade burst fire makes it one of the higher single-shot damage outputs among Kuva primaries. A Cold elemental bonus gives the Tonkor a Blast damage pathway when combined with Heat mods (Cold + Heat = Blast), which knocks down groups of enemies with each detonation.

Cold damage on its own is effective against Corpus and Infested. Against Corpus, Cold damage targets shields rather than bypassing them, but Cold procs slow enemy movement speed, making it excellent for crowd control in missions that require zone denial. A 60% Cold Tonkor paired with a Viral mod setup (Cold combines with Toxin for Viral) adds health-halving capability to the grenade's blast radius.

Spawn a Cold Larvling using Frost -- the most reliably associated Cold Warframe in the game.

## Acquisition

### Larvling spawning

Enter a level 20+ Grineer node with Frost equipped. After the Kuva siphon event triggers and enemies are killed, the Larvling will spawn. A snowflake or crystal icon indicates Cold element. Perform the Mercy kill to convert the Larvling into a Lich.

### Cold Tonkor rarity

The Kuva Tonkor is a relatively rare Kuva weapon compared to the Bramma or Nukor. Players may need to spawn and abandon multiple Liches before the Tonkor appears. To abandon a Lich, allow it to gain enough influence to reach its lair, defeat it without the correct Requiem mods, and let it escape. It eventually becomes a recruitable ally at the Crimson Branch or can be traded.

### Murmur efficiency

For Cold Tonkor farming, consider running Exterminate missions in the Lich's territory. Exterminate missions are shorter than Survivals but provide a guaranteed thrall count proportional to the total enemy count. Each thrall killed fills the murmur bar, and Exterminate allows a full clear without time pressure.

## Weapon performance

The Kuva Tonkor fires two grenades per trigger pull that bounce briefly before detonating. The grenades travel in an arc, which requires practice for accurate placement. At close range, the grenades may bounce unpredictably -- aim slightly past the target group and let the bounce bring the grenade into position.

With Cold element and Blast modding (Cold + Heat mods), every detonation knocks down groups of enemies within the blast radius. Blast-proc knockdown is a consistent crowd control tool, and the Tonkor's damage per detonation is high enough to eliminate standard enemies immediately, leaving the knockdown relevant only against heavy units and bosses.

For Viral builds (Cold Lich bonus + Toxin mod), the grenade applies Viral on each target within the blast. Viral reduces enemy max health by 50%, making every subsequent shot from any weapon twice as effective against that target for the duration of the Viral proc.

## Recommended mod setup

- Primed Firestorm for blast radius increase
- Firestorm (base) as an alternative if Primed is unavailable
- Infected Clip (Toxin) to combine with Cold for Viral
- Speed Trigger for draw speed reduction
- Point Strike and Vital Sense for critical path investment
- Terminal Velocity to increase projectile speed and reduce arc deviation at range

## Required Requiem mods

The Requiem sequence shown in frontmatter is one example. The actual pattern for any Lich is randomized at spawn. Gather all eight Requiem mods beforehand to avoid mid-cycle shortages.

## Lore

The Tonkor was originally a Grineer anti-fortification weapon used during siege operations on Corpus trade stations. Its grenade payload was designed to penetrate hardened bunker walls and detonate inside enclosed spaces, maximizing the shock wave effect in confined rooms. Kuva-infused Tonkors were issued to elite Grineer combat engineers -- soldiers who specialized in breaching operations and who had earned their Lich status through distinguished combat engineering service.

A Cold-element Kuva Tonkor carries an unusual origin: Cold-affinity Liches typically arose in environmental operations in frozen regions such as Pluto or Europa, where the Grineer maintained mining outposts in subzero conditions. These soldiers adapted their equipment to function under extreme cold, and Kuva infusion converted their environmental resilience into an offensive capability, channeling the same cold they had once struggled to survive into weapon form.
