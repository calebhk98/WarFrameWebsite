## Overview

The Kuva Shildeg is a heavy-impact hammer belonging to the Kuva weapon family. As a melee weapon, it is categorized separately from the ranged Kuva weapons, and its Electric elemental bonus adds charged electrical damage to each strike. The Shildeg's heavy slam attacks create a large ground-impact area, and the Electric element applies Electric procs that stagger enemies, giving it natural crowd-control capability.

Electric damage is effective against Corpus Robotic units and against enemies with significant shield pools when combined with Toxin (to create Magnetic damage). The Shildeg's high Impact damage value is effective against Corpus shields directly, and the Electric bonus compounds this with AoE chain damage that can arc between clustered Corpus robotic enemies.

To spawn an Electric Larvling, equip Volt or Valkyr before entering level 20+ Grineer nodes. Confirm the electric bolt icon on the Larvling before performing the Mercy kill.

## Acquisition

### Larvling preparation

Volt is the most reliable Warframe for guaranteeing an Electric Larvling due to its direct association with the Electric element. Run a Grineer node such as Hydron (Sedna) or Cassini (Saturn) with Volt equipped, kill enemies after the Kuva siphon passes, and wait for the Larvling to appear. The Electric element is shown as a lightning bolt icon near the Larvling.

### Murmur process

After converting the Larvling into a Lich, the Lich claims territory and begins spawning thralls. Enter claimed territory missions to kill thralls for murmur progress. The Kuva Shildeg Lich tends to be moderately aggressive in claiming territory, so the player may encounter the Lich during missions before all three Requiem slots are identified.

If the Lich appears before identification is complete, use a partial Requiem guess (any three mods in slots) to attempt a down -- even a failed sequence provides one correct mod reveal per attempt. This can accelerate identification if the player is willing to risk the Lich leveling up.

### Melee consideration during farming

Melee weapons benefit from the finisher damage mechanic on thralls. Thralls can be executed with a Mercy kill (ground finisher) for bonus murmur progress per thrall. Bringing a fast melee weapon to trigger this consistently is advised even if the Shildeg is not yet in inventory.

## Weapon performance

The Kuva Shildeg is a hammer with slam-focused mechanics. Its primary stat investment is in Impact and Blast (from slam attacks). The Electric element bonus enhances the standard melee attacks with chain lightning on hit, arcing to nearby enemies. This makes the Shildeg particularly effective in dense enemy groups.

For a melee build:

- Primed Reach extends the hammer's hit range, affecting more targets per swing
- Condition Overload scales with unique status effects on the target -- Electric, Heat, Viral, and others all contribute independent damage multipliers
- Blood Rush increases critical chance as the combo counter builds
- Weeping Wounds increases status chance as the combo counter builds
- Sacrificial Steel provides additional critical chance for umbral builds

The Electric element combines with Toxin to produce Magnetic, reducing Corpus shield capacity. For Shield-heavy Corpus bosses, the Shildeg with an Electric bonus and a Toxin mod on the weapon creates Magnetic procs on every hit, stripping shields rapidly.

## Recommended mod setup

Focus on the combo counter and status-chance path for maximum Condition Overload scaling:

- Primed Fever Strike or Volcanic Edge to add a second element alongside Electric
- Corrupt Charge for instant combo start at 45
- Gladiator Might combined with Blood Rush for high critical multiplier
- Amalgam Organ Shatter for heavy attack speed increase
- Relentless Combination to maintain combo through status procs

## Required Requiem mods

The Requiem pattern is randomized per Lich. The pattern shown in frontmatter is one possible example. Stock all eight Requiem mods before initiating the Lich cycle.

## Lore

Grineer hammers have a long tradition as execution weapons in their culture -- the Shildeg's design descends from ancient Grineer gladiatorial combat forms where strength and direct confrontation were the highest virtues. The Kuva Shildeg represents the pinnacle of this tradition: a weapon forged with Kuva-infused alloys that resonate with Void energy, delivering strikes that carry both physical and metaphysical weight.

A Kuva Shildeg Lich with Electric affinity often rose from Grineer combat arenas where electrical containment fields were used to prevent prisoner escapes. These soldiers developed an intuitive understanding of electrical hazards and electrical weaponry, a knowledge that Kuva infusion transformed into an innate ability to channel electrical energy through their weapon strikes.

The Shildeg was introduced in Update 26 alongside the full Kuva Lich system and is one of the less commonly farmed Kuva weapons due to melee builds being a secondary priority for many players. However, for dedicated melee players, a high-percentage Electric Shildeg remains a competitive endgame weapon.
