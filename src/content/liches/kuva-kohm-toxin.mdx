## Overview

The Kuva Kohm is a Grineer shotgun that fires an expanding spread of pellets, with its fire rate increasing as the trigger is held. The Toxin elemental bonus on a Kuva Kohm creates a weapon optimized for bypassing Corpus shields while dealing high pellet-count status procs per second. At sustained fire, the Kuva Kohm delivers a remarkable number of pellets, each with an independent chance to proc status effects.

The Toxin element is particularly valuable on this weapon because Toxin damage bypasses shields and deals directly to health. Against Corpus enemies with large shield pools, the Kuva Kohm with Toxin makes shield management irrelevant, reducing every fight to a straightforward health removal challenge.

Spawning a Toxin Larvling requires a Toxin-affiliated Warframe. Saryn is the most reliable choice, as her association with Toxin damage is consistent across all Saryn variants. Nidus is an alternative option.

## Acquisition

Run level 20+ Grineer nodes with Saryn equipped. The Larvling spawns after a Kuva siphon passes through the map and the player kills enough enemies to trigger it. The Toxin element is confirmed by a green droplet icon near the Larvling before the Mercy kill prompt.

### Murmur farming

The Kuva Kohm is effective for its own murmur farming phase due to its high status output. Thralls in the Lich's territory can be dispatched quickly with sustained Kohm fire, and each killed thrall advances murmur progress. For efficiency, run Survival or Exterminate missions in the Lich's occupied nodes and prioritize thrall kills over completion speed.

The Lich itself may appear during murmur missions to challenge the player. If the Requiem mods are not yet identified, the Lich encounter should be survived rather than won -- flee or use crowd control to escape the Lich and continue farming thralls.

### Valence Fusion for maximum bonus

The Kuva Kohm is a weapon where the bonus percentage meaningfully affects output due to the high pellet count. Each pellet benefits from the Toxin multiplier, so a 60% bonus applies to every pellet in the spread. Valence Fusion allows a player to accumulate up to a 60% Toxin Kuva Kohm by combining lower-percentage copies.

## Weapon performance

The Kuva Kohm's ramp-up mechanic rewards sustained fire. The longer the trigger is held, the more pellets are fired per shot, eventually reaching full automatic multi-pellet spread. This mechanic makes it a strong choice for drawn-out endurance missions where the weapon can remain spun up throughout encounters.

At 60% Toxin bonus, the Kuva Kohm applies Toxin status on virtually every trigger pull at full fire rate. Toxin procs stack, dealing escalating poison damage over time that continues after the initial burst. Against high-health Corpus Techs or Corpus Bosses, a short Kohm burst followed by letting the Toxin DoT resolve can be more ammo-efficient than continuous fire.

Combine with a Viral setup (Heat + Toxin from mods) to make the innate Toxin Lich bonus pair with a Cold mod, producing Viral status procs on each pellet hit. Viral halves the enemy's effective health, and with the Kohm's pellet count the Viral status is applied almost instantly.

## Recommended mod setup

- Primed Point Blank for base damage
- Hell's Chamber for multishot (increases pellets per shot)
- Primed Charged Shell for additional Heat or Electric to combine with Toxin
- Contagious Spread (Toxin augment) for additional Toxin effectiveness
- Blaze for Heat + damage combo
- Vigilante Supplies for ammo recovery

## Required Requiem mods

The Requiem pattern listed is one example from a specific Lich spawn. Each new Lich generates a new randomized three-mod sequence. Use the murmur system to identify the correct combination for each Lich. Keep all eight Requiem mods in stock before spawning a new Lich.

## Lore

The Kohm is a Grineer industrial weapon repurposed from mining extraction equipment. Its rotating barrel assembly was originally designed to disperse chemical solvents in enclosed excavation tunnels. Kuva-infused variants retain this spray-fire mechanic but replace the solvent compound with Kuva-saturated munitions.

A Toxin-element Kuva Kohm Lich likely rose from the ranks of Grineer hazardous materials specialists or extraction team leaders -- soldiers who spent their pre-Lich careers working with corrosive compounds. The Kuva infusion amplified their natural affinity for chemical warfare, producing a Lich whose preferred weapon channels their professional expertise into lethal form.

The Kohm family of weapons predates the Kuva Lich system and has existed in Warframe's arsenal since Update 15. The Kuva variant was introduced in Update 26 alongside the Lich system, and the Toxin-element version quickly became a popular farming target for players building toward high-efficiency Corpus content.
