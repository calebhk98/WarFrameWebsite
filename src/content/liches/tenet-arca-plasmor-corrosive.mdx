## Overview

The Tenet Arca Plasmor is the Sisters of Parvos variant of the Arca Plasmor, a Corpus primary shotgun that fires a wide plasma disc. The disc travels horizontally, striking all enemies in its path simultaneously. The Tenet variant improves upon the base Arca Plasmor with higher critical chance, status chance, and base damage.

Corrosive damage (Electric + Toxin combined element) strips Ferrite Armor, the most common armor type on Grineer enemies. With an elemental bonus that contributes toward Corrosive, the Tenet Arca Plasmor can strip armor from large groups simultaneously as the plasma disc passes through them. Because the disc hits every enemy in its horizontal path, a single shot can apply Corrosive procs to an entire row of Grineer units.

Note: the schema element field for this entry uses "toxin" as the component element toward the Corrosive combination (Electric + Toxin). Players who add an Electric mod alongside the Toxin Sister bonus on the weapon achieve a Corrosive damage output.

## Acquisition

The Tenet Arca Plasmor Sister spawns via the same Granum Void challenge system as all Sisters of Parvos. Use a Toxin-affiliated Warframe (Saryn or Nidus) to increase the probability of a Toxin-element Candidate, then combine with an Electric mod on the weapon to create Corrosive. Alternatively, use an Electric-affiliated Warframe and add a Toxin mod.

### Disc mechanics for farming

The Arca Plasmor disc hits all enemies in a wide horizontal cone. During murmur farming in Corpus missions, the Plasmor's disc can clear groups of Thralls in a single shot, rapidly filling the murmur bar. In Corpus Survival missions, the weapon's wide clearing capability makes it one of the most efficient Thrall-killing tools.

### Sister lair in Veil Proxima

The Sister Lair mission is accessed via the Veil Proxima. Railjack skills are not strictly required to board and complete the Lair -- the railjack portion is minimal, and a good crew or a solo run with Archwing can navigate to the boarding point efficiently. The confrontation within the Lair follows standard Parazon mechanics.

## Weapon performance

The Tenet Arca Plasmor fires a plasma disc that travels roughly fifteen meters and strikes all enemies in a wide horizontal band. It functions as a crowd-clearing primary shotgun. The disc does not penetrate obstacles but strikes every enemy in the open horizontal area.

With a Corrosive element path (Toxin from Sister bonus + Electric from mod):

- Every enemy struck by the disc receives a Corrosive proc, reducing Ferrite Armor by 26% per proc (stacking up to ten times for 80% total armor reduction)
- Wide horizontal strikes against lined-up Grineer units apply Corrosive to the entire formation simultaneously
- After sufficient Corrosive stacks reduce target armor, subsequent disc hits deal significantly higher effective damage

The disc's wide spread means it can hit targets partially obscured by cover at the edges of the horizontal arc. Shotgun mods apply to the Arca Plasmor, making Primed Point Blank, Hell's Chamber, and similar mods relevant.

## Recommended mod setup

- Primed Point Blank for base damage
- Hell's Chamber for multishot (more disc damage instances per fire)
- Seeking Fury for partial punch-through and reload speed
- Vigilante Armaments for additional projectile count
- Charged Rounds (Electric) to combine with Toxin Sister bonus for Corrosive
- Toxic Barrage (Toxin shotgun mod) as an alternative if the Sister bonus is Electric instead

## Required Requiem mods

The Requiem pattern shown is one example. Each Sister has a unique randomized Requiem sequence. Collect all eight Requiem mods before starting each Sister cycle.

## Lore

The Arca Plasmor's plasma disc technology was a joint development between Corpus weapon manufacturers and Corpus science division researchers studying Orokin plasma weaponry. The disc design was inspired by Sentient energy patterns observed during early Old War archaeology. Parvos Granum, an admirer of Orokin aesthetics alongside his distaste for Orokin governance, approved the Arca Plasmor as a Sister weapon for its visual similarity to Orokin-era energy technology.

A Corrosive-affinity Tenet Arca Plasmor Sister is associated with Corpus industrial operations where corrosive chemical processes were standard -- smelting, materials refinement, hazardous waste processing. These operators developed a resistance to corrosive environments and, through Parvos Granum's Granum Void trials, transformed that chemical affinity into an offensive capability channeled through the Arca Plasmor's plasma field.
