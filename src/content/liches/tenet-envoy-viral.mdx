## Overview

The Tenet Envoy is a Sisters of Parvos rocket launcher that fires guided missiles. Unlike conventional rocket launchers, the Envoy's rockets track targets after launch, curving to hit moving enemies. A Viral elemental bonus on the Tenet Envoy makes every explosive impact apply Viral status, halving enemy health. This combination of area-of-effect rocket damage and Viral health reduction makes the Envoy one of the most effective Sisters weapons for general content.

Viral damage is created by combining Cold and Toxin elements. On the Sisters of Parvos weapon system, a Sister can carry a weapon with an innate Viral bonus (a combined element), meaning both the Cold and Toxin damage components are baked in without using mod slots. This frees two mod slots that would otherwise be spent on elemental damage mods.

The guided missile mechanic of the Tenet Envoy reduces the precision requirement compared to traditional rocket launchers. Players can fire rockets in the general direction of enemy groups and rely on the tracking to land hits, maintaining mobility during combat.

## Acquisition

Viral element is a combined element (Cold + Toxin). For a Viral-element Candidate spawn, the Warframe used should be associated with a combined element. Certain frames that deal combined elemental damage through their abilities may be associated with Viral. Because Viral is a derived element, spawning a Viral-element Sister is less straightforward than primary elements -- the Candidate's element pool may include combined elements under specific conditions.

If direct Viral spawning is unreliable, target a Cold or Toxin element Sister carrying the Tenet Envoy, then combine via mods on the weapon to achieve the desired damage profile.

### Granum Void challenge

Use Gold Granum Crowns for the highest percentage floor on the Sister weapon. Gold Crowns drop from Corpus Treasurers in the Veil Proxima. Running Corpus Proxima Exterminate missions with a group increases the Treasurer spawn rate, allowing more Crown collection per session.

### Trading for the Tenet Envoy

The Tenet Envoy with a specific element and high percentage is a sought trade item. Converted Sisters can be traded through the Crimson Branch. If farming the exact combination proves inefficient, check Warframe's trading community for players offering high-percentage Viral Tenet Envoys.

## Weapon performance

The Tenet Envoy fires guided rockets with significant area of effect. Each rocket applies the weapon's elemental bonus and status chance to all targets in the blast radius. With Viral element:

- Every enemy in the blast receives a Viral proc, halving their effective health for the proc duration (six seconds, stackable to ten Viral stacks for a maximum 95% health reduction)
- Multiple targets in the blast receive Viral simultaneously, spreading the debuff across a group with a single shot
- The guided tracking means the player can fire rockets from behind cover or while airborne, maintaining mobility

Viral is the highest-value status effect in general content due to its universal applicability -- every enemy type has a health pool, and Viral halves that pool. Combining Viral from the Tenet Envoy with a Warframe ability that applies Heat or Corrosive creates a layered damage stack that trivializes high-health endgame targets.

Self-damage from the Tenet Envoy rockets is mitigated by the tracking mechanic -- rockets curve away from walls and obstacles before impacting the target. However, firing at close range or in enclosed spaces can still result in self-damage.

## Recommended mod setup

The Viral element from the Sister bonus frees mod slots typically used for Cold and Toxin mods:

- Serration and Split Chamber for damage foundation
- Heavy Caliber for significant damage increase at accuracy cost (less relevant for a tracking weapon)
- Firestorm or Primed Firestorm for blast radius increase
- Sinister Reach for rocket tracking range extension
- Point Strike and Vital Sense if the Envoy has a critical damage path worth investing in

## Lore

The Envoy represents a uniquely Corpus approach to the rocket launcher: rather than building a weapon for soldiers who aim well under pressure, Corpus engineers created a weapon that compensates for poor aim through tracking technology. This reflects Parvos Granum's business philosophy -- work smarter, not harder, and use technology to remove the need for exceptional skill. The Tenet variant is a premium version of this philosophy, given to Sisters who demonstrated that they could leverage resources effectively rather than relying on brute strength.

A Viral-affinity Tenet Envoy Sister is narratively associated with biological warfare research. The Virus metaphor -- an infection that spreads from one target to the next, halving their functional capacity -- matches the Corpus biological division's approach to enemy neutralization. Parvos approved the Viral affinity for Envoy-carrying Sisters because the guided rocket's "hunt and infect" behavior mirrors how a viral agent operates in nature.
