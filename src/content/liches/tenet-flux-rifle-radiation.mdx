## Overview

The Tenet Flux Rifle is the Sisters of Parvos variant of the Flux Rifle, a Corpus continuous-beam primary weapon. The Tenet variant significantly improves the base weapon's critical chance and status chance while maintaining the continuous beam mechanic. A Radiation elemental bonus on the Tenet Flux Rifle creates a sustained-beam weapon that applies Radiation procs continuously, causing enemies to attack each other for the beam's duration.

The Flux Rifle's beam mechanic allows for 100% status application on the target: every tick of the beam has an independent status chance roll. With the Tenet variant's enhanced status chance and a Radiation Lich bonus, the weapon can have virtually guaranteed Radiation procs on any target held in the beam for more than a fraction of a second.

Radiation damage is particularly effective against Alloy Armor, which is common on Grineer and Corpus heavy units. Radiation procs cause enemies to temporarily target and attack their allies, providing both crowd control and damage reduction from friendly fire.

## Acquisition

### Granum Void for Radiation Sister

The Candidate's weapon and element are determined at spawn. For a Radiation-element Tenet Flux Rifle specifically, use a Radiation-affiliated Warframe (Chroma) when the Candidate spawns. However, since Candidate weapon type is separate from element, both the correct weapon and the correct element must align -- a low-probability combination that may require many Granum Void runs.

An alternative strategy: accept any Radiation-element Sister of Parvos weapon, complete the Lich cycle, and trade the converted Sister at the Crimson Branch for a Tenet Flux Rifle from another player. Converted Sisters can be traded like Kuva Liches, making it possible to obtain specific weapon-element combinations without farming them directly.

### Murmur progress in Corpus missions

Sister murmur farming takes place in Corpus Proxima railjack missions and Corpus ground missions in claimed territory. Corpus missions in the Veil Proxima are particularly efficient as they spawn multiple Sister Thralls per run. The Tenet Flux Rifle's beam nature makes it efficient for clearing Corpus Crewship boarding parties during Railjack missions, accelerating both murmur progress and mission completion.

### Confronting the Sister

The Sister Lair is a Corpus Proxima mission. Upon entering, navigate through the Corpus station, defeat the accompanying Hound, and confront the Sister with the correct Requiem sequence. If the sequence is wrong, the Sister retreats and gains power. A correctly executed sequence allows vanquish or conversion.

## Weapon performance

The Tenet Flux Rifle fires a continuous laser beam that deals damage per second at a high tick rate. Each tick applies the weapon's status chance independently, and with the Radiation bonus and potential mod investment, the status proc rate on a stationary or slow-moving target is extremely high.

For endurance missions where enemies remain in the beam for extended periods, the Radiation proc's crowd-control effect compounds: multiple enemies within the beam's range receive Radiation procs simultaneously, turning the entire group on each other. Combined with a good armor-stripping Warframe ability, the Tenet Flux Rifle clears heavy-unit groups without requiring multiple high-damage shots.

Weaknesses include beam falloff at extreme range and the necessity to maintain line of sight. The Flux Rifle is less useful in missions with abundant cover and more useful in open corridors and arenas.

Mods that benefit the beam:

- Combustion Beam (augment mod for Flux Rifle) adds a Heat explosion on status proc, creating a Radiation + Heat combo that produces Gas or additional Blast with Cold
- Ruinous Extension increases beam range, maintaining full damage at longer distances
- Sinister Reach for further range increase

## Recommended mod setup

Status-focused build exploiting the Radiation proc mechanic:

- Galvanized Aptitude for bonus damage per status type on the target
- Vigilante Fervor for increased fire rate (beam damage tick rate)
- Infected Clip (Toxin) plus Heavy Caliber for raw damage increase
- Serration for base damage
- Hunter Munitions on a version that can apply Slash (check WFCD for Flux Rifle damage type composition)

## Required Requiem mods

The Requiem pattern shown is one example. Each Sister has a unique pattern. Farm Requiem Relics via Kuva Siphon and Flood missions; crack them in Requiem Fissures. Stock all eight Requiem mods before each Sister cycle.

## Lore

The Flux Rifle's design is considered a masterwork of Corpus engineering -- a focused-beam weapon capable of maintaining a coherent laser through Void-disrupted environments. Parvos Granum commissioned the Tenet variant as a symbol of technological supremacy over the Grineer's brute-force approach. A Radiation-affinity Tenet Flux Rifle Sister represents a scientist-warrior archetype: precise, calculating, and capable of turning the environment itself into a weapon through the confusion Radiation procs create.

Sisters of Parvos with Radiation affinities are described in the game's lore as those who mastered the Granum Void's disorienting properties, learning to channel that disorientation into their combat methodology. The Flux Rifle became their preferred tool because its precision beam matches their philosophy of targeted disruption rather than mass destruction.
