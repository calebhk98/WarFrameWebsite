## Overview

The Tenet Detron is the Sisters of Parvos variant of the Detron, a Corpus secondary shotgun. The base Detron is a fan-spread pistol shotgun that fires a wide pattern of pellets, and the Tenet variant enhances its critical chance, status chance, and base damage significantly. An elemental bonus from the Sister system adds a fifth element to the weapon. This entry covers the Heat-element variant; combining the Heat bonus with a Cold mod creates the Blast combined element, which provides knockdown utility against grouped enemies.

Blast damage (Heat + Cold) is not a source element in the Sister system -- Sisters roll one of eight source elements (Heat, Cold, Electric, Toxin, Impact, Magnetic, Radiation, Viral). A Heat-element Tenet Detron is the preferred starting point for a Blast build because adding a Cold mod results in Blast on the final weapon.

The Tenet Detron's close-range shotgun pattern is most effective in corridors and tight spaces where all pellets land on the target. Secondary shotguns do not benefit from the Shotgun mod category -- they use Pistol mods, which generally have higher scaling potential.

## Acquisition

### Granum Void entry

To spawn a Candidate for the Tenet Detron, complete The Deadlock Protocol quest to unlock Treasurer enemy spawns in Corpus missions. Defeat a Treasurer to obtain a Granum Crown. Use the Crown at a Granum Void portal (found in Corpus ship missions) to enter the Granum Void. Defeat enemies within the time limit to complete the challenge and trigger the Candidate spawn.

The Candidate's weapon is randomized at spawn. The Tenet Detron is one of several possible Sister weapons. If the wrong weapon appears, the player can abandon the resulting Sister and respawn. Abandoning a Sister without engaging it allows a new Candidate to spawn on the next Granum Void run.

### Candidate weapon confirmation

When a Candidate appears, a prompt shows the weapon it will carry. This is viewable before committing to the Mercy kill that triggers the Sister creation. If the displayed weapon is not the Tenet Detron, leave the mission (aborting the mission, not extracting -- aborting forfeits the candidate) and retry.

### Sister lair access

Once murmurs identify all three Requiem slots, the Sister Lair is accessed via the Veil Proxima navigation screen. Unlike Kuva Lich lairs on the Kuva Fortress, Sister Lairs appear as Corpus Proxima railjack missions. The player must fly through the Proxima, board the station, defeat the Sister's Hound, and then confront the Sister with the correct Parazon sequence.

## Weapon performance

The Tenet Detron fires a burst of pellets in a wide fan pattern. At point-blank range, a high proportion of pellets land on target. The Tenet variant's improved status chance means that even a partial pellet hit applies multiple status effects.

With a Blast element (created via Heat + Cold mods on the weapon if the Sister provides a different base element, or if the Sister element itself is Heat, by adding Cold via a mod), the Detron's pellet fan applies Blast procs that knock enemies down. Blast knockdown combined with the shotgun pattern of the Detron creates a stagger loop: enemies knocked down stay on the ground while the player follows up with another burst.

Secondary shotguns use Pistol mods. Key mods for the Detron:

- Barrel Diffusion for multishot
- Lethal Torrent for fire rate and multishot
- Primed Pistol Gambit for critical chance
- Primed Target Cracker for critical multiplier
- Augur Pact for base damage

## Recommended mod setup

Blast damage combines Heat and Cold elements. To achieve a Blast final damage type:

- Add Heated Charge (Heat) and Ice Storm (Cold) -- these combine on the weapon to create Blast
- Stack remaining slots with critical mods and a damage buff mod
- Arcane Deadhead amplifies headshot damage, synergizing with the point-blank accuracy required for all-pellet contact

## Required Requiem mods

The Requiem pattern shown is one example. Each Sister generates a unique pattern. The murmur farming process is identical to the Kuva Lich system. All eight Requiem mods are shared between both systems.

## Lore

The Detron's design traces to the early Corpus expansion era when personal defense weapons needed to function in zero-gravity environments aboard trade vessels. The wide-pattern pellet spread of the Detron was ideal for shipboard corridors where precise aiming was difficult. Parvos Granum, as the Corpus patriarch whose ghost inhabits the Granum Void, approved the Tenet Detron as a Sister weapon because of its practical elegance -- a weapon that functions reliably without requiring perfect aim reflects the Corpus principle that effective tools outlast flashy ones.

Sisters who carry the Tenet Detron are typically those who proved themselves in close-quarters combat trials within the Granum Void, where the pocket dimension's compressed geometry rewarded weapons like the Detron that covered wide angles with minimal precision input.
