## Overview

The Tenet Cycron is the Sisters of Parvos variant of the Cycron, a Corpus secondary beam weapon. The Cycron is unique in that it has a self-recharging energy cell rather than ammunition -- it regenerates ammo passively when not firing. The Tenet variant enhances the base Cycron's already strong critical and status profiles, and a Magnetic elemental bonus makes it one of the best secondary weapons for Corpus content.

Magnetic damage is the premier anti-shield damage type. Against Corpus shields, Magnetic deals bonus damage and applies Magnetic procs that temporarily reduce the maximum shield pool. A sustained Tenet Cycron beam at 60% Magnetic bonus strips Corpus shields rapidly, often outpacing the shield regeneration of Nullifiers, Corpus Techs, and other high-shield enemies.

The self-recharging ammo mechanic means the Tenet Cycron never runs dry -- players with a trigger discipline habit of short-burst fire and brief recharge pauses can maintain the weapon indefinitely without requiring an ammo carrier.

## Acquisition

Spawn a Magnetic-element Candidate using Mag equipped during the Granum Void challenge. Since both weapon type and element are random, multiple runs may be needed. The Tenet Cycron is a highly sought Sister weapon, and players frequently trade converted Sisters carrying it through the Crimson Branch relay system.

### Granum Void efficiency

Completing the Granum Void at higher ranks (using Gold Granum Crowns) improves the percentage floor of the resulting Sister weapon. Gold Crowns drop from Corpus Treasurers on Corpus Proxima missions. Organizing a group for Corpus Proxima Exterminate missions allows multiple Treasurer kills per run, generating Crowns efficiently.

### Murmur farming for the Cycron

Sister Thralls in Corpus missions drop murmurs for the Tenet Cycron's carrier Sister identically to the Kuva Lich murmur system. Run the Sister's claimed territory missions until all three Requiem slots are identified. The Cycron's self-recharge mechanic makes it useful for sustained firing during thrall clears, maintaining combat without ammunition pressure.

## Weapon performance

The Tenet Cycron's self-recharge means a full reload requires stopping fire for approximately three seconds. High damage-per-second builds can deplete the energy pool quickly, but burst fire discipline (firing for two seconds, pausing for one) allows near-continuous engagement without running dry.

With Magnetic element bonus:

- Corpus enemies lose shields faster per beam tick than with any other single element
- Proto Shield enemies (specific Corpus types with physical-damage resistance) are particularly vulnerable to Magnetic
- The Magnetic proc's shield reduction stacks, so sustained fire permanently reduces a target's maximum shields until death

For combining Magnetic with additional elements via mods:

- Adding Heat creates a Magnetic + Heat combination (no combined element -- these remain as two separate elements)
- Adding Toxin bypasses shields and deals directly to health, creating a two-damage-path weapon
- The beam applies status chance on every tick, making the Cycron excellent for building Galvanized Aptitude stacks

## Recommended mod setup

Prioritize status application and secondary beam range:

- Barrel Diffusion and Lethal Torrent for multishot (more beam bounces)
- Primed Pistol Gambit and Primed Target Cracker for critical path
- Galvanized Aptitude for stacking damage bonus with each unique status applied
- Seeker to allow beams to hit targets through cover (partial punch-through for Cycron's chain arc)
- Arcane Momentum to reduce secondary fire drain and improve recharge efficiency

## Required Requiem mods

Requiem patterns are randomized per Sister spawn. Farm Requiem Relics via Kuva missions and crack them in Requiem Fissures to build the complete Requiem mod set.

## Lore

The Cycron's self-contained energy cell was a Corpus breakthrough in compact power technology -- the ability to regenerate its own power supply made it ideal for Corpus operatives working in environments where resupply was impossible, such as the Granum Void itself. Parvos Granum favored the Cycron's self-sufficiency as a metaphor for the Corpus ideal: a weapon that never needed outside support, just as the greatest Corpus warriors rely only on themselves.

A Tenet Cycron Sister with Magnetic affinity is characterized as a Corpus weapons researcher who spent extended time in the Granum Void studying its electromagnetic properties. Extended Granum Void exposure is described as disorienting for most; those who emerged stable and stronger -- particularly those who developed an affinity for Magnetic resonance from the Void's electromagnetic environment -- were selected by Parvos as Sisters, bearing the Tenet Cycron as a symbol of their unique adaptation.
