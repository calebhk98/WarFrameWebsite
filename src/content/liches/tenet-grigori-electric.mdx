## Overview

The Tenet Grigori is the Sisters of Parvos variant of the Grigori, a scythe-class melee weapon. Scythes are a wide-swing melee archetype with long reach and horizontal sweep attacks, making them naturally effective in groups. The Tenet variant adds Corpus-enhanced critical and status properties, and an Electric elemental bonus turns the scythe's broad strikes into chain-lightning attacks that arc between nearby enemies.

Electric damage is effective against Corpus Robotic units and applies Electric procs that stagger enemies, interrupting their attack animations. The stagger effect from Electric procs is particularly valuable on a scythe, which requires the player to be in close melee range -- a staggered enemy cannot counter-attack during the proc duration.

Electric combined with Toxin (via mod) creates Magnetic on the Tenet Grigori, making it an anti-shield melee weapon. For Corpus assassination missions, the Tenet Grigori with Magnetic from Electric + Toxin pairing can strip shields from Corpus bosses rapidly through the scythe's wide sweeping attacks.

## Acquisition

Equip Volt to maximize Electric Candidate probability. Run Corpus missions after The Deadlock Protocol to trigger Treasurer spawns, defeat Treasurers for Granum Crowns, and complete Granum Void challenges to spawn Candidates. Confirm the Tenet Grigori as the weapon and Electric as the element before performing the Mercy kill.

### Scythe mechanics in farming

Scythes have a wide horizontal sweep stance, making them highly effective for clearing groups of Sister Thralls. The reach of the Tenet Grigori allows hitting multiple thralls in a single swing, accelerating murmur accumulation per swing compared to single-target melee weapons.

### Corpus Proxima missions for murmur efficiency

Sister murmur farming in the Corpus Proxima via railjack missions spawns Thralls more densely per mission than standard Corpus ground missions. The scythe's sweep range makes it ideal for quickly clearing the Thrall-dense areas aboard Corpus capital ships during these missions.

## Weapon performance

The Tenet Grigori's scythe archetype delivers wide, horizontal sweeping attacks with significant range. In open combat areas, a single heavy attack can strike four or more enemies simultaneously. The Electric element bonus arcs between targets on critical hits or status procs, applying the Electric stagger to secondary targets not directly struck.

For critical builds:

- Scythes benefit from Blood Rush (critical chance scales with combo counter)
- Maiming Strike can provide high critical chance for the horizontal swing attacks that scythes specialize in
- Weeping Wounds increases status chance with combo counter, multiplying Electric proc frequency

For Condition Overload scaling, applying multiple status types (Electric, plus Toxin from a mod, plus Viral from the combination) provides three unique status contributions, tripling the Condition Overload damage multiplier from a single attack sequence.

Arcane Fury triggers on melee hits to provide stacking damage boost per kill, synergizing with the scythe's multi-target sweeps in group combat.

## Recommended mod setup

Status-focused endgame scythe build:

- Primed Fever Strike (Toxin) to combine with Electric for Magnetic, or combine with Cold (separate mod) for multiple status paths
- Shocking Touch (Electric augment or base Electric mod) to boost Electric damage alongside Lich bonus
- Drifting Contact for 12-second combo decay instead of 5
- Blood Rush for escalating critical chance with combo counter
- Weeping Wounds for escalating status chance with combo counter
- Condition Overload for damage scaling per unique status on target
- Gladiator Might for bonus critical multiplier

## Required Requiem mods

The Requiem pattern shown is one example. Each Sister has a unique randomized Requiem sequence. Collect and maintain all eight Requiem mods before beginning each Sister cycle. The same Requiem mods (Ris, Lohk, Fass, Vome, Netra, Khra, Jahu, Xata) are used for both Kuva Liches and Sisters of Parvos.

## Lore

The Grigori -- named after a class of angelic watchers in ancient religious texts -- carries a symbolic weight appropriate for a weapon designed for a warrior who serves a god-like figure. Parvos Granum, who survived death by anchoring his consciousness to the Granum Void and now watches over his chosen Sisters from that realm, fits the watcher archetype that the Grigori's name invokes. The scythe's design, associated with harvest and judgment, reflects Parvos's philosophy that resources -- and enemies -- exist to be claimed.

A Tenet Grigori Sister with Electric affinity typically comes from Corpus power grid operations or electrical engineering divisions. These operatives worked with high-voltage systems regularly, and Parvos's Granum Void trial converted their familiarity with electrical hazards into an offensive channeling ability. Their scythe strikes leave crackling arcs of electricity in the air, and the broad sweeps of the scythe deliver that electrical charge across the widest possible target area.

The Tenet Grigori was introduced alongside the Sisters of Parvos system in Update 30.5, providing melee players a dedicated Corpus-faction endgame weapon that rewards the aggressive group-combat style that scythes excel in.
