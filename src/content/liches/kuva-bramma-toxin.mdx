## Overview

The Kuva Bramma with a Toxin elemental bonus provides one of the most effective damage paths for content where enemies have high shields. Toxin damage bypasses Corpus shields entirely, dealing directly to the health pool beneath. With a 25%-60% Toxin bonus added to the Kuva Bramma's already powerful cluster-bomb payload, this variant excels against Corpus factions and in sustained endurance missions.

To spawn a Toxin-element Larvling, use a Toxin-affiliated Warframe such as Saryn or Nidus when entering any level 20+ Grineer mission node post-The War Within quest. The Larvling's element matches the Warframe's associated element, and a mushroom or toxin droplet icon confirms the Toxin element before committing to the Mercy kill.

Toxin on the Kuva Bramma is also a popular choice for players who want to mod toward Gas. Adding a Heat mod to a Toxin-element Bramma creates Gas damage, which procs a cloud of Gas status around the affected target, dealing Toxin damage to nearby enemies in a radius. This is particularly effective in dense rooms on Corpus maps and Infested missions.

## Acquisition

### Identifying and spawning the Larvling

Navigate to any level 20+ Grineer mission (examples: Cassini on Saturn, Adaro on Sedna). Equip Saryn or Nidus to maximize the chance of a Toxin Larvling. Once enemies appear and a Kuva siphon beam passes through the area, kill several enemies to trigger the Larvling spawn. When the Larvling appears, hover over it before performing the Mercy kill to confirm the Toxin element symbol.

### Murmur progress

After converting the Larvling to a Lich, enter missions in the Lich's occupied territory to kill thralls. Each thrall killed advances murmur progress. When a murmur bar fills for a given Requiem slot, the correct mod for that slot is revealed. Continue farming until all three slots are identified. The process typically requires 50-100 thrall kills per slot, varying by luck and group size.

Teaming with other players who also have active Liches allows shared thrall kills, accelerating murmur progress. Liches from different players can spawn in the same mission, creating a more efficient farming session.

### Requiem mod acquisition

Requiem mods are obtained by running Kuva Siphon missions (appearing on the star chart as alerts with a Kuva icon) and Kuva Flood missions (higher-level, more rewarding variant). These missions reward Requiem Relics. The relics are then opened in Requiem Fissure missions -- specific missions that appear on the star chart with a Fissure modifier and allow Requiem Relic cracking.

The eight Requiem mods are: Ris, Lohk, Fass, Vome, Netra, Khra, Jahu, Xata. Each mod has three charges. Using a mod in a Parazon attempt consumes one charge; a destroyed mod must be re-acquired.

### Finalizing the encounter

With all three Requiem mods identified and placed in the correct Parazon slots, confront the Lich in its lair (accessible via the Kuva Fortress navigation node or by waiting for a direct Lich confrontation mission). The Lich must be downed three times with the correct sequence to trigger the final kill prompt. On success, choose vanquish (receive the weapon) or convert (gain a recruitable ally).

## Weapon performance

The Toxin Kuva Bramma pairs exceptionally well against Corpus targets, where the innate Toxin bonus bypasses shield calculations and deals full damage to health. The cluster-bomb explosion pattern ensures that multiple bomblets hit the same enemy, multiplying the Toxin procs applied per shot.

Against Grineer, Toxin is less favorable because Grineer health types (Ferrite Armor, Alloy Armor, Cloned Flesh) do not benefit from Toxin's bypass mechanic in the same way. For Grineer content, a Heat or Radiation bonus would be more optimal.

For general use, a Toxin Bramma running a Heat mod creates Gas damage, which can one-shot cluster groups of lighter enemies in mid-game content.

## Recommended mod setup

Pair this variant with Viral mods (Cold + Toxin) to apply Viral status, which halves enemy health and makes subsequent shots deal doubled effective damage. Combining the Toxin element from the Lich bonus with a Cold mod creates Viral natively. For maximum damage:

- Primed Point Blank or Point Strike for critical enhancement
- Firestorm (or Primed Firestorm) to expand the cluster bomblet detonation radius
- Terminal Velocity to reduce arrow flight time on the primary projectile
- Vigilante Fervor to improve draw speed

## Required Requiem mods

The Requiem pattern listed in frontmatter is one example; the actual pattern for any given Lich is randomized at spawn. Stockpile all eight Requiem mods before spawning a Lich to avoid mid-farm bottlenecks. Requiem mods can also be traded between players, making them accessible through Warframe's in-game trade system.

## Lore

The Toxin-element Kuva Lich represents Grineer warriors who underwent Kuva infusion while channeling alchemical compounds derived from Infestation research. The Grineer Queens have historically studied Infested biology for military applications, and some Lich candidates emerged from experimental programs testing Infested-derived mutagenic compounds.

These Liches display a greenish tint in their Kuva energy manifestations and are known for favoring biological warfare tactics before their transformation. Their personal weapon of choice -- the Kuva Bramma -- serves as a delivery system for toxin-laced cluster munitions that exploit the armor gaps in Corpus and lightly-armored Grineer units.

The Old Blood update that introduced Kuva Liches drew narrative inspiration from the Kuva Guardians encountered in The War Within, establishing continuity between the Orokin-era Queens' power and the modern Grineer's attempts to replicate that immortality through stolen Void science.
