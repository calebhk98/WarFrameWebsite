## Overview

The Kuva Hek is a powerful Grineer shotgun modeled on the base Hek's design but significantly enhanced through Kuva infusion. Its high damage per shot and tight spread make it one of the more precise shotguns in the Kuva weapon family. A Magnetic elemental bonus adds direct shield damage and Magnetic procs, which reduce an enemy's shield capacity -- a devastating combination against Corpus heavy units with regenerating shields.

Magnetic damage is the strongest damage type against Corpus shields, dealing bonus damage to Proto Shields and triggering Magnetic procs that temporarily reduce the maximum shield pool. With a 60% Magnetic bonus on the Kuva Hek, a single shotgun blast against a Corpus Tech or Nullifier can deplete a substantial portion of their shields in one trigger pull.

To spawn a Magnetic Larvling, Mag is the most consistent option. Its innate Magnetic damage theme carries over to the Larvling element selection.

## Acquisition

Equip Mag and run level 20+ Grineer nodes. Identify the Larvling's Magnetic element via the magnet/iron filing icon before performing the Mercy kill. The Kuva Hek is a notable Kuva weapon drop -- it retains the base Hek's reputation as a powerful shotgun with even higher base damage in Kuva form.

### Notes on Magnetic element viability

Magnetic is considered a niche element because its primary use case (Corpus shields) is already handled by Toxin's bypass mechanic in many builds. However, the Kuva Hek's Magnetic bonus is valued for:

1. Players who run organized Corpus assassination missions where shield management matters
2. Corpus Disruption missions where enemy conduits are guarded by Shield Ospreys and Nullifiers
3. General Corpus content where maximizing shield damage allows faster kills on full-shield Corpus Techs

For content outside Corpus-heavy missions, the Magnetic Kuva Hek can still be used effectively by relying on its raw pellet damage rather than the element bonus.

### Murmur farming

Run Corpus-adjacent Grineer missions (Grineer missions in Corpus-border regions sometimes feature mixed spawns, accelerating learning). Standard murmur farming applies: kill thralls in claimed territories until all three Requiem slots are identified. Cassini on Saturn and Hydron on Sedna are reliable high-density thrall spawn locations.

## Weapon performance

The Kuva Hek fires a concentrated spread with high per-pellet damage. Unlike the Kuva Kohm (which widens its spread over time), the Hek maintains a tight spread that rewards accurate aiming and optimal engagement distance. At close-to-mid range, all pellets land on target, making each shot devastating.

Magnetic procs from the Lich bonus reduce the target's shield maximum. Against Corpus Bosses with large shield regeneration pools, applying multiple Magnetic procs before the kill attempt prevents the shield from recovering between shots. The Hek's fire rate supports rapid Magnetic proc stacking in burst fire.

For critical builds:

- Primed Point Blank for base damage increase
- Hell's Chamber for multishot (doubles effective pellets)
- Seeking Fury for punch-through and reload speed
- Full Contact for increased Magnetic pellet count contribution
- Targeting Subsystem for additional Magnetic-element enhancement (if riven or augment provides)

## Recommended mod setup

The tight-spread design of the Kuva Hek rewards a critical-damage focus:

- Vigilante Supplies for ammo return
- Blaze for Heat bonus (Heat + Magnetic = none, use for raw damage instead)
- Charged Shell (Electric) -- Electric + Magnetic combines into ... avoid overlap; use Virulent Scourge (Toxin) instead for Viral via Toxin + Cold
- Build around raw pellet crit rather than element combination for non-Corpus content

## Required Requiem mods

The Requiem sequence in frontmatter is one possible example per spawn. Patterns are randomized. Stock all Requiem mods in advance and use the murmur system for each new Lich.

## Lore

The Hek family of shotguns is named after Councilor Vay Hek, a Grineer political official and military commander who appears as a boss on Earth. The Kuva Hek was presumably designed or commissioned to honor Vay Hek's role in the Queens' court, reflecting the Grineer tradition of naming powerful weapons after loyal commanders.

A Magnetic-affinity Kuva Hek Lich is narratively unusual -- Magnetic resonance is more closely associated with Corpus technology than Grineer metallurgy. These Liches are theorized to have undergone experimental Kuva infusion procedures that incorporated stolen Corpus magnetic-field research, reflecting the constant low-level technological espionage between the two factions. Their Kuva-infused form allows them to channel electromagnetic forces through the Hek's barrel, turning a blunt-force shotgun into a precision shield-stripping instrument.
