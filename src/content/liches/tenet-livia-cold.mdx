## Overview

The Tenet Livia is the Sisters of Parvos variant of the Livia, a two-handed nikana (large katana-style melee weapon). Tenet melee weapons follow the same element bonus system as ranged Tenet and Kuva weapons -- the Sister carries the weapon with a 25%-60% elemental bonus applied to the weapon's base damage. A Cold element bonus on the Tenet Livia adds Cold damage to every strike, applying Cold procs that slow enemy movement and attack speed.

The Tenet Livia is one of the few Tenet melee weapons available through the Sister system. Melee players who pursue the Sister system for the Livia are often looking for a high-percentage Cold bonus to build toward Viral damage through modding (adding Toxin to combine with Cold for Viral).

The Cold element's proc effect -- slowing enemies -- pairs well with heavy attack builds that require setup time between strikes. Slowed enemies cannot dodge or interrupt the player's heavy attack animation as easily.

## Acquisition

Equip Frost to maximize Cold Larvling chance. The Candidate spawning process is identical to all Sisters: defeat a Treasurer to get a Granum Crown, enter the Granum Void portal, complete the challenge, and the Candidate with the Tenet Livia will appear (if the RNG rolls a Livia carrier with Cold element).

### Melee weapon element percentage considerations

For melee builds, the element percentage matters differently than for guns. Melee weapons scale primarily through critical damage and condition overload mechanics rather than raw elemental damage. A 60% Cold Tenet Livia is still preferable to a 25% for direct damage, but the performance difference is smaller compared to a 60% vs 25% Kuva Nukor.

For Viral melee builds, the Cold element from the Tenet Livia combines with a Toxin mod to produce Viral damage on every hit. This makes even lower-percentage Cold Livias functional for the Viral build path.

### Hound companion from the Sister

Defeating the Tenet Livia Sister also yields the Sister's Hound imprint. The Hound can be converted into a companion, adding a Corpus robotic companion to the player's available roster. Hounds are mechanically similar to MOA companions with unique precept mods from the Sister system.

## Weapon performance

The Tenet Livia is a two-handed nikana, a weapon type known for wide, sweeping attacks that can hit multiple enemies in a horizontal arc. Two-handed nikanas have a unique stance that blends broad horizontal swings with thrusting stabs, making them versatile in dense combat scenarios.

With Cold element bonus:

- Each strike applies Cold procs on the target, slowing them by 30% movement and attack speed per proc (stacking)
- Cold + Toxin mod = Viral damage; combining the Livia's Cold element with a Toxin melee mod makes every swing apply Viral procs
- The Cold slow synergizes with heavy attack builds by reducing enemy counter-attack frequency during the heavy attack animation

For endgame melee builds, the Tenet Livia benefits from:

- Blood Rush for escalating critical chance with combo counter
- Weeping Wounds for escalating status chance with combo counter
- Condition Overload for damage scaling with unique status types applied to the target
- Primed Reach to extend the nikana's swing arc to a wider radius
- Corrupt Charge or a Gladiator mod for combo management

## Recommended mod setup

Focus on the Condition Overload + Viral proc loop:

- Primed Fever Strike (Toxin) to combine with Cold Lich bonus for Viral
- Volcanic Edge (Heat) or Shocking Touch (Electric) for additional status type diversity
- Drifting Contact for combo duration extension
- Amalgam Organ Shatter for heavy attack speed buff
- Sacrificial Steel and Sacrificial Pressure for Umbra-spec builds with additional crit chance

## Required Requiem mods

The Requiem pattern shown is one example. Each Sister has a unique randomized pattern. Stock all eight Requiem mods before beginning each Sister cycle.

## Lore

The Livia takes its name from a Corpus noblewoman who served as one of Parvos Granum's early financiers during his initial commercial expansion. The weapon's elegant, elongated design reflects the Corpus upper class's aesthetic sensibility -- a sword that communicates status alongside combat capability. Parvos selected the Livia as a Sister weapon to honor his historical financial backers and to attract Sisters from the Corpus noble caste who valued elegance.

A Cold-element Tenet Livia Sister is typically drawn from Corpus operations on cold planets -- Europa, Pluto, or Ceres -- where the Corpus maintain cryogenic processing facilities. These operatives spent years working in near-absolute-zero environments where precision and measured movements were essential. Parvos's Granum Void trial transformed their environmental composure into a weapon-borne Cold affinity that leaves frost patterns on surfaces struck by the Livia.
