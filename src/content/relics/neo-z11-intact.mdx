## Overview

Neo Z11 Intact is a Neo-era void relic representing high-value endgame fissure content. Neo relics appear in challenging void fissures (level 40-60) and reward rarer prime components compared to Lith and Meso tiers. Neo Z11 stands out for offering Zylok Prime Blueprint as its rare reward and multiple uncommon prime weapon components, including Acceltra Prime Blueprint and Bronco Prime Receiver. The relic also includes Forma Blueprint, a universal crafting component highly valued across all gameplay modes.

## Drops

Neo Z11 Intact distributes six distinct rewards spanning weapon blueprints, weapon parts, and utility blueprints. The rare-tier Zylok Prime Blueprint drops at 2% probability. Five uncommon-tier items each appear with specific probabilities: Okina Prime Handle and Shade Prime Blueprint at 11% each, followed by three 25.33% drops (Forma Blueprint, Bronco Prime Receiver, Acceltra Prime Blueprint). This mix balances weapon component farming with universal resources like Forma, benefiting diverse player goals within single relic runs.

## Refinement choices

Neo Z11 Intact baseline drop rates serve players farming weapon components without Forma urgency. Exceptional refinement (+10%) begins favoring rare drops; Flawless refinement (+20%) makes Zylok Prime Blueprint probability approach 4%. Radiant refinement (+30%) brings rare probability to approximately 5%, nearly tripling the intact rate. Players hunting Zylok Prime (a secondary weapon) should reserve void traces for Radiant refinement, as higher tier relics consume significantly more traces per refinement attempt.

## Where to farm

Neo void fissures activate on outer star chart planets including Void Cascade, Void Void, and end-game void tear locations. Popular farming nodes include Void Capture on Jupiter and Void Sabotage on Saturn, where 20-40 minute runs reward both Neo relics and void traces. Void Excavation on Void Cascade (endless) provides the highest relic drop concentration and supports multi-hour farming sessions with parties. Neo missions scale to level 50+ enemies; players require properly modded weapons and tanky frames for efficient solo completion. Void Storms (Railjack void fissure variant) also generate Neo relic rewards and provide alternative farming paths.
