## Overview

Requiem relics are a fundamentally different category from the standard Lith, Meso, Neo, and Axi tiers. Introduced with Update 26 and the Kuva Lich system, Requiem relics contain Requiem mods -- eight unique special mods (Fass, Vome, Ris, Lohk, Xata, Jahu, Netra, and Khra) that are exclusively used to permanently defeat or convert Kuva Liches and Sisters of Parvos.

Unlike standard relics, Requiem relics do not drop from Void Fissure missions by default. They are tied specifically to the Kuva Fortress tileset and the Kuva Siphon/Flood mission types. The Requiem system creates a dedicated grind loop separate from the standard void relic economy, requiring players to engage specifically with Kuva Lich content to progress.

Each Requiem relic is numbered I through VIII and named for the Orokin script characters displayed as glyphs on the Kuva Lich's Parazon sequence. When a player encounters a Kuva Lich for the first time, the Lich imprints three randomized Requiem mod requirements onto its Parazon, which the player must discover through trial-and-error stabs and then gather the correct Requiem relics to unlock the mods needed.

Requiem relics, like standard relics, must be cracked in a Void Fissure mission (specifically Requiem-tier fissures) to extract the contained mods. Each relic has three common slots for Forma Blueprints and three rare slots for the specific Requiem mod, following the same 2%/11%/25.33% drop weight distribution as standard relics.

## Drop Locations

**Kuva Siphon missions** are the primary source of Requiem relics. Kuva Siphon missions appear as special nodes on planets that have been taken over by a Kuva Lich (which includes most planets after the Lich generates). Completing a Kuva Siphon mission rewards one Requiem relic guaranteed. These missions involve destroying a Kuva Siphon drone as it harvests kuva from a geyser, requiring players to intercept the drone's spiral travel pattern.

**Kuva Flood missions** are the harder variant of Kuva Siphon, generating higher enemy levels (50-80+) and rewarding a guaranteed Requiem relic with bonus drop chances for higher-quality relics. Kuva Floods are the fastest way to accumulate Requiem relics for experienced players.

**Kuva Fortress missions** of any type can drop Requiem relics as mission completion rewards or from rare containers, though the rate is lower than dedicated Siphon and Flood missions.

**Thrall kills** during active Lich hunts contribute additional Requiem relic drops. Thralls are special Grineer enemies converted by the Kuva Lich that appear in any mission on planets the Lich controls. Killing thralls has a moderate chance to drop a Requiem relic or provide Parazon sequence information.

| Location | Mission Type | Reward |
|---|---|---|
| Any Kuva Lich-controlled planet | Kuva Siphon | 1 Requiem relic guaranteed |
| Any Kuva Lich-controlled planet | Kuva Flood | 1 Requiem relic guaranteed (higher quality) |
| Kuva Fortress | Any | Requiem relic (chance) |
| Active Lich territory | Thrall kills | Requiem relic (chance per kill) |

## Refinement Costs

Requiem relics use the identical void trace refinement system as standard relics:

| Refinement Level | Void Traces | Rare Mod Chance |
|---|---|---|
| Intact | 0 | ~2% for the Requiem mod |
| Exceptional | 25 | ~4% for the Requiem mod |
| Flawless | 50 | ~6% for the Requiem mod |
| Radiant | 100 | ~10% for the Requiem mod |

Requiem mods can be stacked up to 3 times before being exhausted. Each use of a Requiem mod on a Lich (successful or not) consumes one charge. A freshly obtained Requiem mod has 3 charges. Exhausted mods can be re-charged by fusing them with a duplicate using Transmutation.

Because Requiem mods must be applied in the correct sequence on the Kuva Lich's Parazon, and players must determine the sequence through trial-and-error (which consumes mod charges), maintaining a supply of all eight Requiem mods is important for efficient Lich hunting. Radiant refinement helps when specifically targeting a mod needed urgently to complete a sequence.

## Notable Requiem Mods and Their Uses

The eight Requiem mods form the vocabulary of the Kuva Lich vanquishing system. Each mod is displayed as an Orokin glyph in-game:

- **Fass (Requiem I)** -- Named for the Orokin day cycle on the Cambion Drift. Fass interacts synergistically with Infested and Void-based mechanics in narrative lore.
- **Vome (Requiem II)** -- Named for the Orokin night cycle. Vome and Fass represent opposing forces in the Cambion Drift ecosystem and appear frequently in the same Lich sequences.
- **Ris (Requiem III)** -- Associated with the Orokin concept of separation. Ris appears in roughly one-third of all randomly generated Lich Parazon sequences.
- **Lohk (Requiem IV)** -- An Orokin term related to void energy manipulation. Lohk-containing relics are among the most frequently traded Requiem relics on warframe.market.
- **Xata (Requiem V)** -- Connected to Orokin concepts of binding or connection. Xata is required for approximately the same frequency as other mods statistically.
- **Jahu (Requiem VI)** -- One of the more distinctive-looking Orokin glyphs, Jahu is associated with time or cycles in Orokin religious texts referenced in lore entries.
- **Netra (Requiem VII)** -- The Netra glyph appears on several Orokin architectural elements in the Void tileset, tying narrative lore to gameplay mechanics.
- **Khra (Requiem VIII)** -- Khra is the final Requiem mod and completes the set of eight. With all eight mods, players can attempt any Lich sequence combination through permutation testing.

## Strategy

**Efficient Kuva Siphon Routing:** Complete Kuva Siphon missions on planets with the lowest enemy level (Phobos or Sedna when under low-level Lich control) for fastest mission completion speed. A single siphon mission takes 3-5 minutes and guarantees one Requiem relic per run. Chain 10-15 siphon runs to build a stock of relics before cracking them in a batch during Requiem Void Fissure events.

**Kuva Flood Priority:** When Kuva Flood missions are available (they spawn periodically and are time-limited like void fissures), prioritize them over Siphon missions. Floods reward relics at a better quality rate despite the higher enemy difficulty. Bring a meta survivability frame (Nidus, Inaros, Revenant) for Flood missions to handle the level 50-80 Grineer spawns.

**Requiem Mod Stockpiling:** Maintain at least 3 charges of each of the 8 Requiem mods at all times. Having a full set means you can attempt any Lich sequence without waiting for specific relics to crack. Warframe.market trading is a viable shortcut -- Requiem mods with full charges are traded frequently and are modestly priced due to the Siphon farming availability.

**Sequence Determination Strategy:** Rather than burning all mod charges randomly, use a systematic approach: test positions 1, 2, and 3 in sequence. Each failed stab on the Lich reveals whether the correct mod was in that position (red X) or incorrect (no indicator beyond the Lich surviving). Narrowing down the sequence efficiently minimizes total mod charge consumption per Lich vanquished.

**Sister of Parvos Adaptation:** The Sister of Parvos system introduced in Update 30 uses the same Requiem mods as Kuva Liches (Tenet weapons use similar but distinct mod interactions). Players who farm both Liches and Sisters simultaneously can maintain a shared Requiem mod pool, reducing the total time investment for both content types.
