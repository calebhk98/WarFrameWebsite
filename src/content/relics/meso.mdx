## Overview

Meso relics represent the second tier of the void relic system and are named after the Greek prefix meaning "middle" -- fitting for their position between the entry-level Lith tier and the more advanced Neo and Axi tiers. Meso relics contain components for a broad range of Prime warframes and weapons, spanning releases from the early Void Era through several years of content updates.

Players engaging with Meso relics typically have progressed past the mid-point of the star chart and possess frames or weapons capable of handling level 20-40 enemies with efficiency. Meso relics are less numerous than Lith relics but still represent a significant portion of the total relic pool, with over 500 unique relics catalogued across multiple unvault cycles and new Prime releases.

The Meso tier is notable for containing components for some of the most universally recommended Prime warframes: Nova Prime (top-tier crowd control), Saryn Prime (area damage and nuke capacity), Equinox Prime (damage and healing hybrid), and Zephyr Prime (utility and flight). Weapons such as the Boltor Prime, Tigris Prime, and Rubico Prime -- all considered top-tier in their respective classes -- also appear in Meso relic drop tables.

## Drop Locations

The most efficient farming node for Meso relics is **Io Defense on Jupiter**. Io is a well-known farming destination that rewards a Meso relic on rotation B (every 20 waves) while also being extremely efficient for experience farming. The defense mission can be cleared rapidly with strong area-of-effect warframes -- Saryn, Mesa, or Equinox can solo or near-solo the waves for fast rotations. Organized squads can complete 40-wave sessions for two Meso relics while maximizing experience yield.

**Ukko Capture on the Void** provides a fast guaranteed Meso relic similarly to how Hepit provides Lith relics. The Capture mission takes 60-90 seconds for players with agile frames and allows chaining for rapid relic accumulation.

**Taveuni Disruption on the Kuva Fortress** provides Meso relics at rotation A when run as a Meso Void Fissure. Disruption missions have a unique reward structure that allows skilled players to earn rewards every 2-3 minutes per conduit destroyed, making them excellent for both relic acquisition and void trace farming.

| Location | Mission Type | Reward |
|---|---|---|
| Io, Jupiter | Defense | Meso relic rotation B (every 20 waves) |
| Ukko, Void | Capture | Guaranteed Meso relic |
| Taveuni, Kuva Fortress | Disruption | Meso relic rotation A |
| Any Meso Fissure | Varies | Meso relic + void traces |

## Refinement Costs

Meso relics use the same void trace refinement system as all other tiers:

| Refinement Level | Void Traces Cost | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Intact | 0 | Baseline rare chance (~2%) |
| Exceptional | 25 | Improved rare probability |
| Flawless | 50 | Higher rare probability |
| Radiant | 100 | Maximum rare chance (~10%) |

For highly targeted farming (e.g., completing a Nova Prime or Saryn Prime set), refining to Radiant is strongly recommended. A four-player Radiant share squad using the same relic has a combined probability of approximately 34% for at least one member to receive the rare drop, enabling efficient cooperative farming of specific components.

## Notable Prime Parts in the Meso Era

Meso relics contain components for many popular and high-value Prime sets across the full history of prime releases:

**Rare Drops (high-value targets):**
- **Meso S4** -- Saryn Prime Systems Blueprint (10% Radiant). Saryn Prime is the top AOE DPS warframe, essential for high-level farming.
- **Meso N6** -- Nova Prime Chassis Blueprint (10% Radiant). Nova Prime's Molecular Prime ability slows and amplifies damage to all enemies in range, making her indispensable in organized squads.
- **Meso Z3** -- Zephyr Prime Blueprint (10% Radiant). Zephyr Prime offers extreme mobility and projectile redirection, popular in Corpus-heavy content.
- **Meso T2** -- Tigris Prime Blueprint (10% Radiant). The Tigris Prime shotgun delivers devastating burst damage and remains popular for close-range steel path and assassination content.
- **Meso E3** -- Equinox Prime Chassis Blueprint (10% Radiant). Equinox Prime combines healing and damage in a hybrid playstyle suitable for diverse content.
- **Meso K2** -- Kogake Prime Blueprint (10% Radiant). The Kogake Prime melee weapon has strong modding potential for unarmed melee builds.

**Uncommon Drops (reliable rewards):**
- **Meso B4** -- Boltor Prime Barrel (20% Radiant). The Boltor Prime automatic rifle is a consistent high-damage primary weapon with good ammo efficiency.
- **Meso R2** -- Rubico Prime Stock (20% Radiant). Rubico Prime is the preferred sniper for Eidolon Teralyst hunting and long-range Steel Path content.

## Strategy

**Io Defense Four-Player Farm:** The recommended method for organized groups is four players running Io Defense as a Meso Void Fissure, each carrying four copies of the target Meso relic. Run to rotation B (20 waves), selecting the desired reward at each extraction. This method combines Meso relic acquisition with massive experience income -- Io is a top-3 experience farm in the game.

**Ukko Chain (solo):** Solo players benefit from chaining Ukko Capture runs for rapid Meso relic accumulation without the commitment of endless missions. Collect 20-30 relics, then refine and run targeted fissures in a separate session.

**Radiant Share Coordination:** For Saryn Prime, Nova Prime, or other highly demanded Meso targets, form a dedicated Radiant share squad via recruit chat. All four members bring identical Radiant relics, maximizing the probability that at least one member receives the targeted rare part on each run.

**Relic Deconstruction:** Unwanted Meso relics can be refined to generate void traces rather than run through fissures. If you have surplus common Meso relics containing only items you already own, refine them at a relay console (costs nothing, yields 5-20 traces depending on relic quantity in your inventory).
