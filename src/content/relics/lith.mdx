## Overview

Lith relics are the first tier of void relics in Warframe, introduced alongside the Void Fissure system as a replacement for the original key-based void tower runs. The Lith tier targets lower mastery-rank players and returning veterans looking to build early-generation Prime items efficiently. These relics drop from missions in the inner star chart -- low-to-mid level nodes where enemy levels range from 1 to roughly 30.

Lith relics are named after the Old Norse word for stone, reflecting the ancient Orokin aesthetic that pervades the void. Each relic is a sealed container that players crack open in a Void Fissure mission by collecting 10 reactive void particles called Reactant. Upon extraction, every squad member sees their own reward list and may trade drops with each other using a shared-selection system. This cooperative reward mechanic makes Lith fissures excellent for organized farming squads.

The Lith pool contains items from many Prime sets, including warframes like Ash Prime, Volt Prime, Trinity Prime, and Saryn Prime, as well as weapons such as the Braton Prime, Cernos Prime, Paris Prime, and Gram Prime. Because Lith relics are abundant and their missions are short, Lith farming sessions can produce dozens of relics per hour for experienced squads.

## Drop Locations

Lith relics drop from a variety of accessible missions across the star chart. The most efficient method for raw relic volume is **Hepit Capture on the Void** -- a 30-to-60-second mission that guarantees a Lith relic reward upon completion. Players with fast frames such as Volt, Gauss, or Titania can chain Hepit runs to generate a Lith relic every 90 seconds including loading times.

For players who prefer endless missions, Lith Void Fissures appearing on the Alert overlay offer the best combination of void trace income and relic rewards. Defection missions on Caracol (Saturn) provide Lith relics in rotation A (every two rounds completed), rewarding patient players with multiple relics per session while also generating void traces from fissure kills.

Plains of Eidolon Tier 1 bounties occasionally reward Lith relics in their reward tables, offering an alternative for players who enjoy open-world gameplay. Syndicate daily missions sometimes award bonus Lith relics for players at rank 3 or higher with syndicates.

| Location | Mission Type | Reward |
|---|---|---|
| Hepit, Void | Capture | Guaranteed Lith relic |
| Caracol, Saturn | Defection | Lith relic rotation A/B |
| Any Lith Fissure | Varies | Lith relic + void traces |
| Plains Tier 1 Bounty | Bounty | Lith relic (rotation) |

## Refinement Costs

Void relics can be upgraded at the Void Fissure console in any relay using void traces. Refinement improves the drop probability of rare rewards within the relic:

| Refinement Level | Void Traces Cost | Rare Chance Bonus |
|---|---|---|
| Intact | 0 | Baseline (~2%) |
| Exceptional | 25 | +2% to rare |
| Flawless | 50 | +4% to rare |
| Radiant | 100 | +10% to rare |

Radiant relics increase rare drop chances to approximately 10%, which is the ceiling for most Lith relics. For targeted farming (e.g., hunting a specific rare blueprint to complete a prime warframe), refining to Radiant is recommended. For general farming where any reward is acceptable, running Intact relics conserves void traces and maximizes the total number of fissures completed per session.

Void traces are earned by participating in Void Fissure missions -- each mission rewards 6 to 30 traces based on the number of Reactant collected and any squad bonuses.

## Notable Prime Parts in the Lith Era

Lith relics gate access to numerous Prime items from warframes released across multiple years of content. Key highlights by rarity:

**Rare Drops (targeted farming):**
- **Lith A6** -- Ash Prime Neuroptics Blueprint (10% Radiant). Ash Prime remains popular for its Smokescreen ability and Bladestorm usage in Steel Path content.
- **Lith S6** -- Saryn Prime Neuroptics Blueprint (10% Radiant). Saryn Prime is widely considered the top DPS warframe for large AOE content including arbitrations and steel path farming.
- **Lith V8** -- Volt Prime Chassis Blueprint (10% Radiant). Volt Prime is both a speed utility frame and a solid elemental DPS option.
- **Lith T1** -- Trinity Prime Blueprint (10% Radiant). Trinity Prime is the definitive team-support healer and energy provider.
- **Lith B4** -- Braton Prime Stock (10% Radiant). The Braton Prime is a beloved starter primary weapon with excellent all-round stats.

**Uncommon Drops (efficient early farming):**
- **Lith C6** -- Cernos Prime Lower Limb (20% Radiant). The Cernos Prime is a top-tier bow with exceptional damage.
- **Lith P4** -- Paris Prime Upper Limb (20% Radiant). Paris Prime is available early and delivers strong impact/puncture damage for star chart progression.
- **Lith G3** -- Gram Prime Handle (10% rare). The Gram Prime is a heavy blade with very high base damage, valued for riven disposition.

Rare drops at Radiant refinement have a 10% chance per relic opened. With a four-player squad all using Radiant relics of the same type and choosing the rare reward, each player can receive the rare at approximately 34% effective probability (1 - 0.9^4).

## Strategy

**Hepit Chain (fastest volume):** Solo players and small squads should chain Hepit Capture runs for maximum Lith relic acquisition speed. A single run takes under 2 minutes including loading, and yields one Lith relic per run guaranteed. Refine collected relics to Exceptional or Radiant before running fissures for efficiency.

**Organized Radiant Share:** Form a squad of four players, each using a Radiant version of the same targeted Lith relic. In the end-of-mission selection screen, at least one squad member statistically gets the rare drop about 34% of the time, and everyone can select that reward for sharing credit. Coordinate via recruit chat or Discord farming groups.

**Defection Farming (endless relics + traces):** Run Caracol Defection for back-to-back rotation A rewards (2 squads every cycle = 1 relic per 5-8 minutes) while passively accumulating void traces from fissure kills. This method scales well for squads willing to run longer sessions.

**Relic Rotation Management:** Sort collected Lith relics by the specific Prime part you need. Identify the 2-3 relics that contain your targeted rare, refine those to Radiant, and run them through fissures in focused sessions. Sell or dissolve relics for void traces if they contain only items you already own.
