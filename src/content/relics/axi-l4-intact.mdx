## Overview

Axi L4 Intact is the highest void relic tier, appearing exclusively in challenging void fissure missions (level 60-80+) and endgame content. Axi L4 targets experienced farmers seeking Loki Prime Systems Blueprint and Volt Prime Systems Blueprint, two fundamental warframe components from classic prime releases. The relic's supporting uncommon drops include companion and weapon parts, making Axi L4 valuable for completing multiple prime warframe and equipment builds during dedicated farming campaigns.

## Drops

Axi L4 Intact offers six rewards: one rare-tier and five uncommon-tier items. Loki Prime Systems Blueprint drops at 2% rarity, representing the premium reward. Volt Prime Systems Blueprint and Odonata Prime Blueprint split 11% probabilities each, while three items share 25.33% drops: Forma Blueprint (universally crafted), Bo Prime Ornament (melee cosmetic), and Wyrm Prime Carapace (companion component). This distribution supports multi-frame farming without forcing specialization toward singular objectives.

## Refinement choices

Axi L4 Intact demands significant void trace investment for refinement due to relic scarcity. Exceptional refinement pushes rare probability to ~4% (2.2x baseline), while Flawless reaches ~5% (2.5x), and Radiant climbs to ~6-7% (3-3.5x baseline). Hardened farmers running Void Fissure farming rotations typically invest Radiant refinements when hunting specific rare components. The void trace cost per relic (27-45 traces depending on refinement) necessitates efficient squad composition and extended farming sessions to justify refinement expenses.

## Where to farm

Axi void fissures spawn at endgame star chart locations: Void Capture on Void itself, Void Sabotage on Void stations, and Void Excavation (endless) on Void Cascade. Mission levels scale to 60-80+; effective soloing demands meta frames (Saryn, Equinox, Khora for high-damage support) and optimized weapon builds. Squad fissures unlock additional void tear bonuses; organized farming teams complete rotations in 15-20 minutes per relic, maximizing void trace yields alongside relic rewards. Void Fissure rotations (Mission Rewards A, B, C, beyond) determine Axi relic availability; persistent farming requires tracking active fissure cycles.
