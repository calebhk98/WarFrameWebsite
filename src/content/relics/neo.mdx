## Overview

Neo relics form the third tier of the Warframe void relic system. The name "Neo" derives from the Greek prefix for "new," and fittingly the Neo pool frequently receives additions from the most recently released Prime warframes and weapons. Players who reach the Neo tier are generally mid-to-late game veterans who have unlocked the majority of the star chart and possess capable loadouts for level 30-50 missions.

Neo relics represent a significant step up in the value and recency of Prime components compared to the Lith and Meso tiers. Many sought-after warframes appear exclusively or primarily in the Neo pool: Khora Prime (pet-based CC and damage), Gara Prime (glass-themed damage and defense), Ash Prime (stealth and global assassination), and Banshee Prime (sonic damage amplification). Weapons such as the Rubico Prime (premier Eidolon hunting sniper) and Tigris Prime (burst shotgun) also have key components in Neo relics.

The Neo tier also benefits from having several farming-optimized mission nodes, most notably Hydron on Sedna -- one of the most popular experience farms in the game that doubles as an excellent Neo relic source.

## Drop Locations

**Hydron Defense (Sedna)** is the premier Neo relic farming node in the game. As a Wave 20/40 defense mission, Hydron rewards a Neo relic at rotation B (every 20 waves). More importantly, Hydron is the top experience farm for leveling warframes and weapons, meaning players get maximum utility from every session. A 40-wave Hydron run typically takes 20-30 minutes with a competent squad and yields 2 Neo relics plus experience for two full weapon or warframe cycles.

**Ukko Capture on the Void** at Neo tier provides a guaranteed Neo relic per run, enabling fast solo accumulation at 60-90 seconds per run. This is the preferred method for players who want raw relic volume without the time commitment of endless missions.

**Sanctuary Onslaught** at conduits 5 and 6 provides Neo relics as rotation rewards, offering an alternative for players who enjoy Onslaught's chaotic gameplay and the bonus of earning focus at the same time.

| Location | Mission Type | Reward |
|---|---|---|
| Hydron, Sedna | Defense | Neo relic rotation B (wave 20/40) |
| Ukko, Void (Neo fissure) | Capture | Guaranteed Neo relic |
| Xini, Eris | Interception | Neo relics rotation A/B |
| Sanctuary Onslaught | Onslaught | Neo relics conduits 5-6 |

## Refinement Costs

Neo relics use the standard void trace refinement costs shared across all relic tiers:

| Refinement Level | Void Traces | Effect on Rare |
|---|---|---|
| Intact | 0 | ~2% rare drop chance |
| Exceptional | 25 | ~4% rare drop chance |
| Flawless | 50 | ~6% rare drop chance |
| Radiant | 100 | ~10% rare drop chance |

The Neo tier is one of the most active for Radiant share groups because of the high market value of items like Khora Prime Chassis and Rubico Prime Receiver. Many players in recruit chat advertise specifically for Neo Radiant share runs targeting these items.

## Notable Prime Parts in the Neo Era

The Neo pool contains components for recent and highly valued Prime releases, making it a priority farming tier for many players:

**Rare Drops (high demand):**
- **Neo K3** -- Khora Prime Chassis Blueprint (10% Radiant). Khora Prime uses her Strangledome ability to crowd-control entire rooms and her Venari companion for adaptive damage. She is a top-tier Steel Path warframe.
- **Neo G3** -- Gara Prime Chassis Blueprint (10% Radiant). Gara Prime's Splinter Storm ability creates a high-damage scaling defense build popular in Arbitrations.
- **Neo R5** -- Rubico Prime Receiver (10% Radiant). The Rubico Prime is considered the best sniper rifle for Eidolon Teralyst hunting, dealing high critical damage with the Hunter Munitions mod.
- **Neo A5** -- Ash Prime Systems Blueprint (10% Radiant). Ash Prime's Blade Storm ability can clear entire rooms instantly, making him excellent for Steel Path and sortie clearing.
- **Neo B5** -- Banshee Prime Systems Blueprint (10% Radiant). Banshee Prime's Sonar ability creates weak points that multiply damage from all sources, enabling team-based damage multiplication strategies.
- **Neo E1** -- Ember Prime Neuroptics Blueprint (10% Radiant). Ember Prime is a classic AOE fire damage dealer, re-released with improved stats and ability synergies.
- **Neo Z8** -- Zephyr Prime Systems Blueprint (10% Radiant). Zephyr Prime's Turbulence ability deflects projectiles, making her excellent against Corpus and Grineer gunfire.

**Uncommon Drops (reliable value):**
- **Neo T4** -- Tigris Prime Barrel (20% Radiant). The Tigris Prime shotgun delivers the highest per-shot burst damage of any primary weapon, popular for one-shotting heavily armored targets in Steel Path.

## Strategy

**Hydron Defense with Purpose:** The ideal Neo farming session combines Hydron experience runs with Neo Void Fissure activation. Queue for Hydron during a Neo Void Fissure event with the specific Neo relic you want to crack. Collect 10 Reactant during the waves, then at extraction select the reward. This maximizes utility per session -- you farm relics and level gear simultaneously.

**Targeted Radiant Share for Khora/Gara:** Khora Prime and Gara Prime components command high platinum prices on warframe.market when sold between players. Organize a four-player Radiant share squad via recruit chat targeting Neo K3 or Neo G3. Each player uses 4 Radiant copies of the target relic per session, cycling through for maximum efficiency.

**Onslaught for Dual-Purpose Farming:** Sanctuary Onslaught Neo runs provide focus points alongside relic rewards. Players leveling focus schools can gain thousands of focus per conduit while passively accumulating Neo relics for later refinement and fissure runs.

**Relic Sorting:** Keep a dedicated inventory of Neo relics sorted by target item. Refine only the relics containing your current priority item to Radiant; leave others at Intact for future use or void trace generation. This prevents trace waste on non-priority relics.
