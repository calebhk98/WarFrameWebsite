## Overview

Requiem I Intact is a specialized void relic exclusive to Kuva Lich hunting missions, representing a distinct reward branch separate from standard Lith-Axi tiers. Requiem relics contain Kuva Lich-specific rewards including requiem mods (Lohk, Xata), utility resources (Kuva, Ayatan Stars), and crafting components (Exilus Weapon Adapter Blueprints, Riven Slivers). This relic serves players engaged in long-term Kuva Lich elimination campaigns and riven customization paths, making it essential for endgame weaponcrafting.

## Drops

Requiem I Intact exclusively rewards items tied to Kuva Lich mechanics and weapon modification. The rare-tier drop (2% chance) is Exilus Weapon Adapter Blueprint, enabling players to unlock exilus mods on weapons. Five uncommon drops (11% and 25.33% probabilities each) include two requiem mods critical for Kuva Lich combat (Lohk and Xata), bulk Kuva for riven cycling, Ayatan Amber Stars for Endo farming, and Riven Slivers for riven meteorite research. This mix directly enables both riven farming and Lich progression simultaneously.

## Refinement choices

Requiem I Intact provides baseline reward rates for Kuva Lich Sister missions. Exceptional refinement (+10%) moderately increases rare drops to ~4%, while Flawless (+20%) climbs to ~5%. Radiant refinement (+30%) approaches 6-7%, significantly boosting Exilus Adapter chances. Kuva Lich hunters often preserve Radiant refinements specifically for requiem farming, as multiple Liches require sustained requiem mod supplies. Riven farmers may prioritize intact or exceptional refinement, since Kuva and Riven Slivers already appear at high frequencies without refinement investment.

## Where to farm

Requiem relics appear exclusively in Kuva Lich assassination missions and Sister of Parvos encounter variants. Kuva Lich spawning requires murdering thralls in regular star chart missions to fill meter progress. Each Lich assassination attempt (whether successful or not) yields Requiem relic rewards alongside Kuva acquisition. Sisters occupy Void Fissure missions within Zariman and engage through Void Cascade encounters. Farming efficiency depends on Lich murmur progress (requiem mod sequence research); veteran Lich hunters maintain queues of known Lich murmurs, enabling rapid assassination and relic collection cycles. Average Lich elimination from thrall spawning to final assassination spans 8-12 missions.

## Notes

Requiem I is permanently available during Kuva Lich and Sister of Parvos activities; no vaulting mechanics apply. Relic acquisition depends on active Lich progression; inactive accounts without spawned Liches cannot access Requiem relics. Exilus Weapon Adapter Blueprints demand consistent investment as players expand weapon mod accessibility. Requiem mods themselves drop from relic cracks; players must spend void fissure currency (void traces) to crack relics even during Lich assassination. Kuva Lich farming paradoxically generates both massive Kuva income and Riven Slivers simultaneously, creating secondary farming loops for riven meteorite enhancement. Community Kuva Lich databases track frame weapon combinations and Lich generation patterns, enabling optimized Lich queue planning.
