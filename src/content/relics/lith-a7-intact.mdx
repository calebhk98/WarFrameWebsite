## Overview

Lith A7 Intact is a Lith-era void relic valued for its access to prime component drops from multiple contemporary and recent prime release cycles. As a Lith (lowest) era relic, it offers lower difficulty fissure missions and is frequently sought by newer players building their prime arsenal. This relic contains components from Wisp Prime and Sevagoth Prime, two popular warframes with strong gameplay mechanics in mid-game content.

## Drops

Lith A7 Intact contains six distinct reward items across common, uncommon, and rare tiers. The uncommon tier (25.33% chance each) features diverse prime components including Cedo Prime Stock, Sevagoth Prime Systems Blueprint, and Nautilus Prime Carapace. The rare drop (2% chance) yields the Akarius Prime Receiver, a high-value component. Fulmin Prime Blueprint and Wisp Prime Neuroptics Blueprint round out the uncommon pool with 11% individual chances each.

## Refinement choices

Lith A7 Intact represents the base refinement tier, providing the foundation drop table. Players refinement through void fissure missions by using void traces to enhance relic integrity. Exceptional and Flawless refinements increase rare drop chances, while Radiant refinement offers the maximum rare-drop probability. For Lith A7, intact refinement delivers reliable access to common and uncommon components with manageable drop rates.

## Where to farm

Lith-era fissure missions are the primary source for Lith A7 Intact relics. These missions scale to low enemy levels (10-30) and can be accessed on any planet from the star chart during void fissure activation events. Void capture and void sabotage missions on the Origin System nodes (Earth, Venus, Mercury, Mars) typically offer Lith relics as mission rewards in early rotations. Lith fissure missions are ideal for players mastering weapon leveling while farming components, as the reduced difficulty allows focus on void damage cracking relics rather than survival mechanics.

## Notes

Lith A7 is currently vaulted, meaning it no longer appears in fissure reward tables from active void tear events. Historical void fissure records confirm that Lith A7 dropped during the tennocon period and may return during future unvault rotations. The relic's components span multiple prime access eras, reflecting the procedural assembly of void relic loot pools. Trading on warframe.market remains the primary acquisition path for players seeking this relic outside fissure missions. Void trace investment into relic refinement increases rare-component probability, making Radiant refinement worthwhile if seeking Akarius Prime Receiver or completing warframe systems blueprints.
