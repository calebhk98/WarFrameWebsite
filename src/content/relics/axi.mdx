## Overview

Axi relics are the pinnacle standard tier of the Warframe void relic system, named after the Greek word for "worthy" or "deserving." This naming reflects the endgame challenge required to farm them efficiently and the high value of the Prime components contained within. Axi relics drop exclusively from the most demanding missions in the game -- high-level endless missions in the Void, Eris, Pluto, and Lua -- with enemy levels typically ranging from 40 to 100 or beyond.

The Axi pool contains components for the most recently released Prime warframes and weapons, making Axi fissures the primary destination for players chasing the newest Prime additions to the game. When a new Prime Access launches, the featured warframe and weapon components appear in Axi relics first, driving significant player traffic to Axi fissure missions during the initial weeks of a new Prime release.

High-demand Axi targets historically include Mesa Prime (the dominant mobile turret warframe), Nidus Prime (the resilient adaptive warframe with stacking mutation), Wisp Prime (the premier damage-and-buff support warframe), and Khora Prime. Weapons like the Rubico Prime, Gram Prime, and Akbronco Prime also have key components in the Axi tier.

## Drop Locations

**Mot Survival (Void)** is the standard endgame Axi farming node. Mot is a survival mission in the Void with Level 40-60+ Corrupted enemies (scaling infinitely over time). A typical Axi Void Fissure run on Mot yields one relic reward at 5 minutes, with additional rewards at 10, 15, and 20 minutes for extended runs. The Void tile set is efficient for Axi fissure Reactant collection due to the density of Corrupted enemies and the prevalence of narrow corridors that funnel enemy spawns.

**Xini Interception (Eris)** is a popular Axi farming node because interception missions provide reliable Axi relics at rotation C (after three full rounds of capturing four towers), while also being highly conducive to organized squad play. Xini has an extremely large player base and is frequently listed in recruit chat for organized Axi farming.

**Hieracon Excavation (Pluto)** offers Axi relics at rotation B and C, making it a good choice for players who prefer excavation missions. Each extractor provides a reward, and multiple extractors can be defended simultaneously in a coordinated squad, enabling rapid rotation cycling.

| Location | Mission Type | Reward |
|---|---|---|
| Mot, Void | Survival | Axi relic every 5 minutes (rotation A onwards) |
| Xini, Eris | Interception | Axi relic rotation B/C |
| Hieracon, Pluto | Excavation | Axi relic rotation B/C |
| Mithra, Void | Interception | Axi relic all rotations |
| Apollo, Lua | Disruption | Axi relic rotation A |

## Refinement Costs

Axi relics share the same void trace refinement costs as all other tiers, but the high value of Axi rare drops makes Radiant refinement especially important:

| Refinement Level | Void Traces | Rare Drop Chance |
|---|---|---|
| Intact | 0 | ~2% |
| Exceptional | 25 | ~4% |
| Flawless | 50 | ~6% |
| Radiant | 100 | ~10% |

Because Axi rare drops (Mesa Prime Neuroptics, Wisp Prime Blueprint, Nidus Prime Neuroptics) command the highest platinum prices on the player market, Radiant refinement is almost always worth the 100-trace investment. A four-player Radiant share squad targeting the same rare item achieves approximately 34% probability that at least one player receives the rare, making organized Axi squads the most efficient path to expensive Prime components.

## Notable Prime Parts in the Axi Era

The Axi tier contains the most sought-after and highest-value Prime components in the current rotation:

**Rare Drops (top priority):**
- **Axi W2** -- Wisp Prime Blueprint (10% Radiant). Wisp Prime is the top-ranked support warframe with Motes that provide flat health bonuses, regeneration, and speed buffs. Her damage amplification from Breach Surge and Sol Gate makes her the ideal support for high-end content. Blueprint components are highly valued.
- **Axi N5** -- Nidus Prime Neuroptics Blueprint (10% Radiant). Nidus Prime has infinite scaling through his Mutation stacks and is completely immune to status effects at max mutation, making him one of the most durable warframes in endurance content.
- **Axi M1** -- Mesa Prime Neuroptics Blueprint (10% Radiant). Mesa Prime's Peacemaker ability makes her the definitive stat-stick DPS for Steel Path and void fissure farming. She is among the most used endgame warframes.
- **Axi K6** -- Khora Prime Neuroptics Blueprint (10% Radiant). Khora Prime combines crowd control, companion synergy, and high damage in a package suitable for diverse endgame missions.
- **Axi H5** -- Hydroid Prime Blueprint (10% Radiant). Hydroid Prime brings consistent water-based crowd control and resource boost with the Pilfering Swarm build variant.
- **Axi R4** -- Rubico Prime Blueprint (10% Radiant). The Rubico Prime sniper is the premier weapon for Eidolon hunting, capable of stripping Eidolon shields with Corrosive+Cold modding.
- **Axi A12** -- Akbronco Prime Receiver (10% Radiant). Akbronco Prime is a secondary shotgun with high pellet count, valued for secondary-focused builds.
- **Axi G6** -- Gram Prime Blade (10% Radiant). The Gram Prime heavy blade has the highest base damage of any melee weapon and excellent modding potential for high-strength builds.

## Strategy

**Organized Mot Survival Squad:** The classic Axi farming meta involves four experienced players running Mot Survival as a Void Fissure. Standard compositions include a Nekros Prime with Despoil (for Reactant drop amplification from enemy desecration), a Khora with Pilfering Strangledome (additional drop chance), a damage frame (Saryn or Equinox for efficient wave clearing), and a tank/utility frame (Nidus or Inaros for survivability at high enemy levels). This composition maximizes Reactant collection speed, enabling multiple reward rotations per session.

**Mithra Interception (Best AFK-Friendly Option):** Mithra Interception in the Void is commonly cited as the most efficient Axi farming node for players who use wave-clearing frames. A Saryn or Mesa can hold all four capture points simultaneously while the squad collects Reactant passively. This method rewards patient players with multiple Axi relics per 10-15 minute session.

**Coordinated Radiant Share for Mesa/Wisp:** Mesa Prime and Wisp Prime command 50-200 platinum each on warframe.market for their rarest components. Forming a dedicated four-player squad targeting Axi M1 (Radiant) or Axi W2 (Radiant) generates significant trade value per hour. Coordinate via Discord farming communities or Warframe's official recruit chat.

**Endurance Scaling:** For players farming Axi relics in endless missions like Mot Survival, extending beyond 20 minutes provides no additional relic reward improvement (rotation rewards cycle through A-B-C-A). The decision to stay longer should be based on personal survivability capability and void trace income rather than relic quantity.
