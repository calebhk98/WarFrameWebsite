## Overview

Meso D6 Intact is a mid-tier void relic containing diverse prime components spanning multiple past prime release cycles. As a Meso-era relic, it appears in moderate-difficulty void fissure missions (level 20-40) and serves as a bridge between early-game Lith farming and late-game Axi hunting. Meso D6 is particularly valued for access to Nezha Prime Blueprint and Octavia Prime Systems Blueprint, two sought-after warframe components that enable players to construct powerful prime frames mid-game progression.

## Drops

Meso D6 Intact features six reward items including the rare-tier Dethcube Prime Cerebrum (2% chance) and five uncommon-tier drops. Three items share 25.33% drop probabilities each: Ivara Prime Neuroptics Blueprint, Octavia Prime Systems Blueprint, and Paris Prime Blueprint. Two additional uncommons appear at lower 11% individual rates: Aksomati Prime Barrel and Nezha Prime Blueprint. This distribution reflects the relic's focus on supporting multiple warframe build paths simultaneously.

## Refinement choices

Meso D6 Intact provides baseline drop rates for void fissure farming. Refinement progression (Exceptional, Flawless, Radiant) increases the rare-tier drop probability by approximately 10% per tier, making Radiant refinement valuable when specifically hunting Dethcube Prime Cerebrum. Players with sufficient void traces can invest in Flawless or Radiant refinements to stack probability bonuses, especially during dedicated farming campaigns for critical warframe blueprint pieces.

## Where to farm

Meso-era void fissures spawn primarily on mid-chart planets including Venus, Mars, Phobos, and Ceres. Void Sabotage and Void Capture missions on these nodes offer Meso relics in standard rotation rewards. Void Excavation on Phobos (Endless) provides consistent Meso relic rewards and doubles farming efficiency by allowing players to collect relics while farming other resources. Recommended Meso fissure nodes scale to level 30-45 enemy forces, supporting efficient mission completion with properly modded frames and weapons. Party composition matters less in Meso fissures than in higher tiers; solo players can complete missions efficiently with minimal difficulty.
