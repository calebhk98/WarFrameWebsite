## Overview

Axi H4 Intact is a premium endgame void relic offering high-value warframe and weapon components from classic and contemporary prime releases. Axi H4 stands out for dual warframe system blueprints (Hydroid Prime and Mesa Prime chassis), making it attractive for players building multiple prime frames simultaneously. The rare drop (Hydroid Prime Systems Blueprint) and abundant uncommon reward slots enable efficient multi-objective farming: warframe completion, weapon component acquisition, and Forma scaling in parallel runs.

## Drops

Axi H4 Intact contains six distinct items with a 2% rare drop (Hydroid Prime Systems Blueprint) and five uncommons. Notably, the relic offers a 2X Forma Blueprint drop (double Forma per relic), exceptional for players mass-crafting Forma for weapon builds and frame leveling. Mesa Prime Chassis Blueprint and Mirage Prime Chassis Blueprint appear at 25.33% each, supporting two distinct warframe completions. Supporting drops include Akbolto Prime Link (secondary weapon component, 11%) and Paris Prime Upper Limb (primary bow component, 25.33%).

## Refinement choices

Axi H4 Intact baseline provides reliable access to Mesa Prime and Mirage Prime chassis without refinement investment. Exceptional refinement raises rare probability to ~4%, meaningfully increasing Hydroid Prime Systems odds. Flawless refinement (+20%) targets ~5%, while Radiant (+30%) approaches 6-7% probability. Given void trace costs (27-45 traces per relic based on refinement), players should reserve Radiant refinements for Hydroid Prime Systems hunts. Intact refinement offers excellent value for Forma and warframe chassis farming without premium trace expenditure.

## Where to farm

Axi void fissures spawn on outermost star chart nodes: Void itself, end-game void locations, and void tear encounters. Void Capture on Void provides consistent Axi relic rewards in reasonable 10-15 minute mission windows. Void Sabotage supports longer farming rotations while generating void trace rewards alongside relics. Void Excavation (endless) on Void Cascade enables 40-60+ minute infinite farming, maximizing relic accumulation per session. Mission scaling reaches level 60-80+; meta farming frames (Saryn Prime, Equinox Prime, Mesa Prime for self-sufficiency) enable solo execution. Organized squad farming adds void trace bonuses and accelerates rotation completion.
